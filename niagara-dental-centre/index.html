The practice

Niagara Dental Centre is a modern dental clinic specializing in advanced diagnostics and the treatment of dental and oral conditions for patients of all ages, proudly caring for families in our community with attentive, personalized treatment in a warm and welcoming setting.

We proudly care for patients of all ages, creating a welcoming environment where every member of your family feels comfortable and valued.

Our office uses high-quality, state-of-the-art equipment to provide accurate diagnoses and efficient, effective treatment.

From our refined setting to in-office sleep sedation dentistry, we are committed to making your experience as relaxed and stress-free as possible.

Dr. Sante Aceti and Dr. Juliana Galessiere are committed to providing personalized, high-quality dental care for patients of all ages, combining advanced technology with a gentle approach to help every family member feel comfortable and confident in their smile.
