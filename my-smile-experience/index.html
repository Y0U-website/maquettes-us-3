The practice

The path to a healthy smile often begins much sooner than people realize. While many wait until the teenage years, Dr. Butt recommends that the orthodontic journey start as early as age six .

Our approach distinguishes between the alignment of the teeth and the foundation of the jaw. When we see a child early, we can address orthopedic concerns the way the jaws sit together. If a jaw is too far forward or back, addressing it while the child is still developing prevents more complex deformities later in life.

As the permanent teeth begin to emerge around ages 10 to 11 , we transition into the art of alignment. Whether using traditional braces, functional appliances, or clear aligners, we tailor the treatment to the specific biological needs of the patient.
