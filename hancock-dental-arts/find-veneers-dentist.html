Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front of teeth to improve shape, color, and alignment. They are crafted for natural esthetics and durability, transforming the smile while maintaining tooth structure.

Veneers bond to the front of teeth, creating a smooth, natural look. Digital scanning and lab artistry ensure a precise fit and lifelike translucency.

What’s the difference between veneers, crowns, and bonding?

Veneers cover only the front of the tooth, crowns encase the entire structure, and bonding uses resin for minor corrections. Veneers are the ideal balance of esthetics and conservation.

Porcelain veneers are made from high-strength, translucent ceramic that mimics natural enamel. They resist stains and last for many years.

With proper care, they can last 10–20+ years. Dr. Amy Hughes has seen cases lasting 20–30 years with minimal wear.

How do prepless veneers compare to traditional porcelain veneers?

Prepless veneers are less invasive but may not correct significant misalignment or color issues. Porcelain veneers offer more versatility for dramatic smile transformations.
