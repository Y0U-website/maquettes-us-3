Braces, Invisalign & Retainers

Treatments Orthodontic Care for All Ages At Mums Orthodontics, we create personalized treatment plan for kids, teens and adults in Paoli, PA and the surrounding area including Malvern, Chesterbrook, King of Prussia, Wayne and Berwyn.

We recommend an orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early treatment can guide jaw growth, create space for incoming teeth, and help prevent more complex issues later. Dr. Jessie Chang customizes gentle, effective plans for growing smiles.

We offer teen-friendly options like metal braces, ceramic braces, and Invisalign Teen. Our flexible scheduling—including Saturdays—makes it easy for busy families to get the care they need.

It’s never too late for a confident smile! We provide options like Invisalign and clear braces for adults, with customized plans that fit your lifestyle and goals.

Braces

Our modern metal braces are durable, effective, and designed for comfort. Dr. Jessie Chang uses the latest techniques to gently guide teeth into alignment for a healthy, beautiful smile.

Ceramic braces blend with your natural tooth color for a more discreet look. They offer the same reliable results as metal braces, making them a popular choice for teens and adults.

Invisalign®

Invisalign® The Clear Alternative to Braces® uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gently move your teeth. Dr. Jessie Chang customizes each plan using advanced 3D scanning for a precise, comfortable fit. Enjoy the freedom to eat your favorite foods and brush with ease without brackets or wires.

Retainers

Keep your new smile looking its best! Mums Orthodontics offers custom retainers to maintain your results for years to come. We provide ongoing support and care so your smile stays amazing.

Advanced Technology

We use the latest digital technology to make your orthodontic experience more comfortable, precise, and efficient. From digital X-ray to 3D scanning, Mums Orthodontics invests in tools that help you achieve your best smile.

Our iTero scanner creates fast, accurate 3D images—no goopy impressions needed! This technology helps us plan your treatment and design aligners or retainers with precision.

Digital X-rays provide detailed images with less radiation than traditional film. We use these images to diagnose and monitor your orthodontic progress safely and efficiently.
