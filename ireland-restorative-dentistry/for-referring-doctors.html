Dr. Michelle Ireland, DMD

Dr. Ireland is a certified prosthodontist with over 30 years of experience in fixed and removable prosthodontics. She received her DMD from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry in 1994 and her Certificate in Advanced Prosthodontics in 1996 — going on to serve as a professor in the UA Prosthodontic Department from 1999 to 2008.

General dentists across Cherokee County and North Georgia refer their most complex cases to Dr. Ireland knowing those patients will receive specialist-level care, be returned to them with a full treatment report, and experience a practice that treats them like family.
