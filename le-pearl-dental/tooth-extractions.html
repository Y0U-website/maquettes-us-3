Tooth extractions made easy

At Le Pearl Dental, we believe in preserving your natural teeth whenever possible, but sometimes an extraction is the best option for maintaining your oral health. Tooth extractions are a common procedure to relieve overcrowding, remove severely damaged teeth, or prepare your mouth for orthodontic treatment.

How tooth extraction benefits your oral health

Alleviates pain and discomfort

Extraction can relieve pain caused by severely damaged or decayed teeth that cannot be saved.

Prevents infection spread

Supports orthodontic treatment

Eliminates wisdom tooth complications

Tooth extractions at Le Pearl Dental

  • Initial examination – Our Alexandria tooth extraction team will assess the tooth and surrounding area using x-rays to determine the best approach for extraction.
  • Comfortable anesthesia – We administer local anesthesia to ensure you remain comfortable and pain-free throughout the procedure.
  • Gentle removal – Using precise techniques, we carefully extract the tooth from its socket with minimal discomfort.
  • Post-extraction care – You’ll receive detailed aftercare instructions to promote healing, and a follow-up visit will be scheduled to monitor your recovery and discuss tooth replacement options.
