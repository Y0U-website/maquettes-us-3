Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults are good candidates for dental implants. During your consultation, we evaluate your oral health, your jawbone support, and your goals for restoration. Dental implants may be a strong option if you:

If you have been told you do not qualify for implants, a second opinion can be helpful. In some cases, options like bone grafting can make implant treatment possible.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a fixed, non-removable solution
  • Have healthy gums or are willing to treat gum disease first
  • Have adequate jawbone support (or may benefit from bone grafting)
  • Are in overall good health

What to Expect: The Dental Implant Process

Dental implant treatment is typically completed in phases to ensure predictable healing and strong long-term results. Your exact timeline depends on your unique needs, but most implant treatment follows these steps:

Our team will clearly explain your recommended steps, expected healing time, and what you can do to support successful outcomes.

  • Consultation and planning: Exam, digital imaging, and a personalized treatment plan
  • Implant placement: The implant is placed into the jawbone
  • Healing and integration: The implant bonds naturally with the bone over time
  • Final restoration: A custom crown or restoration is attached for full function and aesthetics

Implants for One Tooth, Multiple Teeth, or Full-Arch Restoration

Dental implants can be used in several ways depending on how many teeth you need to replace. Common implant-supported solutions include:

We will recommend the option that best fits your health, comfort, budget, and long-term goals.

  • Single-tooth implants: Replace one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown
  • Implant-supported bridges: Replace multiple teeth without relying on neighboring teeth for support
  • Implant-supported dentures: Improve stability for partial or full dentures (if recommended)

Benefits Beyond Aesthetics: Restorative Outcomes

Implants are not just cosmetic. Replacing missing teeth helps maintain healthy function and protects your oral health over time. Dental implants can help:

  • Reduce shifting and bite changes caused by open spaces
  • Support healthier jaw joints by improving balance and chewing function
  • Lower the risk of bone loss after tooth loss
  • Improve speech and comfort compared to removable options

Dental Implants FAQ

How long do dental implants last? With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last for many years and often decades. Longevity depends on health factors and maintenance.

Does getting a dental implant hurt? Most patients report that implant placement is more comfortable than expected. We prioritize your comfort and will explain what to expect before treatment begins.

How long does the implant process take? Timelines vary. Some cases require additional healing time, while others can be completed more efficiently. Your consultation will clarify your expected timeline.

Can implants replace multiple missing teeth? Yes. Implants can support crowns, bridges, and implant-supported dentures depending on your needs.

What if I’m missing bone? Bone grafting may be an option to rebuild support for implant placement. We will evaluate this during your consultation.
