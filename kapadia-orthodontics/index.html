Starting orthodontic treatment early in childhood can help prevent dental problems from worsening later on. Early intervention can also help avoid tooth extractions, tooth impactions, severe crowding, and even crossbites.

It’s never too late to enjoy the benefits of orthodontic treatment. Kapadia Orthodontics is proud to have many treatment options available for adult braces .
