How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups. The replacement tooth (crown) may need to be replaced after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is done with local anesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during the surgery. After the procedure, you might feel some mild discomfort, which can be managed with pain killer medication.
