Dr Rebecca Tsuei

Dr. Tsuei grew up in Cupertino and went to Monta Vista High School like many of her patients. She went on to complete both her undergraduate degree and dental training at UCLA. During her time there, she served on student leadership, worked in a lab that collaborated with NASA to send mice to space, and did research on the effect of micro-implant skeletal expansion on orthodontic tooth movement.

After dental school, Dr. Tsuei moved to the windy city of Chicago to pursue her post graduate training in orthodontics at the University of Illinois Chicago. She survived the cold winters by drinking a lot of hot chocolate and running away her winter blues along the Chicago lake front, even completing a half marathon with her fellow co-residents! Upon receiving her orthodontics certificate and Masters in Oral Biology, Dr. Tsuei went through a rigorous board certification process to ensure that she was able to provide the highest level of patient care.

Now a Board Certified orthodontist back in the South Bay, Dr. Tsuei is excited to join Dr. Chen, bringing her esthetic eye for detail and her love for helping children and adults achieve their best and most confident smiles. Her goal is to combine the latest technologies with exceptional patient care to give back to the wonderful community she grew up in. In her free time, Dr. Tsuei enjoys teaching pilates, going on hiking trips with her husband, and visiting new coffee shops in search of the city’s best matcha.
