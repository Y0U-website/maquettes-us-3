Les soins

If you want to maintain your healthy smile, we provide preventive services like teeth cleanings and exams; if you have issues like cavities, missing teeth, or gum disease, we ll personalize a treatment plan to resolve them and restore your healthy smile.

Many people prefer dental implants to replace missing teeth because they offer several benefits that dentures don’t. Unlike a denture that sits on top of the gums, an implant is a titanium post placed surgically into the jawbone.

Nearly anyone with missing teeth is a good implant candidate. Your gums must be healthy and free of periodontal disease, and there must be enough dense bone to support the implant; if not, bone grafting is an option that would make an implant possible for you…

Want to improve your smile s appearance? We offer cosmetic dentistry, including veneers and teeth whitening, to help you achieve your dream smile.
