The practice

Dr. Mike Sulens is the beating heart of Sulens Dental Studio. He loves his patients, community and family and it shows. His sense of fun and joy in his work inspires every staff member in our office. And his skill and insistence on excellence of care create crazy levels of loyalty among our patients.

When you visit our office, you’ll receive an extraordinary quality of care. It starts with a beautiful office, an atmosphere where you are treated like family and Dwight, our therapy dog. Of course, it includes superlative dental care in which your comfort and health are top priorities. It’s important to us that your visits are so enjoyable that you hardly wait for your next visit.

We created this office for your comfort and enjoyment. Yes, it’s a treat for the eyes but it was also designed from the ground up for the comfort and efficient care of our patients. We are actively redefining the art of dentistry with both our service and our facilities.

We take pride in providing our community with high-quality comprehensive and cosmetic dental care in an exceptional environment. Our goal is to ensure our patients maintain excellent dental health and have smiles they are pleased with. Please let us know if you have any concerns about your dental health or if you wish for a more attractive, aesthetic smile.
