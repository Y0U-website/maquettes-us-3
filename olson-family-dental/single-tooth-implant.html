Single-Tooth Implant

A single-tooth implant is one of the most effective ways to replace a missing tooth while preserving the health and function of the surrounding teeth. At Minot Smiles Dental Co., Dr. Megan Olson provides implant-supported tooth replacement designed to look, feel, and function like a natural tooth.

What Is a Single-Tooth Implant?

Permanent Tooth Replacement in Minot

A single-tooth implant consists of a small titanium implant placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. After the implant integrates with the bone, a custom restoration is attached to complete the replacement. Unlike traditional bridges, single-tooth implants do not require modification of neighboring teeth and help maintain healthy jawbone structure.

According to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, more than 35 million Americans are missing all of their teeth, and millions more are missing individual teeth. Benefits of Single-Tooth Implants in Minot

Natural Function

Single-tooth implants allow patients to chew, speak, and smile comfortably while closely replicating the function of a natural tooth.

Preservation of Jawbone Health

The implant stimulates the jawbone in a way that helps reduce the bone loss that commonly occurs after tooth loss.

Protection of Neighboring Teeth

Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, helping preserve healthy tooth structure.

Questions Minot Patients Ask About Single-Tooth Implants

Am I a candidate for a single-tooth implant?

Many healthy adults with adequate jawbone support are candidates for implant treatment. Dr. Olson evaluates each patient’s oral health, medical history, and bone levels before making a recommendation.

How long does the implant process take?

Treatment timelines vary, but most implant cases involve several months of healing between implant placement and final restoration placement.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes. Implant restorations are custom-designed to match surrounding teeth in color, shape, and size, creating a natural appearance.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for decades and often for a lifetime.

Will I be without a tooth during treatment?

Depending on the location of the missing tooth and treatment plan, temporary replacement options may be available while the implant heals.
