About Our Kerrville Dental Practice

Welcome to your new dental home.

Why choose Paige Priour DDS?

We know you have many options for your dental home, and choosing the right dental office is critical. You want to be sure that your dentist is a good fit for you and your family. Could we be the right fit for you? Take a look at what makes us, us.

Comfort and privacy

When you walk into our dental office, the first thing you ll notice is how comfortable and relaxing the environment is. We ve worked hard to create a space that feels like home, where you can feel at ease. From our reception area, to our private patient rooms and plush chairs, to our caring staff, we want to make sure your visit is as pleasant as possible.

All-female staff

We re proud to have an all-female staff who are dedicated to providing quality care. We believe that this allows us to create a warm and welcoming environment for our patients.

Modern dentistry

Dentistry today is not the type of dentistry that you might have experienced as a kid, or even five years ago. Our clinical team completes gobs of dental continuing education each year to ensure that your visit is comfortable, quick, and most importantly, effective. What’s more, our dental office is equipped with the latest diagnostic technology to provide you with the best possible care.

Focus on whole body health

At our dental office, we believe whole body health is essential when it comes to achieving optimal oral health. We strive to optimize your overall well-being as well as your oral health. We work collaboratively with specialists and other healthcare providers in our community to ensure that all aspects of your health are considered when developing a personalized treatment plan.

Accommodating

We understand that everyone s needs are different, which is why we offer a variety of appointment times that fit your schedule, as well as payment options such as CareCredit. So if you re looking for a dentist who puts your needs first, look no further than our practice!
