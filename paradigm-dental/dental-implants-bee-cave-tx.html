Am I a candidate for dental implants in Bee Cave, TX?

If you want to restore your smile with dental implants, you’re likely a good candidate. You must have enough jawbone to support the implant post itself, but if you do and have generally good dental hygiene, dental implants are likely a great option for you. Your Bee Cave dentist at Paradigm Dental will help you determine whether or not implants are right for you.

What to expect when getting dental implants?

Getting dental implants usually takes a few visits. Although we have the capacity to fabricate your crowns in one day with our in-house lab, your implants themselves will need to heal. At your first appointment, Dr. Shalaby will implant the post into your jawbone. Once placed, your implants will need to fuse to your jawbone through a process called osteointegration.

Once your jawbone is healed, you will come back to our Bee Cave dental office to get your porcelain crowns placed on top of your implants. Because your crowns are custom made in our in-house lab, you can expect handcrafted workmanship, high-

quality materials, and a truly customized crown.
