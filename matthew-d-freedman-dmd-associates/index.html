The practice

By entrusting and embracing our dental practice, patients partner with us, expressing a commitment to themselves and a lifetime of superior dental health. Our patients develop relationships with our team based on mutual respect and are ambassadors for our practice.

We are a family of exceptionally trained, compassionate dental professionals. Working collectively, we are dedicated to consistently delivering superb care, striving to exceed patient expectations.

Dr Matthew D Freedman and his team of experienced and well-trained staff strive to provide the highest professional care for you and your entire family.

Our dental practice, started by the late Dr. Edward S. Freedman, has been providing comprehensive dental care in the Lancaster Community since 1956.

Our office at 416 North Duke Street has undergone a major renovation. An additional 500 square feet have been added and every system and square foot of the practice has been improved. There are 5 brand new treatment rooms, new sterilization and laboratory space, reception area, and staff lounge. We are ADA accessible.
