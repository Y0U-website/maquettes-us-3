Welcome To Our Office!

We are located in Takoma Park, Maryland, on the grounds of the Washington Adventist Hospital. We provide dental care in a comfortable, relaxed atmosphere to all ages.

The practice

Monday – Thursday: 8AM – 5PM Friday: 8AM – 4PM Saturday and Sunday: Closed

Routine Dental Care: This includes diagnostic dental radiographs and panorex x-rays, dental prophylaxis (cleanings). Dr. Siu personally does all the dental prophylaxis himself, as well as other procedures related to dental prophylaxis, such as deep cleanings and scaling/rootplaning procedures. We treat patients of all ages.

Periodontal/Prosthedontic Treatment: Periodontics is the treatment of the gums and supporting tissues to the teeth. This includes scaling and root planing (deep cleanings), surgical gum procedures and bone grafting. Prosthedontics is the treatment and restoration of the dentiton, which includes all the procedures listed in the Restorative Dental Care category as well as more sophisticated…

Oral Surgery: These procedures include extractions, biopsies, implant placements, bone and soft tissue grafts.
