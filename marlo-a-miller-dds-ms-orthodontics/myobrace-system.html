Alternatives to Braces

How Does It Work?

The Myobrace System® involves wearing a removeable Myobrace® appliance for just a few hours a day and while sleeping.

The treatment works by addressing the poor oral habits that are causing the crooked teeth and jaws, so your teeth and jaws can grow the right way.

Straighten Teeth Naturally

Myobrace Activities

Myobrace Activities™ program is designed specifically for patients about to commence Myobrace® treatment and achieve straight teeth the natural way, often without braces. It involves stretching, strengthening, and retraining your tongue, lip, and cheek muscles as well as improving the way you breathe.
