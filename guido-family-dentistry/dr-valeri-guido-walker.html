As the lead dentist at Guido Family Dentistry, Dr. Walker blends advanced clinical training with a calm, thoughtful approach that prioritizes long-term wellness and genuine connection.

A Legacy of Care, Reimagined

Dr. Walker’s journey in dentistry began early, growing up immersed in the profession and later joining her father’s practice in 2015. While rooted in tradition, her vision for the practice reflects a modern, wellness-focused approach — one that values time, trust, and intentional care.

She earned her undergraduate degree from Indiana University Bloomington in 2011 and completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2015.

Today, she leads the practice with a deep respect for its history while continually evolving how care is delivered for patients in Lafayette, West Lafayette, and the Greater Lafayette community.

A Thoughtful, Personalized Approach to Dentistry

Dr. Walker is known for taking time to explain not just what is recommended, but why. She believes informed patients make confident decisions — and confidence is essential to long-term health.

Her approach emphasizes:

Clear, jargon-free communication

Collaborative treatment planning

Conservative care whenever possible

Comfort-focused visits with protected time

Dentistry that supports whole-body wellness

Patients often remark on how calm and unrushed their visits feel — a reflection of Dr. Walker’s commitment to being fully present.

Clinical Interests & Advanced Care

Dr. Walker has a strong interest in technology-enhanced dentistry that improves comfort, accuracy, and outcomes. Her clinical focus includes:

Dental implants

Solea laser dentistry

These tools are used intentionally to support precision and comfort, as outlined on our Our Technology page.
