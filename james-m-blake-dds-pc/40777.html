Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Dental implants are the gold standard and are ideal for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent, natural-looking restoration that functions just like your own teeth. Many people are surprised to learn they re good candidates for this life-changing treatment!

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Generally, ideal candidates for dental implants are adults who have lost one or multiple teeth due to trauma or injury, decay, or gum disease, and want a long-term replacement option. You ll need adequate jawbone density to support the implant, as the titanium post fuses with your bone to create a stable foundation. If bone loss has occurred, bone grafting procedures can often rebuild the area to make implant placement possible.

Good overall health is essential for successful implant placement and healing. Conditions like uncontrolled diabetes, active gum disease, or certain autoimmune disorders may affect healing and implant success, though many of these can be managed with proper treatment and medical collaboration.

Dental Implants in Hamburg, NY

At James M. Blake, DDS, PC , our dentists have the skills and expertise to guide you through the process of getting a dental implant from start to finish. This ensures that you will feel comfortable throughout the entire process, working with the dental team you already know and trust. Here’s how the process works, step-by-step.

  • The Initial Evaluation Once it’s determined that dental implants are right for you, there’s a lot of planning that goes into the process. Our dentists use x-rays, 3-D imaging , a thorough exam , and photos to plan your surgery for precise implant placement and exceptional outcomes.
  • Placement Surgery Our dentists use detailed preplanning to place your dental implant effectively and comfortably into your jawbone on the day of surgery.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Your implant will naturally integrate and fuse with your bone during a process called osseointegration. This process takes three to six months, during which our dentists will monitor your healing process.
  • Restoration Once your implant is healed, our dentist designs a custom restoration for it. This might be a porcelain crown for a single tooth, or a thoughtfully designed bridge or denture to replace multiple missing teeth.
