Resinous material of the various esters of acrylic acid, used as a denture base material, for trays or for other restorations.

The permanent teeth of adulthood that either replace the primary dentition or erupt distally to the primary molars.

Referring to the bone to which a tooth is attached.

An alloy used in direct dental restorations. Typically composed of mercury, silver, tin and copper.

The process of lightening the teeth, usually using a chemical oxidizing agent and sometimes in the presence of heat.
