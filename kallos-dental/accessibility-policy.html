Accessibility Policy

  • Zoom out: Ctrl – (Windows) or Cmd – (Mac)
  • Pause / Stop / Hide – Wherever possible, moving, blinking, scrolling, or auto-updating content includes controls to pause, stop or hide the movement.
  • Volume / Media Controls – Video and audio content will include accessible volume controls, typically located in the bottom-right corner of the media player.
  • Logical Structure & Semantic HTML – Pages use proper headings (H1–H4), landmarks (e.g., nav, main, footer), descriptive link text, and ARIA roles where needed to support navigation via assistive devices.
  • Color Contrast & Readability – Text and background combinations are chosen for sufficient contrast; images include descriptive alt-text or captions where appropriate.
  • Keyboard Navigation – All interactive elements (forms, menus, buttons, links) can be accessed via keyboard (tab, shift-tab, arrow keys) without requiring a mouse.

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure the accessibility of our entire website, we cannot guarantee full accessibility of third-party content that may be linked to or embedded within our site (for example, social-media plugins, external PDFs, booking platforms). If you encounter a barrier in external content, we encourage you to contact us so we can raise the issue with our vendors and explore alternatives.

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is not a one-time project but an ongoing commitment. We regularly monitor updates to accessibility standards, conduct user-testing with assistive technologies, and plan periodic audits of our site to enhance usability for all visitors. As we build new features and content (including blog posts, patient resources, forms, videos), we prioritize accessibility from the start.
