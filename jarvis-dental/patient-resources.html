Get Ready for Your First Appointment

What to Expect

During your first visit, we will review your dental and medical history forms, and take X-rays of your teeth and mouth. The doctor will meet with you to discuss your X-rays, help you create your own individual treatment plan, and answer any questions that you may have. One of our hygienists will then clean and polish your teeth, check your teeth and gums for any signs of gum disease, decay, or bone loss, and screen for oral cancer. Routine cleanings, exams, and x-ray appointments typically take around 60–90 minutes.

What to Bring:

  • Your completed patient information form
  • Your insurance card and ID
