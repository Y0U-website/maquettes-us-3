Professional Teeth Whitening

Life happens usually in the form of coffee, tea, or red wine. Over time, those habits leave their mark. Our professional whitening doesn’t just scrub the surface; it lifts deep-set stains in a way that’s safe and controlled. You’ll walk out with a smile that’s noticeably brighter but still looks like it belongs to you, not a piece of neon paper.

Porcelain Veneers

If you’re dealing with chips, awkward gaps, or stains that just won’t budge, veneers are often the gold standard. These are ultra-thin porcelain shells we custom-fit to the front of your teeth. They’re durable, stain-resistant, and designed to match the translucency of your natural enamel perfectly.

Same-Day Aesthetic Crowns

Usually, getting a crown involves two appointments and a clunky temporary that falls off while you’re eating. We do things differently. With our in-house digital scanner and crown mill , we design and create your final porcelain crown right here in the office. You leave your single appointment with a permanent, color-matched restoration. No goopy impressions, no second visits.

Clear Aligners for a Straighter Smile

Straightening your teeth shouldn’t have to mean a metal mouth for two years. We use clear aligner therapy, a series of nearly invisible, removable trays that gradually shift your teeth into place. They’re much easier to clean than traditional braces, and for most of our adult patients, they’re a far more comfortable way to get a balanced look.

A Complete Frame: Botox & Dermal Fillers

A great smile is about more than just the teeth; it’s about how they fit your face. Since Dr. Yersky is AAFE certified , we’re able to offer Botox and dermal fillers to complement our dental work. Whether we’re smoothing fine lines or framing your new smile with more lip volume, we look at the big picture of your facial aesthetics.

Sometimes a single treatment isn’t enough. A smile makeover is a tailored plan where we combine things like whitening, bonding, and veneers to reach your specific goal. We’ll talk through your budget and your timeline to build a plan that feels right for you.

FAQ

A cosmetic dentist focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth and smile, using treatments like whitening, veneers, bonding, and crowns. The goal is a natural-looking, confident smile.

Common treatments include teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, crowns, clear aligners, and custom smile makeovers. Each is chosen based on your goals and tooth condition.

Restorative dentistry repairs damage and restores function, while cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving appearance. Many treatments combine both for a healthy, attractive smile.

Longevity depends on the treatment, your oral care habits, and regular dental checkups. Professional guidance helps ensure results last for years.

For minor stains, professional teeth whitening works well. For chips or cracks, bonding or veneers are often recommended to restore both appearance and strength.
