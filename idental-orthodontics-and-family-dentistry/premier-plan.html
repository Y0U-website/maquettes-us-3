The iDental Premier Plan

Comprehensive dental care for $25 a month — no insurance required.

Built for Racine Families

In a world where dental costs can sometimes feel like a hurdle, we at iDental in Racine, Wisconsin, understand the importance of accessible and comprehensive dental care. The Premier Plan was created for patients who don’t qualify for government coverage but can’t afford premium private insurance.

We accept Medicaid patients and offer discounts based on household income. Please contact our office to learn which financial options may be available.
