Comprehensive treatment

We are here to help you avoid dental problems with a hygiene program that includes cleanings, x-rays, and your exams. Which will allow the hygienist and dentist to work together to help you treat all of your dental needs by prescribing a comprehensive treatment plan.

Example procedures: fillings, dentures, veneers, crown and bridge, night-guards, and clear aligners to help straighten teeth

sedation

Are you scared of the dentist. We are committed to easing our patient’s anxieties. By offering sedation dentistry this helps our patients become more comfortable and relaxed while receiving quality dental care.

Orthodontic

If you aren’t completely happy with your smile and don’t want to wear traditional braces with wires and brackets, clear correct might be just the thing you are looking for to fix your smile. And once you have the smile you love, you can’t help but show it off.
