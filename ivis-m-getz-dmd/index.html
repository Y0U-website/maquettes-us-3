Dr. Ivis Getz received her dental degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. In her senior year of dental school she received an award of recognition in pediatric dentistry from the NJ Society of Dentistry for Children. After graduation, Dr. Getz completed an advanced two year training program in pediatric dentistry at Bronx Lebanon Hospital in New York.

Dr. Getz is a board-certified pediatric dentist holding Diplomate status in the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is a recipient of the Richard C. Pugh award for scoring in the top 3 % of examinees on the written component of the Board Exam.
