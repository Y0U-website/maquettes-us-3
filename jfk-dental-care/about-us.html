About Us

WE CARE ABOUT YOUR ORAL HEALTH!

Our team of professionals work to ensure each patient receives uncompromised excellence in dental care. We strive to create a caring and relaxing environment to provide the patient an exceptional experience. We invite you to join our dental family.

Dr. Stephanie Flaherty, DDS

Dr. Flaherty graduated with honors from Arkansas State University in 1994 with a degree in biology. She continued on to The University of Tennessee School of Dentistry where she graduated with honors in 1998.

Dr. Flaherty was able to combine her love of art and science into a career as a general and cosmetic dentist. After graduating, she returned to the central Arkansas area where she has successfully practiced for 13 years. For the past eight years she has been a partner at JFK Dental Care.

Dr. Flaherty’s exceptional skills enable her to provide each patient a pleasant experience while keeping the patient’s comfort a top priority.

Dr. Julie Alford Routon, DDS

Dr. Routon was born and raised in Magnolia, AR. She is a 2004 graduate of Hendrix College with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Chemistry.

Upon graduation, she followed in her father’s footsteps and attended dental school. In 2008, she graduated with honors from the University of Tennessee School of Dentistry.

Dr. Routon is dedicated to providing the highest standards in quality dental care to every patient.
