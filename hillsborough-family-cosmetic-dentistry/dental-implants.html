Dental Implants FAQs

At Hillsborough Family and Cosmetic Dentistry, we understand that considering dental implants can raise many questions. Whether you’re curious about the procedure, the benefits, or what to expect during recovery, we’re here to help. Read answers to popular dental implant FAQs below.

How long does the tooth implant process take?

The tooth implant process generally takes 4 to 9 months from start to finish. After the initial consultation and planning, the implant is placed in the jawbone. Healing usually takes 3 to 6 months as the implant fuses with the bone. Sometimes, pretreatments like bone grafting can extend the timeline.

How long does swelling last after a dental implant treatment?

You can experience swelling for 3 to 5 days after you get implants. It usually peaks around the second or third day, then gradually decreases. Applying ice packs during the first 24 hours can help reduce the swelling. Most people find that the swelling is mostly gone within a week. If it persists beyond that, contact our office for an appointment!

Is there an age limit for dental implants?

No, there’s no age limit for dental implants. As long as you have sufficient bone density and good overall health, you can be a candidate for implants, regardless of age. Older adults can benefit from implants just as much as younger people. Dr. Williamson will assess your situation to determine if implants are the right option for you.

Can you get tooth implants after having dentures for years?

Yes, you can get tooth implants after having dentures for years. However, long-term denture use may lead to bone loss in the jaw. If this is the case for you, we might require a bone graft before implant placement.

Tooth implants provide a stable and permanent solution compared to dentures. Dr. Williamson will evaluate your bone density and oral health to determine the best approach. Even after years of wearing dentures, implants can improve your comfort and function.

How long does the dental implant process last?

A dental implant post is permanent, and many implants last a lifetime. However, the crown attached to the implant might need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear.

Can you have dental implants if you have no teeth?

Yes. Dental implants are a great option for people with no teeth. They can replace a full set of teeth in both jaws.

You can also get All-on-4 or All-on-6 techniques are popular for full-mouth restoration. These use four to six implants to support a full arch of teeth. Dr. Williamson will need to assess your jaw bone health first. Sometimes, patients require bone grafts before implant placement.
