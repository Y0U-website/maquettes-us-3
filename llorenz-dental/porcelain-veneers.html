How long do veneers last?

High-quality porcelain veneers typically last 10-15 years or longer with proper care. Regular brushing, flossing, and avoiding excessive force (like biting hard objects) help maximize their lifespan.

How many visits do I need for veneers?

The number of visits depends on your specific treatment needs. During your consultation, we&#x27;ll create a personalized treatment plan and discuss the expected timeline for your case.

Are veneers painful?

No. The procedure is minimally invasive and performed under local anesthesia. Most patients experience no pain during the procedure. Some mild sensitivity may occur for a few days after, which is easily managed.

Will veneers look natural?

Yes! We custom-craft each veneer to match your facial features, skin tone, and desired shade. The porcelain mimics the translucency of natural tooth enamel, making them virtually indistinguishable from your real teeth.
