meet dr. Bateman.

Dr. Vincen Bateman

Welcome to a fresh take on dentistry with Dr. Vincen "JB" Bateman, your dedicated Arizona dentist. Born with the spirit of Las Vegas s vibrant energy and raised amidst the natural beauty of Idaho Falls, JB has always been drawn to connecting with people and improving their lives.

This passion led him to the world of dentistry, where he s not just a dentist; he s a smile architect, relationship builder, and a firm believer in the transformative power of quality dental care.

After completing his Bachelor s Degree in Human Biology at BYU-Idaho, JB further honed his skills at Midwestern University in Arizona, earning his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry. This journey was fueled by a simple yet powerful mission: to make dentistry not just a necessity but a positive, impactful experience for every patient and team member.

Dr. Bateman stands out not just for his dental expertise but for his genuine commitment to his patients well-being. He s known for transforming the dentist office into a welcoming space where every visit is about more than just teeth —it s about building trust, understanding individual needs, and ensuring everyone leaves with a brighter smile and heart.

In Arizona s dynamic dentistry scene, JB is a breath of fresh air. He brings a unique blend of professional precision and personal warmth to his practice at North Valley Family Dentistry. Whether it s crafting beautiful smiles, offering comprehensive dental solutions, or simply sharing a moment of laughter, Dr. Bateman is redefining what it means to be a dentist.

Outside the clinic, JB is as adventurous in life as he is meticulous in dentistry. From wakesurfing to snowmobiling, and staying active, he embraces every moment with the same enthusiasm and care he brings to his patients. It s this zest for life, combined with his commitment to excellence in dental care, that makes Dr. Bateman not just an exceptional Arizona dentist but a trusted companion in your dental health journey.

the way we work.

Four things we believe — and live by — that most dental offices don’t bother with.

treat every patient like they’ll come back.

Retention isn’t a metric to us. It’s the whole job. If a patient leaves and never returns, we did something wrong — and we want to know what.

do most of the work in this building.

Crowns milled on-site. Implants placed and restored under one roof. We refer out when it makes sense for you — not when it’s convenient for us.

say what something costs before we do it.

No surprise bills. No ’we’ll talk after.’ We tell you the price, what insurance covers, and what you’ll actually pay. Then you decide.

treat kids like they’ll like us.

Because they usually do. Parents in the room. Real explanations. No baby-talk. Kids leave excited about brushing — and that’s not an accident.
