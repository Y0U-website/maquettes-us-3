Why More Adults Are Choosing Braces at Shasta Orthodontics

You’re not alone. More adults than ever are choosing to invest in their smiles and the benefits reach far beyond appearances.

  • Healthier Teeth and Gums: Straighter teeth are easier to clean, reducing your risk of decay, gum disease, and even bone loss.
  • Corrects Past Shifting: If your teeth have moved since childhood braces, adult treatment can restore the alignment you once had or never got to finish.
  • Boosts Self-Esteem: A healthy, beautiful smile can empower you at work, in social settings, and in every mirror you pass.
  • Discreet Options Available: Choose from traditional metal braces or ceramic braces that blend in for a subtler look.
  • Treatment Built Around You: We personalize every treatment plan to work with your lifestyle.

Adult Braces FAQ: What You Should Know

  • Am I Too Old for Braces? Absolutely not. Healthy teeth can be moved at any age. If your gums and bone structure are strong, orthodontic treatment is absolutely possible.
  • What Kind of Braces Do Adults Get? We offer both metal braces for reliable results and ceramic braces for a more discreet appearance perfect for adults who want subtlety without compromise.
  • Will Braces Interrupt My Life? Most adults find they can continue their regular work, activities, and social life with minimal disruption. A short adjustment period is all it takes.
  • Can I Afford Adult Braces? We offer flexible payment plans and no-interest financing to make care accessible.
