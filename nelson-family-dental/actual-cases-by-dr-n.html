Choosing a Dentist?

Look at Their Actual Work.

Most dental websites say some version of the same thing: quality care, friendly team, modern technology. Those things matter, but they are hard for patients to judge from a website alone.

One of the best ways to choose a dentist is to look at actual cases.

Can you see examples of their work? Do they explain what made the case difficult? Do they show how they think through worn teeth, broken teeth, old crowns, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, dentures, and full mouth reconstruction?

Dr. Chris created a video on how to choose a dentist because patients deserve more than generic claims. If you are comparing dentists in Redding, CA, we encourage you to look closely at real examples, ask good questions, and choose an office that helps you understand your options.
