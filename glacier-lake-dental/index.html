The practice

At Glacier Lake Dental, we treat you and yours as if you were our own family members. Our practice is built on attentive, personalized care because it s crucial to us that you have a relaxed, comfortable dental visit. Here, you can expect outstanding service and the highest standards of dentistry that exceed your expectations.

At the heart of our practice is Dr. Tracy Grasdal, who offers the perfect blend of professionalism and empathy. She spent time abroad studying for her dental degree at the University of Bergen in Norway and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Dr. Grasdal has been treating families in the Twin Cities area since 1996 and is passionate about staying current with dentistry s best and latest techniques. She is dedicated to getting to know the person behind the smile and nurturing trust with patients who are more like family.
