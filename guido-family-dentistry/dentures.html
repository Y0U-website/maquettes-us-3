FAQs

How many implants are needed for implant dentures?

The number varies based on the type of denture and your oral health. Your dentist will guide you through recommendations.

Are implant dentures removable?

Implant overdentures are removable, while implant-supported dentures are typically fixed in place.

Will implant dentures feel more secure than traditional dentures?

Yes. Implant dentures are designed to reduce movement and improve comfort and function.

How long does the implant denture process take?

Timelines vary depending on healing and preparation needs, but the result is a long-term solution.
