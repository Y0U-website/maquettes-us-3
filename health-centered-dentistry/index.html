The practice

We help you re-establish proper TMJ function, by assuring the correct cranial balance of the head, neck, and jaws.

We educate and encourage proper and good nutrition for the prevention and reversal of degenerative dental diseases.

What does that mean? At HCD we practice what we refer to as “Whole Person Dentistry”, an approach to dentistry that promotes health and wellness instead of only treating “dis”ease.
