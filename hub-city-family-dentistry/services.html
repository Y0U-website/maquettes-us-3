Dentures and Partials

Dentures are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace missing teeth and restore function and aesthetics to the mouth. They play a vital role in enhancing the quality of life for individuals who have lost their teeth due to various reasons, such as age, injury, or dental disease.

Partial dentures are dental appliances designed to replace one or more missing teeth while preserving the remaining natural teeth. They offer several benefits, making them a popular choice for those who have lost teeth due to decay, injury, or other dental issues.

There are two main types of dentures: complete and partial. Complete dentures are used when all teeth are missing from the upper or lower jaw, while partial dentures fill the gaps created by one or more missing teeth, anchoring to remaining natural teeth for support.

Benefits One of the primary benefits of dentures is improved oral function. They allow individuals to chew properly, which can help in maintaining a balanced diet. Additionally, dentures help in improving speech by preventing the tongue from moving to gaps left by missing teeth, which can affect pronunciation.
