The practice

You ll notice Dr. Palm s passion for working with people right away through his friendly and caring demeanor. He believes in building long-term relationships with our patients, so he takes the time to listen to you so he can understand what you would like to achieve for your oral health.

He diagnoses treatment with integrity and truly strives to help his patients meet their goals for their smiles by recommending only the treatment procedures that will truly benefit their oral health.

Families and patients of all ages are welcome at Palm Family Dentistry, and we look forward to exceeding your expectations of what dentistry should be each time you visit our office. By providing two Louisiana locations, we hope to meet the needs of our patients by making high quality dentistry accessible to several local communities. Your comfort is a top priority at Palm Family Dentistry . In addition to a modern and welcoming environment, you also will find our office is equipped with the latest dental technologies such as digital x-rays for quick, clear images and a dental laser that makes many different types of procedures more comfortable.

Dr. Palm utilizes digital x-rays to deliver a more accurate and timely diagnosis because these images allow him to detect certain dental issues in their earliest phases, giving you the opportunity to choose less invasive treatment procedures.

The dental laser offers accuracy, precision, and even accelerates healing in a number of procedures. It helps us deliver a more efficient and comfortable experience to our patients that we know you will appreciate.

Dr. Palm is highly skilled in many areas of dentistry, including complex extractions and restorative dental procedures. This allows him to offer comprehensive care so patients are rarely referred anywhere else for aspects of their dentistry so you’ll almost always receive your care from the team that you know and have built a relationship with.

Are you ready to experience dentistry in a new way? Would you like to learn more about what Palm Family Dentistry has to offer? We look forward to meeting you and we encourage you to contact us today if you have any questions or are ready to schedule your next appointment.
