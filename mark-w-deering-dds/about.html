Meet Dr. Anna Kolentsova

Dr. Anna Kolentsova approaches dentistry with a focus on precision, intention, and long-term thinking.

She earned her degree in molecular, cell, and developmental biology from UCLA before completing her dental training at the USC School of Dentistry. Her scientific background allows her to understand oral health beyond surface-level treatment, approaching each case with a deeper awareness of structure, function, and longevity.

Over the course of her career, Dr. Anna has trained extensively in modern restorative and aesthetic dentistry, working alongside leading clinicians and continuing to refine her skills through advanced education. Her work is guided by a commitment to thoughtful, detail-oriented care where every decision is made with purpose.

Dr. Anna places a strong emphasis on communication, ensuring that patients feel informed and confident in every decision. What truly sets Dr. Anna apart is her connection with patients. Her goal is to create an environment where patients feel comfortable, respected, and genuinely cared for, without pressure or unnecessary treatment.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Anna enjoys spending time in nature, hiking, and exploring the outdoors. This connection to nature and balance is something she values deeply and brings into her work—creating an environment that feels calm, grounded, and thoughtful.
