2. Measures to Support Accessibility

  • Use of semantic, standards-compliant HTML.
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation.
  • Sufficient color contrast for text and background elements.
  • Support for screen readers and other assistive technologies.
  • Responsive design optimized for various devices and screen sizes.

4. Requesting Alternative Formats

If you require information on our website in an alternative format (e.g., large print, accessible PDF, plain text, or audio), please contact us using the information above. We will make every reasonable effort to provide the content in the format you need.

5. Privacy and GDPR Compliance

If you contact us regarding accessibility, we may collect limited personal data (such as your name, email address, and any details you provide in your message). This data will only be used to respond to your accessibility inquiry or request.

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) :

For more information on how we handle personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.

  • We process only the data necessary to handle your request.
  • We will not share your information with third parties without your consent, unless legally required.
  • You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.
  • For EU residents, you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

6. Ongoing Improvements

While we strive to adhere to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards, we acknowledge that some areas of our website may not yet be fully accessible. We are committed to ongoing improvements, regular audits, and incorporating user feedback to enhance accessibility.

We continually evaluate new technologies, including artificial intelligence where appropriate, to improve the accessibility, usability, and overall digital experience of our website.

7. Third-Party Content

Some content or services on our site may rely on third-party platforms (e.g., embedded videos, maps, or plugins). While we aim to ensure accessibility across all areas of our site, we cannot control the accessibility of third-party components. We encourage third-party providers to comply with accessibility standards.

8. Legal Compliance

This Accessibility Statement is intended to comply with:

  • EU Directive 2016/2102 (Web Accessibility Directive)
  • EN 301 549 Accessibility Standard
  • WCAG 2.1 AA Guidelines
  • U.S. ADA Title III and Section 508, where applicable.

9. Updates to This Statement

We may update this Accessibility Statement as our website evolves or as legal requirements change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
