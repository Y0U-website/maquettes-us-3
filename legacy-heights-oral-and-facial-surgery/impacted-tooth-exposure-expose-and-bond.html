Tooth exposure is used to expose a tooth that has not fully erupted or is partially covered by gum tissue, which can cause a variety of issues.

Tooth exposure may also be done in preparation for orthodontic treatment which will necessitate moving a tooth into the correct position. Once the tooth is exposed, a bracket or chain is attached to it to aid in its movement during orthodontic treatment.

Bonding, can be used to treat a variety of dental issues and to improve the appearance and function of a tooth. If you have chips, cracks, gaps, and stains on your teeth, bonding may be a cost-effective alternative to more invasive treatments such as veneers or crowns.

Tooth exposure and bonding can be great options for improving the alignment, health, and appearance of your teeth.
