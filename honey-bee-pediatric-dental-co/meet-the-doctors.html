Rebecca Ferns, DDS

Dr. Ferns began her dental career in 2015 when she practiced as a general dentist in Lawrence, Kansas. Working in a public health environment around many children, she realized her passion for pediatrics. Following her pediatric dental residency, she returned to the Kansas City area to continue her career.

Dr. Ferns’ goal as a pediatric dental specialist is to care for her patients and families in a way that encourages a lifetime of great oral health habits. Her bubbly, personable manner instantly puts patients at ease and enables her to grow long-lasting bonds.

“I want children to have as much fun at our practice as they can!” she says of treating pediatric patients. Dr. Rebecca Ferns understands that getting kids excited to visit the dentist and comfortable once they are in the chair makes all the difference.

When she isn’t in the office, Dr. Ferns enjoys spending time with her husband, daughter, and son. She also likes to run, cook, and ride along in the golf cart while her husband golfs.

Meredith Knabe, DDS

Dr. Meredith Knabe, a Kansas native from Overland Park, discovered her passion for pediatric dentistry at a young age after a positive experience with her own pediatric dentist. That early inspiration never wavered. She earned her degree in Nutritional Sciences with a minor in Spanish from Kansas State University before completing her dental training at the UMKC School of Dentistry. She then went on to receive specialized training in pediatric dentistry at Children’s Mercy Hospital.

Dr. Knabe’s goal as a pediatric dentist is to create an environment where children feel safe, curious, and excited to learn about their dental health. She believes that each visit is an opportunity not just to treat teeth, but to build confidence and trust that lasts a lifetime. Her kind and energetic demeanor helps patients feel at ease, and she finds great joy in watching them grow more comfortable at each visit.

“To me, the best part of this job is seeing kids light up when they feel proud of themselves at the dentist,” she says. “I love being able to turn what could be a stressful experience into something positive and even fun.”

Outside of the office, Dr. Meredith Knabe enjoys cooking and baking, staying active on her Peloton or in hot yoga, and spending weekends at the lake with her husband and puppy. She’s a proud K-State fan and loves cheering on the Wildcats and the Kansas City Chiefs.

Bri Kerns, DDS, MDS

Dr. Kerns is a caring and approachable pediatric dentist who chose this specialty because she loves working with kids and creating positive dental experiences from an early age. She enjoys that no two days are ever the same, often filled with funny stories, silly conversations, and proud moments that make her work both meaningful and fun.

She values building trust with families and helping children grow in confidence; while also giving parents clear options so they can make the best decisions for their child’s care. Dr. Bri believes pediatric dentistry should be positive, personalized, and fun, and she strives to meet each child where they are with patience, encouragement, and kid-friendly explanations that help them feel safe and comfortable.

Outside of the office, Dr. Bri enjoys traveling, trying new Kansas City restaurants, and cheering on the Kansas City Chiefs. She shares life at home with her husband, daughter, two dogs, and two cats.
