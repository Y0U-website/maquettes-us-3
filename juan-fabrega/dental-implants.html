Are implants covered by insurance?

Some dental insurance plans may cover portions of implant treatment. We also offer financing options to help make dental implants more affordable with manageable monthly payments.

How long does it take to recover from implant surgery?

Most patients recover from the initial procedure within a few days, but full healing and integration with the bone can take several months.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Dental implants are cared for like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits. Good oral hygiene helps ensure long-term success.
