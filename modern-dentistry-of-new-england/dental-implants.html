What Are Dental Implants in Bristol, CT

Dental implants in Bristol CT, give you a secure, natural way to replace missing teeth. An implant is a small titanium post that we place into your jawbone during a precise surgical procedure. It acts like a tooth root and supports strong replacement teeth that look and feel like natural teeth.

Over time, the implant bonds with your bone in a process called healing and integration. This creates a stable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike removable options, implants stay in place. You can eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

At Modern Dentistry of New England in Bristol, CT, we focus on implant dentistry that protects your oral health and restores your quality of life.

Benefits of Dental Implants in Bristol, CT

Natural Look and Feel

Dental implants closely match your natural teeth in shape and color. They blend with your smile and restore your ability to eat and speak without discomfort or fear of slipping.

Protects Your Jaw and Bone

When teeth are missing, the jawbone can shrink. Implants stimulate the bone and help maintain healthy jaw structure. This support protects your facial shape and prevents a sunken look.

No Damage to Nearby Teeth

Unlike bridges, implants do not rely on nearby teeth for support. We preserve healthy enamel and protect long-term oral health. Your surrounding teeth stay strong and stable.

Long-Term Confidence

With proper care, implants can last for decades. Many adults see them as a permanent solution for missing teeth. You regain comfort, pride, and confidence in daily life.

The Process of Getting Dental Implants

Step One: Your First Visit And Consultation

Your first visit includes a full exam, digital imaging, and a clear review of your needs. Dr. Gabr explains the process, answers questions, and designs a plan. We discuss bone, jaw health, and whether bone grafting is needed before placement.

Step Two: Precise Surgical Placement

We perform the surgical procedure in our modern Bristol, CT, office using advanced equipment. Dr. Gabr and our team place dental implants with care and precision. We monitor the jawbone and sinuses to ensure safe placement and strong healing.

Step Three: Healing And Final Teeth

During healing, the implants fuse with the bone. This creates a stable foundation. After healing, we attach your custom replacement teeth. You leave our practice able to eat, speak, and smile with renewed confidence.
