IMPLANTS

Intelligent Touch Dental Laboratory offers an extensive amount of different dental implants. Doing so allows us to better accommodate our dentists. Many doctors have their own dental implant preference, which is great! We work with a lot of different implant companies because we want our doctors working with the implant system they are most comfortable with.

Dental implants are becoming a very common choice for restorations today. A big reason is they deliver natural esthetics along with strength and long-term stability. They also have the added advantage of helping to preserve oral health by preventing bone recession. Dental implants can support single crowns, bridges, and full denture restorations. I.T.D.L does not mill any implants in-house. We only use abutments from specific manufactures to guarantee the implants’ warranty coverage and precise fit.
