Volunteer

NSO is incredibly grateful for all of our generous volunteers. It is only with their support that we can fulfill our mission every day.

We would love to have you partner with us as a volunteer. Please see below to explore volunteer opportunities with NSO.

Please note: We ask for at least two weeks notice to adequately prepare for most group volunteer projects. Group projects are limited to no more than 15 participants.

For more information about our volunteer program, please contact NSO at (405) 236-0452 x129 or by email at volunteer@nsookc.org .

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Outdoors Outdoor Beautification Project In this role, you will assist with maintaining NSO properties located throughout Oklahoma City. These projects can include lawn care, weeding flower beds, planting flowers, edging, trimming shrubs, power washing and outside painting. This volunteer commitment can be one-time or long-term depending on the project or volunteer request.
