How Bioclear Works

The result? A smooth, natural, and beautifully enhanced tooth—customized just for you.

  • Consultation & Smile Design – We assess your smile and goals to determine if Bioclear is right for you.
  • Tooth Preparation – The tooth surface is gently cleaned—no major drilling needed.
  • Composite Placement – A clear form is placed around your tooth and filled with warm composite resin.
  • Shaping & Polishing – Once set, the material is shaped and polished to perfection.

Bioclear vs. Traditional Dentistry

Traditional crowns or veneers often require significant removal of healthy tooth structure. Bioclear, on the other hand, uses additive dentistry — building onto your tooth rather than cutting it down. This means less drilling, less sensitivity, and longer-lasting results.
