Affordable Payment Options

At Shasta Orthodontics, we believe every smile deserves a chance to shine no matter your budget. That’s why we offer flexible payment options tailored to fit your family’s needs. Whether you’re exploring traditional metal braces or ceramic braces , our in-house financing helps ease the financial burden, so you can focus on what truly matters: feeling confident in your smile journey.

No-Interest Financing

You shouldn’t have to choose between your smile and your finances. With our no-interest financing, you can begin treatment with confidence knowing there are no hidden fees, no surprise charges, and every dollar supports your care, not interest payments. It’s one less worry on your journey to a brighter, more confident you.

Low Monthly Payments

We know that cost can sometimes feel like a barrier but it doesn’t have to be. That’s why Shasta Orthodontics offers low monthly payments designed to ease the weight on your wallet and your mind. Our team will work closely with you to find a payment path that feels right, because your smile is too important to put on hold.

No Large Down Payment Required

We’re committed to making treatment accessible from day one. That’s why we don’t require a large down payment to get started. Our goal is to help you begin your orthodontic journey with ease removing financial stress so you can take the first step toward a healthier, more confident smile without delay.

No-Cost Consultation

Every life-changing smile begins with a first step and at Shasta Orthodontics, that first step is on us. Your no-cost consultation gives you the chance to explore what’s possible without pressure or obligation. We’ll listen to your goals, evaluate your needs, and help you see a path forward that’s filled with hope and clarity.

Compassionate, Affordable Care

We believe orthodontic care should feel personal, uplifting, and rooted in true compassion. From the moment you walk through our doors, we’re here for you not just as providers, but as partners in your smile journey. Our care is designed to make you feel supported, understood, and confident every step of the way.

Personalized Plans for Every Smile

No two smiles are the same, and neither are the people behind them. That’s why we take the time to design payment solutions that reflect your unique circumstances financially and personally. When you’re ready, give us a call at (530) 223-6850 to schedule your no-cost consultation. Your new smile is closer than you think and we’ll be with you every step of the way.
