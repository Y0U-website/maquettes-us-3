WHAT IS CEREC?

A milling system using modern software and state-of-the-art equipment to provide individual crowns. Dr. Karimi begins by preparing your tooth according to the type of restoration needed. Then, while you are sitting in the chair, we use the system’s 3D Cad/Cam photography system to take the place of an impression. Dr. Karimi designs your restoration while you watch, using the CEREC software.

We then use our inhouse CEREC milling machine to create a customized, beautiful new porcelain crown in minutes, and install it in your mouth — all in one visit.

We use the CEREC system to design crowns, inlays (fillings), onlays (partial crowns), bridges and veneers. It is economical and convenient and produces beautiful results all in one dental visit.

CEREC CROWNS

Paradise Dental Group uses the latest technology to fabricate personalize crowns, we do it in house with our CEREC milling system. Just in one visit provides the patient with a beautiful new ceramic crown. No more messy impressions, no more temporary crowns, no more waiting days or weeks for your crown to be fabricated.

CEREC BRIDGES

When a tooth is missing, the adjoining teeth tend to shift into the empty space. Opposing teeth may also begin to grow up or down into that space as well. These movements cause not only an unflattering appearance, but it can cause significant bite problems. A bridge made of today’s advanced materials can prevent this occurrence, replacing the missing tooth or teeth and helping you keep a beautiful smile for your lifetime.
