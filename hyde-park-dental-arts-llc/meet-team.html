Dr. Daniel Prywitch

As a native of St. Louis, Missouri, Dr. Daniel Prywitch obtained his bachelor’s degree in biology at Indiana University and his doctorate from the University of Missouri Kansas City.

He then chose to pursue a one-year residency from the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at the University of Cincinnati Medical Center.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Prywitch enjoys hiking at local parks, watching movies, and rooting for Cincinnati sports.

With a passion for providing high-quality comprehensive care, Dr. Prywitch is excited to join Hyde Park Dental Arts to improve the oral health of the community!

Dr. Paulette Duncan

Dr. Paulette Duncan was born in Venezuela and moved to Florida when she was young. She grew up in a Spanish-speaking community outside of Miami, eating arepas and hamburgers, truly enjoying the chaos of blending cultures.

She earned her undergraduate degree in Health Education and Behavior from the University of Florida (UF). She then attended and graduated from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2017.

​ After graduating, she moved around the country, following her husband, David, as he served in the United States Air Force. Having practiced as a general dentist in San Diego, Nashville, and San Antonio, Dr. Duncan is now back in the Midwest in order to settle down in Cincinnati, near her extended family.

Dr. Duncan stays busy with her two kids, Leo & Amelia, but still carves out time to work on projects and hobbies around the house and to travel to cheer on the Florida Gators.

Dr. Duncan can’t wait to meet you and work with you to improve your oral health and achieve your smile goals using a comprehensive, patient-focused approach
