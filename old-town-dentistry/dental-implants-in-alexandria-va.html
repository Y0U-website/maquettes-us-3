Dental Implants in Alexandria, VA

Dental implants are replacement tooth roots that provide a strong foundation for permanent artificial teeth. They are small and slim and comfortably fit in a patient’s mouth. Just like natural tooth roots, they stimulate the jaw to prevent it from losing bone mass.

When replacing missing teeth, our dentist often recommends implant-supported restorations because they look and feel more like natural teeth than other restorations. They can replace one missing tooth, several missing teeth in a row, or even an entire arch.

What Happens During the Dental Implant Procedure?

During your initial consultation, you will discuss your oral health needs and goals with our dentist in dentist in Alexandria, VA, and will also be able to ask any questions about the dental implant procedure. This consultation is an important opportunity to learn about your treatment options so that you can choose the solution that is best for you.

An implant procedure is performed in two stages, with several weeks in between. First, the titanium post is surgically inserted into the jaw at the site of the missing tooth root. Once your implant has fused with the jawbone, our dentist will attach an abutment to the post. The abutment is the connecting piece that attaches the implant to the dental crown.

During your final appointment, the dentist will attach the crown and finalize the restoration.

A temporary restoration is placed at the end of the first appointment so that patients do not go without a tooth while waiting for the permanent one to be completed.

What Is the Success Rate for Dental Implants?

The success of dental implants depends on many factors, including the type of implant you receive and your oral care routine. In general, however, dental implants have a very high success rate of over 95 percent, and nearly 98 percent of patients report that they would consider getting the procedure again in the future.

With proper oral hygiene and regular visits to your dentist, you can significantly increase your chances of a successful outcome.

Whether you are missing one tooth or all of your teeth, dental implants provide an effective and permanent solution for restoring your smile’s appearance and function.

Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots, providing a strong foundation for the crown, bridge, or denture that will be attached to the post. Once the implants heal, the replacement teeth are securely connected to the implants, allowing you to eat and speak normally without worrying about your restoration slipping or other issues.
