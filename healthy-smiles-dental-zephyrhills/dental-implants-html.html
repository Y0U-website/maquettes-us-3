Dental Implants

What does getting dental implants involve?

Once implants integrates with the bone, artificial teeth are made and fitted to the post portion of the anchor. Because several fittings may be required, this step may take one to two months to complete. After a healing period, the artificial teeth are securely attached to the implant, providing excellent stability and comfort to the patient.

At Healthy Smiles Dental, you will receive care instructions during your every visit and when your treatment is completed. Good oral hygiene, eating habits, and regular dental visits will aid in the life of your new implant.
