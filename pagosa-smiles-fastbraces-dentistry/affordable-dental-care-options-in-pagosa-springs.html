Making Dental Care Affordable: Payment Options at Pagosa Smiles

Discover flexible payment options, accepted insurances, and financing solutions at Pagosa Smiles that make quality family dentistry accessible and budget-friendly for residents in Pagosa Springs and surrounding areas.

Cherry Financing (New Option)

A modern, patient-friendly financing solution:Pay over time with flexible terms.

Cherry simplifies access to care by making approval quick and hassle-free.

  • 0 % APR plans available.
  • No impact to your credit when applying.

Dental Insurance Acceptance

If you have dental insurance, Pagosa Smiles makes it easy to maximize your benefits: They perform free benefits checks so you understand your coverage before treatment.

Simply bring your insurance card or details to the office (no appointment needed for a check). Accepted providers include:

This approach helps insured patients reduce out-of-pocket expenses significantly.

  • Cigna
  • Delta Dental
  • Anthem
  • Blue Cross Blue Shield
  • Aflac
  • United Concordia Elite Plus Plan (new acceptance for Jicarilla Apache Nation members)

Cash or Check Payments (With Discount)

  • Cash or check payments are due at the time of visit.
  • Enjoy a 5 % discount for using these methods.

Credit Card Payments

This provides immediate payment flexibility without needing special applications.

  • Visa, MasterCard, and Discover are all welcome.

CareCredit Financing

This is ideal for larger treatments like crowns, orthodontics, or comprehensive care.

  • For purchases of $200–$1,000: 6 months to pay.
  • For purchases over $1,000: 6 or 12 months options.
  • Extended plans up to 60 months (with interest) to lower monthly payments.
  • Apply directly through their site or in-office for budget-friendly terms.
