There’s more to preventive dentistry than brushing and flossing daily. Regular dental cleanings and exams allow our dentists to identify problems and take care of them right away. The goal of preventive dentistry is to prevent problems from developing, such as cavities, gingivitis, enamel loss, and periodontitis.

Our restorative dentistry focuses on repairing or replacing your damaged or missing teeth to help improve oral health and function. Common dental restorations include crowns, bridges, implants, and dentures. You might need restorative dental care if you have cavities, or damaged or missing teeth.
