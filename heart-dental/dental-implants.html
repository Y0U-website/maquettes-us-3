Why Dental Implants Are the Best Tooth Replacement Option

Dental implants offer unique benefits that other tooth replacement options can’t match:

Bone Preservation : Dental implants replace the entire tooth, including the root. This stimulates the jawbone and prevents bone loss over time.

Natural Look and Feel : They look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

Closest Thing to Natural Teeth : Implants are the closest option to regrowing your own teeth.

Chewing efficiency : Fixed teeth with dental implants are more efficient at chewing food compared to partial or complete dentures. They provide better stability, allowing people with one or more missing teeth to enjoy a wider variety of foods, such as salads and chewy items, with greater ease.

For a single tooth implant, you can return to non-strenuous activities the next day. Mild discomfort may last for 2-3 days, but it’s usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. I was told that I do not have enough bone for dental implants?
