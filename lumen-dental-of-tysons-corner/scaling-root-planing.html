Signs You May Need Scaling & Root Planing

You may need this treatment if you experience:

  • Bleeding gums when brushing or flossing
  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gum recession or deep pockets around teeth
  • Loose or shifting teeth

What to Expect During Treatment

Scaling and root planing is typically performed over one or more visits depending on your needs.

Comfortable, Numbed Procedure

  • Local anesthesia is used to keep you comfortably numb during treatment.

Cleaning Below the Gumline

  • Specialized hand instruments and cavitrons are used to remove plaque and tartar from deep periodontal pockets where bacteria accumulate.

Smoothing the Tooth Roots

  • Root surfaces are smoothed to eliminate rough areas and help the gums heal and reattach properly.

Benefits of Scaling & Root Planing

Stops the Progression of Gum Disease

  • Removes bacteria and plaque buildup that cause infection and inflammation.

Helps Prevent Tooth Loss

  • Treating gum disease early helps preserve the bone and structures supporting your teeth.

Promotes Healing and Gum Reattachment

  • Smoothing the roots helps the gums heal and reattach to the teeth more effectively.

Improves Overall Oral Health

  • Reducing harmful bacteria supports both oral and overall health.
