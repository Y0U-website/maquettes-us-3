What is Family Dentistry?

Family dentistry is a branch of dentistry that provides dental care for patients of all ages. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a family dentist?

A family dentist provides dental care for patients of all ages, from young children to adults and seniors. Instead of visiting different specialists for each family member, a family dentist like Dr. Ng can handle most routine and preventive treatments in one place, making dental care simpler and more convenient for busy families.

At what age should my child first visit the dentist? K L

We recommend bringing children for their first dental visit by age one or when their first tooth appears. Early visits help children become comfortable at the dentist and allow us to monitor tooth development, prevent cavities, and guide parents on proper oral care.

What services are included in family dentistry? K L

Family dentistry includes preventive, restorative , and cosmetic treatments. Common services include dental cleanings, exams, x-rays, fluoride treatments, fillings, crowns, mouthguards, orthodontic options like Invisalign®, and emergency dental care. These services help maintain oral health for every stage of life.

How often should my family visit the dentist? K L

Most patients should visit the dentist every six months for exams and professional cleanings. Regular visits help prevent cavities, monitor gum health, and detect dental issues early. Some patients may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health needs.

Why choose a family dentist instead of different dentists for each person? K L

A family dentist provides convenience and continuity of care. When the same dentist treats everyone in the family, they understand your dental history, track changes over time, and create personalized treatment plans. It also makes scheduling easier since multiple appointments can often be coordinated together.

Do you treat both children and adults? K L

Yes. At Hometown Smiles Dentistry, we provide dental care for patients of all ages. From children receiving their first cleanings to adults needing restorative treatments or cosmetic care, our goal is to support healthy smiles for every member of the family.

What if someone in my family has a dental emergency? K L

If a family member experiences severe tooth pain, a broken tooth, swelling, or another urgent dental problem, contact our office as soon as possible. Emergency dentistry allows us to relieve pain quickly and prevent further damage to the tooth or surrounding tissues.

How can families maintain healthy smiles at home? K L

Daily brushing twice a day, flossing, limiting sugary snacks, and attending regular dental visits are the best ways to protect your family’s oral health. Teaching children good habits early and maintaining consistent routines can help prevent many common dental problems.
