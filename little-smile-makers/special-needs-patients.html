Special Needs Dentistry

  • Pediatric dentists undergo 2-3 years of rigorous training after completion of dental school.
  • This specialty emphasizes the treatment of all children, regardless of age, medical history, and dental treatment needs.
  • The specialty also stresses the growth and development of the child and their relationship with the child’s oral health.
  • Pediatric dentists are also trained in providing dental treatment utilizing various modalities, such as behavior management techniques, conscious sedation, and general anesthesia.
