Meet Dr. Judith Kimmelman

Dr. Judith "Judy" Kimmelman has been practicing general dentistry for over 30 years and is dedicated to providing her patients with the highest level of care. She strives to educate her patients while treating them with compassion and respect. A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine (1990) and Lehigh University (1986), Dr. Kimmelman continues to take numerous continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of the latest advances and techniques in dentistry.

Since completing dental school, she has practiced privately in New Jersey and is highly skilled in all phases of general dentistry. Originally from Central Jersey, Dr. Judy and her husband moved to South Jersey in 2006, where they currently live in Mt. Laurel. Together, they enjoy family life with their two sons, one daughter, and two dogs.
