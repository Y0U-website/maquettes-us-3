Dr. Chelsey Patton

CONTACT US Meet the Doctor Button Dr. Chelsey Patton Dr. Chelsey Patton is a native of northern Indiana. Dr. Patton invested much of her life to the performing arts, as she has been dancing and singing since the age of three.

Dr. Patton graduated from Purdue University in West Lafayette, IN with a Bachelors of Health Science. During her sophomore year she founded the Purdue University Timmy Global Health Foundation in order to share her love for missions, medicine, and global awareness with the student population. She traveled to Ecuador and Honduras leading students, physicians, dentists, nurses, physical therapists, and pharmacists to create remote clinics aiding in forming a revolving system in which groups now travel to the same sites four times a year to provide continuity of care.

Dr. Patton attended the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in Louisville, KY. She traveled to Kenya, Africa her Freshman year as a medical missionary with Dr. David Stevens, Christian Medical and Dental Association CEO with Global Health Outreach. She also traveled as a missionary dentist with her husband to Ethiopia, Africa her Senior year with Dr. Samuel Molind, Director of Global Health Outreach.

Dr. Chelsey Patton completed advanced training at University Hospital in Louisville, KY as a General Practice Resident following graduation from dental school. Dr. Patton has advanced education and training in comprehensive general dentistry within both an ambulatory and hospital environment with certifications in Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), Category II- hard/soft tissue laser certification, and management of dental and medical emergencies.

Her residency training included advanced procedures in oral surgery, endodontic, periodontic, restorative, and implant placement surgery taught in outpatient clinics and in the operating room under general anesthesia.

Dr. Patton is happily married to Dr. William Farmer, an equine veterinarian, and together have two beautiful baby girls. She enjoys spending her time with her husband, daughters, and two dogs. Chelsey loves the Lord and wants Him to be glorified in all she does as a wife, mother, and dentist.

We offer a wide variety of treatments ranging from general to cosmetic dentistry.
