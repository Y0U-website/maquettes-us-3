Dr. Justin Modugno

Dentist and Owner

Dr. Justin Modugno is proud to serve the North Canton community with exceptional dental care built on trust, experience, and a personalized approach. A native of North Canton and graduate of Hoover High School, Dr. Modugno went on to earn both his Bachelor of Science in Biology and Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University. He further expanded his clinical skills by completing a General Practice Residency at Mercy Hospital in 2016, where he gained advanced training in comprehensive patient care.

For over seven years, Dr. Modugno worked at St. Paul’s Square Community Health Clinic, providing care to underserved populations while also serving as a faculty member for Mercy Hospital’s dental residency program. His passion for education and community health has shaped his commitment to treating each patient with empathy and excellence. In 2023, Dr. Modugno transitioned fully into private practice, where he now focuses on creating a welcoming, family-friendly environment where patients feel seen, heard, and cared for.

Dr. Modugno is an active member of the Stark County Dental Society, the Ohio Dental Association, and the American Dental Association. He has also contributed to public health initiatives through his involvement with the Dental Public Health and Access Council.

Known by many of his patients as “Dr. Mo,” he combines modern dental techniques with a calm, caring chairside manner. Whether you’re coming in for a routine checkup or more complex treatment, his goal is to deliver high-quality dentistry in a relaxed, judgment-free space.

Outside of the office, Dr. Modugno enjoys spending time with his wife Kelsey and their two children, Teddy and Lena. He also has a passion for photography, reading, and gathering with friends for board game nights.
