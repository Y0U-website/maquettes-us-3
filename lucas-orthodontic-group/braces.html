Life with Braces

Getting braces is an exciting step toward a beautiful smile, but it also comes with some adjustments to everyday life. Our team will provide your teen with all the guidance they need to maintain their braces and keep their treatment on track.

Dietary Adjustments

While wearing braces, certain foods will need to be avoided to prevent damage to the brackets and wires. Sticky, chewy, or hard foods like gum, popcorn, and candy can cause breakages. However, plenty of braces-friendly options like soft fruits, vegetables, and dairy products will keep your teen’s diet balanced.

Oral Hygiene

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is crucial during orthodontic treatment. Teens will need to brush and floss regularly to keep their teeth and braces clean. Our team will demonstrate the best techniques for brushing around brackets and using special tools, like floss threaders, to clean between teeth.

Managing Discomfort

It’s normal to experience some discomfort after braces are first applied or following adjustments. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help, and orthodontic wax can be applied to braces to reduce irritation in the mouth. Any discomfort usually subsides within a few days as the teeth begin to shift into their new positions.

Playing Sports & Music

Your teen can still participate in their favorite activities with braces! For athletes, we recommend wearing a mouthguard to protect their teeth and braces during contact sports. Teens who play musical instruments, like wind or brass instruments, may need a short adjustment period, but most are able to adapt quickly.

At Lucas Orthodontic Group, we strive to make life with braces as easy as possible for teens. With the right care and commitment, your teen will be on their way to a beautiful, healthy smile that will last a lifetime!

Braces FAQ

How do braces work? Braces work by applying gentle, continuous pressure to your teeth over time, gradually moving them into the desired position. The braces consist of brackets attached to your teeth and wires that connect the brackets. These wires are adjusted periodically to guide your teeth into proper alignment.

Do braces hurt? Braces may cause some discomfort, especially after they’re first applied or after adjustments. This is normal and typically lasts only a few days. Over-the-counter pain relievers and orthodontic wax can help ease any irritation or soreness you may feel.

How long will I need to wear braces? The length of time you’ll need to wear braces varies depending on your unique treatment needs. On average, most patients wear braces for 12-24 months. However, minor alignment issues may be corrected more quickly, while more complex cases may take longer.

Are there foods I should avoid with braces? Yes, certain foods can damage your braces and should be avoided during treatment. Hard, sticky, or chewy foods like popcorn, gum, candy, and ice can cause brackets to come loose or wires to break. It’s best to stick to softer foods and cut harder items into smaller pieces.

How do I care for my teeth with braces? Maintaining good oral hygiene is especially important when you have braces. You’ll need to brush after every meal and floss at least once a day. Special tools like floss threaders, interdental brushes, and water flossers can help you clean around your brackets and wires. Our team will provide detailed instructions to help you keep your teeth and braces in top condition.

What happens if a bracket or wire comes loose? If a bracket or wire comes loose, don’t panic! Call our office right away, and we’ll schedule an appointment to fix it. In the meantime, you can cover any poking edges with orthodontic wax to protect your mouth from irritation.

Will I need to wear a retainer after my braces come off? Yes! Wearing a retainer after your braces are removed is essential to maintaining your new smile. Retainers help keep your teeth in their new positions and prevent them from shifting back. Our team will customize a retainer for you and provide instructions on how often to wear it.
