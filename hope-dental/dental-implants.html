Mini Dental Implants for Dentures

At Hope Dental, we offer “Hope” to those suffering from loose fitting dentures. Dr Ras has had great success stabilizing dentures with Mini Dental Implants. Special housing in your denture snaps onto MDIs in your upper or lower arch creating a comfortable, tighter fitting denture. Call our office today to see if you are a candidate for MDIs to tighten your denture.

The Dental Implant Process

With our up to date and top of the line technology, Dr. Ras will first evaluate if you are a good candidate for dental implants. Candidates must have healthy gums and sufficient bone to support and anchor the implants. Dr. Ras will then work with you to create a treatment plan that best suits your smile and budget.

Crown and Bridge Placement

After placement of the MDI, an impression is taken and sent off to the lab. After two weeks later, a permanent crown will be cemented onto the implant.

Denture Stabilization Procedure

After confirming that your jaw has sufficient bone, Dr. Ras will place your mini dental implants. If possible, he will use your existing denture. If not, our lab will make a denture with special housing that snaps’ onto the implant, creating a fit that gives you more confidence throughout the day.
