About Us

​Lone Peak Dental provides affordable dental services and is contracted with most major dental insurance companies. We also offer third-party financing for those without insurance and have our own in-house discount dental care program .​Our knowledgeable team will help you maximize any benefits you have while ensuring that you are aware of any financial obligation for out of pocket expenses. We are driven by our desire to not only serve the dental health needs of our community, but to also build trusting relationships that last a lifetime.
