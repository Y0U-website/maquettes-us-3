Our Doctors – Mouth Peace Dental

After specialty training, Dr. Wells worked as a full-time faculty member and Assistant Professor at Meharry Medial College School of Dentistry in the Restorative Dentistry Department. As a faculty member she was co-founder with her mentor Dr. Charles T. Smith of the Restorative Club. She also served on the dental school board of admissions.

Prior to her foray into the dental arena, Dr. Wells acquired her Bachelor’s of Science degree in Biology at the University of Nebraska at Kearney where she earned numerous awards for academic achievement. She also completed an Associate’s Degree in History at the College of the Bahamas, Nassau Bahamas. Dr. Wells was born and raised in Nassau, Bahamas…..242!

Dr. Wells is a member of the American College of Prosthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association and an inductee of the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

Outside of dental practice, she enjoys spending time with family and friends, providing dental care to people who have limited access to dental care (dental missions), traveling, fitness (former marathon runner) and preparing & eating vegan cuisine.

  • Maxillofacial Prosthodontic Fellow - University of Chicago Hospital- Zoller Clinic (Head and Neck Cancer Team)
