Who Are Veneers For?

Veneers are becoming extremely popular and not only among models and film stars whose smiles are a professional asset. With veneers anyone can correct gaps, conceal discoloration and change the shape and size of your mouth. Veneers can even replace braces for those patients with top or bottom front teeth that require only minor movement.

Veneers are a great for:

  • Crooked Teeth
  • Discoloration
  • Worn Teeth
  • Chipped Teeth
