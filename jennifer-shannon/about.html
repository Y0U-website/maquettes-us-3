Practice Smiles

Smiles by Shannon Dental serves patients in Harrisburg, Camp Hill, Mechanicsburg, and surrounding communities. We’re a family of caring professionals who go the extra mile to make you and your family smile! From the moment you walk into our brand-new office, you’ll be our top priority.

Our dental home departs from the typical dentist clinical setting to become a true sanctuary of peace and relaxation. Every dimension of our office is designed for comfort. From our buttery-soft leather chairs, equipped with massage and heat control features, to ceiling-mounted TVs, to our gentle approach to cleaning, no detail has been overlooked to make you and your family feel at home.

Our lobby has a refreshment bar, a TV you can control, and an iPad station for little ones and adults who want to relax before their appointment. With a dash of Southern charm and a dose of Northern innovation, we don’t stop at treating your teeth and gums. We treat you , so you can enjoy a dazzling smile, fresh breath, a healthy mouth, and dental health that leads to overall well-being.
