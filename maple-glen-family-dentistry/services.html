Denture Services

When you are missing teeth, our dentists may recommend a denture to replace your teeth and restore your smile. Dentures can be an affordable and time-effective solution for restoring your smile and improving your quality of life.

There are two main types of dentures available:

When you visit our office for your consultation, our dentists will work with you to determine which type of denture will be most beneficial for you.

Your dentures may also be securely supported by dental implants. Implant-supported dentures are the only denture treatment that helps prevent bone loss, preserve your appearance, and give you completely secure protection against movement of your appliance during talking and chewing.

You may want to consider implant-supported dentures to:

  • Complete dentures – for when all of the teeth in your upper or lower arch are missing
  • Partial dentures – for when some of your natural teeth remain; they also help prevent your remaining teeth from shifting.
  • Preserve bone loss that will occur with traditional dentures
  • Maintain facial structure and support
  • Increase denture stability
  • Eliminate the need for denture adhesives
  • Help reduce denture pain or discomfort from a poor fit
  • Help improve eating habits to improve overall health
  • Restore chewing ability – you can eat what you like!
  • Increase self-confidence while eating, speaking and being active

Implant Dentistry

Dental implants restore the natural appearance and function of missing teeth, improving your quality of life so that you can speak and function normally. Implant placement can also relieve joint pain and discomfort as a result of tooth loss.

Other benefits of dental implants include:

The implant process typically takes 6-9 months to complete. After your initial evaluation, the implant site will be prepared, and the implant post will be placed. Several months of time will be allowed for proper healing and for the implant post to integrate with the bone. An implant restoration, such as a crown, bridge, or denture, will be created and placed atop the post. Our team will keep you informed every step of the way during your treatment to provide you with complete instructions and answer your questions.

Dental implants are extremely strong, stable, and durable, and when you practice good oral hygiene, healthy eating habits, and keep your regular dental appointments, maintaining your implants is easy.

Call 716-688-5717 and schedule your free implant consult today.

  • Aesthetics – implants look and feel like your natural teeth.
  • Function – speaking and eating is more comfortable and natural
  • Less Invasive – your adjacent teeth are unaffected by your treatment
  • Health – Implants join with your existing natural bone, stimulating the bone to stay healthy
  • Reliability – Implants have a success rate of over 97 %!
