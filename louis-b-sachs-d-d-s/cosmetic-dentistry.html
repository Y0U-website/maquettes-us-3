How a Cosmetic Consultation Works

Your consultation is about understanding the smile you want and showing you what is realistic. Here is what to expect: Photos and a smile evaluation — we record your current smile from multiple angles. Listening session — what bothers you, what you love, and what you would change. Treatment options — clear explanation of veneers vs. bonding vs. whitening, with honest pricing. Written plan and estimate — you leave with the numbers in writing. There is no pressure and no obligation. Many patients pursue a single treatment; others combine two or three.

Veneers are considered a permanent procedure because a thin layer of natural enamel is removed to bond them. They typically last 10–15 years and are replaced rather than removed.

Professionally supervised whitening is safe for enamel. The hydrogen peroxide in Zoom works on stain molecules, not tooth structure. Mild temporary sensitivity is normal and resolves within 24–48 hours.
