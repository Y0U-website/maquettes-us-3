Dr. Michael S. Malka

Dr. Michael S. Malka has been practicing dentistry in Linden NJ for over 25 years. Dr. Malka was raised in New Jersey and attended University of Medical and Dentistry in New Jersey graduating in 1983. Since then, and in his practice in Linden, Dr. Malka has focused on providing the most advanced dental treatment in the calmest of settings. Treating anxious or fearful patients with sedation and sometimes sleep dentistry, Dr. Malka has been able to provide a most relaxing environment.

Dr. Malka provides comprehensive dentistry and is most experienced in crowns, bridges, veneers, implant restoration, root canals, dentures, partial dentures, extractions, oral surgery, treating periodontal disease and tooth whitening.

Dr. Malka provides treatment to patients from surrounding towns of Elizabeth, Rahway, Clark, Cranford, Colonia, Roselle and Roselle Park. In addition patients travel from far off places in New Jersey and New York to see Dr. Malka.

Our office helps to maximize the available benefits of your insurance plan.
