Meet Dr. Britt McCarty

Dr. Britt McCarty will tell you he has the greatest job in the world! He truly loves and enjoys being with children and he gets to be with them all day in his pediatric dental office. Some of his most rewarding moments are when an apprehensive child undergoes a dental procedure and sits up to give him a big smile and a hug. Dr. Britt feels that is one of the most important parts of your child’s dental visit. This love and concern for your child will be the atmosphere you will find at his office.

Dr. McCarty can see looking back how God was preparing him for a career in Pediatric Dentistry. Through his educational experience and studies at Mississippi College and the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry, God’s hand was leading him to a Pediatric Dental Residency at Children’s Hospital Medical Center in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Dr. McCarty is actively involved at Highland Baptist Church serving as a Sunday school teacher and deacon. He has also coached in youth basketball and baseball leagues. Currently, he is troop master of the Trail Life troop in Meridian.

Blessed with a wonderful wife, Amy, and six children, Dr. McCarty is excited to work with his son Ross to continue to deliver the highest quality dental care possible to the children of East Mississippi and West Alabama.

We both look forward to making children smile and cultivating habits to make those smiles last a lifetime.
