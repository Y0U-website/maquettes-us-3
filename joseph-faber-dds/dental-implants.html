Dental Implants

If you are missing teeth, you don’t have to live with this problem forever. Getting your smile back could mean improved self-esteem, not to mention better health for your teeth! Gaps in your teeth can lead to bone loss and shifting teeth. Replacing your missing teeth is always a good decision for the future of your smile! If you are interested in treatment options, your dentist will examine your teeth and let you know the best plan for your smile. Dental implants may be recommended for treatment, as they are a great option for replacing missing teeth!

Dental implants are one of the most durable and long-lasting replacements for missing teeth. Dental implants are made of three parts the implant, abutment and dental crown. Although this treatment can take time, the investment is worth it! Each part will be placed separately, creating a lasting foundation for your future implant.

When your custom dental implant is made, it will be color-matched to your surrounding teeth. Your dental implant will be created to look and feel like a natural tooth, allowing it to blend in with your smile and also give you full functionality like a real tooth. With proper upkeep, your implant should last for years! Your dentist will examine your implant at regular visits to ensure it remains properly placed and secure.
