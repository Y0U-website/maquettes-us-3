How do I know if my teen needs braces or Invisalign?

The best way to know is to schedule a complimentary consultation for your teen.

We’ll examine their smile, explain what we see, and walk you through your options with no pressure to get started.

In the meantime, here are a few signs that may indicate your teen needs orthodontics:

  • Early, late or irregular loss of baby teeth
  • Crooked, crowded or gapped teeth
  • Lack of confidence when smiling or hiding smile regularly
  • Jaw shifting, popping or clicking
  • Jaws or teeth are disproportionate to the rest of the face
  • Thumb sucking / finger sucking habits in childhood
  • Grinding or clenching of teeth
  • Teeth that are blocked and cannot erupt
  • Jaw pain or facial pain
  • Difficulty or pain when chewing or biting
  • Persistent mouth breathing
