What Is Dental Cleaning and Why It Is Important

Dental cleaning is a professional procedure that removes plaque, tartar, and stains from the teeth and along the gumline. It is essential for preventing tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health problems.

This treatment also improves the appearance of your teeth by removing discoloration and promoting a brighter smile. Regular cleaning is a key part of maintaining overall dental health and hygiene.
