  • Step 1: Consultation Meet with our restorative dentist, Dr. Zielinski Learn more about our doctor , to see if you qualify for dental implants. We’ll complete a thorough exam, take x-rays and images, plus discuss your needs. You must be in overall good health and have enough bone density in your jaw to support the implant.
  • Step 2: Implant A small titanium post, which is the dental implant, replaces your tooth root. In time, the implant permanently bonds to your jawbone. We then add a small connector, AKA an abutment, onto the implant, which will hold your new restoration in place.
  • Step 3: Restoration Our cosmetic and restorative dentist combines beauty and function to plan your tooth replacement. We take impressions of your mouth and design your new crown, bridge, or denture to match your appearance. Once complete, our dentist attaches the restoration and double-checks for fit and comfort. Enjoy your new and improved smile for decades!
