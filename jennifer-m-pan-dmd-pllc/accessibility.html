Website Accessibility

  • Provide descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
  • Structure pages with proper headings for screen-reader navigation
  • Support keyboard navigation throughout the site
  • Design responsively so content works across devices and screen sizes

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website and work to improve the experience for all users. While we aim to adhere to accepted guidelines and standards, it may not always be possible to do so in every area of the website.

Need Help or Have Feedback?

If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you have suggestions on how we can improve, please let us know. We welcome your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to provide the information you need through an alternative communication method.
