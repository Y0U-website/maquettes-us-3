Our Dental Implant Process

Every implant case at Hawaiian Island Dental follows a careful, technology-guided process. Precision matters and Dr. Jaurequi takes the time to plan each placement thoroughly.

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a one-on-one appointment with Dr. Jaurequi. He ll take digital x-rays of your jaw, perform a thorough oral health evaluation, and determine whether you re a good candidate for an implant.

There are no age restrictions. The only real requirement is enough dense bone in your jaw to securely hold the implant.

If a bridge or partial denture would actually serve you better, he ll tell you. Honest, straightforward recommendations are part of how this practice operates.

Advanced 3-D Cone Beam Imaging

Before placement, Dr. Jaurequi uses leading-edge 3-D cone beam imaging (CBCT) to plan exactly where your implant will go.

Cone beam imaging generates three-dimensional views of your teeth, soft tissue, bones, sinuses, and nerve paths details that aren t visible with standard x-rays.

This level of detail is what makes precision placement possible. You can read more about how the practice uses Technology to improve every step of your care.

Implant Placement

The implant itself is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. Dr. Jaurequi places it in your jawbone during an in-office procedure that takes about an hour.

For patients who d like extra relaxation, nitrous oxide is available to help you stay comfortable throughout the appointment.

Healing and Integration

After placement, your implant needs several months to heal. During that time, it fuses with the surrounding bone and tissue in a process called osseointegration.

When healing is complete, the implant becomes a permanent part of your dental anatomy just like the tooth root it replaced.

Final Crown Restoration

Once your implant has fully integrated, Dr. Jaurequi attaches a custom dental crown on top. The result is a complete tooth replacement from root to crown.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a titanium tooth root that s placed in your jawbone and restored with a dental crown. Once healed, it looks, acts, and functions like a natural tooth and because it s anchored in bone, it provides the same kind of stability your original tooth root did.

How long do dental implants last?

A dental implant can potentially last a lifetime when properly cared for. Brushing, flossing, and routine six-month dental cleanings are the keys to long-term success.

Is the implant procedure painful?

The placement itself is performed as an in-office procedure that takes about an hour. Nitrous oxide is available if you d like gentle relaxation to stay comfortable throughout treatment.

Can implants replace more than one tooth?

Yes. Implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even serve as a stable base for partial dentures. Dr. Jaurequi will recommend the right approach based on your specific situation.

What if I m missing an entire arch of teeth?

In that case, All-on-4 may be a better fit than traditional individual implants. Visit our All On 4 Implants Lihue page to learn more.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. If you re uninsured or your plan falls short, financing through Cherry can help make treatment more affordable.

What else does Hawaiian Island Dental offer?
