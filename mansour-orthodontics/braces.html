Choosing the Right Braces

Thanks to modern technology, patients now have more options than ever when it comes to different types of braces. While standard metal braces are still widely used, clear braces are becoming increasingly popular along with removable appliances that can be used for mild cases of misaligned teeth. Schedule an adult orthodontic treatment or pediatric orthodontic treatment consultation to determine eligibility. Types of Braces

Our orthodontic treatments include a variety of braces to correct crooked teeth and bite issues. Schedule an appointment at one of our Kissimmee orthodontist or St. Cloud orthodontist offices today! We look forward to meeting you.

Metal Braces

Metal braces are the most common type of corrective braces and are made of high-grade stainless steel. Despite the negative stigma around metal braces, today’s options are smaller, more comfortable, and more visually appealing than those of the past.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces are made of translucent (clear) material and are most popular with adult patients due to their cosmetic appeal. The only drawback to ceramic brackets is that they are more fragile and the elastic ties can discolor between orthodontic visits.

Invisalign®

Invisalign® is a series of clear plastic aligners that straighten teeth without the noticeable bulky materials associated with traditional braces . No one will know you’re improving your smile unless you tell them!

Empower® Braces®

Empower® is a series of self-ligating braces from American Orthodontics. Self-ligating braces utilize lighter wires and lower friction brackets to move teeth, resulting in faster treatment times and greater overall comfort.
