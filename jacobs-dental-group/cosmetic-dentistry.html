First visit to Jacobs Dental Group?

Maybe you’ve been thinking about whitening your teeth for a while. Maybe you’ve always wanted to fix that one little thing. Whatever’s been holding you back, you’re worth it.

Your first visit is simple. Just a conversation about what you’d like to change, what’s possible, and how we can help. Whether it’s a small touch-up or a complete transformation, we’ll make sure you feel confident in your decision.

Financing and Insurance

Most dental insurance plans don’t cover cosmetic treatments, but that doesn’t mean a brighter, more confident smile is out of reach. We offer flexible financing options through CareCredit so you can break up the cost of treatments into manageable monthly payments.

For restorative and general dental care, we accept most insurance plans and will help you understand your coverage. If a referral to a specialist is needed, we’ll also consider insurance compatibility whenever possible. Call us at (308) 382-6660 to explore the options we have for you.
