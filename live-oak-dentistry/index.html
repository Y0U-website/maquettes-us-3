Dr. Beatriz Berríos Ouslán, DMD, MPH, is a patient-focused general dentist dedicated to providing comprehensive, personalized care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) and Master of Public Health (MPH) degrees from the University of Puerto Rico Medical Sciences Campus, following a Bachelor of Science in General Biology from the University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez.

To further her clinical expertise, she completed an advanced General Practice Residency (GPR) at the prestigious Montefiore Medical Center in New York, equipping her with advanced training in managing medically complex cases, oral surgery, endodontics, and prosthodontics. She is also a proud member of the American Dental Association.

As a GPR-trained clinician, Dr. Berríos combines hospital-grade experience with a passion for high-quality restorative and cosmetic dentistry, comprehensive family care, and Invisalign treatment. Fully fluent in both English and Spanish, she is committed to making every patient feel comfortable and confident in the chair through clear communication and trust-based relationships.

Having called the Orlando area home since 2021, Dr. Berríos loves serving her local community. Outside the oﬃce, she prioritizes quality time with her husband and their young daughter, enjoying family walks, local dining, and weekend trips to Disney. In her personal time, she is an avid reader, movie enthusiast, and loves traveling and diving into puzzles.
