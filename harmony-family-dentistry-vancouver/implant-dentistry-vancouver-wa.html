What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are inserted by a dentist into the jawbone. Over time, the bone grows around the implant. This process is called osseointegration, and it helps the implant stay strong and stable.

Types of Implant Solutions We Offer

Single Dental Implants

If you’re missing one tooth, a single implant is the perfect solution. Our dentist will place a titanium post in your jawbone where your missing tooth was located or used to be. After healing, we attach a custom-made crown that matches your other teeth perfectly.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 is a treatment for patients missing most or all teeth in one arch. Instead of replacing each tooth individually, we use just four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth. This approach is faster, more reasonable, and often doesn’t require bone grafting procedures.

The four implants act like pillars that hold a complete set of fixed teeth. You’ll leave our office with a beautiful, functional smile in just one day.

Implant Dentures

Implant dentures combine the stability of implants with the affordability of dentures. We place several implants in your jaw, and these implants snap onto a removable denture. This keeps your denture secure while eating and talking, but you can still remove it for cleaning.

Implant dentures are ideal if you want more stability than traditional dentures but prefer a removable option.
