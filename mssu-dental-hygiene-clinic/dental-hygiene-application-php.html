Program length: 2 years (including summer classes)
  • Class size: 30 students admitted each fall semester
  • Schedule: Courses meet five days per week

How to Apply

New test requirements for all applicants. Please review in application criteria.

Applicants must submit/complete the following:

  • Application for Admission to MSSU .
  • Complete the Application Checklist - Application Criteria .
  • Complete and submit Application for Admission to Dental Hygiene Programs .
  • Complete and submit Application for Job Shadowing
  • Dental Hygiene Application Rubric For Review

Admissions Requirements

To be considered for admission, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.00
  • Completion of all prerequisite courses with a grade of C or higher
  • Minimum of 16 hours of clinical job shadowing experience
  • Completion of all prerequisite coursework by the end of the spring semester prior to program entry
  • TEAS exam score of 50 or higher
  • Review Candidate Technical Standards Requirements

Prerequisite Courses & Testing Requirements

Prerequisite Courses

Applicants must complete the following courses:

  • COMM 100: Oral Communications
  • ENG 101: College Composition (WI)
  • MATH 030: Intermediate Algebra (or higher)
  • BIO 121: Human Anatomy & Physiology I (or BIO 201/BIO 301)
  • BIO 221: Human Anatomy & Physiology II
  • BIO 231: General & Medical Microbiology
  • CHEM 121 & CHEM 122: Chemistry for Allied Health Sciences (or CHEM 140/141)
  • Anatomy & Physiology I and II must have been completed within 5 years of application
  • Prerequisite courses may be attempted a maximum of two times
  • Withdrawals ( W ) are not counted as attempts

Testing Requirements

  • Minimum composite score: 50
  • Unlimited attempts allowed
  • Study resources available through ATI Testing
  • Registration through ATI Testing website or Student Success Office at 417-625-9577
  • Required for applicants whose first language is not English
  • Minimum scores: 600 (paper-based) or 200 (internet-based)
  • Must be scheduled independently through approved testing sites

After Acceptance into the program

Students accepted into the Dental Hygiene Program must complete additional requirements prior to enrollment, including:

Accepted students must also review and submit the Applicant Acceptance of Position Criteria form. A formal acceptance letter and orientation fee must be submitted by the stated deadline.

  • Medical consultation with Willcoxon Health Center
  • Completion of a physical examination and required health records
  • Dental examination
  • Required immunizations
  • CPR certification at the Healthcare Provider level
  • Proof of malpractice and health insurance
  • Signed Communicable Disease Policy acknowledgment
  • Criminal background check and drug/alcohol screening (at student expense)

Important Deadlines
