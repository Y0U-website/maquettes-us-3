Reasons to See an Orthodontist Milton, MA

Determining When It’s Time to Visit the Professionals

Our talented team here at Milton Orthodontics is proud to offer a wide variety of orthodontic treatments to help anyone achieve the happy, healthy smile they deserve. Whether you are facing crooked teeth, an impacted canine, or a misaligned bite, a highly experienced orthodontist in Milton is here to help.

However, it’s not always so easy determining when it’s time to visit the professionals on your own. A small issue that is easily ignored or seen as normal could potentially turn into a much more severe orthodontic issue later. That’s why we’ve gathered a list of some of the most common reasons to see an orthodontist in Milton, MA. If you have one or more of the following issues, give us a call today to schedule your appointment and take the next step toward a better smile.

Overcrowding

One of the most common problems we treat is crowded, crooked teeth. Overcrowding weakens your teeth and can dramatically increase your risk of cavities and gum disease. Thankfully, we can get your smile and oral health back in balance with treatments like traditional braces or Invisalign.

Gapped Teeth

While gaps between the teeth can be endearing, they may also make you feel self-conscious of your smile. What’s more, these gaps will collect food debris and plaque over time and put you at higher risk of tooth decay. Orthodontic treatments can gently bring your teeth back together and eliminate these problematic spaces.

Overbite

An overbite is when the front teeth sit too far in front of the lower teeth, causing them to protrude outwards. This puts extra wear and tear on the teeth, and it can also negatively impact your appearance and the health of your jaw joints. Orthodontic treatments can help bring your jaws into alignment so the front teeth rest just in front of the lower ones.

Underbite

When the lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth instead, it’s known as an underbite. Over time, this wears away at the enamel on the top teeth, leaving them much more susceptible to damage, decay, and sensitivity. With orthodontic services like braces, we can bring the lower jaw back into the correct position to correct your bite and protect your smile.

Crossbite

Crossbite is a mixture of underbite and overbite where only some of the lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth. This miss-matched bite problem puts excess stress on the jaw joints, which can cause them to become irritated, stiff, and sore over time. Crossbite is usually best addressed with traditional braces, which can help guide teeth into better positions for an improved look and feel.

Open Bite

If your upper and lower teeth do not touch when your mouth is fully closed, you have what’s called an open bite. Whether your open bite is the result of prolonged thumb-sucking, an injury, or genetics, traditional orthodontic treatments can help realign both the teeth and the jaws for a stronger, healthier smile.

Impacted Canines

If your child’s canine teeth are not erupting when expected, or if they’re baby teeth aren’t falling out when they should, we can use orthodontic treatments to help. One of our experienced orthodontists will design a personalized treatment plan that can help pull down the impacted canine tooth and encourage permanent teeth to erupt as they should. This could save your child’s precious smile from much more severe issues later in life!
