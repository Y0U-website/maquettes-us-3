Our Team

Harrington Endodontics is a family operated clinic.

Dr. Bernard Harrington

Dr. Bernie has been an endodontist for 30 years. He earned his doctorate of dental surgery and his general practice residency degrees from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and specialty training from the University of Detroit Mercy. He has been a clinical faculty member at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, a military endodontist at Fort Leonard Wood, MO and a community health endodontist at Lac du Flambeau, WI. He has completed a masters series in restorative driven implants. Dr. Bernie is a member of the ski patrol at Spirit Mountain in Duluth.

Dr. Pamela Harrington

Dr. Pam is a Duluth native. She has been an endodontist for 16 years and a hospital dentist for 10 years. She earned her doctorate of dental surgery from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and completed her residency in endodontics from West Virginia University. She was a hospital dentist at the Royal C Johnson Veterans Hospital in Sioux Falls, SD. Currently, she is an associate professor for the University of Minnesota teaching clinical endodontics at the Hibbing Community College Dental Clinic. Dr. Pam enjoys reading, skiing and horseback riding.
