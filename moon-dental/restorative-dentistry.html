Fillings

Fillings restore the shape and function of damaged/decayed teeth, preventing further decay and maintaining tooth structure. Different materials offer various benefits in durability, aesthetics, and cost.

Amalgam

Amalgam is made from a mixture of metals like silver, tin, mercury, and others. It’s a durable, cost-effective material that provides excellent resistance to wear and is commonly used for dental fillings, particularly in back teeth where strength is essential.

Silver

Silver fillings are a type of dental amalgam made from a mixture of metals, and they provide durability and resistance to wear, which makes them suitable for posterior teeth. While silver fillings provide a longer-lasting and cheaper alternative, their noticeable silver-gray color can lead patients to choose a more discreet material.

Tooth-Colored

Tooth-colored, or composite, fillings are typically composed of a putty-like composite resin or glass ionomer and blend seamlessly with natural tooth color, providing an aesthetically pleasing option. These materials are ideal for visible areas of the mouth, but may not be as durable or inexpensive as other options.

Dentures

WeDentures are removable replacement teeth that replace missing or extracted teeth, ensuring your smile is functional and beautiful. They can be full or partial, tailored to fit comfortably in the mouth, and typically require adjustments over time due to changes in the gums and oral structure.
