All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Improved chewing, speaking, and confidence
  • Eliminates the need for traditional dentures
  • Often avoids bone grafting
  • Fewer implants = reduced surgical time and cost
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure

Who Is a Candidate?

You may be a good candidate for All-on-4 if you:

If bone volume is a concern, our team may recommend bone grafting to enhance your eligibility for implants.

  • Have lost most or all teeth in one or both arches
  • Are currently wearing removable dentures
  • Have failing dental work or bridges
  • Want a long-term, fixed alternative to dentures
  • Have sufficient bone or want to avoid bone grafting

What to Expect from the All-on-4 Process

We offer complete surgical care in our modern Greenbrae facility, including IV sedation and advanced 3D diagnostics.

Step 1: Comprehensive Evaluation

Using cone beam CT imaging, we assess bone quality and plan precise implant placement.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Four implants are placed into the jaw in a single surgical visit. In many cases, a temporary set of teeth is attached the same day.

Step 3: Healing

Over the next few months, the implants integrate with the jawbone for long-term stability.

Step 4: Final Restoration

Your general dentist or prosthodontist will create and place your final custom prosthesis, matched to your bite and smile.

FAQs About All-on-4 Full Arch Implants

Is All-on-4 better than dentures?

Yes. All-on-4 provides a fixed, secure solution that eliminates the discomfort, movement, and maintenance of removable dentures.

Can I get All-on-4 if I’ve had bone loss?

Often, yes. The angled placement of the implants maximizes bone contact. If needed, bone grafting can be done beforehand.

How long does the procedure take?

Implant placement is typically completed in a single day, and temporary teeth may be placed immediately. Healing occurs over several months before final teeth are placed.

Will I be sedated for the procedure?

Yes. We offer IV sedation and other anesthesia options to ensure a safe and comfortable surgical experience.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With proper care, the implants themselves can last decades. The prosthetic arch may need to be replaced over time due to normal wear.
