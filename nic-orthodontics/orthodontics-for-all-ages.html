Surgical Orthodontics

For some patients, the best results come from combining braces with jaw surgery. This approach can improve the way the teeth fit together while also enhancing facial balance and creating a healthy, confident smile.

Tooth-Colored Braces

Interested in braces but looking for a less noticeable option? Ceramic braces are made out of a translucent ceramic material that matches the natural color of your teeth. It’s a great way to get all the benefits of traditional braces, without shiny metals or bright colors.

Iconix – A Stylish Alternative

Iconix Champagne Gold braces combine the strength and precision of traditional metal braces with a warm, champagne-colored finish for a more refined appearance. They blend beautifully with many skin tones and can be a great choice for patients who want an alternative to silver braces without sacrificing durability or effectiveness.
