Services

Valley Family Dentistry offers comprehensive general dentistry for your entire family. We provide a full array of dental services including preventative, restorative, reconstructive, cosmetic, and emergency care. By treating everyone, from the children in your family to the adults, we believe our office provides a convenient and enjoyable experience. It is important that we offer the best possible treatment for all of our pratients.

Explore this page to learn more about all the dental services we provide.

Your initial oral examination includes a visual examination, dental charting, periodontal probing, diagnosis and treatment recommendations. We will also take x-rays to help properly diagnose areas of teeth and structures that are not visible intraorally.

Our goal is to help you minimize the risk of emergency treatment. That is why we commit a generous amount of time designing a comprehensive treatment plan that will help you offset the possibility of unforeseen pain or tooth breakage. If you do have an emergency, call our office at 540-343-5521 to schedule an appointment. We make every effort to see emergency patients as soon as possible.
