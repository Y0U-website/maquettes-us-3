Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a dental procedure which is used to improve the color and aesthetic appearance of one’s teeth. In brief, it is a procedure where a whitening gel is used to remove the stains from the surface of the teeth, making them look whiter. Teeth whitening has been in practice for many years now and is becoming more popular, as boasting a bright smile is a great sign of confidence. There are several methods through which whiter teeth can be obtained.

What are the widely used teeth whitening techniques?

This is the most widely used teeth whitening treatment. It is very simple and can be done in just one visit to the dentist’s office. The teeth are thoroughly cleaned before starting the procedure. Next, a whitening gel or a bleach is applied to the teeth and spread evenly. The gel removes the stains from the surface of the teeth. The procedure can be repeated several times, depending on the degree of staining on the teeth and the level of whitening the patient prefers.

  • Whitening gets rid of all the stains on the teeth, making them look whiter. Thereby, it enhances your smile.
  • It gets rid of the bacterial accumulation on the surface of the teeth.
  • It is a serious confidence booster.
  • It helps maintain whiter teeth for a long time, provided healthy oral habits are practiced.
