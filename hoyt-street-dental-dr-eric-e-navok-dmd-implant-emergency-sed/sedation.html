Sedation Options at Hoyt Street Dental

Nitrous Oxide

Oral Sedation

An oral sedation medication is prescribed for you to take approximately one hour before your appointment. You will feel deeply relaxed, possibly drowsy, and many patients have little memory of the procedure afterward. You will need a driver. Ideal for moderate anxiety or longer procedures.

IV Sedation

IV sedation delivers a controlled sedative directly into the bloodstream for a deeper state of relaxation. You remain conscious but extremely relaxed, with no awareness of the procedure. This option is ideal for severe dental anxiety, complex procedures, or patients requiring extensive work in a single session. You will need a driver home. IV sedation is administered by a qualified sedation specialist.

Is Sedation Dentistry Right for You?

You may benefit from sedation dentistry if:

  • You have a low pain threshold or sensitive gag reflex
  • You cannot sit still in the dental chair
  • You have years of dental work that needs to be completed
  • You have had a traumatic dental experience in the past
  • You are deeply afraid of needles, drills, or dental sounds
