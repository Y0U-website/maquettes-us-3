All-on-X Dental Implants

All-on-X dental implants provide a long-lasting solution for patients seeking greater comfort, improved functionality, and a confident smile. With advanced technology and personalized treatment planning, this full-arch restoration option is designed to deliver stable, natural-looking results.

  • Designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Helps restore chewing, speaking, and smiling confidence
  • May reduce slipping or movement compared to traditional dentures
  • Supports jawbone health and long-term facial structure

All-on-X Dental Implants FAQs

All-on-X refers to a full-arch dental implant treatment where a customized number of implants are used to support a fixed set of replacement teeth.

Traditional dentures are removable, while All-on-X restorations are fixed to dental implants for improved stability, comfort, and chewing function.

Candidates often include patients with multiple missing teeth, failing teeth, uncomfortable dentures, or those seeking a more secure full-arch solution.

With proper care, regular dental visits, and good oral hygiene, All-on-X implants can provide a long-lasting solution for full-arch restoration.

All-on-X restorations are fixed in place and are not removed daily like traditional dentures. Your dental team can remove them if maintenance is needed.
