The practice

Our friendly dentist, Dr. Christopher Mast, along with our professional staff go out of their way to make every patient feel comfortable during their appointment. With a relaxed and inviting atmosphere, complimentary coffee and wine, and comforting music; our office ensures that every patient feels at ease when they come in to see us.

Aislinn has been a part of Mast Dentistry from the beginning. Beyond managing the dental practice and coordinating appointments for our patients she trains and educates staff, as well as Carroll College students. She says what keeps her coming to work are the wonderful patients at Mast Laser Dentistry. She loves the Helena community and loves taking care of her friends, neighbors and local people…

After 20 years of dental experience, she knows people of any age value their time, their money, and their teeth. Aislinn works hard to make sure patients understand what role dentistry plays in their overall health and well-being. She wants people to enjoy a health filled life and understand Mast Laser Dentistry can provide them with the best in laser dentistry.

Outside of the office she enjoys the abundance of hiking and biking trails in Helena. Being a mother of two high energy children and raising them in Montana is the best part of her life. She also supports the Cohesion Dance Project and performed in their first year presentation of Nutcracker on the Rocks.

Aislinn is our in-house lactation specialist. She finds it so rewarding to work with mothers and babies supporting them through this important relationship and journey. Having had two tongue tied children and working through the same struggles as many mothers experience, it is edifying to bring advice and relief to new moms.

At Mast Laser Dentistry, we take the comfort of our patients seriously. We’re dedicated to creating a relaxed, comforting environment where every patient feels safe and can receive the dental care they need without hesitation.

While you wait for your appointment time, feel free to enjoy a fresh-brewed cup of coffee or a glass of wine (if you’re old enough, naturally) so that your appointment starts off inviting and wonderful.

If you’re a good friend or family member and are waiting for a patient while they receive care, we want you to also feel comfortable! Relax in our Boxwood-designed décor, sip on complimentary wine or coffee, and enjoy the calming music we play.
