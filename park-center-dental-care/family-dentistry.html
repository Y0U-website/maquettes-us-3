Here at Park Center Dental Care, we are focused on providing outstanding dental care for in Sioux Falls, SD, and the surrounding areas. As a family dental practice, we care for patients of all ages. Our experience in providing top-notch care with a gentle touch ensures that all of our patients, from young children to senior citizens, get the care they need without the usual stress and unpleasantries of visiting the dentist. As we grow and age, our oral health needs change. Our dentists work with patients to address their dental health needs throughout every stage of life.

It’s recommended that a child should have their first dental exam at around one year of age. This first appointment will include an examination of your child’s head and neck and an evaluation of the teeth, gums, and bite. Preventative appointments may consist of a fluoride treatment, tooth cleaning, x-rays (if needed), and an exam by one of our experienced dentists. As your child ages, we will monitor the eruption of their adult teeth and recommend orthodontics if needed.

Our goal is to be a one-stop-shop for all of your family’s dental needs. We have patients that came to us as children that are now bringing their children to us as well. In addition to our general dentistry services , we also provide cosmetic dental procedures, including teeth whitening, crowns, bridges, dentures, veneers, and more .
