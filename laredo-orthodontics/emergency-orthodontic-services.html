Common Orthodontic Emergencies And First Aid

Be prepared to handle common emergencies until you reach our office:

  • Displaced or broken brackets: Cover with orthodontic wax to prevent tissue irritation.
  • Poking wires: Use a pencil eraser or wax to reposition or cover the sharp end.
  • Lost aligners: Keep previous sets as temporary replacements and contact us promptly.

Our Approach To Handling Orthodontic Emergencies

  • Rapid Response: Quick assessment and treatment to alleviate pain and prevent further damage.
  • Expert Care: Skilled orthodontic interventions to address the root cause of the emergency.
  • Clear Communication: Guidance on managing the situation and preventing future incidents.

Post-Emergency Follow-Up And Care

Ensuring your recovery and continued treatment success:

  • Follow-up Appointments: Monitoring your recovery and making necessary adjustments.
  • Revised Treatment Plans: Adapting your ongoing treatment if required.
  • Preventive Advice: Tips to minimize the risk of future orthodontic emergencies.
