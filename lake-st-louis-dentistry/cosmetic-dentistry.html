What Treatments Are Included in Cosmetic Dentistry?

Our practice offers a range of cosmetic dental treatments designed to enhance the appearance of your smile while maintaining strong oral health. Each procedure is customized to match the patient’s goals, facial features, and dental condition.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening can dramatically brighten teeth that have become stained from coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, or natural aging. Our office uses professional-strength whitening systems that lift deep discoloration safely and effectively, producing noticeably brighter results compared to store-bought products.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of teeth. They can correct several cosmetic concerns at once, including discoloration, chips, uneven edges, and minor spacing issues. Veneers are carefully designed to create a natural, balanced appearance that blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair minor damage and reshape teeth. This treatment is often recommended for small chips, cracks, or gaps. The material is sculpted directly onto the tooth and hardened with a special curing light, restoring both appearance and strength.

Dental Crowns

A dental crown completely covers a damaged tooth, restoring both structure and visual harmony. Crowns are often used when a tooth has experienced significant wear or breakage. Modern materials allow crowns to mimic the natural color and translucency of enamel.

Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover combines multiple aesthetic procedures to achieve a comprehensive transformation. Treatment plans may include whitening, veneers, bonding, or crowns depending on the patient’s needs. Each smile makeover is carefully planned to create natural-looking results that complement facial structure and personal preferences.

What Is the Difference Between Cosmetic Dentistry and General Dentistry?

General dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health through prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of dental conditions. Common services include exams, cleanings, fillings, and treatment for gum disease.

Cosmetic dentistry, on the other hand, focuses primarily on improving the visual appearance of teeth and the smile as a whole. These treatments enhance symmetry, brightness, and alignment.

Although these two areas have different goals, many procedures provide benefits in both categories. For example:

A comprehensive approach often blends preventive care with aesthetic improvements to maintain long-term dental health.

  • Dental crowns restore strength while improving appearance
  • Bonding repairs damage and improves tooth shape
  • Veneers protect enamel while enhancing aesthetics
