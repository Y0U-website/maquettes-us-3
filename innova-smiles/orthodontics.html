Life With Clear Aligners: What to Expect

Starting aligner therapy is a commitment, and knowing what to expect makes the journey far smoother. Here is a realistic phase-by-phase overview of treatment at Innova Smiles.

The first few days with a new set of trays typically bring mild pressure or tenderness as your teeth begin to move. Most patients describe it as a dull ache rather than sharp pain, similar to the feeling after a workout. Over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen or acetaminophen are usually sufficient. By day three or four, the discomfort fades as your teeth settle into their new position.

Tips for a smooth aligner experience

  • → Set phone alarms as reminders to reinsert after meals.
  • → Always carry your aligner case, never put trays in a pocket or bag unwrapped.
  • → Use chewies (small foam cylinders) for 5–10 minutes after inserting a new tray to seat the aligner fully against your teeth.
  • → Avoid coffee, tea, and red wine while aligners are in, even light staining accumulates over months.
  • → Take photos of your smile every 4 weeks to track your own progress.
  • → If a tray feels unusually loose after 48 hours, contact us, it may indicate a tracking issue.

Orthodontic Investment & Financing

What determines your investment?

  • → Number of aligner trays needed, longer, more complex movements require more trays.
  • → Complexity of tooth movement, rotations, bite corrections, and vertical movements are more involved than simple spacing.
  • → The aligner system selected — Invisalign®, Spark™, or Invisalign® Teen have slightly different pricing structures.
  • → Refinement trays, if additional trays are needed at the end of your series to finalize the result.
