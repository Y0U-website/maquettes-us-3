The practice

At Beltline Family Dentistry, we believe dental care should be straightforward, comfortable, and centered around you. We are committed to honest recommendations, high-quality treatment, and building long-term relationships with every patient

As knowledge grows, so does our practices. Each of our staff is trained to have the most up-to-date skills and are actively engaging in continuing education. We value your dental health, but most of all we also value you , the customer. No decision will be made on your behalf, we’ll keep you educated and informed on any procedures that may be in your future. No pushy salesmen tactics. Above all…

Dr. Matthew Hudson was born and raised in Grand Rapids, Michigan. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Michigan and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry.

Dr. Hudson is married and lives in the Grand Rapids community with his wife and their two boys.

His faith is central to his life and practice. Dr. Hudson views dentistry not only as a profession, but as a vocational calling an opportunity to serve others and share the love, hope, and good news of Jesus Christ. He is passionate about using his skills to care for patients while reflecting Christ through compassion, integrity, and service.
