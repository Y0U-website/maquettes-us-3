All Services

Cleanings & Fluoride

Regular cleanings and fluoride applications to keep teeth healthy and strong.

✨ Dental Fillings Quick, comfortable treatment to repair cavities and protect teeth.

📷 Dental X-Rays Low-dose digital X-rays to detect cavities and issues before they become problems.

👑 Dental Crowns Durable restorations to protect and restore damaged baby and permanent teeth.

🌟 Baby Nerve Treatment Removal of infected pulp from baby teeth to eliminate pain and prevent further issues.

🌈 Simple Extractions Comfortable removal of teeth that cannot be saved, keeping your child at ease throughout.

🦄 Space Maintainers Custom appliances that hold space for permanent teeth after early tooth loss.
