Deep Teeth Cleaning Scaling & Root Planing

Periodontal therapy may involve various treatment options. Regardless of the treatment chosen, the primary goal is to remove harmful bacteria and help diseased gum tissue return to a healthier state. Scaling and root planing (deep teeth cleaning) is one of the most effective nonsurgical treatments for managing gum disease and preventing it from progressing.

A significant element of maintaining a good oral health routine is scheduling regular exams and cleanings. Patients who have developed or are at risk of developing periodontal disease may need to schedule these cleanings with more frequency. Benefits of Deep Teeth Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing)

Deep teeth cleaning helps to address gum disease that has progressed to the point where a surface-level cleaning isn’t enough. During a scaling and root planing procedure, the teeth are cleaned below the gum line. This can help to reverse gingivitis and stop the progression of periodontitis. Patients can experience an elimination of bleeding and swelling, a shallowing of pockets, improvement in breath odor, and the overall elimination of tartar, plaque, and debris below the gum line.
