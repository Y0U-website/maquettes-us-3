Legal Services

We provide community members with information and counseling on immigration and criminal law matters, as well as referral to attorneys we know and trust.

Health Education

We provide health education classes such as the Diabetes Prevention Program to help patients manage their chronic conditions.

Food & Diaper Distribution

We work with the Utah Food Bank and the Utah Diaper Bank to provide food and diapers to CBC clients and community members.

Afterschool & Enrichment

With the support of our volunteers, we provide afterschool programs, including music classes, for elementary school children.
