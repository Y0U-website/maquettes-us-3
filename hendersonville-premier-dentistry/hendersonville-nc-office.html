OUR OFFICE

Hendersonville, NC

WE TAKE GREAT CARE TO MAKE YOU COMFORTABLE ON YOUR FIRST VISIT .

Dr. Chris Planer and his wife, Jessica, who are both dentists, own and operate the practice. On your very first visit, you will quickly learn that the entire office has been carefully designed to provide a calm, private, and relaxing environment. From the hand painted murals on the walls to the low volume of patients, everything has been thoughtfully planned to make your visit with us comfortable and stress-free.

We spend the time needed to fully understand your needs and desires and then work together to develop a plan that is right for you. Our goal is to educate you on the state of your oral condition and the available treatment options to achieve the results you want. We are not “sales” people and we do not pressure you to do treatment. Our patients make the decisions about their care. We provide you the information necessary to make informed, educated decisions that are best for your unique circumstances.
