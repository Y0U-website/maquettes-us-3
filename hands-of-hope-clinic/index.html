HANDS OF HOPE CLINIC

Henry County’s only charitable medical, dental, and mental health counseling clinic.

The practice

At Hands of Hope Clinic, we care for the whole person—body, mind, and smile. Through our medical, dental, and mental health programs, we provide compassionate, high-quality care for uninsured adults in Henry County. Every visit helps restore health, dignity, and hope.

How you can help Hands of Hope At the foundation of Hands of Hope Clinic are selfless and knowledgeable volunteers who dedicate their time to providing our patients with exceptional care. Basic and specialty medical services,dental procedures, and professional counseling are performed by doctors, nurse practitioners, registered nurses, dentists, and other licensed professionals within the clinic…

We rely on the time and knowledge of licensed professionals such as dentists, doctors, physician assistants, nurse practitioners, nurses, counselors and social workers, as well as medical and dental assistants. Volunteering with a nonprofit like ours is a great way to serve your community, and feel fulfilled as you make a difference in the lives of others.
