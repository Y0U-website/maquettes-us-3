  • Full dentures - To receive full dentures, any remaining teeth may need to be removed. After extraction, your gums will require time to heal before your custom-made dentures can be fitted. During this healing period, you’ll be provided with temporary dentures for comfort and function.
  • Partial dentures - These dentures are typically used when you’re missing one or several teeth in a row. They rely on your remaining healthy teeth for support, with a custom framework built to securely hold the denture in place.
