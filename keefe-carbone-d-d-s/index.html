The practice

Dr. Carbone enjoys working with the Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry as a care provider with the Give Back A Smile Program which connects victims of domestic violence with cosmetic dentists to restore their smiles to perfection.

His favorite aspect of dentistry is getting to know his patients and taking care of them and their families. He loves getting to visit with his patients about their dental needs, but also about what is going on in their lives. Dr. Carbone is a true believer in treating people, not teeth.

When Dr. Keefe is not caring for patients you will find him spending time with his wife and two sons, Parker and Chase. They usually are cheering on local high school teams, the OKC Thunder, or spending time outdoors taking in all the activities Oklahoma has to offer.

Dr. Micah Coleman was born and raised in Lawton, Oklahoma, where he developed a deep appreciation for community and service. He earned his undergraduate degree in Chemistry from Cameron University before pursuing his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry.

Dr. Coleman’s passion for dentistry is rooted in personal experience. After having a difficult dental visit as a child, he made it his mission to ensure his own patients feel comfortable, respected, and genuinely cared for. Today, he is committed to creating a positive, stress-free experience for every patient who walks through his doors. He believes dentistry is not just about healthy…

He especially enjoys helping patients overcome dental anxiety and takes pride in combining clinical excellence with compassion and clear communication.

Outside of the office, Dr. Coleman enjoys life with his wonderful wife, Emily, and their two children, Strider and Sophie. As a family, they love playing board games, spending time outdoors, and hiking at the Wichita Mountains Wildlife Refuge. Whether at home or in the practice, Dr. Coleman values connection, laughter, and making others feel at ease.
