Types of Dental Implant Procedures

At Spring Valley Dental Group, we design treatment plans around your needs and goals:

Single-Tooth Implants

Perfect for replacing a single missing tooth. A crown is attached to the implant for a natural look and feel.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Used to replace multiple teeth in a row without altering the surrounding natural teeth. Anchored by implants instead of crowns.

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing most or all teeth, dentures can be secured with implants for greater comfort, stability, and chewing power. Learn more about implant-supported dentures →

What to Expect During Dental Implant Treatment

Consultation – We evaluate your oral health, take 3D imaging, and determine your candidacy. Implant Placement – Titanium posts are placed into the jawbone. Healing Phase – Implants bond naturally with your bone (osseointegration). Restoration – The final step of your implant journey is a custom-shaded porcelain crown that matches your natural teeth.

FAQs About Dental Implants in O’Fallon, IL

Am I a candidate for dental implants in O’Fallon?

Most healthy adults in St. Clair County are candidates for implants. While adequate jawbone is required for stability, we offer bone grafting at our O’Fallon office to help patients with low bone volume achieve a successful result.

Do dental implants hurt?

We understand dental anxiety is real; however, the procedure is performed under local anesthesia for your total comfort. Most patients report that the sensation is no more intense than a routine extraction, and we provide clear post-operative care to manage any minor tenderness.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The timeline from placement to your final custom crown or bridge typically takes several months to ensure the implant properly integrates with the bone. To ensure you never leave our office with a gap, we provide comfortable temporary options during the healing phase.

How much do dental implants cost in O’Fallon?

Costs vary based on the number of teeth replaced and if preliminary treatments are needed. We believe in financial transparency; our team provides a detailed plan including membership savings and financing options to make permanent tooth replacement accessible.

Will my insurance cover dental implants?

While many plans cover the restorative components like dentures or crowns, they may not cover the surgical post. We are experts at navigating insurance benefits and will perform a complimentary review to maximize your specific coverage.

Can I get dental implants if I have a heart condition or take blood thinners?

Many patients with heart conditions can still be candidates for dental implants, but treatment timing and medical coordination matter. If you have had a recent heart attack, take blood thinners, or have another cardiac concern, our O’Fallon dental team will review your health history and may coordinate with your physician or cardiologist before implant placement.
