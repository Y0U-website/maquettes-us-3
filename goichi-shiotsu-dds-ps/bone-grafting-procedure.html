Types of Dental Bone Grafts and Their Role in Dental Health

At Mercer Island Dental, Dr. Shiotsu specializes in two types of dental bone grafting procedures. These bone graft procedures help fix damaged bone tissue. They create a stable base for your dental implants. This ensures lasting results and better oral health.

Allograft

An allograft involves using bone from a donor or cadaver bone. The allograft bone is carefully cleaned, processed, and stored in a tissue bank to ensure safety before use. Unlike organ transplants, allografts do not contain living cells, reducing the chance of rejection and infection.

One of the significant benefits of grafting using allograft bone is that no additional surgical procedure is needed to harvest the bone from your own body, which helps reduce the risk of infection and minimizes recovery time.

Autograft

An autograft is a bone graft tooth procedure that uses bone from your own body. Familiar sources for harvesting autograft bone include the ribs, hips, jaw, or pelvis. How to Get Ready for Your Bone Graft Dental Treatment

Before your bone grafting tooth procedure, Dr. Shiotsu will review your medical history. They will also examine the health of your teeth.

This step is essential to help dentists understand your bone health and the side effects that could affect your dental bone graft procedure. Our expert dentists will customize a treatment plan based on the amount of bone you need to be grafted in your jaw.

You might also discuss any essential medications, over-the-counter drugs, or supplements you take. These can affect your treatment plan and surgical procedure outcome.

For patients who have chosen to have the procedure performed under oral conscious sedation or IV sedation, they may need to fast (not eat or drink) for 8-12 hours before the procedure. This helps lower risks linked to anesthesia, like nausea or aspiration.

Dr. Shiotsu will provide clear, detailed instructions for your surgical procedure. These instructions may include fasting guidelines, medication adjustments, and recovery expectations. Following these instructions carefully is crucial for your smooth surgical procedure.

Anesthesia can sometimes make it hard for you to drive. Having someone drive you home after your procedure is also essential.

Following pre-surgery guidelines is essential. It can help lower side effects and support health. This will also lead to better results from your bone graft dental treatment.

Easy Tips for Recovering After Bone Graft Dental Procedures

A dental bone graft recovery can take a few days to a few weeks. The complete bone healing for mature bone can take a few months to 6 months. The healing process depends on the amount of bone treated and other factors. Dr. Shiotsu will give you specific instructions for your post-surgery period.

To help reduce swelling, apply ice to the area and keep it elevated to avoid blood clotting. A balanced diet rich in nutrients will also support healing and encourage bone growth and bone healing.

Smoking can severely slow your healing process of dental bone grafting procedures and may increase the risk of graft failure. Research shows that bone grafts often fail in smokers.

Some surgeons may refuse to do elective bone graft teeth for smokers. If you smoke, it’s highly recommended to quit before and during your recovery to ensure the best outcome for your bone graft tooth.

You can have a smooth recovery by following the expert recovery tips from Dr. Shiotsu. This will help reduce the risk of side effects.
