The practice

At Kinston Smiles, we accept most insurance providers, as well as health savings account (HSA) and flex spending account (FSA) plans.

We will file all necessary claims and paperwork with your insurance provider. We will provide you with an explanation of your dental benefits prior to dental treatment.

Our staff is very knowledgeable and experienced in handling insurance matters and will be happy to answer any questions you have about your coverage.

At Kinston Smiles Family Dental, we put your needs first. We provide compassionate and personalized care for patients of all ages. Your time and overall health matters to us.

We understand that for most people, taking a trip to the dentist is not the easiest thing to do. So, our goal is to make sure our patients always get the best quality dental care in a friendly, calming environment.

We live up to our reputation for having a friendly and welcoming staff who makes sure every patient is at ease during their visit to our clinic. More importantly, our dental professionals aren’t only experienced with different procedures; they’re also committed to guiding our patients toward better oral care and a healthier lifestyle.

We will give you the perfect smile using the latest technology and techniques in Dentistry. Common procedures used to improve your teeth include crowns, bridges, dentures, teeth whitening, and many other procedures.

We provide emergency and comprehensive exams. We conduct oral cancer screenings, x-rays, periodontal screening, a test of jaw issues and bite problems, or tooth-specific exams. These tests help the dentist identify the seriousness of a patient’s dental condition, and determine the best personalized treatment.
