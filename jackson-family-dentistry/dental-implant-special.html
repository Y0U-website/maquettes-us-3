With proper care, dental implants can last 30+ years, and most patients keep their implants for the rest of their lives. Since implants are surgically inserted into your jaw, they bond with the surrounding tissue and for all intents and purposes, become a natural part of your mouth.

Once your dental implant has healed, you can care for it as you would a natural tooth. Simply brush twice a day and floss once per day, and come in for routine checkups every six months. One of the biggest benefits of dental implants is that there is no special care required!
