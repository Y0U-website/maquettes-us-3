Types of Dental Implants: Single Tooth

While there are various types of dental implants, one of the most common is the single-tooth implant. This option is ideal for patients who are missing one tooth or need to have a single tooth replaced. Here’s what you need to know:

The result is a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth.

  • A titanium post is surgically placed in your jawbone to act as a new root.
  • An abutment is attached to the post, which will hold the crown.
  • A custom-made crown is created to match your natural teeth and is attached to the abutment.

Are Implants Safe?

Dental implants are considered a safe and effective treatment for tooth replacement. With decades of clinical research and a success rate of up to 98 %, implants have become the gold standard in tooth replacement. At Noel Dental Arts, we use only high-quality materials and follow strict sterilization protocols to ensure your safety throughout the implant process.

Dental Implants Pros and Cons

  • Look and function like natural teeth
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care
  • Preserve jawbone and facial structure
  • Don’t require altering adjacent teeth
  • Easy to care for (brush and floss like natural teeth)
  • Initial cost may be higher than other options
  • Requires a surgical procedure
  • The process takes several months to complete
  • Not everyone is a suitable candidate (depends on bone density and overall health)

Are Implants Worth It?

When considering the long-term benefits, dental implants are often the most cost-effective and beneficial option for tooth replacement. Unlike dentures or bridges that may need to be replaced every 5-10 years, implants can last a lifetime with proper care. They also help maintain your jawbone health and facial structure, preventing the sunken appearance that can occur with long-term tooth loss.

While the initial cost may be higher than other options, the durability, functionality, and natural appearance of implants make them a worthwhile investment in your oral health and quality of life. Many patients find that the confidence and comfort they gain from implants far outweigh the cost.

Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process at Noel Dental Arts typically involves these steps:

Throughout the process, our team will ensure your comfort and answer any questions you may have.

  • Initial Consultation : Dr. Noel will examine your mouth, take X-rays, and discuss your options.
  • Treatment Planning : We’ll create a customized treatment plan based on your specific needs.
  • Implant Placement : The titanium post is surgically placed in your jawbone.
  • Healing Period : Over several months, the implant fuses with your bone (osseointegration).
  • Abutment Placement : Once healed, we’ll attach the abutment to the implant.
  • Crown Placement : Finally, we’ll attach your custom-made crown to the abutment.
