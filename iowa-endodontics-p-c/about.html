An endodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on diagnosing and treating problems inside the tooth, particularly infections or injuries affecting the dental pulp.

After completing dental school, endodontists undergo additional years of advanced training in root canal therapy, endodontic surgery, and complex procedures designed to preserve natural teeth.

At Iowa Endodontics, our practice is dedicated exclusively to procedures within the field of Endodontics . This specialized focus allows us to provide highly accurate diagnosis and treatment for patients experiencing tooth pain, infection, or dental trauma.

Our Commitment to Saving Natural Teeth

One of the primary goals of endodontic care is to preserve natural teeth whenever possible . Saving a natural tooth helps maintain proper bite function, supports oral health, and often prevents the need for more complex restorative procedures. Our team provides advanced treatments designed to eliminate infection and restore healthy tooth function. These procedures may include:

Root Canal Treatment to remove infection inside the tooth

Endodontic Retreatment when a previously treated tooth requires additional care

Through these specialized treatments, we help patients maintain healthy, functional smiles.

Advanced Technology for Accurate Diagnosis

Iowa Endodontics uses modern dental technology to improve diagnostic accuracy and treatment outcomes. These tools allow our specialists to detect problems that may not be visible during routine dental examinations.

These technologies allow us to diagnose problems earlier and perform treatment with exceptional precision.

Our technology includes:

3D Cone Beam CT (CBCT) imaging

Surgical operating microscopes

Digital radiography

Advanced laser technology

Frequently Asked Questions About Iowa Endodontics

What does an endodontist do?

An endodontist specializes in diagnosing and treating conditions affecting the inner structures of teeth, including infections of the dental pulp and root canal system.

Why would my dentist refer me to an endodontist?

Dentists refer patients to endodontists when specialized treatment is needed for infections, trauma, or complex root canal cases.

Is endodontic treatment painful?

Modern endodontic procedures are designed to relieve pain rather than cause it. Advanced anesthetics and technology help ensure a comfortable treatment experience.

What technologies does Iowa Endodontics use?

Our practice uses advanced tools such as CBCT 3D imaging, surgical microscopes, and laser technology to improve diagnosis and treatment precision.
