Comprehensive Podiatry Services in Hot Springs

Podiatry Services We Offer

Healthy feet are essential to mobility, independence, and overall well-being. Our podiatry services are designed to diagnose, treat, and manage common and complex foot and ankle conditions with a focus on long-term function and comfort. Whether you are dealing with chronic pain, an injury, or a condition related to diabetes or aging, our team provides clear answers and practical treatment options tailored to your needs.

Nail Fungus

Nail fungus can cause thickened, discolored, or brittle nails that worsen over time without treatment. Our podiatry team provides accurate diagnosis and effective treatment options to improve nail health, reduce discomfort, and prevent recurrence.

Arthritis

Arthritis in the feet and ankles can lead to chronic pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility. We focus on relieving symptoms and preserving joint function through personalized treatment plans designed to support daily movement and long-term comfort.

Gout

Gout is a painful form of arthritis caused by uric acid buildup, often affecting the foot and ankle joints. Our approach emphasizes proper diagnosis, flare-up management, and ongoing care strategies to reduce pain and lower the risk of future attacks.

Fungal Infections

Fungal infections of the feet can cause itching, redness, cracking, and persistent irritation if left untreated. We offer targeted treatments to eliminate infection, restore skin health, and help prevent reinfection through practical care guidance.

Warts & Bunions

Warts and bunions can cause ongoing discomfort and interfere with walking and daily activities. Our podiatry services focus on relieving pain, addressing underlying causes, and providing treatment options that improve function and long-term foot health.

Wounds

Foot and ankle wounds require prompt, specialized care to prevent infection and support proper healing. We provide thorough evaluation and treatment plans designed to promote healing, protect tissue, and reduce the risk of complications.

Diabetes

Diabetes can significantly impact foot health, increasing the risk of nerve damage, poor circulation, and slow-healing wounds. Our podiatry care emphasizes prevention, regular monitoring, and early intervention to help protect mobility and avoid serious complications.

Hammertoe

Hammertoe is a deformity that causes toes to bend or curl, often leading to pain and pressure issues. We offer treatment options focused on relieving discomfort, improving alignment, and helping patients stay active and comfortable.

Corns & Calluses

Corns and calluses develop from repeated pressure or friction and can become painful if left untreated. Our podiatry care focuses on safe removal, pressure relief, and prevention strategies to keep feet comfortable and protected.

Plantar Fasciitis

Plantar fasciitis is a common cause of heel pain that can worsen with activity and prolonged standing. We provide targeted treatment plans to reduce inflammation, relieve pain, and support long-term foot function.

Ingrown Toenails

Ingrown toenails occur when the nail grows into the surrounding skin, causing pain, swelling, and infection risk. Our team offers effective treatment to relieve discomfort, promote healing, and prevent recurrence.

Orthotics

Orthotics are custom or supportive inserts designed to improve foot alignment and reduce strain. We assess gait and foot structure to recommend orthotic solutions that enhance comfort, stability, and everyday movement.
