Dental crowns and bridges

A dental crown covers the entire visible portion of a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength. Crowns are ideal for teeth with large fillings, root canal treatments, or significant decay where a simple filling won’t provide enough support. Modern crowns are crafted to match the color and contour of your natural teeth, blending seamlessly with your smile.

Dental bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to the natural teeth on either side. Bridges restore your ability to chew properly, maintain facial structure, and prevent remaining teeth from shifting out of alignment. We design and place bridges right here in our Gaithersburg office, ensuring a perfect fit and natural appearance.

The crown process

Creating a crown typically requires two appointments. At your first visit, Dr. Ali prepares the tooth, takes impressions, and places a temporary crown to protect the tooth while your permanent restoration is fabricated. During your second visit, we remove the temporary and place your new crown, adjusting the bite and ensuring it feels comfortable. Most crowns last 10 – 15 years or longer with proper care.

Fillings, inlays, and onlays

Dental fillings repair cavities and restore decayed areas of teeth. We use tooth-colored composite fillings that blend naturally and bond directly to the tooth structure, strengthening it while maintaining a more conservative approach than older silver amalgam restorations.

For larger areas of decay or damage, inlays and onlays offer a more durable alternative to standard fillings. These custom-fabricated restorations fit precisely into the damaged area and are often made from porcelain or composite materials that resist staining and last longer than conventional fillings.
