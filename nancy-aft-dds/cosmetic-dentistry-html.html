Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, durable porcelain sheaths that are specially designed to fit over the visible surface of individual teeth. Once attached, they can make any damage, slight misalignment, gaps, and discoloration disappear, revealing an even and bright grin. Dental porcelain is a non-porous, stain-resistant material, so your new-and-improved teeth will continue looking bright for longer.

Metal-Free Dental Restorations

In the past, dental restorations like fillings and crowns were predominantly made from metals because they were more durable. However, thanks to advancements in the dental world, our team at Brooklyn City Dental is able to protect and restore damaged teeth without drawing attention to them. We craft our dental restorations out of natural-looking, strong materials like ceramic, porcelain, and porcelain fused to metal.

Cosmetic Dental Bonding

It’s not uncommon for teeth to become chipped and broken due to accidents. Even the smallest amount of damage can create a great change, making your grin feel less beautiful. Fortunately, you don’t have to invest in an extensive and invasive treatment to fix these minor imperfections. Cosmetic dental bonding allows our team to erase dental damage by simply rebuilding the missing portion of teeth with the same biocompatible composite resin that we use to fill cavities.

Which Cosmetic Dental Service Is Right for Me?

What do you hope to accomplish with cosmetic dentistry? Most patients want straighter teeth, brighter teeth, and/or differently sized or shaped teeth. We offer a variety of services that can help to achieve those goals, and they come at a range of price points:

  • Cosmetic dental bonding. Also known as direct bonding, this simple and quick service can hide chips, cracks, discoloration, and other imperfections in a single appointment. It starts out at a few hundred dollars per tooth.
  • Teeth whitening. The cost of teeth whitening starts out at several hundred dollars. We may be able to brighten your smile by several shades in just an hour or so!
  • Veneers. Veneers can disguise a broad range of imperfections, so they are sometimes regarded as an all in one cosmetic treatment. They are much more durable than cosmetic bonding. Depending on how many of them you get, the cost can amount to thousands of dollars.
  • Invisalign. Invisalign usually costs at least a few thousand dollars. It is an excellent option for individuals with mild to moderate dental misalignment.
  • Smile makeover. A smile makeover combines multiple treatments to help you achieve your ideal smile. Although it is the most expensive option, it is also the most comprehensive and efficient way to transform your smile’s appearance.

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

Cosmetic dentistry can vary in cost depending on the specific procedure, materials used, and your unique dental needs. Treatments such as teeth whitening are often more affordable, while more advanced procedures like veneers or dental implants may require a greater investment.

Your safety is our highest priority throughout every cosmetic dental procedure. We use high-quality materials and follow strict protocols to promote a safe environment. Our practice stays updated on the latest techniques to provide you with reliable and effective care. Indeed, cosmetic dentistry can rightly be considered a safe way to spruce up your appearance!

Of course, keep in mind that some approaches to cosmetic dentistry carry significant risks. For example, you should not seek care from someone who is not qualified. You should also avoid risky DIY hacks, such as homemade braces or whitening techniques that use abrasive ingredients that could harm your tooth enamel.

Cosmetic dentistry is primarily elective in nature, so no one truly needs it. However, you may benefit from it if you are unhappy with the appearance of your teeth, gums, or smile. Common signs include discoloration, chipped or broken teeth, gaps, or misalignment. We encourage you to share your concerns with us during a consultation.

Our dental team will assess your oral health and discuss your goals. Together, we can create a personalized treatment plan that matches your needs and desires.

The longevity of cosmetic dental treatments depends on the procedure, materials, and your oral hygiene habits. For example, teeth whitening may need periodic touch-ups, while crowns and veneers can last for many years potentially even decades with proper care. We will educate you about maintenance to help you get the most from your investment.

Regular dental visits and good home care are important for preserving the results of cosmetic procedures. We will work with you to set realistic expectations and provide guidance on keeping your smile beautiful and healthy for as long as possible.

Many patients are concerned about discomfort during cosmetic dental procedures. We prioritize your comfort by using gentle techniques and offering options such as local anesthesia. Most treatments are quite easy for patients to tolerate, and we take steps to minimize any discomfort throughout your experience.

Some treatments may cause a degree of sensitivity. This should be temporary. Until it wears off, you can manage it by taking over-the-counter pain relievers, eating mild foods, and being gentle during your oral hygiene routine.

If you experience any severe or lasting side effects after your treatment, call our Brooklyn cosmetic dentistry team for assistance.
