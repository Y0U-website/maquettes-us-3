Meet Dr. Kristin Raineri

In July 2017, Dr. Kristin Raineri joined the practice. Dr. Raineri received her Undergraduate and Master’s degree from Syracuse University and her dental degree from SUNY Buffalo. Dr. Raineri completed her residency program at Upstate University Hospital. Her father is Dr. William Raineri, an orthodontist with offices in Liverpool and Baldwinsville. She is a local, who grew up in Liverpool.

Dr. Raineri spends most of her free time with her husband and 2 daughters, and of course their black lab, Brooklyn. She is an SU superfan, and tries to never miss a game! She also spends time cheering on the Buffalo Bills. She enjoys traveling, running and volunteering at the girl’s school. She has completed multiple dental missionary events, both international and national.
