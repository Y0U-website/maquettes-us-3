Family Dental Services in Owatonna, MN

Owatonna Dental Care is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for the entire family. Our experienced and friendly team strives to ensure your dental visit is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. We offer a wide range of dental services, including professional cleaning, x-rays, cavity fillings, tooth extractions, and root canals.

Professional Cleanings

Regular professional cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral health. Our dental hygienists use state-of-the-art equipment to remove plaque and tartar buildup, which can lead to gum disease, cavities, and other oral health issues. During your cleaning, we will also check for any signs of tooth decay or gum disease and offer advice on how to improve your oral hygiene routine.

X-rays

X-rays are an essential diagnostic tool that allows us to see what is happening beneath the surface of your teeth and gums. Our digital x-ray system provides high-quality images while minimizing your radiation exposure. X-rays help us detect and diagnose cavities, gum disease, jaw problems, and other issues that may not be visible during a visual exam.

Cavity Fillings

Cavities are a common dental problem that can easily be treated with a filling. Our team uses tooth-colored composite fillings that blend in with your natural teeth, providing a seamless and discreet solution. We will remove the decayed portion of the tooth and fill the cavity to prevent further damage and restore your tooth’s function.

Gum Treatments

When you visit our family dentistry office for your regular dental checkup, we will also examine the health of your gums. Gum disease is a prevalent dental issue that can lead to tooth loss if left untreated. Our dentist will check for signs of gum disease, such as bleeding, swelling, or redness of the gums, and recommend treatment if necessary.

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extractions may be necessary if a tooth is damaged or decayed beyond repair or has overcrowding in the mouth. Our team will carefully evaluate your situation and provide a personalized treatment plan to address your needs. We use a gentle and compassionate approach to ensure comfort throughout the extraction process.

Oral Cancer Screenings

At our dental office, we prioritize your overall health and well-being. That’s why we offer oral cancer screenings as part of our comprehensive dental services. Oral cancer is a severe disease that can affect anyone, regardless of age or lifestyle. Early detection is critical to successful treatment, so we recommend regular screenings.

Our experienced dentists will examine your mouth, tongue, and throat for abnormal tissue growth or other warning signs during an oral cancer screening. We may also use advanced technology, such as VELscope, to detect any hidden areas of concern. If we identify an area of concern, we will work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that meets your unique needs.
