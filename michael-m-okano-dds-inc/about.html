About Us

Dentist

Michael M Okano DDS

Dr. Okano was raised in Ewa Beach and graduated from Mililani High School. He graduated from the University of Hawaii, Manoa Campus and the University of Missouri, Kansas City - School of Dentistry in 1988. He established Family & Cosmetic Dentistry- Kapolei in 2001 after 13 years of working with his father, Dr. Thomas Okano, DDS in Ewa Beach. Prior to becoming a dentist, Dr. Okano was an EMT on C&C Honolulu Ambulance. When he’s not in the office, Dr. Okano enjoys spending time with his family, giving his son golf lessons and taking him to his sporting events, surfing, and Football.
