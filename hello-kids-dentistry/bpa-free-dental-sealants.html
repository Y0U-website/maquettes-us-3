BPA-Free Dental Sealants for Kids

By choosing preventive treatments like sealants, you’re helping your child avoid more serious dental procedures in the future and giving them the confidence to smile bright at every stage.

  • Non-toxic, BPA-free materials for your peace of mind
  • Quick, painless, and non-invasive application
  • Ideal for children and teens still developing good brushing habits
  • Long-lasting protection with simple maintenance

What to Expect During the Sealant Appointment

Sealant application is simple, gentle, and stress-free. Here’s what happens during the visit:

There are no drills, no needles, and no discomfort just safe, lasting protection in minutes.

  • We clean and dry the tooth surface
  • A special solution is applied to help the sealant bond properly.
  • The BPA-free sealant is carefully painted on the tooth
  • A curing light hardens the sealant in seconds.

What to Expect During the Sealant Appointment

When should my child get dental sealants? Most children benefit from sealants as soon as their first permanent molars appear around age 6 and again with the second set, typically around age 12.

How long do sealants last? With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, sealants can last for several years. We’ll check them at every checkup and reapply if needed.

Do sealants replace brushing and flossing? Not at all sealants are an extra layer of protection, not a substitute. Daily brushing and flossing are still essential for a healthy smile.
