Welcome to McDermott Orthodontics, where orthodontic care is delivered with a smile!

We treat children, teens, and adults with everything from clear and metal braces to Invisalign and Invisalign Teen.

The practice

When you complete treatment at our office, you will leave with not only a beautiful smile but a healthy bite as well. Dr. Mike knows there is more to a healthy smile than just straight teeth.

Dr. McDermott and his superstar staff know how important a beautiful smile is to your self-esteem and your health, so we make it easy to achieve this goal.

We offer free consultations where you can meet with the doctor and go over your concerns.

At this time, Dr. Mike can offer you the various options available in orthodontic treatment and customize a treatment plan for you. In addition, we offer flexible financing and payment options.

You will have your smile for the rest of your life, let Dr. Mike, and his team, make sure that smile is excellent!

We want all of our patients to have the best experience possible in our office. The best part of practicing in orthodontics, according to Dr. Mike and his staff, is getting to know the patients over the course of treatment.

Our office is a comfortable space, where you can grab a cup of coffee and chat with our front desk. By the end of your treatment, you will know what it is like to be a member of the McDermott Orthodontic Family.

We are your Orthodontists in Baxter , Brainerd , Delano and Pequot Lakes .

Les soins

Dr. Michael McDermott, aka Dr. Mike, has been practicing orthodontics for 15 years. He uses his orthodontic expertise in Invisalign, braces and the latest technology to offer personalized treatment to each patient.
