Cosmetic dentistry enhances the appearance of teeth through procedures like whitening, veneers, and bonding, improving aesthetics while maintaining dental function and overall oral health.

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing damaged teeth and restoring function using fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants to improve oral health and maintain proper function.

Root canal therapy involves removing infected or damaged pulp from a tooth’s interior, cleaning, disinfecting, and sealing the canal to prevent further infection while preserving the tooth.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium, surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for replacement teeth, enhancing function and restoring aesthetics.

Botox is a neurotoxin injection that temporarily relaxes facial muscles, reducing wrinkles and fine lines, and is also used for treating medical conditions like migraines and TMJ.
