Your Journey Toward a Restored Smile

The process of receiving dental implant restorations involves several stages, beginning with a thorough evaluation to determine if you are a suitable candidate. Following this, the implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. After a period of healing, during which the implant fuses with the bone in a process known as osseointegration, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant post. Throughout this journey, Dr. Gilreath and our team will provide you with detailed care instructions and support.

Setting Expectations: The Experience Ahead

Patient education is paramount at Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston. Here’s what you can expect during your dental implant restoration journey:

  • Consultation and Planning: An initial assessment to discuss your goals and evaluate your oral health.
  • Surgical Procedure: Placement of the implant post by Dr. Gilreath, using advanced techniques to ensure minimal discomfort.
  • Healing Period: A few months may be required for osseointegration to occur fully.
  • Final Restoration: Fitting of the crown or bridge onto the implant post, ensuring it blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restorations

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution and can last many decades with proper care and maintenance.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure. Local anesthesia is used to ensure comfort, and options for sedation are available for those who may experience anxiety.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants involves routine oral hygiene practices similar to those for natural teeth—regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential.
