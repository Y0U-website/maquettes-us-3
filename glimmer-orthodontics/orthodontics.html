Braces for All Ages in Salem, NH

A straight smile isn’t just about looks—it helps keep your child’s mouth healthy too. When teeth are properly aligned, they’re easier to brush and floss, which means fewer cavities and less chance of gum problems down the line. Straight teeth also help with chewing and speaking, and they prevent extra wear on enamel or stress on the jaw. Braces can address all of these issues while boosting your child’s confidence. And with today’s gentler, more comfortable options, orthodontic care is easier than ever—for kids and parents.

Early Interceptive Orthodontics | Phase 1

Orthodontic care for your child might seem extreme, but it can save time and money in the future. The sooner you get treatment for your child, the better. Being proactive early can correct problems while your child&#x27;s teeth are still growing, eliminating the need for complex procedures as they get older.
