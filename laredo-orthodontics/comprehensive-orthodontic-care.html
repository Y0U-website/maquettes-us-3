Comprehensive Orthodontic Care

Retainer Repair

Congratulations on achieving your perfect smile! Now, to keep your teeth in their newly aligned positions, retainers are essential. Whether you have a clear, Hawley, or lingual retainer, Laredo Orthodontics is committed to ensuring your retainer functions flawlessly, just like the day you received it.

Spacers

The placement of spacers is a straightforward procedure that marks the beginning of your smile transformation. While you might experience a slight pressure or discomfort as your teeth begin to adjust, this sensation is a normal part of the process.

Headgear

Gone are the days of cumbersome and uncomfortable headgear. Today, devices like the Reverse Pull Headgear are designed with your comfort in mind, ensuring a fit that&#x27;s both effective and barely noticeable. Customized to each individual, these headgear solutions integrate seamlessly into your nightly routine, requiring wear only during sleep.

Overbite/Underbite/Crossbite Treatment

Ignoring bite issues can lead to severe consequences. A misaligned bite can accelerate wear and tear on teeth, altering facial aesthetics. This imbalance can reshape your lips, deepen wrinkles, create creases around your mouth and nose, cause your lower lip to roll outward, and even lead to the formation of jowls.

Night Guards

At Laredo Orthodontics, we recognize that a beautiful, healthy smile requires protection both during and after orthodontic treatment. That&#x27;s why we offer custom-fitted night guards designed not only to safeguard against the effects of teeth grinding (bruxism) but also to help maintain the alignment of your teeth once your orthodontic journey is complete.

Custom Athletic Mouthguards

In the competitive world of middle and high school sports, where basketball and football reign supreme, safeguarding your smile is as crucial as perfecting your game. At Laredo Orthodontics, we specialize in crafting high-quality, custom-fitted athletic mouthguards that offer superior protection, comfort, and performance for young athletes.
