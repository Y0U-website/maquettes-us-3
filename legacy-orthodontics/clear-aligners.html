Clear Aligners

We know how life-changing a beautifully aligned smile can be for your confidence and oral health. Clear aligners are a modern solution designed to gently and effectively guide your teeth into their ideal position, improving bite function and overall dental health.

Discreet, Effective Orthodontic Treatment

We offer Invisalign® and Spark Aligners as a modern and discreet solution for straightening your teeth. Clear aligners provide an effective alternative to traditional braces, allowing you to achieve a beautiful smile without the visibility of metal brackets and wires.

Clear aligners are removable, making it easy to maintain your oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. Our experienced team will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs, ensuring efficient and effective results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do clear aligners work?

Clear aligners work by gradually shifting your teeth into their desired positions using a series of custom-made, transparent trays. Each set of aligners is worn for a week before moving on to the next set, following a personalized treatment plan designed by our orthodontists. Over time, these subtle adjustments lead to a beautifully aligned smile.

How long does treatment with clear aligners take?

The length of treatment with clear aligners varies depending on the complexity of your case and your specific orthodontic needs. On average, treatment lasts between 12 to 18 months. During your initial consultation, our orthodontists will provide a more accurate estimate based on your individual treatment plan.

Are clear aligners comfortable to wear?

Yes, clear aligners are designed for comfort. They are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth and are made from smooth, medical-grade plastic, which minimizes irritation to your gums and cheeks. While you may experience slight discomfort or pressure when switching to a new set of aligners, this is temporary and a sign that the aligners are working.

Can I eat and drink with clear aligners?

Clear aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water. This helps prevent staining and damage to the aligners. After eating, it’s important to brush your teeth before reinserting your aligners to maintain oral hygiene and keep your aligners clean. This removable feature allows you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.
