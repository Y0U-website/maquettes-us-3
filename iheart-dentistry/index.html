The practice

We treat patients of all ages, from young to old, and have served generations of family members in our office .

We know that dental problems do not always arise Monday through Friday during business hours. This is why we offer emergency dental care right when you need it!

Cavities should be treated as soon as found with dental treatment known as dental fillings. Our office proudly uses composite resin fillings, which are tooth-colored and much safer than alternative materials, such as silver amalgam fillings used by other area dentists .

Professional teeth bleaching is a fast and affordable way to get the results you desire in a shorter period of time. Our whitening processes help patients brighten their smile up to eight shades whiter with medical-grade ingredients .

We know that keeping your teeth healthy includes maintaining proper bite alignment. Overcrowding, overbites, underbites, and other alignment issues can be addressed using metal braces .

Invisalign is an alternative to traditional orthodontics and uses clear aligner trays to reposition one’s teeth instead of metal bracket-and-wore braces

Partial, full, and implant-retained dentures are all available at iHeart Dentistry to help restore function, beauty, and health to the smile following tooth extraction and loss .

The third molars are notorious for becoming problematic for many of our patients, and we offer both simple and surgical extraction of wisdom teeth .
