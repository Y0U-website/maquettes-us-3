Not every pediatric dentist is board-certified, but Dr. Sheu has taken the extra steps to assure her patients she is a capable and disciplined pediatric dentist. To become board-certified, a pediatric dentist must complete a graduate program approved by the Commission on Dental Accreditation then pass a difficult written examination and oral clinical examination. This process is a demonstration of a doctor’s pursuit of continued excellence in their field.

Despite being more than qualified for her job, Dr. Sheu is always trying to stay on top of new research and dental techniques in pediatric dentistry. She believes it is important to stay updated in order to provide her patients with the best service possible. In her pursuit to be the best pediatric dentist she can, Dr. Sheu holds membership at the following organizations:

  • Bachelor’s of Science in Biology & Psychology – UCLA
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery – Loma Linda School of Dentistry
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery – University of Maryland

  • American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry

  • American Dental Association
