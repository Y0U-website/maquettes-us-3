Meet Dr. Chris Nelson, a Redding…

0 Hi, I’m Dr. Chris Nelson. I’ll let you in on a little (not so secret) secret.

Professionally, I’m obsessed with these things: detailed dentistry, leading our team, helping patients, case documentation, and continuing the family legacy.

Personally, I’m a husband to my beautiful wife Katie, dad, hockey coach, mountain biker, and generally can-not-sit-still kind of guy.

I went to St. Joseph’s School in Redding, St. Francis Middle School in Palo Cedro, Shasta High School (across the street from our office), UC Davis, and finally an incredibly competitive Dental School: The University of the Pacific in San Francisco; the same as my father and grandfather attended.

The first 13 years of my dental career I worked full time at Hill Country Health And Wellness Center; most of those years as Dental Director, while also working with my dad on Fridays at Nelson Family Dental.

In 2023 I purchased the practice from my dad and we essentially flipped our schedule: I work full time as the owner while dad works one-to-two days a week, sharing time with our patients of many years.

This picture says a lot about my history with the office. Why? Because the little kiddo with the glasses is me (next to my younger sister Kendra)! In about 1990 our office opened in the current location after moving from another Redding location. Grandpa Leon Nelson started the family business in 1960. Probably Dr. Mike (my dad) took this photo of my sister and I in the office. When patients ask “how long have you been a dentist?” I usually say that I graduated dental school in 2009, but I have been in this office nearly my entire life.
