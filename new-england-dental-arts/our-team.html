Meet Our Team

Mission Statement

We are a relationship-based practice providing comprehensive dental care in a relaxed, professional space, guiding patients on their journey to optimal health.

Office Environment

Beyond our excellent dentistry, we place a premium on the energy in our office. We strive to maintain an easy, breezy roll that we hope carries you through your day. Each team member is not only outstanding individually, but their make-up together is truly special. You can’t train someone to be nice, so we hire nice people.

Martha Bisson

Longtime Dental Assistant: Martha is Dr. Bloom’s longtime dental assistant, and if you’re in the office for more than ten minutes, chances are you will hear her sing. We cherish her sunny disposition, calm demeanor, sense of humor, and professionalism. Marty has been with David since 1986, (we all her David’s second wife).

Dedication to Patients: Thank God, she doesn’t want to retire, she still wants to toe in the water as she’d miss our patients—from her words— we have such a good time laughing all day, our patients are the best. Passion for Reading: Want to get Marty talking? Ask what she’s reading. She consumes books; I’m always asking what I should be on my reading list. She and her husband Richie enjoy the simple things in life—kids, grandkids, and their cabin in Maine.

Friendly and Dedicated: She’s super friendly, fun, soft-spoken, and sharp as a tack.
