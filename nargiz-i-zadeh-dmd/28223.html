What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Getting a dental implant comes with numerous benefits that go far beyond just filling in missing teeth. Some of the key benefits of dental implants include:

  • Improved Aesthetics: Dental implants are designed to seamlessly blend with your natural teeth, giving you a realistic, attractive smile that looks and feels just like your own.
  • Jawbone Preservation: The titanium post stimulates the jawbone just like a natural tooth root, and prevents the bone loss that typically happens after tooth loss.
  • Adjacent Teeth Protection: Dental implants do not require shaving down healthy teeth on either side, leaving your natural teeth completely safe.
  • Permanent and Long-Lasting: With proper care, dental implants can last decades, making them a durable and reliable choice for missing teeth.
  • Better Chewing and Speech: Implants restore your ability to chew food properly and speak clearly, without the slipping or discomfort that comes with dentures.

Who Is an Ideal Candidate for Dental Implants?

An ideal candidate for dental implants is someone missing one or more teeth and seeking a permanent, natural-looking solution. If you have overall health and enough jawbone density to support the implant post, then you are a good candidate. Most adults with healthy gums and no uncontrolled medical conditions are good candidates for this treatment.

You also need to be committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental visits to ensure your implants last for decades. Frequently Asked Questions

How Long Does a Dental Implant Last?

With proper care, a dental implant can last 15 25 years or more. In many cases, they last a lifetime, especially the implant post itself, which fuses with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration.

Are Dental Implants Painful?

No, Dental implants are not typically painful during the procedures as the surgical site is numbed during placement. Most patients report only mild to moderate discomfort.

Is a Dental Implant Covered by Insurance in Tarzana?

What is the Success Rate of Dental Implants?

Dental implants have a very high success rate. A large cohort study of 10,871 implants in 4,247 patients found that dental implants had cumulative survival rates of 98.9 % at 3 years, 98.5 % at 5 years, 96.8 % at 10 years, and 94.0 % at 15 years.
