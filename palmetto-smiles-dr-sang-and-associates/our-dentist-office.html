What Makes Our Florence Dental Office Different

Choosing a Dawson dentist ensures that you receive a level of care that goes beyond routine dental services. The Dawson Academy emphasizes a comprehensive, patient-centered approach known as Complete Dentistry. This approach considers not just the immediate dental issue but the overall health and functionality of the entire chewing system. By focusing on the relationship between teeth, muscles, and joints, Dawson-trained dentists aim to provide treatments that result in long-lasting, healthy, and aesthetically pleasing outcomes.

Our practice follows the Dawson Academy’s principles, which means we prioritize identifying and addressing the root causes of dental problems rather than just treating symptoms. This method allows us to create stable, functional, and beautiful smiles for our patients. Dr. Sang and our team are trained to perform thorough examinations and develop personalized treatment plans that cater to your specific needs and goals.

Preventative Dentistry and Patient Education

Your natural smile should last a lifetime. The dental care team at Palmetto Smiles works to help you maintain your teeth and preserve your beautiful smile. Through preventative care and routine dental visits, we help our patients smile brighter.

Through age-appropriate patient education, we encourage our patients to take an active role in their dental health care. We strive to provide personalized preventative dental care tailored to your unique risk factors to help you avoid oral disease.

We take a holistic approach to your dental health care and consider all aspects of your chewing system to form a compressive overview of your dental health. We tailor treatment plans to meet your unique dental health care needs. We address underlying functional issues first so that we are able to build stable, healthy, and beautiful smiles that last.

Your safety and comfort are our top priority.
