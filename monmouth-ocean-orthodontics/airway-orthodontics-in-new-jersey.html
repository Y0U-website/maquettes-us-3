Who is Airway Orthodontics Suitable For?

Airway orthodontics can benefit patients of all ages who may be experiencing breathing or development-related concerns. Early evaluation is especially helpful, as guiding jaw growth during childhood can support better long-term outcomes.

Children are often strong candidates, especially if they show signs such as mouth breathing, snoring, restless sleep, or narrow jaw development. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial screening around age 7, which allows orthodontists to identify concerns early.

Teens may benefit if they have bite issues, crowding, or symptoms related to breathing that affect daily comfort. Adults can also explore treatment if they experience concerns like disrupted sleep, jaw imbalance, or long-standing alignment issues that may impact airflow.

A consultation helps determine if this approach is appropriate based on your specific needs.

Treatment Duration & What to Expect

Treatment time varies based on age, growth stage, and the severity of airway and bite concerns. Some patients benefit from early, shorter phases of care, while others may follow a more comprehensive plan over time.

Initial Evaluation

Your first visit includes a detailed assessment using digital imaging and advanced diagnostics. This allows our team to evaluate how your teeth, jaws, and airway work together and identify any underlying concerns affecting breathing or development.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Based on your evaluation, a customized plan is created. Treatment may involve expanders to guide jaw growth, braces or clear aligners to improve alignment, and ongoing monitoring to support healthy airway development.

Ongoing Care & Progress

Regular visits ensure your treatment stays on track. Adjustments are made as needed to improve both function and comfort, with a focus on gradual, lasting results.
