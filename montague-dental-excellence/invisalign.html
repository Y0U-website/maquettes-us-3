How Invisalign Therapy Works

  • Consultation and digital scan. A 3D scan or impressions capture your current tooth positions and bite.
  • Treatment planning. A digital plan maps each step so you can review projected movements and estimated timeline.
  • Attachment and aligner delivery. Small attachments may be placed, and the first sets of trays are fitted.
  • Daily wear. Wear aligners 20–22 hours per day , removing them only for meals, drinking anything but water, and oral hygiene.
  • Progress checks. Visits every six to ten weeks help confirm tracking and provide the next aligner sets.
  • Refinements. Additional trays may be ordered to fine-tune alignment near the end.
  • Retention. After active movement, retainers maintain results and prevent relapse.

What to Expect During Treatment

Expect a snug fit and temporary pressure when starting a new tray. Most people notice mild tenderness for a day or two that eases quickly. Speech may feel different for the first 24 to 48 hours; reading aloud helps adapt.

You can remove aligners to enjoy your usual foods. Brush and floss after meals, then place trays back promptly to maintain wear time. Clean aligners with a soft brush and cool water or a non-abrasive cleaner; avoid hot water, which can warp trays.

Staying on track requires consistent wear and following the prescribed change schedule. Lost or damaged trays should be reported so the sequence can be adjusted. Near the end, a short refinement phase is common to polish details. Retainers are essential after treatment to hold teeth in their new positions long term.

Who Invisalign Treatment May Be Right For

Clear aligners work well for many teens and adults who want straighter teeth with a discreet look. They are often suited to crowding or spacing up to a few millimeters, mild overbite correction, and alignment of rotated or tilted teeth. Severe skeletal bite issues, impacted teeth, or large jaw discrepancies may require alternative orthodontic approaches or braces. A clinical exam will outline your options, estimated treatment time, and cost factors that might influence your choice.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most cases take 6 to 18 months. Shorter cosmetic alignments can finish sooner, while complex movements may take longer. Consistent wear speeds predictable progress.

What do Invisalign attachments do? Attachments are small tooth-colored shapes bonded to teeth to give aligners extra grip. They help achieve precise movements, such as rotations or vertical changes.

How does Invisalign therapy compare to braces? Both can straighten teeth. Clear aligners are removable and discreet, which many patients prefer. Some complex bite corrections are still more efficient with braces.

Can I eat and drink with the aligners in? Remove trays for meals and any drinks besides water. Eating with trays can damage them, and colored drinks can stain them. Rinse and brush before reinserting.

How do I keep my aligners clean? Rinse with cool water, brush gently with a soft brush, and use approved cleaners. Avoid hot water and colored mouthwashes that can warp or discolor trays.

What happens after Invisalign treatment? Retainers keep teeth in place while bone and gums stabilize. Most patients start with full-time retainer wear, then transition to nightly wear as directed.
