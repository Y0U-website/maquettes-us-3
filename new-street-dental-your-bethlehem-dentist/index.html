An implant has three parts, and understanding them makes every conversation easier:

Because the post fuses with your jaw, an implant does something no bridge or denture can: it keeps the bone alive. When a tooth is lost, the bone that held it starts to shrink from disuse. An implant loads that bone the way a natural root does, which is why implants are the only replacement that prevents the sunken look that can follow long-term tooth loss.

Replacing one tooth. One implant plus one crown. The gold standard for a single missing tooth, and the option that leaves every neighboring tooth untouched.
