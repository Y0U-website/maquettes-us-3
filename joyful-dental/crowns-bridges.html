Clinical Insights

How long do professional crowns and bridges last? With proper hygiene, our restorations typically last 10–15 years. Longevity depends on gum health and avoiding habits like ice-chewing or teeth-grinding.

Does the procedure hurt? No. We ensure the tooth and surrounding area are completely numb. Most patients feel only mild pressure during the preparation phase.

What is a &#x27;Temporary&#x27; and why is it needed? While our lab crafts your permanent ceramic restoration, we provide a temporary crown to protect the prepared tooth and maintain your smile&#x27;s appearance.

How do I clean under a bridge? Bridges require a special type of floss (floss threaders) to clean the area between the bridge and the gum line. We will provide a hands-on tutorial at your fitting.
