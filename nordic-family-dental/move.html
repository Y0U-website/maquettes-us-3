We’re Moving

We’re Moving. We’re Becoming Nordic Dental Club.

For over 40 years, we’ve been a staple of the Poulsbo community and now we’re opening an exciting new chapter! The most beautiful privately-owned dental office in Kitsap County is opening this November and it comes with the same doctors, same team, and same insurance you already trust.

Nordic Family Dental is becoming Nordic Dental Club.

This isn’t just a new name, it’s a new standard. After years of growth, we made a decision: stop dividing our team across multiple locations and build something worthy of the community we serve.

Same doctors, same staff, same insurance accepted. What changes is everything around it. A stunning new home, a more personal experience, and a practice more committed than ever to Poulsbo.

So, Why "Club"?

We chose the word Club because it’s exactly what this new space is built to feel like. This will be the most beautiful privately-owned dental office in Poulsbo, designed in a clean, Scandinavian, minimalist style that reflects everything we believe about care done right, personal, unhurried, and built around you.

The name is also connected to our reinvented membership program, a completely optional option we’re building for patients without dental insurance. If you’re already an insured patient, nothing changes for you. Club simply describes the building and the experience, not a fee.

What We’re Building Scandinavian-Inspired. Modern. Minimalist. Every detail, from the technology to the finishes to the experience, reflects the level of care we’ve always wanted to deliver.
