"Loving God, Loving People" Matthew 22:37 A free clinic for those in need. We provide medical and dental care by licensed Physicians/CNPS’s, Nurses, Pharmacists, and Dentists.

The practice

Our highly trained staff of doctors and nurses can provide care for qualified patients of any age with common ailments and more.

Our free clinic is operated 100 % by volunteers through the donation of time and talent from our medical, dental, and lay volunteers.

Our support comes from individuals, businesses, community organizations, and churches. Our partners make this ministry possible.

Hope Clinic of Ross County is committed to providing quality care. Our organization earned a 2026 Rating from the National Association of Free & Charitable Clinics (NAFC) Quality Standards. This is the highest standard of care that can be received according to national standards! Thanks to the hard working quality team and volunteers at Hope Clinic of Ross County! The mission of Hope Clinic is to share the love of Christ by providing basic, sound, quality medical care at no cost to those who have no health insurance in a climate that is accepting, authentic, and inviting for people.

This information is provided in partnership with the Hunger Coalition, an initiative of the United Way of Ross County.
