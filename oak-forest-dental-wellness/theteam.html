Dr. Jonelle is passionate about dentistry because she loves helping patients, whether it’s by improving their oral health or making them feel confident about their smile. She loves that dentistry combines both art and science.

Dr. Shawn Thomas

Dr. Thomas is a Specialist in Periodontics. As a periodontist, he focuses on treating the gums and bone that support the teeth. Dr. Thomas is highly trained in both surgical and non-surgical periodontal therapy and also places dental implants.
