Aside from the obvious gap in your smile, missing teeth can also create problems you can’t see. That empty space in your smile inhibits you from presenting your true self to the world because you just don’t feel like smiling. It may also be difficult to eat a proper, healthy diet if you have difficulty chewing certain foods.

At Palm View Dental Alhambra , our goal is to assist you in achieving exceptional dental health so you can feel confident about your appearance when you smile. Using dental implants, we can restore function and beauty to your smile while protecting your oral health. Dental implants replace one or more missing teeth or can be used to secure a bridge or denture.

Implants are easy to care for, too – simply continue to follow a diligent oral hygiene routine as you always have. With proper oral hygiene and regular exams and cleanings, your implants may last a lifetime.

Before we decide to go ahead with dental implants , one of our Alhambra dentists will sit down with you and provide more information about implants as well as our other tooth replacement options.

We’ll take the time to learn about your goals so you can feel comfortable with the decision you make about your treatment.
