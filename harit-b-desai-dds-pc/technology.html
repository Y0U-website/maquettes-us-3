Rotary Endodontics for Comfortable Root Canals

Root canals are one of the most misunderstood procedures in dentistry. The fact is that root canal treatment doesn’t cause pain; it relieves it. At Desai Family Dentistry, we’ve made root canals more comfortable using rotary endodontics technology .

Instead of manual instruments that many dentists use, we use leading-edge rotary endodontics. This electrically-powered technology has a flexible tip that our dentist uses to carefully remove the infected material inside the tooth.

The result is root canal treatment that is more comfortable and efficient and delivers more reliable long-term outcomes than the traditional method.

Intraoral Cameras

Have you ever had a dental exam and wished you knew what your dentist was looking at? With intraoral cameras, now you can!

Dr. Desai performs your exam using a small handheld digital camera that records live images of your mouth and displays them on your monitor. You can see what he sees during your exam as he moves the camera around your mouth. You can watch the process in real-time as he points out problems like cavities, cracked or broken teeth, broken fillings, and signs of gum disease.

We save the images from your exam and add them to your patient files for future reference or to share with your insurance company as needed.
