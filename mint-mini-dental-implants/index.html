The practice

At Mint Mini Dental Implants, we go beyond being a typical dental office. We are a devoted team driven by our passion to provide you with an unparalleled patient experience.

Our core belief revolves around creating an environment that places your comfort and well-being as our top priority, while delivering exceptional dental services. As soon as you enter our practice, you will be warmly welcomed by our dedicated team members: Henriette, Mary, Joanie, Brittany and Pam.

Their commitment is simple yet powerful: to ensure your visit is efficient and comfortable. We understand that dental treatments can sometimes be overwhelming, whether you are facing a sudden emergency or planning for a major procedure.

Think of it as having a single tool that can address multiple problems, providing both convenience and consistency. By minimizing the need for referrals, we streamline the experience, ensuring your care remains consistent and personalized.

All accepted procedures will be completed in our office by Dr. Coolican and his staff. He sees one surgical patient at a time to deliver the best dental treatment personalized to you.

As a practitioner focused on dental implants, Dr. Coolican maintains an unwavering dedication to excellence. He completed a comprehensive general dental practice residency at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center and the VA Hospital System.

Dr. Coolican’s expertise is unparalleled, having successfully placed over 12,000 Mini and One Piece Traditional Dental Implants since 2004 and completing over 1,700 hours in advanced continuing education. Dr. Coolican’s experience and expertise in this technique surpasses any other doctor in Eastern Pennsylvania and other surrounding states.
