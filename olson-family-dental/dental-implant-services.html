Dental Implant Services

Minot Smiles Dental Co. provides comprehensive dental implant services designed to replace missing teeth, restore oral function, and support long-term oral health. Dr. Megan Olson and her team offer personalized implant treatment plans ranging from single-tooth replacement to full-arch restoration, helping patients regain confidence in their smiles with durable, natural-looking results.

Single-Tooth Implant

Replacing One Missing Tooth Permanently

A single-tooth implant replaces a missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant functions as an artificial tooth root, supporting a custom restoration that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Multiple Teeth Implants

A Stable Solution for Several Missing Teeth

For patients missing multiple teeth, dental implants provide a secure foundation for custom restorations. Implant-supported treatment helps preserve jawbone health while restoring chewing ability and smile appearance.

Full-Arch Replacement

Restoring an Entire Smile With Implants

Full-arch replacement uses strategically placed implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth. This treatment offers greater stability, comfort, and function than traditional removable dentures.

Dental Implant Restoration and Repair

Protecting the Long-Term Success of Dental Implants

Even successful implants may occasionally require maintenance or restoration repairs. Dr. Olson provides implant restoration and repair services to address worn components, damaged restorations, and other concerns that affect implant function.

Patient Education

Helping Patients Make Informed Decisions

Dental implant treatment begins with education. Minot Smiles Dental Co. helps patients understand available options, treatment timelines, expected outcomes, and long-term maintenance requirements so they can make confident decisions about their care.

Emergency Services

Prompt Care for Implant-Related Concerns

Dental implant emergencies can include loose restorations, discomfort, damaged components, or trauma involving implant-supported teeth. Dr. Olson provides prompt evaluation and treatment to help protect the health and function of implant restorations.

Questions Minot Patients Ask About Dental Implant Services

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last for decades when properly maintained. Regular dental visits, excellent oral hygiene, and healthy lifestyle habits all contribute to long-term success.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many adults with missing teeth are candidates for implant treatment. Dr. Olson evaluates oral health, bone support, medical history, and treatment goals to determine eligibility.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes. Implant-supported restorations are custom-designed to match surrounding teeth in color, shape, and size, creating a natural appearance.

What is the difference between traditional dentures and implant-supported replacement?

Traditional dentures rest on the gums, while implant-supported restorations are anchored to dental implants placed in the jawbone. This provides greater stability, chewing ability, and long-term support.

What should I do if I experience a problem with an existing implant restoration?

Patients should contact Minot Smiles Dental Co. as soon as possible. Prompt evaluation can help prevent minor concerns from becoming more significant complications.
