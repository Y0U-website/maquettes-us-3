Your patients come back better informed

Every patient we treat receives a comprehensive written consultation report sent to your office within 24 hours of their visit. We include detailed treatment plans, high-quality diagnostic imaging, and clear documentation of findings and next steps. When your patient returns to your care, they’ll have complete information about their endodontic treatment and what to expect moving forward.

We view our role as specialist support, not competition. Your patients remain your patients—we’re here to solve the endodontic challenge and return them to you for ongoing care and restoration.

  • 01 Same-Day Urgent Scheduling We reserve appointment slots daily for urgent cases. Call before noon to get your patient seen the same day.
  • 02 Detailed Consultation Reports Every patient visit includes a detailed written report to your office documenting findings, treatment completed, and recommendations.
  • 03 3D Imaging On-Site Our CBCT scanner is in-office, so diagnostic imaging happens chairside without additional referrals or delays.
  • 04 Experienced Care Every case receives the full attention of our board-certified endodontists—no delegation, just specialist-level expertise on every patient.
