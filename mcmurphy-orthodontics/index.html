The practice

Rest assured that regardless of the treatment system you choose, whether braces or Invisalign® , it’s our expertise that will achieve the amazing smile that you want and deserve. During your free initial consultation at McMurphy Orthodontics, we’ll present and discuss options that will fulfill your smile goals comfortably, efficiently, and affordably.

McMurphy Orthodontics uses DynaFlex Norris Braces, an innovative and cutting edge system that achieves perfect results faster and easier than you ever thought possible. The brackets are smaller and more comfortable to boot!

For clear braces, McMurphy Orthodontics utilizes the Damon System (available in both ceramic and metal). Damon brackets offer efficient and comfortable tooth movement that has made it a preferred option for top orthodontists around the world.
