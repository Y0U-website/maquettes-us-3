Average Cost Summary

  • Single Tooth Implant (All-In): $3,000 – $6,000 per tooth (National average is around $4,200 ) .
  • Implant-Supported Bridge (3–4 teeth): $7,000 – $16,000 .
  • Full-Arch / All-on-4 (Per Jaw): $14,000 – $35,000 .
  • Full Mouth (Both Arches): $35,000 – $80,000+ .

Cost Breakdown (Single Tooth)

  • Implant Post (Titanium screw): $1,000 – $3,000
  • Abutment (Connector): $300 – $1,000
  • Crown (Visible prosthetic tooth): $800 – $3,000

Common Extra Costs (If Needed)

  • Tooth Extraction: $150 – $450
  • Bone Grafting: $300 – $2,500
  • 3D CT Scan / Imaging: $100 – $350
