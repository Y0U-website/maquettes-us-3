The practice

Individuals who are afraid of seeing the dentist or uncomfortable with the current condition of their teeth often visit our office and leave with confidence in knowing that their smiles can be restored and discomfort will end. Dr. Andrea always addresses your concerns first and is willing to involve you in your own personalized care.

Dental hygiene – Cleaning of teeth, removal tartar build up and stain, polishing, flossing, and application of fluoride

Restoration of teeth with tooth colored filling materials, crowns, veneers, and bridges

Root canals – removal of the pulp and nerve tissue from the inside of the tooth

We welcome all patients with no judgement as to their past dental history. If its been 20 years or 1 year since your last dental visit, we are happy to see you and get you on the path to dental health.
