The practice

At JL Family Dentistry , patients are the foundation of our care philosophy. In addition to offering only the highest quality of family and cosmetic dental options, Dr. Jowett and Dr. Lewis believe that transparency and honesty are instrumental to facilitating optimal oral health.

We strive to educate and guide our patients through the various dental options available to help curate an individualized experience at all times.

We believe that a beautiful set of teeth leads to a beautiful smile! As a result, we offer a variety of modern dental services to help you achieve a natural and healthy smile for the entire family.

From start to finish, we want you to feel like a member of our family. Our office amenities are designed around making your experience comfortable and free of dental anxiety.

Dr. Lewis and Dr. Jowett both consider patient care an extremely important factor when providing dental treatment. Our practice will only make recommendations based on what we believe is the best treatment available for you at all times.

At JL Family Dentistry, we want you to know that we care. From start to finish, we want you and your loved ones to feel supported, comfortable, relaxed, and ready to receive quality dental care! That is the promise that we make for every patient that enters our office.

At JL Family Dentistry, we want our patients to know that receiving dental care can be both easy and comfortable. We take pride in providing a warm and relaxing atmosphere to ensure that your dental care will be provided in a “no-pressure” environment. We will always be sure to thoroughly explain your options, risks, and benefits of treatment.
