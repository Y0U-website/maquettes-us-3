About

Our Mission Crafting Happiness, One Smile at a Time At Murdock Orthodontics, our mission is simple: to create radiant smiles that ignite confidence and joy. We’re not your average orthodontist! We’re the ones who believe that laughter, fun, and personalized care are essential elements of your orthodontic journey.

We treat every patient like family, ensuring comfort and trust throughout your smile transformation With us, you’ll experience excellent service, VIP treatment, and a journey that leaves you smiling every step of the way.

Dr. John Murdock Education Associations Family Dr. John Murdock earned his bachelor’s degree in biology from Morehouse College before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of North Carolina’s School of Dentistry, where he went on to earn a Master of Science degree and receive orthodontic specialty certification.

To stay up-to-date on the latest advancements in the field of orthodontics, Dr. Murdock maintains membership in numerous prestigious professional organizations, such as the American Association of Orthodontists, the Southern Association of Orthodontists, the South Carolina Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

In his spare time, Dr. Murdock enjoys CrossFit, watching sports, volunteering in Elevation Church’s eKidz Ministry, and spending time with his young family. This includes his beautiful wife, Kyla, and their two young children, Gabriel and Isabella.

Dr. Sean Murdock Education Associations Family Dr. Murdock earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery, Certificate in Orthodontics, and Master of Science in Orthodontics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Dentistry.

A published author, he is a member of several professional organizations, including the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Southern Association of Orthodontists, and the South Carolina Dental Association.

Dr. Murdock is a native of Richburg and currently resides in Rock Hill. He and his lovely wife, Amber, have three active teenagers: Eden, Evan, and Ethan. In his spare time, Dr. Murdock volunteers at his church, Transformation Church 521, in Indian Land. He also enjoys working out, watching sporting events, grilling, and traveling.
