When do I need an implant and what is implant?

Dental implants are ideal for those who have lost a tooth due to injury or big cavity. Dental Implants are titanium screws that are used to support restorations in order to replace missing teeth. Once the implant is placed in bone, the implant is allowed to fuse to the bone over a period of months. Once the implant is secure in the bone, rest of the procedure is similar to getting a crown.

Nothing can replace your natural tooth but implants and well restored crowns are the closest thing to your natural teeth!
