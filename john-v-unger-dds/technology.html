Cone Beam CT

To aid in our diagnosis and treatment planning, we have the capability to acquire CBCT images of your jaws and teeth! This technology makes it easier for us to detect any issues before they become a problem for you, and helps eliminate multiple diagnostic visits! When combined with our Intra-oral scanner, it also makes the planning of the implant placement process extremely accurate.

Same Day Crowns

We are now happy to offer same day crowns! With same day crowns we eliminate the annoying temporary crown phase and you leave our office with your brand new, tooth-colored ceramic crown! Our patients have found this a convenient option to avoid taking multiple days off work, leaving on vacation with a temporary crown, and fixing broken teeth quickly!

3D Scanners

No more "goop"! Everyone remembers "traditional" impressions with a mouthful of bad tasting impression material. Any error required an uncomfortable and lengthy repeat. Now we have intra-oral scanners that digitally capture your mouth in seconds. No more bad taste, no more slimy goop! It s quick, convenient, and accurate! When combined with our same day crowns, it modernized the crown process and makes it way faster and more pleasant!

Intraoral Cameras

With this technology, your dentist uses a small camera no larger than a penlight to capture images of your teeth and gums. These images tell a story and allow Dr. Unger to share information visually with insurance providers and other specialists.

We are also dedicated to patient education. Your dentist can pull up these images chairside to better explain your diagnosis to you. This allows you to make informed choices for your oral care and understand why Dr. Unger recommends a specific treatment course.
