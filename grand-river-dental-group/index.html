The practice

We provide a gentle approach in all aspects of dentistry, including the most important – the dentistry itself. Options for control of pain and apprehension will be discussed, so even the most sensitive, apprehensive patient will be able to tolerate dental treatment.

Our office participates with many insurance companies Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) Plans. We also offer options for patients without dental insurance. Some insurance companies allow uninsured patients to purchase discount dental cards. These plans offer uninsured patients discounts that vary from 20-40 % over standard fees.

Dr. Carla Randolph joined the practice in December 2019. Originally from Romania, Dr. Randolph did a post-graduate program in restorative dentistry at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, receiving her dental license in 2007. In addition to private practice, she is a clinical associate professor at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in Ann Arbor.

Dr. Randolph enjoys all aspects of general dentistry with a particular interest in preventative and restorative dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the Livingston District Dental Society. Dr. Randolph and her son reside in Brighton. Her hobbies include biking, skiing, and restoring old homes.
