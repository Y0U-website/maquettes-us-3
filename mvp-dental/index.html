At MVP Dental in Utah, we understand the importance of a healthy smile. This goes beyond just aesthetics and deep into the health of your gums with our effective scaling and root planing treatments.

Scaling and root planing, often referred to as deep cleaning, is a non-surgical procedure that combats gum disease right at its root – pun intended! If plaque and tartar are allowed to build up, they can cause inflammation and potential damage to your gums and the bone that supports your teeth. That’s where we come in.

One of the many benefits of choosing MVP Dental for your scaling and root planing needs is our commitment to providing thorough and gentle care. Our skilled dental professionals meticulously remove plaque and tartar from below the gumline, cleaning each tooth down to its root. This not only treats gum disease but also prevents its progression, safeguarding your smile’s future.
