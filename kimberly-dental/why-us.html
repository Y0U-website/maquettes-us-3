Why Choose Us?

Balancing modern dentistry with a welcoming and relaxing environment, Kimberly Dental’s staff offers a comprehensive list of general, cosmetic, and restorative dental services designed to meet the needs of your entire family.

When you visit our office, you can expect the very best care from a knowledgeable and compassionate team that makes you feel like family. Whether it’s a child’s first visit, a simple filling, an emergency cracked tooth, or the treatment of a complex case, our staff provides care that is efficient and tailored to each patient.

Our Mission

Kimberly Dental provides discounts for seniors, non-insured patients, and patients paying the day of service.

Kimberly Dental treats patients from age 3 to 103—so the whole family can visit one location.

Kimberly Dental files all insurance claims for patients.

Kimberly Dental now offers patients digital X-rays that emit half the amount of radiation of traditional X-rays.

Kimberly Dental practices the strict sterilization methods recommended by the WDA.

Patient Education

At Kimberly Dental, we help our patients maintain long-term dental health and an attractive smile through preventive dentistry and patient education. Our staff is committed to spending undivided time with every patient to answer questions, provide additional information, and relieve any anxiety they might be feeling. You will always have our full attention at every visit.

Also, using our innovative intraoral camera in tandem with our large viewing screens, we will show you close-up pictures of your teeth and gums. The video camera allows you to see what we see—so we can discuss your smile together!
