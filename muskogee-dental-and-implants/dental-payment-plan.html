Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment are accepted? We accept cash, checks and major credit cards for services and treatments covered by the in-house membership plan.
  • When can I start using my membership? Your membership is activated as soon as you sign up, allowing you to start enjoying savings immediately.
  • Can I use dental insurance or third-party financing with my membership plan? Treatments and services under the in-house membership plan cannot be combined with third-party financing or any active dental insurance plans.
  • How do I apply my member-exclusive discount to treatment? Simply inform us of your membership when scheduling your appointment, and we’ll verify your status to ensure that your discounts are applied automatically.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Our in-house membership plan is exclusive to Muskogee Dental and Implants and may not be transferred to another dental clinic.
