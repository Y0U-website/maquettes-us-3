The practice

500+ Reviews Welcome to Madrona Dental Care Madrona Dental Care is a relationship-centered dental practice serving Anacortes and nearby communities with care that feels personal, thorough, and clear.

Dr. Gray’s approach begins with listening, detailed evaluation, and a genuine desire to understand what is happening beneath the surface. He looks at your teeth, gums, bite, bacteria, risk factors, medical history, and goals so your care is built around the full picture, not just the most obvious concern.

The result is dentistry that is personalized to you from the very first visit.

At Madrona Dental Care, we look at the factors that shape your oral health over time, including bacteria, saliva, diet, genetics, gum health, and home care. With cleanings, periodontal care, oral cancer screenings, and clear education, we help you protect your smile and support your overall wellness.

Intentional smile restorations When something breaks, wears down, or no longer feels right, Dr. Gray looks for the reason behind it.

Our restorative care is designed to rebuild comfort, strength, and function while helping you understand what caused the problem in the first place. From fillings and crowns to bridges and more complex treatment planning, every recommendation is made with long-term stability in mind.

A gentler way to clean Madrona Dental Care was the first office in Skagit County certified in Guided Biofilm Therapy with the EMS Airflow Prophy Master.

This advanced hygiene technology helps remove biofilm in a way that is thorough, efficient, and more comfortable for patients. It is one way we make routine care feel more modern, precise, and patient-centered.
