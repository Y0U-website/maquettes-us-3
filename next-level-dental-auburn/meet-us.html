Next Level Dental is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, nextlevel-dental.com , for everyone. Next Level Dental aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA). Next Level Dental is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone.

Meet the Team

General Dentistry

Dr. Elias Khoury

Dr. Elias Khoury has been a dentist in the Greater Sacramento area for over four years. He went to Saint Mary’s College of California, not far from his hometown of Pleasanton in the Bay Area. After graduating with honors, Dr. Khoury completed his dental school training at Midwestern University in Arizona before returning to Northern California. The awesome patients and comfortable lifestyle here have allowed Dr. Khoury to make this his new home! In his spare time, he enjoys working out, golfing, trying local restaurants and seeing family and friends!

Dr. Alister Man

Dr. Alister Man grew up in Lexington, KY and completed his undergraduate studies at Pomona College in Claremont, CA. He returned to his hometown and attended the University of Kentucky for Dental School, graduating in 2008. Over the last 12 years, he has found great satisfaction in providing dental care to the utmost of his ability to patients in the Sacramento area. He considers his patients honored friends and treats them like family.

He is a member of the ADA (American Dental Association), CDA (California Dental Association) and GDIA (Global Dental Impant Academy). Dr. Man enjoys all aspects of dentistry, especially surgery and dental implantology.

In his spare time, his three sons keep him busy with their sports. He also enjoys gold prospecting in the Northern Sierra as well as spending time with his two dogs, a Corgi and a Wirehaired Pointing Griffon.

General Dentistry

Dr. Scott Dexter

Dr. Dexter spent a year of his life serving in Prague, the capital of the Czech Republic. After communism fell in 1989, there were three generations of Czechs who had not had access to western language training. Dr. Dexter served the Czech people by teaching English as a second language to adults and children.

Dr. Dexter decided to attend Loma Linda University School of Dentistry, a school that has service as one of its main charters, operating clinics locally and internationally. While in school, Dr. Dexter took the time to serve the dental needs of the Coachella Valley locally and abroad in Loreto, Mexico.

Dr. Dexter lives in El Dorado Hills, where you’ll see him running, hiking, biking, skiing, and camping with his wife and two daughters.
