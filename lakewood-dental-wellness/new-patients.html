First Visit

We understand that visiting the dentist can be a little scary. To take stress out of the equation, here’s precisely what you should expect:

  • We’ll text you our Welcome Packet in advance so you aren’t waiting in the reception area.
  • We’ll review your medical history in case of a medical emergency.
  • If you have any pain or problems, we want to address these first!
  • We explain x-rays and photos of your mouth so you’re a part of any treatment decisions
  • Our licensed dentist will thoroughly evaluate your mouth, lips, cheeks, palate, teeth, gums, bone health, and TMJ to form a complete treatment and preventive plan designed to fit you.
