Our Empowering Team

This team is the most important part of our orthodontic office. Our team is critical to the environment of Mbrace Orthodontics. Every team member plays such a critical role in our patient’s care.

As a team, there is nothing better than seeing a patient get through treatment and have a big, beaming smile filled with self-confidence and pride. It’s what we strive for, and every single person here is brought into that goal.
