Our Practice Through the Years

Our story began in 2003 with the realization of Dr. Gregory W. Mohr s vision: a dental practice that blends advanced technology with a warm, welcoming atmosphere. He designed our Manlius office with a unique Adirondack lodge style, creating a serene and patient-friendly environment for cutting-edge dental treatment.

In May 2010, as the practice grew, Dr. Mohr welcomed Dr. Jason D. Henderson as a full-time associate. Originally from Michigan, Dr. Henderson came to Central New York after completing dental school at the University at Buffalo in 2008 and his residency at the Eastman Institute of Oral Health in Rochester. He was drawn to Dr. Mohr s practice by its strong reputation for quality patient care and saw an opportunity to establish his family roots in the region.

Five years later, in February 2015, Dr. Henderson purchased the practice from Dr. Mohr. He embraced the established mission of building relationships through patient comfort, exceptional service, and advanced technology, committed to both honoring Dr. Mohr s legacy and investing in the practice s future.

As Dr. Mohr prepared for his retirement in the autumn of 2017 after over three decades in dentistry, Dr. Jeremy DeBottis joined the practice as a dental associate in August 2017. Dr. Henderson and Dr. DeBottis were dental school classmates at the University at Buffalo, with Dr. DeBottis graduating in 2007. Recognizing their shared passion for dentistry, similar clinical backgrounds, and aligned practice philosophies, Dr. Henderson eagerly welcomed Dr. DeBottis to the team.

Today, we continue to build upon this history, upholding the commitment to providing excellent dental care in a comfortable, technologically advanced setting, always focused on the health and well-being of our valued patients.
