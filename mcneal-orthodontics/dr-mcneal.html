Dr. McNeal Orthodontist in Celina

Dr. McNeal’s path to orthodontics began with a deep understanding of whole-body health. After earning her Master of Science in Microbiology and Infectious Diseases, she saw how closely oral health is connected to overall wellness. This led her to dentistry and ultimately to orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, where she could address the root causes of complex conditions. With over 30 years of professional experience, she remains committed to delivering thoughtful, science-driven care using advanced technology and proven techniques.

As a proud Texas native raised in a small-town environment, Dr. McNeal feels a strong connection to the Celina community. She values building lasting relationships with local families and fellow business owners, creating a practice rooted in trust and personalized care. Her goal is to be a reliable resource for patients, offering guidance, expertise, and a supportive experience that helps every patient feel confident in their care.

Expertise:

Dr. McNeal brings decades of expertise in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, treating crowding, bite issues, jaw misalignment, and facial aesthetics. She is Damon Ultima Certified and Master Certified in Spark Aligners, with 15 years of experience working with Damon Braces and 10 years of experience using Spark Aligners. Her skill in digital bonding and comprehensive orthodontics allows for precise, efficient, and highly customized treatment outcomes.

Academic Excellence and Advanced Training

Dr. McNeal received her Bachelor of Science degree from Angelo State University, where she was inducted into the top three invitation-only Honor Societies for students in the top 1 % of her class. She then attended Monmouth University in New Jersey for an associate’s degree in Sociology and studies in Human Societies. Following that, she earned a scholarship to Eastern Kentucky University to study Microbiology with an emphasis in Infectious Disease and earned a Master of Science degree.

After Emory University, Dr. McNeal continued to pursue advanced certifications, such as Mastering the Business of Orthodontics from the Wharton School of Business at Penn State University. She stays at the forefront of orthodontic care through research and continuing education. She is an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists, Southern Society of Orthodontists, the Texas Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Texas Dental Association.

With a concierge-style approach, the team prioritizes clear, consistent communication at every step, ensuring patients and their healthcare providers stay informed and confident throughout treatment. From your first visit to your final smile, you’ll experience a level of attentiveness and accessibility that sets McNeal Orthodontics apart, including direct access to Dr. McNeal when you need it most.

Recognized Expertise in Advanced Orthodontics

Dr. McNeal is widely regarded for her advanced expertise in both Damon Braces and Spark Clear Aligners , two of the most innovative systems in modern orthodontics. With 15 years of hands-on experience using Damon technology, she remains actively involved in its evolution, participating in advanced training, peer collaboration, and protocol development. Her deep understanding of this system allows her to treat even complex or previously unsuccessful cases with confidence and precision.

In addition, Dr. McNeal is Master Certified in Spark Aligners, with a decade of experience using the system to deliver highly controlled, predictable results. By combining these technologies, she provides flexible, customized solutions designed around each patient’s needs and lifestyle.
