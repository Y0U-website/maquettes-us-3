The practice

All-on-4 and All-on-X dental implants help patients restore their smile, confidence, and ability to eat and speak comfortably—all in one streamlined treatment.

The All-on-4 method uses a combination of straight and angled implants to maximize support using the patient s existing bone. In many cases, this approach eliminates the need for bone grafting, making the process faster and more affordable.

Similar to All-on-4 dental implants, All-on-X dental implants are designed to replace multiple missing teeth. The "X" in the name represents the number of implants that a provider determines that a patient needs to restore their smile. This can be anywhere from five to seven implants, unlike the All-on-4 method, which places four implants.

On the day of your surgery, if available/elected, your dentist will administer sedation to ensure you are relaxed during your procedure. Your dentist will then place the implants into your jaw. Les soins

All-on-4 is a dental implant technique that replaces a full arch of missing teeth using just four strategically placed implants. These implants anchor a custom-made set of teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
