Our services

See below for some of the general dentistry services we offer.

Fillings

Over time tooth structure can decay. Dental fillings repair cavities so that they can continue to function in high form. Failure to have cavities fixed can lead to infections that have to be fixed with root canals, fracture of teeth, or even extraction.

Thorough Hygiene and Exams The most important part of good health is knowing what is going on with your teeth. Our friendly hygienists bring years of experience to the practice, providing gentle and thorough care. To evaluate your teeth and keep them clean is the best way to ensure good oral health!

Root Canals

Sometimes the nerve inside our tooth can become irritated due to decay or trauma. When it is irritated it cannot heal properly and often leads to pain. A root canal is a procedure that removes the damaged root so that the tooth can be saved .

Emergency / Wisdom Tooth Extractions At Hometown Dental we know there is nothing worse than tooth pain ! Removing a tooth is always a last resort, but may be necessary when dealing with severe decay, infection, or transitioning to dentures.

Bridges

We can bridge the gap that you have! Bridges, a permanent solution to missing teeth , can be done in the front of the mouth for aesthetic results or in the posterior region to ensure better chewing capacity.

Crowns Dental crowns are used when a tooth has had a large filling that is breaking down, a fracture, or decay. Crowns can be extremely durable and aesthetic .

Implants

Contact us today to see if dental implants are right for you. We are happy to answer any questions you may have.

Dentures Custom fitting dentures are offered in either a traditional style or with implants. We can deliver a new smile that you will love .
