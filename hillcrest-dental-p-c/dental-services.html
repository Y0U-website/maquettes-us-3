Cosmetic

Your smile is probably one of your biggest assets. Enhance it with our cosmetic dentistry services that include the following:

  • Tooth-colored buildup
  • Crowns and veneers
  • Whitening
  • Bonding (crack repair, gap repair, fillings)

Endodontics

Endodontics is that part of dentistry that deals with the nerves of your teeth. If the pulp in your tooth becomes infected, then it’s time for you to get a root canal treatment . Rely on our experienced doctors to perform the root canal procedure efficiently and help alleviate your tooth pain.

Sometimes, a tooth that has already received the root canal treatment may get infected again due to a number of reasons like a broken crown or filling and salivary contamination to name a few. Visit our office and let our skilled dentists perform retreatment on your diseased tooth.

Restorative

Restorative dentistry deals with the rehabilitation of the dentition as per your functional and aesthetic needs. Visit our clinic for any of the following restorative services:

  • Bridges
  • Crowns
  • Fillings
  • Dentures

Pediatric

If your little one is experiencing any pain or difficulty related to their oral health, you can bring them to us. We provide the following services:

  • Sealants
  • Mouthguards

Periodontic

Count on us to provide you with effective treatment if you’re suffering from any periodontal (or gum) disease. We perform the following procedures:

  • Crown lengthening
  • Frenectomy
  • Occlusal adjustment
  • Periodontal splinting
  • Scaling and root planing

Oral Surgery

If you need to remove a tooth surgically, be sure to come and see one of our skilled dentists. We also perform oral surgeries to place dental implants and extract damaged teeth.

Dental Sleep Medicine

If you’re suffering from sleep apnea or TMJ pain , rely on us to provide you with the necessary treatment. We can also give you night guards to help prevent or reduce the effects of teeth grinding while sleeping.

Our Technology

We use nitrous oxide, also known as the laughing gas, for sedation. This anesthetic gas is very safe and will make you feel comfortable during certain dental procedures.
