Meet Our Northampton, MA Dentist…

Dr. Claire Edwards

Dr. Claire

I grew up in the small town of Klamath Falls, Oregon, the type of town where you could not go anywhere without seeing somebody you knew. I am now happy to call this friendly valley my home, where I can learn and grow with my community and with my patients. Having grown up with one consistent, compassionate and dependable dentist throughout my youth, this is the type of family dentist I strive to be for my patients. Although initially a Northampton transplant as an undergraduate at Smith College (class of 2009), I now reside in Southampton with my growing family.

I obtained my dental education at Rutgers Dental School, formerly known as the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, in 2015. There I was inducted to Gamma Pi Delta Prosthodontic Honor Society. Regularly participating in continuing education courses, I am dedicated to providing current and quality dentistry to my family of patients. Most recently I have completed extensive continued training in implant dentistry. My dental memberships include the Massachusetts Dental Society, American Dental Association, and the Pioneer Valley Women Dentists.

When I am not at work, you can find me attending a classic car show with my husband, checking out a new local restaurant, or on an adventure with my family seeking to explore as much of this blue-green planet as possible.

I very much enjoy serving my community and love being able to transform my passion for science, art and patient care into my profession.
