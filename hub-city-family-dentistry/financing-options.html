Care Credit

We are proud to accept CareCredit as a financing option for treatments and procedures that may not be covered by insurance. You can learn more and apply online here.

Insurance Plans

At Hub City Family Dentistry, we proudly participate in-network with a wide range of major PPO plans, including but not limited to Cigna, BCBS, Humana, Aetna, United Healthcare, and MetLife, among others. While each insurance provider and plan varies, our team is delighted to help you navigate your specific coverage. Feel free to reach out to us with any inquiries you may have.

Cash and Credit Cards

If CareCredit and Insurance are not suitable for you or your family, we also accept cash and all major credit cards. We do kindly request that payment arrangements be made at the time services are rendered.
