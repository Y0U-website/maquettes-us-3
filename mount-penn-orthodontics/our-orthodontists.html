Meet Our Team

Kayla Jolivet, DMD, MS

Dr. Kayla Jolivet is excited to be part of the Mount Penn family and serve patients in Reading and surrounding areas. Dr. Jolivet earned her biology degree from Rutgers University in Camden, NJ and her dental degree (DMD) from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. She went on to complete her specialty training in Orthodontics at Stony Brook University, where she also earned a master’s degree in Oral Biology and Pathology.

She is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Pennsylvania Dental Association. Originally from Burlington, NJ, she now calls West Reading home. When not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Kayla enjoys knitting, crocheting, reading, exploring new places, and spending time with her cat, Chester.
