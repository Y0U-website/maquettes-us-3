PRIVACY POLICY

GoldTree Endo is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information collected through our 10DLC program.

1. User Information: We collect information provided during program registration, including name, email, and phone number.

2. Message Content: We store message content for delivery and analytics purposes.

3. Usage Data: We collect usage statistics, such as message volume and engagement metrics.

1. Program Delivery: We use collected information to deliver messages and provide program services.

2. Analytics: We analyze usage data to improve program performance and user experience.

3. Compliance: We use collected information to ensure compliance with 10DLC program requirements.

1. Service Providers: We share information with authorized service providers for program delivery and support.

2. Partners: We share aggregated analytics with partners for program optimization.

3. Regulatory Bodies: We disclose information as required by regulatory bodies to ensure compliance.

1. Encryption: We encrypt message content and user information.

2. Access Controls: We implement secure access controls to protect collected information.

1. 10DLC Program Requirements: We adhere to 10DLC program guidelines.

2. TCPA Compliance: We comply with Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) regulations.

1. Opt-Out: Users can opt-out of messages by replying "STOP."

2. Data Access: Users can request access to their collected information.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted here.
