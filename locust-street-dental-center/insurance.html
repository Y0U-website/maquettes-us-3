Insurance Information

Our office takes pride in spending intentional time with each patient to receive the highest quality of care tailored to their individual needs. While our office will bill your insurance company as a courtesy, our approach allows us to prioritize your care over the limitations of insurance companies.

  • 0 Dental insurance is not required at Locust Street Dental. We offer our own In-House Membership plan for those who choose to be without dental insurance but would like to save money on their necessary treatment. If you or your family are interested in this membership plan, please don’t hesitate to ask us about what it entails. Locust Street Dental accepts all dental insurances. As a courtesy to our patients, we submit all claims to your insurance to help streamline the process. We have chosen to not have a contract with these companies so we can always provide the best care for our patients.
  • We expect all our patients to pay at the time of service. Before arriving to your visits, we make sure that you don’t have any questions, and you are aware of the costs associated with treatment and your insurance breakdown. We don’t withhold any of that information from you, and we do our best to utilize your insurance as efficiently as possible. It is important to understand that with insurance, there are many factors that affect their reimbursement that are outside of our control.
