About Rockwall Oral Surgery

Oral and maxillofacial surgery requires additional years of hospital-based surgical and anesthesia training after graduation from dental school. As oral and maxillofacial surgeons, Dr. Pollock and Dr. Dombrowski manage a wide variety of problems relating to the mouth, teeth, and facial regions.

Your Choice For Oral Surgery We have a long-standing relationship with Rockwall HEALTH

Dr. Kevin Pollock and Dr. Nathan Dombrowski practice a full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery with expertise ranging from dental implant surgery and wisdom tooth removal to corrective jaw surgery. This also includes techniques designed to rebuild bone structure with minimal surgical intervention and optimal patient comfort. Our office can also diagnose and treat facial pain, facial injuries, and fractures.

The surgical staff at Rockwall Oral Surgery are experienced, certified oral and maxillofacial surgical assistants, who assist in the administration of IV sedation and surgery. We also have a trained registered nurse (RN) to assist in surgery. All staff are also informed administrative personnel, well versed in health and insurance policies, and are CPR certified. Our staff is trained in assisting with Intravenous (IV) sedation and outpatient general anesthesia in our state-of-the-art office setting. Patients are continuously monitored during and after surgery.

Surgical Staff

For almost twenty years, Rockwall Oral Surgery has proudly served Rockwall and the surrounding communities. Community and relationships are important to us, which is why we have prioritized our services for an excellent, caring experience.

As a fixture in the dental and medical community in the area, Dr. Pollock has long, trusting relationships with most dentists in the area. He has committed time and effort to provide educational oral surgery resources for the local dental community for years. Now, Dr. Dombrowski is building on that trusted legacy and bringing his knowledge and expertise to Rockwall to ensure our services are available for years to come. Together they ensure that the local dental community is as up to date as possible and it builds strong bonds of trust among the dental professionals in the community.

In addition, Rockwall Oral Surgery is always active supporting local charities and causes. As an active part of the community, we hope to extend the qualities of trust and service out from our practice to members of our community. Thank you, Rockwall, for allowing us to serve you for all this time.
