Frequently Asked Questions

What level of education is needed to become a dentist?

Are you curious about what makes providers qualified to work on patients’ teeth and gums? Rest assured, all dentists are highly educated individuals and must meet strict criteria to be licensed to practice.

For instance, they must first earn a bachelor’s degree in an appropriate field of study. Then, they can choose to achieve either a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) or Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from one of the 70+ institutions accredited by the American Dental Association. From there, they can choose to focus on one of 12 officially recognized specialties.

At our office in San Antonio, Dr. Kadamani graduated from the Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in California and is committed to continuing education to stay current with the latest developments in dental technology and techniques.

How much does a routine checkup and cleaning cost in San Antonio?

The American Dental Association recommends having a routine checkup and cleaning every six months to allow your provider to monitor your condition and treat any areas of concern before they worsen. However, if you’re unsure what basic services cost, you might worry it’s out of your price range and avoid scheduling a visit.

Typically, you can expect to pay anywhere from $75 to $200 for a standard visit in San Antonio, though this can vary based on your individual circumstances. For example, dental insurance can significantly reduce the cost of your appointment, but you might pay more if our team finds issues, like cavities or gum disease, that need to be addressed.

What do you do if you can’t afford a dentist?

Our team believes that everyone deserves to have a happy, healthy smiles regardless of their personal finances. As a result, we’re proudly in-network with all dental PPO insurance plans. We also gladly welcome Texas Medicare, Adult Medicaid & Medicare, and CHIP (Children’s Medicaid).

If you’re uninsured or don’t participate in these programs, don’t lose hope! You can apply for a flexible payment plan through CareCredit. If approved, this allows you to divvy your total invoice into more manageable installments over a designated period.
