Dentist in Centerville OH

Dr. Linty John-Varghese

Education is an opportunity that brings more into our lives. Having been taught and trained by the best dental leaders in this country and other countries, Dr. Linty John-Varghese brings forth a lot more specialty training from the dental world, which allows her to offer more options and treatment to her patients and practice. She enjoys helping patients understand their dental health from a whole-body wellness perspective. Healing begins when the relationship between our teeth and body is brought to light, why it happened, and how we can get to better dental health together.

Born and raised in Dubai, Dr. Linty graduated from New York University College of Dentistry with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with Honors in implants in 2004. She then did a general practice residency at Dayton VA Medical Center and practiced at Wright Patterson Air Force Base in Dayton. She subsequently ventured into private practice, purchasing Lyons Dental and transitioning to the name Ohio Holistic Dental Center in early 2024, allowing more patients to find their new holistic dental home.

However, Dr. Linty did not stop there. She pursued her continuing education with enthusiasm and a passion for learning. She is a graduate of the Kois Center, Misch Implant Institute, Clinical Mastery Series, Wellness Dentistry Masterclass, and Rondeau orthodontic seminars. Dr. Linty is also trained in airway and sleep dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, cosmetic dentistry, Digital Smile Design, laser dentistry, Invisalign , Six Month Smiles braces, BOTOX for TMJ treatment, and cosmetic fillers.

Dr. Linty has now turned her attention to the practice of biological dentistry, with a focus on whole-body wellness for better dental health. Her pivot to biological dentistry stems from her concern with the whole-body effects of dental materials, techniques, and procedures. She is a member of the International Academy of Biological Dentistry and Medicine (IABDM), the International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology (IAMOT), and the Holistic Dental Association (HDA).

With the firm belief that patients are not just part of the treatment, they are part of the process; she will listen to you, spending quality time and a world of patience. You will be heard and understood, and together, we will build trust that’s when the healing begins. Dentistry is not just treatment for teeth, gums, and jaw; it is the gateway to better health and life.

Dr. Linty has three children, and her spouse is a physician in Springfield. She loves spending time with her family and siblings and even has a sassy little fur-baby doggie named Mango. She enjoys writing and has a dental blog educating other dentists. Her interest in interior design shows her creative side, which you will see reflected in our practice. Dr. Linty considers herself a visionary with the courage to put ideas into action.

When she’s not with her family or at her practice, she’s on a flight chasing the next dental class with her insatiable hunger to learn more, be more, and bring back the next best cutting-edge technology and equipment to Dayton. She takes particular pride in always being available to her patients because it is important to her, and they are like family. Now that’s PRICELESS!
