Dr. Brian Smith

Dr. Smith’s curiosity about this relationship between oral and systemic health inspired him to continue advanced studies with the Institute of Nutritional Dentistry, along with three additional years of training in Oral-Systemic Balance (OSB) therapy. He expended his approach even further with studying through the American College of Integrative Medicine and Dentistry. Dr. Smith is also a certified massage therapist and trained cranial sacral therapist, which only enriches his unique, holistic perspective to dentistry.

A part of the Marin Dental Wellness team for several years now, Dr. Thomas Nguyen brings over a decade of experience practicing dentistry throughout the San Francisco Bay Area and serves as both a general dentist and a specialist in dental surgery.

Dr. Nguyen earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from UCSF School of Dentistry in 2015. During his time there, he volunteered through the Science Education Program and the Community Dental Clinic, serving patients in need and giving back to the community. Following graduation, he was accepted into the General Practice Residency program with the Community Medical Centers and the U.S. Department of Veteran Affairs, where he treated patients from all walks of life — including medically compromised patients in operating rooms under general anesthesia.

His dedication to advanced training led him to achieve the honor of Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantology (ICOI), an international recognition of skill in both the surgical and restorative aspects of dental implants. During his early years at Marin Dental Wellness, Dr. Nguyen also trained under our former oral surgeon Dr. Jarvis until his retirement, further refining his surgical expertise.

Dr. Nguyen practices with an open mind and a holistic philosophy, combining advanced techniques with compassionate care. His enthusiasm and poise are energizing, and his gentle nature shines through in the way he cares for patients. Outside of the office, he enjoys staying physically active, traveling the world, and spending time with his large family including his 11 nieces and nephews, who keep him smiling and on his toes.

Dr. Jiawei Li General Dentistry & Dental Surgery

Dr. Li’s practice philosophy centers on minimally invasive dentistry, using advanced techniques and materials that closely mimic the natural properties of teeth. Her goal is always to preserve and strengthen her patients’ smiles while supporting their overall health.

Originally from a family of medical professionals, Dr. Li’s passion for dentistry began early. She graduated at the top of her class from the University of Southern California Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, where she was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU), the National Dental Honor Society. She also demonstrated strong leadership in study clubs and contributed to philanthropic dental missions in Mexico, Guatemala, and China, earning one of USC’s largest merit-based scholarships.
