New Patient Exam

We love welcoming new patients to our family-owned and -operated practice! You’ll be greeted with a warm smile and friendly welcome from our team when you arrive. We’ll do an intraoral scan, X-rays and check for infections, cavitations, sinus issues, vitamin D deficiency and oral cancer. Dr. Rodriguez will meet with you to discuss your wants, needs and concerns, and she’ll explain your treatment options, answer all your questions and create a personalized treatment plan just for you.

Gentle, Thorough Cleanings

Routine cleanings are efficient, thorough, and safe at Magnolia Family Dental Care. Our skilled hygienists use clean, fluoride free products along with minimally invasive ultrasonic scalers and advanced air polishing to comfortably clean your teeth.

For many patients, we use glycine air polishing , a gentle method that uses warm water, air, and a fine natural powder to remove plaque, bacteria, and surface stains. It is especially comfortable for sensitive teeth and gums and allows for effective cleaning along the gumline without harsh scraping.

We may also use our state of the art intraoral scanner to show you detailed images of your teeth and gums, helping you better understand your oral health and areas that may need extra attention. Ozone therapy is often incorporated as part of your cleaning to support a thoroughly decontaminated mouth.

Gum Disease Treatment

If you have gum disease, Dr. Rodriguez can help. You will be comfortably numbed, and she will use Hybenx, a professional dental decontaminant to dehydrate the bacteria and calculus build-up for easier and more complete removal. She also provides tooth-by-tooth ozone therapy to fully decontaminate your mouth, and uses PRF to boost natural healing. StellaLife® Rinse—a natural, homeopathic mouthwash—is recommended for home care.

For patients with severe gum disease, Dr. Rodriguez strongly recommends LANAP® minimally invasive laser treatment and will recommend a specialist to provide that.

Tooth Extraction With Socket Preservation

Dr. Rodriguez gently extracts teeth and provides socket preservation (a type of bone grafting) to prevent a painful dry socket and prep the area for potential implant placement in the future. She also applies platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), a natural healing agent derived from your own blood, to the extraction site to minimize discomfort, reduce inflammation and accelerate healing.

Comfort & Sedation

Your comfort is a priority for us. We will take the time to explain your treatments, answer your questions and make sure you feel comfortable at all times. We offer complimentary food and drinks, including delicious, award-winning gourmet coffee from Colombia.

We offer CBD oil, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) or oral sedation to help you feel at ease. Dr. Rodriguez will help you decide on which, if any, are right for you and your procedures. To help you not feel stressed about cost, Dr. Rodriguez charges a flat fee per appointment for sedation instead of charging by the hour, half hour or in 15 minute-increments as other dentists often do.

Two-Appointment Dentures

Dr. Rodriguez provides comfortable, esthetic and well-fitting new dentures, as well as in-office denture repairs and relines. She can provide a new denture in as few as two appointments, and she works with a top-quality dental lab that offers a five-year guarantee on their dentures.

Dental Bridges

Dr. Rodriguez provides minimal prep bridges that preserve as much natural tooth structure as possible for long-term stability and health.

Children’s Dentistry

Dr. Rodriguez loves helping whole families get the dentistry they need to be healthy and happy. She welcomes children of any age and helps them to form positive early impressions of going to a dentist’s office. She provides fluoride-free sealants with ozone, fillings, cleanings, oral appliance therapy and more.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canals are sometimes necessary, but they are not automatically the best solution for every tooth or every patient. Our approach starts with careful evaluation and a focus on prevention whenever possible.

In some cases, we may recommend supportive therapies such as homeopathic options and ozone treatment to help reduce bacterial load and support the tooth before a root canal becomes necessary.

When a root canal is the appropriate choice, we also offer additional maintenance and supportive care to promote healthier healing and long-term stability after treatment.

Our goal is to help you make an informed decision that fits your overall health, not to rush you into a one-size-fits-all procedure.

Mouthguards

Dr. Rodriguez offers custom-made mouthguards designed to protect both natural teeth and dental restorations from damage caused by nighttime teeth grinding (bruxism).
