About Us

“When Jesus was here, he helped so many people with physical problems. Because he was healing their physical needs, he was really healing their hearts - all their worry and discouragement and despair. This is exactly what Healing Hands does. They reach out their hands to heal our bodies so God can heal our hearts.” Tammy Shady Valley, TN Patient

  • 1997 Healing Hands Health Center served our first patient at First Presbyterian Church in Bristol, TN. An essential partnership with Bristol Memorial Hospital was established, evolving into Bristol Regional Medical Center.
  • 1998 We relocated to 104 Memorial Drive, Bristol, TN, securing a lease from Bristol Memorial Hospital at the cost of $1/year.
  • 1999 Healing Hands Health Center moved to 210 Memorial Drive, Bristol, TN, facilitated by the generous donation of the building by Dr. Fred Greear, Dr. Fred Vance, Jr. & Dr. Ben Cowan, Sr.
  • 2002 We began our partnership with the ETSU Dental Hygiene program.
  • 2003 We initiated a partnership with ETSU Quillen College of Medicine that includes our community health fair initiative.
  • 2014 We launched our pediatric dental outreach program.
  • 2015 We moved to 245 Midway Medical Park, funded through a Capital Campaign. We established our partnership with UT College of Dentistry, with students initially residing in a house purchased by Healing Hands Health Center.
  • 2017 We forged a partnership with Appalachian College of Pharmacy.
  • 2018 We joined the Northeast Tennessee Accountable Care Community, later becoming the Strong ACC in 2021.
  • 2021 We hired our first Community Health Worker, and we collaborated with Ballad Health to place one of their navigators in our clinic, providing additional community health worker support.
  • 2022 We completed construction and opened the J.D. and Lorraine Nicewonder Education Center, funded through a Capital Campaign. We hired our first-ever full-time dentist, and we completed a dental expansion to serve more patients in our region.
  • 2023 We completed a rebranding initiative made possible by Tennessee Charitable Care Network and the Tennessee Department of Health, resulting in a name transition to Healing Hands Health.
  • 2023 We purchased 225 Midway Medical Park to expand services and integrate behavioral health with medical services.
  • 2026 Our Medical, Behavioral Health, and Community Health Worker programs officially moved to our new facility at 225 Midway Medical Park.

News and updates from Healing Hands Health

BRISTOL, Tenn. (WJHL) – Leaders from across the state toured the Healing Hands Health facility in Bristol, Tennessee, to learn how the facility provides services to patients who are underinsured or uninsured.

Helen Scott, Executive Director of Healing Hands Health, said it provides dental and medical healthcare to those around the area.

“We serve the whole region of Southwest Virginia and Northeast Tennessee,” Scott said. “We help people who don’t have medical insurance, don’t have health insurance, dental insurance. So, we help the uninsured and then also the underinsured. So, we help people who have TennCare and Virginia Medicaid and adult dental.”

BRISTOL, Tenn. – State health officials toured Healing Hands Health Center’s new medical/behavioral health clinic and expanded dental clinic on Wednesday.

A delegation of 10 state and area health leaders including state Health Commissioner Dr. John Dunn and Dr. Ken Tilashalski, dean of the college of dentistry at UT Health Science Center, spent about an hour learning about how the clinic utilizes state support and its relationships with colleges and universities.

BRISTOL, Tenn. – About 75 people attended Thursday’s ribbon-cutting ceremony for Healing Hands Health’s 9,800-square-foot medical services building, where organizers revealed they had significantly exceeded a fundraising campaign goal.
