Hannah Raffoul, DDS

Dr. Hannah Raffoul originates from the Dayton, Ohio region. She completed her bachelor s degree in biology at the University of Dayton before pursuing her DDS degree at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. Following her graduation from dental school, Dr. Raffoul completed a general dentistry residency at the Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Dayton, Ohio.

After residency, Dr. Raffoul made her way to Nashville, TN, where she served as an associate dentist at a private practice for the past six years. Dr. Raffoul takes great pride in her commitment to delivering top-tier dental care while fostering meaningful and enduring relationships with her patients.

Dr. Raffoul calls East Nashville home, where she shares her life with her husband, two daughters, and two dogs. She enjoys frequenting locally owned restaurants and breweries, exploring farmers markets, appreciating local parks, and practicing her culinary skills (including the art of sneaking vegetables into dishes her toddler will actually eat).
