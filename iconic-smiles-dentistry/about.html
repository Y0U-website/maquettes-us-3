Meet your dentist

Combining heart and handcrafted smiles - meet Dr. Simrat Sehmi

Dr. Simrat Sehmi didn’t grow up dreaming of becoming a dentist-but once she discovered the impact a smile could make, there was no turning back. Originally from India, she moved to the U.S. as a teenager and began a journey that would lead her to helping patients smile brighter every day. That journey included completing her DDS at Virginia Commonwealth University, where she solidified her clinical expertise and nurturing approach to dental care.

With over a decade in practice, Dr. Sehmi knows dentistry isn’t just about teeth-it’s about trust. That’s why she built Iconic Smiles Dentistry to be a place where patients feel heard, supported, and never rushed. Her proudest achievement? Being recognized with the Presidential Award for Volunteering. Helping her community has always been part of who she is, and now that she has her own practice, she&#x27;s excited to offer that same sense of service and connection to the families of Leander and beyond.

Patients appreciate Dr. Sehmi’s calming presence and the way she makes even the most nervous patient feel at ease. Her favorite part of the job is working with her hands-especially when it comes to crafting beautiful crowns-and seeing the confidence a restored smile can bring.

When she’s not at the office, you’ll likely find Dr. Sehmi exploring local restaurants (big fan of Terry Black’s BBQ), tackling escape rooms, or roaming the city with her husband and their dog, Zazzu.

Feel comfortable from the very first visit

What to expect when you walk into Iconic Smiles

Your first visit is all about getting to know you-your needs, your smile goals, and your comfort level. We’ll greet you with a friendly face, walk you through everything step-by-step, and make sure you leave feeling confident about the care you&#x27;re getting.

Patient Experience Expect a stress-free vibe, a transparent conversation about your dental health, and a team that truly listens. We believe in building relationships over transactions, so you’ll never feel like you’re just another patient in the chair.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

New patient paperwork (if not filled out ahead of time)

High-tech comfort meets hometown hospitality

At Iconic Smiles Dentistry, we’ve combined modern technology with thoughtful amenities to create a space that feels both advanced and welcoming. From digital forms to cozy touches in every room, you’ll find that we’ve thought of everything to make your visit easier-and maybe even enjoyable.

Beverage station

Enjoy a sip while you wait with our relaxing beverage station, stocked with drinks to help you feel right at home.

Netflix & monitors in operatories

Catch up on your favorite show while we take care of your smile. Each room is equipped with screens and Netflix streaming for your entertainment and distraction.

Headphones

Tune out and relax. We offer noise-cancelling headphones so you can listen to music during your appointment or simply enjoy some quiet.

iPads for check-in or entertainment

Whether you want to speed through check-in or pass time with your favorite apps, our iPads offer convenience and fun.

Warm towels

After your treatment, enjoy the simple luxury of a warm towel to refresh and relax.

Digital X-rays

We offer advanced digital x-rays to provide faster, clearer imaging with less radiation, ensuring safer, more precise diagnostics and a more comfortable patient experience.

Intraoral scanner

Goodbye goopy impressions. Our intraoral scanner takes precise digital images for more accurate-and more comfortable-treatment planning.

Rooted in care, growing through giving

Giving back is part of who we are

At Iconic Smiles Dentistry, Dr. Sehmi brings a lifelong dedication to service—recognized by her Presidential Award for Volunteering. She has proudly served with Mission of Mercy and volunteered at a free dental clinic in Houston, providing care to underserved communities. While we’re just getting started, community outreach plays a big part in our vision. Dr. Sehmi plans to host free dentistry days in the future and looks forward to building meaningful partnerships with local organizations. Our goal? Support the community that supports us, one smile at a time.
