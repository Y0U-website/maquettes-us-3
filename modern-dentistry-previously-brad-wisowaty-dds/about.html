Lori

Office Admin and Financial Coordinator

Lori

Office Admin and Financial Coordinator

Chances are, if you call our office, Lori will be the one who answers the phone. Lori’s career in dentistry began as an assistant, and then grew to EFDA. For the past nine years however, she has held her role as front desk at this office. Lori has an unmatched ability to remember specifics about people, details about their family, small changes in people’s personal lives, and random facts. Having been involved in all aspects of dentistry for so many years, Lori is exceptionally knowledgeable and a great help to patients who come into the office.

Each morning Lori sets the mood in our practice by turning on our stereo system and deciding which music we will all listen to — it&#x27;s usually classic rock 97.1, The Drive. She loves concerts, and has missed attending them in the last few years. In the meantime, a self-proclaimed gear head, she’s been enjoying weekend trips to her camper cabin and her annual spring break trip to Mexico.

Chris

Registered Dental Assistant

Chris

Registered Dental Assistant

Chris is from Kouts, Indiana, and has been in the dental field since 1984. She started as an assistant and went on to get her Expanded Functions Dental Assistants certification in 1990. As an EFDA, she was trained to place fillings and administer nitrous as well as all the other tasks of an assistant. Chris began working in this building in 1988, but stepped away from dentistry for a period while she was raising her two kids.

Brad Wisowaty DDS LLC is lucky to have her back, as in addition to her EFDA certification, Chris also has an advanced degree in sarcasm! She is quick to smile and does a great job of easing tension or nerves in anyone that sits in our chair. She can also always help fill you in on famous Northwest Indiana geographic landmarks like the Malden grain elevator and church or John Deere store.

Angie

Registered Dental Hygienist

Angie

Registered Dental Hygienist

Angie is a Valparaiso native who has a laid back personality, and when you’re in her chair, an hour long teeth cleaning appointment feels short and sweet! She attended Wheeler High School and then went on to Purdue University. She completed Hygiene School at Ivy Tech, and she started her dental career in the same dental office in Northwest Indiana as Dr. Brad.

Angie’s husband is a doppelgänger for NFL quarterback Matt Ryan, and they have 2 young children. In her free time, Angie has an artistic eye and loves to make personalized glitter glassware. If you notice leftover paint on her hands it is probably from the glassware, or her daughter’s valiant attempt at painting her nails

Ashley

Registered Dental Hygienist

Ashley

Registered Dental Hygienist

Ashley has been working in this office longer than anyone currently here. She graduated hygiene school in 2006 and has been a mainstay since she started here a year later. Ashley is a thorough and even nitpicky hygienist, and her patients love her for it. People walk away feeling refreshed and clean without worrying about wincing through their cleaning.

Ashley comes from a close knit family. She is the oldest of 4 sisters, and her ability to take care of any patient that comes into our office, from ages 1 to 99, is apparent from the moment you meet her. Ashley helps take care of her niece as well as being a caregiver for her grandma. This experience translates to how well she takes care of patients.

Ashley was diagnosed cancer free in 2015 after battling with Hodgkin&#x27;s Lymphoma. During her treatments she would work when she could manage, and would lay down on the leather couch in the cold basement of the office during hot flashes. Through it all, Ashley continued to work at the office due to her deep rooted dedication to her patients.

Ashley and her husband Allen are diehard Pittsburgh Steeler fans and every time it rains, there’s a Steelers umbrella that dries out in the panoramic x-ray room. When not at the office Ashley can probably be found golfing, working out at Fit-Train, or helping her grandma.
