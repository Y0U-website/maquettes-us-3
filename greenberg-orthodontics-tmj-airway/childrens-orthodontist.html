The Children’s Orthodontics Process

Our practice specializes in clear aligner orthodontics (Invisalign® First and Invisalign Teen®).

After digital scans, photos, and imaging, Dr. Greenberg maps a step-by-step plan for your child. Custom aligners are created to move teeth in small increments. Your child wears each set 20 to 22 hours a day and removes them for meals, drinks, brushing, and flossing. New trays are introduced on a set schedule, and brief check-ins keep progress on track.

Some kids benefit from growth-guidance tools, such as a palatal expander or a space maintainer, before or alongside aligners. Once active movement is complete, a retainer holds the new position while bone and soft tissues stabilize. Consistent wear is the key to a smooth process and lasting results.

Parent Prep Guide

You don’t need to do much to prepare , but a little bit of planning can help your child feel more comfortable and confident ahead of their orthodontic start.

Before your first visit:

  • Talk to your child about what to expect in simple, reassuring terms
  • Let them know they’ll meet friendly people and take pictures of their smile
  • If they’re nervous, consider showing them photos of our office or team beforehand

After treatment begins:

Encouragement goes a long way . Celebrate milestones — whether it’s their first scan or last aligner tray.

  • Help your child stick to oral hygiene routines—brushing after meals, flossing nightly
  • Stock up on soft foods for the first few days after new appliances are placed
  • Remind them to avoid sticky or hard snacks (especially with braces!)
  • Use apps or sticker charts to help Invisalign Teen users stay consistent

Pediatric Orthodontics Frequently Asked Questions

How long will my child be wearing braces?

That really depends on whether your child will be getting braces or Invisalign Teen and what are the specific changes needed to straighten your child’s teeth. On average, your child will wear Invisalign Teen between a year to 18 months , but this time may be longer or shorter. During your consultation with Dr. Greenberg, she will be able to give you a more specific length of time.

How much do braces and Invisalign Teen cost?

The price for these orthodontics depends on what kind of straightening is needed, which affects how long the braces or Invisalign will be worn. Most dental insurers do cover orthodontics, so make sure to contact your provider to get more information on that. If your provider does not fully cover your Pasadena Child’s Orthodontic treatment or if your child does not have insurance, make sure to ask about possible payment plans or alternate ways to finance.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children see an orthodontist by age 7. This allows us to catch any potential issues early—like crowding, crossbite, or jaw misalignment—before they become more complex.

Does my child need to wear a retainer?

Yes . Once braces or aligners are finished, retainers help hold your child’s teeth in place. Skipping the retainer phase can undo the progress made during treatment.

Is Invisalign Teen as effective as braces?

For many mild to moderate cases, Invisalign Teen is just as effective as traditional braces , especially when your child wears their aligners consistently. Dr. Greenberg will help determine if it’s the right fit for your child.

What if my child forgets to wear their aligners?

It happens! Invisalign Teen includes a blue compliance indicator that helps both parents and Dr. Greenberg track wear time. If your child struggles with consistency, we can offer coaching or explore other options like ceramic braces.

How often will we need to come in for visits?

Braces typically require check-ins every four to six weeks. Invisalign Teen patients come in every six to eight weeks for new aligners. Either way, we keep visits efficient and kid-friendly .
