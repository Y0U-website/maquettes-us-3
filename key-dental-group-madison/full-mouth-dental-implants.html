What Are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants are a set of artificial teeth permanently anchored to your jaw using titanium posts. These posts act like natural tooth roots and support crowns or bridges that look, feel, and function like real teeth. This treatment is ideal for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth and want a strong, stable smile that can last a lifetime. The Process

At Key Dental Group, you can expect the following steps when you get full mouth dental implants in Madison, MS:

Step 1: Free Consultation

We start with a one-on-one consultation. Our dentist will review your dental health, discuss your goals, and create a customized treatment plan. Many patients can start treatment immediately or after a few preparatory steps.

Step 2: Implant Placement
