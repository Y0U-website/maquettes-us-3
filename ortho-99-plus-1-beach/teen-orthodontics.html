It differs from early orthodontic treatment in that it’s focused on adjusting the full set of permanent teeth, correcting malocclusions, and enhancing the overall aesthetics of the smile.

Do Teens Have a Different Experience?

The experience for teens undergoing orthodontic treatment is distinct in several ways. Teens are more actively engaged in their treatment, capable of understanding the process, and usually more motivated by the aesthetic outcomes. They have more options available for treatment, from traditional braces to clear aligners, depending on their lifestyle and preferences.

How Long Does Treatment Last?

The length of teen orthodontic treatment can vary significantly, depending on the complexity of the case and the type of orthodontic appliance used. On average, treatment tends to last around two years.

Is it Painful or Uncomfortable?

There may be some initial discomfort when appliances are first fitted or adjusted. However, modern orthodontic technology and techniques have made the process much more comfortable than in the past. At Ortho 99 Plus 1, we prioritize patient comfort in all our orthodontic procedures.
