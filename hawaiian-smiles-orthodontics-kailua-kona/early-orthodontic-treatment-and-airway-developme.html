Early Orthodontic Treatment: Phase 1 and 2

Once most or all permanent teeth have erupted, the second stage of care is known as Phase 2 orthodontic treatment . This phase typically takes place during the teenage years and focuses on precise alignment of the teeth and bite. Phase 2 often uses braces or clear aligners such as Invisalign to complete the orthodontic correction. Because Phase 1 treatment has already addressed underlying growth and airway concerns, Phase 2 treatment is often more efficient and produces more stable long-term results.

Benefits of Early Orthodontic Evaluations

At Hawaiian Smiles Orthodontics, early orthodontic evaluations help determine whether a child may benefit from airway-focused orthodontic care. The goal is not simply to straighten teeth, but to support healthy growth, comfortable breathing, and balanced facial development. By evaluating children early and guiding jaw growth at the right time, orthodontic care can help set the stage for healthier smiles and healthier breathing throughout life.
