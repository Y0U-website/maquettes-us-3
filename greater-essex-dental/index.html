The practice

We treat individuals of all ages, from children to geriatric patients. Each new patient will meet with Dr. Avdagic first, giving both the patient and Dr. Avdagic a chance to develop a relationship, allowing for a team approach to individual patient needs. Building a friendly working relationship with Dr. Avdagic and his team is critical in addressing patient needs.

Dr. Avdagic is a skilled dental expert and has engaged a professional, friendly staff. We care for our patients as individuals and strive to meet their needs in every way possible. We pride ourselves on the personal touch we have with our patients, and we work to create a family atmosphere with maximum-quality dental care by utilizing the latest technology and standards of care.

We want to provide you with the best possible dental experience. Our office environment is friendly and welcoming, and our staff handles patient issues with compassion and skill. We offer the highest-quality care available using updated technology and materials.

Our office provides a broad range of dental services, adding to your convenience and the peace of mind that comes with having your care under one roof. Our services include:

Dr. Avdagic sees patients of all ages. We provide extensive preventative services, including routine cleanings, periodontal maintenance, comprehensive oral exams, digital radiographs, and CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) scanning.

Dr. Avdagic uses his implant expertise to enhance his patients’ smiles. The implant process involves nearly no pain thanks to the techniques and methods Dr. Avdagic implements. We offer single-tooth implants as well as bridges and full arch replacements.

We use the Clear Correct system to provide orthodontic care. We can close spaces between teeth and realign them to restore them to ideal positions.

We expertly perform root canals procedures with a variety of advanced tools, such as our Zeiss microscope for difficult to see and access canals.
