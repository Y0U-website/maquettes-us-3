Get to know Dr. Lindsay Michael, DDS

Dr. Lindsay Michael was born and raised in Topeka, Kansas, and is proud to now serve the community that shaped her. As a lifelong patient of Dr. Johnson, she experienced firsthand the impact a thoughtful, relationship-centered dental practice can have. Those early experiences shaped her vision of creating a practice grounded in trust, familiarity, and clinical excellence.

She earned her undergraduate degree from Eckerd College, where she studied Biology and pursued additional studies in Biochemistry, Business, and Leadership. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City. After several years of clinical experience and mentorship, Dr. Lindsay transitioned into practice ownership with the goal of creating a modern dental environment rooted in both clinical excellence and meaningful patient relationships.

Dr. Lindsay is deliberate and precise in her clinical work, balancing meticulous attention to detail with a comprehensive understanding of each patient’s long-term goals. She leads with humility and confidence, cultivating a culture of accountability, warmth, and shared standards.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Lindsay enjoys time with her husband and two young children. Running, reading, and family time keep her grounded and energized, often all within the same day.

With Dr. Lindsay, patients can expect dentistry delivered at the highest standard, approached with sincerity, respect, and a level of personal investment that reflects how she would care for her own family.

Dr. Frank Johnson, DDS

Dr. Frank Johnson has set the standard for excellence in dentistry in Topeka for over 35 years. Born and raised in Topeka, he is proud to serve the community that shaped him. Dr. Johnson’s journey in dentistry began with his undergraduate studies in physics at Kansas State University. He then went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City.

Having worked in the community for more than two decades, Dr. Johnson is deeply embedded in Topeka’s fabric. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Johnson has been an avid supporter of local schools, sports teams, and the Topeka Civic Theater, further solidifying his dedication to the community.

Dr. Johnson’s time in the office is built on a foundation of trust, integrity, and quality care, values that he instilled in his team and passed on to Dr. Lindsay Michael, who was a lifelong patient of his. Now, as a mentor and colleague, Dr. Johnson continues to guide the practice, ensuring the highest standard of care for every patient.

Technology strengthening our standard

At Lindsay Michael Dentistry, we combine artistry with cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional results with greater comfort, accuracy, and efficiency. Our office is equipped with advanced digital tools that elevate every step of your care—from diagnosis to final results.

iTero Digital Scanner

Say goodbye to messy impressions. With our iTero digital scanner, we capture fast, comfortable, and highly accurate 3D images of your teeth. This technology not only improves precision but also allows you to see a simulation of your potential results—so you can feel confident moving forward with your treatment.

3D CBCT Imaging

Our in-office CBCT (Cone Beam Technology) scanner provides highly detailed, three-dimensional images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This advanced imaging allows us to diagnose with incredible precision and plan treatments more safely and predictably—whether you’re considering veneers, implants, or other restorative procedures.

A Better Experience, Start to Finish

These advanced technologies help us streamline your visits, reduce guesswork, and deliver beautifully precise outcomes. The result? A smoother process, fewer surprises, and a smile you’ll love with confidence.
