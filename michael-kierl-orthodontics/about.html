About

About Us One Doctor, One Focus: Your Family’s Smile At Michael Kierl Orthodontics, our mission is to provide individualized, compassionate care in a state-of-the-art, comfortable environment. Dr. Kierl and our dedicated team work closely with each patient and their family to achieve the healthiest, most confident smile possible, all while making every visit safe, enjoyable, and stress-free.

We are committed to respecting your time, keeping you informed at every step, and offering treatment that meets your goals and fits your lifestyle. With a focus on excellence, continuing education, and the latest advances in orthodontics, we deliver precise, effective care with results you can trust. Above all, we strive to create a positive, welcoming experience where every patient feels valued, cared for, and confident in their winning smile.

Meet Dr. Mike Kierl

Dr. Kierl believes in providing personalized orthodontic care tailored to each patient’s needs. He is committed to creating a comfortable, positive experience while achieving the healthiest, most confident smiles. His approach emphasizes clear communication, careful treatment planning, and a patient-first philosophy that guides every aspect of care.

When he’s not creating beautiful smiles in the office, Dr. Kierl loves spending time with his wife, Ann, and their four children, Peter, Patraic, Maureen, and Clare. He is active in his church and enjoys giving back to the community, including coaching little league basketball and junior high football for many years. Outside of work and volunteering, he loves sports, family adventures, and curling up with a good “whodunit” mystery whenever he gets a quiet moment.
