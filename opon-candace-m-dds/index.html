Dental sealants are thin, plastic coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars and premolars to protect against decay by sealing out food and bacteria.

Tooth-colored fillings, made of composite resin, match the natural color of teeth, providing a discreet solution for filling cavities while restoring strength and function.

Dental bonding involves applying a tooth-colored resin to repair chips, cracks, or gaps, improving the appearance of teeth in a non-invasive, cost-effective procedure.

Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to improve their appearance by covering stains, chips, or gaps, creating a natural-looking, radiant smile.
