Divya Anugu, DMD

Dr. Divya Anugu is a graduate of Boston University, Henry M Goldman School of Dental Medicine where she earned her DMD Prior to attending Boston University, Dr. Anugu earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery form Sri Sai College of Dental Surgery in Hyderabad, India.

Dr. Anugu provides her patients with high-quality, comprehensive dental care to meet all oral health needs. Dr. Anugu is a strong believer in preventative dentistry, and works with her patients to educate them on the importance of consistent, effective oral care.

Dr. Anugu is a member of the American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society.
