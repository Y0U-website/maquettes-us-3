The practice

Wherever you are on that spectrum, your mouth plays a bigger role than you might think.

Your jaw and your posture. Your bite and your sleep. The materials in your mouth and your immune response. The makeup of your oral microbiome and that of your gut. All of it is connected.

Whether you’re working to heal or working to maintain the vitality you’ve cultivated, paying attention to these relationships can be nothing short of transformative.

Since the mid-1990s, our team at Marin Dental Wellness has partnered with patients who value a different kind of dentistry one that honors your body’s wisdom and works with its natural healing capacity. We combine advanced diagnostic technology with biocompatible materials, minimally invasive techniques, traditional healing wisdom, and a genuine curiosity about how everything in your body talks…

Located in Mill Valley and serving patients from across Marin County and the greater San Francisco Bay Area, our office is fluoride-free, mercury-free, and mercury-safe. Biocompatibility is a priority. We stress prevention and, when dental work needs to be done, we favor the least invasive options for meeting our patients’ needs effectively.

Above all, we get to know you as a unique individual and your personal needs, values, and goals. We encourage you to take an active, engaged role in optimizing your oral health to enhance your overall health and well-being.

We are honored by each and every patient who entrusts their dental care to us.

We incorporate advanced procedures such as the use of ozone and platelet rich fibrin in oral surgeries to promote rapid natural healing and prevent the formation of cavitations (jawbone osteonecrosis). Where cavitations have formed from earlier surgeries, infected root canal teeth, or other issues, we can remove the dead, infected tissue and provide therapies to spur better healing.
