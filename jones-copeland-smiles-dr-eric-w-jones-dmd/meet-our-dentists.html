Meet Dr. Eric Jones

Dr. Eric Jones earned his dental degree from the Medical University of South Carolina, after earning his bachelor’s degree from Clemson University. He maintains professional memberships in the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and Hinman Dental Society. Dr. Jones has also been awarded Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry, and Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

In addition to owning a private practice, Dr. Jones lectures (www.DrJonesSpeaks.com) on cosmetic dentistry nationally to both students and dentists. He is also published in the Journal of Dental Research and presented at the International Association of Dental Research.

Currently, Dr. Jones teaches other dentists systems and techniques for cosmetic dentistry, digital photography, and dental materials - serving as a clinical instructor for the Clinical Mastery Series (www.clinicalmastery.com). His well-rounded approach to education means he is constantly evaluating emerging dental methods and technologies to provide his patients with the best results in the least invasive manner with a high emphasis on comfort. This commitment ensures the highest level of care, both today and tomorrow.

Dr. Jones is an active participant in the community, serving on the leadership committee of the Georgia Missions of Mercy. He and his wife Margaret live in the area with their four children, Eloise, Teddy, Finn, and Georgia. In his spare time, he enjoys getting out on the lake and watching college football (Go Tigers!).

Meet Dr. Katherine Copeland

Dr. Katherine Copeland earned her dental degree from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry, after earning her bachelor’s degree in Biology from Furman University. She maintains professional memberships in the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Hinman Dental Society, and The Academy of General Dentistry. She is currently working towards achieving her Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Copeland also places a high value on giving back to the community. She is involved in various aspects of her local community and has served on mission trips both in and out of the country. She volunteers at Truth’s community clinic in Lawrenceville and for Community of Smiles, an event that provides free dental care to over 1,500 people in Gwinnett County each year. She also serves on the executive board and regularly volunteers at Community Dental, a clinic that provides free dental services to underprivileged residents of Northeast Georgia.

Dr. Copeland lives in the area with her husband, Taylor, their daughter, Charlotte and their goldendoodle, Derby. In her spare time, she enjoys cooking, reading, exercising, and weekends on Lake Rabun, as well as spending time with family and friends.

Meet Dr. Paul Hanna

Dr. Paul Hanna earned his dental degree from the Dental College of Georgia, and his bachelor in science from Kennesaw State University. He maintains professional memberships in the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, Academy of General Dentistry, and Hinman Dental Society. Dr. Hanna has also been awarded Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

Dr. Hanna has completed extensive training in many aspects of dentistry, and is currently concluding a 9-month comprehensive dental implant program through the Dental College of Georgia. This commitment to staying on top of the latest techniques in modern dentistry allows Dr. Hanna to better serve his patients and their oral health.

Dr. Hanna grew up in Gwinnett County, where he currently resides with his wife Nancy and newborn son Timothy. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his family outdoors, swimming, and watching the Atlanta Falcons.

Meet Dr. Caitlyn Long

Dr. Caitlyn Long earned her degree from the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University after receiving her bachelor’s degree in Chemistry with Biology and Mathematics minors from Georgia Southern University. Prior to private practice, Dr. Long was a Captain in the United States Air Force, serving four years.

She completed extensive training through a 1-year Advanced Education of General Dentistry program while in the Air Force. The training included advancements in prosthodontics (crowns and implant crowns), occlusion (your bite), sedation, crown lengthening, extractions (including wisdom teeth), and root canals. She has since attended many hours of CE to ensure that she provides the best quality care for all her patients while encouraging a comforting atmosphere.

Dr. Long recently moved to the area with her husband, one-year-old daughter, and many animals. She is excited to be closer to family and to be more established in her community. She is looking to volunteer at local clinics and animal shelters. In her spare time, she enjoys baking, reading, running, and visiting family as often as possible.
