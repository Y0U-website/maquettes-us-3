Dental Implants

Dental implants are a super way to replace missing teeth and also provide a solid solution to having removable partial or complete dentures. Implants provide excellent stability for these dental appliances.

Dental implants are artificial roots and teeth (usually titanium) that are surgically placed into the upper or lower jaw bone by a dentist or Periodontist a specialist of the gums and supporting bone. The teeth attached to implants are very natural looking and often enhance or restore a patient’s smile!

Dental implants are strong and durable and will last for many years. Occasionally, they will have to be re-tightened or replaced due to normal use.
