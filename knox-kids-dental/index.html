The practice

From compassionate care for anxious kids to cosmetic crown options that boost confidence, we use advanced techniques and a gentle approach to create a positive experience for children of all needs and backgrounds. White zirconia crowns that look and feel natural We proudly offer Sprig zirconia crowns that are strong, tooth-colored, and perfect for restoring your child’s smile with a natural appearance they’ll feel good about.

Less-invasive treatment options From silver diamine fluoride to nitrous sedation, we offer minimally invasive alternatives that reduce fear and create a better experience for kids; especially those with anxiety or special needs.

Compassionate care for every child We take extra time with kids who are fearful, neurodiverse, or have had a rough experience at other offices. Your child is never rushed and always welcomed with warmth and patience.

Family-centered, relationship-based approach We emphasize education and connection, helping parents understand treatment options and giving every child the confidence to succeed at the dentist.
