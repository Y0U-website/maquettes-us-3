Dr. Miralda Moreno

Dr. Miralda Moreno graduated from the University of Missouri at Kansas City School of Dentistry in 2000. Previously, she had graduated from the University of Missouri at Kansas City School of Dentistry receiving her BS in Dental Hygiene. A Wichita native, she returned to Wichita, Kansas to practice as a Dental Hygienist for 5 years . She also practiced in the Kansas City area while in dental school.

After graduating from Dental School in 2000, Dr. Moreno practiced dentistry in Arizona until 2004. Wishing to be closer to family, she returned to the Kansas City area in 2004 and established Moreno Family Dentistry. With 30 years of combined experience, Dr. Moreno has thoroughly enjoyed her career in dentistry.

She is a member of the ADA, KDA, and is the Secretary/Treasurer for the 1st District Dental Society.

Dr. Moreno enjoys volunteering her time to various local organizations. She enjoys swimming, yoga, gardening, and traveling. Her favorite of all is spending time with her 15 year old son and husband. The newest hobby includes bicycling with her family.
