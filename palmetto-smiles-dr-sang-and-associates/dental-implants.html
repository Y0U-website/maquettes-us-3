Dental Implants in Florence, SC

Dental implants provide superior long-term benefits when compared to traditional solutions for missing teeth. They can secure any type of dental restoration and are a viable solution for a single missing tooth or a full arch of missing teeth.

The dental implant process starts with a consultation at our office. While many patients are eligible for dental implants, there are some requirements. We’ll discuss your medical history and lifestyle habits and check if you have sufficient bone structure to properly support implants.

Moving forward, the implant process requires surgery to implant a titanium post into your jawbone. It requires 3-6 months for the post to fully integrate with the jawbone. This step is essential because it’s what gives implants the stability they’re known for. Once this heals, we attach a piece called an abutment. This serves as the connector between the implant post and the final restoration.

Dental implants can support various final restorations. Most commonly, we use a custom dental crown . We design it to match the shape and size of the rest of your teeth so that it blends in with your smile. If you get multiple dental implants, they can also support a bridge or denture.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants have many benefits compared to other tooth replacement options. First, they’re the only restorations that replace both the tooth root and the crown. This gives it stability that other solutions don’t have. You get back almost 100 % of your bite function and don’t have to worry about the restoration slipping or falling out.

The implant post stimulates jawbone regeneration. When you lose a tooth, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate. Since other solutions don’t replace the tooth root, the deterioration process continues. You have to readjust or replace the restorations so that they fit properly and you can continue to use them. This isn’t a problem with dental implants.

Implants look and feel the most like natural teeth. You don’t really need to adjust to eating and speaking with them. Since you can eat most foods, you don’t have to worry about changing your diet or not being able to break down things. There are no nutrition issues because you know you can consume a balanced diet.

Unlike dentures, there are no extra steps to caring for dental implants. You don’t need any special adhesives or cleaners to ensure things go smoothly. You simply brush and floss as you would normally. Implants are a durable, long-term solution that should last a lifetime.
