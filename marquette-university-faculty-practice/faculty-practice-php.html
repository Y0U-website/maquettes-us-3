Faculty Practice

Our Faculty Practice clinic offers dental care by licensed dental school faculty using state-of-the-art technology and modern practices.

Insurance and Payments

  • The Faculty Practice is in-network only with Delta Dental Premier . The Faculty Practice does not accept Medicare or Medicaid ( see predoctoral clinic insurance information for Medicaid patients ). For all other insurance, we are out-of-network, but will submit claims on your behalf as a courtesy.
  • Please be prepared to pay at the time of service, as coverage may vary by plan. Our goal is to help you maximize your dental benefits.
  • For any questions, contact us at 414-288-0788 .

call to schedule meet our faculty providers

  • Adults: Yes

What to expect

This one-stop private practice has all specialties represented by licensed dentists. As dental educators, our treatment is modern and high-quality. Our fees are similar to dental practices in the city.

Read more about the Faculty Practice in Marquette Today .

Dr. Ay Bike Eke

Dr. Ay Bike Eke received her D.D.S. degree at Istanbul University in Turkey. She worked as a general dentist for several years in a private dental clinic in Istanbul before moving to Milwaukee. She is a Clinical Assistant Professor at Marquette University’s School of Dentistry. She welcomes patients on Wednesday and Friday afternoons in the Faculty Practice.

Dr. Ahmed Elbaz

Dr. Jeffrey Janosik

Dr. Janosik received his Bachelor of Science degree from Marquette in 1974 and went on to graduate from Marquette with his D.D.S. in 1978. Dr. Janosik practiced in Racine for 40 years while also committing to the future of dentistry by teaching part-time. He did this for several years before joining Marquette as full-time faculty in 2019. Dr. Janosik’s interests place high importance on metal free restorations, sleep apnea and facial aesthetics. Dr. Janosik is honored to be at Marquette making a difference for students, patients and staff. He welcomes patients on Thursdays in Faculty Practice.
