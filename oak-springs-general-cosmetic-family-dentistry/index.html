What types of insurance do you accept? Dental Insurance has been a topic of debate in recent years and there always seems to be questioned as to what is covered and what is not! Whenever in question, please feel free to call us. In general, we accept all PPO insurances including companies like Anthem PPO, Beechstreet, CIGNA, Interplan Health Group, MultiPlan Network, PPO USA, Preferred Health…

What type of payment do you accept for those without insurance? As previously stated, dental insurance has been an item of debate in recent times, as many people with basic health insurance have reliance they have no dental coverage. For those without or in need of procedures not covered by insurance, we offer special discounts that can be charged to any major credit card.

Can I be sedated during treatment? Yes. We offer nitrous oxide sedation (also known as laughing gas), especially for patients with dental anxiety or during complex procedures. It’s a light, safe form of sedation that keeps you relaxed while remaining awake. 🔗 More on Sedation Dentistry
