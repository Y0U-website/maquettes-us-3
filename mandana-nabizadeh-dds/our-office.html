We were originally Ali Behzadi, D.M.D, General & Cosmetic Dentistry at 515 fl-436, Casselberry, fl, ​Now we are Family Dental home at 945 east state road 436 Casselberry, Fl 32707. So come and check out our new office and join our growing happy family.

We at Family Dental Home are dedicated to providing the most thorough and advanced dental care available in the most relaxing environment possible.

In addition to preventative dentistry examinations and cleanings, our team offers a wide array of cosmetic and restorative dentistry solutions. From smile makeover treatments such as porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, and cosmetic bonding, to smile restoration treatments such as root canals, dental crowns, and even dentures, our patients have the full range of dentistry treatments at their disposal.

Great oral health begins with regular dental examinations and dental cleanings. Family Dental Home team of dental assistants and hygienists provided personalized routine dental examinations. During their twice-yearly check-up, patients receive a thorough examination of their teeth, gums, tongue, jaw, and neck. If necessary, we may recommend X-rays, an oral cancer screening, or a periodontal (gum) inspection. After the comprehensive examination, one of our professional and knowledgeable dental hygienists will perform a thorough cleaning and polishing.

Smile, it’s a great thing to do! With the advanced techniques in cosmetic dentistry that are offered at Lifetime Dentistry, we are sure to give you something to smile about. Our team has helped many of our patients’ correct gaps, chips, stains and missing teeth with cosmetic procedures and treatments. Our dentists will be happy to outline your personalized cosmetic dentistry treatment plan and explain all your options. Dr. Ali Behzadi, DMD

Dr. Ali Behzadi received his Bachelor of Science degree from Rutgers University and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. (Rutgers now).

He then completed a one-year residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Lutheran Medical Center in Brooklyn, New York. After practicing dentistry for 10 years in New York he moved to Florida in 2001 and chose Orlando as his home.

Since then he is faithfully committed to providing the utmost care and comfort for his patients, whether they need a root canal or are coming in for their very first dental visit. Dr. Behzadi is on the cutting edge of the latest advancement in his field by maintaining membership with professional organizations such as the American Dental Association , Florida Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry , and SPEAR education club .

His state of the art practice is equipped with the latest technology, and he, along with a team of an oral surgeon, implantologist and pediatric dentist, has created one of the finest multi-specialty family practices in Central Florida. Dr. Behzadi welcomes you to his practice and looks forward to meeting your dental needs and making you smile with confidence.

Dr. Mandana Nabizadeh, DDS

Dr. Nabi graduated from Shiraz University School of Dentistry in 1994 with a Doctorate of Dental Medicine. After 4 years of experience as a general dentist in the public sector, returned to school to pursue a three-year specialty program in pediatric dentistry at Shiraz University in Iran.

She graduated with honors and received her certificate in pediatric dentistry and a postdoctoral Masters of Science. Her thesis was focused on the conservative treatment of fractured teeth and was accepted with honors.

After moving to the United States in 2002, she attended the University of Illinois at Chicago and earned her D.D.S. degree in general dentistry. Since then she has earned three certificates from the prestigious Mini residency programs at the University of Florida in Gainesville, the University of Indiana, and the University of Minnesota. She has also completed a 2-year course in the orthodontics field and familiarized herself with the detailed aspect of early orthodontic treatment.

Her many years of experience in the private, public health, and academic sectors make her an experienced and trustful dentist for your children. She believes in utilizing the latest technology and she has equipped her state-of-the-art practice with the finest and newest dental material and equipment.

Her office has been selected by one of the lead dental insurance companies as a center of excellence for providing outstanding service to the members.

Dr. Nabi is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the Florida Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is affiliated with Florida Hospital and utilizes hospital dentistry for needed cases. Working with children is her passion and she truly enjoys putting smiles on their faces. Dr. Nabi’s goal is to introduce a positive dental image early on in your child’s life with her excellent management techniques.

Dr. Nabi’s commitment to lifelong education means she is constantly evaluating emerging dental methods and technologies in order to provide her patients with the best results in the least invasive manner with a high emphasis on comfort. This commitment ensures the highest level of care, both today and tomorrow.

Dr. Nabi, her husband; Dr. Ali Behzadi, also a dentist practicing general & cosmetic dentistry, and their daughter, live in Orlando. When she is not working on little faces, Dr. Nabi enjoys swimming, interior design, traveling, and spending time with her family and friends. She is an active swimmer and has won several championships.
