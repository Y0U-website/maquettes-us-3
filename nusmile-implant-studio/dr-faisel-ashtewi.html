Dr. Faisel Ashtewi

Dr. Faisel Ashtewi earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Detroit Mercy. He went on to complete an advanced three-year residency in prosthodontics at the Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University, where he received a Certificate of Advanced Graduate Study (CAGS) in Prosthodontics along with a Master of Science degree in Biomaterials (MSD). Since 2013, Dr. Ashtewi has practiced as a specialist in prosthodontics, dedicating his career to the restoration and replacement of teeth with precision and artistry.

Committed to academic excellence and the advancement of his profession, Dr. Ashtewi has served as a clinical instructor at the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry. Prior to relocating to Chicago, he also held a clinical teaching appointment at the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry, where he mentored dental students for over two years. His involvement in dental education reflects his dedication to shaping the next generation of clinicians while remaining at the forefront of evolving techniques and technologies.

He maintains active membership in several distinguished professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, the American College of Prosthodontists, the American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics, the Chicago Dental Society, and the Academy of Osseointegration.

Outside of his professional life, Dr. Ashtewi enjoys an active and well-balanced lifestyle. He is passionate about fitness and the outdoors, with interests that include hiking, biking, and snowboarding. A dedicated traveler and culinary enthusiast, he enjoys exploring new cities, cultures, and cuisines around the world.

With a strong educational background and active involvement in professional organizations, Dr. Ashtewi stays updated with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques. This dedication reflects his passion for both his craft and the well-being of his patients, making him a trusted choice for those seeking specialized dental services.
