A dental home where your child can grow with confidence

At Little Fox Pediatric Dentistry, we don’t believe in rushing kids through treatment or overwhelming parents with confusing options. Instead, we create a calm, informed, and prevention-focused experience from the very first visit.

Experience gentle techniques, honest education, and access to minimally invasive care, sedation options, and everything in between! We’re here to meet your family exactly where you are. Our goal is to help kids build confidence and help parents feel like true partners in their child’s care.

Meet Dr. Kelsey Stehli

Before becoming a board-certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Kelsey Stehli learned about hard work, responsibility, and the importance of connection while working on her family’s blueberry farm in Northeast Ohio. Though farming didn’t end up being her calling, it taught her how to listen, care deeply for others, and nurture something with patience—skills she brings to every patient interaction today!

Dr. Kelsey earned both her undergraduate and dental degrees at The Ohio State University before completing her pediatric specialty training through Case Western Reserve University and Rainbow Babies & Children’s Hospital, where she also served as Chief Resident. She is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, a published researcher on pediatric dental anxiety, and an active member of AAPD, ADA, ODA, and local dental societies.

She’s passionate about prevention, early education, and behavior guidance—and she especially loves helping anxious kids find their confidence in the dental chair. As a mother of two, Dr. Kelsey believes in giving families clear, honest information about their options, without pressure or judgment. Whether your child is here for a routine exam, a restorative visit, or a procedure under general anesthesia, Dr. Kelsey’s calm, comforting presence helps everyone feel at ease.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kelsey enjoys traveling with her husband and their children, hiking, listening to live music, practicing yoga, and supporting her Cleveland-area community.

Start strong from the first smile

What to expect at your child’s first visit

Your child’s first visit is all about connection. We’ll begin with a warm welcome, a gentle exam, and a conversation about your goals, questions, and preferences. No pressure, no surprises—just thoughtful, age-appropriate care that sets your child up for a healthy smile.

Please bring any insurance information or medical history forms you’ve completed ahead of time.

Feel free to stay with your child in the treatment room—we want you both to feel at ease.

Let us know ahead of time if your child is especially anxious. We’ll take extra steps to prepare for a calming, supportive experience.

What makes us different?

Designed for comfort

Our office was created with real families in mind.

Family-friendly comfort

Our play area and Disney+ in treatment/quiet rooms help children relax—and parents, too.

Family rooms

Our open-concept hygiene area allows families with more than one kiddo to stay connected during cleanings without sacrificing privacy or comfort.

Thoughtfully inclusive

From our welcoming team to our on-site lactation room, we offer support for families at every stage of life.

Advanced care with a gentle touch

Technology that supports better outcomes (and calmer visits)

Nitrous sedation

Also known as laughing gas, nitrous is a safe and gentle way to help ease anxiety during dental visits—especially helpful for younger or nervous patients.

General anesthesia

For more complex cases, we partner with hospital-based anesthesiologists to provide safe and effective care in a single visit while your child is fully asleep.

Minimally invasive materials

From enamel-conserving fillings to alternatives options like silver diamine fluoride or hydroxyapatite, we use modern materials that support long-term oral health with less intervention.

Rooted in our region

Giving back to the community that raised us

Dr. Kelsey is proud to call the Cleveland area home—and she’s passionate about giving back to the place that shaped her. That’s why Little Fox Pediatric Dentistry plans to support local wildlife conservation organizations, with a focus on the penitentiary glen.

Through these efforts, we hope to foster a deeper connection between families, nature, and the wellbeing of the next generation.
