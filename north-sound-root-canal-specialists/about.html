0 How our practice is different North Sound Root Canal Specialists is designed all around the patient experience . Your experience. We focus on efficiency because that means the procedures take less time and feel seamless and relaxed to the patient.

At North Sound, our root canal is truly a single visit procedure. You will not need to come in for a consultation because we can assess your need over the phone and then schedule the root canal, if necessary. No extra driving, parking, wasted time, missing work or having to hire baby sitters.

We gather all information and answer all questions before you come for your procedure so you can feel relaxed and informed as you walk through the door. We follow-up to make sure you’re doing well and we’re available via cell phone whenever you need us.
