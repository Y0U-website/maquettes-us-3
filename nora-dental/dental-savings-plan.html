Frequently Asked Questions

Is NoraCare dental insurance? No. It is an in-house membership plan for patients without insurance. Because it isn’t insurance, there are no deductibles, waiting periods, or annual maximums.

Can I add my family to the plan? Yes. While individual membership is $199/year, you can add additional adults for $150/year and children for $99/year. Each member receives their own full set of annual benefits.

When does my coverage start? Your membership is active immediately upon enrollment and is valid for one full year (12 months).

How do I join? Simply call our office or contact us through the website to get started.
