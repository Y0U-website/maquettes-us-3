Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fixed denture?

In the dentistry business, we refer to fixed dentures as all-on-x implants , a full-arch tooth replacement that’s permanent. The all-on-x implant procedure involves securing a full set of stable, permanent replacement teeth on several strategically placed implants embedded in the jawbone.

The x in all-on-x implants simply refers to the number of implants used, generally four to six, which your dentist will determine based on your jaw condition and treatment plan.

The Benefits of All-on-X Implants

All-on-X implants give you the stability and freedom to eat what you want without worrying about the slippage or shifting that can happen with dentures. You’ll never wonder in the middle of enjoying an apple or during a belly laugh if your teeth are at risk of moving around.

These implants are made from high-quality, durable materials and crafted to look like your natural teeth. We take your facial structure into account when designing your implants. All-on-x implants also help your jaw maintain bone density and stimulate bone growth. Over time, your jawbone fuses with the implant, creating a strong base. This process is called osseointegration .

All-on-x implants represent a long-term investment in your oral health. When properly taken care of, they can last decades or even a lifetime.

What’s the difference between a single dental implant, All-on-X, and full-mouth reconstruction?

If you are considering tooth replacement, it is important to understand the difference between single-tooth implants and all-on-x treatment options. At Hite Family Dentistry, we will help you determine which option best fits your needs, including the number of missing or damaged teeth, your oral health, and your overall treatment goals.

What Is a Single Dental Implant?

A single dental implant is made to replace one missing tooth. An implant post is placed in your jawbone and topped with a custom dental crown. This option can provide you with a natural-looking, stable replacement while helping to preserve your surrounding jawbone. We recommend a single implant when only one tooth is missing, and you do not need a full-arch solution.

What Are All-on-X Implants?

All-on-x implants are a good option if you have lost all or most of your teeth in an arch. We typically place four to six implants to support a full set of replacement teeth. Unlike traditional removable dentures, All-on-x implants are secured to the implants, providing more stability for eating, speaking, and smiling. This option is different from a single dental implant because it supports a full arch of teeth.

What Is Full Mouth Reconstruction?

Full-mouth reconstruction (FMR) is a comprehensive, individualized treatment plan that can help address multiple dental concerns. Treatment may include dental implants, All-on-x implants, crowns, bridges, periodontal treatment, root canals, tooth extractions, and other restorative or cosmetic procedures. The goal is to restore the function and appearance of your entire smile with a personalized treatment plan. It differs from single-tooth implants and All-on-x implants because it can combine multiple treatments.

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)
