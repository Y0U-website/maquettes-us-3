Dental Services

Our Services

We complete every step carefully — complete dental care for adults and children, all under one roof.

Keep Your Smile Healthy

Prevention comes first at Mount Airy Dental Center. Regular visits let us catch small problems before they become big ones.

Exams & Cleanings

Routine checkups and professional cleanings (prophylaxis) to remove plaque and tartar and keep your teeth and gums in top condition.

Deep Cleaning

Scaling and root planing for patients with gum disease — a thorough cleaning below the gumline that helps stop periodontal problems from progressing.

Oral Cancer Screening

Oral health and head/neck cancer screenings as part of your regular visit. Early detection makes all the difference.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, patient care for our youngest patients — building healthy habits and positive dental experiences from the very first visit.

Emergency Dental Care

Toothache, chipped tooth, or dental injury? Call us at 301-829-2220 — we’ll get you seen and out of pain as quickly as possible. For life-threatening emergencies (difficulty breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, serious facial trauma), call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings that restore decayed or damaged teeth while blending naturally with your smile.

Rebuild & Replace with Confidence

If you are looking for the best long-term, healthy option for replacing missing teeth, look no further than implant-supported restorations.

Dental Implants

The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth — and implant-supported overdentures offer the best long-term, healthy option for replacing multiple missing teeth.

Dental Crowns

Custom-made caps that restore strength and appearance to teeth that are cracked, worn, or weakened after a root canal or large filling.

Dental Bridges

A fixed, natural-looking way to fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth, anchored securely to neighboring teeth or implants.

Dentures

Comfortable full and partial dentures, including implant-supported options that stay secure so you can eat and speak with confidence.

Love the Way You Smile

From subtle brightening to a complete smile refresh, our cosmetic treatments are tailored to you.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Personalized smile makeovers combining whitening, bonding, and restorative techniques to achieve the look you want.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening that safely lifts years of stains — dramatically brighter results than over-the-counter kits.

Invisalign

Straighten your teeth discreetly with clear, removable aligners — no brackets or wires, and fewer office visits.

Botox

Administered by trained clinical professionals, Botox can smooth fine lines and also help manage jaw tension and TMJ discomfort.

Dermal Fillers

Restore volume and soften facial lines with dermal fillers, delivered with the precision you’d expect from dental professionals who know facial anatomy.
