Dr. Weinberg’s Philosophy

Every patient deserves personalized attention and the highest quality care. I believe in taking the time to listen to each person’s concerns and goals, and working together to create a treatment plan that achieves beautiful, lasting results. Orthodontics is more than just straightening teeth—it’s about improving lives, one smile at a time.

Dr. Weinberg’s approach combines clinical excellence with genuine compassion. He understands that starting orthodontic treatment can feel overwhelming, and he’s committed to making every patient feel comfortable, informed, and confident throughout their journey.

His dedication to staying current with the latest techniques and technology ensures that patients benefit from the most advanced, efficient treatment options available, while his warm, friendly demeanor puts even the most anxious patients at ease.

Beyond the Office

When he’s not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Weinberg enjoys spending time with his family, including his daughter Dr. Erica Weinberg Gatta, who joined the practice to continue the family tradition of orthodontic excellence.

Dr. Weinberg is an avid supporter of local Bay Shore youth sports programs and has sponsored numerous Little League teams over the years. He believes in giving back to the community that has given him so much.

In his free time, he enjoys skiing, cooking , and reading. He’s also passionate about mentoring young orthodontists and regularly volunteers his time to teach at dental education seminars.
