0 Dental Implants Missing teeth can affect more than just your smile. They can impact chewing, comfort, confidence, and long term oral health. Dental implants are designed to replace missing teeth in a way that looks natural, feels stable, and functions like a real tooth.

At Olive Tree Dental Care, implant treatment is carefully planned using modern digital technology to improve precision, comfort, and long term success.

3D Planning and Guided Implant Placement

Every implant case begins with detailed planning.

Using 3D imaging and guided technology, we evaluate bone structure, anatomy, and implant positioning before treatment even begins. This allows for more accurate placement and a more predictable outcome.

Guided implant placement also helps improve efficiency and minimize unnecessary trauma to surrounding tissues.

Single Tooth Implants

A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without relying on neighboring teeth for support.

The implant acts as an artificial root, helping maintain bone support while restoring appearance and function.

Implant Bridges

When multiple teeth are missing, implant bridges can provide a stable and long lasting replacement option.

Unlike removable dentures, implant supported restorations are designed to stay securely in place and function more naturally.

Why Dental Implants Matter

Replacing missing teeth helps maintain balance throughout the mouth and may reduce shifting, bite changes, and bone loss over time.

Dental implants are one of the most predictable and durable tooth replacement options available in modern dentistry.
