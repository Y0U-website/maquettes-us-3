Dr. Chris Mohr

Dr. Mohr completed his undergraduate education in Lincoln, Nebraska, where he attended Union College. He graduated summa cum laude with his Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with an emphasis in Pre-Dental Studies in 2008.

After completing his undergraduate studies, he moved to Colorado to pursue his dream of becoming a dentist. Dr. Mohr attended the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine and graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2012.

Upon graduating, he began his practice of dentistry as an associate in the Metro Denver area before eventually establishing his own private practice. After practicing in Colorado for six years, he desired a change of pace and decided to move and settle in Pampa, TX. Dr. Mohr and his family are glad to call Pampa home and enjoy being part of the community.

Dr. Mohr is an experienced, dedicated, and caring dentist who makes it a priority to provide skilled dental treatment in a friendly manner. He always wants his patients to have a positive dental experience and put them at ease with the help of his compassionate and highly trained dental team at Pampa Dental Arts.

Dr. Mohr constantly improves the scope and level of dentistry he provides by pursuing high-caliber continuing education courses. His particular focus has been taking advanced training courses in endodontics, oral surgery, and dental implants.

Outside the office, Dr. Mohr enjoys spending time with his son Cameron and daughter Natalie. No matter the season, you will often find Dr. Mohr and his kids exploring the Rocky Mountains of Colorado and New Mexico. He enjoys pursuing activities such as snow skiing, riding side-by-sides, river rafting, and occasionally fly fishing with his brother. An avid NFL fan, he looks forward to watching and attending games each new season. Dr. Mohr has also been a member of the Pampa Lions Club since 2019.
