Our Doctors

Dr. Jason Clapp

Dr. Clapp is recognized by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry as a board-certified pediatric dentist. Dr. Clapp grew up in Iowa, where he received his Bachelor of Arts degree from Wartburg College in 1995, and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa in 1999. Following dental school, he completed a two-year residency in pediatric dentistry at Baylor College of Dentistry, Children’s Medical Center of Dallas and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital for Children.

Dr. Jessica Bell

Dr. Bell is recognized by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry as a board-certified pediatric dentist. Dr. Bell is a native Texan and received her Bachelor of Art degree from the University of Texas at Austin. She attended Baylor College of Dentistry and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2002. Following dental school, she completed a two-year residency in pediatric dentistry at Baylor College of Dentistry, Children’s Medical Center of Dallas and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital for Children.

Dr. Jennifer Lee

Dr. Jennifer Lee was raised in Plano, Texas. She is recognized by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry as a board-certified pediatric dentist. She is a graduate of Baylor University and the Baylor College of Dentistry. Dr. Lee completed a two-year residency in Pediatric Dentistry at Baylor College of Dentistry, Children’s Medical Center of Dallas and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital for Children.
