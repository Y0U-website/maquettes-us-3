The Process of Getting Dental Implants

What to expect from consultation to new tooth.

Why Choose Larrondo Family Dentistry Dedicated, family-centered dental care you can trust. Dr. Larrondo Dr. Larrondo believes every patient deserves personalized care, clear communication, and modern treatment. His approach focuses on comfort, trust, and long-term oral health for every family member.

  • Step one: planning and preparation Your dentist completes a full exam, digital x rays, and reviews your gums and bone. This planning stage ensures safe, personalized treatment and answers every patient question.
  • Step two: implant placement The implant is placed using local anesthesia and precise imaging, including CT scans and x rays. The titanium post bonds naturally with bone during healing.
  • Step three: restoring the tooth After healing, an abutment and crown are placed to restore function, comfort, and natural smiles that last.
