Do you offer virtual consultations or teledentistry?

Absolutely! We offer virtual consultations so you can discuss your oral health concerns, explore treatment options, and receive estimates—all from the comfort of your home.

Are clear trays as effective as braces?

Yes—clear aligners can be just as effective as braces for many mild to moderate cases. They move teeth gradually using custom-made trays, without the need for brackets or wires. However, severe or complex orthodontic issues may require traditional braces.

Can I whiten my teeth while using clear trays?

You can! In fact, many patients apply whitening gel inside the trays, making them double as whitening trays. We’ll guide you on safe whitening options during treatment.

What should I expect at my first visit? What is a records appointment?

At your first visit, we’ll talk about your smile goals and see if clear aligners are right for you. You can also expect upfront pricing so you know what to expect from the start. No pressure, just the info we need to design your best smile.

Am I a candidate for clear aligners?

Clear aligners are great for mild to moderate alignment issues. Severe cases may require traditional braces.

Do I need a consultation or x-rays before starting treatment?

Yes, a consultation and x-rays are important first steps. We need to evaluate your bite, gum health, and bone support to create a safe and effective plan for you.

How Long Do They Take to Align Teeth?

Most cases take about 9 months after the trays are made . Your timeline may vary depending on how complex your case is and how consistently you wear them.

How do I track progress—are photos or scans required?

We’ll schedule in-person or virtual check-ins to review progress. In some cases, you may be asked to send photos or scans to ensure your teeth are shifting as planned.

Why Not D.I.Y. Mail-Order?

With in-office care, you get a customized plan, real-time check-ins, and dental oversight to protect your gums and jaw. Mail-order options skip those important steps—and can lead to long-term issues.

What dental issues can clear trays fix?

Clear aligners can fix mild to moderate crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. If you’re not sure if they’re right for you, we can find out during your consultation.

Why get Clear Orthodontic Trays (“Aligners”)?

Clear orthodontic trays, or “aligners,” offer a comfortable and discreet way to straighten teeth. They’re removable for eating and brushing, and custom-made for a precise fit. Aligners allow you to improve your smile without metal brackets or wires, while making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene during treatment.

How do clear trays work?

Clear trays gradually shift your teeth into place by applying gentle pressure. You’ll wear each set of trays for about 1-2 weeks before switching to the next set in the series.

Will I Have to Change My Diet?

The simple answer is no. This is one of the best benefits of using clear aligners. Unlike conventional braces, you can take off the clear aligner tray whenever you feel like eating.

However, it is advisable to avoid foods that are dark-colored such as wine or coffee as they might stain them. Also, sticky foods that might get stuck between the tray and your teeth are not a good idea. Nonetheless, if you clean your teeth and aligners as directed, your diet should not change.

How often do I need checkups for clear trays?

Checkups are typically every 6-8 weeks to monitor your progress and provide the next sets of aligners.

What are Attachments?

Attachments are similar to handlebars on a bicycle: They help grab a hold of a tooth to more predictably rotate it into place.

What is IPR?

IPR (interproximal reduction) is a great and common way to close spaces filing a small amount (like filing your nails but even less than 1mm typically) without removing teeth to create space or leaving “black triangles” (see video) at the end of treatment by aligning teeth that are naturally shaped without much of a flat surface to keep calculus and plaque from forming between them. This is an added benefit from DIY aligners that our office offers in order to make sure your case tracks well and ends up with great results!
