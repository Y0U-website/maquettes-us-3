About Dental Implants

Natural-Looking, Long Lasting

What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a replacement for a missing tooth or teeth that look and function just like natural teeth. This method of tooth replacement maintains the surrounding jaw bone and helps prevent the appearance of premature aging. Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime and have become the gold standard of care for tooth replacement.

Missing teeth? You’re not alone

You’re not alone! Did you know that 69 % of adults between the ages of 35-44 have lost one or more of their permanent teeth? There are lots of reasons to correct this issue. It is important to note, a large space between your teeth can affect how you speak or eat. Even if it’s not visible, a missing molar can affect how you chew. This can also cause the remaining teeth to move or shift and, bone loss can occur around the missing tooth due to the exposure of the tissue.

Stabilize your denture with dental implants

Dental implants provide a permanent solution to individual missing teeth, perhaps caused by tooth extraction or decay. They can also be used to stabilize dentures instead of pastes and adhesives, giving you a stable bite while promoting bone retention. If you require tooth extraction or if you are already missing teeth, dental implants can improve your life. Do not settle for a temporary solution. Dental implants are the optimal choice

Dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dental bridges and dentures, tooth implants promote bone retention and they often last a lifetime without requiring replacement.

Toss your dentures!

Many patients dread having dentures because they slip and slide, making normal tasks such as talking and eating extremely difficult. Thankfully, modern dentistry has provided us with more options.

Dental implants function very much like natural teeth. Eating becomes easier and more comfortable, and implants promote better oral health. Many dental implant patients also report improved speech, since there is nothing in the mouth to cause the slurred words often experienced by wearers of bridges, partials, or dentures.
