Pediatric Dental Fillings

Space Maintenance

As your child grows, their baby teeth naturally fall out to make room for adult teeth. However, if a baby tooth is lost too early, it can cause nearby teeth to shift and may lead to alignment problems later on. A space maintainer helps by keeping the empty space open, allowing the adult tooth to come in properly and reducing the risk of future orthodontic issues. It’s custom-made for your child, comfortable to wear, and simply does its quiet job in the background while their smile continues to grow.

Pulpotomies

If your child is experiencing tooth pain or sensitivity, a pulpotomy at Little Tooth Crew Pediatric Dentistry in Franklin Square may ease their discomfort and help restore their smile. A pulpotomy is a common pediatric dental procedure that removes the damaged or infected part of a tooth’s nerve (or pulp) while preserving the healthy portion.

Pediatric Dental Crowns

Stainless steel crowns are a strong, protective “cap” for baby teeth that are damaged by large cavities, fractures, or after treatments like a pulpotomy. Baby teeth are smaller and softer than adult teeth, so when they have extensive decay, a regular filling might not be enough to keep them strong.

The crown completely covers the tooth, protecting it from further decay and helping it last until it’s naturally ready to come out. They’re durable, safe, and specially designed for children’s teeth to do their job quietly in the background.

In certain cases we may be able to offer a zirconia “white" crown depending on the needs - be sure to ask us for further information during your visit!

Pediatric Extractions

Sometimes, helping kids maintain their brightest smiles means removing a problematic tooth. Pediatric tooth extractions are a gentle, carefully planned procedure to remove teeth that might be causing discomfort, crowding, or not making way for healthy development.
