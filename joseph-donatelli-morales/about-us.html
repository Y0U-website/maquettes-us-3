What to Understand Before You Wait on Urgent Dental Care

Hospital emergency rooms charge significantly more for dental visits than dental offices, and they typically cannot provide the treatment needed to resolve the problem. Most ER dental visits end with a prescription for pain medication or antibiotics, which means patients still need a follow-up dental appointment to address the source. Treating a dental emergency directly through a dental practice reduces total cost by getting to the cause on the first visit. Jaw width can decrease 25 % in the first year after tooth loss

This clinical study, published in the International Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Implants, examines bone changes in the jaw following tooth extraction. It documents the rate at which the jaw ridge loses width in the first year after a tooth is removed and evaluates techniques for preserving bone volume.

What Full Arch Restoration Means for Your Safety

When natural teeth are lost and not replaced, the jawbone loses density over time because it no longer receives stimulation from tooth roots. This bone loss changes facial structure and makes future implant placement more complex. Implant-based restorations are the only tooth replacement option that stimulates the jawbone and helps preserve bone volume. Restoring full arch function also supports the ability to eat a varied diet, which has well-documented effects on nutrition and general health.
