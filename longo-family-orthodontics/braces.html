Indirect Bonding – Precision and Comfort

One of the innovative methods we use at Longo Family Orthodontics is indirect bonding . This advanced technique allows for more accurate bracket placement, which not only enhances the effectiveness of your treatment but also reduces the time you spend in the dental chair. Indirect bonding involves positioning the braces on a model of your teeth first, ensuring precise placement and a more comfortable fit when applied to your actual teeth. This method is particularly useful for achieving faster and more efficient results.

Benefits of Indirect Bonding

Fewer Visits : Since the brackets are positioned more precisely from the start, this leads to fewer adjustments and office visits. This means less time away from work or school for you or your child.

  • Less Time Needed for Bonding : With indirect bonding, the braces can be placed in just 20 to 30 minutes —about half the time of traditional bonding methods.
  • More Precision : The brackets are pre-positioned on a model of your teeth, which allows Dr. Longo to place each bracket exactly where it needs to be. This increases the overall effectiveness of the treatment.

Treatment Time

In traditional bonding, placing each bracket can take up to an hour. However, with indirect bonding at Longo Family Orthodontics , braces can be placed in as little as 20 to 30 minutes . This not only reduces the time spent in the dental chair but also ensures greater accuracy and comfort during treatment.
