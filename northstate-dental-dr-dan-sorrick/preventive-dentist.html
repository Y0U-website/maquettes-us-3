Dental Cleaning

Routine dental cleanings are an important part of maintaining optimal oral health. It is recommended to have your teeth cleaned twice a year. Plus, there’s nothing more refreshing than leaving the dentist with clean, pearly whites.

Northstate Dental uses cutting edge Cavitron technology, an ultrasonic scaler that is more effective and less invasive than manual scrapers. Ultrasonic scalers allow the hygienist to clean your teeth better, getting into harder to reach areas.

We’re committed to staying up to date with modern technology and bringing you the best dental experience possible. Don’t neglect your smile, schedule your dental cleaning today!

More Preventive Services

In addition to traditional preventive dental services, we also offer auxiliary treatments and accessories to elevate your oral hygiene maintenance. From fluoride and sealant to oral saliva testing and mouthguards, Northstate Dental has your preventive dentistry needs covered.

During your next visit to the dentist, ask about our preventive services and how they can help improve your oral health.

  • Fluoride Fluoride is an easy rinse treatment that you can work into your daily dental routine to boost the health of your teeth. Northstate Dental offers a variety of fluoride application options with different flavors, making this natural chemical that promotes tooth strength and helps prevent cavities a pleasant experience.
  • Sealant Sealants are a protective coating for the surface of teeth that help prevent cavities and decay and are generally applied to the pre-molars and molars. The CDC recommends sealants as a preventative measure of maintaining oral health and avoiding cavities and excessive wear.
  • Oral Saliva Testing Oral saliva testing is an incredibly easy way to maintain oral health and prevent periodontal diseases from developing into serious conditions. Plus, the tests are as easy as one, two, swish!
  • Mouthguards Mouthguards are perfect for people who grind their teeth, play aggressive sports, or need to maintain tooth alignment. Having a mouthguard professionally fitted by your dentist will ensure a proper fit, so you’re more likely to wear it and give it a chance to protect your smile.
