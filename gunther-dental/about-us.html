About Us

Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are kind, friendly, and caring. Our staff is here to ensure a pleasant experience during your visit to our office. Our goal is to communicate well with our patients and provide the best care possible.

Meet Our Dentist

DR. JOSHUA GUNTHER

Dr. Joshua Gunther prides himself on practicing outstanding dental care while providing excellent customer service. He attended the University of Pittsburgh and graduated in 1996 with a B.S. He then continued his education at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he graduated in 2002 with a Doctorate of Dental Surgery. Following his graduation, Dr. Gunther served on active duty in the United States Navy while completing additional training in an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program.

After completing three years of active duty, he returned to his hometown of Erie, PA, in 2005 and practiced in a group setting for 18 years. In 2024, he established his new spacious and state-of-the-art practice. Dr. Gunther has received advanced training in various areas, including General Dentistry, Endodontics, traditional braces and Invisalign, as well as IV sedation. He is a proud member of the American Dental Association and has earned the Fellowship Award in the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of his professional commitments, Dr. Gunther enjoys fishing, hiking, golfing, and spending quality time with his wife and three teenage children. He believes in building lasting relationships with his patients, ensuring they feel comfortable and cared for during every visit.

Meet Our Dentist

DR. EMILY JOHNSON

Dr. Emily Johnson is a dedicated dentist with over 10 years of experience in providing exceptional dental care. She graduated with honors from the University of California, San Francisco, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Dr. Johnson is passionate about patient education and strives to create a comfortable and welcoming environment for her patients.

Throughout her career, Dr. Johnson has focused on comprehensive family dentistry, including preventive care, cosmetic procedures, and restorative treatments. She stays current with the latest advancements in dentistry through continuous education and training.

Outside of the office, Dr. Johnson enjoys volunteering in her community, promoting oral health awareness, and participating in local health fairs. In her free time, she loves hiking, cooking, and spending time with her family. Dr. Johnson looks forward to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile!
