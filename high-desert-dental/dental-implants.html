Dental Implants in Kingman, AZ

Do you have a smile that you’re proud of? Or are you worried about the appearance and health of your mouth after losing one or more teeth? Perhaps you’re interested in returning your mouth to its normal function and appearance. Dental implants at High Desert Dental might just be the solution you’ve been searching for. They are one of the best ways to fix your smile after losing teeth to decay or injury.

While other treatments only focus on the crown of your tooth, implants are great because they will replace the root of your tooth as well. This helps stop your jawbone from possibly deteriorating.

Why Dental Implants in Kingman, AZ?

After losing one or more teeth, it’s still important that you continue to maintain good oral hygiene habits. This makes dental implants a great option for you because they function like normal teeth and will allow you to brush and floss daily, just like normal. You’ll be able to eat properly and speak clearly as well with dental implants.

Because they look like normal teeth, you don’t have to be concerned with people noticing that they’re not your natural teeth. They also are very durable. With the proper care – brushing, flossing, and keeping up with your dental appointments – there’s no reason why your dental implants shouldn’t last for the rest of your life.

Once your dentist at High Desert Dental has decided that implants are a good option for you, the procedure can begin. First, implants will be inserted into your jaw. This can take many visits, all depending on how many you need for your mouth. You’ll have to wait a few months after this while your jaw’s tissue and bone heals and creates a solid foundation.

Once you are healed, the next step can begin. This involves creating the restorations that will be fitted over your implants. This is typically done with crowns. Ceramic towns can be used for individual teeth, whereas an implanted-retained bridge is going to work great for covering multiple implants at once. The bridge can be an easier and more affordable option if you have multiple implants that need restorations.
