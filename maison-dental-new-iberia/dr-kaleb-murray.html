What procedures does Dr. Murray perform?

Dr. Murray provides comprehensive dental care including preventive cleanings, restorative treatments , veneers, cosmetic procedures, dental implants , full-arch reconstructions, Invisalign orthodontics, and IV sedation dentistry . His advanced training allows him to handle complex cases while maintaining a focus on conservative, patient-centered treatment planning prioritizing long-term oral health.

How does Dr. Murray make dental visits more comfortable?

Dr. Murray has transformed the patient experience through technology integration including overhead TVs, noise-canceling headphones, and light-blocking glasses during procedures. His practice utilizes AI-guided diagnostic tools and advanced imaging displayed on chairside monitors, allowing patients to see and understand their treatment needs. For anxious patients, he offers IV sedation options to ensure complete comfort.

What makes Dr. Murray’s approach different?

Dr. Murray focuses on collaborative care where patients are active partners in treatment decisions. Rather than simply telling patients what they need, he takes time to educate them about their oral health using visual aids and clear explanations. His practice emphasizes preventive care, conservative treatment options, and the connection between oral health and overall wellness.

Does Dr. Murray accept insurance?

Maison Dental Studio accepts several major insurance plans and works with patients to maximize their benefits. The practice also offers flexible financing options through Care Credit and Cherry, as well as in-house membership and payment plans. Dr. Murray believes quality dental care should be accessible, and his team will help you understand your insurance coverage and payment options.

How can I schedule an appointment with Dr. Murray?

Scheduling with Dr. Murray is simple and convenient. You can call our office directly, use our online booking system, or reach out through our website. New patients receive comprehensive consultations where Dr. Murray personally reviews your dental history, discusses your goals, and creates a personalized treatment plan fitting your needs and timeline.
