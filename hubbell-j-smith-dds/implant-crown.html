The Benefits of Implant Crowns

Natural-Looking Results Implant crowns are designed to match the shape and color of your natural teeth. This means they blend in seamlessly with your smile, giving you a confident, polished appearance that feels completely your own.

Long-Lasting Strength Crafted from strong, dental-grade ceramic materials, implant crowns are built to handle everyday chewing and speaking with ease. They stay securely in place and offer years of reliable performance when maintained with regular dental visits.

Better Oral Health Unlike traditional bridges, implant crowns don’t require reshaping healthy neighboring teeth. They help preserve bone structure and prevent shifting in nearby teeth, supporting a more stable and healthier smile overall.

What to Expect From Implant Crowns Custom Design and Consultation Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam and digital imaging at our Cincinnati practice. Dr. Hubbell J. Smith will evaluate your implant site, take impressions, and discuss your goals and preferences. This ensures your crown will be shaped, sized, and shaded to complement your unique smile. Every detail is considered so your final result looks and feels just right from the start.

Crown Fabrication and Placement

Once your custom crown is ready and your dental implant post has healed, we’ll schedule placement. The crown is securely attached to the implant using a small connector called an abutment. Dr. Smith will make sure the fit is perfect and your bite feels comfortable. The procedure is efficient and minimally invasive, and most patients return to their normal routine the same day with little to no downtime.

Follow-Up and Smile Maintenance

After your crown is placed, we’ll check in to ensure everything is healing properly and the crown is functioning as it should. With regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups, your implant crown can last for many years. Our team is always here to answer your questions and help you maintain a healthy, strong smile for life.
