General & Preventive Services

Endodontic Treatment

Commonly referred to as a root canal, this procedure saves a tooth by removing inflamed or infected pulp tissue.

Periodontal Treatment

A thorough deep-cleaning processes called scaling and root planing to address gum disease by removing plaque and tartar.

Dental Implants

Surgically placed to serve as artificial tooth roots for replacement teeth like crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Prosthetics

Dental appliances are custom-made, replacing or repairing missing or damaged teeth, restoring function and aesthetics.

Cosmetic Dentistry Invisalign After an initial consult and treatment plan, custom clear aligners are crafted to help correct teeth.

Extractions

Teeth that are severely decayed, infected, broken, or overcrowded are removed.

Teeth Whitening

A strong hydrogen peroxide gel is applied and activated by a special light to safely and quickly whiten teeth.

Cosmetic Bonding

A tooth-colored composite resin is used to repair chips, cracks, and gaps, and to improve a tooth’s shape, color, or length.
