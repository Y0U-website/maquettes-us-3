Friendly Team cup of coffee relaxing office headphones cozy blankets Welcome to our Practice Welcome to Lakeland Family Dental, the Oconomowoc dental office of Drs. Eric and Samantha Ruiz!

Our practice has served families from all over Lake Country and beyond , and we are proud to do so while providing outstanding, gentle, compassionate, family-centered care!

When you’re visiting our practice, you are our main focus! As a husband-and-wife team , Drs. Eric and Sam understand and respect how important it is to treat people like a part of the family, and you can expect this kind of attention and care at each and every appointment.

We offer our care to the entire family. From toddlers to great-grandparents, we provide personalized care that’s customized to meet your needs, regardless of what stage of life you are in!

We know how much simpler life is when you don’t have to hunt all over town to get the care your family needs. Along with providing treatment to all ages, we provide a complete selection of services , from the simplest preventive treatments to advanced and complex care such as orthodontics with Invisalign or tooth replacement with dental implants.

At Lakeland Family Dental, we follow a conservative treatment philosophy. This means that we won’t try to sell you on services that you don’t need. One of our goals is to preserve your good health and help your teeth last you a lifetime. We know that with regular preventive visits, you can avoid many common problems, and just like you, we’d much rather avoid a problem in the first place!

But when you do need treatment, don’t hesitate to give us a call. From fillings to crowns to emergency root canals and extractions, we are here to help you when you need us!
