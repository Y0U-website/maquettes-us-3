Meet the Optimum Team

Dr. Jessica Le received her dental doctorate training from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco and loves helping our patients care for their oral health. Outside of the office, her hobbies include reading, playing guitar, and hiking with her husband, daughter, and twin sons.

Cyndi has worked in the dental field for more than half her life and helps provide our patients high-quality care and excellent customer service. She knows teeth from the inside (endodontics) out (periodontics), and believes “a smile is happiness you’ll find right under your nose.”

Sonia helps patients experience a pleasant visit to the dentist with a friendly greeting and assistance with any pre and post-appointment questions. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with family and friends, reading, hiking, traveling, plus photography and needlecrafts.

Addalem helps our visitors at Optimum Dental Care when they come into the office. From time to time, you can find her helping out in the sterilization room or taking x-rays as well. In her free time, she loves spending time with family and friends, watching home makeovers and historical movies, and is a Sunday school teacher at church.

Ha is a registered dental assistant and loves seeing our patients progress through treatment and improve their confidence. She loves working alongside our hardworking team and dentist in Milpitas and likes to challenge herself with new, complex treatment techniques.

Yen has been practicing dental hygiene for over 15 years and loves seeing patients reach their oral health and hygiene goals (especially when they finally enjoy flossing). In her free time, she stays busy with her family and likes to hike and run with friends.

Dentistry in Comfort

Are you nervous about your visit? Find it difficult to relax at the dentist? We’re here to change all that.

Going to the dentist after many years or overcoming dental anxiety is a huge achievement, and we’re cheering you on! We invite you to meet our Milpitas dentist in a judgment-free and relaxing environment as we get to know you and help you understand your best treatment options.

Experience more convenience and comfort with:

  • Advanced sanitization / air filtration
  • Online patient registration forms
  • Comfy chairs with TVs
  • Insurance and financing options
  • In-house membership for uninsured patients
  • Multilingual dental care (Mandarin, Cantonese, Vietnamese, Spanish)
