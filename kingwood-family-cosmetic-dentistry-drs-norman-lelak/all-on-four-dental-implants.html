All on Four Dental Implants…

If you currently have dentures and are unsatisfied with the way they feel and the maintenance required, and/or if you are missing most or all your teeth, All-on-4 dental implants may be the best treatment option to provide you with a long-lasting solution that will significantly improve your overall quality of life.

  • More comfortable and less maintenance required compared to dentures
  • Keeps adjacent teeth healthy
  • Reduces additional bone loss from occurring
  • Implant can be placed even if bone loss has already occurred
  • Implant can be placed in one visit, which significantly reduces the treatment time involved
  • Eliminates the need for dentures
  • Faster recovery time than traditional implants
  • Easy to maintain
  • Returns your ability to enjoy your favorite foods
  • Enhanced self-confidence and a younger appearance
  • Evenly distributes bite forces
  • Long-lasting results
