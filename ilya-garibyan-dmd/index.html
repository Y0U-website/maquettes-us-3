Tooth loss not only compromises your smile, but also affects your ability to bite and chew as normal. Dental implants represent the gold standard for tooth replacement, offering you a chance at complete aesthetic and functional restoration.

Our practice uses real-time 3D navigation to ensure clinical precision, abbreviating your implant placement time and minimizing the risk of complication. Once fully healed, your implants will look and behave like natural teeth and allow you to enjoy your favorite foods.

Most people get their wisdom teeth during their late teens or early 20s. Because the jaw is too small to accommodate these teeth, they become impacted, leading to problems such as chronic discomfort, cyst formation, and damage to your other teeth.

The best way to avoid these long-term complications is to have your wisdom teeth proactively removed under IV sedation. Our surgeons have considerable experience with this safe, common procedure, and we are known for always emphasizing the comfort and well-being of each patient.

If you’re missing multiple teeth, or need to have multiple teeth extracted, then you may be a good candidate for All-on-4® dental implants. This advanced treatment concept allows us to replace entire rows or arches of teeth using a small number of strategically positioned implants.

The All-on-4® treatment concept is popular not only because it provides a second chance for patients faced with significant tooth loss, but because advanced surgical methods help to abbreviate treatment and recovery times. With All-on-4® dental implants, we can provide total smile rehabilitation.

Your dentist’s primary goal is to preserve your natural teeth for a lifetime. In some cases, this goal may not be achievable, as a tooth can become so damaged or diseased that it is no longer viable.
