Dental Implants

Are missing teeth causing you embarrassment and discomfort? If so, we can help! Dental implants are a state-of-the-art replacement tooth that looks great and feels natural. Whether you’re missing just one or multiple teeth, a dental implant can provide a secure, stable, and permanent tooth replacement solution.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is composed of a small titanium post that is surgically embedded in your jaw and acts as a replacement root. Titanium is a safe, biocompatible material, which means that it is easily accepted by the body.

Over time, the implant actually bonds with surrounding bone, providing a secure base for the dental restoration we place over it. The finished result is a beautiful replacement tooth that restores your appearance and allows you to speak and eat with ease.

Dental implants replace the appearance of a missing tooth, but they also help to maintain your ideal dental health. Implants prevent your natural teeth from moving or becoming unstable. And since the titanium post is embedded into the jawbone, dental implants prevent jawbone deterioration, a common result of tooth loss.
