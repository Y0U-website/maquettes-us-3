Meet The Doctors

Dr. Peter Sawka

Dr. Sawka opened Hopmeadow dental in 1985, promising to positively affect people lives by solving their dental issues and relieving them from dental pain. Because of his expertise, his patients enjoy food more comfortably and navigate social interactions with confidence. With over 30 years of experience in cosmetic dentistry, and more than a decade in dental implantology, Dr. Sawka continues to meet his guarantee of aesthetic and reconstructive success for his patients.

In his spare time, you can find him out on the golf course or spending time with his kids down by the shore.

Dr. Dikshant Kathuria (Dr.K)

Dr. Dikshant Kathuria has been crafting beautiful smiles since 2015. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from New York University, finishing in the top tier of his class. He is improving his skills by attending innovative educational seminars and progressive personal development courses. He has performed wisdom teeth extractions for several years, and is certified for Invisalign. In addition, Dr. Kathuria is a strong advocate of patient care; he takes his time to thoroughly explain each and every treatment recommendation, and answer any questions his patients have.

In his free time, he loves spending time with his family and friends, and is almost always caught up with all the latest shows on Netflix. He loves playing board games, going bowling and shooting some pool.
