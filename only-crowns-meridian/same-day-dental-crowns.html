Choosing same-day crowns at Only Crowns offers numerous benefits, including:

What is CEREC Technology? CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics) is a state-of-the-art system that allows us to create high-quality dental restorations in one visit. With CEREC, we use digital imaging to capture a precise 3D model of your tooth. This model is then used to design and mill your custom zirconia crown on-site. The result is a perfectly fitted, durable crown that can be placed immediately.

  • Time-Saving: Complete the entire process in one visit.
  • Comfort and Convenience: No need for temporary crowns or multiple appointments.
  • Precision Fit: Advanced CEREC technology ensures a perfect fit for your crown.
  • Immediate Results: Walk out with a restored smile on the same day.
