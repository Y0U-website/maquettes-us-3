Welcome to Our Practice

A Commitment to Comfort and Care

As a patient at Horizon Dental Associates, our goal is to deliver compassionate oral healthcare to you; whether you are visiting us for a routine dental exam or undergoing a more intricate dental implant procedure, our focus is making sure you leave our dental office with a smile on your face, knowing that you have received the best possible treatment in a phenomenal environment.

Whether it s a warm blanket to keep you comfortable before your dental exam, a reassuring hand on your shoulder during your dental treatment, or the home care bag containing soup, tea bags, ice packs, and gauze that we send home with you after a root canal or other procedure, you will know that we genuinely care for you and that your oral health needs have been met.
