Meet our doctors

Two pediatric dental specialists, one shared goal: children who grow up comfortable in the dental chair and proud of their smiles.

Dr. Rayes at the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD).

Steve K. Rayes, DDS, MS

Dr. Rayes is a board certified pediatric dentist, the owner of Just Kids Pediatric Dentistry, and an Instructor of Surgery at the Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth. Alongside full-time private practice, he teaches pediatric medicine and nursing students and provides operating room care at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center. He is a Past President of the Vermont State Dental Society and represented Vermont as a Trustee and Delegate to the American Dental Association.

He provides the full range of pediatric dental care — prevention, restorative treatment, sedation, and hospital dentistry under general anesthesia — with deep experience caring for children with special healthcare needs and complex medical histories.

In 2015, he and his family moved east to purchase a private practice in Vermont, where he has cared for Upper Valley children ever since.

Dr. Rayes is the proud father of three. He has been a private pilot for more than 40 years and speaks 3.5 languages — English and French fluently, Arabic and Greek conversationally. When he’s not in the office, you’ll find him flying, tending his small farm and beehives, or out fishing, hiking, and camping with his kids.

Rachel Davis, DDS Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Davis graduated with her dental degree from The Ohio State University, where she received the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry Achievement Award. She then completed two additional years of training in the Advanced Education in Pediatric Dentistry Program at the Alaska Native Medical Center in Anchorage, Alaska.

After her residency training, Dr. Davis practiced in Bangor, Maine, and served as faculty for the pediatric dental residents at Penobscot Community Dental Center. She then practiced at several locations in New Hampshire before joining Just Kids Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Davis is Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, and has served as Secretary/Treasurer and President of the New Hampshire Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

In her free time, Dr. Davis loves to bake and spend time outdoors — hiking, snowshoeing, and exploring New England with her family.
