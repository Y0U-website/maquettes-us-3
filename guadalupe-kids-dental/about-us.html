Why Families Choose Guadalupe Kids Dental

The combination of Dr. Bennion’s clinical credentials, his personal experience as a parent of a child with special needs, and our team’s genuine dedication to every child who walks through our doors is what sets us apart. We take the time to build trust with both children and their caregivers, because we know that a positive dental experience early in life sets the foundation for a lifetime of healthy habits.
