Traditional braces are a time-tested method for achieving beautifully aligned teeth. Made from high-quality stainless steel, traditional braces utilize metal brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their proper positions. Modern advancements have made traditional braces more comfortable, efficient and sleek.

Ceramic braces are excellent for patients seeking a more discreet orthodontic option. These braces feature clear or tooth-colored brackets that blend seamlessly with your natural tooth color, offering a subtle alternative to traditional metal braces. Ceramic braces are as effective as their metal counterparts.

Invisalign blends effective alignment paired with the convenience of removable aligners, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain optimal oral hygiene. With no food restrictions and easy removal for brushing and flossing, Invisalign offers a comfortable and discreet way to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. Say goodbye to metal braces and hello to the clear choice for a confident smile transformation.

Retainers

Retainers are a crucial part of your orthodontic treatment, helping maintain the alignment of your teeth after braces or aligners. At Lenhart Orthodontics, we provide customized retainers to fit your unique smile. By wearing your retainer as directed, you can ensure that your teeth remain in optimal positions, preserving the results of your orthodontic treatment.

Surgical Orthodontics

Surgical orthodontics is a specialized treatment approach for correcting severe jaw abnormalities that cannot be fully addressed with braces alone. This involves collaboration between an orthodontist and an oral surgeon. At Lenhart Orthodontics, our experienced team provides personalized surgical orthodontic solutions that improve your smile’s appearance and function.

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable traditional impressions with iTero digital scanning technology. Our state-of-the-art iTero scanners create highly accurate 3D images of your teeth, ensuring precise treatment planning and a more comfortable experience for our patients.

CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) technology provides detailed 3D images of your teeth, jaws, and surrounding structures. This advanced imaging allows our team to diagnose and plan treatment with unparalleled precision, resulting in more effective and customized orthodontic care.

Our in-house 3D printing technology can create custom orthodontic appliances and models with unmatched accuracy and efficiency. From clear aligner trays to retainers, our 3D printing capabilities enable us to deliver personalized treatment solutions.

Appliances

Elastics

Elastics, or rubber bands, are used in orthodontic treatment to apply gentle pressure and facilitate specific tooth movements. Our team will provide personalized instructions on how to wear and change your elastics for optimal results.

Forsus Appliance

The Forsus appliance is a fixed orthodontic device used to correct excessive overjet and other bite issues. By applying continuous force, the Forsus helps to align the teeth and improve the overall function of the bite, resulting in a more harmonious smile.

Carriere Appliance

The Carriere appliance, or American Orthodontics PowerBar, helps to correct misaligned jaws and improves bite function. These appliances help to achieve proper tooth alignment and create a balanced facial profile.

Palatal Expander

Palatal expanders widen the upper jaw, creating space for crowded teeth and correcting crossbites. This orthodontic appliance achieves orthopedic change by gently separating the bones of the palate, allowing for proper tooth alignment and improved bite function.

Retainers

Retainers, including lower fixed retainers, are essential for preserving the alignment achieved through orthodontic treatment by preventing teeth from reverting to their initial positions. Our tailor-made retainers are designed for a comfortable and secure fit, promoting lasting stability for your beautiful smile.

Separators/Spacers

Separators, also known as spacers, are small elastic rings that create space between teeth for orthodontic bands and are removed before placement. Avoid sticky foods and certain dental tools while the spacers are in your mouth.

MARA

Both the MARA (Mandibular Anterior Repositioning Appliance) and Herbst appliances are functional orthodontic treatment options used to correct overjet and other bite misalignments. By guiding the growth of the lower jaw, these appliances help achieve a more balanced bite and facial profile.

SISU Mouthguards

SISU mouthguards offer superior protection during orthodontic treatment, providing a comfortable and secure fit for athletes of all ages. These lightweight and durable mouthguards withstand impact and minimize the risk of dental injuries during sports activities.
