A full upper or lower set of teeth can be replaced with a fixed bridge supported by implants. This restoration is permanently secured in your mouth and cannot be removed by the patient, offering a stable and natural-feeling solution.

An implant can lose attachment to the bone, even after it has successfully fused. There are two ways your implant could lose attachment: Poor oral hygiene, it’s especially important to brush and floss, and schedule regular dental cleanings.

For patients with moderate alignment issues, Invisalign may be your best bet for a quicker, straighter smile. For patients with more severe cases, including over and under bites, crooked teeth, or overcrowding, traditional braces are for you.
