Total-Health Approach

Did you know that your mouth can positively or negatively affect the rest of your body? This total-health approach is at the core of everything we do. We look at the big picture when diagnosis and providing treatments, focusing on how the oral cavity is affecting the entire body and visa versa. Our job does not stop at the teeth and gums. We are here to ensure you also achieve a healthier life.

Lasting Relationships

Our patients feel like family. We relish the personal relationships that we have built with our patients over time, and we look forward to getting to know you too. We care about our patients on an individual level and take the time to get to know them personally. This not only helps us deliver better care, but helps you have a more enjoyable, personalized experience. Your needs are unique, and we are here to meet them.

Advanced Technology

At Matlock Dental Care, we employ state-of-the-art technology that definitively and significantly enhances the patient experience. Our technologies — including CEREC CAD/CAM technology, a Solea CO2 laser, Nomad X-ray units with Schick sensors, and Sirona Digital imaging — mean better accuracy, improved comfort, faster treatments and better results for our patients. Additionally, we have been a paperless office since 2002.

Knowledgeable Team

Our exceptionally skilled team bring years of knowledge, experience and cutting-edge treatment to our patients. We are passionate about what we do and this passion is what drives us to work to the best of our abilities for you and your family. We work at a high standard of quality and integrity. We deliver advanced treatments and exceptional results that we can proudly stand behind.

Comfortable Environment

Everything we do is meant to put our patients at ease. From the coffee bar in the reception area to our free guest WiFi, we are focused on the needs of each specific patient no matter the level of care they need. Our front office team is always alert and will remain sensitive to your individual needs. You will receive a kind greeting and genuine concern throughout your visit.

Continual Improvement

We deliver exceptionally skilled dentistry, but it doesn’t stop there. The dental industry is constantly changing, and we are changing with it. Dr. Matlock is passionate about continuing education and learning. He thrives on learning new treatments, new techniques and new technology and is constantly challenging himself to improve his skills and knowledge, so he can better serve his patients. You will be in expert hands here.

If you value these qualities in a dentist, call us today! We’re passionate about your smile and passionate about helping you achieve optimal health!
