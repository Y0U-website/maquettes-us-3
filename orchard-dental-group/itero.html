iTero™ Digital Scanning System

Advanced Technology for More Precision and Comfort

At Orchard Dental Group, goopy molds are a thing of the past. So whether you’re being fitted for Invisalign clear aligners or receiving other orthodontic procedures, we’ll use iTero digital scanners for a highly accurate visual of your mouth.

The iTero scanner is a handheld device that your dentist will use to scan your mouth using advanced optical and laser technology. Within a couple minutes, it captures a precise, 3D model of your teeth and gums, so we can fully understand your needs and even show you what your smile could look like after treatment.
