Orthodontic Appliances Q & A

What are orthodontic appliances?

Mandana Mozayeni Orthodontics uses orthodontic appliances, which are specialized dental devices, to correct alignment and bite issues. Orthodontic appliances apply gentle, controlled pressure to move teeth, guide jaw growth, or improve how your upper and lower teeth fit together.

Some appliances are fixed (attached to the teeth throughout treatment), while others are removable so you can take them out for meals and teeth-brushing.

Who can benefit from orthodontic appliances?

Orthodontic appliances can benefit patients from childhood to adulthood. They can guide jaw development in children while their bones are still growing, making it easier to correct bite issues and create enough space for permanent teeth.

Teenagers are among the most common orthodontic patients, as this is often the ideal time to straighten teeth and address alignment concerns. And many adults choose treatment to improve their smile, correct bite problems, or prevent long-term dental issues like uneven tooth wear.

If you experience crowding, spacing, bite problems, or jaw discomfort, Dr. Mozayeni may recommend a customized orthodontic appliance.

What types of orthodontic appliances are there?

Mandana Mozayeni Orthodontics uses many types of appliances to address a wide range of needs. These include:

  • Braces: Traditional braces are highly effective at addressing bite problems, using brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their correct positions.
  • Clear aligners: Clear aligners, such as Invisalign , are removable trays that gently shift teeth over time. They’re ideal if you want a more discreet orthodontic treatment option.
  • Rapid palatal expander (RPE): A rapid palatal expander is often used for younger patients to widen the upper jaw. This appliance helps create space for crowded teeth and improves how the upper and lower teeth fit together.
  • Overbite correction appliances: Dr. Mozayeni uses specialized appliances to help reposition the jaw and reduce excessive overbite.
  • Retainers: Retainers keep your teeth in place once they’re in the right position.

What’s the general process when getting an orthodontic appliance?

You’ll start with an initial visit, such as a braces or Invisalign consultation, where Dr. Mozayeni evaluates your mouth using an exam and 3D scanning. If you’re a good candidate for an orthodontic appliance, she will customize one for you and fit it to your teeth.

You’ll attend regular follow-up appointments so Dr. Mozayeni can monitor your progress, answer questions, and adjust your plan as needed.
