Patient Resources

Our community has been through a lot over the last year and we want to thank you for your continued support and understanding. While many things have changed, one thing has remained the same: our commitment to your health and safety.

Our infection control processes are made so that when you receive care, it is both safe and comfortable. To keep patients and our team members safe, we follow infection control recommendations made by the American Dental Association (ADA), the US. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA).

We ask for your patience and understanding as we will do our best to accommodate your busy schedule. Thank you for being our patient. We value your trust and loyalty and look forward to welcoming back our patients, neighbors and friends.

Financial Options

We will gladly assist you with any questions you may have regarding dental insurance plans we accept as well as explain any billing or dental financing options we have available prior to your dental treatment.

We will help determine the approximate coverage provided by your dental insurance carrier, however, please keep in mind that we can only provide an estimate for dental treatments. We cannot guarantee the amount any insurance company will authorize.
