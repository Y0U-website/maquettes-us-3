We Do Digital

The development and integration of digital technology have entered into the field of orthodontics. Digital orthodontics simplifies workflow by reducing your chair-time and improves patient satisfaction. With 3D imaging, doctors can scan their patients’ mouths with an intraoral scanner. In turn, reducing the need for traditional impressions. With this scan, we can print a digital model that we can make practically any appliance off of.

Removable Appliance: These are orthodontic devices that are made from acrylic and wires that use clasps on the teeth. The devices can be removed from the mouth by the patient. I.T.D.L makes over 100+ removable appliances.

Fixed Appliance: These are orthodontic devices where brackets, wires, and screws are fixed to the teeth and the archwires or screws move the teeth. I.T.D.L also makes over 100+ fixed appliances.
