Benefits of Dental Implants: Prevents neighboring teeth from shifting due to changed biting forces
  • Avoids extracting opposing teeth that might extrude from their sockets
  • Maintains proper chewing function and reduces jaw joint damage
  • Easier tooth cleaning and reduced plaque buildup
  • Implant crowns are practically indistinguishable from natural teeth
  • Secure fit with no noticeable difference when chewing and speaking
