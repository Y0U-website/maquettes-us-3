Insurance, Billing and Payment Options

Here at Oak Hill Dental we understand that patients who have dental insurance benefits want the ability to utilize them. We recognize this want, which is why years ago we teamed up with insurance companies and began accepting the PPO plans offered within the various dental insurance companies listed below. We also, as a courtesy, offer a discount to cash payments helping make dental care more affordable. Please feel free to contact our office with any questions and we will gladly help in any way we can. As always, we accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards, as well as HSA/FSA cards.
