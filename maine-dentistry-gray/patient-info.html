Patient Info

  • Where is Maine Dentistry? We have three office locations in Auburn, Gray, and Portland. Scroll down to the footer of this page to find the address and contact information for each office.
  • Does Maine Dentistry accept insurance? We are currently in-network with Delta Dental and are happy to coordinate and request payment from any insurance provider you may have.
  • How often should I schedule appointments? Most dentists recommend a checkup every six months. Regular exams help detect dental issues early and can reveal broader health concerns. Catching problems early allows for easier, more effective treatment and prevention.
  • What is a comprehensive exam? A comprehensive dental exam ensures that your dentist can evaluate overall your health and oral hygiene as well as determine and discuss with you any factors that might contribute to the wellness of your teeth, jaw, gums, bones, and more. Oral health offers your dentist insight into the overall health of your body, so dental check-ups are a must for complete full-body health.
  • I need to cancel my appointment. No problem! Patients are allowed to cancel or reschedule anytime before 24 hours of the appointment. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours’ notice will be charged a $90 fee. For more details about our cancellation policy please watch this short video.
  • Are there any exceptions to the cancellation fee? Unfortunately, no. To keep our schedule running smoothly and care accessible for all patients, we cannot make exceptions to the cancellation policy.
  • Why do you need my credit card information before my appointment? Keeping a card on file allows us to reserve your appointment time and ensure fairness for all patients. Your card will only be charged if an appointment is missed or canceled outside our policy.
