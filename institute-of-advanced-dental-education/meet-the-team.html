Dr. Ross Nash

Dr. Nash of The Nash Institute is an iconic authority in cosmetic and esthetic dentistry and has helped pioneer many of the materials and modalities commonly used today. His numerous lectures to dental professionals around the world have solidified his reputation as an entertaining educator. His thriving dental practice in Charlotte, North Carolina provides Dr. Nash with excellent “real-world” information and everyday stories to share with his students.

Melanie Jost

Melanie Jost, RDH, is a clinical dental hygienist with a passion for preventive care and patient education. With ten years of clinical experience, she is committed to advancing oral health through evidence-based practice and lifelong learning. Her professional interests include preventive dentistry, clinical education, and staying current with emerging research. In her free time, she loves running and spending time with her two little girls. Her motto in life and in hygiene is the cure for anything is a little saltwater!

Kelly Fox-Doctors

With more than 20 years of clinical experience, Kelly has always been passionate about expanding her reach in dental hygiene beyond the operatory. Now stepping into a role as a continuing education speaker, she is dedicated to empowering hygienists through informative live lectures and hands-on workshops that emphasize practical, evidence-based techniques and modern technology—elevating patient care, strengthening clinical confidence, and inspiring growth within the profession.
