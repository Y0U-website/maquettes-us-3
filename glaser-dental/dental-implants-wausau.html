Consult With Dr. Glaser Step 2

If you are a candidate for dental implants the second step is to meet with Dr. Glaser for a consultation. Remember, dental implants are a major step in your tooth health and there are many factors to determine if they are your best option. Certain factors like the health of your jaw bone and prior dental procedures can impact the effectiveness of the implant procedure. Dr. Glaser will take the time to explain the process as well as all your treatment options following his WIDIOM philosophy of treatment.

If both you and Dr. Glaser agree dental implants are the best fit for your smile and health goals an examination will be scheduled. Dr. Glaser will map out a treatment plan based on industry leading technology like our 3D scanning machine as well as traditional x-rays, and a physical exam of your mouth.

Costs and appointments can be determined after Dr. Glaser has a treatment plan in place.
