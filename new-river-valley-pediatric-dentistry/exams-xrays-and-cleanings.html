Kid-Friendly Teeth Cleanings

Even young children can benefit from regular professional cleanings to prevent plaque buildup, gum disease, and cavities. Our gentle teeth cleanings at New River Valley Pediatric Dentistry are tailored to the specific needs of children, ensuring that they leave with a healthy and fresh smile.

We know that some children are anxious about dental cleanings. That’s why we provide a relaxed, friendly environment. We want to make your child’s dental cleaning experience enjoyable so that they look forward to it and feel confident about maintaining their oral hygiene.

  • Remove plaque and tartar gently from your child’s teeth, particularly in areas that are hard to reach and where brushing and flossing cannot always be effective.
  • Polish your teeth to remove surface stains and leave them shining.
  • To ensure that they take care of their teeth, teach them the proper techniques for brushing and flossing in an engaging and fun way.
  • If necessary, apply fluoride treatments and dental sealants to prevent future cavities.
