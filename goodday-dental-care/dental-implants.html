Dental implant FAQs

Honest answers to the questions implant patients ask us most at our Orange and Anaheim offices.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates. The best way to know for sure is a consultation with a CBCT 3D scan, which lets Dr. Kim evaluate your jawbone and plan placement precisely. Certain conditions, like active gum disease or insufficient bone, may need to be addressed first.

How long does the dental implant process take?

It varies by patient. After placement, the implant typically needs a few months to fuse with the jawbone before the final crown is attached. Many cases are completed in a handful of visits over several months — Dr. Kim will give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Implant placement is done under local anesthesia, and most patients report less discomfort than they expected. Mild soreness for a few days afterward is normal and is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, implants can last many years — often decades. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement over time due to normal wear.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies widely by plan — some PPO plans cover part of the treatment. Our team verifies your benefits before any treatment begins and explains your options in plain language, including CareCredit financing for larger procedures.
