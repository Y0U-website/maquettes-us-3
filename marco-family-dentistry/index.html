The practice

Essential for the removal of plaque and tartar buildup, hygiene also polishes the teeth to remove stains not otherwise removed during regular brushing. Essential to overall oral health, the ADA recommends this be performed twice per year.

Dental x-rays are essential, preventative, diagnostic tools that provide valuable information not visible during a regular exams. This information allows us to safely and accurately detect hidden dental abnormalities and complete an accurate treatment plan. Without x-rays, problem areas may go undetected.

Cosmetic treatments can help change the size, shape and alignment of teeth. It can fill spaces between teeth and improve or correct bites. Veneers and whiteing can also improve the aesthetics of your smile.

The most common procedure on teeth, composite resin fillings provide durability and resistance to fracture and are a good choice for a more natural looking appearance.

Necessary for restoring weakened, damaged or missing teeth crowns can also protect teeth from further damage and improve overall appearance.
