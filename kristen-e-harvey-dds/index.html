The practice

We practice comprehensive dentistry designed to give you and your family the healthy and attractive smiles you deserve. From routine preventive check-ups to gentle yet thorough teeth cleanings to more complex restorative and cosmetic treatments, the team at White Park Dental is here to meet your needs.

At White Park Dental, we take pride in providing friendly, comfortable, personalized dentistry to our neighbors in Concord, NH and the surrounding area. The practice has been on Pleasant Street for nearly 30 years, and Dr. Harvey has continued the tradition of caring for her patients with trust and mutual respect.

As our patient, you’ll find that we rely on conservative, proven, time-tested techniques that protect your oral health and prevent problems from developing. We have built our practice on a foundation of preventive care. Whenever possible, we’d prefer to prevent cavities, gum disease, and other common dental problems with regular cleanings and comprehensive examinations.

Dr. Harvey will create a customized treatment plan for you based around your own individual needs and preferences. We believe that you should always have a voice in your own health care, and we will listen carefully to make sure that we are always on the same page.

Because we believe so strongly in providing individualized care, we’ll take the time needed to get to know you. Dr. Harvey does not rush from patient to patient; she takes as long as is necessary to provide the best possible care. It s our policy to not double book appointments when you are here, you are our top priority!

Dr. Harvey will take a personal interest in your care and make it their mission to give you exactly what you need and want. When it comes to dental restorations, the labs we trust know our work and know the quality that we demand. You’ll always get the highest quality restorations from the labs that we trust to do the best work every single time.

At White Park Dental, you are never simply a number in a chart. You are a one-of-a-kind individual with unique needs, wants, and goals. We take that seriously, and you’ll see it in our care. We believe that the best possible family dentist treats every patient like their own family and that’s exactly what we do.

Call our Concord, NH dental office today to schedule your next appointment. We’re looking forward to meeting you!
