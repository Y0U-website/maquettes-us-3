Ceramic Reconstruction Step 1

Begin the restoration process by removing the faulty filling and existing decay. To prepare the tooth for imaging, apply a powder layer with reflective properties. Ceramic Reconstruction Step 2 Capture a 3-Dimensional optical image of your tooth with a tiny in-mouth camera. Utilizing this image data, design the restoration on screen. Next, utilize diamond-coated tools to precisely carve a ceramic block, faithfully replicating the design. This entire procedure is completed in a single visit, courtesy of computer-aided design/computer-aided manufacturing (CAD/CAM) technology.
