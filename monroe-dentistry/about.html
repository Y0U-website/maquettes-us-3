0 Who we are For over 30 years, Monroe Dentistry has proudly served the Monroe community by providing high-quality dental care in a welcoming, family-oriented environment. Today, Dr. Samuel Gersch continues that tradition by focusing on personalized care and taking the time to understand each patient’s needs, concerns, and goals for their smile.

We understand that visiting the dentist can feel stressful for some people. That’s why our team works hard to create a relaxed, comfortable experience at every appointment. Our office uses modern dental technology, including advanced 3D imaging and digital scanning, to help diagnose problems accurately and make treatment more comfortable and efficient for our patients.

Many treatments can now be completed more efficiently than ever before, including custom dental crowns that can often be designed and placed in a single visit—saving you time while still delivering beautiful, long-lasting results.

Dr. Gersch graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry and is passionate about continuing education so he can bring the latest advancements in dentistry to his patients. He regularly pursues additional training in dental implants, clear aligner therapy, and modern digital dentistry to provide patients with comfortable, predictable treatment options.

Outside of the office, Dr. Gersch enjoys spending time with his wife, their two children, and their two dogs. Family is an important part of his life, and that same sense of care and connection carries over into how he treats his patients—like part of the Monroe Dentistry family.

We’re proud of the trust our patients place in us. Our team believes in clear communication, taking time to explain conditions and treatment options so you feel confident in every decision about your care. Whether you’re visiting for preventive care, restorative treatment, or cosmetic dentistry, our goal is always the same: to treat every patient like family while delivering safe, comfortable, and exceptional dental care.
