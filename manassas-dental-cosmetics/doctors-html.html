Dr. Truc Mai, DDS

Dr. Truc Mai is an experienced general dentist with two decades of experience across many facets of dentistry. Through open, honest communication, she takes the time to understand each patient’s concerns and goals — building the comfort and trust that lead to truly personalized treatment plans.

When she’s not creating smiles, Dr. Mai enjoys biking, swimming, and snowboarding. She is fluent in English, Vietnamese, and Spanish — making care accessible to a broader community of patients.

Dr. Paul Byers, DDS, MS

With 37 years of clinical experience, Dr. Paul Byers brings a rare combination of deep expertise, steady professionalism, and genuine compassion to every patient he serves. He earned his DDS from Loyola University of Chicago College of Dentistry and an MS from the University of Michigan, building a strong academic foundation that continues to guide his evidence-based approach to care.

Dr. Byers spent 12 years practicing Navy dentistry, where he refined his skills across diverse clinical settings and developed the calm, confident chairside manner patients value today. For the past 26 years, he has continued that commitment in private practice, providing comprehensive care rooted in integrity, clear communication, and long-term oral health.

Experienced in all phases of dentistry, Dr. Byers believes that excellent treatment begins with listening. His patient-centered treatment planning ensures that every individual understands their options, feels supported in their decisions, and receives care tailored to their comfort, goals, and overall well-being. Patients can expect a thoughtful, respectful, and collaborative experience — one grounded in trust and delivered with the highest standard of clinical excellence.

Dr. Victor James Lim, DDS

Dr. Victor James Lim grew up right here in Northern Virginia. His thirst for knowledge took him to sunny California, where he majored in Biology at California Polytechnic Institute, then to the snowy cold of Michigan to attend the University of Michigan School of Dentistry for his Doctorate in Dental Surgery.

Since then, Dr. Lim has practiced in a range of dental environments — including high-end cosmetic and multi-doctor practices — continually investing his time in attaining the latest dental techniques through continuing education courses. He dedicates himself to providing comprehensive, quality dentistry.
