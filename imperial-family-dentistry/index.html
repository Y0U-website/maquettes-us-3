0 Your Smile Team Meet the friendly faces behind your care. Laimarie Alas , DMD DENTIST

Dr. Alas attended Indiana University where she earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Spanish. From there she went on to earn a Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences and then attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts to earn her degree of Doctor of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Alas aims to provide compassionate care tailored to each patient’s needs, fostering trust and well-being in every interaction.

In her free time Dr. Alas enjoys cooking, traveling, and spending time with her family.

Dr. Alas is a proud member of the ADA and the Hispanic Dental Association.
