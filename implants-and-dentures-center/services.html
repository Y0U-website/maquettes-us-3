ROOT CANALS

ROOT CANALS

During the root canal procedure, we remove the nerve and pulp at the root of the tooth which has been damaged due to decay or any other infection. The canal is cleared and sealed to avoid any further decay and that can cause infection to the gums below your tooth. Our expert staff performs all procedures with great care so that the patients have a comfortable experience.

TOOTH EXTRACTIONS

TOOTH EXTRACTIONS

Tooth extraction may be necessary if the tooth is severely damaged or caused the whole jawline to dislocate. While preparing for tooth extraction, dentists will give their patients a local anesthesia, which numbs the surrounding area of the infected tooth. Dentists then hold the teeth to be extracted with forceps and gently shake it back and forth to loosen it from jawbone before finally pulling it out with force. [7]

INVISALIGN

INVISALIGN

Get an invisible solution for straightening your teeth! Invisalign, also known as a clear-align treatment, is a transparent substitute of braces that can be used to adjust the alignment of the teeth. With Invisalign, you can experience a better and more comfortable fit for your teeth. All this, so that you can smile confidently.[1]

ALL ON 4

ALL ON 4

All on four is a cost-effective and highly efficient solution for everyone looking to replace lost teeth. With the introduction of All on 4 in the world of dentistry, you can eliminate the need for getting implants for each of your teeth. Instead, you get a solution in which you can support all your teeth with the help of four implants only.[2]

SNAP-IN SNAP OUT DENTURES

SNAP-IN SNAP OUT DENTURES

As the name suggests, these types of dentures snap on to a dental plant fixed on a patient’s gum. The more implants patients have on their gum the stable will be snap-in snap-out denture. Since snap-in dentures are not porcelain or real teeth, they need to be frequently cleaned to avoid any infection on the gums. Snap-in Snap-out dentures are quick fix for people who have lost all their teeth due to a gum disease or old age. [6]

WISDOM TEETH EXTRACTION

WISDOM TEETH EXTRACTION

This intricate procedure requires extra care and attention. The purpose of the surgery is to take out one or more wisdom teeth. As wisdom teeth are present at the back end of your mouth, sometimes it does not have enough space to grow which leads to an impacted wisdom tooth. In such a situation, the surgery has to be performed as soon as possible.[4]

SURGICAL EXTRACTION

SURGICAL EXTRACTION

A complex procedure that is performed with utmost precision to get rid of a tooth that has been severely damaged. Such a procedure is performed if your tooth has broken at the line of the gum or has been unable to erupt inside the mouth. An incision is made into your gum, and the damaged tooth is removed.[5]

[5] https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/procedures/tooth-removal/when-surgical-extraction-of-teeth-is-necessary-0215

CONSCIOUS SEDATION

CONSCIOUS SEDATION

Conscious Sedation is a method used to make you feel relaxed and free of pain while any procedure is being performed. The recovery from conscious sedation is quick and therefore does not affect your ability to carry on with your routine significantly. Since you are awake and able to respond to touch and other stimuli in your surroundings, it is considered extremely safe. The administration can be oral, I.V., or via inhalation.
