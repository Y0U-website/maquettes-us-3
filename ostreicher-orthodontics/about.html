MEET DR. ABAEYV

Dr. Ronna Abaeyv was born and raised in Queens, New York City, where she excelled academically, graduating at the top of her class from Forest Hills High School. She earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry from the City University of New York, graduating Magna Cum Laude. Her passion for education led her to serve as an adjunct professor of biochemistry at Hunter College following her graduation.

Dr. Ronna pursued her dental education at the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree and received the Honors Orthodontics Award from the American Association of Orthodontics. She continued her training at NYU, completing her post-doctoral residency in Orthodontics and Dental Orthopedics. Shortly after, she became a Board-Certified Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, a testament to her dedication and expertise in the field.

Throughout her career, Dr. Ronna has contributed to numerous research projects, including behavioral neurobiology studies at Rockefeller University and research on fluorosis, for which she received the Craniofacial Biology Award from the International Association for Dental Research. She remains committed to staying at the forefront of orthodontic advancements, utilizing the latest techniques and technologies to provide exceptional care.

Dr. Ronna’s greatest joy as an orthodontist comes from helping her patients feel comfortable and confident throughout their treatment journey.

When she’s not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Ronna loves spending quality time with her family and young children. She’s also an avid runner, currently training for her first 5K race.

MEET DR. TOVILO

With a passion for creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Tovilo continued his dental career with Post-Graduate Orthodontics Studies at New York University. During residency. Dr. Tovilo was exposed to a wide variety of dental malocclusions ranging from interceptive-based treatment for children to complex multi-disciplinary and orthognathic surgical adult cases.

Upon completion of the program, Dr. Tovilo returned home to Toronto having passed both the American and Canadian Dental Licensing Board examinations and was granted fellowship as an Orthodontic Specialist by the Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario.

MEET DR. ZAK

I love orthodontics; it is my passion. As an orthodontist, I have the chance to meet amazing families and give them something that will change their life for the better: an amazing, healthy smile. The personal connections I’ve made over the past 18 years are my greatest reward. Dr. Zak

Dr. Efraim Zak attended the New York University College of Dentistry for his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. He spent additional time and training at NYU to complete his orthodontic residency and become a specialist in orthodontics. He brings with him an extensive background in research, including many awards and publications.

Dr. Zak attends yearly seminars and study groups to keep up on all advanced orthodontic techniques. He has also lectured at dental study clubs on Align Technology’s behalf, the producer of Invisalign®. He is a member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontists, and the OKU dental honor fraternity.

Dr. Zak grew up in New York City and now resides in Long Island. He and his wife, Susan, have two amazing children, Justin and Chelsea, who keep him very busy. When he has the time, Dr. Zak enjoys playing golf, skiing, biking, working out, and going to movies. He is involved with his children’s PTA and supports the Shriners Hospital for Children and research for scleroderma, ALS, multiple sclerosis, cancer, and many others.
