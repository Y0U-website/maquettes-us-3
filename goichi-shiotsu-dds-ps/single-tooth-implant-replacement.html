The Process of a Single Tooth Implant Replacement

Getting a single-tooth implant replacement is a detailed and careful process. Our expert dentists make this process worry-free for you. Here’s what the single-tooth implant process looks like at Mercer Island Dental.

Evaluation and Planning: First, you will have a thorough check-up with Dr. Shiotsu. Our expert dentists for implants near you will examine your mouth, check your bone health, and see if an implant tooth is right for you.

Tooth Extraction (if needed): If the tooth you need to replace is still in your mouth, our dental experts will remove it first very carefully.

Healing Time: After placing your implant, our dentist will suggest a healing period. Depending on your condition, the healing time may be a few weeks to a few months.

Abutment Attachment: Once your implant is set, an abutment is attached to it. This part will hold your new tooth (crown).

Crown Placement: A custom-made artificial tooth that looks like your natural teeth is placed on your abutment. Dr. Shiotsu, the expert dentist for implants near you, will ensure it matches your other teeth’ color, shape, and size perfectly.

Final Adjustments: At this stage, our esteemed dentist will make any last tweaks to ensure your new tooth fits well and works as it should. The result is a single-tooth implant replacement that looks and works like your natural teeth.

Though the process can take a few months, it gives you a strong and long-lasting replacement for your missing tooth.
