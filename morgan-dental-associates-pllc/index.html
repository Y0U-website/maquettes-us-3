The practice

Morgan Dental Associates believes if patients are to attain optimal benefits from healthcare, patients become an integral part of their own care. High quality care is offered in an accessible manner so that personal relationships can be established between the patient and provider.

As a dental provider, we understand the integral role that optimum oral health plays in an individual s wellness and world.

We strive to extend this understanding to the student population at Florida State University. Students are at a critical point in life where the decisions made each day are creating habits for the future.

With a combined 50+ years experience, our doctors and clinical staff are ready to make a difference in treating the students, staff, and faculty of Florida State University and the Tallahassee Communities.

Routine Prophylaxis Cleanings are recommended twice a year to remove plaque, calculus, and stains from teeth in order to maintain oral health.

A filling is the process of restoring lost tooth structure. Typically the site will be filled with composite material that resembles tooth color and structure.

Periodontal Services pretain to the gum tissue. Services such as Scaling and Root Planing will be done to remove harmful debris and bacteria from the tissue if needed to preserve a healthy mouth.

Accidents do happen. Our office is equipped and ready for any oral trauma case with the majority of treatment starting on the initial visit. We strive to repair your smile quickly and efficiently.

Les soins

Exams are a vital part of the treatment process. Dental Exams can range from a full Comprehensive Exams for new patients to Limited Exams in the cases of trauma or oral pain.

When a tooth is non-restorable, an implant is a great option in order to replace the extracted tooth. The implant is an artificial tooth that replaces the tooth root from an existing oral area.
