Our mission

Family-centered dentistry

Dr. Frank began her dental career practicing alongside her father, Dr. Richard Gibbon. After her father retired, Dr. Frank took over her father’s practice and is currently the lead doctor at Legacy Dental. She is honored to continue her father’s legacy of providing excellent dental care with integrity and gentleness.

Located in the heart of Post Falls, ID, Legacy Dental provides excellent general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry to the community. Our office has a warm, inviting atmosphere, complete with modern dental technology and comfortable amenities, including blankets, headphones, coffee, water, and kids’ toys.

New and familiar smiles are welcome here!

Dr. Whitney Frank, DDS

In her free time, she enjoys North Idaho with her husband and their three daughters. She loves triathlon training, hiking, skiing, and sunshine.

Dr. Niko Pintos, DDS

Originally from Spokane, Dr. Pintos (pronounced Pin-toes) has called the Inland Northwest home for the past 13 years. He earned his undergraduate degree from Walla Walla University before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery at Loma Linda University School of Dentistry.

Since graduating, Dr. Pintos has spent years helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. What drew Dr. Pintos to dentistry was the unique blend of science, hands-on problem solving, and meaningful patient relationships. He believes every smile tells a different story, and every treatment plan should be just as unique. By taking a thoughtful, personalized approach to each case, he’s committed to helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their care.

When he’s not in the office, you’ll likely find Dr. Pintos watching (or debating!) football, basketball, baseball, or soccer and he’s recently added hockey to the lineup. He also enjoys taking weekend road trips with his wife and discovering new restaurants throughout the region.

Modern Technology

Modern dentistry, old-fashioned care

Patient care is our top priority, and we go above and beyond to ensure your smile remains healthy and bright. In pursuit of this, we have integrated cutting-edge dental technology into our practice to enhance your comfort and well-being during each visit. Some examples are Invisalign and implant dentistry.

We offer financing options such as CareCredit for patients without dental insurance.
