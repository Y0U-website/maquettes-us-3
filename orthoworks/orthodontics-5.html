What to Expect

Your first visit is a complimentary orthodontic consultation. We complete a full bite exam, take photos and digital scans, and present every viable treatment option in plain language. For adults, that means a side-by-side comparison of Invisalign, ceramic braces, and metal braces with realistic timelines and investment. We coordinate with your general dentist, periodontist, or restorative team when a case requires it. Treatment visits run every 6 to 10 weeks and are designed to fit a workday. Every case ends with custom retainers and a long-term retention plan.
