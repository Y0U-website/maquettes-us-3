Meet Dr. Parigini

“Watching mothers, at times, cry when they first see their child’s new smile was a real eye-opener to how orthodontics can make a huge impact on people’s lives.”

Dr. Parigini keeps current with the latest technology, including digital radiography and photography, as well as computerized treatment cards and computerized treatment planning, 3D imaging, 3D scanner of teeth which eliminates the need for impressions and along with 3D printer technology allows him to make invisible aligners in house. All these technologies insure the most advanced treatment available.

Dr. Parigini has four children and each is very accomplished and remarkable in their own way. He also has the privilege of saying he truly met his soulmate and they have unconditional love for one another. He enjoys many local outdoor activities, in fact, he will tell you he finds true paradise being on snow skis or in the water, wakeboarding, and wake surfing.
