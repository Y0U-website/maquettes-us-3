Dr. Joanne Kim

Dr. Joanne Kim has been making little smiles brighter for over a decade. A graduate of the USC Ostrow School of Dentistry, she went on to serve in the U.S. Army before returning to her true calling: caring for kids.

Dr. Kim&#x27;s approach is anything but ordinary. She believes every child deserves a dental visit that feels like an adventure — not something to dread. Her gentle hands, cheerful personality, and genuine warmth put even the most anxious patients at ease, and she treats every family like her own.

When she&#x27;s not in the operatory, you&#x27;ll find her alongside Hodoo (Walnut) Kim — her beloved dog and the office&#x27;s unofficial director of fun. Hodoo has a remarkable talent for turning nervous kids into giggling ones, which we think is a pretty important job.

Dr. Kim proudly serves children from Pomona, Ontario, Chino Hills, La Verne, Claremont, Diamond Bar, and throughout the Inland Empire. She accepts Medi-Cal, Denti-Cal, and most PPO plans because she believes exceptional dental care should be accessible to every family.

Meet Hodoo (Walnut) Kim

The happiest, wiggliest tooth-lover around. Hodoo shares an office (and snacks!) with her roommate Dr. Kim, and she has a remarkable talent for turning nervous kids into giggling ones. Consider her our Director of Fun — an honorary position she takes very seriously.

Our Mission
