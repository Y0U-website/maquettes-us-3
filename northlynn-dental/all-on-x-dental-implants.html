TMJ problems may develop due to several factors, including teeth grinding (bruxism), jaw misalignment, stress, injury, arthritis, or muscle tension.

  • Clicking, popping, or grinding sounds in the jaw
  • Difficulty opening or closing the mouth
  • Frequent headaches or migraines
  • Ear pain, ringing in the ears, or facial discomfort
  • Jaw stiffness or locking
  • Pain while chewing or speaking

TMJ Treatment Options

Our office offers personalized TMJ treatment plans designed to relieve discomfort, improve jaw function, and prevent further joint damage. Treatment recommendations will depend on the severity and cause of your symptoms.

By addressing the underlying cause of TMJ dysfunction, we can help reduce pain, restore comfortable jaw movement, and improve your overall quality of life.

  • Custom night guards or occlusal splints
  • Bite adjustment therapy
  • Orthodontic treatment
  • Muscle relaxation therapy
  • Lifestyle and stress management recommendations

Early Treatment Matters

Ignoring TMJ symptoms may lead to worsening discomfort, chronic headaches, tooth wear, or long-term joint problems. Early diagnosis and treatment can help protect your oral health and provide lasting relief.

SEDATION

Comfortable, Stress-Free Dental Care

Many people avoid visiting the dentist due to fear, anxiety, or previous negative experiences. Unfortunately, delaying dental treatment can often lead to more serious oral health problems that require more extensive care.

At Advanced Dental Solutions, we believe every patient deserves a calm, comfortable, and positive dental experience. Sedation dentistry provides a safe and effective way to help patients feel relaxed and at ease throughout their treatment.

Whether you experience mild nervousness or severe dental anxiety, our sedation options can help you receive the care you need comfortably and confidently.

Our Sedation Options

We offer several proven sedation methods tailored to your individual comfort level and treatment needs.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

Nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas, is a mild sedative inhaled through a small mask placed over the nose during treatment. It is one of the safest and most commonly used forms of dental sedation.

Patients should inform the doctor if they are pregnant or planning to become pregnant before using nitrous oxide sedation.

  • Promotes fast-acting relaxation
  • Adjustable throughout the procedure
  • Wears off quickly after treatment
  • Allows most patients to drive themselves home
  • Safe and effective for many dental procedures

Oral Sedation (Sedation Pill)

Oral sedation involves taking a prescribed medication before your appointment to help you feel deeply relaxed during treatment.

Because oral sedation may cause drowsiness, patients will need a responsible adult to drive them to and from their appointment.

  • Reduces dental anxiety and nervousness
  • Creates a calm and relaxed experience
  • May provide a mild amnesic effect with limited memory of the procedure
  • Ideal for patients with moderate dental fear or lengthy treatments

Relax While We Restore Your Smile

No matter which sedation option you choose, your comfort and safety remain our top priorities. In addition to sedation, the treatment area will be fully numbed to ensure a comfortable experience throughout your procedure.

Sedation dentistry also allows many treatments — including gum therapy, dental implants, oral surgery, and full smile restorations — to be completed more comfortably and often in fewer visits.

Your Comfort Comes First

Fear and anxiety should never prevent you from achieving a healthy, confident smile. Our experienced team is here to help you feel relaxed, supported, and comfortable every step of the way.

Speak with our doctors to determine which sedation option is best for your needs.
