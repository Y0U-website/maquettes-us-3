Meet Dr. Ruwa Irsheid

Originally from Jordan, Dr. Irsheid has always been passionate about creating healthy and confident smiles. She earned her dental degree from Jordan University of Science and Technology, where she graduated with honors before moving to the United States to further her education. At the University of Florida, Dr. Irsheid completed two years of advanced education in general dentistry, followed by three additional years of specialty training in orthodontics, where she earned a Certificate in Orthodontics and a Master of Dental Science.

As a proud member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Southern Association of Orthodontists, and the Florida Association of Orthodontists, Dr. Irsheid is committed to delivering exceptional care. By attending local, regional, and national conferences and pursuing continuing education, she stays at the forefront of her field, ensuring she delivers the highest standard of care while further enhancing her expertise.

Dr. Irsheid is passionate about creating beautiful, long-lasting smiles while fostering meaningful connections with her patients. She strives to make each individual feel comforted and at ease throughout their orthodontic journey. Fluent in both Arabic and English, she connects with patients from diverse backgrounds and ensures their experience is both comfortable and rewarding.

Outside the office, Dr. Irsheid cherishes quality time with her family, sharing giggles and hugs with her daughter, Talia. She enjoys discovering new restaurants, working out, and reading thriller novels.
