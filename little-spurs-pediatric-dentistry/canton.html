Welcome to Little Spurs Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric Dental Care With a Whole Lot of Heart

At Little Spurs Pediatric Dentistry, we make dental visits feel easier for kids and their parents. Led by Dr. Kameron, our Canton office was created to be warm, welcoming, and genuinely fun, while still providing the specialized pediatric dental care growing smiles need.

From first visits and preventive cleanings to restorative care, special needs dentistry, and anxiety-friendly visits, our team takes the time to meet each child where they are. Around here, we believe great dental care starts with trust, patience, and a little bit of fun.

What Makes Us Different

Where Little Smiles Feel Right at Home

At Little Spurs, every detail is designed to help kids feel safe, seen, and supported. Our Canton office combines expert pediatric dental care with a friendly, down-to-earth experience that helps children build confidence at the dentist from the very beginning.

Gentle, Personalized Visits Every child is different, so every visit should feel a little different too. We take the time to understand your child’s comfort level, personality, and needs so we can move at a pace that feels right for them.

A Calm, Kid-Friendly Environment We want kids to feel excited, not overwhelmed, when they come to see us. With a welcoming team, a playful atmosphere, and our beloved mascot Russ the Highland Cow, Little Spurs is built to make dental care feel positive from the start.

Special Needs & Anxiety-Friendly Care Our team is experienced in caring for children with sensory sensitivities, ASD, dental anxiety, and other unique needs. We also offer nitrous oxide when appropriate to help make visits feel calmer and more comfortable.

Pediatric Dental Care for Every Stage of Childhood

From baby teeth to teen smiles, Little Spurs provides the care your child needs to grow up with a healthy, confident smile.

Preventive Care

Emergency Dentistry Dental pain, injuries, and unexpected tooth trouble can feel stressful, especially when it involves your child. We offer gentle emergency care to treat urgent concerns and get your child’s smile back on track.
