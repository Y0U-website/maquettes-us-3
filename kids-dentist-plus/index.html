Les soins

We start preventative dentistry at age one or with the eruption of the first tooth, as recommended by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. At age three, children should begin a schedule of regular cleaning.

However, when a tooth decayed severely, the placement of a crown is an alternative. If needed, our dentists may extract teeth during regular checkups or schedule additional visits.
