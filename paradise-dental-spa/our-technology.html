CONE BEAM CT SCANNING MACHINE

A radiographic imaging method that allows accurate, three-dimensional (3D) imaging of hard tissue structures. These include images of the patient’s teeth, soft tissues, and nerve pathways and bone in a single scan.

DIGITAL X-RAY

A form of X-ray imaging where digital X-ray sensors are used instead of traditional photographic film. This helps our dentists diagnose problems like tooth decay, bone loss in a patient’s jaw, and many other problems in a patient’s teeth or jaw.

INTRAORAL CAMERA

An pen-like tool that has tiny digital cameras at the end of the devise. This help our dentists communicate and educate our patients by showing the interior of their mouth in color. This is an alternative to using a dental mirror, and is an effective tool that can provide a visual aspect in a patient’s teeth, even in areas that are hard to reach.

DIGITAL DENTURES

An advanced dental technology that uses precise 3D scanning and printing to create custom-fitted dentures with unmatched accuracy and efficiency. It streamlines the entire denture-making process, offering patients a quicker, more comfortable experience and dentists a reliable, state-of-the-art solution for prosthetic design.

ONE DAY CROWN

A wet milling device that molds and cuts various materials by using a liquid coolant to remove excess materials from it’s piece. After receiving an image from our dental advisors, our dental technicians are able to create implants or crowns for patients.

CLEAR ALIGNERS

Clear aligners are a nearly invisible alternative to braces used to straighten teeth. Made from smooth, clear plastic, they fit over your teeth and gradually shift them into place. Treatment involves wearing a series of custom-made aligner trays, each slightly adjusted to move your teeth over time. Clear aligners are great for fixing issues like crowded teeth, gaps, or bite problems, but not everyone is a candidate, so it’s important to consult with a dentist or orthodontist.

ULTRASONIC CAVITATION SKIN TIGHTENING/ COOL SCULPTING MACHINE

Ultrasonic Cavitation Machines help break down fat cells below the skin using radio frequencies and low-frequency ultrasonic waves. This non-surgical method helps reduce cellulite and localized fat.

CoolSculpting Machines helps get rid of stubborn fat in certain parts of the body (thighs, belly, under the chin, etc.) by cooling the fat to a temperature that destroys it. This is a process called cryolipolysis.
