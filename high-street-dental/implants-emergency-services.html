How It Works:

The process consists of:

  • The Implant- Its mainly made of titanium and is placed in the upper or lower jawbone.
  • The Abutment- It can be made of titanium, gold or porcelain and is attached to the implant with a screw. The abutment and connects the implant to the crown.
  • The crown- This part looks like the tooth and is made of metal, porcelain or porcelain fused to a metal alloy. The crown is either cemented or screwed on the abutment.

Procedure

The dental implant procedure consists of the following steps:

The first step involves detailed discussion with the dentist followed by a dental examination. The condition of the bone tissue is checked by doing an X-Ray of the jaw. Patients with strong bone density are considered good candidates of this procedure as it allows good support for the implant.

When the orthodontist and the patient make the decision to go ahead with dental implants, it is important to be prepared for some tests and procedures that must be done before the new teeth can be placed. The doctor will take X-rays of the mouth to understand the patient’s bite and where the implants need to be placed. The doctor will then make a plan to accurately set the new teeth in order to avoid any potential problems during the procedure.

A root system is placed where the old tooth root system was. Connected to the jaw bone, the root system of a tooth implant provides a strong base to hold the synthetic tooth on top.

The root systems need to be strong hence a strong material has to be used in making it. The common materials are titanium and zirconium. The titanium is very strong and lightweight making it a good solution for the implants root system. The zirconium based tooth implants are even stronger. You probably know zirconium in the form of cubic Zirconia diamonds. This man made synthetic material is very strong and makes a very good root system

On top of the root system, you have the actual synthetic tooth. The tooth is a natural looking tooth and people don’t recognize that it is a synthetic tooth. Made to high standards, the dental implants are a perfect solution to missing teeth with a substitute that lasts a very long time. The root in the implants are designed to last like for forever. Some of the specialists who perform dental implant treatment give 25 year guarantees on the root system which shows the kind of quality that is in dental implants.

Recovery

A common misconception about dental implant is that the recovery can be painful. Fortunately, many patients report that the pain afterward is quite minimal. There may be a bit of soreness the day after, similar to what one would experience after a filling or extraction.

However, the only pain medication that is typically needed is some ibuprofen every few hours for any lingering discomfort. The doctor may provide a prescription for pain medication, but many patients often decide that it is not necessary. In addition to ibuprofen, the doctor may also provide an oral antibiotic as well as a mouth rinse to keep the mouth from becoming infected.

Getting any major oral work can often be nerve-racking. However, it is important to remember how much technology has improved procedures by decreasing discomfort. If dental implants or other orthodontia are needed, be sure to make an appointment with an orthodontist to discuss all available options or to ease the concerns and anxiety about the procedure.

After recovery Post Dental Implant Care

  • Initially, eat soft and light food to avoid irritating sensations that can delay a recovery time.
  • Avoid smoking to keep away unwanted dental infections.
  • Take medications regularly such as antibiotics and anti-inflammatory drugs for fast recovery.
  • Follow regular dental checkups to ensure a full recovery.
