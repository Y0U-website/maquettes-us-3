Virtual Consultations

  • Current gallery of 8 pictures (profile, frontal non-smiling, frontal smiling, left & right side pictures of the teeth, upper & lower occlusal pictures of the teeth). These do not have to be taken by a dentist but can be taken with a cell phone.
  • If easily available, a current panoramic x-ray.
  • If easily available, a current cephalometric/lateral head x-ray
  • If available, any previous x-rays/ or pictures that would help document changes that have occurred over time.
  • Any additional details of your medical/dental history that may help Dr. Weddle understand your situation.
  • If available, tomograms (x-rays of the temporomandibular joints or TMJ) if pertinent.

There is a fee of $300.00 for this service.

Follow-up Virtual Consultations

Follow-up virtual consultations are available to discuss your case. If you would like to have a follow-up virtual consultation to discuss specific questions or next steps in your treatment, we are able to schedule it on select Fridays.

The fee for a follow-up consultation is $85.00 for 15-20 minute follow-up sessions. Please send any additional information since the last time you saw Dr. Weddle. Ideally, we will receive this information two weeks prior to your scheduled consultation. Please contact us at (908) 561-0225 or reception@greatsmilesnj.com if you have any questions or want to email information. We’re very much looking forward to speaking with you!
