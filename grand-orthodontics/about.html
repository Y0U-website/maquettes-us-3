Meet Dr. Kendall Woolridge

“I am extremely blessed to be able to do what I love! Helping patients transform into their best self is what I live for.”

Education

Dr. Woolridge earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Rutgers University and her dental degree (DDS) from Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry. After dental school, she returned to the academic arena to complete her Specialty in Orthodontics at Jacksonville University School of Orthodontics.

Dr. Woolridge constantly seeks new continuing educational seminars that relate to overall health and orthodontics. She knows these courses will help to ensure her patients receive the highest quality treatments using the most advanced methods and innovative techniques available.

request an appointment In the Practice Dr. Woolridge loves helping others improve their self-confidence by transitioning their existing smiles into healthy and beautiful ones! She enjoys seeing the transformation of her children, as well as adult, patients. It makes her ecstatic to know that she contributes to making so many people happy!

Family and Community

While still a Jersey girl at heart, Dr. Woolridge loves the yearlong sun-shiny days of Southern California. Together with her husband Geno, her sons Bryson and Brayden, and her mom Diane, Dr. Woolridge loves to spend time outdoors in parks, camp grounds, and at the beach. Their favorite museum is the Museum of Natural History and they often spend many weekend hours enjoying the many exhibits. At home, board games, “Just Dance” games for Wii, and cooking meals together are their favorite activities.

As is the saying of her alma mater, Dr. Woolridge is committed to “Serving the underserved.” Dr. Woolridge, her family, and her Grand Orthodontics team all make it a priority to participate each month in volunteer activities in El Segundo and in the surrounding communities. School-based visits from the “Grand Tooth Fairy,” oral hygiene education seminars, health and wellness fairs, and supporting local education and athletic teams are just a few of the ways Dr. Woolridge and Grand Orthodontics reach out to the community.
