About MacGregor Dental

MacGregor Dental is a patient-centric family dentistry practice that offers the highest standards of care and respect in Juneau, Alaska. We specialize in everything from maintenance dental care to oral surgery in our welcoming, judgment-free office. No matter your dental and oral health needs, we’ll meet them with our expert knowledge and a gentle approach.

Where Quality Care and Compassion Meet

Our husband-and-wife duo not only provide the highest standards of dental care to Juneau residents, but they also build lasting and meaningful connections with each patient. Receive top-tier care in the comfort of our family-friendly dental office.

About the MacGregors

Dr. Savannah MacGregor

Dr. Savannah earned her Bachelor of Science degree from The University of Colorado, Colorado Springs, before graduating with her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 2020.

While in dental school, Dr. Savannah was awarded the Darrel J. Lower Pediatric Dentistry ‘Hall of Fame’ Award , was chosen by the clinical faculty for her dedication to pediatric care and was also a prosthodontics mentor to incoming clinical students.

Dr. Savannah married her “dental school sweetheart,” Dr. Mitchell, in 2020, and has since had their two children while living in Juneau. In her spare time, she enjoys baking treats and being with her children. Having lived in hot and humid climates in the past, Dr. Savannah loves the chilly days, rainy days, and snowy days that living in Juneau brings. (Thankfully for her, Dr. Mitchell takes care of the snow removal at their house).

Dr. Savannah is passionate about all things dentistry and is grateful to be serving the Juneau and surrounding communities of SE Alaska.

Dr. Mitchell MacGregor

Dr. Mitchell earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Creighton University before graduating with his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 2020.

While in dental school, he was an advanced standing student in oral surgery, a community outreach volunteer for dental services, and received the ‘American Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Radiology Award’ for outstanding knowledge and skill set.

Dr. Mitchell and Dr. Savannah married in 2020 and have since had two children, both delivered at Bartlett Regional Hospital in Juneau. In his spare time, Dr. Mitchell can be found nurturing his children, cooking, exercising, or site seeing. Having experienced his first ever bear encounter, whale sightings, northern lights show, and many other outdoor wonders in Juneau, he has loved calling it his home.

Dr. Mitchell is honored to serve Juneau and the surrounding communities of SE Alaska and aims to provide the highest quality of care and most comfortable dental experience possible for his patients.
