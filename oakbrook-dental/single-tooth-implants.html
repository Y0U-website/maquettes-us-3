The Benefits Of Single Tooth Implants

Single-tooth implants use a single dental implant and a dental crown to restore one tooth. Compared to other treatments like dental bridges and partial dentures, this treatment has a variety of benefits.

Looks & feels natural

Single-tooth implants will look and feel just like natural teeth. The design of a dental implant mimics that of your natural teeth, and the crown will be built of durable, tooth-colored porcelain, and matched to your adjacent teeth. Your new smile will look, feel, and function perfectly. Nobody will ever have to know that you’ve lost a tooth.

Comfortable, never moves or shifts

Dental bridges can be a bit bulky and take some time to get used to, while partial dentures may tend to shift when you eat, speak, or chew. In contrast, dental implants feel more natural and are indistinguishable from natural teeth. They also will never move or shift in your mouth, since they are securely anchored to your jaw.

Strengthens jaw bone

Bridges and partial dentures may restore your tooth, but they lead to a condition called “bone resorption,” where the underlying jaw bone begins to weaken due to tooth loss. Dental implants help you avoid this issue. They are implanted directly into the jaw bone, and stimulate the bone to prevent you from losing bone mass in your jaw.
