Implant Support Dentures

Missing teeth left untreated can also cause your cheeks to look sunken and hollow and it makes talking more difficult. Implant dentures provide you with a fantastic alternative if you don’t feel like regular traditional dentures are the thing for you.

Implant dentures can address issues that many experience with traditional dentures. They are fixed in place when worn and won’t fall out when you chew, speak, or laugh. They are implanted with a titanium screw that is fused into the jawbone to replace the tooth root. Your dentist will make a small opening in your jawbone where they then place the implant. After this, they stitch the site up. They repeat this for every implant in your mouth. This becomes a solid foundation for a replacement tooth or denture appliance.

There are two types of implant dentures. These are:

Removable Implant Dentures These dentures are secured using implants but are still removable when needed. They are typically smaller than traditional dentures and are less expensive than permanent implant dentures. You may need a removal tool to take them out, but will remain stable in your mouth when you wear them again. No more worrying about them feeling loose!

Fixed Implant Dentures These are also known as “permanent dentures”. These dentures are permanently attached to your implants and are also smaller than traditional dentures. Due to them being fixed in place, food doesn’t get beneath them while you eat, which means cleaning them is similar to taking care of your natural teeth. They need to be flossed and brushed on a regular basis.
