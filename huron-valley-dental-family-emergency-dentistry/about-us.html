Meet Dr. Zaid Anbaki

Clinical Director

Patients from throughout Michigan travel to see our Chief Clinical Director, Dr. Anbaki, who has been working in the dental field since 2002. He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania; top of his class. Dr. Anbaki is proud to be a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Maryland Dental Association, Detroit Dental society, and academy of general dentistry. Dr. Anbaki will provide his patients with the best care possible and loves to see his patients leave with a big smile.

In addition to his Ivy League expertise and years of experience, Dr. Anbaki prides himself and his team on being able to provide patients of all ages with exceptional servicing that helps patients meet their smile goals and needs. Whether you’re looking to book an exam and checkup for your child or a veneer or implant consultation for a parent, Dr. Anbaki and the rest of our team at Huron Valley Dental offer a full array of top-rated dental services or the entire family!
