Our Doctors

Dr. Mark Jerman

Dr. Mark grew up in Central Ohio and graduated from Upper Arlington High School in 1984. In 1990 he graduated from The Ohio State University in Economics. After several years of working in the dental industry for Patterson Dental Supply, Dr. Mark returned to OSU to fulfill his dream of becoming a dentist like his father. View Bio

Dr. Jennifer Rekos

Dr. Jenn joined the Jerman Family Dentistry family in 2006. She has a simple philosophy in dentistry, which is to treat every patient she meets with compassion, professionalism, and with a gentle touch. It is her goal to educate patients and families and guide them toward optimal oral health with preventative, conservative, high-quality dentistry. View Bio

Dr. Steve Jerman

Dr. Steve Jerman grew up in Worthington, Ohio and graduated from Worthington High School in 1955. While attending college, Dr. Jerman did masonry work. He has always enjoyed working with his hands and in 1966 graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. View Bio
