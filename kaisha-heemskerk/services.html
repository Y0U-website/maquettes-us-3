Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have a dental checkup and cleaning?

Most patients benefit from a checkup and cleaning every six months. However, if you have a history of gum disease or other conditions, your dentist may recommend more frequent visits—typically every three to four months. We’ll help you figure out the right schedule at your first visit.

What’s included in a new patient exam?

Your first visit is a comprehensive 1.5-hour appointment. We’ll complete a visual examination, periodontal probing, TMJ screening, oral cancer screening, and a full series of X-rays. If time allows, we’ll complete your cleaning the same day. You’ll leave with a clear picture of your dental health and a treatment plan if anything needs attention.

Do you offer same-day crowns?

Yes—in most cases, we can design, craft, and place your crown in a single visit using CEREC technology. No second appointment, no temporary crown. Complex cases may occasionally require the traditional two-visit process, and we’ll let you know upfront if that applies to you.

Can I see the same dentist every time?

We do our best to keep you with the same doctor for continuity of care. Our three-doctor team works collaboratively, so if your regular dentist isn’t available, you’ll always be seeing someone who knows your history and is familiar with your care.
