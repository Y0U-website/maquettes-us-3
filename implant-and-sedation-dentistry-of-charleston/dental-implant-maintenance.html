Your Dental Implant Maintenance Routine

Maintaining your dental implants involves a combination of at-home care and professional check-ups. Here’s what you can expect:

At-Home Care

Daily brushing and flossing are as essential for your implants as they are for natural teeth. Special interdental brushes and non-abrasive toothpaste are recommended to clean hard-to-reach areas without damaging the implant surface.

Professional Care

Regular visits to Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston allow Dr. Gilreath and our team to monitor your implants’ condition closely. These check-ups include cleaning the implants with tools that do not scratch their surface, checking for signs of gum disease, and ensuring that the implant remains tightly secured in your jawbone.

What Patients Can Expect During Visits

During your maintenance visits to our Goose Creek office, you will experience a thorough examination of your oral health. This includes professional cleaning designed specifically for dental implants to ensure that they remain free from harmful bacteria buildup. Dr. Gilreath emphasizes the importance of these visits as an opportunity to address any concerns early on and adjust care routines as necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Implant Maintenance

How often should I visit my dentist for implant maintenance?

The frequency of professional check-ups can vary depending on individual needs. Generally, visiting every 6 months is recommended, but Dr. Gilreath may suggest a different schedule based on your specific situation.

Can I use regular toothpaste on my implants?

We recommend using non-abrasive toothpaste formulated for use with dental implants to avoid scratching the surface of the implant or prosthetic tooth.

What happens if I neglect my dental implants?

Neglecting dental implant maintenance can lead to complications such as infections, bone loss around the implant, and ultimately failure of the implant itself.
