Single-Tooth Implant

A single-tooth implant restores not just your smile but also your oral health. When a tooth is missing, the surrounding teeth shift, affecting your bite and jaw alignment. Shifting will eventually lead to decay and gum diseases. A dental implant replaces the tooth root, keeping all your teeth intact, improving oral health, and preventing bone loss.

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth. They are durable, strong, and can last for a lifetime. With proper care and regular visits to your dentist, an implant can provide a permanent solution for your smile and speech as they function like natural teeth.

A missing tooth can affect the appearance of your face, making your cheeks sink in and giving you an aged appearance. A single-tooth implant fills the gap, maintaining your facial features and the natural shape of your face. Additionally, implants look and feel like natural teeth, improving your confidence and self-esteem.

Implants require the same care as natural teeth, including brushing twice daily, flossing, and regular visits to your dentist. Unlike dentures, implants don’t need any special adhesives or cleaning solutions. With proper maintenance, implants can last for years and help you maintain a healthy and radiant smile.

What is the Placement Process?

If you are considering getting a dental implant, it helps to understand the steps involved in the process. The following details what you can expect before, during, and after the procedure.

The first step in getting a dental implant is to schedule a consultation with Dr. David Henry or Dr. Stephen Greer . During this appointment, your dentist will assess the condition of your teeth and gums, take X-rays or 3D images to determine if you are a good candidate for an implant, and discuss your options with you. Your dentist will start planning the implant placement procedure if you are deemed a good candidate.

Once the consultation is complete, the actual implant placement procedure can begin. During this process, your dentist will make an incision in your gum tissue and then carefully insert the implant into your jawbone. The implant is usually made of titanium and functions as a replacement root for the missing tooth. This procedure is generally done under local anesthesia, so you should not experience any pain.

After we place the implant, the next step is to wait for it to heal and integrate with your jawbone. This process can take several months, during which we will give you a temporary prosthetic tooth to wear. The healing phase is critical as it allows the implant to fuse with your jawbone, forming a strong and durable anchor for the replacement tooth.

Once your implant has fully healed, your dentist will place an abutment, which is a small connector piece, on top of the implant. The abutment will serve as the foundation for your prosthetic tooth.

Finally, the prosthetic tooth can be attached to the abutment. This restoration will be matched to the shade and shape of your natural teeth, giving you a seamless and beautiful smile.
