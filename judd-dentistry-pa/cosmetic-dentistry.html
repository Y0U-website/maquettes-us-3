FAQs for Cosmetic Dentistry

You can visit Judd Dentistry PA for professional cosmetic dentistry in Columbia, MD. We offer personalized smile-enhancing treatments for discoloration, chips, gaps, worn teeth, and alignment concerns.

Many patients notice a brighter smile after professional whitening in a short period. Results vary based on staining, enamel condition, and the whitening method recommended during your visit.

Dental bonding uses tooth-colored resin to reshape and repair chipped teeth. The material is applied, sculpted, hardened, and polished so it blends naturally with your smile.

Porcelain veneers can improve the appearance of mildly crooked, stained, worn, or uneven teeth. Severe alignment issues may require orthodontic treatment before veneers are recommended.

Judd Dentistry PA offers Invisalign® consultations to determine whether clear aligner treatment is right for your smile, bite, and long-term dental goals.

Coverage depends on your insurance plan and whether treatment is cosmetic, restorative, or medically necessary. Our team can review options and discuss available payment solutions.
