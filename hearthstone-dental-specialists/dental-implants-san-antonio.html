Your Journey to a New Smile: The Dental Implant Process

A brief step-by-step guide on what to expect during the dental implant procedure, from consultation to aftercare. With our skilled team guiding you through each step, you can feel confident in achieving your goals for an improved, healthy smile. Let’s get started!

Initial Consultation

We’ll conduct a thorough evaluation of your oral health and discuss options to determine if implants are right for you.

Treatment Planning

Next, we develop a customized treatment plan based on 3D imaging of your mouth and bite.

Implant Placement

During this minor surgery, the implant post is placed into the jawbone to serve as the tooth root. Healing time is required before the next step.

Abutment and Crown

An abutment is attached to the implant, then a custom crown is placed to create a natural-looking and feeling tooth.

Follow-Up Care

We’ll schedule regular visits to monitor your implants and oral health. Proper care keeps implants functioning optimally.
