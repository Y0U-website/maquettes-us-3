In those three years, Faith was able to get the opportunity to experience doing dental work at East Tennessee Children’s Hospital. She has develop the ability to create a comforting environment for not only adults, but children!

Destiny

Destiny has been in the dental field for 4 years now. She worked as a dental assistant in oral surgery for 2 years before continuing her education at Lincoln Memorial University, where she earned her degree in dental hygiene. In addition to her dental hygiene degree, she is also certified in the administration of local anesthesia as well as the monitoring and administration of nitrous oxide. She has now been practicing as a registered dental hygienist for 2 years.

Destiny is passionate about providing patients with the best oral care while also making sure they feel comfortable and at ease in the dental chair. Her goal is to help you leave with a healthy smile and a great experience… all while, yes, reminding you to floss.

Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband Jake. As newlyweds as of last September, they love traveling and seeing the world together. She also enjoys spending time with friends and family, and of course her yorkie Bru.

Adrienne

Adrienne is a skilled and compassionate Registered Dental Assistant (RDA) with over 15 years of experience in the dental field. She began her career after graduating from Walters State Community College in 2010 and has been dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered care ever since!

Along with her clinical expertise, Adrienne has extensive knowledge of dental insurance — helping patients understand their benefits, estimate treatment costs, and ensure claims are processed smoothly and accurately. Her well-rounded background makes her an incredible asset both chairside and at the front desk.

Adrienne also holds certifications in coronal polishing and nitrous oxide monitoring, allowing her to assist in a wide range of procedures while keeping patient safety and comfort top of mind. Known for her professionalism, attention to detail, and warm personality, Adrienne is truly a valued member of our team!

When she’s not in the office, Adrienne enjoys spending time with her husband and their family in Newport, Tennessee.
