Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the dental implant process?

At Moore Smiles in Sweetwater, we guide our patients through each step of the dental implant process to ensure a successful and comfortable experience.

Initial Consultation

The dental implant process begins with an initial consultation during which we will evaluate your oral health, review your health history, and determine if you re a suitable dental implant candidate.

Treatment Planning

Next, we create a plan outlining the steps involved in your implant procedure, the number of implants needed, and any additional treatments, such as bone grafting or extractions.

Dental Implant Placement

Performed under local anesthesia to ensure comfort, the implant placement procedure involves surgically inserting the dental implant post into the jawbone.

Abutment Placement

Next, we attach an abutment (connector piece) to the implant post. The final restoration will eventually be secured to the abutment.

Restoration

The final step in the process is placing your custom-made dental crown, bridge, or denture onto the abutment. We craft each restoration to seamlessly match your smile and complement your appearance. We even consider your eye and hair color, skin tone, bone structure, and more to ensure your new smile looks beautiful and natural.

Follow-Up Care

Regular follow-up appointments are essential to monitor the health and function of the implant.

How long do full arch implants last?

With proper care, your full arch implants can last a lifetime; however, the prosthetic teeth that are secured to the implants typically last between 10-15 years.

What Are Full Arch Implants?

If you’re missing an entire upper or lower arch , we can strategically place several dental implants into your jaw. As they heal, they’ll fuse with your jawbone, giving you a stable foundation for a full denture.

Instead of worrying about denture creams and adhesives to hold your denture in place, dental implants make it possible to eat all your favorite foods and smile without hesitation.

To increase the longevity of your implants and dentures, follow these tips:

  • Brush and floss your teeth each day.
  • Use a water flosser to clean around your implants.
  • Visit your Sweetwater dentist for routine exams and cleanings .
  • Try to avoid biting down on hard foods, as they can damage your dentures.
