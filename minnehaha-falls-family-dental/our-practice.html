Our Doctors

Dr. Jenna Swenson

Dr. Swenson’s awareness of the importance of compassion and communication while providing dental care began at a young age. Her older brother was born with cerebral palsy, and her family struggled to find a dental home for her brothers’ needs. With this personal experience in mind, Dr. Swenson is committed to providing quality and compassionate comprehensive care to all of her patients no matter their limitations.

Dr. Swenson grew up in Minnesota. Upon graduation from the University of Minnesota- Morris, Dr. Jenna Swenson wasn’t certain where her degree in Biology would take her. She accepted a position working in Immunology at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, MN, which she held for 4 years. At this same time, Dr. Swenson was also a student studying Immunology at Mayo Graduate School.

In Dr. Swenson’s spare time she completed volunteer work at a community clinic that provided medical and dental care. Volunteering at this community clinic was truly life-changing and rewarding. It was here she realized that her true calling was a career in dentistry.

Dr. Jenna Swenson graduated from the University of Minnesota, School of Dentistry in 2013. Following dental school, she completed a one year General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Minneapolis .

Through her work in the community and the VA Hospital, Dr. Swenson cultivated her desire to work closely with her patients, making them comfortable by listening and responding to their concerns. She feels strongly about educating and informing her patients. She takes time to explain procedures and options to patients in an easy to understand way. In addition, she understands that we all have unique and individual needs and by taking time to discuss treatment we are able to be partners in maintaining dental health.

Dr. Swenson has enjoyed being involved with organizations such as: Mission of Mercy, Give Kids a Smile and Ready, Set, Smile.

Dr. Swenson is a member of the American Dental Association , the Minnesota Dental Association , the Academy of General Dentistry , Seattle Study Club, Spear Study Club and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Swenson is also a certified Invisalign provider.

Dr. Joan Jakubas

For Dr. Joan M. Jakubas, nurturing strong relationships is as important as maintaining healthy teeth. She takes an avid interest in her patients’ lives and health. In turn, she and her team of dental professionals work hard to find the best solutions for their patients’ individual dental care needs.

Dr. Jakubas grew up in West Bend, Wisconsin. She attended Marquette University for her undergraduate training and graduated as a Dental Hygienist prior to attending Dental School. Dr. Jakubas worked as a Dental Hygienist for a few years prior to returning to school for her Dental Training.

Dr. Jakubas graduated from the University of Minnesota’s Dental School in 1987. She began private practice a year later.

Dr. Jakubas has a deep connection with the community in South Minneapolis. She enjoys donating her time and volunteering through Donated Dental Services, Give Kids a Smile, Mission of Mercy and Ready, Set, Smile.

She stays on top of the latest trends in dental specialties through post-graduate programs and study groups, ensuring patients receive the best treatment for endodontics, implants and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Jakubas has trained with the Pankey Institute and the Bioclear Institute. She is also a member of the American Dental Association , the Minnesota Dental Association , the Seattle Study Club and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Alexa Klunder

Dentistry has allowed Dr. Klunder to combine her love for artistic expression and critical thinking to enhance her patients’ quality of life. While patients are in her dental chair, Dr. Klunder aims to provide patients with a comfortable, informative, and pleasant experience.
