(972) 771-2213 Get a Full Smile with All-on-4 Dental Implants What are All-on-4 Dental Implants? Replacing an entire arch of missing teeth once required years of treatment, multiple surgeries, and long healing periods. All-on-4 dental implants have transformed that process. This advanced technique uses four strategically placed implants to support a full set of fixed replacement teeth, often allowing patients to receive a new smile in as little as one appointment.

Two implants are placed vertically in the front of the jaw, while two are angled in the back to maximize contact with available bone. This unique positioning helps many patients qualify even if they have been told they lack enough bone for traditional implants, often without needing grafting. The result is a secure, natural looking smile that restores comfort, function, and confidence.

  • A Full Smile In One Day Unlike traditional implants, All-on-4 places implants and temporary teeth in one visit, so you leave the same day with a full smile.
  • No Bone Grafts Needed Angled posterior implants anchor into denser jawbone, often eliminating the need for bone grafting and reducing treatment time and cost.
  • A Permanent Restoration All-on-4 implants support a fixed set of teeth that stay securely in place. No shifting, no adhesive, just long-lasting, natural function.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is ideal for patients missing most or all teeth who want a fixed, long-term solution. It’s especially helpful for denture wearers seeking better stability and for those told they lack enough bone for traditional implants.

With strategically angled implants, many patients can avoid bone grafting, reduce surgeries, and shorten healing time, making All-on-4 a faster, more efficient full-arch solution.
