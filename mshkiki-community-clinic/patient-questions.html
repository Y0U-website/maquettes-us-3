Patient Questions

0 How can I be seen at Mshkiki Community Clinic?

For more information about our services and to schedule an appointment, please call (269) 945-5854.

At Mshkiki, we accept ONLY Michigan Medicaid Health Plans and Dual Eligible Medicare-Medicaid Plans. If you are uninsured and think you might qualify for a Medicaid Health Plan, please give us a call and we can assist you with your application.

Can I be seen for an acute dental issue if I have not been seen before?

Yes, you can. We can see you for an acute issue. Mshkiki Community Clinic can be your Primary Dental Care Home going forward.

(269) 945-5854 — mshkiki.clinic@pokagonband-nsn.gov
