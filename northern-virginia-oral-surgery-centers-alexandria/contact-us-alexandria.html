How is corrective jaw surgery different from cosmetic surgery? Corrective jaw surgery, also known as orthognathic surgery , is a functional procedure performed to address problems with how the upper and lower jaws align. These issues can cause difficulty chewing, speaking, or breathing, chronic jaw pain, and excessive wear on the teeth.

While the results often improve facial balance and appearance, the primary goal is to restore proper function and long-term oral health. Our Alexandria surgeons work closely with your orthodontist to plan and execute jaw surgery as part of a coordinated treatment approach.

Every dental treatment plan is tailored to your individual concerns, and our team will recommend the path that best helps you achieve a healthy, comfortable outcome.

What role does platelet rich growth factor play in oral surgery recovery? Platelet rich growth factor (PRGF) is a regenerative therapy that uses a concentration of growth factors derived from your own blood to accelerate tissue healing after oral surgery.

When applied to a surgical site — such as an extraction socket, bone graft, or implant site — PRGF can promote faster soft tissue repair, reduce post-operative discomfort, and support more predictable bone regeneration. Our Alexandria surgeons incorporate PRGF therapy when it offers a meaningful benefit to your healing process.
