We recommend extraction of impacted wisdom teeth because they can cause problems in your mouth and with other teeth. Ask Manchester RVA Dental Center to show you the position of your teeth on the x-ray, and explain the significance of their position.

Wisdom teeth are the third set of molars, the chewing teeth that appear in the back of your mouth. They usually appear in your late teens or early twenties.

Ask Manchester RVA Dental Center can explain the details and options available for extraction of teeth. The removal of wisdom teeth is a common procedure and is only necessary if there is lack of room in the jaw or they are at an angle, crowding other teeth. Manchester RVA Dental Center focuses on the two best defenses against plaque, proper oral hygiene and regular visits . The goal is to preserve natural tooth structure, and detect any small problems before they become bigger ones.

In the long run, good hygiene and dental visits cost less. Teeth repair and replacement cost more and that is what you want to avoid.
