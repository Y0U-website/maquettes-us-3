The practice

Our goal is to deliver the highest quality standards of oral medicine, periodontal and implant care to our patients in a compassionate and friendly atmosphere.

Dr. Oakley provides a variety of periodontal (gum) therapies based on your individual needs.

Missing teeth lead to bone and tissue loss which is progressively detrimental. This can affect your bite, eating choices, and speech. When left untreated it often becomes impossible to restore the teeth with dental implants.

Your teeth and gums affect your whole body health. When they are healthy, you are healthier too.

Do you avoid smiling because of unsightly, sensitive teeth or excessive gum display (gummy smile)? Cosmetic periodontal surgery can help.

Les soins

When gum recession occurs the body loses a natural defense against bacterial penetration, cavities, and resistance to trauma created by eating and toothbrushing.

Dr. Oakley is a Board Certified Specialist in periodontology and dental implant surgery. He specializes in treatment of gum disease, including cosmetic procedures to enhance your smile and dental implants to replace any missing teeth.
