Dental implant restorations provide significant advantages over traditional tooth replacement options:

Compared to removable options, implant restorations offer a more secure and permanent solution.

  • Long-lasting and durable solution
  • Natural appearance and feel

What is the difference between a dental implant and a restoration?

The implant is the titanium post placed in the jawbone, while the restoration is the crown, bridge, or denture attached on top.

How long do implant restorations last?

With proper care and regular visits for dental cleanings , implant restorations can last many years.

Can a damaged implant crown be replaced?

Yes, in most cases the crown or restoration can be replaced without removing the implant itself.

Does implant restoration hurt?

The restoration process is generally comfortable and does not involve surgery. Any minor discomfort is typically minimal.

How do I care for my implant restoration?

Care includes brushing, flossing, and maintaining good oral hygiene along with routine dental checkups.
