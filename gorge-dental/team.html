Dr. Olsson’s Background

Dr. Andrew (Andy) Olsson graduated from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2006 with honors in implant dentistry. He began his dental career in 2006 working in public health at One Community Health in both Hood River and The Dalles, OR. In 2007 Dr. Olsson transitioned into private practice with Dr. Michael Murat at Columbia View Dental Care in The Dalles before opening his own practice, Gorge Dental in March of 2010.

Dr. Olsson is committed to continuing education and is known by his loyal patients, co-workers, and friends as a caring, compassionate, and honest individual.

Andy grew up in Bellevue, Washington and graduated from high school in Steamboat Springs, CO. He was an accomplished alpine ski racer who competed at an international level. Andy attended Whitman College in Walla Walla, Washington where he graduated with a Bachelors of Science in Biology in 1998.

In 1998 Andy moved to Hood River to learn to windsurf and fell in love with the Gorge–as well as his wife JJ. Andy and JJ have called Hood River home ever since. When not working you are likely to catch Andy, JJ and their daughter Pep on the river, up on the mountain, or out on the trail.
