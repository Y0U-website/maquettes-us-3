Restorative Dental Services

Restorative dental services focus on repairing damage to the teeth and gums to support long-term oral health. For some patients, restorative dental services can help improve their ability to chew, improve their speech, and help them feel more comfortable about their oral health.

  • Root canals remove inflamed or infected tissue inside the tooth and can help extend the life of a person’s natural tooth.
  • Crowns are necessary following a root canal or when medium to large amounts of tooth structure are missing or affected by cavities. Crowns can be white or gold for adults, while stainless steel crowns may be used on baby teeth when needed.
  • Extractions are needed once the tooth is deemed nonrestorable. After a tooth is extracted, there are typically multiple options to get the tooth replaced.
  • Bridges are a permanent solution used to replace one or more missing teeth and are attached to the teeth on both sides using crowns.
  • All-On-X Dental Implants are an option for people with very few to no remaining teeth who want a permanent solution to teeth replacement. Dr. Kessler partners with a local oral surgeon to offer a screw-less option for permanent full-mouth implant restorations at a very reasonable price.
  • Dental Implants are another option to replace missing teeth by surgically placing a dental implant in the upper or lower jaw and then attaching a crown after the implant heals. Our office can place new dental implants or restore existing implants.
  • Bone grafts are sometimes needed to create better bone structure prior to implants.
  • Dentures can help restore a person’s smile and make eating and speaking easier when they are missing multiple teeth. Options include conventional dentures, immediate dentures, and implant-retained dentures.
  • Partial dentures are a removable option to help replace some missing teeth while retaining the other natural teeth. There are flexible partial dentures, which are better for aesthetics, and metal partial dentures, which are better for chewing

Sedation Dentistry

We offer a range of sedation dentistry services in Edmond, OK to help patients experiencing dental anxiety receive the care they need.

  • Nitrous oxide , sometimes referred to as laughing gas, is a short-acting sedative that can help patients feel more relaxed while in the dental chair. The gas is out of your system within minutes and you are free to drive yourself to and from the dental appointment.
  • Conscious sedation is a moderate level of sedation typically administered by taking a medication the night before the appointment, as well as 1 hour prior to the appointment. This level of sedation requires that patients have someone to drive them to and from the appointment.
  • IV sedation is a form of unconscious sedation administered through an IV. This type of sedation requires that patients have someone to drive them to and from the appointment.
