Meet Dr. E

Dr. E (Dr. Ninus Ebrahimi) is a board certified pediatric dentist who has dedicated his career to providing exceptional dental care for children. With over 10 years of experience, Dr. E combines clinical expertise with a genuine passion for working with kids.

Education & Training

Dr. E received his Bachelor&#x27;s Degree in Psychobiology from UCLA before pursuing his dental education at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. His academic excellence was recognized when he was inducted into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU) Dental Honors Society.

Following dental school, Dr. E completed an additional two-year pediatric dental residency at Tufts Medical Center, where he received specialized training in caring for infants, children, adolescents, and patients with special healthcare needs.

Professional Experience

Dr. E has practiced Pediatric Dentistry for over 10 years, with experience in both Los Angeles and New York City. He was also appointed to the faculty at NYU College of Dentistry as a Clinical Instructor, where he helped train the next generation of pediatric dentists.

Our Philosophy

At North Star Pediatric Dentistry, we believe every child deserves a positive dental experience. Our goal is not just to treat teeth, but to help children develop healthy habits and a positive attitude toward dental care that will last a lifetime.

We take the time to explain procedures in child-friendly terms, create a welcoming environment, and ensure both children and parents feel comfortable and informed throughout every visit.

Why Choose Us?

Board Certified Expertise: Dr. E is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, demonstrating the highest level of training and expertise in pediatric dental care.

Child-Centered Approach: Our office is designed with children in mind, from the welcoming waiting area to the gentle, patient-focused care we provide.

Comprehensive Services: From first visits and preventive care to restorative treatments and emergency services, we offer complete pediatric dental care under one roof.

Convenient Location: Located in Westlake Village, we proudly serve families from Thousand Oaks, Agoura Hills, Calabasas, and surrounding communities.
