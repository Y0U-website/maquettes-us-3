What Sets Us Apart?

At Lilac Pediatric Dental, we pride ourselves on offering a unique and integrative approach to pediatric dental care. Our history encompasses both traditional and integrative dentistry, allowing us to provide well-rounded, comprehensive preventative and restorative treatments tailored to your child’s needs.

Family-Centered Philosophy

Custom Approach to Dentistry

Every child is unique, and so is our care. We develop personalized treatment plans that cater to the specific needs of each child. Our boutique, high-end practice ensures that your child receives the utmost attention and care in a comfortable and private setting.

Our integrative approach blends the best of traditional and holistic dentistry. This means we utilize modern techniques and technology while also considering natural and alternative methods. Our goal is to offer safe, effective, and gentle care that supports overall health.

Comprehensive Team Approach

We believe in a team approach to dental care. By working together with families, pediatricians, and specialists, we offer comprehensive and coordinated treatments. This collaboration ensures that all aspects of your child’s health are considered, leading to optimal outcomes.

At Lilac Pediatric Dental, our commitment to integrative, personalized care sets us apart. Our boutique, high-end practice, coupled with our family-centered philosophy and experienced team, ensures that your child’s dental health is in the best hands.
