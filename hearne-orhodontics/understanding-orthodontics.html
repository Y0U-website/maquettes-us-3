Understanding Orthodontics

Comprehensive Orthodontics for a Healthy, Beautiful Smile

In many cases, when patients pursue orthodontic treatment, they have one request: “Straighten my teeth” . While this is certainly possible to do, it is wise to take a complete look at the oral maxillofacial anatomy. Are there other things that can be improved? What about your bite? Or your jaw joints? Comprehensive orthodontics can address a wide array of issues in addition to making your smile beautiful. Here, we will explore the many elements of oral health that can be improved with well-planned treatment at Smile Suffolk Braces .
