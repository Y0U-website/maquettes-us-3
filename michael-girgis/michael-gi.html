Encore Dental – A Smilist Dental Company of Jackson, NJ now offers IV Sedation (Intravenous Sedation) for patients who undergo oral surgery. Medications are administered through the vein before the procedure.

When this happens the patient experiences “twilight anesthesia”, as it is a depressed level of consciousness, with the ability to respond to verbal commands. Most patients will not remember anything about the procedure that was performed under this type of anesthesia.

Supplemental oxygen is delivered through the nose, and the patient’s vital signs are closely monitored during and after the procedure. Many patients find IV Sedation helpful during wisdom teeth extraction procedures.

Invisalign® is the clear and removable alternative to braces. It’s an excellent, non-invasive type of orthodontics that Encore Dental – A Smilist Dental Company of Jackson, NJ offers with no metal brackets! Instead, the process involves custom-fit, clear plastic aligners that you wear to gradually move your teeth into the desired position.

Because the aligners are clear, they are virtually invisible. The process is subtle, ensuring that your daily life will not be affected by them. These are a great choice for patients with crowding, minor spaces between the teeth, overbites, and underbites. They also work well for patients of all ages.
