About Us

Who We Are

Excellence is at the heart of everything we do. Through education and top tier patient care and support, we treat patients of all ages with the highest standard of care and service. Above all, we express this excellence through the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of oral disease and health issues.

A Legacy of Family Dentistry in Southeast Texas

Leifeste Dental has proudly served patients across Southeast Texas and Southwest Louisiana for more than 50 years . Our longevity reflects a deep commitment to ethical care, clinical excellence, and long-standing patient relationships built on trust.

Generations of families have relied on our team for consistent, high-quality dental care delivered with integrity and compassion. While dentistry has evolved over the decades, our core values remain unchanged—treat every patient with respect, provide honest recommendations, and deliver care that supports lifelong oral health.

Clinical Leadership Rooted in Experience and Service

Her dedication to continuing education, mentorship, and professional involvement allows Leifeste Dental to remain at the forefront of modern dentistry while maintaining a personalized, relationship-driven approach.

Education

Dr. Nina Scales Leifeste was born and raised in Orange County. She graduated with highest honors from Bridge City High School and attended Lamar University, Beaumont, Texas, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 1984.

Dr. Leifeste attended dental school in Houston, graduating with honors from The University of Texas Dental Branch (UTDB) Houston, with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1988. She then completed a post-doctoral fellowship in General Practice and Hospital Dentistry through Hermann Hospital.

Professional Organizations:

  • The Academy of General Dentistry: Dr. Leifeste achieved Fellowship status with a commitment to career long continuing education.
  • The American Dental Association: Life Member
  • The Texas Dental Association
  • The Dental Society of Southeast Texas

Commitment to Lifelong Learning and Clinical Excellence

This dedication translates into precise diagnostics, conservative treatment planning, and outcomes designed to support long-term oral and overall health.

Community Outreach as a Core Mission

Community service is not an extension of our mission—it is a central part of who we are. Leifeste Dental is deeply invested in improving access to dental care for underserved populations through education, outreach, and hands-on service.

By supporting mobile dental programs, student mentorship, and charitable initiatives, Dr. Leifeste and her team work to close gaps in access to care while helping future dental professionals develop both skill and compassion.

Philanthropy

Dr. Leifeste is passionate about serving her community and beyond. This commitment can be seen in projects such as The Nina S. Leifeste, DDS Community Outreach Endowment , which supports the ongoing operation of the UT Health Mobile Dental Van in numerous areas of service.

The Mobile Van offers community outreach services to Give Kids A Smile, providing much needed dental care for children from low-income families, as well as Give Vets A Smile, serving our veterans without dental insurance or access to care.

The Mobile Van also provides hands on dental care and community learning experience for UT Dental Students directly to underserved populations in the Greater Houston area and rural East Texas, delivering free preventive care and treatment services to people who may not receive it otherwise.

Philanthropic Partnerships & Endeavors:

  • Assistant Professor at UT Health Houston School of Dentistry for over 30 years; clinical teacher/mentor to senior dental students;
  • Orange Christian Services Volunteer Dentist for over 13 years: staffing the Mobile Dental Van in collaboration with OCS and UT Health School of Dentistry
  • Established the Dr. William "Sam" Leifeste, DDS Memorial Scholarship @ UT Health Houston School of Dentistry; awarded annually to senior dental students exhibiting excellence in the discipline of Prosthodontics .
  • Established The Nina S. Leifeste, DDS Community Outreach Endowment
  • Co-Established The General Practice Residency (GPR) Endowment in Dentistry ; Directed for the use and support for the advancement of education, patient care and research within the General Practice Residency Program, in the Department of General Practice and Dental Public Health

A Collaborative Team Approach to Patient Care

Exceptional dentistry is achieved through teamwork. At Leifeste Dental, our clinical and administrative team works collaboratively to ensure every patient experience is seamless, informative, and comfortable.

Jessica Reinhardt

Jessica has been a dental hygienist for over twelve years and has had the pleasure of working with Dr Leifeste for the past three years.
