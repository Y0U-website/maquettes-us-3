Meet Dr. Jessie Chang

Dr. Jessie Chang has been helping families achieve confident, healthy smiles since 2008. Originally from Taiwan and raised in Vancouver, Dr. Jessie brings a rich blend of cultural care and world-class training to every patient she sees.

As both a mother and an orthodontist, Dr. Jessie understands the importance of gentle, personalized care — especially when it comes to little ones (and busy parents). Whether it’s your child’s very first orthodontic visit or you’re considering clear aligners for yourself, she offers thoughtful, flexible treatment options tailored to your family’s lifestyle.

At Mums Orthodontics, Dr. Jessie is honored to be part of your smile journey — and treats every patient the way she’d want her own family treated.

Dr. Jessie earned her dental degree from Columbia University and completed her orthodontic residency at UCLA.

Outside the office, you’ll likely find her in the garden, on a yoga mat, or sharing quiet moments with a good book and her family.
