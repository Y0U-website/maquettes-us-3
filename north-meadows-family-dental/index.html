The practice

Scaling and root planning is a gentle deep-cleaning procedure that removes plaque and tartar, smooths tooth roots, and helps treat and prevent gum disease.

Brighten your smile quickly and effectively with our in-office Philips Zoom Accelerator treatment, which uses LED light technology for fast results and includes a take-home kit to maintain your brighter, whiter teeth.

Dental fillings safely restore teeth weakened by decay or damage using natural-looking, durable, tooth-colored composite material, helping maintain healthy, strong smiles.

Dental bridges restore missing teeth, maintain proper tooth alignment and bite, and improve your smile, typically supported by crowns or implants.

Our customized TMJ treatments, including nightguards, bite adjustments, exercises, and Botox, relieve jaw pain, reduce muscle tension, prevent teeth grinding, and restore comfortable jaw function.

Soft tissue grafting restores receded or uneven gums by adding healthy tissue to protect teeth, improve gum health, and enhance your smile.

Custom night guards protect your teeth from damage caused by nighttime grinding (bruxism), reducing jaw pain, headaches, and tooth wear.

Dentures are custom-made, removable appliances that replace missing teeth—complete or partial—restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Les soins

What We Offer Cleaning & Exams Regular dental cleanings and exams keep your smile healthy by removing plaque and tartar, polishing your teeth, and helping our dental team identify and treat problems early using thorough exams and digital X-rays.

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that restore the strength, function, and appearance of damaged or missing teeth, providing a long-lasting solution for a healthy, confident smile.

When a tooth is beyond repair, dental implants provide a long-lasting solution by replacing the root with a metal post to support a crown that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth, expertly placed by our experienced periodontal specialist.

Invisalign uses custom, nearly invisible aligners to gently straighten teeth, offering a comfortable, removable, and effective alternative to traditional braces for a healthier, more confident smile.
