Our team stays up-to-date on innovative dentistry and best practices. We participate in continuing education and are consistently looking for ways to improve. Our commitment to delivering exceptional dentistry will always be a priority.

At Grove & Platt, not only do you get first-class dental care, you also get a collaborative environment with experienced dentists and staff. As a family based clinic, we always look out for our patient’s best interest.

We understand the importance of investing in excellent technology and creating a comfortable environment for our patients. Our advanced equipment and knowledge enables us to provide a variety of dental services for your entire family.

Our team has countless hours of training with all our equipment. Whether you need teeth whitening, a crown, dentures or routine hygiene treatment, you’re in good hands.

As a family owned dental clinic, we understand how life can get in the way of your dental care. We offer teledentistry and easy appointment scheduling.
