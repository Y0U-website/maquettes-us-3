  • Fully 3D Guided Implant Surgery Our doctors use state of the art implant planning software and 3d printers to remove uncertainties and utlilize the precision of fully 3D guided implant surgery. Using advanced imaging techniques, we create a detailed digital map of your mouth, allowing us to precisely plan the placement of your implants. This ensures optimal positioning for long-term success and natural-looking results.
  • Minimally Invasive Technique Our minimally invasive techniques prioritizes your comfort and recovery. It is designed to be gentle on surrounding tissues, promoting faster healing and reduced post-operative discomfort. You can now enjoy the benefits of dental implants with minimal disruption to your daily life.
  • Immediate Implant Placement Whenever possible, our surgeons will immediately place your implant, saving you time and simplifying the process. With this technique, we remove the damaged tooth and place the implant in a single appointment, streamlining your journey to a new smile.
  • Immediate Temporization In some cases, our doctors can even place a temporary crown on your implant on the day of the implant surgery. This means you can leave our office with a tooth or teeth on the same day as your procedure. No more waiting for months with gaps in your smile.
