BRACES AND ORTHODONTICS In San Antonio, TX

At Oliver Family Orthodontics in San Antonio, TX , we know that orthodontic care is about more than just appearance. Getting your teeth aligned properly is crucial for your oral health and overall well-being. Our wide range of orthodontic services is designed to meet the individual needs of each patient, ensuring that your treatment is both effective and comfortable.

Why is Orthodontic Treatment Important?

Having misaligned teeth and jaws can lead to:

  • Jaw pain and discomfort
  • Trouble with chewing and speaking
  • A higher chance of tooth decay and gum disease
  • Excessive wear on your tooth enamel
