Implant supported dentures are removable or fixed prosthetics that attach to dental implants embedded in the jawbone. Instead of resting solely on the gums, the denture connects to implant anchors for improved retention. This solution combines: The coverage of a full denture
  • The stability of dental implants
  • Reduced movement during daily activities

How the Process Works

The treatment process typically involves several stages.

Advanced imaging tools described on our Dental Technology page allow for precise implant planning.

In some cases, procedures such as Bone Grafting may be recommended to create adequate support before implant placement.

  • Comprehensive evaluation and imaging
  • Placement of dental implants
  • Healing period for integration
  • Attachment of the custom denture

Benefits of Implant Supported Dentures

Compared to traditional Full & Partial Dentures , implant supported dentures offer:

Patients in Danville and Berwick often appreciate the ability to eat more comfortably without relying on adhesives.

  • Improved stability while chewing
  • Reduced slipping or shifting
  • Enhanced speech clarity
  • Better jawbone stimulation
  • Greater overall confidence

Implant Supported Dentures vs. Other Implant Options

For some patients, fixed full-arch solutions such as All-on-Four Dental Implants or All-on-X may be considered. Implant supported dentures differ in that they may remain removable while still gaining the support of implants.

We review all appropriate options to help you determine the right balance between stability, cost, and maintenance preferences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are implant supported dentures removable?

Some designs are removable for cleaning, while others may be fixed in place. We discuss the best option for your needs.

How many implants are required?

The number varies depending on the arch and individual case. We determine this during your evaluation.

Is the procedure painful?

Implant placement is performed with appropriate anesthesia to ensure comfort. Mild soreness afterward is typically manageable.

How long does the process take?

Healing time varies, but we provide a clear timeline during your consultation.

How can I review costs before treatment?

We provide a detailed treatment plan outlining expected fees before beginning care. Payment options and financing details are available on our patient information page .
