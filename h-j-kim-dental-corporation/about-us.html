About Dr. Kim

Dr. Kim, a graduate of New York University (NYU) College of Dentistry (Class of ’01) has been working as a General Family Dentist since 2001. With her friendly and caring personality along with her background and experience in nursing and juvenile counseling, Dr. Kim truly enjoys working as a Family Dentist.

B.S. degree in Nursing - Seoul National University (Seoul, Korea)

M.S. degree in Counseling - California State University, Los Angeles (Los Angeles, CA)

D.D.S degree in Dentistry - New York University (New York, NY)

Member of ADA, American Dental Association

Member of CDA, California Dental Association

Member of AGD, Academy of General Dentistry

Member of SGVDS, San Gabriel Valley Dental Society

Member of NYU College of Dentistry Alumni Association
