What You Can Expect

Your First Visit

Your first visit starts with a warm greeting, followed by a thorough dental examination. This includes checking your teeth and gums, assessing oral health, and discussing any concerns you might have. Our state-of-the-art technology ensures a comprehensive evaluation, while Dr. Shanna and Dr. Travis make sure you understand each step of the process.

What to Bring

For your first appointment at Happy Teeth Dental Company, please bring a form of identification, your insurance information, and any relevant medical history. Also, consider bringing a list of questions or concerns about your dental health. This helps us provide you with the most personalized and effective care from the very start.
