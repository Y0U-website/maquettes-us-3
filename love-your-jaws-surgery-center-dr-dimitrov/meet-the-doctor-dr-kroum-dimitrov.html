Why Choose a Board-Certified Surgeon?

As a Board-Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon , Dr. Dimitrov has reached the highest level of certification in his field. This designation ensures that he stays at the forefront of surgical innovation and maintains the strictest safety standards for anesthesia and complex jaw reconstruction .

His determination and dedication to providing excellent oral and facial care make him a trusted choice for patients in South Miami , Coconut Grove , and the greater Miami area.

Dr. Kroum Dimitrov’s clinical specialties are:

  • Anesthesia, General, and IV Conscious Sedation
  • Robotically Assisted Dental Implant Surgery, Including Zygomatic Implants
  • Wisdom Teeth Management and Exposure of Impacted Canine Teeth
  • Extractions and Other Oral Surgeries
  • Facial Cosmetic Surgery and Facial rejuvenation with Botox, Fillers, Implants and Laser
  • Corrective Jaw Surgery
  • Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)
  • Facial Injury/Trauma Surgery
  • 3D Cone Beam Guided Surgery
  • Oral, Head, and Neck Pathology
  • Dr. Kroum Dimitrov is board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and the National Dental Board of Anesthesia. He is also certified in General Anesthesia/Deep Sedation, Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS), Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS), and Basic Life support (BLS).

A Foundation of Excellence in South Florida

Following his residency, Dr. Dimitrov joined the prestigious Sclar Oral Surgery & Implant Dentistry , a practice that has set the standard for comprehensive oral and maxillofacial care in South Florida for over three decades. By working alongside industry leaders and utilizing the most advanced surgical technologies, Dr. Dimitrov refined his skills in minimally invasive techniques and complex facial reconstruction.

Today, he leverages this 31-year legacy of innovation to provide his patients with predictable, high-quality results using the latest digital workflows and 3D surgical planning.

Beyond the Operating Room

Dr. Dimitrov believes that a disciplined mind and a healthy body are essential to being a precise surgeon. Outside the office, he is a dedicated martial arts practitioner and regularly participates in athletic events, including marathons. These pursuits reinforce the focus, endurance, and discipline he brings to every surgical procedure.

He is a passionate traveler and an enthusiast of art and music, often finding inspiration in the creativity and culture of the cities he visits. Above all, Dr. Dimitrov values the time he spends enjoying the company of his family and close friends, believing that a balanced life is the key to providing compassionate, centered care to his patients.
