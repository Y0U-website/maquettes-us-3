What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. Once the implant integrates with the bone, a custom-made crown is attached to create a restoration that is virtually indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants are a standalone solution that does not rely on neighboring teeth for support. They preserve your jawbone, maintain your facial structure, and allow you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

The Dental Implant Process

From consultation to your final restoration, here is what to expect at each stage.

Consultation & Treatment Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive examination, including digital X-rays and a 3D scan of your jaw. We evaluate your oral health, bone density, and overall candidacy for implants. Together, we will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your goals and budget.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is precisely placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Local anesthesia ensures your complete comfort. If needed, bone grafting may be performed at this stage to ensure a solid foundation for the implant.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your natural jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates an incredibly strong and stable anchor for your new tooth. A temporary restoration may be provided during this period so you can smile confidently.

Final Restoration

Once the implant has fully integrated, an abutment is attached and a custom-made crown is placed on top. The crown is carefully matched to the color and shape of your surrounding teeth, giving you a beautiful, functional smile that looks completely natural.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Find out if dental implants are right for you.

Most adults who are missing one or more teeth and are in good general health may be excellent candidates for dental implants. During your consultation, our team will assess several factors to determine if implants are the best option for you.

Ideal Candidates

  • Sufficient jawbone density
  • Healthy gums free of periodontal disease
  • Good overall health
  • Non-smoker or willing to quit
  • Committed to oral hygiene

May Need Additional Treatment

  • Low jawbone density (bone grafting may help)
  • Active gum disease (treatable first)
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Heavy smoking
