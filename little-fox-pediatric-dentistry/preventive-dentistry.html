Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. Typically, x-rays are recommended once a year, though children with a higher risk of oral disease may need more frequent scans.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Pediatric Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child&#x27;s mouth healthy and happy!

Nutritional Counseling

We believe in a "whole health" approach, which includes yummy, healthy food choices. It&#x27;s not always easy to change eating habits, but it&#x27;s definitely worth it. Eating well benefits not only your child&#x27;s smile but their overall wellbeing. Let us help guide you in creating tooth-friendly menus that the whole family will love!

Oral Habits

Building a strong foundation for healthy oral habits will establish your child&#x27;s dental routine for their entire lives. With our educational, compassionate care, we are here to give your child the guidance they need to have healthy dental hygiene that will stick with them for a lifetime.

Orthodontic Assessments

Every child’s smile develops at its own pace. Our orthodontic assessments help us monitor your child’s bite, tooth alignment, and jaw growth—so we can catch issues early and guide their smile in the right direction. The earlier we start, the more options we have to prevent or simplify future treatment.

Airway Assessments

Breathing well is just as important as brushing well. Our airway assessments check for signs of issues like mouth breathing, snoring, or restricted airflow that could affect your child’s sleep, focus, and development. With early detection, we can guide you toward solutions that support healthy growth and overall wellness.

Curodont

Curodont is a non-invasive treatment that helps stop early-stage cavities before they require a filling. Applied directly to the enamel, it supports natural tooth healing by promoting remineralization—giving your child’s smile a second chance to stay strong and cavity-free, without the drill.

Hydroxyapatite Application

Hydroxyapatite is a natural mineral that strengthens enamel and reduces sensitivity—perfect for growing smiles! We apply it to help protect against cavities and support overall tooth health, especially for kids who may be more prone to decay or have early signs of enamel wear. It’s a great alternative to fluoride too!
