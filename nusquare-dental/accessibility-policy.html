We aim to meet 218 S Newtown Street Road, Newtown Square, PA 19073 Call:(610)-735-7011, and related accessibility laws. This policy will be updated as needed with revisions clearly posted on the website.

  • Consistent, clear menus and site layout
  • Logical heading order for screen readers
  • “Skip to content” links
  • Descriptive page titles and navigation paths
  • High contrast text and background colors
  • Text that can be enlarged (up to 200 %) without losing functionality
  • Easy-to-read fonts and sizes
  • Color is never the only means to convey information
  • Useful images include alternative text (alt text)
  • Captions and transcripts for videos when available
  • Audio descriptions added when needed
  • Keyboard navigation for all links and forms
  • Visible focus indicators for keyboard users
  • Proper labeling of form fields and buttons
  • Clear error messages and adjustable time limits when possible
  • Screen readers (e.g., JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack)
  • Voice recognition programs
  • Screen magnifiers
  • Keyboard-only navigation devices
  • The webpage or feature you had trouble with
  • The issue you encountered
  • Your contact details for follow-up
  • Phone support for appointments and information
  • In-person assistance at the office
  • Providing documents in alternative formats upon request
