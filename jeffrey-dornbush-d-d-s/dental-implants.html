  • Collaborative Planning If surgical placement is required, our office will refer you to a trusted dental implant specialist for the surgical phase of treatment. Dr. Dornbush works closely with these specialists to ensure that each implant is positioned ideally for strength, comfort, and esthetics before the restorative phase begins.
  • Restorative Phase After healing, Dr. Dornbush fabricates the final restoration — whether that’s a custom abutment and crown , a multi-unit bridge , or a full-arch prosthesis . Every restoration is designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing both function and long-term durability.

💎 Why Choose a Prosthodontist?

As a prosthodontist with over 25 years of clinical experience, Dr. Jeffrey Dornbush specializes in complex restorative and implant cases. His expertise in occlusion, esthetics, and precision restoration ensures that every implant is placed and restored for long-term success — not just appearance.

At Aesthetic Dental Innovations of Marblehead , we combine digital imaging, occlusal analysis, and meticulous craftsmanship to achieve beautiful, functional results that stand the test of time.
