All-on-X

Overview

All-on-X is a comprehensive solution that replaces failing teeth or loose dentures with a full arch of fixed teeth. Using four to six strategically placed implants, we can restore your entire smile — often with a temporary set of teeth attached the same day.

Benefits of this

Eliminates the slipping, clicking, and discomfort of traditional dentures. Restores the ability to eat the foods you love — steak, apples, and more. Helps preserve facial structure and bone health. Fixed, stable teeth you can rely on every day .

Who is this for

Experience precise digital planning and sedation for dental implants. Enjoy immediate loading with a temporary smile after surgery.

Our process

Digital Planning & CT Analysis

We begin with a CBCT 3D scan to map your unique bone structure. Every implant position is digitally planned before surgery begins, ensuring safety and predictability.

Sedation & Comfort

Your comfort is our priority. We use IV or oral sedation so you can rest comfortably through the procedure.

Precision Placement

Using digital surgical guides, Dr. Hagar places the implants into optimal bone density. This approach typically avoids the need for separate bone grafting.

Immediate Loading (Teeth in a Day)

In most cases, we attach a temporary set of fixed teeth immediately after surgery. You leave with a smile, not gaps, while your mouth heals for the final zirconia restoration.
