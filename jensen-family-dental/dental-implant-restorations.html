How Long Does It Take to Restore a Dental Implant? How Long Do Implants Last?

The restoration process can take several months to a year, depending on the necessary healing time. However, the longevity of these restorations is a testament to their quality. While the crown itself is resistant to decay, it’s essential to maintain proper care to prevent fractures and wear. With adequate maintenance, dental implant restorations can last as long as individual crowns, if not longer.
