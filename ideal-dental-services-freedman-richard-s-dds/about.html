About

Founder Dr. Freedman wanted to create a practice where patients got excellent care, and got everything they need. Ideal Dental Services has a Periodontist and Orthodontist in house.

Dr. Freedman graduated from Tufts University in 1965, and New York University College of Dentistry in 1969. He has been practicing dentistry in North Babylon for 40 years.
