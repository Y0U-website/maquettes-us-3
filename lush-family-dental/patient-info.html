Financing

Your treatment plan will include a breakdown of all applicable fees, and we will inform you of all costs before treatment is administered. Charges are payable at the time of treatment. For your convenience, we accept cash and credit cards. We also offer in-house dental savings plans.

If special arrangements are needed, please talk to our office manager prior to receiving service.

We will submit all insurance claims for you. We will fully attempt to help you receive full insurance benefits; however, you are personally responsible for your account, and we encourage you to contact us if your policy has not paid within 30 days.

We do not accept Medicaid or Medicare at this time. We apologize for the inconvenience.

We want to help make investing in your care more manageable. That’s why we’ve partnered with Cherry to spread your costs into monthly payments. Take up to 60 months for costs up to $35,000. Qualified applicants may also receive 0 % APR options.

We have you covered with Kleer Membership

At Lush Family Dental, we are proud to be offering Kleer Membership- an in-house savings plan that helps you eliminate the hassle of insurance.

It’s an affordable, subscription-based savings plan that makes maintaining oral health easy. With Kleer, you pay a low monthly or annual fee directly to us, unlocking access to preventive care like cleanings and exams, plus discounts on treatments—all with no insurance, deductibles, or hidden costs. The best part? Enrollment is quick and online, giving you a transparent, personalized plan to fit your needs and budget.
