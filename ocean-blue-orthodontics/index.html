Leading orthodontist dedicated to creating beautiful smiles for over 20 years

Dr. Mark Weinberg has been creating beautiful smiles in Bay Shore for over 30 years. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and also completed his orthodontic specialty training at Tufts University.

Throughout his career, Dr. Weinberg has remained committed to continuing education, staying at the forefront of orthodontic advances. He is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists and has served on numerous professional boards.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Weinberg enjoys cooking, spending time with his family, and giving back to the Bay Shore community through various charitable initiatives. He’s also passionate about mentoring young orthodontists and regularly volunteers his time to teach at dental education seminars.

Memberships: American Association of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, Suffolk County Dental Association
