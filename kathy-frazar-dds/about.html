Check Out Our Office

A Better Experience & Better Results Advanced Technology We invest in cutting-edge dental technology to enhance your comfort, improve diagnosis accuracy, and deliver superior treatment outcomes. Our state-of-the-art equipment ensures you receive the best possible care.

Imaging & Diagnostics

Our advanced imaging suite includes the Trios intraoral scanner for comfortable, precise digital impressions, and the Prexion CBCT for detailed 3D x-rays. We use the Myotronics K7 and Tekscan systems for accurate bite analysis and TMJ diagnostics. Our Pearl AI X-ray software enhances cavity detection, while the Diagnodent laser cavity detector and Velscope oral cancer screening device allow for early, accurate diagnosis of potential issues.

Laser Dentistry

We utilize both Diode and CO2 lasers in our practice. These advanced tools allow us to perform many procedures with greater precision, less discomfort, and faster healing times. From gum treatments to cavity preparation, our laser technology ensures a more comfortable experience and optimal results.

3D Printer

Our in-house 3D printer allows us to create custom dental appliances, and temporary restorations with incredible accuracy and efficiency. This technology not only improves the fit and function of your dental work but also reduces wait times, allowing us to provide same-day services for many treatments.
