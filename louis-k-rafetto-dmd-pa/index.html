The practice

Oral Facial Surgery of Delaware welcomes patients seeking comprehensive care for complex structural concerns affecting the function and appearance of their smiles. As oral surgeons in Wilmington, Dr. Campbell and Dr. Rafetto provide orthognathic surgery and corrective procedures that can reshape the jaw and improve function.

Working with local dental health professionals to provide oral surgical services in the Wilmington area.

Dr. Campbell is certified in IV sedation and offers sedation options for complete comfort.

Our dental care team is committed to your healthy recovery and lasting results.

Dr. Conor Campbell is a highly skilled and compassionate oral surgeon joining us after completing his surgical residency in New York at Montefiore Medical Center. As a recent graduate, Dr. Campbell brings a fresh perspective and cutting-edge techniques to his practice, along with a commitment to excellence and patient-centered care.

He then completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency training at The Medical Center of Delaware in 1980 and received certification from the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons 1983.

Dr. Campbell prioritizes patient safety, comfort, and well-being throughout every stage of treatment. He takes the time to thoroughly educate his patients about their treatment options, answer questions, and address their concerns, empowering them to make informed decisions about their oral health.
