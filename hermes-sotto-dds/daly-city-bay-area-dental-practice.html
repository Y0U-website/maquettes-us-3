0 Neighborhood dentists dedicated and committed to your health and comfort. Sotto Dental is a people-centered practice where providing expert dental care is just the beginning. You can also expect a gentle touch in all our procedures, transparency in our services, and open communication for your peace of mind. Because we’re not just your dentists, we’re your neighbors too.

We provide all major dental procedures with expertise and a gentle touch.

Comprehensive exam including digital x-rays and oral cancer screening

Teeth cleaning for oral hygiene and gum disease prevention

Restorative procedures including fillings, crowns, root canals, bridges, implants, and prosthesis

Emergency dentistry such as toothache, gum swelling or any dental pain

Periodontal treatment for those with mild to moderate gum disease

Teeth whitening with Zoom (laser whitening) or home kit

Nightguards for jaw pain, headaches or grinding teeth

Cosmetic restoration with Bioclear and Veneers

If you have questions about any of our services, or about a procedure that isn’t listed above, contact us to speak with a friendly member of our team.
