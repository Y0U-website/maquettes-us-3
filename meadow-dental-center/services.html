Our Philosophy Is Simple

WE TREAT OUR PATIENTS THE WAY WE WOULD WANT TO BE TREATED.

When we first opened, we provided comprehensive exams, including oral cancer and periodontal screenings, cleanings, and any necessary x-rays, all for a flat fee. We will continue this practice because we found that many patients without insurance value a fair, straightforward pricing structure.

Our fee schedule is transparent, easy to understand, and readily available. Our team is always here to discuss it with you personally and welcomes your questions. After all, that’s how we would want to be treated ourselves.
