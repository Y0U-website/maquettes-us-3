0 Implant Retained Dentures Stability. Comfort. Convenience. If you have lost most or all of your teeth, implant retained dentures may be right for you. While traditional dentures can sometimes feel loose or unstable, implant retained dentures snap onto fixed implants. Implant retained dentures can give you increased confidence in chewing and speaking, as well as prevent bone loss that naturally occurs with traditional dentures. Call us today to see if you are an eligible candidate for implant retained dentures!
