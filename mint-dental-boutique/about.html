Meet Dr. Saterfiel

At The Dental Boutique, we want to change the way you feel about going to the dentist.

I grew up in a dental family. What’s a dental family? Well, my dad is an orthodontist. Both my sister’s and my godfathers are general dentists. My mom was an orthodontic assistant before she ever met my dad ha! My grandmother, “Honey” as she is charmingly referred to as, worked in a dental office for 30+ years, and my aunts, too! Basically, I come from a long line of people who just get teeth. We LOVE teeth. I knew when I was a child that I wanted to see people SMILE their biggest and best smiles.

I am a true southern girl. I love everything about the south, which explains why I stayed near home for college (LSU, Baton Rouge, class of ‘06) and also dental school (LSU School of Dentistry, New Orleans, class of ‘11). Tradition is big around here. I got married to Drew after my first year of dental school and right after graduation, returned home to focus on work, and also to start our family. 6 years later, we’ve had three offspring, Searcy Cate, Graham, and Monroe.

And if all that’s not enough of a blessing, I’ve chosen to practice directly next door to my dad, my confidant, and my dental role model. Doesn’t hurt that I’ve got some of Lafayette’s best dental specialists at an arm’s length away, talk about comprehensive care!

Let me introduce you to my 4th baby’, The Dental Boutique.
