Directions

From the interstate take exit 105B (Old Agency) and take a right onto Highland Colony Parkway at the roundabout. At the 2nd light take a right by BankPlus and then take your first right into the Renaissance.

Parking close to here will allow you to enter our courtyard by walking between Local 463 and Koestler Prime. You will see our sign on the building straight ahead over Zeenia and the door to our building is on the left side of that building.

1000 Highland Colony Pkwy, Suite 7201 Ridgeland, MS 39157
