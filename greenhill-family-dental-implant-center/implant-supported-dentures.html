Who Is a Candidate?

Implant-supported dentures may be appropriate for patients who:

The first step is a comprehensive evaluation to determine whether implant-supported treatment is appropriate for your situation.

  • Are missing most or all of their teeth
  • Currently wear dentures
  • Want greater denture stability
  • Have healthy gums
  • Have adequate jawbone or may qualify for bone grafting
  • Are in good general health for dental treatment

What Is the Treatment Process?

Every treatment plan is personalized, but the process generally follows several stages.

Comprehensive Consultation

We’ll examine your mouth, evaluate your existing dentures if applicable, review your medical history, and obtain diagnostic images to help determine your treatment options.

Implant Placement

Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Greenhill Family Dentistry may coordinate this phase of treatment with an experienced oral surgeon or periodontist.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the following months, the implants gradually integrate with the surrounding bone, creating a stable foundation for your restoration.

Fabrication of Your Denture

Once healing is complete, detailed impressions or digital scans are used to create your custom implant-supported denture.

Final Delivery

Your restoration is carefully fitted, adjusted, and evaluated to ensure proper function, comfort, and appearance.

We’ll explain your individual treatment timeline during your consultation.

Implant-Supported Dentures vs. Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures continue to be an excellent solution for many patients. However, implant supported dentures offer additional stability that some individuals prefer.

Traditional Dentures

Implant-Supported Dentures

We’ll help you compare both options and recommend treatment based on your individual needs rather than assuming one solution is right for everyone.

  • May reduce movement during speaking

Caring for Implant-Supported Dentures

Proper maintenance plays an important role in the long-term success of implant treatment.

Our team will demonstrate the best way to care for your specific restoration.

  • Cleaning around the implants every day
  • Brushing your restoration as instructed
  • Following recommended cleaning techniques
  • Maintaining regular professional dental cleanings
  • Scheduling routine examinations
  • Avoiding tobacco whenever possible

Frequently Asked Questions

How are implant-supported dentures different from traditional dentures?

Traditional dentures rest on the gums, while implant-supported dentures attach to dental implants, providing additional stability and support.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

Some are removable for daily cleaning, while others are fixed and removed only by your dental professional. We’ll discuss which option may be appropriate for your treatment plan.

Will implant-supported dentures feel more secure?

Many patients report greater stability and confidence compared to traditional dentures because the restoration is anchored to implants.

How many implants are needed?

The number of implants depends on your anatomy, the type of restoration being planned, and your treatment goals. We’ll discuss your personalized treatment plan during your consultation.

Can I convert my current dentures into implant-supported dentures?

In some situations, an existing denture may be modified, while other patients benefit from a new custom restoration. We’ll evaluate your current dentures and explain the available options.

Do implant-supported dentures require special care?

Yes. Daily cleaning around the implants and regular professional maintenance are essential to help keep the implants and surrounding tissues healthy.
