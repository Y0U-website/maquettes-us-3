Tooth replacement is about more than filling the gaps in your smile. It’s also about restoring your ability to chew properly, speak with confidence, and feel good about yourself. You want a restoration that is comfortable, permanent, and most importantly, undetectable. Dr. Levingart specializes in planning.

When you suffer from tooth loss, tooth implants are recommended over dentures. Although dentures have long been a staple of restoration dentistry, many find their temporary nature to be problematic. Poorly adhering pastes and sliding dentures can leave you feeling insecure about your smile, but with dental implants, you can regain your confidence. Eating, laughing, and talking becomes easy and…

After carefully planning your implant, Dr. Levingart is ready to insert the screw into the bone. The area will be thoroughly numb with local anesthesia and Nitrous Oxide may be offered to allow the most comfortable experience. The implant will be carefully placed by the doctor with bone grafting material and closed tightly with sutures.
