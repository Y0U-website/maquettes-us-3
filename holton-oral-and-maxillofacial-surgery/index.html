The practice

Extractions are done as a way to preserve your oral health and remove problematic teeth. In some cases, you may need to have teeth removed.

Wisdom teeth will typically begin to develop around the time a person reaches adulthood. Unfortunately, if there isn&#x27;t enough room for the teeth to come in properly, they become impacted underneath the gums.

Dental implants are a permanent solution for patients seeking that perfect white smile. Implants are a great choice for patients that have broken, damaged, or missing teeth.

Bone grafting is a surgical procedure that is essential for patients who have areas of bone loss around the jaw. Bone loss can often occur as a result of a variety of different conditions.

Orthognathic surgery is a type of surgery that is used to correct jaw and teeth misalignments. This can include anything from correcting a bite problem to repositioning the jaw.

IV sedation is typically necessary for oral surgeries and more complicated dental procedures. It involves placing a small IV into the arm and delivering a safe sedative into your system.

If you&#x27;re comparing treatment options, explore our full range of dental implants to better understand your choices.

The All-on-4 system uses four implants placed in areas of the jaw with the strongest bone support. These implants anchor a full arch of prosthetic teeth, often placed the same day as surgery.
