The practice

Erie, PA dentist Dr. Kevin Sambuchino and the rest of the team enjoy working together, and this positivity spills over into all areas of the practice. Our patients enjoy our low-key atmosphere that makes people feel comfortable when they are here.

Low key should not be mistaken for low tech. Dr. Sambuchino maintains a cutting-edge dental practice that takes advantage of all of the latest advancements in dental technology. His goal is to make dental care more comfortable as well as more affordable, and technology is one of the best ways to do that.

Patients see us for a full range of general, cosmetic, and restorative dental services. We see patients of all ages, from your youngest family member to the most senior. As a full-service practice, it is rare for Dr. Sambuchino to refer cases elsewhere, so you can expect to get most of your needed treatment right here in this office.

We offer preventive dental care, including examinations and cleanings. We are pleased to offer digital x-rays for improved accuracy as well as safety. We also provide root canal therapy, periodontal treatment, and TMJ/bruxism assessments.

Our cosmetic and restorative treatment options include teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental crowns, dental implants, dentures, and invisible braces. Dr. Sambuchino can also provide Botox Cosmetic and dermal fillers.

We understand that there are many reasons people avoid going to the dentist fear, cost, and time are just a few. If it’s been a while since your last dental appointment and you are embarrassed about the state of your teeth, don’t be. We can’t change the past, but we can set you on a course for improved oral health in the future.

We firmly believe in educating our patients, so we will take all the time that is needed to ensure you understand treatment recommendations. If you have questions about taking care of your oral health at home, we are happy to answer them.

There is an unfortunate trend these days for corporate dental practices with several dentists in the same office. Patients at these practices may see a different dentist every time, and there s no opportunity for relationship building.
