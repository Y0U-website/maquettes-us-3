Our Treatment Plans Treatment for the Whole Family

Lay the groundwork for a healthy smile with our Phase One Treatment. By addressing orthodontic issues early on, we guide the growth and development of young smiles, correcting bite problems and creating space for permanent teeth to erupt naturally.

Whether you’re a teenager or an adult, our advanced techniques and personalized treatment plans will address misalignment, crowding, and bite issues. From traditional braces to clear aligners, we’re dedicated to helping you achieve a stunning smile that boosts your confidence.

It’s never too late to enhance your smile. Our adult orthodontic treatments are tailored to your unique needs, providing discreet solutions for pronounced misalignment and crowding.

Clear Aligners

Achieve a straighter smile with the convenience and comfort of clear aligners. Our clear aligner treatment offers a discreet and nearly invisible way to correct misaligned teeth. Using advanced 3D imaging technology, a series of custom-made aligner trays are designed to gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions.

Made from smooth, transparent material, they are comfortable to wear and can be easily removed for eating, brushing, and special occasions. With clear aligners, you can straighten your teeth without disrupting your daily life or sacrificing your confidence. Experience the convenience and effectiveness of clear aligner treatment, and achieve the smile you’ve always wanted.

Braces

Braces are a time-tested and effective orthodontic treatment option for correcting misaligned teeth. With braces, a system of brackets and wires is carefully applied to your teeth to gradually shift them into proper alignment. Whether you need mild or extensive correction, braces can address a wide range of orthodontic issues, including overcrowding, gaps, and bite irregularities.

Our experienced orthodontic team will customize your braces to fit your unique needs and preferences. While braces require regular adjustments, they are a reliable and proven method for achieving a straight and beautiful smile. Embrace the transformative power of braces and unlock your full smile potential today.

Surgical Orthodontics

In some cases, surgical orthodontics may be necessary to achieve optimal results when dealing with complex bite issues or severe jaw misalignment. Surgical orthodontics involves collaboration between an orthodontist and an oral surgeon to address underlying skeletal problems that cannot be corrected through orthodontic treatment alone.

This comprehensive approach combines orthodontic appliances and surgical procedures to achieve proper alignment of the jaw and teeth. If surgical orthodontics is recommended for you, our skilled team will guide you through the process and ensure the best possible outcome for your smile.

Retainers

Retainers are an essential part of orthodontic treatment that helps maintain the results achieved through braces or clear aligners. Once your teeth have been aligned, retainers are custom-made to fit your mouth and hold your teeth in their new positions. Wearing retainers as prescribed by your orthodontist will ensure that your smile remains stable and prevents any unwanted shifting.

Retainers are typically worn full-time initially and then gradually transitioned to nighttime wear only. They are comfortable and discreet, allowing you to maintain your smile without drawing attention. By diligently wearing your retainers, you can preserve the beautiful results of your orthodontic treatment for years to come.

Whitening

Few things are more pleasing than a completely white and sparkling smile. Fortunately, we have several effective treatments available today that can help you achieve that whiter, brighter smile. One popular whitening option is professional teeth whitening. This involves the use of specialized bleaching agents that can effectively remove stains and discoloration from the surface of your teeth. The procedure is safe, quick, and can often provide noticeable results after just one session.

Another option is at-home teeth whitening kits. These kits consist of custom-fitted trays and a whitening gel containing a lower concentration of bleaching agents than professional treatments. While at-home kits may take longer to produce noticeable results, they offer the convenience of being able to whiten your teeth in the comfort of your own home.
