The practice

At Hassan and Liu DDS, we provide a wide range of dental services, including preventive care, restorative treatments, cosmetic procedures, and more. Our team is dedicated to ensuring your oral health and giving you a confident smile.

​ Our administrative staff is ready to help you with questions about scheduling, financial policy and insurance, to make that part of the process as simple as possible.

Regular check-ups and cleanings help maintain your oral health and prevent dental issues.

Restorative dental treatment is essential for repairing damaged or missing teeth, helping to restore both function and appearance. Whether it s through fillings, crowns, bridges, or dental implants, these treatments not only improve your smile but also enhance your ability to eat, speak, and live comfortably.

Our highly trained providers and staff are dedicated to ensuring you have a pleasant visit and achieve results you’ll be proud to show off.

Enhance the appearance of your smile with our cosmetic dentistry options, including teeth whitening and veneers.

We seek to provide the highest level of care in all aspects of general, cosmetic, and family dentistry and can literally redesign your smile!
