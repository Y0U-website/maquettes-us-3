Dr. Matthew Burns, DDS – General Dentist in Joliet, IL

Dr. Matthew Burns is a general dentist at Magis Dental in Joliet, IL, providing comprehensive preventive and restorative dental care for patients of all ages. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry and is committed to delivering thoughtful, patient-centered care.

Dr. Burns is known for his clear communication, gentle approach, and dedication to helping patients feel comfortable and informed throughout their treatment.

Professional Experience

Magis Dental OWNER

In 2023, Dr. Burns acquired Pellegrini Dentistry from Dr. Richard Pellegrini in Joliet, Illinois, continuing the practice’s long-standing commitment to compassionate, high-quality dental care. As owner and dentist at Magis Dental, he proudly serves individuals and families throughout the Joliet community.

Naperville Commons Dental DENTIST

After relocating to the Chicago area in 2018 with his wife and young children, Dr. Burns practiced at Naperville Commons Dental, where he spent five years providing comprehensive dental care and building lasting relationships with patients.

United State Air Force LT.

Dr. Burns proudly served as a Lieutenant in the United States Air Force and continues to serve as a reservist. His military background reflects a strong commitment to integrity, discipline, and service—values that guide his approach to patient care.

Education

Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) Western University Of Health Sciences

Dr. Burns completed his dental education at Western University of Health Sciences, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2014. His training emphasized comprehensive patient care, evidence-based dentistry, and a strong foundation in preventive and restorative treatment planning, preparing him to deliver high-quality care to patients of all ages.
