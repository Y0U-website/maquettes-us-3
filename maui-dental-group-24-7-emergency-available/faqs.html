Does Maui Dental Group offer emergency dentistry?

Yes. Maui Dental Group provides 24/7 emergency dental services seven days a week. Emergency appointments are available for urgent situations including severe tooth pain, broken teeth, knocked-out teeth, dental abscesses, and other acute dental problems. After-hours emergency services may have additional fees. Call or text (808) 633-6931 to schedule an emergency appointment.

Does Maui Dental Group offer virtual consultations?

Yes. Maui Dental Group offers telehealth video consultations with same-day appointments available. Virtual consults are ideal for initial assessments, follow-up questions, emergency triage, or determining if an in-person visit is needed. Book a virtual consult through the contact form or call (808) 633-6931.

Does Maui Dental Group offer pediatric dentistry?

Yes. Maui Dental Group provides comprehensive pediatric dentistry for infants, children, and adolescents. The American Dental Association recommends a first dental visit by a child’s first birthday, and Dr. Gupta welcomes patients as young as one year old. These early visits focus on education, cavity prevention, and evaluating baby teeth development. Parents are welcome to stay in the room during treatment. The practice also offers hospital-based sedation dentistry for children who need extensive treatment completed in one visit while sleeping.

Does Maui Dental Group offer cosmetic dentistry?

Yes. Maui Dental Group offers cosmetic dentistry with a prevention-based, conservative philosophy. The practice believes "less is more" and prioritizes maintaining natural teeth over aggressive treatments like veneers or crowns. Cosmetic services include professional teeth whitening, cosmetic bonding, white fillings (no mercury/amalgam), crowns, and minimally invasive smile enhancements. Dr. Gupta completed externships in cosmetic procedures and received an Achievement Award in Implant Dentistry from UCSF.

Which insurance does Maui Dental Group accept?

Maui Dental Group does not accept dental insurance directly. Payment is due at the time of service. However, the practice is happy to provide you with a completed claim form that you can submit to your insurance company for reimbursement. This allows you to receive care without waiting for insurance authorization while still maximizing your insurance benefits through direct reimbursement. Thank you and Aloha.

Can Maui Dental Group help with my broken tooth?

Yes. Maui Dental Group treats broken teeth as dental emergencies with same-day and after-hours availability. Treatment options depend on the severity of the break and may include dental bonding, crowns, root canals if the tooth’s nerve is exposed, or extractions if the tooth cannot be saved. Call or text (808) 633-6931 immediately if you have a broken tooth, and the practice will schedule you as quickly as possible, including evenings and weekends if needed.

Yes. Maui Dental Group offers professional teeth whitening as part of their cosmetic dentistry services. Professional whitening provides superior results compared to over-the-counter products and is customized to your teeth and desired outcome. Does Maui Dental Group do crowns?

Yes. Maui Dental Group provides dental crowns for teeth that are severely damaged, weakened, or require restoration after root canal treatment. Crowns are used when fillings are insufficient to restore tooth structure and function. The practice uses high-quality materials and prioritizes conservative preparation techniques to preserve as much natural tooth as possible. Dr. Gupta’s training includes advanced crown preparation and her philosophy emphasizes long-lasting, functional, and aesthetically pleasing results.
