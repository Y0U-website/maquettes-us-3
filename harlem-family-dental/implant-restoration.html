What Is Dental Implant Restoration?

Dental implant restoration involves the placement of a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture on top of a dental implant. This process completes the dental implant procedure, restoring the function and appearance of missing teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restoration

Natural Appearance: Our restorations are designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and attractive result.

Durability: Implant-supported restorations are highly durable and can last for many years with proper care.

Improved Function: Dental implants restore the full function of your teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Bone Health: Implants help preserve the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.

Types of Dental Implant Restorations

Single Tooth Replacement: A single dental crown is used to replace one missing tooth, providing a natural look and feel.

Multiple Tooth Replacement: Implant-supported bridges can replace several missing teeth in a row, offering a stable and long-lasting solution.

Full Arch Replacement: Implant-supported dentures or full-arch bridges can replace all the teeth in the upper or lower jaw, providing a secure and comfortable fit.

The Dental Implant Restoration Process

Consultation and Examination: Your dentist will evaluate your oral health and discuss your restoration options to determine the best solution for you.

Implant Placement: If you haven&#x27;t already undergone implant placement, this surgical procedure will be performed to place the implant posts into your jawbone.

Healing Period: After implant placement, a healing period of several months is necessary to allow the implants to integrate with the jawbone.

Restoration Placement: Once the implants have fully integrated, your custom-made crown, bridge, or denture will be attached to the implants, completing the restoration process.
