What Conditions Does Surgical Orthodontics Treat?

Surgical orthodontics is used to address a range of skeletal bite concerns, including:

Severe Overbite or Underbite

When the upper and lower jaws are misaligned, jaw surgery braces may be required to correct the relationship between them.

Open Bite

An open bite occurs when the front or back teeth do not meet properly, often requiring orthodontic surgery for long-term correction.

Facial Asymmetry Related to Jaw Position

Jaw position can influence facial balance. Corrective orthodontics combined with surgery may be recommended in these cases.

Bite Discrepancies Affecting Chewing or Speech

When jaw alignment interferes with normal function, surgical orthodontic treatment can help restore balance.
