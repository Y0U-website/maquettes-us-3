Our Practice

We’re making smiles great in Auburn, AL

We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a soothing environment and a scheduling system that never keeps you waiting long. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the best care in the District.

Jason Washburn, D.M.D.

Dr. Washburn is a native of Alexander City, Alabama and received his undergraduate degree in Microbiology from Auburn University. He received his doctorate degree from UAB School of Dentistry and graduated with honors in the top ten of his class

He has practiced in Auburn since 2002 and enjoys serving his church and local community. He and his wife Dru have three boys; Jackson, Will and Sam.

Dr. Washburn has served as President of his local Dental Society and attends multiple hours of continuing education courses in order to offer the latest and best care possible.

He enjoys church activities, sporting events, playing guitar and going on annual international mission trips.

Andrew Donaldson, D.M.D.

Dr. Donaldson is an Auburn native and a 1995 graduate of Auburn University and has proudly served the dental needs of the residents of Auburn, Alabama, and its surrounding areas since 2003. He is a 2001 graduate of the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry.

Dr. Donaldson is a member of the American Dental Association, the Alabama Dental Association and a past president of the 9th District Dental Association.

He is married and has 3 boys and is involved in sponsoring and coaching youth sports. He is an active member of Auburn United Methodist Church. His hobbies include reading, playing and watching sports, and running.
