The practice

The office will be located in the Awakea at Mehana apartments directly across the neighborhood park

We are a private practice dental office located in Mapunapuna and Kapolei who pride ourselves on our customer service. We believe that our customer service, friendly staff and technology set us apart from other offices. We provide a wide range of services which include cosmetic fillings, crown and bridge, root canals, extractions, invisalign, dentures, and we enjoy working with children.

We are committed to providing you with the highest standards of personalized dental care in a warm and caring environment. We believe that dentistry isn’t how we treat your teeth; it’s about how we treat you.

Your initial appointment will consist of a new patent exam and a series of digital x-rays followed by a cleaning and a consultation.

Leong Family Dental is open Monday through Friday 7:00am - 5:00pm and Saturdays 7:30am - 1:00pm. If you are in an emergency situation we will make every effort to see you that day.

If this is your initial appointment, you can request new patient forms by calling our office.
