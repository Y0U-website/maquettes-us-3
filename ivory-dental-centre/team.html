Dr. Ivory Hancock CEO + FOUNDER

Dr. Ivory Hancock , CEO + Founder of IVORY Dental Centre shapes her patient’s smiles through an extraordinary passion, built on a wealth of experience in the dental field.

She began her study of the science at Howard University , where she majored in Biology and minored in Chemistry. Upon graduating, she went on to pursue her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Temple University’s School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, PA. After completing her residency at Bronx-Lebanon Hospital Center in New York she furthered her training as Chief Resident. An avid proponent of further education and perfecting her craft, she then moved on to advanced cosmetic restorative dentistry instruction at the prestigious Rosenthal Institute at NYU School of Dentistry .

Professional Affiliations: American Dental Association Academy of General Dentistry American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation

Dr. FATMI

Dr. Fatmi is a skilled and experienced dentist with a proven track record providing comprehensive dental care. She is proficient in a wide range of procedures, including extractions, root canals, denture adjustments, and full mouth rehabilitation cases. Dr. Fatmi particularly enjoys working with patients on cosmetic cases, such as veneers, crowns, bridges, and implant restorations, to help them achieve their desired outcomes. She is dedicated to educating patients on recommended care and promoting oral health.

Dr. Fatmi holds a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Howard University College of Dentistry and has completed her Residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Howard University, where she served as Chief Resident.

Throughout her academic and professional journey, Dr. Fatmi has actively held leadership positions in various dental organizations. She has served as President of the Howard American Dental Education Association (ADEA), Vice President of the Howard American Student Dental Association (ASDA), and Secretary of the Howard Hispanic Dental Association (HDA). Additionally, as an Admissions Committee member, she has played a crucial role as a pre-dental applicant interviewer.

In her free time, Dr. Fatmi enjoys reading, visiting museums, traveling, attending concerts, and spending time with her friends and family. Originally from Miami, FL, she has been living in Washington, D.C. for the past 7 years. Dr. Fatmi is fluent in Spanish, Urdu, and Hindi, and she is also certified in scuba diving.
