As Featured On

At Oasis Dentistry, we go beyond traditional dental care by merging advanced treatment with luxury and relaxation. We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful, which is why we’ve created an experience that soothes both the mind and body.

With the addition of VLUXE Spa located on the second floor of our Concord location, we offer a holistic approach to patient care. Our spa services are designed to help ease dental anxiety and fear, allowing patients to relax before, during, and after their treatments. From calming facials and massages to serene aromatherapy, VLUXE Spa transforms your visit into a tranquil escape, making dental care something you can actually look forward to.

Led by Drs. Antonio and Veronica Landers, Oasis Dentistry is more than just a dental practice, it’s a patient-centered sanctuary where every detail is designed for your comfort and confidence. From massaging treatment chairs to a highly skilled and compassionate team, we provide a level of care that is second to none.

With two locations in Charlotte and Concord, NC, we offer convenience, cutting-edge dentistry, and a seamless connection to luxurious spa treatments all under one roof.

Meet Our Founders

Dr. Veronica Landers

Driven by her passion for aesthetic dentistry and total well-being, Dr. Veronica Landers saw firsthand how anxiety affected her patients’ experiences. Determined to change this, she founded VLUXE Signature Spa, a sanctuary where dental care and relaxation intertwine. Inspired by her travels to world-renowned spas, she created an environment that helps patients feel calm, confident, and stress-free before their treatments, allowing them to embrace self-care as part of their wellness journey.

A Mississippi native, Dr. Landers has called Charlotte home since 2009. She is an award-winning cosmetic dentist and an active member of: American Med Spa Association American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry American Dental Association North Carolina Dental Society

She has also completed advanced training in Invisalign, Laser Dentistry, and Cosmetic Dentistry.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Landers is deeply involved in community service, supporting organizations like the March of Dimes, Boys Girls Clubs, and Habitat for Humanity. She enjoys traveling, cooking, and spending time with her husband and their three children Isabella, Penelope, and Ethan.

Dr. T. Antonio Landers

With a career spanning dentistry, military service, and community outreach, Dr. T. Antonio Landers brings an exceptional level of expertise and dedication to patient care. As Enlisted and Officer in the Army, Army National Guard and Reserve with over 30 years of service, he has deployed overseas to Germany, Korea, Iraq, and Afghanistan acquiring invaluable experience in leadership and healthcare.

Born in Colon, Panama, and raised on the East Coast, Dr. Landers is a graduate of the University of Mississippi Medical Center School of Dentistry. He remains at the forefront of dental advancements through active participation in: American Dental Association North Carolina Dental Society Charlotte Dental Society International Dental Implant Association (Fellowship)

Dr. Landers shares his wife’s passion for providing exceptional care with a focus on patient comfort. He is committed to making dentistry a stress-free and positive experience for all patients, helping them feel at ease from the moment they walk through the doors and back to their active lives.

When not caring for patients, Dr. Landers enjoys traveling, golf, and spending time with his family. He is also an active member of PHA Free Masonry (PM, DDGM) and Lifetime member of Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc.
