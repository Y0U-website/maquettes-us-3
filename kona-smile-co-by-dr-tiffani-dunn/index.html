Meet Dr. Tiffani Dunn

At the office of Dr. Tiffani Dunn, you can expect modern, gentle dentistry that is personalized to your unique needs. We proudly employ biomimetic (minimally invasive) dentistry…

The practice

Dr. Dunn graduated from the Medical College of Georgia in 2009 and has made Kona her home ever since.

Since her graduation, she has continued to pursue the top courses for continuing education. She is a Kois Graduate, the first and only female in the state of Hawaii, and is accredited by the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry where there are less than 450 accredited members in the world. And that’s just the beginning. Learn more about Dr. Tiffani Dunn below!

We go above and beyond to ensure your comfort, offering advanced, gentle dental care along with spa-like amenities such as hot towels. Our dentist will take his time to fully understand your dental concerns and goals, so you can receive top-quality treatment that is customized to your individual needs.
