Financing and Insurance

At Morrell Orthodontics, we believe that everyone deserves access to quality orthodontic care. Our in-house financing includes free financing, adjustable low down payment, and flexible monthly payment plans.

We understand the importance of maximizing your insurance benefits. That’s why we gladly accept most insurance plans, whether in or out of network. Our experienced team will work closely with your insurance provider to ensure you receive the maximum coverage available. We also accept FSA (Flexible Spending Account) payments.

Rest assured, your treatment fee will include all treatment, retainers, retainer adjustments, and future retainer checks – ensuring a worry-free experience for your new, confident smile.
