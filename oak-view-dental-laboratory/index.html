Oak View Dental Lab is your resource for high-quality implant solutions from a wide variety of implant systems, including the following: BioHorizons®, Atlantis™, Zimmer Biomet®, Straumann®, Nobel Biocare®, and more. We offer numerous implant restorations to perfectly fulfill the needs of your patient, whether they need a single abutment or a full mouth reconstruction.

Our implant team fabricates high-quality titanium and zirconia custom abutments that will provide an ideal emergence profile and shape. Through the use of CAD/CAM technology, our team ensures a natural fit and a long-lasting solution. All our custom abutments are compatible with most major implant manufacturers.

Screw-retained crowns are an ideal option due to their elimination of cement from the operatory. The leading cause of implant failure is excess cement left below the margin during oral surgery. Screw-retained crowns are easily retrievable for maintenance and initial seating is efficient. The maryland bridge restorations from Oak View provide a more conservative solution for a missing anterior tooth. We utilize high-quality materials to craft this restoration to ensure a durable, long-lasting product.
