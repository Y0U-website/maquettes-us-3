PROTECT YOUR TEETH THE WAY THE PROS DO

The US Ski and Snowboard Teams have chosen Dr. Harding as the dental care provider for their athletes and families. They expect a level of professionalism and advanced techniques to keep their athletes healthy, and those same skills are available to you at the Harding Dental Group.

At Harding Dental Group, our team of dental professionals are up to date with the latest advances in dental care and comprehensive restorative dentistry. The state-of-the-art equipment necessary to protect, repair, improve, and enhance your healthy mouth is available to you right here in Alamosa, Colorado.

THE BASICS TO BEYOND GOOD DENTAL CARE BEGINS IN CHILDHOOD WITH PREVENTATIVE DENTAL TREATMENT.

As we age, our dental needs become more complex, from fillings to restorative dentistry such as implants, and treatments for gum disease. James Harding, DDS can assist with all kinds of dental problems such as sleep apnea, teeth grinding, and TMJ disorder.

Harding Dental Group offers a full continuum of care for your changing dental needs, from the mechanics of a single tooth to the healthy functioning of your entire mouth and benefit from our cosmetic approach to making all our work as beautiful as it is functional.

OUR PHILOSOPHY IS SIMPLE : THE MORE YOU UNDERSTAND, THE BETTER YOUR CHOICES.

A healthy smile is connected to your overall health and well-being. With proper teeth, you can chew better and improve your nutritional choices. Healthy gums prevent the development of certain illnesses. Attractive teeth can improve your self-confidence.

You may have never considered issues such as how nutrition affects the shape of your palate, how sleep apnea can be corrected through dental remedies, or why headaches might stem from grinding your teeth. We can educate you on these and other issues so that together we can work to maintain your healthy mouth.

LEADING THE WAY WITH CONTINUOUS ADVANCED EDUCATION

Continuing education is our top priority. In fact, Dr. Harding earns an average of 200 continuing education credits each year by participating in courses in advanced dentistry and teaching these to other dentists around the world.

The Harding Dental Group uses state-of-the-art dental equipment such as digital radiographs and intraoral cameras. This equipment enables our highly trained staff to perform the latest dental procedures at the office every day, keeping our patients one step ahead in their oral health.

Call to schedule your consultation so we can educate you on the many options for enhancing your smile.
