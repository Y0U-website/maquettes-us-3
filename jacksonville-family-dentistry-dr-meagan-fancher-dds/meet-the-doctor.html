About

During college, Dr. Fancher took a mission trip to Peru. Hiking from village to village, she offered toothbrushes and dental hygiene instruction to the villagers. In 2019, she also took a mission trip to Brazil where she performed treatment on patients there. These trips helped her find her passion in life to make a difference in people’s lives. She measures her success by every smile.
