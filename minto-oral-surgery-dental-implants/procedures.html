Procedures

We Offer a Variety of Procedures

Nobody wants to go through life with broken or decaying teeth, with big gaps in their smile, or with limited functionality of their mouth. Thankfully, there are oral surgery solutions to address a wide range of pathologies, restoring smiles to their full utility and aesthetics. Dr. David Minto and his team are proud to offer a range of treatments to patients in the Fairhope, AL area.

At our practice, the top priorities are patient outcomes, comfort, and safety. When you join us for your initial consultation, Dr. Minto will be glad to answer any questions about your oral surgery needs and to outline a recommended course of treatment, which may include any of the treatments listed below.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

When left untreated, wisdom teeth may lead to significant pain as well as to misalignment of the surrounding teeth. Our practice can provide prompt, comfortable wisdom teeth removal.

Bone Grafting

Some patients lack the bone tissue required for successful dental implant surgery. Through bone grafting, Dr. Minto can restore tissue and prepare you for your next procedure.

Tooth Extractions

Patients need teeth to be removed for any number of reasons: Their teeth are damaged, broken, or decaying. Minto Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery is pleased to provide extractions.

Sedation Dentistry

We seek to provide each patient with a comfortable procedure, which generally means sedation. We can offer different sedation options depending on the nature of the procedure and the needs of the patient.
