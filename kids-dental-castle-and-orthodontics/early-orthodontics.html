Phase 1 vs. Phase 2 Orthodontics

Phase 1 treatment takes place while a child still has some baby teeth and focuses on specific problems involving jaw growth, bite development, or space for permanent teeth.

Phase 2 treatment typically begins after most or all permanent teeth have erupted. This phase may involve braces or clear aligners/Invisalign to achieve final tooth alignment and bite correction.

Not every child who receives an early orthodontic evaluation will need Phase 1 treatment. If early treatment is not necessary, we can monitor your child’s growth and recommend treatment at the appropriate time.

What to Expect at Your Child’s Orthodontic Evaluation

At your child’s orthodontic evaluation, we will assess tooth alignment, bite development, jaw growth, spacing, crowding, and the eruption of permanent teeth. If needed, diagnostic images may be used to better evaluate your child’s developing smile.

After the evaluation, we’ll explain whether your child would benefit from early treatment, should be monitored as they grow, or can wait until the permanent teeth have developed further.
