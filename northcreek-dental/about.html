About Us

Your caring Goodlettsville dental team

Experience the Northcreek Dental difference

We understand that visiting the dentist might not be your favorite thing, but we’re here to change that. For over 37 years, we’ve proudly served the Goodlettsville community as a family-owned practice that values quality and genuine connections.

We take time to know you and put your well-being first, ensuring every visit feels personal, comfortable, and unhurried. Here, you’re not just a patient—you’re part of our family.

Dr. R. Dean Lang

Originally from Southern California, Dr. R. Dean Lang knew from an early age that he wanted to follow in his father’s footsteps and become a dentist. He earned his B.S. in Biology, graduating Cum Laude from Brigham Young University–Hawaii, with minors in Portuguese and Biochemistry, before completing his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California. Dr. Lang has practiced in California, Florida, and now Tennessee, and is dedicated to providing patient-centered care where every individual feels heard, respected, and treated like family.

Committed to service, Dr. Lang has volunteered with the AYUDA Dental Humanitarian Group, participating in local clinics and international missions in Mexico, Bolivia, Paraguay, and Peru. He also spent two years in Portugal on a service mission, becoming fluent in Portuguese; he is also fluent in Spanish, which he enjoys speaking with patients. Outside the office, Dr. Lang and his wife, Alene, are raising three sons who are their greatest joy.

Dr. Ted A. Beazley

Dr. Ted A. Beazley is a Nashville native who knew as early as the 8th grade that he wanted to become a dentist. After graduating from David Lipscomb College with a B.S. degree, he went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennessee in 1986. He began practicing in Madison, Tennessee, before relocating his practice to Goodlettsville in 2002.

With nearly four decades of experience, Dr. Beazley is dedicated to providing patients with the right treatment at the right time. His patient-centered philosophy emphasizes communication, individualized care, and building lasting relationships. Serving generations of families, he considers it an honor to care for his community.

  • D.D.S., University of Tennessee (1982–1986)
  • Life member of the American Dental Association (ADA)
  • Member: Tennessee Dental Association (TDA), Nashville Dental Society (NDS)

  • Awards & Honors: Richard Doggett Dean & Marguerite Taylor Dean Honor Society
  • 1991 Nashville Outstanding Young Dentist Award
  • Prosthodontic Award, Endodontic Award, Delta Sigma Delta Highest Academic Award
