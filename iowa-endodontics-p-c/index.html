The practice

Endodontic treatment requires accuracy, specialized training, and advanced diagnostic tools. At Iowa Endodontics, we use modern technology to treat complex cases and improve patient comfort and outcomes.

These tools help our specialists identify hidden infections, treat complex root canal systems, and support predictable healing.

Our practice provides specialized endodontic care designed to relieve pain and preserve natural teeth.

Root Canal Treatment Root canal therapy removes infected tissue inside the tooth, disinfects the canal system, and seals the tooth to prevent reinfection. This treatment often saves teeth that might otherwise require extraction.

Endodontic Retreatment If a previously treated tooth fails to heal or develops a new infection, retreatment may be required to clean the root canals again and restore the health of the tooth.

Apicoectomy (Root-End Surgery) An apicoectomy is a microsurgical procedure used when infection persists at the tip of the tooth root. The infected tissue is removed and the root end is sealed to prevent further infection.

Cracked Tooth Treatment Cracked teeth can cause significant pain and sensitivity. Early diagnosis and specialized endodontic care can often preserve the tooth and prevent further damage.

Regenerative Endodontics Regenerative endodontic procedures help stimulate healing and continued root development in teeth that have experienced trauma or infection.

Les soins

Depending on your treatment, your general dentist may place a crown or permanent restoration on the tooth to protect it and restore full function.

Most root canal procedures can be completed in one visit lasting approximately 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the complexity of the tooth.
