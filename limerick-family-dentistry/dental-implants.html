FAQs

How long do dental implants last? With proper oral care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits are key to ensuring their longevity.

Is the dental implant procedure painful? The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, so most patients feel minimal discomfort. Any post-surgery soreness or swelling can be managed with prescribed medication.

How long does the dental implant process take? The entire process takes a few months, including consultation, implant surgery, healing, and the placement of your final restoration.

Are dental implants a replacement for natural teeth? Yes, dental implants are designed to function just like natural teeth, offering a permanent, stable, and comfortable solution for missing teeth.

How do I care for dental implants? Caring for dental implants is just like caring for natural teeth—regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential to maintain their health and appearance.
