Who is suitable for Ortho-buddy? Ortho-buddy is a patented orthodontic toothbrush specifically designed for orthodontic patients. Additionally, Ortho-buddy can also be helpful for patients who have lingual braces or permanent retainers.

What makes Ortho-buddy better? The 360-degree bristle design makes it possible to clean all of the areas a regular toothbrush is incapable of. This means that the removal of plaque around the brackets, in between the brackets and the entirety of the tooth up to the gum line can be perfectly clean. Our super soft bristles makes brushing your teeth and gums safe, effective and comfortable.

Why should I use Ortho-buddy? You’ve invested a lot of time and money with your orthodontic treatment. We&#x27;ve created Ortho-buddy so that when your braces come off, you haven&#x27;t experienced any decalcification (white spots), decay, or swollen gums due to poor hygiene - only a beautiful healthy smile.

How to use Ortho-buddy? Use Ortho-buddy for 30 seconds, twice a day.

Step 1. Gently hold the handle in a HORIZONTAL position

Step 2. Apply a pea-sized amount of toothpaste.

Step 3. Brush ABOVE and BELOW the wire on the UPPER teeth

Step 4. Brush ABOVE and BELOW the wire on the LOWER teeth

Step 5. Follow up with your regular toothbrush

How long does Ortho-buddy last? The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends replacing your toothbrush every three to four months if the bristles become matted, frayed or there is an accumulation of bacteria.

What happens if I don’t use Ortho-buddy? Patients with poor oral hygiene typically end up with white spots, decay, gingivitis and/or fibrotic gum tissue (swelling).

These problems can lead to expensive dental repairs after orthodontic treatment. When used twice a day, Ortho-buddy eliminates poor hygiene and thereby all of the associated problems.

Does Ortho-buddy replace a typical toothbrush? No. Ortho-buddy is an additional step used prior to brushing your teeth.

Ortho-buddy care, cleaning and replacement

How to clean Ortho-buddy? Remember to use Ortho-buddy with a pea-sized amount of toothpaste. After using Ortho-buddy, rinse your toothbrush thoroughly after each use to remove any remaining paste and debris.

After use, store your toothbrush in an upright position and allow it to air-dry. Avoid storing a wet brush in a closed container, which promotes bacteria growth more so than leaving it exposed to the open air.
