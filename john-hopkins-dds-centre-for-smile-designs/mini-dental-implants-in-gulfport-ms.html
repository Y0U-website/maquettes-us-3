After Mini Dental Implant Surgery

Every patient responds differently after undergoing surgery for mini dental implants. Most patients heal relatively quickly. If you feel discomfort, it can be managed using over-the-counter pain relief, like ibuprofen. Patients often return to their normal routine within 24 to 48 hours.

Dr. Hopkins recommends sticking with a soft diet after surgery. Opt for foods like oatmeal, soups, and yogurt for a few days. Eating sticky or hard foods could increase the risk of the implant becoming loose.

  • Severe pain

How Long Do Mini Dental Implants Last?

Mini dental implants can provide patients with stable dentures and act as a tooth replacement for many years. To get the most out of your investment, it’s important to practice the same routine care as you do with your remaining natural teeth.

Brush twice daily, and floss once daily. If you grind your teeth at night, you may wear your implants down prematurely. You can protect your new investment with a custom nightguard.

These habits and routine dental visits should allow you to enjoy your mini dental implants for around six to nine years.
