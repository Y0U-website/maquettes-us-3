What procedures does Dr. Mullen perform?

Dr. Mullen provides comprehensive dental services including preventive care, restorative treatments, veneers, cosmetic dentistry , single and multiple dental implants, full arch implant restoration, Invisalign clear aligner therapy, and IV sedation dentistry. His training enables him to handle complex cases requiring surgical precision while ensuring patient comfort through advanced sedation techniques and gentle care approaches.

How does Dr. Mullen make dental visits more comfortable?

Dr. Mullen understands dental anxiety is real and takes multiple approaches to ensure patient comfort. Every treatment room features overhead TVs for entertainment, noise-canceling headphones blocking clinical sounds, and light-blocking glasses reducing harsh operatory lighting. For patients with significant anxiety or undergoing extensive procedures, he provides IV sedation creating deeply relaxed, comfortable experiences with minimal memory of treatment.

What makes Dr. Mullen’s approach different?

Dr. Mullen’s approach emphasizes transparency, education, and personalized care. He takes time during appointments to show you what’s happening in your mouth using intraoral cameras and digital x-rays. Rather than rushing through explanations, he ensures you understand treatment options, associated costs, and expected outcomes before proceeding. His focus on holistic, comprehensive care means addressing not just individual teeth but your overall oral health and its connection to systemic wellness.

Does Dr. Mullen accept insurance?

Maison Dental Studio currently accepts several insurance plans, and Dr. Mullen’s team works diligently to maximize your benefits. The practice offers multiple financing solutions including Care Credit, Cherry, and in-house payment plans making quality dental care accessible regardless of insurance status. Dr. Mullen believes financial concerns shouldn’t prevent anyone from receiving necessary treatment, and his staff will help you find workable solutions.

How can I schedule an appointment with Dr. Mullen?

Scheduling an appointment with Dr. Mullen is straightforward. Contact our office by phone, through our website, or via the contact information on our Google Business Profile. New patient appointments include comprehensive examinations where Dr. Mullen reviews your complete dental and medical history, discusses your concerns and goals, and develops customized treatment plans addressing your unique needs within your budget and timeline.
