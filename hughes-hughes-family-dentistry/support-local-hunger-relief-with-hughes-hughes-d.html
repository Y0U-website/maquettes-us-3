Support Local Hunger Relief with…

At Hughes & Hughes Family Dentistry, we believe great care doesn’t stop at the dental chair—it extends into the community we proudly serve. That’s why we’re proud to support the Helping Hunger Food Drive , a powerful local effort led by the inspiring mother-son team, Jackie and Dylan Houck.

Now in its 11th year, this grassroots food drive is helping feed food-insecure families throughout Chester County. This year’s goal is to collect 15,000 pounds of food for the Chester County Food Bank by May 8th —and there’s still time for you to help make it happen.

Meet the Heart Behind the Drive

“I started the food drive when I was 4 years old because the power went out in our area, and I learned that not everybody had the food they needed.” —Dylan Houck

What began as one child’s desire to help has grown into a county-wide movement. Last year alone, the Helping Hunger Food Drive collected over 13,000 pounds of food. Dylan and Jackie hope to surpass that this year and are calling on the community to step in and support the cause.

Why This Matters Right Now

The timing of this food drive is critical. As Jackie Houck explains:

“This time of year is really important for our friends who get access to breakfast and lunch programs at school that they won’t have during the summer.” —Jackie Houck When school lets out, many children lose access to reliable meals. The Chester County Food Bank helps bridge that gap—but they can’t do it without local donations.

Drop Off Donations at Hughes & Hughes Family Dentistry

As part of our support, Hughes & Hughes Family Dentistry is proud to serve as an official drop-off location for non-perishable food donations.

📍 Find the donation box just inside our front door. 🕒 Donations can be dropped off during regular business hours. 👉 View our hours here

Whether you’re a current patient, a new visitor, or simply someone who wants to help, we welcome you to stop by and contribute.

More Ways to Get Involved

Can’t make it to a drop-off location? No problem—there are several other ways to support the Helping Hunger Food Drive: • Donate via Venmo, cash, or check. Jackie and Dylan will purchase food in bulk to maximize your donation. • Volunteer or help spread the word. Every share, post, and conversation helps. • Contact Jackie Houck directly at 484-716-3937 if you’d like to arrange a pickup or get more involved.

You can also check out the Helping Hunger Food Drive Facebook page .

At Hughes & Hughes Family Dentistry, we are proud to be a trusted dental practice serving Downingtown and Exton PA , offering Family, Pediatric, Cosmetic, and Restorative Dental Care for Adults, Seniors, and Kids. But what truly sets us apart is our commitment to people—whether that means providing exceptional dental care or supporting causes that strengthen our community.

Let’s Help Fill the Shelves

The Helping Hunger Food Drive is already underway—but there’s still time to get involved. Every box of cereal, every can of soup, and every dollar donated helps support families across Chester County this summer. Together, we can help the Houcks reach their goal of 15,000 pounds of food—and make sure no child goes hungry when school is out.

Stop by Hughes & Hughes Family Dentistry and place your non-perishable food items in the donation box during regular business hours.

Thank you for helping us care for our community—one meal, one smile, and one act of kindness at a time.
