How do I care for my dental implant?

Dental implants are designed to feel and function like natural teeth. Like natural teeth, they require strong oral hygiene and routine professional care to ensure their long-term effectiveness.

Tips for Caring for Your Dental Implant

Although implants cannot develop cavities, the surrounding gums and bone still require committed care to prevent plaque buildup or infection. Thus, good implant care includes:

  • Brushing twice daily for oral health
  • Flossing carefully around the implant
  • Using non-abrasive toothpaste
  • Avoiding tobacco products when possible
  • Wearing a nightguard if you grind your teeth

Why Regular Dental Visits Still Matter

Routine dental checkups remain extremely important after implant placement. Professional cleanings help reduce the risk of peri-implant disease, a condition similar to gum disease that can threaten implant success.

These visits also allow your dentist to monitor the implant, surrounding tissues, and bite alignment to ensure everything remains healthy and stable.
