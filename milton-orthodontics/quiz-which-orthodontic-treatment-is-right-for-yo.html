Quiz: Which Orthodontic Treatment Is Right for You?

Have you finally decided to commit to orthodontic treatment? This is an exciting first step in your journey to a straighter, healthier smile! That said, you might have one big question on your mind – which treatment is better for your needs, Invisalign or metal braces? To get a better idea of which might best suit your needs, take this quiz!

Question 1: How old are you?

Question 2: Do you still have some of your baby teeth?

Question 3: Are you missing any teeth?

Question 4: Why do you want to get braces?

Question 5: Do You Have an Overbite, Underbite, Crossbite, or Open Bite?

Question 6: Do you experience jaw pain or headaches more than one day a week?

Question 7: What is your desired treatment timeline?

Question 8: What is your biggest concern about braces?

Question 9: Have you had braces in the past?

If you answered “A” five or more times…

Then you should consider Invisalign! These exciting clear aligners have assisted countless patients with minor orthodontic issues over the last couple of decades. Many patients prefer this form of treatment since the aligners are fully removable, meaning they don’t come with any dietary restrictions. Plus, they’re also made from special, clear plastic material that’s transparent and hardly noticeable.

If you answered “B” five or more times…

Then traditional metal braces might be your best option. This tried-and-true orthodontic solution has assisted generations of dental patients, and braces can tackle virtually all degrees of orthodontic issues. It’s also considered an ideal treatment for children, as well as adults, who frequently replace things since the brackets aren’t removable. And if you’re worried about their noticeable appearance, clear/ceramic braces are another option that we offer here at our office.

What To Do Next

Your next step is easy – schedule a consultation with us at our Milton office! During this appointment, you’ll meet with Dr. Saunders to discuss your dental history, your current oral health, and your long-term smile goals. From there, it’s a matter of reviewing your treatment options and deciding which treatment is best for your needs and suits your lifestyle. There are so many benefits to seeking orthodontic treatment; you’ll be so glad you finally went through with it!
