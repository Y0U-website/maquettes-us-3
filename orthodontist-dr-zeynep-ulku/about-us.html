Academic Achievements

Dr. Zeynep Ulku brings a wealth of international experience and high-level education to her orthodontic practice, embodying a commitment to excellence that reflects her professional ethos. Her journey in dentistry, driven by a childhood aspiration and lifelong dedication, has culminated in notable achievements in the field.

Dr. Ulku’s academic credentials are exemplary. She obtained her doctoral degree from the Harvard School of Dental Medicine in 2002, followed by a post-doctoral degree from Columbia University’s College of Dental Medicine in 2007.

Since 2002, Dr. Ulku has been based in New York City, where she balances her appreciation for the metropolitan lifestyle with a rigorous pursuit of professional development. She consistently updates her orthodontic expertise, staying abreast of emerging techniques and technologies. Her approach involves thorough research, analysis, and application of cutting-edge knowledge to enhance patient care.

Recognized for her diligence and precision, Dr. Ulku has contributed significantly to academic discourse in orthodontics. She has presented lectures on various specialized topics, including Early Orthodontic Intervention, Temporary Anchorage Devices, and Early Orthodontic Treatment, further solidifying her standing in the field.

  • Harvard School of Dental Medicine , Department of Growth and Development, Advanced Graduation Education Program and The Forsyth Institute
  • Columbia University – Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) Columbia University College of Dental Medicine
