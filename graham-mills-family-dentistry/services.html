  • Dental Hygiene & Cleanings Make sure you’re booking your appointment every six months! Routine dental cleanings are essential for keeping your mouth, teeth, and gums healthy. Our skilled hygienist will check for any signs of gum disease and customize your cleaning to fit your personalized needs. Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing alone can’t reach, helping you maintain a bright, healthy smile.
  • Scaling and Root Planing Sometimes a deep cleaning is recommended to optimize your periodontal health. This treatment, also known as non-surgical periodontal therapy, helps remove plaque and tartar from hard-to-reach areas below the gum line that a routine cleaning cannot access.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Oral cancer screening is always part of our comprehensive and preventive exams. We look beyond your teeth, performing a thorough head and neck examination to screen for potentially cancerous lesions. Early detection can save lives.
  • Pediatric Dentistry We love children of all ages! Oral health starts with the first baby tooth and is an important part of your child’s overall health and development. Services for Kids Include: Routine exams and cleanings Fluoride treatments Sealants to protect against cavities Space maintainers We create a positive, comfortable experience for children, building healthy habits that last a lifetime.
  • Dental Fillings We use tooth-colored restorative materials to repair chipped, cracked, broken, and decayed teeth. Fillings blend seamlessly with your natural tooth, providing strength and a beautiful appearance. By identifying and addressing tooth decay in its early stages, you can prevent more extensive dental work from becoming necessary.
  • Dental Crowns Full-coverage crowns are sometimes necessary to strengthen and protect teeth that have been severely compromised by large decay, chipping, or existing restorations that put the tooth at risk for fracture. Crowns restore both function and appearance.
  • Bridges Bridges replace missing teeth by using full-coverage crowns on adjacent teeth as anchors. This restores your ability to chew comfortably and prevents neighboring teeth from shifting.
  • Veneers Veneers are a conservative way to improve your smile. By removing minimal tooth structure, we can transform your teeth with beautiful, natural-looking porcelain veneers. Give us a call to see if you’re a good candidate for this life-changing procedure!
  • Dental Implants Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They’re carefully planned using our advanced 3D imaging technology, and we use precise surgical guides custom-made to fit your unique anatomy. Once the implants are firmly in place, we restore them with beautiful, tooth-colored crowns that look and function just like natural teeth. Implants offer a permanent, stable solution that preserves your jawbone and supports long-term oral health.
  • Dentures And Partials A denture or partial is a removable replacement for multiple missing teeth and surrounding tissues. Our dentist will thoroughly conduct an exam to see which option would be best for your needs.
  • Root Canal Therapy When decay or fractures extend to or near the tooth nerve, a root canal becomes necessary to save the tooth. Depending on the complexity of the case, we remove the diseased nerve and replace it with a biocompatible filler, relieving pain and preserving your natural tooth. For more complex cases, we work closely with trusted specialists to ensure you receive the best possible care.
  • Bone Grafting After an extraction, bone grafting (also called site preservation) is often recommended to help preserve bone structure. This prevents bone loss that naturally occurs after a tooth is removed and is essential if you’re considering a dental implant in the future.
  • Alveoplasty An alveoplasty is a surgical procedure that reshapes and smooths bony protrusions. This is sometimes necessary to ensure a smooth transition and proper fit for a denture or partial.
  • Teeth Whitening Brighten your smile with professional teeth whitening. We offer safe, effective whitening treatments that deliver noticeable results.
  • Clear Aligners Straighten your teeth discreetly with Invisalign clear aligners. No metal brackets or wires—just a clear, comfortable path to a straighter smile.
  • Custom Oral Appliances Calling all nighttime teeth grinders and athletes! If you’re looking to protect your teeth, you’ve come to the right place. We Offer: Nightguards to protect against teeth grinding (bruxism) Custom mouthguards for athletes Retainers
  • Advanced Technology We use the latest dental technology to provide superior care: Digital X-Rays: Lower radiation, instant results 3D Imaging: Precise diagnostics for implants and complex cases Intraoral Scanners: Comfortable digital impressions—no messy molds Fully Guided Implant Surgery: Computer-guided precision for optimal outcomes
  • Referrals for Complex Cases While we provide comprehensive care for most procedures, we work closely with trusted specialists for more complex root canals, extractions, implants, and orthodontic cases. This ensures you receive the absolute best care for your unique needs.
