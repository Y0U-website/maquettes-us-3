What counts as a dental emergency?

Not all dental issues are emergencies, but several situations require urgent attention. Severe tooth pain often signals infection, decay deep into the pulp, or an abscess requiring immediate treatment. A knocked-out tooth can often be saved if treated within the first hour, so time is critical. A tooth that’s cracked or broken might have exposed nerve endings causing severe pain and sensitivity.

Swelling in the jaw, neck, or face accompanied by pain and fever may indicate a spreading infection requiring immediate care. A lost or broken crown or filling, especially if it leaves the tooth sharp or exposed, should be addressed quickly to prevent further damage and infection. Bleeding that won’t stop after injury, severe gum infection, or a foreign object stuck between teeth may also need emergency attention.
