What Is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an orthodontic solution that works identically to traditional metal braces; however, there are a few distinct differences. Rather than relying on bulky brackets that are bonded to the teeth and connected by wire, Invisalign utilizes a system of custom-made clear plastic aligners.

These aligners are designed to fit over a patient’s teeth and encourage them to gradually move over an extended period of time. These micro-movements are so subtle that they can hardly be felt, but the treatment requires patients to wear their aligners nearly full-time roughly 22 hours each day, only removing them to brush and eat.

Many patients prefer the idea of Invisalign over metal braces due to the convenience of the aligners. Not only are they virtually invisible when being worn, but they’re also fully removable, meaning that oral hygiene is unaffected and there are no crucial dietary restrictions that need to be followed.

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Although most patients will almost immediately gravitate toward Invisalign, it’s important to note that it isn’t an ideal solution for everyone, which is why the initial consultation is so important. That said, it’s typically effective when addressing mild to moderate orthodontic issues, including the ones listed below!

Crowded Teeth

Do your teeth overlap? If so, you might be wondering if Invisalign can fix overcrowding. Good news: the answer is yes! So, don’t assume that your only path to a straight smile involves metal brackets and wires. If we determine that you’re a good candidate for clear aligners, then we can deliver the same beautiful results without drawing any unwanted attention to your smile.

Gaps Between Teeth

Some patients are struggling with the opposite problem: there are considerable gaps between their teeth. If that’s the case for you, don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with us so we can determine if Invisalign is an option! If it is, then we can gradually and gently move your teeth into proper alignment with clear and custom-made aligners.

Overbite

While your upper teeth should sit in front of your lower ones, several problems can arise if they jut out significantly. So, even if you are happy with your smile, it’s important to explore your orthodontic treatment options. For many patients, Invisalign is one of them. If you’re a good candidate, then we will answer any questions you have before working on your custom treatment plan.

Underbite

Underbites are an orthodontic problem that can negatively impact your life in several ways. Fortunately, mild to moderate cases can often be addressed with Invisalign clear aligners. Rubber bands, buttons, and other orthodontic accessories are sometimes needed as well, but you don’t have to worry they are still more aesthetically pleasing than traditional brackets and wires.

Crossbite

Do you have a crossbite? If so, we have good news: you may be a candidate for Invisalign! At your consultation, we’ll perform a detailed examination of your mouth and assess the extent of your orthodontic issues. We’ll also ask about your lifestyle habits; for example, patients who don’t feel like they’re able to commit to the daily diligence required by Invisalign might opt for a different solution instead.

Open Bite

Did you suck your thumb as a child? If so, you might have a space between your upper and lower teeth that prevents you from biting down all the way. This orthodontic problem, which is referred to as an open bite, can often be corrected with Invisalign. If you are interested in finding out if that’s the case for you, then take the next step: schedule an appointment at our Great Lakes Dental Excellence office!

Invisalign FAQs

Does Invisalign Hurt?

Invisalign is generally more comfortable than traditional braces thanks to its smooth plastic construction. However, you may feel some pressure when you first start treatment and when switching to a new set of aligners. This mild achiness is a sign that the aligners are gently moving your teeth.

Most patients at Great Lakes Dental Excellence find the feeling manageable and temporary, usually lasting just a day or two. Generic pain medication, ice packs, and drinking cold water are all easy ways to ease soreness. If discomfort persists, let us know. We’ll offer more guidance to help ensure your treatment stays on track and is as comfortable as possible.

What Happens After Invisalign?

Once your Invisalign treatment is complete, you’ll receive a retainer to help maintain your results. This is because your teeth will naturally want to shift back to their original positions over time, so wearing a retainer is key to keeping your new smile aligned.

Dr. Van de Vyver or Dr. DeBellis will give you personalized instructions on when and how long to wear your retainer. Our team will also schedule follow-up visits to monitor your progress and make adjustments if needed. Compliance with wearing your retainer means a beautiful, perfectly aligned smile for life!

Is Invisalign Cheaper than Braces?

In many cases, Invisalign is comparable in cost to traditional braces, though the final price depends on the complexity of your case. On average, Invisalign in New Baltimore costs around $5,000. While it may seem like a higher upfront investment, many patients find the convenience, comfort, and discreet appearance well worth it.

Our expert team offers personalized consultations to figure out what is best for your smile and your budget. We also provide flexible financing with CareCredit and Sunbit to make Invisalign more affordable.
