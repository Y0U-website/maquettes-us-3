Most children benefit from a dental visit every 6 months for a cleaning and exam. Dr. Kim may recommend more frequent visits depending on your child&#x27;s individual needs.

Dental anxiety is very common in children, and we specialize in making visits as comfortable and fun as possible. Dr. Kim&#x27;s gentle approach, our playful office environment, and Hodoo the office dog all help nervous kids relax. Let us know in advance if your child has anxiety so we can take extra time.
