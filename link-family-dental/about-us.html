Meet the Team at Link Family Dental

At Link Family Dental, our team loves working together to take care of our patients because, to us, you re the most important patient in our dental practice!

We are a group of dedicated professionals looking to provide exceptional dental care to our patients, enjoy each other s company and yours, and have a little fun while we maintain or restore your smile.

Get to Know Our Team

We would love for you to take some time to get to know more about your trusted Platteville dentist, Dr. Caroline Link, and the rest of our team here. That way, you ll feel right at home when you arrive!

Dr. Caroline Link

When she s not caring for patients, Dr. Link loves cooking, baking, painting, and taking her dogs on walks. A fun fact people might not know about Dr. Link is that she has two rescue dogs Toby and Daphne!

Sarah

Sarah has been in the dental field for 27 years, starting as an assistant before attending hygiene school at Northeast Wisconsin Technical College. She s been a hygienist for 18 years and worked with Dr. Link since 2018.

Sarah is passionate about dental health and providing the highest quality of care possible. She loves building long-term relationships with her patients and educating them on their overall oral health. She always loved going to the dentist as a child but did not have very good home care. So she wanted to be able to educate patients on their oral health and how it can also affect their systemic health.

When she has free time, Sarah loves hiking, kayaking, camping, snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, shopping, and reading, especially anything dental-related. She even belongs to a group of hygienists who meet weekly via Zoom!

Cassie

Cassie received her training in dental hygiene from Madison College. Her favorite thing about working with patients is the opportunity to sit down and learn more about them while assisting them with any concerns they may have with their oral health.

She says, "I enjoy meeting new patients and revisiting with current ones when they come to sit in my chair. I love working directly with patients and educating them on their home oral care and how oral health is essential as it contributes to overall body health."

When she s not caring for patients, Cassie loves going on walks with her husband and their dog, Ellie. She also loves cooking, gardening, and learning new things about homesteading. She has a unique hobby: making her own self-care products, cleaning products, and kitchen staples from scratch. A fun fact about Cassie that people may not know is that she can also communicate in sign language!

Sheila

Sheila is one of the friendly faces that will greet you when you come to Link Family Dental. She s been in the dental field for over 35 years and has been at this practice since 2020.

Sheila loves taking care of patients! She says, "We have some of the sweetest patients. I love talking to patients about their families, hobbies, and personal lives. I love helping them, whether it be writing out a check or opening a door for them."

You ll find Sheila outdoors in her garden, caring for her chickens and working in her flower beds when she s not caring for patients. A fun fact people may not know about Sheila is that she loves to bake with sourdough, whether it be bread, brownies, or muffins. She also enjoys the challenge of making dairy products such as yogurt, cheese, sour cream, and ice cream.

Brenda

Brenda is one of the first faces you ll see when you visit Link Family Dental! She s a graduate of the Milwaukee Area Technical College in both the dental hygiene and assisting programs. She loves building relationships with her team and the practice s friendly patients.

Outside of the dental office, you ll find Brenda baking, gardening, being outside, and traveling!
