Meet Dr. Habin Park

Habin Park D.D.S., M.M.Sc.

Following a year in general practice, Dr. Park pursued advanced specialty training at Harvard University, School of Dental Medicine, earning a certificate in Endodontics and a Master of Medical Science (MMSc) degree. His research on dental pulp stem cells has been published in several peer-reviewed journals and recognized nationally with the prestigious Alvin Krakow Research Award at Harvard and the AAE/Endo1 Partners Top 10 Resident Award.

After practicing in Hawaii for several years, he moved to the Chicagoland area and joined Dr. Montatskiy at Grayslake Endodontics. Dr. Park’s mission is to provide the highest level of care in a comfortable and friendly environment. Today, he combines his rigorous academic background with a gentle clinical approach and is also proud to offer care in Spanish.

Outside of clinical care, Dr. Park loves spending quality time with his family and is a dedicated supporter of his alma mater, the Michigan Wolverines.
