Being a teenager comes with its own unique challenges, but poor orthodontic health doesn’t have to be one of them. At Malaga Orthodontics, we work hard to create beautiful smiles that boost confidence and self-esteem for our patients.

Adolescence is the most common time for our patients to begin orthodontic treatment. There are several good reasons why 11 and 15 is the optimal age for orthodontic treatment. Orthodontic problems don’t improve with age — they simply become harder to treat. It’s easier to treat many orthodontic problems during adolescence because the body is still growing rapidly at this time. In later years, when the bones of the face and jaw are fully developed, many conditions become more difficult to treat.
