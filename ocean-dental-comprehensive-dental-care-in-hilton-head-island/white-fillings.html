Frequently Asked Questions About White Fillings

Are white fillings as strong as silver ones?

Yes, restore your teeth with natural-looking, long lasting, durable white fillings that are designed to withstand normal chewing forces, especially in smaller to medium restorations. The bonded fillings actually provide superior strength and support than their outdated mercury counterparts.

Will people be able to see my filling?

No, Our experienced dental team at Ocean Dental color-match the new fillings to your teeth, making them virtually invisible.

Does getting a filling hurt?

The simple procedure is typically quick and performed with local anesthesia, so you’ll feel little to no discomfort. Book an appointment for safe and healthy dental restoration

Can I replace my old silver fillings with white ones?

Absolutely. Many patients choose to replace mercury fillings for health or cosmetic reasons. A new white bonded filling will easily fix an old failing silver one.
