About Us

Dr. Kim is a leading expert in maxillofacial prosthetic rehabilitation and denturism, having served over 40,000 denture and Maxillofacial Prosthetic patients in King, Pierce, and surrounding areas with exceptional, patient-focused care.

Licensed in Maine, Washington, and Ontario (CA), Dr. Kim excels in implant dentures, digital dentures, and flexible partial dentures, leveraging advanced 3D digital workflows and top-rated oral scanners and milling machines for precision and efficiency. As a Maxillofacial Prosthetist, Dr. Kim is an expert in crafting all facial prosthetics and providing maxillofacial rehabilitation.

Dr. Kim is an active member of the International Anaplastologist Association, American Academy of Maxillofacial Prosthetics, Washington Denturist Association, and National Denturist Association.

Dr. Kim is actively engaged in scholarly research and professional advancement within the field of maxillofacial prosthetics. He is the author of "Comparing Educational Pathways for Denturists to Become Maxillofacial Prosthetic Specialists in the US and the UK," published in Oral Health , Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, University of the Pacific. His additional manuscript, "The Influence of Prosthetic Obturators on Quality of Life in Individuals with Maxillectomy Defects," is published in The Pacific Journal of Dentistry .

Dr. Sara Sobhani DDS

Dr. Sara Sobhani is a highly skilled Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon known for delivering sophisticated, patient-centered care with precision and artistry. She completed advanced clinical training in Orofacial Pain and Sleep Medicine at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA).

Dr. Sobhani earned her specialty certification in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery from Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences in 2006, following her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Isfahan University of Medical Sciences in 2001. With over a decade of experience in both hospital and private practice settings, she offers comprehensive expertise in implantology, preprosthetic surgery, orthognathic surgery, facial trauma, oral pathology, TMJ disorders, and aesthetic and reconstructive facial procedures.

In addition to her clinical work, Dr. Sobhani has held an academic appointment as an Assistant Professor at Jundishapur University of Medical Sciences, where she mentored dental students and surgical residents. She is also recognized for providing advanced training to fellow dentists in dental implantology and complex surgical procedures.

  • Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery — Shahid Beheshti University
  • Advanced Training — UCLA (Orofacial Pain & Sleep Medicine)
  • Former Assistant Professor, Jundishapur University
