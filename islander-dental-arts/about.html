Meet Dr. Allena Novak

A trusted new generation bringing compassionate care with a modern approach and a shared commitment to the values that define us.

Dr. Allena Novak’s passion for dentistry began early, shaped by her own unique dental journey and a natural curiosity about how teeth work. What could have been a difficult experience became a source of fascination and purpose, guiding her toward a career where she could help others feel confident and understood in their care.

Her connection to this practice runs through her life. Dr. Novak’s mother and Dr. York were friends from second grade. She grew up coming to this very office as a patient, later joined the team as an intern in high school and college, and today she’s practicing alongside Dr. York. That lifelong bond brings a rare sense of continuity, familiarity, and shared values to the care they provide together.

Dr. Novak is known for her warm, educational approach and love of building long-term relationships with families. She enjoys guiding patients through each stage of life and finds great joy in watching children grow into confident, healthy adults.

Outside the office, she loves spending time outdoors with her family, camping, fishing, hiking, and relaxing with a good book or movie.
