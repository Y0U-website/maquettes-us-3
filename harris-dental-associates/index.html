Harris Dental is now offering a Dental Savings Plan as a convenient, simple and affordable option for for those who don’t have dental insurance. The goal of our Plan is to make dental care more accessible and manageable to all. We hope our Savings Plan will help you to come in and see us whenever you need to keep you in good oral health!

For an annual membership fee, you will receive preventative dental care plus enjoy reduced fees on restorative services such as fillings and crowns.

Les soins

You’ll also receive $1,000 off Invisalign and Orthodontics. ( Once per lifetime. Must remain a plan member for the duration of treatment.)
