Dr. Anne Wang

General Dentist

Dr. Anne Wang is a general dentist whose philosophy is to provide trust and integrity to her patients while restoring form and function to their teeth and gums. She loves working with patients to maintain and improve their beautiful smiles.

Dr. Wang grew up on the east coast in the Philadelphia suburbs. After graduating with honors from the University of Pennsylvania, she completed her dental degree and public health degree at the University of Pittsburgh. She subsequently completed a general practice residency at Northwestern University. After working for several years on the East Coast, she is happy to move to the Bay Area to be closer to her family.

In her free time, she likes to read, hike, and travel with her husband, their dog, and the rest of her family. She has gone on several dental mission trips and enjoys volunteering and interacting with different cultures.
