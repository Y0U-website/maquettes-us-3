The practice

Mt. Vernon Smile Center is proud to serve families throughout Mt. Vernon, Centerburg, Gambier, Fredericktown, Danville, Howard, Bellville, and surrounding Knox County communities . Whether you’re looking for a new family dentist, interested in Invisalign , dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, or need emergency dental care, our experienced team is here to help.

As the new home of Gordon Family Dental , we’re committed to making every visit comfortable and stress-free. Using the latest technology, gentle techniques, and personalized treatment plans, we help patients of every age achieve healthy, confident smiles that last a lifetime. We welcome new patients and look forward to caring for you and your family.

As many of you know, my husband relocated to Virginia two years ago for his career. During that time, I remained local as a commitment to my patients and to this practice. However, the time has come for me to join my husband and begin a new chapter with my family on the East Coast.

Making the decision to transition my practice was not easy and one of my top priorities was finding the right dentists to continue caring for you and your family. After a careful and thoughtful search, I am pleased to share that I have found an excellent fit.
