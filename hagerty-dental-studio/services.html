Our Services

At Hagerty Dental Studio, we combine advanced technology with personalized care to provide a full range of dental services for patients of all ages. Whether you’re here for a routine checkup or a more advanced procedure, we’re committed to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.

As your child grows, they will need regular checkups and hygiene visits to ensure optimal oral health. Our team teaches oral home care techniques so your child learns to effectively brush and floss, with parent help at first and eventually on their own. Different treatments may be recommended to ensure optimal health for your growing child throughout their development.

A professional teeth cleaning, or prophylaxis, is a routine preventive treatment to keep your smile healthy and bright. Our hygienist will remove plaque, tartar, and stains that brushing and ﬂossing at home cannot reach. Routine cleanings help prevent cavities and gum disease and keep your oral health in optimal condition.

Restore your smile with tooth-colored composite resin ﬁllings. Unlike traditional metal ﬁllings, these restorations are custom-shaded to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, creating a virtually invisible result. Resin ﬁllings not only repair damage from decay but can also be used to cosmetically ﬁx chipped, cracked, or worn-down teeth.

A professional ﬂuoride treatment is a dental procedure that strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent or reverse early tooth decay. During the treatment, a concentrated ﬂuoride varnish is applied directly to the teeth to protect them from acids and bacteria. This process helps remineralize weakened areas.

Periodontal treatment focuses on the health of your gums and the underlying bone the vital tissues that support your teeth.

Periodontal treatment prevents, diagnoses, and treats gum disease, which can range from mild gingivitis to more advanced periodontitis. When left untreated, gum disease can lead to gum recession, tooth and gum sensitivity, and even tooth loss. Our goal is to eliminate infection, restore health, and preserve your natural smile.

A CEREC crown is a dental restoration created and placed in a single visit using CAD/CAM technology, eliminating the need for a temporary crown. The process involves a dentist preparing the tooth, taking a high-precision 3D digital scan, designing the crown on-screen, and using an in-oﬃce milling machine to fabricate it from a ceramic block. The ﬁnished crown is then ﬁtted and bonded to the tooth, all within a few hours.

Dentures are custom-made false teeth that replace missing teeth and help restore a person’s smile, ability to eat, and speech. They can be either complete (replacing all teeth in an arch) or partial (replacing one or a few teeth). Dentures can be removable or ﬁxed to dental implants for a more secure ﬁt.

Sleep apnea devices are medical tools that help people breathe more easily and continuously during sleep, preventing pauses in breathing. Common types include CPAP machines, which use a mask and a stream of pressurized air to keep the airway open; oral appliances, which are custom-ﬁtted mouthguards that reposition the jaw or tongue; and implantable systems that use neurostimulation to keep the airway from collapsing.

A dental bridge is a ﬁxed dental prosthetic that replaces one or more missing teeth to restore your smile, chewing function, and speech. It consists of one or more false teeth, called pontics, that are anchored to natural teeth or dental implants on either side of the gap or supported by a framework that attaches to the back of the remaining teeth. This helps prevent your remaining teeth from shifting and maintains the shape of your face.

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth that replace the root of the tooth with a titanium post, which fuses with the jawbone to create a stable base for a new crown. This innovative procedure looks and feels like a natural tooth, offering a durable and secure alternative to dentures or bridges, while also helping to preserve your natural bone structure.
