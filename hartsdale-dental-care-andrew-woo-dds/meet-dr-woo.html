Meet Dr. Woo

0 About Andrew Woo, DDS Andrew Woo, DDS Cosmetic Dentistry located in Hartsdale, NY

Andrew Woo, DDS, is a caring and devoted dentist specializing in cosmetic dentistry at Hartsdale Dental Care in Hartsdale, New York. Dr. Woo is committed to helping his patients improve their oral health and achieve more beautiful smiles. He is multilingual, speaking both English and Korean.

He attended the University of Minnesota to earn his medical degree.

Dr. Woo then completed his general practice residency at the Wyckoff Heights Medical Center before studying at New York University to learn about prosthetics and surgical implants.

Dr. Woo is accomplished in all five areas of dentistry, which include endodontics, periodontics, pediatric dentistry, orthodontics, and oral surgery.

Dr. Woo routinely provides his patients with orthodontic braces, implant surgery, restorative dentistry, root canal therapy, wisdom tooth removal, and pediatric dental care. Dr. Woo combines his passion for dentistry with his comprehensive, multidisciplinary, and interdisciplinary education to ensure his patients receive the highest quality dental care possible.

Dr. Woo serves his community through Donated Dental Services, which provides free dental services for the disabled or the underprivileged for up to five years.

When not treating patients at Hartsdale Dental Care, Dr. Woo enjoys learning and playing the violin, and he is passionate about his family, which includes his wife and their son and daughter.

Dr. Woo and the Hartsdale Dental Care team welcome new patients to the practice.
