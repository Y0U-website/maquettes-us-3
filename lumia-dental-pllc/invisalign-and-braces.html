Invisalign® and Orthodontic Services

Invisalign uses very thin, clear, virtually invisible plastic trays to painlessly move your teeth horizontally, vertically, and even rotationally to straighten them and improve your smile. You change the trays every weeks, and a few weeks later, your smile has transformed. At Lumia Dental, these custom-fit aligner trays can be fabricated using state-of-the-art digital dentistry to scan your teeth instead of taking uncomfortable impressions.

Well-positioned teeth are important not only for cosmetics of your smile and face but also for function:

If you are ready to straighten your teeth, do not hesitate. Your alignment only gets worse over time when left untreated. Crowding of teeth is a sign of aging as our jaws tend to decrease in length as we get older. Book an appointment today for a consultation with Dr. Goodman to start your Invisalign treatment. Your provider will select which aligner will best fit your needs.

  • Fewer food particles build up around your teeth when they’re better positioned;
  • You can chew better and prevent pathological wear of your teeth;
  • It helps prevent gum recession and bone loss.
