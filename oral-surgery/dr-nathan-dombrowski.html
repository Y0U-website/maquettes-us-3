About Dr. Dombrowski

Dr. Dombrowski is a Board-Certified Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He is a fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS), a member of the Texas Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (TSOMS) as well as a member of the American Dental Association (ADA).

Dr. Dombrowski holds an undergraduate degree from University of Dayton where he majored in biology. He earned his dental degree from Texas A&M College of Dentistry and completed a six-year Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency, where he also earned his medical degree, at the University of Louisville.

Dr. Nathan Dombrowski

His areas of surgical expertise include wisdom teeth, dental implant surgery, All-on-X procedures (teeth in a day), bone grafting and sinus grafting, pathology, orthognathic surgery and facial reconstructive trauma surgery.

Dr. Dombrowski strives to provide excellence in all aspects of his care. His ideology is based upon providing exceptional, patient-centered care in a comfortable and safe setting.

Outside of oral surgery, Dr. Dombrowski enjoys spending time with his wife, Rylie, and his two daughters, Sloane and Lainey. His wife, Rylie, is a general dentist and is a Rockwall-Heath native. Dr. Dombrowski enjoys sports, fitness, barbecue, and traveling.
