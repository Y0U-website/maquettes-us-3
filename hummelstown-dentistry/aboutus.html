About Us

Joshua S. Han , D.D.S.

Dr. Joshua S. Han is excited to be living and practicing dentistry in Hummelstown, PA. He grew up in Maryland and graduated from the University of Maryland - College Park with a degree in Physiology and Neurobiology. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Maryland - School of Dentistry, the world’s first dental college. Dr. Han brings his broad experience and love of dentistry to the community of Hummelstown. When not striving to keep his patients happy and healthy, he enjoys running.
