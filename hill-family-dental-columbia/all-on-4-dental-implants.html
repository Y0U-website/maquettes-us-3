How the Process Works

Getting started with All-on-4 implants isn’t as complicated as it might seem. Your dentist begins with a detailed consultation, walking you through every step before any work begins. 3D X-rays, digital scans, and hands-on assessments help create a customized plan tailored to your specific needs.

Your dentist can keep the process transparent and help you feel comfortable every step of the way.

Who is a Candidate for All-on-4? Many patients wonder if they qualify for All-on-4 implants. This treatment is ideal for adults who’ve lost most or all of their teeth in one arch, or who are dealing with failing dental work. If you are generally in good health and your jawbone is strong enough to support implants, you’re likely a great fit. Don’t have perfect bone health? The All-on-4 approach enables many individuals who don’t qualify for traditional implants to achieve a stable, long-lasting solution.

  • Comprehensive exam and planning visit
  • Gentle placement of four dental implants in a single appointment
  • Temporary new teeth so you don’t go without a smile
  • Customized final bridge after healing, usually a few months later

Post-Procedure Care and Maintenance

Once your All-on-4 implants are in place, you won’t be left wondering about the next steps. Patients can return home the same day with temporary, natural-looking teeth, allowing them to continue living your life while your mouth heals. You may notice some swelling and mild tenderness—totally normal and usually gone within a week or two. Your dentist will provide clear instructions on diet and cleaning to help your implants settle in strong.

Follow-up visits help us keep things on track as your dentist monitors healing progress and addresses questions as they arise..
