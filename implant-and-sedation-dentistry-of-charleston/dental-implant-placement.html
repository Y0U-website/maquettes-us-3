Your Journey to Getting Dental Implants

The dental implant process is meticulous and requires several visits to Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston over a few months. Initially, we conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health to ensure suitability for implants. Following this assessment:

  • The implant site is prepared, and the titanium post is precisely placed into the jawbone.
  • A period of healing allows for osseointegration to occur securely.
  • Once integration is complete, an abutment is attached to serve as a base for your new tooth.
  • The final step involves placing the custom-made crown or prosthetic tooth onto the abutment.

Setting Realistic Expectations: The Impact on Your Smile

Dental implant placement is recognized for its high success rate and transformative results. Patients can expect not only an improvement in aesthetics but also enhanced oral functionality and health. Dr. Gilreath emphasizes that while the process may seem lengthy, the outcome – a permanent solution to tooth loss – is well worth it. Moreover, patients appreciate the seamless integration of their new teeth into their daily lives without the inconvenience often associated with removable dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long does the entire process take?

The duration varies based on individual healing times and whether additional procedures (such as bone grafting) are needed. Generally, it spans several months from implant placement to final crown attachment.

Is dental implant placement painful?

Pain management techniques have advanced significantly; most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Local anesthesia and sedation options are available to ensure your comfort throughout.

What care do dental implants require?

Maintaining good oral hygiene practices is crucial. This includes regular brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups at Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston to ensure longevity and success of your dental implants.
