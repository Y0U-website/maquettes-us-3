About Our Dental Clinic in Edmond, OK

Meet Dr. Kessler

Dr. Hal Kessler, DDS is a proud Edmond native and Deer Creek High School graduate who has dedicated his career to serving the community that shaped him. After earning both his undergraduate and dental degrees from the University of Oklahoma, he returned home to provide exceptional care as a general dentist in Edmond.

With over a decade of experience in dentistry, a practicing dentist since 2019, and earlier work as a dental assistant, Dr. Kessler delivers comprehensive care across all areas of general dentistry in Edmond, OK, including restorative dentistry, dental implants, fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions, bridges, and dentures. His patient-first philosophy centers on education, transparency, and comfort at every stage of treatment.

Dr. Kessler believes in meeting patients where they are and helping them achieve confident, healthy smiles. His treatment approach as a dentist for his Edmond patients is built around three guiding principles: maintain what you have, save what you can, and replace what you must .

Passionate about giving back, Dr. Kessler is a volunteer board member for D-DENT and also donates his time as a volunteer dentist for the Oklahoma Mission of Mercy (OKMOM), providing care to underserved populations across the state. He is also an active member of the Oklahoma Dental Association, American Dental Association, Oklahoma County Dental Society, and local dental study clubs.

Outside the office, Dr. Kessler enjoys spending time with his wife, Tori, their two children Harold IV (Court) and Carlisle (Carlie), and soon to be third child due December 2025! Together they love exploring local events, discovering new restaurants, and traveling. He’s also an avid fly fisherman and a loyal fan of OU Football, OU Softball, and the Oklahoma City Thunder.
