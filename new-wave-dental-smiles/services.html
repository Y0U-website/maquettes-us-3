Dental Services

  • Dental Exams : In addition to a dental cleaning, our Burien dentist will perform a comprehensive dental exam at your routine checkups to ensure everything looks normal and catch any potential dental problems.
  • Extractions : While we strive to help our patients keep their natural teeth whenever possible, sometimes, the best way to achieve a great smile is through tooth extraction.
  • Root Canals : Despite their bad rap, root canals are a simple, routine, and virtually-painless procedure that allows us to save a tooth that’d otherwise be lost.
  • Composite Fillings : We place a strong focus on preventive care, but if you do develop a cavity, we can use a strong, tooth-colored composite filling to treat the cavity and restore your beautiful smile.
