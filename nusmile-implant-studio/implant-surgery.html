Implant Surgery

Implant surgery involves placing a titanium post into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. This creates a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration. The procedure is carefully planned to ensure precise placement and optimal long-term outcomes.

At NuSmile Implant Studio, we utilize advanced 3D imaging and digital planning to guide implant placement with a high level of accuracy. This approach enhances both safety and predictability while minimizing discomfort during the procedure.

Our focus is on achieving strong integration between the implant and the bone, allowing for a durable and natural-feeling restoration that functions like a real tooth.
