Emergency Dentistry Services

If you recognize any of these symptoms, get in touch with us right away for immediate care.

  • Swelling
  • Chipped tooth
  • Broken tooth
  • Lost filling or crown
  • Infection or abscess
  • Injury to jaw
  • Severe toothache
  • Swollen gums
  • Persistent bleeding
  • Sudden or persistent jaw pain
  • Loose tooth
