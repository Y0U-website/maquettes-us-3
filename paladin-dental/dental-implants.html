Dental Implants

We offer detailed consultations to provide you with a personalized estimate, and we can discuss how your insurance may cover part of these costs or how our Paladin Membership Plan might be applied.

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The success of dental implants depends on several factors, including oral hygiene and regular dental checkups. The crown attached to the implant typically lasts 10 to 15 years before it may need replacement due to wear and tear.
  • Are dental implants painful? Patients are often surprised to find that the discomfort associated with getting dental implants is less than expected. Local anesthesia and sedation options are used during the procedure to minimize pain. Post-operative discomfort is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers, and most patients report feeling much better within a few days.
  • How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants at Paladin Dental varies based on the type of implant and the complexity of the case. Single dental implant : Costs range from $999 to $4,499, depending on individual needs and additional procedures required.
  • All-on-4 dental implants : This full-mouth restoration is more complex and thus costs more, but provides significant value in terms of functionality and aesthetics. Please contact us for a detailed consultation to get a precise estimate tailored to your situation.
