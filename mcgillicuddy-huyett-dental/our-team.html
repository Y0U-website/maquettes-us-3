Laura Lee, RDH

Laura has over 24 years of experience as a Registered Dental Hygienist. Laura is highly proficient in several areas of modern dental practice including: digital radiography, intraoral camera, Cari-vu for caries detection, Velscope for oral cancer screenings, soft tissue management and application of fluoride treatments and sealants. Laura has experience in periodontal evaluation and treatment planning including: periodontal charting, use of ultrasonic scaler, scaling and root planing, placement of Arestin and oral hygiene instruction, geared towards the specific needs of the patients.

Laura joined Healthcare Accreditation Resources in October 2016 and conducts peer training and continuing education seminars on current dental standards throughout the greater Boston area. Laura trains dental offices on numerous dental standards, including but not limited to, OSHA regulations, HIPPA, CPR, infection control protocols, Bloodborne Pathogens Standard and Hazard Communication Standard.

Laura earned her Bachelor of Science in Human Science and Services from the University of Rhode Island and her Associate Degree in Dental Hygiene from the Community College of Rhode Island. Laura is a nationally certified Dental Hygienist by the Northeast Regional Board, is licensed in administering local anesthesia and has additional training and experience in administering CPR.
