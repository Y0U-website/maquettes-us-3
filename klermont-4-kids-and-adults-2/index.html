We enjoy treating patients of all ages. With minors, however, an important part of the dentist/patient relationship is the legal relationship.

Minors (children under the age of 18) not being accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, need to come in with the notarized consent form signed by the parent/legal guardian.

Without this consent form we will not be able to see the minor patient.
