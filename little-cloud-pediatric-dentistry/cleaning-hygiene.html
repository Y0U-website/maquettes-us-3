The Benefits of Cleaning & Hygiene

Promotes Long-Term Oral Health Regular cleanings prevent the buildup of harmful plaque and tartar, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. By catching potential problems early, you’re not just protecting your child’s smile. You’re protecting their overall health too.

Boosts Confidence and Freshness A bright, clean smile can make kids feel more confident about showing off their teeth. Plus, professional cleanings help eliminate stubborn stains and lingering odors caused by food or plaque, leaving their mouth fresh and clean.

Teaches Lifelong Habits Our friendly team uses each visit as an opportunity to teach kids proper brushing and flossing techniques. We make oral hygiene fun and relatable, setting them up for success well beyond childhood.

What to Expect From Cleaning & Hygiene

A Warm Welcome

Your child’s visit begins with a friendly welcome from our team, complete with the chance to meet one of our certified therapy dogs. This helps your child feel relaxed and comfortable from the start. We’ll take the time to explain what to expect, answer any questions, and ensure they feel at ease before beginning.

Gentle, Thorough Cleaning

During the cleaning, our skilled hygienists use advanced tools to carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup. We pay extra attention to those hard-to-reach spots and finish with a polish that leaves teeth smooth and shiny. Our gentle, child-friendly approach ensures comfort throughout, and we always cheer them on for being such a great patient!

Guidance for a Healthier Smile

Before your appointment wraps up, Dr. Forer conducts a thorough exam to identify any areas of concern. We’ll share easy-to-follow tips and recommendations to help maintain your child’s healthy smile at home. Whether it’s tips on proper brushing or advice on diet, we make sure you feel equipped to support your little one’s dental health between visits.
