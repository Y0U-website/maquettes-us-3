The practice

Restorative dentistry at Minot Smiles Dental Co. rebuilds the strength, function, and appearance of teeth affected by decay, damage, or wear. Dr. Olson offers conservative solutions including composite fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures to preserve natural teeth wherever possible.

Dr. Olson places tooth-colored composite fillings that restore decayed or damaged teeth to full function while blending naturally with the surrounding smile. Every filling is placed with precision and finished to match the patient’s natural tooth color and bite.

A dental crown is a custom-crafted covering that encases the entire tooth surface, restoring its original shape, size, and strength. Dr. Olson recommends crowns when a tooth is significantly cracked, decayed, or weakened and ensures every crown is fitted for long-term comfort.

Dr. Olson provides evaluation and treatment for temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, which affect the jaw joints and muscles responsible for chewing and speaking. Each patient receives an individualized management plan aimed at relieving jaw pain, headaches, and related discomfort.

At Minot Smiles Dental Co., dentistry is personal. Dr. Megan Olson is the only provider patients will ever see, so every relationship is built on consistency and trust that deepens over time. The practice is intentionally structured to feel different from high-volume dental offices, with an unhurried pace and a team that genuinely knows each patient by name.

The environment at Minot Smiles Dental Co. is quiet, clean, and free of the rushed atmosphere common in larger practices. Patients who have previously avoided dental care due to anxiety frequently describe it as the first office where they felt genuinely comfortable.

Before recommending any treatment, Dr. Olson conducts a thorough assessment, asks detailed questions, and ensures patients fully understand their options. Her approach reflects a belief passed down through three generations of family dentistry: that listening is just as important as treating.

Whether a patient needs a single filling or a multi-step restorative plan, Dr. Olson evaluates the full picture before making any recommendation. No two care plans at Minot Smiles Dental Co. look alike because no two patients are alike.
