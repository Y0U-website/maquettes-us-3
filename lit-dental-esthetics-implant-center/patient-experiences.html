Phased Treatment Planning in Wayland, MA

Structured, transparent dentistry designed for clarity and long term stability

Why we use a phased approach

Comprehensive dentistry often involves multiple procedures completed over time. Rather than presenting long lists of line item charges, we organize care into clearly defined treatment phases.

Each phase reflects a logical clinical sequence designed to support healing, structural stability, and predictable outcomes.

What a Treatment Phase Includes

A treatment phase may include coordinated procedures that are completed within a defined timeframe. Instead of itemizing every component separately, each phase is presented as a structured segment of care.

• The clinical objective of the phase • The anticipated timeline • The financial investment associated with that stage

For longer or more complex treatment plans, phased structuring also allows patients to plan financially with greater clarity. Each phase has a defined cost and a general timeline for when that phase is expected to begin, providing transparency around when financial commitments will occur.

Transparency Before Commitment

• Scope of treatment • Clinical sequencing • Healing considerations • Financial structure and anticipated timing

Patients are able to make informed decisions with a clear understanding of both treatment progression and financial planning.
