Dr. Laurie Novinsky grew up in the Bay Area. After attending UCLA as an undergraduate, she continued her studies at the University of the Pacific Dental School in San Francisco.…

The practice

As a life-long learner, Dr. Novinsky recently earned her Masters Degree in Counseling from the California Institute of Integral studies which she hopes to integrate into her practice. Her practice is caring, fun, community-oriented and includes the most updated clinical care. She is especially versed in caring for patients who have been fearful of the dentist office for many years.

Personally, Dr. Novinsky has two grown boys who live in Colorado. She enjoys attending workshops, traveling, and going to concerts.

Due to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, we greatly reduce the number of patients in our office as well as mask, and have hospital grade air filters in our operatories.
