Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are the only tooth replacement option that offers true permanence and stability. Since the artificial tooth root bonds to your surrounding jaw bone and tissue, you won’t have to worry about your false tooth moving or shifting in your mouth. You’ll never need to remove an implant to eat or sleep, and you’ll have peace of mind knowing that your oral health is fully restored.

Supports Jaw Health

Unlike other restorative treatments, dental implants stimulate and support your jaw bone, preventing bone loss.

Natural Look & Feel

Dental implants are designed to look and feel just like a natural tooth. You will be able to eat, speak and smile with confidence.

No Special Care Required

The Dental Implant Procedure

Full-Arch Implants

Full-arch implants are an ideal alternative to traditional dentures. This treatment uses a series of 4-6 implants to support a full arch of false teeth. Both removable overdentures and non-removable, fixed dentures are available. During your consultation, one of our dentists will discuss your needs to determine which option is best for you, your budget, and your lifestyle.

Single-Tooth Implants

Single-tooth implants consist of two parts: the implant itself and a dental crown. They are used to replace a single missing tooth, and they have many advantages over dental bridges and dentures. Bridges and dentures will need to be replaced every 7-10 years, whereas an implant will last a lifetime. Dental implants are also the only replacement option that will support your jaw bone and structure.

Dental Implant Frequently Asked Questions

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can easily last 30+ years. Most patients keep their implants for the rest of their lives, making them the best long-term method of smile restoration. Simply care for your implant as you would a natural tooth, and come to our office every six months for a cleaning and checkup.

How Do I Take Care of My Dental Implant?

One of the benefits of dental implants is that they require no special care. Since the titanium posts acts as an artificial tooth root, the implant essentially becomes a natural part of your mouth. This means that you simply need to brush and floss as you normally would, and come to Oakbrook Dental for regular checkups. It’s that simple!
