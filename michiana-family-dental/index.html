The practice

We believe great care starts with listening. Our team takes the time to understand each patient’s individual needs, goals, and lifestyle so we can create personalized treatment plans that truly make a difference. By focusing on what matters most to you, we help improve your overall health while enhancing your smile’s natural beauty and confidence.

Our office is conveniently located at 3575 Portage Ave, Suite A, South Bend, IN , and we are currently accepting new patients.

Michiana Family Dental offers a full range of dental services and procedures to meet the complete oral health needs of every patient we serve. By combining compassionate care with modern dental technology, we create a comfortable, relaxing environment where patients of all ages feel at ease.

Our website provides detailed information about our services, including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, family dental care, emergency dental treatment, dental implants, affordable dental solutions, teeth whitening, dental surgery, dentures, dental crowns, and more.

Our dentist and experienced team are dedicated to more than just treating symptoms — we focus on understanding the root cause of each dental concern. This comprehensive approach helps our patients achieve lasting results, improved overall health, and confident, healthy smiles for years to come.

Our office is conveniently located in South Bend and offers a complete range of dental services designed to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile in a comfortable setting.
