What to Expect at Your First Family Appointment

New patients receive a complete evaluation, not just a quick look: Health and medication history — important for both adult and pediatric care. Digital X-rays — low radiation, immediate review on screen. Comprehensive oral exam — teeth, gums, jaw, bite, soft tissue, oral cancer screen. Cleaning — thorough and scaled to age and oral health. Personalized plan — recommendations for prevention, treatment, or simply a return in six months.
