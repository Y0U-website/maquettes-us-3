Dr. Patricia Pasnikowska, (Dr. Patty)

Born in Poland, Dr. Patricia Pasnikowska (Dr. Patty) is the dedicated and compassionate dentist behind HP Dentistry PC. With nearly 20 years of experience in the dental field, Dr. Patty is known for blending clinical excellence with a warm, upbeat approach that puts patients at ease.

A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, she brings advanced training and a deep commitment to personalized care to every appointment. Since founding HP Dentistry, Dr. Patty has created a practice where patients of all ages feel comfortable, informed, and genuinely cared for. She loves working with her team and patients alike, saying, "They make dentistry a blast!" Dr. Patty entered the dental field in 2007 with a clear purpose: to help people and positively impact their lives.

Outside of the office, you ll find her running, swimming, or walking her dog around town. A lifelong learner, she s currently exploring the art of fine jewelry making. She s also fluent in multiple languages and is always full of fun facts.
