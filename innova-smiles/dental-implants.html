Caring for Your Dental Implant: Recovery & Long-Term Success

The First 48 Hours

  • • Bite gently on gauze for 30–45 minutes if placed same day; change as needed
  • • Apply ice packs: 20 minutes on, 20 minutes off for first 24 hours
  • • Take prescribed pain medication before numbness wears off
  • • Soft diet only: yogurt, scrambled eggs, mashed potatoes, smoothies
  • • No smoking, alcohol, or carbonated beverages for 72 hours
  • • Sleep with head slightly elevated

Weeks 1–4: Osseointegration Phase

  • • Continue soft diet for 1–2 weeks; gradually reintroduce firmer foods
  • • Brush gently around the implant site; avoid direct brushing of surgical area for first week
  • • Use chlorhexidine rinse if prescribed, rinse gently, do not spit forcefully
  • • Mild swelling and bruising are normal and peak at 48–72 hours
  • • Avoid strenuous exercise for 3–5 days
  • • Attend all follow-up appointments, they are critical for implant success

Long-Term Implant Care

  • • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • • Floss daily using implant-specific floss or a water flosser
  • • Avoid chewing ice, hard candies, or very hard foods on the implant
  • • Wear a night guard if you grind your teeth
  • • Professional cleanings every 3–6 months (implants need specialized care)
  • • Annual X-rays to monitor implant and bone levels

When to Call Us Immediately

  • • Persistent or increasing pain after 3–4 days
  • • Implant feels loose or has shifted position
  • • Fever over 101°F
  • • Pus or discharge from the implant site
  • • Excessive bleeding that does not stop with pressure
  • • Implant crown feels "off" in your bite

Honest Talk About Risks

  • Bone Graft Complications When bone grafting is needed, graft failure or slow healing occurs in fewer than 5 % of cases. We use premium graft materials and monitor healing with imaging before proceeding to implant placement.
