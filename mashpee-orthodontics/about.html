Mashpee Orthodontics Accessibility Statement

Mashpee Orthodontics is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, mashpeeortho.com , for everyone. Mashpee Orthodontics aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortiums Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA). Mashpee Orthodontics is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone.

We highly recommend using the userway accessibility widget linked in the footer, but should you experience any difficulty in accessing any part of this website, please feel free to call us at 508•539•0355 or email us at info@mashpeeortho.com and we will work with you to provide the information or service you seek through an alternate communication method that is accessible for you consistent with applicable law (for example, through telephone support).

Dr. Nicholas P. Zafiropoulos, DMD Approach Credentials Personal Our patients love Dr. Nick for his patient-focused approach. Dr. Nick will be with you every step of the way, from discussing your smile concerns and walking you through treatment options to leaving our office with a healthy, stunning smile. Parents trust Dr. Nick as he will never push for radical or unnecessary procedures.

Dr. Nick graduated on top of his class when he completed his BA in Biology from Tufts University in 1988. After graduating Summa Cum Laude from his dental class at Tufts Dental School, he continued to the prestigious Tufts Postgraduate Orthodontic Program, where he spent an additional two years of full-time training in the art and science of orthodontics. He is President of the Executive Board of the Massachusetts Association of Orthodontists and a past president of the Tufts Association of Orthodontists.

When not working on smiles in our office, Dr. Nick can be seen enjoying the beaches of the Cape, boating, playing basketball, or traveling with his wife and his son Payi. He regularly attends conventions and symposiums related to orthodontic advancements to apply them in our practice and provide our patients with the best care possible.
