What are dental implants?

3D Cone Beam imaging is an innovative scanning technology that provides a full and complete 3D scan of your bone and soft tissue, giving your dentist an incredibly accurate look at your oral structure.

These images give your dentist a clear and detailed view from any angle, making an accurate diagnosis and the creation of a treatment plan much more precise.

This imaging option is extremely beneficial for diagnosing complex cases, such as impacted teeth, oral reconstruction, jaw disorders and tumors, unexplained oral pain.

Socket preservation

Socket preservation is a bone graft designed to preserve the socket of the tooth, enabling it to maintain strength to support an eventual dental implant. These grafts are often recommended after tooth extraction to preserve the socket and allow for the 3-to-6-month healing time before an implant can be placed.
