About Our Leander Dental Team

About Us We Understand Children At Hero Way Pediatric Dentistry, you can be confident in the dental office you have chosen for your child. From your first visit, we want you to feel welcomed and well cared for.

Our dentists and staff promote preventative dental care including a healthy diet and great oral hygiene at every visit. Because of this, we love to have parents with their children during their first visit. This gives us the opportunity to educate you as well so you can help reinforce these healthy habits at home.

Our Mission At Hero Way Pediatric Dentistry, we are committed to providing optimal dental care in a friendly, caring, and family-oriented atmosphere. Our Mission is to ensure our patients are set up for a lifetime of dental health by having consistently positive experiences at the dentist. We value the relationships we form with patients and parents, and would be honored to serve as your loving “dental home.”

Meet Dr. Yu Li

Fluent in Mandarin

Dr. Li believes that pediatric dentistry is not just about treating baby teeth, but the general well-being of the child. Her approach focuses on prevention, and prioritizes the child’s psyche and overall development when forming a treatment plan. As the oldest of three girls (with a 12-year age gap), she has always assumed a caretaker role in the family and learned to communicate with children at a young age.

After receiving her B.A. in Biology and English from the University of Pennsylvania, Dr. Li was accepted to Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She graduated summa cum laude from dental school and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honor Society for exemplary character and scholarship. After practicing general dentistry for one year, she applied to pediatric dentistry residency and was extremely grateful to have matched to her top choice at the NYU Langone site in Phoenix, AZ.

Dr. Li is a board eligible pediatric dentist who has been practicing in the Austin area since 2020. She is fluent in Mandarin, which allows her to connect with and serve a wider range of families in the community. In her spare time, she enjoys eating, and traveling, and chasing her toddlers around the playground.
