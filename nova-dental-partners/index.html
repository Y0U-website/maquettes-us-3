The practice

Dental implants at Nova Dental Partners offer the following benefits and more:

If you’re missing permanent teeth, dental implants can perfect your smile to boost your quality of life.

Learning if dental implants are right for you requires a comprehensive dental exam. Your dentist examines your gums and teeth, reviews your dental history and desired outcome, and takes dental X-rays. Nova Dental Partners provides tooth extraction prior to implant placement.

Good candidates for dental implants have one or more missing teeth, enough jawbone to hold the implants in place, and a jawbone that’s reached full growth. If you lack enough bone, ask the Nova Dental Partners team about first undergoing jawbone grafting.

Your specialist numbs your mouth to ensure dental implant placement feels comfortable. They insert metal implants into your jawbone and allow time for the bone to heal and grow around the implant posts.

Take good care of your new dental implants by maintaining good oral hygiene habits. Brush your teeth after meals, avoid chewing on ice and other hard objects, and floss every day. See the Nova Dental Partners experts every six months for dental exams and cleanings .
