Diagnostic Wax-Ups

Diagnostic Wax-Ups are a valuable tool for any smile restoration. This will provide the patient with an accurate preview of how his or her finished smile will look, making them feel comfortable and restoring their confidence. The Natural Reflections Dental Lab team will accurately prepare a model of a patient’s restored smile. A Diagnostic Wax-Up also helps many patients determine whether to pursue treatment by comparing the waxed-up model to their existing smile. For this reason, all Diagnostic Wax-Ups are designed to resemble natural dental structures.
