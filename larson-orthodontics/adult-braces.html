Aligners

Aligners are typically clear trays that dentists switch out after every 2 weeks to readjust the teeth movement. Clients have to wear them for at least 20 to 22 hrs a day and can remove them for taking meals or brushing teeth. Invisalign is one of the most common brands of aligners.

Aligners consist of composite components and are much less noticeable. However, they are not invisible. They require proper cleaning and a timely changing schedule. Delays in changing out trays may lead to various other problems.

Conventional Dental Braces

There is a huge distinction between the old type of dental braces and modern dental braces. Today, you do not need to band metal around every single tooth. All it needs to hold to wires with a single bracket cemented on the front row of teeth. There are a few metal bands at the back of the teeth to secure these wires.

The only downside these dental braces have is that they are visible even if you choose the ceramic option. Self-Ligating Braces

These dental braces pass the wires via a small metal clip. With self-ligating dental braces, you do not need elastic bands to move the teeth position.

Adult Lingual Braces

It is another great option for adults who are seeking effective and unnoticeable braces. Lingual dental braces are more like traditional braces. The only difference is that these braces are at the backside of teeth. The feature that makes lingual braces stand apart among other kinds of braces is their virtual invisibility. They make the ideal alternative for the people who are conscious about their looks and appearance.
