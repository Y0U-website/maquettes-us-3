Our Team

Our wish is for you to maintain healthy teeth and a beautiful smile. Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are kind, friendly and caring. They are happy when you are happy, so please feel free to address any concerns with our front office. Our energetic and experienced staff will ensure a pleasant experience from the moment you walk through the door to the end of your appointment.

Dr. Michelle Tompkins

Dr. Michelle Tompkins graduated from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 1994. She has enjoyed serving the needs of her dental patients for the past 20 years.

Dr. Tompkins attends many continuing education seminars each year and is a member of the Indiana Dental Association and the American Dental Association. She also participates in promoting good oral health through schools and the Give Kids a Smile Program.

Dr. Tompkins resides in Greenfield, IN with her husband and three children.

Rachel Tarak

With over 25 years in dentistry, I’ve built a career centered around compassion, confidence, and making people feel genuinely cared for. I love helping patients walk in nervous and walk out with a smile. Creating comfort, connection, and trust is what I do best, and it’s the part of my work that keeps me passionate year after year.

Outside the office, my heart belongs to my husband Mike and our daughters. We’re always ready for a new adventure — whether it’s traveling someplace we’ve never been, catching a game, or cheering on our favorite teams. And at home, our love for animals is a big part of our life. There’s nothing better than ending the day with pets curled up beside us.

I’m proud of the life I’ve built — a mix of meaningful work, family, adventure, and am proud to be part of the team at Harrison Dental.
