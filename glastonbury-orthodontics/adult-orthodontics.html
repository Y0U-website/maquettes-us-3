Our Office 277 New London Turnpike Glastonbury , CT 06033 (860) 430-9889 Visit Us Online Adult Orthodontics Return to Treatment A generation ago it was extremely unusual to see an adult with braces on their teeth. Today that is no longer the case. Thanks to advances in dental technology, greater access to care, and an increased awareness of the benefits of a beautiful and healthy smile, now over twenty percent of people wearing orthodontic appliances are adults.

With the wide variety of orthodontic treatments available today, the process of wearing braces has become significantly more convenient, comfortable, efficient, and discreet than ever before. Bulky metal braces are truly a thing of the past. The latest metal appliances are substantially smaller and far less noticeable than earlier versions.

In addition, adults seeking orthodontic care now have the option to choose from treatments that are barely visible. Tooth-colored ceramic braces and removable clear aligners both offer effective solutions for achieving successful and aesthetically pleasing results. The value of a healthy and attractive smile is priceless. Thanks to modern orthodontics, having teeth that look good and function well is something that can be enjoyed at any age.

Healthy teeth can be moved successfully at any age. Orthodontic treatment creates a better bite, making teeth fit better, and decreases the risk of future dental problems regardless of age.

Can I get an appliance that is less noticeable?

Thanks to the variety of “appliances” (such as clear aligners and various bracket types) that are available, adults may be able to inconspicuously achieve the great smile they want. Many of today’s treatment options are designed to minimize the appearance of the appliance and better fit an adult lifestyle.

Will orthodontic treatment fit my lifestyle?

You can do almost anything during orthodontic treatment — sing, play a musical instrument, dine out… Some temporary adjustments will be necessary, but if you’re like the vast majority of adult patients, you’ll adapt quickly.

I’m missing some teeth. Can orthodontic treatment improve my bite?

Whether you are planning to replace missing teeth or not, you may benefit from a consultation. If replacements for missing teeth are planned, orthodontic treatment can help keep the necessary space open. If no replacements are planned, your teeth can be stabilized your so that shifting does not occur or is minimized. We will work with your dentist or dental specialist to achieve the best result.

I’ve been treated for gum disease. Can I have orthodontic treatment?

We will need to consult with your dentist and/or periodontist about the health of your gums. If you are a candidate for orthodontic treatment, you will continue regular visits with your dentist and/or periodontist.
