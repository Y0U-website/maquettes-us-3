Orthodontics

Creating Confident, Healthy Smiles

Every child deserves a smile they can be proud of! If your child feels self-conscious about their teeth or bite, orthodontic treatment can do more than just straighten their smile — it can boost their confidence and support long-term oral health.

At Kids Dental Smiles, our caring team is committed to making your child’s orthodontic experience comfortable, positive, and tailored to their unique needs. From the first consultation to the final reveal, we’ll be with you every step of the way.

How Orthodontic Treatment Works

Orthodontic care gently guides teeth into better alignment using customized appliances. These may be metal, ceramic, or clear brackets bonded to the teeth, or removable aligners, depending on your child’s needs.

By applying a consistent, gentle pressure over time, braces help shift teeth into their ideal position — improving both function and appearance.

Today’s orthodontic options are more comfortable and discreet than ever! Kids can even choose from fun colors for their braces, while clear or tooth-colored options provide a more natural look. Modern materials also help teeth move more efficiently, reducing treatment time and improving comfort.

How Long Does Treatment Take?

Every child’s orthodontic journey is unique. Treatment time depends on several factors — such as your child’s age, the complexity of their case, and the type of treatment used. On average, orthodontic treatment lasts between one and three years.

Starting with early, interceptive orthodontic care can sometimes reduce treatment time later on by addressing small problems before they become major ones.

Your child’s cooperation also plays a big role! Following care instructions, maintaining great oral hygiene, and attending regular checkups all help keep treatment on track for the best possible results.
