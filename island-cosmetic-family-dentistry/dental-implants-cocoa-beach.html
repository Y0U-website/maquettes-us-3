Dental Implants in Cocoa Beach, FL

A missing tooth changes more than a photo. Chewing shifts. Neighboring teeth drift. Dental implants are one way Dr. Bhawna Chandra, DDS, replaces missing teeth at Island Cosmetic & Family Dentistry in downtown Cocoa Beach. We are at 41 N Orlando Ave, Suites 1 and 2, minutes from Port Canaveral. An implant is a small post placed in the jaw to stand in for a root. After it integrates with bone, a custom crown is attached.

Who implants are for

Adults on the Space Coast missing one tooth or several, someone whose partial moves when they eat, someone told they might need a bone graft. Cruise passengers should know implant treatment is a process, not a one-hour port stop. It may not be first if gums are infected or medical conditions are poorly controlled. Dr. Chandra will say so.

Implants, bridges, and dentures

A bridge uses neighboring teeth as anchors. A partial or denture comes out. An implant stands on its own and does not grind down a healthy neighbor. Bone grafts are sometimes needed if the site has already lost volume. They are not automatic.

What to expect

Consult with exam and digital X-rays, then placement with local anesthesia, then months of healing, then the crown. Anyone promising a finished tooth for every patient in a single visit is overselling. Call (321) 799-1090 to talk through your case.
