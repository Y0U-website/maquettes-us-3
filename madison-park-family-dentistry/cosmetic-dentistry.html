Our cosmetic dentistry services

BioClear

BioClear is a minimally invasive way to reshape teeth, close black triangles, repair chips, and improve symmetry — all without drilling away healthy enamel. It’s fast, natural-looking, and a great alternative to veneers or bonding.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made covers that mask chips, stains, or gaps while enhancing the overall look of your smile. We design every detail around your goals, creating a result that’s polished, long-lasting, and uniquely yours.

Teeth Whitening

Our professional whitening treatments lift deep stains safely and effectively. Whether you want a quick refresh or dramatic brightening, our Torrance cosmetic dentist will help you find the option that fits your goals.

Botox

Botox isn’t just for aesthetics, though it can soften fine lines around the mouth and forehead. We use it to help relieve jaw tension, headaches, and symptoms of clenching or TMJ disorders for a more relaxed, balanced smile.

Smile Makeover

This is a custom plan designed around what you want to change — whether it’s one detail or your full smile. We combine multiple cosmetic treatments to create results that are seamless, natural, and fully aligned with your lifestyle and goals.

How long does cosmetic treatment take? K L

That depends on what you’re looking for. Professional whitening can be done in a single visit, while veneers or a full smile makeover may take a few appointments. Our Torrance cosmetic dentist will walk you through the timeline up front so there are no surprises.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost? K L

Pricing varies based on the treatment. During your consultation, we’ll review costs, payment options, and whether any part of your care may be covered by insurance. We also offer financing and a membership plan to help make care more accessible.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance? K L

In most cases, cosmetic treatments aren’t covered by dental insurance, but there are exceptions. If a procedure also improves function or addresses damage, some plans may provide partial coverage. We’re happy to look into your benefits and explain what’s possible.
