We have a dedication to providing world class clinical care that is gentle, empathetic, timely, and most importantly, individualized. We spend extra time diagnosing each patient and explaining their treatment recommendations. We want our patients to understand the “why” and “how” of their procedure, which helps to alleviate any anxiety they may have about the process.

Our team is highly talented and works hard to bring both cutting-edge technology and a human touch to each and every one of our patients. Serving the Scranton Wilkes-Barre area, and beyond!

Dr. Legg was born and raised in NEPA. He attended college and earned his BS in biology at the University of Pittsburgh. While not studying to be a dentist, he played defensive end for the Panther’ s football team!

Dr. Legg later attended Temple University school of dentistry where he earned his DMD. After practicing as a general dentist for a few years and developing a passion for endodontics in private practice, Dr. Legg returned to Temple to complete his post doctoral residency in endodontics.
