Dr. Christopher Goding, DMD

Dr. Christopher Goding grew up in York, Pennsylvania, before moving to Cumberland, Maine, where he attended Greely High School. He went on to earn his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Providence College. Coming from a family with several dental professionals, he developed an appreciation early on for the meaningful, positive impact dentistry can have in everyday life.

After completing his undergraduate studies, Dr. Goding attended the University of New England College of Dental Medicine. He continued his training through a General Practice Residency at WellSpan York Hospital in York, Pennsylvania. During this time, he gained experience treating patients with complex medical histories, managing dental emergencies, and providing on-call care in a hospital setting. He stayed for a second year as Chief Resident, offering leadership and mentorship to incoming residents while continuing to deliver high-quality patient care.

Following his residency, Dr. Goding returned to Maine to provide compassionate, comprehensive dental care to the community he calls home. In his free time, he enjoys cooking and exploring new restaurants with his girlfriend, Morgan. He also loves running and spending time outdoors during all four of Maine’s beautiful seasons.
