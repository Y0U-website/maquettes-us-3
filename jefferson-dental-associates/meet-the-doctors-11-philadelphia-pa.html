Meet The Doctors

Dr. Nicolo is currently a member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Pennsylvania Dental Association, and the Philadelphia County Dental Society. Committed to providing the best care possible for his patients, Dr. Nicolo completes over forty hours of continuing education per year, and has taken postdoctoral courses in prosthodontics, endodontics, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry and oral surgery.

Dr. Nicolo has over 20 years experience as a cosmetic dentist and has extensive training including the advanced use of digital imaging and the use of sophisticated digital x-ray and intra-oral imaging.

Dr. Nicolo has also been restoring dental implants for over 20 years and has extensive experience in complex restorative cases. Dr. Nicolo is committed to providing the highest quality dental care in a state of the art practice. He uses the latest pain control techniques to minimize discomfort. He believes excellent patient communication is the foundation of a trusting doctor patient relationship.

Dr. Gordon graduated from Temple Dental School in 1977. He also completed a hospital-based, general practice residency program with the United States Public Health Service in New Orleans in 1978. He continued in the uniformed services as a commissioned officer at the Customs Building in Philadelphia treating active duty military and coast guard personnel until 1980.

Dr. Gordon joined Jefferson in 1980 as Director of the Division of General Dentistry and Director of the Residency Program. In 1985, he became co-owner of Jefferson Dental Associates and the following year, the practice moved to the Ben Franklin building as a private practice. Dr. Gordon continues his hospital affiliation as an active member of the medical staff at Jefferson.

Dr. Rola lives in Center City with his wife and enjoys spending time with his family and friends, traveling, and soccer.
