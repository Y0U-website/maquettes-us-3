Why Choose Preventative Dental Care?

Early Detection of Issues: Preventative dental care enables us to catch small, potentially developing problems before they escalate into more complex and costly issues. Regular dental exams and cleanings are essential in monitoring your oral health, allowing for timely interventions when necessary.

Reducing Treatment Costs: By prioritizing preventative care, you can significantly decrease your chances of needing large, expensive treatment plans down the line. Maintaining routine dental examinations and professional cleanings ensures that minor problems are addressed immediately, saving you time and money in the future.

Life-Saving Oral Cancer Screenings: At our Lunenburg practice, we understand the importance of oral cancer screenings. Regular screenings are vital to identify any abnormal changes in your mouth that could indicate early signs of oral cancer. Early detection greatly increases the chance of successful treatment and may ultimately save your life.

Preserving Your Teeth and Gums: Gum disease is one of the leading causes of tooth loss, yet it can often go unnoticed in its early stages. Our thorough exams allow us to identify and treat gum disease early, preserving your teeth and contributing to your overall health. Through education, we empower you to understand the significance of gum disease prevention in your dental care journey.

Maintain Your Oral Health: Schedule Your Regular Dental Visit

Dental Exams and Cleanings: One of the most critical components of preventative dental care in Lunenburg is regular dental exams and cleanings. These visits help to monitor your oral health, remove harmful tartar, and address any emerging issues promptly. At Dr. Gianino’s office, we prioritize a friendly and relaxed atmosphere, ensuring that your visits are stress-free and rewarding.

Professional Teeth Cleaning: In addition to regular examinations, professional teeth cleaning is the best way to remove plaque and tartar that at-home care can’t eliminate. Our team uses specialized tools to deep clean your teeth and gums, ensuring a brighter smile and a healthy mouth.

Night Guards and Sports Guards: For our patients who grind their teeth at night or engage in contact sports, we offer custom mouthguards in Lunenburg. Dr. Gianino creates these dental appliances tailored to your unique needs. Whether you need protection against nighttime grinding or safeguarding during sports activities, our custom mouthguards provide comfort and reliable defense.

Sealants and Fluoride Treatments: Protecting your teeth from bacterial attacks requires proactive measures. Dr. Gianino offers dental sealants in Lunenburg, which act as a protective coating on your molars, preventing cavities and decay. Additionally, we recommend fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel, significantly reducing the risk of cavities especially for children and those prone to dental issues.
