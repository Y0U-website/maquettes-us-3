New equipment at Bainbridge Island Dentist Office Todd H. Adams

Todd is native to Bainbridge Island. Growing up, he had the opportunity to work with his father, Harmon, in the dental office where he was trained as Harmon s lab technician. After graduating from Whitman College, Todd taught conversational English in Japan, worked for a biotechnology firm studying cancer diagnostics before choosing to pursue a career in dentistry.

Todd joined Harmon s dental practice at the Winslow Green in 1997 where he has become the second-generation dentist treating the Bainbridge community. 2022 marked the 60th year of care provided since Harmon first set up the dental office on Bainbridge in 1962.

Todd (along with his late wife Yaroslavna) is the proud parent of two children, Quincy and Nora, who both occasionally visit the dental office as Todd often did with Harmon, when he was a boy.

Todd enjoys the personalized nature of the care he provides to patients within his dental practice. He takes the meaning of practice as a challenge that it is never enough to stop learning or contributing within the profession. He looks forward to continue years of care and commitment within the Bainbridge community that is his lifelong home.
