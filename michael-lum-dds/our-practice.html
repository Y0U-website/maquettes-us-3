High Standards

Our Milpitas family dental practice is devoted to restoring and enhancing the natural beauty of your smile using minimally invasive, state-of-the-art dental procedures that will result in beautiful, long-lasting smiles!

Dr. Lum continually stays up to date on the latest advancements in dentistry in order to provide the most current treatment options for his patients. To give you the best possible service and results, he is committed to continual education and learning by attending dental lectures, meetings, and dental conventions. In addition, Dr. Lum is a proud member of various professional dental associations that help him stay updated on the latest changes and recommendations for our profession.

Technology and Services

Our practice utilizes state-of-the-art technology including Digital X-Rays, Digital Imaging, and Digital Photography. Digital X-rays vastly reduce radiation exposure by more than 80 % compared to traditional film X-rays. Dr. Lum also uses electric dental handpieces and rotary endodontics which are more precise, efficient, and comfortable to shorten your appointment duration. We offer a full spectrum of aesthetic and comprehensive dental services to improve your appearance, confidence, comfort, and general health.

Sterilization Protocol

At our Milpitas dental office, we take strict safety and infection control measures to protect our patients and our staff. To ensure the highest standards, we apply sterilization and cross contamination processes using standards recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA), the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and the Center for Disease Control (CDC).

Our doctors and staff are dedicated to providing you with the excellent, personalized care and service you deserve in order to make your visits to our Milpitas dental office as comfortable and pleasant as possible.
