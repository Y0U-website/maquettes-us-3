A HISTORY OF PRECISION PERFORMANCE

Kitsap Dental Lab has been operating on the Olympic Peninsula for over 20 years, using top of the line products and creating quality restorations with the ever-evolving dental industry.

Working out of Bremerton, this team of specialists- led by certified dental technician Ron Walsh- use the leading technologies on the market in tandem with 3Shape design service and Roland milling, KDL provides work that is second to none.

We offer a variety of services, from crown and bridge to digital scans; if your office needs it done, our technicians are ready to create a product you love. Using any of the leading brands of implant, we have you covered! Our office also offers appointments for custom shade so that your patients can be assured of a smile they will love to see in the mirror.

With competitive pricing, incredible customer service, and quick turnaround times, Kitsap Dental Lab is the premier choice in oral restoration.
