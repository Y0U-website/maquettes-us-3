The Bone Grafting Process

  • Evaluation & Planning – We assess your bone health with advanced imaging technology.
  • Graft Placement – Bone material is carefully placed in the needed area to rebuild structure.
  • Healing Phase – The bone integrates and strengthens over several months, preparing the site for dental implants.

Benefits of Bone Grafting

  • Restores jawbone strength and volume
  • Creates a stable foundation for dental implants
  • Helps preserve facial structure and appearance
  • Improves long-term oral function and health
