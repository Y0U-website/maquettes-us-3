0 oral surgery & Implants Replacing teeth, restoring bite function, and supporting long-term the jaw structures and oral wellness with precise, minimally invasive care.

Dental implants are about more than replacing missing teeth. They restore the ability to chew food and stabilize the jaw bones. Our fresh approach combines advanced surgical techniques with biocompatible materials for optimal results. From single-tooth replacements to full-arch reconstructions, we focus on predictability, long-term results and patient comfort along the way.

Single Tooth & Multiple Tooth Replacements Using dental implants , we can replace one or several missing teeth. Each implant is carefully planned for placement after a review of the patient’s medical history. Implants support the jaw bones and prevent their shrinkage and support the other teeth and proper bite alignment. Our approach ensures the replacements feel natural, function well, and integrate seamlessly with your smile.

All - on - X

Full-arch implant solutions (commonly known as all-on-4) provide a fixed, permanent, stable set of teeth using multiple implants. This revolutionary approach will be the closest to replacing natural teeth that patient’s once had. Full arch implants restore bite function, improve chewing efficiency, and enhance overall facial aesthetics. Patients are able to gain durable, natural-looking results with minimal recovery time.

Third Molar / Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth, also called third molars, are often removed because they frequently do not have enough space to grow properly in the mouth. When there isn’t enough room, they can become trapped in the gums or jawbone or grow at abnormal angles. This can cause pain, infection, and damage to nearby teeth. We can remove most wisdom teeth with utmost care and gentle management of the surrounding tissues.

PRP / PRF (Platelet-Rich Therapies)

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) and Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) are advanced therapies that enhance healing after oral surgery. By using your body’s own natural growth factors, they can accelerate tissue regeneration and reduce inflammation. This holistic method ensures better recovery, quicker implant integration, and sets patients up for successful results.

Zirconia Dental Implants

Zirconia implants offer a metal-free, ceramic alternative to traditional titanium for patients who prefer a biocompatible option. The material is strong, durable, and well-tolerated by the body, making it a good fit for patients with metal sensitivities or those committed to a holistic approach. Each case is planned in 3D to confirm zirconia is the right choice for the bone and bite. Patients receive a stable, natural-looking replacement that integrates with the jaw and lasts for years.

3D Implant Planning

An implant goes in one exact spot, and the plan is what finds it. Before any surgery, we map your jaw in 3D — bone, nerves, and sinus — so the placement is decided on a screen first. Our 3D implant planning turns a tricky case into a known one, which is a big part of why implants last.

Computer-Guided 3D Implant Placement

A good plan only helps if the surgery matches it. From your 3D scan we make a small guide that carries the plan into your mouth, so the implant lands at the planned spot and angle the first time. Computer-guided placement often means a smaller opening, a shorter visit, and a result you can count on.

Bone Grafting for Implants

An implant needs solid bone to hold it. When a tooth has been gone a while, that bone thins out. Bone grafting rebuilds the site first, often with PRF from your own blood to steady healing, so the implant goes into strength instead of a soft spot. It’s frequently the step that turns a no into a yes.

Sinus Lift for Implants

In the upper back jaw, the sinus sits close above the teeth. Once those teeth are gone, there’s often too little bone height left for an implant. A sinus lift gently makes room and adds bone, planned in 3D so we can see the space before we touch it. It gives an implant something firm to anchor into.

Socket Preservation

When a tooth comes out, the bone beneath it starts to shrink within weeks. Socket preservation fills that space at the same visit, so the bone keeps its shape for a future implant or bridge. It’s a small step with a large payoff — a little graft today can save a bone rebuild down the road.

Dental Implant Repair

An implant is meant to feel solid, so anything that doesn’t is worth a look. A loose crown or worn part is often a quick fix; a loose implant or lost bone is a different plan. Our implant repair starts with f inding the real cause, because fixing the wrong thing only buys a little time before it returns.
