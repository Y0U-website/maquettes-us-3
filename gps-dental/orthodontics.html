What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. By using various orthodontic appliances and techniques, orthodontists can straighten crooked teeth, correct bite issues, and enhance the overall appearance and function of the smile. Orthodontic treatment improves the smile’s aesthetics and contributes to better oral health and overall well-being.

Orthodontics Treatments in Warren, NJ

At GPS Dental, we offer a range of orthodontic treatments to meet each patient’s unique needs and goals. These options include

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a popular option for discreetly straightening teeth. These custom-made trays move teeth without metal braces, offering comfort and effectiveness for teens and adults.

Invisalign

Invisalign, a top clear aligner system, has transformed orthodontic treatment. Using advanced 3D imaging, Invisalign crafts personalized plans for each patient’s dental needs. Treatment includes wearing clear aligners, which are changed every two weeks to gradually shift teeth. Virtually invisible, Invisalign is favored by those seeking a discreet orthodontic solution.

Retainers

Retainers are custom orthodontic appliances to maintain treatment results. After braces or aligners, wearing a retainer prevents teeth from shifting. Typically worn at night or as directed by your orthodontist for long-term stability. Patients preserve straight smiles and benefit from treatment for years by following instructions.
