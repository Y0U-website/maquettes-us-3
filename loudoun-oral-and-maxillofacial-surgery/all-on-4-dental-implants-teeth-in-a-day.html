  • Have sufficient jawbone for implant support : Even patients with some bone loss may be eligible, as All-On-4 requires less bone density compared to traditional implant placement.

Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

All-On-4 dental implants offer numerous advantages, including:

  • Same-Day Transformation : Patients can enjoy a fully functional and natural-looking smile on the day of surgery, making All-On-4 a convenient option.
  • Stability and Comfort : The implants are securely anchored to the jawbone, eliminating the discomfort and movement issues associated with traditional dentures.
  • Bone Health Preservation : Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth.
  • Long-Term Solution : All-On-4 implants are designed to last, with proper care offering a lifetime of improved oral health and confidence. ‍
  • Enhanced Aesthetics : The customized prosthetic teeth look and feel like natural teeth, giving patients a beautiful smile they can feel proud of.

The All-On-4 Procedure

  • Consultation and Planning : During your initial consultation, our team will discuss your goals and evaluate your oral health. We may recommend bone grafting if additional support is needed. Advanced imaging technology, including the i-CAT™ Cone Beam Scanner , allows us to create a personalized treatment plan with precise implant placement.
  • Preparation and Anesthesia : On the day of the procedure, we provide Anesthesia & IV Sedation to ensure comfort throughout the surgery.
  • Implant Placement and Prosthetic Attachment : Four implants are strategically placed in the jaw. Once positioned, a custom-made prosthetic arch is attached to the implants, providing patients with a fully functional and aesthetically pleasing smile. ‍
  • Healing and Follow-Up : After the initial healing period, our team may recommend Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) to speed up recovery and enhance tissue regeneration. Regular follow-ups help ensure proper healing and long-term implant success.
