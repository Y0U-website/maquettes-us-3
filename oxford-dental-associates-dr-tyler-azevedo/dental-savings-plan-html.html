Dental Savings Plan

Great for patients with limited or no dental insurance coverage, the Dental Savings Plan at Oxford Dental Associates allows patients to spend less money for quality dental care. For a low annual fee, each member will receive two exams, two simple cleanings, and one set of x-rays per year. Any additional treatment required during the year will be provided at a discounted rate.

Eligibility

Any patient in the office is eligible to join the plan. No preexisting conditions will prevent membership. To be eligible, existing accounts must have a zero balance.

Annual Cost

  • $549.00 per year for adults, 18 years of age and older
  • $449.00 per year for children under 18 years of age
  • $999.00 per year for couples
  • $1,399.00 per year for a family of four
  • $199.00 per year for each additional child on family plan

Coverage

Coverage begins upon receipt of the contract application and payment which extends for one year from the date of receipt.

Other dental services and treatment will be provided at a 15 % billing courtesy. This excludes Care Credit Accounts. These services include:

For extensive treatment, totals over $2,000, would include: bridges, implant cases, cosmetic veneer cases, etc.; payment will be required by the completion of treatment.

  • Two (2) dental examinations and simple cleaning visits
  • Evaluation of teeth and gums
  • Evaluation of head and neck
  • Cancer screening exams
  • Blood pressure checks, upon request
  • Cosmetic evaluation, upon request
  • All necessary x-rays and diagnostic photographs
  • Emergency examinations and x-rays
  • Consultations for restorative and esthetic treatment
  • Two (2) dental examinations and cleanings
  • Fluoride treatment
  • Necessary x-rays
  • Emergency examinations and x-rays
  • Consultations for restorative and esthetic treatment
  • Treatment for periodontal (gum) disease
  • Crowns (excluding implant crowns) , bridges, and dentures
  • Root canals
  • Metallic and tooth-colored fillings
  • Sealants
  • Laser-assisted treatment
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Extractions

Exclusions

The following services are not covered by this plan:

  • Any treatment by dentist or staff outside of this office
  • Any treatment by specialist, oral surgeons, orthodontists, periodontists, endodontists, etc.
  • Inclusive Implant and Denture packages
  • Implants, implant crowns, implant-supported dentures, or implant-supported bridges
  • The crowns, dentures, and dental appliances ARE covered, however, the attachments for those appliances that utilize special hardware are not covered
  • Hospitalization for dental procedures
