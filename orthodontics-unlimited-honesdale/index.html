The practice

Our office is pleased to offer you a complimentary consultation at your first visit. A comprehensive oral exam will be performed, and we will give you professional recommendations regarding your orthodontic care.

We will inform you if any treatment is needed, what type of treatment is available, when your treatment would start, and an estimated treatment time. If treatment is necessary, diagnostic records are taken to determine the best course of treatment.

This information is used to develop a custom treatment plan for each patient. After the doctor has evaluated your records, they will discuss your treatment with you in detail, including the cost for your particular case.

No two people are exactly the same, so the approach to your orthodontic treatment shouldn’t be either.

Every treatment plan is customized specifically to your needs. We are pleased to have many types of Orthodontic Technology to utilize in your custom plan.

Our practice is a Carriere Class II and Class III motion appliance provider.

Corrects the bite to an ideal Class I platform first by rotating and up righting the maxillary first molars while moving the posterior segment, from canine or premolor to molars, into a perfect bite.

Simultaneously produces a light, uniform force for distal molar movement.
