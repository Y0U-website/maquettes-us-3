Initial Consultation

Prior to your initial appointment please provide us with all your insurance information. Having this information prior to your appointment gives us enough time to obtain your benefits, eligibility, and answer any coverage questions you might have.

Your initial appointment will consist of any necessary X-rays, diagnosis and a consultation regarding your treatment options. Some Treatments can be rendered the same day. If you are in pain we will do our best to see you the same day that you call. We will make every effort to relieve your pain through emergency treatment, and then schedule you for the necessary follow-ups.

If you have dental and medical insurance please bring us your current insurance cards along with a current picture ID. If you don’t have an insurance card, we will need the subscriber, name, social security number, and birthday. This will save time and allow us to help you in the processing of your claim.

If you have been referred by another doctor please bring the referral slip with you to your appointment so that we may provide the care for which you have been referred.
