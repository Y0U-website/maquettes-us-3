Meet the team

The doctors and staff at Smilin Dental are highly-trained, well-educated, and caring individuals, who are strongly committed to providing genuine, comprehensive care. We partner with our patients to help them achieve their ideal dental health.

Steven J. Bacalar, D.M.D.

Dr. Bacalar was raised in the northwest suburbs of Chicago, and earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, Master’s degree in Biotechnology from Rush University Medical Center, and Doctor of Dental Medicine from the progressive College of Dental Medicine at Midwestern University in Arizona. He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and American Dental Association, as well as active in numerous study clubs. He is passionate about continuing education and provides his patients with personalized, comprehensive and comfortable dental care.

Dr. Bacalar lives in Northbrook with his wife, Rachel, and two daughters. He is an avid golfer, and enjoys cooking, traveling and cheering on his beloved Cubs and Blackhawks.

Kenneth N. Milin, D.D.S.

Dr. Milin grew up in the northern suburbs of Chicago. He graduated from the University of Illinois, Urbana and the University of Illinois School of Dentistry, Chicago. He completed a general practice residency at the University of Chicago and has been practicing in Winnetka since 1983. Dr. Milin has taken hundreds of hours of continuing education focusing on Cosmetics and Implant Dentistry. He enjoys lecturing to the residents at Illinois Masonic hospital and to various study clubs.

Dr. Milin is married to Nicki and has two children, Samantha and James, and one granddaughter Fallon. Dr. Milin is passionate about providing the highest level of personalized patient care. He feels lucky to have the opportunity to provide dental services that improves the quality of life for each of his patients.

Hannah M. Leahey, D.M.D.

Dr. Leahey is a Tufts graduate who grew up right outside of Seattle, Washington. She earned a B.S. in Biology from Dickinson College, and then attended Tufts University of Dental Medicine. During her time, she contributed to research projects in the Oral Surgery Department. Her dental research has been presented at the American College of Oral Maxillofacial Surgery Annual Conference and the International Association of Dental Research General Session. In her free time she enjoys hiking, painting, yoga, and spending time with her family.

Tamara P.

Tamara grew up on the Northwest side of Chicago and graduated from Loyola University’s Dental Hygiene program over 25 years ago. Tamara is a people person and develops and nurtures relationships with her patients through education and humor (fun fact: she trained at Second City!). Tamara strives to help her patients obtain and maintain healthy smiles for life. In her free time, Tamara likes to garden, bake and see live theatre performances with her two daughters and husband.

Ilyse B.

Ilyse started her career as a dental assistant and has worked as a dental hygienist for 25 years. She builds trusting relationships with her patients and helps them to achieve dental goals. She earned a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology from the University of Colorado and Associate’s degree in Dental Hygiene from Harper College. She lives in Deerfield with her husband and large extended family, and is the proud mom to a son and daughter. She is an avid athlete who participates in triathlon and running events, and volunteers with the Dare2tri, GLASA and CBO organizations.

Diana S.

Diana started in dentistry in 1980, and has been with our practice since 2000. Her passion is caring for her patients and she strives to improve the quality of her patients lives through excellent oral care. Diana lives in Chicago with her husband Tom and rescue dog, Ben. She has three grown children, one daughter-in-law, and two incredible grandchildren. Diana works out with a trainer several times a week in order to maintain her 75 lbs. weight loss - and feel awesome! Diana earned her degree in Dental Hygiene from the University of Illinois at Chicago.

Karin N.

You will often hear Karin singing to the office music.
