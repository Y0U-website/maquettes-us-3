Advanced Dental Care in Lihue and Kauai

Dentistry has changed significantly over the past few decades, and Dr. Jaurequi makes it a priority to stay current. He completes ongoing continuing education so the techniques and materials he uses reflect modern standards of care.

Digital dentistry plays a major role in the patient experience at our practice. Digital x-rays , intraoral cameras , and 3-D cone beam imaging help us diagnose conditions and plan treatment with accuracy.

Digital impressions also replace the older messy paste process, making restorative appointments more comfortable and the impressions more precise. You can learn more on our Technology page.

For patients who feel anxious, nitrous oxide is available to provide gentle relaxation during treatment. It wears off quickly so you can return to your day without lingering drowsiness.
