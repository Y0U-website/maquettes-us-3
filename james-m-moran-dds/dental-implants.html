Dental Implants

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a type of restoration that can be used for individuals who have lost one more teeth. Years ago, individuals with tooth loss had to choose between dentures and bridges. While both solutions are still around today, they only replace the visible portion of tooth above the gumline. Without a tooth root, the jawbone will begin to resorb, which can lead to further tooth loss as well as premature aging of the face. Dental implants themselves replace the tooth root for a fully functional replacement.

The dental implant process

The implant itself can be made from titanium or zirconia and is a screw-like piece that is surgically placed into the jawbone. As the area heals, the bone and implant will fuse together to create a stable foundation for your artificial tooth. Once the healing process is complete, an abutment (connector) is attached to the implant and finally, the restoration is completed with a custom fabricated dental crown. For individuals missing an entire arch of teeth, several strategically placed implants can be used to support an arch of dentures.

Benefits of dental implants

There are so many reasons patients choose dental implants for missing teeth. Here are some of our patients’ favorite benefits.

  • Improved confidence – With dental implants, you will be able to eat and speak with full confidence and never have to worry about your dentures slipping out of place.
  • Improved oral health – Dental implants stimulate the jawbone like the natural tooth root, so you don’t have to worry about bone degeneration or healthy teeth slipping out of place. Additionally, you can practice regular oral hygiene to maintain oral health.
  • Long-lasting – With good oral hygiene, dental implants can last for decades or even longer making them a sound investment in your smile and your oral health.
