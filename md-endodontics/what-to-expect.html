&#x2714; Pre and Post Op Care

We are fully committed to your dental health and well-being.

Our goal is to provide you with a dental experience that exceeds your expectations. Our helpful staff will answer any and all questions you may have to ensure confidence and comfort during your visit. We offer several payment options, and we will help you check into any insurance benefits.

1. Consultation

During your visit, our gentle team will thoroughly examine your tooth, conducting tests and an x-ray to determine the best treatment plan for you. We understand that you may have questions, and we’re more than happy to address them before we begin any procedures, putting your mind at ease.

3. Follow-up

Following your root canal, we’ll make sure you’re well taken care of. You’ll need to visit your dentist for follow-up treatment, and we’ll send them a detailed report. Your dentist will then determine the best type of restoration to ensure your smile is as good as new.

4. Post-Op Care

Remember, we’re here to support you every step of the way, providing compassionate care and understanding. We provide you with a step-by-step guide to post-operative care. If you ever have any questions or concerns, give us a call and we’ll be glad to address those for you.
