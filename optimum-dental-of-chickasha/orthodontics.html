Orthodontics

Straightening crowded or spaced teeth can improve appearance, cleaning access, bite function, and long-term comfort. Optimum Dental offers both Invisalign clear aligners and traditional braces.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

A planned series of removable aligners can gradually move teeth for appropriate patients while allowing the trays to be removed for meals and routine hygiene.

Traditional Braces

Brackets and wires provide precise control for a wide range of alignment and bite concerns. Your dentist will explain the recommended approach and expected timeline.
