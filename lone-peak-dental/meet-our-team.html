Meet Dr. Lorscheider

Sean Lorscheider, D.M.D.

Cedar Hills Dentist

For Dr. Lorscheider, it s all about family. Regardless of whether it s his Lone Peak Dental team or his patients, everyone here is treated as one of the family.

Dr. Lorscheider s history is rooted in Utah. Born and raised in Utah, he grew up with the unique opportunity of observing his father practice dentistry, and knew from an early age that he too, had a passion for dentistry.

After serving an LDS mission in Buenos Aires, Argentina, he returned home to Utah and completed his undergraduate studies at Brigham Young University. It is here where he met and married his college sweetheart, Ardice. After graduating from the University of Louisville, School of Dentistry in Louisville, Kentucky, he returned to Utah once again where he had the honor of working in the same practice with his father in Orem, Utah. During this time, he also spent time working at a specialized pediatric practice in Salt Lake City.

In January of 2004, Dr. Lorscheider proudly opened Lone Peak Dental to the community. It has been, and continues to be, an on-going opportunity for Dr. Lorscheider to provide a practice where both exceptional dental care and a warm, family-friendly environment are unparalleled.

Dr. Lorscheider and his wife, Ardice, live in North Utah County where they stay busy raising their three beautiful daughters, their lively dogs, Summit and Alta, and two sweet bunnies. Away from the office, he loves nothing more than to spend time with his family. He also enjoys skiing, hiking, camping, and taking trips to the beach any time he can. Dr. L is a true Blue and White Fan and loves to watch BYU sporting events.

"We proudly offer the very best in dental technology, treatment options, and make our patients comfort a top priority. Whether you re looking to improve your smile or searching for the gentlest dental care, I warmly welcome you to be a part of our growing dental family."
