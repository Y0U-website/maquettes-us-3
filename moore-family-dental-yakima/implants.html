Implants

  • Say goodbye to embarrassing gaps or loose dentures.
  • Dental implants don’t require the alteration of adjacent teeth, preserving your overall oral health.
  • They stimulate bone growth, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.
  • Implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • No need to worry about frequent replacements, as with other dental solutions.
  • Dental implants eliminate the discomfort and inconvenience of removable dentures.
  • You can eat your favorite foods without restrictions.
  • Implants blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring a beautiful and harmonious smile.

Our Dental Implant Process

At Moore Family Dental, our dedicated team of professionals specializes in providing comprehensive dental implant services. We prioritize your comfort and well-being throughout every step of the process. Here’s an overview of our dental implant services:

  • During your initial visit, we’ll assess your oral health and discuss your goals and concerns.
  • We’ll answer all your questions and address any apprehensions you may have about the implant procedure.
  • Our experienced dentists will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.
  • We use cutting-edge 3D imaging and digital technology to ensure precision and accuracy.
  • Our skilled oral surgeons will surgically place the dental implant in your jawbone.
  • We prioritize your comfort and use local anesthesia to ensure a painless experience.
  • Over a few months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration.
  • This ensures a stable and secure foundation for your new tooth.
  • Once the implant has fully integrated, we will attach a custom-made crown that matches the color and shape of your natural teeth.
  • Your new tooth will blend seamlessly with your existing smile.
  • We’ll provide detailed instructions on how to care for your dental implant to ensure its long-term success.
  • Regular check-ups and maintenance are crucial for maintaining your oral health.
