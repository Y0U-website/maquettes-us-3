Our clear braces system, Lightforce, started as a small idea in 2015, and is now a major player in the digital braces world and has its corporate office and full 3D printing factory in Massachustess. Click here to learn more.

Energy Saving Technology

All of our lights in the office are high output energy efficient LED, and are on a master switch, which turns them off every night.

Our air compressor, and suction unit are designed to be energy saving, and turn on automatically only when the demand is needed.

Our suction unit is a “dry” suction, the next generation in suction technology, which does not waste water in order to create the suction force, like the older “wet” suction units.

Our smart thermostat saves energy on heating and cooling while the office is empty.

Less Plastic Waste, More Recycled Materials

At Mosery Orthodontics we try to reduce landfill waste as much as possible.

All of our drinking cups are paper, and recyclable.

We focus on paperless communication and patient forms, to save both time, and paper waste.

We use high quality sterilizable and autoclavable equipment whenever possible to save on single use plastics.

Our digital scanner saves on the plastic impression trays, as well as the carbon footprint of shipping impressions to be processed.

Invisalign, our clear aligner company has a recycling program, focused on reducing plastic waste. To learn more about Invisalign’s social and environmental initiatives, click here.
