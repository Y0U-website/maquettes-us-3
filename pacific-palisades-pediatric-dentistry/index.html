Using state-of-the-art Waterlase technology, we perform precise, gentle frenectomies that improve nursing, speech, and airway function. These treatments are safe, efficient, and can often be completed in just one visit.

We offer laughing gas and general anesthesia for children who need a little extra support during treatment. Our goal is to make every procedure as comfortable and low-stress as possible - for both kids and parents.

From thorough cleanings and fluoride treatments to sealants and silver diamine fluoride, we focus on preventive care that keeps little smiles healthy from the start. Our gentle approach helps set the stage for a lifetime of oral wellness.

We want every family to feel confident about their child’s treatment plan. That’s why we offer free second opinions and free consultations for lip and tongue ties. Whether you&#x27;re unsure about a diagnosis or exploring new options, we’re happy to help.
