About Oakberry Dental

Our philosophy centers on patient education and empowerment. We take the time to get to know you, understand your oral health goals, and walk you through your treatment options. Using advanced imaging and diagnostic tools, we show you exactly what we see — so you feel informed and confident in every decision you make about your care.

We’re committed to staying at the forefront of dental technology to ensure your comfort, safety, and results. From digital X-rays and intraoral cameras to modern sterilization techniques and materials, our tools help us deliver efficient, minimally invasive care.

We’re also proud to be an insurance-friendly practice and strive to make quality dental care accessible and affordable. Our team is happy to help you understand your coverage and treatment plan, and we offer flexible scheduling to fit your busy life.

New patients are always welcome, and we look forward to helping you achieve a healthier, brighter smile — on your terms.

  • Cosmetic Dentistry – Treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding to enhance the appearance of your smile.
  • Restorative Dentistry – Solutions such as crowns, bridges, and dentures to repair or replace damaged teeth.
  • Emergency Dentistry – Prompt care for toothaches, chipped or knocked-out teeth, and other urgent dental needs.
  • Oral Surgery – Common procedures like tooth extractions, wisdom teeth removal, and implant placement.

Meet Dr. Kaur — a compassionate family dentist dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered care to the Lemoore community. Dr. Kaur earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Nicolae Testemitanu University of Medicine and Pharmacy and brings years of experience in restorative dentistry.

Known for her gentle approach and caring personality, Dr. Kaur is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, confident, and informed about their dental health. She believes that great dentistry starts with trust, communication, and creating a positive experience for every patient who walks through the door.

Dr. Kaur is committed to utilizing the latest dental technology to make treatment more efficient, comfortable, and convenient. She enjoys treating patients of all ages and values the long-term relationships she builds with families in the community.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kaur enjoys hiking with her family and volunteering at local community events. Her warm personality and dedication to exceptional care make her a valued part of the Oakberry Dental team.
