Our Practice

At Monica Valencia, DDS , we proudly continue a legacy of exceptional dental care that began over 30 years ago. Our Story

Marta Ward, DDS built this practice with a focus on patient-centered care and lasting relationships. After practicing together for five years, Monica Valencia, D.D.S. took over the practice when Dr. Ward retired. Dr. Valencia continues to uphold the standards of excellence and personalized care that our patients have come to expect.

Meet Monica Valencia, D.D.S.

Monica Valencia, D.D.S. brings a wealth of experience and a gentle touch to our practice. Originally from Colombia, she earned her first Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2009 and later pursued her second DDS degree at the University of Southern California , where she excelled academically.

Dr. Valencia is a strong advocate of Biomimetic Dentistry , a modern approach that focuses on minimally invasive techniques using materials that mimic the natural properties of teeth. This philosophy allows her to preserve more of your natural tooth structure while delivering long-lasting, aesthetically pleasing results.

To stay at the forefront of dental innovation, Dr. Valencia is a proud graduate of the prestigious Kois Center , a leading continuing education program known for its advanced, evidence-based approach to dentistry. Her Kois Center training reflects her commitment to providing patients with comprehensive, precise, and modern dental care.

Known for her compassionate approach, Dr. Valencia excels at making even the most anxious patients feel at ease. She is a proud member of the American Dental Association , California Dental Association , and Western Los Angeles Dental Society , staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Valencia enjoys completing puzzles, traveling, and enjoying a cup of matcha tea.
