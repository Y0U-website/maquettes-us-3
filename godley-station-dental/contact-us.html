Meet Our Doctors

Matthew J. Allen, DDS

After receiving a BS in biology from the University of Toledo, Dr. Allen earned his DDS from Ohio State University. He is committed to helping our patients understand their overall oral and facial health while providing them with personalized, state of the art treatment solutions. Dr. Allen founded Godley Station Dental in 2004 with a desire to build a patient centered practice where he could offer his experience and a level of service that helped patients look forward to trips to the dentist.

Constantly in pursuit of excellence, Dr. Allen has trained extensively with some of the nation’s finest cosmetic and reconstructive dentists including the Ross Nash Institute and the Hornbrook Group. In addition, he is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, The American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, and The Crown Council. Dr. Allen strongly believes in continuing education and strives to stay at the forefront of the profession.

Dr. Allen and his wife, Melinda, are the proud parents of two sons, Brayden and Avery, and one daughter, Leighton. Outside of the practice, Dr. Allen enjoys playing golf and tennis, boating, travel, and spending time with his friends and family. He has also been a frequent volunteer at the Union Mission homeless shelter in Savannah.

Joseph E. Brown, III, DMD

Dr. Joseph E. Brown, III joined Godley Station Dental in December of 2024 . A native of Florence, S.C, he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical University of South Carolina, College of Dental Medicine, Charleston. He also earned a Bachelor of Science degree in biology from the College of Charleston. Dr. Brown brings 16 years of dentistry experience to the practice, having also served as a lieutenant commander in the U.S. Navy Reserve for seven years. He has special training in Invisalign treatment, short-term orthodontics, Botox and dermal fillers.

Dr. Brown had the honor of serving as past President of the South Carolina Coastal District Dental Association, and as President of the Medical University of South Carolina Alumni Association. He is a fellow of the Pierre Fauchard Academy, an international honorary dental service organization whose fellowship is by invitation only. He is also a diplomate at the Academy of Clinical Sleep Disorders Disciplines.

Dr. Brown volunteers as a youth athletics coach for soccer, futsal, baseball and basketball, and serves as Treasurer of the Board for the Jenkins Athletic Club. He has two children, Jeb, 11, and Lawson, 9. In his free time, he enjoys disc golf, boating and chess. He is a Clemson Tigers fan, and he has a twin sister. Dr. Brown resides in Hardeeville, S.C.

Chase W. Fetters, DMD

Dr. Chase W. Fetters graduated from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine in 2013. He was selected for the U.S. Navy Health Professions Scholarship Program and served almost six years in the United States Navy as a dentist supporting marines at both Camp Lejeune and Parris Island. Dr. Fetters completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry certificate program and is passionate about quality dentistry in a relaxed atmosphere.

Dr. Fetters has a loving family, his wife Kimberly, who is also a dentist, and their two children Caden and Avery. He spends his free time reading Sci-Fi/Fantasy epics and making valiant attempts at gardening. When he moved to the Lowcountry, he fell in love and knew this was where he wanted to stay.

Catherine H. Hatch, DMD

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Hatch retains memberships in the American Dental Association, the State of Georgia Dental Association, the Savannah Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. Additionally, Dr. Hatch has completed numerous post-doctoral training programs including a 15 hour dental esthetics continuum and training in short term orthodontics, Botox and dermal fillers, and dental sleep medicine for the treatment of obstructive sleep apnea.

Dr. Hatch was raised in a tight-knit family and was the oldest of four children. Her husband, David, is a native of Rome, GA. They enjoy listening to music and attending concerts. Dr. Hatch is an active member of St. John the Baptist Parish of Savannah. When Dr. Hatch has time, going to the beach, running, and a self-proclaimed foodie she enjoys cooking. One of Dr. Hatch’s favorite things to do is travel, and she spent several months following college backpacking through Europe. Additionally, Dr. Hatch has participated in mission trips to Costa Rica and Nicaragua.

Richard L. Stayer II, DMD

Dr. Richard Lee Stayer II joins Godley Station Dental as a third-generation dentist with over four years of professional experience and a deep-rooted family legacy in oral healthcare. A summa cum laude graduate of the Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry at Temple University, Dr. Stayer is a certified Invisalign provider dedicated to restorative excellence. He is driven by the immediate, positive impact dentistry has on a patient’s life, focusing his practice on procedures that eliminate pain and restore both a patient’s smile and their self-confidence.

Originally from the Northeast, Dr. Stayer and his wife, Gabriela, recently relocated over 700 miles from Royersford, Pennsylvania, to make their home in the Lowcountry. Outside of the office, he is an avid golfer and a passionate college football fan who enjoys staying active and exploring the Bluffton area. Dr. Stayer looks forward to building long-term relationships with our patient family and providing the compassionate, high-quality care that is the hallmark of our practice.
