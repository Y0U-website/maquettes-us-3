The practice

Transparency and education are at the heart of our approach. With advanced dental imaging technology, we clearly explain why specific treatments are necessary and how preventive strategies can protect your smile. We use only the highest quality dental materials and the latest innovations in dental technology to deliver precise, comfortable, and beautiful results you can trust.

Cosmetic Dentistry Porcelain veneers, professional whitening and bonding to refine the shape, color and proportion of your smile — designed to look natural.

Oral Surgery Wisdom teeth, surgical extractions, sinus lifts and bone grafting — performed in-house and guided by low-dose 3D CBCT imaging for accuracy.

Dental Implants Replace missing teeth with natural-looking, stable implants — including same-day options where suitable, planned and placed right here in Kentfield.

See all services All-inclusive, under one roof Implants, oral surgery, cosmetics, Invisalign and more — the specialty work most offices refer out, we do right here in Kentfield.

Comfort & sedation Nitrous, oral and IV sedation, ceiling-mounted monitors and noise-cancelling headphones — care should feel calm, even if you’re anxious.

24/7 emergency care We reserve time for same-day emergencies — and established patients can reach us on-call any time, including weekends and late evenings.

Conservative & preventative We treat small problems early — preserving your natural teeth and helping you avoid bigger, costlier work down the road.

Les soins

Cleanings, exams, tooth-colored fillings, crowns and root canals — the preventative and restorative care that keeps your smile healthy for the long term.

Once the implant is solid, we craft and attach a custom porcelain crown — color-matched to your smile — completing a replacement tooth designed to look and function like the one nature gave you.

Using a 3D-printed surgical guide made in-house, Dr. G places the titanium post into the jaw in a comfortable, focused visit. If needed, a temporary tooth can keep your smile looking whole while you heal.

Invisalign A clearer way to straighten your smile — removable, nearly invisible aligners, planned digitally and guided by a dentist in our Kentfield office.

Porcelain Veneers Thin, custom porcelain shells designed to refine the shape, color, proportion and alignment of your smile — previewed before we ever touch a tooth.
