Dental Implants

The Dental Implants Procedure

The Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Improving your speech and your ability to eat and enjoy your favorite foods.
  • Replacing missing teeth with a permanent solution that looks and feels natural.
  • Restoring your confidence by helping you to smile again and feel proud of your smile.
  • Helping you maintain your youthful appearance by preventing jawbone loss when a tooth is missing.
  • Protecting your oral health allows you to continue performing normal oral functions such as chewing, speaking, and smiling quickly and confidently.
  • Preserve the health of your surrounding teeth by not requiring support from adjacent natural teeth or using adhesives in most cases.

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Dental implants are a safe and effective way to replace any number of missing teeth. They are made of titanium, a biocompatible material that does not interfere with other bodily systems. Titanium is strong enough to support dental appliances, including dentures and crowns. It is also the only metal the human body does not reject when implanted.
