Les soins

Extensive and thorough prophylaxis is done to preserve health and prevent disease. This is a protective and preventative treatment combined with patient education.

We offer same day crowns with out state of the art digital technology.

Dr McKinley uses Biohorizon lazer lock technology to place the implant in the bone as the support anchor for new teeth. This provides the best fusion to bone available today.

We offer a variety of procedures including white fillings, bonding, and non-metal crowns; all of which are very common in treatment when taking esthetics into consideration.
