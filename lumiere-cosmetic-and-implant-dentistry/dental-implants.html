Dental Implants in Maitland, FL

Why Dental Implants Are the Gold Standard

Dentures and bridges have their place, but they don’t address the root of the problem, which is the loss of the tooth root itself. A dental implant is a small titanium post placed directly into the jawbone, where it melds with the bone through a natural process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation that functions exactly like a real tooth root. The custom crown placed on top is matched to your surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color, so the result looks completely natural from day one.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults who have lost one or more teeth are candidates for implants, but a thorough evaluation is always the first step. If you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone, that doesn’t automatically rule you out. Bone grafting is a well-established procedure that can rebuild the necessary foundation before implant placement begins, and our team has the surgical training to perform both procedures in-house.

At Lumiere, we use 3D CBCT imaging to assess your bone density, anatomy, and nerve positioning before a single step is taken. This technology allows us to assess your implant candidacy with a level of detail that flat X-rays simply can’t provide, and guided surgery protocols allow us to place implants exactly where they need to go for the best long-term outcome. Patients who smoke, manage diabetes, or have other health considerations are not automatically disqualified, and we take the time to review each case individually before making a recommendation.

Your Dental Implant Options in Maitland, FL

Dental implants aren’t one-size-fits-all, and the right solution depends on how many teeth you’ve lost and where. A single-tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting the teeth on either side, while implant-supported bridges offer a fixed solution for multiple adjacent missing teeth. For patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, full-mouth dental implants provide a permanent, fully anchored restoration that looks and functions like natural teeth, with no removal required at night.

Each option varies in timeline, cost, and surgical complexity, which is why we take the time to walk you through every available path before making any recommendations. Our dental implant FAQs cover many of the questions patients have going into their first consultation, including what to expect during recovery, how to care for implants long term, and how long the process typically takes from evaluation to final restoration.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

For most patients, the implant process unfolds in a few distinct phases. After your initial evaluation and imaging, the implant post is placed into the jawbone during a surgical appointment. A healing period follows, typically a few months, during which the implant integrates with the surrounding bone. Once that integration is confirmed, a custom abutment and crown are placed, completing the restoration. The total timeline depends on whether bone grafting is needed and how quickly the body heals, but the end result is a fixed, functional tooth built to last.

What patients often appreciate most is how closely the final restoration resembles a natural tooth. Because we use CAD/CAM technology and intraoral scanning at Lumiere, the crown is designed digitally and fabricated with a level of precision that ensures a proper fit and a seamless appearance alongside your existing teeth. Our team walks you through each phase so you always know what’s coming next, and there are no surprises along the way.
