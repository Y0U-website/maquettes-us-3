Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults missing one or more teeth are good candidates. The main factors are healthy gums and enough jawbone to anchor the implant — and if you’ve lost bone over time, a bone graft can often rebuild it so implants are still possible.

Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, or gum disease can affect healing, but they don’t automatically rule you out. The only way to know for sure is a quick exam and X-ray, which is exactly what your consultation covers.
