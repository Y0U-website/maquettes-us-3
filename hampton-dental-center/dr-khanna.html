Introducing Dr. Anshu Khanna “Dr. K”

Dr. Khanna is a graduate of Howard University College of Dentistry, where she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) Residency. She also earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the Army College of Dental Sciences in India, and holds a Master’s in Public Health from the UTHealth Science Center in Houston. She is an active member of the American Dental Association.

Dedicated to advancing her skills, Dr. Khanna regularly pursues continuing education in complex cosmetic and restorative dentistry, laser dentistry, and esthetic dentistry. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending quality time with her family, exploring the outdoors, traveling, hiking, and cooking.

Drs. Sam and Khanna are thrilled to join the team at Hampton Dental Center. They are excited to bring their shared passion for dentistry and commitment to excellence to the vibrant Hampton community and surrounding areas. As a patient at our practice, you’ll benefit from modern, personalized care delivered by this compassionate husband-and-wife team.
