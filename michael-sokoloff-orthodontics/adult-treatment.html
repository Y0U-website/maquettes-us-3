Orthodontic Treatment Options for Adults

Michael Sokoloff Orthodontics provides several treatment options to address your orthodontic needs while fitting your lifestyle. Here’s a look at the choices available:

Each option has its own unique benefits, giving you the flexibility to choose what works best for your goals and lifestyle.

  • Invisalign® Clear Aligners These nearly invisible aligners are a favorite for adults who want a discreet option. They’re removable, allowing you to eat your favorite foods and keep up with your normal brushing and flossing routine. Their flexibility makes them ideal for busy lifestyles.
  • Ceramic Braces Ceramic braces blend with the natural color of your teeth, offering a subtle yet effective way to straighten your smile. They work similarly to traditional braces but are far less noticeable, making them a great middle-ground option.
  • Lingual Braces Placed behind the teeth, lingual braces are completely hidden from view. This makes them a perfect choice for adults who want their orthodontic treatment to be invisible while still addressing complex alignment issues.
  • Traditional Metal Braces Modern metal braces are smaller and more comfortable than ever. They are a reliable and efficient choice for adults who need a straightforward solution for correcting their smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Adult Orthodontic Treatment

Starting orthodontic treatment as an adult can bring up a lot of questions. Here are some of the most common concerns we hear and the answers you need:

Am I too old for orthodontic treatment?

Absolutely not! Orthodontic treatment works at any age, and many adults are choosing to improve their smiles. The techniques and tools available today are designed to be effective and discreet, no matter your age. Whether it’s braces or aligners, the results can benefit your health and confidence.

How long does treatment typically last?

Treatment length depends on your specific needs. On average, most adults complete their orthodontic journey in 12 to 24 months. During your initial consultation, we’ll provide a personalized timeline so you know exactly what to expect.

Will braces or aligners affect my lifestyle?

Not as much as you might think! Invisalign aligners are removable, making it easy to eat, brush, and floss without disruption. Even if you choose braces, modern designs are more comfortable and less intrusive than ever before. Our team helps ensure your treatment fits into your daily routine with minimal inconvenience.
