What to Expect When Getting Veneers

How Long Do Veneers Last?

With proper care, veneers can last anywhere from 10 to 15 years, and sometimes, even longer. Porcelain veneers tend to be more durable and stain-resistant than composite resin, which is why they’re often the preferred choice for long-term results. That said, composite veneers are still a solid option and can be more affordable. The lifespan of your veneers depends on how well you take care of them and your overall oral health. Regular checkups help us monitor your veneers and catch any issues early, so they stay looking great for as long as possible.

Caring for Your Veneers

Caring for veneers doesn’t need to be complicated. Treat them like you would your natural teeth. Brush twice a day with a soft toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Floss gently along the gumline to keep your gums healthy. Avoid biting down on hard objects like popcorn kernels, ice, or pens, as this can chip or crack your veneers. If you grind your teeth at night, ask us about a nightguard to protect your investment. With the right routine, veneers can look great for years. And since they resist stains better than natural enamel, your fresh smile will stay brighter between visits.

Are Veneers Right for You?

Veneers aren’t the right solution for everyone, and that’s okay. If you have significant decay, gum disease, or severe misalignment, those issues need to be addressed first. Veneers work best on healthy teeth and gums. If you grind your teeth heavily or play contact sports without a mouthguard, veneers might not hold up as well. During your consultation, Dr. Carillon will evaluate your oral health and discuss whether veneers are a good fit for your situation. If they’re not, we’ll recommend other options that can still give you the results you’re looking for.
