Benefits of a Single Tooth Implant

Natural Appearance

Your implant crown is custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth.

Stable & Comfortable

Unlike removable appliances, a dental implant is securely anchored in the jawbone, providing excellent stability while eating and speaking.

Preserves Healthy Teeth

A traditional bridge often requires preparing the neighboring teeth. A single implant restores the missing tooth independently, preserving healthy tooth structure.

Supports Jawbone Health

After tooth loss, the jawbone in that area naturally begins to shrink over time. A dental implant helps stimulate the bone, supporting its long-term health.

Long-Term Solution

With proper home care and regular dental visits, dental implants can provide a durable, long-lasting tooth replacement option.

What Is the Dental Implant Process?

Consultation & Planning

Your visit begins with a comprehensive examination, digital imaging, and a discussion of your treatment goals.

Implant Placement

The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. Greenhill Family Dentistry may coordinate this phase of treatment with a trusted oral surgeon or periodontist when appropriate.

Healing

Over the following months, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone through a natural process called osseointegration.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom implant crown is attached to the implant, completing your smile with a natural-looking restoration.

We’ll explain your personalized treatment timeline during your consultation.

Single Tooth Implant vs. Dental Bridge

Both dental implants and dental bridges are excellent options for replacing a single missing tooth.

We’ll help you compare both treatments and determine which is most appropriate for your smile, oral health, and goals.

  • Does not rely on neighboring teeth
  • Helps preserve jawbone
  • Provides independent support
  • Offers a natural appearance
  • Uses adjacent teeth for support
  • May be completed in a shorter timeframe
  • Can be an excellent option in certain clinical situations

Caring for Your Dental Implant

Dental implants require many of the same healthy habits as natural teeth.

Consistent preventive care helps protect both the implant and the surrounding gum tissue.

  • Brushing twice daily
  • Flossing carefully around the implant
  • Attending regular dental cleanings
  • Scheduling routine examinations
  • Avoiding tobacco products
  • Wearing a custom night guard if you grind your teeth

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a single tooth implant last?

With excellent oral hygiene, routine dental care, and healthy surrounding tissues, a dental implant can provide long-term support. The implant crown may eventually require maintenance or replacement due to normal wear.

Is getting a dental implant painful?

The implant is placed using local anesthesia, and many patients report that the procedure is more comfortable than expected. Some temporary soreness during healing is normal.

Can I replace a front tooth with an implant?

Yes. Single tooth implants are commonly used to replace front teeth, with careful attention given to aesthetics, gum health, and the appearance of the final crown.

Will my implant look natural?

Yes. Your custom crown is designed to complement the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth for a seamless appearance.

How soon after losing a tooth should I consider an implant?

Every situation is unique. Some patients may be candidates for immediate implant placement, while others benefit from allowing the area to heal first. We’ll recommend the timing that’s most appropriate for your situation.
