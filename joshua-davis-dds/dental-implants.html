Dental Implants in Tuckahoe, NY

One of our highly qualified practitioners will start with an overview of your oral health to see if you are a good candidate for a dental implant. This will include a comprehensive visual exam, x-rays, and impressions.

If the whole tooth, or part of the tooth, is still intact, our dental staff will need to remove it to make room for the post and artificial tooth. Sometimes, this can be done at the same time as the insertion of the post.

In some situations, a bone graft is necessary before placing the post. However, if your jawbone is strong and healthy enough, this step is not needed. If a bone graft is needed, you will need to wait for the area to heal before the post is placed.

When your jaw is ready, we’ll insert a post into the bone and attach an abutment at the gumline for proper healing. At this point, you will need to wait up to nine months for the area to heal before proceeding to the final step.

One of our highly trained and experienced dental professionals will monitor the healing process. Once your gums have healed successfully, an artificial tooth (crown) will be permanently installed. An x-ray will be taken to ensure you have a proper, comfortable bite. Any adjustments will be made at this time.
