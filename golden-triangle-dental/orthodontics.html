Orthodontics

What are Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is the field of dentistry that involves itself with correcting problems with smile alignment. If your teeth are crooked, crowded, unevenly spaced out or if you have a bite problem, orthodontic treatment is ideal in that it can create a more uniform smile. Not only is this beneficial to your confidence, but it can make cleaning and caring for your teeth so much easier.

Why are Orthodontics needed?

Orthodontic treatment might be needed if you have more serious problems with dentition alignment. It’s ideal for both adults and children alike. In fact, many adults who underwent orthodontic treatment as teenagers find that they need re-treatment later on because they failed to maintain their results with a retainer. Orthodontic treatment is specific to each individual patient.

Who’s a candidate for Orthodontics?

Both kids and adults alike can and should undergo orthodontic treatment if it’s needed. We can conduct a thorough examination to determine if you or a child would benefit from treatment. The sooner than treatment is started, the quicker and easier it will be. By correcting the alignment of the teeth, this can help patients to brush and floss more easily.
