Advanced Dental Implant Solutions in Youngsville

Dental implants represent the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, and at Maison Dental Studio, restorative dentists Dr. Murray and Dr. Mullen bring advanced training and extensive experience to every implant case. As Fellows of the International College of Oral Implantology (ICOI), our doctors completed intensive residency training in implant surgery allowing them to handle both straightforward and complex cases in-house, without referrals to outside specialists.

Unlike traditional bridges or dentures that rely on adjacent teeth for support or rest on the gums, dental implants function like natural tooth roots. Titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone and fuse with surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating stable, permanent foundations for crowns, bridges, or dentures. The result is a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions like the real thing and with proper care, can last a lifetime.

Our comprehensive implant services range from single tooth replacement to full arch restoration. We use advanced 3D imaging, digital treatment planning software, and surgical guides to ensure precise placement, optimal aesthetics, and predictable long-term success. From your initial consultation through your final restoration, you receive coordinated care from doctors who understand both the surgical and restorative sides of implant dentistry a significant advantage over practices that split those phases between different providers.

Dental Implant Options We Offer Every patient’s situation is different, which is why we offer a full range of implant solutions. Whether you’re missing one tooth or need a complete arch restored, our team builds a treatment plan around your specific anatomy, bone structure, and long-term goals.

Not every implant case is straightforward. Some patients require preparatory procedures before an implant can be placed. If the sinus cavity sits too close to the upper jawbone, a sinus lift creates the space needed for a successful placement.

If bone volume has diminished after tooth loss, bone grafting rebuilds the foundation that supports the implant long-term. These steps aren’t setbacks. They’re part of a careful, thorough process that gives your implant the best possible chance of lasting.

Next-Day Implant Restorations

Losing a tooth does not have to mean waiting months for a complete smile. Next-day implant restorations allow you to receive a temporary prosthetic within 24 hours of surgery, so you can leave our office with a functional, natural-looking tooth almost immediately. Both Dr. Murray and Dr. Mullen completed advanced education in general dentistry residencies with focused training in implant surgery and IV sedation — meaning your procedure is guided by hands-on clinical experience well beyond what dental school provides.

Our approach begins with precise 3D imaging and digital treatment planning to map out the exact placement of each implant before the procedure begins. This technology-driven process reduces guesswork, supports more predictable outcomes, and allows us to show you the plan on-screen at your consultation so you understand every step before committing to anything. Not every patient qualifies for the accelerated same-day timeline — sufficient bone density and healthy gum tissue are both required — but we evaluate each case individually and are transparent about what’s possible.

Because we offer everything from implant surgery to cosmetic dentistry under one roof, patients who want to pair their implant restoration with veneers, whitening, or a broader smile makeover can complete the entire transformation with the same doctors who already know their case. That continuity of care makes a meaningful difference in both outcomes and experience.
