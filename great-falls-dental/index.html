The mini dental implant procedure can be a much less involved process than conventional dental implants. For this reason, the procedure can sometimes be completed in as little one dental appointment.

In addition to saving time in the dental office, you may have less discomfort following this procedure due to its minimally involved nature (may not require surgical flaps or sutures).

Because mini dental implants are so time efficient, this procedure can sometimes be more cost effective than larger conventional implants.

Mini dental implants may also be more comfortable, have less healing time, and require fewer visits to the office.
