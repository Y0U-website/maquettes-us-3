What Is an Implant Crown?

An implant crown is a custom-fabricated replacement tooth that attaches to a dental implant post via a small connector called an abutment. Unlike a traditional crown that sits on a natural tooth, an implant crown replaces the entire tooth structure, from root to surface.

  • Durable, tooth-colored materials for a natural look
  • Custom-shaded to match your existing teeth
  • Restores full biting and chewing function
  • Designed for long-term daily use
