Fluoride for Kids in Brighton CO

Tooth decay is caused by a buildup of bacteria-filled plaque which wears away at the enamel of children’s teeth. When enough enamel wears away, cavities can begin to form which can lead to a whole host of issues with children’s oral health. Practicing good oral hygiene can go a long way toward reducing plaque and preventing issues with tooth decay, but if you’re looking for something to aid in protecting your children’s teeth by strengthening enamel you should consider fluoride for kids.

Fluoride is a natural mineral that is used in many oral hygiene products such as toothpastes and mouth rinses, and can sometimes be found in tap water. It aids in the strengthening of tooth enamel which helps to make teeth more resistant to cavities. Fluoride for kids are most often a topically applied gel, foam, or rinse that provides a concentration of fluoride higher than what you’ll find in over-the-counter products.

Fluoride can help your children protect their teeth and maintain a beautiful smile. If you’re considering fluoride for kids, here are some of the major advantages.

Sugary or acidic beverages wear away at the protective layer of your children’s teeth every time they drink them. This includes fruit juices, tomato juice, lemonade, soda, and more. Fluoride for kids helps to protect teeth from the corrosive qualities of sugary and acidic beverages by strengthening enamel, which can also reduce tooth sensitivity.

Receiving fluoride treatments and using oral hygiene products with added fluoride can drastically reduce your child’s odds of developing cavities and tooth decay. It does this by killing off cavity-causing bacteria that has lodged itself in difficult to reach areas of the teeth. Fluoride for kids also fills in microscopic holes in the teeth that will eventually form into cavities if left untreated.

While the oral health benefits may be the most important aspect of fluoride treatment, the cosmetic element should not be overlooked. If your child suffers from tooth decay significant enough to affect their smile, it can be detrimental to the development of their sense of self-confidence. With some simple fluoride for kids and oral hygiene products, you can drastically reduce the odds that your child’s smile, and their self-esteem, can be harmed by the effects of tooth decay.

Fluoride for kids and oral hygiene products are not free, but they are significantly cheaper than the extensive dental procedures that your child may have to undergo should their teeth become significantly decayed. Paying for the preventative care will ultimately save you a considerable sum of money in the long run, and it will save your child from the stress and anxiety of having dental work done.
