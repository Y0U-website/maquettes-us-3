Dentures: This young man knew he was ready for complete dentures. This is a difficult transition for some people, but David is an adaptable person and was motivated to improve his overall health. Aesthetic Crowns: this young lady, Selena, (age 25 in this photo) has a developmental anomaly with her teeth which resulted in increased cavities. As a child, her dentist placed white composite fillings, but she was still unhappy with the look of her front teeth. We placed Emax all-ceramic crowns and she was very happy with this conservative improvement.

Gum graft: Chris’s chief concern was the recession of his lower font teeth. We did a short procedure and took some tissue from the palate to graft the front area. This after picture was taken about 6 weeks after we completed the procedure.

Gum Modification: Marty has a condition which causes his gums to over grow, an issue which can result from certain medications. We used a combination of laser and surgery techniques to refine the gums. We had completed orthodontics previously, and he is dutifully wearing his retainer.

Health: This young lady, Tracy, was having a lot of health concerns and did not like the look of her tooth health. We addressed all her cavities and did a short course of orthodontics to straighten her teeth and make them easier for her to keep clean. We completed everything in less than 6 months and no extractions were needed.
