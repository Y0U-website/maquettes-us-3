Advantages of Dental Implants

PROCESS OF AN IMPLANT The process of an implant can take anywhere from 4 to 8 months

We take a scan to assess your bone level and health. The tooth is extracted and PRF is placed in the extraction site to allow for best healing.

After a healing phase of 3-4 months, we see you in office to take a scan to assess your bone level, and digitally plan the placement of your implant. The implant body is placed and a healing cap is secured to the implant to allow the site to heal.

After another healing phase of 3-4 months, we remove the healing cap and place a custom abutment and crown to complete your implant. From that point, the implant works and feels like a normal tooth.

Ceramic Dental Implants At Janssen Dental Clinic, we are proud to offer ceramic (zirconia) dental implants as a natural, metal-free alternative to traditional titanium implants. Ceramic implants are made from zirconium oxide, a biocompatible material that integrates safely with your bone and gum tissue. They’re ideal for patients who are seeking a more holistic, aesthetic, or hypoallergenic solution to tooth replacement.

Which implant type Is Right for You?

Both titanium and ceramic implants are safe, durable, and effective. The best choice depends on your unique needs, medical history, and personal preferences. During your consultation, we’ll discuss all options and help you make the most informed decision for your long-term oral and whole-body health.
