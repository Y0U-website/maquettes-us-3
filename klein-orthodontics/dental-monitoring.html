Dental Monitoring

How does Dental Monitoring work? At Dr. Brian’s office, we’re committed to making your smile transformation as smooth and convenient as possible. Here’s a step-by-step guide to how our app-based process works, ensuring that you stay connected with our team every step of the way.

Step 1 At an in-office, Dr. Brian and our team will provide instructions on how to use the app.

Step 2 Next, you’ll take weekly photos of your smile that come automatically, digitally, and securely back to Dr. Brian and our team.

Step 3 Dr. Brian will review each submission and respond with guidance on next steps via the app.

Step 4 You can monitor your smile transformation as your progress is updated with each new scan.

  • More efficient treatment progress
  • Secure, easy communication with our team
