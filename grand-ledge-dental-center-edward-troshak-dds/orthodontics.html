What Orthodontic options are available at Grand Ledge Smiles?

At Grand Ledge Smiles, we proudly offer three popular options for orthodontic treatment:

Invisalign: Made from transparent plastic, Invisalign aligners are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth. They are virtually invisible, making them a good choice for individuals conscious about their appearance during treatment. The removable aligners allow easy oral hygiene and the freedom to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. Invisalign aligners are smooth and do not irritate the gums or cheeks.

Traditional Braces: These are the most common and time-tested orthodontic treatment options. Braces consist of metal brackets affixed to the teeth and connected with wires. Traditional braces are known for their strength and effectiveness in correcting complex misalignments and bite problems. They are suitable for patients of all ages and offer precise control over tooth movements, providing predictable results and making them a trusted choice for orthodontic treatment.

Clear Braces: Also known as ceramic braces, clear braces offer a discreet alternative to traditional braces. They are made from translucent or tooth-colored materials that blend with the natural color of teeth. Clear braces provide the same benefits as traditional braces but with a more aesthetic appeal. They are durable, stain-resistant, and a good alternative for patients who want a more subtle orthodontic treatment.

How many visits to the orthodontist for braces?

The number of visits to the orthodontist for braces can vary depending on individual needs and the complexity of the case. Typically, patients visit every 4-8 weeks for adjustments and monitoring. The total treatment time usually ranges from 18 to 24 months.

How to shop around for orthodontics?

When shopping around for orthodontics, consider the following steps:

  • Research Credentials: Ensure the orthodontist is certified and has the necessary experience.
  • Read Reviews: Look for patient testimonials and reviews to gauge satisfaction levels.
  • Compare Costs: Get quotes from multiple providers and understand what is included in the cost.
  • Evaluate Technology: Check if the practice uses modern equipment and techniques.
  • Consultation: Schedule consultations to discuss treatment options and get a feel for the practice.
