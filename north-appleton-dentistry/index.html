The practice

I was drawn to dentistry at a very young age. My father is a general dentist in Florida and I grew up watching him, and eventually assisting in his practice, as he cared for patients. I saw how he listened to each patient, respected their concerns, and helped improve their quality of life. I knew, early on, that I wanted to do the same.

Dr. Daniel Cepeda , Dr. Emily Ruthven , and the North Appleton Dentistry team , do more than work on teeth: we care about the person who owns the smile!

You are important to us. We will always listen to your concerns, seek to understand your goals, and partner with you as you achieve a healthier smile and become your friends in the process.

Our practice offers a range of personalized preventive care and lots of patient education, so you’re able to keep your teeth healthy for life. When more intensive treatment is required, our doctors recommend advanced, conservative care for the best results.
