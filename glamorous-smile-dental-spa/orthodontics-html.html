Invisalign

Invisalign is a clear aligner system that shifts teeth using customizable trays that are designed to keep treatment more discreet while also creating healthier, more attractive smiles. The aligners are to be worn 20-22 hours a day when addressing crooked and gapped teeth, as well as overcrowding and misalignment. At the end of treatment, results will leave you with a straighter set of teeth that allows for a more confident appearance and improved oral health.

ClearCorrect

ClearCorrect is a set of aligners that are similar to Invisalign but are made with different materials and can (at times) produce faster results. Designed to be virtually undetectable, this unique system’s thinner trays ensure optimal comfort and easy use. While each case is different, ClearCorrect can address mild and even complex dental issues. Dr. Alkamary will discuss whether this treatment option is suitable for your smile during a scheduled consultation.
