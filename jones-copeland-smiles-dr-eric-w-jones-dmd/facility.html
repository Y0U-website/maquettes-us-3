Our Facility

In 2023 our facility relocation was complete. The new 7,500 square foot facility incorporates modern fully digital equipment, 3D imaging, 3D digital scanning, and spa-like surroundings to serve all patients — with no lectures or judgments.

In addition to technology updates, the facility also features extensive upgrades to enhance patient comfort. Patient treatment areas include comfortable lighting, 50” overhead HD tv’s, noise canceling headphones, and Ultraleather padded chairs. All rooms are also equipped for nitrous oxide sedation, along with 2 surgical suites for oral sedation, IV sedation, and General Anesthesia administration and monitoring.

We are excited to welcome new and longtime patients alike to our new space.
