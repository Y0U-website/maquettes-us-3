What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small, biocompatible posts that replace missing tooth roots and bond naturally with your jawbone. They create a stable foundation for custom crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring your smile’s strength and function. With the right care, implants can deliver lasting results that look and feel completely natural.

Acts like a real tooth root for secure chewing and biting

Matches surrounding teeth for a natural finish

The Dangers Of Resorption After Tooth Loss

When a tooth is lost, the bone beneath it no longer receives stimulation from the tooth root, causing gradual shrinkage. This process, called bone resorption, can alter your facial shape and make eating or speaking more difficult. Acting early is essential because untreated bone loss can complicate future tooth replacement, although solutions like dental implants can help preserve your bone structure.

The jawbone weakens and thins without support from a tooth root

Bone deterioration can create a sunken or aged appearance

Significant bone loss can limit replacement options later

Types Of Dental Implants

Every smile is unique, and implant options can be tailored to your specific needs. Whether you need to replace one tooth or several, each solution is designed for long-lasting comfort and function. Your dentist will guide you in selecting the right option for your smile.

A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. It acts independently while restoring natural balance and beauty to your smile. This option is perfect for patients seeking a permanent, seamless solution.

Matches perfectly with surrounding teeth for a uniform look

Feels secure during daily activities like chewing and speaking

Implant-supported bridges replace multiple missing teeth while preserving the surrounding natural ones. The implants act as anchors, supporting the bridge for stability and durability. It’s a comfortable solution that restores both function and confidence.

Evenly distributes pressure across your bite

Avoids grinding down adjacent teeth for support

Implant-supported dentures are ideal for patients missing most or all of their teeth. These dentures securely attach to implants for comfort and confidence throughout the day. They restore full chewing ability, making it easier to enjoy life without limitations.
