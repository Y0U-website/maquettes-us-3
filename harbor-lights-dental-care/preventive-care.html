What is Preventive Care?

Having a healthy, beautiful smile is something we all deserve. That’s why Dr. Pellegrom helps patients achieve a healthy smile that can last a lifetime with our general dentistry services. General dentistry consists of treatments and procedures designed to protect the longevity of your natural smile.

Whether that’s providing regular screenings for oral exams and cleanings, or remineralizations and sealants to strengthen enamel and protect against future decay, Harbor Lights Dental Care can help preserve the oral health of you and your family.

Scheduling regular dental visits are crucial as they prevent and treat oral health problems, keeping your smile healthy, strong, and picture-perfect.

Oral Exam & Cleaning

When you visit Dr. Pellegrom every six months, we’ll keep your smile fresh, clean, and shining bright. Your regular checkups include professional cleaning and a complete dental examination to ensure your teeth both look and feel great.

Daily brushing and flossing are essential for maintaining your oral hygiene. During your appointment, we’ll check the overall health of your teeth and gums, perform an oral cancer screening, and thoroughly clean your teeth, making sure to remove any built-up plaque.

Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride Treatment and Remineralization Therapy play crucial roles in maintaining dental health and preventing tooth decay . Fluoride treatment prevents and ever reverses early decay by making enamel more resistant to acid attacks from plaque bacteria and sugars in the mouth.

Remineralization Therapy helps the enamel form stronger, more organized crystals, which are harder and more acid-resistant than before. Both treatments are gentle, non-invasive and cost-effective.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings made of plastic or resin that a dentist applies to the chewing surfaces of back teeth (molars and premolars). These areas have deep grooves that can easily trap food and bacteria, making them harder to clean and more likely to get cavities.

Laser Therapy

Gum health, a.k.a. periodontal screening, is important for detecting gum disease and gingivitis. When bacteria infect your teeth above the gum line, cavities form. But when bacteria get into your gums, it can develop into periodontal disease, also known as gum disease.

Oral Cancer Screening

Oral cancer kills tens of thousands of people every year. In many cases, the disease goes undetected until it’s too late. That’s why every dental exam with Dr. Pellegom includes a full screening for oral cancer.

Wellness Scan

Harbor Lights Dental Care uses the latest advanced imaging technology to create a detailed, three-dimensional picture of your teeth, jaw, sinuses, nerves, and bone structure. It gives Dr. Pellegrom a full view of your oral health from every angle.

This precise imaging provides the best means of diagnosis, whole-health insights and accurate treatment planning. As a patient, you get a perfect 3D view of your mouth--empowering you to make well-informed decisions for your oral health. Best of all, scans are non-invasive, quick, and comfortable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s included in a dental exam?

A typical exam includes an oral health check, digital X-rays (if needed), gum evaluation, and screening for oral cancer or other concerns.

Do I really need dental X-rays?

Yes—X-rays help detect problems below the surface, like decay, bone loss, and impacted teeth, before they become serious.

Are dental cleanings really necessary?

Absolutely. Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can’t.

What age should my child start seeing the dentist?

At Harbor Lights Dental Care, we recommend a first visit around age 1 or within six months of their first tooth.
