OUR TEAM

OUR STAFF THE HEART OF OUR PRACTICE Behind every great dental practice is an exceptional team, and ours is no different. Our staff members are more than employees—they’re the heart and soul of Graham Mills Family Dentistry. Many have been with this practice for over 25 years, building relationships with patients that span generations.

What makes our team special? Dedication to your health, a genuine commitment to treating everyone like family, and a passion for working together to provide the best possible care. When you visit our office, you’ll feel the warmth and expertise that comes from decades of experience.
