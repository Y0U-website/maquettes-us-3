Please Note

The Howard University College of Dentistry (HUCOD) is a teaching institution. Some patients may be beyond the scope of treatment that can be rendered by a dental student. In addition, some patients may not have the ability to commit to the time it takes to receive treatment by a student dentist. Finally, we are a Comprehensive Care clinic. This means that we look at all of your treatment needs and take a sequential approach to completing all care needed to treat your entire mouth from cleaning, to fillings, to replacing missing teeth.

If you are beyond the scope of treatment we will provide you with information to assist you on how to find a dental home.

Referral to any specialists is not an endorsement by the COD but merely information to assist the patient to see the appropriate type of practitioner.

Patients who are advised to seek dental care in a private dental office can have copies of their records and x-rays sent to the dentist of their choice, upon written request

Emergency/Urgent Care Clinic

If you are a new patient or unassigned returning patient experiencing a dental emergency, please call Howard Dental Care at (202) 806-0007 and choose option 1. We also try to accommodate walk-in patients Monday – Friday, 8:30am – 3pm as capacity allows.

If you are an assigned patient, please contact your student doctor.

For patients under the age of 17 or patients with special needs, please call our Children’s Clinic at (202)806-0307.

If you are a patient who is experiencing a dental emergency after hours, on weekends or during holidays, please call the Howard University Hospital at (202) 865-6100 and ask for the dental resident on call.
