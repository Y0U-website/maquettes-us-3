About Health Star Dental

Located on Long Island’s north shore just 40 miles east of New York City, Health Star Dental serves dentists from coast to coast in the USA.

The practice

We are a full-service dental lab that combines personal customer service with high quality fabrications and a quick turnaround time that does not sacrifice craftsmanship.

Whether sending traditional conventional cases or using the latest digital scanning technology, Health Star Dental can accommodate any dentist through iTero, Trios/3Shape, CEREC, and through our Web Portal .
