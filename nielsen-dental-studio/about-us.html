Dr. Middlebrook, raised in the Northwest suburbs of Chicago, pursued his undergraduate studies at Michigan State University, earning an honors degree in Biochemistry. He then attained his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Kentucky, where he was recognized with an Excellence Award in Restorative Dentistry.

Post-graduation, Dr. Middlebrook, alongside his wife and their Labrador, Huckleberry, relocated to Texas to establish their home.

In his practice, Dr. Middlebrook provides a diverse range of dental treatments. He is passionate about helping patients achieve their dream smiles, offering services such as veneers, implants, and Invisalign. Known for his personalized patient care, Dr. Middlebrook combines his love for dentistry with a commitment to offering the highest quality of care.

Outside of his professional life, he enjoys family time, travel, exercise, and, somewhat ironically, indulges in his sweet tooth. Dr. Middlebrook is also an avid sports enthusiast, always ready to discuss anything related to the Chicago Bears or Michigan State sports teams.
