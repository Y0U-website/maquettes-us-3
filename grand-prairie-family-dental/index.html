The practice

Finding a dentist your whole family trusts is harder than it sounds. Grand Prairie Family Dental has been that practice for families across Grand Prairie, Arlington, Irving, and Cedar Hill since 1988. Dr. Behrooz Khademazad opened his doors on August of that year and has spent every year since building a practice around one standard: recommend only what the clinical findings actually support.

Most people do not need a specialist for every problem. They need one dentist who knows their full history, handles their emergencies, and tells them exactly what is going on before asking them to spend money.
