Meet the doctor

Dr. Selen Tolu, DDS

Welcome to the family dental practice of My Greenbelt Dentist . Our dental practice is led by Dr. Selen Tolu, DDS. Dr. Tolu and team welcome children and adults. We strive to serve patients with individualized treatment and deliver unmatched patient care in a calm and compassionate environment.

Our mission is to create the most comfortable patient experience. Our team is comprised of dental professionals, equipped with the latest technology and needed skills to provide outstanding service to families and individuals within our community. Dr. Tolu specializes in a full range of dental procedures including general, cosmetic, restorative and implant dentistry. At our practice, we pride ourselves in delivering exceptional care for our patients.

Dr. Tolu is committed to staying current with the latest technology available. In fact, she is a certified Invisalign® provider and a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry. Dr. Tolu’s certification in laser dentistry allows her to provide the most minimally invasive treatment options to all patients.

Dr. Tolu’s office is conveniently located in Belle Point Office Park in Greenbelt, Maryland near the intersection of Greenbelt Road (MD-193) and the Capital Beltway (I-495).

Thank you for giving Dr. Tolu the privilege to care for you and your family. Schedule an appointment today and start your journey toward a beautiful and healthy smile!

Dr. Selen Tolu
