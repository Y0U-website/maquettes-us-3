When Should Impacted Wisdom Teeth Be Removed?

Timing plays a key role in successful treatment. In many cases, removal is recommended before complications develop, especially when teeth are unlikely to erupt properly.

Situations where removal may be advised include:

Early evaluation allows for a more controlled procedure and often supports a smoother recovery.

  • Ongoing pain or pressure
  • Signs of infection or gum inflammation
  • Damage to adjacent teeth
  • Limited space in the jaw

Do All Impacted Wisdom Teeth Need to Be Removed?

Not every case requires immediate removal. Some teeth may be monitored if they are not causing symptoms or complications. However, many impacted teeth eventually create issues that make surgical treatment the best long-term option.

We believe in providing guidance without pressure, so you can make informed decisions with confidence.

  • Careful evaluation of your condition
  • Clear explanation of your options
  • Recommendations based on clinical necessity

What Happens If I Don’t Remove Impacted Wisdom Teeth ?

Leaving impacted teeth untreated can lead to progressive complications that affect both comfort and oral health.

Addressing the issue early can help prevent more complex problems and support better long-term outcomes.

  • Infection and recurring gum irritation
  • Damage to surrounding teeth
  • Development of cysts or other complications
  • Increased difficulty of removal over time
