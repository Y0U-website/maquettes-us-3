Your Journey Towards Receiving Dental Implants

The process begins with a thorough evaluation to determine if you’re an ideal candidate for dental implants. This involves assessing your oral health and jawbone density. Once cleared for the procedure, the implant post is surgically inserted into the jawbone. Over several months, it will fuse with the bone in a process known as osseointegration. Finally, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing your new smile.

Setting Realistic Expectations for Dental Implant Surgery

Patient education is paramount at Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston. Dr. Gilreath emphasizes that while dental implant surgery has a high success rate and offers numerous benefits, it’s crucial for patients to have realistic expectations. Healing times can vary, and meticulous oral hygiene is essential post-surgery to ensure the longevity of the implant.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long does the entire implant process take?

The timeline varies depending on individual healing rates and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are required.

Are dental implants painful?

Discomfort is minimal and manageable with medication during the recovery phase.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Treat them as you would natural teeth – with regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups.
