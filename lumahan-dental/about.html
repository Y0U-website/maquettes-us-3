ABOUT

Eleanor M. Lumahan, DDS

Dr. Eleanor M. Lumahan has been practicing dentistry for more than 30 years, providing patients with compassionate, high-quality care built on experience, professionalism, and a gentle approach. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Centro Escolar University in 1992.

From 1994 to 2000, she served as a professor and clinical instructor at the same institution, helping educate and mentor future dental professionals. She also completed her Master of Arts in Teaching in 1996, further strengthening her dedication to education and patient care.

Dr. Eleanor M. Lumahan is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and San Diego Filipino Dental Association. She remains committed to continuing education by regularly attending seminars and advanced training courses to stay current with the latest techniques and innovations in dentistry.

Known for her gentle touch and professional manner, Dr. Eleanor M. Lumahan is highly appreciated by her patients for making every visit comfortable, personalized, and positive.
