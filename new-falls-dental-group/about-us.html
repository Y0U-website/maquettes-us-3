Why Choose Us?

CEREC

iTero Element Scanner

Remember the old putty-based impressions that dentists used to take? Well, it is time we moved on to a less messy and state-of-the-art impression systems like the iTero Element Scanner. With this digital system, we can get a detailed 3D image of your teeth and gums. It becomes much easier for us to diagnose problems and offer the correct and more accurate solution. For a treatment like Invisalign, these 3D digital scans help us with accurate imagery of the before and after conditions.

3D Cone Beam CT

With revolutionary technologies like Cone Beam CT, dental care has become a collaborative process between the dentist and the patient. It is one of the most sophisticated devices of all times.

Where is it used?

  • Orthodontics: An accurate image of the teeth and jaw structure helps in determining precisely where and how a tooth should be moved
  • Dental implants: CBCT images help us determine where exactly to place the implants while avoiding sinuses, nerves, and areas of low bone density
  • Oral surgery. Tumors and teeth impacted by them can be treated better with the aid of the detailed scans by CBCT
  • Endodontics: While performing intricate procedures like a root canal treatment, CBCT helps with a clearer scan so that the dentists are able to plan and perform the procedure with precision for a better outcome
  • Sleep Apnea: the images pertaining to the tissues and structures of the nose, throat and mouth help in better diagnosis of the condition
