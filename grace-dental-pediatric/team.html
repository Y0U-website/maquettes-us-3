Dr. Grace Kwon DDS

Dr. Grace Kwon DDS is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

She completed her undergraduate studies at University of Colorado with a BS in Biology. She then received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS) from UCSF School of Dentistry. She then went east to complete her pediatric dentistry residency at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, as well as a clinical fellowship at Children’s Hospital Boston, where she served as Chief resident.

Dr. Kwon is dedicated to providing the highest quality dental care by teaching at Stanford Hospital/Lucile Packard Children’s Hospital where she works with children (who have cleft lip/palate, pending organ transplant, special needs, and cancer).

Dr. Kwon is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and California Society of Pediatric Dentistry and is a recipient of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry Richard C Pugh Achievement Award.

Dr. Kwon takes her time to build positive relationships by communicating openly with patient and parent. She takes great joy in finding the best way to communicate with her patients.

She is married to Eric Kwon, a general dentist in Palo Alto and they have 2 sons. In her free time, Dr. Kwon enjoys baking, cooking, and travelling.

Dr. Shelly (Xuerui) Wu DMD

Dr. Wu is a pediatric dentist dedicated to creating a calm, welcoming dental experience for children of all ages and needs. She enjoys helping kids feel comfortable and confident at the dentist.

Originally from Shanghai, Dr. Wu grew up in Zimbabwe before moving to Michigan, where she completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Michigan. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pittsburgh and completed her pediatric dentistry residency at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF).

Dr. Wu is fluent in Mandarin and enjoys working closely with families to build healthy habits from an early age. Her approach to care is gentle, thoughtful, and focused on each child’s unique needs.

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys yoga, baking, and trying new restaurants around the Bay Area.
