The practice

With a warm smile and an artist’s eye, Dr. Maria Chirivella is known for her compassionate care, artistic touch, and dedication to helping patients feel confident in their smiles. With over 15 years of experience in dentistry, she combines advanced clinical skills with a personalized approach to create beautiful, healthy results for patients of all ages.

Originally trained as a dentist in Venezuela and Brazil, Dr. Chirivella continued her education in the United States, earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree. Her diverse training and global perspective allow her to provide a unique blend of precision, empathy, and innovation in every treatment she performs.

Her areas of focus include cosmetic enhancements, smile makeovers, and comprehensive family care all delivered with a gentle, patient first approach. Dr. Chirivella takes time to listen and explain, ensuring each visit is comfortable and empowering.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Chirivella enjoys traveling, and spending time with her husband (and fellow dentist), Dr. Eduardo Cortes Contreras and their friends. Together, they lead Lake Wales Dental Group with the shared mission of making dentistry feel personal, modern, welcoming, and full of heart.
