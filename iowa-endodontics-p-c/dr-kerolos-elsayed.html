Frequently Asked Questions Dr. Kerolos Elsayed

What is Dr. Kerolos Elsayed’s specialty?

Dr. Kerolos Elsayed is a board-certified endodontist who specializes in diagnosing and treating conditions affecting the dental pulp and root canal system.

What does board certification mean for an endodontist?

Board certification from the American Board of Endodontics indicates that the specialist has completed rigorous written, clinical, and oral examinations demonstrating advanced expertise in endodontics.

Where did Dr. Elsayed complete his endodontic training?

Dr. Elsayed completed his specialty training and earned a Master of Science degree in Endodontics at Case Western Reserve University .

Does Dr. Elsayed use modern technology during treatment?

Yes. Dr. Elsayed incorporates advanced tools such as CBCT imaging, surgical microscopes, and laser technology to improve diagnosis and treatment precision.

Where does Dr. Elsayed practice?

Dr. Elsayed practices at Iowa Endodontics in Ankeny, Iowa , where he provides specialized endodontic care to patients throughout the surrounding communities.
