Delivering High-Quality Dental Care With State-of-the-Art Technology

At Joseph Family Dental, patients can expect gentle and compassionate dental care combined with the latest technology. We utilize cutting-edge tools like digital X-rays for precise and efficient diagnostics. With handheld X-ray devices and 3D X-ray cone beam CT, we ensure accurate imaging while minimizing patient discomfort.

Additionally, we can capture detailed images by utilizing digital scanners instead of traditional impressions. This technology allows for cosmetic assessments, such as Invisalign®, enhancing accuracy and convenience. Experience the benefits of advanced technology and compassionate dental care at Joseph Family Dental. Our Doctors Dr. Paula Joseph Hasson Dr. Hasson graduated from the University of Pittsburgh and the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. She has over 30 years of experience as a practicing dentist.

She owned a practice in downtown New Castle for 18 years before moving to Maine with her husband, David, and two sons. While in Maine, she worked at a thriving 3-doctor practice specializing in cosmetic and preventative dentistry. She spent over 11 years in Maine returning to her native Lawrence County in 2018.. Dr. Hasson is thrilled to utilize all the knowledge she has gained to give patients the best of care and a beautiful, healthy smile.

Dr. Hasson is certified in Botox for treating TMJ and Migraines and for cosmetic use. She practices with high integrity, honesty, and compassion and strives for clinical excellence.

Affiliations for Dr. Hasson include being a member of the American Dental Association, International Academy of Facial Aesthetics, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and Lawrence County Dental Society. She is on the Board of Directors of the New Castle Christian Academy and is a part-time clinical instructor in the Dental Hygiene Department at Youngstown State University.
