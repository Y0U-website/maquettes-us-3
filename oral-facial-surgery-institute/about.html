Our Story

Welcome to the Oral Facial Surgery Institute in St. Louis, Missouri.

We represent the leading-edge medical and dental care in terms of technology, expertise, and client satisfaction in our industry.

Our Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons are dental specialists who treat conditions of the teeth, mouth, jaws and face. Our specialty encompasses diagnosis, surgical, and related management of disease, injury, and defect of both the functional and aesthetic aspects of the oral and maxillofacial regions including preventive, reconstructive, and emergency care.

Our practice is the only teaching practice in St. Louis. Since 2005, oral surgeons from all over the United States have applied for our accredited one year fellowship training program. These select surgeons come for additional, specialized training in our true full scope practice. Our surgical case load at Mercy Medical Center, a Level 1 trauma hospital, provides a one of a kind education with a hands on surgical approach. The focus of our program encompasses facial cosmetics, reconstructive, orthognathic, and TMJ surgery, facial trauma and complex implantology.
