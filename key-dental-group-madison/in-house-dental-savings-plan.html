Important Program Guidelines

Before enrolling, please note the following:

There are no refunds for paid benefits, even if the plan is not used during the participation period

The annual savings fee is due at the time of enrollment

The plan must be renewed yearly before it expires

The patient’s portion of treatment is due on the day of service

Members must remain enrolled for the duration of treatment to retain discount program benefits

This program is not dental insurance. It is a reduced-fee savings plan created to help patients receive needed dental care at a more affordable cost.
