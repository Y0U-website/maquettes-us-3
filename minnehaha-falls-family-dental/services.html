0 Services Comprehensive Care Accumulation of plaque and calculus on the teeth and under the gums can lead to a plethora of dental problems: gingivitis, bone loss, tooth decay, and even tooth loss. Let us help you establish routine dental care. In doing so, you will develop habits that will support a lifetime of exceptional oral health.

Porcelain Veneers

Let us help you fall in love with your smile. Porcelain Veneers can help change the color, size, and shape of the teeth.

Invisalign

Move over, traditional metal brackets! Clear aligners are a healthy and effective way to achieve tooth movements. Having well-aligned teeth not only aids in the beauty of your smile but also ensures the teeth are in the proper position for long-term health.

Crowns

Crowns are full-coverage restorations. Crowns are used to protect teeth from fractures. Crowns are also used when a tooth is too damaged to be fixed by a traditional filling.

Cosmetic Bonding

Cosmetic bonding is placing tooth color bonding over a single tooth or multiple teeth to restore a fracture or help reshape a tooth or change its color.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth colored fillings allow us to remove decay and restore the health of a tooth while also mimicking the natural esthetic of a tooth.

Bridges

A missing tooth can change the chewing scheme of the entire mouth and cause long-term damage. A bridge is an option for replacing a missing tooth.

Boost™ Whitening

Boost ™ Whitening is in-office whitening. You’ll have your teeth whitened in a few hours by one of our talented and friendly team members. Whitening does not damage the teeth but it does enhance a smile!

Emergencies

Are you suffering and worried about both scheduling a dental appointment and your pain? We know that emergencies do not have a schedule. Give us a call and allow us to ease your fears.

Implants

Have you lost a tooth? Dental implants are a great option to restore a single missing tooth or multiple missing teeth.
