Meet your Elgin dentists.

Get to know the dentists who bring thoughtful, personalized care to the Elgin community.

Dr. Wes Guenther

Dr. Wes Guenther grew up in Overland Park, Kansas, as the youngest of five children. He enjoyed athletics, especially basketball and baseball.

After high school, he served a two-year mission in Concepción, Chile, where he became fluent in Spanish. He later studied Exercise Science at Brigham Young University, graduated with a 3.9 GPA, played catcher for the BYU baseball team, and served as team captain during his senior year.

Dr. Guenther earned his dental degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry, graduating summa cum laude in 2017. He moved with his family to Oklahoma City and began his career with a small dental group.

In January 2023, he founded Optimum Dental to bring comprehensive care to more Oklahoma communities. He particularly enjoys implant dentistry, crowns and veneers, and helping patients achieve and maintain oral health.

Dr. Guenther and his wife, Christen, have four active children. Outside the office he enjoys golf, exercise, family time, and service in his church community.

Dr. Taylor Petersen

Raised in southeastern Idaho, Dr. Taylor Petersen pursued undergraduate study in biomedical science before earning his dental doctorate from the University of Utah School of Dentistry.

He is fluent in Spanish and enjoys outdoor adventures with his family. Dr. Petersen emphasizes preventive care, patient education, and a friendly, approachable dental experience.

Dr. Tevin Renken

Dr. Tevin Renken graduated from the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry in 2022. He completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at Fort Benning, Georgia, with training in complex treatment planning, restorative dentistry, oral surgery, and comprehensive care.

He values lasting patient relationships and a comfortable, welcoming environment. Outside the office, Dr. Renken enjoys time with his wife and two daughters, sports, and travel.
