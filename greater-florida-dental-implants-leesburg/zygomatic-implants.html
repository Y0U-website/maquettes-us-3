FAQ About Zygomatic Implants

  • What are zygomatic implants? Zygomatic implants are longer dental implants that anchor into the cheekbone, also called the zygomatic bone, instead of relying only on the upper jawbone.
  • Who is a candidate for zygomatic dental implants? Candidates may include patients with missing upper teeth, severe upper jaw bone loss, loose dentures, or those who have been told they do not have enough bone for traditional dental implants.
  • Are zygomatic implants used for full-arch treatment? Yes. Zygomatic implants are often used to support full-arch implant restorations for patients who need replacement teeth in the upper jaw.
  • Can zygomatic implants help avoid bone grafting? In some cases, zygomatic implants may offer an alternative to extensive bone grafting because they use the cheekbone for support.
  • How are zygomatic implants different from traditional implants? Traditional implants are placed in the jawbone, while zygomatic implants are longer and placed at an angle to anchor into the cheekbone.
  • How long do zygomatic implants last with proper care? With proper care, regular dental visits, and good oral hygiene, zygomatic implants can provide long-term support for implant restorations.
