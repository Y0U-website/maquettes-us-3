Orthodontic Appliances – Support for Growing Smiles

Orthodontic appliances are commonly used during early (Phase I) treatment but may also complement braces or clear aligners for teens. At Glow Dental, every appliance is custom-designed to meet your child’s unique needs and goals.

  • Space Maintainers : Preserve space for permanent teeth when a baby tooth is lost too early, preventing crowding and unwanted shifting.
  • Habit Appliances : Gently discourage thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting to promote proper jaw growth and bite alignment.
  • Palatal Expanders : Widen the upper jaw to correct crossbites, crowding, or narrow arches – most effective while the jaw is still developing.
  • Transpalatal Arch (TPA) : Connects upper molars to maintain arch width, stabilize molars, and assist with bite correction.
  • Lower Lingual Holding Arch (LLHA) : Connects lower molars to preserve space and maintain arch length when baby teeth are lost prematurely.
  • Facemask / Reverse Pull Headgear : Helps correct underbites by encouraging forward growth of the upper jaw for balanced jaw alignment
  • Class II / III Corrector : Aligns the upper and lower jaws for more harmonious function and facial symmetry.
  • Bite Plates: Help correct deep bites and guide proper jaw positioning.

Benefits of Orthodontic Appliances

  • Guide proper jaw growth and tooth eruption
  • Create space to reduce crowding
  • Improve bite alignment and comfort
  • Simplify and shorten future orthodontic treatment
  • Support long‑term smile stability

Who Might Need Orthodontic Appliances?

Orthodontic appliances are most beneficial for children and teens:

  • Children : Early evaluations (by age 7) can detect issues with jaw growth, spacing, or oral habits. Space maintainers, expanders, or habit appliances can prevent more serious problems later.
  • Teens : Appliances may be used alongside braces or aligners to improve bite discrepancies and overall treatment efficiency.

Your Path to a Straighter Smile

At Glow Dental, we use advanced 3D digital scanning and custom 3D-printed orthodontic appliances to make treatment faster, more comfortable, and more precise. Our digital workflow eliminates spacers, metal molar bands, and messy impressions—reducing multiple traditional visits down to just two: a scan and a delivery.

Step 1: Free Consultation & Digital Scan

Your visit begins with a complimentary consultation. We take digital x-rays, photos, and a quick 3D scan to evaluate your child’s teeth, bite, and development. Our orthodontist reviews the findings and outlines the recommended treatment plan.

Step 2: Custom Appliance Design

Your child’s digital scan is sent to our lab, where a fully customized orthodontic appliance is designed and 3D printed for a precise fit—no spacers, no metal bands, and no impressions required.

Step 3: Appliance Delivery

At the next visit, the appliance is placed. Because it is custom-designed from the scan, it typically fits seamlessly with minimal adjustment. We review simple care instructions and what to expect during treatment.

Step 4: Progress Visits

Short follow-up visits allow us to monitor progress and ensure treatment is moving as planned.

Step 5: Completion & Retention

Once treatment is complete, the appliance is removed. Retainers may be recommended to maintain long-term results.

No spacers. No fitting metal rings on molars. No messy impressions.

Just a digital scan and a custom appliance designed for a precise, comfortable fit.
