Self-Ligating Braces

Self-ligating braces are a modern orthodontic system designed to provide a more comfortable and efficient treatment experience. Unlike traditional braces, self-ligating braces do not use elastic bands or metal ties. Instead, they feature specialized clips that hold the archwire in place, reducing friction and allowing teeth to move more smoothly.

This advanced design often results in fewer adjustments and shorter dental visits while effectively treating both simple and complex orthodontic cases.

Comparable in cost to traditional braces, self-ligating braces are a practical and efficient option for achieving a healthier, more confident smile.

  • Passive Self-Ligating Braces – Use lower friction and gentler forces for smoother tooth movement
  • Active Self-Ligating Braces – Apply more controlled pressure for more complex orthodontic corrections
  • Improved comfort during treatment
  • Easier to clean than traditional braces
  • Reduced buildup of food and plaque
  • Fewer office visits in some cases
  • Effective for a wide range of orthodontic concerns

Clear (Ceramic) Braces

Clear braces, also known as ceramic braces, provide a more discreet alternative to traditional metal braces. Made from translucent, tooth-colored materials, they blend naturally with your teeth for a less noticeable appearance during orthodontic treatment.

Clear braces are a popular choice for both teens and adults who want to straighten their teeth while maintaining a more natural smile throughout treatment.

Using the same effective principles as traditional braces, ceramic braces can successfully treat most orthodontic conditions, including crowding, spacing, and bite alignment issues.

Clear braces allow you to improve your smile with confidence while maintaining a more aesthetic appearance throughout your orthodontic journey.

  • Less noticeable than metal braces
  • Blend naturally with tooth color
  • Effective for most orthodontic cases
  • Durable and reliable treatment option
  • Suitable for teens and adults
