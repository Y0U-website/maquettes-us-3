Meet the Team at Media Lima Dental

At Media Lima Dental Pediatric & Family Dentistry, our team loves working together to take care of your smile! We are a team of highly skilled professionals dedicated to caring for our patients and each other. We genuinely have your best interests in mind and want you to have the best experience possible regarding your dental care.

Take a minute to meet our dentists and team members so you ll feel right at home when you visit us!

Dr. David Sung , DMD

When he is not at work, he devotes most of his weekends to his wife and two children. He is an avid sports fan, especially of the University of Michigan, and enjoys taking on DIY projects in his home. During snowstorms, you might find him creating giant snow sculptures for his kids!

Dr. John Kwon , DMD

Not every child enjoys visiting their doctor or dentist for his or her regular check-up. Having a beautiful princess of his own and an adventurous son with boundless energy, Dr. John understands the challenges parents face when it comes to starting and maintaining their child’s oral hygiene. It was only natural that Dr. John decided to combine his desire to create a positive dental experience for children with his passion for dentistry.

During his two-year post-doctoral fellowship in pediatric dentistry and advanced hospital training at The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP) and the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine , Dr. John recognized that treating children from infancy through their teenage years was not the same. Thus, Dr. John helps his patients and their families understand that every child’s oral health care need and management is different. But what remains constant is the privilege Dr. John feels when he can share each patient’s unique journey with them.

Dr. John loves bringing smiles to children and makes his patient’s oral health care his top priority. In his spare time, Dr. John enjoys volunteering his time with international medical/dental mission organizations, playing tennis with his son, tasting new cuisines, and watching Philadelphia and Boston sports teams.

Dr. Travis Perry , DMD

Dr. Perry received a Bachelor of Science in Computer and Information Sciences from Temple University. In 2012, he received a DMD degree from Temple University School of Dentistry. Next, he completed a one-year General Practice Residency at St. Joseph Medical Center in Reading, Pennsylvania.

Dr. Perry loves making patients smile, whether it is by saying "no cavities" or by helping patients reclaim their confidence through dentistry. He likes to believe that if nitrous oxide (also known as "laughing gas") were a person, it would be him. However, if his soft voice, calm demeanor, and gentle touch aren t enough, no worries - he s also licensed to administer nitrous oxide!

When he s not taking care of patients, you can also find him at his second home, the gym, either swimming, participating in a group fitness class, or lifting weights...or maybe all three in one day! Most people don t know that he s also a group fitness instructor.
