Meet Dr. Yoo

Dr. Yoo is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist, born in South Korea and raised in Northern Virginia. She studied violin performance and received her music degree at the Catholic University of America in Washington, DC. After graduation, Dr. Yoo traveled to different countries performing and teaching before discovering her passion for dentistry.

She received her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry from the University of New England in Portland, ME, then completed her specialty training in pediatric dentistry at the University of Connecticut as well as at the Connecticut Children’s Medical Center in Hartford, CT. She is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Yoo enjoys working with children and teens, and her goal is to create positive dental experiences while helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Outside of work, she loves watching movies and trying new restaurants with her husband, doing crafts with her two kids, and going on hikes with her Shiba Inu, Riley.
