Accessibility statement

We want everyone who visits our website to feel welcome, to have a rewarding experience, and to easily find and access the information they need.

What are we doing?

To help make https://www.kindersmile.org/ a great website for everyone, we are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our site using the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. These guidelines explain how to make content on the web more accessible for everyone, including people with disabilities.

The guidelines have three levels of accessibility standards: A, AA, and AAA. We have chosen AA as the target for our site as this is the current standard target for the US Federal Government’s websites.

We are working hard to meet the AA standards, but please understand that it is not always possible to do so for all areas of the website due to technical hurdles or ambiguity in the specifications. We are continually seeking out solutions that will help us improve the usability and accessibility of our website.

Let us know what you think

If you had a great experience on our website, or if you had trouble with any part of it, please let us know. And if you did have trouble, we will make necessary accommodations for you, so you have access to the content in question.
