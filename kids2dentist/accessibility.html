Accessibility Statement

  • All interactive elements are keyboard-focusable and operable
  • ARIA labels and roles where native semantics are insufficient
  • Color contrast ratios meet or exceed 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text
  • Descriptive alt text on all meaningful images
  • All form fields have associated labels
  • Skip-to-content link available on keyboard navigation
  • Language attribute set correctly on all pages (lang="en" / lang="es" / lang="tl")
  • No content flashes more than 3 times per second

Known Limitations

  • Embedded YouTube videos use the YouTube player interface, which is not under our direct control. YouTube&#x27;s own accessibility varies by browser and assistive technology.
  • The photo gallery lightbox is not fully optimized for all screen reader workflows. We are working to improve this.
  • Google Maps embeds (clinic location pages) are provided by Google. Map interactions may have limited screen reader support.
  • The homepage hero includes a silent, muted, decorative background video (no audio track, no dialogue, no meaningful information). Under WCAG 1.2.2, captions are not applicable to media without audio content. The video is marked aria-hidden so assistive technology ignores it, and the Seizure-Safe accessibility profile hides it entirely to satisfy WCAG 2.2.2 (pause/stop/hide mechanism).

ADA & Physical Accessibility

All Kids2Dentist clinic locations in Fresno, Visalia, Delano, Porterville, and Clovis are physically accessible in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Our facilities offer accessible parking, ramp access, and accessible restrooms. If you require any specific accommodation for your visit, please call us ahead of your appointment.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our website or need information in an alternative format, please contact us. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
