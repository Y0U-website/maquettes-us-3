What are FASTBRACES®?

FASTBRACES is an innovative, patented orthodontic treatment that combines traditional brackets and wires and phototherapy and subsonic wave treatments to move teeth more efficiently and effectively.

Applying specially designed brackets and wires that are patented for treatment with FASTBRACES , Dr. Sung can achieve exceptional results moving teeth into alignment during a fraction of the time it would take with standard braces. That s because FASTBRACES combines the movement of teeth and roots by restoring alveolar bone through its patented treatment methods. This cuts down the time and the two-step method required by standard braces.

We are thrilled that Dr. Sung is recognized as a master provider for FASTBRACES and can offer this unique and efficient orthodontic treatment in our Media dental office. He s achieved exceptional results treating hundreds of patients with FASTBRACES , and patients love that they can enjoy their straighter, more beautiful smiles in a matter of months instead of years.
