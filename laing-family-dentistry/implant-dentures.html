Types of Implant Dentures We Offer

1. Fixed Implant Dentures

Permanently Attached for Maximum Stability Fixed implant dentures are permanently secured to dental implants, providing the most natural feel and functionality. Once placed, they do not need to be removed, offering a solution closest to natural teeth.

Who Are They Best For? Patients seeking a permanent, long-term solution that mimics the function and aesthetics of natural teeth.

Cost : $50,000 - $70,000 (complete process, including implants, dentures, and placement).

2. Removable Implant Dentures

Snap-On Dentures with Implant Support Removable implant dentures securely attach to implants but can be easily removed for cleaning and maintenance. They offer a great balance of stability and convenience.

Who Are They Best For? Patients who want a secure solution but prefer the flexibility of removable dentures.

Cost : $10,000 - $20,000 (all-inclusive).

3. Conventional Dentures vs. Implant Dentures

Upgrade Your Smile Traditional dentures rest directly on the gums and may slip or feel uncomfortable over time. Implant dentures eliminate these issues by anchoring to implants for added stability and comfort.

Cost of Conventional Dentures : $5,000 - $10,000 .

The Implant Denture Process

  • Consultation and Planning Dr. Laing will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and goals to determine the best type of implant denture for you.
  • Implant Placement Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, they fuse with the bone, creating a stable foundation.
  • Denture Fabrication Once the implants heal, custom dentures are designed and fitted to your exact specifications.
  • Attachment The dentures are securely attached to the implants, completing your new, confident smile.

FAQs About Implant Dentures

How long do implant dentures last?

With proper care, implant dentures can last over a decade. The dental implants themselves can last a lifetime.

Are implant dentures better than traditional dentures?

Yes! Implant dentures provide greater stability, comfort, and function compared to traditional dentures.

How many implants are needed for implant dentures?

Typically, 2-6 implants are used per arch to support implant dentures. The exact number depends on your oral health and the type of denture selected.

Does the implant process hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and most patients experience minimal discomfort during recovery.
