The All-On-3 Dental Implants Procedure in Denver

The Trefoil™ All-On-3 dental implant procedure is minimally invasive and typically requires only one surgical visit. During the procedure, your Denver prosthodontist will make small incisions in the gums and use precise measurements to determine the best locations for implant placement.

Once the implants are placed, your doctor will perform a procedure known as osseointegration, which allows the implants to bond securely with your jawbone. After your mouth has healed, replacement teeth will be securely attached to the implants.

All-On-3 Implant Recovery

The recovery time for Trefoil™ All-On-3 dental implants varies depending on the patient and their individual case. Generally, patients can expect to experience some swelling and discomfort for a few days. However, most patients are able to resume normal eating and speaking habits within just a few weeks. To ensure a successful recovery, it is important to follow your doctor’s instructions. This includes eating soft foods and refraining from smoking during your recovery period.
