Invisalign Teen

Invisalign Teen aligners utilize custom-made, clear aligners to gently shift your teen’s teeth into place. These aligners are made of a proprietary transparent material that seamlessly blends into the teeth, providing maximum comfort for their smile! These aligners also feature compliance indicators, just to make sure your teen is wearing them regularly.

Invisalign for Teens: Benefits, Treatment Timeline, and What to Expect

We can provide you with the estimated treatment duration during your visit to our office. The typical treatment duration ranges from 6 to 18 months, though teens with more complex alignment issues may take longer. The exact treatment time with Invisalign Teen may vary based on the frequency of aligner change.

Since teens are supposed to wear the aligners for 20-22 hours a day, excessive removal can prolong treatment time. Committing to and properly caring for their aligners is key to having a successful experience with Invisalign for teens.

NOTE: Invisalign® Teen is designed specifically for adolescents, including those who may still have some baby (deciduous) teeth. The treatment adapts to growing mouths and can be customized based on each child’s dental development.

  • Fewer dietary restrictions
  • Removable aligners make oral hygiene easier
  • Virtually invisible treatment
  • Fewer in-office appointments
  • More comfortable design

How Much Does Invisalign for Teens Cost?

The cost of Invisalign Teen aligners at our practice depends on several factors, such as the length of treatment, the complexity of the case, and patient compliance. Pricing typically falls within a broad range and is customized based on individual needs and goals.
