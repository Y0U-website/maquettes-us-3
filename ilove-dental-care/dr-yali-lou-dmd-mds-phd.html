Meet Dr. Yali Lou

Dr. Yali Lou

General Dentist

DMD, MDS, PhD, C(implant, Invisalign, facial injection)

Dr. Lou lives in Newton and she received her first DMD from Peking University, the top university in China with highest honor in 1999. Following 3 years residency training in Prosthodontics, she started her PhD program in the University of HongKong. With higher education and experience, she started her Post-doc life in USA and gained second DMD degree from Boston University. Her publications could be found in many top journals: Science, Nature, Cell, J Dent.

Dr. Lou frequently takes continuing eduction courses to gain the latest techniques and procedures to provide patient best comprehensive care. Dr. Lou is a member of the American Dental Assosiation, the Massachusetts Dental Association, the American Academy Facial Esthetics and International Academy of oral medicine and Toxicity. With high education and more than 20 years experience, she started owning her own office. In her free time, she is a fan of photograph, traveling and painting.
