Services We Offer

From routine cleanings to advanced restorative procedures, we provide gentle, high-quality dentistry for every member of your family. Explore our core services below.

Cleanings & Preventive Care

Adult and child cleanings, comprehensive exams, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, and sealants. The single most important thing you can do for long-term dental health — and the cheapest. We catch problems early, before they grow.

Crowns & Bridges

Best for: restoring damaged or missing teeth.

Full-coverage porcelain crowns for cracked, broken, or root-canal-treated teeth. Bridges to replace one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth as anchors. Custom-matched to your other teeth.

Kids&#x27; Dentistry

Best for: building lifelong dental habits.

Cleanings, exams, fluoride, sealants, and fillings for children of all ages — including first dental visits as early as age 1. Patient, gentle approach. Parents welcome in the operatory.

Deep Cleanings (Periodontal Therapy)

Best for: patients with gum disease or heavy buildup.

A deeper cleaning that goes below the gumline to remove tartar and bacteria. We follow up with periodontal maintenance every 3–4 months to keep gums healthy. Often covered by insurance when medically necessary.

Root Canals

Best for: saving a tooth that&#x27;s badly decayed or infected.

A root canal removes infected tissue from inside the tooth, sealing it from further infection. With modern anesthesia, most patients are surprised at how comfortable it is. Followed by a crown to protect the tooth long-term.

Dentures & Partials

Best for: replacing several or all teeth.

Full and partial dentures custom-made to fit comfortably and look natural. We also repair and reline existing dentures. Options to stabilize with implants if you want a more secure fit.

Fillings

Best for: tooth-colored composite, no silver.

We use only tooth-colored composite fillings that blend invisibly with your tooth. Same-day in one visit. Long-lasting, mercury-free, and far better-looking than older silver amalgam fillings.

Extractions

Best for: when a tooth can&#x27;t be saved.

Simple and surgical extractions including wisdom teeth. We use gentle technique, local anesthesia, and oral sedation when needed. We always discuss tooth-replacement options (implant, bridge, partial) before extracting.
