Why Choose Us?

At Implant & Periodontal Therapy, we have over 40 years of experience creating beautiful smiles that are healthy and stable for our patients. We are leaders in the dental community, and have all the latest treatments and technology to make the patient experience as comfortable as possible. We take the time to go through all the options for treatment, and help our patients choose the one that is right for them.

Although general dentists often treat the more mild forms of periodontal disease (commonly known as gingivitis) we specialize in the treatment of all forms of periodontal disease, which includes the more advanced forms of the disease. Unfortunately, many patients don’t realize they even have periodontal disease unless they are told by another dental professional, as many patients do not feel any symptoms at all.

We specialize in all periodontal conditions, which includes treatment of both gum recession and smile makeovers. We will analyze the entire smile and overall condition of the gum tissue, and discuss all options with you. If there is an issue around your natural teeth, we have the experience and treatment options available to help.

In addition to treatment of natural teeth, we are specialists in dental implants as well. In fact, Dr. Rasmussen authored a textbook on dental implants, and has placed over 22,000 of them. He is one of the most experienced clinicians in the world in the realm of dental implants, and as such he has lectured both nationally as well as internationally on the topic.
