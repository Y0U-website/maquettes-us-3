  • Accessibility Statement | Dr. Reina Kawazoe top of page Accessibility Statement This statement applies to the http://www.akfamilydentistry.com website. OUR COMMITMENT Reina Kawazoe DDS Inc. is committed to ensuring our services are accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to conform with the WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This accessibility statement forms an integral part of our Terms and Conditions: http://www.akfamilydentistry.com/terms-conditions . ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES You should be able to: Zoom in up to 200 % without loss of information or functionality
  • Stop moving images
  • Download materials in alternative formats
  • Use transcripts or closed captions with audio and video materials
  • Listen to the website using a screen reader
  • Navigate the website with just a keyboard

CONFORMANCE STATUS

Status: Partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA

We conducted our most recent accessibility evaluation in April 2026.

PREPARATION OF THIS STATEMENT

This statement was prepared on 06 February 2026 and is reviewed bi-annually or when we make significant service changes.

We maintain records of accessibility feedback and our responses for regulatory compliance.

FEEDBACK AND CONTACT INFORMATION

Please let us know if you experience accessibility barriers using our services or need information in an alternative format.

We respond to accessibility feedback within 15 business days.

You can also contact us by post or telephone:

  • Email: info@akfamilydentistry.com (mark subject line "Accessibility")
  • Contact form: http://www.akfamilydentistry.com/contact
