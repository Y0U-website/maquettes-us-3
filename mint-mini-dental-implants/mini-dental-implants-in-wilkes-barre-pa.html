Am I a Good Candidate for Mini Dental Implants?

Mini dental implants are a modern solution to missing teeth, but eligibility is based on your specific needs and medical history.

In general, you might be a strong candidate for mini implants if you meet the following criteria:

However, mini dental implants are not for everyone. The following medical and lifestyle factors could make you ineligible:

  • You would like to replace one or many teeth.
  • You are tired of unsecured bridges or loose dentures and are seeking a permanent fix.
  • You are looking for an immediate result without the need for bone grafting.
  • You experience dental-related anxiety and prefer a one-day procedure.
  • You’d like to save money.
  • You are diabetic.
  • You have gum disease (periodontitis).
  • Your blood does not clot properly (hemophilia).
  • You have vascular or bone disease.
  • You have a condition that prevents wounds or bones from healing, or you take medications that slow healing.
  • You smoke.
  • You are receiving chemotherapy or radiation.
  • You have oral hygiene concerns.

The Mini Implant Process and Procedure

At Mint Mini Dental Implants, we are committed to helping you achieve the oral health you deserve. We’ll begin by discussing your medical history and dental goals. We’ll take X-rays to assess the specifics of your case, evaluate your eligibility for mini implants, and determine how many implants you require. Some patients require full-mouth reconstruction to replace all or most of their missing teeth. Depending on your needs, we may need to extract teeth or treat other conditions first. If mini implants are the proper treatment option for you, we’ll schedule your surgery.

On the day of the surgery, Dr. Coolican will use a local anesthetic to numb your gum and jaw. If you experience dental anxiety and would benefit from additional sedation options, we can discuss these at your consultation. Those that receive additional anesthesia will need to arrange transportation to and from their appointment and plan to arrive a bit earlier.

During the surgery, Dr. Coolican will make a minor incision in your gum before drilling a small hole in your jawbone to accommodate the implant. He’ll place, secure, and tighten the implant using specialized tools and then mount the artificial tooth to the post. You’ll leave your appointment with a beautiful new smile.

When it comes to dental surgery, a common concern is how much pain is associated with the procedure. Because the mini implant procedure is minimally invasive, you’ll experience only minor discomfort. You’ll be able to eat immediately, but a soft food diet is recommended until you feel comfortable eating your favorite foods. In the days following the procedure, you may feel some soreness. Over-the-counter pain medications like ibuprofen can be used to lessen any lingering discomfort.
