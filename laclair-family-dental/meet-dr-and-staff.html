Dr. Robert Allen LaClair III

Dr. Robert Allen LaClair III

Dr. Rob LaClair is a member of the American Dental Association, Fifth District New York State Dental Society as past Board of Governors member, the Jefferson-Lewis County Dental Society as past President, EDPAC, the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. LaClair has a unique perspective on dentistry and healthcare in general. His family is full of healthcare workers. Of course, Dr. Rob has also been a dental patient. A sports injury was the cause for root canals, posts, buildups, crowns, partial dentures, dental extractions, bone grafts, and dental implants. All of this helps his sense of experience and compassion when treating his patients.

Emily Fuller

Emily Fuller

Emily is from Edwards, NY and has lived there for most of her life. She lived in the capital region for a couple years but decided the North Country was much better suited for her. She attended SUNY Canton graduating May 2011 as a Registered Dental Hygienist. Emily is also certified to administer and monitor local infiltration anesthesia and nitrous oxide analgesia. She joined the LaClair family dental team in September of 2014.

Emily currently resides in Edwards with her boyfriend, Ryan and dog, Bailey. She loves spending time with friends and family. She enjoys crafts, especially wood burning. She also likes to stay active by hiking, camping and playing in a women’s summer soccer league every summer.

Theresa LaClair

Theresa LaClair

Born in North Dakota and daughter of a North Country Surgeon, Theresa moved to Northern NY as an infant. She has been a Registered Nurse since 1986 and holds an additional degree in Health Services Management, as well as a number of certifications from the American Association of Dental Managers.
