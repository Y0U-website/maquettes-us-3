Meet the Team

0 Meet our team Glenn Yowell Doctor of Dental Surgery

With 40 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Glenn Yowell brings a lifetime of passion and dedication to his practice. Growing up in a dental household, he saw firsthand the meaningful impact his father made in patients’ lives — an experience that inspired his own path in dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Yowell enjoys spending time with his children whenever they’re home, golfing, scuba diving, and relaxing on a beach somewhere warm. If he’s in charge of the playlist, expect a mix of Kenny Chesney and some classic rock favorites.

Andrew Yowell

Dr. Andrew Yowell, son of Dr. Glenn and a third-generation dentist, grew up with a deep appreciation for the positive and immediate impact dentistry can have on people’s lives. Andrew was drawn to the profession for its unique combination of science and art, and the opportunity to make meaningful change every day.

Outside the office, Andrew is all about the outdoors — whether it’s biking, fishing, camping, hiking, or catching live music with friends. If he’s got the aux cord, expect some smooth Frank Sinatra to set the tone.

Stacy

Though her degree opened up many career paths, Stacy feels lucky to have found her home in this dental office right after college — and she hasn’t looked back since, with over 25 years at Iowa City Dental Health. She loves the fast pace of the office, getting to know patients, and being part of a team that truly cares about the people it serves.

When she’s not at work, you’ll find her with her husband, Matt, and their two boys, Cole and Cade — whether it’s cheering them on in sports or spending time together at their land on the Cedar River or out on the boat. Stacy’s music taste covers just about everything, but if she has to pick one favorite, it’s Goodbye Earl.

Finn Duffy

Dr. Finn Duffy is passionate about staying on the cutting edge of dentistry, always exploring new technologies and techniques to enhance patient care. For him, there’s nothing more rewarding than helping people feel more confident and improving their quality of life.

Outside the office, Finn enjoys reading, playing soccer, and spending time with family. And if he’s got the aux cord, you might hear the iconic sounds of Pink Floyd’s Time playing in the background.

Mary

Mary, a registered Dental Hygienist, finds fulfillment in helping patients understand and improve their oral health. She enjoys building relationships with patients and empowering them with the knowledge they need to take charge of their dental care for over 23 years at Iowa City Dental Health.

Outside of work, Mary cherishes time with her husband, family, and especially her grandchildren. She’s an avid reader with a love for all kinds of books. If she’s in control of the playlist, you’ll likely hear something country!

Heather

Heather, Stacy’s sister and dental assisting Kirkwood graduate, has been with Iowa City Dental Health for over 28 years. With decades of experience, she’s passionate about helping patients overcome dental anxiety and feel at ease during their visits. For Heather, there’s nothing more rewarding than seeing patients become comfortable with their care.

Outside of work, Heather is the proud grandmother of three beautiful granddaughters, with the oldest just two years old. They are the light of her life, and family has always been her top priority.

When it comes to music, Heather’s playlist is a true mix-tape — from country to classic rock to oldies, she loves it all. And if you’re lucky, you might catch her singing along to her favorites!

Bailey

Bailey is passionate about helping people feel confident in their smiles while making a lasting impact on their overall health. As a dental hygienist, she values the opportunity to build meaningful connections with patients, educate them on proper oral care, and contribute to their long-term well-being.

Outside the office, Bailey loves spending time outdoors with her husband, Drake, and their two German Shepherds, Appa and Vail. Whether they’re traveling or just enjoying time with family, quality moments together are what she values most. If she’s got the aux cord, you can count on something from the ’80s to be playing.

Jordan

As a dental hygienist, Jordan loves getting to know new people and finds real satisfaction in helping patients leave with a cleaner, healthier smile. Building connections while making a difference is what keeps her excited to come to work every day.

A small-town Iowa native, Jordan thrives in the beautiful chaos of raising three wild kiddos in a country home where DIY projects and crafting meet dirt bikes and outdoor adventures. She’s always chasing a new hobby, hosting a get-together, or sneaking off to Target for a little peace and quiet. Her goal? To make everyday moments a little more creative — and a lot more fun.

As for music? Definitely not Andrew’s playlist. Think country-pop remixes that make you want to be on a boat with friends, soaking up the sun with a cooler full of cold drinks. 🤌🏼

Macey

With a dedication to being a friendly and trusted face for patients, Macey brings warmth, care, and confidence to every appointment.

Outside the office, Macey loves spending time with her boyfriend and their beloved pets. When she isn’t at work, you’ll likely find her soaring through the sky. She’s a licensed skydiver who jumps with her friends whenever the chance arises. And if she has control of the playlist? Expect a Michael Jackson marathon playing non-stop.

Courtney

Courtney earned her A.A.S in Dental Assisting from Kirkwood Community College and followed it up with an A.A.S in Dental Hygiene from Indian Hills Community College. With a heartfelt dedication to patient care, she thrives on helping people improve their oral health and overall well-being, and finds deep fulfillment in patient education and preventive care, building strong relationships along the way.

When she’s off the clock, you’ll likely find Courtney enjoying quality time with her beloved 8-year-old Bichon Frise, Pebbles. She also loves traveling, hitting the golf course, crafting and selling Bomb Party jewelry live on TikTok, and cheering on the Hawkeyes and the Denver Broncos. If Courtney’s in control of the aux cord, you’ll hear a mix of classic ’80s oldies and country tunes! Her favorite concert so far was Morgan Wallen with Ernest, Bailey Zimmerman, and Hardy as openers.

Nancy Yowell

Nancy didn’t plan on a career in dentistry, but sometimes life lands you right where you’re supposed to be. Over the years, she’s come to value the relationships built through the work and the energy of being part of a dedicated, supportive team.

Outside the office, Nancy is always on the move — whether it’s early-morning runs with friends (yes, even at 5:30am!) or spending time with her favorite people: her family. With adult kids who still love to hang out, she cherishes every game night, hike, and shared meal. As for music, give her anything from the ’70s or ’80s — especially John Denver — and she’s in her happy place.
