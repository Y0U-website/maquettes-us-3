Welcome to Our Pediatric Dental Office!

Dr. Brit E. Bowers & Dr. Laurel Bateman - Our pediatric dental office is conveniently located in Johnson City, TN.

Your scheduled appointment time has been reserved specifically for you. We request 24-hours notice if you need to cancel your appointment. We are aware that unforeseen events sometimes require missing an appointment.

Our new office has a waiting area specifically designed to entertain your child while they wait to be seen by our staff. We have toys and a train set for younger children and iPads with games to entertain older children.

Each exam room table has a television above it on the ceiling to entertain your child during their examination.

Also, be sure to check out our giant fish tank before you leave!

If there is anything we can do to make your visit to our office more comfortable, please consult one of our staff members.
