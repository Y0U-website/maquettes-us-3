What Are Braces ?

Braces are a fixed orthodontic system used to straighten teeth and improve the way the upper and lower teeth fit together. They include small brackets attached to the teeth and a wire that works through those brackets to guide teeth into better alignment over time.

This treatment can address a wide range of orthodontic concerns, from minor spacing or crowding to more involved bite problems. How Orthodontic Braces Work

Orthodontic braces work by placing steady, controlled force on the teeth through the brackets and archwire. As that force is maintained and adjusted over time, the teeth gradually move into new positions that support better alignment and bite function.

Throughout treatment, braces are monitored and adjusted so movement stays on track according to the treatment plan. As the teeth shift, the bone around them responds and adapts, which allows those changes to happen in a careful, guided way at Hoffman Orthodontics in Leawood and Prairie Village, KS.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces use metal brackets and wires to correct alignment and bite concerns with a time-tested orthodontic system. They remain a dependable choice because they are versatile, precise, and well suited for a wide range of treatment needs.

For some patients, metal braces may be a strong option when tooth movement or bite correction is more involved. Today’s metal braces are smaller and more streamlined than many people expect, which makes them a familiar and trusted part of modern orthodontic care.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces work much like traditional braces, but they use brackets made from tooth-colored or clear materials for a less noticeable appearance. Many patients choose them because they want the strength of braces treatment with a more subtle look.

This option can appeal to patients who want braces that blend in more naturally with the smile. Ceramic braces still provide the structure of a fixed orthodontic appliance, but their appearance feels more discreet than metal braces.

Self-Ligating Braces

Self-ligating braces use a bracket design with a built-in clip or door that holds the wire in place, rather than relying on traditional elastic ties. That design changes the way the appliance is secured while still offering the control and consistency expected from braces treatment.

FAQs About Braces

Do braces hurt?

Braces can cause mild soreness at first and after adjustments, but that usually fades within a few days. Most patients find the discomfort manageable as they get used to treatment.

Are there foods to avoid with braces?

Yes. Hard, sticky, and chewy foods can damage brackets or wires, so they are usually best avoided during treatment.

How should braces be cleaned?

Braces should be brushed carefully around the brackets and along the gumline, with flossing or other orthodontic cleaning tools used to reach between the teeth. Good oral hygiene helps keep both the teeth and braces in better shape throughout treatment.

How often are braces appointments needed?

Most patients return for periodic adjustment visits so treatment can stay on track. Hoffman Orthodontics will recommend a visit schedule based on the patient’s treatment plan.

How long does braces treatment usually take?

Treatment time depends on the complexity of the case and how the teeth and bite respond over time. During a consultation in Leawood or Prairie Village, KS, Hoffman Orthodontics can give a more personalized estimate based on the patient’s needs.
