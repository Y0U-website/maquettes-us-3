Dr. Spina takes great pride in…

There is an old expression that goes something like, that was as much fun as spending an hour in a dentist’s chair , well, that negative line has been completely reversed since i started visiting Dr.Spina four years ago. i actually do enjoy going to the dentist since Dr. Spina and his entire staff are really fun, and more importantly, a fully competent group. We enjoy chatting about everything else outside of my mouth!

It’s also remarkable the way Dr. Spina takes great pride in his work, its not unusual to hear him mutter to himself in the middle of a procedure, oh good, yeah, that looks good, that’s nice, perfect, I’m fortunate to have found him and look forward to many more years of professional friendship.
