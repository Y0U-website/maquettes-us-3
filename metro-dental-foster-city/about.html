About Dr. Stephanie Huynh Metro Dental Foster City

Born and raised in San Francisco, Dr. Huynh received her undergraduate degree in Nutritional Science from UC Davis. She went on to receive her doctorate in dentistry from University of the Pacific in San Francisco.

Dr. Huynh is passionate about biomimetic dentistry and has completed extensive training with Dr. David Alleman and Dr. Davey Alleman.

Out of the office, Dr. Huynh enjoys traveling, hiking, and trying new restaurants.
