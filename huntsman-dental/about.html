About Huntsman dental

Your First Visit

Your first visit is an important time to get to know us and to understand how, together, we can enhance and maintain your overall dental health. During this time, we will examine your entire mouth, take any necessary radiographs (X-rays) and work with you to develop a treatment plan that will help ensure your beautiful smile for a lifetime.

We will introduce you to our intraoral camera so that you can see exactly what your doctor sees. You will also receive digital radiographs (X-rays) that produce the highest quality images with the least exposure possible. You may also bring or email bitewing X-rays taken less than a year ago and large panoramic X-rays taken less than three years ago.

We will present all of our findings to you regarding your oral health and will make recommendations for any treatment. It is important to share any questions or concerns you may have before we move forward with your personalized treatment. After your initial comprehensive examination, you will receive a professional cleaning by one of our hygienists.

Meet the Staff

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Dr. Stuart Huntsman

Dr. Huntsman enjoys providing Lafayette residents with exceptionally comfortable, lasting dental care. His favorite aspects of dentistry include esthetic cases, implants, and general dentistry for all ages. His patients appreciate his personal approach, as together they build and maintain beautiful smiles.

Dr. Huntsman and his wife have five amazing children . Outside of the office, Dr. Huntsman loves to spend time with his family, exercise, hunt, fish, and work on his family’s small farm. He has also devoted time to taking mission trips to provide people around the globe with compassionate dental care.

Dr. Huntsman attended LSU in Baton Rouge to receive his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences and attended LSU in New Orleans to receive his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. Dr. Huntsman is a member of the American Dental Association and the Louisiana Dental Association, and is an active participant in a local dental study club. He has also trained at the Pankey Institute and the Dawson Academy.
