Are Veneers Right for You

Veneers are a strong choice for Maitland patients with healthy teeth and gums who want to improve the look of their smile. The best candidates have good oral health, realistic expectations, and enough natural enamel to support the bonding process. During your consultation, we evaluate your teeth and discuss whether veneers or another option will deliver the results you are after. If issues such as gum disease or untreated decay are present, we address those first to give your veneers a healthy foundation.

Some patients get the best results from a combination of treatments, depending on what they want to change. If the main concern is tooth color, professional teeth whitening may be a simpler place to start. Porcelain vs. Composite

Porcelain Veneers:

  • Custom-made in a lab
  • Highly stain-resistant
  • Lasts 10–15 years
  • 2+ visits
  • Higher cost
  • Superior aesthetics

Composite Veneers:

  • Applied directly in-office
  • Single visit possible
  • Lower cost
  • Less stain-resistant
  • Lasts 4–8 years
  • Good for minor corrections

What to Expect With Veneers at Lumiere

Dr. Mersh reviews your goals and evaluates candidacy using digital X-rays and intraoral scans.

2. Digital Design

Using iTero/Trios scanner, we create a digital preview of your future smile.

3. Tooth Preparation

A thin layer of enamel (0.3–0.5mm) is removed to accommodate the veneer.

4. Impressions & Temporaries

A digital impression is sent to the dental lab; temporary veneers protect your teeth in the meantime.

Your custom porcelain veneers are bonded to your teeth with precision adhesive. Adjustments are made until your bite and appearance are perfect.

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With proper care, porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15 years or longer. Avoiding habits like nail-biting, chewing ice, or using your teeth as tools can significantly extend their lifespan.

Are porcelain veneers permanent?

Veneers are considered a permanent treatment because a thin layer of enamel is removed during preparation — and enamel does not grow back. However, the veneers themselves can be replaced if they chip or wear over time.

Do porcelain veneers look natural?

Yes — when designed and placed correctly, porcelain veneers are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. Dr. Mersh works with skilled dental ceramists to match the shade, translucency, and texture of your surrounding teeth.

Am I a good candidate for porcelain veneers in Maitland?
