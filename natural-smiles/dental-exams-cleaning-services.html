The Importance of Regular Professional Cleanings

Even with the best brushing and flossing habits, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas. Our professional cleaning services are designed to give your teeth a fresh start. Here’s what our hygienists do during your cleaning:

Our cleanings help reduce your risk of gum disease and tooth decay while keeping your smile bright and fresh.

  • Remove plaque and tartar from above and below the gumline
  • Polish your teeth to remove surface stains and smooth enamel
  • Floss between your teeth to eliminate debris
  • Apply fluoride treatment (if needed) for extra protection

How Often Should You Get Dental Exams and Cleanings?

We recommend that most patients come in every six months for exams and cleanings. However, those with a history of gum disease, frequent cavities, or other oral health issues may need more frequent visits. During your first appointment, our team will develop a personalized schedule based on your oral health history.

Preventive dental care is closely linked to your overall well-being. Who Needs Dental Exams & Cleanings?

Dental exams and cleanings are vital for everyone, from children getting their first tooth to seniors managing their long-term oral health. Here’s a look at who should prioritize these services:

Every age group benefits from consistent dental care. At Natural Smiles Dental Care, we tailor our approach to suit your unique needs.

  • Children & Teens : Helps prevent cavities, monitor jaw development, and reinforce healthy habits.
  • Adults : Maintains dental health, detects early signs of gum disease or decay.
  • Seniors : Ensures dentures or implants are functioning correctly and checks for age-related oral health issues.

What Happens If You Skip Dental Cleanings?

Neglecting your regular dental cleanings can lead to serious issues, including:

Preventive care is always more affordable and less invasive than emergency dental work. Keep your smile healthy with routine exams and cleanings.

  • Tooth decay and cavities

  • Tooth loss
  • Costly restorative dental treatments
