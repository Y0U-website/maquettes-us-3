General Dentistry

Our Xenia dental office

is devoted to providing the best general and family dentistry services. As your primary dental care provider, we are responsible for the general diagnosis, prevention, treatment, and management of a wide range of dental conditions, disorders, and diseases that affect the teeth, gums, jaw, and face. We provide the coordination of services related to the oral health needs for patients of all ages so everyone in your entire family can visit our practice.

With a highly educated, well-trained team, we will develop the best course of treatment for your complete dental needs. Give us a call to schedule an appointment today!

General Dentistry Includes

Preventative care visits

Dental cleanings, digital Xrays, periodontal treatment, flouride treatments, care for the entire family

Fillings

Restoring teeth from damages caused by dental decay, trauma, or wear

Full mouth rehabilitation

Combining restorative dental treatments to fix or rebuild your smile

Bridges

Non removable tooth replacement using a patient’s existing teeth can improve looks and function

Crowns

Teeth that are structurally compromised benefit from lifelike looking crowns to improve function

Pediatric

We believe that early dental visits are the key to a healthy smile

Extractions

Surgical as well as non surgical extractions performed

Dentures

Complete dentures, partial dentures, implant supported dentures

Root canal therapy

To repair and save a badly damaged or infected tooth instead of removing it
