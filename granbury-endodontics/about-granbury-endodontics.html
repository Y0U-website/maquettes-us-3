About Us

0 About Granbury Endodontics We’re Granbury’s expert endodontists. If you’re suffering from tooth pain, our team of endodontists at Granbury Endodontics have the experience, technology and care to save teeth and relieve tooth pain. We specialize in root canal therapy using our GentleWave® machine, the highest level of clean and comfort for endodontic procedures.

Make an Appointment Meet our doctors. Dr. Francisco Nieves, DDS, MDS Dr. Francisco Nieves is a board-certified diplomate of the American Board of Endodontists (ABE) and has been practicing since his graduation in 2018.

He grew up in southwestern Fort Worth, attending North Crowley High School and then Saint Bonaventure University on a swimming scholarship for his undergraduate studies.

Following his undergraduate studies, Dr. Nieves received his DDS from the UT School of Dentistry in Houston, then pursued his passion for endodontics by completing specialty training at the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine .

If you do live closer to Fort Worth, Dr. Nieves is proud to also see patients at West Fort Worth Endodontics , a partnership he shares with Dr. Alex Fitzhugh of Burleson Endodontics .

Outside of his passion for patient care, Dr. Nieves enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, hosting his family for sports-watching and other gatherings and drinking the finest craft beer and coffee in Dallas-Fort Worth.
