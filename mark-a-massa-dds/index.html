The practice

Located in Mansfield OH, our dental office provides a wide range of general dentistry services to ensure you and your family can keep on smiling with confidence. Dr. Mark A. Massa doesn’t just practice dentistry in Mansfield, OH It’s also where he was born! Even at the age of 8, when his mother asked what he wanted to be when he grew up, he told her he was going to be a dentist.

He attended Lexington High School and graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University Dental School. He is a member of The American Dental Association (ADA) and The Ohio Dental Association (ODA)

Since 1993, he’s been practicing dentistry with a gentle touch and providing his patients with the healthy, beautiful smiles they deserve.

Dr. Massa’s goal as a dentist is to help his patients fully understand their dental condition and work with them to devise a treatment plan that will get them to a healthy and stable state of oral health. He is passionate about dentistry and loves to finish cases with results that far exceed his patient’s expectations.

When Dr. Massa isn’t treating patients or keeping up with the latest advances in dentistry, he enjoys playing golf, fishing, and hunting. He also plays guitar. His family and close friends are important to him. He values honesty, loyalty, and dependability, and strives to be responsible and accountable in all aspects of his life.

Dr. Massa likes to keep his office a bright and welcoming place to come for your next appointment. We have a reputation for friendly smiles and quality dental work and it’s our mission to ensure that all of our patients are comfortable while we give their smiles that lustrous shine. we serve the greater Mansfield, Ohio area.

We stand by all that we offer and are proud of the quality of dental services we provide. In addition, we guarantee that by the time our work for you is complete, you will be nothing less than completely satisfied.

If you are interested in what we can offer you or have questions, we encourage you to contact us! One of our experienced team members will be delighted to take your call. Will be happy to help you. We look forward to hearing from you.
