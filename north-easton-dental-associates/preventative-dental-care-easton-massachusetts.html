Preventive Care

Our goal is to protect your smile for years to come with gentle, proactive care that keeps your teeth and gums healthy.

Keep Your Smile Healthy

Preventive dental care is the key to maintaining a healthy, confident smile. At our practice, we focus on identifying and addressing potential issues before they become problems. By staying proactive with preventive care, you can enjoy stronger teeth, healthier gums, and fewer unexpected dental visits. Regular checkups are an investment in a lifetime of smiles.

Our preventive services include:

Hygiene Cleanings

Regular professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, helping to prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath.

Dental Exams & Diagnostics

Comprehensive exams, including digital X-rays, allow us to detect early signs of tooth decay, gum disease, or other dental concerns. Early detection means less invasive treatment and healthier teeth over time.

Sealants

Sealants provide an extra layer of protection that can prevent cavities before they start. By covering the grooves and pits on the chewing surfaces of your back teeth, sealants make it easier to keep your smile clean and healthy.
