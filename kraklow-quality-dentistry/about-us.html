About Us - Kraklow Quality Dentistry

OUR MISSION.

At Kraklow Quality Dentistry, we provide high-quality dental care to you and your family in a comfortable, relaxing environment and are committed to helping you feel, look and smile your best. We pride ourselves on our stress-free and family-friendly atmosphere while making no compromises when it comes to the quality of your dental care.

With a focus on preventing any dental problems before they develop through our preventative services and patient education, our goal is to help you achieve a healthy smile for a lifetime. We look forward to serving you and your family members!

Tom Kraklow, DDS

Dr. Thomas Kraklow is a 1988 graduate of Marquette University School of Dentistry. Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Kraklow was commissioned as a Captain in the US Army Dental Corp and completed a one year General Practice Residency at Fort Jackson, SC. After completing his residency, Dr. Kraklow was assigned to the 2nd Armored Division Forward in Garlstedt, Germany. While serving in the Army Dental Corp, Dr. Kraklow became the Division Dentist for his unit and served his division in Desert Storm where he received two Army commendations for service.
