A native of Chandigarh, India, Dr. Setia graduated at the top of her class in India. After being the only student in India to be awarded an International Certificate of Merit for her contributions towards dental literature, academics and community service in 2013, she moved to the United States to further her education and training.

She completed her DDS degree at University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine and in recognition of her academic achievement was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

With Dr. Setia you can expect a gentle and personalized approach to oral care using the latest technology along with a customized treatment plan that best suits your needs for a long-lasting, beautiful smile. She has been recognized with various awards in Esthetic Dentistry and that remains her passion.
