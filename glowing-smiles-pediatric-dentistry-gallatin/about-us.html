About Us

Pediatric Dentist In Gallatin, TN

At Glowing Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, our goal is to provide exceptional dentistry while educating our patients and their parents on the importance of achieving and maintaining health. We provide dental services in a welcoming and compassionate environment that reduces anxiety and creates trusting relationships.

When you’re in our office, we promise to be more than dental providers. We’ll make you and your child feel at home while providing the most current and latest treatments. Come join our family at Glowing Smiles Pediatric Dentistry to see why we’re kid experts!

Dr. Bora Kim Board Certified Pediatric Dentist Dr. Bora Kim is passionate about children having healthy, glowing smiles. She strives to provide a gentle approach while also creating a positive dental experience for children.

After receiving her Bachelor of Science from Georgia Institute of Technology, she graduated from University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, with her heart set on pediatric dentistry. She went on to complete her pediatric dental training at the University of Southern California. She is a diplomat of American board of Pediatric Dentistry and has advanced training in tongue and lip tie releases.

In her free time, Dr. Bora Kim loves adventures, baking, and spending quality time with family. All children deserve a big, beautiful smile, and Dr. Bora Kim is happy to be part of that journey.

A Personalized, Hands-On Approach to Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Bora Kim is dedicated to being hands-on with every child she treats, ensuring that they receive the personalized care they deserve. She takes the time to educate both children and parents about proper oral hygiene in an engaging way, helping them feel empowered and comfortable during their visits. Dr. Kim’s approach is to spend quality time with each child, creating a trusting environment while promoting positive dental habits that last a lifetime.

Meet Leo!

Leo is not just any therapy dog; he is a vital part of our team at Glowing Smiles Pediatric Dentistry. With his boundless energy and love for fun, Leo brings joy and comfort to our young patients and their families.

Leo is exceptional at making dental visits more relaxing. His presence in our office is always a delight, as he warmly greets everyone with a wagging tail. Leo also sets an example in dental hygiene, patiently getting his teeth brushed, demonstrating to children that dental care can be a positive part of daily routines.

His gentle nature and friendly demeanor make him an indispensable part of the Glowing Smiles family, helping every child feel at home during their dental journey.
