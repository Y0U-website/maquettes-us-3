Patient Information

Patient Information First Visit Your first visit to our office is a great opportunity for us to get to know each other and introduce you to the treatments we offer. The visit begins with an initial consultation, and we recommend setting aside at least an hour to ensure there’s plenty of time for us to discuss your needs, address any concerns, and answer all your questions without feeling rushed.

During the visit, you’ll meet one of our patient coordinators, who will review your medical history and personal information before guiding you through the office. Afterward, we’ll take x-rays, photos, and perform a clinical exam to get a comprehensive look at your dental health and plan the best course of treatment for your smile.

Financing and Insurance We want to provide you with affordable, high-quality treatment that fits your budget! That’s why we communicate clearly about costs from the start, offering a detailed treatment plan with a breakdown of all fees. Before any treatment begins, we’ll make sure you understand all associated costs so there are no surprises along the way.

We work with almost all insurances and most often even when we are not in network, the benefits are the same! Please call our office for more information.

To make payment easy, we accept cash, checks, VISA, MasterCard, and Discover, and we also offer in-house financing options. Additionally, we work with third-party financing through CareCredit. If you have any questions about payment or financing, just ask! We’re happy to explain your options and will do our best to find a solution that works for you
