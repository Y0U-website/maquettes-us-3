Dr. Michael Carley, DDS

Bringing bright smiles and providing long-term quality and affordable dental services to Hood River residents has always been the mission and vision of the dental office of Michael D. Carley, DDS. Dr. Katie Weaver, DDS

Dr. Weaver provides exceptional dental care with a personal touch. She believes that a visit to your dentist should be personalized, warm, and inviting. So, since beginning her practice in 2004 she has asked a simple question: “What would I want to experience as a patient?” every single day to help provide the best possible experience for each patient.

Dr. Weaver grew up in Yakima, WA, graduated from Pepperdine University with a B.S. in Biology in 1996, and earned her D.D.S. from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2001. She graduated in the top of her class with awards from the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and Academy of General Dentistry as well as in Restorative Dentistry and Practice Management.

Since then, her main areas of interest have been restorative and cosmetic dentistry. She is a graduate from the Kois Center which is the leader in dental continuing education programs delivering the latest advances in esthetics, implants and restorative dentistry.

She excels in providing comprehensive dentistry, which for some people may be just a cleaning and a few fillings, and other may involve a full mouth reconstruction, crowns, veneers, composite filling, or invisalign (clear braces).

Dr. Weaver lives in Husum, WA, is married to her husband Simon, and has 2 beautiful daughters, Vivian and Lucie. They love all things outdoors including, biking, skiiing, and sailing. Vivian went to be with her heavenly Father in March of 2020 after a 2 year battle with DIPG. Dr. Weaver continues to be focused on spreading awareness of and helping to fund a cure for DIPG. More information can be found at vivianrosedipg.org
