What is the Procedure for Getting Single Tooth Dental Implant in Flowood, MS? We can complete the single-tooth dental implant procedure in two steps. First, we place the titanium post in the jawbone. The second step is to place the abutment on top of the implant and attach the single-tooth replacement. This procedure is also done under local anesthesia and sedation if needed.

The single-tooth dental implant procedure usually takes one to two hours.

How Long Does the Implant Process Take? The single-tooth dental implant process usually takes two to six months. This process includes the time it takes for the implant to fuse with the jawbone and the time it takes to make and place the single tooth replacement.

What Type of Post-Operative Care is Required After Getting Single Tooth Dental Implants? Once the single-tooth dental implant procedure is complete, it is crucial to take care of the implants to ensure they last a lifetime. There are a few things you need to do to care for your single-tooth dental implants:

  • Brush and floss your teeth twice a day
  • Eat a healthy diet
  • Visit us regularly for checkups and cleanings
