The practice

Orthodontic care is more than just straightening smiles. We invest in the overall facial harmony and function of our patients by offering a number treatments from traditional braces or Invisalign clear aligners to additional appliances that help with bite correction and improve jaw position. We tailor our approach for each patient as an individual with unique needs!

Our office is a safe space for our patients. At each visit, we bring our expertise and a joyful spirit to the chair so that your appointments are comfortable, efficient, and impactful on your orthodontic journey.

Meyer Orthodontics has been treating families for generations and will always be proud to be a part of our local Brookings and Madison area communities. Our team works and lives here with you.

0 Free Treatment Consultation It all starts with the initial exam. Every orthodontic journey starts with a simple, stress-free appointment . Our team will review your smile goals, your dental and medical history, and take x-rays.

We work with our patients directly to collaborate on your specific concerns and treatment goals to determine the treatment plan that would be best for you. Dr. Paul Meyer will personally visit with you about his treatment recommendations.

If a young patient is not developmentally ready to begin treatment, we will schedule free periodic recall visits to check in to monitor growth and ensure proper dental development. This can make future orthodontic treatment shorter and simpler.
