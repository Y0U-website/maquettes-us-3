What to Expect During Treatment

Every implant restoration begins with a thorough evaluation of your existing implant, including digital imaging to check the health of the surrounding bone and gum tissue. If the implant itself is stable and healthy, we move forward with designing your new restoration. We take precise digital impressions, select a shade that blends naturally with your smile, and work with a trusted dental lab to fabricate a crown or prosthesis built specifically for your mouth.

Once your custom restoration is ready, we attach it securely to the implant abutment and check your bite to make sure everything feels comfortable and functions properly. Most implant restoration appointments are quick and require little to no downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does implant restoration hurt?

Most patients feel little to no discomfort during implant restoration since the procedure typically does not involve surgery on the implant post itself. Any mild sensitivity usually fades quickly.

How long does a restoration typically last?

With proper care, an implant crown or restoration can last well over a decade. Regular checkups help us catch wear or damage early.

Can you restore an implant placed by another dentist?

Yes. We regularly provide implant restoration for patients whose original implant was placed elsewhere.

Will my new restoration match my other teeth?

Absolutely. We custom shade and shape every restoration so it blends naturally with your smile.
