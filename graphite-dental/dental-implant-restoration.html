What Is Dental Implant Restoration?

Dental implant restoration involves creating and attaching a custom crown, bridge, or denture to the dental implant post. This process completes your tooth replacement, giving you a durable and natural-looking solution. Implant restorations are essential for both functionality and aesthetics, enabling you to bite, chew, and smile with confidence.

Why Choose Dental Implant Restoration?

If you’re new to dental implants, visit our page on dental implants to learn about the entire process.

  • Natural Appearance: Implant restorations are designed to match your surrounding teeth in color, size, and shape.
  • Durability: Properly restored implants can last decades with regular care.
  • Functionality: Restorations restore full chewing and speaking capabilities.
  • Bone Health: By supporting the jawbone, implants prevent bone loss caused by missing teeth.

Types of Implant Restorations

At Graphite Dental, we offer various implant restoration solutions tailored to your specific needs:

Using advanced tools like digital impressions with iTero , we ensure precise, comfortable, and perfectly fitting restorations.

  • Single-Tooth Restoration: Custom crowns are attached to individual implant posts for a seamless, natural look.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Replace multiple missing teeth with a bridge supported by implants instead of natural teeth.
  • Full-Arch Restoration: Complete your smile with implant-supported dentures, a secure and comfortable alternative to traditional dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restoration

What materials are used for implant restorations? Implant restorations are often made from porcelain, zirconia, or ceramic for durability and a natural appearance.

How long does an implant restoration last? With proper care, implant restorations can last 15 years or longer. Regular dental checkups help ensure their longevity.

Can I replace a damaged restoration on an existing implant? Yes, we specialize in replacing and upgrading restorations for existing implants. Schedule a consultation to discuss your options.

Is the restoration process painful? No, implant restoration is a minimally invasive procedure, and most patients experience little to no discomfort.

What is the cost of dental implant restoration? The cost varies depending on the type of restoration and materials used. Contact us to receive a personalized estimate.
