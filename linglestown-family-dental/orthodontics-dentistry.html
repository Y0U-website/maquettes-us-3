Orthodontic Dentistry

At Linglestown Family Dental, we begin the treatment process with a thorough orthodontic examination to determine if Invisalign is suitable for the patient’s specific needs. Using advanced computer imaging technology, a 3D model of the teeth is created to design a customized treatment plan. The aligners are then fabricated and replaced periodically to accommodate the shifting teeth.
