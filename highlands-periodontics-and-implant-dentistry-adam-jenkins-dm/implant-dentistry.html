How Do Dental Implants Work?

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation and Evaluation : We begin with a comprehensive oral exam, including X-rays, to assess your suitability.
  • Implant Placement : One or more titanium posts are carefully implanted into your jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. In certain cases, we can fit a temporary crown on the tooth at the time of implant placement, depending on the bone’s strength around the implant. However, if the bone is not sufficiently strong, we allow time for it to grow and stabilize before placing a permanent crown on the implant.
  • Healing Period : Over the next few months, the bone naturally fuses with the implant, providing a strong foundation.
  • Implant Placement : Once healing is complete, a custom dental implant is attached to the post, restoring the look and function of your natural teeth.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Choosing dental implants comes with several long-term benefits:

  • Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Improved Functionality : Unlike dentures, implants stay in place, allowing you to eat and speak comfortably.
  • Aesthetic Appeal : Implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Bone Preservation : Implants help preserve the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often accompanies missing teeth.

Common Questions About Dental Implants

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for decades, making them an excellent long-term investment in your oral health.

Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. We use local anesthesia to ensure your comfort, and any post-operative pain can be managed with over-the-counter pain relief. For those with intense dental anxiety, we also offer several different sedation options.

How Long Is the Recovery Time for Dental Implants?

Most patients experience some sensitivity for a day or two following the procedure, and there is typically very little pain associated with implant placement. It will take about 3-6 months for the bone to fully heal around the implant, but the area is not sore for the entirety of this healing time.
