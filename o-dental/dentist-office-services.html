Our Services

Zoom! Whitening

Brighten your smile up to 8 shades in just about an hour and a half under the Philips Zoom whitening lamp at our dentist office—it’s quick, easy, and totally worth it! Plus, you’ll get custom take-home trays to keep your smile sparkling long after your appointment.

Cleanings

Keep your smile healthy and sparkling with regular dental cleanings. Routine cleanings every six months help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues before they start. For patients who need extra care, we also offer periodontal maintenance to manage gum health and protect your teeth long-term. A clean mouth isn’t just about looking great—it’s about feeling great too!

Fillings

From strong posterior fillings that can handle the full force of your bite to natural-looking anterior fillings that blend seamlessly with your smile, we’ve got you covered. Durable, comfortable, and designed to look—and feel—just right.

Crowns

Protect and restore your teeth with custom dental crowns. Whether it’s strengthening a weakened tooth, restoring a broken one, or improving your smile’s appearance, our crowns are designed to fit comfortably, look natural, and last for years to come. Make an appointment at our dentist office to get started.

Dentures

Restore your smile and confidence with custom dentures designed for comfort and a natural look. Whether you need full or partial dentures, our dentist office team will create a solution that fits well, functions beautifully, and helps you smile, eat, and speak with ease.

Bridges

Fill the gap and restore your smile with a custom dental bridge. Bridges are designed to look natural, restore function, and keep your teeth properly aligned—so you can chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Extractions

Sometimes a tooth needs to come out to protect your overall oral health. Our gentle extractions are performed with care to keep you comfortable and ensure a smooth recovery, so you can get back to smiling and feeling your best.

Cosmetic

Transform your smile with cosmetic treatments like veneers, designed to enhance the look of your teeth and boost your confidence. From correcting chips and gaps to brightening and reshaping, we create smiles that look natural and feel amazing.

Sealants

Night Guards

Protect your teeth from grinding and clenching while you sleep with a custom night guard. Comfortable, durable, and designed just for you, night guards help prevent wear, reduce jaw pain, and keep your smile healthy for years to come.

Exams & X-Rays

Regular dental exams and X-rays help us catch problems early and keep your smile healthy. During your Port Lavaca dentist office visit, we’ll check your teeth, gums, and bite, while X-rays give us a clear view of what’s happening beneath the surface—so you get proactive, personalized care.

Emergencies

When tooth pain, a broken tooth, or another dental emergency strikes, we’re here to help—fast. Our Crossroads area Dentist Office team provides prompt, compassionate care to relieve pain, address the issue, and get your smile back on track.
