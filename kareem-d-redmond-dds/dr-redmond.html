Blue Wolf Dental

Kareem Redmond

DDS

Dr. Kareem Redmond established Blue Wolf Dental with a clear mission: to pair advanced digital dentistry with compassionate, patient-first care. Driven by a passion for elite clinical precision and predictable outcomes, Dr. Redmond specializes in high-volume clear aligner transformations, specialized airway sleep solutions, and comprehensive family dentistry.

Dr. Redmond completed his undergraduate studies at North Carolina State University before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Dentistry. Following dental school, he completed an intensive two-year General Practice Residency at Wake Forest Baptist Hospital, gaining extensive experience in complex restorative care, surgical procedures, and full-mouth health.

Elite Clear Aligner Expertise & Advanced Training

To provide the highest caliber of clear aligner care available, Dr. Redmond has dedicated hundreds of hours to advanced clinical training and biomechanics. His focus on precision digital treatment setups earned him the prestigious American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA) Done in One Award , a national recognition highlighting clinical excellence in finishing complex aligner cases predictably in a single set of aligners without mid-course delays.

His advanced orthodontic aligner background includes:

Reingage Trained: Advanced training in precise tooth movement, biomechanics, and single-refinement clear aligner setups.

American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA): Active member committed to mastering cutting-edge digital smile design and non-invasive alignment techniques.

By combining 3D iTero digital scanning with custom, doctor-led treatment planning, Dr. Redmond delivers straight, healthy smiles in fewer office visits, with shorter treatment times, and without messy impressions.

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Redmond is a devoted family man. He and his wife, Cintasha, are proud parents to four children. When he isn’t in the office, you can find him coaching youth basketball teams at the local YMCA, playing pickup games, or hosting Blue Wolf Dental’s annual One Smile At A Time community volunteer day to give back to neighbors in need.

🐾 The Story Behind Blue Wolf : Ever wonder where our practice name comes from? Blue Wolf Dental is a personal tribute to the educational journey that shaped Dr. Redmond’s clinical foundation right here in North Carolina. He earned his undergraduate degree as part of the Wolfpack at NC State, followed by his dental degree at UNC Chapel Hill (Carolina Blue)—bringing the best of both worlds together to serve our Clemmons community!
