Root Canals

This treatment can help maintain your natural teeth and prevent the need for dental implants or bridges. Root canals are necessary to treat infection of the pulp which is a collection of blood vessels that helps to build the surrounding tooth. This can be caused by trauma to the tooth, deep decay, cracks and chips. We look for symptoms of the infection like visible injury or swelling of the tooth, sensitivity to temperature or pain in the tooth and gums. We use technology with high magnification to be able to clean the diseased pulp and bring your tooth back to normal.
