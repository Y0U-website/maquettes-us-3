The practice

I’m Dr. James Nelson, and I’ve called McMinnville home since 1957. My father Richard was a beloved physician in this community, and my brother John carries on that tradition in family medicine to this day.

We believe relationships matter. We take the time to truly get to know you. That connection allows us to deliver care that is comfortable, consistent, and built on genuine trust. This is why many of our patients have been with us for decades.

General Dentistry Comprehensive exams, professional cleanings, X-rays, and preventive care — the foundation of a healthy, lifelong smile.

Cosmetic Dentistry From teeth whitening to smile makeovers, we help you achieve the confident, radiant smile you’ve always wanted.

Dental Crowns Precision-crafted crowns that restore strength, function, and beauty to damaged or weakened teeth.

Dental Implants Natural-looking, permanently secured implants that restore your full smile and improve quality of life.

Sedation Dentistry Anxiety-free dentistry for every patient. We offer comfortable sedation options so fear never stands between you and your health.

Preventive Care Routine care, sealants, fluoride treatments, and patient education to protect your smile for decades to come.
