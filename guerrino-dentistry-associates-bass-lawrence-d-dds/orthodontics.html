Providing Traditional Braces

One of the options we offer at Guerrino Dentistry and Associates is traditional braces, also called metal braces . This type of orthodontic treatment can be ideal for patients with a wide variety of orthodontic needs, including teens and adults. Traditional braces use wires, brackets, and bands to gradually push your teeth to their desired positions.

This type of treatment is used to address routine and complex cases. You can rest assured that we’ll give you all the information you need to maintain your new braces and avoid complications that can impact treatment. Additionally, traditional braces can be used in combination with other orthodontic treatments, like dental expanders, to help you reach your goals.

Offering Invisalign Clear Aligners

If you’re interested in an alternative orthodontic option, Invisalign clear aligners are a popular offering. Invisalign treatment is normally used to address mild to moderate dental misalignment and is ideal for teens and adults. Treatment consists of a series of clear plastic aligners that place gradual pressure on your teeth until they’re in their proper positions.

Given the clear nature of the aligners, you can confidently wear them throughout the day without feeling like you’re drawing unwanted attention to your smile. At-home care is easy, as the aligners can be removed at any time to eat, drink, or care for your teeth. In addition, our practice uses an iTero™ scanner to create digital impressions of your smile without messy putty.

Keeping Your Smile Straight With Retainers

It can be easy to forget that your smile continues to need care following orthodontic treatment. Over time, your teeth can gradually shift back to their unaligned positions. That’s why our dental office is proud to provide retainers that keep your teeth in their desired positions.

Our orthodontist will sit down with you to outline what our retainers entail and how they can ultimately protect your hard-earned dental results. You can breathe easy knowing that we’ll customize your retainer for a secure and comfortable fit, no matter how long you’re required to wear them.
