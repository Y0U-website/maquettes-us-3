Dental Implants FAQs

Are Dental Implants Painful?

While the dental implant procedure involves a minor surgery, your dentist will use local anesthesia to make it as comfortable as possible. If you have dental anxiety, we can also offer sedation dentistry services to help you relax during the procedure. These include nitrous oxide sedation (laughing gas), oral conscious sedation, intravenous sedation, or general anesthesia.

Some discomfort or swelling may occur for a few days after getting your surgery, but the pain is typically managed effectively with over-the-counter painkillers. The process is often described more as discomfort than actual pain.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most people in good general health and have adequate bone density in their jaw can be good candidates for dental implant treatment. You must also have healthy gums and be free of periodontal disease. Certain specific conditions, such as diabetes or osteoporosis, may affect the success of dental implants, but it doesn’t necessarily mean you are not a good candidate.

Our dental implants in Normandy Park, Washington are custom-made to fit your unique needs and our experienced team can help determine if you are a good candidate for the procedure.

What Is the Best Age for Dental Implants?

There’s no specific best age for tooth implants, but they’re typically recommended for adults who have completed their jaw growth. This usually occurs around age 18 for girls and 20 for boys. However, it’s always best to visit us so we can determine if you’re a good candidate for the procedure. We will assess your overall health, jawbone density and other factors to determine if you’re ready for dental implants.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With good oral care and regular checkups, dental implants can last 25 years or more. However, the dental crown may need replacement every 10-15 years due to wear and tear, but the titanium implant itself is designed to be a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth.

How Many Teeth Do You Need for Implants?

The number of teeth needed for implants depends on the amount and location of the missing teeth. Bone loss is a significant factor but can frequently be addressed by a bone grafting procedure by our experienced dental implants Seattle team. Typically, one dental implant is required per missing tooth. However, if you are missing two or more teeth in a row, only two implant placements might be needed to support 1-3 pontic teeth.

When it comes to full-mouth replacements, methods vary. Some procedures use as few as two or four implants, while others may require six or more. For instance, the teeth in a day procedure typically involves placing a minimum of four implants to suspend 12 teeth in each jaw.

In the case of implant-supported dentures, the number of implants can range from two to four, and sometimes even more, depending on individual patient needs.
