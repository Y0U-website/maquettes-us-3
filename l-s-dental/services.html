Our Dental Services

L’s Dental Comprehensive Dental Services Welcome to L’s Dental, where our dedicated team is committed to providing exceptional dental care tailored to your individual needs. We offer a diverse array of services designed to improve both your oral health and the appearance of your smile. Please take a moment to discover the comprehensive range of dental treatments we offer below.

Essential Restorative Procedures

Dental Crowns

Crowns restore the shape, function, and appearance of damaged teeth. Our custom crowns offer strength and durability, ensuring long-lasting results.

Same-Day Crowns

Dental Bridges

Bridges fill gaps left by missing teeth, preventing shifting and maintaining proper bite alignment. Improve your smile and oral health with our expertly crafted bridges.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Implants offer a permanent solution for missing teeth, providing stability and a natural appearance. Restore your smile with our full mouth dental implants.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

A cost-effective dental implants for replacing one or all of your teeth in one arch using only one to four implants. Enjoy a full set of fixed teeth with this innovative procedure.

Tooth Extractions

Performed when necessary to maintain oral health, our tooth extractions are conducted with care to ensure minimal discomfort and quick recovery.

Root Canals

Save infected teeth with our root canal therapy. This procedure relieves pain and restores the function of damaged teeth, preventing the need for extraction.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile with our professional teeth whitening services. Enjoy significantly whiter teeth compared to over-the-counter products, with results that can boost your confidence.

Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a durable and aesthetically pleasing option to correct imperfections like chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth, giving you a flawless smile.

Invisalign®

Invisalign® provides a discreet way to straighten your teeth using clear aligners. It’s an ideal option for both teens and adults seeking a beautiful, straight smile without the hassle of traditional braces.

Supportive Procedures & Advanced Techniques

Full Mouth Reconstruction

A comprehensive approach to restoring all teeth in the mouth, addressing both functional and aesthetic concerns. Our full mouth reconstruction services provide a complete dental makeover.

Tooth Retreatment

Retreatment of previous root canals addresses issues and saves the tooth. Benefit from our expertise in endodontic retreatment to maintain your dental health.

Bone Grafting

Essential for patients needing dental implants, bone grafting restores lost bone structure, providing a solid foundation for successful implant placement.

Soft Tissue Grafting

Address gum recession and improve your smile’s aesthetics and health with our soft tissue grafting procedures, which restore and enhance your gum line.

Sinus Lift Surgery

Necessary for some dental implant candidates, sinus lifts create a solid foundation for upper jaw implants. Our sinus lift surgery ensures successful implant placement.

CBCT

Our advanced Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) provides 3D imaging for precise diagnosis and treatment planning, ensuring the best outcomes for complex dental cases.

Preventive Care

Dental Exams & X-Rays

Regular exams and x-rays are essential for early detection of dental issues. Our thorough exams and state-of-the-art x-rays help us create personalized treatment plans to maintain your oral health.

Dental Teeth Cleaning

Professional teeth cleanings remove plaque and tartar that regular brushing can’t reach, preventing cavities and gum disease. Schedule your bi-annual cleaning for optimal dental health.

Oral Cancer Screening

Early detection of oral cancer is crucial. Our screenings involve a detailed examination of your mouth to identify any suspicious areas, helping ensure early intervention.

Mouth Guards & Sports Guards

Custom-fitted mouth guards protect your teeth during sports and prevent teeth grinding at night. Protect your smile with our tailor-made solutions.

Periodontal Care

Gum Disease

Gum Disease

Early detection and treatment of gum disease are vital to prevent tooth loss and other health issues. Our periodontal treatments range from deep cleanings to advanced therapies.

TMJ Treatment

TMJ Treatment

Alleviate pain and improve jaw function with our TMJ treatments. We offer effective solutions for managing TMJ disorders, enhancing your comfort and quality of life.
