Clear Aligner Therapy

Non-Invasive and Comfortable

Looking for an effective way to straighten teeth and improve your smile? Clear aligner therapy is a non-invasive, comfortable, and convenient way to align teeth without the need for metal brackets or wires.

Benefits

Clear aligners are removable, so they can be taken out for brushing, flossing, and eating. This makes them a desirable option for those who have difficulty maintaining dental hygiene due to orthodontic braces. Clear aligners do not interfere with day-to-day activities such as eating or speaking.

Clear aligner therapy can also improve bite, correct spacing issues, and reduce the risk of gum disease. Schedule a consultation with Dr. Mabry today about choosing clear aligner therapy to achieve your ideal smile!
