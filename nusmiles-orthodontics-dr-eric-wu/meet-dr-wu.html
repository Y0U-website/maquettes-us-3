Meet Dr. Wu

My journey into Orthodontics started at Duke University, where I studied Biomedical Engineering. I was always drawn to healthcare and found Orthodontics to be t he perfect balance in that I could care for patients while using engineering principles to create great smiles. After finishing dental school at the University of Maryland and Orthodontic residency at the prestigious Mayo Clinic, where only one resident is accepted per year, I am very happy to come home and practice in Maryland.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine – University of Maryland
  • Orthodontic Fellowship – Mayo Clinic
  • Masters of Orthodontics – Mayo Clinic
  • Diplomate, American Board of Orthodontics

Outside the Office

When I’m not fixing smiles, I enjoy spending time with my family, reading, traveling, and trying new things and foods. In the past few years, I’ve ran a marathon and several half marathons, climbed two mountains in the Pacific Northwest, experienced farm life and farm work, and traveled to numerous countries in Europe, South America, and Asia! I am all ears if you have restaurant suggestions and I’d love to hear about your trips as well!
