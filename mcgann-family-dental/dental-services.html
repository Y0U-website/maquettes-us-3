Dental Services

At McGann Family Dental, we’re committed to providing high-quality, personalized dental care for patients of all ages. From preventive care to restorative and cosmetic treatments, our full range of services is designed to enhance your oral health, restore confidence in your smile, and support long-term wellness. Whether you need a routine check-up, a filling, or a more complex procedure, our skilled team is here to make your visit as comfortable and effective as possible. Let us help you achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are designed to provide a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel and function like natural teeth.

Dental Sealants

Simple procedure in which a tooth-colored acrylic “coating” is painted onto the surface of the tooth.

Tooth Extractions

We make every effort to save a tooth, but sometimes removal and replacement is the smartest option.

Crowns

This is an extremely reliable technique for repairing the most severe of dental problems, even permanently replacing missing teeth to offer a complete smile and a functional bite.

Fillings / Restorations

Today’s fillings are tooth-colored and the restorations are stronger than the old silver/mercury-type fillings.

Gum Disease Laser Therapy

The laser gently disinfects and evaporates diseased tissues from around the tooth and inside the gum pocket.

Bridges

A device used to replace missing teeth by attaching artificial teeth to adjacent natural teeth.

Dentures

Dentures can be partial (replace some teeth) or complete (replace all teeth).
