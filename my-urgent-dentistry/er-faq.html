Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

Services include: emergency dental treatment relating to or including: extractions, denture and partial repair, repair of chipped/broken teeth, emergency root canals and repair of broken fillings.

how do i make an appointment?

Our staff is on-call 24 hours/7 days a week. We are not a 24-hour walk-in clinic and hours may vary.

Please call 877-734-3131 for information and availability before arriving.

Do We accept Your Insurance?

Although we request payment in full at the time care is provided, we will help you to submit claims to your insurance. Please bring your dental benefit card so that we can gather the necessary information for you to submit your claim. We accept cash, personal checks, debit cards and all major credit cards. If you require extended payment plans, we accept payment plans through CareCredit . Please ask our office staff for more information if you would like to learn about this option.
