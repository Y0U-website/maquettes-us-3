Welcome to our Practice

Meiss Family Dental is about providing dental services in Peoria, Illinois to your entire family. Our staff consists of well trained professionals who work together as a team. Therefore, they will provide you and your family with the highest quality treatment possible, at a fair and reasonable fee.

Your Comfort Comes First

Patients can expect exceptional, comprehensive dental care to protect the health and add beauty to their smiles. We work diligently to provide excellent service, extraordinary dentistry, and gentle treatment to make a positive, lasting impression for all our patients in Peoria.

Convenient and Accessible Dentistry

Our convenient office hours make it easy for you to fit appointments into your busy schedule. Especially, If you have an emergency we can usually see you the same day in order to give you the relief you need. Also to make it affordable, we accept most insurance plans and we work hard to make sure you receive the maximum benefits allowed. If you’re interested in payment options for out-of-pocket expenses, we will be happy to discuss CareCredit financing .
