Dr. Souvik Sarkar is an internationally trained cosmetic dentist and Smile Designer whose work reflects a refined balance of science, artistry, and precision. Born in 1982 in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, Dr. Sarkar developed an early appreciation for detail, symmetry, and craftsmanship—principles that now define his approach to modern esthetic dentistry.

He completed his early education and undergraduate studies in Kolkata before advancing to dental school in Mangalore, Karnataka, where he earned his dental degree in 2007. Seeking global excellence and advanced clinical exposure, Dr. Sarkar relocated to the United States in 2009.
