Welcome to Ocean Isle Family Dentistry, office of Laura Douna, DDS. At Ocean Isle Family Dentistry everyone will receive quality, comfortable, honest and friendly care at a fair price. It is important for every patient to leave better and happier than they came. I want you to be proud to show your smile to everyone you meet.

The office is a state of the art facility with brand new equipment, digital x-rays, intraoral cameras and an electronic patient record. Treatments include preventative dentistry, oral cancer screenings, pediatric dentistry, emergencies, cosmetic dentistry, whitening, extractions, crowns and bridges, root canals, implants, dentures, partials and many other services.
