What exactly is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post inserted into the gum line where your tooth is missing. Once this anchor is placed, and healing is complete, we cover the post with a tooth crown (or replacement tooth). In the case of a missing front tooth, we create a temporary artificial tooth to improve your smile’s appearance while we design your permanent tooth crown.

Your dental implant dentist in Pikesville, MD will be with you every step of the way, and our team can’t wait to see your new smile reveal.

What s the best way to care for dental implants?

The best way to care for dental implants in Owings Mills, MD is to treat them like natural teeth.

With the help of your Pikesville, MD dentist, develop a good oral hygiene routine at home and visit our dental office regularly for exams and cleanings.

Generally, we recommend brushing and flossing at least twice per day and visiting our office twice-yearly for exams and dental cleanings. Periodically, your dentist in Pikesville, MD will recommend x-rays to make sure no potential problems are brewing below the surface.

Also, make sure you never use your implant or a natural tooth as a tool to pull a knot or open a bottle.

If you grind your teeth some people do this while sleeping it’s essential to ask your dentist in Pikesville, MD about a custom nightguard.

Remember, dental implants cannot decay, but they also cannot prevent periodontal disease or infection, which can lead to further tooth loss and implant failure.
