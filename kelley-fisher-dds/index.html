A dental implant is a titanium device that is surgically placed in the missing tooth. This piece of titanium looks very similar to a tooth root, and once it’s in place, it acts in the same method. Special threads in the implant help the device securely bond to the jaw bone, which keeps it permanently in place. Unlike dentures, implants prevent bone loss.
