FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Orthodontics is a dental specialty that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. They created treatments to help improve both function and appearance in their patients’ teeth.

Thanks to modern advances in dental technology, traditional metal braces are not the only option for straightening teeth. Other alternatives like clear ceramic braces or clear aligners (Invisalign®) are becoming more common. Your orthodontist will recommend the best solution based on your needs and lifestyle.

Children should have their first orthodontic evaluation around age 7, but treatment can begin at any age. Many teens and adults also benefit from orthodontic care.

Treatment time can depend on the complexity of the case, but most people undergo treatment through braces or aligners for 12-24 months. During your consultation, your orthodontist will give you a personalized estimate that aligns with your timeline.

While having a straighter smile boosts confidence, orthodontic treatment corrects bite issues, improves oral function, and makes it easier to maintain good dental hygiene.
