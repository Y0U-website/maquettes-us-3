The Teeth Whitening Procedure

At Handcrafted Dentistry, we pride ourselves on providing a comfortable and efficient teeth whitening experience. Here’s what you can expect during your visit:

Initial Consultation

Your teeth whitening journey begins with an initial consultation. Dr. Singleton or Dr. Borchardt will assess your oral health, discuss your whitening goals, and determine the most suitable treatment plan.

Preparation

Before the whitening procedure, a thorough cleaning removes any plaque and tartar. This ensures that the whitening agent can work effectively on the surface of your teeth. Protective barriers for your gums and lips are also applied to prevent irritation.

Application of Whitening Agent

We will carefully apply the professional-grade whitening gel to your teeth. At Handcrafted Dentistry, we use advanced whitening systems that offer significant results in just one session. The gel is activated using a particular light or laser, which helps to accelerate the whitening process.

Final Steps

Once the desired level of whiteness is achieved, the whitening gel is removed, and a fluoride treatment is applied to help reduce any potential sensitivity. You’ll leave our office with a noticeably brighter smile!

Aftercare

for Teeth Whitening

To maintain your newly whitened smile, it’s essential to follow some aftercare guidelines:

  • Avoid Staining Foods and Beverages : For at least 48 hours after your treatment, avoid foods and drinks known to stain teeth, such as coffee, tea, red wine, and dark-colored berries.
  • Good Oral Hygiene : Brush your teeth twice daily and floss daily to keep your smile looking its best. Using a whitening toothpaste can also help maintain your results.
  • Regular Dental Visits : Schedule regular check-ups with Dr. Singleton or Dr. Borchardt to monitor your oral health and touch up your whitening as needed.
  • Avoid Tobacco Products : Smoking or using tobacco products can quickly undo the results of your teeth whitening. Consider quitting for your overall health and to keep your smile bright.
