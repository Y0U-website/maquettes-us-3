Les soins

Routine exams, professional cleanings, and digital x-rays help detect dental problems early and protect your teeth and gums. Our office also uses the advanced iTero® digital scanner to perform detailed digital exams and monitor changes in your oral health.

Dental implants provide a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. An implant acts as an artificial tooth root that supports a custom crown, restoring both the appearance and function of your smile.

Invisalign uses clear, custom-made aligners to gradually straighten teeth while improving bite alignment and overall oral function. The aligners are comfortable, removable, and virtually invisible.

Cosmetic treatments such as professional teeth whitening and smile enhancements are designed to improve the appearance of your smile while maintaining healthy teeth and gums.
