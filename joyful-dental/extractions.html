Recovery & Aftercare

What is a &#x27;Dry Socket&#x27; and how do I avoid it? A dry socket occurs if the protective blood clot is dislodged. Avoid straws, smoking, and vigorous rinsing for at least 72 hours to allow the site to stabilize.

When can I return to work or exercise? Most patients return to work the next day. However, we recommend avoiding strenuous exercise for 3-5 days to keep your blood pressure stable and prevent bleeding.

How do I manage discomfort at home? We typically recommend a combination of anti-inflammatories and cold compresses. You will receive a detailed, printed recovery plan before you leave our office.

What are my options for replacing the tooth? Preserving your bite is important. Once healed, we can discuss Dental Implants or Bridges to prevent your other teeth from shifting into the empty space.
