Rick Tananis has committed himself to excellence in dentistry as a student, a practitioner, and as a teacher. He received his bachelor’s degree from St. Joseph’s College, his dental degree from Temple University and a fellowship in oral medicine from the University of Pennsylvania.

Dr. Tananis began the practice of dentistry in 1983. He has served as an instructor at Temple Dental School, Christiana Care Health System, Delaware Technical and Community College and as a clinical assistant professor at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. He is also a member of the ADA, and is a diplomate of the American Academy of Oral Medicine.

After practicing for 30 years in Philadelphia, he took over the practice of Dr. James Robert Carmean in Laurel, Delaware. In doing so, he was able to fulfill his lifelong ambition to live near the Atlantic Ocean. He and his wife Linda have no children but are close to their 4 nieces.

Dr. Jamie Green, D.D.S is a native of Wilmington and 1980 graduate of Archmere Academy. She received her D.D.S. degree from University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery. Dr. Green has been an associate in group dental practices in PA, Wilmington, DE, and most recently Sewell, NJ.

Dr. Green and her husband relocated to Lewes to be closer to family. Their daughter attends college and works at Nemours Children s Hospital in Florida. They enjoy boating, biking, hiking, and travel. Dr. Green is a proud fan of all the Philadelphia sports teams!
