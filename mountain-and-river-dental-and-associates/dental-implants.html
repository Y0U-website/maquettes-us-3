Dental Implants

Whether you’re missing one tooth or more, dental implants are the only tooth replacement option that incorporates every aspect of your mouth into treatment. Here at Mountain and River Dental and Associates, Dr. Carley and his team work closely with patients to help them achieve a strong, beautiful smile with dental implants and implant-supported bridges and dentures.

Scheduling a no-obligation dental implants consultation is the first step. During this visit, you will learn more about the dental implant process and Dr. Carley will conduct a thorough examination to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants.

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants have become a leading restorative treatment to replace missing teeth. They act as a natural tooth root, providing your jawbone with its necessary stimulation to remain strong and supporting a natural-looking restoration.

Durable, stable, and long-lasting, dental implants are designed to look and function just like your natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

We offer different implant solutions depending on your specific dental needs, including:

  • Single or multi-tooth replacement
  • Full-arch tooth replacement
  • Overdentures

The Dental Implant Process

Your implant process begins at your initial consultation with Dr. Carley where he’ll examine your health and discuss your options with you. Once you decide to move forward with treatment, we’ll use our advanced CBCT technology and CEREC system to view and curate a customized, precise treatment plan.

You’ll be able to see how your implant will look and be made with the digital map our CEREC system creates before placement. This also allows your procedure to be less invasive and more efficient because of the enhanced accuracy our technology provides.

Your final restoration will be made the same day as your procedure, so you’ll be able to leave our office with a beautiful smile in just one day.

Are you a Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you want to restore your smile, enhance your appearance, and improve your health, dental implants are a great option for you. Before considering dental implants, it’s important to ask yourself, “Do I have good oral health?” In order to ensure success with your dental implants, having healthy gum tissue and a strong jawbone is crucial.

However, if you need to refine your oral health before receiving our restorative treatment, Dr. Carley can help. We offer multiple supplemental procedures that will reinstate your candidacy for dental implants. During your consultation, Dr. Carley will be able to determine if one of these procedures is necessary for you.
