Dr. Wenger practiced in Columbus before returning to Wayne County to purchase Dr. Zacher’s practice. He enjoys learning new things, staying up to date with dental technology and providing comfortable dental care for families. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and Stark County Dental Society.

Dr. Wenger met his wife, Kristin, during dental school and they got married in the fall of 2015. She is a primary care physician and practices locally at the Viola Startzman Clinic. They have 2 boys who are full of energy and keep them busy. Outside of the office, Dr. Wenger enjoys spending time with family, trying new foods and restaurants, and golfing in his spare time.
