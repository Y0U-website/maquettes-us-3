About Us

Helping Your Teeth Stay Healthy!

We know there’s nothing in the world that is more important than keeping your teeth healthy! At Implants and Dentures Center of Bedford, we make sure all of your dental needs are met. We want you to feel more confident than you have ever been with your smile!

Implants and Dentures Center (IDC) of Bedford has been providing best-in-class Dental Care services to the Texas community for over 3 years now. Our dedicated team of dental care professionals is known for its quality of work and comprehensive experience in the field. We guarantee client satisfaction by using latest technology and advanced techniques for our surgical procedures and cater to all age groups.

We provide various services for you and your family which include dentures and implants, family services including teeth cleaning, tooth extraction and root canals along with cosmetic makeover such as teeth whitening, Veneers, root planning, crowns and bridges and all other essential dental care procedures that keep your smile bright and shiny always!

Dr. Alawadi has succeeded in making a strong name for himself in the world of dentistry. Practicing dentistry for over 18 years now, Dr. Alawadi holds a DDS degree from the prestigious College of Dentistry at the University of Illinois in Chicago. He specializes in oral and maxillofacial surgery and has completed his residency program at Kharkov State University in Ukraine. Dr. Alawadi has received many hours of continual training in Dental Implants and Dental technologies including CEREC, Eaglesoft, and Sybron PRO), making him extremely skilled at performing all kinds of dental procedures.

Dr. Alawadi is a family man and loves to spend time with his two twin boys. He spends his free time with family and enjoys Tennis, Hiking, and Traveling. His ability to speak multiple languages allows him to communicate effectively with his patients. He is fluent in English, Ukrainian, Russian and Arabic. He has practiced dentistry at the center of Maxillofacial Surgery, School of Oral Health, Kuwait and Forsyth International Inc. Boston.

Dr. Alawadi Rushdi believes in using advanced technology and uses 3D scanning and 3D cone beam computed tomography in his treatment plan for best results. Using such dental techniques combined with an evidence-based approach, Dr. Alawadi makes his dental practice more efficient, affordable and palatable. His patients consider him an extremely passionate and caring dental practitioner who strives to make their every visit more pleasant.
