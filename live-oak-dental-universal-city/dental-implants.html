What To Expect From The Process of Implants in Live Oak, TX

As a recognized dental practice, we have the skills and resources to replace your missing teeth with high-grade dental implants. The process starts with a consultation, during which we will examine your oral cavity and take dental X-rays to develop a personalized treatment plan.

The first step of the procedure is implant surgery. After numbing the treatment site, our dentist will place an incision and drill a small hole to create space for the implant. We will secure the implant in the jawbone and allow it to heal for a few months.

Once it has integrated well with the surrounding bone, we will connect it with the replacement tooth/teeth. These are designed to match the aesthetics of your smile, restoring a flawless appearance.
