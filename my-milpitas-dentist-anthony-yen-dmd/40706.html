Who Can Get Dental Implants?

If you’re missing teeth and have a healthy jawbone, chances are you’re a candidate for dental implants in Milpitas, CA. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, advanced techniques can make implants possible for many patients. We will evaluate create a customized plan based on your oral health, your smile, and your lifestyle.

The Dental Implant Process: Simple and Comfortable

At My Milpitas Dentist, we make the implant process as smooth and comfortable as possible:

  • Consultation and Planning We assess your oral health and map out your treatment using advanced imaging.
  • Implant Placement We carefully place the titanium post in your jawbone. Local anesthesia ensures a pain-free experience.
  • Healing and Integration Over a few months, the implant fuses with your bone, forming a strong, natural foundation.
  • Crown Placement Finally, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing your new smile.

A Smile That Lasts a Lifetime

Choosing dental implants as your tooth replacement isn’t just about filling a gap in your smile. With dental implants, you are investing in your long-term oral health and self-confidence. Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants a good solution for missing teeth? If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants in Milpitas are one of the most effective and natural-looking solutions available today. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants are designed to look, feel, and function just like real teeth. They fill the gap in your smile, and they restore your confidence, comfort, and oral health for the long term.

Built to Last a Lifetime

A dental implant is made up of a small titanium post that fuses with your jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. This creates a strong, stable foundation for a custom-made crown that looks completely natural. With proper care, implants can last for decades, often a lifetime, making them one of the best long-term investments in your smile.

Keep Your Smile and Jaw Strong

When you lose a tooth, the surrounding bone can start to shrink over time. Dental implants help prevent that by stimulating your jawbone just like a natural tooth root. This keeps your bone healthy and your facial structure full and youthful.
