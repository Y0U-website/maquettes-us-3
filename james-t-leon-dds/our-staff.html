At Xenia Dental Care, we strive to create a welcoming environment and are passionate about creating a positive experience for you and your family. Our professional, highly trained team works together to help patients with all facets of your dental experience, including scheduling, hygiene treatment planning, insurance processing and financial assistance. Dr. James Leon DDS

Improving smiles, improving lives that is what Dr. Leon is passionate about. His training, continuing education and over 30 years of practice experience provide him with a strong foundation to improve your health and wellbeing.

Dr. Leon received his Bachelor of Science and Doctor of Dental Surgery degrees from West Virginia University (1979-1987). He has practiced in Ohio and Florida and has been an owner of 6 dental practices. This has given him a wealth of experience and knowledge to be able to relate to patients of all walks of life. Dr. Leon has attended hundreds of hours of continuing education, as well as The Dawson Academy, to be able to provide comprehensive care to the Xenia community.

Dr. Leon and his wife, Mary, have 2 children. He enjoys exercise, cooking, golf, reading and his church. In light of Mary’s Parkinson’s disease diagnosis in 2015, he is also passionate about supporting those affected through care and research.

“I’m at the point in my career where I just want to help people feel better about themselves. If that means helping them get out of pain, or replacing missing teeth, or giving them a smile that makes them feel good about who they are and how they look. It’s all about taking care of people, and that’s what I like about my profession. Simple as that.”
