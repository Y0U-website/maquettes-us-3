  • What is the difference between fixed and snap-in teeth? Fixed teeth are attached to the implants and are not removed at home, while snap-in dentures are removable and click into place over implant attachments. Both options use implants for support, but they differ in daily care, feel, and how they are maintained over time.
  • How many implants are used for a full arch? The number of implants depends on your bone support, bite forces, and the type of restoration you choose. Many plans use at least four implants, and some cases benefit from additional implants for added stability and support.
  • How long does healing take after All-on-X surgery? Healing varies by patient, but the integration phase often takes several months while the implants fuse with the bone. During this time, you may wear a temporary restoration while the practice monitors healing and plans the final teeth.
  • Am I a candidate if I have bone loss or missing teeth for a long time? Some patients with bone loss may still qualify, but candidacy depends on bone quality, general health, and anatomical factors. Your evaluation and imaging help confirm whether All-on-X is appropriate, or if another option would be safer and more predictable.
