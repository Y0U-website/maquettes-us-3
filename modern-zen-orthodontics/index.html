The practice

We’re proud to offer Damon® braces at Modern Zen Orthodontics. The state-of-the-art system relies on tie-less brackets and very gentle, light forces to achieve exceptional results more quickly and comfortably.

As an airway orthodontist in Germantown, MD, Dr. Robles focuses on airway health and breathing. She provides solutions for sleep apnea and other breathing issues to help patients improve their overall health and quality of life.

We’re a leading Spark™ Clear Aligners provider. The custom aligners are virtually invisible, comfortable, and convenient. Dr. Robles uses advanced imaging technology and Spark’s proprietary software to design each patient’s ideal smile.

Dr. Elizabeth Robles is a second-generation orthodontist with over 25 years of experience and knowledge in treating patients of all ages. After earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, she pursued a 1 year general practice residency in Albany Medical Center and returned to NYU to complete a 2 year post-doctorate orthodontic residency program to become a Certified Specialist in Orthodontics…

At her private practice, Dr. Robles’ primary focus is on the airway and breathing with beautiful, straight teeth and solid function coming close behind. In addition to being an orthodontist, she is also an Access Bars Consciousness Facilitator, 3rd Degree Reiki Practitioner, and Reconnective Healing Practitioner.
