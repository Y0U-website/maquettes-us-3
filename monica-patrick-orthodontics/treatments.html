Treatments

Treatments Treatments All Ages Phase One Early orthodontic intervention, known as Phase One treatment, focuses on addressing developmental issues in children before they become more severe. This proactive approach helps guide jaw growth and create space for permanent teeth, setting the foundation for a healthy smile.

Orthodontic care for teens offers various options, from traditional braces to clear aligners, designed to suit their active lifestyles. We work closely with teens to ensure their treatment is efficient, comfortable, and fits seamlessly into their day-to-day lives.

It’s never too late to achieve a beautiful smile, and our adult orthodontic treatments are tailored to fit your needs and preferences. From subtle options like clear aligners to modern braces, we offer solutions that prioritize both aesthetics and functionality.

Braces

Traditional metal braces are a time-tested, reliable option for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues. With durable metal brackets and wires, they offer precise control and effective results for a wide range of orthodontic issues.

Ceramic braces provide the same effectiveness as traditional braces but with clear or tooth-colored brackets for a more discreet appearance. They are a great choice for patients seeking a less noticeable treatment while still achieving excellent results.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are an alternative to traditional braces, designed to gradually shift your teeth into place. Made from smooth, comfortable plastic, they are removable, allowing you to eat, drink, and maintain your oral hygiene with ease.

This treatment option is ideal for patients seeking a more discreet and flexible approach to orthodontic care. Clear aligners are custom-made for each patient, ensuring a personalized fit that delivers effective and predictable results.

Retainers

Retainers are essential in maintaining your beautiful smile after orthodontic treatment, as they help keep your teeth in their new, aligned position. Wearing your retainer as instructed ensures that the results of your treatment are long-lasting and effective.

There are several types of retainers, including removable and fixed options, each designed to fit your specific needs. During your final consultation, we will guide you on the best retainer choice and how to care for it properly.

Technology

Our intraoral scanner provides precise digital impressions of your teeth without the need for traditional molds, making the process faster and more comfortable. This advanced technology enhances treatment planning and allows for more accurate results in orthodontic care.

Our CBCT offers a more advanced imaging solution compared to traditional panoramic and cephalometric X-rays. This enhanced imaging capability allows for more accurate diagnosis, treatment planning, and improved outcomes in orthodontic care.

Digital photography allows us to document your orthodontic progress with clear, high-resolution images throughout your treatment journey. These photos help us track changes and ensure your treatment stays on the right path.
