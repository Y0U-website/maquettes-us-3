Root Canal Treatment

If a cavity gets deep enough, it will compromise the nerve of your tooth. This results in infection that travels down/up through the inside of the tooth roots into your jaw bone, often causing pain and swelling. Root canal treatment cleans out this infection in your jaw and tooth. Watch the video to learn about the whys and hows of root canals.
