Orthodontics

Traditional Braces

Achieve a beautifully aligned smile with our traditional braces. At our practice, we offer high-quality metal braces that effectively correct misaligned teeth, bite issues, and gaps. Traditional braces are a reliable and time-tested orthodontic solution for patients of all ages. Our experienced team will guide you through every step of the process, providing personalized care and adjustments to ensure optimal results. With our traditional braces, you can look forward to a straighter, healthier smile that boosts your confidence and enhances your oral health.

Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably with our clear aligners. At our practice, we offer advanced clear aligner treatments to help you achieve a beautifully aligned smile without the hassle of traditional braces. These virtually invisible aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth and gently shift them into place over time. Ideal for both teens and adults, clear aligners are removable, making it easy to maintain your oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods. Our skilled team will create a personalized treatment plan to ensure effective and efficient results.

Early Intervention Orthodontics

Our early intervention approach provides safe and effective early treatments to guide jaw growth, correct bite issues, and create space for incoming permanent teeth to ensure a healthy and aligned smile. Using advanced orthodontic methods, this “phase 1” step in the orthodontic journey allows us to achieve timely, noticeable improvements with different types of functional appliances. Each treatment plan is customized to meet your child’s unique needs, ensuring personalized care. By correcting issues early, it sets your child up for success, which can minimize the time in braces in the future.
