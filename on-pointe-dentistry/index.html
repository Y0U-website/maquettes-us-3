For the past 30+ years our dental practice has been located at Donaldson’s Crossroads Shopping Center. We are happy to say many of our patients who started with us 30 years ago are still patients today. We now have many 2nd and 3rd generation patients. We are extremely pleased that our practice continues to grow and attract new patients from many surrounding communities.

It is with great pride that we can announce our relocation to 1900 Main Street at Southpointe Town Center. We are extremely excited to be associated with such an outstanding, progressive, and inviting environment. We already feel at home after meeting so many great people, and cannot wait to meet more of you!

Our patients are extremely excited about our relocation and are currently scheduling their next appointments in the beautiful new office. Also, due to increased demand we are expanding our days and hours to accommodate our patients. We will be open Monday through Friday. Call our experienced staff today to schedule your appointment.

We look forward to showing off our beautiful, state-of-the-art office. Feel free to stop by for a tour, we would love to meet you!

Dr. John McShane received his dental degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. Upon graduation, he opened a dental practice in Donaldson’s Crossroads Shopping Center, Peters Township. After treating families—some for two or three generations—in McMurray and the surrounding communities, he recently relocated his practice to Southpointe Town Center.

Although Dr. McShane receives much praise for his new office from patients, colleagues and many visitors, he is most proud of his staff!
