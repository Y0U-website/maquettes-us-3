Dental implants are the most advanced way to replace missing teeth. They are small titanium posts that act like natural tooth roots, providing a strong foundation for your new teeth. Your Journey to a New Smile: The Implant Process

1. Consultation & Planning: We design your custom treatment plan. 2. Implant Placement: Gentle procedure to place the titanium post. 3. Healing (Osseointegration): Bone fuses with the implant. 4. Attaching Your New Tooth: Your beautiful, custom crown is placed.

We ensure your comfort every step of the way with local anesthesia and mild sedation options .
