What Are Same-Day Dental Implants?

Same-day dental implants are a modern full-mouth restoration solution that allows patients to receive dental implants and a fixed temporary prosthesis on the same day as surgery. Instead of waiting months without teeth, patients leave with a functional smile immediately after implant placement.

Our goal is to provide a permanent-looking solution that restores confidence, comfort, and quality of life.

  • Multiple missing teeth
  • Failing bridges or crowns
  • Advanced gum disease
  • Severe tooth decay
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Full-mouth reconstruction

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do same-day dental implants take?

Most same-day implant procedures are completed in a single surgical visit. Patients often leave the office with temporary fixed teeth the same day.

Are same-day dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during treatment. Local anesthesia and sedation options help make the procedure comfortable.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and routine dental care, implants can last many years and are considered one of the most durable tooth replacement options available.

Can implants replace dentures?

Yes. Full-mouth implants are commonly used to replace loose or uncomfortable dentures with a fixed, more stable solution.

Will my new teeth look natural?

Yes. Every smile is customized to match your facial features, bite, and aesthetic preferences for a natural-looking appearance.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental cleanings are essential for maintaining healthy implants and gums.
