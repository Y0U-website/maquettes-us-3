How you can help Hands of Hope

Give back to your community

We rely on the time and knowledge of licensed professionals such as dentists, doctors, physician assistants, nurse practitioners, nurses, counselors and social workers, as well as medical and dental assistants. Volunteering with a nonprofit like ours is a great way to serve your community, and feel fulfilled as you make a difference in the lives of others.

How do I donate?

Make a tax deductible financial contribution through our donation link on this website, or checks made payable to Hands of Hope Clinic and mailed to:

Hands of Hope Clinic 1010 Hospital Drive, Building B, Suite 1010 Stockbridge, GA 30281
