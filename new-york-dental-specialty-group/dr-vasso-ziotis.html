Dr. Vasso Ziotis

Dr. Vasso Ziotis is a specialist in dentofacial orthopedics and orthodontics.

Dr. Ziotis is from New York, born and raised. She got her Bs. in Biology from SUNY at Birmingham, New York. She received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from SUNY at Brook School of Dental, finished top of her class, and was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon (the National Dental Honor Society). Dr. Vasso finished her orthodontic training at the Montefiore Medical Center in NYC.

She treats young kids (starting at age 7), teenagers, and adults alike with a gentle touch and a great sense of humor, immediately putting every patient at ease. Passionate about orthodontics, her primary goal is to provide each patient with tailored, individualized treatment to achieve a beautiful, natural smile and an ideal bite. She always takes the time to give each patient the care and attention he or she needs.

She has completed further training at Long Island College Hospital, Brooklyn, NY; Lukes-Roosevelt Hospital Center, NY; Jamaica Hospital, Queens, NY; Nassau County Hospital, Levitown, NY; Oral Surgery-Dental Internship.

She is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, and the New York County Dental Society.

Dr. Vasso resides in New York with her husband and children. She enjoys visiting family and friends in New York City, working out, or trying new restaurants.
