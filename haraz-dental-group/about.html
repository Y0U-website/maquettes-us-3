Meet the Dentists Behind Your Healthy Smile

Dr. Muhammad Nadeem

Dr. Muhammad Nadeem brings over two decades of hands-on surgical experience to every patient he treats. A graduate of de Montmorency College of Dentistry in Lahore, Pakistan, Dr. Nadeem launched his oral surgery practice in 2005, spending a decade mastering complex surgical cases before relocating to the United States in 2015 and establishing Haraz Dental Group in Fresno, CA.

Dr. Amandeep Kaur Randhawa

Dr. Amandeep Kaur Randhawa is a highly accomplished dental professional whose journey in dentistry spans over two decades. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Baba Farid University of Health Sciences in India in 2003, the foundation of a career built on clinical excellence, continuous learning, and genuine patient care.

With 20+ years of experience, Dr. Randhawa specializes in general dentistry, restorative treatments, root canal therapy, dental crowns, and gum disease management. She is widely recognized for her ability to make even the most anxious patients feel completely at ease, ensuring every individual fully understands their treatment plan before any procedure begins.

Her warm chairside manner, meticulous attention to detail, and preventive-first philosophy make her a trusted choice for patients of all ages.
