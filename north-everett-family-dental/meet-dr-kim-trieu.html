Meet Dr. Kim Trieu

My name is Dr. Kim Trieu, and I am passionate about providing comprehensive, ethical, and compassionate dental care. As an Affiliate Faculty member at the University of Washington School of Dentistry, I have the privilege of teaching future dentists. You will see that same passion for education here at North Everett Family Dental, where I take time to explain your oral health, answer your questions, and empower you to make confident decisions about your oral health care.

My philosophy in dentistry is that every patient deserves to feel comfortable, heard, and genuinely cared for. With advanced technology from digital sensors and charts; intraoral scanners, and laser treatment. I am proud to offer CEREC same-day dentistry, allowing many crowns and restorations to be completed in a single visit.

When I am not practicing or teaching dentistry, I spend time with my husband and five children (Peter, Andrew, Christopher, Faustina, and Clare). My husband grew up on Hoyt Avenue and attended Whittier Elementary.

Fun Fact: Our office has served the Everett community from this location since the 1950’s. I am honored to continue the legacy as the 5th dentist to practice in this office!
