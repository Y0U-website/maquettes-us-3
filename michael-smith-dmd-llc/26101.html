What’s Involved with Getting a Dental Implant?

Our dentists will first determine if you are a good candidate for an implant by taking digital x-rays. They will check the x-ray to see if you have sufficient strong, healthy bone in your jaw to hold the implant in place.

We place most dental implants here at our comfortable Belleville dental office, and before we begin, we will make sure your mouth is numb, so you don’t feel any pain. Then our implant dentist, Dr. Michael Smith, places the post in your jaw in a procedure that takes about an hour.

Most patients tell us that the procedure was more comfortable than they thought it would be, but we do offer nitrous oxide if you would like something to take the edge off of your anxiety.

There is a healing period for your implant where your own healthy bone grows around the implant. This process is called osseointegration and can take anywhere from 3-6 months.

When the implant has integrated fully with your jawbone, we will add the abutment and the restoration to it. The result is a beautiful new tooth that looks, acts, and feels like your own tooth.
