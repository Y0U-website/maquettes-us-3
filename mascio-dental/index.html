The practice

Mascio Dental was started as Mascio Family Dentistry in 1962 by Dr. George Mascio of Weirton. He practiced at this office for almost 60 years before retiring in 2020. His son, Dr. Christopher Mascio, and his daughter in law, Dr. Dana Mascio, joined the practice in 1993 and have been operating and providing the high quality care and friendly service established by Dr. George ever since.
