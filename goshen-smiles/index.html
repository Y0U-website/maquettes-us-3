An occlusal guard, also known as a night guard or bite guard, is a custom-made oral appliance worn while you sleep. It prevents damage caused by clenching or grinding (bruxism) and helps reduce jaw tension and related discomfort.

Bruxism treatment is often most effective when combined with a custom night guard, helping preserve the integrity of your teeth and prevent long-term TMJ issues.
