Dr. Gustin and Dr. Andrews are ready to get to the root of your tooth pain. Read below to meet the team ready to assist you.

Wylie Gustin

Dr. Gustin was born in Savannah and grew up on Tybee Island, where he spent much of his youth fishing and exploring the local waterways. He attended Armstrong Atlantic University and later transferred to the University of Georgia where he graduated Cum Laude with a degree in Biology. After college, he attended dental school at the Medical College of Georgia (now the Dental College of Georgia). Immediately upon completion of his general dental training, he entered periodontics residency at the Medical College of Georgia.

When his schedule allows, Dr. Gustin enjoys traveling, boating, fishing, bird hunting, and cheering on the Georgia Bulldogs. He is happy to call Savannah home and hopes to never be too far from the water.

Clyde Andrews

A Jacksonville, Florida native, Dr. Andrews obtained his DDS from Emory University School of Dentistry. After practicing general dentistry for 4 years in Atlanta, he was offered the opportunity to attend endodontic residency at the Medical College of Virginia. Immediately following the completion of his residency, Dr. Andrews opened Southeastern Endodontics in Savannah. In 2022, he joined Dr. Wylie Gustin at Live Oak Endodontics.

Dr. Andrews continues to look forward to many years of continued endodontic excellence at Live Oak Endodontics.
