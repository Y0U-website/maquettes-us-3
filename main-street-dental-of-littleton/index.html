The practice

If you re in need of a dentist here in Littleton that provides a comprehensive variety of treatment options for patients of all ages , look no further than Main Street Dental. With over 20 years of experience, Dr. Benenati has the advanced skills and dedication needed to create true smile transformations, and our entire team works hard to help patients have fulfilling and comfortable care…

We are proud to be a Northeast Delta Dental Premier Provider. We will also submit claims to most major dental insurance companies on your behalf. Care Credit is also available as a financing option for treatments and procedures not covered by insurance or for patients who do not have dental insurance.

Our success is helping you achieve your oral health goals. Dr. Mike is happy to talk with you one on one about your dental needs with no obligation. Whether you have a question, need a second opinion or would like to schedule an appointment, don t hesitate to give us a call.

Our practice is conveniently located on historic Main Street in Littleton, NH. We have ample parking directly behind our building with a wheelchair accessible ramp. We take great care in ensuring our parking lot and walkways are free of snow and ice for our patients.

Michael Benenati, DMD, is a general dentist who received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Connecticut. He went on to complete a residency in general practice at Hartford Hospital in Hartford, CT. In addition, he holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from SUNY Binghamton. Dr. Benenati has been in private practice for the past 20 years.

Dr. Benenati continues to be active in academics and enjoys staying up to date on prevalent topics in his profession.

Prior to dental school, Dr. Mike hiked the entire length of the Appalachian Trail from Georgia to Maine in what is commonly referred to as a "thru-hike". He is an outdoor enthusiast who enjoys hiking, running, cycling and occasionally tinkering with engines.

Dr. Mike enjoys living in the Littleton area with his wife and three children. When not in the office, you will most likely find him hiking the North Country.
