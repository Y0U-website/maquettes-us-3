Getting Started with Dental Implants

Getting a dental implant is a process that requires several steps and months to complete.

First, Dr. Bagley will evaluate your oral health using state-of-the-art technology to determine if you re a good implant candidate. He then collaborates with top specialists in our area who place your implants and ensure you are in the best hands every step of the way.

Over the next 3 6 months, the bone and tissue in your jaw fuse with the biocompatible implant in a process called osseointegration .

Once healed, Dr. Bagley places a dental crown, bridge, or denture on the implant. We use only the highest quality materials for your replacement tooth, so you can be confident it will look realistic and blend perfectly with your smile.

How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies based on your specific needs and treatment plan. Dental implants are an investment in your long-term oral health, and we are committed to helping you find a financial solution that works for your budget.

Factors That Impact Dental Implant Costs

Several factors influence the total cost of dental implant treatment:

Dr. Bagley provides detailed treatment plans and transparent cost information during your consultation, allowing you to make informed decisions about your care.

  • Number of implants needed Replacing a single tooth costs less than multiple teeth or full-arch restorations
  • Preparatory procedures Some patients require bone grafting or sinus lifts before implant placement
  • Type of restoration A single crown, multi-tooth bridge, or implant-supported denture each has a different cost
  • Materials used High-quality, biocompatible materials ensure the best results but affect overall pricing
  • Complexity of your case Each patient s oral health situation is unique, affecting treatment requirements
