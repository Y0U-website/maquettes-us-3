Dr. Emma Palmer, DMD

About

Dr. Palmer was born and raised in Richmond, KY, where her father is also a dentist, inspiring her love for the dental field from an early age. After college, she worked as an orthodontic assistant, where she discovered her passion for orthodontics. Her favorite part of the job is seeing patients’ smiles light up when they look in the mirror after completing treatment! Outside the office, Dr. Palmer enjoys exploring new coffee shops and restaurants, traveling, and spending time with her two kittens. She’s passionate about fostering cats whenever she can!
