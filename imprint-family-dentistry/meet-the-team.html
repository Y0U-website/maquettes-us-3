About Our Arcadia Team

Lupe B. Lupe is a seasoned RDA and has been in the dental field since 2011. She and her husband currently reside in nearby La Puente with their adorable son Henry. Lupe makes all our patients feel welcome, loves to swap stories, and is empathetic to the needs of others. Her special ability is reading Dr. Lee’s mind, often predicting his next steps during dental procedures. In her free time, she loves hiking the outdoors and of course spending time with her precious family.

Chelsea J. Chelsea graduated from Loma Linda School of Dentistry in 2012 (BS, RDH). She is a Napa Valley SoCal transplant and now resides in nearby Covina. She is loved by many for her amiable personality, delicate touch, and compassionate care as a hygienist. She has many creative past-times and is multi-talented, often hosting floral; succulent workshops in nearby venues in the San Gabriel Valley and even designing her own jewelry!

Kolleen P. Kolleen is an experienced and detail-oriented RDA, graduating from Citrus College in 2001. Extremely outgoing and bubbly, Kolleen makes our patients feel instantly welcome and is always respectful to their needs & concerns. She loves serving the community in the field of dental care and prides herself in maintaining a safe, organized, and hygienic environment for all our patients. Although an Arcadia-native (and actually once neighbors with Dr. Lee back in the day!), she and her husband now happily live in Upland with their beautiful son Aidan and two doggos.
