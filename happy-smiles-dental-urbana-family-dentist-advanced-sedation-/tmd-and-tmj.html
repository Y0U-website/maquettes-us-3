  • Facial pain or swelling

How TMD and TMJ Affect Daily Life

Living with TMD can be challenging. The constant discomfort can affect your ability to eat, speak, and engage in social interactions. Sleep disturbances often accompany jaw pain, leading to fatigue and decreased quality of life. Individuals may find themselves avoiding certain foods or activities due to the fear of triggering pain.

How is TMD Treated?

Goals of TMD Therapy:

Pain Relief: Reduce the pressure on the TMJ, alleviating pain and discomfort associated with TMD.

Muscle Relaxation: Promotes relaxation of the jaw muscles, which may help decrease tension and reduce the frequency of jaw clenching or grinding (bruxism).

Improved Sleep Quality: Minimizing jaw pain and discomfort during the night allows you to wake up feeling refreshed.

Protective Barrier: Protects your teeth from wear caused by grinding or clenching, preventing further dental issues down the line.
