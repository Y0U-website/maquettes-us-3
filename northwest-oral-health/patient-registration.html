Patient Registration

Click on the Register here button below for the location at which you wish to be seen. If you are already registered for one location, you do not need to re-register for the other. We look forward to serving you soon!

Please have your current DENTAL insurance plan information ready including: insurance company name, employer name (if through your work), subscriber name and date of birth (and address if different than your own), your relationship to subscriber, member ID, and group number.

Bring a copy of your dental insurance card to your first appointment, or whenever you get a new dental plan.

Our offices are contracted with DeltaCare of WA!

Make sure you are assigned to Dr. Brenden Davis at one of our office locations before making an appointment. You will not be covered if you are not assigned on the date of your visit.

NOTE: Most Medicare health plans do not have dental coverage, unless you have purchased an additional supplement that has dental benefits (e.g., plan C or Medicare Advantage).
