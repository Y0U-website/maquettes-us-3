To guarantee a successful first visit, it is important to properly prepare your child for their visit. Be careful in the words you choose when telling your child about their first dental visit. Avoid using negative words, such as "hurt", "needles" or "shot". These words may induce unnecessary dental anxieties. Instead, speak in general terms and stay positive.
