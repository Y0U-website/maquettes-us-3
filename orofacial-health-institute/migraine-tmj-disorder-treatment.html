Treatment Options

For TMJ Disorder

At the Orofacial Health Institute, we offer a range of treatment options to relieve TMJ pain and restore oral function. After an initial evaluation, your doctor will divide your treatment plan into two phases, each focusing on a different aspect of TMJ rehabilitation.

Comprehensive Evaluation

We will begin by conducting a thorough evaluation of your current condition. Your doctor will try to determine the underlying cause of your TMJ dysfunction so they can develop a treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

The evaluation process includes a physical evaluation and 3D imaging with a CT scan and MRI. Additionally, we use neuromuscular evaluation using stimulation to assess muscle function and pinpoint areas of concern.

Phase I: TMJ Stabilization and Healing

The goal of Phase I treatment is to stabilize your TMJ to reduce painful symptoms. Our primary aim is to provide immediate relief.

During this phase, we’ll work to find the most ideal jaw position for each individual; there is no one-size-fits-all solution. Phase I typically lasts about nine months.

Phase II: Occlusal Rehabilitation

Phase II treatment focuses on adjusting your bite to maintain relief achieved during Phase I. The goal is to ensure long-term stability and function by addressing any remaining misalignment that could cause TMJ issues to reoccur. The treatments used in Phase II will depend largely on the outcomes of your Phase I treatment. There is no one single approach, and the specific needs of each patient determine their treatment pathway.

Treatments during Phase I and II may include:

  • Neuromuscular dentistry to restore the proper function of the jaw muscles and joints
  • Splint and orthotic therapy to help realign the jaw and reduce the stress on your TMJ
  • Orthodontics and orthopedics to correct structural misalignments that may be contributing to your condition
  • Collaboration with chiropractors, physical therapists, myofunctional therapists, and massage therapists as needed

Can TMJ disorder affect my overall health and well-being?

Yes, it can. TMJ disorder can cause a range of issues beyond just jaw pain. You might suffer from headaches, neck pain, poor sleep, and even systemic health problems. Treating TMJ disorder can positively impact your overall health.

Yes, at the Orofacial Health Institute, we can treat TMJ disorder in both children and adults. Early intervention in children can help in the prevention of more serious issues later in your child’s life.

A variety of treatment options are available, including neuromuscular dentistry, splint therapy, and orthodontics. We also collaborate with other specialists, such as chiropractors and myofunctional therapists, if the need arises.
