Dr Carmen Barrientos

Born in South America and raised in New Jersey, Dr. Barrientos brings a distinguished military career and extensive clinical experience to our practice. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey and served in the United States Navy Dental Corps for over 25 years, both on active duty and in the Active Drilling Reserves.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Barrientos enjoys traveling the world with her two sons, exploring new cultures, meeting new people, and embracing outdoor adventures such as hiking, sailing, and skiing.
