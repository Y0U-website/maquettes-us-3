About O’Dental

Formerly Port Lavaca Dentistry, O’Dental’s Port Lavaca Dental Office is now under the care of Dr. Bryce O’Donnell and his wife, Reagan. While we’ve got a fresh new look, the team you know and love is still here, ready to provide the same exceptional care in the same convenient location. Expect plenty of smiles, laughs, and top-notch service—because at O’Dental, your smile is our favorite thing to work on!

Our Port Lavaca Dental Office Team

Dr. Bryce

Born and raised right here in Port Lavaca, TX, Dr. Bryce O’Donnell has deep roots in the community. A 2015 graduate of Calhoun High School and a 2018 graduate of Texas State University, he went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas School of Dentistry at Houston in 2022.

Dr. Bryce always knew he wanted to give back to the community that shaped him. He grew up as a patient in this very office, shadowed here in high school, returned as a dentist after graduation, and now proudly serves as the owner of O’Dental .

Outside the office, Dr. Bryce enjoys spending time with his wife and their beautiful baby girl. He’s also passionate about cooking, fishing, hunting, and making the most of every moment with family and friends.

Corrina

Corrina is passionate about organization, communication, and building strong patient relationships. She keeps everyone in the office in line and makes sure the patients have a seamless experience.

Outside the office, you’ll find her spending time with her husband, chasing after their 4 kids, and cheering on their 2 oldest from the sidelines—whether it’s Friday night football or Saturday cheer events. Life is full, busy, but rewarding.

Keri

With 28 years of dedicated experience in dental hygiene, Keri is known for building great rapport with patients, creating a comfortable and trusting environment that encourages open communication and effective oral care. She takes pride in fostering positive relationships with patients of all ages, making dental visits less stressful and more engaging.

Outside of work, Keri likes to spend time with her husband Mike and her two kids Korbin and Kerigan.

Landra

Landra has been RDH for 28 yrs, with 22 years here in this office. She strives to give her patients the best cleaning experience possible and educate them as well about their dental health. She enjoys interacting with patients and trying to keep their anxiety about dental treatment to a minimum.

Outside of the office Landra enjoys spending time with her family, working in the yard and traveling.

Giovana

Giovana is a skilled dental assistant with a heart for patient care and a love for adventure. Outside the clinic, you can find her trying new restaurants or spending quality time with her beloved pets. She believes in creating joy—whether through healthy smiles or unforgettable moments.

Sandra

Sandra served her patients at Port Lavaca Dentistry for over 10 years. While we miss the joy of having her with us every day at O’Dental, her legacy lives on in the way we care for our patients. If you get lucky, you may even be able to get Sandra’s chocolate donut.

Reagan

With a background in the business side of dentistry, Reagan brings her expertise to O’Dental to help create a seamless and memorable experience for every patient. Reagan loves making memories with Dr. Bryce and their daughter—and looks forward to teaching their little one the ins and outs of running a business one day!
