The practice

At Montague Hills Dentistry, Dr. Oscar Celis and Dr. Alejandro Celis pair advance technology with a gentle, personal touch — creating healthy, confident smiles that last a lifetime. En español, también.

Every recommendation is one we would make for our own family. If something can wait, we will tell you. If it can’t, we’ll explain why in plain language and show you exactly what we see on the screen.

From your first visit forward, Dr. Oscar and Dr. Alejandro look after your care personally. You’ll see familiar faces at every appointment, and your history stays with us — no starting over, no being passed around.

Ergonomic chairs, soft lighting, and a calm pace create a comfortable environment where you can enjoy a movie, documentary, or music while you relax. We schedule appointments thoughtfully so no one ever feels rushed—your time in the chair should feel as carefully considered as the work itself.

Regular checkups and cleanings are the foundation of a healthy smile. These preventive visits protect your teeth, gums, and jaw — catching small issues early, before they turn into bigger ones.

Les soins

05 / Service Same-Day Ceramic Crowns Our intraoral scanner captures your tooth digitally — no goopy impressions, no gagging. The result is a computer-designed crown, veneer, or implant crown that fits precisely and looks completely natural.
