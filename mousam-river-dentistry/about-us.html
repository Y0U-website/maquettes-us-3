Meet the Team at Mousam River Dentistry

Welcome to Mousam River Dentistry! We are the former dental practice of Dr. Ross Wyman, now led by Dr. James Cormier. Those patients who know Dr. Wyman understand the long-standing tradition of exceptional dentistry and outstanding service that he provided to the Kennebunk community for over 60 years! Dr. Cormier brings those same values to Mousam River Dentistry and a commitment to providing our patients with the best that dentistry has to offer.

Even though our name has changed, you will still see many of the familiar faces you have come to know and trust over the years. If you re new to our dental practice, take a minute to learn more about Dr. Cormier and our dedicated team members so you ll feel right at home when you arrive. We look forward to meeting you!

Dr. Jim Cormier

A native of Maine, Dr. Cormier is thrilled to be providing the highest level of care for patients in Kennebunk and neighboring communities. At a young age, he knew he wanted to be a dentist. He loved working with his hands, was passionate about studying medicine, and thrived when helping people.

Dr. Cormier attended the University of New England (UNE), where he received his Bachelor of Science in Medical Biology. Upon graduating, he became quite the accomplished researcher working for UNE and a startup biotech company, focusing on pain management and drug discovery. Dr. Cormier published several articles containing essential research findings on chronic pain and ways to manage it. He has found his research background has served him well in helping patients to manage their own pain by providing them with the latest advances in medicine.

After working several years in the research field, Dr. Cormier enrolled in UNE’s Dental school and graduated with his Doctorate of Dental Medicine. During his time at UNE, he did a rotation at Goodwin Community Health, treating low-income patients. Dr. Cormier is humbled to have been the recipient of several scholarships for his clinical skills, public service, and academic performance.

With his background in pain management, Dr. Cormier takes proper steps in managing the comfort of each of his patients throughout their care at Mousam River Dentistry. Dr. Cormier emphasizes preventative care and his patients take an active role in developing a comprehensive, personalized approach to promote overall health. Dr. Cormier is always accepting new patients and we do our best to see same day emergencies. We look forward to welcoming your family to our dental home.

Education 2009 Bachelor of Science Major: Medical Biology College of Arts and Science University of New England Biddeford, Maine 2019 Doctorate of Dental Medicine College of Dental Medicine University of New England Portland, Maine

Professional Affiliations American Dental Association American Dental Education Association Maine Dental Association York County Dental Society

Dr. Cormier’s patients are his top priority, but when he’s not at the office, you can find him enjoying all that Maine has to offer in the great outdoors. Dr. Cormier is married to his wife Krissy where they reside in Saco with their daughters, Hannah and Amelia, and their black lab, Denali. Together, they enjoy hiking, skiing, and playing pickleball.

Dr. Will Fenn

A native of the small town of Stetson, Maine, Dr. Fenn s journey into dentistry was fueled by his innate curiosity for science, his aptitude for problem-solving, and his appreciation for detailed, hands-on work. These attributes led him into a fulfilling dental career dedicated to helping his patients achieve healthy, confident smiles and an exceptional standard of oral health.

His formal education began at the University of Maine at Orono, where he earned his degree in Biology. Following this, he was part of a pioneering group: the inaugural graduating class at the highly respected University of New England s College of Dental Medicine. This foundational training equipped him with the modern skills and evidence-based knowledge necessary to excel in general dentistry.

Dr. Fenn understands that a visit to the dental chair isn t everyone s favorite place. His primary goal for every single patient is to make their appointment as comfortable and stress-free as possible. He believes in a truly patient-centered approach, which means taking the time to listen intently, explaining every procedure clearly, and answering all questions with the same care and detail he would use for a family member. To Dr. Fenn, the ability to alleviate anxiety and help people every day is what makes his chosen profession so deeply special and rewarding.

Dr. Fenn spent five years practicing in New Hampshire, providing care for patients with a wide range of dental needs. Two years ago, he returned to his home state of Maine, where he has been offering general dentistry services in southern Maine. Today, he is excited to be part of Mousam River Dentistry, where he utilizes the latest dental technology to help patients achieve their healthiest, most confident smiles all delivered with a commitment to high-quality care and genuine compassion.

2010 Bachelor of Science Major: Biology from University of Maine Orono, Maine

2017 Doctorate of Dental Medicine College of Dental Medicine University of New England Portland, Maine

Professional Affiliations American Dental Association

When he’s not in the office, you can often find Dr. Fenn embracing the vast Maine outdoors. He is an avid backpacker and fly fisherman and enjoys adventuring with his wife, Brittany, and their two dogs, Pepper and Hazel.

Dr. Noah Frost

Dr. Frost attended Brigham Young University (BYU) in Provo, Utah, where he received his Bachelor of Science in Geography. While at BYU he played on the school’s club hockey team. Upon graduating, he worked in a dental lab as he applied to dental school. During that time, he developed an understanding of how to provide quality dental care to patients.

After graduating from BYU, Dr. Frost returned home to attend dental school at The University of New England (UNE) and graduated with his Doctorate of Dental Medicine. During his time at UNE, he completed an externship at Jessie Albert Dental and Orthodontic Center, serving low-income patients. Dr. Frost received the 2025 Community-Based Education Award for his service and commitment to the Community-Based Education system at UNE.

Dr. Frost strives to provide a positive experience and quality care for his patients. He wants everyone to feel comfortable going to the dentist and strives to provide the optimal patient experience.

Dr. Michael Truscott

Dr. Truscott is a native son of Kennebunk, the only village in the world so named . He attended Kennebunk High School, the University of Maine at Orono, and Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Truscott enjoyed the first stage of his career on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, operating his private practice while raising a family. When the last of his three children fled the nest, he and his wife, Margo, also a native of Kennebunk, moved back to their home state. The second leg of Dr. Truscott’s career was spent teaching clinical dentistry at the University of New England College of Dental Medicine where he met Dr. Cormier. He made a lasting impression upon Dr. Truscott because of his commitment to patient care, his clinical skills, and unquestioned integrity.

When the opportunity presented itself, Dr. Truscott welcomed the chance to join Mousam River Dentistry. Dr. Cormier’s practice was the only one that he would have considered for the third leg of his career.

1982 Doctorate of Dental Medicine, Tufts University
