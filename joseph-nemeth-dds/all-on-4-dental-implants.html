Introducing the All-on-X

The All-on-X is a cost-effective dental implant technique in which the upper or lower arch is replaced with four to six implants. The “X” stands for the number of implants used in the procedure, which will vary from patient to patient. These implants support a fixed new set of teeth that hold the same function, permanence, and appearance as natural teeth. A brand new smile and a more stable bite are attainable in just one visit!

On a future visit, once the implants have fully fused with the bone, a more permanent set of teeth are attached. At this point, the teeth are fixed in place and the patient is ready to experience an improved quality of life.
