The practice

We provide complete care for the whole family, including cleanings, exams, radiographs, cancer screenings, fillings, crowns, whitening, night guards, gum treatment, implants, dentures and more.

Our dental practice uses the latest dental technology to insure the best dental experience. Digital x-rays allow us to quickly take and view images. Text message appointment reminders help you keep track of upcoming appointments.

While our friendly staff gives you that small office feel, with several dentists, we can often take care of your emergency the day you call. Also, multiple hygienists mean an entire family can have their cleaning appointments at the same time.
