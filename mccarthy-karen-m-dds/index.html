The practice

Dr. McCarthy is dedicated to helping patients achieve their best smiles possible. Shasta Orthodontics offers no-interest financing options with low monthly payments, making orthodontic care affordable for patients and their families.

Whether you’re looking for traditional metal braces for your child or are looking for a more discreet option like clear ceramic braces as an adult, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan that fits your goals and lifestyle. Explore our orthodontic treatment options below!

Dr. Karen McCarthy received her dental degree from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) School of Dentistry in 1990, where she graduated first in her class.

The University of North Carolina is considered by many to be one of the best orthodontic programs in the world. Dr. McCarthy received her masters degree from the University of North Carolina School of Dentistry, Department of Orthodontics in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, in 1993.

In 1993, Dr. McCarthy began her orthodontic career, working in Salt Lake City, Utah. In 1998, she moved to beautiful northern California and opened her offices in Chico and Redding.

Dr. McCarthy is currently a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, the American Association of Orthodontists, and the Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists. In 2003, she became the first female president of the Northern California Dental Society.

Dr. McCarthy is pleased to be offering her high quality orthodontic treatment to people in the North Valley. Her patients appreciate and enjoy her high standards of orthodontic excellence.
