The practice

Cone-beam imaging and digital scans used before surgical decisions.

We focus on adult restorative, implant, cosmetic, and surgical work.

Most patients come to us for one of these. If you are unsure where you fit, request a consultation and we will guide you.

Dental Implants Single missing tooth or several teeth in a row - replaced with titanium implants planned in 3D, restored with a crown matched to the rest of your smile. Treatment is staged so each step is predictable.

02 All-on-X / Full-Arch Restoration A fixed set of teeth on as few as four implants - for adults tired of removable dentures or facing extensive tooth loss. We start with a consultation and a written plan; we do not promise a same-day result without examining you first.

04 Cosmetic Dentistry Veneers, bonding, and full smile makeovers planned around your face, your bite, and your daily life. We review the design and written estimate before we start work.

Our process A consultation, not a sales meeting. Before we plan an implant, full-arch, or cosmetic case, we examine you, take only the imaging your case needs, and write down the plan. You leave with a clear next step and a written estimate.

We talk first. What you noticed, what bothers you, what you have already tried, your timeline. No diagnosis from a form — we examine you in person.

Les soins

06 Adult General Dentistry Routine cleanings, exams, and fillings for adult patients. Our team confirms the right appointment time by phone.

05 Crowns and Restorative Crowns, bridges, and onlays to restore broken or worn teeth. Modern materials, careful fit, and a clear written estimate.

Implants are an option for adults who have lost a single tooth to decay, fracture, or extraction, and want a fixed replacement that does not depend on neighboring teeth (as a bridge would) or rely on removable dentures.
