The practice

With advanced technology and the highest degree of service, Orthodontics on the Line offers state-of-the-art treatment to help you get the smile you deserve. Dr. Jaz is an expert in alignment, smile esthetics, and achieving a healthy bite.

We provide personalized care to determine which treatment option is right for you.

Dr. Jaz is a Board-Certified Orthodontist who has built Orthodontics on the Line from the ground up to provide the community with a premier orthodontic practice. He strives to have a positive influence on all his patients’ lives by creating beautiful, healthy smiles.

Early diagnosis and treatment can guide erupting teeth into a more favorable position, preserve space for permanent teeth, and reduce the likelihood of fracturing protruded front teeth.

Dr. Jazaierly, or Dr. Jaz, as his patients like to call him, is a Board-Certified Orthodontist who received his orthodontic specialty training from the prestigious New York University in 2013.

Dr. Jaz is originally from Beirut, Lebanon, one of the oldest cities on the Mediterranean Sea. Growing up he traveled the world and spent summers in France. He is fluent in three languages and is happy to converse with kids learning French.

He pursued his dreams of attending NYU for his specialty education. Dr. Jaz is uniquely qualified to treat children of all ages and utilizes the newest technology to do so. Dr. Jaz has been in the field of orthodontics for over a decade and is happy to make Wayne his permanent home.

After completing his last degree, after 15 years of school, Dr. Jaz and his wife settled in Philadelphia. He met his wife at NYU, who is an endodontist. Dr. Jaz maintained his connection with NYU as an adjunct faculty member in the post graduate orthodontic department.
