Thank you for your interest in employment with Hāmākua-Kohala Health. We believe that fostering a culture that recognizes, appreciates, and involves our employees is essential to our ability to deliver exceptional patient-centered care and support. We offer a comprehensive benefits package including medical, dental, vision, and retirement program, as well as a generous paid time-off program.
