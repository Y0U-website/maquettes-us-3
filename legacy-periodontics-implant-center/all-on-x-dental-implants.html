How Do All-on-X Implant Dental Implants Work

The All-on-X is an innovative technique that involves placing four to six implants in the upper or lower jawbone. They act as secure anchors for a custom full-arch prosthesis, replacing all missing teeth. During the procedure, we place the implant posts at certain angles, which helps engage more jawbone support for better stability.

Unlike traditional removable dentures, these replacement teeth are permanently fixed and do not shift or slip during everyday activities. With All-on-X dental implants, our Duncanville specialist can replace your missing teeth and help improve your smile and oral health.

Why are All-on-X Dental Implants a Good Choice

Enjoy the various benefits of full-arch restoration with this treatment, as it offers:

  • Fixed, secure fit: The replacement teeth stay firmly in place without the need for adhesives.
  • Improved chewing: Eat many of your favorite foods again with strong, stable bite support.
  • Clearer Speech: Say goodbye to mumbling or slurred words caused by ill-fitting dentures.
  • Lifelike appearance: Your custom restoration is crafted to look just like natural teeth and gums.
  • Bone loss prevention: Implants stimulate the bone and help avoid the risk of further jawbone deterioration.
  • Faster results: Fewer implants mean shorter surgery time and less frequent dental visits.
