Meet Dr. Arya Bahreini

Meet Dr. Arya Bahreini, a dedicated dentist passionate about providing compassionate care and bringing years of diverse experience to her patients.

The Professional Side

Everyone in Dr. Bahreini’s family will tell you how inspired he was to follow in his mother’s footsteps and become a dentist. Growing up in Edmond, Oklahoma, he saw firsthand how dentistry could transform lives by restoring not only smiles, but also confidence.

Dr. Bahreini, known to his patients as “Dr. B”, began working toward his dream at Oklahoma City University , where he earned his undergraduate degree. He went on to graduate from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2022 and then completed a General Practice Residency at the Oklahoma City VA Medical Center from 2022 to 2023.

The Personal Side

Dr. Bahreini grew up in a household that valued care, family, and community. Outside the office, he enjoys staying active by working out, playing video games with friends, and relaxing at home with his wife and their beloved pets.

While in the operatory, Dr. B is known for his calming presence and thoughtful approach. He takes the time to educate each patient, making sure they feel heard, informed, and at ease. His goal is to help everyone leave the chair feeling healthier, more confident, and truly cared for.
