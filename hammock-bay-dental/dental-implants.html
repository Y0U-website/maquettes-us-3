Dental Implants

  • How painful is a dental implant? The thought of dental implant surgery can be intimidating, but the procedure itself is typically well-tolerated with minimal discomfort. During the surgery, local anesthesia or IV sedation is used to ensure you feel no pain. Some post-operative soreness and swelling are normal, like what you might experience after a tooth extraction.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for tooth replacement, with many lasting a lifetime when properly cared for. The implant, made of titanium, fuses with the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth. The crown placed on top of the implant may need to be replaced after several years due to normal wear, but the implant itself is highly durable. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are key to ensuring the longevity of your dental implants.
  • What are full mouth dental implants? Full mouth dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing one or both arches of your teeth. They involve securing a full set of artificial teeth onto dental implants that are strategically placed in your jawbone. This approach restores your smile, improves chewing function, and prevents bone loss, offering a more stable and long-lasting alternative to traditional dentures.
