How Veneers Are Made at Our Office

Veneers are typically placed across two visits, with a third try-on visit for larger cases: Design — digital photos and impressions; we agree on color, length, and shape together. Preparation — a very thin layer of enamel is reshaped so the veneer sits flush. Temporary veneers — placed the same day; you preview the new shape for a week. Final placement — custom porcelain veneers are bonded permanently. Adjustments are dialed in until the bite, lip line, and color all read as natural.
