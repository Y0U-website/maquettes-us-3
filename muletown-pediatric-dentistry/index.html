The practice

Muletown Pediatric Dentistry provides comprehensive pediatric dental care, including both hygiene and restorative care. We utilize a range of sedation techniques during treatment, including partial and full sedation.

Preventative care is crucial for maintaining your child’s oral health. During their regular exam, we will carefully examine their teeth and gums for any signs of cavities, tooth decay, gingivitis or periodontal disease.

We will also perform a thorough teeth cleaning to ensure their oral hygiene is in top shape. In addition to regular exams, we offer various treatments such as extractions, fluoride treatments, mouthguards and nightguards to further protect their teeth and gums.

Restorative services are available to address tooth damage caused by cavities or chipped teeth. Our fillings use ceramic and plastic compounds to closely resemble the natural appearance of teeth. Additionally, sealants are used to cover up small cracks that are difficult to reach with a toothbrush, helping to prevent tooth decay. We utilize several safe and effective sedation techniques ranging from the application of ‘laughing gas’ to utilizing our anesthesiologist partners to provide general anesthetic sedation.
