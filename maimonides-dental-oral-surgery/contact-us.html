One Call Patient Transfer Center

The Maimonides 1 -Call Patient Transfer Center is available 24 hours, 7 days per week, to connect physicians and physician offices with our team. Registered nurses (RNs) connect you with the right specialists and can arrange transfer services from a doctor’s office to our hospital, and from hospital to hospital. This dedicated hotline supports all medical specialties.

Also the Contact Center collaborates seamlessly with the ER, facilitating communication between patients’ families, external physicians, and internal staff. Contact us at 718-283-7444 or extension 7222.

Patient Concerns and Complaints

Maimonides is dedicated to providing quality health care to patients of all faiths and backgrounds. If you have a concern regarding the care received, we encourage you to report it to your attending physician, or you may contact our Patient Relations Department on (718) 283-7212 .

If your concerns are not addressed to your satisfaction you may report a complaint to:

New York State Department of Health Centralized Hospital Intake Program Bureau of Hospital and Primary Care Services Mailstop/CA/DCS Empire State Plaza Albany NY 12237 800-804-5447 Email: hospinfo@health.state.ny.us

The Office of Quality Monitoring of the Joint Commission on Accreditation of Healthcare Organizations One Renaissance Blvd. Oakbrook Terrace, IL 60181 Fax: (630) 792-5636 Email: complaint@jcaho.org
