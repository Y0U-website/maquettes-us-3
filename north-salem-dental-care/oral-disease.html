Signs You Need Oral Disease Treatment

It’s essential to know when to seek professional help for oral health issues. Below are common signs indicating that it’s time to contact North Salem Dental Care:

  • Persistent tooth pain
  • Bleeding gums
  • Difficulty chewing
  • Jaw pain or clicking sounds
  • Loose teeth
  • Visible tooth decay or cavities
