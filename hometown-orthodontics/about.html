About Hometown Orthodontics Our legacy We feel honored to have taken over a practice that has been dedicated to serving families in Bismarck, Mandan, and Dickinson for over 40 years. We’re thrilled to continue providing the same cherished ’hometown’ care that has been a cornerstone of this community. Your continued trust in us is our greatest motivation and we look forward to being a part of your family’s orthodontic journey.

Meet Dr. John Welk

Dr. John has wide ranging experience in all aspects of orthodontics and has particular interest in the use of digital technology in enhancing treatment options for patients.

Dr. John was born and raised in Bismarck. He enjoys the outdoors, running, working out, golfing, and spending time with family and friends. He is known by his friends and family as someone who loves to fix things, and is always learning something new.

Meet Dr. Megan Welk

Dr. Megan loves being an orthodontist because it gives her the chance to be creative and inventive, while also meeting new people and providing positive change in her patient’s lives.

Dr. Megan completed her Bachelor of Science degree at the University of Wyoming where she studied Physiology, American Sign Language, and earned a minor in Honor’s Studies. She then went on to complete her Doctor of Dental Surgery training at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. As a dental student, Megan graduated with high honors and served as President of her school’s chapter of the American Association of Women Dentists. She completed her orthodontic speciality training and master’s degree at the University of Missouri Kansas City.

Dr. Megan was born and raised in Gillette, Wyoming. In her free time, she enjoys exercising, playing basketball, visiting local coffee shops, and enjoying a good book or podcast.

The Welks

Dr. John and Dr. Megan met in dental school in 2015 and were married in Bismarck in 2020. They are both inductees of Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society and are members of the American Association of Orthodontics, American Dental Association, and the North Dakota Dental Association. Dr. John and Dr. Megan have two sons, Jordy and Jude.
