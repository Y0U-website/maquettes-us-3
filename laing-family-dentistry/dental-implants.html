Bone Grafting for Implants

Patients with jawbone loss may require bone grafting to ensure the implants have a strong foundation. Bone grafting rebuilds bone density, allowing even patients with severe bone loss to qualify for implants.

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular checkups and good oral hygiene are essential for longevity.

Does the implant procedure hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort. Most patients report manageable soreness after surgery.

How long does the process take?

The dental implant process typically takes 3-6 months, depending on healing time and whether bone grafting is needed.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants offer greater stability, durability, and functionality compared to traditional dentures. For many patients, they’re the superior choice.
