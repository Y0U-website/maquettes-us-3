New Patient Registration Asset 22 Make An Appointment Asset 23 Referring Doctor Dr. Dayne R. Jensen “Doctor Dayne” MD, DMD Board Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon Cosmetic Surgeon & diplomate of the American Board of Cosmetic Surgery

Dayne R. Jensen, M.D., D.M.D. was born and raised in Utah. As a scholar athlete, he received his Bachelors of Science Degree from Weber State University in Ogden, Utah, while playing football. He then received his first graduate degree in dentistry at the University of Nevada Las Vegas. Dr. Jensen graduated Summa Cum Laude and received multiple awards during his time in dental school.

Doctor Dayne then began residency and obtained a medical degree from the University of Nebraska Medical Center, widely considered one of the finest residencies in the world in facial surgery.

He completed a total of 5 years of residency in Oral/Maxillofacial and General surgery during this time in Omaha. After completion of residency, he elected to further his training and accepted a position as fellow in Cosmetic Surgery in Oklahoma City.

During his fellowship Doctor Dayne completed over 1200 cosmetic surgery procedures.

Doctor Dayne currently serves as the surgical director for the organization Ghana make a difference where he and his team return to Africa each year to perform surgery. He is also a board member for the Utah chapter of the Cystic Fibrosis foundation.

Dr. Bentley Anderson

DDS Board Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Bentley Anderson grew up in Mamaroneck NY, a town just 30 minutes northeast of Manhattan. He attended Utah State University and received a bachelor’s degree in Spanish graduating Magna Cum Laude. He also received two minors, one in biology and one in chemistry.

During his undergrad he was awarded the title of Undergraduate Research Scholar for his work in drug treatment of influenza and vaccinia virus. He obtained a grant for his research, had his finding presented internationally and published in the Journal of Virology.

Dr. Anderson attended the University of Utah School Of Dentistry as part of the inaugural class earning his doctoral degree in dental surgery. While in dental school he served as treasurer for the Utah Chapter of the American Dental Association (ASDA). He also participated in charity organizations including Give Kids a Smile and a dental charity trip to the Philippines.

Following dental school, he completed a residency training program in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at the University of Arizona College of Medicine-Phoenix. He served as chief resident at Banner University Medical Center from June 2020-June 2021.. After residency, he elected to further his training and completed a fellowship in the treatment of facial trauma and temporomandibular joint reconstruction.

Empathy, compassion and a constant desire for improvement are the central components in his treatment philosophy. Quality patient care is his goal.

Dr. Anderson has been married to his beautiful wife, Adrea, for 13 years. They have 3 wonderful children. They are the driving force for his hard work and dedication.

Dr. Ian McComb

Dr. Ian McComb was born and raised in Lansing, Michigan. He received his Bachelor of Science in Biology at the University of Michigan in 2010. During that time, he developed an interest in dentistry and stayed in Ann Arbor to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. While in dental school, he was drawn to the specialty of oral surgery and identified it as a field in which he could make a profound impact for his patients over the coming years.

Upon graduation from dental school, he immediately began an oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at Loyola University Medical Center in Maywood, Illinois, a level one trauma center in the Chicago metro area. He received comprehensive training in the full scope of oral surgery, including management of facial trauma, pathology, skeletal deformities and TMJ disorders. He was also trained extensively in anesthesia techniques and takes pride in his ability to deliver safe and effective treatment for his patients.

We know you have many options when it comes to oral surgery. Thank you for trusting our team to walk with you on this journey. If there is anything we can ever do to improve, please let us know!
