About Us

Meet Dr. Elisa Perez

Hello! I’m Dr. Elisa Perez, a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist. I received my dental degree from the School of Dentistry at the Universidad Central de Venezuela in 2001. Then I moved to Spain, where I obtained a Master of Science in Oral Medicine from the University of Valencia.

In 2013, I completed my Pediatric Dentistry Residency at UT Health in San Antonio, and Became Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2015. I bring extensive experience and a passion for helping children achieve healthy, beautiful smiles. My approach to pediatric dentistry focuses on supporting your child’s overall growth and development. I believe in treating each child as an individual, ensuring their dental experience is positive, stress-free, and tailored to their unique needs.

Additionally, I am fortunate to work as an adjunct faculty member, where I mentor the next generation of pediatric dentists at University of Texas San Antonio. This opportunity allows me to stay on the cutting edge of the latest advancements in pediatric dentistry, which I then bring to my patients here in Bulverde / Spring Branch, Texas.

My husband Richard and I have 4 beautiful children Daniel (16), Catherine (9), Samuel (9) yes twins! and David (7). On our “free” time we are blessed and busy having fun as a family, from shopping groceries and cooking, attending to sports, playdates with friends, piano lessons, Church, visits to parks and other recreational activities. I know you may feel related.

As a parent myself, I understand the importance of trust and communication, and I am committed to making each visit a positive experience for both you and your child. I look forward to helping your little one achieve a lifetime of healthy smiles!

Our Approach to Pediatric Dentistry

At Hill Country Children’s Dentistry, we understand that a visit to the dentist can feel daunting for young children. That’s why we’ve created a kid-friendly environment that is inviting, fun, and comforting. Our entire team is specially trained to work with children and to provide a positive dental experience, helping our young patients feel confident about their smiles and learn to love visiting the dentist.

We focus on preventative care and education, guiding parents and kids alike on the best ways to maintain healthy teeth and gums. From teaching proper brushing techniques to offering dietary advice for optimal oral health, we’re here to support families every step of the way.
