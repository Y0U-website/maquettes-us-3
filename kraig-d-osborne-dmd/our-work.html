Our Work

Our work illustrates completed treatment plans from our family of patients. This plan starts with listening to our patients and establishing an “end result” in mind that we will ultimately create a harmonious relationship between health, function and naturally beautiful esthetics. This process includes a teamwork approach between our office, other dental specialists and artistic lab technicians, collaborating together to complete the desired results. If you have any questions about how we may assist you in improving your dental health, please call us for a consultation.

The Condition

This patient happened to be missing her lateral incisors next to her front teeth with conspicuous spaces between them. Our challenge was to create a natural smile by filling in the spaces and changing the contours of her teeth. The process included porcelain restorations done in a natural beautiful way.

Our Solution

We achieved the patients goal of closing excessive space between her teeth and changed the appearance of her teeth providing a more natural smile in harmony with her lips and face.

Our Solution

Our Solution
