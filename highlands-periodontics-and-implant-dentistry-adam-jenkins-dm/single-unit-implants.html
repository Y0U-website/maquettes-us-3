Unlike removable partials or bridges, a single implant fixture preserves your natural bone and protects the health of neighboring teeth. It’s the most stable and long-lasting option for restoring both function and aesthetics.

  • An implant abutment, which connects the post to your restoration.

Am I a Candidate for a Single Tooth Implant ?

If you’re missing a tooth or need to have a dental extraction , you may qualify for a single dental implant . Ideal candidates have:

During your consultation, Dr. Stephanie Till or Dr. Adam Jenkins will evaluate your bone and gum health to determine if a bone graft or other preparatory procedure is needed before implant placement.

  • Healthy gum tissue and adequate bone density.
  • Good overall health and oral hygiene habits.
  • A commitment to follow post-treatment care recommendations.

How Long Does the Single Tooth Implant Procedure Take?

The process of receiving a single tooth implant typically takes several months from start to finish. For most patients:

In some cases, especially for front teeth with healthy bone, immediate implant placement may be possible on the same day as extraction.

  • The tooth is extracted, and a bone graft may be placed to preserve the site.
  • After about three months of healing, the titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone.
  • Once the implant has fully integrated, a custom implant crown is attached to complete your smile.

Will I Need a Bone Graft for a Single Tooth Implant ?

Having sufficient bone is essential for long-term implant success. Typically, at least 2 millimeters of bone is needed around the implant in every direction. If bone loss has occurred, our doctors may recommend a bone grafting procedure to rebuild the area and ensure proper support for your implant body . This step helps prevent implant failure and promotes long-term stability.

How Long Is the Recovery After a Single Tooth Implant?

Healing time varies depending on your oral health and whether a bone graft was performed. Generally, the area needs about three months to heal after extraction and another three months following implant placement. Throughout this period, our team will monitor your progress and provide guidance to ensure a smooth recovery and successful osseointegration—the process where the implant fuses with your natural bone.

How Long Will My Single Tooth Implant Last?

With proper care, a single tooth implant can last 15 to 20 years or more. Longevity depends on factors such as bone support, gum health, and daily hygiene. Implants are designed to function like natural teeth, but they still require brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits to maintain long-term success.

What Happens If I Don’t Replace a Missing Tooth with an Implant?

When a missing tooth is left untreated, several issues can occur:

A single unit dental implant helps prevent these complications by maintaining healthy bone structure and preserving your smile’s natural shape.

  • Bone loss in the jaw due to lack of stimulation.
  • Shifting teeth, which can alter bite alignment.
  • Difficulty chewing and speaking clearly.
  • Aged or sunken facial appearance over time.

How Do I Care for My Single Tooth Implant Once It’s Placed?

Caring for a single tooth implant is simple and similar to caring for your natural teeth:

Good oral hygiene and regular maintenance visits are key to preventing issues such as peri-implantitis or bone loss.

  • Brush and floss daily around the implant crown and abutment.
  • Attend professional cleanings and exams every six months.
  • Have routine x-rays taken to check bone health around the implant post.
