Periodontal Health

Joseph Family Dental cannot emphasize the significance of periodontal health enough! Regular check-ups and cleanings, combined with proper oral hygiene practices, help prevent gum disease (periodontal disease) and maintain good periodontal health. Healthy gums are essential for overall oral health as they provide a foundation of support for the teeth.

Laser Therapy

At Joseph Family Dental, we utilize a cutting-edge soft-tissue diode laser, which has many practical uses in dentistry. We can shape gingival tissue to increase the length of teeth or fix the appearance of a gummy smile , perform frenectomies to help close the gap between 2 front teeth, and also to treat cold sores and ulcers. A laser is able to cut tissues without bleeding, kill viruses and disinfect periodontal pockets, and much more.
