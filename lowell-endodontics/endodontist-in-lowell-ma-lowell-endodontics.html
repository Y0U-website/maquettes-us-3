Meet Dr. John Miller, DDS Director

Dr. John Miller is an Endodontist, also known as a root canal specialist, originally from Bozeman, Montana. In 2005, Dr. Miller received his Doctoral Degree in Dental Surgery from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. Dr. Miller then pursued an additional two years of advanced study at Boston University, where he completed his specialty training in the field of Endodontics in 2007.

Dr. Miller is skilled at diagnosing and treating symptoms of pain originating from the teeth and jaws. He routinely performs complex root canal treatments and root canal surgeries, allowing patients to save teeth that might otherwise require extraction.

Our expert team, led by respected endodontic specialist Dr. Miller, includes an experienced endodontic staff. We provide the highest standard of professional care in a friendly, comfortable environment.
