A Permanent Solution, Not a Temporary Fix

Unlike bridges or dentures, an implant replaces both the root and the crown of a missing tooth — giving you back the closest thing to your natural tooth in strength, function, and appearance.

Preserves Your Jawbone

Implants stimulate the jawbone the same way a natural tooth root does, preventing the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth, bridges, or dentures.

Doesn’t Affect Neighboring Teeth

A bridge requires grinding down healthy teeth on either side. An implant stands on its own, leaving your other teeth untouched.

Fixed — Never Slips

Unlike removable dentures, an implant is permanently anchored, so there’s no slipping, clicking, or need to remove it at night.

Built to Last Decades

With proper care, implants commonly last 10–15+ years, and often decades — far longer than a bridge or denture typically lasts.

Looks & Feels Natural

A custom crown is matched to your natural teeth, so implants blend in seamlessly rather than looking like an obvious replacement.

Restores Full Bite Function

Implants restore up to full chewing strength, letting you eat the foods you enjoy without the limitations dentures often impose.

What to Expect: The Dental Implant Process

Getting an implant is a multi-step process that typically spans several months from start to finish, most of it healing time rather than time in the chair.

Consultation & Imaging

A dental exam and imaging assess your bone health and plan exact implant placement.

Treatment Planning & Cost Estimate

You’ll receive a personalized plan and written estimate — see our cost guide for typical pricing.

Extraction & Bone Grafting (If Needed)

Damaged teeth are removed and bone grafting is done if there isn’t enough bone to support an implant.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone, creating a stable, permanent foundation.

Abutment & Crown Placement

Once healed, a connector piece and a custom-matched crown complete your new tooth.

Total timeline varies by case, but typically runs 3–8 months from first consultation to final crown.
