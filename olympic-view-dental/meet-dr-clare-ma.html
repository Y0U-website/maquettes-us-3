Dr. Clare Ma

Dr. Clare Ma grew up in Boston, MA where she ate lobster rolls and endured harsh blizzards. From there she traveled to New York and graduated from University of Rochester. Longing for more east coast living, she went on to dental school at Temple University in Philadelphia, PA. Dr. Clare always envisioned herself in dentistry where she could make a difference first hand in people’s lives. She grew up working in her father’s dental office and witnessed firsthand how impactful and gratifying the career can be when you put patients first.

Dr. Clare practices all aspects of General Dentistry, including crowns, fillings, invisalign, implants, root canals and extractions. She does her utmost to provide the same type and level of care to her patients that she would provide to her own family.

Dr. Clare likes to stay active through pilates and golf in her free time. When she’s not being active, Dr. Clare enjoys exploring new restaurants and occasionally finds herself binging reality tv or true crime podcasts.
