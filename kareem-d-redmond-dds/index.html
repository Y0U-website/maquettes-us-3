The practice

At Blue Wolf Dental, we believe going to the dentist should be comfortable, transparent, and engineered around your busy life. Whether you’re looking to transform your smile with custom 3D clear aligners, seeking comfortable sleep apnea solutions, or simply keeping your family’s smiles healthy, our team combines advanced digital technology with warm, personal care.

Dr. Redmond combines advanced dental technology with compassionate family care. Winner of the national AACA Done in One Award, he specializes in custom clear aligners, comfortable sleep apnea solutions, IV sedation treatment for dental anxiety, and comprehensive dentistry for all ages.

Patients often ask where our name comes from! Blue Wolf Dental is a personal tribute to the educational journey that shaped Dr. Redmond’s clinical foundation right here in North Carolina. He earned his undergraduate degree as part of the Wolfpack at NC State , followed by his dental degree at UNC Chapel Hill (Carolina Blue) .
