Porcelain Veneers: Transform Your Smile Naturally

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin ceramic shells bonded to the front of your teeth to improve shape, color, and overall appearance. They’re an ideal solution for correcting discoloration, chips, gaps, uneven teeth, and worn enamel.

Because every smile is different, veneers are custom-designed to match your natural tooth shape, size, and shade. When placed correctly, they blend seamlessly with your teeth and enhance your smile while still looking completely natural.

The Standard for Better Smiles

Porcelain Veneers: Appearance, Durability & Long-Term Care

Porcelain veneers are virtually undetectable once bonded and are highly resistant to stains from coffee, tea, and smoking. Their strength and light-reflecting properties closely mimic natural tooth enamel, making them one of the most lifelike restorative options available.

With proper care, porcelain veneers can last well over 10 years. Avoid using them as tools to open or cut objects, as this can cause damage. In the rare event a veneer breaks, the individual veneer can usually be replaced without disturbing the rest of your smile.
