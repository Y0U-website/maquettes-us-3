Meet our staff

0 MEET OUR DOCTORS Mebane Oaks Family Dentistry was a vision that Dr. Kandice Lee and Dr. Dayena Lee turned into a reality.

With their combined extensive dental knowledge and ability to cater treatments to meet your individualized needs, Dr. Kandice Lee, Dr. Dayena Lee, and Dr. Jeanie Chung will help you achieve the smile you deserve.

This trio is ready to welcome you to the centre with open arms, and have you leaving with a smile that has you feeling nothing less than confident.

Dr. Dayena Lee grew up in Greensboro NC after moving to the U.S. from South Korea at the age of 5! She attended UNC-Chapel Hill for undergraduate studies, double majoring in Psychology and Asian Studies. She then received her master’s degree in Physiology from NC State University. She went on to attend UNC-Chapel Hill School of Dentistry receiving her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree.

During dental School, Dr. D. Lee was part of many student organizations focused on giving back to the community and was awarded the International College of Dentistry Philanthropy award.

Before Mebane Oaks Family Dentistry, Dr. D. Lee worked in public health Dentistry as one of the lead dentists and also as the assistant dental director for Piedmont Health Services. During this time, Dr. D. Lee also worked in several private dental offices around the triangle area.

Dr. D. Lee, alongside Dr. K Lee, is an adjunct faculty member at UNC School of Dentistry and continues to give back to the community by helping dental students in various community outreach dental programs.

In her free time, she loves travelling and experiencing new adventures with her husband, Dr. Alex Chong, also a dentist in the triangle area, and their son, Carter. They currently live in Durham, NC with their 17-year-old Chihuahua, Choco.

Dr. D. Lee is a member of the NC Dental Society, American Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Kandice Lee was born in California but raised in North Carolina. Moving to Charlotte at a young age, she considers herself a true North Carolinian. Her parents, immigrants from South Korea, taught her from an early age what it meant to work diligently, respectfully, and with compassion.

Following graduation, she went on to work in public health being able to refine her skills and give back to a community in need. It was here that she learned the significance between the mouth and a patient’s overall health as well as the importance of oral health education.

In her free time, Dr. K. Lee enjoys spending time with her family and friends, travelling, reading, and doing yoga. On most weekends you can find her relaxing with her husband Dr. Matt Barnes, also a dentist in Durham, their daughter, Avery and fur baby Rylee.

Dr. K. Lee is a member of the NC Dental Society and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Jeanie Chung was born in South Korea and grew up in several countries due to her parents’ work, giving her a deep appreciation for diverse backgrounds and perspectives. She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, majoring in Biology with a minor in Chemistry, and went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the UNC-Chapel Hill Adams School of Dentistry. A proud double Tar Heel, Dr. Chung may not be Tar Heel born, but she is Tar Heel bred—and one day, Tar Heel dead!

During dental school, Dr. Chung was actively involved in numerous student organizations focused on community service, which helped shape her passion for providing comprehensive, compassionate care to all patients. Known for her attention to detail, joyful demeanor, and understanding nature, she strives to ensure that every patient feels valued and receives the highest quality of care—leaving more satisfied than when they arrived. In recognition of her clinical and academic excellence, Dr. Chung was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Outside the office, Dr. Chung enjoys mentoring college students through her church, running, spending time with friends and family, and exploring great food. You can often find her and her husband trying out new restaurants and coffee shops around the Triangle. Dr. Chung is a member of the North Carolina Dental Society and the American Dental Association.

Hope - Office Manager As the Office Manager at Mebane Oaks Family Dentistry, Hope works closely with our team to ensure every visit is smooth, comfortable, and stress-free—from check-in to check-out.

Often described as the glue that holds our dental family together, Hope brings warmth, organization, and dedication to everything she does.
