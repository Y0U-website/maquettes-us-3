The practice

It was my pleasure to acquire the practice of John M. Lazor, Jr., D.D.S. and Bruce Canning, D.D.S. in North Canton, Ohio, after their retirement in June 2011 and October 2016.

After graduating from Ohio State University in 1990, with both a Doctorate of Dental Surgery and Bachelor of Science degree, I continued at O.S.U. with a one year residency program in Advanced Education in General Dentistry. I moved back to Northeast Ohio, after the residency, to be near family in the Cleveland suburbs and my hometown of Orrville.

I’ve treated patients in Stark, Summit, and Portage counties for the last thirty years in private practice. We welcome patients of all ages, children to seniors, by creating a relaxed environment, especially for those that are apprehensive.

Our office offers a flexible payment program called CareCredit. Ask our office manager for an application and begin treatment today.

We pride ourselves with staying current with state of the art dentistry. Keep as updated as us with dental health topics in the news from A-Z with the latest information from the American Dental Association.
