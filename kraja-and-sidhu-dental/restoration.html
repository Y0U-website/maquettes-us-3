Dental Implant Restoration…

Ultimately, dental implant restorations act as a reliable and long-lasting option for individuals seeking to restore their smiles.

  • The restoration process is a tooth replacement option that revitalizes the oral cavity by maintaining jawbone integrity, which is often compromised when teeth are lost.
  • By choosing dental implants, patients can regain their confidence and improve their quality of life significantly.
  • Dental implant restoration is preferred over traditional dentures or bridges, as it offers a more permanent solution that integrates seamlessly with the patient’s existing dental structure, providing both functionality and aesthetic appeal.

Kraja and Sidhu’s Dental Implant Restoration Process

Dental implant restoration is a two-phase process involving the surgical placement of titanium anchors into the jawbone (the actual implant) and the attachment of posts for replacement teeth (restoration).

The entire dental restoration procedure usually takes six to eight months. Most of our Silver Spring patients do not experience any disruption in their daily life.

Personalized Evaluation

Embark on your restoration journey with a welcoming dental consultation. Dr. Kraja or Sidhu will conduct a comprehensive dental and radiographic assessment, delving into your health history. These consultations will cater to your individual needs and concerns. Remembering the context of tooth loss is crucial for selecting the appropriate materials and design for the new implant restoration.

Precision Extraction (If Necessary)

We offer gentle teeth extraction services for teeth that are damaged beyond repair, ensuring a clean slate for your implant. This is needed to make sure we can fit in the restored implants seamlessly.

In most cases where jawbone integrity is compromised due to the defect in the bone left after the extraction, we provide bone grafting to ensure a solid foundation for your implant, utilizing the latest techniques for optimal results.

With precision and care, our surgeons insert the titanium implant, the cornerstone of your new smile, ensuring it perfectly fuses with your jawbone for unparalleled stability.

After the implant integrates with your jaw, we meticulously design and place your customized abutment and crown, mirroring the appearance and function of natural teeth for a seamless, vibrant smile.

Yes, dental implants can be renewed or replaced if necessary. While the implant post (titanium screw) is designed to last a lifetime , the crown, bridge, or denture attached to it may need replacement over time due to wear.

When should a dental implant be renewed?

  • Crown or bridge wear – Most implant crowns last 10-15 years before needing replacement.
  • Bone loss around the implant – If significant bone loss occurs, the implant site may need bone grafting and replacement.
  • Implant failure – In rare cases, an implant may fail due to infection (peri-implantitis) or improper integration with the bone .
  • If only the crown or bridge is damaged, it can be replaced without removing the implant post .
  • If the implant itself fails , it may require removal and reinsertion after the area has healed.
