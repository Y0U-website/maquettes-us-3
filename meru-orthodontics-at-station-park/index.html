of creating a broad, functionally sound, and absolutely stunning smile!

We treat each patient who comes into our office like family, and ensure that when they finish treatment with us, that they have a perfect smile. We not only treat the alignment of your teeth, but ensure an ideal bite, a broad smile, perfect contours of each tooth, ideal gingival esthetics, proper smile arc, and so much more. If you re not familiar with these terms, we d love to explain at your…

In our practice, we use 3D-Imaging for a more accurate diagnosis, Damon Braces to create broad and beautiful smiles, clear aligners, and soft tissue lasers to ensure perfect esthetics.

The day your braces or aligners come off is an exciting day, but it isn t the end of the process. Each patient who completes treatment with us will get two sets of retainers (1 permanent set, and 1 clear removable set), tooth whitening, and laser gum sculpting. These extra steps take your smile to a whole new level of perfection!

Dr. Meru s passion is creating broad, gorgeous smiles, and has been practicing in Salt Lake & Davis Counties since 2012. With his new office opening in Farmington, he s thrilled to bring the latest in technology and innovation to his future patients.
