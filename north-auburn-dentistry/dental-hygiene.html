Dental Hygiene Tips and Tricks

Dental hygiene is an important aspect of our lives crucial for maintaining our oral health and well-being. We must maintain proper dental hygiene practices to prevent dental problems such as cavities, gum diseases, and bad breath. Dr. Brandon Dever and the team at North Auburn Dentistry understand the importance of dental hygiene, and we aim to educate our potential dental patients about its significance.

Brushing and Flossing: Brushing and flossing are fundamental to maintaining excellent dental hygiene. We recommend brushing your teeth twice a day for two minutes each time and flossing at least once a day. Brushing and flossing remove food particles and plaque from your teeth, which can cause cavities and gum problems.

Eating a Balanced Diet: Eating a balanced diet is essential to maintaining healthy teeth and gums. Foods rich in vitamins and minerals, such as calcium, phosphorus, and vitamins A, C, and D, can help keep your teeth and gums healthy and strong. Avoid foods high in sugar and starch, as they can contribute to tooth decay.

Drinking Plenty of Water: Drinking plenty of water benefits your oral health. Water helps to rinse away food particles and sugar, which reduces the risk of cavities. Drinking water also helps to keep your mouth hydrated, which prevents dry mouth and bad breath.

Regular Dental Checkups: Regular dental checkups are crucial for maintaining excellent dental hygiene. We recommend that you visit Dr. Dever at least twice a year for a teeth cleaning and examination. Our team at North Auburn Dentistry will clean your teeth, assess your dental health, and treat any dental problems that may arise.

Use Mouthwash: Using mouthwash is a simple yet effective way to maintain excellent dental hygiene. In fact, some mouthwashes contain antiseptic agents that help kill bacteria responsible for bad breath and plaque buildup. However, it is important to choose an alcohol-free mouthwash to prevent dry mouth and ensure optimal oral health .

Chew Sugar-Free Gum: When it comes to dental hygiene, brushing and flossing regularly can only take you so far. But did you know chewing sugar-free gum can help promote good dental health too? Not only does it freshen your breath, but it also increases saliva production, which helps to wash away food and neutralize bacteria in your mouth. So next time you’re looking for a quick and easy way to keep your teeth clean, grab a piece of sugar-free gum and get chewing! Make sure the gum is sugar-free, as sugar can contribute to tooth decay and other dental issues.
