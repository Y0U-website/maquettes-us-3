Dr. “Trey” Nelson was born and raised in Manassas. After graduating from Osbourn High School, he attended Randolph Macon College in Ashland, Virginia. He received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree with honors cum laude from the Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry in 1994. He was awarded the Humanitarian Award at graduation.

Dr. Nelson believes that education is a lifelong pursuit. He continues to seek out additional knowledge, using the latest technology to help him provide the best care for his patients. He is a member of the American Dental Association as well as the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Nelson enjoys spending time with his family and friends. His hobbies include wood working, traveling and a variety of outdoor activities.
