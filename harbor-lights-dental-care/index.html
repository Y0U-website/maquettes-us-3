At Harbor Lights Dental Care, we use top-tier tech—Dental Microscope, CBCT, PerioLase, and iTero—to make your dental care faster, easier, and more precise.

A dental microscope provides enhanced visibility for precise, accurate treatment. It helps detect tiny issues early, improves outcomes, and ensures minimally invasive care—leading to healthier teeth and faster healing.

3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) captures detailed, 360° images of your teeth, jaw, and nerves. It improves diagnosis, planning, and precision—especially for implants, root canals, and surgical procedures.

The PerioLase dental laser targets and removes infected gum tissue with precision and minimal discomfort. It promotes faster healing, reduces bleeding, and supports gentle, effective treatment for gum disease.
