Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Dental technology has come a long way in just a few short decades, to the point where the majority of generally healthy adults with missing teeth can qualify for implant dentures. You might need to undergo some preliminary treatments, such as tooth extractions beforehand, but on the whole, most adults suffering from tooth loss are eligible.
