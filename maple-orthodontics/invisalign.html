Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

  • Is Invisalign more expensive than traditional braces? At our office, Invisalign costs the same as standard metal braces or clear braces. We’ve made the decision not to add any upcharges for choosing Invisalign, ensuring that cost isn’t a barrier to selecting your preferred treatment option.
  • Does treatment take longer than traditional braces? Treatment time varies based on individual needs. With proper compliance, Invisalign treatment takes the same amount of time as traditional braces. In some cases, it can be even quicker because we can move individual teeth more precisely.
  • How much does it hurt compared to traditional braces? Invisalign typically offers better pain management than traditional braces because it allows for more controlled tooth movement. While traditional braces activate all teeth at once, Invisalign can slow down movements, especially in the first four aligners, making the process more comfortable.
  • What is the success rate and compliance requirements? For the best success, aligners should be approximately 22 hrs/day, only removing them to eat. Typically aligners are changed weekly, and compliance is crucial for staying on schedule. While some patients may get results with less wear time, this can prolong treatment.
  • What happens if Invisalign isn&#x27;t working for my teen? Through the Teen Guarantee Program, patients can switch to traditional braces at no extra charge if we discover Invisalign isn’t working out in the first 6 months. The switch rate is very low.
