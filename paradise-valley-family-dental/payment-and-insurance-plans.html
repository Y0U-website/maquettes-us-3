Smart Smiles Finance Plan

For patients who do not have the benefit of dental insurance coverage we offer a cost savings option called, Smart Smiles Finance Plan. The Smart Smiles Finance Plan allows patients to obtain two preventive care hygiene visits per year (dental cleanings, examinations, x-rays and fluoride treatment) at a significant cost savings. Additionally, patients who are members of the plan also qualify for a 10 % reduction off any dental care we provide.
