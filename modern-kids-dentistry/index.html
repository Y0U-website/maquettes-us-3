The practice

Whether you have an infant or a teenager, your child will receive individualized care, education, and treatment. We want our patients to experience the benefits of a healthy mouth and a confident smile for the rest of their lives, so we help your child develop confidence in treatment, and instill healthy dental habits that last a lifetime. We provide comprehensive pediatric dental care services for infants, children, and teens with the goal of your child having the best possible experience, both in the dental chair as well as the waiting room.

We will discuss the best ways to brush and floss your child’s teeth, which areas you might be missing, create an individualized hygiene program, review your child’s diet, assess oral habits, and determine decay potential.

Our top priority is your child’s health and safety. Our friendly and compassionate staff is committed to maintaining a fun and stress-free environment and to making your child feel at home or even on vacation.
