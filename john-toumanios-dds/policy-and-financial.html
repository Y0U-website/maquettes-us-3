Financial Options

Our office will advise you on financial options after your treatment plan has been discussed with the doctor. We offer customized payment plans.

As a service to our patients, our practice participates in most PPO insurance programs. We will prepare all necessary forms for your dental claims; however, we remind you that your specific policy is an agreement between you and your insurance company. Please keep in mind that you are responsible for your total obligation should your insurance benefits result in less coverage than anticipated. Our office will pre-authorize all major work at your request, eliminating the unknown financial obligation.

The fees charged for services rendered to those who are insured are the usual and customary fees charged to all our patients for similar services. Your policy may base its allowances on a fixed fee schedule, which may or may not coincide with our usual fees. You should be aware that different insurance companies vary greatly in the types of coverage available and you are responsible for confirming your coverage.
