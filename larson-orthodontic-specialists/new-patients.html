Common Questions

Drs. Matt & Katie are Board-Certificatied Orthodontists; why is it important to choose a Board-Certified Orthodontist?

Our Orthodontists graduated from accredited orthodontic residency programs after dental school, and then went the extra mile to voluntarily become Board-Certified by the American Board of Orthodontics to show their commitment to the highest level of patient care and continuing education.

When should a child be seen for their first orthodontic exam?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child’s first orthodontic exam no later than age 7. At this age, some of the adult teeth have started to erupt and issues with growth and development can be identified before they become more serious. Occasionally, early orthodontic treatment is needed to correct issues that are otherwise irreversible.

What are my treatment options?

Treatment plans are customized to fit the specific needs of each patient. We offer a variety of treatment plans, including limited and comprehensive treatment with braces and aligners.

How long will orthodontic treatment take?

Every treatment plan is customized for our patients and the treatment length depends on the complexity of the case. On average, our cases finish ~3 months faster than national averages. Our doctors are involved in your care each step of the way, creating a smooth treatment plan and consistent care. Our advanced technology also improves patient comfort and speeds up the treatment process.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

Treatment cost varies depending on treatment length and complexity. We offer a variety of treatment options, including limited and comprehensive treatment with braces and aligners. We accept most insurance plans and can customize a payment plan that best fits your family’s budget.

Are mail-in aligners just as good as aligners prescribed by an orthodontist?

Simply put, the answer is no. When you seek treatment with aligners from an orthodontist, you will have a thorough examination by an orthodontist with X-Rays of your teeth to determine issues that need correction, including potential problems hiding under the surface. Close supervision by an orthodontic specialist is recommended during orthodontic treatment in order to provide safe treatment and avoid irreversible damage to teeth and surrounding structures which can be expensive to repair.
