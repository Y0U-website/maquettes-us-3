Meet the Dentists

5237 Douglas Drive N, Crystal, MN 55429 763-536-1118 Schedule Now! Dr. Cahow grew up in New Brighton and attended Hamline University, majoring in biology. He has a love for football (SKOL) and was a four-year starter on the Hamline football team, being a tri-captain his senior year. A 1987 graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, he was the recipient of a Regents Scholarship.

Dr. Cahow is an active member of the American Dental Association®, Minnesota Dental Association and the Minneapolis District Dental Society. In addition, he is on the professional staff at Children’s Minnesota Hospitals and Clinics.

He enjoys the four seasons Minnesota offers by boating and Jet Skiing in the summer and snowmobiling in the winter with his family and friends. Dr. Cahow and his wife are “empty nesters” and enjoy long walks together and spending time with their two adult children.

Dr. Thomas Vincent earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Minnesota. In his practice, he combines rigorous clinical preparation with a compassionate, relationship-focused approach that guides every aspect of his practice. He is committed to ongoing learning and regularly participates in continuing education to stay current with new techniques and research, ensuring his patients receive reliable, long-lasting care.

He has learned over the years that the most meaningful part of dentistry begins long before any procedure. It begins with listening. Taking time to understand each patient’s story, their concerns, and their goals allows him to provide care that feels personal. He doesn’t believe in a cookie-cutter approach, because every person is different and every smile deserves thoughtful attention. For him, dentistry is about helping people, supporting them through fears, preventative care and guiding them toward better oral health and genuine confidence.

Dr. Vincent is originally from Nigeria and moved to the United States in 2007, eventually settling in Minnesota. He and his wife enjoy spending time with friends and family and staying involved in their church and community. Gratitude and service shape how he lives and works. When he is not in the clinic, he is often traveling for leisure or for mission trips, reading, spending time at church, or catching up on soccer, the NBA or tennis.
