What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the most reliable and long-lasting solution to missing teeth. They are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone, where they act as artificial roots for your replacement teeth. They are designed to mimic the natural structure and function of your teeth, providing a permanent and comfortable solution for people with missing teeth.

Dental Implant Procedures

Dental implant procedures involve the surgical placement of a small titanium post into the jawbone, which serves as an artificial tooth root. The post fuses with the surrounding bone tissue through a process called osseointegration, which typically takes several months. Once the post has fully integrated with the bone, an abutment is placed on top of it to hold the artificial tooth or teeth in place.

We use advanced technology, such as 3D imaging and guided implant placement, to ensure precise and accurate placement of the implant. Our team of experienced professionals will also provide detailed instructions on post-operative care to ensure proper healing and long-term success of the implant.
