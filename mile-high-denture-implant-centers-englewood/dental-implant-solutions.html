Understanding Dental Implant Procedure

Getting a dental implant is a safe, effective way to replace missing teeth with a natural-looking, long-lasting solution. Here’s a simplified look at the four key steps:

1. Consultation and Planning

Your dentist will assess your oral health, take X-rays or 3D scans, and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants. This step may include planning for tooth extraction or bone grafting if necessary.

2. Implant Placement

Once you’re ready for the dental implant procedure , a small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as the new tooth root. If bone grafting is done, this step may be scheduled after healing. The implant will need a few months to fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration.

3. Abutment Attachment

After the implant fully integrates with your jawbone, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached. This serves as the base for your final crown.

4. Crown Placement

Finally, a custom-made dental crown is placed on the abutment, restoring your smile with a tooth that looks and functions like the real thing.
