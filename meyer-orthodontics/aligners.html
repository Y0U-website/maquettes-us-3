Clear Aligners

When you’re beginning clear aligner treatment with us at Meyer Orthodontics, you can choose to opt-in to Dental Monitoring. This remote monitoring program, using scans from your smartphone at home, may allow you to have fewer in-person visits to our office over the course of your active treatment.

If you haven’t already received Dental Monitoring information and are interested in leveraging this technology during your treatment, click below.

  • 0 Your Treatment with Clear Aligners What are Clear Aligners? Aligner treatment involves wearing a series of clear, removable trays that gradually straighten your teeth. This treatment is available for a variety of ages and tooth movement prescriptions, and we’ll let you know if clear aligners are an option for your treatment.
  • No Goopy Impressions We will take a 3D scan of your teeth at your initial consultation and throughout treatment to ensure the best fit of aligners from beginning to end. Your aligners are custom-ordered with the 3D scan, so there is some time between placing the order and their manufacturing and delivery.
  • Optimized Attachments On the day of your start appointment, we’ll bond attachments to some of your teeth. These are geometrically shaped filling material that allow the aligners to grip and move the teeth more efficiently and predictably. Placing these takes minutes.
  • Take Your Smile Home We’ll seat the aligners to ensure a comfortable fit, smoothing any surfaces as needed. You’ll go home with a few weeks’ worth of trays. Each week, when you switch to the next aligner, you may notice some tightness, perhaps even localized to a single tooth. This will decrease over the course of the week.
  • A Tech-Savvy Treatment Option With your aligner treatment, you’ll have the option to participate in remote Dental Monitoring. By scanning your teeth at home on your smartphone, you may be able to limit the number of in-person visits you need during your treatment.
  • Brushing and Flossing Take out your aligners to eat, brush your teeth, and drink anything other than water. Whenever possible, brush your teeth before putting your aligners back in. If not possible, swish some water to remove larger pieces of food debris. To clean your aligners, brush them with water when brushing your teeth. Ask our clinic about a DentalPod for more thorough cleaning of your aligners.
  • Lost an Aligner or Broken Attachments? If you lose a set of aligners, return to the previous week’s tray and let us know so we can rush order your current tray. If you’re near the end of the week, it may make sense to jump to the next week early. These visits and replacement orders are expected from time to time and are included in your treatment plan at no additional cost to you. Broken attachments mean that the tooth isn’t moving as expected - call the clinic immediately if this issue arises, and we’ll determine the urgency with you and schedule a comfort appointment.
