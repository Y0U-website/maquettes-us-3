Dr. Brittney Ward

We strive to make every dental visit a comfortable, positive, and engaging experience! As a trusted pediatric dental office in Exeter, NH, Dr. Brittney recognizes that every smile is unique and provides care that’s tailored to your child’s needs. Our goals are to help every child establish a strong foundation for a lifetime of oral health and to help every patient enjoy the benefits of a healthy and beautiful smile.

Brittney Ward, DDS Dr. Brittney is a Michigan native, receiving her Bachelor’s Degree from Central Michigan University and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The University of Michigan School of Dentistry. After relocating to New Hampshire, she started in private practice with all ages. Her current focus is treating adolescents and individuals with special needs.

Dr Brittney is a part of the American Dental Association and Great Seacoast Dental Society. She was nominated by her peers for New Dentist of Year Award in 2024.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Brittney enjoys spending time with her husband and daughter! She also loves to practice yoga, ski, hike, and go to the beach!
