About Our Practice

Newton Dental Studio opened in October 2019 when Dr. Bo Davidson took over the office of Dr. Pam McCullough.

We are conveniently located at 229 N Main St. Newton, KS 67114 , on the corner of North Main Street and 3rd Street. We are right next door to the popular Breadbasket. Come in for your appointment and grab a scratch-made pie on your way home!

Please call (316) 283-0870 or email us at NewtonDentalStudio@gmail.com if you would like to make an appointment or have any questions.
