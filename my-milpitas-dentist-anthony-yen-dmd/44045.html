Dentist in Milpitas CA

Dr. Anthony Yen

Dr. Anthony Yen is the proud owner and lead dentist at My Milpitas Dentist. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Arizona School of Dentistry & Oral Health at A.T. Still University.

Dentistry runs in Dr. Yen’s family. His father was also a dentist. Growing up, Dr. Yen saw firsthand the positive impact his father had on his patients’ lives. That experience inspired him to follow the same path and dedicate his career to helping others improve their oral health and confidence.

Today, Dr. Yen is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment. He takes pride in building lasting relationships with his patients and strives to ensure every visit is a positive experience. Dr. Yen is also passionate about creating a supportive, team-centered culture within the practice, where both patients and staff feel valued and cared for.

Outside the office, Dr. Yen enjoys staying active through mountain biking, weightlifting, and playing basketball. He also loves spending time outdoors and is an avid car enthusiast.
