Your Family’s Trusted Dental Home

Lake Marion Dental Care is the trusted dental home of many Lakeville patients. Our team of caring dental professionals and support staff is passionate about helping patients discover their healthiest, most beautiful smiles. Your comfort and individual oral health needs are our priorities.

We are happy to serve families and individuals from Farmington, Elko/New Market, Prior Lake, Apple Valley, Credit River, Savage, Burnsville, Rosemount, and the surrounding areas.

We always look forward to welcoming new patients to our dental family!

Dr. Noah Rounds

Dr. Noah Rounds is the owner and sole practitioner at Lake Marion Dental Care in Lakeville, MN. He earned his Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Concordia College in Moorhead, Minnesota, and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Dr. Rounds has been practicing dentistry since 2005 and purchased Lake Marion Dental Care in October of 2020. He enjoys many aspects of general dentistry, but he particularly enjoys helping people and improving patients’ smiles. Quality patient care and long-term dental health are key values for Dr. Rounds.

When Dr. Rounds is not practicing dentistry, he enjoys spending time with his wife, Ruth, and their dog Baxter. They frequently soak in the sun, lift kettlebells, and play backgammon, cribbage, chess, and Kubb.

Dr. Rounds loves being outdoors in the North Star State, whether camping in the Boundary Waters or hiking in the Beartooth Mountains. He has a passion for endurance sports and particularly enjoys cross country ski racing. Over the years, Dr. Rounds has completed 16 Birkebeiner cross country ski races!

They welcomed their daughter, Olivia, in the winter of 2022. They are thoroughly enjoying being parents.

Ruth

Ruth Rounds is our office administrator and Dr. Rounds’s wife. Ruth earned her degree from Carlson School of Management at the University of Minnesota.

Ruth finds the greatest joy in helping patients feel welcome and at ease, and she values the connection she makes with each person who visits the dental office. In addition to serving as office administrator, Ruth assists patients at the front desk and schedules their treatments.

Ruth and her husband, Dr. Rounds, are very social and involved in their church. Ruth also loves to spend time with family and friends and experiment in the kitchen with new recipes. In the winter of 2022, Ruth and Dr. Rounds welcomed their daughter Olivia, and they are absolutely thrilled to be parents.

Sandy

Sandy has been dental assisting since 1989 and has been a valued member of the Lake Marion Dental Care team since 2010. She earned her degree at Normandale Community College and loves getting to know patients and their families at our dental office. Sandy helps patients feel comfortable and safe from the moment they sit in the dental chair for their treatment.

Sandy wears many hats and is always ready to share a fun story or a sweet smile with our patients. She welcomes patients, schedules appointments, files insurance, and answers patients’ questions as our friendly receptionist. Sandy also assists Dr. Rounds with performing specific treatments.

In her spare time, Sandy enjoys making memories with her husband, son, daughter-in-law, and granddaughter. She loves being Abigail s grandmother and creating memories with her family, whether it s a fun-filled outing or a relaxing night at home.

Kirsten

Kirsten has been practicing dental hygiene since 2005 and joined the Lake Marion Dental Care team in 2012. She earned her degree from North Dakota State College of Science and pursued a fulfilling and exciting career as a dental hygienist in our community.

As a dental hygienist, Kirsten provides compassionate care when performing cleanings, periodontal maintenance, x-ray diagnostics, and fluoride treatments. She loves working with patients of all ages and learning about their lives, families, and interests. Patients can always count on Kirsten for a kind word or friendly smile. She s one of the many reasons why patients continue to see us for their family s dental care.

Kirsten enjoys adventuring with her husband and their fur baby, Elsa. Whether they re exploring the outdoors or watching their favorite TV shows at home, Kirsten and her family always enjoy their time together.

Holly

Holly is one of our exceptional dental hygienist who graduated from Herzing University. Her duties include providing dental prophylaxis, periodontal maintenance, scaling root planing, dental x-rays, and fluoride treatments. To Holly, the most rewarding aspects of her career are creating long-lasting patient relationships and improving patients oral health and home care.

From a young age, Holly wanted to pursue a career in patient care and dental health. When Holly was younger, she saw a kind and friendly hygienist who completely changed her view of dentistry and helped her feel comfortable in the dental chair. This hygienist helped her on her path to dentistry in the hygiene world, and she is grateful for their support and inspiration. Holly has been working in dentistry since 2011 and joined our team in the spring of 2022.

In addition to spending time with friends and family, Holly enjoys being outdoors, sailing, and learning more about gardening and essential oils. She also loves spending time with her dog and 16-year-old cat.

Romi

Romi is our talented dental assistant who graduated from Minneapolis Community and Technical College. Romi helps patients with their dental health and collaborates with our dental team to deliver exceptional service every time. Since 2010, Romi has been serving patients in the dental field, and she s been a dental assistant at Lake Marion Dental Care since August 2022. Romi s favorite aspects of dental assisting are interacting with people from different backgrounds and performing a wide range of procedures.

Romi finds herself inspired and at peace when surrounded by the sights and sounds of nature. When she s not in the dental office, Romi enjoys traveling and being in or around water, especially cascading waterfalls and babbling brooks.
