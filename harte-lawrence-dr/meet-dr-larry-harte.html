Meet dr larry harte

Dr. Lawrence Harte is a graduate of Brooklyn Technical High School. He received his Bachelor of Arts from Columbia College , and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Pennsylvania . Dr. Harte received his orthodontic training at the Eastman Center at the University of Rochester .

Dr. Larry chose orthodontics when, at the age of 20, he researched career aptitude selections. He read the following: likes to sculpt, loves kids, enjoys being involved with the community, and is very fussy. The answer was orthodontics! Says Dr. Larry, I am fortunate to have the opportunity to change people’s lives. My ultimate goal is to have every patient leave our office with renewed confidence, a feeling of self-esteem from a great experience, and, of course, an incredible smile.

Memberships

Dr. Harte is a fellow of the American College of Dentists and the International College of Dentists . He served as a captain in the United States Air Force.

Dr. Larry has lectured internationally and written numerous scientific articles which have appeared in a variety of professional publications. He is one of the pioneers in the treatment of patients in a caring and professional manner.

He is a lecturer and a leader in the American Association of Orthodontics, past president of the New Jersey Association of Orthodontics, and he currently represents the profession in Washington, DC as a member of the Council on Governmental Affairs. Dr. Larry has also been chairman of the Public Health Council of New Jersey, a prestigious position that addresses the health of all citizens of the state.

Personal Life

Dr. Larry has three children: Jonathan, a physician, mountain climber and skier in Colorado; Helaine, a marketing major and triathlete; and Doug, a world-class sailor who shares the practice with him.

From a personal point of view, Dr. Larry has interests in cartooning, sculpting, and directing television shows. He is the founder of the Facial and Cosmetic Enhancement Center of New Jersey, is a National Geographic traveler and sailor, and enjoys writing poetry.
