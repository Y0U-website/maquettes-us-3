The practice

Transforming smiles and lives through cosmetic dentistry services is one reason we are your top choice for dental work. We offer a range of procedures to enhance your smile’s beauty and function and increase your confidence in the process.

At Lake Forest Smiles, we specialize in tongue-tie treatment for newborns, children, and even adults. We collaborate closely with lactation consultants and offer targeted solutions to support your baby’s feeding and oral development. And our myofunctional therapists can guide children or adults through treatment of tethered oral tissues as well.

Lake Forest Smiles is home to the top airway specialists in the North Shore area. Our dentists are experienced in treating sleep apnea at the source of the problem and offer many avenues to better sleep. Through custom oral appliances, we can help you breathe easily.

If you’re facing a dental emergency, don’t hesitate to contact us immediately. Our experienced team is prepared to handle a wide range of dental emergencies and, in many cases, can save your tooth and restore your oral health. We prioritize prompt, effective care to relieve your pain.
