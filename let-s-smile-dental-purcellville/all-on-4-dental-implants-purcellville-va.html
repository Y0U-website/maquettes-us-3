FAQ

What are All-on-4 dental implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses four strategically placed dental implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth.

Can I receive teeth the same day as surgery?

In many cases, temporary teeth may be attached shortly after implant placement through an immediate loading process.

Do All-on-4 dental implants require bone grafting?

Many patients may qualify for All-on-4 treatment with reduced need for extensive bone grafting because implants are placed in areas with stronger available bone support.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch restoration, while All-on-6 uses six implants to provide additional stability and support.

Are All-on-4 dental implants removable?

No. Implant-supported restorations are designed to remain securely attached and are not intended to function like removable dentures.
