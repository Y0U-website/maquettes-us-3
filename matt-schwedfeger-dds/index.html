The practice

Conveniently located in Pittsford, we proudly serve patients throughout the greater Rochester area with high-quality, compassionate dental care.

Our professional team is dedicated to making every patient experience comfortable and positive. We take a gentle, patient-first approach to help you feel at ease during every visit.

We offer options such as comfortable novocaine injections, nitrous oxide, and oral conscious sedation to ensure your treatment is as stress-free as possible.

Using the latest techniques and state-of-the-art technology, we provide efficient and gentle dental care in the Rochester area.

Our team takes the time to understand your needs, explain your options clearly, and create personalized treatment plans that support your long-term oral health.

At Village Smiles, we proudly provide dental care for patients of all ages. Dr. Matt brings the experience and expertise needed to treat everyone from young children to seniors.

We understand that children require specialized care, including monitoring baby teeth, early education on brushing and flossing, and building positive dental habits . Our team is committed to setting your child up for a lifetime of healthy smiles.
