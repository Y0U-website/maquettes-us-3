Comprehensive Oral Exams

Why Are Regular Oral Exams Important?

A comprehensive dental exam is the foundation of good oral health. During your exam, our dentist and dental hygienist evaluate your teeth, gums, jaw, and oral tissues to detect problems early — before they become painful or expensive to treat. Early detection of cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, and other conditions allows us to provide effective, conservative treatment.

What’s Included in Your Oral Exam?

  • Visual examination of your teeth, gums, tongue, and mouth for cavities, discoloration, or abnormalities
  • Gum assessment — measuring pocket depth to check for signs of gum disease
  • Oral cancer screening — checking for suspicious lesions, lumps, or color changes that may indicate cancer
  • TMJ evaluation — checking your jaw joint and bite for dysfunction or misalignment
  • Assessment of previous dental work — evaluating the condition of crowns, fillings, and other restorations
  • Bone and tooth positioning — identifying spacing issues, crowding, or bite problems
  • Digital X-rays (when recommended) — to detect hidden decay, bone loss, and other issues not visible during a visual exam

How Often Should You Get an Oral Exam?

Most patients need a comprehensive oral exam every 6 months. However, your individual needs depend on your oral health status:

  • Low-risk patients: Every 12 months
  • Average-risk patients: Every 6 months
  • High-risk patients (gum disease, frequent cavities, etc.): Every 3–4 months
  • New patients: A comprehensive exam at your first appointment

What to Expect During Your Visit

Your appointment typically takes 30–60 minutes. Our hygienist will perform a gentle cleaning and take measurements. Our dentist will then perform the comprehensive exam, review your X-rays (if taken), and discuss any findings with you. If we identify issues, we’ll explain your treatment options and work with you to create a plan.
