New Patients Are Always Welcome!

Get ready for your visit to Northcreek Dental

What to expect as a new patient

We’re thrilled to welcome you to Northcreek Dental! Your first visit is all about making you feel comfortable and cared for. Here’s what you can expect as you start your journey with us:

Start by filling out new patient forms so we can get to know your health and dental history. You can complete these ahead of time online or bring them with you.

We’ll take any necessary x-rays to get a clear view of your oral health and perform a thorough cleaning to leave your smile feeling fresh.

You’ll meet with our dentist for a complete exam to assess your oral health, discuss any concerns, and create a personalized treatment plan just for you.
