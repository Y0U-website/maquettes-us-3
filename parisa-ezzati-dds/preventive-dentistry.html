Common Questions About Preventive Dentistry

What is preventive dentistry?

Preventive dentistry involves regular dental check-ups, cleanings, and education to maintain oral health and prevent dental issues.

How often should I visit the dentist for preventive care?

It’s recommended to visit the dentist every six months for a routine check-up and cleaning to ensure optimal oral health.

What are the benefits of preventive dentistry?

Preventive dentistry helps avoid cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems, saving you time and money in the long run.

Does insurance cover preventive dental services?

Most dental insurance plans cover preventive services like cleanings and exams. Check with your provider for specific details.

Can children benefit from preventive dentistry?

Absolutely! Starting preventive care early helps children maintain healthy teeth and gums as they grow.

What should I do between dental visits?

Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and eating a balanced diet to support dental health.

Are there specific foods that promote dental health?

Foods rich in calcium and vitamins, like dairy products and leafy greens, help strengthen teeth and gums.

How can I prevent cavities?

Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups, along with a healthy diet, are key to preventing cavities.

What is fluoride treatment and why is it important?

Fluoride treatments strengthen tooth enamel and help prevent decay, making them an essential part of preventive care.
