Meet Dr. Nikolina Nielsen

Dr. Nikolina Nielsen

Dr. Nielsen is recognized as a talented and compassionate dentist who is known for her gentle touch. Her enthusiastic spirit, passion for dentistry, and dedication to the community at large make her the perfect fit for our Sumner community. Dr. Nielsen feels privileged to have the opportunity to take care of her patients and have an outstanding dental team working alongside her.

Dr. Nielsen has European roots. She was born in Bosnia but has a great love for the Pacific Northwest. She graduated with honors from Pacific Lutheran University and went on to the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine to earn her Doctorate of Dental Medicine. She is a board-certified member of the American Dental Association, Washington State Dental Association, and Pierce County Dental Association, and a member of Rotary Club, South Hill branch. She continues to pursue and advance her education actively and is a member of several local study clubs.

Dr. Nielsen lives with her family in Bonney Lake. They love the outdoors, and you can frequently find her perusing local markets, snow skiing, kayaking, or spending time with family and friends.
