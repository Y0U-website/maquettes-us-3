What to Expect

1. Personal Scheduling Outreach

If you’re a patient of Dr. Lowe and are due for a recall appointment within the next few months, our dedicated team will reach out directly to schedule your next visit.

2. Information Verification

During the scheduling call, we will confirm your contact details, verify any dental insurance provider information, and gather everything required to streamline your check-in.

3. Digital New Patient Forms

Once your visit is officially on the calendar, we’ll send a text message containing a secure link to your new patient forms. Completing these ahead of time is highly encouraged but entirely optional.

4. Arrival Preferences

If you prefer not to fill out the digital forms beforehand, please plan to arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to complete them comfortably in our office before your scheduled time.

5. Your Comprehensive First Visit

You will be scheduled with one of our four wonderful dental hygienists for a thorough initial visit, which includes:

  • A complete set of diagnostic digital X-rays
  • A comprehensive oral examination with one of our doctors
  • A professional dental cleaning, if clinically appropriate based on your diagnostic exam

6. Digital X-Ray Requirement

To properly establish new patient care, a fresh set of digital X-rays must be captured using our advanced diagnostic imaging system. While existing X-rays from Dr. Lowe’s office cannot be utilized for active diagnosis in our system, they will be securely appended to your permanent records file.

7. Seamless Chart Transfer

Your clinical charts, treatment logs, and dental history from Dr. Lowe’s office will seamlessly transfer to Paladin Dental. This enables our doctors to review past records fully and ensure continuity of your care.

8. Ongoing Care at Paladin Dental

Following your first visit, you’ll be fully established within our practice family. We look forward to partnering with you on future preventative maintenance and personalized treatment recommendations.

Questions about Paladin Dental?

If you have any questions about the Paladin Dental team, our office, or the patient transition process, please let us know. We are excited to meet you and help you with your oral care!
