Your First Visit

Your first visit to Holistic Dentistry of Hawaii establishes a vital foundation for our relationship with you. During the first visit, we make sure to obtain important background information like your medical history, and give you time to get to know our staff.

New Patient Registration Forms MUST be Completed Before Your Appointment

To make your first visit as productive as possible, please download and fill out the forms below – and remember to bring them with you. If you would like to fill these out at our office, please arrive 20 minutes before your scheduled appointment time. Patients arriving at their appointment time who have not completed these forms may be asked to reschedule the appointment.
