Dr. Tsumura (a.k.a. Gadget Doc) is a Colorado Native who graduated from Overland High School in 1987. After high school, he attended Colorado College, where he graduated with a degree in Biology, and an emphasis in Ornithology. He then received his Doctorate from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry when he graduated in 1995.

Dr. Tsumura is a member of the American Dental Association, Colorado Dental Association, Metropolitan Dental Society, who in 2001 awarded him the Outstanding Service to Children Award.

He enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, playing racquetball, and has an interest in electronics and high-tech gadgets.

Dr. Gutierrez was born and raised in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Growing up in a family of dentists, he felt a calling to dentistry from an early age. He attended the University of Arizona in Tucson, Arizona, where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Physiology. He then went on to attend the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

The moment he moved to Colorado for dental school, he knew that he had found his new home. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his wife and son, family and friends, skiing, snowboarding, hiking and playing sports.

Dr. Gutierrez is a member of the American Dental Association, the Colorado Dental Association, and the Metro Denver Dental Society.
