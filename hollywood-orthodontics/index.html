The practice

Invisalign is the invisible way to straighten teeth without braces. Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten teeth, without wires. Almost 80 % of cases can be treated with Clear Aligners. If you’re looking for an indiscrete way to straighten your smile, this is it.

For adults who want aesthetic treatment but do not qualify for Invisalign, Clear Braces can be used that are the same color as the teeth or are for the most transparent. This is strictly a cosmetic preference and no different that treatment with metal braces.
