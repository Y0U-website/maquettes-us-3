About Lawson Family Dentistry

We’re a family of dentists who love to see you smile.

Established in 1980, we consistently provide quality care to our patients in our Bluefield and Peterstown, West Virginia locations. Our dentists, Dr. Chad Lawson and Dr. Gary Lawson, along with our friendly and caring staff, strive to make you feel as comfortable as possible during your visit and to leave feeling confident and happy with your smile.

Please take a look around our new website which was created for the convenience of our current and prospective patients. Our website allows you to get to know our staff, our office locations, services provided, and the ability to even make an appointment online! So make your appointment today, and come see how fun and rewarding coming to the dentist can be!

Chad E. Lawson, D.D.S.
