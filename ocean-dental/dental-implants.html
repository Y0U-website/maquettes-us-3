Whether you need a single implant or multiple replacements, our practice offers personalized treatment plans. Call us today to schedule a consultation and learn how dental implants can improve your smile and overall dental health.

  • Durability : With proper care, implants can last for decades.
  • Preserved bone structure : Implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent bone loss.
  • Better oral health : Gaps from missing teeth can lead to shifting, but implants help maintain alignment.
  • Enhanced confidence : Implants restore smiles, helping patients feel more self-assured.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a stable base for crowns, bridges or dentures.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The process can take several months. It involves placing the implant, allowing time for the jawbone to heal and then attaching the final restoration.

Are dental implants permanent?

Yes, with proper care, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. Regular check-ups and good oral hygiene are essential for maintaining them.

Is the implant procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so patients typically feel minimal discomfort. Mild soreness during recovery is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Candidates for implants should have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone to support the implant and be committed to good oral hygiene.
