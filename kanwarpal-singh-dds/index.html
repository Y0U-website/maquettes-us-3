The practice

We provide high quality dental care in Manchester in a relaxed and comfortable environment. Our state of the art office and fully digitalized techniques bring cutting edge dentistry to a modern patient experience.

Dr. Kanwarpal Singh is passionate about helping people feel confident in their smiles and comfortable in the dental chair. He practices all phases of general dentistry, but his true focus is on dental implants, full mouth rehabilitation, and Invisalign® - the life-changing services that can restore health, function, and confidence.

Dr. Lawrence obtained a Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences from Barry University in Miami, Florida. He then went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston. Following dental school, Dr. Lawrence completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.
