Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime . Many patients enjoy their implants for 10 to 15 years or longer. Factors that influence the longevity of implants include oral hygiene practices, regular dental check-ups, and lifestyle choices such as smoking. It’s essential to follow your dentist’s aftercare instructions and maintain good oral health to maximize the lifespan of your implants.

What is the recovery process like after dental implant surgery?

After dental implant surgery, patients typically experience mild swelling and tenderness, which can be managed with prescribed pain relief. The healing phase usually lasts between three and six months, during which the implant fuses with the jawbone. During this time, it’s crucial to follow your dentist’s aftercare instructions, including dietary recommendations and oral hygiene practices, to ensure a smooth recovery and successful integration of the implant.

How do dental implants compare to dentures?

Dental implants offer several advantages over traditional dentures. Unlike dentures, which can slip and require adhesives, implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, providing stability and comfort. Implants also help preserve jawbone density and facial structure, which dentures do not. Additionally, implants function like natural teeth, allowing for better chewing and speaking abilities, making them a more permanent and effective solution for tooth replacement.
