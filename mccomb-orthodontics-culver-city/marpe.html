How We Approach Adult Palatal Expansion

Expansion in an adult is not one appliance for every case. We match the approach to the palate, and the right answer is sometimes the lighter one.

MARPE for a Fused Adult Palate - When the palate has fused and needs to widen at the bone, MARPE uses mini-implant anchorage to separate it. We design the appliance from a 3D scan and run Invisalign at the same time to close the gap expansion opens between the front teeth.

Arch Development With Invisalign - When the bone is already broad and only the teeth are crowded, we can widen the arches gently with Invisalign and create more room for the tongue, without a palatal expander. This is often the right call when there is no true skeletal narrowness.

Coordinated Care With an Oral Surgeon - MARPE is a collaboration. We handle the design, the appliance, and the tooth movement, and an oral surgeon places the mini-implants under local anesthesia. Keeping the surgical step with the specialist is how we plan every MARPE case.

Can adults really get a palate expander without surgery

Yes. In children the palate has not fused, so a simple expander widens it. In adults the palatal bones are fused, so MARPE uses mini-implant anchorage to separate them without the surgical cuts older techniques required. We treat adults who were told expansion was only possible with surgery.

How is MARPE different from a childhood expander and from surgical SARPE

McComb Orthodontics uses a three-tier ladder. RPE, a rapid palatal expander, works for children ages 7 to 10 before the palate fuses. MARPE uses mini-implants to expand a fused palate in older teens and adults. SARPE, surgically assisted rapid palatal expansion, has an oral surgeon section the bone for fully fused adult palates. In our view, MARPE has largely replaced SARPE for most adult cases.

How does MARPE work and how are the mini-implants placed

McComb Orthodontics designs the MARPE from a 3D iTero scan and has it custom made by a lab, then cements it onto your upper teeth. An oral surgeon places the mini-implants into the palate under local anesthesia, similar to a filling. You then turn the expander a small amount to slowly separate the fused suture, and the bone fills in as the palate widens.

Who is a good candidate for MARPE, and what rules someone out

MARPE fits older teens and adults whose palate has fused and who have a functional or airway reason to expand, such as a narrow arch, a posterior crossbite, or restricted breathing. It is not the right first step when a deviated septum or enlarged tonsils are the real problem, which an ENT should address first. We evaluate your palate and airway before recommending it.

Do I need to see an oral surgeon, and how does that collaboration work

Yes. McComb Orthodontics handles the design, the appliance, and the tooth movement, and an oral surgeon places the mini-implants. The sequence is a consultation with us, a consultation and 3D evaluation with the oral surgeon, cementing the custom MARPE at our office, then implant placement with the surgeon. We keep the implant placement with the surgeon so the surgical step stays with the specialist trained for it.

Does MARPE hurt

Most patients feel light pressure rather than pain. McComb Orthodontics uses a slow turning schedule instead of an aggressive one, so the sensation is mild pressure around the nasal area. Placing the mini-implants involves only local anesthesia in the palate, similar to a filling.

How long is the MARPE appliance worn, including the hold phase

The MARPE stays in place for roughly six to twelve months to expand the palate and then hold the new width while the bone stabilizes. Holding the width is critical. Taking the appliance out too soon is a common mistake that lets the arch collapse back toward where it started.

Will there be a gap between my front teeth, and how does it close

Expansion often opens a gap between the front teeth, which is a sign it is working. Because adults do not want that gap, McComb Orthodontics runs Invisalign at the same time to close the space as it forms, so you are not left with a visible gap.

Can Invisalign expand my palate instead of MARPE

Sometimes. When the palatal bone is already broad and only the teeth are crowded, we can use Invisalign to gently develop the arches and create more room for the tongue, which is called arch development. When the palate itself is narrow and fused, arch development is not enough and MARPE is needed to widen the bone.

Does MARPE help sleep apnea or breathing

It can help in the right cases, but it is not a cure and not a replacement for CPAP. Widening a narrow palate can improve nasal breathing when jaw width is restricting the airway. McComb Orthodontics coordinates with your ENT and sleep physician rather than treating sleep apnea alone, and recommends MARPE for airway reasons only when there is a real functional indication.

What are the risks, including relapse and gum recession

The main risks are relapse if the width is not held long enough, gum recession as the teeth are pushed wider, and a mini-implant that does not hold and needs to be replaced by the oral surgeon. McComb Orthodontics watches for these and holds the expansion long enough to stabilize the result.

Will MARPE change my facial appearance

MARPE broadens the upper jaw and can widen the smile, and some patients notice a fuller smile as the arch expands. The change is skeletal width in the palate rather than a change to the outside of the face. We review the expected changes with you using your 3D scan before treatment.
