His passion in expanding his skillset led him to obtaining an additional implant placement certification from Loma Linda University. He loves working with his hands and takes pride in his work. Dr. Hsieh stands out for his calm demeanor and ability to put patients at ease with his gentle care. Outside the clinic, he enjoys spending time with his family, hanging out with his cat, and exploring the outdoors!
