MEET OUR DENTAL ASSISTANTS

I have been a dental assistant for over 20 years and absolutely love working with patients! I especially enjoy helping patients who may be anxious or nervous so they can get the care they need and maintain their oral health for a lifetime!

I am originally from Williamsburg, Virginia. I have been a dental assistant for 37 years and my favorite part is meeting so many wonderful people and helping them smile with confidence. Outside of working, I enjoy spending time with my family, geocaching and traveling.

I was born in southeastern North Carolina but moved to the triad area in 2022. I now reside in Pinnacle with my husband, our dog, and cat. Outside of work I volunteer as an EMR with my local fire department and work with the youth ministry at my church! I also enjoy visiting new coffee shops, exercising, and spending quality time with my family. I look forward to meeting patients and providing the best care to obtain their best smile.

I have been in dentistry for 4 years and I love it what I do! I love helping people fall in love with their smile and making sure patients are comfortable and have the best experience. These reasons are what led me to further my education in dentistry. I have a cat named Lilly and a huge family that I love and adore. Outside of work, you can find me at the beach or in the gym!
