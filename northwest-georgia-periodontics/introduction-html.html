A Beautiful Smile Begins with a Healthy Foundation

Our focus is on the F oundation of the natural tooth or tooth replacement. That F oundation should be both Beautiful and Healthy. The three main components of a healthy F oundation are Bone support, Periodontal Ligament, and Gum Tissue.

Our primary goal is to restore oral health and maintain that health through the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease. As a member of your dental team, we will coordinate with your dentist to ensure you have the best treatment options, provided in an environment that is modern and comfortable.

Dr. Fletcher is a specialist in the field of periodontal surgery and implant dentistry. As a specialist, he received extensive training in these areas. He utilizes the latest techniques for diagnosing and treating all types of periodontal disease while also offering cosmetic periodontal procedures to enhance your smile.

Our staff is comprised of dedicated, caring professionals who strive to provide the highest level of care for our patients. We encourage you to call our office to discuss any question or concerns you may have.
