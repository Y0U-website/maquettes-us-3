Dr. Mays’ goal is to provide the best service to all patients, and to do this she has created a highly-trained team and works with the newest technology.

Cosmetic dentistry involves treatments and procedures that improve the appearance of your teeth and smile. From color, to shape, to size — Dr. Mays offers multiple services that can greatly improve the appearance of your teeth and smile. You will, without a doubt, be able to find a service that works for you.

Preventive dentistry works to keep potential issues in your mouth from morphing into a true problem. Preventive measures include regular brushing and flossing, and regular visits to your dentist. Dr. Mays will be sure to provide you with oral hygiene instructions to follow at home that will maximize your preventive care.

Sometimes, damage or decay happens. In these cases, restorative dentistry will be your go-to. Restorative dentistry works to correct and restore damage or decay to your teeth or other structures of your mouth. Dr. Mays is well-versed in the many different procedures used for restoring your teeth.

Dr. Mays focuses on the total health of all of her patients. She’s not only concerned with the health of your mouth and jaw, but also with how the health of your mouth can affect your whole health. She accomplishes this by taking a big-picture view of oral health, focusing on how it relates to the whole body.
