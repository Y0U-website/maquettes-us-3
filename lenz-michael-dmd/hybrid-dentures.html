Hybrid dentures are more comfortable than traditional dentures because they stay in place, are relatively affordable, and don’t hurt as much as you would think they would. If you want your replacement teeth to be as much like the originals as possible, hybrid dentures are the perfect option for you.

Wilmington Dental Associates, located in Wilmington, DE , is the hybrid denture specialists. Read on for more in-depth information about what hybrid dentures are, and their benefits, so you can see if they are the right fit for you.

What Are Hybrid Dentures?

Hybrid dentures are created to replace a whole row of teeth via a dental implant. They fuse to your jaw so they don’t move around. Hybrid dentures are also designed to replace missing gum tissue.

Sometimes, a titanium or gold bar is put in to give the denture additional support. Having hybrid dentures put in does involve surgery which means they can only be removed by your dentist or an oral surgeon. The good thing is you won’t have to remove them to clean them or perform maintenance.

How Much Do Hybrid Dentures Cost?

Dental implants aren’t the cheapest tooth surgery in the world. You’ll probably pay over a thousand dollars for just one tooth. This means, that it’s hard for the average person to get a full set of implants.

Hybrid dentures fix this issue. In this surgery, only four dental implants are used, so it allows your surgeon to charge much less for them. The average person can get back to having the smile that they want with hybrid dentures.

You will also save a lot of money in the long run on denture adhesives, cleaners, and pastes.

Are They Painful?

Your surgeon will supply you with plenty of anesthetics to keep the pain away during the surgery. After the surgery, you’ll be supplied with all the pain medications you’ll need while you’re healing. So, unless you skip out on taking your dose of pain pills, you’ll never be given the chance to really be in any pain.

Who Can Benefit From Hybrid Dentures?

These dental implants are great for anyone who has lost their teeth in an accident, or to a mouth-related disease. They are also good for someone who has lost a large portion of their jawbone from wearing traditional dentures for years upon years.

So really, there is a smaller list of people who can’t benefit from these dentures than those who can. They’re just about for everyone.

How Are Hybrid Dentures Different From Classic Dentures?

The biggest difference is the one that we’ve already mentioned: hybrid dentures go into your mouth via dental implants. This means that they are secure and won’t snap out of place, even if you’re the most aggressive eater. They have a pink acrylic attached to them to make them look more natural than some dentures.

Unlike other dentures, there is a little gap between the jaw bone and the denture. This is just so you can clean them. You can’t take them out like regular dentures, however.

Lastly, not only do they have a more natural look, but they’re the closest that you’ll get to feeling like you still have your original teeth.

They re More Comfortable

If you have hybrid dentures, you can basically go through your life as normal. They’ll be created to not be much bigger than your natural teeth. This means that it won’t be hard for you to adjust to them.

They won’t become dislodged when you’re kissing, talking, or eating, so you can avoid any embarrassing hang-ups that could happen with traditional dentures.

Any Drawbacks To Hybrid Dentures?

The only drawback is that there is still a way for your favorite foods to get stuck under them. If the gum manages to get infected by it, you’ll be in for a world of hurt.

The good news is that this rarely happens, and if you’re seeing your dentist regularly then the chances are seriously slim. This one drawback isn’t a complete game-changer. There are still more benefits than setbacks for hybrid dentures.
