Our Mission To create positive, lasting dental experiences by providing specialized pediatric care in an environment where children feel safe to be themselves and families feel supported in their choices. We believe in building trust through genuine connections, expert care, and a touch of fun.

As the owner and founder of Little Bee’s Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Ally is passionate about providing the highest quality dental care in a warm, welcoming environment tailored specifically for children. She believes in treating every child as if they were her own and works closely with parents to ensure they feel informed and confident in their child’s dental care.

Dr. Ally looks forward to welcoming you and your family to Little Bee’s Pediatric Dentistry and is committed to helping your child achieve a lifetime of healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Ally’s academic journey began at Villanova University, where she earned a degree in Biochemistry—Go Cats! After graduating, she spent her post-baccalaureate year at the National Institutes of Health (NIH) in Bethesda, MD, as part of the NIH Intramural Research Program. This experience deepened her appreciation for science and evidence-based healthcare, ultimately leading her to a career in dentistry.

She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Pennsylvania. During her time in Philadelphia, she embraced the city’s rich history and culture before returning to Washington, DC, to complete her pediatric dental residency at Children’s National Hospital, one of the nation’s top children’s hospitals. Her training there provided her with extensive experience in treating children of all ages, including those with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Ally is dedicated to staying current with the latest advancements in pediatric dentistry through ongoing education, training, and the integration of best practices to ensure the very best for her patients. She is also a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) and is a board-certified diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry (ABPD).

Outside of the office, Dr. Ally enjoys spending time with her husband, Ryan, their son Jameson, and their Bernese Mountain Dog, Harper. Together, they love being outdoors—hiking, traveling to new destinations, and attending concerts. Dr. Ally is a total foodie! She enjoys cooking new recipes in her kitchen, exploring new restaurants, and growing vegetables in her garden. She also enjoys practicing yoga and listening to music in her free time.
