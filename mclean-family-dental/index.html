The practice

McLean Dentistry is focused on helping patients smile from within. That means that every team member is there to help restore your oral health and smile. As "super general practitioners," our practice performs a wide range of dental treatments and offers a more diverse list of dental services than many practices in the area. This means you likely never need to be referred to a specialist s office…

✓ We are specially trained to perform a wide range of dental services, from preventive treatments , to cutting-edge restorative solutions, to cosmetic dentistry , to sedation dentistry for nervous patients, and beyond.

✓ Our office is equipped with everything you need to keep you comfortable during treatment . Each exam room has a television with Netflix, and we will even offer you blankets, pillows, and sunglasses, too. And, if you suffer from dental anxiety or have a special healthcare need, Dr. McLean will study your medical history to determine if sedation dentistry is right for you.

When you re a patient of Dr. McLean , you can expect one-of-a-kind care from a practice that has been serving the community since 2010!
