Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dental insurance to join the care plan? ‍

No, the care plan is specifically designed for patients without dental insurance.

Who is eligible for the child care plan? ‍

The child plan is available for patients ages 12 and under.

Does the plan include emergency visits? ‍

Yes, the adult plan includes one emergency exam, and the child plan includes emergency exams as needed.

Do care plan members receive savings on treatment? ‍

Yes, both plans include 10 % savings on treatments.

How do I enroll in the care plan? ‍

You can contact our office by phone to learn more and enroll.

Affordable Preventive Care for the Whole Family

Preventive care is one of the best ways to avoid more complex dental issues in the future. Routine visits allow our office to monitor your oral health, identify concerns early, and keep your smile healthy year-round.

If dental concerns arise unexpectedly, our Emergency Dentistry services are available to provide prompt care.
