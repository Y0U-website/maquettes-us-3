IMPLANT RESTORATIONS IN GREEN BAY​

Dental implants are among the most effective procedures for returning your mouth to normal function. Implants are doubly effective in that they provide a functional upgrade compared to the missing teeth they replace while stimulating the underlying bone so it doesn’t waste away from lack of use. Retaining bone volume in the long term is as important as the immediate gains in appearance and function that implants provide.
