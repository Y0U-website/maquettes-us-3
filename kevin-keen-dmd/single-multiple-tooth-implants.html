How Long Do Single and Multiple Tooth Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, single and multiple-tooth dental implants are designed to last for many years and often decades. Their longevity depends on surgical and restorative precision, oral hygiene, and routine dental care.

Dental implants are considered one of the most durable tooth replacement options available and offer long-term stability that traditional solutions cannot match.

What Is the Healing and Recovery Process After Dental Implant Surgery ?

Healing after dental implant surgery typically occurs in stages. Initial recovery focuses on comfort and tissue healing, followed by a longer period where the implant integrates with the jawbone.

Most patients experience mild swelling or soreness that resolves within a few days. Dr. Keen and our surgical team prioritize techniques that support minimal discomfort and optimal recovery, and you will receive clear post-operative instructions to guide healing.
