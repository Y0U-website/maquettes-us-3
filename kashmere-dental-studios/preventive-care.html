Preventive care in Converse, TX

At Kashmere Dental Studios, we make preventive care fun, gentle, and judgment-free, so your child can feel confident from their very first visit. And because parents deserve great care too, we’re proud to offer preventive services for adults as well.

What is preventive care and why is it important?

Preventive dental care is all about stopping problems before they start. For kids, this means regular cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments to keep baby and adult teeth strong and healthy as they grow. For parents, it means personalized checkups that protect your smile long-term. As a children&#x27;s dentist, Dr. Kash uses kid-friendly explanations, modern tools, and a gentle approach to make dental visits feel easy—not scary. With a focus on early detection, we help kids and adults avoid bigger problems down the road, like cavities, gum issues, or orthodontic concerns.

The benefits of preventive care

Build healthy habits early

We help your child learn how to brush, floss, and care for their teeth in a way that makes sense for their age and stage of development.

Catch small issues before they grow

Routine exams allow us to spot signs of decay, growth delays, or enamel problems before they turn into more complex treatments.

Personalized care for growing smiles

From toddler to teen—and even parents—we tailor our care to meet your family’s changing needs at every stage.

Our preventive treatments

Digital X-rays Fluoride treatments Sealants Oral health education Periodontal screenings and treatment Cleanings and exams We make cleanings kid-friendly with a calm, supportive approach and fun touches to keep them comfortable. During the exam, Dr. Kash checks for cavities, gum health, bite alignment, and early orthodontic needs.

With low-radiation digital X-rays, we can safely monitor your child’s dental development over time. It helps us detect issues that aren’t visible to the eye—without any scary equipment.

Sealants are a clear, protective coating that shields the back teeth from food and bacteria. We often recommend them for kids once their permanent molars come in.

From brushing tips to snack swaps, we take time to explain healthy habits in ways kids understand—and help parents reinforce them at home.

For adults, we perform regular periodontal screenings to detect early signs of gum disease. If needed, we offer non-surgical periodontal therapy, such as deep cleanings (scaling and root planing), to help restore gum health and protect your smile.

Does my whole family need to come every six months?

Yes—routine care every six months is recommended for children and adults alike. This schedule allows us to stay ahead of potential issues and keep your smile healthy year-round.

What are signs of gum disease in adults?

Common signs include bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, gum recession, and sensitivity. If you notice any of these symptoms, it’s important to come in for an evaluation and, if needed, periodontal treatment.

Is fluoride safe?

Fluoride is widely recognized for strengthening enamel and preventing cavities in both children and adults. If you’re prone to decay or sensitivity, it can be a highly effective part of your preventive care routine. For families who prefer to avoid fluoride, Dr. Kash can also recommend clinically proven alternatives that are safe and effective, so you can feel confident in your child’s care.
