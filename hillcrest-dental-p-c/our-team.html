Meet Our Doctors at Hillcrest Dental, P.C.

Dr. Patrick Brannon

Dr. Brannon received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from King’s College. He then obtained his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania in 1991. Dr. Brannon received his advanced training in general practice and hospital dentistry at Sacred Heart Hospital.

He continues his dental education by attending classes with the Lehigh Valley Hospital’s Dental Education Program. He is also a member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the Tri-County Dental Society. He supports local charities and offers free dental services to the less fortunate.

Dr. Brannon provides dental education to local elementary school students. His reputation as a skilled general, cosmetic and restorative dentist makes him a preferred practitioner in Phillipsburg.

Dr. George Thomas

Dr. Thomas is a native of New Jersey and has completed all his education in the Garden State. He got his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology with a minor in History from the College of New Jersey in 1997. He then went to the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey - New Jersey Dental School, and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2002. Dr. Thomas then proceeded to complete his General Practice Residency at Morristown Medical Center.

While in dental school, Dr. Thomas was involved in educating inner city Newark and Philadelphia elementary school children about the importance of dental hygiene and nutrition. He was also actively involved in the New Jersey Dental School community where he was the president of his junior year class. He was one of the only five students selected from his senior year class to participate in the highly competitive Community Oriented Dental Education (CODE) program where he served the underprivileged citizens in the Northfield, New Jersey region in a private practice setting.

When Dr. Thomas is outside of the office, he enjoys spending time with his lovely wife, Ellen, and his four children, Georgie, Julianna, Abby, and Katie. He also enjoys playing baseball, coaching youth sports, outdoor activities, and home improvement projects in his free time.

Meet the Staff

Jo Ann

Jo Ann has been a dental assistant since 1979 and has been with us since 1990. She provides excellent patient care and always puts the needs of our patients first.

She and her husband, Matt, have three children and three grandchildren. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with family and friends.

Vicki

Anne started her dental career as a dental assistant while attending Wilson High School. This led her to become a Certified Dental Assistant and an Expanded Function Dental Assistant.

She then attended Northampton County Area Community College and received her Dental Hygiene degree in 1983. She was also a recipient of the Hollister Award for outstanding dental education and the local Edward and Arnold Cook Award.

Anne has been a caring and conscientious employee since 1984. During her free time, she enjoys tennis, biking and spending time with her husband, and three children.

Rosemary

Rosemary graduated from Northampton County Area Community College with an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene in 1980. While working as a hygienist, she decided to return to school and earned her bachelor’s degree from East Stroudsburg University in 1993.

Rosemary has worked with several dental offices since 1980, and she joined our office in 1997. Rosemary is a member of the American Dental Hygienist Association, the Pennsylvania Dental Hygienist Association, and the Lehigh Valley Dental Hygienist Association. She enjoys spending time with family and her little dog Christopher.

Tracie

Tracie was raised in Palmer Township, Pennsylvania. She attended Easton Area High School and graduated in 1990. After high school, she attended Northampton County Area Community College, where she graduated in the Dental Hygiene program in 1993 with honors. She worked at a dental office in Allentown for a little over eight years before joining our team in 2002.

Tracie continues her education through a variety of dental courses annually. She enjoys running and spending time with her husband, David, and her three children, Elise, David, and Elaina.
