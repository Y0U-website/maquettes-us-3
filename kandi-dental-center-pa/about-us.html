About Kandi Dental

Dr. Nicholas Marcus, DDS

Dr. Marcus grew up in the Willmar area and is a second-generation dentist, following in his father Dr. Daryl Marcus’ footsteps. Dr. Marcus graduated from CMCS in 1999. Following his undergrad degree at Calvin College in Grand Rapids, MI, he graduated from the University of MN Dental School in 2007.

While in dental school, Dr. Marcus had the opportunity to join the US Air Force and provided dental care to USAF Airmen and women. Dr. Marcus completed an AEGD (Advanced Education in General Dentistry) at Nellis Air Force Base in Las Vegas, NV for a year. From there, he moved to Great Falls, MT where he was the dental commander at Malmstrom Air Force Base. Around 2010, Dr. Marcus and his family moved back to the Willmar area where he purchased Kandi Dental Center from Dr. Daryl Marcus, who retired shortly thereafter.

For Dr. Marcus’ first 7 years in private practice, Kandi Dental remained in a small corner of the Kandi Mall. By 2017, Dr. Marcus’ practice had really grown and he built a new dental office to accommodate his growing practice. Dr. Marcus’ passion lies in treating dental disease. Whether completing a filling, crown, or RCT, he truly enjoys the variety that general practice brings.

Dr. Marcus is happily married to his high school sweetheart, Jonikka (also a Willmar native). They have 3 teenage daughters enrolled in Willmar schools: Eliana, Victoria, and Malin. In his spare time, you will find Dr. Marcus walking his dog Kirby (a mini schnauzer), cooking, watching Minnesota sports, or traveling on a new adventure.
