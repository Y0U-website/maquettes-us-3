Our Zirconia crowns offer great esthetics, strength and versatility. They are some of the highest translucent levels of all Zirconia tested. Since they are milled from a single block, they are up to 5 times stronger than a PFM.

PFM , or porcelain fused to metal, provides a reliable alternative to e.max and zirconia. Combining metal with porcelain provides a durable solution that also maintains aesthetic appeal. Dentists can utilize this metal in implants, partial dentures, and other dental applications.

IPS e.max® CAD is a durable material used for crowns, inlays, onlays, bridges, and more. Due to its high translucency, this material is ideal for creating realistic-looking crowns or veneers. It also offers high durability, withstanding the demands of everyday wear and tear. Our skilled technicians work closely with dentists to match the patient’s surrounding teeth.

Our flexible partials are made from biocompatible, thermoplastic nylon, featuring unique physical and aesthetic properties. Their translucency and varying shades allow the patient’s natural tissue tone to appear through the partial material. The flexible frame of a partial denture helps provide stability and comfort when a patient chews and talks.

We customize our full dentures to provide a proper fit based on each patient’s unique mouth, shape and natural gum and tooth color. We offer Advantage, Standard, and Premium dentures depending on your goals and the needs of your patients. We can even do digital dentures! Reach out to our team to learn more about each of our different denture lines.
