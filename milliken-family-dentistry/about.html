Dr. Milliken knows our area well. He was born and raised in Ferriday. After finishing his undergraduate work at Louisiana State University, he then earned his DDS at the Louisiana State University School of Dentistry in 2009. Dr. Milliken attends continuing education courses annually and has advanced training in conscious sedation for children and adults. He began practicing in 2009, and Milliken Family Dentistry was opened in 2015. As a member of the American Dental Association and the Louisiana Dental Association, he strives to provide good, honest dental care for the community.

Dr. Milliken and his wife have three children. When he isn’t in the office, he enjoys hunting, fishing, and spending time with his family.
