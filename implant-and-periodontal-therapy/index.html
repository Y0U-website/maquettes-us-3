Locally, Dr. Rasmussen has been considered a resource to the dental community by continuing a presence with county and regional dental societies. In addition, Dr. Rasmussen frequently holds lectures and programs for dentists and hygienists on topics such as implant dentistry, recognition and treatment of periodontal disease, periodontal plastic surgery and dental photography.

In total, Implant & Periodontal Therapy provides more than 200 hours of continuing education per year to the local, regional and international dental communities.

Dr. Rasmussen has been selected by nationally and internationally recognized dental study clubs to lead the local chapters for the Tampa Bay dental community. As a proud director of the Seattle Study Club Dr. Rasmussen would like to invite all general dentists to join as members and become a part of this international network of clinicians.

The Seattle Study Club, Tampa chapter was established in 1997, which is an invitation-only continuing education organization that focuses on quality interdisciplinary care for patients. This study club brings internationally-renowned speakers into the Tampa area in order to keep our members continually on top of relevant topics in all phases of dentistry.
