Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Kayla Muxlow Fisher, DMD

Dr. Muxlow has been working to further her education, completing several courses through the Pankey Institute. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, and West Michigan Dental Association.

In 2025, Dr. Muxlow became the owner of Lowell Dental Care. She is passionate about building strong relationships within the community and providing exceptional dental care to patients of all ages. She and her husband, Riley, have two daughters, Margaret and Lucille. Outside the office, she enjoys traveling, trying new foods, spending time with family and friends, and playing with their dog, Murphy.

Cara Office Manager
