Accessibility Statement

  • Alt text on all meaningful images
  • High contrast support, color contrast ratios meeting WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1 on body text)
  • Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Skip-to-content link at the top of every page
  • Forms with associated labels and clear error messaging
  • Video and media that respect prefers-reduced-motion

WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance

Our goal is full conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This means we work to ensure our website is:

  • Perceivable , content is presented in ways all users can perceive, with text alternatives for non-text content and sufficient color contrast
  • Operable , all functionality is available via keyboard, with no time-dependent interactions required
  • Understandable , text is readable and predictable, with input assistance where needed
  • Robust , content is compatible with current and future assistive technologies
