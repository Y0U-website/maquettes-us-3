About Dr. Kosh

0 Meet the Doc’ Originally from Turkey, Dr. Kosh moved to Long Island, NY with his family at the age of 12. His love of dentistry and desire to be in the field started at a very young age when he was first introduced to his Family Dentist.

Dr. Kosh received his undergraduate degree from Stony Brook University where he double majored in Biology and Chemistry and was inducted into the National Golden Key Honor Society. Afterward, he attended University at Buffalo for his Doctorate in Dental Surgery and completed a residency program at Stony Brook Hospital, gaining experience in complex surgical cases and multi-disciplinary dental treatment. Post successful completion of his general practice residency, he accepted a job offer in Upstate NY working beside the nation’s top mentors providing state-of-the-art dental care.

Dr. Kosh loves to socialize and get to know all of his patients, he is very empathetic and loves his job. He is very excited to continue providing high-quality dentistry to his patients here in Buffalo, NY!
