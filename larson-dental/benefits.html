Does a dental implant set off metal detectors?

No, a titanium dental implant rarely sets off metal detectors . Titanium is a non-magnetic metal, so you shouldn’t worry about setting off a metal detector with your implants.

Do dental implants get cavities?

No, dental implants don’t get cavities . Since they’re made from titanium, they’re resistant to tooth decay. Just make sure you’re maintaining good oral health so your implants last as long as possible and your natural teeth get the proper care they need.
