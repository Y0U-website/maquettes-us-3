Our Dental Services

Oral Hygiene Care

We recommend seeing us every six months for general checkups and examinations. These six-month visits are highly recommended to keep your smile as healthy and attractive as possible.

Teeth Cleaning

Flossing and brushing daily is essential in your oral hygiene, but professional cleanings are still needed to remove buildup, which brushing and flossing just can’t do. Cleanings usually are done on your routine six-month visits.

Sealants

To prevent cavities, we recommend applying top-quality sealants. We offer durable and invisible sealants that help "seal" your teeth to prevent decay and cavities from taking hold. This does NOT mean you don’t need routine cleanings and hygiene anymore. It’s just an extra layer of protection to keep your teeth healthy.

Bonding

Bonding is another excellent option to fill in cracks, chips, or shape teeth. Dental bonding is a resin applied to the affected area, shaped, and then cured to create a durable, aesthetic, and functional tooth-like structure.

Fillings

We offer top-quality tooth-colored fillings to fill any holes caused by cavities or other damage. We use composite fillings, which can be colored to match your natural tooth/teeth. The tooth-colored composite filling is invisible to others and a healthy option.

Crowns

Crowns are teeth-like ceramic coverings. After custom designing the crown, we place it over an existing tooth to strengthen and restore it when there is not enough structure for fillings. The crowns also improve the appearance of your natural teeth.

Bridges

Like a crown, a bridge is a custom-made tooth-like covering made of ceramic, which anchors to your natural teeth to replace one or more missing teeth. During the dental procedure, we prepare the teeth on either side of the missing tooth to hold the bridge in place to give you fully functional and natural-looking teeth.

Full or Partial Dentures

Full or partial dentures are removable dentures that consist of replacement teeth attached to a pink or gum-colored base. Connected to the mouth by a framework, partial dentures are used when one or more of the natural teeth are still in place on the upper or lower jaw. Full dentures, on the other hand, are a replacement for all of your natural teeth.
