How Much Do Braces and Invisalign Cost Near Ontario, CA?

Orthodontic treatment costs vary depending on the type and complexity of treatment.

At Kids Dental Castle & Orthodontics, Phase One orthodontic treatment is approximately $3,000, comprehensive braces are approximately $3,500, Invisalign First is approximately $3,500, and comprehensive Invisalign is approximately $4,000.

Additional orthodontic appliances, when necessary, may increase the total treatment cost.

We offer flexible payment options, including zero-interest in-house financing , to help make orthodontic treatment manageable for families.
