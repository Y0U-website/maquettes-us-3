Children’s Dentistry

We suggest scheduling your child’s first dental appointment between eighteen months and two years of age. At that age, we can begin to monitor the growth and development of their teeth and mouth. We try to make their visit as fun and comfortable as possible.

We also take the time to instruct parents and children on proper brushing and flossing techniques so they can enjoy good oral health for a lifetime.

Periodontal Therapy

When you are missing teeth, the bone can weaken, and the jaw can recede. Dental implants keep your jaw strong and youthful-looking.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are used to prevent tooth decay in children. They are applied to teeth, especially molars with large crevices where bacteria can accumulate and cause cavities. The sealant fills in the crevices, forming a barrier to protect the tooth surface. With regular exams and cleanings, fluoride, a healthy diet, and good at-home dental care, cavities can be greatly reduced or prevented.
