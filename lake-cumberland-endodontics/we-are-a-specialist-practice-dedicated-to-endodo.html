How do I prepare for my appointment?

Please bring your insurance information, a list of current medications, and any relevant dental records to your appointment.

What should I do in case of a dental emergency?

Contact our office immediately for guidance and to schedule an emergency appointment. We prioritize urgent cases. You can also visit our Appointments Page and put yourself on our Emergency call list. We book emergency appointments on a first-come, first-serve basis.
