Dental Care to Achieve the Beautiful Smile You Deserve

Our entire staff, from our hygienists to our receptionists, work hard to provide a warm and welcoming environment for every single patient. If you ever have any questions about your appointment or treatment, feel free to talk to a member of our staff today.

Located in the heart of West Haven, Connecticut

Established in 2000, our doors were opened to provide the West Haven community with comprehensive dental services. Through the years, we have expanded our services and staff in order to continue to provide the best care to our growing patient list.

Our dental team always takes time to discuss one-on-one with every patient about their dental options, and what would be best for their overall oral health. We schedule as much time as needed, so our patients never feel rushed or pressured to accept treatment. We strive to fit in appointments that are convenient for our patients as much as we possibly can.

Mark T. Ryan, DMD

Dr. Ryan has been a General Dentist since 1996. After completing his dental residency at Beth Israel Medical Center in New Jersey, he practiced as an associate in Fairfield County. He then purchased the West Haven Family Dental Center in December 2000.

He received his Dental degree from University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 1996. He received his Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Fairfield University.

Dr. Ryan practiced as a Registered Nurse in Manhattan prior to and during his dental education. This allows him to evaluate a patient from a unique perspective. Due to his ability to combine his physical assessment skills gained during his years as a Registered Nurse with his dental education and expertise.
