Is Professional Whitening Right for You?

Works best for:

  • Surface stains from coffee, tea, wine, or tobacco
  • Natural yellowing due to age
  • Teeth that are structurally healthy but discolored

Not recommended for:

  • Patients with crowns, veneers, or tooth-colored fillings (these do not whiten)
  • Severe intrinsic staining (veneers may be a better option)
  • Pregnant or nursing patients

How long does professional teeth whitening last?

Results typically last 1 to 3 years with proper maintenance. Avoiding staining foods and drinks (coffee, wine, tea, dark berries), not smoking, and using whitening toothpaste can extend results significantly.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, professional teeth whitening performed under dental supervision is safe. Some patients experience temporary tooth sensitivity or mild gum irritation — both typically resolve within 24–48 hours. Dr. Mersh will assess your enamel health before treatment.

What is the difference between professional whitening and whitening strips from the store?

Over-the-counter strips use a lower concentration of hydrogen peroxide (typically 6–10 %) and produce gradual, limited results — usually 1–2 shades lighter. Professional whitening at Lumiere uses clinical-grade solutions that can lighten teeth by 4–8 shades, with results visible after a single visit.

Can whitening help with all types of tooth stains?

Professional whitening is most effective on extrinsic stains caused by food, drinks, and tobacco. Intrinsic stains — caused by medications like tetracycline, fluorosis, or trauma — typically do not respond as well. In those cases, Dr. Mersh may recommend porcelain veneers as a more effective alternative.
