Our Doctors

Jared Lee, DDS

Dr. Jared Lee graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS ) degree from Loma Linda University in 2008. After dental school, he spent 6 years practicing dentistry in Southern California before moving up to Alaska in 2014 to make Juneau his permanent home. Dr. Lee has lived in Idaho, Utah, Peru and California and speaks Spanish fluently. He married his wife Kristin in 2005 and they have four kids. In his spare time, he enjoys reading, hiking, going to the gym, traveling to new places, and spending quality time with his family.

Dr. Lee loves calling Alaska home and is proud to be serving the great community of Juneau.

In Juneau , Dr. Lee served several years with the Rotary club in improving Juneau through service and donations that helped to make our community better.

Providers

Kristin Lee, MSN, APRN, FNP-BC, FNP-C

Kristin is the wife of Dr. Lee and is a board-certified family nurse practitioner (FNP) and owner of AesthetIQ | 49. She has a genuine passion for aesthetic treatments when combined with a natural-looking approach to care. She has been injecting and working in the field of aesthetic medicine since 2018 and has completed numerous advanced trainings in cosmetic injectable services, skin rejuvenation treatments, and laser technologies.

She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) with a minor in Spanish from California State University, San Bernardino in 2009. In 2024, Kristin completed her Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) at Purdue University Global and became a board-certified nurse practitioner with a focus in family medicine.

Kristin is deeply committed to ongoing education and continually refining her skills while staying current with the latest advancements in aesthetic medicine. She places great importance on spending time to actively listen, establish trust, and develop customized treatment plans that enhance her patients’ self-confidence and comfort.

Outside of aesthetics, Kristin stays busy with her four school-aged children and helping out at Jared Lee, DDS. In her free time, she enjoys traveling wherever possible, going to concerts, attending group fitness classes, and relaxing with a good Netflix series.
