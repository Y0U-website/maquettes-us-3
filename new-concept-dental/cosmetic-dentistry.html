PORCELAIN VENEERS

Porcelain veneers at our Wilmington, Delaware, practice allow us to craft beautiful smiles for our patients. Whether teeth are spaced or crowded, there is hope for a fantastic smile with porcelain veneers.

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin wafers that are bonded onto the front surface of teeth to hide imperfections. They not only look 100 percent natural, they also last for years and require only minimal preparation of the tooth, unlike older techniques such as caps or crowns. Porcelain veneers are also highly resistant to stains, so you can continue to enjoy coffee, tea, and soda without fading your new smile.

The dream of having a smile free of gaps, cracks, discolorations, and other imperfections can become a reality with porcelain veneers. If you would like to learn more about porcelain veneers at our Wilmington, Delaware, practice, please contact New Concept Dental today.

CEREC DENTAL PROCEDURE

CEREC is a procedure which helps create the restoration of your tooth. With the latest technology, the 3D software can take digital photos of your teeth and convert it into a 3-dimensional virtual model on a computer screen. Your tooth will be designed for you with the computer software in only a few minutes. The design will be finalized then it gets sent to a milling machine which takes about 10-20 minutes and your all ceramic tooth is ready to bond in place.
