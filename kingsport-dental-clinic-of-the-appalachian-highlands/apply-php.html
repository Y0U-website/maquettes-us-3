Apply to the UT Health Science Center

How to Apply

Submit the Application(s) for Admission

  • All UT Health Science Center academic programs require the submission of an application through a central application service (AMCAS, NursingCAS, PharmCAS, etc.).
  • Use the drop-down menu under "Information by Program" to discover program information, application links, and contact information.

Submit Transcripts and Supporting Materials

  • Applications are considered incomplete until all applicable documents, including official transcripts and test scores, are received through the central application service in which you applied.
  • Sending transcripts, test scores, and other documentation directly to UT Health Science Center may prevent your application from being considered complete.
  • After you are admitted to the university you may be asked to submit additional final transcripts.
