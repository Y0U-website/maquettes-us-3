Smiles on Wheels: Serving the Communities of Central Texas with Kids Tooth Team Outreach

Play Play Kids Tooth Team Outreach Kids Tooth Team Outreach is a non-profit organization that we have created based on the belief that all children deserve the best care in the world by the most well-trained doctors and staff in the safest and cleanest facilities, regardless of their socioeconomic status or geographic location. That’s why our mobile pediatric dental clinic brings a safe, fun environment to patients who need access to quality care—free of charge.

Proud Community Partner of St. David’s Foundation

At Kids Tooth Team, we are honored to serve as a community provider for the St. David’s Foundation Dental Program — the largest dental nonprofit in the nation and a trusted partner dedicated to improving the oral health of children across Central Texas.

Through this partnership, our team proudly provides comprehensive care for some of the highest-risk and most deserving children in our communities, ensuring they have access to the same level of compassion, safety, and clinical excellence as every other child we serve.

Supporting the Next Generation of Dentists

At Kids Tooth Team, we believe in growing the next generation of compassionate, community-minded dental professionals.

In partnership with the University of Texas Pre-Dental Society, we have developed a Leadership and Service Program that allows aspiring dentists to gain hands-on experience through our Kids Tooth Team Mobile Outreach program.

Participants are immersed in real-world public health dentistry — learning not only the clinical and operational aspects of care delivery, but also the values of advocacy, access, and empathy that define our mission. Through mentorship and service, students build leadership, communication, and teamwork skills that prepare them to become the kind of healthcare providers our communities need most. Together, we’re ensuring that the future of dentistry is rooted in excellence, service, and heart.

Texas Children&#x27;s Hospital- North Austin: Partnership with Texas Children’s Hospital

Kids Tooth Team is proud to manage the Pediatric Dental Service Line at Texas Children’s Hospital – North Austin Campus, expanding access to specialized care for children across Central Texas.

Our founder, Dr. Alexandra Otto, serves as the pediatric dentist on the hospital’s Cleft Lip and Palate Team and oversees the care of all dental patients treated in or referred to the hospital. Under her leadership, our collaborative program ensures that every child — from those requiring hospital dentistry and complex medical coordination to those with craniofacial conditions — receives compassionate, evidence-based, and comprehensive care.

How We Serve

Schools & Other Organizations We work together with schools, community organizations, and shelters to pre-screen children and obtain the necessary releases for treatment on the van. We work directly with the school or other organization to stage the van onsite to provide preventive procedures such as sealants as well as minor restorative procedures.

Finding Children a Dental Home The ultimate goal is to match children with a permanent dental home, whether it be our foundation or one of our community partners.
