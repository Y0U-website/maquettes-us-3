MacGregor Dental Cancellation and No-Show Policy

Out of courtesy for our staff and to avoid any inconveniences, please be aware that we require 36-hour advance notice for cancellations due to any reason.

• First No-Show: A $75 fee may be assigned. • Second No-Show: A $100 fee is assigned and due before further treatment is provided; your appointment time will be double booked until confirmed. • Third No-Show: Dismissal as a patient.

*Please Note: Appointments that are not confirmed by 12:00 PM the day before your scheduled appointment time may be assigned to other patients.
