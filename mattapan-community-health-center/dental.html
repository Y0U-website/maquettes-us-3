About Us

Who We Are

The Mattapan Community Health Center Dental Clinic was established in the early 70’s to address the dental health needs of the Mattapan and Hyde Park Communities.The clinic has created healthy smiles for patients of all ages and from all walks of life. Our team of caring and experienced dental health professionals will take time to get to know you and understand your unique needs. We’ll work with you to develop a dental treatment plan that will result in the beautiful and healthy smile you’ve always wanted.

Our Dental Department offers screenings, exams, diagnosis, and basic restorative services such as fillings. Specialty services including periodontal treatment, root canals, crown and bridge work, teeth whitening and dentures. Department hours are weekdays from 9 am to 12noon and 1 pm to 5 pm.

The Dental Department also offers walk-in care for emergencies. We ask that patients arrive no later than 9 am for the morning session and no later than 1 pm for the afternoon session. Patients will be seen and scheduled on a first come first served basis.

Features of MCHC Dental Clinic: - Utilization of the latest in digital radiography, digital imaging and computerized medical record technologies for improving quality of care to our patients. - Panoramic X-ray makes a complete half circle from ear to ear, producing a complete two-dimensional representation which gives an overall picture of all teeth and jaw bones. - A secure Patient Portal where you can access documentation of recent visits and medications. You can also send secure messages to your doctor and schedule appointments.

We are proud to be a training site for various dental schools in the greater Boston area including Harvard University, Boston University, and Tuft University School of Dentistry.

Call today to book an appointment and experience the exceptional care we provide.
