Meet The Team

Ask our wonderful patients—they’ll tell you that getting orthodontic treatment at Harbour Pointe Orthodontics is more than just having straight teeth!

We take the time to get to know you and your family. We laugh together, we cry together, and we pray together. We take personal pride and satisfaction in knowing we are helping to change people’s lives through orthodontics and make a positive impact in their lives. We are well trained and have years of experience in clinical and office management in the field of orthodontics—you will always receive the highest level of care possible. We will make every effort to make you feel comfortable and welcome to our office every time you visit us.

Samantha has been in the medical field for over 13 years. She graduated with an Associate degree in Applied Science at Lake Washington Tech with honors. She scheduling, billing, insurance, and many of the administrative duties to help run the office. She also is cross trained to help with some procedures in the back office. Samantha has leadership skills in speech communications and is bilingual (Cambodian and English). Her hobbies consist of hiking, soccer, interior decorating, and building woodwork. When not at work, she spends time with her family which is her ultimate priority.

She belongs to a Bible Study group where they meet once a week for fellowship and to learn about the Word of God and its applications. She enjoys teaching kids, getting to know them and playing with them. She loves working at Harbour Pointe Orthodontics because it is more like family. She also loves to connect with people and seeing them smile.

She was in college for career prep and went to school designed for biology/medicine. When Britt is not at work, she enjoys lifting weights, watching movies and spending time with her dog. She likes being an orthodontic assistant because she can contribute to making good changes in people’s lives, no matter how big or small. She likes working for Harbour Pointe Orthodontics because of the positive, family-friendly atmosphere and the staff.

Shelbi was born in a small town in Michigan. Her love for dentistry grew while working as a sterilization tech during college. After living in Texas for a year, her and her husband moved back to Washington to be with his family. Shelbi and her husband are very involved in various ministries of their church. She enjoys a good cup of coffee, rainy days, thrift shopping, and several outdoor activities (hiking, paddle boarding, camping). She is so grateful to be a part of the Harbour Pointe Orthodontics Family and for the opportunity to work with some amazing ladies.
