Braces

Braces can be metal brackets bonded to the teeth and connected with archwires. Or they can be clear, molded braces worn during the day or at night. Braces help move teeth into the proper positions over time.

Functional Appliances

Functional appliances are removable or fixed devices to correct jaw growth imbalances and improve the relationship between the upper and lower jaws. These help with issues like under or over bites, including:

  • Headgear
  • Palatal Expander
  • Herbst Appliance
  • Twin Block Appliance
  • Fixed or Removeable

Retainers

Retainers are used to help maintain achieved results, such as after a successful braces trial. Retainers keep the teeth in their corrected positions to stabilize and prevent relapse.
