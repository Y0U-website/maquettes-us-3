Dental Implant Process

Before beginning the implant process, we will consult to ensure you have enough jaw bone structure to support implants. If you do not have enough bone structure, we can perform bone grafting to give you more support. During the initial consultation, we will take x-rays and discuss each step of the implant process to ensure you know each step before beginning.

We will place an implant after we ensure you have enough bone structure to support implants. First, we will surgically insert implants into the jaw bone. This will take some time to heal, so you must wait until you have healed to have the rest of your implant and restoration attached. It typically takes 3-9 months for the implant to heal.

Once you have healed, we will attach an abutment to the implant. This is what is used to connect the implant to the restoration. After your gums have healed from the placement of your abutment, we will attach your restoration. Depending on your needs, we will attach a crown, bridge, or dentures to the abutment. These are fabricated in a dental lab and are custom-made to match your natural teeth’ exact size, shape, and color. Your restoration will blend in seamlessly with the rest of your smile.

Once your restoration has been placed, patients may leave our office with a fully functioning and aesthetically pleasing smile. It may take some time for patients to adjust to their new restoration, but you should feel back to normal after a few weeks. Many patients report increased self-confidence after getting implants, as they are more comfortable speaking and smiling!

At any point during the implant process, if you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact our office. We are happy to answer any questions and help problem solve if you have any concerns.

Flapless Dental Implant Treatment in Gaithersburg, MD

Dr. Stan uses CBCT scans to plan precise implant placement. Digital x-rays also allow us to see the nerves, bones, and jaw. With this technology, we can find additional dental problems and address them before implant treatment.

Dr. Stan does not need to make incisions in the gums to access the jaw bone. Flapless implant surgery places the implant posts directly through the gums and into the jaw bone. He uses anesthetic and dental sedation so you will not feel any pain during your implant treatment.

Dr. Stan will take impressions used to create the dental crown, bridge , or denture prosthetic. While the implant heals, technicians will make the permanent restoration in a local lab. The restorations use high-quality porcelain materials that match the shape and color of natural teeth.

How To Care For Implant Restorations

Dental implants support dental crowns, dental bridges, and dentures. Patients must still brush and floss between restorations at and above the gum line. Even if patients miss all of their natural teeth, bacteria can build up on dental restorations, leading to problems like gum disease.

Directly after patients receive implant restorations, they must avoid hard or crunchy foods. Sticking to a soft diet for several days after treatment minimizes pain and sensitivity. In our office, we can teach you how to use traditional floss or an interdental brush to clean around the restoration. We will also avoid using metal instruments on implant restorations at routine dental cleanings.
