Ashlee is a Registered Dental Hygienist at the Northumberland and Danville locations. She joined Legacy Dental in April of 2026.

She started her dental career in 2005 by completing a co–op program with Sun Technical Institute as a Dental Assistant with Dr. Gregory S. Bruce, D.D.S, Endodontist. This is where she later became a full-time dental assistant until he retired in 2020.

Ashlee continued her dental knowledge in 2018 by earning an Expanded Function Dental Assistant Certification from YTI Technical Institute. Later, she went to Fortis Institute and earned an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene in 2022.

Ashlee lives in Selinsgrove with her husband Allen, their silver lab, Cooper, and two cats. In her free time, Ashlee enjoys working outside, cooking, getting lost in a good book or spending quality time with their friends and family.
