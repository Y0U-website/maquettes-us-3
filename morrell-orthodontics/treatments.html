Complex Cases

Complex cases sometime require a little extra help. That is why we utilize advanced appliances to get the job done. These appliances are only for complex cases, and not everyone will require them.

M.A.R.A. (Mandibular Anterior Repositioning Appliance), a specialized orthodontic device designed to address jaw discrepancies and promote balanced jaw development, gently guides the lower jaw forward to help improve bite alignment. With our experienced orthodontic team, we’ll customize the M.A.R.A. appliance to fit your child’s unique needs, ensuring a comfortable and effective solution for achieving a harmonious and functional smile.

Imagine expanding your child’s smile with a palatal expander. A palatal expander is a specialized device that gently widens the upper jaw, creating space and allowing for proper alignment of the teeth. This treatment is particularly beneficial for kids and teens with crowded or narrow arches. Our skilled orthodontists will guide your child through the process of using a palatal expander, ensuring a comfortable and effective treatment experience.

Experience the transformation of your child’s smile with a banded expander. A banded expander is a customized orthodontic appliance that effectively widens the upper jaw, making room for teeth to align correctly. This treatment is especially well suited for children and adolescents dealing with crowded or narrow dental arches. Our expert orthodontic team will carefully oversee your child’s journey with a banded expander, ensuring a comfortable and efficient treatment process that leads to a beautifully aligned smile."

Enhance your bite and facial harmony with the Herbst appliance. The Herbst appliance is a fixed device that helps correct overbites and improves bite function. By promoting the forward growth of the lower jaw and improving the relationship between the upper and lower teeth, the Herbst appliance creates proper bite alignment and enhances overall facial harmony. Discover the power of the Herbst appliance and achieve a balanced smile for a lifetime.

The distalizing appliance is a solution that helps create space in the jaws, making room for crowded teeth or facilitating the eruption of impacted teeth. This appliance utilized elastics under your control with a patented technique to ensure a proper overbite. By applying gentle pressure, the the distalizing appliance allows for proper tooth alignment and a confident smile. Our experienced orthodontic team will customize the distalizing appliance to your specific needs, ensuring optimal results and a comfortable treatment experience.

Surgical orthodontics is a type of treatment used to correct severe bite discrepancies that exist due to skeletal imbalances of the jaws. The goal of treatment is to reposition the jaws to achieve a proper bite.

Retainers

Discover the range of retainer options available at Morrell Orthodontics, designed to help maintain the alignment of your teeth and preserve your beautiful smile.

Preserve your beautiful smile discreetly and affordably with clear retainers made in-house at Morrell Orthodontics. Our custom-made, clear retainers are designed to fit snugly over your teeth, ensuring they maintain their alignment after orthodontic treatment. Thanks to our quick turnaround time and cost-effective approach, you can enjoy the benefits of easy and affordable clear retainers.

Active retainers are custom-designed devices that apply gentle pressure to specific teeth, keeping them in their ideal, new positions. These retainers are particularly useful in maintaining orthodontic corrections for specific tooth movements or bite adjustments or bite adjustments after the completion of treatment. They can even be used for a fast and easy touch up to your smile.

Bonded retainers are fixed devices attached to the back of your teeth, providing continuous support to maintain the alignment achieved through orthodontic treatment. These retainers are discreet, comfortable, and convenient since they require no daily removal or adjustment.

Hawley retainer is a time-tested retention solution for maintaining your smile’s alignment. Hawley retainers consist of a durable acrylic base and a wire that wraps around your teeth, holding them in their desired positions. These removable retainers are adjustable and allow for minor adjustments if needed.

Technology

Experience the cutting-edge technology utilized at Morrell Orthodontics, enhancing precision and efficiency in orthodontic treatments for optimal results.

No more traditional, messy impressions! Our Dexis wireless Intraoral Scan allows us to capture high-quality images of your teeth and jaw, aiding in accurately assessing your orthodontic needs. With this state-of-the-art imaging technology, we can ensure precise treatment planning and deliver exceptional orthodontic care for a healthier and more confident smile.

Our digital in-house 3D printing enables us to be more responsive and efficient to your needs. We harness the power of 3D printing to create precise models of your teeth, enabling us to visualize your orthodontic treatment plan and craft personalized aligners, retainers, and other orthodontic appliances. We no longer have to wait for a dental lab to return your retainer or aligner when we fabricate it in-office.

We use a digital panoramic machine, which allows the most precise and high-quality panoramic images while maintaining the “ALARA” rule – “As Low As Reasonably Achievable.” Panoramic X-rays capture a wide view of your upper and lower jaws, allowing our orthodontic team to evaluate the position of teeth, jaw alignment, TMJ, and overall oral structures. This advanced imaging technology aids in precise treatment planning and ensures that we provide you with the most effective orthodontic care tailored to your specific needs.
