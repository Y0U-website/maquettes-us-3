Workforce

Paramount Dental Studio employs and partners with the best technicians from all over the world: from Asia and South America to North America and Europe. If you are ever in Southern California, please drop by the lab to put a face to a name.

Paramount is also a certified Veterans Affairs job-training site in addition to being combat-veteran-owned. We have one of the largest veteran workforces in the dental lab world and its thanks to their expertise, talent and dedication that we have built one of the premier dental laboratories.
