Meet Your Chesapeake Oral Surgeons

The same board-certified surgeons you’d find at our Virginia Beach office — bringing their full expertise to Chesapeake.

Dr. Scott R. Goodove, DDS

A Virginia Beach native and the founding surgeon of the practice, Dr. Goodove sees patients at both the Virginia Beach and Chesapeake offices. He earned his DDS from Virginia Commonwealth University and completed his residency at Seton Hall University. His philosophy has always been simple: treat every patient like family.

Dr. Peter Zagursky, DDS

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon | Retired U.S. Army Colonel

A Temple University graduate and retired U.S. Army Colonel, Dr. Zagursky completed his residency at Madigan Army Medical Center and deployed to Afghanistan in support of Operation Enduring Freedom. He brings a unique perspective to patient care — combining military precision with genuine warmth. He joined the practice in 2014.

Oral Surgery Services in Chesapeake

Every procedure available at our Virginia Beach office is also available here in Chesapeake — same surgeons, same standards, closer to home.

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement designed to restore your confidence — including All-on-4 full-arch solutions for patients missing most or all teeth.

Emergency Tooth Extraction

If you experience worsening pain, persistent bleeding, or signs of infection such as fever or increasing swelling, contact our office promptly

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Safe, comfortable extraction of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth with IV sedation options.

Oral Pathology

Thorough diagnosis and treatment of oral lesions, tumors, cysts, and other abnormalities — with results you can trust from board-certified specialists.

Sedation Dentistry

No single sedation approach is right for every patient, and that is precisely why we offer a full range of sedation and anesthesia options for patients.

Bone Grafting

Restoring jawbone structure to prepare you for successful dental implants or to preserve bone integrity after tooth loss.
