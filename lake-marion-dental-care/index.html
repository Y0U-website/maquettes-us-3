Welcome to Lake Marion Dental Care, the dental practice of Dr. Noah Rounds.

We provide comprehensive dentistry to families in Lakeville, Farmington, Elko/New Market, Apple Valley, Burnsville, Rosemount, and surrounding areas.

Led by solo practitioner Dr. Rounds, our dynamic team is committed to helping patients improve their smiles and maintain long-term oral health. With every interaction at our Lakeville dental office, we strive to create positive and pleasant dental experiences.

Our team is committed to upholding the highest standard of dental medicine and considerate, uncompromising patient care. We value quality over quantity, and we work as a team to create a friendly and inviting atmosphere.

At Lake Marion Dental Care, we treat every patient like family. We genuinely believe in the positive impact of compassionate and patient-focused dentistry, and we offer individualized care based on your specific oral health needs and smile goals.
