MEET OUR TEAM

DR. ILENE CHOAL

OWNER, COSMETIC AND GENERAL LARAMIE DENTIST

Dr. Ilene Choal brings a deep passion for patient care and lifelong learning to her practice. Born and raised in Wyoming, she embodies the spirit of the West resilient, dedicated, and always striving for excellence. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Anthropology from the University of Wyoming, where she also competed in rodeo, before pursuing her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry.

With extensive education in treating worn and failing teeth, Dr. Choal is committed to helping patients restore their smiles and confidence. She believes in continuous learning and takes advanced courses to stay at the forefront of dental care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Choal enjoys spending time with her three boys, riding horses, and embracing the great outdoors through hunting and camping, especially when there’s a campfire and s’mores involved! Her favorite quote by John Wayne, Courage is being scared to death, but saddling up anyway, perfectly reflects her approach to both life and dentistry.

DANNI BURY

REGISTERED DENTAL HYGIENIST AND CERTIFIED OROFACIAL MYOFUNCTIONAL THERAPIST

Danni is a friendly, bubbly, and detail-oriented dental hygienist dedicated to patient-centered care. As a Registered Dental Hygienist and Certified Orofacial Myofunctional Therapist, she is passionate about continuing education and specializes in myofunctional therapy and soft tissue laser therapy.

Danni believes in creating a comfortable and understanding environment for her patients, ensuring they receive the best possible care. She takes the time to educate patients about their oral health, helping them make informed decisions for long-term wellness. Her gentle approach and commitment to excellence make her a trusted provider among patients of all ages.

Outside of work, she loves spending time with her twin girls and is always eager to learn new things. Whether it’s exploring new skills or staying updated with the latest advancements in dental care, she embraces every opportunity to grow. She lives by the motto: If you stumble, make it part of the dance.
