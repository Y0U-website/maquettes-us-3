About

Welcome to Nelson Family Dentistry, where we prioritize the oral health and smiles of your entire family.

Our warm and friendly team is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. With years of experience and a gentle approach, we strive to make every visit a positive and enjoyable experience for patients of all ages.

What We Offer

At Nelson Family Dentistry, we understand the unique needs of families and the importance of building long-lasting relationships.

From toddlers to grandparents, our comprehensive range of dental services is tailored to address the specific needs of each family member. Whether you require preventive care, routine check-ups, restorative treatments, or cosmetic dentistry, we have you covered.

Main Approach

We believe that education and communication are essential in maintaining optimal oral health.

Our knowledgeable team takes the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and provide personalized dental solutions. We are passionate about educating our patients on proper oral hygiene practices and empowering them to make informed decisions about their dental health.

New Patients

What To Expect

We are excited to welcome you as a new patient to our practice.

To ensure a smooth and efficient experience during your first visit, we kindly ask that you arrive 15 minutes early.

This will give you ample time to complete any necessary new patient forms, which can also be conveniently filled out before your appointment using a link we’ll provide to your email or cell phone number.

Additionally, please remember to bring your correct dental insurance information or card to the appointment. By arriving early and having your forms and insurance information ready, you’ll help us provide you with the best possible care from the moment you step into our office.

We look forward to meeting you and providing exceptional dental care tailored to your needs!
