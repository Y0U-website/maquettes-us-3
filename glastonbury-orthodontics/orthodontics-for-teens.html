Orthodontics For Teens

Our Office 277 New London Turnpike Glastonbury , CT 06033 (860) 430-9889 Visit Us Online Orthodontics For Teens Return to Treatment More than half of all orthodontic patients are teenagers between the ages of 12 and 17. At this stage, most of the permanent teeth—excluding the wisdom teeth—have erupted, making it an ideal time to identify and address issues such as misaligned teeth, bite problems, and jaw discrepancies. For many teens, this is the perfect window for orthodontic care.

The teenage years are a period of rapid physical and emotional growth. As teens begin to develop a more adult appearance, they also become more self-aware and sensitive to how they are perceived by others. Crooked, crowded, gapped, or protruding teeth can negatively affect their self-esteem and confidence. Beyond aesthetics, untreated malocclusions can also contribute to problems like TMJ disorders, headaches, and an increased risk of dental issues.

Helping your teen achieve a well-aligned, healthy, and attractive smile is a worthwhile investment in their long-term well-being. While orthodontic treatment requires time and commitment, the benefits—a beautiful, confident smile and improved oral health—are lasting.

The good news is that today’s teens have more options than ever before. Advances in modern orthodontics mean that braces are now smaller, more comfortable, and less noticeable. From traditional metal braces to clear ceramic options and even removable clear aligners, image- conscious teens can choose the solution that best suits their lifestyle and preferences.
