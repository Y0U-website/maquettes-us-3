The practice

Regular dental visits will help your hygienist and your dentist will help you keep your gums and teeth clean and detect any signs of teeth or gum problems.

Root Canal is performed when the nerve of a tooth is hurting due to decay or infection. In order for the dentist to save the tooth, the pulp, bacteria, nerves, and any decay is removed. The resulting space is then filled with a special medicated, dental material, which restores the tooth to it normal everyday function.
