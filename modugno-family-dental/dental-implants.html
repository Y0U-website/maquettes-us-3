Dental Implant Restorations in North Canton

Dental implants are a reliable, long-term solution for missing teeth. A properly designed restoration completes your implant and ensures your smile looks natural and feels comfortable. We provide custom implant restorations such as crowns , bridges , and dentures that attach securely to your dental implants to restore both function and confidence.

What Is an Implant Restoration?

An implant restoration is the visible part of your dental implant that replaces the missing tooth above the gum line. It is designed to look, feel, and function like a natural tooth. Common options include:

Each restoration is carefully crafted to match your bite, color, and enamel texture for a seamless, natural-looking smile.

  • Dental Crown : Replaces a single missing tooth.
  • Dental Bridge : Restores several missing teeth using one or more implants for support.
  • Implant-Supported Denture : Provides better stability and comfort compared to removable dentures.

Why Choose an Implant Restoration?

At Modugno Family Dental, Dr. Justin Modugno and the team take pride in creating restorations that look natural, function perfectly, and help patients feel confident in their smiles again.

  • Restore Your Smile: Your new teeth blend beautifully with your natural ones for a confident, complete look.
  • Eat with Ease: Strong, stable restorations let you enjoy your favorite foods without discomfort.
  • Built to Last: With proper care, implant restorations can remain durable and attractive for many years.

Custom Design

Once your assessment is complete, a crown, bridge, or denture will be carefully designed to match your bite, shape, and tooth color. Each restoration is crafted to blend perfectly with your natural teeth, improving both the appearance and function of your smile.

Secure Placement

After your restoration is ready, it is precisely attached to your implant for a secure, comfortable fit. You’ll be able to chew, speak, and smile with confidence right away, enjoying a natural look and feel that restores full function to your mouth.

Follow-Up Care

Once the restoration is placed, your dental team will check the fit, bite, and comfort of your new tooth or teeth. If any small adjustments are needed, they will be made to ensure an ideal fit. We also provide guidance on caring for your implant to help it stay strong and healthy for many years.

Maintaining Your Implant Restoration

Taking care of your implant restoration is simple. Brush and floss daily just like you would with natural teeth, and visit your dentist regularly for checkups and cleanings . Good oral hygiene and routine care will keep your restoration looking great and functioning properly for years to come.

Cost of Implant Restorations in North Canton

The cost of an implant restoration depends on your individual dental needs and the specific type of restoration. Several factors can influence the overall price.

What Can Affect the Cost

  • Type of restoration, such as a crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture.
  • Materials used, including porcelain, zirconia, or other options.
  • Number of teeth being restored.
  • Complexity of the case, including your bite, existing implants, and oral health.

Typical Price Ranges

Every case is unique. After a complete consultation and examination, you will receive a clear treatment plan and an itemized estimate. Our team will also help you review insurance benefits to maximize coverage whenever possible.

  • Single-tooth implant crown: $1,200 to $2,500.
  • Implant-supported bridge: $3,000 to $6,000+.
  • Implant-supported denture: $4,000 to $8,000+.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an implant restoration?

An implant restoration is the visible replacement tooth that attaches to a dental implant. It restores function and appearance while protecting your jawbone and surrounding teeth.

How long do implant restorations last?

With proper oral care and routine checkups , implant restorations can last for many years. Regular cleanings and exams help maintain the strength of both your implant and restoration.

What types of restorations can be attached to implants?

Depending on your needs, implants can support a single dental crown , a dental bridge , or a full denture for multiple missing teeth.

Will my restoration look natural?

Yes. Each restoration is customized to match the color, shape, and contour of your natural teeth, blending seamlessly for a confident smile.

What can I expect during the restoration process?

The process involves several steps, including an initial assessment, custom design, placement, and follow-up care. This ensures your restoration looks natural and feels comfortable.

How should I care for my implant restoration?

Brush and floss daily, just as you would with natural teeth. Visit your dentist regularly to keep your gums and implant healthy and to ensure your restoration continues to fit properly.

Are implant restorations better than traditional dentures?

In most cases, yes. Implant restorations provide greater stability and comfort than removable dentures, allowing you to eat and speak naturally without worrying about movement or slipping.
