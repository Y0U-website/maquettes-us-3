Dental Implants

What Are Dental Implants

Dental implants are a groundbreaking solution for replacing missing or damaged teeth. Titanium roots are surgically implanted into the bone, where they serve as the foundation for artificial teeth. Acting like the roots of natural teeth, dental implants provide a sturdy base for fixed (permanent) or removable replacement teeth designed to match your natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants look and function like your real teeth, but they also offer stability and strength that other options, such as dentures or bridgework, might lack. Dental implants can improve your oral health, appearance, speech, comfort, and self-esteem. They’re designed to fuse with bone, becoming permanent, so they don’t slip or cause bone damage the way fixed bridgework or dentures might. Implants also preserve more natural teeth since they don’t require the reduction of other teeth like tooth-supported bridges do.

Our Implant Options

Single-Tooth Implants

Standard implants are the most commonly used form of dental implants. This process involves inserting a titanium post directly into the jawbone, acting as an artificial root. Once the implant bonds with the bone, a process known as osseointegration, a replacement tooth or a bridge, is mounted onto the implant. Dr. Brumley offers personalized implant solutions, ensuring you’ll receive the care suited to your specific needs.

Snap-in Dentures

Snap-in dentures represent a fantastic advancement in implant technology, providing an alternative for those seeking a more stable solution than traditional dentures. Unlike regular dentures that rest on the gums, snap-in dentures are anchored to implants fixed in the jawbone, offering improved stability and comfort. This option is ideal for patients lacking teeth but who have enough bone in the jaw to support implants.
