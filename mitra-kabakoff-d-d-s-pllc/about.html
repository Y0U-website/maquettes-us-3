When should my child have their first visit?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends kids visit the dentist by the age of one. These early appointments are important for building a foundation for oral health. When kids actually like going to the dentist, it can be a powerful way to set the stage for a healthy smile that carries them into adulthood. That’s why from the moment you step through our doors, you’ll notice we’re not your average dental office. By creating a comfortable and happy environment, our goal during the first visit is to build a trusting relationship with you and your child.
