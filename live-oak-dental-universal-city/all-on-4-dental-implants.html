Process of All-on-4 ® Dental Implants

Our Live Oak specialist will start with a consultation during which we will assess your eligibility for All-on-4 ® implants. If you are a good candidate, we will create a personalized plan to replace your teeth.

During implant surgery, our dentist will incise the gum line to expose the underlying jawbone. We will place 4 implant posts at specific angles to maximize their efficiency.
