0 ABOUT View fullsize Dr. Lauren Mathews-Bryant & The Mathews Dental Group Team

Some dentists treat teeth. Dr. Lauren Mathews-Bryant treats people.

With a philosophy rooted in precision, compassion, and an unwavering commitment to excellence, Dr. Mathews-Bryant founded Mathews Dental Group on a simple but powerful belief , that every patient deserves to feel genuinely cared for, not just treated.

Her approach blends clinical mastery with a warmth that immediately puts even the most anxious patients at ease, creating an environment where trust is built from the very first hello.

Backed by years of advanced training and a passion for staying at the forefront of modern dentistry, Dr. Mathews-Bryant leads a team that shares her exacting standards and her heart for people. Together, they’ve built something rare in healthcare, a practice that feels both world-class and deeply personal.

At Mathews Dental Group, you’re never just a patient on a schedule. You’re a person with a story, a smile worth protecting, and a care team that’s fully invested in both.

The Faces Behind Your Smile (Meet the Team)

Great dentistry is never a solo performance. Behind every exceptional patient experience at Mathews Dental Group is a team of skilled, passionate professionals who take as much pride in how you feel as they do in the quality of their work.

From our front desk to our hygienists and clinical staff, every member of the MDG team is hand-selected for their expertise, their character, and their genuine love of people.

We invest in continuous education, the latest techniques, and just as importantly, in creating a culture where every patient feels welcomed, respected, and at home.

When you walk through our doors, you’ll feel the difference that a truly cohesive, deeply committed team makes. These are the people who will remember your name, ask about your family, and make sure every visit exceeds your expectations.

We designed Mathews Dental Group for people who expect more, and we deliver on that expectation at every turn.

From the moment you book your first appointment to the moment you leave with a refreshed, confident smile, every detail of your experience has been thoughtfully considered.

Our environment is calm and beautifully appointed.

Our communication is clear, honest, and always respectful of your time and intelligence.

But beyond the aesthetics and the advanced care, what truly defines the MDG Experience is feeling — the feeling that you are in exceptionally capable hands, that your comfort matters, and that your smile is being treated as the worthy investment it is.

This is dentistry without compromise. Elevated, intentional, and designed entirely around you.
