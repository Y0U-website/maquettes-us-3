How Daily And Professional Care Work Together

At-Home Routine

  • Brush twice daily for two minutes with a soft-bristled manual or electric toothbrush.
  • Use fluoride toothpaste to strengthen enamel and combat decay.
  • Clean between teeth once daily using floss, interdental brushes, or a water flosser to access areas beyond the brush’s reach.
  • Rinse strategically with a fluoride or alcohol-free mouthwash if included in your plan.
  • Be mindful of your diet by reducing frequent snacking and intake of sugary or acidic beverages.

In-Office Prevention

Professional cleanings eliminate tartar that clings to teeth. Most individuals benefit from six-month intervals, but those with gum disease, dry mouth, or orthodontic devices might require a more personalized schedule. These cleanings involve scaling to remove deposits, polishing for enamel smoothness, and topical fluoride application. Regular exams and X-rays identify issues like early decay and bone loss before they escalate.

Your Visit: What To Expect

At a preventive appointment in Memphis, TN, the dental team reviews your health history and oral questions, assesses gum health, records periodontal measures, and screens for oral cancer. A hygienist removes above- and below-gumline plaque and tartar, polishes your teeth, and offers personalized home-care advice. If essential, X-rays are taken to evaluate unseen areas. You’ll receive follow-up steps and a suggested next visit timeframe.

Before And After Care Tips

  • Arrive with brushed and flossed teeth so any concerns are identifiable.
  • Discuss any sensitivity, bleeding, or recent changes in medications or health.
  • Anticipate temporary tenderness from substantial tartar removal.
  • Avoid staining foods and drinks post-polishing or fluoride treatment.
  • Utilize desensitizing toothpaste if needed to alleviate post-cleaning sensitivity.
