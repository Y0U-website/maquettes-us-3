Office Policies: What You Need to Know

Cancellations & Changes:

Life happens. If you need to reschedule, just give us 24‑hour notice so we can open your spot to another young smile. A cancellation fee of $25 per child applies if under 24 hours.

Late Arrivals:

If traffic or tiny feet slow you down, call us as soon as you can. We’ll do our best to keep your appointment on track.

Health & Safety: Our Promise to You

Your family’s well‑being is our top priority. We follow CDC‑recommended sterilization protocols, and perform routine staff health screenings. If anything changes in our guidelines, you’ll hear about it via email before your visit.

FAQs: Quick Answers for New Parents

At what age should my child have their first dental visit?

We recommend scheduling the first check‑up by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth. Early visits set the stage for a lifetime of healthy habits.

What if my child feels nervous?

Let us know in advance! We’ll plan extra time for a “tour and meet” in the office, and nitrous oxide is always available if they need a little extra bravery boost.

What should we bring on Day One?

Your completed online forms (we’ll have them on file), insurance card, a favorite comfort item (blanket or stuffed friend), and any questions you have—we love chatting with parents!
