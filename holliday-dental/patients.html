Appointments

Appointments are an important part of your treatment, as time with our dental professionals has been reserved specifically for you. Please arrive on time for your appointment and notify the office if you are running late. If you are more than 15 minutes late, you may be asked to reschedule.

We kindly request a 48-hour notice if you need to cancel your appointment. We understand that unforeseen events sometimes require missing an appointment, and we will work to accommodate these requests. Thank you for helping ensure our office runs smoothly.

Financial

We understand financial concerns often stand in the way of quality dental treatment, and we strive to provide our patients with care that is both effective and affordable.

When reviewing your treatment plan, our staff will present you with a thorough breakdown of all applicable fees, and we offer a variety of flexible payment options to meet your needs, including CareCredit and Cherry Payment Plans.

Additionally, we are committed to helping our patients maximize dental insurance benefits. For patients with insurance, please bring a current copy of your insurance card to your dental appointment, and our team will file your insurance claim.

Our team is here to answer any financial questions to ensure you are educated on all options, and we will work together to accommodate your needs.
