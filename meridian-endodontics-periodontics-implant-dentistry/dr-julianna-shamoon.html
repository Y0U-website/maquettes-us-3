DR. JULIANNA SHAMOON

Dr. Julianna Shamoon Periodontist & Dental Implant Specialist

Dr. Julianna Shamoon is a Board-Certified Periodontist and Dental Implant Surgeon who believes great care starts with compassion, clarity, and trust. Known for her gentle chairside manner and calm presence, she takes time to listen, explain, and guide patients through treatment options.

In private practice, Dr. Shamoon focuses on helping patients protect and restore their oral health using modern, patient-friendly techniques. She treats gum disease, receding gums , and bone loss , and helps patients replace missing teeth with dental implants designed to look and function naturally. Her care often includes laser therapy for gum disease (LANAP®), minimally invasive gum grafting (VISTA and tunneling techniques), as well as bone and site development (ridge augmentation, sinus lifts, implant site optimization) .

Dr. Shamoon is an experienced specialist and maintains active membership in the American Academy of Periodontology and the Academy of Osseointegration .

Patients often choose Dr. Shamoon for her combination of technical skill and genuine warmth. Whether addressing gum disease, preparing for dental implants, or restoring oral health in collaboration with endodontic colleagues, her goal is always the same: thoughtful care, clear communication, and results that support long-term health and confidence.

Outside the office, Dr. Shamoon enjoys time with family and friends, staying active, and exploring the outdoors with her dog, Addison. A proud Chicago native, she may be caught cheering for the Bulls when they play the Bucks.
