Root Canal Treatment

Left untreated, a root canal infection can become incredibly painful and might even lead to tooth loss. A root canal treatment can nip this infection in the bud before it causes permanent damage and allows you to keep your natural tooth. It’s also much less expensive to treat the infection at this stage than to have to get dental implants or bridges down the line.
