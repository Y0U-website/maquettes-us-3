Common Concerns Adults Have

"Will it be noticeable?"

Not if you don’t want it to be. Invisalign trays are virtually invisible, and ceramic braces blend with your natural tooth color.

"How long will treatment take?"

Many adult cases are completed in 12 to 24 months. Some mild Invisalign cases can finish in as little as 6 months.

"Is it too late for me?"

Absolutely not. We regularly treat patients in their 30s, 40s, 50s, and beyond.

"How much will it cost?"

We accept most major insurance plans, offer flexible monthly payment plans, and accept outside financing. We’ll give you a comprehensive and transparent cost estimate at your consultation .
