Blakely Cingolani, D.D.S.

Dr. Blakely, as her patients refer to her, is an Arkansas native who was born in McGehee, Arkansas. After Highschool she attended the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville, where she learned to love yelling WOO PIG SOOIE!

She graduated from the University of Arkansas in 2011 and worked as a dental assistant for several years before deciding to continue her education and become a dentist.

She attended The University of Tennessee Dental School at Memphis, earning her DDS in 2019. She moved to Benton, Arkansas shortly after graduation from dental school and has been practicing general/family dentistry including restorative, implant, and cosmetic dentistry in Saline County ever since.

As a dentist, Dr. Blakely takes immense pride in doing her absolute best to make her patients feel comfortable and completely happy with their dental treatment while establishing long term and trustworthy relationships.

Dr. Blakely is a member of the American Dental Association, Arkansas State Dental Association, the American Academy of Implantology, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

In Dr. Blakely’s spare time you can find her spending time outdoors, traveling, going to the lake, and of course CALLING THOSE HOGS! She also loves spending time with her amazing family and friends every chance she gets!
