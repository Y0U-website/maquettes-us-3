  • What is a dental savings plan? A dental savings plan, such as our VIP Dental Savings Plan, is an alternative to traditional insurance. Members pay an annual fee to receive discounted dental services, including 15 % off additional treatments, without deductibles, annual maximums, or waiting periods.
  • Are dental savings plans worth it? Yes! Dental savings plans can be a great option for patients without insurance, offering reduced rates on preventative and restorative treatments. They help make dental care more affordable while providing savings on services like cleanings, X-rays, and more extensive procedures.
