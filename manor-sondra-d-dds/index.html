The practice

Our robust inpatient and outpatient curricula give residents the foundational knowledge to excel as an internist. Our evidenced based medicine curriculum helps residents decipher the ever-evolving literature base that is foundation to effective patient care while our anti-racism in medicine and social determinants of health curricula help create physicians who can deliver holistic care.

We are fortunate to have UCLA faculty that not only teach our residents but also engage residents in scholarly work through research. We offer the ability to be part of NIH grants, health services research and quality improvement research.

Meet our dedicated Program Leadership and Administrative team that work to support our residents every step of their journey here at Olive View.

Meet our wonderful residents and alumni and learn more about what they love about training in our program.

Learn more about how to apply to the UCLA- Olive View Internal Medicine categorical and preliminary medicine tracks.

Our program is one of two UCLA-sponsored programs in Internal Medicine.

We share a strong relationship with the UCLA Internal Medicine program based at Ronald Reagan-UCLA Medical Center and the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA. Over 90 UCLA-appointed faculty train our residents, and Olive View is well-known as one of the best teaching sites among UCLA medical students (don’t take our word for it, ask them).

We are devoted to the medical needs of our underserved community—from caring for patients during their most acute illnesses in the hospital to promoting health through longitudinal care in clinic. Learn more about our diverse and predominant Spanish-speaking patient population as well as how we strengthen our own community.
