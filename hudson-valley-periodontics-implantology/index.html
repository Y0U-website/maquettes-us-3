The practice

At Hudson Valley Periodontics and Implantology, we strive to help everyone find the right solution for their dental health problems. Each patient is treated separately so that we can do a full analysis and find the best solution for each person.

Hudson Valley Periodontics and Implantology provides a wide range of periodontic and implant-related procedures, including:

Dental Implants - Dental implants are used to replace teeth with a realistic-looking crown. We have extensive experience placing dental implants.

Bone Grafting - In some cases, there is not enough bone for an implant. We can do bone grafts to prepare your jaw for an implant.

Laser Dentistry - Lasers cauterize cuts on contact, making them heal faster and cleanly. We use lasers instead of scalpels when possible to give you the best chance to heal quickly.

Gum Grafting - We can graft gum tissue to help your gums heal in places. It is an effective way to help build up support for your teeth.

Bone Regeneration - We use multiple methods to help your bone regrow in places where it is weakened or damaged. We can use this method to protect your teeth from further damage or falling out.

Scaling and Root Planing - Scaling and root planing cleans around your gum line. It helps fight plaque buildup while reducing the chance of bacterial infections.

Les soins

Scaling and root planing is our first line of defense against gum disease. In fact, this simple, non-invasive procedure can often reverse gum disease in a single visit.

Dental implants replace tooth roots in the mouth. Implants provide a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth and can aid in the prevention of bone loss in the jaw.
