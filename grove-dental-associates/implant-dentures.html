IMPLANT DENTURES

Implant-retained dentures provide the optimal and ideal solution for patients with missing teeth or those struggling with loose, ill-fitting or poor quality dentures.

When evaluating the treatment options available, it is necessary to consider that this investment should be long-term, secure, comfortable, aesthetically pleasing and functional.

Grove Dental Associates utilizes digital x-rays and a 3D scan of your jaw to plan the exact position of the implants providing the stable support or anchor for a custom-crafted denture. By connecting the denture to the permanent implants placed within the jawbone, the denture is then removable yet simultaneously dependable in it’s fit and security. It will literally snap on and off, and you will no longer need messy adhesives to keep it in place!

With our team’s advanced training in implant dentistry and technology, we feel privileged to provide patients with nothing short of the best, in the process of restoring health, function, and confidence.
