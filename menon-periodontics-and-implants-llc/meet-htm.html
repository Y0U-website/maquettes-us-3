Meet the Doctor

Dr. Menon is a Board Certified Diplomate of the American Academy of Periodontology (AAP), which certifies that she is a periodontist who has made significant achievements beyond the mandatory educational requirements of the specialty. She is also a member of the American Academy of Periodontology (AAP) and the American Board of Periodontology (ABP).

Dr. Menon enjoys cooking, traveling, reading and spending time with her daughter Reema and husband Rajesh.

Dr. Menon and her staff work as a team to provide a pleasant dental experience for each patient and help get their smile back!
