ORTHODONTIC TREATMENTS

Our comprehensive range of services includes the most advanced orthodontic treatments available today. In addition to traditional braces, we also offer Invisalign. Thus, our mission is to provide affordable orthodontic treatments tailored to your unique needs and lifestyle. Our services include:

Braces:

Whether for minor adjustments or significant realignments, our braces are designed to address a wide range of orthodontic needs. Not only do they boost your child’s appearance, but they also enhance their oral function, leading to a healthier, more confident smile.

Invisalign:

Recognizing that some children may desire a less visible treatment option, we offer our advanced Invisalign aligners. These virtually invisible aligners straighten your child’s teeth, restoring their smile and boosting their confidence.

Retainers:

After completing braces or Invisalign treatment, our customized retainers help maintain the perfect alignment of your child’s teeth. This ensures the long-term stability of the treatment results, keeping that beautiful smile intact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Orthodontics is a dental specialty that addresses the diagnosis, prevention, and correction of dental and facial irregularities, including malocclusion (bad bite). It primarily focuses on treating patients with braces, aligners, and other appliances to move teeth or adjust bone.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children first see an orthodontist at age 7 or earlier if a problem is detected by parents, a family dentist, or the child’s physician.

Orthodontics treats dental and facial issues, such as crowded teeth, gaps, overbite, underbite, crossbite, and other alignment and jaw growth problems. As a result, orthodontics improves both appearance and function, leading to better dental health.

Treatment duration depends on the severity of the case and can range from 6 months to 3 years. On average, treatments last around 2 years.

Absolutely! Orthodontic treatment is successful at any age. In fact, adults especially appreciate the benefits of a healthy, beautiful smile, whether for aesthetic or functional reasons.

The cost varies based on the case complexity, type of appliances, and treatment length. Therefore, it’s best to discuss costs during your consultation to understand the financial commitment.

Many dental insurance plans cover orthodontic treatment, though coverage varies. Therefore, it’s important to review your plan or speak with your provider to understand coverage. Since every patient’s needs are unique, scheduling a consultation with our orthodontist will help you determine your treatment options.
