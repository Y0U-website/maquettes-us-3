Treatments

Dr. Travis provides all phases of general and cosmetic dentistry for children, adults, and seniors.

Preventive & General

Routine care that keeps problems from starting.

Cleanings & Exams Regular cleanings and thorough exams to keep every smile healthy.

Periodontal Maintenance Ongoing care to keep gum disease under control and your smile secure.

Dental Sealants A protective barrier that helps keep cavities from ever starting.

Night Guards Custom protection against the wear and cracks caused by grinding.

Restorative

Repairing and replacing teeth so they look and work naturally.

Tooth-Colored Fillings Composite fillings matched to your tooth, usually in a single visit.

Dental Crowns Full-coverage restorations that protect a damaged tooth and look like your own.

Root Canal One of the most common dental procedures performed, well over 14 million every year.

Dental Implants A permanent foundation for replacement teeth that look and feel like your own.

Implant-Supported Dentures A denture that clips onto implants instead of resting on the gum.

Porcelain Bridges Natural-looking replacements for missing teeth, bonded for the long term.

Dentures & Partials Comfortable, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth.

Tooth Extractions Careful, comfortable removal with a full discussion of your options.

Cosmetic

Refining the color, shape and balance of your smile.

Porcelain Veneers Thin porcelain facings that transform the color and shape of your smile.

Teeth Whitening Safe, quick and inexpensive whitening for a brighter smile.

Cosmetic Bonding Repair chips and gaps in a single visit with tooth-colored bonding.
