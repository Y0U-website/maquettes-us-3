Types of Dental Implant Restorations

At Handcrafted Dentistry , we understand that each patient is unique, and so are their dental needs. Our commitment to providing comprehensive and personalized care extends to the various types of dental implant restorations we offer, ensuring that your smile receives the tailored attention it deserves.

Our single-tooth implant restorations offer a seamless solution for those with a single missing tooth. This option involves the placement of a single dental implant in the gap, topped with a custom-crafted crown. The result is a natural-looking and fully functional tooth that integrates seamlessly with your existing dentition.

When multiple adjacent teeth are missing, implant-supported bridges provide an effective and stable solution. Dental implants are strategically placed to support a bridge, which spans the gap created by the missing teeth. This bridge not only restores the aesthetics of your smile but also ensures the preservation of adjacent teeth by eliminating the need for traditional bridgework.

Implant-retained dentures provide a secure and comfortable alternative for those seeking a comprehensive restoration for a full arch of missing teeth. Dental implants anchor the denture securely, eliminating the concerns of slippage or discomfort often associated with traditional dentures. This solution restores the appearance and functionality of a full set of teeth.

Handcrafted Dentistry proudly offers advanced solutions like All-on-4 and All-on-6 implant-supported dentures for patients with extensive tooth loss. These techniques involve the strategic placement of a reduced number of implants to support a complete set of upper or lower dentures. All-on-4s enhance stability and reduce the overall treatment time, allowing patients to enjoy the benefits of a fully restored smile more efficiently.
