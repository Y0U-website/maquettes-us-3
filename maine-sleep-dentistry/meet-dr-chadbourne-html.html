Get to Know Dr. Kyra Chadbourne, DDS, D-ABDSM

Dr. Chadbourne is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine. She has completed advanced training in sleep apnea treatment, making her the trusted referral for physicians in Falmouth.

Snoring Sleep Apnea?

Dr. Chadbourne’s passion for dental sleep medicine was sparked by recognizing the significant health correlations between sleep disordered breathing, genetics, and comorbidities. The connection between quality rest and overall well-being deeply fuels her dedication to help patients improve both their sleep and their health as a sleep dentist.

Dr. Chadbourne’s Background

Dr. Kyra Chadbourne received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the State University of New York at Buffalo, following her undergraduate studies at Yale University, where she earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology. She further enhanced her general dentistry education by completing a residency in Advanced Education for General Dentistry, also at the State University of New York at Buffalo.
