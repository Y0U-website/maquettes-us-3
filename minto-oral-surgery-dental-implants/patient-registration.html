Patient Registration

Before your first visit with us, please complete our secure online Patient Registration Form. After you have completed the form, please make sure to press the Submit button at the bottom to automatically send us your information. On your first visit to our office, we will have your completed form available for your signature. The security and privacy of your personal data is one of our primary concerns and we have taken every precaution to protect it.

Please contact Minto Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at (251) 990-5959 with any questions or concerns.
