Our Doctor

My mission:

“To provide my patients with the best and the highest level of dental care

and treatment and to help them and serve them in achieving optimal dental health,

smile and confidence in their life.”

Since his early childhood, Dr Mathew has been very passionate and thrilled about dentistry, and that passion prompted and persuaded him to pursue dental profession and ultimately become a dentist.

He earned his DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) degree from the highly esteemed and prestigious New York University, and because of his academic qualifications he was in the Advanced Placement DDS Class.

He has more than 20 years of skillful experience in the field of dentistry as a dentist,and these vast years of experience has given him the knowledge and skills,to diagnose,treat and provide the highest level of care to his patients.

In his spare time, he loves to spend time with his wife Elizabeth and two kids Jonathan and Sarah. In his leisure time, he loves to cook, and oil paint.
