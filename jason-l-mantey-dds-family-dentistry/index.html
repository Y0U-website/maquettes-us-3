The practice

Recently, Dr. Howard sold his practice to Dr. Jason L. Mantey and has retired from his career in dentistry. All the original staff is still with Dr. Mantey at the original location of Dr. Howard’s practice.

Dr. Mantey’s focus is the same as Dr. Howards: providing excellent dental care to families in his community. The office is welcoming new patients, and Dr. Mantey and his staff look forward to years of providing you and your family with the quality dental care.

Dr. Jason L. Mantey grew up on his family’s corn farm in Caro, Michigan. He attended Lake Superior State University where he earned a bachelor of science in biology. He then attended the University of Michigan School of Dentistry where he earned his doctorate in dental surgery.

Dr. Mantey has had years of experience, and has been in private practice in Bay City and Gaylord as an associate dentist. Recently Dr. Mantey took over the practice of Dr. Robert C. Howard, and moved with his family to Traverse City. He and his wife, Elise, have a two year old son, Lukas, and are expecting their second child in July.

Dr. Mantey enjoys spending time with his family, and enjoys all that northern Michigan has to offer, especially skiing and hunting.

He places an emphasis on continuing his education to stay current with the ever changing art and science of dentistry. He is a member of the Michigan Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the Resort District Dental Society. Make an appointment with Dr. Jason L Mantey, Traverse Family Dentistry today!
