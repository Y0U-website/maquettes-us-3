Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a good solution if only one or a few teeth are missing. In this case, your dentist will create false teeth in the form of a bridge. If you are fitted with a removable bridge, it will clip to the side of your teeth next to the empty space from the missing tooth. If you are fitted with a fixed bridge, the false teeth will be cemented permanently over the missing teeth.

Dentures

Full dentures may be required if most or all of your natural teeth have been lost. These can be held in place by implants, or attached to the gums with adhesives. Our dentists will create, fit and polish your dentures so that they are totally customized, comfortable, functional and natural looking.
