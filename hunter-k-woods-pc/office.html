Meet the Doctor

Meet Dr. Hunter K. Woods -- the name, talent and guiding force of Woods Orthodontics, and the dedicated creator of strong, lasting, beautiful smiles.

Dr. Woods in an expert in his craft. A graduate of UVA, the WVU School of Dentistry, and the WVU Department of Orthodontics, he is exceptionally well educated. A member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Virginia Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Virginia Dental Association, he is extraordinarily well qualified. But, for Dr. Woods, the practice of orthodontics goes far beyond education and qualification.

"Our logo states that a great smile is in our nature, notes Dr. Woods. "There s no doubt whatsoever about that. Creating healthy, radiant smiles is not just what I do. ..it s who I am."

And what of Dr. Woods other passions? He enjoys golf, tennis, travel and college football. But most of all, he is a quiet, committed family man. Dr. Woods reveals his own contented smile the smile of a man who has found both professional and personal fulfillment. Great smiles are definitely in his nature!
