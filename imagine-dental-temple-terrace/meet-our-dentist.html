Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Ernesto Guerra

Born and raised in Caracas, Venezuela, my parents instilled in me the value of education, hard work, and enjoying life. When I wasn’t working or studying, I sailed the Caribbean waters professionally for 12 years and in 1996, as I watched my older brother perform dentistry in our town, I developed an insatiable curiosity about the world of dental care. I then worked as a dentist for 6 years in my home country, before electing to complete a dental residency in Puerto Rico.

In addition to completing my residency in Puerto Rico, I married my amazing wife, Laura and we moved to Gainesville, Florida, where I worked as an assistant professor at the University of Florida. For the following 9 years, I practiced as an associate dentist at a large, busy office in town, building relationships with the community through innovative, functional, and compassionate dentistry.
