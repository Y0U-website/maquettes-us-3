Cosmetic Dentistry

The most remarkable thing we see after we have helped someone achieve a nicer smile is the positive effects in their life. They tell us how much better or different they now feel, and experience life, as a result of improving the appearance of their smile. It may be called ‘cosmetic dentistry’ but its effects far more reaching than the surface. We like to think of it as ‘life-changing dentistry’.

You only get one chance to make a great first impression, so why not be your best?

The health and unique beauty of your smile is a reflection of you and it should shine just like your personality. We want you to be able to feel confident in any situation, no matter where you are. We also want you to have a more beautiful confident smile without the hassle of multiple appointments or ‘abandoned’ monitoring.

For example, in the case of Invisalign, we use adaptive technology called Dental Monitoring and a custom acceleration system called Aevo. By incorporating these modern technologies we are able to get you to the finish line in half the time a normal case would likely take! Most cases are with Luxe are complete in only 8-12 months and rarely go over a year. Dr. Bown is also a one of Canada’s Masters in Digital Smile Design so she will examine your smile considering your whole face so you get something that is custom suited to you and doesn’t look like a denture.

During a consult, we decide if there is any need for additional services like bonding or repairs to damaged or misshapen teeth.

The most important way to give you a smile you love is to get the shapes right. Whiter and straighter teeth alone is not what makes the smile, it’s the shapes that are crucial in the final outcome.

By offering you years of experience in Smile Design and our unique technologies along with our Express Consult, we offer you much more than just straight teeth.

We realize each case is unique, and so Dr. Bown and Dr. White carefully analyze all aspects of your face, your bite and your smile before developing a custom treatment plan for you. Feel free to see many of our more recent before and afters on our Instagram , FB or Linkedin pages.
