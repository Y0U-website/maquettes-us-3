Dr. Shalise Holt: Orthodontic…

Dr. Holt has practiced in various settings, including Myorthos, Mulford Dental Group, and now as owner of Holt Orthodontics. She has extensive experience with self-ligating and twin brackets, aligner therapies (Invisalign®, Angel Aligner™, Essix), early interceptive treatment, and surgical orthodontics. She also trained with the craniofacial team at Lurie Children’s Hospital.

Dr. Holt combines efficiency, precision, and compassion in every treatment plan. She is skilled in digital orthodontics, using tools such as iTero® and CareStream scanners, Dolphin Imaging, and Cloud9 software to ensure accurate, comfortable, and personalized results.
