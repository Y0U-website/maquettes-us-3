About

Mentor, Ohio’s Family Dental Home

We’re your neighbors, and we’re honored to care for the smiles of the families we grew up alongside. Grand River Dental is built on community, compassion, and clinical excellence.

Built for Ohio Families

Grand River Dental was founded with a simple belief: every family deserves access to outstanding dental care delivered by people who genuinely care about them. We’re proud to open our doors in 2026 and excited to build lasting relationships with Ohio families for generations to come.

From day one, we’ve invested in the latest technology because it means better outcomes, less discomfort, and faster visits for you. Digital X-rays, 3D imaging, and same-day crowns are just a few of the tools we use to deliver exceptional care.

But technology is only part of the story. The other part is a team that listens, explains, and never rushes you — a team that will learn your name, remember your kids, and treat your family like our own.

Our Values

These aren’t just words on a wall — they’re the principles we apply to every patient interaction, every single day.

Compassion First We treat every patient the way we’d want our own family cared for — with patience, kindness, and genuine warmth.

Clinical Excellence Continuous education and advanced technology — we hold ourselves to the highest clinical standards in every procedure.

Honest Communication

We explain everything clearly, present all options, and never recommend treatment you don’t need. No surprises, no pressure.

Family Focused From toddlers to seniors, we welcome every generation and build lasting relationships that span decades.

Safety Always Rigorous ADA and CDC sterilization and infection control protocols — your safety is never negotiable.

Accessible Care We accept most major insurance and offer flexible financing so quality care is within reach for every family.
