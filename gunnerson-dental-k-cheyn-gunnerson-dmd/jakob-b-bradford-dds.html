Dr. Jakob B. Bradford

Jakob B. Bradford, DDS Loves Transforming Patients’ Smiles & Lives

Dr. Jakob Bradford grew up in the small town of Nephi, Utah, where he enjoyed playing football and basketball for Juab High School. After graduating from high school, he spent two years in Louisville, KY, on a mission for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, which introduced him to the world of dentistry.

Upon completing his mission, Dr. Bradford pursued a bachelor’s degree in nutrition at Southern Utah University, where he met his best friend and future wife. After getting married, he decided to pursue a career in dentistry. During his undergraduate studies, the couple welcomed their first beautiful baby girl and were later blessed with a second sweet girl, right after Dr. Bradford graduated from SUU.

The Bradford family then moved to Indianapolis, IN, where Dr. Bradford attended and graduated from Indiana University School of Dentistry . While there, they welcomed two more girls to their family.

Dr. Bradford is thrilled to join the wonderful team at Gunnerson Dental and be a part of the Payson, Utah community. He loves serving this community as a general dentist. He especially enjoys transforming patients’ lives with dental implants, the gold standard in tooth replacement.

Dr. Bradford’s family is his motivation. When not working, he spends all his free time with them. As a resident of Utah, he enjoys exploring the state’s natural beauty and spending quiet nights at home, watching movies and eating popcorn.
