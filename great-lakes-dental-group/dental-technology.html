Digital X-Rays

The Digital X-rays provide detailed images of your teeth and surrounding structures while using significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays.

These images allow us to detect issues early and monitor your oral health with greater precision.

  • Faster image capture and instant viewing
  • Enhanced image quality for better diagnosis
  • Safer, low-radiation technology

Panoramic X-Rays

Panoramic X-rays give us a full view of your entire mouth in a single image, including your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures.

This technology is especially helpful for:

With a comprehensive view, we can create more accurate and effective treatment plans.

  • Evaluating overall oral health
  • Planning treatments like implants or extractions
  • Detecting hidden issues beneath the surface
