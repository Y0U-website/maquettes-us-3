Orthodontic Services We Offer

At My Dentist of Port St. Lucie, we offer personalized orthodontic care for patients of all ages. Whether you’re looking for something discreet or need to correct more complex bite issues, our services are designed to give you options so you can choose what feels right for you.

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

A discreet, removable option that uses clear trays to gradually shift your teeth. Invisalign® is great for teens and adults looking for flexibility and comfort.

Braces

With options like metal or ceramic braces, they’re a reliable way to correct bite issues and misalignment for patients of all ages.

Retainers

After treatment, retainers keep your teeth in place and help preserve your smile for years to come.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

The length of orthodontic treatment can vary depending on your specific needs and the type of treatment you choose. On average, braces are worn for 18 to 24 months, while Invisalign® treatment may range from 6 to 18 months. Some cases may be shorter or longer depending on the complexity of the alignment issues.

During your consultation at My Dentist of Port St. Lucie, we’ll evaluate your smile and provide a personalized timeline so you know exactly what to expect.
