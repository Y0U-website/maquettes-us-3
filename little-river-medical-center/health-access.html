Serving Myrtle Beach & Surrounding Communities

Conveniently located on Mr. Joe White Avenue in Myrtle Beach , our medical office serves individuals and families from nearby areas including Arcadian Shores, Carolina Forest, Pine Island, Forestbrook, Socastee, and Surfside Beach. Patients visit this location for a wide range of services, including: routine checkups, preventive screenings, chronic disease management, women’s health, behavioral health care, and same-day sick visits.

Access to reliable healthcare close to home is important for families across Grand Strand and Horry County . For many residents, local health centers play an essential role in providing routine care, preventive services, and support for long-term health. LRMC is committed to supporting the health of the entire community by making affordable, high-quality care available to all.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can minimize wait times and make sure you see the right provider. However, same-day appointments may be available for urgent health concerns. Call the Myrtle Beach Health Access Center or request an appointment online .

Can I schedule a same-day appointment?

Yes. The Myrtle Beach Health Access Center often offers same-day appointments for sick visits and urgent medical concerns when availability allows. Calling early in the day can help secure a same-day visit.

What services are available at the Myrtle Beach Health Access Center?

The Myrtle Beach Health Access Center offers a variety of healthcare services including primary care, preventive checkups, chronic disease management, pediatric care, and dental services. Patients can also be connected with additional services across the Little River Medical Center network when needed.

What areas does the Myrtle Beach Health Access Center serve?

Patients visit the Myrtle Beach Health Access Center from across the Grand Strand and Horry County, including nearby communities such as Arcadian Shores, Carolina Forest, Pine Island, Forestbrook, Socastee, and Surfside Beach. The location provides convenient access to primary care for residents throughout the surrounding area.

Do you accept Medicaid or other insurance plans?

Little River Medical Center accepts a variety of insurance plans, including Medicaid and Medicare. Coverage can vary depending on the service and provider, so it’s best to contact the clinic directly to confirm your specific insurance plan. Learn more about our payment options here .

How do I become a new patient?

New patients are welcome at Little River Medical Center. You can call the Myrtle Beach Health Access Center directly or submit an appointment request through the website to get started. Learn more about becoming a new patient here .
