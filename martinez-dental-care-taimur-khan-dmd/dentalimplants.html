DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants are the most durable and lifelike way to replace missing teeth. The process begins with placing a small titanium post into the jawbone, where it fuses naturally through a process called osseointegration, creating a strong, lasting foundation. Once healed, the implant is restored with a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture. Dr. Khan works closely with trusted surgical specialists during placement and personally designs your final restoration to ensure a perfect fit, natural look, and comfortable bite.

After the implant has successfully integrated with the jawbone, a custom crown is placed on top carefully designed to match the shape, size, and shade of your surrounding teeth. Dr. Khan uses durable, tooth-colored materials for a seamless finish that restores both the aesthetics and function of your smile. Every crown is precisely crafted to feel comfortable and perform like a natural tooth.

For patients missing all upper or lower teeth, full-arch implant restorations provide a secure, long-term alternative to traditional dentures. Using as few as four to six implants, we can anchor a full set of fixed teeth that look and feel incredibly natural. These full-arch solutions restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence—without slipping, discomfort, or adhesives.

At Martinez Dental Care, we understand that replacing missing teeth is about more than just function, it’s about restoring your confidence, health, and quality of life. Dr. Khan takes a personalized, thoughtful approach to every dental implant case, ensuring that your final result feels just as good as it looks. From your initial consultation to the final crown placement, you’ll experience expert guidance, transparent care, and a seamless, supportive process tailored to your smile goals.
