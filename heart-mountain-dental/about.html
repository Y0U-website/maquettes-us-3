Our Story

Cody Is Home and So Is Heart Mountain Dental

At Heart Mountain Dental, our story starts right here in Cody, Wyoming. Founded in 2015 by Dr. Josh George, a proud Cody native, our practice was built on a vision of providing honest, compassionate, and expert dental care to the Big Horn Basin. With deep local roots and a commitment to community, our team treats every patient like family because to us, you are.

Dr. George and his team combine modern dental techniques with the values that matter most: trust, kindness, and excellence. Over the past decade, Heart Mountain Dental has grown into a trusted name in the region, serving patients of all ages with everything from routine cleanings to complex dental procedures. As the only VA-approved dental provider in the Big Horn Basin, we’re also proud to care for our nation’s Veterans with the respect and attention they deserve.

Whether you’ve been with us for years or are scheduling your first appointment, we’re honored to be a part of your dental journey.
