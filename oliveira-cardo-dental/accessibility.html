We regularly review hartsdaledentalcare.com and make updates when we identify accessibility barriers or receive feedback from visitors. Some website content may be provided by third-party tools, embedded appointment systems, maps, videos, plugins, or outside platforms. While we do not control every third-party service, we encourage accessibility improvements wherever possible.

Need Help Accessing Information?

If you experience difficulty using any part of this website, or if you need assistance accessing information, scheduling an appointment, understanding services, or contacting the office, please call us. When Contacting Us

To help us respond quickly, please tell us the page or feature you were trying to use, the nature of the accessibility issue, your preferred method of contact, and any assistive technology or browser you were using if you are comfortable sharing that information.

We take accessibility feedback seriously and will review requests as promptly as possible.

Third-Party Content

Our website may link to or include content from third-party websites or services. Oliveira and Cardo cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party platforms, but we encourage vendors and partners to provide accessible digital experiences.
