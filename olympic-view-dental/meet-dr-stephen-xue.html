Cosmetic and Family dentist in Seattle, WA

Dr. Stephen Xue

Dr. Stephen grew up in San Ramon, California, where he spent winters snowboarding and high school years playing ice hockey and tennis. He completed his undergraduate studies at UC Davis, and earned his dental degree from Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia.

In his free time, Dr. Stephen enjoys mountain biking, golfing, skiing, and hiking. All things that drew him to the beauty of the Pacific Northwest.

Following dental school, Dr. Stephen practiced as an associate in New England, where he received advanced training in: Cosmetic Dentistry, Dental Implants, Orthodontics, and Full Mouth Rehabilitation. He aims to uphold Olympic View Dental’s commitment to exceptional dentistry with compassion, attention to detail, and a genuine dedication to your dental health.
