Implant-Supported Bridges

Implant-supported bridges provide a reliable way to replace several missing teeth in a row while maintaining strength and stability across the smile. Unlike traditional dental bridges that rely on neighboring teeth for support, implant-supported bridges are anchored to dental implants placed in the jawbone. This approach provides greater durability and helps protect surrounding natural teeth.

At NuSmile Implant Studio in Chicago, implant bridges are designed using detailed digital diagnostics and prosthodontic treatment planning.

Dr. Faisel Ashtewi carefully evaluates the bite, jaw structure, and overall smile aesthetics before creating a customized restoration.

This level of planning allows the bridge to distribute bite forces evenly while maintaining natural-looking results. For patients missing multiple teeth, implant-supported bridges offer a long-term solution that restores chewing ability, improves smile appearance, and supports overall oral health. With proper planning and high-quality materials, these restorations can provide exceptional durability while blending seamlessly with natural teeth.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Bridges

Implant-supported bridges offer numerous advantages over traditional dental bridges, including improved stability, enhanced chewing ability, and a more natural appearance. These bridges are anchored securely to dental implants, which are surgically placed in the jawbone, ensuring that they remain firmly in place while providing optimal support for your smile.

Additionally, implant-supported bridges help maintain the integrity of the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth. This long-term solution not only restores functionality but also contributes to overall oral health, making it a preferred choice for many patients.

The Process of Getting an Implant-Supported Bridge

The journey to a new smile with an implant-supported bridge begins with a thorough evaluation by Dr. Ashtewi. This initial consultation includes assessing your oral health and discussing your goals, ensuring that the treatment plan aligns with your expectations.

Following the evaluation, the implant placement procedure is performed, which typically involves a minor surgical intervention. After a healing period, the custom-made bridge is attached to the implants, resulting in a seamless and durable restoration that enhances both function and aesthetics.
