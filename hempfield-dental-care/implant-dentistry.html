Implant Dentistry

  • Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, making them a long-term solution for tooth loss.
  • Protects Jawbone Health : When a tooth is missing, the jawbone in that area can deteriorate over time. Dental implants stimulate the bone, helping to preserve its structure and strength.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth : Unlike dental bridges, which require adjacent teeth to be filed down, implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support.
  • Improved Functionality : Because we anchor implants into the jawbone, they provide stability similar to natural teeth, making it easy to eat, speak, and confidently smile.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

At Hempfield Dental Care, we believe in making your experience as smooth and stress-free as possible. Here’s a step-by-step look at what you can expect during the dental implant process:

Step 1: Initial Consultation

Your dental implant journey begins with a thorough consultation at our office. During this visit, we’ll assess your oral health, review your medical history, and take any necessary images to determine if you’re a good candidate for a dental implant. This is also the perfect opportunity for you to ask questions and discuss your goals for restoring your smile.

Step 2: Implant Placement

The next step is placing the dental implant, a small titanium post, into your jawbone. Don’t worry—this is a routine procedure performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Titanium is a biocompatible material, which means it’s safe for your body and integrates naturally with your bone.

Step 3: Healing and Osseointegration

After we place the implant, your jawbone needs time to heal and bond with the implant in a process called osseointegration. This step is crucial for creating a strong, secure foundation for your new tooth. Healing typically takes a few months, but we’ll guide you every step of the way and monitor your progress closely.

Step 4: Crown Placement

Once the implant fully integrates with your jawbone, it’s time to complete your restoration. We’ll customize a crown to blend perfectly with your natural teeth. Once the crown is securely attached to the implant, your new tooth will be ready to shine!

Are You a Candidate for a Dental Implant?

Most people missing a tooth can be candidates for a dental implant. However, certain factors, like the health of your gums and jawbone, play a role in determining eligibility. If your jawbone has deteriorated, you might still be able to get an implant after a bone grafting procedure. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate these factors and work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan.
