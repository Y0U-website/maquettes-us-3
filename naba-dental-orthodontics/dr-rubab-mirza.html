DR. RUBAB MIRZA

Dr. Rubab Mirza was born and raised in India before immigrating to the United States in 1995. She attended the University of Southern California and graduated with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2004.

Dr. Mirza favors the practice of conservative dentistry, emphasizing the aspects of protecting the teeth and mouth. She especially focuses on restoring teeth appearance and function, helping both children and adults maintain a healthy mouth. She is a certified member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Texas Dental Association, and the Greater Houston Dental Society. In her spare time, she continually devotes herself to expanding her knowledge and pursuing additional education in the field of dentistry, keeping up with the latest trends in treatments and technology.

Dr. Mirza loves working with children and adults. She brings this love with her to NABA Dental. She helps keep patients at ease during procedures, making sure they are comfortable. Dr. Mirza keep their focus on friendly conversation and teaching them about dental health in a fun and positive manner, which keeps their minds off any procedure at hand, from cleanings to fillings .

She is a very driven and passionate individual with an intense focus on her family. Dr. Mirza is very family-oriented, often volunteering and being a very active participant at her children’s school. Dr. Mirza is married and has five children.
