Bonnie Mac Lean Donzella, R.D.H.

Bonnie Donzella graduated from Onondaga Community college Syracuse, New York in 1970. With 42 years of experience as a dental hygienist has helped many generations of patients with their dental health. She moved to California in 1979 and has worked at this office since then. She has her son Mathew who teaches K-8th multi subjects. She loves hummingbird and warm days in Napa. She is a wonderful baker and we get to enjoy her recipes on holidays.

Sarah Ramirez, RDA, Patient Coordinator

Sarah Ramirez has been in the dental field for the past 12 years. She attended East Bay Regional Occupational Program’s dental assisting course during her senior in high school. Sarah enjoys working as a clinical assistant as well as servicing patient’s needs in the Front Office. Sarah is experienced in General dentistry, Orthodontics and Pediatric dentistry. In her free time, Sarah prefers to spend time with her family and dogs. They enjoy outdoor activities like dirt bike riding, camping, swimming and bicycle riding. Sarah and her son are active in Boy scouts and church activities.

Debbie Alo, R.D.H.

Debbie is a long time local resident, growing up in Newark and Graduating from Newark High in 1980. She went on to graduate from Chabot College in 1988 with an AA in Dental Hygiene, from Ohlone College in 1990 with an AA in Natural Science and from CSUH in 1992 with a BA in Biological Science. Debbie is also a long time member of the American Dental Hygienist’s Association. When she’s not educating patients about dental health issues, she enjoys travelling, swimming and training for 5K’s.
