Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Alireza Hamidzadeh

Dr. Hamidzadeh (Dr. H) has been with Happy Smiles Dental since its inception in 2016. He focuses on surgical and same day interventions and is unique among general dentists in his ability to offer the comfort of IV sedation in the private practice setting.

Prior to Happy Smiles Dental, Dr. Hamidzadeh proudly served as a Comprehensive Dentist in the US Army, where he received advanced training in all specialties of dentistry both didactically and clinically. He completed a rigorous two-year residency at Carl R. Darnall Medical Center at Fort Cavazos (formerly known as Fort Hood), Texas, culminating in a Masters of Science degree in advanced Oral Biology from the Uniformed Services University at Walter Reed National Medical Center.

During his residency, he conducted the first ever human randomized clinical trial on application of a certain wound dressing (Sockit!) after impacted wisdom teeth extraction and received the U.S. Army Commendation Medal for his achievements.

As a lifelong learner, Dr. Hamidzadeh has continued to push the boundaries of his clinical skills through intensive continuing education training. His focus has been on Oral Surgery, the placement and restoration of Dental Implants, Advanced Grafting, Oral Medicine, Pain Management, and IV Sedation. He also has extensive experience in restorative dentistry such as Full Mouth Reconstruction and Rehabilitation, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Root Canal Therapy.

Dr. Hamidzadeh first graduated from engineering school with the highest honor (summa cum laude) and worked as an engineer for many years before studying dentistry. At New York University School of Dentistry, Dr. Hamidzadeh graduated at the top of his class with extra recognition in Oral Medicine.

After the Army, he served as a Clinical Adjunct Professor at the U.S. Navy Postgraduate Dental School at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center, where Navy dentists receive their specialty training. As a Veteran, he values honor, integrity and the concept of service before self.

Patients from all over the mid-Atlantic region and beyond seek care with Dr. Hamidzadeh for his unique combination of skills and credentials. Dr. Hamidzadeh welcomes new patients and looks forward to serving the community near or afar at Happy Smiles Dental. If You Don’t Have a Happy Smile, You Can Always Take One of His!

Dr. Omar Salam

Dentist Dr. Omar Salam is a dedicated general dentist known for his calm demeanor and ability to help patients feel comfortable and confident throughout their care. He believes exceptional dentistry begins with trust, clear communication, and a personalized approach, and he is committed to creating a welcoming environment for every patient.

Dr. Salam completed his undergraduate studies in Oregon, an experience that contributed to his well-rounded perspective and patient-focused approach to healthcare. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University, where he received comprehensive training in general dentistry with an emphasis on restorative, preventive, and comprehensive treatment planning.

With a thoughtful and conservative clinical philosophy, Dr. Salam focuses on providing care that is precise, ethical, and tailored to each individual’s needs. He takes time to clearly explain treatment options and believes in partnering with patients to help them make informed decisions about their oral health. His calm chairside manner helps patients feel at ease, with a focus on comfort, reassurance, and clear communication throughout every visit.

Dr. Salam is committed to continuing education and staying current with advances in dental materials, techniques, and technology. Outside of the office, he enjoys exercising, gaming, and exploring the DMV area. He looks forward to serving his community and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.
