Dentist in Detroit MI

Dr. Megan M. Faircloth

Dr. Faircloth began her education at Ferris State University, then earned her Master’s Degree from Case Western Reserve University and ultimately her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Michigan.

For Dr. Faircloth, dentistry has always been about more than healthy teeth. It s about helping people feel better about themselves. That sense of purpose is what drew her to the field, and it continues to drive her work every day. She takes particular pride in creating positive experiences for patients who come to the office with dental anxiety, taking the time to make every visit feel comfortable, unhurried, and judgment-free.

Dr. Faircloth is committed to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry. She has pursued advanced training in techniques like Bioclear, clear aligner therapy, and biomimetic dentistry, allowing her to offer her patients the most innovative, minimally invasive options available.

Outside the office, Dr. Faircloth leads a full and varied life. She loves to read, garden, travel, cycle and soak up family time in Detroit with her partner Daniel, their two little ones, Walter and Beatrice, and Tilly, their long-haired miniature dachshund.
