Dr. Quyen Ying

Dr. Quyen is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, a member of the College of Diplomates, and a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also a member of the American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, Alabama Academy of Pediatric Dentists, Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry, and Birmingham Pediatric Dental Association.

From North Carolina to Pennsylvania to Massachusetts, then to Oregon, Dr. Quyen and her family have finally decided to call Alabama home. She currently resides in Mountain Brook with her husband and two sons. In her free time. Dr. Quyen loves to read, cook, explore new restaurants, and travel. Meet our Staff
