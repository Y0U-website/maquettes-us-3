The practice

Pediatric Dentistry We provide gentle, kid-first dental care from the very first tooth through the teen years. Cleanings, exams, and fillings build healthy habits that last a lifetime.

Special Needs Dentistry Finding the right dentist for a child with special needs can feel overwhelming. Our team provides patient, adaptive care in a calm and welcoming setting.

Sedation Dentistry Nitrous oxide, oral conscious sedation, and general anesthesia help anxious children and kids with special needs receive care comfortably.

Orthodontics Our in-house orthodontic team straightens smiles for kids, teens, and adults. Braces, Invisalign, and clear aligners are all available close to home.

Cleanings & Exams Routine cleanings and exams do more than keep teeth bright. They help prevent cavities and catch small problems early.
