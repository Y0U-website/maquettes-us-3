Braces

Braces are a proven orthodontic treatment that use small brackets and a thin wire to gently guide teeth into better alignment over time. They can correct crowding, spacing, and bite problems like overbites, underbites, and crossbites, making your smile easier to keep clean and more comfortable to use. You may feel mild pressure after adjustments, but visits are quick, and most patients get used to braces within the first week or two.

Learn More Explore Your Orthodontics Treatment Options Clear Aligners Clear aligners are a series of custom, removable trays that gently move your teeth into better alignment over time. They are nearly invisible, smooth to wear, and you can take them out to eat, brush, and floss, which many teens and adults find convenient. For best results, aligners need to be worn most of the day, and we will check your progress regularly to keep your treatment on track.

Learn More Limited Orthodontic Treatment Limited orthodontic treatment focuses on correcting a specific concern, like mild crowding, spacing, or a small area of teeth that need to be straightened, when your overall bite is stable. It is often shorter than comprehensive treatment and can be a good option if your main goal is improving alignment in one area. At your consultation, we will confirm whether limited treatment is appropriate and explain what it can and cannot fix so expectations are clear.

Learn More Comprehensive Orthodontic Treatment Comprehensive orthodontic treatment addresses the full alignment of your teeth and how your bite fits together, not just one area. It is designed for cases with more significant crowding, spacing, or bite concerns like overbites, underbites, crossbites, or open bites, where long term function matters as much as appearance. Treatment may use braces or clear aligners, followed by retainers to help keep your results stable.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

An early orthodontic evaluation helps spot crowding, bite issues, and jaw growth concerns while your child is still developing. Many kids do not need treatment right away, but a check around age 7 can help determine the best timing. If early treatment is recommended, Phase 1 orthodontics focuses on guiding growth and creating space for incoming permanent teeth, which can make later treatment simpler and support a healthier, more comfortable bite.

Smile Transformation

Orthodontic treatment can do more than straighten teeth, it can improve how your bite fits together and make your smile easier to keep clean. As your teeth move into better alignment, many patients notice increased confidence and a healthier bite over time. Your results and timeline will depend on your specific needs, and during your consultation, we’ll walk you through what’s realistic and create a plan tailored to you.
