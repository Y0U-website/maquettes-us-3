Meet Our Doctors

Drs. Sage and Alene Humphries bring over 37 years of combined experience, having treated more than 25,000 orthodontic cases. Their specialized training and clinical expertise ensure high-quality care tailored to each patient’s needs. As Sapphire Invisalign providers, they are among the top clear aligner treatment specialists in the country.

Whether you choose traditional braces or clear aligners, you can trust in their care. They prioritize patient education, helping you understand your options and stay motivated throughout your treatment.
