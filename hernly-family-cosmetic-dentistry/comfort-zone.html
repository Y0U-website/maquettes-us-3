At Hernly Family & Cosmetic Dentistry, our friendly team of professionals offers personalized care, which will quickly put you at ease. We pride ourselves in making your dental experiences as pleasant as possible. The first step toward a relaxing visit is educating patients about their treatment in advance. If you have any questions before or during a procedure, we carefully review everything so that you completely understand your treatment. Building trust reduces fear and can help overcome any negative experiences you may have had in the past.

We use the latest techniques to reduce potential dental discomfort and create a relaxing atmosphere. All options are discussed with our patients during the treatment planning consultation. These options include local anesthesia, nitrous oxide, oral sedation and intravenous sedation.
