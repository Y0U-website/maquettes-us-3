The practice

Whether you are visiting for a routine cleaning, cosmetic treatment, or restorative care, our team is here to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile.

As a locally owned family dental practice, we take the time to get to know you and your family, understand your goals, and provide care tailored to your individual needs. Many of our patients have been with us for generations, and we consider it a privilege to be part of their lifelong oral health journey.

Unlike many large corporate dental organizations, our practice is guided by the relationships we build within our community. You&#x27;ll see familiar faces, experience continuity of care, and work with a team that values communication, compassion, and clinical excellence.

General Dentistry We provide comprehensive care for your entire family, from routine cleanings to complex restorative treatments.

Cosmetic Dentistry Elevate your smile with our advanced cosmetic treatments, including whitening and aesthetic restorations.

Oral Health Care We prioritize your long-term health with preventive care and specialized treatments for optimal wellness.

Dr. Obenauf is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered dentistry with a focus on clinical excellence and long-term oral health. He believes in taking the time to understand each patient&#x27;s unique needs and goals, creating personalized treatment plans that help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Outside of the office, Dr. Obenauf enjoys spending time outdoors with family and friends. His interests include sailing, water sports, winter sports, and exploring new adventures whenever possible.
