Dr. Kyle Katynski

Dr. Katynski (also known as Dr. K) has enjoyed over a decade of practice in the dental field. He proudly attended the University of Michigan and graduated from both the dental program in 2016 and the dental hygiene program in 2012. Over this time, Dr. Katynski has enjoyed helping patients gain more confidence in their smile through smile transformations and cosmetic makeovers. Placing and restoring dental implants is another service Dr. Katynski is happy to provide for patients to help replace missing teeth, support dentures or even restore full arch function.

Outside of Work...

When he isn’t practicing, Dr. Katynski enjoys biking, kayaking, running and has completed numerous 5ks, a marathon and Olympic triathlon. He is a big sports fan of the University of Michigan and Detroit teams, especially the Red Wings. Together with his fiancé Alysa and their dog Lola, they enjoy traveling around Michigan to enjoy small towns, cider mills, scenic hikes and all the treasures Michigan has to offer.
