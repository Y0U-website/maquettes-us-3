What Is Invisalign®?

Dentist Durham

Invisalign® is an orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, removable aligners to straighten your teeth gradually and effectively. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign® aligners are virtually invisible, custom-fitted, and designed for comfort, so they blend seamlessly into your smile. These aligners gently guide your teeth into their ideal positions without the need for metal wires or brackets.

Worn for at least 22 hours each day, Invisalign® aligners are easily removable for meals, brushing, and flossing, making them convenient for maintaining oral hygiene throughout the treatment process. This system can address a variety of dental issues, including crowding, gaps, and general misalignment. Invisalign® provides a discreet and flexible orthodontic experience, allowing you to enhance your smile without the visibility or fixed nature of traditional braces. It’s a great option for those seeking a comfortable, clear solution to straighten their teeth.

Did You Know…

Because Invisalign® aligners are removable, you won’t face the food restrictions or hygiene challenges that come with traditional braces.

Benefits of Invisalign®

Dentist Durham

Aesthetic Appeal

Invisalign® aligners are clear and virtually invisible, offering a discreet way to straighten teeth without the visibility of traditional metal braces. They allow you to improve your smile with confidence, maintaining your appearance throughout treatment.

Comfort and Convenience

Made from smooth, comfortable plastic, Invisalign® aligners are gentle on your mouth, reducing the risk of irritation and sores commonly caused by traditional braces. Their removable design offers added convenience, allowing you to easily eat, drink, and maintain your oral hygiene routine.

Enhanced Oral Health

With no metal brackets or wires, Invisalign® aligners make it easier to brush and floss your teeth thoroughly, reducing the risk of plaque buildup, tooth decay, and gum disease. Their removability ensures better oral hygiene during treatment, promoting long-term dental health.

The Invisalign® Process

Dentist Durham

Digital Impressions & Treatment Planning

Dr. Gaddis begins by using advanced 3D scanning technology to take precise digital impressions of your teeth. This allows her to create a fully customized treatment plan and show you a virtual preview of your smile transformation before treatment even begins.

Series of Custom Aligners

You’ll receive a series of clear, removable aligners designed to gradually shift your teeth into place. Each set is worn for about two weeks, applying gentle pressure to guide your teeth into better alignment with minimal discomfort and no metal brackets.

Monitoring & Results

Throughout treatment, Dr. Gaddis schedules periodic check-ins to ensure everything is progressing as planned. If adjustments are needed, she’ll update your aligners accordingly. Once your teeth are aligned, you’ll be fitted with a retainer to help maintain your results and keep your smile looking its best.
