The practice

Once your child starts teething or has their first birthday—whichever comes first—it’s time for their first dental appointment at Hero Pediatric Dentistry!

We serve kids throughout infancy, childhood, and adolescence with any of their dental needs. If you bring your child consistently to our office to see a pediatric dentist in northern Virginia and encourage good habits for dental health from a young age, they’ll have the superpower of healthy and happy teeth for years to come!

We have locations in Gainesville and Herndon so you can easily access great dental care for your child.

Regular dental care is essential for lifelong, healthy smiles!

We’re the experts in making dentist visits fun, safe, and effective for kids. See for yourself what you can expect from Hero Pediatric Dentistry!
