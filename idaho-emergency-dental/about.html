Dr. Walters originally grew up in the foothills of South Carolina hiking and rock climbing. He decided to go to dental school because he loves working with his hands and being able to provide a patient with immediate relief and a more confident smile.

Dr. Walters went to dental school at LSU then moved to Boise to receive more training in implants and surgery at Idaho State University.

When asked why he joined Idaho Emergency Dental he said, “I wanted to work for Idaho Emergency Dental because there were no options for patients to receive care after hours or on the weekend, and I can help fill that need.”

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality emergency dental care to every patient who walks through our doors. We believe that no one should have to suffer through dental pain while waiting for an appointment. Our team is committed to offering same-day care, affordable treatment options, and a compassionate approach that puts your comfort and well-being first.

Our Values

Every decision we make is guided by what is best for our patients. Your comfort, safety, and satisfaction are our top priorities.

Excellence in Treatment

We maintain the highest standards of dental care through continuous education, advanced technology, and proven techniques.

Integrity & Trust
