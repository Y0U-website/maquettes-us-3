What Is the Process for Getting Dental Implants?

We believe that informed patients make the best decisions for their health. Here’s what you can expect from the dental implant procedure in Des Moines at our office:

  • Consultation & Imaging – Using CBCT scans and 3D imaging, we evaluate your bone structure and create a personalized plan.
  • Surgical Placement – Dr. Cholewa places the implant post precisely where your tooth root once was.
  • Healing & Osseointegration – Over several months, your implant fuses naturally with your jawbone.
  • Final Restoration – Once healed, we place a crown, bridge, or denture for a complete and functional smile.

Can I Get a Dental Implant After a Tooth Extraction?

Yes, many of our patients receive dental implants after a tooth extraction. In some cases, Dr. Cholewa may even place the implant the same day the tooth is removed. For others, a short healing period or bone graft may be recommended. Our CBCT technology helps us assess the best timing for your implant to ensure lasting success.

Is Bone Grafting Needed Before Dental Implants?

Not everyone needs a bone graft, but it’s common in areas where the jawbone has deteriorated. Grafting helps provide the necessary support for the implant and increases the likelihood of long-term success.
