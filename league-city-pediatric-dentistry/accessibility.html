0 ACCESSBILITY Our Commitment to Accessibility Improvement Updates (Last Updated: May 31, 2026)

League City Pediatric Dentistry is committed to making our website and online services accessible to all patients and visitors, including people with disabilities. We are actively working to improve the accessibility and usability of this website and are prioritizing the pages and functions families use most, including appointment requests, contact information, office location details, and core service pages.

Accessibility Help & Contact Info

As part of our continued commitment to accessibility for all, we are currently remediating known accessibility issues on our website to better align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our current accessibility target. During this work, some portions of the website may not yet fully meet that standard.

We are actively working on accessibility improvements and expect substantial updates to be implemented within the next 3 weeks. We will continue reviewing, testing, and improving the website as part of our ongoing accessibility efforts.

If you experience difficulty accessing any content, feature, or function on this website, please contact us so we can provide assistance and work to address the issue promptly.

When contacting us, please include the page URL, a brief description of the issue, and, if possible, the device, browser, or assistive technology you were using. This information helps us investigate and respond more effectively.

While updates are in progress, patients and families may also contact our office directly by phone or email for help with appointment scheduling, office information, forms, and other services.
