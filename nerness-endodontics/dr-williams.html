Board Certification

In the summer of 2026, Dr. Williams completed board certification, a voluntary process, which places him in a distinguished group (23 %) of all endodontists who have demonstrated exceptional knowledge and technical skill according to standards established by the American Board of Endodontics. Certification is a 3-part process that requires passing a written exam, oral exam, and critique of a portfolio of completed endodontic cases. Certification demonstrates a commitment to the highest standard of excellence in the specialty of endodontics.
