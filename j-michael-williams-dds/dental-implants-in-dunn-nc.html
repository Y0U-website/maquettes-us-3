Dental Implants

We ensure the stability of dental implants by inserting them beneath the gumline and into the jawbone. This important step allows the implant to form a strong connection with the bone tissue. The bone cells fuse with the implant through osseointegration, creating a strong bond. This process results in a stable foundation capable of withstanding the forces of biting and chewing for many years.

  • Abutment: A connector piece that is attached to the titanium post
  • Prosthetic tooth or teeth: A restoration, such as a crown , bridge , or denture , that connects to the abutment

What To Expect During Dental Implant Placement

On the day of your implant surgery, we insert the dental implants into the jawbone. We first administer local anesthesia to numb the treatment area so that your procedure isn’t painful. Once you are comfortable and relaxed, we will make an incision in your gums, exposing the jawbone. We then drill a hole and place the implant post deep into the bone to serve as an artificial tooth root. The process typically takes between one and two hours per implant needed.

After we place the implant, a healing period of 3-6 months is necessary so that the implant can fuse with the bone. Once healing is complete, we attach the abutment to the implant and place the prosthetic tooth on top.

It’s important to note that when you lose a tooth, your jawbone gradually weakens over time because it is no longer stimulated by the tooth’s roots. This loss of bone can lead to insufficient bone density or volume for a dental implant. We often recommend bone grafting to establish a solid foundation in such cases. After we perform the bone graft, it takes several months for the graft material to integrate with the existing bone before we can place the dental implant.

Explore Your Dental Implant Options

For patients who find the surgical requirements and timeline of standard dental implants challenging, we offer mini dental implants as an alternative. While traditional implants are roughly 5 millimeters in diameter, mini implants are less than 3 millimeters. Mini implants also have a simplified design that streamlines the procedure. A mini implant consists of a small, one-piece titanium post with a ball-shaped end that extends above the gum line. After we place the mini implant, we attach the prosthetic tooth to the ball-shaped connector.

Their slim size and straightforward design allow us to place the implant using a minimally invasive technique. Instead of making incisions and using stitches, we create a small pilot hole through the gums and insert the implant. This technique typically results in minimal bleeding and much faster healing.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer significant advantages for oral health, starting with bone preservation. Unlike conventional dentures or bridges, implants stimulate the jawbone just like natural tooth roots. This stimulation helps prevent bone resorption, which is a common issue after tooth loss. By preventing bone loss, dental implants help maintain the jawbone’s functionality and prevent changes to your face’s appearance.

Another key benefit is that dental implants do not impact adjacent teeth. Unlike bridges that require the alteration of nearby healthy teeth for support, implants stand independently. This preservation of remaining natural teeth helps maintain their strength and integrity, reducing the risk of future dental issues.

Additionally, dental implants are a durable and long-lasting solution for tooth replacement. Unlike other replacement solutions that may require replacements or adjustments, implants can last a lifetime. This makes them a reliable and wise investment in your oral health.
