Dr. David Collier

Dr. David followed in his father’s footsteps and graduated from the University of Minnesota. After completing his pediatric residency, he participated in a rotation through the Gillette Specialty Children’s Hospital in their outpatient and inpatient areas. This provided the opportunity to spend additional time with the cleft palate and craniofacial team, and Dr. David has since joined their team as a staff clinician.

When Dr. David is not working at K.O.A.L.A Dental Care or Gillette Children’s Hospital, he enjoys spending time with his wife and three children, two daughters and one son. They also enjoy their two dogs. He combines family life with his other passion, sports. Together they attend Gopher games, ski and enjoy the outdoors.

Dr. David is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

In addition, he is a member of the following:

  • American Cleft Palate-Craniofacial Association
  • Minnesota Academy of Pediatric Dentistry
  • Minnesota Dental Association
  • The American Orthodontic Society
  • American Dental Association
  • American Cleft Palate-Craniofacial Association
  • Minnesota Academy of Pediatric Dentistry
  • Minnesota Dental Association
