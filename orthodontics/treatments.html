Confident Smiles Through Adolescence

Between the ages of 11 and 15 is the most common time for orthodontic treatment – and for good reasons. This is when most permanent teeth have erupted and are in place, making it easier to detect and correct crooked teeth, gaps, and bad bites. Additionally, many negative associations with braces are not present at this age, and children are often willing to wear them to fit in with their peers. Orthodontic treatment during this time also allows us to take advantage of the child’s rapid growth to correct their bite and teeth properly, shorten overall treatment time, and reduce discomfort.

Ceramic braces are a trendy and subtle way to straighten your teeth! Unlike traditional metal braces, ceramic braces are clear, making them less noticeable. Combination Treatment

Combination treatment is a customized approach to orthodontics involving braces, aligners, and orthodontic appliances like palatal expanders. This approach is often recommended for patients with more complex orthodontic needs, such as significant bite issues or multiple alignment problems. Using a combination of treatments can help address these issues more efficiently and effectively, resulting in a beautiful, healthy smile.
