The Dental Care You Need, All in One Place

Our Hillsborough practice is built on the belief that oral health is connected to your overall health. We take a comprehensive approach to your care. We want to make it easy for you and your family to access all the dental services you need without having to visit multiple offices.

General Dentistry

Routine cleanings, exams, fillings, sealants, and root canals keep your smile healthy. We focus on prevention and early detection to help you avoid complex dental problems. During your regular visits, our team will check for signs of decay, gum disease, and other issues before they become serious. We also take the time to show you the best ways to care for your teeth at home.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers, bonding, teeth whitening, and Botox can help you achieve the smile you have always wanted. Dr. Williamson has an artist’s eye for balance, symmetry, and proportion. He will work with you to create a treatment plan that fits your goals and budget. Whether you want a subtle improvement or a complete smile makeover, we have the skills and technology to make it happen.

Restorative Dentistry

Our same-day crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, and TMJ treatment restore the function and look of your teeth and gums. We can repair damaged teeth, replace missing teeth, and relieve jaw pain. Our goal is to help you eat, speak, and smile with confidence again. We use high-quality materials and advanced techniques to ensure your restorations look natural and last for years.

Children’s Oral Health

We know that childhood is a time to learn and build routines. Early good oral hygiene habits can set the stage for a lifetime of healthy smiles. At our Hillsborough dental office, we teach our youngest patients the right way to brush and floss. We also show parents and guardians how to care for their child’s growing teeth.

We make dental visits fun and stress-free for kids. Our team is patient, kind, and experienced with nervous children. We believe that a positive first visit sets your child up for success. Dr. Williamson wants all his patients to feel prepared and confident about their oral health routine. When children learn to value their teeth early, they are more likely to carry those habits into adulthood and avoid major dental problems later in life.

We Make Dental Care Affordable

We want you to get the care you need without financial stress. Our office will file your insurance claims and handle the paperwork to maximize your dental insurance coverage. We accept cash, debit, and major credit cards. Our team is always available to answer your questions about payment and insurance.
