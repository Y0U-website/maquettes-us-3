Dr. Nick Koenigsknecht

Dr. Nick joined Koenigsknecht Dentistry in 2005 after graduating from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. He serves as the 2024-2025 President of the St. Johns Rotary Club and is also a member of the Knights of Columbus. He served on the St. Johns Public Schools Board of Education for nine years. He is a 1997 graduate of St. Johns Public Schools and lives with his wife, Erin, children, Roman and Faye, and pets Carlo and Poe in St. Johns.

Dr. Nick maintains a high level of continuing education coursework at the KOIS Center in Seattle, WA and Spear Education in Scottsdale, AZ. This training by some of the world’s best in dentistry, focuses on the latest advancements in esthetics, implants and restorative dentistry. Dr Nick believes in life-long learning and always improving.

From these experiences Dr Nick has formed a particular interest in identifying and treating airway disorders and how sleep dentistry can help his patients from children to adults. He also applies new research based techniques and knowledge to help patients not just have a beautiful smile, but a better functioning smile for life!

Dr Nick is a proud member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and is a certified provider for Healthy Start .

Dr. Nick sees patients Monday, Wednesday and Thursday.
