How Many Implants Are Needed for an Implant Bridge?

The number of dental implants required depends on the size of the bridge and the location of missing teeth. The most common configuration involves two implants supporting a three-tooth bridge, but in cases where more teeth are missing, additional implants may be necessary.

During your consultation, we’ll determine the ideal number of implants based on:

  • The location of missing teeth
  • Your bite alignment
  • The amount of bone available for implant placement

What is the Procedure for Getting an Implant Bridge?

The dental implant bridge process involves multiple steps to ensure a secure and long-lasting restoration:

Our team works closely with your general dentist to ensure a seamless process, delivering high-quality results that feel completely natural.

  • Consultation & Digital Imaging – We evaluate your oral health and take 3D scans to plan implant placement.
  • Implant Placement Surgery – The implants are surgically placed into the jawbone in a minimally invasive procedure.
  • Healing & Osseointegration – Over 3–6 months, the implants fuse with the bone to create a stable foundation.
  • Custom Bridge Placement – Once healed, your general dentist attaches the custom dental bridge, restoring your smile.
