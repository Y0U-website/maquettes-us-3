The practice

Welcome to North Idaho Dental Group, with comfortable modern dental practices in Coeur d Alene and Ponderay, also serving Sandpoint, ID.

At North Idaho Dental Group, we have had the privilege of providing exceptional dentistry to friends and neighbors since 2002. We are proud to be part of our wonderful community, where we all work, play, and raise our families.

Dr. Ben Gates, Dr. Paige Landers, Dr. Chace Mickelson, Dr. Hatta Clark, and their amazing team welcome patients of all ages, from toddlers to grandparents. Our goal is to help patients find their healthiest, most beautiful smiles at each stage of life.

As a team, we believe in the power of personalized care and prioritizing the quality of our patients experience. We take time to nurture relationships with our patients and are on a mission to redefine what they can expect from a dental visit.

While each of our dentists brings unique skills and experience, ours is a collaborative atmosphere. We continually consult with each other on cases throughout the day, and the exceptional outcomes we achieve are the result of collaboration and combined expertise.

And that s the benefit of a group practice with the individual expertise of each dentist, we can provide all the oral health care your family needs in one of our comfortable, familiar locations.

We understand many people fear dental visits, but it doesn t have to be that way! At North Idaho Dental Group, we help our patients work through their anxiety by proving that stress-free dentistry really is possible.

When you arrive for your appointment, you can help yourself to our coffee bar for a refreshing beverage. Our treatment rooms are stocked with cozy blankets and neck pillows to ensure your comfort during procedures.
