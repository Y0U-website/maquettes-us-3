The following are some common dental emergencies and how you can immediately respond to them.

Thoroughly rinse your mouth with warm water, then gently use dental floss to remove any food or other debris caught between the teeth. See Dr. Adams as soon as possible.

Rinse the mouth using warm water, and save and rinse any broken pieces of tooth. Apply gauze to the area to stop any bleeding, and use a cold compress on the outside of the mouth near the affected area to reduce swelling and relieve pain. See Dr. Adams, who schedules fast emergency appointments in Firestone, CO, for chipped and broken teeth.

Retrieve the tooth and rinse the tooth root with water, but do not scrub it or remove any attached tissue fragments. Reinsert the tooth in the correct position if possible, but never try to force it. If you can’t reinsert the tooth, place the tooth in a small cup of milk, water with a pinch of salt, or a product such as Save-a-Tooth. For the best chances of saving the tooth, you should see Dr. Adams within an hour of the tooth being knocked out

Apply a cold compress to the outside of the mouth or cheek in the affected area until you can see Dr. Adams for emergency appointments in Firestone, CO. Take an over-the-counter pain reliever if necessary.

See Dr. Adams as soon as possible and stick a piece of sugarless gum into the cavity, or use an over-the-counter dental cement as a temporary measure.

If possible, reattach the crown using over-the-counter dental cement, toothpaste, vaseline, or denture adhesive. Do not eat or sleep with a loose crown. Then, bring the crown in with you to your emergency appointment.

Abscesses are infections that occur around the root of a tooth or in the space between the teeth and gums. See Dr. Adams as soon as possible if you discover a painful OR even a non-painful, pimple-like swelling on your gum.

To control bleeding to the tongue, cheeks, gums, and lips, rinse your mouth with a mild saltwater solution and apply pressure to the affected area with moistened gauze. Continue to apply pressure until you can see Dr. Adams.

(Reduced Radiation) Digital X-Rays

At Longs Peak Dentistry, we specialize in digital X-rays in Firestone, CO. This innovative imaging technology uses up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film X-rays and delivers instantly visible results. Digital X-rays also have a smaller environmental impact than film X-rays.

How Do Digital X-Rays Work?

Dr. Adams inserts a sensor into your mouth to capture images of your teeth, similar to the process for conventional film X-rays. Once the X-ray is taken, the image is projected via a digital sensor onto a computer screen, for Dr. Adams to view.

When Would I Need a Digital X-Ray?

Dr. Adams uses intraoral digital X-rays in Firestone, CO during your initial and routine dental check-ups, to get a detailed picture of the health of your teeth and gums. Intraoral X-rays allow Dr. Adams to detect cavities, monitor developing teeth, and check the general health of your teeth and jawbone.
