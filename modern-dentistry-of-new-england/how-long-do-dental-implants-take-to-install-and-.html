Let’s walk you through what happens and what you can expect.

What are same-day dental implants?

Same-day dental implants let us place your implant and attach a crown or temporary tooth in one appointment. Instead of waiting several months between steps, we skip the delay. You leave our New England office with a complete smile. This isn’t magic it’s modern dentistry.

Dr. Gabr checks if you have enough bone to hold the implant and ensures your oral health is strong. Not everyone qualifies, but many patients with a missing tooth or teeth are good candidates.

If you want quick results and have proper bone quality, you might be a perfect fit.

The installation process: what’s involved?

So, what happens during the implant surgery? It starts with your initial consultation at Modern Dentistry of New England. We take X-rays, check your mouth, and talk about your goals.

On the day of the implant placement, we use local anesthesia to keep you comfortable. Dr. Gabr places the implant into your jaw it acts like a tooth root. If your bone is strong, we can attach a temporary crown or restoration right away.

  • Initial consultation and exam
  • X-rays and planning
  • Implant placement
  • Crown placement (same day or temporary)
  • Follow-up and healing

Healing to full function: What to expect

Even with same-day dental implants , your mouth needs time to heal. The healing process, called osseointegration, is when the bone grows around the implant. This gives it strength and stability.

For most people, healing takes a few weeks to several months. You might feel some swelling, soreness, or mild pain, but it usually eases with over-the-counter medicine. During this time, you’ll eat soft foods and avoid chewing directly on the new tooth.

Proper care, brushing, and regular visits to Dr. Gabr help ensure long-term success. Once healed, we place your final crown it looks and feels like a natural tooth.

How long does the crown last?

Your final crown is strong, but it needs care. With brushing, flossing, and regular cleanings, your crown can last for years. It works like a natural tooth, and no one will know the difference.

Dr. Gabr uses top-quality materials for each crown placement. That means your final restoration is made to last.

Avoid biting ice or hard candy to protect your crown.

What if I need bone grafting first?

If you have severe bone loss, we may need to do bone grafting. This adds bone to your jaw to support the implant. In this case, we might not be able to do same-day implants. But don’t worry we’ll explain every option and make sure you understand the full time frame.

Even if you need extra steps, you’re still on your way to a better smile.
