Interests

When Dr. French is away from the office, he enjoys family time with his wife, his two boys and his dogs, Costa and Hobie. A lways on the go, he enjoys soccer, biking, endurance sports, and attending Atlanta United games with his family. Dr. French is a two time Ironman Triathlete and he has run several marathons and ultramarathons. On the weekends, Dr. French will usually be found around the soccer fields cheering on his boys.
