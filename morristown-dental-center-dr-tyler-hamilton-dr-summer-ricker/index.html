If you’re in the Lakeway area and missing one or more of your natural teeth, dental implants may be a great option for you! Dental implants can aid in chewing ability, help maintain adequate tooth spacing, and improve esthetics without having to cut neighboring teeth.

At your implant consult, we will begin by taking a thorough health history to confirm that you are healthy enough for a dental implant. Next, our staff will take a 3D X-ray to ensure you have adequate bone quantity and quality in the site of implant placement. Finally, we will get a scan of your mouth to help us fabricate a surgical guide to aid in the implant placement.

At this appointment, the implant fixture will be surgically placed into the bone. We offer nitrous oxide to help comfort and relax our patients. Bone grafting and/or sinus lifting may be necessary at this stage. Bone integration of a dental implant typically takes 4-6 months. .

After the 4-6 month healing period, the implant will need to be uncovered by removing a small amount of gum tissue. A healing cap will be placed on the implant, which helps contour the gum tissue for the final crown.
