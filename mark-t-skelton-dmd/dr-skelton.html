Meet Dr. Skelton

Dr. Skelton grew up in Gadsden, Alabama with two older sisters and a brother. He attended Auburn University and still makes it back to campus to see the Tigers play. During the summers, he worked in Alaska, in dairy farming and in construction.

Dr. Skelton Graduated from the University of Alabama Dental School in the Spring of 2001 and opened Skelton Family Dentistry in Fort Payne that summer. He built his brand new, state-of-the-art facility in 2007. He attends continuing education meetings all over the country to stay current on the latest innovations in cosmetic dentistry. In his free time, Dr. Skelton is an avid water skier and runner.
