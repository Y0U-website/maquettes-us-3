Going to the dentist is not on the top of the list of fun things to do for most people. We understand. It only gets worse if you are shuffled through a large dental office with too many doctors to count. We want to offer you and your family a safe and as calm a dental experience as possible.

We offer the highest quality of dental care for children and adults, even if it has been a while since your last dental visit. Dr. Nix has completed American Dental Association programs in Evidenced-Based Dentistry. This means that treatment decisions will be guided by the best available research and not whims or old wives tales.

Our office strives to build and maintain a respectful, personal and caring dental care community.

Dr. Nix has been practicing in the Second Avenue practice location since the fall of 2014. Prior to moving to Silver Spring, Dr. Nix was an associate dentist in Laurel, Maryland.

Dr. Nix is a member of the American Dental Association and the Maryland Dental Association.

Dr. Nix was born and grew up in Hendersonville, North Carolina, nestled in the Blue Ridge Mountains and attended the University of North Carolina at Asheville for undergraduate studies.

Dr. Nix spends his free time traveling, watching college basketball, and hiking with his wife, Laura and their dog, Fiona.
