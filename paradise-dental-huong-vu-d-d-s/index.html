Paradise Dental have extensive experience working with people of all ages. We know that fear of the discomfort associated with dental work is what deters people from getting their dental health in line. This is…

Dr. Vu has worked both in the public health sector and also in the corporate sector where numbers is the only thing that matter. She quickly learned that making the patient comfortable was the key to a perfect smile.

Dr. Vu wants every single patient that sits in her chair to have a perfect smile without the fear of the procedure! Dr. Huong Vu, DDS is a board certified general dentist that graduated from Indiana University Dental School. She is a member of the ADA, IDA, and CDS.

Fear of getting treatment comes down to a lack of knowledge, knowledge that can be avoided if the physician listens.

That is something that Dr. Vu never forgets, to listen. Explaining the procedure and making sure that all questions are answered no matter the time is critical when getting rid of fear of a certain treatment.

Dr. Vu has years of experience working with kids both as a single mother and by working at the Marion County Health Department where she almost exclusively worked with kids and pregnant women.

Our team will explain all procedures to you and assure that comfort is key. We’ll listen to you and answer all of your questions. Paradise Dentistry wants to make sure that all patients have a healthy smile without discomfort. Come to Paradise Dentistry where we’ll guide you to the destination of a beautiful smile.
