Meet Dr. Bhatt

Dr. Neal Bhatt

A Passion for Smiles, A Heart for People

When I was a kid, I was always working with my hands—building elaborate LEGO creations, playing sports, and tinkering with projects around the house (with varying degrees of success). My father, an artist-turned-dentist, showed me that skilled hands could do more than create things—they could change lives. Watching him transform smiles and help people feel better about themselves inspired me from an early age. Before long, I found myself following a similar path, combining precision, creativity, and compassion through dentistry.

I grew up in Philadelphia and attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill before returning home to earn my dental degree from Temple University. My journey eventually brought me to Texas, where I opened my first dental practice in Sanger. After years of caring for patients and building lasting relationships within that community, I was inspired to create something new—a practice built around warmth, trust, and truly personalized care. That vision became Greenhill Family Dental in Rockwall.

At Greenhill Family Dental, my goal is simple: to ensure every patient feels comfortable, respected, and confident in the care they receive. I understand that visiting the dentist isn’t everyone’s favorite activity, which is why we work hard to create an environment that feels welcoming and stress-free. From the moment you walk through our doors, we want you to feel like family.

Whether you need a routine checkup, advanced restorative treatment, or a complete smile transformation, our team is here to listen, guide, and support you every step of the way. If you leave our office with a healthier smile and a little more confidence than when you arrived, then we’ve accomplished exactly what we set out to do.
