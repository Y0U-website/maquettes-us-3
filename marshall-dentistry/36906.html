Frequently Asked Questions

What are the advantages of dental implants?

Dental implants in Kingsport are known as the gold standard in tooth replacement for several reasons.

#1 The Look, Feel, and Function of a Natural Tooth

Dental implants replace your root and tooth, making them the closest thing to a natural tooth. You ll be able to chew, bite, eat, smile, talk, and laugh confidently.

#2 Easy Home Care

Because they closely mimic your natural tooth, you can brush and floss your implant tooth just like you would any other tooth, making them simple to care for.

#3 Excellent Longevity

Tooth implants have the potential to last a lifetime with consistent home care and regular dental checkups with your Kingsport dentist.

#4 Protection for Dental Health

Dental implants protect your jawbone from the deterioration that occurs with tooth loss since they replace your lost root. They also protect tooth and bite alignment, which guards against jaw joint disorders and tooth movement, and allows you to chew and eat with ease.

#5 High Success Rate

Dental implants are a very reliable treatment with a success rate of 95 percent. Dr. Marshall can complete your dental implant from start to finish at our Kingsport dental office, so you can always work with the team you trust.
