Meet our doctors

Dr. Bradley Burtwistle, DDS

Dr. Bradley Burtwistle’s journey into dentistry began long before dental school. Earlier in his career, he served patients as both a paramedic and a surgical nurse, experiences that shaped the way he approaches care to this day. Those years taught him the importance of meeting patients with genuine empathy, understanding the full picture of their health, and delivering thoughtful, high-quality care at every step.

After earning his DDS from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry, Dr. Burtwistle brought that same patient-first mindset into dentistry. Today, he combines advanced technology like CEREC same-day crowns and the SOLEA laser with a philosophy that remains refreshingly simple: treat people with honesty, respect, and genuine care.

With more than 15 years of experience, Dr. Burtwistle is proud to be affiliated with the American Dental Association, the Colorado Dental Association, and the Colorado Springs Dental Society. His goal has always been the same: help patients become healthier while ensuring they feel comfortable and confident in their care.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Burtwistle enjoys spending time with his family and making the most of Colorado’s incredible outdoors, whether that means skiing, snorkeling on travels, or simply exploring new places.

Get to know the team

Dedicated to your comfort and care

Behind every great dental practice is a team that truly cares about the people they serve, and that’s something we take seriously at Monument Family Dentistry. Our team is known for being welcoming, supportive, and genuinely invested in helping patients feel comfortable from the moment they arrive.

From scheduling appointments to answering questions about treatment or insurance, we’re here to guide you through every part of your visit.

What to Expect

A welcoming first visit

Your first visit with us is an opportunity for us to get to know you and understand your dental health goals. We’ll take time to review your dental history, perform a thorough exam, and talk through any concerns or questions you may have. Our goal is always to provide care that feels thoughtful, transparent, and tailored specifically to you.

Patient Experience We believe dental visits should feel calm, respectful, and surprisingly comfortable. Our modern technology allows us to diagnose and treat dental concerns efficiently, while our team focuses on creating an environment where patients feel genuinely heard and supported. From cozy comforts like blankets and personalized music to thoughtful communication throughout your appointment, every detail is designed to help you feel at ease.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

A list of medications you are currently taking

Any dental records you’d like us to review

Advancing care. Building lasting smiles.

Same-day crowns

With our CEREC system, we can design and create custom dental crowns in a single visit. This advanced technology eliminates the need for temporary crowns and multiple appointments, allowing you to restore your smile quickly and comfortably.

Hard and soft tissue laser

The SOLEA laser allows us to perform many procedures with minimal discomfort and, in many cases, without traditional anesthesia. This technology helps create a more comfortable dental experience for patients who may feel anxious about treatment.

Advanced dental imaging

Our Axios cone beam CT technology provides detailed 3D views of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, supporting safer procedures, improved accuracy, and better overall outcomes for your care.
