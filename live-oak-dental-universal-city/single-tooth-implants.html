What To Expect From The Process of Single Tooth Implants

The procedure begins with a consultation, where our experts in Live Oak will evaluate your oral health. If you have sufficient jawbone and good oral health, we will plan the implant surgery. Our dentists perform this procedure under local anesthesia to avoid discomfort.

During the process, we will carefully place a small incision on the gumline to expose the bone. Our dentist will then prepare the bone and insert the implant post, which is then allowed to fuse with the jawbone for 3 to 6 months.

After the healing period, we will place an abutment and attach a customized dental crown that resembles the appearance of your natural teeth.
