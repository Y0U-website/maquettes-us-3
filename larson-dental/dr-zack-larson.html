About Dr. Zack Larson

Dr. Zack Larson grew up in Chino Valley and is excited to be returning to the tri-city area. He graduated from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2012 and has been practicing in Bullhead City, Arizona for the past 7 years. He and his wife have 5 kids four girls and one boy. He enjoys spending time with his kids, working in his yard, and playing pickleball.

From a young age, Dr. Zack demonstrated an amazing eye for detail. His ability to see what others couldn’t led him directly into a career in dentistry. His perceptual ability score on his dental admission test was in the 100th percentile. He loves dentistry, helping people feel better about their smiles, and interacting with his patients. Dr. Zack is a gifted clinician who truly cares about providing exceptional care to every patient he sees.
