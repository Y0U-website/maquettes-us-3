What to Expect During Treatment

Your Invisalign journey begins with a consultation, digital scan, and customized treatment plan. You&#x27;ll receive a series of aligners that should be worn for 20–22 hours each day , changing to a new set approximately every one to two weeks.

We&#x27;ll schedule periodic checkups to monitor your progress and ensure your teeth are moving as planned. In some cases, damaged or weakened teeth may require a Dental Crown before or after orthodontic treatment to restore long-term strength and function.

Treatment time varies but is typically 6 to 18 months , depending on the complexity of your case.

Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Invisalign is an excellent option for many mild to moderate orthodontic cases. During your consultation, we&#x27;ll determine whether Invisalign is the best treatment for your smile.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment within 6 to 18 months , depending on their individual orthodontic needs.

Can I eat while wearing Invisalign?

No. Remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water, then brush your teeth before placing them back in.

Does Invisalign hurt?

You may experience mild pressure for a day or two when switching to a new aligner, but most patients find Invisalign more comfortable than traditional braces.

How often should I wear my aligners?

For the best results, wear your aligners 20–22 hours each day and follow your treatment plan as instructed.
