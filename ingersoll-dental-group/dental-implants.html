Dental Implants

While losing a tooth from decay and injury is common, you may be born with missing teeth. These patients can also benefit from dental implant surgery.

  • Severe cavities
  • Gum disease
  • Clenching or grinding teeth
  • Facial injury

How They Work

A dental implant is surgically placed into your jawbone. After bone grows around the metal post, the abutment and crown are attached. Keep in mind the entire process can take several months to complete. This is to allow for adequate healing time. Because implants fused together with your jawbone, they have exceptional durability. In fact, dental implants typically last for 20 years or longer!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants provide a lot of unique advantages, including:

Better Appearance

If you’re missing a tooth, this can greatly impact your appearance. You may feel less confident and even feel embarrassed when smiling. Once you choose dental implants, you’ll be blown away at your appearance! Many people can’t stop smiling after receiving dental implants.

Improved Chewing Ability

A lot of patients choose dental implants to improve their chewing function. In fact, dental implants give you chewing power that is very similar to that of natural teeth! Dental implants are fused together with your jawbone, giving you durable artificial teeth that can chew through just about anything. This means you can still enjoy all of your favorite foods without worrying about breaking your oral device!

No Impact On Surrounding Teeth

If you choose dentures or a dental bridge, then your dentist must shave down some of your natural teeth. This helps the device stay secure in your mouth. However, this practice can also lead to dental issues down the line. With dental implants, this process doesn’t happen! Dental implant surgery doesn’t require shaving or filing down surrounding teeth.
