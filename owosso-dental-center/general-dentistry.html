General Dentistry Services in Owosso, MI

Comprehensive general dental care including cleanings, exams, fillings, and preventive treatments to maintain optimal oral health for the whole family.

Helping our patients maintain a healthy mouth and smile is the main goal of general dentistry. We prefer to provide more minor, preventive care than to see patients suffer with more intensive treatments from a problem that was not managed in time.

Emergency Care Our highly trained doctors and staff are ready to manage all of your dental emergency concerns with prompt, compassionate care.

Extractions An extraction is performed to remove a tooth, whether because of disease, crowding, or damage, with gentle care and comfort.

Fillings Composite fillings act as a dentistry solution for problems such as tooth decay or cracks, restoring your teeth with natural-looking materials.

Night Guards Protect your teeth from grinding while sleeping with custom-fitted night guards for optimal comfort and protection.

Oral Cancer Screenings Early detection of oral cancer through comprehensive screenings. We are proud to assist our patients with oral cancer screening to help survive this often fatal disease.

Root Canals Root canal treatment to save teeth affected by deep decay and infection, relieving pain and preserving your natural smile.

Sealants Dental sealants provide a protective coating against tooth decay and cavities, especially effective on back teeth.

Teeth Whitening A common request we receive is how to make a smile whiter and brighter. Professional teeth whitening is a relatively simple and quick treatment.

TMJ Treatment Temporomandibular joint dysfunction treatment to provide relief from jaw pain, headaches, and discomfort.

Wisdom Teeth Extractions Safe and comfortable wisdom teeth removal to prevent misalignment, nerve damage, and other complications.

COMMON QUESTIONS FAQs About General Dentistry Services in Owosso, MI How often should I have dental cleanings and exams? Most patients should have dental cleanings and exams every six months. However, Dr. Dinh may recommend more frequent visits based on your individual oral health needs.

What happens during a comprehensive exam? Dr. Dinh will examine your teeth, gums, jaw, and oral tissues, take digital X-rays if needed, screen for oral cancer, and discuss any findings or recommendations.

Important Terms to Know

Prophylaxis A professional dental cleaning that removes plaque, tartar, and stains to prevent gum disease and tooth decay.

Digital X-rays Advanced imaging technology that uses significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays while providing clearer images.

Preventive Care Dental treatments and practices focused on preventing oral health problems before they develop.

Oral Health The overall health of your teeth, gums, and entire mouth, which is connected to your overall health and well-being.
