Did you know that the American Association of Orthodontists recommends that all children have an orthodontic exam at age 7? Doing so helps ensure that small problems can be corrected with as little intervention as possible. Oftentimes, something as simple as a space maintainer or expander may prevent severe crowding and avoid the need to extract teeth or undergo jaw surgery during or after adolescence.
