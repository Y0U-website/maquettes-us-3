Meet your pediatric dentist

Dr. Christi Walsh

Dr. Christi Walsh, known affectionately as Dr. Christi by her patients, brings over a decade of pediatric dental expertise rooted in education, community service, and a true love for working with children. Her journey began in 2007 while teaching English and self-care at an orphanage in Peru. That formative experience inspired a lifelong commitment to youth wellness and education, ultimately leading her into dentistry.

She earned her Bachelor of Science from Bowling Green State University before obtaining her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University in 2014. Dr. Christi further honed her clinical skills in a General Practice Residency, followed by her Pediatric Dental Residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, New York.

Dr. Christi is Board Certified in Pediatric Dentistry and is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Ohio Dental Association, and the Cleveland Dental Society. Her clinical experience includes providing care within school-based health centers and at a Federally Qualified Health Center, where she realized the significant need for pediatric dental services in her own community.

As a mom of toddlers herself, Dr. Christi understands both the joys and challenges of early childhood, and she brings that empathy to every appointment. Her care philosophy centers on honest communication, direct kindness, and teamwork, both internally with her staff and in partnership with families.

Dr. Christi especially loves helping children face their fears and build confidence by creating a calm, welcoming experience. Whether it’s a gentle cleaning or an airway screening, her goal is always to make sure your child leaves the office smiling. With strong ties to the community and a clear focus on prevention and comfort, Dr. Christi is proud to offer a dental home where families can grow together.

Your child’s first visit

What to expect

We know the first visit to the dentist can be a big step, especially for little ones. That’s why we’ve created a calm, playful environment where your family will feel right at home. From the waiting room to the exam chair, our gentle approach and patient-centered care ensure your child is as comfortable as possible every step of the way.

Patient Experience When you arrive at Little Roots Pediatric Dentistry, you’ll be welcomed into a fun, friendly setting designed just for kids. Expect smiles, laughter, and a team that truly understands children. We take the time to get to know your family, answer your questions, and build trust with your child, creating the foundation for a lifelong dental home.

What to Bring To help us get started, please bring the following items to your child’s first visit:

Complete list of medications your child is currently taking

Kid-friendly comforts and modern technology

We’ve invested in advanced tools and thoughtful amenities to create a safe, effective, and enjoyable experience for every child and parent who walks through our doors. Our goal is to make dentistry feel less intimidating, and a lot more fun.

CO2 lasers

Our state-of-the-art lasers allow us to provide minimally invasive procedures with less discomfort, less noise, and faster healing. These laser treatments are perfect for little ones who may be sensitive to traditional dental tools.

Soan local anesthetic delivery system

The Soan Electronic Dental Anesthesia System is a cordless, pen-like handheld device used by dentists—primarily in pediatric dentistry—to deliver local anesthesia precisely and comfortably.

Play area and kids&#x27; entertainment

From interactive play spaces to TVs with streaming services in every operatory, we’ve built an atmosphere of fun and distraction to help your child feel relaxed and happy before, during, and after their appointment.
