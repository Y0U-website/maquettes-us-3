Dr. Saif Alankarli

He previously completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) at October 6 University, Egypt, in 2012, followed by a General Residency in 2014. Outside of his professional life, Dr. Alankarli enjoys staying active through outdoor activities such as cycling and swimming. His love for cooking and baking allows him to explore new cuisines, and he’s passionate about traveling and discovering new cities.
