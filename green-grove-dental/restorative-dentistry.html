Achieve a Beautiful, Functional Smile

As your team standing by you in dental health, we’re here whenever you encounter pressing problems or insecurities about your teeth. We offer a suite of restorative dental services that can fix painful or missing teeth, so you can get back to a full, healthy smile.

Tooth Replacement Options

Missing one or more teeth? Dr. Albert can go over your tooth replacement options to get you back the smile you deserve. With dentures or dental implants, you’ll not only be filling in any gaps, but also preventing other issues associated with missing teeth from developing.

Dentures

Dentures are now more comfortable than ever thanks to advances in dentistry. Besides restoring full function to your mouth, they also give you more facial definition and help you look younger. If you decide dentures are right for you, we’ll ensure they are properly fitted so your smile looks and feels great.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a proven, highly recommended method for replacing teeth. These biocompatible titanium posts are placed in your jaw and fuse with your bone to form a strong foundation for artificial teeth. Call our office to see if you’re a good candidate for dental implants!

Oral Surgery

If you require oral surgery—such as a tooth extraction or root canal therapy—know that you’ll be treated by an experienced, gentle dentist and a highly trained team. We will walk you through preparation and expectations, and ensure proper anesthesia or sedation is used for your comfort.

Tooth Extractions

We always consider all other options before extraction. However, in cases of severe infection or crowding, removal may be necessary. If so, we’ll make the process as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Endodontics / Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy treats infection inside a tooth. We’ll clean out the diseased area to eliminate pain and preserve your natural tooth, so you can bite and chew comfortably again.

Additional Restorative Treatments

In addition to tooth replacement and oral surgery, we offer restorative services like fillings, crowns, and bridges. These treatments restore strength, function, and aesthetics to your teeth. Contact us to learn more!

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings are a natural-looking alternative to silver fillings. Made from tooth-colored material, they restore a tooth after decay removal and help prevent future issues like cavities or fractures.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns support damaged teeth, secure bridges, and improve appearance. Our custom porcelain crowns blend naturally with your smile and are built to last for many years.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges “bridge” the space left by missing teeth using false teeth anchored by crowns. This treatment helps restore bite alignment, improves speech, and enhances chewing ability.
