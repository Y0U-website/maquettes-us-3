Ortho-buddy was founded to improve oral hygiene for orthodontic patients.

It’s a question that Orthodontists ask their patients every day.

For the most part, orthodontic patients do their best to brush all areas of their teeth while wearing braces. However, ordinary toothbrushes are bulky and are not designed to easily and effectively clean above, below and in between the brackets.

Ineffective removal of plaque leads to decalcification (white spots), decay, gingivitis and/or fibrotic gum tissue (swelling). These problems can cost thousands of dollars to remedy.

With over 35 years of Orthodontic experience, Dr. Spinosa knew there had to be a better way for orthodontic patients to maintain healthy oral hygiene while wearing braces.

Our journey began several years ago when Dr. Spinosa created the first prototype of Ortho-buddy. Fast forward through years of trial testing and design iterations, we received a patent for our innovative 360-degree radial bristle design technology.

Ortho-buddy is the most effective orthodontic toothbrush.

Patients with braces can easily and effectively maintain healthy oral hygiene, allowing the orthodontist and patient to focus on the task of treatment rather than hygiene.

Dr. Spinosa and his son Clarke are the Co-founders of Ortho-buddy. The aim is to help orthodontic patients maintain excellent oral hygiene while wearing braces.

Our commitment

Our goal is to help all orthodontic patients improve their hygiene in an easy and efficient manner.
