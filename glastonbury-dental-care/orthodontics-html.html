How Invisalign Works

With Invisalign, your teeth will be moved into their ideal positions with a series of clear, plastic aligners. Since they are see-through and customized to fit your progressing smile, your orthodontic treatment will be virtually undetectable, free of dietary restrictions, and considerably more comfortable than traditional braces. Plus, the average treatment timeline is only 12-18 months, so you won’t have to wait long to achieve the smile of your dreams!

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Our talented dentists, Dr. Mimansha Agarwal and Dr. Shobhit Agarwal , are here to help you achieve your healthiest, happiest smile. In order to determine if Invisalign is right for you, they will need to assess the complexity of your case and learn all about your smile goals. The good news is that clear aligners are suitable for many patients, including those struggling with mild to moderate cases of the following:

Crowded Teeth

When your teeth aren’t properly aligned, it’s harder to remove harmful substances, like plaque and tartar. Not only that, but it can make it difficult to smile confidently. Fortunately, if your case of overcrowding isn’t too severe, there’s a chance that we can improve the health and appearance of your smile with clear, barely noticeable aligners.

Gaps Between Teeth

Both small and large gaps in your smile can take a toll on your oral health. That’s why we recommend exploring your orthodontic treatment options, even if spaces between your teeth don’t bother you. If Invisalign is an option, then we will gradually close the gaps, giving you a smile that not only looks great but also reduces your risk of common oral health problems, like tooth decay.

Overbite

In addition to improving the alignment of your teeth, Invisalign can improve the alignment of your bite. So, if your upper teeth jut out considerably, you may not need traditional braces! The best way to find out if you’re a good candidate for clear aligners is by scheduling a consultation with us. That way, we can assess the severity of your case and the health of your smile.

Underbite

If you have an underbite, then you know firsthand how difficult it can be to enunciate clearly, chew properly, and smile confidently. There is good news, though: Invisalign clear aligners can correct common bite problems like this when used in tandem with other orthodontic accessories, like rubber bands and buttons. As a result, your orthodontic treatment may not involve any metal.

Crossbite

For decades, patients who wanted to align their teeth and their bite had one choice: wearing metal braces for 2-3 years. Fortunately, that’s no longer the case for patients with mild to moderate crossbites! If we determine that we can achieve the desired results with Invisalign, then we can have a custom series of aligners made. Before you know it, you’ll have the healthy, confident smile that you deserve.

Open Bite

If you haven’t heard of an open bite before, it’s when there is a space between the upper and lower teeth that prevents them from meeting evenly when a patient bites down. Unfortunately, this can lead to issues like premature wear, so it’s important to have it corrected. If you suspect that you may have this bite problem, then don’t hesitate to schedule an Invisalign consultation with our team.

The Benefits of Invisalign

Over two decades since its debut, Invisalign continues to be a sought-after orthodontic treatment. Here are a few reasons why:

  • The aligners are completely see-through, making them hard to detect.
  • There aren’t any dietary restrictions you can eat whatever you want!
  • Brushing and flossing are easy because the aligners are removable.
  • The average treatment timeline is only 12-18 months.
  • There aren’t any metal components, which makes the aligners more comfortable.

How Much Does Invisalign Cost?

The cost of Invisalign isn’t the same for everyone, which is why the prices you see online are often misleading. If you are interested in getting an estimate of the cost of your teeth-straightening journey, then we recommend scheduling a consultation with us. That way, we can provide you with an accurate number after taking into account the complexity of your case, if single-arch treatment is possible, if orthodontic accessories (like buttons) are needed, and the type of retainer you need to preserve your results.
