Dr. Abraham Beder

Dr. Abraham ( Abe ) Beder was raised in Brooklyn, New York. He completed his undergraduate studies at the City University of New York (CUNY) in Brooklyn, where he earned a degree in Chemistry. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine.

Following graduation, Dr. Beder practiced general dentistry for six years, gaining broad clinical experience and a strong foundation in comprehensive patient care. Driven by a desire to further his expertise, he pursued advanced specialty training in Endodontics at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey (UMDNJ), now known as Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Beder is deeply committed to helping patients find relief from dental pain and preserving their natural teeth through precise, compassionate endodontic care. He takes great professional satisfaction in delivering effective treatment that improves both comfort and long-term oral health.

On a personal level, Dr. Beder has been married for 39 years and is the proud parent of two grown children. Outside of his professional practice, he enjoys staying active through regular exercise and playing tennis.
