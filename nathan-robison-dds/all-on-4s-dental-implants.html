What Are All-On-4s?

All-on-4s is a dental implant system that helps replace multiple teeth in a patient’s mouth using four implants. This revolutionary technique provides an entire arch of teeth that function and feel like natural teeth. All-on-4 implants permanently attach to your jawbone and require no special care beyond your regular oral hygiene routine. This also means you can enjoy all your favorite foods without any restrictions.

Depending on your treatment needs, we can provide Alll-on-4s, All-on-5s, and even All-on-6s. We may recommend four, five, or six dental implants for your procedure.

The process typically involves placing a few dental implants at the front of the jawbone and a few at the back. These implants are strategically placed at an angle to maximize the use of existing bone structure and provide the necessary support for permanent prosthetic teeth.

Why All-On-4s Are Worth Considering

There are many benefits of All-on-4s! The following are just a few.

1) Improved Quality of Life:

The most significant advantage of All-on-4s is its improved quality of life. With All-on-4 dental implants, you never have to worry about loose dentures or bridges falling out. You can speak, chew, and laugh confidently without any concern. We design your All-on-4s to feel and function like natural teeth.

2) Healthy Jawbone:

When you lose teeth, the jawbone slowly degenerates, causing various oral health problems. All-on-4s prevent this by stimulating the jawbone, promoting natural bone growth. The implant posts used in All-on-4s replace the tooth root, preventing jawbone loss and enhancing facial aesthetics.

3) Cost-effective:

All-on-4s can be more cost-effective than other dental implant solutions, saving you time and money in the long run. Traditional dental implants require one post per tooth, whereas All-on-4s require just four posts for an entire arch. Additionally, you can benefit from less treatment time for All-on-4s, which means fewer dentist visits, sedation administrations, and less time taken off work.

4) Improved Digestion:

Wearing dentures makes biting and chewing food correctly challenging, causing digestive problems. With All-on-4s, you can chew and digest food as naturally as you would with natural teeth, leading to a healthier digestive system.

Get Your Smile Back

The first step in getting an All-on-4s placement is a consultation with Dr. Robison. During the appointment, our dental team will assess your overall dental health and medical history and take X-rays and impressions of your mouth. This step is crucial as it helps determine if you are the right candidate for the procedure. Once deemed suitable, we will discuss the treatment plan the cost involved, and answer any questions you might have.

Once we securely place the implants, we attach a customized, temporary denture so you leave our clinic the same day with a new smile. Your new teeth may look and feel foreign at first, but in no time, you’ll adjust to them and love how they look and function.

After the initial placement and temporization, we schedule a follow-up appointment in a few months to ensure the bone and implant integration. Once we confirm the integration, we fabricate the permanent prosthetic teeth and attach them to the implants. We ensure that your final set of teeth is aesthetically pleasing, comfortable, and functions well.
