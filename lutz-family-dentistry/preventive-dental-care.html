Cleanings

Regular professional cleanings are the cornerstone of preventive dental care. Even with diligent brushing and flossing, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas of your mouth. Our skilled hygienists use advanced tools and techniques to remove these deposits, leaving your teeth clean, smooth, and free of harmful bacteria.

  • Prevent Cavities: Removing plaque and tartar helps prevent tooth decay.
  • Gum Disease Prevention: Cleanings reduce your risk of gingivitis and periodontitis.
  • Fresh Breath: A clean mouth is a fresh-smelling mouth.
  • Early Detection: Regular cleanings allow us to spot potential issues early.

The Importance of Preventive Care

Neglecting your oral health can lead to a host of problems, including cavities, gum disease, and even tooth loss. Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining your oral health and catching potential issues early, when they are easier and less expensive to treat.

What Happens Without Preventive Care?

  • Cavities: Untreated decay can lead to pain, infection, and tooth loss.
  • Gum Disease: Early-stage gingivitis can progress to severe periodontitis, affecting your overall health.
  • Costly Treatments: Preventive care helps you avoid the need for more complex and expensive procedures.
