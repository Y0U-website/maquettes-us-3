The practice

Comprehensive care in one office – As a full-service dental office, we provide preventive dentistry, oral exams, dental cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, root canal treatments, periodontal therapy, dentures, snap-on dentures, dental implants, bone grafting, Zoom Whitening, veneers, bonding, Invisalign® clear aligners, sleep apnea treatment, and emergency dental care.

Our private dental office model allows more time for each visit. We listen to your concerns, explain options in simple language, and adjust the pace of treatment to reduce anxiety and discomfort.

Our modern dental clinic uses high-quality digital X-rays and imaging systems to evaluate teeth, roots, and bone structure while reducing radiation compared with traditional methods.

Patients describe our team as professional, attentive, and reassuring. Many who previously avoided dental visits feel more comfortable returning for regular care at our private dental office.

NewAgeDental offers a wide range of services, so most of your care can be completed in our convenient Jenkintown and Philadelphia dental offices.

Urgent dental care for tooth pain, infection, broken or lost restorations, damaged teeth, and other time-sensitive problems.
