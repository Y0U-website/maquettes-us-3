The practice

At Modern Dentistry, you can count on honesty and genuine care during every visit. We are a small practice that can provide consistent quality care, and we make a point to get to know each of our patients like family! You can rely on our skilled team for any of your dental needs or emergencies, even over the weekend!

Preventive Dentistry Many things we do are to keep you healthy so that you do not have any issues or problems. Common preventive services include cleanings, x-rays, fluoride, sealants, and occlusal guards.

Restorative Dentistry We provide many services that can restore your mouth back to normal health, helping you feel more confident. Common restorative services include tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and implants.

Emergency Dentistry We keep our patients in great dental health to prevent emergency dental needs . But when accidents happen, Dr. Brad Wisowaty does his best to be available and help remedy the situation.
