About Our Office

Jeter & Welch Dentistry has provided a range of cosmetic and general dentistry services in North Charleston, SC since 1976. Our practice is locally owned and operated and is proud to serve multiple generations of patients. From our beginning, we have been committed to building quality relationships with our patients and make it our priority to provide you and your loved ones with the gentle, professional care you deserve.

After practicing at 7535 Northside Drive for 30 years, our practice moved two doors down to 7509 Northside Drive in April 2016. We are loving our new space, and look forward to serving you there!
