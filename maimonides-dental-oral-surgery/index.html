The practice

Maimonides is a world-class care center serving Brooklyn and beyond. Here, you’ll find clinical excellence, a patient-centered approach, and a culture of innovation that makes a difference in our community.

Formerly New York Community Hospital, Maimonides Midwood is an acute care hospital located in the Midwood and Madison Park section of Brooklyn that provides the highest level of care for patients in the communities we serve.

Located at the corner of Vanderbilt and Atlantic Avenues, Maimonides Doctors Clinton Hill Pavilion consolidates three practices—Maimonides Doctors Sunrise Medical Group, Maimonides Obstetrics and Gynecology Women’s Health, and Maimonides Multispecialty Care—into one modern facility designed for convenience, comfort, and coordinated healthcare.
