Placing the implant

Dr. Bianco gently places a small titanium post where the tooth root used to be. You&#x27;ll be numb the whole time, and sedation is available if you want to sleep through it.

Healing

Over the next few months, your jawbone naturally grows around the post, giving you a real, solid foundation, just like a natural root.

Your new tooth

We attach a custom-made tooth that matches your bite and your smile. It looks, feels, and works like your own, and you can chew anything.
