The Process of Orthodontic Treatment

Now that you know about the different types of orthodontic treatment available, including orthodontics for teens , you may be wondering what the process looks like. Don’t worry, we’ve got you covered! Here are the general steps that patients can expect when getting orthodontic treatment at Meyer Dental Clinic:

  • Appointment: The first step is scheduling a consultation with our orthodontic specialist. During this appointment, we assess the condition of your teeth and bite and discuss your treatment goals. We will then create a personalized plan for your orthodontic treatment.
  • Personalization: After selecting your preferred treatment, we will develop a personalized plan that is specifically designed for your individual smile. As part of the process, we may need to capture x-rays, impressions, and photos of your teeth.
  • Installation: Based on the treatment you select, we will either apply brackets and wires or give you your initial set of aligners.
  • Regular check-ups: Throughout your treatment, we will ensure that we schedule frequent appointments to closely monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments.
  • Finishing up: After your treatment is finished, we will take off your metal braces or give you a final set of aligners. After that, you will be provided with a retainer to wear in order to keep your newly aligned smile intact.
