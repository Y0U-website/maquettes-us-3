When to Seek Oral Health Care and Prevention

Knowing when to consult a dental professional is crucial for maintaining your oral health. If you experience any of the following issues, it might be time to schedule an appointment with us:

  • Persistent bad breath
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Gum bleeding
  • Pain or discomfort
  • Changes in bite
  • Visible plaque buildup
