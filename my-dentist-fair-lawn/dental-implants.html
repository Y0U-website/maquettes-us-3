Dental Implants Fair Lawn, NJ

Dental implants are small, titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once the implant is placed, a crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the top of the implant to create a natural-looking and functional replacement for the missing tooth or teeth.

Benefits Of Dental Implants

Dental implants at My Dentist Fair Lawn offer several benefits over traditional tooth replacement options. They are designed to mimic the look and function of natural teeth, providing patients with a comfortable and natural-looking solution. Dental implants also help to prevent bone loss in the jaw, which can occur when a tooth is lost and the root is no longer present to stimulate the bone.

Am I A Candidate For Dental Implants?

Most patients who have lost one or more teeth are good candidates for dental implants. However, the ideal candidate for dental implants should have good oral health and enough bone density in the jaw to support the implant. Patients who smoke or have certain medical conditions may not be ideal candidates for dental implants.
