Family Planning

The Family Planning Program is dedicated to providing accessible family planning and reproductive healthcare to eligible women and men in Texas. The free or low-cost services offered through the Family Planning Program help avoid unintended pregnancies, allow people to determine the number and spacing of their children, and improve birth outcomes and overall health.

  • Family Planning
  • Pregnancy Testing
  • STD Testing and Treatment
  • HIV Screening
  • Well-Woman Care
  • Prenatal Orientation/Education
  • Regular Exams
  • Sonograms
  • Post-Partum Care
