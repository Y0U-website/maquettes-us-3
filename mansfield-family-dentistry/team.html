0 Meet The Team Dr. Josh Mansfield Dr. Mansfield grew up in Jacksonville, Arkansas. During high school, he played baseball, he was the Vice President of Student Council, and also the Valedictorian of his graduating class. Dr. Mansfield received a Chancellor’s scholarship at the University of Arkansas - Fayetteville, where he earned a Bachelor of Medical Sciences degree. He then went to the University of Tennessee Health Science Center for dental school, where he graduated in 2014.

Dr. Mansfield enjoys spending time with his growing family and friends. Some of his favorite activities include going to the lake, hunting, fishing, and attending Razorback events, but his absolute favorite is spending time with his daughters and wife, as they take in the joys of parenthood.
