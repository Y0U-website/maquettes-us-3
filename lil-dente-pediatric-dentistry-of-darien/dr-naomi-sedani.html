Meet Dr. Naomi Sedani

5-Star Pediatric Dentist At Lil&#x27; Dente

Dr. Naomi became a pediatric dentist to make a lasting difference in children’s lives. Her journey began at Wellesley College, where attending dental mission trips sparked her passion for working with children. She later earned her dental degree from The University of Pennsylvania’s School of Dental Medicine, graduating with honors in community health and research. Dr. Naomi continued her training in pediatric dentistry at NYU Langone Health in Brooklyn, deepening her expertise in caring for young smiles.

The Journey of Dr. Naomi Sedani

Before founding her own practice, Dr. Naomi gained valuable experience practicing dentistry in Connecticut and New York City. Her years of working with diverse families and children helped shape her compassionate, personalized approach to pediatric care. Every step of her career has been driven by her desire to build trust and comfort with every child she meets.

Why Darien Families Choose Dr. Naomi Sedani at Lil’ Dente?

Dr. Naomi founded Lil’ Dente in Darien because she instantly connected with the town’s welcoming, family-oriented atmosphere. She wanted to create a dental home where kids could feel safe, supported, and excited about their oral health journey. At Lil’ Dente, Dr. Naomi is proud to offer a place where both children and parents know they are genuinely cared for.

Beyond Pediatric Dentistry, Get to Know Dr. Naomi Sedani

When she’s not busy brightening smiles, Dr. Naomi enjoys reading, traveling, and staying active, you might even spot her at a local Pilates class! Above all, her greatest passion is helping every child she meets become the happiest, healthiest version of themselves, both inside and out.
