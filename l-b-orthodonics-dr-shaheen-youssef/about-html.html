About

Your smile is unique.

So is our approach to orthodontics.

We mean it when we say, “smile like you mean it.” At LB Orthodontics, we stand behind every smile we create—not only for our craftsmanship, but for the lasting results it creates for you. We’re committed to providing the highest quality orthodontic care for adults, teens and children in a fun, relaxing environment that promotes overall wellness.

We know that every smile is unique, so we create a customized treatment plan just for you. Our team uses a variety of treatment options, including different types of braces and/or Invisalign to meet your desired goals.

Meet Dr. Youssef

With compassion and understanding, Dr. Youssef gets to know his patients to understand their orthodontic treatment goals before offering a solution. He believes visiting the orthodontist should be a positive experience and works to ensure every patient receives the personal attention necessary to feel comfortable and have peace of mind during their treatment, and beyond.

Our Team

Our dental assistants and staff work as a team to ensure your family’s experience is always exceptional. Their education and experience allow them to help manage a wide variety of orthodontic cases, assisting with the consultations, questions and adjustments on the way to your new smile.

Our staff is committed to our vision of providing the highest quality orthodontics and participates in ongoing continuing education courses to remain at the forefront of changing orthodontic needs. They are a great source of additional information and education, which is key to obtaining your desired results. Each member of our team is also skilled at working with children and adults alike and truly loves what they do.
