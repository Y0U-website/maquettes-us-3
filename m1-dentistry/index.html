The practice

We don’t just fix teeth, we focus on treating each patient in an individualized approach, taking into consideration their unique needs. A healthy mouth means a healthy body. The oral-systemic link has been proven.

Patient education is of the utmost importance in our office. We want you to understand why dental treatments need to be done, and what you can do at home to ensure your smile lasts a lifetime. What we do between your nose and your chin is just as important as what we do between your ears.

While we may not be in network with your insurance provider, please know that typically we can still bill your treatment to your carrier. We are here to help you maximize your insurance benefits, and are happy to answer any questions or concerns you may have regarding them.
