Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, they support a custom crown, bridge, or denture to fully restore your smile. Implants are used to replace: A single missing tooth
  • Multiple missing teeth
  • A full arch of teeth

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer significant advantages over other tooth replacement options:

They are the closest option to replacing natural teeth.

  • Permanent, stable solution
  • Natural look and feel
  • Preserve jawbone and facial structure
  • Do not rely on surrounding teeth for support
  • Restore full chewing ability
  • Long-lasting with proper care

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are ideal for patients who:

For patients needing full arch replacement, implants can also support Full Dentures for improved stability and comfort.

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have healthy gums
  • Have sufficient jawbone density
  • Want a long-term, fixed solution

Who May Not Be a Candidate?

Some patients may require additional preparation before implants, including:

In certain cases, alternative solutions like Dental Bridges may be recommended.

  • Bone grafting for insufficient bone
  • Treatment for gum disease
  • Management of chronic health conditions

The Dental Implant Process

Step 1: Consultation & Planning

We perform a comprehensive exam using advanced imaging to assess your bone structure and create a customized treatment plan.

Step 2: Implant Placement

The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it integrates over time.

Step 3: Healing & Integration

Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation.

Step 4: Final Restoration

A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your smile.

How Long Do Dental Implants Take?

The full process typically takes several months, depending on healing time and individual needs.

Some steps may vary, but we provide a clear timeline so you know what to expect at every stage.

Dental Implants vs Other Tooth Replacement Options

Implants vs Dentures

Dentures are removable, while implants provide a fixed, more stable solution.

Implants vs Bridges

Bridges rely on adjacent teeth, while implants stand independently and preserve surrounding tooth structure.

Implants vs No Treatment

Leaving missing teeth untreated can lead to bone loss, shifting teeth, and bite problems.
