Some dental appointments are routine. Some reveal something more. Dr. V’s Desk is where those cases are shared — not to impress, but because an informed patient is a better-protected one. Insights from the desk, in plain language.

Case 1 - An unexpected finding.

The patient came in for implant planning. The teeth were the least important thing we found.

Advanced 3D imaging — the same CBCT technology we use to place implants with millimeter precision — revealed bilateral calcifications in the carotid region. Both sides. Visible in the cross-section above, marked by the arrows: bright dense deposits flanking the airway, precisely where they shouldn’t be.

In plain terms: early calcium buildup in the arteries that carry blood to the brain. Quiet. Symptomless. Already there.

Dentistry has a unique window into the body that most people don’t think about. We look at everything, because everything matters.

This patient came in about their teeth. They left with something far more valuable.
