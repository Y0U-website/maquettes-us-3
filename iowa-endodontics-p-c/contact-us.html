Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office at (515) 965-4456 during regular business hours.

Do I need a referral to visit Iowa Endodontics?

Many patients are referred by their dentist, but you may also contact our office directly if you are experiencing tooth pain or infection.

What should I do if I have severe tooth pain?

Severe tooth pain may indicate infection or inflammation inside the tooth. We recommend contacting our office as soon as possible for evaluation.
