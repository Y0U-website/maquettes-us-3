The practice

THE CALMING POWER OF A FURRY FRIEND Buddy Astro, our certified therapy dog, brings an extra sense of comfort to the office for many children. His calm, friendly presence helps make visits feel softer, easier, & a little more reassuring.

DESIGNED WITH FAMILIES IN MIND Our dental space was designed with the needs of families in mind. Kids get a play area built for fun, and parents get a moment to breathe.

Here are some things we offer to help make your visit more enjoyable:

PRIVATE ROOMS FOR EVERY VISIT Unlike traditional offices that share one open clinical area between several families, every operatory at our practice is a private room. It’s a small change that makes a big difference, especially for kids who feel more at ease in a quiet, focused space.

Each room is set up for comfort, with multiple TVs for entertainment, including a few mounted in the ceiling for treatment time. Every visit is shaped to feel calm, personal, and never rushed.

We’re honored to care for children of all needs, taking the time to create a treatment plan that’s right for each patient.

SEDATION OPTIONS Sedation options matter for little smiles, especially for kids with heightened anxiety or those who need extra support to stay comfortable during certain procedures. Dr. Kwak will walk you through the available options and help you choose a plan that fits your child’s needs and your family’s preferences.
