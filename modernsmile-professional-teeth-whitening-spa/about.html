About Dr. Barbara Wachs

Where Clinical Expertise Meets Luxury

Expertly-led by Dr. Barbara Wachs and hosted within a serene spa environment, we provide clinical precision with a boutique touch for the modern lifestyle.

At ModernSmile Professional Teeth Whitening Spa, we redefine the dental experience by blending clinical excellence with a serene spa atmosphere. Under the expert care of Dr. Wachs, we offer professional one-hour in-office teeth whitening and Invisalign®—the discreet, clear alternative to braces. Whether you are preparing for a milestone like a wedding or interview, celebrating a healthy new smoke-free lifestyle, or simply investing in your self-esteem, a brighter and straighter smile is your most powerful accessory.

We believe your journey to a radiant smile should be as relaxing as the result. Our spacious, tranquil studio is designed to put you at ease, offering a luxurious escape from the traditional dental setting. To complete your transformation, we invite you to enjoy the full suite of amenities at the Erika Cole Salon, including master hair styling, manicures, and advanced aesthetic treatments. At ModernSmile, your confidence is our craft. Schedule your complimentary consultation with Dr. Wachs today and step into the smile you’ve always dreamed of.

With over 20,000 procedures performed and a recurring role as a dental expert on QVC, Dr. Barbara J. Wachs brings an unparalleled level of experience to teeth whitening. After years of perfecting smiles on Madison Avenue, she established her premier teeth whitening and Invisalign spa to offer a sophisticated alternative to traditional dental office whitenings. Dr. Wachs earned her DDS from SUNY Stony Brook and remains a leader in the industry as the Clinical Director of Smileactives and is a Preferred Invisalign Provider.

Her philosophy is simple: everyone deserves a confident, youthful smile delivered in a serene environment. When she isn’t helping clients transform their appearance, Dr. Wachs enjoys life on Long Island with her family and puppy.
