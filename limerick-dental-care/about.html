We’re Green

Our primary goal is to help you maintain and improve your oral health. Limerick Dental Care also believes in taking care of the Earth. From reducing our waste water and use of toxic materials, to shrinking our carbon footprint, we’ve taken steps to reduce our environmental impact.

For example, we’re teaming up with TerraCycle and the Colgate Oral Health Brigade to make a positive impact on the environment by recycling used toothbrushes, empty toothpaste, floss containers and their packaging. Bring them to Limerick Dental Care and TerraCycle will recycle these items into park benches, tote bags, watering cans, and more. Learn more about the recycling program.

Our Building

Dr. Vanessa Adolf entered a partnership with Dr. Cohen in 2001, and eventually took ownership of the practice and purchased the building in 2010. In 2011-2012, Dr. Adolf renovated and modernized the first-floor dental office, added on a new patient entrance lobby, reception area, two new treatment rooms, and expanded employee space into one of the second-floor apartments.
