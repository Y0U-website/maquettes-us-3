Oleynik Dental

Education MAGD Academy of General Dentistry, 2005 DMD University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, 1984 BS Tufts University, 1980

Dr. Kenneth Oleynik was born and raised in Shelton, Connecticut. He attended Fairfield College Preparatory School in Fairfield, Connecticut. After graduating from Prep, Dr. Ken enrolled at Tufts University. He graduated from Tufts University with a BS in Biology in 1980. Following his undergraduate studies, Dr. Ken attended the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, where he graduated in 1984. After graduating from UConn Dental, Dr. Ken began private practice with his father, Dr. Joseph W. Oleynik F.A.G.D.

Dr. Ken and his wife Joann have two children, Colleen and Ryan. In his free time, Dr. Ken enjoys biking, skiing and hiking.
