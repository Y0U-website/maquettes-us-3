FAQs on Orthodontics

How long will orthodontic treatment take?

Most cases take 12–24 months, depending on your specific needs.

Can adults get orthodontic treatment?

Yes—many of our adult patients choose braces or Invisalign to correct alignment issues that developed over time.

Do Invisalign and braces work the same way?

They both straighten teeth effectively, but Invisalign is best for mild to moderate cases and offers more flexibility.

Will I need to wear a retainer?

Yes, retainers help preserve your new smile after active treatment ends.
