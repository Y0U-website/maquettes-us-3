Your sedation journey.

We follow a careful, individualized process to ensure every sedation patient receives the safest and most comfortable experience possible.

Before any sedation is considered, our doctors conduct a thorough health history review and consultation. We evaluate your medical background, current medications, anxiety level, and treatment complexity to determine the appropriate sedation approach for you specifically.

A precisely calibrated oral sedative is prescribed and taken prior to your appointment. By the time you arrive, you are already in a deeply relaxed state. A trusted adult must accompany you and drive you home.

Your vital signs, oxygen levels, and comfort are monitored continuously using the most advanced patient monitoring technology available. Our team maintains vigilant oversight at every stage of your treatment.

Following your procedure, you rest comfortably as the sedation gently wears off. We provide detailed post-care instructions and are available by phone should you have any questions during your recovery at home.
