When It Became Personal

My perspective was shaped not only by years of advanced training, but also by my own experience as a patient.

While seven months pregnant with my first child, I was involved in a car accident and suffered bilateral fractures in my spine. The fractures healed, but my pain did not — and that experience taught me that healing on an image does not always mean the body has returned to normal function.

The body continues to adapt, compensate, and protect itself long after an injury has healed.
