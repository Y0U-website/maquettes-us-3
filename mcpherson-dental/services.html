  • Bridges: A dental bridge is a false tooth that fills in the gap left by one or more missing teeth. Anchored by crowns on adjacent teeth, bridges restore functionality and prevent neighboring teeth from shifting, which helps maintain proper alignment and bite.

Dental Exams and X-Rays

Regular dental exams and x-rays are crucial for maintaining your oral health and detecting potential issues early. During your exam, Dr. McPherson will review your medical history, check for signs of tooth decay, cracks, or other damage, and assess your gum health. We also perform screenings for oral cancer and evaluate your bite for any signs of grinding or clenching. Existing restorations such as fillings and crowns are also checked to ensure they remain in good condition.

Diagnostic x-rays are an integral part of our dental exams, providing a detailed view of your teeth and supporting structures. We use digital radiography, which offers several benefits over traditional x-rays, including reduced radiation exposure and immediate image processing. Bitewing x-rays are taken annually to monitor for decay between the teeth, while panoramic x-rays are taken every five years to provide a comprehensive view of your entire mouth.
