  • Is sedation dentistry safe for children? Yes, sedation dentistry is very safe when administered by trained professionals. Your child is carefully monitored the entire time, including their breathing, heart rate, and oxygen levels. Our Little Dreamers team is specifically trained to provide sedation in a controlled, safe environment.
  • Why would my child need sedation? Sedation can be helpful if your child: Feels anxious or fearful about dental visits
  • Has difficulty sitting still for longer procedures
  • Needs multiple treatments completed in one visit
  • Has special healthcare needs or sensory sensitivities
  • What types of sedation are used? Depending on your child’s needs, your dentist may recommend: Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas): A mild sedation that helps children relax while staying awake
  • Oral Sedation: Medication taken before the appointment to reduce anxiety
  • IV Sedation or General Anesthesia: Used for more complex procedures or higher anxiety levels
  • Will my child be asleep during the procedure? Not always. Some forms of sedation (like nitrous oxide) keep your child awake but relaxed. Others, like general anesthesia, allow your child to sleep through the procedure. The recommendation depends on the treatment and your child’s comfort level.
  • How do I prepare my child for sedation? Preparation instructions may vary, but typically include: Limiting food and drink before the appointment
  • Dressing your child in comfortable clothing
  • Bringing a favorite toy or comfort item
  • Are there any side effects? Most children tolerate sedation very well. Some mild, temporary side effects may include: drowsiness, slight grogginess, or nausea (rare). These usually wear off quickly after the appointment.
  • Can I stay with my child during the procedure? In many cases, yes. Especially for mild sedation. For deeper sedation or general anesthesia, parents may be asked to step out for safety and focus. Our dental team will explain what to expect for your appointment ahead of time.
  • How will I know if sedation is right for my child? Dr. Sanford and his team will carefully review your child’s medical history, behavior, and treatment needs before making a recommendation. You’ll have the opportunity to ask questions and feel confident in the plan.
