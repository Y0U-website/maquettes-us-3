First Visit

Our main goal is education and prevention of dental disease. Along with the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), the American Dental Association (ADA), and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) we recommend establishing a dental home for your child by one year of age. It is very important that your child receives proper dental care and practices good oral hygiene from birth.

At Lawton Kids Dentistry & Braces in Lawton, OK , we want your child’s first visit — and every one to follow — to be as relaxing and easy as possible.
