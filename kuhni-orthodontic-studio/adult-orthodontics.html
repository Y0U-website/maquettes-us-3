Adult Braces: Life-Changing Solutions

Benefits of Adult Braces

Our adult braces are here to treat your cosmetic and health concerns. Everything from plaque build-up to jaw bone strength can be addressed.

Decrease in Plaque & Tartar Buildup and Cavities

Straight teeth make brushing and flossing easier and more efficient. Once you have straight teeth you will likely see a decrease in plaque buildup, tartar buildup, and cavities. Maintaining your oral hygiene will be easier than ever!

Crooked teeth make plaque and cavities more likely because there are sections of the teeth that are exposed that should not be. Crooked teeth can make flossing less effective or even impossible. If you are not flossing you increase the chances of cavities.

Fewer Toothaches and Gum Pain

Wondering how to fix a toothache? Straighter teeth are less likely to give you toothaches. Having straight and even teeth prevents the wear and tear on your teeth that can cause toothaches or gum pain.

Appear Younger, Healthier, and More Confident

Beautiful and straight teeth will change not just how people see your smile but also how people see your face. You may appear younger or healthier with straight teeth!

Your confidence will soar as you care for your teeth. Don’t feel like you need to hide your smile anymore. With straight teeth and discreet adult braces, you will want to show it off.

Resolve Lisps, Whistling, or Other Speech Impediments

Have you ever noticed a lisp or a whistle between your teeth when you talk or breathe? Lisping and whistling can be fixed with adult braces! You do not have to live with your speech concerns for the rest of your life.

Our Adult Braces Options

We have two primary kinds of adult orthodontics, making it easier than ever to have straight teeth. Each one has its pros and cons, and we will educate you on our offerings to make sure you get the best care available.

Invisalign

Invisalign clear aligners are an invisible orthodontic solution best for treating small orthodontic shifts, such as:

  • A misaligned mid-center
  • Small gaps
  • Tilted teeth
  • Crooked teeth

Fast, Discreet, and Convenient

Invisalign is amazing because it is fast and discreet! You can smile with confidence knowing that no one can tell you are straightening your teeth at the same time. With Invisalign, you will not have to deal with brackets or tightening.

Easily Removable When Necessary

You can also remove Invisalign to eat, brush, and during other necessary times. Unlike traditional braces, you can eat whatever you want with Invisalign without worrying about breaking brackets.

Invisalign works by using clear aligners that you will switch out every two weeks . The aligners move your teeth into their final position. This option can be quicker than typical braces.

Damon Braces

Our Damon braces are an amazing and discreet kind of metal orthodontic braces . They are more flexible than normal braces, and may also be quicker than typical braces.

Discreet Design Built for Comfort

Damon braces blend in with your smile because they utilize very small metal brackets.

The new technology they use also makes your treatment more comfortable. The self-tightening system it employs means:

Damon braces can treat bigger issues like overcrowding. If you need more complex oral treatment, Dr. Kuhni may recommend Damon braces.

  • Less in-office visits
  • Less time in braces
  • No discomfort from tightening

Damon Braces or Invisalign?

Invisalign

Invisalign is a very cosmetic solution that is also quick. If you use Invisalign, no one can tell you are getting orthodontic work done! You will also have fewer appointments with Invisalign, and you will not have to handle tightenings like regular braces.

However, Invisalign is limited in what it can treat. If you need to fix major tooth displacement or crowding, Invisalign cannot help with that. That is why we have Damon braces!

Damon Braces

While Damon Braces are metal braces, they use very small brackets and a flexible wiring system. This makes them more comfortable and discreet than regular braces. If you need more than a light straightening, Damon braces are for you.

Damon braces do not get tightened either. They use a self-tightening system that applies consistent pressure across all your teeth. These braces will cause less discomfort than regular braces.
