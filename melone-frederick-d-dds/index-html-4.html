SCHOOL CODES

College is a big investment, but SUNY Orange provides a quality education at an affordable price. To help you pay for a great education by using fewer of your own dollars, we offer scholarships, grants, and work-study programs and can guide you toward a variety of state and federal financial aid programs for which you may qualify.

Getting an education is important, but you don’t have to go into debt to achieve your dream. Our financial aid staff is here to keep you on track while you get a great value from one of the best community colleges in the State University of New York system.
