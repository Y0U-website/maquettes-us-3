Since 2004 Godley Station Dental and its doctors have strived to provide its patients with exceptional, personalized dental care at a fair and honest price. Traditionally the Dental Health industry has lagged behind other Healthcare services when it comes to modernization. This is exactly the case when it comes to insurance fees for services. Gone are the days of a set fee for a service. Instead, fees vary from providor to providor and from service to service with the patient stuck paying the bill.

This inconsistency

and disregard is what prompted GSD to create our Elite Dental Savings Plans. Through our plan offerings patients are able to feel confident when they accept treatment knowing exactly what they will pay – as well as having access to area leading technology and services at a discounted rate. We currently provide two plan offerings for both Adults and Children centered on our Standard Plan and including our add on Periodontal plan for patients in need of those services.
