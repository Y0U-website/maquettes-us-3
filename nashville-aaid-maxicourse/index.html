The practice

The Nashville AAID MaxiCourse is a 300 hour AAID course. The program is designed to give a thorough understanding of diagnostic and treatment modalities necessary to properly treat patients with dental implants.

The Nashville AAID MaxiCourse utilizes case-based learning methods that will include lectures, demonstrations, interactive seminars, hands-on sessions and relevant reviews of literature emphasizing evidence-based learning.

The Nashville AAID MaxiCourse participants perform hands-on surgical simulations and prosthetic implant procedures at all 8 modules.

Designed to give a solid foundation to your implantology career, the Nashville AAID MaxiCourse provides comprehensive hands-on training to ensure an in depth education in implant dentistry. This premier MaxiCourse utilizes not only lectures by some of the world’s leading implantologists, but Webinars, Cadaver Training, and Hands-on Modules.

Perform daily hands-on surgical simulations and prosthetic implant treatment.

Provide an intensive overview of state–of–the–art implant dentistry.

Discuss the basic sciences as they relate to implant dentistry.

Learn root form surgical protocol, immediate implants, implant prosthesis, computer milled abutments, and removable overdentures
