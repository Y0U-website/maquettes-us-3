Same-Day Dental Implants in…

One of the primary benefits of same-day dental implants is that the implant and extraction site heal together. This combined healing process can shorten your overall treatment time and make recovery more comfortable. Once your custom crown arrives from the lab, we’ll swap out the temporary one and make sure your new crown fits perfectly.

  • Gently remove the damaged tooth: If your tooth can’t be saved, we’ll carefully extract it. We make sure you’re fully numb and comfortable during this step.
  • Check your bone: Right after we remove the tooth, we check to make sure there’s enough healthy bone to hold the mini dental implant securely. Even though mini implants don’t need as much bone as traditional ones, a solid foundation is still important.
  • Add bone graft material, if needed: If the bone is a little thin or soft, we can place a small amount of bone grafting material. This helps strengthen the area and encourages new bone growth.
  • Place the mini dental Implant: Once the site is ready, we gently position the implant in the bone. It’s a quick process since the implant is a single, slim piece of titanium.
  • Attach a temporary crown: In most cases, we attach a temporary crown right away so you can leave your appointment with a full smile. In specific instances, we may be able to have your final crown ready the day we place your implant.

Don’t Wait Any Longer To Love Your Smile

Why schedule separate procedures to remove a tooth and place an implant when we can handle both in one visit?

Same-day dental implants offer a faster and simpler way to restore your smile without the long wait. By combining tooth removal, implant placement, and a temporary crown in one visit, our team helps you save time and avoid unnecessary appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Taking care of your same-day dental implants is key to long-term success. Right after your appointment, we’ll give you clear aftercare instructions to keep the area clean and ensure proper healing. That means sticking to softer foods for a few days, avoiding smoking, and gently rinsing your mouth as recommended.

Long term, treat your implant like a natural tooth: brush twice a day, floss daily, and keep up with regular dental visits. We’ll monitor your healing and crown placement to make sure everything’s on track. With good home care and routine checkups, your implant can last for many years!

Same-day dental implants offer a big advantage: convenience. Instead of waiting months (or even a year) for a new tooth, you can walk out of the office with a full smile the same day we remove your tooth. That means fewer appointments, a shorter recovery, and no need to live with a gap.

This faster timeline is made possible by mini dental implants, which require less bone and use a gentler procedure. On top of that, implants help preserve your jawbone and protect your oral health by replacing the missing tooth root. It’s a win-win: your smile looks great and stays strong for the future.
