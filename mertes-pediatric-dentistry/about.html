Our Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Greg Mertes

Dr. Greg Mertes is the owner and founder of Mertes Pediatric Dentistry that was established in 2006.

Dr. Greg Mertes discovered his calling to pediatric dentistry during dental school when he witnessed firsthand how specialized care could transform a child&#x27;s dental experience. Growing up in Canton, Ohio, Dr. Mertes developed his passion for helping others while being inspired by his close-knit family&#x27;s values of care and community.

After receiving his undergraduate degree in psychology from Kent State University in 1997, Dr. Mertes graduated from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine in 2001. He completed a general practice residency at MetroHealth Medical Center in Cleveland in 2002, followed by specialty training in pediatric dentistry at Children&#x27;s Hospital of Wisconsin in Milwaukee, where he served as chief resident.

Dr. Mertes has been an active memeber of the medical staff at Akron Children"s Hospital since 2006.

Dr. Mertes takes a unique approach to pediatric dentistry, focusing on building trust and creating positive experiences. He understands that dental visits can be intimidating for children, so he uses child-friendly language and techniques to put young patients at ease. His conservative treatment philosophy means recommending only what&#x27;s truly necessary for each child&#x27;s dental health.

When not helping children achieve healthy smiles, Dr. Mertes enjoys playing soccer, traveling abroad, attending concerts (especially Phish!), gardening, cooking, boating, skiing, building with Legos, and fishing – experiences that help him connect with his young patients and their diverse interests.

Our Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Meredith Guggenheim

Dr. Meredith Guggenheim grew up in Chagrin Falls, Ohio, where a love of working with children took root early. That passion, paired with a growing interest in science and healthcare that emerged during high school, set her on the path to a career in pediatric dentistry.

Meredith&#x27;s educational journey began at The Ohio State University in Columbus, where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Health Sciences with a minor in Dance. She continued on at Ohio State&#x27;s College of Dentistry, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Following dental school, she returned home to Cleveland to complete her residency in pediatric dentistry at Case Western Reserve University and University Hospitals Rainbow Babies and Children&#x27;s Hospital.

Today, Dr. Guggenheim is passionate about educating patients, promoting lifelong health, and building happy, healthy smiles. She values the wonder of learning and the joys of childhood, and brings that same curiosity and care to every patient she sees.

Outside the office, Meredith lives in Shaker Heights and loves to travel, practice yoga, and spend time in nature. She&#x27;s also a dedicated painter and creative at heart — she illustrated the children&#x27;s book Tansy, published in 2024.

Your Child&#x27;s Dental Experience Transforming Dental Visits Into Positive Adventures We&#x27;ve thoughtfully designed every aspect of our practice to create dental experiences that children truly enjoy. From our animated waiting area to our child-centered treatment approach, we&#x27;ve reimagined what pediatric dentistry can be.

The Patient Experience From the moment you step into our lively office, you&#x27;ll notice we&#x27;re not your typical dental practice. Our team uses child-friendly language (no talk of "shots" or scary dental terms), plays fun lively music throughout the office, and ensures each child leaves with a smile. We prioritize making visits quick but thorough, so children don&#x27;t develop dental anxiety.

What to Bring To make your first visit smooth and efficient, please bring:

Your child&#x27;s dental insurance information

A list of any medications your child is taking

Any questions or concerns you&#x27;d like to discuss

Your child&#x27;s complete medical history

Awesome Tools & Cozy Comforts

Our practice combines state-of-the-art pediatric dental equipment with thoughtful amenities designed to keep children entertained and comfortable. We focus on the human aspect of dentistry while utilizing modern technology for precise, efficient care.

Fun "Under-the-Sea" Theme

Our colorful "under-the-sea" themed environment creates an exciting, imaginative space where children can feel comfortable and engaged from the moment they walk through our doors.

Entertainment During Treatment

Every treatment room is equipped with ceiling-mounted TVs, allowing children to watch their favorite shows during procedures. This simple distraction helps time pass quickly and keeps young minds engaged on something positive.

Open-Bay Hygiene Area

Our hygiene cleaning area features an open-bay design with music playing throughout the day. This creates a less intimidating atmosphere where children can see others having positive experiences during routine cleanings .

Child-Friendly Communication

We&#x27;ve developed special child-friendly terminology for dental procedures that avoids scary words and creates a more positive experience. This thoughtful approach to communication helps children feel safe and understood during their visit.

Giving Back to Hudson and Beyond

Championing Children&#x27;s Dental Health

Mertes Pediatric Dentistry is deeply committed to our local community. During Children&#x27;s Dental Health Month, our team visits local schools to provide educational "tooth talks," helping children understand the importance of dental health in fun, engaging ways. We also participate in preschool fairs and city events throughout the year, making dental education accessible to families across Hudson and surrounding areas.

Beyond education, we proudly sponsor youth activities including boys&#x27; baseball teams, ballet troops, and community runs. We believe that supporting children&#x27;s physical activities complements our mission of promoting overall health and well-being. By maintaining a strong presence in our community, we help create positive associations with dental care that benefit children long before they ever sit in our dental chair.
