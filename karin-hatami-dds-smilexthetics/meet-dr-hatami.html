Meet the Doctor

Karin Hatami

Dr. Karin Hatami has received numerous awards for her achievements both in her dental profession as well as her accomplishments as an educator.

Quick Facts

Unparalleled Quality & Hospitality

Born and raised in Nuremberg, Germany, Dr. Karin Hatami brings her unparalleled focus on quality and excellence to her dental practice. The California Dental Association has recognized her for her outstanding academic and clinical accomplishments.

Her passion for dentistry began after a chipped tooth in the 8th grade led to a visit to her dentist. She was fascinated by how her own dentist had fixed it better than new and decided that her purpose was to change people’s lives through high quality dental care.

In 1988, she graduated from the University of San Francisco with a Bachelor of Science in Biology. In 1992, she earned her Doctorate in Dental Surgery at University of the Pacific School of Dentistry, receiving Honors Cum Laude.

Dr. Hatami’s commitment to excellence extends beyond her clinical practice. As an accomplished educator, she has dedicated herself to advancing the field of dentistry through teaching and mentorship, combining her technical expertise with a genuine passion for improving oral health outcomes.

Airway Health & Nutrition Focus

Dr. Hatami has advanced training in airway health and understands the crucial connection between proper breathing, sleep quality, and overall health. She evaluates each patient’s airway as part of their comprehensive dental examination.

Many dental and health issues can be traced back to airway problems, sleep disorders, and nutritional deficiencies. By addressing these root causes, Dr. Hatami helps patients achieve not just better oral health, but improved overall well-being.
