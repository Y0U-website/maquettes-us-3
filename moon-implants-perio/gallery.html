EXTRACTIONS, BONE REGENERATION & IMPLANTS

One Tooth, Start to Finish

When a tooth cannot be saved, the site is preserved and rebuilt so a new implant tooth can take its place.

1 Extraction & Site Preservation

The failing tooth is gently removed, and the socket is protected with a graft to prevent bone loss and hold the ridge shape for the implant to come.

2 Bone Regeneration & Grafting

Grafting material integrates with the natural bone over several months, rebuilding a stable foundation strong enough to support an implant long-term.

3 Implant Placement andCrown

The implant is placed in the regenerated bone and finished with a custom crown that blends seamlessly with the neighboring teeth for optimal function.

CONNECTIVE TISSUE GRAFT

Gum recession can cause sensitivity and pain, especially when a root is exposed. We correct this through Connective Tissue Grafts so your teeth are well-protected again.

Free Gingival Graft (FGG)

A free gingival graft uses a small piece of tissue from your palate to thicken and protect delicate gums, especially in the lower front teeth. Healing is predictable and comfortable for most patients, with only mild, short-term soreness.

Extractions, Bone Regeneration & Implants

If you have a missing tooth, we can replace it with a natural-looking dental implant. If you also have bone loss due to an infected tooth, our treatment plan will include a restorative bone graft before the implant is placed.

Full Arch Implant Solutions

Full arch implant treatment replaces all the upper or lower teeth with a secure, natural-looking set of teeth supported by implants, offering a long-term solution when existing teeth can’t be saved. Every case is fully customized to your health, goals, and anatomy to ensure the best outcome.
