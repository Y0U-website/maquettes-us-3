What are Dental Implants?

If you are looking for a long-lasting and natural-looking solution for missing teeth, arch dental implants are a popular option. These artificial roots are made of biocompatible materials, such as titanium, and are placed directly into the jawbone to provide support for a dental prosthesis like a crown or bridge. The fusion of the implant with the jawbone through osseointegration ensures a stable and durable foundation for the prosthesis, resulting in a natural-looking and comfortable restoration.

A full mouth dental implant is also a great option for individuals who are missing multiple teeth or have significant tooth loss. This procedure involves strategically placing multiple dental implants in the jawbone to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth. This provides a permanent and secure solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring full dental function.

Types of Dental Implants:

There are several types of dental implants available, and the type that is right for you will depend on your specific needs and oral health. Here are the most common types of dental implants:

  • Endosteal Implants
  • Subperiosteal Implants
  • Zygomatic Implant
  • All-on-4 implants
  • Mini Implants

Why should you get Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer several benefits over other tooth replacement options, such as dentures and bridges. They are more stable and secure, allowing you to eat and speak with confidence. They also help prevent bone loss in the jaw, which can occur when teeth are missing. Additionally, dental implants are easy to care for and can last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The procedure is a multi-step process that requires careful planning and execution to achieve optimal results. During the initial consultation, our periodontist will evaluate the arch of the patient’s jawbone and the condition of your natural teeth to determine if you are a good candidate. If you have a weak or insufficient jawbone, our periodontist may recommend a bone graft procedure to strengthen the bone and prepare it.

Once you are deemed a good candidate, dental implants are placed into the jawbone. This involves making a small incision in the gum tissue and placing it into the bone. It is then left to heal for several weeks or months, allowing it to fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates a stable and durable foundation for the dental prosthesis, which is the next step in the restoration process.

The dental prosthesis, such as a crown or bridge, is then attached to it to replace the missing tooth or teeth. This involves creating an impression of your teeth and jaw to ensure a precise fit and natural-looking restoration. Our team uses high-quality materials and advanced technology to create dental prostheses that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth and enhance your smile.
