Meet Dr. Sonia Sehgal, DMD

Dr. Sonia Sehgal is a highly experienced cosmetic and restorative dentist with a passion for creating confident, healthy smiles . With over 13 years of experience , she believes in minimally invasive techniques to provide the best possible care for her patients.

Dr. Sonia Sehgal, DMD – Passionate About Creating Beautiful Smiles

Dr. Sehgal earned her Doctorate in Dental Medicine DMD from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in 2012. With over 13 years of clinical experience, including eight years in Canada and five years in Texas, she offers a well rounded and elevated standard of care. Her expertise spans cosmetic dentistry, digital dentistry, Invisalign, and implant restorations, blending precision, advanced technology, and a strong aesthetic sense.

Dr. Sehgal has completed extensive training in cosmetic dentistry under some of the top clinicians in the United States and Canada, further refining her skills in smile design and restorative excellence. Her artistic eye and deep understanding of dental form and function allow her to create naturally beautiful, customized results.

She is widely known for her compassionate nature, calm demeanor, and for being a thoughtful listener, helping patients feel truly seen, understood, and comfortable throughout their treatment journey.

Outside the office, Dr. Sehgal enjoys reading, cooking, watching movies, and cherishes quality time with her family and friends.
