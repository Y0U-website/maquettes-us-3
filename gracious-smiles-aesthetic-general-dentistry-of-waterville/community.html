Gracious Smiles Presents: Freedom Day 2019

A Day of Free Dentistry for Military & Veterans Save the Date: Thursday, October 10, 2019 8:00am-4:00pm

We re Here, Ready to Serve! (567) 200-4045

Dr. Davis and our office were excited to announce our participation in Freedom Day USA in 2019. We were blessed to have found an organization to partner with that allowed us to express our gratitude to the military personnel and their families who sacrifice so much for our nation and all its citizens.

Freedom Day dental appointments were scheduled on a first-come, first-served basis. Scheduled appointments were given priority, but walk-ins were also welcomed as time allowed. Our honored guests were asked to bring military ID or military discharge papers (DD214).

Freedom Day services included: Cleanings, exams, x-rays, and simple extractions.

Gracious Smiles salutes all the brave men and women who put their lives on the line to serve the greater good of our nation. This was our first Freedom Day, but we hope it won’t be our last!

If you have any other questions about Freedom Day USA, please contact us at (567) 200-4045 .

FIRST ANNUAL HEALTH FAIR, ROSSFORD BAPTIST CHURCH AUGUST 10, 2019

Dr. Davis participated in the Rossford Baptist Church Health Fair August 10 to reach out to the community with oral health information and options. Health screenings, massages, and information about other medical services and resources were also offered, and some folks even got up close and personal with a giant kidney!
