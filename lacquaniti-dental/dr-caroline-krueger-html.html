Get to Know Dr. Caroline Krueger

Originally from Annapolis, MD, Dr. Caroline Krueger has been part of the world of dentistry for over half a decade, and she loves expanding her expertise so that she can provide a high level of care for patients. One of the most fulfilling parts of her job is connecting with people. Not only does she like listening to and understanding patients’ stories, but she also enjoys being able to create the perfect plan to restore or improve their smiles. Keep reading to learn more about her credentials and background before arriving for your appointment.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Krueger first attended the Florida Institute of Technology in Melbourne for her undergraduate studies. She then studied at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine to receive her dental doctorate, graduating Summa Cum Laude in the top 5 of her class. She was also inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU) National Dental Honor Society. Aside from pursuing continuing education courses to refine her skills and expertise, she’s also an active member of professional organizations like the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Florida Dental Association (FDA).

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Krueger met her husband in 2015 while they were studying at Florida Tech. During that time, she was an NCCA All-American Women’s Lacrosse Player and was even recruited by her school to be part of the first Women’s lacrosse team. Today, she lives with her husband and their puppy named Murphy. They enjoy doing outdoor activities like paddle boarding, biking, and playing pickleball. They also like taking Murphy to the local farmer’s markets as well as going to the annual Army vs. Navy Football games.
