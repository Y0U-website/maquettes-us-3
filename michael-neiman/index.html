The practice

Dr. Mike’s goal as a pediatric dentist is to mitigate the anxiety that kids associate with dental visits, and for his patients to actually enjoy their time spent at The Tootharium. Through his whimsical use of songs about cleaning teeth, Dr. Mike instills lifelong positive dental habits in his patients.

Dr. Neiman resides locally in New Jersey with his wife and four children.
