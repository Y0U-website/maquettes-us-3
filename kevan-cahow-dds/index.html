The practice

If you are looking for a dentist that puts their patients first, Dr. Kevan Cahow is right for you. His thorough and attentive care helps facilitate an environment of trust, where you know that your dental needs and wants are always taken care of.

Dr. Thomas Vincent believes great dentistry starts with listening. His compassionate, relationship-focused approach allows him to understand each patient’s concerns and goals so he can provide thoughtful, personalized care that supports better oral health, lasting confidence and a more comfortable dental experience.

Dr. Cahow is an active member of the American Dental Association®, Minnesota Dental Association and the Minneapolis District Dental Society. In addition, he is on the professional staff at Children’s Minnesota Hospitals and Clinics.

He enjoys the four seasons Minnesota offers by boating and Jet Skiing in the summer and snowmobiling in the winter with his family and friends. Dr. Cahow and his wife are “empty nesters” and enjoy long walks together and spending time with their two adult children.

Dr. Thomas Vincent earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Minnesota. In his practice, he combines rigorous clinical preparation with a compassionate, relationship-focused approach that guides every aspect of his practice.
