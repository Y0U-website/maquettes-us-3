How Fixed Implant Dental Bridges Work

Fixed implant bridges use fixed implants for support and smile enhancement. Fixed implants are small titanium posts placed into your jawbone like artificial tooth roots. They fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong base for your dental bridge.

Abutments are attached after your fixed implants heal and bond with your bone, usually taking a few months. Abutment posts that stick above your gum line and connect to your tooth bridge.

Your dental bridge is a custom-made piece with artificial teeth that look natural. Made from materials like porcelain or zirconia, these teeth can be designed to match the size, shape, and color you want. They are securely attached to your abutments with screws, making them a permanent part of your mouth.

Benefits of Fixed Implant Dental Bridges

A fixed implant bridge is often regarded as the best type of tooth bridge to bridge your dental gap.

Fixed implant bridges can last many years, even decades, with proper care. They are a lasting solution for missing teeth that improves your smile.

These dental bridges look and feel just like natural teeth. They blend seamlessly with your remaining teeth, providing a natural appearance.

Fixed implant bridges are more comfortable than removable dentures because they are firmly anchored in your mouth. This makes them an excellent choice for patients missing all upper or lower teeth.

Fixed implant bridges do not rely on nearby natural teeth for support. This means your neighboring teeth remain unaltered and healthy with your new tooth bridges.

These dental bridges provide a strong, stable structure that handles normal biting and chewing forces. They function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile confidently.

With fixed implant bridges, you can eat, talk, and smile without worrying about them becoming loose or falling out. This restores your ability to live normally.

Fixed implants stimulate your jawbone, helping to prevent bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.

Fixed implant bridges are often less expensive than replacing each missing tooth with its own implant, making them a cost-effective solution. Hence, you can rightly say that dental bridges cost less than several other replacement options.
