What Is Full-Arch Replacement?

Full-Arch Dental Implants in Minot

Full-arch replacement uses strategically placed dental implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth. Rather than replacing each tooth individually, a small number of implants can support an entire arch of teeth. This approach provides greater stability than traditional dentures while helping preserve jawbone health and improve overall oral function.

Questions Minot Patients Ask About Full-Arch Replacement

How many implants are needed for full-arch replacement?

The number of implants varies depending on anatomy, bone availability, and treatment goals. Many full-arch restorations are supported by four to six implants per arch.

Is full-arch replacement better than traditional dentures?

Many patients prefer implant-supported restorations because they offer greater stability, improved chewing ability, and better preservation of jawbone health.

How long does a full-arch replacement last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for decades, while the restoration itself may require maintenance or replacement over time.

Will my replacement teeth look natural?

Yes. Full-arch restorations are customized to complement facial features, smile appearance, and functional needs.

Am I a candidate for full-arch replacement?

Many adults with significant tooth loss are candidates for treatment. Dr. Olson evaluates bone levels, oral health, and medical history to determine the best options for each patient.
