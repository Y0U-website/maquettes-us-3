About Koup Family Dental

Providing Quality Dental Care for Life

At Koup Family Dental, family is the focus.

For over 40 years, Dr. Richard Koup , and more recently his son Dr. Mark Koup , have been treating patients as if they were family. The relationships and trust developed over the years has resulted in patient families that span generations, currently as high as 4 generations!

The Drs. Koup have spent their entire lives in this area — growing up in Paoli, going to school in Great Valley, going to college in the greater Philadelphia area, opening the first practice in Paoli in 1985.

They know the area. They know the people. And they know dentistry!

An Experienced and Caring Support Team

The staff at Koup Family Dental is also a family. The majority of the hygienists have been part of the practice for 20+ years. The bonds and relationships we have formed are strong: some patients even report they look forward to an office visit just to see their dental team! At our front desk, the office manager has been greeting people, fighting for their benefits and assisting Dr. Koup for over 20 years as well.

Our team is ready to treat all members of your family. From the very young to the seasoned patient, our emphasis is on maintaining your best oral health. It’s not uncommon for parents and kids to come at the same time to receive treatment, allowing our team to forge even better relationships and, therefore, better care.

Why are the relationships with our patients so important to us? Put simply, it allows our team to work with each individual to develop the dental treatment that works best for you. Each patient is treated like family — we know most on a first-name basis. And for those considering a visit to us, we look forward to creating that special relationship with you.
