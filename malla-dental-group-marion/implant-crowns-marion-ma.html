What Is an Implant Crown?

An implant crown is a dental restoration used to replace a missing tooth. It is placed on top of a dental implant, which is a small titanium post inserted into the jawbone. The implant acts like a tooth root, while the crown looks like a natural tooth. This combination helps restore normal chewing and speaking. Implant crowns are fixed, stable and designed to match the shape and color of your natural teeth for a functional and natural appearance.

Who Is a Good Candidate for an Implant Crown?

Implant crowns are a great choice for many patients. You may be a good candidate if:

You are missing one or more teeth due to decay, injury, or extraction

Your jawbone is healthy enough to support an implant (or can be built up with a bone graft)

Your gums are healthy — no active gum disease

You do not smoke, or you are willing to quit during the healing process

You are in good overall health and can heal after a minor surgical procedure

If you are not sure whether you qualify, do not worry. Our dentist in Marion , MA, will review your full dental history and imaging during your consultation to determine the right plan for you.

How Does the Implant Crown Process Work?

Getting an implant crowns in Marion, MA takes a few steps. It does not happen all in one day, but each step is important. Here is how it usually goes:

Consultation and X-rays

Our dentist will check your mouth and take X-rays or 3D scans. This helps them see your jawbone and plan everything properly before any work begins.

Implant Placement

The implant post is placed into your jawbone. Local anesthesia is used so you do not feel pain during this step. After placement, your mouth needs time to heal. The bone slowly grows around the implant. This healing period usually takes a few weeks to a few months.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant is stable, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to it. This piece connects the implant post to the crown that will sit on top of it.

Placing the Crown

Our dentist near you takes impressions (or digital scans) of your teeth. These are sent to a dental lab where your custom crown is made. When it is ready, the crown is attached to the abutment. Our dentist will check the bite and make any necessary small adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Taking care of an implant crown is simple. Brush twice a day, floss daily, and rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash if our dentist recommends it. Come in for regular cleanings and check-ups. Avoid chewing on ice or very hard foods that could crack the crown.

With proper care, implant crowns near you can last 15 to 25 years or longer. The implant itself may last a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need replacement due to normal wear, but many patients keep the same crown for decades.
