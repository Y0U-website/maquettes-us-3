How We Match Orthodontic Options to Your Life

For many, the idea of braces brings back memories of metal and wires, but today’s choices go far beyond that. Kids, teens, and adults all see results when the plan fits their needs. We look at your teeth, your schedule, and what matters most to you. Then we recommend what actually works, not just what’s popular.

Some cases need the steady control of braces. Others respond well to aligners. We break down what fits your mouth and your lifestyle, so you know exactly what to expect. No pressure, just straightforward guidance.

  • Traditional Metal Braces: Reliable for younger patients or anyone needing full correction, especially when appearance isn’t the top concern.
  • Clear Aligners: Removable trays that blend into daily routines, favored by adults and older teens who want subtle changes without visible hardware.
  • Ceramic Braces: Tooth-colored brackets that offer the strength of metal with a less noticeable look, ideal for those who want effective results with less attention.
  • Lingual Braces: Hidden behind the teeth, these work for anyone who wants straightening without showing any brackets at all.

Why Patients Choose Braces or Aligners With Us

Choosing between braces and aligners isn’t just about looks. Braces stay put, working around the clock. Aligners come out for meals and brushing, but only work if you wear them as directed. Complex cases often move faster with braces, while aligners suit those who want flexibility and can stick to the routine.

We walk you through the pros and cons, focusing on what’s happening in your mouth not just what’s trending. For tough alignment, braces may be the answer. For discreet changes, aligners often fit the bill. Either way, you’ll know what’s ahead before you start.

  • Braces: Fixed in place, adjusted regularly to guide teeth into position over time.
  • Clear Aligners: Swapped out every week or two, worn most of the day for steady progress.
  • Treatment Speed: Braces handle tough crowding or bite issues quickly; aligners work best for mild to moderate shifts.
  • Visibility: Braces show, aligners nearly disappear when worn.
  • Maintenance: Braces need careful cleaning around brackets; aligners must be rinsed and kept clear of buildup.

What Happens at Each Orthodontic Visit With Us

Every visit moves you closer to your goal. Appointments run about 20 to 30 minutes, spaced out every few weeks. We check progress, make adjustments, and answer your questions. No rushing, no confusion. Braces get wires tightened and bands replaced. Aligners get checked for fit, and you pick up your next set of trays. These visits keep your treatment on track and let us spot any issues before they slow you down. Patients who stick to their schedule see smoother, faster results.

When You’ll See Results and How We Track Progress

With orthodontics, everyone wants to know the timeline. The answer depends on how much your teeth need to move. Minor shifts can wrap up in six to twelve months. More involved cases may take up to two years. We lay out a realistic plan from the start, so you’re never left guessing.

Progress shows up early. Teeth start moving within weeks, and we track every change at each visit. If you’re ahead of schedule or need a tweak, you’ll hear it straight from us. Madison Dental Works keeps you informed from start to finish.

  • Mild Cases: Six to twelve months for small gaps or slight crowding, using braces or aligners.
  • Moderate Cases: Twelve to eighteen months for more noticeable crowding or bite adjustments.
  • Complex Cases: Eighteen to twenty-four months or longer for severe misalignment or jaw concerns.
  • Visible Changes: Most people notice their teeth shifting within the first few months, even if the full process takes longer.

How We Help You Care for Teeth During Treatment

Keeping teeth clean during orthodontic care isn’t optional. Braces catch food and make brushing trickier. Aligners need to stay spotless to avoid stains and odors. We show you how to brush around brackets, use floss threaders , and keep aligners clear. Brush after meals if you wear braces. Rinse aligners every time you remove them. Skip sticky or hard foods that can damage wires or brackets. If something breaks or feels off, let us know right away. Patients who follow our care plan finish faster and avoid setbacks.
