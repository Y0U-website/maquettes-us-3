Community Outreach

Dr. Kinard has a passion for giving back. She supports the Helotes community and is a Chairman Circle member of the Helotes Chamber of Commerce. She also works closely with St. Jude and JDRF. She has worked with Haven for Hope to provide dental care to those in need in San Antonio, and is always looking for ways to give back to the community.
