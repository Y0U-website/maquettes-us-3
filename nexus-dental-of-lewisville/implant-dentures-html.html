What Are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures are very similar to traditional dentures, but the key difference is that they are secured by dental implants. Often, patients who wear dentures complain that their device becomes loose and floats around the mouth, which can make eating and speaking more challenging. Since they’re fully supported by dental implants beneath the gumline, implant dentures are far more stable!

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

Fixed implant dentures are permanently attached to the implant posts beneath the gums that support them. They’re only removed during dental visits when maintenance or deep cleaning is needed. Many patients prefer fixed dentures since they make brushing and flossing easier, but they tend to require more implant posts than removable options.

Removable Implant Dentures

With this option, the dental implants are still placed securely into the jaw, but the restoration on top can be removed at any time, allowing you to keep it clean more easily. We recommend this type of denture for patients with lower jawbone density, since it usually requires fewer implant posts.

The Implant Denture Process

During your initial consultation, we’ll confirm that you’re a good candidate for implant dentures by assessing your jawbone density and your overall oral wellness. If something like bone grafting or periodontal treatment is needed beforehand, we’ll be sure to help prepare your mouth for the procedure. The dental implant posts will be placed at price angles and locations beneath your gums, and they’ll need a few months to fully heal and fuse with your jaw. From there, the abutments (metal connector pieces) can be placed, and finally, once your final denture is ready, it’ll be attached.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Patients who receive implant dentures in Lewisville also gain several quality-of-life benefits, including:

  • Dental implants are capable of restoring almost all of your original bite force, meaning you can enjoy a varied diet without worrying about your new teeth shifting around.
  • Since they provide stimulation to your underlying jaw tissue, the implant posts can prevent further bone resorption, which is a common side effect of tooth loss.
  • Your custom-made dentures that go on top of the implant posts will look and feel just like your original teeth.
  • Implant dentures are very easy to keep clean since the dental implants do not require any type of specialized at-home maintenance.
