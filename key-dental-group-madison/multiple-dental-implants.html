What Are Multiple Dental Implants?

Multiple dental implants are artificial tooth roots placed in your jaw to support several replacement teeth. Unlike dentures, implants are fixed in place. They feel like natural teeth and help maintain the shape of your jawbone. Multiple dental implants can replace a few missing teeth or support a full arch of replacement teeth, depending on your needs.

Dental implants are usually made of titanium so they can fuse with your jawbone over time. Once healed, they provide a stable foundation for crowns , bridges, or implant-supported dentures.

The Process of Getting Multiple Dental Implants

At Key Dental Group, you can expect the following steps for getting multiple dental implants in Madison, MS:

We examine your teeth, gums, and jawbone. X-rays or 3D scans may be taken to plan your treatment.

Our dentists create a customized plan to place the right number of implants in the best positions.

Small titanium posts are placed into your jawbone during a minor procedure. Local anesthesia is used to help keep you comfortable.

The implants need time to fuse with your jawbone, which usually takes a few months. Temporary teeth may be provided during this time.

After healing, permanent crowns, bridges, or dentures are attached to the implants.

Our dentist checks your implants regularly to help make sure they remain healthy and stable.
