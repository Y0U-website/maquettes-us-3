Braces

Traditional braces are a time-tested and effective method for correcting a wide range of orthodontic issues. Using metal brackets and wires, they gradually move teeth into their ideal positions. With advancements in design, today’s traditional braces are smaller, more comfortable, and can even be customized with colorful bands for a personalized touch.

Damon braces are a type of self-ligating system that uses a slide mechanism to hold the archwire. This reduces the amount of pressure on the teeth and allows the teeth to move more freely, quickly, and comfortably. They apply gentle, continuous pressure to move teeth more efficiently, often without the need for extractions. Damon braces are available in both metal and clear options, offering a more discreet appearance.

Ceramic braces function similarly to traditional braces but use clear or tooth-colored brackets, making them less noticeable. They are a popular choice for patients seeking effective treatment with a more aesthetic appeal. Ceramic braces blend in with the natural color of your teeth, providing a more subtle option while still delivering excellent results.

Clear Aligners

Invisalign ®

Invisalign treatment uses a series of virtually invisible, removable aligners that are made with advanced 3D computer imaging technology for precision fit and reliable tooth movements. Dr. Klein and Dr. Brian are experts in clear aligner orthodontic treatment having worked on +1,000 cases.

Spark ™

Spark aligners offer another discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth using a series of clear, custom-made aligners. These aligners are nearly invisible and are designed with advanced technology to provide a precise fit and gentle, effective tooth movement. Each aligner is tailored to your unique dental structure, gradually guiding your teeth into their optimal position.

Retainers

Retainers are an essential part of maintaining the results of your orthodontic treatment. After your treatment with braces or aligners is finished, retainers help to keep your teeth in their new, correct positions. Retainers are custom-made to fit your teeth precisely, offering a comfortable solution to protect the investment you’ve made in your smile.

There are different types of retainers available, including removable and fixed options, each suited to different needs and lifestyles. Removable retainers are easy to clean and can be taken out during meals or special occasions, while fixed retainers are bonded to the back of your teeth for continuous support. Dr. Klein or Dr. Brian will recommend the best type of retainer based on your treatment plan and lifestyle.

Technology

Our iTero scanner is a cutting-edge tool that allows us to create precise digital impressions of your teeth without the need for traditional, messy molds. Using advanced 3D imaging technology, the iTero scanner captures detailed images of your mouth in minutes, providing a clear, accurate model that we use to plan your treatment.

Digital X-ray technology provides high-resolution images of your teeth and jaw with minimal radiation exposure. Unlike traditional X-rays, digital X-rays offer immediate results that can be easily enhanced, zoomed, and shared, allowing for a more accurate and efficient diagnosis. These detailed images help us to identify issues that may not be visible during a regular examination, such as impacted teeth, bone loss, or hidden cavities.

DentalMonitoring is a revolutionary technology that allows us to track your orthodontic progress remotely, providing greater convenience and flexibility in your treatment. Using a specialized app, you can take regular photos of your teeth from the comfort of your home, which are then analyzed by our team. This continuous monitoring helps us to ensure your treatment is on track and allows for timely adjustments without the need for frequent in-office visits.
