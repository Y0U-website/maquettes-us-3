Meet our new dentist

Katharine Woehling Antoian, DMD

Dr. Katie proudly calls the Philadelphia area home. Raised in Villanova and educated in the Radnor public school system, she developed a deep appreciation for community and service early in life. With strong role models in both the medical and dental professions within her immediate family, she knew from a young age that helping others would be central to her future.

She graduated from The Schreyer Honors College at The Pennsylvania State University before completing her post-baccalaureate studies at Bryn Mawr College. Dr. Katie earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Pennsylvania and went on to complete her specialized pediatric dental training at The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia.

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Katie is passionate about preventive care and establishing healthy habits early in life. She believes that positive early dental experiences shape lifelong oral health. Through hands-on education, encouragement, and a warm, welcoming environment, she strives to create a true dental home where children feel safe, confident, and empowered.

Outside the office, Dr. Katie treasures time with her husband, Tim, and the life they have built together. They are proud parents to their seven-year-old son, Aiden, and four-year-old twin boys, Connor and Charlie. The newest addition to their family is their energetic puppy, Auggie!
