Quick Efficient Service We understand that waiting may be hard for little ones, so we ensure our pediatric dental service is done quickly and efficiently.

Infants or toddlers should visit a pediatric dentist for a checkup no later than one year of age. We recommend seeing your infant dentist every 6 months so that we can work together on making sure we keep your child cavity free with a healthy smile!

Our hygienist will clean your child’s baby teeth with a special “toothbrush” that will take plaque and calculus off on teeth. After the cleaning, the pediatric dentist will complete an exam and address your child’s dental care needs.

Nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas, is an adjunctive therapy that is use very frequently at Magic Squad Dental. It is a safe sedative with few side effects like lightheadedness, nausea or tingling in the arms and legs. Laughing gas will help your child to be comfortable, calmer and relax during treatment.
