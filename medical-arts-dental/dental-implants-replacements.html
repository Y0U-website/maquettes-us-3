Single Tooth Implants

An implants looks and functions like a natural tooth. It is surgically placed into the jaw to replace a missing too. Implants are the most similar to a natural tooth and often feel more comfortable.

Implant Bridges

If you’re missing several teeth in a row, implant supported dentures are a great option for restoring the missing space. With implant supported bridges, two implants or “metal roots” are placed into the jaw on each side of the implants so they look and function like natural teeth.

Dentures

Our office is happy to make dentures to replace your missing teeth. Dentures restore the appearance of having a full set of teeth and can help restore chewing function as well.

Implant Dentures

If you have dentures or are looking to get dentures, considering an implant supported denture is a great option. If you’ve felt frustrated with your dentures moving around, falling out or having difficulty eating with your dentures, an implant supported denture may be the perfect solution for you. An implant supported denture is when several implants are placed into the jaw, a period of healing is allowed, and then the denture is made to clip into the implants for maximum retention, support and stability.

Partial Dentures

Removable partial dentures are used to replace a section of missing teeth. Removable partial dentures have replacement teeth fixed to an acrylic base that will match your gum tissue. Removable partial dentures also will have clasps that will hook on to your remaining teeth. The partial denture can be easily taken out of tour mouth for cleaning. Partial dentures can improve esthetics and help with chewing function.
