Fixed vs. Removable Implant Supported Dentures

Implant supported dentures are not all the same. Some are designed to be removed by the patient, while others stay fixed to the implants.

Snap In Implant Supported Dentures

A removable denture attaches securely to dental implants but can still be taken out for daily cleaning.

  • greater stability than a traditional denture
  • less movement during eating and speaking
  • easy access for cleaning
  • removable by the patient

Fixed Implant Supported Teeth

A fixed full arch restoration stays attached to the implants during everyday use and is removed only by a dental professional when necessary.

Neither option is automatically better. Your bone, oral health, bite, hygiene needs, expectations, and treatment goals all help determine which approach makes sense.

  • teeth stay in place
  • no daily removal by the patient
  • greater stability for many patients
  • requires careful cleaning underneath

What About All on 4 Dental Implants?

All on 4 is a specific type of fixed full arch implant treatment for patients who need to replace most or all of the teeth in an upper or lower arch.

Strategically positioned implants support one connected set of replacement teeth. Despite the name, treatment is not always limited to exactly four implants. The number and position depend on your anatomy, available bone, bite, and restorative needs.

If you are primarily interested in a fixed solution rather than a removable snap in denture, our All on 4 page explains candidacy, temporary teeth, cleaning, long term maintenance, cost considerations, and how fixed treatment differs from removable options.

How the Process Works

We evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, medical history, and goals. Imaging helps us determine where implants may be positioned and whether additional treatment is needed.

Dental implants are placed in planned positions in the jaw. When surgical complexity, bone conditions, or other clinical considerations warrant specialist involvement, we can coordinate that portion of care.

The implants need time to integrate with the surrounding bone. Your temporary tooth replacement during this period depends on your individual treatment plan.

After healing, we take the records needed to create your restoration, evaluate the fit and bite, and connect the denture or fixed restoration to the implants.

How Long Do Implant Supported Dentures Last?

Dental implants are designed to provide long term support, but the implants and the denture or bridge attached to them are different components.

With good oral hygiene and regular professional care, implants may remain healthy for many years. The restoration itself can experience normal wear and may eventually need adjustment, repair, relining, or replacement.

Longevity depends on oral hygiene, bone and gum health, bite forces, general health, smoking, the type of restoration, and ongoing dental care.

FAQ About Implant Supported Dentures

Patients often have questions about how implant supported dentures work, whether they are removable, how long treatment takes, and what happens during healing. These answers cover some of the most common questions we hear when patients are comparing traditional dentures, snap in options, and fixed implant supported teeth.

Do implant supported dentures come out?

Both options exist. Removable snap in dentures come out for cleaning, while fixed implant supported restorations stay in place and are removed only by a dental professional when necessary.

Are implant supported dentures comfortable?

Many patients find implant supported dentures more stable than traditional removable dentures. Comfort depends on fit, bite, tissue health, and the type of restoration.

Am I a candidate if I have bone loss?

Possibly. Bone loss does not automatically rule out implant treatment. Imaging helps us determine how much bone is available, where it is located, and whether additional treatment may be needed.

How long does the whole process take?

Timing varies based on your treatment plan, healing, whether teeth need to be removed, and whether additional treatment is needed before or during implant care.

Will I be without teeth while the implants heal?

The temporary tooth replacement used during healing depends on your treatment plan. We will explain how your teeth will be replaced during each stage before treatment begins.
