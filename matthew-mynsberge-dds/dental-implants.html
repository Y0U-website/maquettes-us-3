How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental care, implants can last for decades or even a lifetime.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Implant placement is typically done under local anesthesia or IV sedation. Most patients experience mild discomfort for a few days post-op.

Can I get implants if I’ve had bone loss?

Yes. In many cases, bone grafting can rebuild the jawbone to allow for successful implant placement.

Do you offer sedation for implant surgery?

Yes. We offer IV sedation and other in-office anesthesia options to keep you comfortable throughout the procedure.

Do dental implants look natural?

Absolutely. Once restored by your general dentist, the final crown or prosthesis looks and functions just like a natural tooth.
