Dental Services

John M. Beasley Family Dentistry offers preventive, restorative, and general dentistry services to patients in Lawrenceburg, TN, and surrounding areas. For four decades, our dentist and team have helped patients of all ages achieve the smiles they have always wanted.

Preventive Services

Healthy gums have been shown to help prevent heart attacks and stroke.

Dental Sealants

Preventive services also include sealants for kids. Sealants are applied to the chewing surface of the teeth and help seal out plaque and food. Sealants have been shown to reduce the risk of decay by nearly 80 % in molars!

Restorative & General Dentistry

Restoring your teeth to normal function is a very important part of our jobs. This includes fillings, dental crowns, dentures, and other procedures to repair problems including decay, missing teeth, loss of tooth structure, and more.

Intraoral Cameras

For procedures that used to require messy molds, we use a technologically advanced intraoral camera. We will take computer images rather than subject you to the molding process!

Dental Implants

If you have a dental implant that has a loose, broken, or lost crown, Dr. Beasley can restore your implant to full function. Please bring your crown with you if it is has come off!

Dental Extractions

We also perform extractions. There are times that require a tooth to be pulled, either due to extensive decay, injury, or not fitting into the jaw. Ask us about wisdom tooth removal!

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge is utilized when tooth loss occurs due to decay, gum disease, or injury. The bridge closes gaps between teeth and looks and acts like a natural tooth. Dental bridges are permanently cemented to adjacent teeth and will help to maintain the position of your remaining teeth.

Veneers

Veneers are thin porcelain shells that are bonded to the teeth to enhance the look of chipped, stained, or broken teeth.

Dentures

When you are missing teeth, dentures are used to restore the functionality and health of your mouth and teeth. Dentures can help restore your confidence in your smile!

Teeth Whitening

Over time, coffee, smoking, tea, and other factors can cause tooth discoloration. With professional teeth whitening, you can help reverse the effects of this discoloration, leaving you with a smile you are proud to show off.

FAQs

1: How often should you visit the dentist?

We recommended a visit to the dentist every six months. Regular professional cleanings and exams will prevent cavities and gum disease!

2: What’s the best way to prevent cavities?

Brushing your teeth twice a day and flossing every day is the best way! John M. Beasley Family Dentistry believes regular maintenance of your teeth is key to having a good smile. Regular dental check ups will help too.

3: When should my child have their first dental visit?

A first visit ought to occur when a child is one or when the first teeth start to appear. Early visits help monitor oral development and prevent dental issues.

4: How can I help my child with teething discomfort?

Teething rings, cold washcloths, and gentle gum massages can help soothe discomfort. Avoid using numbing gels with benzocaine for infants.
