EdgePRO® laser cleaning

EdgePRO® laser technology allows us to thoroughly clean and disinfect the root canal system with high-energy light. It eliminates up to 99 % of bacteria, biofilm, and smear layer—especially in areas traditional tools can’t reach. This not only increases your chances of long-term success but also speeds up healing and enhances comfort.

We’re one of the few endodontic offices in New Jersey to offer this advanced system, and we use it daily to ensure each treatment is as effective—and gentle—as possible.

  • Superior disinfection in hard-to-reach spaces
  • Up to 99 % removal of bacteria and debris
  • Faster healing with less inflammation
  • Greater comfort during and after treatment

3D CBCT imaging

Our CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) scanner gives us a 3D view of your tooth and surrounding structures—far beyond what a standard X-ray can show. It’s essential for detecting fractures, hidden canals, infections, or bone loss before we begin any procedure.

The scan costs $250 and may be partially covered by insurance. We also offer financing for accessibility.

  • Accurate diagnosis of complex cases
  • Precise treatment planning
  • Less guesswork, fewer surprises
  • Better long-term outcomes

ZEISS operating microscopes

Every treatment room at Liv Endodontics is equipped with a high-powered ZEISS microscope. This allows our endodontists to see inside the tiniest spaces of the tooth root—places invisible to the naked eye. Whether locating hidden canals or confirming the removal of infection, this level of magnification means nothing is left behind.

  • Enhanced visibility inside complex root structures
  • Precise detection of hidden canals and fractures
  • Greater accuracy during every step of treatment
  • Reduced risk of missed infection

Digital radiography

Digital X-rays give us crystal-clear images with up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film. The results appear instantly on screen, allowing us to explain what we see and make informed decisions on the spot.

This technology supports more accurate, comfortable, and confident treatment—both for our team and for you.

  • Reduced radiation exposure
  • Enhanced visibility of fine details
  • Faster diagnosis and planning
  • Better patient communication

Intraoral cameras & digital records

Our intraoral cameras are small, high-resolution tools that let us capture and share real-time images of your teeth. We use them to educate you, monitor your progress, and communicate seamlessly with your referring dentist.

We also use these images to build detailed, secure records of your case—making continuity of care effortless and more collaborative.

  • You get to see exactly what’s happening
  • You’ll understand why treatment is needed
  • You can follow your healing process visually
