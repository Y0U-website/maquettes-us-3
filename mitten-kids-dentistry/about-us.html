Parental Involvement

Communication is Key

We believe that communication between doctors, staff, parents, and children is essential. We are always here to answer your questions! Whether it’s related to your child’s growth and development, the need for braces, cavities, or why they occur, we will always do our best to deliver the answers in an easy, fun, simple, and positive manner.

Any Child. Any Need.

We pride ourselves in being able to serve any child, no matter their needs. Medically compromised? Special needs? Scared of the dentist or new places? Rest assured, there&#x27;s little we haven&#x27;t encountered. We’ll work with you and your child to deliver the best care.

Repeat

We’ll get together at least every 6 months to make sure your child is staying healthy. Oh, and don’t be at all surprised if your child starts looking forward to dental check ups…crazier things have happened!

Our Journey

The Beginning

Our journey began with a vision to redefine our name and embody a brand reflecting our core values of evidence-based, quality care. Dr. Raby began as the first pediatric dentist in Grand Rapids in the 1970s, followed by Dr. Davis and Dr. Payne. Dr. Brett Kingma (Dr. Brett) succeeded Dr. Payne in 2015, and in 2018, Dr. Grady Randall (Dr. Grady) joined him, fortifying our commitment to excellence.
