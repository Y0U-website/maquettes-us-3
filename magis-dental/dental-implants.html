What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone to act like the root of a missing tooth. Once healed, the implant supports a crown, bridge, or denture. Because implants are anchored in bone, they provide a secure foundation that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

What are the advantages of dental implants?

The main advantage of dental implants is that they replace missing teeth without relying on neighboring teeth for support. Implants can restore bite strength and chewing comfort, and they can also help support jawbone structure after tooth loss. For many patients, implants provide a stable, long-term solution that feels natural while eating, speaking, and smiling.

What are the disadvantages of dental implants?

The main disadvantages of dental implants are the time needed for healing and the total cost compared to some other tooth replacement options. Implants require a healing phase while the implant integrates with the jawbone. Some patients may also need additional procedures such as extractions or bone grafting. However, for most patients, the long-term stability and function of dental implants outweigh these disadvantages.

Dental Implant FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a permanent tooth replacement option that uses a titanium post to support a natural looking crown, restoring function and appearnce.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth and good overall health are candidates for dental implants. A consultation is required to determine eligibility.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can easily last many years and often a lifetime.

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants replace missing teeth using a small titanium post placed in the jawbone. After healing, an abutment and crown are added to restore a natural look and function.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant timeline varies by patient. Some cases can be completed in a few months, while others take longer depending on healing time and whether additional procedures are needed.

What if I need more than one dental implant?

Dental implants can replace one tooth or multiple teeth. Options may include individual implants, implant-supported bridges, or implant-supported dentures depending on your needs.
