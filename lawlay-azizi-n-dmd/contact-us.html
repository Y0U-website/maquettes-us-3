Common Questions

Contact & Scheduling FAQs

Here are answers to some of the questions we get asked most frequently about contacting our practice and scheduling appointments.

How quickly do you respond to online inquiries?

We review all online messages and contact requests within 24 business hours. If you need immediate assistance or are experiencing a dental emergency, please call our office directly at (480) 515-1464 .

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card, and any medical/dental history lists. You can speed up check-in by arriving 15 minutes early to finish any required registration paperwork.

What is your cancellation policy?

We value your time and request at least 24 to 48 hours notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment. This allows us to offer the slot to other patients in need of dental care.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Yes, we accept and file claim paperwork with most major PPO dental insurance providers. Please contact our office to confirm your specific coverage details with our front-desk specialists.
