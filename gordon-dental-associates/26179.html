Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?

Most patients are pleasantly surprised to find that getting a dental implant is not as painful as they expect. At Gordon Dental in St. Augustine, FL, the procedure is performed using local anesthesia to keep you comfortable throughout the process. You may feel slight pressure during the placement, but not pain.

After the procedure, it’s normal to experience mild soreness or swelling for a few days, similar to what you might feel after a tooth extraction. This can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and following your dentist’s aftercare instructions.

Our team is committed to making your experience as smooth and comfortable as possible. If you have concerns or would like to learn more, call us at (904) 460-0999 or schedule an appointment at our St. Augustine, FL office today.

What is the process of an implant?

At Gordon Dental in St. Augustine, FL, the dental implant process is completed in several stages to ensure long-term success and a natural-looking result. It begins with a comprehensive consultation where we evaluate your oral health, take digital images, and determine if you’re a good candidate for implants.

Next, the implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it acts as a replacement root. Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation.

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to connect the implant to your new restoration. Finally, a custom-made crown is placed on top, designed to match the shape and color of your natural teeth.

Our team will guide you through each step and ensure you’re comfortable throughout the process. To learn more, call us at (904) 460-0999 or schedule an appointment at our St. Augustine, FL office today.

How long after an implant placement can I eat?

Right after we place your implant, your mouth needs a little time to heal. Most patients start eating soft foods shortly after the procedure. You can eat foods like yogurt, mashed potatoes, smoothies, eggs, or soup.

Within about 24 hours, many patients feel ready to resume light eating, as long as they continue to avoid hard, crunchy, or chewy foods. Protecting the area during this early stage helps your implant heal properly.

Following Post-Op Instructions

Every patient heals a bit differently, so Dr. Gordon provides detailed post-operative instructions tailored specifically to you. These guidelines play an important role in ensuring proper healing and minimizing the risk of complications.

Following these instructions closely helps you recover more comfortably and efficiently.

  • Avoid chewing directly on the implant site.
  • Stick to soft foods for a few days.
  • Maintain gentle but consistent oral hygiene.
  • Avoid very hot foods or drinks at first.

Once your implant fully heals and we place your permanent crown, you can go back to enjoying all your favorite foods. At that point, your dental implant functions just like a natural tooth.
