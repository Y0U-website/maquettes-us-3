The practice

At Unident Dental Group, we offer a full range of dental services — from implants and general care to advanced cosmetic and surgical treatments. Whether it’s a routine checkup or your child’s first visit, you’ll receive compassionate, expert care every time.

We deliver comprehensive care that covers regular checkups, cleanings, fillings, and preventive education. We emphasize the importance of maintaining oral health to prevent more serious dental issues and ensure long-term wellness.

We help patients achieve the smile they’ve always wanted through treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers. Enhancing your smile can boost your confidence and make a lasting impression on others.

We provide a calm, supportive environment for restful sleep and clear breathing. We highlight treatments for snoring, sleep apnea, and airway issues, and guide families on lifelong wellness and healthy sleep habits.

We offer traditional braces and clear aligners tailored for both teens and adults. We emphasize how orthodontic treatment not only improves smile aesthetics but also improves oral function and jaw alignment.
