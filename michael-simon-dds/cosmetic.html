Close gaps, repair chipped or cracked teeth, or cover discolored teeth with custom, long-lasting porcelain veneers. Each veneer is individually shaped and shaded to match your smile by our talented cosmetic dentist in Palos Verdes.

Invisalign® Treatment

An advanced, simple, and discreet orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using a series of removable clear aligners. Wearable by both children and adults, with many benefits over traditional braces.

Implants

A long-term solution for missing teeth, implants are made of titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone to mimic the root of a natural tooth. Covered by a crown, bridge, or denture, an implant is a great replacement, because it appears natural, is cosmetically pleasing, and is secure and stable.

Repair minor cosmetic flaws quickly, effectively, and painlessly with dental resin bonding. Composite resin is applied to your tooth, and shaped and shaded for a perfect match.
