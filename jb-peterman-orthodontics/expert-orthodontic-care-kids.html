FAQs About For Kids Orthodontics

At what age should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7 ! Don’t worry, this doesn’t usually mean they’ll get braces right away. It simply gives us a chance to monitor how their jaw is growing and catch any potential spacing or bite issues early on, keeping their smile on the right track!

What are the signs that my child might need early orthodontic care?

Every smile is unique, but some common signs it might be time to visit us include:

  • Losing baby teeth unusually early or late.
  • Visible crowding, overlapping, or misplaced teeth.
  • Difficulty chewing or biting comfortably.
  • Thumb-sucking habits that continue past age 5.
  • Jaws that shift, click, or pop.
  • Speech difficulties or mouth breathing.

What are the benefits of starting orthodontic treatment early?

Think of early treatment (often called Phase 1) as setting up a great foundation! By guiding jaw development while your child is still growing, we can ensure there’s plenty of room for permanent teeth to arrive safely. This often shortens their overall treatment time later on, reduces the need for tooth extractions or future jaw surgery, and boosts their everyday confidence!

What kid-friendly treatment options do you offer at JB Peterman Orthodontics?

We love making smile transformations fun and comfortable! For our younger patients, we offer:

  • Traditional Metal Braces : Super durable, highly effective, and customizable with colorful bands that kids love to show off!
  • Clear Ceramic Braces : The same reliable power as metal braces, but made from tooth-colored materials for a more low-profile look.
  • Palatal Expanders : A clever, gentle device that helps widen a developing upper jaw to fix crossbites and make room for incoming teeth.
  • Clear Aligners : Removable, virtually invisible, and perfect for keeping up with brushing and flossing easily.

What should we expect during our child’s first visit to your office?

Expect lots of high energy, warm smiles, and zero stress! Your child’s first consultation is completely complimentary. Our team will take comfortable, state-of-the-art digital scans and X-rays (no messy, gooey impressions here!), perform a gentle exam, and chat with you about whether treatment is needed yet. If it is, we’ll build a customized, easy-to-understand plan just for them!
