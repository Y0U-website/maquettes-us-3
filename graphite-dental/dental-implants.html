What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent tooth replacement solution that mimics the structure of natural teeth. Made of biocompatible titanium, implants are surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, they are restored with custom crowns, bridges, or dentures, creating a fully functional and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Discover more about our restorative dentistry services to see how dental implants can transform your smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural Appearance: Implants look and feel like your natural teeth.
  • Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Preservation of Bone Health: Implants prevent bone loss by stimulating the jawbone.
  • Improved Functionality: Restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile confidently.
  • No Damage to Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, implants do not require alteration of surrounding teeth.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are suitable for most patients with good oral and general health. Ideal candidates should:

If you require additional procedures such as bone grafting to support your implants, our team will create a personalized treatment plan for you.

  • Have sufficient jawbone density to support implants.
  • Be free of gum disease or other oral infections.
  • Commit to maintaining good oral hygiene.

The Dental Implant Process

At Graphite Dental, we guide you through every step of your dental implant journey:

  • Consultation and Evaluation Dr. Conway performs a comprehensive examination, including X-rays, to determine if implants are the right solution for you.
  • Implant Placement The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation for your comfort.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Over a few months, the implant fuses with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation for the restoration.
  • Restoration Attachment Once healed, the implant is restored with a custom crown, bridge, or denture. Learn more about dental implant restoration for the final steps in the implant process.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are a long-term solution, lasting 20 years or more with proper care and maintenance.

Are dental implants painful? The implant procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the surgery.

How do I care for dental implants? Implants require the same care as natural teeth, including brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups.

Can I replace multiple teeth with implants? Yes, dental implants can support multiple teeth through implant-supported bridges or dentures.

What is the cost of dental implants? Costs vary depending on the number of implants and the restoration type. Contact us for a consultation to receive a personalized estimate and financing options.
