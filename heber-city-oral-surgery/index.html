After graduating from dental school he practiced general dentistry for 7 years and received a Fellowship in American Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD). He was then accepted into the Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery residency program at the University of California San Francisco Medical Center- Fresno Valley Medical Center (UCSF-Fresno) where he completed his OMFS residency in 1980.

Dr. Sorensen is a fellow of the American Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS), Diplomate of the American Academy of Pain Management. He is a member of the Utah Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery (UAOMS), the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS) the American Dental Association (ADA), Utah Dental Association (UDA) and several other organizations…

Dr. Sorensen and his wife, Rebecca reside in Heber City, Utah. They have 8 children and 30 grandchildren.

Dr. Sorensen‘s surgical staff are experienced, UAMOS/AAOMS-OMSA Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgical Assistants who assist in the administration of IV sedation and surgery.
