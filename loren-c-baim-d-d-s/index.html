The practice

Our team aims to make you comfortable in our office, from the time you call us to the time you leave our office after your treatment is done. We are always ready to serve you and find out what your specific needs are. If, for any reason you feel uncomfortable, anxious or afraid, we will gladly listen to your concerns and do everything we can to make you feel better.

Our goal is to provide our patients with a positive experience and so we always make it a point to take your needs, questions and concerns into consideration in everything we do for you.

We also take pride in our state-of-the-art dental facility and equipment, guaranteeing safe, comfortable and efficient procedures right here in our office.

Our patients who have been with us for awhile know that our practice underscores the importance of traditional patient-dentist relationships. They need to make well-informed decisions about their oral health and they need the empathy and guidance of a trusted dentist who is completely dedicated to guide them through the process. This, along with Dr. Baim’s gentle touch, keeps our patients loyal…

Our office is open Monday through Thursday. We are always on call for our patients as well as to those needing emergency dental services.

To provide you with the highest quality of dental care in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere. We are 100 % committed to excellence in the field of dentistry and want to help you maintain, enhance and restore a healthy and attractive smile.
