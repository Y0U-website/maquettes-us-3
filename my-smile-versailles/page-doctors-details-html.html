Dr. Su T Kang, DDS

Primary Dentist

Dr. Su Kang was born in South Korea and raised in Greensburg, Indiana. He attended Indiana University and completed his postdoctoral dental program in Indianapolis, Indiana. He also served in active duty military and finished his military career as Colonel in the Dental Corp.

Dr. Kang is married to Jina Kim. Together they raised 3 beautiful children, Sujin, Sungmin and Sungjae.

Dr. Kang has been in dentistry since 1989 and continues to serve the community and other medical missions throughout South America.
