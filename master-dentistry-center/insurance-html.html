Insurance we accept

We accept most major PPO plans and file all claims for you. Not sure what your plan covers? Call us with your member ID before your visit and we’ll verify your benefits for free, then explain exactly what your plan pays and what your out-of-pocket cost will be.

No insurance? Join our membership plan.

Our in-house membership covers your cleanings, exams, and X-rays for one flat annual fee, plus a discount on other treatment — no deductibles, no waiting periods, no denied claims. It’s the simplest way for uninsured patients and families to stay on top of their dental health.

Financing for larger treatment plans

We use third-party financial services to cover larger treatment plans. Applying takes minutes, and we’ll help you do it right in our office.
