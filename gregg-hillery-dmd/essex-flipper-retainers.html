Benefits of Essex and Flipper Retainers

Our Essex and flipper retainers offer a range of benefits for those seeking a temporary tooth replacement

Natural Appearance Both retainer types blend with your smile, providing a discreet, seamless look.

Ease of Use Lightweight and removable, Essex and flipper retainers are simple to wear and care for.

Affordable Solution These retainers are cost-effective options for maintaining your smile while preparing for a more permanent replacement.

Our retainers are crafted for comfort and aesthetics, ensuring you feel confident and comfortable wearing them.

Types of Retainers Offered

At Central City Dentistry, we offer both Essex and flipper retainers to meet individual needs

Essex Retainers These clear, thin retainers fit snugly over the teeth, providing a nearly invisible look.

Flipper Retainers With a natural-looking tooth attached, flipper retainers are often used to fill the gap of a single missing tooth, offering stability and a natural appearance.

Our dental team will help you choose the best retainer option based on your needs and aesthetic preferences.

What to Expect During the Retainer Fitting Process

Receiving an Essex or flipper retainer involves a simple, straightforward process

Impressions We start by taking impressions of your teeth to create a custom-fit retainer.

Customization The retainer is tailored to fit comfortably and match the color and shape of your teeth.

Fitting and Adjustments Once ready, we ensure your retainer fits well and provide guidance on wearing and caring for it.

With custom retainers from Central City Dentistry, you can enjoy a seamless, natural-looking smile while you await your permanent solution.
