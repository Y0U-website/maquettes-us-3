What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and treating misaligned teeth and jaws. It involves the use of braces, clear aligners, and other dental devices to improve both the function and appearance of your smile.

At what age should my child see an orthodontist?

It’s recommended that children have their first orthodontic consultation around the age of 7. Early screenings help detect potential issues and allow for timely intervention if necessary.

How long will I need to wear braces?

The length of treatment varies depending on the individual’s dental needs. On average, most patients wear braces for 18 to 24 months, but the duration may be shorter or longer based on the complexity of the case.

Do braces hurt?

Braces may cause some discomfort, especially after adjustments. However, the pain is typically mild and temporary. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help manage any soreness.

Can adults get braces?

Yes! Orthodontic treatment is effective at any age. Many adults choose to get braces or clear aligners to improve their smiles and oral health. It’s never too late to benefit from orthodontic care.

What are clear aligners?

Clear aligners, such as Invisalign, are a popular alternative to traditional braces. These are custom-made, removable plastic trays that gradually shift your teeth into place without the need for metal brackets and wires.

How do I care for my braces?

Proper care is essential for keeping your braces clean and functioning effectively. You should brush your teeth after every meal, floss daily, and avoid sticky or hard foods that can damage your braces.

Will I need to wear a retainer after my braces come off?

Yes, wearing a retainer after braces is crucial for maintaining the results. Retainers help keep your teeth in their new positions and prevent them from shifting back to their original alignment.

Can orthodontic treatment fix my bite?

Yes, orthodontics can correct bite issues like overbites, underbites, and crossbites, improving both the function and appearance of your teeth and jaw.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

The cost of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the type of treatment, the complexity of the case, and the duration of the treatment. We offer flexible payment options and can provide you with a clear estimate during your consultation.
