The Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation

The journey to getting implants begins with a thorough consultation at Lemon Tree Dental. Our team of dental implants in Greenville, SC, will evaluate your oral health and discuss your treatment options. This may include X-rays or 3D imaging to assess the bone structure and determine the best placement for the implants.

Implant Placement

Once you are deemed a suitable candidate, the implant placement surgery will be scheduled. During the procedure, our dentist in Greenville , will insert the titanium posts into the jawbone. This surgery is typically done under local anesthesia, ensuring you remain comfortable throughout the process.

Healing Period

After placing the implants, they need time to bond with your bone. This bonding process, called osseointegration, usually takes a few months. While this is happening, you might get a temporary replacement to help you eat and talk as usual.

Restoration

Once the implants have fully integrated with the bone, the next step is the restoration process. Dr. Lemon will attach custom-made crowns or bridges to the implants. These restorations are designed to match the appearance and function of your natural teeth.

Implant Restoration

After your implant is securely placed and healed, we start the restoration phase. In Greenville, implant restoration means making a custom dental crown , bridge, or denture that fits onto your implant. We use quality materials to make sure the restoration looks like your natural teeth. Dr. Lemon Greenville dentist, will work with you to design a restoration that matches your smile and works well.

Benefits of Dental Implants

It provides several advantages over traditional tooth replacement options like bridges and dentures:

  • Durability : Implants are designed to last decades with good care.
  • Stability: Implants, unlike dentures, are fixed and won’t move around in your mouth.
  • Bone Preservation : When provided by our professional dentist near you, implants prevent any bone loss, which is observed in cases of teeth that are absent.
  • Natural Appearance : With dental implants, you get a natural appearance and feel of your teeth, which will make you confident enough to smile, speak, and eat with comfort.
  • No Effect on the Adjacent Teeth : Implants do not rely on the other teeth, as is the case with dental bridges ; therefore, the natural teeth remain in good health.
