Book Consultation What Is a Root Canal? A root canal is an endodontic procedure that treats infection or damage to the pulp (nerve) inside your tooth. By removing the infected tissue, cleaning the canal, and sealing it, we can save your natural tooth and eliminate pain.

Is a root canal painful?

No. Root canals have an unfair reputation. With modern anesthesia and techniques, the procedure is no more uncomfortable than getting a filling. Most patients experience immediate relief from the tooth pain they had before treatment.

How long does a root canal take?

Most root canals can be completed in a single 60-90 minute visit. Complex cases may require a second appointment. You can typically return to normal activities the same day.

What happens if I don&#x27;t get a root canal?

Without treatment, the infection can spread, leading to abscess formation, severe pain, swelling, and potential tooth loss. In rare cases, untreated infections can spread to other parts of the body.
