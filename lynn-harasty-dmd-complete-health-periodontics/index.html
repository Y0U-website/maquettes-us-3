The practice

Perio Protect is a comprehensive approach to gum disease treatment. The approach combines dental cleanings with the comfortable Perio Tray delivery of medication that patients use at home between office visits. The Perio Tray is similar to a mouth-guard, but it has a special sealing system to deliver medication below the gums. You put medication in the tray and wear it for a few minutes a day.…

The Pinhole Surgical Technique PST is a procedure in which Dr. Harasty uses specialized instruments to make a small pinhole in the gum and then add collagen material to stabilize the area of gum recession.
