Our Mission and Approach

At Knight Pediatric Dentistry, our goal is to create a community of healthy smiles through compassionate dentistry. We know that every little smile is unique, so we offer highly-personalized care for every pediatric patient. Our team takes an enthusiastic approach to every appointment to get your child excited and interested in their oral health. We make sure that your little one leaves our office feeling happier and healthier than when they arrived. Whether you need preventive care, restorative treatments, or an emergency visit for your child, we’re happy to help!

What to Expect

First Visits at Our Office

If you’re bringing your child into our office for the first time, you can always expect friendly, personalized care. If your child has all of their primary teeth, we’ll examine each one and keep an eye out for any issues. One of our hygienists will perform a gentle cleaning and Dr. Knight will any questions you may have about your child’s oral health. Any issues with your child’s oral health and development will be addressed, and you’ll leave our office with peace of mind knowing that your little one is happier and healthier than when you came in.
