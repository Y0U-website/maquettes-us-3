What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are shaped like screws and inserted within the jawbone. They’re specially designed to replace the root structure of missing teeth and are able to naturally fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration. Once this has occurred, a free-standing crown, bridge, or full denture can be secured to the implants for results that look and feel completely natural.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Consultation: The first part of every dental implant treatment is a thorough consultation with our experts. One of our implant dentists in West Bridgewater will examine your mouth, and our team will take X-rays. This will let us know whether you’re a good candidate for treatment. If so, we can create your treatment plan.
  • Computer-Guided Implant Placement: We utilize customized guides to place your dental implants with extreme precision in the most supportive parts of your jaw, allowing for longer lasting and higher quality results.
  • Healing & Abutments: After your surgery, you will undergo a healing period of a couple of weeks, and your implants will fuse with your jawbone over the course of three to six months. Then, our team will have you return to our office so we can attach your abutments to your implants, which are small metal connector pieces that will hold the restoration in place.
  • Enjoy the Final Results: Once your final restoration returns from our dental laboratory, the last step will be to check its fit and attach it. After this appointment, your journey will be complete, and you’ll be able to leave our office with a whole smile again!
