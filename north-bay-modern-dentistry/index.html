The practice

Dr. Marina Shamoun earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree with honors from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. She later completed her specialty training in orthodontics and earned a master’s degree at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF).

As a board-certified orthodontist, Dr. Shamoun provides personalized orthodontic care for children, teens, and adults. She combines advanced treatment techniques with a thoughtful, detail-oriented approach to create healthy, confident smiles.
