The practice

At Hyndman Area Health Center, our commitment is always to you. We offer a variety of high-quality, affordable healthcare and financial resources to keep our community healthy and happy.

Comprehensive primary and preventive care for patients of all ages, from infants to seniors.

Preventive, restorative, and hygiene dental services to keep your smile healthy and bright.

Specialized support, monitoring, and education for managing long-term health conditions.

Specialized primary care dedicated to the health and wellness of infants, children, and adolescents.

Comprehensive gynecological, prenatal, and reproductive health services tailored for women.

Workplace wellness solutions, physical exams, and work-related injury management.

Professional skin examinations, cancer screenings, and treatment for various skin conditions.
