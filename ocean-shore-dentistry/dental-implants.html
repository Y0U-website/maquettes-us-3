How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to provide a durable and lasting solution for missing teeth. When maintained appropriately, these implants can remain effective for many years. To get the most out of your new teeth, exercise good oral hygiene habits; maintain regular dental exams and professional cleanings; minimize risk factors (like smoking); and follow Dr. Shah’s instructions for care and follow-ups.

How do implants compare to bridges or dentures?

Unlike bridges, implants replace missing teeth without relying on neighboring teeth. They keep the neighboring teeth intact. Implants are also fixed in place in the jawbone, unlike removable dentures. Importantly, implants are distinct from bridges and dentures because they stimulate the jawbone in the area where the tooth was lost. This helps maintain bone health and preserve facial structure, appearance, and function.

Dental implants can offer a stable, comfortable, and potentially lifelong solution to tooth loss for the right patients. While they cost more upfront than bridges or dentures, implants are a good value because they can last so long and avoid damaging and costly complications of tooth loss (like bone loss in the jaw).
