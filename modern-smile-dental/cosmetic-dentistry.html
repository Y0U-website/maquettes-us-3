Frequently Asked Questions about Cosmetic Dentistry

What is considered cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry includes treatments designed to enhance the appearance of your teeth, gums, and overall smile. While general dentistry focuses on preventing and treating disease, cosmetic care aims to improve the shape, color, alignment, and proportion of the teeth. Many procedures offer both aesthetic and functional benefits, allowing patients to achieve a healthier-looking smile without compromising long-term oral health.

How do I decide which cosmetic treatment is best for me?

Every smile is unique, which is why treatment begins with a detailed consultation. Dr. Michelle Janbakhsh evaluates tooth structure, gum symmetry, bite alignment, and your aesthetic goals. Some patients may only need whitening to refresh their smile, while others may benefit from reshaping, veneers , bonding , or clear aligner therapy . A customized plan ensures results that complement your facial features and preferences.

Can cosmetic dentistry correct minor alignment issues?

Yes, depending on the severity. Small gaps, slight crowding, or mild unevenness may be improved with cosmetic techniques, such as bonding or veneers, which visually enhance alignment. However, if teeth require significant repositioning, orthodontic treatment such as clear aligners may be recommended to physically move them into healthier positions.

Are cosmetic dental procedures permanent?

Some cosmetic treatments provide long-lasting improvements, while others may require maintenance over time. For example, professional whitening may need occasional touch-ups, whereas porcelain restorations can last many years with proper care. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, lifestyle habits, and routine dental visits.

Is cosmetic dentistry safe for healthy teeth?

When performed conservatively and appropriately, cosmetic dentistry is designed to preserve healthy tooth structure. Modern materials and techniques allow dentists to enhance aesthetics while minimizing enamel removal. Dr. Janbakhsh carefully evaluates whether a treatment is suitable before proceeding, ensuring that health remains the top priority.

How long does it take to see results?

Timelines vary by procedure. Whitening can brighten your smile in a single appointment , while veneers may require multiple visits. Clear aligner therapy unfolds gradually over several months. During your consultation, you will receive a clear understanding of how long your chosen treatment will take and what to expect at each stage.

Can cosmetic dentistry make my smile look too artificial?

Modern cosmetic dentistry focuses on natural-looking results. Materials are selected to mimic the way natural enamel reflects light, and careful attention is given to proportions and symmetry. The goal is subtle enhancement, improving imperfections without creating an overdone appearance.

What if I only want to improve one tooth?

Cosmetic dentistry is highly customizable. Some patients choose to enhance a single tooth that stands out due to discoloration, shape, or damage. Treatment can be limited to that specific area while blending seamlessly with surrounding teeth.

Does cosmetic dentistry improve more than just appearance?

In many cases, yes. Improving alignment can make teeth easier to clean. Restoring worn edges can protect the remaining structure. Correcting uneven surfaces can reduce irritation to soft tissues. Aesthetic improvements often go hand in hand with functional benefits.

Is cosmetic dentistry worth the investment?

For many patients, enhancing their smile positively impacts confidence, social interactions, and professional opportunities. Feeling comfortable smiling openly can influence daily life in meaningful ways. A consultation allows you to explore options and determine whether cosmetic dentistry aligns with your goals and budget.
