The All-on-X Procedure

Consultation and 3D Imaging

We examine your mouth and take a 3D CBCT scan of your jaw. This lets Dr. Borukhov plan the number and position of your implants with precision.

Implant Placement Surgery

The implants are placed in the jaw during a single surgical visit. Back implants are often angled to use available bone and reduce the need for grafting.

Temporary Teeth the Same Day

In most cases you receive a temporary fixed set of teeth on the day of surgery, so you leave with a full arch rather than an empty gap.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over about three to six months, the implants fuse with your jawbone. This healing creates the stable foundation your final teeth rely on.

Final Prosthesis

Once healing is complete, we fit your custom final prosthesis, made to match your bite and the look you want.

All-on-X Implants vs. Traditional Dentures

Recovery and Aftercare

Recovery from All-on-X surgery is manageable for most patients with normal post-operative care. Some swelling and soreness in the first days is expected, and Dr. Borukhov will give you clear instructions on managing it. You will follow a soft diet during the early healing period while the implants integrate with your bone.

As you heal, you gradually return to a fuller range of foods. Once your final prosthesis is fitted, you care for it with regular brushing, the cleaning tools we recommend, and routine checkups at our Flushing office so we can keep your implants and gums healthy over the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many implants will I need?

Most cases use four implants per arch, which is where the name All-on-4 comes from. Some patients need five or six for the best support. Dr. Borukhov confirms the number after reviewing your 3D scan.

Will I leave surgery without teeth?

In most cases, no. Many patients receive a temporary fixed set of teeth on the day of surgery, so you leave with a full arch while your implants heal.

Do I need a bone graft?

Not always. Angled placement of the back implants often makes use of available bone and avoids grafting. If you have significant bone loss, grafting can address it, and we will tell you at your consultation.

How long does the whole process take?

After surgery, the implants fuse with the bone over roughly three to six months. Once that healing is complete, we fit your final prosthesis.

Is the procedure painful?

The surgery is done so you stay comfortable, and most patients manage recovery with routine post-operative care. Some soreness and swelling in the first days is normal.

Are these teeth permanent?

The implants and prosthesis are a fixed, long-term solution that does not come out like a denture. With good hygiene, routine checkups, and occasional prosthesis maintenance, they are designed for many years of use.
