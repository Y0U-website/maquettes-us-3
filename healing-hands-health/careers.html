Registered/Certified Medical Assistant – Part Time

Are you a compassionate and motivated Medical Assistant looking to positively impact patients’ lives? We are seeking a Registered or Certified Medical Assistant (RMA/CMA) to join our dedicated care team. In this part-time role, you will be at the heart of patient care —balancing hands-on clinical responsibilities with vital administrative support to ensure every patient receives high-quality, compassionate service. If you’re detail-oriented, passionate about helping others, and eager to join a mission-driven team making a difference every day , we would love to meet you!

· Greet patients and visitors in a friendly and professional manner

· Prepare patients for examinations, and obtain and document vital signs, medical histories, and chief complaints

· Assist providers with examinations, procedures, and treatments as instructed

· Perform basic laboratory tests and collect specimens (e.g., blood, urine)

· Administer medications and immunizations as directed

· Maintain clean and stocked exam rooms and clinical areas

· Follow infection control and safety procedures

· Required to safeguard the confidentiality of patients’ health information maintained in any form including medical records, electronic systems, oral or written communications

· Schedule patient appointments and provide back up for the Front Office Coordinator, as needed

· Document patient information accurately in the electronic health record (EHR), including patient enrollment documents
