Dr. Azadeh Abraham

Following her clinical training, Dr. Abraham served as a faculty member for more than five years, during which she played an active role in mentoring and educating future dental professionals. This academic experience further strengthened her clinical foundation and reinforced her commitment to excellence in dentistry.

In pursuit of continued advancement, Dr. Abraham completed additional education at the University at Buffalo, where she expanded her clinical knowledge and refined her approach to comprehensive dental care.

She later established her own practice, where she continues to serve families in the Paramount community. Her clinical philosophy centers on providing comprehensive, individualized treatment in a professional and comfortable environment, with a strong focus on quality outcomes and patient trust.Outside of her professional responsibilities, Dr. Abraham enjoys painting, which reflects her appreciation for detail and creativity. In her free time she spends time with her family.
