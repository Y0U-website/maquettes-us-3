Appointment Scheduling

Please call or text 847-827-6300 to schedule an appointment. You can also email us at office@drjhalpern.com. If you reach our office after hours you may leave your name and telephone number and we will return your call the next business day.

If you have pain or an emergency situation, every attempt will be made to see you that day. If you have an after hours emergency, you can reach our doctors by calling the office at 847-827-6300. When the message plays, enter “2” and the doctor can then be paged to return your call as soon as possible.

We try our best to stay on schedule to minimize your wait. Because we treat emergency patients, unexpected delays may arise. We appreciate your understanding and patience. We make every attempt to contact scheduled patients if we are running significantly late.

Please remember to cancel your appointment at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a cancellation fee.

Please print and complete the Patient Instruction Card at home before your first appointment. If you need assistance with downloading or printing these forms, please contact our office.
