Snore Guards & Other Oral Appliances

Snoring is a very common problem that affects both sufferers and their partners alike. It may also be indicative of Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA), which is a serious health condition characterized as a complete or partial physical obstruction of the upper airway while sleeping. This can be potentially life-threatening, since you may be partially or completely deprived of oxygen at the most vulnerable moment. Fortunately, snoring and OSA can be effectively treated and controlled with snore guards and other custom oral devices that comfortably keep your airways open while you sleep.

Sedation

We understand that dental treatment can cause a lot of anxiety, stress, and discomfort for adult and pediatric patients alike. For a calm and relaxing experience, we offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) or pills for oral sedation. If your appointment requires sedation, you will need a companion to take you home. Oral sedation is often stronger than laughing gas and may cause you to fall asleep during treatment. Nonetheless, you can still be woken up under the effects of the medication.
