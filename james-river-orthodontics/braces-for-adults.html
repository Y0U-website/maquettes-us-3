When is it too late to get braces?

We’re glad you asked! Contrary to popular belief, it’s never too late to get braces. The age you are when you decide to transform your smile and improve your quality of life is the perfect age to begin orthodontic treatment.

There’s more to a smile than just perfect alignment. Adult orthodontics can improve not only your appearance and self-esteem, but your breathing ability, chewing and bite functionality, and sleep quality.

Your orthodontic condition affects every aspect of your life—esthetically and functionally.

Options in adult braces

We understand that metal braces aren’t every adult’s cup of tea. That’s why we offer different types of high-quality braces, including clear braces for adults. Each device works by applying constant, gentle pressure to gradually shift your teeth into their proper places.

Whether you prefer a traditional look, a removable, convenient treatment, or a more discreet appearance, we have the option to meet your needs and suit your lifestyle.

  • Invisalign® clear aligners: Choosing clear aligners, like Invisalign® for adults , means you can straighten your smile with a convenient and removable treatment option. The aligners are made out of a plastic material that’s customized to your teeth and worn as long as your orthodontist specifies.
  • Damon self-ligating braces: Self-ligating metal braces feature tie-less technology. This means there will be less friction because no elastic ties are holding the archwire to the brackets. As a result, the treatment process is quicker and more comfortable.
  • Clear self-ligating Damon® braces: For a less visible option, we also offer clear self-ligating Damon® braces. The tooth-colored material seamlessly blends into your teeth, making these adult ceramic braces a more discreet alternative.
