Conditions

Every condition tells a story about how the head and neck are compensating.

Headaches, jaw pain, neck pain, ear symptoms, and poor sleep often seem like separate conditions. Sometimes they are. Sometimes they are connected — the body compensating for compression somewhere else in the system.

Each page below explains one condition: how compression and compensation may contribute to it, and how the Head & Neck Decompression Method™ evaluates it — so that treatment, when appropriate, can be directed at the source rather than only the symptom.
