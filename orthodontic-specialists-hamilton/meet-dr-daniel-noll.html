Outside of the Office

Outside the office, Dr. Daniel Noll is faithful to the Reds and a big Bengals fan. His claim to fame is a reality TV debut as best man to his friend Tres on Married at First Sight . His ultimate weakness? Auntie Anne’s pretzels.

Dr. Noll and his wife Kasey met while taking glassblowing classes at Centre College and now have three awesome young kids. These days, his favorite hobbies include chasing his children, exploring National Parks, staying active with health and fitness, and playing pickup basketball—plus recovering from the injuries that come with it.
