Dental Veneers for Mililani and Pearl City, HI

If you feel that your teeth need a makeover, dental veneers are a great option to consider. Mililani Dental & Implants is here to provide veneers for your teeth in Mililani and Pearl City, HI.

What Are Veneers?

Veneers are transparent coverings for your front teeth. They are custom-made to fit your teeth and are usually made of porcelain. Their main purpose is to hide imperfections in your teeth.

What Are They Good For?

They are great for a wide range of issues. For example, do you have gaps, discolored teeth, chips, cracks, stains, small teeth, or misaligned teeth? Then dental veneers can help hide and shape these problems so that you have a better-looking smile.

How Do We Apply Them?

When you decide on dental veneers, we will look over your teeth to get an accurate picture of them. Then afterward, we’ll do some dental work to ensure the veneers fit properly. Then, we’ll get tooth impressions to send to a lab. The lab will create the veneers. After they’re done, we will place them on your teeth with a special bonding agent. We will ensure that they look well on your teeth.

How Can You Best Care for Your Dental Veneers?

While dental veneers are hardy and durable, they still require care like the rest of your teeth. Brush and floss as normal, and don’t eat hard or crunchy foods often. Plus, if you want to avoid any potential staining, don’t consume any food or drink that leaves stains behind.
