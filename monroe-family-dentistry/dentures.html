DENTURE

At Monroe Family Dentistry , dentures may be the ideal option for people looking for an economical alternative that does not require surgery. We provide partial dentures for people who still have some natural teeth and full dentures for patients who require complete arch restoration. Our dentures are all custom-made to look like your real teeth and fit comfortably over your gums.

We can use several dental implants to secure a partial or complete denture to your jaw, providing a more stable and non-removable repair. This offers superior health protection and long-term results.
