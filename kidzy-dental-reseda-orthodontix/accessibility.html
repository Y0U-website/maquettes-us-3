Accessibility Statement

  • Accessibility Statement | Kidzy Dental Skip to main content Our commitment Kidzy Dental is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including patients with disabilities. We believe that access to dental care begins with access to information. Standard we follow We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA , the standard referenced by the U.S. Department of Justice for ADA Title III web accessibility. What we do Automated accessibility scans run on every build using axe-core.
  • Keyboard navigation tests covering primary navigation and calls to action.
  • Periodic manual review of color contrast, heading structure, and form labeling.
  • Skip-to-content link and semantic landmarks on every page.
  • Respect for system-level reduced-motion preferences.

Known limitations

We continue to improve the site over time. Some third-party content embedded for booking, maps, or analytics may not yet meet the same standard.
