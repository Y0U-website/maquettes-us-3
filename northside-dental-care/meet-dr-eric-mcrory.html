Education and Dental Training

Dr. McRory completed his undergraduate education at Whitman College , where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Geology in 1991 .

He later received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry and graduated in 1995.

After dental school, he began practicing dentistry alongside his father, who served the Bellingham community for nearly 40 years before retiring in 2000.
