Frequently Asked Questions

What age is best for early orthodontic evaluation? Most children should have their first orthodontic screening by age 7. This allows dentists to detect subtle issues with jaw growth or tooth alignment and intervene if needed.

Will my child need braces after early intervention? Not necessarily. Early orthodontic treatment often reduces or even eliminates the need for braces later. If they are needed, treatment is usually simpler and shorter in duration.

What are signs that my child might need early orthodontic care? Watch for mouth breathing, thumb-sucking past age 5, speech issues, crowded or misplaced teeth, or difficulty chewing. An evaluation can help determine if these symptoms need treatment.

Is early orthodontic care painful? No. Most early orthodontic appliances are designed to be gentle and comfortable. Our team takes special care to ensure your child feels safe and confident throughout treatment.

Does insurance cover early orthodontic treatment? Many dental insurance plans offer partial coverage for orthodontic evaluations or interceptive treatments. Our team will help you review your benefits and financing options.
