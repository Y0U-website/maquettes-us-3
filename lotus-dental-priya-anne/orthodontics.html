Orthodontics Q & A

What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized dentistry focused on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws.

At Lotus Dental, Dr. Anne uses orthodontic treatments to improve the look of your smile and how your teeth work together when you chew.

These treatments address problems caused by genetics, crowding, or irregular jaw and tooth growth.

Why might I need orthodontic treatment?

Common reasons for orthodontic treatment include:

Orthodontics isn’t just for cosmetic reasons. Straightening your teeth and aligning your bite improves chewing, reduces tooth wear, and makes cleaning easier. These, in turn, lower your risk of cavities and gum disease.

  • Crowded teeth
  • Overlapped teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Twisted or rotated teeth
  • Poor bite alignment

How is orthodontic treatment planned?

Dr. Anne starts with a thorough evaluation, including digital X-rays and scans of your teeth and jaw. This provides her with an understanding of your specific needs. She then creates a personalized treatment plan to address functional problems, enhance your smile, and promote long-term oral health.

Lotus Dental offers several orthodontic solutions, including:

Invisalign

Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners that are far less noticeable than metal braces.

Traditional metal braces

Traditional bracket-and-wire braces are time-tested and effective.

Clear or tooth-colored braces

Clear or tooth-colored braces are less noticeable than metal braces but just as effective.

Lingual braces

Lingual braces go on the inside (tongue side) of your teeth for a hidden look.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial orthodontic checkup by age 7. By this stage, the first permanent molars have usually erupted, making it easier to evaluate jaw growth, tooth alignment, and bite problems early.

Is orthodontic treatment just for kids?

Orthodontic care can benefit adults of any age. While treatment often takes a bit longer for adults since their jawbone is fully developed, the results can still be excellent.

Many adults choose discreet choices like Invisalign or clear braces.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Orthodontic treatment times vary depending on your needs. Minor adjustments can take as little as six months, while more complex cases can take 18 24 months. Dr. Anne will give you a more accurate estimate during your consultation.
