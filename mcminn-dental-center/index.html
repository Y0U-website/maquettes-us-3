The practice

Transforming dental experiences in Athens through advanced technology and innovative care. From preventive health to full-arch reconstruction.

Our practice integrates advanced technology with innovative care to ensure every procedure is seamless and efficient. From precision diagnostics to thoroughly sterilized environments, we prioritize your safety using state-of-the-art equipment designed for the modern dental experience.

Building trust starts with honesty. We provide up-front cost information and navigate dental insurance on your behalf so there are no surprises. With flexible financing and in-network options, we ensure that high-quality care and full-arch reconstruction remain accessible and affordable.

Your time is as valuable as your smile. We offer a seamless patient experience through extended hours, digital appointment reminders, and rapid response times. Whether by phone or email, accessing professional dental information and scheduling your next visit is always fast and friction-free.

We’ve redefined the "dental office" into a bold and fun environment designed to alleviate anxiety. Enjoy your treatment with sedation options like Nitrous Oxide and clear communication at every step. At McMinn, your comfort isn’t just a courtesy it’s a pillar of our professional practice.

You are never just a number; you are a vital part of our community. We take a personalized interest in your lifestyle and long-term health goals, fostering lasting beauty and functionality through professional friendships. Experience a dental visit that feels as distinct as your own smile.

Our goal is for your first visit to be one of comfort and acknowledgement of your personal dental needs.

We will take the required time to properly diagnose your immediate dental concerns, review your past medical and dental history, and create a treatment plan that provides for your optimal dental health. With your cooperation, we will complete a thorough comprehensive dental evaluation.
