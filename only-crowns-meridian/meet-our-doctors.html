Meet Our Doctors

Founder of Only Crowns

Dr. Curtis Carpenter has been practicing in Idaho and Utah for almost 20 years. He graduated from the University of the Pacific in 2008 and completed his undergraduate studies at Brigham Young University-Idaho. Dr. Carpenter is committed to lifelong learning and regularly participates in continuing education to stay at the forefront of dental advancements.

With over 20 years of experience in CEREC crowns and hundreds of hours of advanced training, Dr. Carpenter is highly skilled in providing precise and efficient dental restorations. His dedication to advanced training ensures that his patients receive the highest quality care.

Dr. Carpenter is a devoted family man with three boys and one girl. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his family, riding dirt bikes and skiing.

Only Crown’s Dental Practitioners

Raised in Clearwater, Florida, Dr Clark earned his undergraduate degree from Brigham Young University before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine at the University of Florida in 2001. As a former U.S. Army Reserve member, he brings dedication and integrity to every aspect of his dental practice.

With over 25 years of experience in crowns, bridges, implants, and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Clark is passionate about lifelong learning and delivering exceptional care to his patients in Utah. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time outdoors with his family and giving back through community service.

Dr Vernon Barruga is a graduate of Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in Southern California and has been serving patients in Las Vegas for more than 25 years. Having completed hundreds of hours of continuing education to stay at the forefront of dental advancements, Dr Barruga offers patients the latest treatments and technologies for efficient, comfortable care from his offices in Las Vegas and Henderson, Nevada .

In his free time, he loves to travel, play poker, surf, snowboard, and stay active.

Dr. Ryan Porter is a proud native of Mesa, Arizona, and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California, where he served as class president for four years and received the Dean’s Award for leadership. With over 20 years of experience, he has focused on pediatric, family, and biological dentistry, earning accreditation through the IAOMT and HUH Institute.

Dr. Porter, who continues to practice in Mesa, Arizona, is also the inventor of the Joey Board, a patient safety device used nationwide. Outside the office, he enjoys time with his wife, Lesley, and their five children, exploring the outdoors together.

Originally from Calgary, Alberta, Dr. Bevans earned a dental degree from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in 2008 after completing undergraduate studies at Arizona State University. With advanced training in neuroinhibitors, dermal fillers, and radiofrequency therapy, Dr Bevans’ extensive experience spans cosmetic, surgical, and laser dentistry. As a national trainer for Denta-Spa Seminars and faculty member of the International Academy of Facial Esthetics, Dr. Bevans also helped pioneer radiofrequency applications in dentistry.

Based in Jupiter Farms, Florida, Dr. Bevans remains dedicated to providing compassionate, detail-oriented care and maintaining an active lifestyle with a focus on family and wellness.

Dr. R Corey Snow was born in Cleveland, Ohio, and raised in Pocatello, Idaho. After earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Oregon Health Sciences University in 1995, he practiced family and cosmetic dentistry in Pocatello before relocating to Charlotte, North Carolina, in 2011 in search of new opportunities and experiences.

With 30 years of experience, Dr. Snow is dedicated to delivering exceptional, patient-centered care and continually expanding his knowledge to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with family, oil painting, and golfing.

Dr. Christopher Capener grew up in Okinawa, Japan and went to high school in North Ogden, Utah. He completed his undergrad at Utah State University and graduated near the top of his class. Dr. Capener graduated from dental school in 2017 as part of the inaugural class at the University of Utah School of Dentistry, where he received one of the best educations in the country.

After graduation, Dr. Capener owned multiple dental practices in Las Vegas, where he gained exceptional experience in cosmetic dentistry, surgery, and general dentistry. He has completed thousands of hours of continuing education in order to stay on top of the latest and greatest procedures in dentistry.

During his free time, Dr. Capener enjoys spending time with his family, playing any sport, and being outdoors.
