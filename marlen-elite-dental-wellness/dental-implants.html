What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are metal, screw-like posts that provide safe, stable and durable anchors for new, artificial teeth that look and function like their natural predecessors.

Think of them as replacement tooth roots that are surgically anchored to your jawbone. Dental implant surgery can provide a welcome long-lasting alternative to dentures or bridgework. How dental implant surgery is performed depends on the type of implant inserted and the condition of your jawbone, and may involve several procedures. If you’re looking for a removable denture option, traditional dentures are both effective and affordable.

TYPES OF IMPLANTS AVAILABLE

All-on-4 Implants

The All-on-4 treatment concept uses just four dental implants to support a full arch of upper or lower teeth . This implant solution is ideal for patients who have lost all their teeth in an arch, have an inadequate bone height or density in the jaw, or wear dentures that are loose and uncomfortable. One major benefit of the All on Four dental implant option is that this process can take place over the course of a day, making them same-day teeth.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

This restorative dentistry procedure can give you a brand-new smile in just one day . We place four to eight implants in the upper or lower jaw and then attach a full set of replacement teeth . The implants fuse with the bone, providing a stable base for the new teeth that feel and function like your natural ones.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

A full mouth restoration is used to repair the damage caused by tooth decay, disease, or injury . We place implants in the upper and lower jaw and then attach a full set of replacement teeth. The implants will fuse with the bone, providing a stable base for the new teeth that feel and function like your natural ones.

Implant Supported Dentures

These dentures are held in place by implants, providing a more secure fit than traditional dentures. Implant-supported dentures are an option for patients who have lost all their teeth in an arch and are not candidates for All-on-4 implants.

Zygomatic Implants

This is a specialized type of dental implant that is used to support a full arch of upper teeth in patients who have lost bone mass in the upper jaw. Zygomatic implants are placed in the cheekbone and fuse with the bones in the face, providing a stable base for the new teeth.

Dental Implants and Gum Disease

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults , and it can also lead to bone loss in the jaw. If you have gum disease, you may be at risk of losing your teeth. Dental implants are placed in the jawbone and fuse with the bones in the face, providing a stable base for the new teeth. The implants will also help to preserve the jawbone and prevent further bone loss.

If you are considering dental implants, it is important to have healthy gums and bones. We can help you to maintain good oral health and prevent gum disease with regular dental cleanings , x-rays, and checkups. We may also recommend additional treatments, such as gum disease treatment and bone grafting, to ensure that your jaw is healthy enough to support the implants.

Reasons for considering dental implants

Dental implants may be right for you if you:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have a fully-grown jawbone
  • Have adequate bone to secure the implants or can undergo a bone graft
  • Have healthy oral tissues
  • Don’t have health conditions that might affect bone healing
  • Are unable or unwilling to wear dentures
  • Want to improve your speech
  • Can commit several months to the process
  • Don’t smoke tobacco or have quit smoking

Benefits of Dental Implants

The main benefit of dental implants is solid support for your new teeth . Because the titanium in the implant fuses with your jawbone, your dental implants won’t slip or cause bone damage . Plus, the materials in the implants can’t decay like your own teeth can. Implants can:

  • Improve your appearance
  • Improve your speech
  • Make you more comfortable
  • Enhance your self-esteem
  • Improve your oral health

Risks

  • Infection at the implant site
  • Injury to surrounding tissues
  • Nerve damage
  • Sinus problems if an implant protrudes into a sinus cavity

Candidates for Dental Implant Surgery

Generally, anyone healthy enough to undergo a routine dental extraction or oral surgery can be considered for a dental implant. Patients must have healthy gums and enough jaw bone structure to hold the implants . Heavy smokers and patients with diabetes or heart disease may be excluded depending on the severity of their health issues.

Preparing for dental implant surgery

You will need to undergo a comprehensive dental exam and a complete review of your medical history . Your dentist will design a comprehensive and customized treatment plan. Make sure you plan to have someone drive you home following your surgery, and expect to rest for the remainder of the day .

The Dental Implant Procedure

1. Removal of the damaged tooth or teeth 2. Jawbone preparation, or grafting if needed 3. Placement of the dental implant 4. Bone growth and healing 5. Artificial tooth placement

The Benefits of Dental Implants

With dental implants , you both feel and look younger. No longer will you have to live with missing teeth. Additionally, you don’t have to replace your dentures every time you want to eat or sleep.

Choosing Your New Artificial Teeth

You and your dental specialist can choose artificial teeth that are removable, fixed, or a combination of the two.

Results and Maintenance

Dental implants have been shown to have a success rate of up to 98 percent . With proper care, results can last a lifetime.

Costs

The cost of dental implants will vary according to the number and extent of the procedures required. Have a thorough conversation with your dental specialist prior to treatment.
