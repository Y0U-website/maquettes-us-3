0 Meet Our Dentist Dr. Jessica Kraich Proctor, DMD Jacksonville’s Trusted Family Dentist Welcome to Mandarin South Dentistry ! Where smiles have been our specialty for over three decades! That’s right, we’re not one of those big corporate dental chains we are a family owned and operated dental practice right here in Mandarin, Florida, and we’re proud of our roots.

Leading our team is the wonderful Dr. Jessica Proctor, DMD . Dr. Proctor’s Florida roots started way down south in sunny south Miami, where she navigated the hallowed halls of an all girls Catholic High School Lourdes Academy before becoming a proud graduate of the esteemed University of Florida College of Dentistry . Upon graduation Dr. Proctor brought her bright smile and passion for quality dental care to Jacksonville after earning her DMD in 2014.

At Mandarin South Dentistry, you’re not just a number; you’re part of our extended dental family. Come experience the difference that three decades of family dedication and a genuine passion for oral health can make. We can’t wait to welcome you and help you achieve your brightest smile!
