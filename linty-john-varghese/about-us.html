Amber Cole

Biological Certified Master Practice Administrator

Amber brings over 16 years of dental expertise to Ohio Holistic Dental Center, where she works alongside Dr. Linty and the team as Master Practice Administrator. From patient relations and insurance coordination to account management and team scheduling, Amber helps ensure the practice runs smoothly while creating an exceptional experience for every patient who walks through the door.

Before stepping into practice administration, Amber spent nine years as a dental assistant. She later attended Clark State University, where she studied Dental Radiology and completed the Master Practice Administrator program. Amber is passionate about helping patients navigate their care journey and finds great fulfillment working with Dr. Linty, whose leadership encourages personal and professional growth. She especially values the positive culture at OHDC, where the entire team is united by a shared commitment to patient-centered care.

Outside the office, Amber enjoys traveling, hiking, biking, picnics, and spending time in nature. She loves quiet moments with a good book and a cup of coffee on her porch. Most of all, she cherishes time with her two-year-old daughter, Goldie, whose endless energy, curiosity, and spunky personality keep life exciting and full of adventure. A natural organizer, Amber enjoys creating order and efficiency wherever she can. One thing many people may not know about her is that she proudly served in the military for six years in the reserve unit at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base.

Desirae Bromley

Biological Certified Expanded Function Dental Assistant

Desirae brings her thoughtful and attentive approach to her role as a Biological Certified Expanded Function Dental Assistant (EFDA) at Ohio Holistic Dental Center. Since starting her dental career with Dr. Linty’s team in 2017, she has been dedicated to ensuring patient comfort, preparing the work area for treatments and procedures, sterilizing instruments, and educating her patients on the benefits of good oral health.
