Braces

  • Correcting the bite helps distribute chewing forces more evenly and may reduce tooth wear.
  • Improved alignment can support clearer speech and more comfortable chewing.
  • Balanced jaw and tooth relationships can ease strain on the jaw joints and muscles.
  • Well aligned teeth can enhance smile symmetry and facial balance.

The Braces Process

1. Consultation and Records

A comprehensive exam includes photos, digital scans or impressions, and X-rays. These records define tooth positions, jaw relationships, and growth considerations.

2. Custom Treatment Plan

A plan outlines the type of braces, expected timeline, and whether rubber bands, springs, or other auxiliaries will be used. Clear aligners may be discussed as an alternative for appropriate cases.

3. Bracket Bonding

Teeth are cleaned, brackets are placed with dental adhesive, and a wire is inserted. Initial light forces begin movement. This visit usually takes a little longer than routine adjustments.

4. Active Adjustments

Regular appointments fine-tune the wire, add elastics, and guide the bite. Most visits are brief. Consistent elastic wear is essential for on-time results.

5. Retention

After active treatment, retainers are essential to hold the new positions while bone and gums stabilize. Wearing retainers as instructed helps protect your investment for years to come.

What To Expect With Braces

Most patients feel mild soreness for a few days after placement and adjustments. Over-the-counter pain relief and a soft diet help ease discomfort. A few foods should be avoided to protect brackets and wires, including hard candies, ice, very sticky caramels, and nuts. Cut crunchy foods like apples into small pieces.

Daily care includes thorough brushing with a soft brush, interdental brushes around brackets, and flossing with a threader or a water flosser. Regular professional cleanings remain important throughout treatment. If a bracket loosens or a wire pokes, contact the office for guidance; orthodontic wax can provide temporary comfort.

Most comprehensive cases take 12 to 24 months, though shorter or longer timelines are possible depending on crowding, bite complexity, and how closely instructions are followed. Wearing elastics as directed and keeping visits on schedule often shortens treatment time.

Understanding Your Braces Options

Metal braces are durable and effective for a wide range of concerns. Ceramic braces function similarly but use tooth-colored materials for a more subtle look. Lingual braces that attach behind the teeth and clear aligners can be options for select needs. An in-person evaluation determines which approach aligns best with your goals, oral health, and lifestyle.
