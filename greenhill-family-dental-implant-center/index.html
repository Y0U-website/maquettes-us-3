Les soins

Dental implants provide an excellent foundation for replacing missing teeth, but the implant itself is only one part of the finished smile. The visible portion—the crown, bridge, or other prosthetic restoration—is what restores your ability to chew…

Dental implants are a long-lasting option for replacing missing teeth. An implant acts like an artificial tooth root and supports a custom restoration, helping restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.
