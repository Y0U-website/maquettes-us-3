Owner and Denturist, Michael Holden was inspired by his father, Ken Holden, who was a denturist as well. Before opening LifeLike Dentures in 2012, Michael worked at Natural Dentures in Eugene, OR.

He earned his Bachelor’s Degree at the University of Oregon and received his denturist program certificate at George Brown Technical College Toronto, Canada. He loves empowering patients by educating them on their dental needs and providing personal care to create the best dentures to suit them.

The family practice has received the “Best of Kent” awards for 6 years running, and Michael has served as Vice President of the Washington Denturist Association. Outside of the office, he enjoys being with family and friends and mountain biking, snowboarding, hiking, camping, and paddle boarding.

Daley Williams works with her husband Josh, our denturist, to run LifeLike Dentures. She is our dental assistant and was also personally trained by Maegan Holden for office and billing procedures.

She was inspired to become a dental assistant after her positive experience while she had braces as a teen. Daley now strives to make patients feel just as great as her dental assistants made her feel. She loves helping patients change their lives for the better with dentures.

Outside of the office, Daley enjoys hiking, working out, and spending time with friends and family. Her favorite quote is, Joy is a decision, a really brave one, about how you’re going to respond to life.

Maegan Holden owns LifeLike Dentures with Michael and continues to help run the office remotely. She received her Bachelors in Journalism from the University of Oregon and helped build LifeLike Dentures to today’s successful practice.

While Maegan has always been interested in marketing and communications, she never knew she’d be using her skills at her own family business. She loves interacting with patients, solving problems, and being involved with every aspect of everyday operations.

In her free time, she enjoys being with family and friends, crafting, gardening, paddle boarding, biking, hiking, and going on evening walks. Her favorite quote is, Freedom is not the freedom to do what you want, but freedom to do what you ought.
