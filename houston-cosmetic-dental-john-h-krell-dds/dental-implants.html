Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

While dental implants are an excellent choice for replacing missing teeth, our Houston team evaluates several factors to determine your suitability. For implants to be successful, one of the main requirements is sufficient jawbone thickness. For patients with severe bone loss, additional treatments like bone grafting may be necessary.

Good oral hygiene is also a crucial factor, as patients with gum disease or other oral health issues may face complications during the procedure or recovery process. At the time of your consultation, Dr. Krell will review your dental and medical history to identify any factors that could affect the success of the treatment.

What to Expect From the Process of Dental Implants at Our Houston Clinic

At our practice, we combine advanced dental technology with expert care to replace missing or damaged teeth. On your first visit, we start by discussing your concerns and examining your overall oral health. Our dentist also takes digital scans of your teeth and jawbone to plan the precise placement of dental implants.

The main procedure (which is performed under local anesthesia) involves surgically placing one or more implant posts into your jawbone. This is followed by a healing period of 3 to 6 months, during which the implants integrate with your bone. Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch denture is placed to restore your smile and oral functions.

Do implants feel like natural teeth?

Yes, implants feel very similar to natural teeth, as they replace both the root part (implant post) and the crown part (prosthesis) of a tooth.

How long do implants last?

Dental implants are one of the most durable tooth replacement options and many last 20 years or more with proper care.
