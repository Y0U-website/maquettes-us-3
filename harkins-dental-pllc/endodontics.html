Maintaining Endodontic Health: A Simple Routine

Just like your natural teeth, root canals require proper care to ensure longevity. Here’s a simple routine:

  • Brushing and Flossing: Maintain a twice-daily brushing and flossing regimen to remove plaque and prevent future decay.
  • Regular Dental Checkups: Schedule regular dental checkups and cleanings for professional monitoring and early detection of any potential issues.
  • Balanced Diet: A healthy diet low in sugar promotes overall oral health, including root-canaled teeth.

Endodontic Treatment Options Tailored to Your Needs

Endodontics offers various treatment options depending on the specific issue:

  • Root Canal Therapy: This procedure removes infected or inflamed pulp, cleans the canals, and seals the tooth.
  • Endodontic Retreatment: In some cases, a root canal may need to be retreated due to new infection or complications.
  • Endodontic Surgery: For complex cases, surgical intervention may be required to access and treat the infected pulp.
