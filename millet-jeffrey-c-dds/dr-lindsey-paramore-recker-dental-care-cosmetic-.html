MEET Dr. Paramore Restorative Dentistry Dr. Lindsey Paramore grew up in Pella and is excited to be back in the community. She attended Iowa State University earning a B.S. degree in Biology and completed dental school at the University of Iowa.

Dr. Lindsey enjoys all areas of dentistry, but especially restorative dentistry. She looks forward to providing compassionate care and creating relationships with her patients.

Dr. Lindsey met her husband Russ while in dental school. Together they live in Pella with their miniature Bernedoodle, Bentley. In her free time, Dr. Lindsey enjoys spending time with family, baking, and going on walks.
