Services

Cleaning and Preventative

Regular dental visits will help ensure your teeth and gums remain in optimal health.

Diagnostic Imaging

Our dentists will utilize x-rays to ensure accurate diagnosis and effective treatment. Routine x-rays help to monitor the general health of your teeth.

Fillings

In order to preserve your teeth, our doctors may recommend fillings for decayed or damaged teeth.

Inlays and Onlays

If decay or damage is too extensive for a filling, our doctors may recommend an inlay or onlay to preserve the integrity of your tooth.

Crowns, Bridges, and Veneers

If you have a tooth requiring a major restoration, our dentists will help you understand your options and recommend the best course of treatment.

Dentures

Our dentists will to do everything they can to help you keep your natural teeth. However, when looking to replace several missing teeth, our doctors will recommend partial or full dentures.

Implant Restorations

Dental implants are the most secure option for replacing a missing tooth. Our doctors refer out for the implant placement, but can complete the final restoration in-office.

Night and Snore Guards

To help our patients with issues related to clenching, grinding, and snoring, we create custom night guards and snore guards.

Whitening

We offer two different options for whitening at our offices: Custom Trays and a Pre-filled Home Kit. Both methods are effective for patients looking for a brighter, whiter smile.
