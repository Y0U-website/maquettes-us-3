3D Precision Planning

Our office utilizes the latest technology to make sure your dental implant and implant crown is in the ideal position. We use a Cone Beam CT which creates a 3D image of your bone and teeth to plan exactly where the implant should be. No more guessing, we can now see what is possible and plan without you even being in the chair!

Then our 3D scanner enables us to create a guide to make sure the implant goes exactly where we planned it.

Finally when we make the crown, the 3D scanner eliminates the "goop" in getting your new dental implant crown created.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help me restore my smile?

If you have missing or broken teeth, deciding to restore your smile is crucial. It not only boosts your self-esteem, but a full smile also contributes to better oral health and promotes a more diverse diet.

Dr. John Unger relies on a variety of methods and treatments to restore smiles. And his approach is based on your unique needs.

If you have a broken or damaged tooth, your Cincinnati dentist may recommend treatment followed by a dental crown. A dental crown is a cap that goes over your tooth to restore function and aesthetics. It is designed to look like a natural tooth and always matches the shade of your neighboring teeth.

For patients with missing teeth, Dr. Unger assesses your oral health and makes recommendations for restoring your smile.

Deciding which option is best for you depends on several factors, including your jawbone health, budget, and smile goals.

You can use one or a variety of options to restore your smile, and Dr. Unger can help you reach your smile goals through innovative dentistry.

  • Dentures and partials
  • Dental implants
  • Implant-supported restorations
  • Dental bridges
