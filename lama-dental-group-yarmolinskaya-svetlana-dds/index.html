The practice

Meet Dr. Svetlana Yarmolinskaya, the general practitioner at Lama Dental Group. She has dedicated her career to improving the oral health, self-esteem, and overall happiness of her patients. She specializes in preventative and cosmetic dentistry, including whitening, implants, and veneers.

Located in Fair Lawn , NJ, Lama Dental Group has been serving and satisfying patients from all over the tri-state area since 2000. Our friendly staff and welcoming office accommodates patients of all ages , especially those with dentist-related anxiety and small children.

Lama Dental Group offers a wide variety of services to optimize the oral health and beauty of every one of our patients. The office is equipped with state-of-the art technology, to ease dental-related anxiety and always produce fabulous results.

Lama Dental Group satisfies families and individuals of all ages. Our friendly staff accommodates patients of all ages.
