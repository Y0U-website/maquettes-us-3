Partial Dentures Niagara Falls

A partial denture comes with a plate where the false teeth are attached, which can be made of all-plastic materials or some combination of metal and plastic. All-plastic dentures consist of false teeth attached to a pink acrylic base, whereas metal and plastic combination dentures come with artificial teeth attached to a metal framework. Both types use clips to secure the denture in your mouth, which may be visible depending on their location.

  • Preserves natural teeth
  • Distributes biting force
  • Restore speaking and chewing function
  • Serves as an alternative to dental bridges

How Much Do Partial Dentures Cost?

Partial dentures can vary in their cost, depending on a variety of factors. Your dentist will look at the following elements:

  • Number of missing teeth
  • Location of treatment area
  • Type of material being used
  • Any procedures that are necessary before getting the denture
  • Complexity of the procedure
