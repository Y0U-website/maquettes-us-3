What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of strong materials like titanium that are placed into the jawbone to hold replacement teeth. They work as a stable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures, making them look and function like natural teeth. Implants help restore chewing ability, speech, and overall appearance. They are a long-lasting solution for people with missing or damaged teeth.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a lasting way to replace missing teeth and bring back a natural smile. They keep your jawbone healthy, make eating easier, and stop nearby teeth from moving out of place. Unlike dentures, they stay fixed and feel comfortable. That’s why many people choose dental implants near The Villages nearby for a strong and dependable solution.
