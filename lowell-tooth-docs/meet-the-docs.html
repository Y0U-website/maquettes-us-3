DEBORAH RATT , D.M.D.

I am a native of Billerica, Massachusetts and attended Stonehill College in Easton MA, where I earned my Bachelor of Science degree in 1989. In 1993 I obtained my degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. I graduated with high honors and awards in prosthetics and endodontics.

Since 1993 I have practiced General and Cosmetic Dentistry in the Dracut and Chelmsford areas before settling down here at Lowell Tooth Docs in 2012. I especially enjoy working with children and families. I make every effort to help them achieve a positive dental experience.

I am dedicated to learning the latest techniques and procedures to ensure that my patients obtain the highest quality dentistry available. I pride myself on turning peoples past negative experiences in dentistry to positive attitudes where they actually enjoy coming to see me.

I am a resident of Chelmsford, MA since 1996 with my husband David & my two sons, David and Evan.
