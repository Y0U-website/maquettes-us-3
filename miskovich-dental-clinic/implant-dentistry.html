The Dental Implant Process

Treatment planning for oral health and overall wellbeing is the key to our practice. After a comprehensive evaluation, Dr.Miskovich will determine if you are a candidate for dental implants. Next, a detailed treatment plan will be designed in coordination with the surgeon who oversee the dental implant and the dental lab technicians in creating the dental crown or dental prosthesis (denture).

The implant is allowed to integrate, or fuse with the bone, for approximately three to four months. Dr. Miskovich will then create the final restoration, after which the dental prosthesis becomes an incredibly durable part of your smile. Regular check-ups are an important part of your ongoing care. With our experienced team we can provide consistent maintenance for existing implants, including situations where repairs are needed.
