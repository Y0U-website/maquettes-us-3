New Patients

Total first visit: typically 60-90 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes early if you have not completed paperwork in advance.

  • Comprehensive exam (20-30 min). Dr. Anand will review your medical and dental history, examine your teeth and gums, take digital X-rays if needed, and perform an oral cancer screening.
  • Professional cleaning (30-45 min). Your hygienist will clean, polish, and floss your teeth — and show you any techniques that could help your at-home routine.
  • Discussion & plan (10 min). We review findings together, answer your questions, and if any treatment is needed, lay out options with transparent pricing.

What to bring

  • Photo ID
  • Dental insurance card (if applicable)
  • List of current medications
  • Previous dental records or X-rays if you have them (we can also request them from your prior dentist)

Insurance & payment

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and will file your claim for you. If you do not have insurance or want to spread out costs, we offer 0 % interest financing through CareCredit. Cash, check, and all major credit cards accepted.

For anxious or special-needs patients

We are a sensory-friendly practice. If you or your child experience dental anxiety, autism-spectrum sensitivity, or need accommodations for any reason, please let us know when you book — we offer longer appointment windows, quiet-room options, noise-reducing headphones, and a step-by-step "tell, show, do" approach so there are no surprises. Dr. Anand has extensive training in caring for special-needs patients and veterans.

Need to cancel or reschedule?

Life happens — we just ask for 24 hours notice when possible so we can offer the time to another patient who needs it.
