0 Who we are We are privately owned dental practice that offers everything from preventative dental care to restorative dentistry.

Dr. Thomas Knape and his team are proud to serve the Wilson, NC community with high-quality, patient-centered dental care for all ages. Our practice is dedicated to creating comfortable, stress-free visits while delivering excellent results you can trust.

We offer comprehensive dental services with a strong focus on same-day dentistry so you can get the care you need, when you need it. Whether you’re visiting for routine care or urgent treatment, your smile and comfort are always our top priority.

Our office is honored to continue a long-standing tradition of dental care in Wilson, previously led by Dr. Isaacs and Dr. Mason. We are proud to build on the strong foundation they established while continuing to provide modern, compassionate dentistry to the community.
