Types of Teeth Cleaning

Our teeth whitening process is straightforward and designed to provide noticeable results. Here’s what you can expect during your visit:

Regular Teeth Cleaning (Prophylaxis)

This is the most common type of cleaning, typically done during your routine checkup. It involves removing plaque, tartar, and surface stains, leaving your teeth polished and fresh.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling and Root Planing)

For those with gum disease or significant plaque and tartar buildup, a deep cleaning is recommended. This procedure goes below the gum line to remove hardened tartar and smooth the roots of your teeth, helping gums heal and preventing further damage.

Gum Disease Treatment (Periodontal Cleaning)

If you’re experiencing gum disease, this cleaning method focuses on treating infection and inflammation in the gums. It involves scaling the teeth and roots to remove bacteria and toxins, promoting gum tissue reattachment.
