Meet Dr. Spina

In 2013, Dr. Spina completed his implant surgical and prosthetic dentistry training at Columbia University School of Dental Medicine . Dr. Spina is one of the few dentists who is trained in both the surgical and prosthetic phases of implant therapy. Because of the amount of education, research, and procedures Dr. Spina has completed, he was awarded a Fellowship in the ICOI (International Congress of Oral Implantologists) . Fellowships are awarded nationally to very few dentists and this distinction sets Dr. Spina apart as one of the countries premier implant dentists.

In addition to maintaining a full time practice in Wayne, Dr. Spina maintains a teaching position at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. In 2003, Dr. Spina was asked to join the faculty at Penn, where he shares his knowledge with dental students. Most recently, He received an appointment to the Department of Oral Medicine of the University of Pennsylvania where he treats patients with multiple complicating medical conditions. In 2007, Dr. Spina joined the staff of Abington Memorial Hospital where he is an attending dentist in Department of Dentistry.

Currently, Dr. Spina continues to study dentistry at the Spear Center for Dental Education in Scottsdale, Arizona, The Dawson Academy for Advanced Dentistry in St. Petersburg Fl., and at the Columbia University Implant Study Group at Columbia University in New York City.
