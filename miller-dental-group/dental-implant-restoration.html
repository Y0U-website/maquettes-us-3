What Is Dental Implant Restoration?

Dental implants are a durable, long-term solution for missing teeth, but like any dental prosthetic, they may eventually require maintenance or repair. Dental implant restoration focuses on replacing or repairing the components above the implant post (typically the abutment or crown) without disturbing the implant fixture embedded in the jawbone.

Common reasons for implant restoration include:

If you’re experiencing discomfort, notice a change in your bite, or feel movement in your implant crown, it may be time to schedule a restoration consultation.

  • Cracked, chipped, or broken crowns
  • Loosened or damaged abutments
  • Wear or discoloration of prosthetic teeth
  • Gum tissue changes affecting the implant’s appearance
  • Functional concerns, such as bite misalignment

Every restoration begins with a thorough evaluation using advanced imaging technology, such as 3D CT scans and digital x-rays. Our team assesses the integrity of the implant and surrounding bone structure to ensure that only the prosthetic components need attention.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restoration

How long does a dental implant restoration last?

While implant posts can last decades or even a lifetime, the crown or bridge restoration typically lasts 10 to 15 years, depending on wear and maintenance. Our use of high-quality materials helps maximize that lifespan.

What’s the difference between implant placement and restoration?

Implant placement involves surgically inserting the titanium post into the jawbone. Restoration refers to attaching or repairing the prosthetic component (like a crown or bridge) that sits above the gum line. We handle both procedures in-house.

Is the restoration process painful?

No. Dental implant restoration is typically a minimally invasive procedure. Most patients experience little to no discomfort, especially when only the crown or abutment is being addressed. Sedation options like IV sedation are available for anxious patients.

Can I restore an implant that was placed at a different dental office?

Yes. Whether your implant was placed months or years ago, our team can assess its condition and offer solutions. We specialize in restoring and repairing dental work completed elsewhere.

Will my restored crown look natural?

Absolutely. We custom-match every restoration to your existing teeth in both shape and shade. Our use of CEREC same-day technology ensures a precise and aesthetic fit.
