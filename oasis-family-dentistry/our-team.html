Our Team

CONTACT US Our Team Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are kind, friendly, and caring. Our staff is here to ensure a pleasant experience during your visit to our office.

Our team includes experienced and energetic people whose goal is to communicate well with our patients and provide the best care possible.

Liz, Hygienist

Liz joined the Oasis family in June, 2018. She is from Louisville, KY and has lived in Jeffersontown for most of her life. She graduated from the dental hygiene program at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2010. Liz’s interest in the dental field began as a young child, seeing her dad practice as a dentist in Louisville. She would often go to work with her parents and was intrigued by the hygienist’s role. She loves educating patients on the importance of their oral health and helping them have a healthy, beautiful smile.

Liz has been married to Roy since 2012 and they have two, precious little ones, Scarlett Rose and Cylas James. In her free-time, she enjoys spending time with her family and friends, going to church, eating out, shopping, and walking her beagle, Sophie.

Shari, Extended Duty Dental Assistant

Shari joined Oasis Family Dentistry in December 2016. She graduated from ATA College receiving her EDDA(expanded duties dental assistant), coronal polishing & radiation certifications from the University of Louisville and has been working closely with patients now for over 10 years.

"The best part about my job is witnessing the complete satisfaction in our patients on a day to day basis. I love seeing the amazing changes that we make happen from start to finish!"

In her free time, Shari enjoys cooking, playing volleyball, cheering on the Louisville Cardinals & spending time with her two daughters.

Olivia, Front Office Coordinator

Olivia is a graduate of Indiana University with a degree in Bachelor in Fine Arts with a concentration in painting. Olivia and her family have been a part of the Oasis Family for over a decade. She grew up coming to this practice and has made an incredible addition to our service team!

She is the lovely first face and voice to greet you here at Oasis Family Dentistry. When not at work, she loves to read, paint in the studio, and spend time with her newlywed husband.

Savannah, Extended Duty Dental Assistant

Savannah is a graduate from the Comprehensive Dental Assisting Program from MedQueest College and has been with us at Oasis since her graduation in 2023. She loves getting to know our patients and seeing the complete confidence boost they get when their dental treatment is completed! Being able to help her community by making a positive impact on people’s lives is the greatest blessing! In her spare time Savannah likes to visit Dale Hollow Lake, cheer on the Kentucky Wildcats, and spend quality time with her family and friends.

Jill, Dental Hygienist

Jill came to Oasis Family Dentistry with so much experience, as has been a dental hygienist since 2010. She is originally from Shelby County but moved to Louisville and attended Assumption High School. Jill graduated from the University of Louisville Dental Hygiene Program in 2010, where she enjoyed getting to know her patients and educating them on the benefits of a healthy smile!

She has been married to her husband, Justin, since 2011 and together share two sweet boys and a Boxer, Piper. In her free time, she loves spending time with family, going to a Broadway show, shopping, or hanging at the pool. We feel so blessed to have her as such a special part of our team!
