Metal Braces.

Metal braces are the most widely used type of braces. They are very strong and can withstand most types of treatment. Unlike the metal braces of the past, today’s metal braces are small, more comfortable and more attractive.

Clear Braces.

Ceramic braces or clear braces are made of composite material. They are more often chosen by adult patients because they “blend in” with your teeth. If you be a little more inconspicuous about your braces, Clear Braces are the best choice!.

Invisalign.

Invisalign are “braces” that consist of strong plastic trays that are fabricated especially for you. Invisalign is for adult and responsible teen patients that have moderate orthodontic problems. Invisalign is truly invisible, no one will know that you are wearing braces.

Laser Gum Removal

Simply put, laser gum reshaping is a cosmetic treatment that we perform to remove excess gum tissue and/or correct the gum line. It can also be used as a part of periodontal procedures to address gum disease.
