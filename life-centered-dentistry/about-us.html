MEET DR. CARLA LUDWIG

Dr. Carla Ludwig is a committed non-profit executive, author, and speaker with a heart for empowering single mothers and their families. As the founding director of Hope for Single Moms, a faith-based organization, she empowers single moms to achieve economic independence, helps their children reach their full potential, and builds stronger, more stable families.

Most recently, Dr. Ludwig was honored with one of the 2024 16 Over 60 Awards in recognition of her impactful work with Hope for Single Moms. With over three decades of experience in non-profit leadership, she has dedicated her career after dentistry to creating opportunities for others to thrive and make a positive difference.
