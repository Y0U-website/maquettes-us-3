Teen Orthodontics in Redding, CA

The teen years are filled with big changes—and a growing smile is one of the most important. At Shasta Orthodontics, we know how much a confident, healthy smile can shape the way a teen feels about themselves. That’s why we’re here to guide your family through every step of the orthodontic journey with compassion, expertise, and care that feels personal.

Whether your teen is dealing with crowding, gaps, or bite issues, we offer trusted solutions like traditional metal braces and low-profile ceramic braces designed to create lasting results and lifelong confidence. We don’t just straighten teeth we help your teen smile more freely, laugh more boldly, and step into the world with self-assurance.

Why Orthodontics Matters During the Teen Years

Adolescence is one of the best times to begin orthodontic treatment because the jaw and teeth are still developing, allowing for smoother, more effective results. But the benefits go far beyond the physical.

Here’s Why So Many Families Trust Shasta Orthodontics with Their Teen’s Smile:

  • Corrects a Wide Range of Concerns From crooked teeth and spacing to overbites and underbites, we create personalized treatment plans that tackle each unique concern with care.
  • Lays the Foundation for Lifelong Oral Health Straight teeth are easier to clean and care for reducing the risk of future dental problems and supporting better health for years to come.
  • Builds Confidence from the Inside Out A healthy, beautiful smile can help teens feel more comfortable in their own skin at school, in photos, with friends, and during those big milestone moments.
  • Braces That Fit Their Style We offer both traditional metal braces and ceramic braces , so your teen can choose what feels right for them whether they want to stand out or keep it subtle.

Your Questions, Answered

We know that getting braces is a big decision. Here are some of the most common questions we hear from parents and teens:

  • What Types of Braces Do You Offer for Teens? We offer traditional metal braces and more discreet ceramic braces . Both are effective, and we’ll help your teen choose the option that fits their goals and lifestyle.
  • When Should My Teen Start Treatment? Most teens begin orthodontic care between the ages of 11 and 15. If your child hasn’t had an evaluation yet, now is a great time to schedule their no-cost consultation.
  • Can My Teen Still Play Sports or Instruments? Absolutely! Braces don’t have to interfere with your teen’s passions. We recommend a custom mouthguard for sports to protect their smile.
  • Is Treatment Affordable? We offer flexible payment plans and no-interest financing to make care accessible for every family.
