Meet Michael Simon, DDS

“I believe everyone deserves high-quality dental care, healthy teeth and gums for life, and a beautiful smile. That is the commitment we make to all our patients: first-class care and high-quality, predictable treatment every time.”

​Extensive Experience. Professional Treatments.

Dr. Michael Simon has been practicing dentistry in Los Angeles for over 23 years. He began his practice in Palos Verdes Estates in May 2005, and is a proud resident of the South Bay LA.

A 1996 Dean’s List graduate of the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Simon quickly relocated to sunny Los Angeles where he undertook a General Practice Residency in 1996-97 at the renowned Cedars-Sinai Medical Center. Upon completing the residency and receiving his California license, he associated in private practice for 8 years prior to setting up his current practice.

Dr. Simon is an active member in the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Alpha Omega International Dental Fraternity, and the West Los Angeles Dental Society.

He has completed (and annually continues) many hours of continuing education in cosmetic dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery, CPR, OSHA regulations, laser dentistry and implant dentistry. Dr. Simon is certified to provide Invisalign® orthodontic treatment. He and his hygiene team are also certified to use Soft Tissue Diode Lasers for a number of clinical applications in hygiene and restorative procedures.

When not working hard to improve his patients’ oral health, Dr. Simon can be found on a mountain riding a snowboard or mountain bike (depending on the weather); or enjoying live music from his favorite bands with friends. He is also an avid traveler and sports fan. He lives right down the street in South Redondo Beach.

STATE OF THE ART TECHNOLOGY

DEXIS Digital X-rays

We are committed to bringing our patients the safest and highest quality of care — and one of the ways we do that is through our digital x-ray system. The DEXIS system provides greatly improved diagnostic capabilities for our patients. It also significantly lessens radiation exposure by up to 80-90 % compared to old film x-rays. It is more comfortable for patients, saves time, and allows patients to see their dental conditions right away — which is helpful in understanding treatment options.

DEXIS Intra-Oral Cameras

The images from our DEXIS Intra-Oral high-definition cameras are clear and highly detailed. They are about the size of your finger, and provide live video, enhanced imaging and detection. They have become an invaluable diagnostic tool and allow us to see in extreme detail everything that’s going on in your mouth.

Sirona Soft Tissue Lasers

A minimally invasive, precise, and comfortable alternative to traditional treatments, soft tissue lasers provide more patient comfort. They work without heat, drilling, or vibration to offer more efficient treatments in less time with less anesthesia required. Greatly reduced swelling, bleeding, post-operative discomfort, and patient anxiety also benefit our patients. Gum reshaping and recontouring, tongue tie procedures, and periodontal pocket reduction are some procedures that are more easily done with a laser — preventing the necessity for conventional scalpel and blade surgical procedures.

3M True Definition Intraoral Scanner

This digital impression system that makes the old physical impressions technique obsolete, improving the quality of many restorative, cosmetic, and preventive treatments. Crowns and Bridges, Veneers, and Clear Aligners will all fit better than ever before using this technology.

Dentrix Paperless Office

Our office is committed to being green. We are totally paperless, with all your charts, billing info, and x-rays kept organized digitally. This makes our office more efficient and responsive to your needs. Know that your digital information is kept secure and private at all times by our office. We are also actively working to lower our office carbon footprint.

Advanced Technology

Our office is outfitted with the latest technological advancements and dental equipment for our patients’ needs. Our patients’ dental health is our first priority. Not only are we committed to upgrading our dental services by using the best materials available, but we are also dedicated to advancing our dental knowledge by taking continuing education classes on a regular basis.
