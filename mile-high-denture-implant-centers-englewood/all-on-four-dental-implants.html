All-On-4 Dental Implants FAQs

While there may be an initial adjustment period, the good news is that with proper care and maintenance , you can savor a wide variety of culinary delights with your new implant-supported teeth.

Several factors, including overall health, bone density, gum health , and the extent of tooth loss, play a significant role in determining eligibility. Your dentist will conduct a comprehensive evaluation, including a detailed medical history review, radiographic imaging, and a physical examination of your oral cavity, to assess your suitability for the All-On-Four treatment.

The healing and integration process for All-On-4 implants typically takes several months. During this time, it is crucial to follow your dentist’s instructions carefully and attend all scheduled follow-up appointments. Your dentist will monitor the healing progress and make any necessary adjustments to ensure the successful integration of the implants.

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is essential for the long-term success of your All-On-Four implants. While implants are not susceptible to cavities like natural teeth, neglecting proper care can lead to other issues, such as peri-implantitis (inflammation around the implant) or even implant failure.
