At Katie Cunningham, DDS, we provide a full spectrum of dental services, including emergency dentistry for those unexpected situations. Our practice is dedicated to ensuring patients receive timely and high-quality treatment, keeping your comfort and oral health our top priority.

Dr. Katie Cunningham earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Ohio State University in 2013 after earning her bachelor’s degree in Chemistry from West Virginia University in 2009. She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and the Akron Dental Society.

Originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico, Dr. Cunningham grew up in an Air Force family and spent most of her childhood in Dayton, Ohio. Upon graduating from Ohio State, she joined her husband, Scott, in Maine, where they practiced dentistry for eight years.
