About Dr. Marianna Zadov

Warm, thoughtful dentistry in Fort Myers

Happy Smiles of Fort Myers exists to make dental care feel understandable, unhurried, and genuinely welcoming.

Meet Dr. Marianna Zadov, DDS

Dr. Zadov is the Clinical Director and lead dentist at Happy Smiles of Fort Myers, an experienced dentist whose work spans general, cosmetic, and restorative care.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Toronto, and her professional experience since then has covered everyday general dentistry, cosmetic treatment, and restorative work that rebuilds function and structure.

Her approach is built on clear communication. Patients see their own photos and scans, hear the options described without jargon, and are given time to decide. Treatment planning is personalized, with attention to how a smile will function and look over decades rather than only today.

At Happy Smiles of Fort Myers, that translates into unhurried appointments, honest explanations, and long-term care for families across Southwest Florida.
