Meet Dr. Savage

Dr. Matthew Savage is a board-certified pediatric dentist dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for children throughout Belmont and the surrounding communities. With extensive training in pediatric dentistry and a genuine passion for working with young patients, Dr. Savage creates positive dental experiences that help children develop healthy relationships with oral care. Originally from Graham, NC, Dr. Savage met his wife, Grace, in dental school. Together, they have three children: Price, Riley, and Ada Bett.

Education and Professional Background

Dr. Savage completed his dental education and pursued specialized training in pediatric dentistry to focus exclusively on children’s unique dental needs. His comprehensive education included advanced coursework in child development, behavior management, and specialized techniques for treating infants, children, and adolescents with various medical and developmental conditions.

As a board-certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Savage maintains the highest standards of professional competence through continuing education and active participation in professional organizations. He stays current with the latest advances in pediatric dental care to ensure his young patients receive the most effective, comfortable treatment available.

Philosophy of Care and Patient Approach

Dr. Savage believes that every child deserves gentle, compassionate dental care that builds confidence and establishes positive associations with oral health. He takes time to explain procedures in age-appropriate terms, listens carefully to both children and parents, and adapts his approach to meet each child’s individual needs and comfort level.

Prevention and education are central to Dr. Savage’s practice philosophy. He works closely with families to establish effective home care routines, discusses the importance of healthy dietary choices, and provides guidance on developmental milestones that affect oral health. His goal is to empower families with the knowledge and tools needed to maintain excellent oral health throughout their child’s growing years.
