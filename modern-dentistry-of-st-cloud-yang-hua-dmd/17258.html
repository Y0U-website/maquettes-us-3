What is the dental implant process?

Dental implants start with an evaluation at our dentist office, which includes a consultation with Dr. Hua and x-rays to determine whether dental implants are right for you.

If our dentist in St Cloud decides you are a good candidate, the next step is the dental implant placement.

A dental implant is a strong post that replaces your lost tooth root. Your dentist surgically implants this replacement root, and it integrates with your jawbone over a period of a few months. Once the integration is complete, your dentist tops your implant with a dental crown, which is your replacement tooth.

What if I have multiple missing teeth or full tooth loss?

If you have multiple missing teeth or full tooth loss, we have options for you everything from multiple single implants to hybrid and overdentures.

An overdenture goes over a supporting structure, typically dental implants. If you’re looking for an upper overdenture, it typically includes a metal frame affixed with three or more dental implants. For the lower jaw, it usually takes only two implants and no frame.

The overdenture holds quality replacement teeth and prosthetic gum tissue. An overdenture is typically removable for cleaning.

This alternative to single implants requires your dentist in St. Cloud, FL to strategically place dental implants in your jaw usually four to six. These replacement roots are sturdy enough to support an arch of new teeth a denture or a bridge.

What are the health benefits of dental implants?

When teeth are missing, your existing teeth tend to shift toward these smile gaps. When this happens, your bite alignment is thrown off. If your bite is not aligned, you may notice wear on your teeth and an increase in cavities.

Missing teeth also cause jawbone deterioration, which can lead to tooth sensitivity, gum health issues, and an aged or sunken appearance to your lower face.

How do I take care of my dental implants?

Dental implants are the gold standard in tooth replacement for many reasons, including ease of care. Dental implants require brushing, flossing, regular dental exams, and professional teeth cleanings just like natural teeth!

Also, just like with natural teeth, you’ll want to make sure you talk to your dentist if you grind or clench your teeth. This can lead to dental crown or tooth wear and breakage. And always protect your smile natural teeth and implants with a mouthguard if you compete in contact sports.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the differences between dental implants and dental bridges?

Dental bridges and dental implants are both great ways at fixing your missing teeth and restoring your beautiful smile. However, there are some differences between them that you may have to consider.

First of all, both implants and bridges are designed to fix missing teeth in our mouth. Secondly, implants are surgically placed to replace missing teeth while bridges are not. Next, when placing an implant, we do not have to remove any adjacent teeth structure while a dental bridge requires that. Lastly, dental implants are hygienic and easy to floss and clean. Dental bridges require a special floss to clean underneath the bridge.

Can dental implants get cavities?

The answer is no, you will never get cavities with any dental implants. However, just like your natural teeth, dental implants are prone to periodontitis which is gum infections. It is still vital to get check-ups and dental cleanings every 6 months when you have dental implants.
