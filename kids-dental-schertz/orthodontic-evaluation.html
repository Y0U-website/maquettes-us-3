When Should Children Have Their Bite and Dental Growth Evaluated?

Children’s dental development should be monitored throughout childhood. At Kids Dental, Dr. Ding evaluates your child’s growth and oral health during preventive visits and may recommend a more focused orthodontic evaluation if she sees signs that need closer attention.

Some children may need an evaluation when permanent teeth begin coming in. Others may be evaluated earlier if there are concerns about tooth eruption, spacing, oral habits, or jaw development.

Because every child develops at a different pace, the goal is not to rush into treatment. The goal is to give parents clear information at the right time. If a specialist’s opinion is needed, Kids Dental can help your family understand why a referral may be recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an orthodontic evaluation for children?

An orthodontic evaluation is a dental development screening that looks at your child’s teeth, bite, jaw growth, spacing, and tooth eruption. At Kids Dental, this evaluation helps Dr. Lisa Ding determine whether your child’s development should be monitored or whether a referral to an orthodontic specialist may be appropriate.

Does Kids Dental provide braces or aligners?

No. Kids Dental does not provide braces, aligners, or ongoing orthodontic treatment. The practice provides orthodontic evaluations only. If Dr. Ding sees signs that your child may need specialized care, she may refer your family to an orthodontic specialist.

Why would my child need an orthodontic evaluation?

Your child may need an evaluation if you notice crowding, spacing, early or late tooth loss, bite concerns, difficulty chewing, thumb-sucking effects, or permanent teeth coming in at unusual angles. An evaluation helps determine whether the concern should be monitored or referred to a specialist.

Can an orthodontic evaluation happen during a regular dental visit?

Yes. In many cases, Dr. Ding can discuss your child’s bite and dental development during a routine preventive appointment. If more focused attention is needed, she may recommend an orthodontic evaluation. Regular preventative dentistry visits help our team monitor your child’s oral growth over time.

What happens if my child needs orthodontic care?

If Dr. Ding believes your child may benefit from specialized care, she may recommend a referral to an orthodontic specialist. That specialist can provide further evaluation and discuss possible treatment options. Kids Dental does not provide orthodontic treatment in-office.
