FAQs About Clear Aligners

  • How long does treatment with clear aligners take?: Treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of your alignment issues.
  • Are clear aligners painful?: Some patients feel mild pressure or discomfort when switching to a new set, but it&#x27;s significantly less than what’s often experienced with braces.
  • Can I eat while wearing clear aligners?: No, aligners should be removed when eating or drinking anything other than water to avoid staining or damaging the trays.
  • How do I clean my aligners?: Rinse them with cool water and brush them gently with a toothbrush. Avoid using hot water, which can warp the plastic.
  • What happens if I lose an aligner?: Contact our office immediately. We’ll guide you on whether to wear your previous tray, move to the next one, or order a replacement.
