Am I Too Young For Braces?

Early orthodontic treatment, also known as interceptive or Phase I orthodontics, is a crucial step in ensuring proper dental health for children. Orthodontic treatment can be started as early as age 7, when most of the adult teeth have emerged. While many parents may assume that orthodontic treatment is only necessary for cosmetic reasons, there are many important reasons why early treatment is important such as tooth eruption and jaw growth.

Make your braces pop!

Braces don’t have to be boring! Instead of the traditional metallic silver or gray, colorful braces come in a variety of hues, from bright pink to electric blue. These options let patients express their personalities and add a pop of color to their smile. Colorful braces can be a great conversation starter and can help patients feel more confident and less self-conscious about wearing braces. Colorful braces can turn a potentially negative experience into a fun and colorful journey towards a healthy and beautiful smile!
