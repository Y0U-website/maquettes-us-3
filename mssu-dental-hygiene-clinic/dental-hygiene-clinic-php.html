Clinic Appointments:

The Missouri Southern State University Dental Hygiene Clinic welcomes patients on Tuesdays and Thursdays during the Spring, Summer and Fall semesters. The patients receive treatment by our student dental hygienists in order for them to learn proper technique. They are supervised by dental hygiene faculty and a supervising dentist at all times.

If you would like to schedule an appointment, please call or click on the contact information above.

Please provide the following information when requesting an appointment:

Dental cleanings, x-rays and other preventive services are provided in our clinics.

NOT offered are services such as extractions, dentures, fillings, crowns, etc.

  • Phone: Clinic office 417-625-9711 or Academic Office 417-625-9709
  • dentalhygiene@mssu.edu
  • Name- clearly stated and spell out
  • Date of birth
  • Phone number
  • Last time you had a dental appointment, teeth cleaned and any dental x-rays

Length of appointments

Since treatment is provided by students in the program, the treatment procedures are completed at a slower pace than in a dental office. Patients should expect to spend approximately 3 hours in the chair per appointment period. It may take multiple appointments to complete treatment. Please do not request an appointment if you are not willing to commit to the amount of time needed.
