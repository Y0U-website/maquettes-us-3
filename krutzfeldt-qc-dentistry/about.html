About Our Doctor

0 About Our Doctors DaNae Krutzfeldt D.D.S. Dr. Krutzfeldt grew up in Waterloo, Iowa. She attended Iowa State University in Ames, Iowa and received her Bachelor of Science degree. Her passion for dentistry began when she worked as a dental assistant for her father. She returned to school and graduated from the University of Iowa with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. In 2009 Dr. Krutzfeldt joined Dr. D’Souza in private practice.

Dr. Krutzfeldt is a member of the American Dental Association, the Iowa Dental Association, the American Association of Women Dentists, the Academy of General Dentists and the International College of Dentists. She volunteers with the I-Smile and Silver Smile programs and the Give Kids a Smile Bethany Bus program. She is a YMCA board member at the Davenport YMCA.

Dr. Krutzfeldt is one of those crazy people that love running. She is a member of the Cornbelt running club and has run several half marathons. A marathon is on her bucket list.

Alaina Pribyl D.D.S.

Dr. Pribyl is a proud Bettendorf, Iowa native. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Iowa, where she discovered her love for oral health while working in research at the College of Dentistry. That experience led her to pursue and complete her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Iowa.

She is an active member of both the American Dental Association and Iowa Dental Association. Dr. Pribyl is also passionate about community outreach and has volunteered with programs such as the UIHC Dental Mobile Clinic, Iowa Mission of Mercy, and Give Kids A Smile.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Pribyl enjoys traveling to new places and spending quality time with her German Shepherd, Scout!
