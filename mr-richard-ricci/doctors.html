Meet Our Doctor

Richard Ricci, DDS

Dr Richard Ricci, born and raised on Singer Island, took over the practice following Dr McIlvaine’s passing. His interest in dentistry began at the age of 14 when he received his first job here helping Dr McIlvaine. He went on to graduate from Ohio State Dental school in 1987 and acquired the practice in 2004. Many of our staff members have been together here for over 25 years.

Richard Ricci, DDS

Dr. Richard Ricci, a proud native of Singer Island, has been dedicated to serving the dental needs of his community for decades. His journey into dentistry began at just 14 years old, when he took his first job doing odd jobs in the very same office he now owns. That early exposure sparked a lifelong passion for dentistry.

Dr. Ricci earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. He acquired the office in 2004, continuing the legacy of compassionate, high-quality care following the passing of his mentor.
