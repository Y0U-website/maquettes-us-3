dr. pete ( dok-ter peet)

Dr. Pete Harbert is committed to providing high-quality dental care and creating enjoyable dental experiences for kids (and their grown-ups) in the greater Omaha community.

Dr. Pete is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He is Omaha’s only pediatric dentist who is also a Breathe Institute Ambassador.

Dr. Pete has been married to his lovely wife Sarah since 2016. They have two very active boys, Ben and Graham, and a sweet little girl named Libby. Free time is family time, and when Dr. Pete is not at work, you can find him coaching or watching his kids’ sports teams, cheering on the Huskers, or seeking out Omaha’s best donut or newest playground.
