Fun Facts about Dr. Tyler

Get to know your Mitchellville dentist.

  • Who is your hero? My hero(es) are my parents. I admire how both of my parents have lived their lives. Neither is perfect no man or woman is. However, I appreciate the foundation and the values they held, which I know came from their own parents and grandparents. They’re not famous celebrities; just everyday people who make better things happen for other people.
