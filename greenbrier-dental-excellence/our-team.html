Chad Ellis, DDS, ® Doctor .

FAMILY AND RESTORATIVE DENTIST

Dr. Chad Ellis is a unique small-town dentist. He is a part of a thirty-five year Greenbrier Dental tradition, and attended the Louisiana State University School of Dentistry, where he learned to use state-of-the-art procedures and techniques to provide exceptional care and quality dentistry.

Each year, Dr. Ellis attends extensive postdoctoral studies in clinical dentistry, enabling him to offer your family some of the latest advances and comforts of modern dental technology.

Dr. Ellis is married to the love of his life, Teresa, and they have two sons, Jackson and Duke, and one daughter, Blaise.

Ashley .

Dental Hygienist

Ashley is a native to the Middle Tennessee area. She lives in White House with her husband and kids. She graduated from TSU in 2000 and has been a dental hygienist at Greenbrier Dental Excellence since 2005. She loves being a dental hygienist and loves helping her patients achieve total oral health.

When she is not at work, she enjoys spending time with her family and watching her kids play sports. She also volunteers her time as her daughter’s Girl Scout troop leader and helps with her son’s Boy Scout troop.

Chris .

Office Administrator

Chris is a native of New York, but moved to the Nashville area in 1984. She’s married with two grown children and blessed with three awesome grandchildren. Passionate about assisting patients overcome their dental fears and financially achieve their dental goals, Chris was a clinical assistant for several decades and now utilizes her dental knowledge in the front office.

When not at work she enjoys having great times and making great memories with her family, and repurposing or renewing old furniture.

Teresa .

Office Manager

Teresa Ellis and Dr. Ellis, both originally from Shreveport, LA, moved to Nashville from New Orleans in 1999. Teresa taught elementary school for seven years before starting their family. They now have three kids, Jackson, Duke and Blaise — two boys and a girl.

Teresa became Greenbrier Dental’s Office Manager once the kids started school. She loves getting to know all the patients and helping them obtain a beautiful smile. “I still feel like I am teaching, just to a different classroom!”

Between Greenbrier Dental and raising three active children, Teresa stays busy but in her “free time”, she enjoys hanging out with her family, yoga, reading and trying out new recipes.

Macy .

Dental Hygienist

Macy, a proud resident of Sumner County, brings a wealth of experience and passion to Greenbrier Dental Excellence. Originally from Shreveport, LA, Macy grew up in Murfreesboro, TN, before making her way to our community.

Macy graduated from Western Kentucky University with a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene, where she honed her skills and deepened her commitment to patient care. With a background in cosmetology since 2008, Macy returned to school to follow her true calling in dental hygiene. Her dedication to oral health and patient education shines through in her work, as she loves helping patients achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Outside the office, Macy enjoys traveling and attending concerts, always seeking out new adventures and experiences. Her vibrant personality and commitment to patient care make her an integral part of our team.

Cherie .

Dental Assistant

Cherie was born in New Jersey and lived in Central Florida for 12 years, where her dental career began. She has been a Registered Dental Assistant (RDA) for over 30 years and takes great pride in helping patients achieve their dental goals and transform their smiles.

Cherie has three grown children—two daughters and a son—and is a proud grandmother to four grandchildren, including two girls and two boys. In her free time, she enjoys traveling and cherishing moments with her friends and family.

LaJohnna.

Dental Hygienist

As a young girl hailing from Jacksonville, Florida, LaJohnna Mitchell, always knew she was going to be a dental hygienist. After graduating from Florida College with a degree in Dental Hygiene, LaJohnna has enjoyed an impressive 46+ year career.

Not only does she have extensive post-graduate education in all phases of dentistry, LaJohnna is also an instructor at First Class Dental Assisting School of Nashville, has experience working with Duval county’s Dental Forensic Team, and has provided hygiene care to those less fortunate in Guatemala, while on medical mission trips.

LaJohnna believes that a comfortable environment and a good relationship is what opens the door to achieving optimal dental health, and she is excited to help her patients reach such a goal.

When not being our superb dental hygienist, you can find LaJohnna hunting for deals in her favorite antique stores, tending to her garden, crafting some D.I.Y. project for her home in Springfield, Tennessee, or spending time with her family. After all, she is quite proud to be the “Jam” to her grandson.
