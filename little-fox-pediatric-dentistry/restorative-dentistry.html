Pediatric Pulpotomies

When your child has a deep cavity or tooth pain, a pediatric pulpotomy can be a gentle, effective solution. Often called a “baby root canal,” this procedure helps save primary teeth that are badly decayed but still essential for eating, speaking, and guiding permanent teeth into place.

Pediatric Extractions

Whether your child needs a baby tooth removed due to decay, trauma, or to make space for adult teeth, our calm and child-friendly approach helps reduce fear and ease the process. With cozy treatment rooms, comforting distractions like Disney+, and parents allowed to stay by their child’s side, we create a safe, soothing space for families facing extractions.

Lip and Tongue Tie Treatment

At Little Fox Pediatric Dentistry in Lyndhurst, lip and tongue tie treatment is a gentle, evidence-based approach to helping infants and young children feed, speak, and grow more comfortably. These oral ties, known medically as “tethered oral tissues (TOTs),” occur when the tissue connecting the tongue to the floor of the mouth or the lip to the gums is too short or thick.

Pediatric Esthetic Fillings

At Little Fox Pediatric Dentistry in Lyndhurst, we understand how important it is for your child to feel confident about their smile. Pediatric esthetic fillings are tooth-colored dental restorations designed to repair cavities while blending seamlessly with your child’s natural teeth.

Pediatric Dental Crowns

When it comes to protecting your child’s smile, pediatric dental crowns can be a game-changer. These custom-made crowns are used to restore and shield a child’s damaged or decayed tooth, helping to preserve its function and appearance.

Space Maintenance

As your child grows, their baby teeth are destined to fall out to make room for their adult counterparts. But when they fall out too early, it can heighten the risk for orthodontic complications later on. We want your child to thrive, which is why we offer space maintenance, a solution that keeps your child&#x27;s teeth from shifting and having misalignment issues as they erupt. If you are concerned about your child&#x27;s baby teeth falling out, we can help them get a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Pediatric Airway Dentistry

Did you know that an airway issue can impact your child&#x27;s overall health, including their quality of sleep? Untreated airway problems can have far-reaching effects. That&#x27;s why our expert treatments are tailored to open up your child&#x27;s airway, allowing them to breathe with ease. Say goodbye to sleep troubles and hello to better overall well-being for your little one.
