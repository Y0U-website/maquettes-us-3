Complex Procedures Made Simple For longer or more involved treatments, sedation allows us to complete necessary work efficiently while your child rests comfortably, reducing the need for multiple appointments.

What we offer

Our Sedation Options

Nitrous Oxide Often called "laughing gas," this mild sedative helps children feel relaxed while remaining fully awake. It&#x27;s our gentlest form of sedation, wearing off quickly after treatment with no lingering effects.

In-Office General Anesthesia

For children who need extensive dental work or have difficulty sitting through treatment, we offer in-office general anesthesia with our trusted anesthesiologist partners in the comfort of your child&#x27;s dental home.

Hospital Dentistry

When deep sedation is needed, we provide hospital-grade anesthesia at Well Span Good Samaritan hospital, ideal for young children, those with special needs, or extensive treatment plans.

Step 5 FAQs Here are some common questions about sedation. Is sedation dentistry safe for children? Yes! We follow strict safety protocols and continuously monitor your child&#x27;s vital signs throughout the procedure. Our team has extensive training in pediatric sedation and emergency response.

How will my child feel during sedation? Depending on the type of sedation, your child may feel relaxed and drowsy or may be completely asleep during their treatment. They won&#x27;t experience pain or anxiety during the procedure.

How long does recovery take? Recovery time varies by sedation type - nitrous oxide wears off in minutes, while IV sedation and general anesthesia may require a few hours of rest at home.
