Are Dental Implants Painful?

It’s natural to feel a bit jittery about the idea of dental implant surgery. But fear not! At Main Street Dental and Implants, we’re committed to making your experience as comfortable as possible. Most patients find the procedure surprisingly tolerable, often comparing it to a simple tooth extraction. Plus, we’ll use local anesthesia to numb the area, and you can discuss additional sedation options with Dr. Beus if you’re feeling especially anxious.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Ready for some fantastic news? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime! Yep, you read that right. While the crown attached to the implant may need replacement after 10-15 years due to wear and tear, the titanium implant post has the potential to stick with you for the long haul. With regular brushing and flossing (you’re already doing that right?) and regular dental checks, your new implants can stay solid for decades.

Can dental implants be done in one day?

Even though you’ll see same-day implants advertised, it’s important to know this isn’t a possibility for everyone. At Main Street Dental and Implants, we take a personalized approach to your dental care and work very hard to come up with the best solution for your needs. For some patients, a traditional dental implant process – involving a healing period for the implant to fuse with the jawbone – may be a better option. Dr. Beus will discuss the best treatment plan for your unique needs and circumstances during your dental implant consultation.

Can everyone get dental implants?

As great as dental implants are, not everyone is a good candidate for the procedure. Factors like your overall health, jawbone density, and commitment to maintaining good oral hygiene can impact your eligibility for dental implants. Don’t worry, though – Dr. Beus and the team at Main Street Dental and Implants will thoroughly evaluate your situation and help you determine if dental implants are the best option for you. If not, we’ll work with you to explore alternative tooth replacement solutions.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

We think you’ll be happy to hear that dental implants boast an impressive success rate! In general, dental implants have a success rate of around 95-98 %. Things like your overall health, which teeth are getting implant replacement, and your dentist’s skill in performing the implants can all factor into the end result. The great news is with Dr. Beus and the experienced team at Main Street Dental and Implants, you can feel confident knowing that you’re in capable hands. We’ll make sure every checkbox is ticked before we go ahead with your investment in new implants.

Will dental implants look natural?

Absolutely! One of the most significant benefits of dental implants is their natural appearance. Dr. Beus will meticulously design a custom-made crown that matches the size, shape, and color of your surrounding teeth. The result? A seamless, stunning smile that looks and feels just like the real deal. Say goodbye to self-conscious grins and hello to a dazzling, confident you!

How do I take care of my dental implants?

Great news – caring for your dental implants is a breeze! In fact, you can treat them just like your natural teeth. Be sure to brush twice a day, floss daily, and use an antimicrobial mouthwash to keep your mouth clean and fresh. And, of course, don’t forget to visit Main Street Dental and Implants for regular check-ups and cleanings. Making the commitment to good dental hygiene will guarantee your dental implants will remain strong and healthy for years to come.

Are dental implants safe?

While dental implants don’t require any special maintenance beyond regular oral hygiene practices, keeping a watchful eye on their condition is essential. Just like natural teeth, dental implants can be affected by gum disease and other oral health issues. Stay proactive about your oral health by brushing, flossing, and attending regular dental check-ups with Dr. Beus at Main Street Dental and Implants. Taking good care of your dental implants sets the stage for a lifetime of a happy, healthy smile.
