Meet Dr. Bradley O Reilly DMD

Dr. Bradley O’Reilly began his education at Benedictine University where he graduated with Magna Cum Laude honors in 2011. He received a bachelor’s degree in Health Sciences. He then attended Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in Alton, IL. Throughout his dental school career he held board positions in the Xsi Psi Phi fraternity and worked as a tutor. He graduated in June, 2015.

Dr. Brad brings experience in current dental technology and implant dentistry to the practice. He received the award for excellence in implant dentistry from the American Academy of Implant Dentistry in June, 2015. He is dedicated to helping patients maintain excellent oral health. He enjoys treating patients of all ages and various dental needs.

Dr. Bradley and his wife, Dr. Ashley met in dental school and were married in 2016. They both enjoy the outdoors and spending time with their rescued black Labrador Retriever, Gunner, and German Shepard, Maggie. They welcomed their first baby, Tucker Brian, in 2021, and most recently welcomed their second boy, Griffin Joseph on May 2, 2023.

Meet Dr. Bernard O Reilly DDS

Dr. Bernard O’Reilly has been in practice 30 years. He received his Bachelors of Science Degree in Biology from Illinois Benedictine College in 1982. He is a 1987 graduate of the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry.

He continues to advance his dental knowledge and skills with continued education courses. He has completed courses with the Misch Implant Institute Prosthetic Program and The Dawson Academy. He is a member of the Seattle Study Club, the American Dental Association, the Illinois State Dental Society, and the Chicago Dental Society.

Dr. O’Reilly and his wife, Kathy, of 30 years met at Illinois Benedictine College. Kathy is an active real estate agent and attorney. They have four children: Bradley (who you know as Dr. Brad), Andrew, Kaitlyn and Allison. In his spare time, Dr. O’Reilly enjoys golf and attending his daughters’ volleyball matches. He is an avid White Sox and Blackhawks fan.
