The practice

Q: Do I need an appointment to receive care? A: No, all patients will be seen on a first come, first serve basis. There are no tickets, vouchers, or appointment spots to be purchased or reserved.

Q: Do I need a driver’s license to receive care? A: No, patients are not asked for any type of identification or paperwork to receive treatment. There are no eligibility requirements.

Q: Can I receive care if I don’t live in Tennessee? A: Yes, there aren’t requirements or restrictions regarding who is eligible to receive treatment. We see patients on a first come, first serve basis until we reach capacity for the day.

Q: What treatment does MidMOM provide? A: We offer fillings, cleanings, and extractions based on the most immediate need. We are able to do a limited number of other procedures each year (excluding dentures, braces, crowns/capping teeth, extractions/procedures that require sedation). You will receive an exam from one of our volunteer dentists to determine what can be done at MidMOM in the time…

Q: Can I get a partial at MidMOM? A: Possibly, if the doctor determines you are a candidate.

Q: What services are NOT provided at MidMOM? A : Unfortunately, we are unable to provide dentures, braces, crowns/capping teeth, extractions/procedures that require sedation.

Q: Is there anything that would prevent me from receiving treatment? A: Yes. You’ll receive a medical exam to ensure you’re able to receive treatment. Some things may prevent you from receiving treatment include, but are not limited to: fever; uncontrollable, high blood pressure; history of bleeding; certain medications (immunosuppressants, blood thinners, etc.).

Q: Will food and water be provided? A : We will provide water during the clinic day.
