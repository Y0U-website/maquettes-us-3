Meet The Doctor Growing Smiles Pediatric Dentist

0 Meet The Doctor! Dr. Denise Vedrenne DDS, MS, MPH Dr. Denise Vedrenne has been a pediatric dentist for over 40 years, with a philosophy rooted in her deep love for working with children. She believes in providing the best care using non-pharmacological approaches, combining her wealth of knowledge and expertise to make each visit a positive experience for young patients. Her focus is always on creating a comfortable, respectful environment where children feel at ease while receiving top-quality care.

A mother of two, Dr. Vedrenne has been part of the Lehigh Acres community for over 10 years and is committed to serving families in the area. She is proud to build long-lasting relationships with both her patients and their families. In her free time, she enjoys traveling, attending dental conferences, and working on her stained glass projects.

Fluent in English, Spanish, and French, Dr. Vedrenne ensures all families feel comfortable and understood in her care. She is dedicated to improving the oral health of children and providing them with a foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.
