About Us

Our experienced technicians work directly with doctors and dental offices locally and all across America, so you can get professional opinions right from the source and be sure every case is given the personalized attention it deserves. We work hard to make and maintain personal working relationships with our clients. You can be sure that once you choose us, you’ll stay with us. Get in contact with us today and find out what we can do for you.

Marko Dental Arts LLC is a high-quality, full-service dental lab based in sunny Rockledge, FL. We have been in operation since 2018 providing dentists all across Central Florida and nationwide with our quality products and services. We understand patient care is your utmost priority, so we take care to make it ours as well. We take pride in our quality craftsmanship because it means your patients can take pride in their smiles.

We focus on forging personal working relationships with clients. Familial relationships between our clients and ceramists is what helps even the most complex cases go smoothly, with individualised attention and experienced solutions, saving you time and fuss.
