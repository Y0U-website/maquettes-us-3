Convenient Care When You Need It

Our office opens at 9 a.m. Monday through Thursday to cater to the busy schedules of our patients who would like to schedule early morning appointments before heading to work or school. We also try to offer same-day treatment post-diagnosis whenever possible, so you can receive the care you need right away instead of making a future appointment. Our team offers emergency dental care for situations that cannot wait until your next scheduled visit. If you need to make an emergency dental appointment for a broken tooth or an unbearable toothache, simply give our office a call so we can advise.
