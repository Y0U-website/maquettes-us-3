  • What is the aftercare process for dental implants? After your dental implant procedure, proper aftercare helps ensure lasting success. We guide you through each step, from managing mild discomfort to maintaining excellent oral hygiene. Most patients return to normal routines quickly, with simple instructions for diet and care. Follow-up visits allow us to monitor healing and address any questions, so you can enjoy a strong, natural-looking smile with confidence. Our team is always available if you need additional support.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to last for decades, a lifetime, with proper care. Made from durable, biocompatible materials, they fuse with the jawbone to create a strong, stable foundation. With routine brushing, flossing and regular dental visits, patients can enjoy long-lasting results. Many implants remain successful for 20 years or more, making them a reliable, long-term solution for restoring confident, healthy smiles.
