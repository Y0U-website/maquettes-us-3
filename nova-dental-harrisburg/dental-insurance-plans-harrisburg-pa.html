No Dental Insurance? No Problem at Nova Dental

CareCredit® with 0 % Interest Plans Available

Finance your dental treatments over time with CareCredit. Many of our patients qualify for interest-free options with easy monthly payments.

Instant Approvals Without a Credit Check

Even if you don’t have a credit history, our financing partners can offer you plans with high approval rates and no credit check required.

Exclusive In-House Membership Plan

For uninsured patients, our in-house savings plan provides routine dental care at a reduced cost. Membership includes discounts on exams, x-rays, cleanings, and more-all for a low annual fee.

We’re Just a Call or Click Away

If you have questions about your dental benefits, want to confirm your coverage, or are exploring affordable financing, our friendly team is here to help. Book online anytime or give us a call during business hours for personalized assistance.
