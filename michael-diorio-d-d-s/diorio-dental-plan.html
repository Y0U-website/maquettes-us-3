Additional Information

The Diorio Dental Plan is an in-house benefit plan and not a dental insurance. It can’t be used with any other dental plan or insurance, or while using care credit or third-party financing. It also can’t be used for services covered under workman’s compensation or automobile medical. This is a nonrefundable plan and can only be used at Diorio DDS.

  • The Diorio Dental Plan expires exactly one year from your enrollment date
  • Your services must be received within that period
  • Payment is due the day the plan is implemented
  • The patient’s portion of the bill is due on the day of service
