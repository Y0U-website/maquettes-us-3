What is Traditional Orthodontics?

Traditional Orthodontics uses wires and brackets to slowly position teeth into a natural position where they are not crowded, spaced or have some other positioning problems (malocclusion). These wires and brackets can be the standard silver-colored or a more discreet clear. Either way, you get a beautiful, straight smile that will serve to prevent or address problems associated with bruxism, cavities, gum disease or diminished self-confidence.

Benefits of Traditional Orthodontics

The great thing about traditional orthodontics is that you ensure that you are continually “activated”. There is no forgetting to put in your trays, or losing trays to deal with. Another benefit is that traditional orthodontics is compatible with Accelerated Orthodontics, a technique of improving the process by which orthodontics works and thus speeding up treatment time by significant margins.
