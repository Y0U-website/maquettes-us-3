Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are mini implants as durable as traditional implants?

Yes, mini implants are made from the same biocompatible titanium as traditional implants and are designed to last many years with proper care.

Is the procedure painful?

Mini implant placement is minimally invasive, and most patients report little to no discomfort during and after the procedure, especially when compared to traditional implant surgery.

How do I care for my mini implants?

Care for mini implants is similar to natural teeth, involving regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups to ensure their longevity.
