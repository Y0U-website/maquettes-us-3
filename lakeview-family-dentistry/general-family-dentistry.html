Gum Treatment

Our periodontal treatments are designed to restore and maintain healthy gums. We provide thorough deep cleanings, scaling, and root planing to stop gum disease and protect your teeth from further damage.

  • Diagnosis and management of gum disease
  • Deep cleaning (scaling & root planing)
  • Periodontal maintenance care

Dental Fillings

We use advanced, tooth-colored materials to repair cavities and restore natural beauty. Our fillings blend seamlessly and provide long-lasting protection with a focus on minimally invasive techniques.

  • Composite, tooth-colored restorations
  • Replacement of old or worn fillings
  • Repair of small chips or fractures

Root Canals

Root canal therapy can save a severely damaged or infected tooth while relieving pain. Using modern tools and techniques, we ensure your treatment is efficient, comfortable, and preserves your natural tooth.

  • Precise diagnosis of infection
  • Pain-managed root canal treatment
  • Post-treatment restoration planning

Extractions

When a tooth cannot be restored, our team performs extractions safely and comfortably. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and discuss replacement options such as bridges, implants, or dentures.

  • Simple and surgical extractions
  • Wisdom tooth removal
  • Comprehensive follow-up care
