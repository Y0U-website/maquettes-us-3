Get To Know

Dr. Ansley Allen

A Jasper native with a lifelong passion for dentistry, Dr. Ansley&#x27;s journey began at age five when she was inspired by her own children&#x27;s dentist, Dr. Bishop. After graduating from Walker High School in 2008, she pursued her Bachelor&#x27;s degree in Biology at the University of Alabama, completing it in 2012. She then earned her dental degree from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Lakewood Ranch, FL in 2018.

While initially practicing general dentistry, Dr. Ansley&#x27;s heart always pulled her toward working with children. A collaboration with Dr. Jill helped confirm her true calling in children&#x27;s dentistry, leading her to join Once Upon a Smile Children&#x27;s Dentistry in 2023! Her approach combines gentle care with a natural ability to connect with young patients, making each dental visit an opportunity for positive experiences and healthy smiles.

When she&#x27;s not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Ansley enjoys quality time with her husband Hunter (also a Jasper native) and their daughter Lelia Mae! Dr. Ansley is an avid tennis player and Disney enthusiast, and she loves planning tropical adventures with her family! Her local roots and genuine passion for children&#x27;s dental care make her a perfect fit for serving the families of her hometown community.

Get To Know

Dr. Haley Burgess

Dr. Haley grew up in Dawsonville, Georgia, where she lived for 27 years before moving to Jasper. Her love for dentistry began in college when she started shadowing her older sister, who was a practicing dentist. Dr. Haley attended the University of Georgia, where she earned a bachelor’s degree in Biological Science. She then went on to attend the Dental College of Georgia, graduating in May 2025. Since July, she has been working as a dentist in Jasper at Once Upon A Smile and Bivona Family Dental.

Dr. Haley’s favorite part about working at Once Upon A Smile is the opportunity to work with amazing hygienists and assistants who encourage her every day to be a great dentist. She especially enjoys spending her Thursdays at Once Upon A Smile caring for her pediatric patients. Dr. Haley says, “It’s such a breath of fresh air when Thursdays come around. The kids always keep you on your toes with their off-the-wall comments and questions.”

In her free time, Dr. Haley enjoys spending time with her husband Brody.Her favorite hobbies include attending Georgia football games and watching college basketball.

Your Child&#x27;s Dental Journey

A Magical First Visit Your child&#x27;s journey begins with a warm welcome and a tour of our whimsical office. During their exam, we&#x27;ll take time to help them feel comfortable, explain everything in kid-friendly terms, and celebrate their bravery. Our team will perform a gentle cleaning, take any necessary X-rays, and provide a comprehensive exam, all while keeping your little one engaged and at ease.

What to Bring Please bring your child&#x27;s insurance information, medical history, and any questions you have. Feel free to pack their favorite comfort item or stuffed animal to keep them company during their visit!
