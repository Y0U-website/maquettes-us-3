Services

We are a Lancaster, PA, dental office that provides family dental services to children, adults, and seniors.

If you have any questions about our services, please contact us .

Dental Exams

We perform dental exams of teeth, soft tissues, gums, jaws and screen for oral cancer.

Teeth Cleaning

Teeth cleaning removes unsightly stains, plaque, and tartar. Our office is unique in that this service is done by the dentist, not a hygienist.

Gum Treatment

Gum treatment, otherwise know as periodontal care, promotes the healing of unhealthy and diseased gums.

Root Canals

A root canal is done to remove the nerve from a tooth, to relieve pain, and/or infection.

Tooth Extraction

Tooth extraction involves removing badly decayed, broken, crowded, or painful teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a form of cosmetic dentistry which significantly brightens your smile.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry can fill gaps/spaces, reshape, resurface (veneer), and whiten teeth, creating a beautiful new smile.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored material can be used to replace old, broken silver metal (dark) fillings, or fill teeth that have cavities/decay.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns (caps) and bridges are used to cover weak or unattractive teeth, and replace missing teeth.

Dentures

A complete denture replaces an entire arch of teeth. A partial denture replaces one or more missing teeth.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are artificial teeth roots, placed in the jaw to substitute for missing teeth. After healing, implants are restored to appear and function like real teeth.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies involve pain, swelling/infection, trauma, or broken teeth.
