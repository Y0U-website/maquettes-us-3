Fillings & Crowns

Even though primary (baby) teeth will eventually fall out, if they&#x27;re damaged or decayed, in many cases they still need to be restored. This is because the primary teeth reserve space for permanent teeth, help with chewing, and play a role in speech and facial development. While a tooth colored filling can often do the trick, when a lot of the tooth&#x27;s structure is missing a pediatric dental crown is the ideal restoration.
