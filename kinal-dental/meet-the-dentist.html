Meet the Dentist

Dr. Liudmyla Kinal

Dentist/Owner

Dr. Liudmyla Kinal is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist based in Bonsall, California, where she leads a dedicated dental practice with her exceptional team. With a strong commitment to patient-centered care, Dr. Kinal focuses on helping individuals achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized treatment and modern dental solutions.

She takes pride in creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, heard, and supported throughout their dental journey. By combining advanced dental techniques with a gentle and attentive approach, Dr. Kinal works to ensure every patient receives high-quality care tailored to their unique needs and long-term oral health.

A Dental Team Dedicated to Your Health, Comfort, and Confidence

At Kinal Dental, Dr. Liudmyla Kinal and her team are dedicated to providing patients with a positive dental experience and results they can feel confident sharing. Our practice is built on personalized care, where every patient is treated with attention, respect, and a genuine commitment to their oral health. Through preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, we help patients maintain healthy, beautiful smiles that support both function and appearance.

Our team stays current with modern dental techniques and technology to provide effective, comfortable care tailored to each patient. From appointment scheduling to insurance and financial questions, our administrative staff works to make every visit smooth and straightforward while offering clear and transparent pricing.

Patient comfort is always a priority. We understand that some patients feel anxious about dental visits, and our team focuses on gentle care, clear communication, and comfort options such as music during treatment or sedation when appropriate. Most importantly, we value the relationships we build with our patients and strive to create a welcoming environment where you feel supported and confident in your care.

At Kinal Dental, you are not just a patient you are part of a community dedicated to helping you maintain a healthy, confident smile for years to come.
