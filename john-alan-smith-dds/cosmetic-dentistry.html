Minor Orthodontics

We perform minor orthodontics (also known as minor tooth movement therapy) for patients who only need modest corrections to their smile. Minor orthodontic therapy is ideal for rectifying: small and uneven spacing between teeth, mild overcrowding or overlapping front teeth, moderately malpositioned teeth, and a slightly improper bite. This treatment offers many benefits, which include significantly reduced pain and discomfort, shorter treatment time that concludes after several months, and lower costs compared to traditional orthodontic treatment.

Veneers

Veneers are thin yet strong pieces of porcelain that are custom-molded to fit over your teeth. They can be a good alternative to dental crowns , and can be used for restorative as well as for cosmetic purposes. They are durable enough to last for many years, though they may need replacement after years of regular use and wear. Veneers are an excellent choice for:

  • Correcting crooked, deformed, or discolored teeth
  • Resizing the teeth to more ideal proportions
  • Eliminating uneven spacing and gaps in your smile
  • Restoring damaged and chipped teeth to their original appearance

Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is an effective non-invasive treatment that dramatically enhances the color of your tooth enamel and brightens your smile by several shades. While teeth whitening therapy produces reliable results for the vast majority of patients, not everyone may benefit from treatment. Some types of stains will be more resistant to bleaching, and teeth whitening cannot brighten discolored dental restorations such as dentures , crowns , veneers, or fillings. To determine if teeth whitening is right for you, you can call our office at (423) 247-7821 to schedule a consultation.

Composite Fillings

Though silver amalgam fillings are known for their safety and reliability in the treatment of cavities , they can have a disfiguring effect on your smile if they are too visible. Fortunately, you can opt for composite fillings instead to preserve the beauty and natural look of your teeth. Like traditional amalgam fillings, composite resin fillings are strong and durable and can last for 5 to 10 years. Composite fillings also offer other advantages, which include color customization to match your tooth enamel and versatility in correcting minor chips, cracks, and gaps in your teeth.

Clear Aligner Teeth Straightening

We include clear aligner teeth straightening as a treatment option for patients who wish to improve their smile discreetly. Clear aligners are removable plastic devices that are custom made to reposition your teeth gradually in a span of several months. Because they are clear, they appear invisible as you wear them, and are generally more comfortable than traditional braces. They are ideal for correcting minor to moderate degrees of teeth crowding, overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites, gap teeth, and typical crookedness.
