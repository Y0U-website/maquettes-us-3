At Happy Smiles Dental, Dr. Alireza Hamidzadeh DDS, MS, a rigorously trained and recognized implant expert, believes that everyone deserves a confident smile. Dental implants, if placed with a level of expertise and planning, can offer a revolutionary solution for replacing missing teeth, providing a natural look and feel while restoring your oral health and functionality.

What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. Once integrated with the bone, these implants provide a stable foundation for custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures, effectively replacing missing teeth.

Why Choose Dental Implants? Natural Appearance: Designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, dental implants look and feel just like natural teeth.

Durability: With proper care, expertly placed dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective long-term solution for tooth replacement.

Improved Functionality: Enjoy the ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence, knowing your implants will provide the stability and strength you need.

Bone Health Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone, significantly reducing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth and helping maintain facial structure.

Our Implant Process Comprehensive Consultation: We start with a thorough evaluation, including digital imaging, to determine your eligibility for implants and develop a personalized treatment plan. Dr. Hamidzadeh will evaluate deficiencies that can reduce your result and develop a plan to give you the best possible outcome.

Implant Placement: Dr. Hamidzadeh DDS, MS, A United States Army trained Comprehensive Dentist and dental surgeon and an Adjunct Professor at the Walter Reed National Medical Center, the flagship of medical facilities in the nation where the highest-ranking officials are treated, will place the titanium implant screw into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation, ensuring your maximum comfort throughout the procedure.

Healing Period: Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a solid foundation for your new teeth.

Abutment and Restoration: Once healed, we will attach an abutment to the implant, followed by a custom crown that matches your natural teeth.
