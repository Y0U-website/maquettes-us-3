Invisalign In Saratoga Springs, UT Looking for a discreet, comfortable way to achieve a straighter smile? Invisalign , a popular treatment offered at Harris Orthodontics, could be the perfect solution for you. This revolutionary teeth-straightening method provides patients of all ages with a modern alternative to traditional braces.

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners that gently and gradually move your teeth into their desired positions. Each set of aligners is worn for a few weeks before moving on to the next set, bringing you closer to your ideal smile with each step. The clear aligners offer improved aesthetics by being virtually invisible, and increased comfort without metal brackets or wires. Plus, they’re removable, making eating and oral hygiene a breeze.

Braces Specialist in Saratoga Springs, UT At Harris Orthodontics, we are proud to be a trusted provider of braces in Saratoga Springs, UT. Our team specializes in the use of self-ligating Damon braces, an innovative orthodontic solution that offers a more efficient and comfortable treatment experience.

Self-ligating braces work by using a unique bracket system that eliminates the need for traditional elastic or metal ties. These brackets come with a built-in mechanism that securely holds the archwire in place, allowing teeth to move more freely and comfortably. This advanced technology reduces friction, potentially shortens treatment time, and requires fewer office visits.
