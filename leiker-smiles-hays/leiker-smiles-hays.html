Our Services

We provide a full range of dental services, combining advanced technology with a conservative, patient-centered philosophy.

Our approach is guided by precision, prevention, and thoughtful treatment planning, ensuring it is both effective and conservative.

  • Preventive Dentistry We believe that prevention is the foundation of oral health. Services Include: Comprehensive exams
  • Professional cleanings
  • Digital x-rays
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Periodontal therapy
  • Restorative Dentistry Whether repairing damage or replacing missing teeth, restorative treatment focuses on rebuilding both health and confidence. Our restorations are designed for strength, function, and longevity. Services include: Fillings
  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Root canals
  • Implant restorations
  • Full-mouth rehabilitation
  • Cosmetic Dentistry Leiker Smiles prioritizes subtlety, balance, and results that complement your natural features. Services include: Professional whitening (at home or in-office)
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Smile design
  • Emergency Dentistry Dental emergencies are unpredictable prompt, compassionate care should not be. We prioritize urgent cases whenever possible to relieve discomfort and stabilize your oral health.

Meet Dr. Alexyss Leiker

Dr. Alexyss Leiker is proud to return home to Hays, Kansas, where her passion for dentistry first began.

She earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology, with a minor in Chemistry, from Fort Hays State University before pursuing her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry.

Dr. Leiker is known for her calm, thoughtful approach to patient care. She believes exceptional dentistry begins with trust, clear communication, and a true understanding of each patient’s goals and concerns.

Her treatment philosophy centers on precision, long-term oral health, and creating results that feel both functional and naturally beautiful.

She maintains active memberships in the American Dental Association, the Kansas Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

For Dr. Leiker, dentistry is not simply about treating teeth it is about building lasting relationships and delivering care that feels comfortable, transparent, and personalized.
