Global Recognition and Educational Leadership

Dr. Ortiz is widely recognized within the dental profession as a leader in restorative innovation and minimally invasive treatment planning. He lectures internationally and has become a respected opinion leader among dentists seeking advanced training.

Clinicians from around the world attend his lectures and hands-on courses to study precision driven workflows, evidence based protocols, and modern restorative methodology.

He is the founder of The DentLit Academy , an organization dedicated to advancing evidence based dentistry and highlighting the intersection of art and science in clinical care. His philosophy, “Follow the DentLit, not the dentist,” reflects his commitment to structured thinking and research driven practice.

Publications, Photography, and Thought Leadership

Dr. Ortiz has authored multiple clinical publications and research papers focused on restorative and implant dentistry. His work emphasizes systems thinking, predictability, and minimally invasive principles.

Beyond clinical dentistry, Dr. Ortiz has also written on artificial intelligence and philosophy, reflecting his broader interest in structured systems, disciplined thinking, and innovation.
