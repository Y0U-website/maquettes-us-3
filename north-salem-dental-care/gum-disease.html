When to Seek Professional Gum Disease Treatment

Recognizing the signs of gum disease early can make a significant difference in your oral health. Here are some indications that it’s time to consult a professional:

  • Persistent bad breath
  • Swollen or bleeding gums
  • Receding gum line
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Pain when chewing
  • Sensitive teeth
