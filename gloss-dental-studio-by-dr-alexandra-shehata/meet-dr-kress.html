Meet the Doctor

Dr. Alexandra Shehata Kress, DDS

Dr. Alexandra Shehata Kress is a Cincinnati native who earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Dayton, where she served as President of the Pre-Dental Society. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University.

Dr. Shehata Kress has a professional focus in cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, Botox and aesthetic injectables, and All-on-4 implant restorations. For implant procedures, she works closely with a trusted oral surgeon who performs the surgical phase while she provides the restorations, follow-up care, and long-term maintenance.

She is an active member of the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics and Spear Education and regularly participates in local dental study clubs to continue expanding her knowledge and clinical skills.

Patients often describe Dr. Shehata Kress as approachable, calming, and attentive. She believes patient education is an essential part of quality dental care and takes time to help patients understand their treatment options and oral health needs.

Personalized and Modern Dental Care

Dr. Shehata Kress provides a wide range of services designed to support long-term oral health and confident smiles. Patients visit Gloss Dental Studio for preventive care such as Cleanings & Exams , cosmetic treatments including Invisalign and Teeth Whitening , as well as restorative solutions like Porcelain Crowns .

Her goal is to create a welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel comfortable receiving care tailored to their individual needs.

Outside the Office

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ali enjoys spending time with her husband and their mini bernedoodle, Louie. Her interests include cooking, boating on Lake Cumberland, supporting local businesses, and cheering on the Cincinnati Bengals.
