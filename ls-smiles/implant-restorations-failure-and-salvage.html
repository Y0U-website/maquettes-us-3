(972) 771-2213 Understanding Dental Implant Restoration Failure and Salvage What are Dental Implant Restorations? Dental Implant restoration failure and salvage refers to the evaluation and treatment of dental implants that are not functioning properly or have begun to fail. While implants are designed to be long lasting, problems such as infection, bone loss, poor integration, or mechanical complications can cause discomfort, looseness, or changes in the surrounding gums. When these issues arise, early diagnosis is essential to prevent further damage and protect your oral health.

Implant salvage focuses on saving the existing implant whenever possible through targeted procedures like infection treatment, deep cleaning, bone grafting, or corrective surgery. In cases where the implant cannot be preserved, removal and replacement may be the most predictable solution. With advanced imaging and specialized surgical care, Dental Implant Restoration failure does not have to mean losing your smile, and there is often a clear path toward recovery and long term success.

Who Needs Dental Implant Restoration Treatment?

Dental Implant Restoration treatment is designed for patients experiencing complications with a previously placed dental implant, whether it was completed at our office or another practice. You may need evaluation if your implant feels loose, the surrounding gums are persistently sore or swollen, or you have been told there is bone loss around the implant. Ongoing discomfort or instability should never be ignored.

We also help patients facing late stage implant failure years after placement or those whose concerns were not fully resolved elsewhere. No matter when or where your implant was placed, our team will perform a thorough assessment and recommend the most effective solution to protect your oral health and restore long term stability.
