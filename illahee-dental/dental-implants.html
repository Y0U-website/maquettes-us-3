Why Choose Dental Implants?

Discover the benefits of this tooth replacement solution.

Get the top tooth replacement in Bremerton with dental implants.

  • Step 1 During the consultation, we’ll review your teeth, gums, x-ray, and medical history to ensure you’re a candidate for dental implants. Patients must have enough jawbone and must also have healthy gums to proceed with the dental implant procedure.
  • Step 2 The procedure includes placement of the implant, a small titanium post, into the missing tooth bone socket. The jawbone fuses with the post so that it’s fixed. Next, an abutment, which is a small connector, is attached to the post where the new tooth (AKA the crown) goes.
  • Step 3 Impressions are taken of your teeth so that a tooth can be created to match your existing smile. The new tooth is custom-made to look and feel like your own natural tooth and is known as the crown. The crown is attached to the abutment.

An All-on-X procedure allows dentists to replace multiple teeth or an entire arch using just 4-8 implants.

Who is a candidate for All-on-X procedures?

Anyone who is missing multiple teeth or who already wears dentures can benefit from All-on-X implants. If you don’t have the budget, time, or bone density to support a full set of individual implants, All-on-X can be a more affordable and faster solution.

FAQ About Dental Implants

  • What is the average cost for a dental implant? The average cost of a dental implant varies from patient to patient. Each dental implant is unique, just like the patient, and prices vary. Please contact us for an estimate.
  • What is the downside of dental implants? While there are slight risks involved with all surgeries like dental implants, our experienced team will ensure a pain-free and stress-free procedure. Dental implants have a very high success rate. Risks involved with dental implants include infection, nerve damage, damage to surrounding teeth, and sinus problems. This is why it’s important to visit an experienced and professional dentist like the team at Illahee Dental.
  • How painful is getting a dental implant? The dental implant procedure is not painful, and you’ll feel little discomfort during healing with medication. While you may feel slight pressure during the procedure, you’ll be pain-free because we use numbing. Local anesthesia numbs the nerves surrounding the site of the implant.
  • Can dental implants be done in one day? Same-day dental implants can be done in one day. The number of visits needed for your dental implant procedure depends on the severity and number of implants required. Each treatment is unique, so please schedule a consultation with our dentists at Illahee Dental.
