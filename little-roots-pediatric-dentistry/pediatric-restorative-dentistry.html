Pediatric Pulpotomies

Is your child experiencing a toothache that just won’t go away? It might be a sign of infection, but fear not! Our pulpotomy procedure can alleviate their discomfort in a single visit. Think of a pulpotomy as a nerve treatment that stops the infection in its tracks. By choosing this treatment, we can restore your little one’s oral health and bring back their bright smile, allowing them to continue enjoying all the laughter and carefree moments of childhood.

Pediatric Sedation

Our dental practice offers pediatric sedation to help children feel calm, comfortable, and at ease during their dental visits. Sedation can be especially helpful for kids who experience anxiety, have trouble sitting still, or need more involved treatment. By tailoring sedation options to each child’s age, comfort level, and medical history, we’re able to provide gentle, stress-free care while ensuring your child’s safety every step of the way.

Pediatric Dental Crowns

We know that your child being happy and healthy is what matters most, and when little bumps in the road happen, it can feel scary. If your little one needs treatment for a damaged or decayed tooth, pediatric dental crowns can help protect their smile and restore healthy function.

At Little Roots, we offer white zirconia crowns and silver stainless steel crowns, depending on your child’s needs, the location of the tooth, and the option that will provide the best long-term fit. Our team will walk you through each recommendation so you feel confident choosing the right solution for your child’s health, comfort, and smile.

Pediatric Airway Dentistry

Did you know that airway concerns can impact your child’s overall health, including their quality of sleep? At Little Roots, we can assess your child’s airway, oral development, and related symptoms to better understand what may be affecting their breathing, rest, and overall comfort.

If we notice signs of a possible airway issue, we’ll walk you through our findings and recommend the next best step for your child’s needs.
