What to Expect During the All-on-X Procedure at Our Clinic in Duncanville At the start of the procedure, we will administer local anesthesia to make sure you remain comfortable. Dr. Solanki will then make small incisions in the gum tissue and prepare the jawbone. We will carefully place each dental implant in the desired position.

In most cases, the implants are inserted at a certain angle, which helps engage maximum thickness of the jawbone. This unique approach makes it a suitable option for patients with bone loss and helps avoid the need for bone grafts. Once the implants are secure, we will close the gum tissues with sutures.

After the surgery, there is a healing period of about 4 to 6 months during which the implants merge with your jawbone. In the final stage, we will attach your custom full-arch restoration, allowing you to speak, smile, and chew with total confidence.

All-on-X implants are fixed in place and anchored to your jawbone, offering better stability and chewing function compared to removable dentures. Can I eat normally with All-on-X implants? Yes, you can enjoy a wide range of foods, including some hard foods that cannot be chewed with dentures.

Restorative space is the amount of vertical room between the top of your bone ridge and the opposing teeth. This space determines whether an implant and its crown will fit comfortably and function properly. If there isn’t enough room, the implant or restoration may fail, crack, or look unnatural.

The only way to know for sure is through diagnostic imaging. At your consultation, our periodontist will take a 3D CBCT scan and digital impressions to measure your bone, ridge height, and available space. CBCT helps assess your bite and anatomy, while clinical exams evaluate bite forces.

These measurements allow the crown to be thick enough to be strong, look natural, and stay secure.

Key factors include ridge shape, tooth position, bite forces, smile line, lip mobility, framework thickness, and your esthetic goals.
