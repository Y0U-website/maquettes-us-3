our approach to orthodontic treatment

it always has been, and always will be, about exceptional patient experience. we’re passionate about delivering quality treatment, embracing effective technology, and utilizing efficient appliances. through our journey, we hope to improve the lives of each of our patients.

focusing on you

one-of-a-kind

You’re different than any other person who’s walked through our doors. Why shouldn’t your treatment plan be?

We know that each patient brings their own uniqueness - sometimes that means there’s not enough space in the mouth, teeth developing off-course, maybe even a missing or extra tooth! Our focus on individualized treatment plans ensures that we’re thinking through every aspect of treatment before we even place appliances.

exceptional diagnosis

Diagnosis revolves around identifying issues. 3D imaging allows us to see more. Simply put, the more we can see, the more we understand. We employ one of the best 3D imaging machines. The iCAT FLX produces clear imaging without any of the magnification and distortion issues inherent in 2D imaging.

virtual treatment planning

Planning is priceless. We are the only orthodontic office in Marin to utilize virtual treatment planning on every single patient case.

What is virtual treatment planning? Once the 3D image is taken, all the structures are surface mapped. Using special software, Dr. Parikh creates virtual setups of the course of treatment. This helps her better understand the issues at hand and obstacles that we may face during treatment and plan for the most effective appliances and efficient systems.

dedicated learning

As with so many aspects of our lives, technology is changing everything. This includes how we treat patients. Dr. Parikh is dedicated to understanding new techniques with careful research and evidence.
