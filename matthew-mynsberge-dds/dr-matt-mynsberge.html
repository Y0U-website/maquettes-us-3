Dr. Matt Mynsberge DDS

While attending Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, Dr. Mynsberge spent time shadowing his own family dentist and oral surgeon and became interested in pursuing a career in dentistry. With this goal in mind, he took additional coursework to work toward applying to dental school while continuing his architectural engineering studies.

At the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry, Dr. Mynsberge excelled and graduated with honors . He then continued his educational journey with an intensive Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Residency at Indiana University’s Medical Center, where he mastered his surgical skills and received several honors for his clinical and academic achievements.
