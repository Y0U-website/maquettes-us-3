The practice

Thank you for your interest in Northern Endodontics, a dental practice dedicated exclusively to Endodontic care. Our mission is to provide you with the highest level of nonsurgical and surgical Endodontic care utilizing advanced clinical techniques to save your tooth. We are committed to the highest standards of personal service in a relaxed, comfortable atmosphere.

Endodontics (root canal treatment) is a dental specialty recognized by the American Dental Association and is the only type of treatment that we perform. Our goal is to provide a pleasant experience with a predictable result. We have the most sophisticated equipment available to deliver state of the art care.

Dr. Gutt and Dr. MacDonald are specialist members of the American Association of Endodontists. Dr. Gutt is Board Certified by the American Board of Endodontics. Our expert team has been providing Endodontic care in northern Michigan for many years. We hope that the information provided here answers many of your questions about Endodontic treatment.

Learn more about the procedures that we perform in our office and how you can prepare before your procedure and after.

A root canal is one of the most common dental procedures performed, well over 14 million every year. This simple treatment can save your natural teeth and prevent the need for dental implants or bridges.

With the appropriate care, your teeth that have had endodontic treatment can last as long as other natural teeth. Yet, a tooth that has received root canal treatment may fail to heal or discomfort may continue to exist.
