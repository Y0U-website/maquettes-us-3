The Benefits of Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are appliances used to replace lost or damaged teeth, either one or multiple. They differ from full dentures that replace all missing teeth because they can be taken out without a dental professional. They are a much less invasive option that also is generally more affordable.

Today’s dentures not only look authentic to real teeth; they are incredibly durable and can last for years with proper care. Other benefits of dentures include restoring proper chewing function, improving speech and confidence, and causing less irritation with gum tissue than some dental implants.
