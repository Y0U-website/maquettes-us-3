FAQs

Is bone grafting painful?

Bone grafting is performed with comfort in mind. Most patients experience mild discomfort that resolves during healing.

How long does bone grafting take to heal?

Healing time varies based on the size of the graft and individual health factors. Your dentist will guide you through expectations.

Is bone grafting always required before implants?

Not always. Grafting is recommended only when additional support is needed for long-term implant success.

Will bone grafting delay my treatment?

Bone grafting is part of thoughtful planning and may improve long-term outcomes rather than delay care.
