Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy can be used in dental procedures to reduce pain and inflammation, accelerating the healing process. By concentrating growth factors from the patient’s own blood, PRP helps to minimize pain and discomfort post-procedure. This can be particularly beneficial for procedures like extractions, implants, and bone grafting.
