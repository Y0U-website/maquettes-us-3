  • 0 If you have periodontal (gum) disease, our dentists may recommend scaling and root planning to restore your oral health. This gentle, non-invasive deep cleaning treatment is designed to remove harmful bacteria and stop the progression of gum disease. Scaling and root planning is one of the most effective early treatments for gum disease. It involves two steps: Scaling: Removing plaque and tartar buildup from the tooth surfaces.
  • Root Planning: Cleaning and smoothing the root surfaces to eliminate bacteria and promote healthy reattachment of the gums.
