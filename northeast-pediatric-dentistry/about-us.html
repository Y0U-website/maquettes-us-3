About Dr. Jennifer Kuger

Dr. Jennifer Kugar has a profound passion for working with children. Originally from Cincinnati, she graduated from Butler University and the Indiana School of Dentistry. She obtained a master&#x27;s degree in Pediatric Dentistry from the Indiana University Pediatric Dental Program and is a Diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

A love for children and deep interest in their oral care needs are the reasons Dr. Kugar entered the dental profession. She worked as a part-time instructor at Riley Hospital for Children for nine years and has always been an active advocate for children&#x27;s dental health. Dr. Kugar is married and has two children of her own: twins, Alex, and Meredith.
