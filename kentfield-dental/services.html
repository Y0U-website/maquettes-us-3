Dental Services in Kentfield…

Everything your smile needs, in one Kentfield office

From conservative everyday care to same-day crowns, implants, in-house oral surgery and the digital technology that makes it precise — here’s the full range of what Dr. G and the Kentfield Dental team provide for Marin patients. Things most offices send out, we do here.

Restorative & General Everyday care

Fillings & Bonding Mercury-free, color-matched fillings and artful bonding designed to repair decay, chips and small gaps — conservatively, and often in a single visit.

Same-Day Crowns A precision-fitted ceramic crown designed, milled and bonded in a single visit — no goopy impressions, no temporary, no second appointment.

Porcelain Crowns Strong, lifelike all-ceramic crowns designed to rebuild a damaged tooth and blend right in with your smile.

Root Canals A calm, modern way to treat a painful or infected tooth and help you keep your natural smile.

Emergency Care A toothache, a broken tooth, a crown that popped off — call us, and we’ll work to get you seen fast and feeling better.

Cosmetic Look & feel

Porcelain Veneers Thin, custom porcelain shells designed to refine the shape, color, proportion and alignment of your smile — previewed before we ever touch a tooth.

Teeth Whitening Professional whitening that’s stronger, more even, and more carefully managed than what you can buy at the drugstore — tailored to your smile in our Kentfield office.

Gummy Smile / Gingivectomy Precise diode-laser gum recontouring that gently reshapes excess gum tissue to reveal more of your natural teeth and create a balanced, even gumline.

Black Triangle Closure Closing the dark “black triangle” gaps near the gumline with the minimally invasive, no-drill Bioclear Matrix System — for a smooth, natural, easy-to-clean smile.

Orthodontics Straighter smile

Invisalign A clearer way to straighten your smile — removable, nearly invisible aligners, planned digitally and guided by a dentist in our Kentfield office.

Retainers Custom-fit retainers that hold your straightened smile in place after orthodontic or Invisalign treatment, in clear, Hawley, and bonded styles to fit your habits.

Implants & Oral Surgery In-house surgery

Dental Implants A natural-looking replacement for a missing tooth — root and all — planned in 3D and placed right here in our Kentfield office.

Same-Day Implants When the timing and the bone are right, we can remove a failing tooth and place your implant in the same visit — often fewer surgeries, less waiting, and a tooth-shaped temporary you can leave with the same day.

Wisdom Teeth Calm, carefully planned wisdom tooth removal — handled right here in our Kentfield office, with 3D imaging that maps the nerve before we ever begin.

Surgical Extractions When a tooth is broken down, impacted or fractured below the gumline, we remove it gently and in-house — and work to protect the bone for whatever comes next.

Sinus Lift & Grafting When the upper jaw is too thin for implants, we can rebuild the foundation first — gently, precisely, and planned in 3D right here in our Kentfield office.

All-on-X Full-Arch Implants A fixed, full set of teeth for an entire upper or lower arch, supported by as few as four to six implants — no slipping, no adhesives, no removable denture.

Technology & Specialty Care Things most don’t do

Same-Day Nightguards A precise, custom-fit nightguard designed and 3D-printed in our Kentfield office — often the same day, with no goopy molds and no weeks of waiting on an outside lab.

3D Printing Custom dental appliances designed and printed right here in our Kentfield office — for a precise, personalized fit, often the same day.

CBCT 3D Imaging A low-dose 3D x-ray that lets us see your jaw in full — bone, nerves, sinuses and roots — before we plan a single step of treatment.

Laser Dentistry A gentler way to care for the gums — precise soft-tissue laser treatment designed for less bleeding, fewer sutures, and a more comfortable recovery.

Sedation Dentistry A calmer way through the dental visit — nitrous oxide, oral sedation, or IV sedation, all carefully monitored, so anxious patients and longer treatments can feel relaxed and at ease.

In-Home Visits Dentistry brought into the home for Marin patients who are homebound, elderly, frail, or medically complex and can’t easily get to a traditional office.

Sleep & Airway A custom-fit Herbst oral appliance that gently holds your lower jaw forward to help keep your airway open — a quiet, travel-friendly alternative to CPAP for the right patients.

TMJ / TMD Treatment Conservative, stepwise care for jaw pain, clicking, headaches, and grinding — we start with the least invasive options first and tailor the plan to what’s actually causing your symptoms.
