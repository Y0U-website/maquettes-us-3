Treatment for Injury or Illness

Even if you do a great job of keeping up with your wellness care and maintaining your overall health, there may be times when you’re under the weather. Fortunately, LISH will be there for you in both sickness and health.

Of course, if you have sudden, severe symptoms or a major injury, the emergency room should be your first stop. For less-critical situations, though, it’s better to set up an appointment with your PCP than to visit the ER. Our doctors can provide a diagnosis and treatment, or they can refer you to a specialist who can help. Most of the time on the same day. It may be in person or through a telemedicine visit.

  • Fever
  • Cough, sore throat or ear pain

  • Stomach pain
  • Skin rash
  • Moderate injury
