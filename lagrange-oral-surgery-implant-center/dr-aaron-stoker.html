Meet Dr. Aaron Stoker, D.M.D

Board-certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Aaron Stoker

Dr. Aaron Stoker is a board-certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon with extensive experience across military, hospital, and civilian clinical settings. Originally from Las Vegas, Nevada, he grew up across the Midwest and Southeast before completing his undergraduate studies at Brigham Young University, graduating Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Business Management. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, where he was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society for academic excellence.

A Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Dr. Stoker remains committed to ongoing education, mentorship, and delivering exceptional care to every patient he treats.
