MEET OUR TEAM

Our friendly and experienced team of dental professionals is committed to helping you improve your oral health and enjoy a beautiful, confident smile for life! We are excited to welcome you to Noel Dental Arts, and when you visit Dr. Jake Noel or Dr. Karter Smith, you can count on us to help you feel at ease so that you can receive the care you need in comfort. Call 256-883-0030 today to schedule an appointment with our dentists in Huntsville, Alabama, and learn more!

KRYSTAL OPERATIONS & BILLING MANAGER Krystal has been in billing since 2011. She started out in medical billing and has been a certified medical biller and coder since 2013. After meeting and working with Dr. Noel previously, she switched over to dental and fell in love.

She has been in her current role as operations/billing manager since she joined our practice in April of 2021. Krystal enjoys helping and interacting with patients. Our patients and staff enjoy her cheerful, positive personality and glowing bright smile. Krystal has been married to her husband since 2014 and they share three kids together, Tristen, Jacob, and Jaxon. In her free time, she loves to travel, dine out, attend sports games for her kids and spends her free time devoted to her family and friends.

BRIANNE INSURANCE COORDINATOR Brianne joined our team in July 2022, but has been in the dental field since 2018. She may be the first person you see or talk to in the office as she handles all front desk duties including insurance.

Her favorite thing about Noel Dental Arts is the family like atmosphere and appreciates how she can depend on her coworkers for any and everything. Outside of work Brianne has one child, Logann, and together they enjoy shopping and more shopping. Out in the wild you may run into them at Target or TJ Maxx.

WHITNEY LEAD DENTAL HYGIENIST Whitney graduated cum laude from Wallace State Community College in 2008 with a degree in dental hygiene and the Hu-Friedy Golden Scaler award. Then, in 2021, she graduated cum laude from Southern New Hampshire University with a Bachelor of Science in public health.

Whitney started with us in July of 2021. She is very dedicated and gives 100 % to our patients, striving to give them quality care and better oral health education. Outside of work, Whitney is married to her husband, who retired from the Army in 2019 after 23 years of service. Being a military spouse has given her the opportunity to work in many different settings, but she returned to her home in Alabama in 2021. She and her husband have two children. In her free time, Whitney likes to read and spend time with family.

VANESSA DENTAL HYGIENIST Vanessa earned her dental hygiene degree at the University of Alabama at Birmingham in 2009 and became a part of our practice in August 2022.

She appreciates the chance to be a part of a team that is like family, and she builds connections with her patients so that they feel comfortable during their visits. Vanessa married her husband, Jake, in 2008 and they have two daughters, Ella and Emma. In her free time, she enjoys weight lifting and CrossFit.

BRITTAN DENTAL ASSISTANT Brittan is a certified dental assistant and has been a part of our team since June of 2022.
