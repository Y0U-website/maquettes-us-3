Patient Information

At Kamilche Dental Center in Shelton, WA, Steve Peters and his entire staff welcome you into a warm environment where you’ll receive the dental care you need . Welcoming You To Our Practice!

Appointments

Lucky you, we’re accepting new patients! Please call our office at 360-427-1784 to make an appointment. Once an appointment is scheduled, that time will be reserved for you. If, for some reason, you are unable to keep your scheduled appointment time, call us as soon as possible to reschedule and avoid missed appointment fees.

Insurance

We accept most dental insurance plans. Please familiarize yourself with your insurance plan, and pay close attention to copays. Our accounting staff will prepare all the necessary forms for your dental benefits. However, we remind you that your specific policy is an agreement between you and your insurance company.

Please know that you are responsible for your total obligation should your insurance benefits result in less coverage than anticipated. We do not render our services on the basis that insurance companies will pay all of our fees. We base our treatment on what is best for you. What to Bring to your First Appointment Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to fill out the necessary paperwork. To provide you with prompt dental treatment, please be sure to bring the following when you visit our practice for your initial visit:

Insurance Information/Card Photo Identification Any medical records pertaining to your visit Payment Payment is expected at the time services are rendered, unless other arrangements have been made. This includes any copayments or deductibles (amount not covered by your insurance).
