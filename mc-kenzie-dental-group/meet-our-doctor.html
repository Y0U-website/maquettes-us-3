Meet Our Doctor

J. David McKenzie, DDS

Dr. David McKenzie was raised in New Smyrna Beach and graduated from New Smyrna Beach High School in 1978 and University of Florida in 1982. He received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Emory University School of Dentistry in 1986.

Dr. McKenzie is an active member of the American Dental Association and a member of numerous professional dental organizations. His love of dentistry keeps him enrolled in multiple continuing education classes to help him stay abreast of current techniques and materials which help he and his staff deliver the latest and most advanced dental services.
