When should my child visit the dentist?

The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends scheduling a child’s first dental visit by age one or when their first tooth appears. At Jacobs Dental Group, we make early dental visits stress-free — helping little smiles stay healthy with gentle, compassionate care.
