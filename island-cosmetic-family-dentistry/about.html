About Island Cosmetic & Family Dentistry

Island Cosmetic & Family Dentistry is a downtown Cocoa Beach practice for families, cosmetic dentistry, and dental emergencies. You will find us at 41 N Orlando Ave, Suites 1 and 2, across from the beach and a few minutes from Port Canaveral.

Dr. Bhawna Chandra, DDS, cares for this office today. She is the dentist named on this website, and she is the person you will see for exams, restorations, smile updates, and urgent visits.

If you have been a patient here through a change of ownership, you are still welcome. The goal is the same: careful dentistry, explained in plain language, in a place that feels like Cocoa Beach rather than a chain clinic.

A Cocoa Beach practice with a new chapter

This office has been part of downtown Cocoa Beach for years. After Dr. Boers retired, Dr. Chandra stepped in to keep dental care available in this same location.

That kind of handoff works best when it is respectful. Dr. Boers’ years with this community matter. Dr. Chandra’s job is to continue the work with her own training, her own chairside style, and a full range of family and cosmetic services.

You do not need to start over just because the name on the door changed. Bring your history, your questions, and your current concerns. We will look at where you are and plan the next step.

Meet Dr. Bhawna Chandra, DDS

Dr. Chandra earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. She also holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Saurashtra University, Dental College & Hospital in Jamnagar, India (2004).

She has more than 20 years of combined clinical experience in India and the United States. She practiced as a general dentist in India for several years after dental school, then continued her education in the U.S. That is a combined career, not two decades of U.S. practice only.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Florida Dental Association, and the Dental Society of Greater Orlando. She keeps up with continuing education so materials and techniques stay current.

Where we are on the Space Coast

The office sits in downtown Cocoa Beach at 41 N Orlando Ave, Suites 1 and 2, Cocoa Beach, FL 32931. There is street parking and rear parking.

We are close to the beach and a short drive from Port Canaveral. That matters if you live on the island, work on the water, or need a dentist before a cruise or after you return to port.

Neighbors come from Cocoa Beach, Cape Canaveral, Merritt Island, and the wider Space Coast. Visitors use the same door. We do not run a separate “tourist clinic.” You get the same exam, the same digital X-rays, and the same dentist.

Who we serve

This is a family, cosmetic, and emergency practice.

Families and locals. Kids, parents, and grandparents can come to one office for cleanings, fillings, crowns, and routine checkups. We see children as early as age 2, and we are glad when a child visits during a parent’s cleaning so the office feels familiar.

People who want a nicer smile. Veneers, Lumineers, bonding, whitening, and smile makeovers are part of everyday care here, not an add-on department in another city.

Anyone who is hurting. Toothache, a broken tooth, a lost crown, or a knocked-out tooth should not wait for “someday.” Call (321) 799-1090 and tell us it is urgent.

Cruise passengers and crew. If you are sailing from Port Canaveral, or you just came back, we will try to see you as soon as the schedule allows. Bring any insurance information you have. If you are paying out of pocket, ask us what to expect before treatment starts.

How visits usually feel

You check in, we review what brought you in, and Dr. Chandra examines your teeth and gums. Digital X-rays are used when they help the diagnosis. Then you hear options, including a simpler path when that is enough.

We accept most major dental PPO plans, including MetLife, Delta, Principal, Cigna, United Concordia, United Healthcare, Aetna, Guardian, Assurant, Standard, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and Florida Combined Life. Call to confirm your plan. CareCredit and major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express) are also options listed on our FAQ page.
