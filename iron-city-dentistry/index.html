The practice

At Iron City Dentistry, every visit is built around you. Our team - led by Dr. Frese - delivers skillful, honest dental care in a practice that treats every patient like family. Whether you are coming in for a routine checkup or a full smile makeover, we are committed to making your experience comfortable, transparent, and long-lasting.

We proudly serve patients from Forestdale, Adamsville, Pleasant Grove, Fairfield, Ensley, Fultondale, and communities throughout western Birmingham.

Protect your smile with regular exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, and fluoride treatments. Early detection means simpler, more affordable treatment - and fewer surprises down the road.

Ready for a smile you love? We offer professional teeth whitening, ClearCorrect clear aligners, porcelain veneers, and cosmetic bonding to help you look and feel your best.

From dental implants and crowns to root canal therapy and same-day CEREC restorations, our team handles complex dental needs with precision and care - right here in our Forestdale office.
