Regular check-ups, cleanings, and fluoride treatments tailored to children’s needs. Your pediatric dentist will help your child learn to prevent tooth decay and promote healthy oral habits from an early age.

Specialized care for pediatric dental emergencies such as toothaches or knocked-out teeth. Your pediatric dentist ensures prompt attention and gentle treatment to alleviate pain and preserve your child’s dental health.

Safe and gentle sedation options tailored to your child’s needs ensure a calm and comfortable experience for those with dental anxiety or special healthcare requirements.

Dr. Josephine Korlei Mensah received her bachelor of arts and dental degrees from the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia. She completed a general practice residency at St. Luke’s-Roosevelt Hospital in Manhattan after dental school. She then obtained her specialty training in pediatric dentistry from St. Barnabas Hospital in Bronx, New York, where she served as chief resident.

Education is one of her passions. Dr. Mensah is currently on faculty at St. Barnabas Hospital Health System in the Bronx, where she enjoys educating and inspiring future pediatric dentists. She is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.
