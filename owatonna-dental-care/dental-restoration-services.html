Crowns & Bridges

Crowns and bridges are restorative dental procedures used to replace missing or damaged teeth. A crown is a tooth-shaped cap placed over a damaged tooth to restore its shape, size, and strength. A bridge is a custom-made device used to fill the space where one or more teeth are missing. Both crowns and bridges are designed to improve the functionality and appearance of your teeth.

Bonded Fillings

Bonded fillings are another restorative dental procedure we offer at Owatonna Dental Care. They are used to repair teeth damaged by decay or trauma. Bonded fillings are made from a tooth-colored composite resin applied to the tooth and then hardened with a special light. This type of filling is durable, long-lasting, and virtually undetectable.

Dental Implant Restoration

Implant restoration is a restorative dental procedure that involves placing a dental implant in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth. The implant is then topped with a crown to restore the tooth’s look and function. Implants are popular for people who want a permanent solution to tooth loss.

Full Mouth Restoration

Full mouth restoration is a comprehensive restorative dental procedure designed to improve the health, function, and appearance of teeth. This procedure may involve a combination of treatments, including crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, and veneers.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a popular cosmetic dental procedure that can improve the appearance of your teeth. Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that are placed over the front surface of your teeth to improve their color, shape, size, or length. They can correct a variety of dental issues, including stained or discolored teeth, chipped or cracked teeth, and misshapen teeth.

Dentures & Partials

Dentures and partials are popular solutions for people who have lost their natural teeth. They are custom-made and can help patients restore their beautiful smiles, improve their speech, and regain their ability to eat properly. Owatonna Dental Care is committed to providing high-quality dental restorative services to its patients, including dentures and partials.

One of the primary benefits of dentures and partials is that they are custom-made to fit each patient’s unique needs. This means they are comfortable to wear and provide a natural appearance that can restore confidence. Additionally, dentures and partials can prevent teeth from shifting and causing further dental problems.
