Finance & Insurance

We accept and bill most dental insurances. We are a Premier Delta Dental and Cigna provider. We are currently not accepting new Medicaid patients.

Our goal is to make all dental procedures and work as easy and simple for our clients as possible. That includes straightforward, no-hassle policies on finance and payment.

Our staff will provide all clients with a treatment plan and written estimate prior to any dental work, so you know the details of all costs.

For the convenience of our customers, we accept most forms of payment, in addition to Care Credit for up to 12 months. For more information on payments and finances, contact our staff today .

Our staff will file dental insurance claims for you and help you with any questions or concerns, but please remember that no insurance company attempts to cover all dental costs.

We’re happy to assist in selecting from our various financial options to find the best fit for you.

Instead of worrying about finances, we want you to concentrate on what you came to us for — beautifying your smile!
