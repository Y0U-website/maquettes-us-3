The Benefits of All-on-X

Fixed, Not Removable

All-on-X teeth are permanently anchored in your mouth. No adhesives, no slipping, no embarrassing moments. You&#x27;ll enjoy a secure, stable smile that stays in place all day, every day.

Eat the Foods You Love

Strong, stable implants restore your bite so you can enjoy a wide variety of foods again — including many that denture wearers often have to avoid.

Look & Feel Younger

All-on-X restores the facial support and lip contour that are often lost with missing teeth or dentures, giving you a more natural, youthful appearance.

Preserve Bone Health

Dental implants stimulate and help preserve your jawbone, preventing the bone loss that continues over time with traditional dentures. This protects both your appearance and your long-term oral health.

Long-Term Solution

With proper care, All-on-X implants are designed to last many years — making them a lasting investment in your health and quality of life rather than a temporary fix.

Improve Your Quality of Life

Many patients report greater confidence, more energy, stronger relationships, and improved overall happiness after All-on-X treatment. A healthier smile affects nearly every part of daily life.

Your All-on-X Journey

Consultation

Dr. Andrus and our team will listen to your goals, review your concerns, and determine whether All-on-X is the right option for you. This is your opportunity to ask questions and get an honest assessment.

Records Appointment

We gather everything needed to plan your case precisely: a 3D CBCT scan, digital impressions, full-face and intraoral photographs, bite registration, and a smile and lip analysis.

Smile Design

Your teeth are custom-designed around your face — factoring in lip support, your bite, smile line, tooth shape, shade, and even phonetics — so your result looks and feels like you.

Surgery Day

Implants are placed and, when clinically appropriate, a temporary set of teeth is delivered the same day. You leave with a brand-new smile.

Healing Phase

Your implants integrate with your jawbone while we monitor your comfort, bite, hygiene, and healing closely. We provide clear aftercare instructions every step of the way.

Final Zirconia Smile

Once fully healed, your custom final teeth are designed, verified, delivered, and adjusted for long-term function and beauty. This is your forever smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I finance All-on-X treatment?

Yes. Our team can review available financing options and monthly payment possibilities during your consultation. We also work with HSA/FSA funds and third-party financing to help make treatment manageable.

Does the procedure hurt?

Most patients report significantly less discomfort than they anticipated. Dr. Andrus reviews all comfort options with you beforehand and provides detailed post-operative instructions to support a smooth recovery.

How long will my implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, All-on-X implants can provide many years of function and stability. They&#x27;re designed to be a long-term investment in your health and confidence.

How long does All-on-X treatment take?

Treatment is completed in stages. Surgery and temporary teeth are followed by a healing phase before your final restoration is made. The full process typically spans several months, and our team will guide you through every phase with clear timelines.

Will I be without teeth during treatment?

In many cases, temporary teeth can be delivered shortly after implant placement. Your exact plan depends on your bone quality, implant stability, and clinical findings — Dr. Andrus will walk you through what to expect at your consultation.
