Meet Our Team

Dr. Justin Raybould

In 2013, Dr. Raybould returned to Lexington to complete his residency in Pediatric Dentistry at UK. Happy to be back in the Bluegrass, he decided to develop a practice that combined his love for the Lexington community, public service, fine arts, and children: Modern Kids Dentistry.

Amanda Grace, DMD, Pediatric Dentist & Associate Partner

During her four years in dental school, Dr. Grace became interested in dental mission trips and traveled to Honduras four times. Combining her love for dentistry, helping others, and speaking Spanish, dental mission trips have become an important part of her life and who she is. Since 2011, each January Dr. Grace has gone on a medical/dental mission trip to Guatemala and hopes to continue this tradition for years to come. She is an active member in her church and where she taught Sunday school for 6 years.

Dr. Grace resides in Nicholasville, KY with her husband Josh and four children, Logan, Ava, Easton and Savannah. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her family and friends, traveling, and running. Dr. Grace looks forward to creating a positive dental experience for your child at Modern Kids Dentistry of Nicholasville.

Angel Lopez, DDS

Dr. Angel Lopez has joined our team! This Texas native was quickly charmed by the southern hospitality of our great state of Kentucky. Before making his way here, Dr. Angel completed his undergrad at Abilene Christian University, then his DDS at Texas A&M College of Dentistry, and finally completed his pediatric dental residency at Cincinnati Children’s Hospital Medical Center. When asked WHY pediatric dentistry, Dr. Angel replied, “I love the kiddos. To me, there is nothing more special than being able to equip children and parents with the knowledge they need to conquer those sugar bugs.”

Fun fact 1: He’s an actor-like the real deal-having been in several commercials, films and short films. He believes his passion for acting equates to his passion for pediatric dentistry. “To be able to ‘act’ with children every day, makes going to work a true joy.”

Fun fact 2: He has a two year old Australian Shepherd named Barley. Dr. Angel is proud to call himself a “Dog Dad.”

Fun fact 3: Traveling the world. He recently started a blog to record his traveling experiences and plans to go on as many adventures as possible.Modern Kids Dentistry is absolutely thrilled to have Dr. Angel join us and we look forward to what he has to offer our patients and their families! If you meet him, please keep that southern hospitality going by giving him a huge welcome!

Dr. Kelly Chrisco

Dr. Kelly Chrisco is a board certified pediatric dentist who joined Modern Kids Dentistry in 2022 after a 15 year span at the University of Kentucky. For the first 10 years, Dr. Chrisco provided treatment on a dental mobile unit throughout the region for underserved children. During these years, her desire to serve children throughout the state became stronger and she left her position on the mobile unit to pursue furthering her education in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Chrisco attended the pediatric residency at the University of Kentucky from 2017-2019 and became faculty in the residency program. Spring of 2022, she was invited to join Modern Kids Dentistry, giving her the opportunity to give back to the community through private practice. Dr. Chrisco’s love for Kentucky began from birth. Born in Lexington, she attended Millcreek and Stonewall Elementary, Jessie Clark Middle School, and Lafayette High School. She then stayed close by to receive her undergraduate and dental degrees at the University of Kentucky.

Dr. Chrisco is blessed with 2 sons and gained 2 daughters and an additional son from her husband. The family and 2 dogs, keep her busy. She can be seen out most weekends at the soccer field and musical events supporting her kids. In addition to being with her family, Dr. Chrisco enjoys traveling near and far, her favorite place being Alaska.

Dr. Andrew Buckles

When not practicing dentistry, you may find Dr. Buckles spending time with his family (his parents, grandparents, a triplet brother and sister, and a 9-month-old nephew); baking desserts for the office and holiday celebrations; attending church; and, rooting for his Hill Toppers, Wildcats, and yes, even the Florida Gators! He is especially looking forward to, once again, enjoying Kentucky’s weather and countryside, especially during the Fall and Winter seasons, sights and experiences he sorely missed while completing his pediatric residency, in Florida!

Dr. Buckles is looking forward to meeting you, and will do everything possible to meet the needs of you and your child(ren)!

Dr. Brett Henson

Dr. Brett Henson is a board-certified pediatric dentist and fellow of the academy of pediatric dentistry. Dr. Henson joined our team in the summer of 2024 after 26 years of providing world class dental care as a Dental Corps officer in the U.S. Army around the world and in multiple deployed theaters of combat.

Fun Fact 1: The campus of SIU-SDM houses a statue of the world’s tallest man who ever lived. You may notice Dr. Henson is a little tall as well, but not that tall. While serving in the U.S. Army, Dr. Henson was selected and attended Texas A&M Baylor College of Dentistry’s Pediatric Dentistry residency program in Dallas, Texas. He received an amazing resident experience while attending Children’s Medical Center of Dallas and Scottish Rite for Children during this residency.

Dr. Henson and his growing young family spent the next nearly 22 years providing pediatric dental care, teaching comprehensive dental residents, deploying and serving in multiple military leadership and executive healthcare policy positions. Dr. Henson also served as Consultant to the U.S. Army Surgeon for Pediatric Dentistry. All the while increasing his passion to advocate and care for children.

Dr. Henson has an amazing wife of (as of 2024) 34 years. They have been by each other’s side for 34 glorious years raising and parenting two beautiful daughters across countless military moves (and schools, and countries, and states, and boys, and teenage years, and…..you get the point). They lived and understand military life and the impact it brings to bear on our children. We feel so blessed to be a part of Modern Kids Dentistry, answered prayers for sure.

Fun Fact 2: Want to make Dr. Henson smile? Ask him about his daughters, his wife and maybe about driving tractors.

Arnessa Raybould, BSDH, Chief Executive Officer

Arnessa has worked in the dental field since 1996. She began her career as a receptionist, but immediately developed an interest in the clinical aspects of dentistry. She quickly transitioned to a dental assisting position while pursuing her Associates in Applied Science of Dental Hygiene from Bluegrass Community and Technical College. She completed her associate’s degree in 1999. In 2011, she furthered her education by earning a Bachelor’s in Applied Science of Dental Hygiene through the University of Bridgeport Connecticut.

Having both a background in clinical and administrative roles, she has been able to facilitate the opening of multiple offices and now fully manages all four locations of Modern Kids Dentistry. As Chief Executive Officer, Arnessa mainly spends time overseeing our four locations but she does still enjoy seeing long time patients from time to time. Also, if you notice some unique features at any of our four locations, these were likely designed with her creativity.
