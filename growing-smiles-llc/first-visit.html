First visit

Arrive 15 minutes early

That gives you time to complete health history and insurance information without feeling rushed.

Bring the essentials

Please bring your photo ID, insurance card, medication list and any questions you want answered.

Meet your care team

We’ll listen, explain what happens next and make room for individual comfort and communication needs.

Complete the visit

When appropriate, the visit includes necessary X-rays, a cleaning and a complete exam.

Leave with a clear plan

If care is recommended, we’ll explain the options and the next step.

Schedule your next visit

Before you leave, remember to schedule your next appointment—especially when it is time for your six-month recall.

Tell us what helps.

If a patient has sensory preferences, anxiety, special health needs or a favorite way to communicate, let the team know before the visit.

Complete forms before your visit.

We can email first-visit forms so you can complete them at home and bring them to the appointment. Downloadable forms will be added here as soon as the office provides the files.

Our office and entrance are wheelchair accessible.
