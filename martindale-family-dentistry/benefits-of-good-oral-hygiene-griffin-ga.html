1. Routine Checkups Can Prevent Major Dental Issues

Regular checkups with an experienced dentist like Dr. Martindale are the first step to catching potential problems before they start. A dental exam will help detect early signs of a cavity or gum disease risk. At Martindale Family Dentistry, the team takes a personalized approach. With state-of-the-art technology and advanced diagnostics, you can be sure you’re in the best possible hands to keep yourself and your family in top-notch oral health.

2. Brighter Smiles with Professional Cleanings

If you’ve ever had a dental cleaning, you know how great your teeth feel when you leave the dental office. However, professional dental cleanings offer more than just the immediate sensation of clean, smooth teeth. During a dental cleaning, the hygienist removes plaque and tartar that cannot be effectively reached through regular brushing and flossing at home.

3. Good Oral Hygiene for Protecting Your Overall Health

Did you know dental issues like gum disease and tooth decay can affect physical health? Taking care of your teeth is about overall body wellness and healthy living. Good oral care can play a role in:

  • Heart health
  • Respiratory health
  • Diabetes management
  • Cognitive function
  • Pregnancy complications

4. Save Money in the Long Run with Preventative Dental Care

Procedures for advanced dental issues can be costly. For this reason, investing in solid preventative care can save you not only discomfort later on but also lots of money. Routine checkups and cleanings are typically covered by most dental insurance plans, so it’s easy to ensure these happen.

5. Experience Personalized Care in a Comfortable Setting

At our office, you’ll receive premium care in a welcoming environment. Dr. Martindale and his team blend small-town hospitality and top-of-the-line technology. Our practice is dedicated to delivering the family-oriented care you deserve, where your healthcare team is familiar with your history and personal preferences.

6. The Benefits of Advanced Preventative Treatments

Beyond dental cleanings and checkups, Dr. Martindale offers advanced preventative treatments. For example, dental sealants give you an extra layer of protection against cavities and tooth enamel erosion. These sealants are a barrier to shield the vulnerable areas of your teeth from bacteria and food particles. Also, fluoride applications strengthen your dental enamel and help prevent tooth decay.
