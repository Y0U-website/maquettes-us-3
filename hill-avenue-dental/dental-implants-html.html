What Are Dental Implants?

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

Getting dental implants is a multistep process that spans several months. While this may seem like quite an involved process, it’s relatively straightforward and the benefits are well-worth the time and effort! Once you’ve completed your dental implant treatment, you can easily expect to enjoy a comfortable, functional, and beautiful smile for the rest of your life! So what does this process entail? While every tooth replacement journey will look slightly different, there are four main stages you can expect when rebuilding your smile.

Initial Consultation & Preliminary Treatments

First, you’ll visit us for a consultation to make sure dental implants are a good choice for you. We’ll carefully inspect your smile for issues that may threaten the success of your dental implants, like gum disease and inadequate jawbone density. Many patients need to complete preliminary treatments such as tooth extractions, periodontal therapy, and bone grafting before they can be considered a good candidate. We can help you schedule the necessary treatments that will get you ready for your new pearly whites.

Dental Implant Surgery

Next, the implant posts will need to be placed in the jawbone. We partner with the best local specialists to ensure you get the best care and results during this essential step of the process. The surgery is relatively straightforward: first, one or more small incisions will be made in your gums to access your jawbone, depending on how many teeth you’re missing. The implant post (or posts) is then positioned into the jawbone and then the gums are closed around it. Finally, a protective cap is placed over it and you’ll be sent home to heal.

Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

After your surgery, it will take four to six months before your mouth has healed enough to move on to placing your new teeth. During this time, osseointegration will take place. This is the process where the titanium implant posts gradually fuse with the bone tissue in your jaw. In the end, your dental implant will essentially be part of your jawbone.

Then, you’ll complete one more minor surgery to have your abutment(s) placed. Of course, you’ll skip this step if your abutment was placed during your surgery. This small connector piece goes on top of the implant post and will eventually hold your final restoration in place. While you heal from this surgery over the next few weeks, your crown, bridge, or denture will be designed and created.

Delivery of the Final Restoration

You’ll return to our office for the final step of your dental implant treatment. We’ll secure your custom-made crown, bridge, or denture into place using the abutment piece. Then, once we’ve made sure that you’re happy with your results and that your bite feels comfortable, you’ll be ready to go out and show off your complete, confident smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Benefits for Your Day-to-Day Life

  • Eat whatever you like : While dentures and dental bridges can only restore about 30 % of your natural biting force, dental implants can restore it by 80 % or more! This means you’ll have no trouble eating apples, nuts, steaks, and anything else on your plate. Not to mention, you won’t have to worry about your prosthetics slipping or falling out!
  • Enjoy a completely comfortable smile : Dental implants won’t cause gum sores, tooth sensitivity, or jaw problems like worn-down bridges or ill-fitting dentures. In fact, dental implants are so comfortable, you may forget you even have them!
  • Smile confidently once again : With dental implants, you’ll never have to worry about your new teeth feeling loose or slipping out of place while you eat or talk. What’s more, your final restoration will be custom-made to blend in seamlessly with the rest of your pearly whites!
  • Embrace a better quality of life : It is believed that 20 % of denture-wearers are afraid to smile because of the embarrassment they have about their dentures. With dental implants, you can feel confident and embrace a fuller, more enjoyable life surrounded by family, friends, and colleagues.

Benefits for Your Health

  • Preserve your jawbone & youthful appearance : As soon as a tooth goes missing, the jawbone begins to deteriorate. Eventually, this can lead to additional tooth loss and a sunken, aged appearance. Dental implants in Superior are the only tooth replacement option that can preserve your jawbone and youthful appearance after losing one or more teeth.
  • Protect your remaining teeth : Dental implants don’t weaken your remaining healthy teeth as a traditional bridge does. Instead, they prevent dental drift just like a natural tooth.
  • Easily maintain great oral and overall health : Since dental implants are easy to clean and don’t wear down the rest of your teeth, it’s much easier to keep them clean and maintain excellent oral hygiene. On top of this, studies show that a healthy mouth can reduce your risk of conditions like heart disease, diabetes, and osteoporosis.

Benefits to Enjoy for Decades to Come

  • Save money on your smile: Dentures and dental bridges need to be replaced every 5-7 years. On the other hand, dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime, which can potentially save you thousands of dollars on replacements, repairs, cleaning products, and extra trips to your dentist.
  • Lasting results you can count on : With great oral hygiene, most patients can easily expect their newly rebuilt smile to last them a lifetime.
  • Live longer : Dental implants can potentially extend your lifespan. Studies show that those with 20 or more teeth at the age of 70 have a considerably higher chance of living longer than patients with fewer teeth at that age.

Who is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Almost any healthy adult can get dental implants, which is one of the reasons why they’re so popular. Usually, you’ll need to have three things to be considered a candidate for dental implants:

  • Good overall health: Dental implants require a surgical procedure from a dental implant dentist in Superior, so you’ll need to be healthy enough to undergo minor surgery.
  • Great oral health: Any preexisting issues like gum disease or cavities must be treated before getting dental implants because they can interfere with the success of your procedure.
  • Jawbone density: Your jawbone must be strong enough to support dental implants. If you don’t have adequate jawbone density, we may recommend bone grafting before you become a candidate for implants.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implant

As the only tooth replacement to rebuild the crown and roots of a missing tooth, dental implants can fully and naturally restore your smile. Not only do they look, feel, and function just like natural teeth, but dental implants can be cared for just as easily as well! You won’t need to buy any special cleaning solutions or take them out to soak every night. Dental implants can last for decades with minimal maintenance, and most patients can expect to enjoy them for life! Read on as we go over the five best ways to care for your dental implants in Superior.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

You won’t need to worry about your dental implants getting cavities, but it’s important that you make your routine oral hygiene a priority nonetheless. Poor dental habits put you at a higher risk of issues like gum disease, which can pose a threat to your dental implants. So make sure you brush for two minutes twice a day, floss daily, and rinse with mouthwash regularly to keep your smile strong and healthy.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Dental implants will help you enjoy all your favorite foods once again! However, don’t forget to keep your diet balanced and nutritious. Eat plenty of lean proteins, low-fat dairy, and other foods with lots of calcium to help keep the foundation of your dental implants strong. Also be sure to get plenty of vitamin C to encourage stronger, healthier gums.

Break Bad Habits

If you want to make the most out of your new smile, you need to protect your dental implants by breaking a few common bad habits. To take care of your dental implants, you need to:

  • Stop smoking: Although easier said than done, quitting smoking will dramatically reduce your risk of dental implant failure (as well as numerous other serious oral health issues).
  • Refrain from chewing on hard objects: Chewing on pens, fingernails, pencils, and other hard objects when you’re nervous or bored can damage your restoration or loosen your implant, so chew a piece of sugarless gum instead!
  • Never use your teeth as tools: Using your teeth to open packages, carry keys, rip off price tags, and to perform other chores can break your teeth and implant restoration in an instant! Always keep the proper tools at hand so you don’t put your smile at risk.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are incredibly strong, but they’re by no means indestructible. If you play a contact sport, you need to wear an athletic mouthguard to protect your dental implants in case of an accident. If you grind your teeth in your sleep, talk to us about getting a custom-made nightguard for bruxism. Mouthguards may be relatively small, but they can make a tremendous difference in keeping your dental implants in great condition.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Whether you’ve replaced one, several, or all your teeth with dental implants, you should still visit us every six months for your routine checkups and cleanings. Even if your teeth look and feel fine, we’ll carefully inspect your smile for any signs of issues that may be developing so we can put a stop to them right away. Of course, we always aim to keep problems of any kind from starting in the first place with thorough cleanings and personalized preventive care!

Dental Implant FAQs

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

How Long Does It Take to Recover from Dental Implant Surgery?

Many people are able to resume their normal activities the next day after surgery. This is because bleeding should stop relatively soon following your procedure. Swelling should also go down significantly after a few days. With that said, you should avoid strenuous activity for the first week or so in order to ensure the best healing process possible. You’ll also be asked to take all prescribed medications as needed. Keep in mind that the fusion of the titanium post to the implant can take as many as six months to complete.

What Can Cause Dental Implants to Fail?

How Soon After a Tooth Extraction Can I Get Dental Implants?

Every cases is unique, but there are instances, particularly in younger patients, where the jawbone is healthy enough to receive a dental implant the same day a tooth is extracted. However, it’s not uncommon for us to have to wait anywhere between three and six months before an implant can be placed as the mouth needs time to heal after a tooth extraction. We don’t want to wait much longer than that, though, as doing so can allow the jawbone to shrink due to the lack of stimulation from the tooth’s roots.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

One of the many reasons dental implants are so loved is because they can last for 30 years or more. The key to prolonging the lifespan of yours is implementing healthy habits into your routine and quitting harmful ones. More specifically, we recommend brushing your teeth twice a day, eating a well-balanced diet, and scheduling a dental checkup every six months. At the same time, avoid using your teeth as tools, biting your nails, and using tobacco products.

Do Dental Implants Decay?

Since dental implants are not made of enamel, they cannot decay. That said, you still need to make your oral hygiene regimen a priority. After all, peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease) is one of the leading causes of dental implant failure. So, it’s crucial that you take good care of your smile at home, even if you don’t have any of your natural teeth left.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants have an impressive success rate of over 95 %. Yet another reason they are considered the gold standard in tooth replacement! From the initial consultation to your final appointment, our team will guide you through what to do to prevent failure, like not using tobacco products. That way, you can enjoy your new and improved smile both now and decades from now!

Am I Too Old to Get Dental Implants?

You might assume that getting dental implants is out of the question if you’re in your 70s, 80s, or 90s. However, there isn’t an upper age limit. Instead, the density of your jawbone, the health of your gums, and whether or not you’re willing to give up smoking will determine whether or not you’re a candidate. So, don’t let your age stop you from scheduling an appointment at our dental office in Superior.
