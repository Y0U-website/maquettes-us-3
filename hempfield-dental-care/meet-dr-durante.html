Serving Patients in Lancaster

Kyle J. Durante

DDS

Dr. Kyle J. Durante grew up in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, before joining the United States Air Force at age 19. He spent four years in service as a dental assistant at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland. After finishing his undergraduate degree, he attended Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry and earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. After graduation, Dr. Durante spent the next few years as an associate dentist in Richmond, Virginia.

Dr. Durante is a member of the American Dental Association and Pennsylvania Dental Association and enjoys learning about new technologies in the dental field.

He lives with his wife, Carley, an English teacher, daughter Isla, and dog, Bentley, in Lititz, PA. Dr. Durante enjoys running, reading, bicycling, and spending time with his family. He is currently learning how to play the guitar as a newfound way to relax. He and his wife are active in their church and enjoy serving in youth ministry.
