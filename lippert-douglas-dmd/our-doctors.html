My approach to your treatment starts with you..

My commitment to providing the highest quality of care to my patients was rewarded in 2010 when I achieved a Fellowship Award from the Academy of General Dentistry at their formal ceremony in New Orleans, LA.

Achieving Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry is one of the few added distinctions that a general dentist can achieve. Only 7 % of general dentists have achieved this honor.

I am a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine . This designation signifies a professional commitment to education, knowledge, and experience in dental sleep medicine.

I am proud to serve as the Norwin School Dentist , a role appointed by the Norwin Board of Education in 2017.

My wife and I have been happily married for over 16 years. Together we have three beautiful children.

In my free time I enjoy attending Pitt sporting events, especially football and soccer games. My family and I enjoy getaways. Walt Disney World and Universal Studios are two of our favorite places.

I am a prolific Disney memorabilia collector, an expert in Star Wars canon and an avid roller coaster enthusiast. I also enjoy debating the merits of comic book art vs. traditional art with my wife.
