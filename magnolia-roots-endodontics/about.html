Why Choose an Endodontist? We only take the X-rays we really need to provide you with the absolute best care. We utilize direct digital sensors, which offer the highest dose reduction of all digital radiology methods. By optimizing our protocols, most root canal procedures only require two digital radiographs—one before treatment and one after treatment.

Digital Radiographs

The high-resolution image from a 3D CBCT scan helps us create the best action plan, treat you most efficiently, and maybe even find infections not visible on regular dental X-rays. We use the lowest possible radiation setting with the highest resolution image to give you the best possible outcome!

3D Cone Beat CT

This little machine helps measure the length of each root canal with nearly 100 % accuracy and is proven to be more accurate at measuring the length of a root canal than using an X-ray. It helps us save time to complete your root canal quickly with precision and accuracy.

Electronic Apex Locators

Dental operating microscopes provide high magnification and high-powered illumination to help visualize the inside anatomy of your tooth during root canal treatment. They ensure we can find, clean, and seal every critical portion of the root canal.
