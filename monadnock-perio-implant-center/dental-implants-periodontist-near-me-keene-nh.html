Will the gum surgery for my implant be painful?

How long will it take to heal after my gums are cut for implants?

Healing times can vary depending on your health and the complexity of your case, but most patients recover from the initial gum surgery within 7–10 days. We will provide personalized post-operative care instructions, including how to manage swelling, what foods to avoid, and how to keep the area clean. You may experience minor discomfort for a few days, but most patients return to normal activities quickly.

Are there ways to avoid cutting into the gums entirely?

n some cases, yes. Thanks to advancements in implant dentistry, certain patients may qualify for flapless or minimally invasive implant techniques that reduce or eliminate the need for incisions. These methods involve creating a small punch in the gum tissue rather than a full surgical flap. Our dentists will evaluate your specific case to determine if you’re a candidate for a less invasive option.

What are the risks of complications with gum surgery for implants?

As with any surgical procedure, there are potential risks, such as bleeding, infection, or delayed healing. However, when performed by an experienced periodontist, the risk of complications is low. We take every precaution to ensure a sterile, precise procedure and provide close follow-up care to keep your recovery on track at our office.

What can I do to prepare for implant surgery mentally?

Feeling nervous is completely normal. One of the best ways to ease your anxiety is through education. Knowing what to expect can greatly reduce fear of the unknown. Drs. Kwon, Tase and Mourad, and their team take the time to explain every step of the process, answer your questions, and help you feel confident about your care plan. You can also discuss sedation options and bring a trusted friend or family member for support.
