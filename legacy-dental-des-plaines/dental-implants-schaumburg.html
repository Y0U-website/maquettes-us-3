What a single dental implant is.

Implants are an option for adults who have lost a single tooth to decay, fracture, or extraction, and want a fixed replacement that does not depend on neighboring teeth (as a bridge would) or rely on removable dentures.

When implants are the right answer - and when they are not.

Implants are not always the best fit for every situation. Bone volume, gum health, general medical history, and timing all matter. The honest answer about whether an implant is right for you comes from an in-person examination - including 3D imaging - not from a website.

At your consultation we look at the site, take the imaging we need, and write down a plan. If implants are not the best option for your specific case, we will say so and discuss alternatives like a bridge or a different restorative approach.

Why 3D planning matters.

Modern implant cases are planned from cone-beam (CBCT) imaging - a low-dose 3D scan that shows bone height, nerve position, and sinus location with millimeter precision. This is how we decide implant length, angle, and the safest path during placement.

For adults considering implants, this is the step that separates predictable outcomes from guesswork. We will not place an implant without it.

What it costs in Schaumburg.

The cost of a single dental implant depends on the implant site, whether bone grafting is needed, the type of crown placed on top, and your specific insurance coverage. Because each case is different, we share the full estimate at your consultation rather than publishing a flat price online.

We submit claims on your behalf for most major dental insurance plans. Implant coverage varies widely - we review your benefits before treatment begins.

From first call to written plan.

  • 01 Consultation and imaging. Panoramic X-ray, digital scan, and CBCT when bone planning is needed. You leave with a written plan and an estimate.
  • 02 Implant placement. A planned surgical placement at the office. Healing follows - usually a few months - before the final crown is delivered.
  • 03 Final crown. A crown shaped and shaded to match your other teeth. We confirm fit and bite before you leave.
