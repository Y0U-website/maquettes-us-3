General Dentistry & Preventative Care

0 General Dentistry & preventative care We focus on your overall wellness — protecting your smile and oral health.

Your oral health is the foundation of total body wellness. Our holistic, patient-focused approach emphasizes preventive care and functional dentistry. We help you maintain healthy teeth and gums while avoiding invasive treatments whenever possible. By addressing oral health in a way that supports your overall well being, we aim to keep your smile functional and healthy for life.

Prophylaxis Routine cleanings remove plaque and buildup that brushing alone can’t eliminate. Regular visits help prevent cavities, gum disease, and inflammation of the tissues.

Our hygienists personalize care for each patient to ensure gentle, comfortable and effective treatments.

periodontal therapy

Targeted care for gum inflammation or bone loss treats the jaw bone and tissues surrounding your teeth. Using a modern, minimally invasive approach, we use therapies like laser treatment and light touch polishing to manage the gum disease, reduce infection, and stabilize the periodontal tissues.

Fluoride-Free Options

We provide fluoride-free preventive protocols for patients seeking holistic care, using evidence-based remineralization products like hydroxyapatite, diet guidance, and protective strategies tailored to your needs. These methods strengthen teeth naturally and encourage long term health of the teeth.

Metal-Free Restorations

Biocompatible, aesthetic restorations restore both strength and function without traditional metals. Using metal-free materials , we put our focus on creating durable, natural-looking restorations.

Night Guards & splints

Custom-fit appliances protect teeth from grinding and clenching while improving comfort, bite function, and jaw alignment. Each nightguard or splint is custom designed to relieve strain on the jaw, treat muscle pain, and preserve your teeth.
