Meet the Doctor

Dr. Safa Kamal, DMD

Known for her calm chairside manner and clear explanations, Dr. Kamal takes time to help patients understand their oral health and feel empowered to improve it—whether it’s perfecting brushing habits or walking them through treatment options. She’s especially passionate about preventive care and helping patients build healthier habits that last beyond the dental chair.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kamal is a hot yoga enthusiast and firm believer in balance—whether it’s in work, wellness, or a perfectly aligned smile.
