Signs You Might Benefit from Tooth Bonding

Many patients aren’t aware that tooth bonding could solve their dental concerns. At Hudson Valley Dental TMD & Facial Aesthetics, we typically recommend this procedure for specific cosmetic and functional issues affecting your smile’s look and function.

If you’ve noticed any of these signs, tooth bonding from our skilled team might be perfect for restoring your confident smile. We invite you to schedule a consultation to explore your options.

  • Visible chips or cracks in your teeth
  • Noticeable gaps between teeth that trap food
  • Teeth that appear too short or misshapen
  • Persistent discoloration resistant to whitening
  • Sensitive teeth from exposed roots
  • Worn tooth edges affecting your bite
  • Minor alignment issues causing cosmetic concerns
