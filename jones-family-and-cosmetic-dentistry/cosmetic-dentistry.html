Botox & Filler

Learn More

Neuromodulators such as BOTOX®, Dysport®, and Xeomin® can be used to treat a variety of conditions beyond just wrinkles. Dr. Jones provides treatment for both cosmetic purposes, such as fine lines and wrinkles, as well as for wellness concerns like migraines, muscle spasms, TMJ, clenching, and grinding.

We understand the importance of feeling and looking your best. Filler can be an excellent aid in achieving the perfect smile. It provides an alternative solution for reducing lip and facial aging lines, by shaping the lips and jaw or creating fuller lips. Our oral and facial anatomy knowledge allows us to use fillers effectively, enhancing the aesthetics of your natural smile. Our approach is to consider each patient’s needs holistically, taking into account aesthetic preferences and oral health concerns, while developing a customized treatment plan.
