Patient came in wanting to improve her smile. Patient stated that she has a missing tooth and overall not happy with aesthetics of her smile. Spoke to patient about her options and she decided to go for front dental implant and then porcelain veneers to improve her smile. Implant was placed and then smile makeover was completed with veneer placement. Patient was thrilled with a result and now wants to do her lower teeth…

Before

After

Patient was unhappy with his unhealthy teeth. His teeth were sensitive, and they were badly decayed and worn. He had infections in his gums and in his teeth. All his infected and decayed teeth were extracted. He had dental implants placed in both arches. Then he received full implant supported dentures in both arches. Now he’s happy to be able to smile with confidence and eat with comfort.
