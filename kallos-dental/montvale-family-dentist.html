Dr. Rachael Cocchia - Your Montvale Family Dentist

New Jersey Native

Dr. Rachael is a NJ native. She grew up in Watchung, and went to graduate magna cum laude from Rutgers University in New Brunswick, where she received a Bachelor of Science degree in Exercise Science and Applied Kinesiology. Then Dr. Rachael continued her education at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine where she graduated in the top of her class. She completed her general practice residency at Hackensack University Medical Center.

Connecting With Patients

For Dr. Rachael, dentistry isn’t just about teeth—it’s about connecting with people and the community. She loves getting to know each patient, building trust, and making every visit as comfortable as possible. Whether it’s a first visit at the office, a smile transformation, or simply easing dental anxiety, Dr. Rachael takes the time to listen, educate, and create personalized treatment plans that fit your needs and goals.

Dr. Rachael takes pride in her ability to connect with patients, and provide the best care possible that meets their needs. She understands that visiting the dentist can be stressful for some, and her goal is to change that dynamic by creating a welcoming, judgment-free environment where you feel heard and cared for.
