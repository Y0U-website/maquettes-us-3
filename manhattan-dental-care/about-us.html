Cleanings & Prevention

When it comes to dental disease, prevention is key. That’s why we focus on maintaining your oral health both in and out of the office.

Prevention includes regular dental and oral cancer exams, cleanings, and x-rays. Sealants and fluoride are also great preventive treatments that help protect the teeth.

Invisalign® treatment consists of a series of custom-made aligning trays. Trays are changed every several weeks to fit the new tooth configuration.

In addition to the reduced visual impact, Invisalign® aligning trays can be temporarily removed for important occasions – meaning that treatment duration is patient-controlled. Many of our patients report complete satisfaction with both the Invisalign® treatment and the stunning results.
