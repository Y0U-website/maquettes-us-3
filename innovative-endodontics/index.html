The practice

Root Canal Therapy A root canal is nothing more than a glorified filling in the tooth. This simple procedure relieves symptoms and restores the tooth to health.

Endodontic Retreatment On occasion, root canal treatment may need to be redone to return a tooth to health. This treatment can be completed comfortably.

Cracked Teeth A cracked tooth can lead to discomfort. We specialize in assessing these teeth to recommend optimal treatment for the life of the tooth.
