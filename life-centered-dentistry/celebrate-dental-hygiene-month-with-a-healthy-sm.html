Celebrate Dental Hygiene Month &…

Think of your toothbrush and floss as your everyday smile defense small habits that prevent big problems!

Even the best brushers and flossers need professional care. That’s where re-care appointments come in your regular cleanings, exams, and yearly bitewing X-rays .

These visits help us: ✅ Remove tartar and plaque buildup that brushing can’t eliminate ✅ Check for early signs of cavities, gum disease, or other concerns ✅ Detect small issues before they become big (and costly!) problems

Preventive visits every six months keep your teeth and gums in top shape and your overall health benefits, too!

Dental health isn’t “set it and forget it.” It’s a partnership. When you stay consistent with daily brushing and flossing and we see you for regular recare visits we’re setting you up for a lifetime of confident smiles. 😁

This Dental Hygiene Month, make a promise to yourself: ✔️ Brush and floss faithfully ✔️ Schedule your next cleaning and exam ✔️ Ask questions we’re always here to help!
