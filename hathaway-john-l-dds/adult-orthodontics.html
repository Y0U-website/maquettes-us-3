Adult Orthodontics

Traditional braces comprising metal brackets and wires have been around for years, helping adults and teenagers achieve gorgeous smiles. They can fix crooked bites and misaligned teeth with great precision. The brackets hold the specialized wires in position and are planned to put pressure on the required areas. And the metal wire will work to move your teeth in the correct direction slowly.

Traditional braces are an affordable option when it comes to orthodontic treatments. Hence if the cost is a significant factor in your overall decision, then this method of treatment can deliver wonderful results.

If you are not enthusiastic about wearing metal to straighten your teeth, then Invisalign® Clear Aligners will be an appropriate solution. Adults using this orthodontic treatment can see incredible results in less time. The process involves wearing transparent plastic trays for 20-22 hours every day. Based on your individual case, you will switch your set for the next in the series every fifteen days. With each new aligner, your teeth will slowly move toward the correct position, helping you attain a flawless smile.

Invisalign aligners should only be removed to perform your oral functions. These nearly invisible trays make it easy to get through your day without being apparent to anyone.

These braces help you achieve the benefits of traditional braces without any metal shining brightly in your mouth. Clear ceramic braces use transparent and tooth-colored materials. In addition, the brackets are not bulky, so it rests comfortably against your tooth, decreasing the chances of possible soft tissue irritation.
