What Qualifies as a Dental Emergency in Missoula?

A dental emergency is any situation that requires immediate dental care. Some common examples of dental emergencies include severe tooth pain, broken or knocked-out teeth, and dental abscesses. Other situations, such as a loose or broken filling or a cracked tooth, may also require emergency dental care.

Benefits of Emergency Dentistry

Expertise Missoula Dental is trained to handle a wide range of dental emergencies, and they have the expertise and equipment to provide effective care.

Prevention By receiving immediate care for a dental emergency, patients can prevent further damage and avoid more extensive treatment down the line.
