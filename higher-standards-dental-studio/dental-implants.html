Dental Implants in Decatur, GA

As a tooth replacement option, implants are in a class of their own: they integrate with the jawbone, function like a natural tooth root, and support restorations that look and feel indistinguishable from your real teeth. If you’re missing a tooth in Decatur, GA, an implant consultation with Dr. Vibhakar is the best place to start.

What Is Implant Surgery Actually Like?

Implant surgery sounds intimidating, but most patients are surprised by how manageable the experience is. We perform the procedure under local anesthesia in our Decatur office, and our team works to keep you as relaxed and comfortable as possible throughout — blankets, headphones, and clear step-by-step communication before we begin. Nitrous oxide is available for patients who need additional support.

How Does Dr. Vibhakar Determine Whether You’re a Good Implant Candidate?

Dr. Vibhakar evaluates bone density, gum health, and overall medical history before recommending implant treatment. If bone grafting is needed beforehand, he performs that in-house as well. If your situation calls for a different approach, we’ll explain why and walk you through alternatives so you can make the decision that’s right for you.

How Does the Dental Implant Process Work, Step by Step?

The implant process typically unfolds over several months. It begins with a comprehensive evaluation — including digital X-rays and CBCT imaging — to assess your bone and plan placement precisely. During the surgical appointment, the implant post (a small titanium fixture) is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Over the following months, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration. Once that healing is complete, a custom crown is attached to the implant to restore the visible portion of the tooth.

For appropriate candidates, same-day implant placement may be possible — where the implant is placed immediately following an extraction rather than waiting for the site to heal first. Dr. Vibhakar will assess whether this approach is suitable during your consultation and explain the factors that determine candidacy.

It’s worth noting that while Dr. Vibhakar performs single implants and implant-supported restorations with confidence, full arch procedures such as All-on-8 or All-on-10 are not currently offered at our practice. If your case requires that level of treatment, we’ll provide an honest referral to a provider equipped to handle it.

Most adults in good general health with sufficient jawbone density are strong candidates for dental implants, though a thorough evaluation is the best way to determine if they are right for you. Our team will review your oral health, bone structure, and medical history to find the best path forward for your smile.

The dental implant process typically takes several months from start to finish, as time is needed for the implant to properly fuse with the jawbone before the final restoration is placed. Every patient’s timeline is different, and our team will walk you through a personalized treatment plan at your consultation.

With proper care and routine dental visits, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, making them one of the most durable and long-term tooth replacement options available.
