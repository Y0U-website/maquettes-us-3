How Dental Implants Can Restore Your Smile, Function, and Confidence

Missing teeth complicate daily life. Eating your favorite Nashville BBQ. Speaking up at work. Smiling without thinking about it. Dental implants fix that by replacing both the root and the crown. Unlike dentures that can shift, or bridges that require grinding down healthy teeth, implants stand alone and function like natural teeth.

We handle implant consultations, 3D imaging, and final crown restorations. The surgery itself is performed by board-certified oral surgeons we trust. This partnership means you get diagnostic precision from us and surgical expertise from specialists. We guide you from candidacy evaluation through surgical healing to your finished crown.

Implants have a 95 %+ success rate—the highest of any tooth replacement. The titanium post doesn’t decay and doesn’t need replacing. Crowns last 10-15 years, often longer. Implants also stimulate your jawbone the way natural roots do, preventing the bone loss that makes faces look prematurely aged. Many Tennessee insurance plans cover 40-80 %, and we offer interest-free financing for qualifying patients.

What to Expect: From Consultation to Restored Smile

Before Your Appointment

  • • Schedule your free consultation at Nations Dental Studio
  • • Bring a list of current medications and any medical conditions (especially diabetes, heart disease, or autoimmune conditions)
  • • Arrange to quit smoking at least 2 weeks before surgery—smoking significantly increases complications and implant failure
  • • Plan for a driver or rideshare after the surgical appointment if your oral surgeon uses IV sedation

During Your Visit

Initial Consultation & Comprehensive Evaluation

We ll discuss your goals, health history, and any concerns about tooth loss. A detailed exam and 3D scan (CBCT imaging) assess your jawbone density, tooth position, and sinus location. You ll see exactly where your implant will go and what your restored smile will look like. This visit typically takes 45-60 minutes.

Treatment Planning & Specialist Referral

Based on your evaluation, we create a personalized treatment plan and refer you to our trusted oral surgeon partners in Nashville. We provide all imaging and recommendations so the surgeon has complete information. We discuss timeline, costs, insurance coverage, and financing options with you.

Surgical Placement with Board-Certified Oral Surgeon

Your oral surgeon performs the implant surgery (typically 1-2 hours per implant). You ll receive local anesthesia, and your surgeon can walk you through their sedation options (including IV sedation if you want to be unaware during the procedure). The titanium post is placed into your jawbone. A temporary restoration or denture may be provided to maintain your appearance while you heal. Post-op instructions ensure smooth recovery.

Healing & Osseointegration (3-6 Months)

The most important phase: your bone gradually bonds with the titanium implant in a process called osseointegration. During this time, you ll have follow-up appointments with your surgeon to monitor healing. We may need to adjust your temporary restoration for comfort.

Abutment Connection & Impression

Once integration is complete, your surgeon confirms the implant is secure and connects the abutment (connector piece). We then take precise impressions of your implant to design your custom crown.

Custom Crown Placement & Final Restoration

We design and fabricate your crown to perfectly match your bite, adjacent teeth, and smile aesthetics. At your appointment, we cement the crown onto your implant. You ll bite and smile naturally—no one will know it s an implant. This typically takes 1-2 hours.

After Treatment

  • • Soft foods for 1 week following surgery (yogurt, smoothies, mashed potatoes, soup)
  • • Ice application for 20 minutes on, 20 minutes off for the first 24-48 hours to reduce swelling
  • • Prescribed antibiotics must be taken as directed to prevent infection
  • • Mild pain is normal and managed with over-the-counter pain relief (ibuprofen or acetaminophen)
  • • Sleep with your head elevated on 2-3 pillows for the first week
  • • Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for 3-5 days after surgery
  • • Gentle rinsing with warm salt water starting 24 hours after surgery (do not disturb the surgical site)
  • • Return to normal oral hygiene after 1 week, brushing and flossing gently around the implant
  • • Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments with your surgeon and Nations Dental Studio for monitoring
  • • Once your crown is placed, care for it like a natural tooth: brush twice daily, floss once daily, and attend regular checkups every 6 months

Good Candidates for Dental Implants in Nashville

You may be a good candidate if:

  • ✓ Adults (18+) with one or more missing teeth and otherwise healthy gums and jawbone
  • ✓ Non-smokers or people willing to quit smoking before and after surgery; smoking dramatically increases failure risk
  • ✓ Those with controlled health conditions like diabetes, heart disease, or high blood pressure (well-managed conditions are usually fine)
  • ✓ Individuals with sufficient jawbone density to support an implant, or those willing to undergo bone grafting to build up bone
  • ✓ People committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups (implants require the same care as natural teeth)
  • ✓ Nashville professionals and active adults seeking a permanent, stable solution they won t have to remove or adjust

Other options may be better if:

  • • Severe bone loss might require bone grafting first, which adds time and cost but makes implants possible
  • • Heavy smokers or those unwilling to quit face much higher failure and complication rates; bridges or dentures may be safer alternatives
  • • Patients with uncontrolled diabetes or blood clotting disorders should discuss risks with their physician first
  • • Those on certain medications (like bisphosphonates) should discuss with both their physician and oral surgeon before proceeding
  • • Very limited budget might make dentures or bridges a more immediate option; financing can help make implants accessible
