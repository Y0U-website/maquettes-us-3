Whitening

Veneers

These thin shells are custom-made and bonded to the front surface of teeth, enhancing their appearance by correcting imperfections such as chips, gaps, or discoloration. They’re typically made from porcelain or composite resin, and provide a natural-looking solution for a more uniform and beautiful smile while maintaining the integrity of the underlying tooth.

Bonding

This cosmetic procedure applies a tooth-colored composite resin to repair, enhance, and overall improve the appearance of teeth, addressing issues like chips, gaps, or discoloration. Similar to veneers, this efficient treatment can improve your smile’s aesthetic and strengthen your teeth.

Inlays & Onlays

Dental inlays and onlays are used to restore teeth with moderate decay or damage that don’t need a crown, offering a resistant alternative to traditional fillings. Inlays fit within the grooves of teeth, while onlays extend over the tooth’s biting surface. Both can be more expensive than fillings, but can last a lifetime and withstand damage.
