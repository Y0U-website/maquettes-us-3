Custom Orthodontic Appliances We use advanced digital design and work with specialized orthodontic labs to create custom appliances tailored specifically to your smile and goals. Patient benefits: Precision fit improves comfort, efficiency, and overall results.
  • Customized designs including expanders, space maintainers, MARPE appliances, and retainers.
  • Digital planning allows for accurate fabrication and predictable outcomes.
  • Lab-crafted quality ensures durable, high-quality appliances designed specifically for you.

iTero Digital Scanner Technology

The iTero digital scanner creates precise 3D images of your teeth without messy traditional impressions making the experience faster, more comfortable, and more accurate.

  • Comfortable, mess-free scanning with no putty or gagging.
  • Highly accurate digital models for planning and appliance fit.
  • Real-time visualization lets you see your smile and progress instantly.
  • Improved efficiency helps streamline appointments and overall care.

Smile Simulator

The Align Smile Simulator allows us to show you a preview of your potential smile before treatment even begins helping you visualize what’s possible.

  • See a projected smile outcome early in the process.
  • Better understanding of planned tooth movement.
  • More confidence when discussing treatment options.
  • Clear communication so expectations and goals are aligned from the start.

Cone Beam Imaging (CBCT)

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) allows us to see your teeth, roots, jaw, and airway in three dimensions giving us a more complete picture than traditional imaging.

Better results for complex cases including impacted teeth, surgical planning, and airway evaluation when needed.

  • Advanced 3D visualization helps us evaluate tooth position, jaw relationships, and bone structure with exceptional clarity.
  • Enhanced planning allows for more predictable orthodontic outcomes.
  • Safer decisions by clearly identifying root positions, impacted teeth, possible pathologies, and surrounding anatomy.

Dental Monitoring

Dental Monitoring allows us to track your orthodontic progress remotely using a secure smartphone app helping our team monitor tooth movement between visits.

  • Quick at-home scans allow you to send updates directly to our office.
  • Closer monitoring helps ensure teeth are moving as planned.
  • Faster feedback allows us to guide your treatment and address concerns sooner.
  • Fewer unnecessary office visits while maintaining consistent supervision.

Laser Dentistry

Our soft-tissue laser allows us to precisely reshape and manage gum tissue during orthodontic care improving esthetics and efficiency with minimal discomfort.

  • Enhances smile esthetics through gentle gingival contouring and smile shaping.
  • Helps uncover teeth such as impacted canines to support movement.
  • Treats excess tissue that can interfere with eruption or appliance fit.
  • Minimally invasive treatment with faster healing, less bleeding, and improved comfort.

Digital X-Rays

At Mischler Orthodontics, digital x-rays help us diagnose, plan, and monitor your orthodontic care with exceptional accuracy while keeping your comfort and efficiency in mind.

  • Fast, efficient imaging means less time waiting and more time focused on your care.
  • Highly detailed images allow us to evaluate tooth position, jaw growth, and root health for accurate planning.
  • Seamless digital records let us track progress and securely share information with your dentist or specialists when needed.
  • Lower radiation and eco-friendly technology reduces exposure compared to traditional film x-rays while eliminating chemicals and film waste safer for patients and better for the environment.

Teeth Whitening

We offer professional whitening kits designed to safely and effectively brighten your smile often as the perfect finishing touch after orthodontic treatment.
