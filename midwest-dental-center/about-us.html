About Us

Best Dental Clinic That You Can Trust

We’ve been a part of the Toledo community for over 25 years, helping smiles shine brighter every day. With three welcoming offices spread across the city, our team of dedicated dentists is here to provide personalized care for you and your loved ones, whether it’s cosmetic touch-ups, restorative work, oral surgery, teeth replacements or routine check-ups for all ages.

Meet The Dentist

Dr. Taiwo Ngo was born and attended dental school in Lagos, Nigeria, one of the best dental programs in the world. She has practiced dentistry for over 32 years and has attended numerous post graduate courses. Dr. Ngo is married to Gift Ngo and has five children, two boys and three girls, who keep her busy when she’s not in the office.
