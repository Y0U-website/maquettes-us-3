Support Team

At Hart Dental Hygiene, we understand that the key to a successful practice lies not only in delivering excellent patient care but also in providing exceptional support to our team of hygienists. Our support team plays an essential role in ensuring that each hygienist can focus on what they do best, providing outstanding care while we handle the operational tasks that make the practice run smoothly.

Our dedicated support team is comprised of skilled professionals in administrative roles, billing, scheduling, marketing, and patient relations. From ensuring seamless appointment scheduling to managing insurance verifications and claims, they work tirelessly behind the scenes to make the practice experience as efficient and hassle-free as possible.

We believe that empowering our hygienists with the tools, resources, and guidance they need to thrive is at the heart of our mission. Our practices are Built by Hygienists. Backed by Hart.
