Meet the Team at Gordon Dental Associates

If you’re looking for a new dentist in St. Augustine to provide all of your family’s dental needs under one roof, you’ve come to the right place!

Dr. Jeremy Gordon and our team at Gordon Dental Associates believe that great dental care should be accessible to everyone, and we strive to uphold that commitment by investing in advanced technology and practicing personalized, compassionate care. You are not just another patient to us, you are family!

If you would like to learn more about our friendly team, we invite you to read through a few of the biographies below.

Dr. Jeremy Gordon, DMD

Dr. Gordon completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Florida in Gainesville, graduating with a degree in psychology. He then continued on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Florida College of Dentistry .

In 2003, Dr. Gordon returned to St. Augustine to practice, enjoying giving back to his community. In the last several years, Dr. Gordon has done hundreds of hours of continuing education, including cosmetics, implants, and preventive care. He is actively pursuing Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry, which requires three years of rigorous study and completion of a comprehensive exam.

In addition to his passion for continuing education and training in the field of dentistry, Dr. Gordon enjoys surfing, yoga, and spending time with his wife, Emily, and their two daughters, Meya and Nani.

Dr. Sarah Hutson, DMD

Dr. Sarah Hutson attended the University of North Florida, where she received her Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology, graduating Magna Cum Laude. She then attended Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine, where she graduated with her Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree.

As a native North Floridian, Dr. Hutson is grateful to have had the opportunity to return to the beautiful city of St. Augustine and be able to provide quality dental care to this wonderful community. Dr. Hutson believes that the comfort and trust of her patients are of utmost importance, and therefore strives to hold each patient at the highest level. She pursued her dream of helping others and is excited to fulfill this passion through dentistry.

On a personal note, Dr. Hutson enjoys the beach, shopping, cooking, and especially enjoys spending time with her family, including her husband, Jordon, and children, Jane, Grant, Leah and Noah.

Dr. Hannah Jones, DMD

Dr. Hannah Jones attended the University of Florida where she received her Bachelor of Science in Microbiology and Cell Science. She then continued her education at the University of Florida College of Dentistry to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. Following graduation, she completed a General Practice Residency at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

Dr. Jones is originally from the panhandle of Florida where she was born and raised. She is happy to have planted roots in Jacksonville and looks forward to serving the St. Augustine community! Her goal is to always make her patients feel understood and taken care of.

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Jones loves going to the beach, walking her boxer (Stevie), reading, traveling, going to concerts and spending time with her friends and family.
