Digital X-Rays

Digital X-Rays provide an in-depth look beneath the surface, identifying hidden issues before they escalate. Using minimal radiation, they offer a safe and detailed insight into your oral health.

  • Detect potential oral issues early.
  • Offers detailed and high-resolution imagery.
  • Ensures quicker diagnosis with less radiation exposure.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments strengthen the enamel and make teeth more resistant to decay. Especially beneficial for both children and adults, these treatments can prevent cavities and even reverse early stages of tooth decay.

  • Enhances tooth resistance against decay.
  • Repairs minor tooth erosions.
  • Supports overall tooth strength and health.
  • Reduces sensitivity, especially in recession areas.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants act as protective shields for the grooves and fissures in molars, preventing food particles and bacteria from settling and causing decay.

  • Provides an extra layer of protection against cavities.
  • Long-lasting and non-invasive.
  • Ideal for children and adults prone to decay.

SDF

Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) is an effective treatment to halt cavities in their tracks. It not only stops decay but also prevents it from progressing.

  • Offers immediate relief from tooth sensitivity.
  • Halts the progression of cavities.
  • Quick, non-invasive, and painless application.

Periodontal Care

For patients with gum issues, our periodontal care targets the root cause, ensuring the gums are healthy and free from infection.

  • Reduces the risk of gum diseases like gingivitis and periodontitis.
  • Halts gum recession and bone loss.
  • Promotes overall oral health.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Early detection is vital in treating oral cancer. Our screenings are thorough, aimed at detecting any anomalies, ensuring timely intervention if needed.

  • Early detection can be life-saving.
  • Quick and painless examination.
  • Provides peace of mind.

Night Guards

Night guards are essential for those who grind or clench their teeth at night, preventing wear and tear and relieving jaw strain.

  • Protects teeth from grinding and clenching.
  • Relieves jaw pain and tension.
  • Prevents long-term dental issues.

Sports Guards

Custom-made sports guards offer protection during physical activities, guarding the teeth and mouth from potential injuries.

  • Shields teeth during contact sports.
  • Offers a comfortable fit and optimal protection.
  • Reduces the risk of dental injuries.
