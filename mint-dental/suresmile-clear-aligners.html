Frequently Asked Questions About SureSmile Clear Aligners

What are SureSmile Clear Aligners?

Dr. Kevin Pulsipher of Mint Dental describes SureSmile Clear Aligners as a series of custom-made transparent orthodontic devices designed to adjust and align your teeth over time. They offer a less noticeable alternative to traditional braces, allowing patients to feel confident while undergoing treatment for a straighter smile!

Who is a suitable candidate for SureSmile?

While the suitability of SureSmile Clear Aligners can only be determined by a dental professional, they are generally recommended for individuals with mild to moderate crowding or spacing issues. They are typically not recommended for severe orthodontic issues.

How long does the treatment with SureSmile Clear Aligners take?

Treatment time with SureSmile Clear Aligners varies from case to case. However, the average treatment time is usually 6-15 months, depending upon the complexity of the case and patient compliance.

Can I eat and drink with my SureSmile Clear Aligners in?

It is recommended that you remove your SureSmile Clear Aligners while eating or drinking anything other than water. This is to prevent damage to the aligners and keep them clean.

Are SureSmile Clear Aligners comfortable to wear?

SureSmile Clear Aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth, so they are generally comfortable to wear. But do note that some discomfort might be experienced initially as your teeth start to shift when a new set of aligners is used.

What is the first step?

We will take a few photos and a digital scan of your teeth to put together a customized treatment plan for you.

Will my insurance pay for the clear aligner orthodontics?

Dental insurance will often cover a portion of the cost, but it depends on the specifics of each insurance plan.

Do I have to wear retainers after completion of clear aligner orthodontics?

Yes. It is necessary to wear retainers so that the teeth will not move back to their original position.
