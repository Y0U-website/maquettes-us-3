The practice

0 Who We Are Thank you for your interest in our dental laboratory. We are a full-service dental laboratory, specializing in removable prosthetics, implant bars, attachments, sleep apnea devices, night guards, relines, and repairs.

MDL began in 1974 by Bob Messersmith, and his legacy is continued by Eric Baydian, owner and technician, for the past 36 years. Over the past 48 years, our team has worked diligently to stay modernized with innovative technology. The expectations of removable appliances have vastly increased, and we take pride in our ability to meet and exceed those needs.

By using the highest quality materials, latest processes and equipment available, we craft dental appliances that cater to each patient. Our goal is to satisfy you as the dentist to ensure an aesthetic removable prosthetic that is the most natural and comfortable.

We strive for an accurate fit to ensure minimal chair side adjustments post-delivery. It is our philosophy to have attentive precision and craftsmanship combined with genuine customer service to provide an excellent experience for the whole dental office.

As a service to our accounts, we offer rush repairs on cases in a timely manner.
