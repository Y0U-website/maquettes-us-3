ICON White Spot Lesion Removal

White Spot Lesions (WSLs) are little white spots that form on the surface of teeth and, if they aren&#x27;t properly treated, can result in demineralization. They are a common issue that patients often notice after completing orthodontic treatment. They may also be a result of an enamel defect, infection, or direct trauma to the teeth.

To reduce the appearance of white spots, we offer Icon resin infiltration treatment, a minimally invasive procedure that can remineralize the de-calcified area while evening out the shade of your affected teeth.
