0 Meet Our Dental Dream Team Our amazing team at Mandarin South Dentistry is the heart and soul of our Mandarin dental practice. We’re a bunch of dedicated individuals who are passionate about dental health and making your visits comfortable (and maybe even a little fun!). From the moment you walk in, you’ll be greeted with a smile and the support you need for a positive dental care experience. Get to know the friendly faces who help us deliver quality dentistry with a personal touch, right here in Jacksonville!

Marina

Marina has worked as a dental assistant at Mandarin South Dentistry for over 20 years. Her family moved to Jacksonville from Russia more than 30 years ago. She loves spending time with her friends and family, she enjoys reading and going for long walks on the beach.

Angelita “Lita”

Lita moved to Jacksonville in 2002. She’s been a hygienist for 20 years. What she enjoys most about her job is helping her patients maintain their healthy bright smiles for life. She enjoys outdoor activities and has a love of gardening. She is happily married for 14 years with a beautiful daughter and a dog.
