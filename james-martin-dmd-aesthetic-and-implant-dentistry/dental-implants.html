What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent fixtures that are placed in your jawbone. They have a small ball on the top, called an abutment. We attach a crown, called a restoration, on that abutment. The crown is perfectly fitted to your smile so you will have a permanent and comfortable solution!

We utilize every tool we have to give you a satisfactory smile. Each type of implant is used in different sections of the mouth and for different concerns. If you need more than one tooth replaced, we will mix and match the kinds of implants to create a perfect smile for you.

LIVING WITH MISSING TEETH

Missing teeth can make you feel self-conscious about how you look and can cause serious pain and toothaches. Missing teeth can affect the health of your entire body. We want you to have a high quality of life, free of embarrassment or sickness, so we focus on restorative dentistry, such as dental implants.

Dental implants are one of the best ways to restore your oral health because they stabilize your teeth and jawbone.

BONE LOSS

Jaw bone density can decrease over time in a specific area of the jaw if there is no tooth root to stimulate the nerves. This can cause several issues, like jaw aches or even widespread tooth loss.

Implants are an essential part of keeping your jaw healthy after tooth loss. Your jawbone will grow around the implant, helping the bone remain strong.

During our initial consultation, we will be sure to evaluate the health of your jawbone. If you are suffering from a decrease in jaw bone density, we have bone grafting and implant options to restore your teeth and jaw.

CREATING A SECURE SMILE

Implants work naturally with your body to create a secure place for restorations. Because implants are so secure and permanent, they replicate natural teeth very closely. You will be able to eat and chew regularly after receiving your restorations.

Our crown restorations look just like natural teeth. They are tooth-colored and stain-resistant. As long as you care for your restorations properly, they can last a lifetime!
