Prevent gum disease and maintain a healthy smile with our expert prophylaxis services. We use gentle cleaning techniques to remove plaque and tartar buildup, reducing the risk of oral health issues. At our practice, we are dedicated to providing our clients with the highest level of care and attention, so schedule your appointment today and experience the difference!

A periodontal maintenance cleaning is performed after a patient has undergone non surgical periodontal therapy (deep cleaning). Periodontal Maintenance is needed to maintain gum and bone health. This procedure includes removal of plaque and tartar from above and below the gumline.

X-rays are essential at helping us see things that aren t visible to the naked eye, like cavities in between the teeth and bone loss around the teeth. Dental radiographs may also reveal abscesses or cysts, cancerous and non-cancerous tumors, problems inside the tooth or below the gumline. X-rays are recommended based on your own personal needs.

Non-surgical periodontal therapy is an effective treatment for gum disease and the build-up of tartar below the gum line. As a dental hygienist, I m trained to provide this treatment safely and comfortably. With this procedure, we can remove harmful bacteria and debris, leaving your teeth and gums healthy and clean.

Fluoride varnish is a simple and effective way to prevent cavities and protect your teeth against decay. With regular application, you can improve your oral health and avoid more serious dental problems down the line. Our fluoride varnish treatment is specifically designed to prevent cavities and sensitivity, keeping your teeth healthier for longer.
