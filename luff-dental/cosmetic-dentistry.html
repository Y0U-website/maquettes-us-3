Porcelain Veneers

Do you have crooked, chipped, discolored, or misshapen teeth? Dental veneers can help you achieve a desirable cosmetic outcome. Dental veneers are wafer-thin shells crafted from high-strength porcelain. The material is tinted to match the surrounding teeth and then cemented onto the front of the teeth. Porcelain veneers can be used to alter the shape, size, shade, and length of the teeth.

Orthodontics

Many times, achieving your dream smile requires moving teeth into the right position. Even if the end goal is veneers on your front teeth, proper alignment of the teeth with braces or clear aligners will ensure a better outcome for the restorative work. At Luff Dental, we want to make sure everything is done carefully and strategically.

Inlays and Onlays

Sometimes referred to as “indirect fillings,” inlays and onlays are dental restorations fabricated from porcelain, composite materials, or even gold. The difference between an onlay and inlay is how the restoration is placed. When the material is bonded inside the tooth (similar to a traditional filling) it is known as an “inlay.” When the material is primarily used on the exterior or biting surface of the tooth, it is referred to as an “onlay.”
