Permanent & Removable Retainers

Once your orthodontic treatment is complete, there’s one more very important step that needs to be completed so that you can keep your teeth perfectly straight. Our team will take impressions of your teeth after your braces are removed or you are done wearing clear aligners to create a custom retainer that will help retain the shape of your new smile.

A permanent retainer can be placed near the gumline behind the front teeth to keep the teeth from moving. These retainers can’t be seen by others, can’t be removed except by an orthodontist, and don’t require anything of the patient except an extra thorough oral care routine.

We also offer clear removable retainers, which are only worn at night. They are similar to Invisalign clear aligners, as they are comfortable to wear, easy to clean, and less noticeable to others. Patients must commit to wearing these aligners regularly, otherwise, the teeth will begin to shift back to their original positions.
