Dr. Cecelia Parker was raised near Cleveland OH and graduated from Berkshire High School. She continued her education at Kent State University where she majored in Biology. She then went to Columbus to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University.

During her time in Columbus, Dr. Parker participated in the Oral Health Improvement through Outreach (OHIO) Project, where she had the opportunity to provide dental services to patients in underserved areas all throughout the state.

Dr. Parker is passionate about providing the best, individualized care to every patient. She enjoys working with patients of all ages and strives to make a lasting impact on her patients through oral health education and positive experiences at each visit.

In her free time, Dr. Parker enjoys spending time with friends, family, and dogs. Her interests include reading, trying new foods, playing volleyball and traveling the world.
