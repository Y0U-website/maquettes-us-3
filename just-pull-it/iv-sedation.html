A Two-Visit Process

Sedation cannot be completed on the first visit. Here&#x27;s how it works.

$49 Consult (D0330 + D0140)

Includes a panoramic X-ray (D0330) and limited evaluation (D0140). We&#x27;ll confirm IV conscious sedation is a safe option for you, review your health history, and schedule your sedation appointment.

IV Conscious Sedation Appointment · +$399

IV conscious sedation is an add-on of $399 in addition to extraction fees ($299 routine D7140 / $399 wisdom D7210). Scheduled in advance — never same-day. You&#x27;ll need a responsible adult driver who stays in the office for the entire visit.

Before Your Sedation Visit

Follow these instructions exactly. Failure to follow them may result in cancellation of your procedure.

  • No food or drink (including water) for 6–8 hours before your visit.
  • Avoid alcohol, marijuana, or narcotics for at least 48 hours prior.
  • Take prescribed medications only as directed by your doctor.
  • Bring a responsible adult driver who stays in the office the whole appointment.
  • If you&#x27;re pregnant or may be, sedation cannot be performed for safety reasons.
  • IV sedation is available only to patients 16 years and older.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

If IV sedation isn&#x27;t right for you, we also offer local anesthesia only. Our office does not provide oral or general anesthesia but can coordinate a referral if needed.

  • • All sedation and extraction fees are nonrefundable if canceled or broken within 48 hours.
  • • You may reschedule once within 30 days of your original date.
  • • After 30 days, your deposit is forfeited and a new payment is required to rebook.
