What to Expect Before, During, and After Treatment

The implant restoration process typically takes place over several months, though your timeline will depend on your individual needs. During your initial consultation, we will take digital X-rays, review your medical and dental history, and discuss your goals. If bone grafting is needed to support the implant, that step comes first.

Once the implant has integrated with the bone, we will design and place your custom restoration. Our team takes precise impressions and uses high-quality materials to craft a crown or prosthetic that blends seamlessly with your smile. After placement, we will schedule follow-up visits to make sure everything is healing and functioning well.

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Restoration

How long does implant restoration take?

The full process, from implant placement to final restoration, typically takes three to six months, depending on healing time and whether preparatory procedures are needed.

Is implant restoration covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. We are happy to help you verify your benefits before treatment. Call our office, and our team will review your options with you.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the process is. We use local anesthesia during placement, and any post-procedure soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.
