Dental Sleep Medicine Do you snore? Have you been diagnosed with sleep apnea? Are you struggling with using a CPAP machine at night? We may be able to help. Dr. Gannon has received extensive training in the treatment of obstructive sleep apnea with FDA approved oral appliances. We work closely with local sleep medicine physicians.

Dr. Gannon is originally from Seattle. She spent her first two years of college at Loyola Marymount University in Los Angeles. She then transferred to the University of Washington in Seattle where she graduated with a degree in Business Administration in 1994.

Upon graduation, she began her dental education at the University of Washington - School of Dentistry. In 1995, Dr. Gannon was awarded the Health Profession’s Scholarship by the U.S. Navy. In 1998, Dr. Gannon graduated from dental school earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Upon Graduation, Dr. Gannon entered naval service as a Lieutenant in the Navy Dental Corps.
