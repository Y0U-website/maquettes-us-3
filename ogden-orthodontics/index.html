The practice

Our office sits on North Donnelly Street, about a mile north of downtown Mount Dora, close to the schools rather than out on the highway. You will see the same doctor at every visit, at whichever of our three offices fits your week, because Dr. Ogden is the only orthodontist here and intends to keep it that way.

Dr. Ogden grew up in St. Clair Shores, Michigan, where a good experience with his own family orthodontist pointed him toward the profession. He earned his DDS from Case Western Reserve University in 1991, then completed the three-year orthodontic residency at the University of Florida College of Dentistry, finishing in 1994.
