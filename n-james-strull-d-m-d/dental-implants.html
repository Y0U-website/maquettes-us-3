WHAT ARE MINI IMPLANTS?

Mini Dental Implants work in the same way as traditional dental implants, but there are a few important distinctions that make mini implants a great choice for many patients.

The main difference between a mini dental implant and a traditional dental implant is the size of the implant. Mini implants are about half the size of conventional implants. Because mini implants are so much smaller, they can work for patients who aren’t candidates for a traditional dental implant.

For patients unable to undergo more invasive surgery or multiple procedures, mini implants may be a great option to help with an ill-fitting dentures.

The surgeons at Strull Oral Surgery will look at your complete health and dental history and recommend the best implant type for your needs.

CARING FOR DENTAL IMPLANTS

HOW DO I CARE FOR DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Great news! Caring for your dental implants is the same as taking care of the teeth you were born with. Brushing, flossing, and regular dental cleanings will keep your dental implants in top condition. Although the implant will never decay, surrounding gum tissues can become inflamed or infected without good oral hygiene.
