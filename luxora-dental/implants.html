What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. They provide a strong foundation for replacement teeth such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Once integrated with the bone, implants offer stability and durability similar to natural teeth. This makes them an ideal long-term solution for restoring both function and aesthetics.
