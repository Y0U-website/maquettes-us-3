Our Team

At Harvey Family Dental, you will find a team made up of compassionate, caring and friendly professionals.

Sueheitie

Sueheitie is our office manager, handling all patient and financial aspects. With 20+ years of experience in the dental field, she does a great job at overseeing the practice. She enjoys meeting new people and finding ways to connect with them.

Sueheitie loves practicing yoga, hiking and all things nature. She also keeps busy with her 3 children, husband and new puppies, Cognac and Chuleta.

Carrissa

Carrissa is our lead assistant with over 7 years of experience. She has recently transitioned to a more administrative role and is looking forward to putting her organizational and leadership skills to use. She enjoys working in the dental field because it’s the happy side of health care!

Carrissa enjoys spending time with her daughters, traveling and trying new foods and restaurants!

Kim

Kim is our dental hygienist. She earned her Bachelor’s Degree in Dental Hygiene from MCPHS University. Kim loves the rewarding nature of her work, especially the ability to contribute to patient’s overall wellbeing and helping them maintain healthy, confident smiles.

In her free time, Kim enjoys going to concerts and comedy shows. She also enjoys spending time with her friends, family and her two cats.

Alyssa

Alyssa is our assistant who is also a full-time student. She earned her Bachelors of Science in Biology and is currently earning her Masters and has plans to attend dental school. She is passionate about forming connections with patients and continuing to expand her skills in dentistry.

Alyssa enjoys going to the gym, playing with her dog and spending quality time with her family.
