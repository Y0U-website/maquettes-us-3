Frequently Asked Questions

The discomfort associated with dental implants varies depending on the type of implant used. Traditional dental implants require a more invasive procedure, which can lead to more post-operative discomfort. However, this is typically managed with pain medication and subsides within a few days.

On the other hand, mini dental implants use a minimally invasive technique that significantly reduces discomfort and facilitates a quicker recovery. Many patients find mini implants to be more comfortable both during and after the procedure, making them an appealing option for those concerned about pain.

Candidates for implant dentures need sufficient jawbone density to support the implants. Traditional dental implants require more bone mass, making them ideal for patients with a strong, healthy jawbone. However, mini dental implants offer an excellent option for those with less bone density, as their smaller size allows for secure placement with less bone.
