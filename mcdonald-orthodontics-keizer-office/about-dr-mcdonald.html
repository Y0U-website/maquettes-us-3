About the McDonalds

Dr. McDonald and his wife, Meredith, have been married for over two decades. Their children, Claire and Isaac, participated in the Spanish immersion program in the West Salem public schools before heading east for college. Dr. John and his family enjoy the outdoors, theater, and traveling together. Dr. John also regularly plays ice hockey at the Sherwood Ice Arena in several of their adult leagues (It is no-contact adult ice hockey, not quite like the NHL!)

Growing Up in Salem

Education and Experience

After graduating dental school, Dr. John joined the Army. He was stationed in Ft. Campbell, KY, where he underwent extensive advanced training in the general dentistry residency program. Dr John then went on to serve for a year in Korea before being stationed in Heilbronn, Germany, where he managed a large army dental clinic.

Following his service, Dr. John returned to Oregon and practiced general dentistry in Salem and Portland before being accepted to attending the orthodontic program at the University of Pennsylvania. It was there he met his wife, Meredith, and together they returned to Salem for Dr. John to join his father Terry at McDonald Orthodontics . Dr. John and Dr Terry practiced side by side until 2008, when Dr. Terry retired.

Joining my father in practice was a proud moment for both of us. For me, I was able to tap into his vast experience and accelerate learning and understanding of the complexities of orthodontic treatment. The mentorship I received from him continues to this day, as he still teaches the orthodontic program at OHSU and we talk (shop) on a regular basis.

DR. JOHN MCDONALD

A Long Legacy of Smiles

Dr. John is a diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics . To achieve board certification, hundreds of hours of study, hands-on patient cases, and critical peer review are required. Less than 30 % of orthodontists nationwide have earned this distinction with only one other orthodontist in Salem/Keizer having achieved this status.

Dr John is excited by the advancements taking place in orthodontics today. He is committed to providing the best available treatment options to his patients. He is one of the few orthodontists in Oregon providing Bollard Plates www.lesschin.com as treatment for the nonsurgical correction of Class 3 patients (Sometimes called Underbites). He is also the only orthodontist in the Salem Keizer area providing Mini implant Assisted Rapid Palatal Expansion (MARPE also referred to as MSE) to provide nonsurgical palatal expansion to adults and older teens.
