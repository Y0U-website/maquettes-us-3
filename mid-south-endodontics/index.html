The practice

We are pleased to announce that we have taken our practice and the quality of patient care to the next level with our new state-of-the-art CS 8100 3D extraoral imaging system. Dr. Klecker and Dr. Lowery are dedicated to providing patients with accurate diagnoses and exceptional treatment and the true-to-life 3D images captured by the new radiography unit will help do just that.

Past member of the Massachusetts Association of Endodontists

Dr. John (Jack) Klecker grew up in Charlotte, NC and attended college at Furman University in Greenville, SC where he received his B.S. in Chemistry. While at Furman, Dr. Klecker was an accomplished NCAA Division I athlete in Cross Country and Track and Field and received recognition for his research in self-assembled monolayers.

Following Furman, Dr. Klecker pursued dental school and was accepted to the Harvard School of Dental Medicine. He was President of the Endodontics Journal Club and his research conducted in Munich, Germany earned him and his colleagues a publication in the Journal of Cranio-Maxillo-Facial Surgery.

Immediately following dental school, Dr. Klecker completed an additional two-year training program to complete his Certificate of Endodontics at Tufts University.

During residency, Dr. Klecker gained clinical experience working in private practice in the Greater Boston Area. Dr. Klecker is a board-eligible endodontist and plans to challenge the Boards in the coming year.

Dr. Klecker and his wife Julianne enjoy running, fishing, hiking with their dog Reagan and trying new restaurants and breweries with friends. Dr. Klecker has run 4 Boston Marathons, and in his free time he enjoys watching football and homebrewing beer.
