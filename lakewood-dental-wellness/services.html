Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most patients benefit from professional cleanings and exams every six months, although some may require more frequent visits based on their oral health needs.

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages and offer comprehensive preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental services.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Contact our office immediately. We’ll make every effort to see emergency patients as quickly as possible to relieve pain and prevent additional complications.
