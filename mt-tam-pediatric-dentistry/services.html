Prevention & Treatment

Thankfully modern dentistry allows us to do more than just “drill & fill.” We now have options and we base our decision to treat (or not to treat) on several factors. We want to see your child succeed, so we’ll make recommendations that will allow them to SHINE and feel proud of their success.

Additional Services

Our services and approach to dentistry covers all aspects of your child’s oral health. From emergency care, to new parents education, to providing a little something extra for those who need it, we’ve got you covered.

Designed to Keep You Safe

You can expect a variety of protocols and procedures to keep you and your family safe. We offer things like text check-ins, online forms, enhanced PPE, air filtration and most importantly, an informed, careful team with your well-being in mind.
