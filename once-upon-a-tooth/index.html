Welcome to Once Upon a Tooth

Our mission at Once Upon a Tooth is providing state-of-the-heart dental care for children of all ages and abilities.

The practice

As parents, we know it takes a village to raise a child, and we want to help you keep your kids healthy.

We believe in evidence-based, minimally invasive dentistry to help guide our recommendations and focus on prevention and education, as we know that a lifetime of healthy teeth begins with good dental habits. We strive to provide a nurturing, respectful, and compassionate environment to make it as fun and enjoyable as it can be, both for our patients and their parents.

Once Upon a Tooth takes pride in fostering a humanistic environment where each kid in our diverse community is valued, respected and empowered. Our pediatric dental office services Eastchester , Tuckahoe , Bronxville , Mt. Vernon , New Rochelle , Yonkers, and surrounding areas.

Despite their best efforts, children may still experience dental problems such as cavities, tooth decay, or chipped teeth from accidents. That’s why we offer a variety of restorative treatments to help children maintain their beautiful smiles.

We understand that children’s dental needs vary depending on their age. Infants, toddlers, pre-teens, and teenagers all have unique dental, developmental, and emotional needs. Our team of experienced pediatric dental professionals has the necessary training to provide the specialized care that each age group requires.

Infants & Toddlers School Age Teens For infants, we know that their teeth and gums are delicate, and we provide gentle care that is essential for their oral health, nutritional intake, and speech development. As toddlers begin to get a little more independent, we work with them to help them learn how to properly brush their teeth, creating a solid foundation for a lifetime of good oral health.

For older children and pre-teens, we understand this age group is undergoing many changes. Having a dentist in Eastchester they feel comfortable with is essential, both physically and emotionally.
