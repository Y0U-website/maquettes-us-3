About Us

Dr. Monica was born in Chicago, IL, moving to Wisconsin Dells, WI as a teenager. She spent the majority of her younger years in the tourism industry where the values of hard work and customer service were instilled in her.

After earning her DMD and MS degrees, Dr. Monica returned to the Badger state. She met her husband, Weston after moving back to Wisconsin and after a few years in Waukesha they decided to settle down in Sun Prairie. They welcomed their first child in November 2024 and are excited to build a life together and become more involved in their new community

Outside of the office Dr. Monica loves to travel, is a huge foodie, loves spending time with her family and enjoys gardening and improving her cooking skills.
