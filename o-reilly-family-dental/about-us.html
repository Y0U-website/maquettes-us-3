INITAL EXAM

Your initial exam is necessary to provide you with an accurate diagnosis of the condition of your mouth, teeth, and gums. We will advise you of our findings and answer any questions you may have related to your current state of oral health.

We believe that your dental health is dependent on sound teeth, healthy gums and supporting bone. Our goal is to eliminate as many problems as possible in these areas. We want you to enjoy the comfort of a healthy mouth. Based on your exam, your dentist will develop a personalized treatment plan and discuss options with you.

WILL I GET A CLEANING MY FIRIST VISIT?

A cleaning will only be completed after the doctor ensures it is the appropriate treatment for your specific oral health. If it’s been a few years since you’ve been to the dentist, a cleaning may not be the highest priority item for your mouth.

There are certain conditions that determine if a cleaning is not the first best course of treatment. Once your oral health is stabilized, you can expect to have your exam and cleaning/hygiene appointments at the same time going forward!
