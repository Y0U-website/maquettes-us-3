About Us - Hacienda Pediatric Dentistry Hacienda Heights, CA

Dr. Sheu’s philosophy in pediatric dentistry combines the latest techniques with the knowledge of a child’s psychological and emotional needs at each stage of life. She hopes that by providing a fun and positive dental experience, she can provide each child with a desire to incorporate dental health as an integral part of their lifestyle.

In her personal life, Dr. Sheu likes to give back to her community. She does so by volunteering with Give Kids a Smile Day and Clinic with a Heart. She has traveled to Central and South America to provide urgent dental care to the indigenous peoples of those countries. In her free time, Dr. Sheu likes to read, hike with her dog Shadow, cook, travel, and spend time with her husband Mark, and her two children Lauren and Liam.

“I think one of the most important things I can do as a pediatric dentist is to give children and their parents the life long tools for ongoing dental health. I wholeheartedly believe that this can be accomplished with knowledge and positive experiences. At my practice, we spend time teaching kids and their parents how to brush, floss, eat well, and take care of their oral health. I am always so proud that my patients follow through and stay cavity free and healthy!”
