The practice

To fully understand the health of your teeth and gums, our dentist needs x-rays. You can transfer current x-rays from your prior dentist or we can take x-rays for a fair fee. If you decide not to stick with us, we can send copies of x-rays from our office to your next dentist.

For most adult patients, the first appointment will include an exam and x-rays. Based on the health of your teeth and gums, we will schedule a return appointment for the appropriate dental cleaning. Many adults will just need a standard teeth cleaning. Others can benefit from extra care for their gums. We will figure this out and discuss with your options at your first visit.

We can often schedule a standard teeth cleaning at the first visit along with the new patient exam and necessary x-rays.

Simply call us to schedule an appointment. We will take it from there.

Our dental practice has been part of the Grand Rapids community for decades. The practice was originally located on Boston Street in Grand Rapids and owned by Dr. Cornelius Doezema. Dr. C Philip Doezema took over the practice from Dr. Cornelius Doezema in 1980. In 1997, Dr. C Philip Doezema moved the practice to 2003 Burton St SE.

In 2015, the practice adopted the name, Grand Rapids Dentistry in anticipation of Dr. Doezema’s retirement.

In early 2016, Dr. Stephanie Sakowicz joined the practice and Dr. Doezema retired.
