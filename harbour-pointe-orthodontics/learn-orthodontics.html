What are some of the orthodontic appliances used in growth guidance?

Orthodontic treatment may involve explanding appliance (expander), headgear, retainer, and a limited number of braces. We will use whatever is best to help shape and widen the dental arches. A larger more appropriately shaped dental arch can mean more room for adult teeth coming into the mouth, as well as a more natural smile.

What’s new in orthodontics?

Orthodontics has changed a lot in the past years! New technology has brought a lot of improvement in our treatment methods and for the comfort of our patients. Cosmetic advances are also being made in orthodontics making braces less visible and more comfortable.

Another new thing is invisible braces—Invisalign. They are clear plastic aligners made using sophisticated 3-D computer imaging technology. They are comfortable, removeable and invisible! Dr. de Leon is certified to treat patients with Invisalign, although patient selection is very important.

One of the latest technology that Dr. de Leon has introduced in her practice is the use of “Self-ligating brackets (braces)”. This system allows the doctor to treat patients with greater control and greater efficiency.

The technology that represents the latest advancements in orthodontic appliances that will soon be introduced in Dr. de Leon’s practice is the use of “Temporary Anchorage Devices”.
