MyoTech Advantage Dental Plan You Should Be In Control. The MyoTech Advantage Dental Plan. We encourage all of our patients, both current and new, to consider signing up for MyoTech Advantage Dental Plan. This plan offers you and your family dental benefits for preventive care and discounts for additional treatments.

Your cleanings, thorough exam, x-rays, and flouride treatment, twice per year as recommended for optimal oral hygiene, are included in this plan.

MyoTech Advantage has no monthly premiums, no deductibles, no benefit limitations, and enrollment is open year round. Your entire family can use MyoTech Advantage to save 20 % on most dental procedures.

MyoTech Advantage isn’t an insurance plan, so there’s no third party dictating what dental treatment you’re eligible to receive. Eliminating the restrictions of an insurance company gives us a more direct relationship with you, leaving you in control of your oral health.
