The practice

Our orthodontists are world renowned educators and scientists, inventors of the latest advances in orthodontics. They are the doctors and leaders shaping the future of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics

Our office is located in Hoboken, New Jersey, around the corner form the Path station, just minutes away from Manhattan and Jersey City.

We welcome you to visit us, and enjoy a sophisticated and calm atmosphere.

Dr. Cristina Teixeira and Dr. Mani Alikhani are world leaders in Orthodontics, Dentofacial Orthopedics and Bone Biology.

They have been recognized both nationally and internationally for their contributions to Orthodontics Translational Research, through their innovative treatment approaches and their patented technology that includes accelerated orthodontics tools like Propel and Vpro5 .

Teeth can be moved at any age. Orthodontic treatment is no longer just for children and teens. At Orthodontia Studio, we offer specialized orthodontics treatment for teens and adults.

Clear aligners are a very esthetic treatment option for patients that do not want to use braces but still wish to correct their bite and improve their smile.

Orthodontic treatment does not need to be very long anymore. Our doctors have developed an innovative treatment approach that reduces duration of orthodontic treatment by as much as 60 %.
