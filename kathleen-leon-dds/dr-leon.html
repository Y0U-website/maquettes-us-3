Dr. Kathleen Leon has spent the past seven years bringing bright smiles and gentle care to children and families. Dr. Leon’s caring nature extends beyond the dental chair. As a proud mom, she understands the joys (and challenges!) of parenthood, which helps her connect with families on a personal level.

In her free time, Dr. Leon enjoys running, CrossFit, and painting. She enjoys activities that keep her energized, creative, and true to her vibrant personality.
