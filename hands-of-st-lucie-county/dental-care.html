Benefits of Dental Care at HANDS Clinic

Regular dental visits do more than keep your smile bright — they protect your overall health. Oral infections can spread. Gum disease is linked to heart disease and diabetes. Our dental team addresses issues early, so small problems don&#x27;t turn into costly, painful emergencies.

Why HANDS Clinic

Since 2010, HANDS Clinic has been closing the gap in healthcare access for St. Lucie County residents. Our 700+ volunteer healthcare professionals — including licensed dentists — donate their expertise because they believe everyone in our community deserves quality care. We&#x27;re not a pop-up clinic or a one-time event. We&#x27;re a consistent, trustworthy medical home for over 1,600 patients.

Have a question?

  • Do I need insurance to be seen at HANDS Clinic? No. HANDS Clinic specifically serves adults who do not have insurance. You do not need to have — or get — insurance to receive care here. Our eligibility requirements are based on income and residency, not insurance status.
  • Do you offer dentures? Yes. Dentures are included in our dental services for eligible patients. Talk to your dental provider at HANDS Clinic to find out if dentures are the right option for your situation.
  • Do you offer services in Spanish? Yes. HANDS Clinic serves a bilingual community and has Spanish-speaking staff available. Key communications and patient-facing materials are available in both English and Spanish. If you have a language preference, let us know when you contact us.
  • How do I know if I qualify for care at HANDS Clinic? Eligibility is based on four factors: income level, St. Lucie County residency, age (19–64), and insurance status. The easiest way to find out is to fill out our eligibility form — it only takes a few minutes and will determine whether you qualify for care.
  • Is care at HANDS Clinic really free? Yes. Services at HANDS Clinic are provided at no cost to eligible patients. There are no copays, no fees, and no surprise bills. Most services are completely free; some specialty referrals may be at a discounted rate. Your care team will always be upfront with you about what&#x27;s included.
  • What if I have a dental emergency? Contact us as soon as possible. While we are not an emergency room, our dental team will work to get you seen and address urgent dental needs in a timely manner. Don&#x27;t wait — dental emergencies can worsen quickly without attention.
