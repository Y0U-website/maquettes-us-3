An orthodontic screening is a simple, non-invasive evaluation that helps us identify potential issues with how your child’s teeth and jaws are growing. These early screenings allow our team to detect concerns such as: ​ Crowding or spacing of teeth
  • Overbites, underbites, or crossbites
  • Jaw alignment problems
  • Bite issues related to thumb-sucking or tongue posture
  • Delays in tooth eruption or premature tooth loss

Why Early Screenings Matter

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children receive their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. That’s because around this age, children typically have a mix of baby and adult teeth, making it easier to spot and address developmental concerns while their bones are still growing.

At Once Upon a Tooth, Dr. Lis and our team provide orthodontic screenings as part of your child’s regular dental checkups. This means no extra appointments and no added stress just consistent, careful monitoring of your child’s smile over time.
