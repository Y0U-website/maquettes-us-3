DENISE K.

Dental Assistant

Denise is a highly skilled and dedicated dental assistant with nearly two decades of experience. She graduated from Trident Technical College’s Expanded Duty Dental Assisting program in 2006. She’s been a valued member of the One Degree Dental team since its inception.

As a DANB Certified Dental Assistant, Denise combines expertise with a warm, friendly approach, ensuring every patient feels comfortable and well cared for.

RACHAEL B.

Hygienist

Rachael is a dedicated dental hygienist with over a decade of experience in exceptional patient care. She earned her degree from Trident Technical College in 2012 and has since refined her skills in top-quality dental hygiene.

She joined One Degree Dental in 2018, first working with Dr. Huch, DMD, and seamlessly transitioning to Dr. "Rez" in 2023. Known for her gentle touch and attention to detail, Rachael is committed to helping patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

Dr. "REZ"

Vladislav Reznichenko, DMD

Dr. “Rez” graduated from the MUSC School of Dentistry in 2022 and has been a valued member of the One Degree Dental team ever since. Dedicated to providing top-quality care, he continuously enhances his skills through advanced training in Endodontics, Orthodontics, and Dental 3D printing. As a bilingual speaker of Russian and Ukrainian, Dr. “Rez” helps make One Degree Dental more accessible to the Goose Creek and Moncks Corner communities.

Outside of work, Dr. “Rez” enjoys quality time with his wife and their energetic dog. In his free time, you’ll often find him fishing at local ponds, tending to his vegetable garden, or visiting his hometown in Upstate South Carolina to hike his favorite trails and spend time with family.
