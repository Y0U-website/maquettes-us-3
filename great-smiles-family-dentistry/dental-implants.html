Unlike dentures or bridges, implants do not decay and help maintain your facial structure for years to come.

  • Prevent Bone Loss: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing deterioration.
  • Provide Longevity: With proper care, implants can last decades.
  • Look Natural: Implants blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.

Our Dental Implant Restoration Process

We make the restoration process simple and stress-free:

Step 1: Comprehensive Evaluation

We begin with a detailed exam and imaging to assess your implant(s) and surrounding teeth. During your consultation, we discuss your goals and create a customized plan for your restoration.

Step 2: Custom Restoration Design

Once your implant has been placed and fully healed by your oral surgeon or specialist, we take digital impressions to design a custom crown, bridge, or denture attachment. Your restoration will be crafted to match your bite, shape, and natural tooth color.
