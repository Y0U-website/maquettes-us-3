Meet Dr. Cole Johnston

Dr. Cole Johnston attended the University of Northern Iowa and majored in Biology with a minor in Chemistry. He then attended The University of Iowa College of Dentistry. Dr. Johnston provides full scope family dentistry services to patients of all ages and needs. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with family and friends, hunting with his dad, fishing, golfing, and playing basketball.

Yes. Patients experiencing urgent dental problems can receive treatment through Emergency dentistry .

Yes. Missing teeth can be restored using options such as dental implants, bridges, or dentures.

Yes. Cosmetic procedures such as veneers and whitening help improve the appearance of your smile.
