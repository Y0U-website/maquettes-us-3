All-on-4 Implant Restorations in Milford, OH

Led by Dr. Alexandra Shehata Kress, DDS, our team focuses on restoring function, comfort, and confidence through carefully planned implant restorations and long-term maintenance.

What Are All-on-4 Implant Restorations

All-on-4 implant restorations use four strategically placed dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. This approach provides a secure and natural-feeling alternative to traditional dentures.

The surgical portion of treatment is completed by a partner surgeon. After surgery, patients return to Gloss Dental Studio for the custom restoration of their smile and continued follow-up care.

Coordinated Surgical and Restorative Care

Our team works closely with a qualified implant surgeon to ensure a smooth and coordinated treatment experience. Once implant placement is complete, Gloss Dental Studio designs and delivers the final restorations and provides continuous care to support long-term success.

This collaborative approach allows patients to benefit from specialized surgical expertise while maintaining a consistent relationship with their restorative dental provider.

Benefits of All-on-4 Implant Restorations

All-on-4 restorations offer several advantages for patients with significant tooth loss:

Our team carefully evaluates each patient to determine if All-on-4 implant restorations are the right option.

  • Improved stability compared to removable dentures
  • Restored ability to eat and speak comfortably
  • Natural-looking, functional smile
  • Long-term solution with proper maintenance

Long-Term Care and Maintenance

Ongoing care is essential for the longevity of implant restorations. After placement and restoration, patients continue routine visits at Gloss Dental Studio for monitoring, cleanings, and maintenance.

Regular Cleanings & Exams help protect both the implants and surrounding oral structures.

If urgent concerns arise during treatment or recovery, our Emergency Dentistry services are available to provide prompt support.

Frequently Asked Questions
