The practice

Our office features modern dental facilities equipped with the latest technology, ensuring safe, high-quality care in a welcoming environment designed just for kids.

Our dentist, Dr. Elisa Perez, combines professional expertise with a fun, friendly approach that helps kids feel comfortable and at ease during every visit.

Dr. Perez is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist with a mission to help kids achieve healthy, confident smiles! With advanced training and years of experience, she provides stress-free, personalized care that supports your child’s overall growth and development.

Let’s partner together to give your little one a lifetime of healthy, happy smiles!

Hello! I’m Dr. Elisa Perez, a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist. I received my dental degree from the School of Dentistry at the Universidad Central de Venezuela in 2001. Then I moved to Spain, where I obtained a Master of Science in Oral Medicine from the University of Valencia.

In 2013, I completed my Pediatric Dentistry Residency at UT Health in San Antonio, and Became Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2015. I bring extensive experience and a passion for helping children achieve healthy, beautiful smiles. My approach to pediatric dentistry focuses on supporting your child’s overall growth and development. I believe in treating each child…

Additionally, I am fortunate to work as an adjunct faculty member, where I mentor the next generation of pediatric dentists at University of Texas San Antonio. This opportunity allows me to stay on the cutting edge of the latest advancements in pediatric dentistry, which I then bring to my patients here in Bulverde / Spring Branch, Texas.
