What to expect at your first visit

At Orono Dental Care, your first visit sets the tone for a lasting relationship of trust and exceptional care.

Step 01

Exam

After a thorough examination of your mouth, teeth, and gums, your doctor will provide insights into your dental health. This ensures you&#x27;re empowered to make well-informed decisions for your smile&#x27;s future.

Step 02

X-Rays

X-rays provide a detailed glimpse beneath the surface, revealing cavities, tooth roots, and bone health. By capturing these images, we can detect issues that might be invisible to the naked eye, ensuring early intervention and comprehensive oral care.

Step 03

Cleaning

Time to brighten that smile! Our team will meticulously remove plaque and other build-ups, even in those tricky spots, leaving your mouth feeling fresh and clean.

Advanced Tech, Exceptional Results

Same-Day CEREC Crowns

With our state-of-the-art CEREC technology, we craft custom-fitted, durable crowns right in our office, all within a single appointment. This not only saves you time but also ensures a perfect fit for enhanced comfort and durability.

Digital Implant Planning

Precision is crucial when it comes to implants. Our digital implant planning tools allow us to visualize and map out the entire implant process before any procedure begins. Digital implant planning results in faster, more accurate implant placements, and a smoother post-operative recovery.

Digital X-Rays

Safety and clarity come first with our digital X-rays. These modern X-rays emit significantly less radiation than traditional methods while providing clearer, more detailed images. Digital X-rays help us diagnose any dental issues early on, ensuring proactive care for your oral health.

3D-CBCT Imaging

3D-CBCT imaging offers a 360-degree view of your teeth and jaw structure, allowing for detailed assessments and planning. This means more precise treatments and a fuller understanding of your unique dental anatomy.

3D Printing

Our 3D printing technology helps us create accurate dental models, guides, and appliances. This translates to quicker turn-around times and treatments tailored specifically to your dental anatomy, which increases the predictability and efficiency of outcomes.
