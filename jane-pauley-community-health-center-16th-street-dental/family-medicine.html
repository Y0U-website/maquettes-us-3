  • Acute health problems: Cold, flu, sore throat, sinus infections, ear infections, UTIs, pink eye, vomiting, and headaches.
  • Chronic health conditions: Complete physical, emotional, and behavioral evaluations.
  • Immunizations: Flu and pneumonia shots; hepatitis A and B series; and vaccinations for tetanus, diphtheria, meningitis and meningitis B, pertussis (whooping cough), measles, mumps, and rubella.

*For emergency care, please call 911 or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance do you take? (Is mine in network?) We take Medicare, Medicaid and most commercial insurance. We are also here to support our uninsured patients.

You can get assistance from our Community Resource Navigators by calling 317-934-0779 .

How can I see who my Provider is or find other Providers? You can see photos and details for each of our Providers on the webpage for the Jane Pauley Practice Location where they see patients, or on the page for the Health Service offered. Find a Location or Service in the main menu above or search here .

What if I don’t have insurance or can’t afford care? No problem. We provide services on a sliding-fee scale based on your income if you can bring two recent pay stubs. If you aren’t working or don’t have income, simply bring your ID.

How can I get help getting insurance? Whether you’re a patient or not, you can schedule a FREE appointment with our Community Resource Navigators to help you enroll in most types of insurance. Includes temporary Presumptive Eligibility coverage, Hoosier Healthwise, Healthy Indiana Plan, or other marketplace insurance.

How can I find transportation help to get to my appointment? Medicaid patients can arrange transport through your insurance at least two days in advance of your appointment by calling 855-325-7586.

For Anthem HIP patients, see if options are included in your plan by calling: 844-772-6632.

For other patients, please visit your Practice Location webpage to see available public transportation options and ask us about a bus pass (if available) if you’re in need
