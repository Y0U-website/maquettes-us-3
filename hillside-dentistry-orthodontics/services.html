Our Services

Comprehensive dental care designed around your unique needs — from preventive essentials to full smile transformations.

✦ Preventive Cleanings & Preventive Care Professional cleanings and comprehensive exams form the cornerstone of your dental health. We use low-radiation digital X-rays and intra-oral cameras to catch issues early and keep your smile healthy for life.

◈ Restorative Restorative Dentistry When a tooth is damaged, decayed, or beyond saving, we restore both function and appearance with natural-looking crowns, tooth-colored fillings, root canal therapy, and gentle extractions — keeping your smile healthy and comfortable.

◇ Orthodontics Invisalign As an Invisalign Premier Preferred Provider, Dr. Sheth offers the full suite of clear aligner treatments — including Invisalign Teen — with the expertise and case volume that puts us among the top providers in the South Bay.

◈ Cosmetic Porcelain Veneers Ultra-thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells that cover the front surface of your teeth — correcting chips, cracks, stains, gaps, and uneven sizing in as little as two visits.

✦ Cosmetic Teeth Whitening We offer Zoom! in-office whitening for dramatic same-day results, as well as custom take-home whitening trays for gradual brightening on your own schedule.

◇ Advanced Laser Dentistry We use both LANAP (Laser Assisted New Attachment Procedure) and Diode laser technology for gum disease treatment and soft tissue procedures — with less pain, less bleeding, and faster healing than traditional methods.

◈ Comfort Sedation Dentistry Dr. Sheth offers nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation — gentle, proven options that keep you calm and comfortable throughout your visit, so every patient, including those with dental anxiety, can receive the care they need with ease.

◇ Orthodontics Orthodontics Beyond Invisalign, we offer a full spectrum of orthodontic options: traditional metal braces, 6-Month Smile cosmetic braces, and ClearCorrect aligners — for children, teens, and adults.

✦ Emergency Emergency Dental Care Dental emergencies can&#x27;t wait. We prioritize same-day appointments for patients in pain, and our office is AED-equipped for any medical situation. After hours, text the office for the fastest response.
