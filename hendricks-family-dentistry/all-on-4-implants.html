How the All-on-4 Process Works

We also offer CEREC same-day crowns for other implant-supported restorations.

  • Evaluation & Planning We use 3D CT scanning and cone beam technology to assess bone structure and plan the placement of your implants.
  • Implant Placement Four dental implants are placed at strategic angles to maximize support and avoid the need for bone grafting.
  • Temporary Smile A natural-looking provisional prosthesis is placed the same day, so you leave our office with a full smile.
  • Final Restoration After healing, a custom full-arch bridge is securely attached for a long-term, stable solution.

FAQs on All-on-4 Implants

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With good care, All-on-4 restorations can last for decades or even a lifetime.

Will I leave with new teeth the same day?

Yes—most patients receive a temporary prosthesis on the day of surgery.

Is the procedure painful?

The surgery is performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort.

Can both arches be done at the same time?

Yes, upper and lower arches can be restored together depending on your treatment plan.
