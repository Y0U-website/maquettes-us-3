What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are replacement teeth that work just like your natural teeth. They consist of a titanium post that goes into your jawbone and acts like a tooth root. A crown that looks exactly like a real tooth is then attached to this post. The implant becomes a permanent part of your mouth and feels completely natural.

What Can You Expect When You’re Getting Dental Implants?

During the first appointment, you will undergo a light surgical procedure with a dentist in Vancouver, WA who will insert a titanium post into your jawbone through the gums at the site of the missing tooth. We will attach a temporary, lightweight crown to the end of this post to ensure your smile looks good.

Once this healing is complete and the post is integrated with your jawbone, you will return for your second appointment, where we will install your permanent dental implant. Your tooth will look identical in shape, size, and shade to your other teeth, giving you a seamless smile. If you decide something doesn’t look or feel right, we are happy to change the prosthetic. Our goal is to give you something you’re completely happy with.
