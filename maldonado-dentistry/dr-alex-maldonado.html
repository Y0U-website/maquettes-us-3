Life Beyond Dentistry

Outside the office, Dr. Maldonado enjoys staying active in the Treasure Island community and participating in local events and initiatives. His dedication to dentistry is matched by his appreciation for the coastal lifestyle and the relationships he builds both inside and outside the practice.

Dr. Maldonado is a lifelong fishing enthusiast and enjoys spending time on the water whenever he can. At home, he and his wife, Olga, value every moment with their two young children and their puppy, Baxter. They love relaxing at the beach, being outdoors, and making memories with their loved ones.
