How Dental Implants Benefit Your Health

Aside from the aesthetic advantages of dental implants, they also have a number of health benefits that will protect the integrity of your smile and your bite. If you’re in need of dental implants in Tuscaloosa, AL 35401, you’ll find they act as a placeholder and keep your natural teeth from drifting toward the gap in your smile. This protects your occlusion (bite) and keeps your natural teeth in their original location.

Additionally, dental implants prevent the bone loss that commonly occurs with tooth loss. Your teeth and bones have a dependent relationship. When you lose teeth, your bone is no longer needed to stabilize your root and begins to resorb. Bone loss creates a number of problems with your dentistry and may even cause you to lose more teeth. When you choose dental implants, you are making an excellent choice for your health as well as your smile!`
