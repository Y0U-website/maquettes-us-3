Dental Insurance

Elective procedures (like cosmetic ones) tend to NOT receive coverage, but most plans DO offer some benefits for orthodontics. What’s important is to talk to your provider or our team before committing to treatment to ensure you know how much your plan will pay.

  • Basic restorative services (fillings, bonding) are covered 80 %
  • Advanced restorative services (crowns, bridges, dentures) are covered 50 %
