Comprehensive Dental Care for a Healthy and Confident Smile.

From routine prevention to full smile transformations, every treatment at our Valley Village practice is tailored to your unique goals and comfort.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the beauty of your smile with treatments designed to brighten, reshape, and perfect your teeth.

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin custom shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct shape, color, and alignment for a flawless, natural-looking smile.

Direct Resin Veneers

A conservative, reversible esthetic procedure in which no tooth structure is removed. Composite resin is sculpted directly onto the teeth in a single visit, delivering beautiful, natural-looking results without any drilling or permanent alteration.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-office and take-home whitening systems that safely lift stains and brighten your smile by several shades.

Smile Makeovers

A fully customized combination of cosmetic treatments planned together to achieve your ideal smile transformation.

Invisalign®

Straighten your smile discreetly with custom-fitted clear aligners — no metal brackets, no wires. Dr. Sands is a certified Invisalign provider.

Gum Contouring

Reshaping of the gum line to create a more balanced, symmetrical smile—often combined with veneers or whitening.

Restorative Dentistry

Rebuild strength, function, and aesthetics with durable, natural-looking restorations.

Dental Implants

Permanent titanium posts topped with lifelike crowns to replace missing teeth — the gold standard in tooth replacement.

Porcelain Crowns

Full-coverage restorations that protect and restore damaged or weakened teeth while blending seamlessly with your smile.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Composite resin fillings that match your natural tooth color, replacing old metal fillings or treating new cavities discreetly.

Dental Bridges

Fixed prosthetics anchored to adjacent teeth to replace one or more missing teeth and restore your bite.

Inlays & Onlays

Precision-crafted porcelain restorations for teeth too damaged for a filling but not requiring a full crown.

Preventive Dentistry

Protect your oral health and catch problems early with regular, thorough preventive care.

Comprehensive Exams

Thorough evaluations of your teeth, gums, bite, and oral tissues to detect issues early and keep your smile healthy.

Professional Cleanings

Expert removal of plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing can’t reach, leaving your teeth polished and fresh.

Digital X-Rays

Low-radiation digital imaging that gives us a detailed view of your teeth and bone structure for accurate diagnosis.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Routine screenings during every exam to detect early signs of oral cancer when treatment is most effective.

Night Guards

Custom-fitted guards to protect your teeth from the damage caused by nighttime grinding and clenching (bruxism).

Dental Cleanings

Professional cleanings every six months remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and help prevent cavities and gum disease before they start.

Periodontal Therapy

Targeted treatment for gum disease — from scaling and root planing to ongoing maintenance — to restore gum health and protect the bone supporting your teeth.
