Invisalign Clear Aligners

With Invisalign, the Difference is Clear

Invisalign and other clear aligner therapy can be used in most cases to correct problems that once was only possible with braces and wires. Tooth movement is prescribed with incredible precision since these are computer generated aligners. Plus, the clear aligners are virtually undetectable so you can straighten your teeth and fix your bite without anyone knowing!

Benefits of Invisalign Aligners for Teens & Adults

Dr. Mansour will determine if Invisalign® is the right solution for you during your first adult orthodontic treatment consultation. The process for treatment with Invisalign® begins with a set of custom made aligners to be worn for one-two weeks. Gradual movement of teeth will occur during the process. At each appointment, you will receive aligners with new settings to further progress the movement of your teeth until the desired outcome is achieved – a healthy, beautiful smile! Our Kissimmee Invisalign and St. Cloud Invisalign dentists are ready to assist you.

Types of Problems Invisalign® Can Help Correct

Although Invisalign® is not right for all patients and cannot treat certain occlusions or problems, it is very effective for straightening crooked teeth, protruding teeth, and common bite problems. Due to the superior comfort, subtlety, and results of this treatment, it is quite popular among patients of all age groups. Schedule a consultation with our St. Cloud orthodontist or Kissimmee orthodontist location to start your path to a new smile!

The following problems can be remedied with the use of Invisalign® clear aligners:

  • Crowded Teeth
  • Spaced Teeth
  • Protruding Teeth
  • Overbite
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
