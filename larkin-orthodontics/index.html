The practice

After the active orthodontic appliances are removed, our patients will be provided with retainers to maintain the dental correction. The bone and soft tissues around the teeth continue to stabilize for several months following the removal of braces. It is crucial that the retainers are worn as directed.

The American Association of Orthodontists advises that most children undergo orthodontic screening by the age of 7. This helps us assess whether orthodontic treatment is necessary and determine the optimal time for the patient to begin treatment.
