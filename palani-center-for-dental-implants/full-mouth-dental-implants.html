Our Smile Transformation Timeline

7:15 AM

Since your procedure will likely begin early, be sure to get a good night’s sleep beforehand. Dr. Palani will review the procedure with you one final time and explain what to expect afterward.

8:00 AM

You’ll be taken to our sedation room, where medication will help you relax into a deep sleep state. Your vital signs will be closely monitored throughout the procedure for your safety.

10:30 AM

Once the implants are placed, Dr. Palani will complete the surgical phase by cleaning the sites and suturing the incisions. Around 10:30 AM, general anesthesia will be stopped, and we will allow the sedation to wear off.

10:30 AM - 1:00 PM

You will rest while your temporary restoration is made in our in-house lab. You will wear it for several months as your implants heal and fuse with the jawbone, preparing for your final restoration.

1:00 PM

The process is typically completed around 1:00 PM, when your temporary restoration is placed and you see your new smile for the first time. We will then review your aftercare and follow-up instructions.

Understanding Full Mouth Dental Implants

Full mouth dental implants are a revolutionary solution for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth. This procedure involves placing implants in the jawbone to support a full set of prosthetic teeth, offering a permanent and natural-looking alternative to dentures.

The benefits of full mouth dental implants extend beyond aesthetics; they also improve functionality and oral health. Patients experience enhanced chewing ability, reduced bone loss, and a significant boost in self-confidence, making it a popular choice for those seeking comprehensive dental restoration.
