Our Services

Preventative Care, Cosmetic Dentistry, Emergency Care and Oral Surgery

Preventative Care: Establish a foundation for a healthy smile with routine comprehensive exams, professional cleanings (prophylaxis), digital X-rays, fluoride treatments (for both adults and children), and dental sealants to protect against decay.

Cosmetic Care: Enhance the beauty of your smile with aesthetic services, including professional teeth whitening and clear aligners.

Restorative Treatments: Restore function and strength to your teeth with services like fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Oral Surgery & Implants: We provide tooth replacement solutions, including the placement of durable dental implants, as well as necessary tooth extractions.

Emergency Care: If you experience a dental emergency, please contact our office immediately. We will do our best to provide timely care or guidance to manage your situation.

Other Services: We offer second opinions to review treatment options for complex dental needs.
