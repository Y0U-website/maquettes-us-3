What We Are About - High Standards and Personalized Care

At Kings Beach Dental, our mission is to offer personalized, high-quality dental services. Our approach combines comprehensive treatment planning with both restorative and cosmetic dentistry to achieve optimal dental health. In emergencies, we ensure timely and effective care.

Education & Prevention - Cornerstones of Our Practice

We believe in preventative care and education as key components of optimal dental health. Our thorough exams, including oral cancer screenings and digital x-rays, are part of our commitment to your overall health and beautiful smile. We also emphasize the importance of routine care, like cleanings and fluoride treatments, in preventing dental diseases.

Uncompromising Safety Standards

Your safety is paramount. We adhere to strict sterilization and cross-contamination processes, following guidelines from the ADA, OSHA, and CDC, ensuring a safe environment for our patients and staff.

A Positive Experience - Our Commitment to Comfort

We strive to create a relaxing and positive dental experience. Understanding patient anxieties, we offer personalized comforts like headphones, warm blankets, or a movie during your visit. Our goal is to build trust and ensure your visits are as comfortable and pleasant as possible.
