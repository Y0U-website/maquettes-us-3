General Dentistry

We Can Handle Any General Dentistry Service You Need

Inlays and Onlays Inlays and onlays are dental fillings used to repair a damaged or decayed tooth. They are made from tooth-colored materials, such as porcelain or composite resin, and are custom-made to fit your tooth.

Dentures & Partials If you have missing teeth, dentures & partials are an excellent option for restoring your smile. Dentures are removable appliances that can replace a full arch of missing teeth, while partials are designed to replace one or more missing teeth.

Root Canal Treatment If you have a tooth that is severely damaged or infected, root canal treatment may be necessary to save the tooth. During this procedure, the damaged or infected pulp is removed from the tooth, and the tooth is then filled and sealed.

Gum Treatments Gum treatments are essential for maintaining good oral health. We offer a range of gum treatments to help prevent and treat gum disease.

Bleaching (Tooth Whitening) If you want to brighten your smile, bleaching (tooth whitening) may be your solution. We offer in-office bleaching to help you achieve a brighter, whiter smile.

Porcelain Crowns and Bridges Porcelain crowns and bridges are excellent for restoring damaged or missing teeth. Crowns are used to cover a damaged or decayed tooth, while bridges are used to replace one or more missing teeth.

Porcelain Veneers Porcelain veneers are a popular cosmetic dentistry option that can help improve the appearance of your teeth. They are custom-made to fit your teeth and are used to cover stains, chips, or other imperfections.

Cosmetic Bondings Cosmetic bondings are a simple and effective way to improve the appearance of your smile. During this procedure, a tooth-colored resin is applied to your teeth to improve their shape or color.
