Meet the Team

Maria

Introducing Maria, our Office Manager. Maria started her dental career 12 years ago as an Orthodontic Assistant and later transitioned to a managerial position. Maria is committed to making our patients experience in our office a positive one while incorporating the wealth of knowledge and customer service that she has acquired to equip our team to best serve our patients. In her spare time, Maria enjoys spending time with her husband and 2 daughters traveling and making memories.

Jessica

Introducing one of our amazing dental assistants Jessica! Jessica has been working in the dental field as an assistant for the past nine years. Her priority is to make all patients feel comfortable and at ease. Outside of work Jessica enjoys spending time with her friends, family, and cat Ollie. Make sure you say hello on the day of your visit!

Taylor

We are thrilled to welcome Taylor to our practice! With three years of experience in dental office administration and a strong background in customer service, Taylor brings both expertise and a warm, friendly attitude to our front desk team. Outside of work, Taylor enjoys spending time with family and friends, as well as exploring new destinations through travel. We’re excited to have her onboard and look forward to the positive energy and skills she brings to the office!

Yudit

Meet Yudit! She joined us in March 2025, bringing with her over a decade of experience in the dental field—since 2013. Yudit is passionate about building trust, comfort, and strong professional relationships with both patients and team members.

She takes pride in helping patients feel at ease, especially those coping with dental fears and phobias, always aiming to provide a pleasant and supportive experience. For Yudit, the most rewarding part of being on our team is the high quality of care that our doctors and staff deliver to every patient.

Outside of the office, Yudit enjoys quality time with her wonderful children—one son and two daughters. Whether it’s sharing a family dinner or spending time with friends, she values creating joyful memories with her loved ones.

Gissel

Gissel is an experienced dental assistant with 5 years in the field and extensive hands-on experience in endodontics. Gissel is passionate about dentistry, patient comfort and teamwork. She brings strong clinical skills, reliability and a caring approach to every role. She also values work-life balance and enjoys family time and fitness. Please help us welcome Gissel to the team!
