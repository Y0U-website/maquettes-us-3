How We Use Your Information

Text Messages, Phone Calls, and Email

By submitting your phone number or email address, you authorize us to contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your request, appointments, dental care, educational resources, or related services.

Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP. You may also contact our office directly to update your communication preferences.

Please do not use email, text message, website forms, or social media forms to send urgent dental or medical information. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, call our office directly. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911.

Facebook, Instagram, and Online Lead Forms

If you submit information through a Facebook, Instagram, or other online lead form, we may receive the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, and responses to form questions.

We use this information to send requested resources, respond to your inquiry, and follow up about dental services that may interest you.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Facebook, Instagram, Meta, Google, or other third-party platforms. Please review those platforms’ privacy policies to understand how they collect and use information.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand website activity, improve our services, measure advertising performance, and deliver relevant content.

These tools may collect information such as pages visited, device type, browser type, approximate location, and how you interacted with our website or advertisements.

You can adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Some website features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.
