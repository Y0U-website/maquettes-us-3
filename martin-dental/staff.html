About Jessica Martin

"I hold a Master’s and Educational Specialist degree in School Psychology, along with certifications in school counseling, special education administration, and private practice school psychology. I am married to Dr. Tony Martin, and while it was never our intention to merge our careers, we soon discovered that our areas of expertise complement one another. Together, our shared vision has transformed Martin Dental into a revolutionary practice that blends psychology with dentistry to create a true dental spa experience.

I love seeing patients feel comfortable at the dentist sometimes for the very first time and helping them move forward with care they may have put off for years due to anxious feelings. Beyond supporting our team internally, I am active in networking throughout the community to promote the practice and build meaningful connections. I also focus on innovating within the practice so that the patient experience remains exceptional, thoughtful, and unlike anything else in the area.

Martin Dental has grown into such a wonderful practice, and I am incredibly proud to be a part of it. We have the kindest patients and a staff that excels at what they do! Outside of work, Tony and I stay busy with our two amazing kids who are involved in a variety of activities. I also enjoy reading, being outdoors, and spending quality time with family and friends."

About Nala
