SERVICES

DENTAL CLEANINGS Regular dental visits are important because they help to keep your teeth and gums healthy. During your check-up, Dr. Pollack will check your overall oral health, perform a very important oral cancer screening and assess the health of your teeth and gums. Research has shown there is a direct correlation between gum (periodontal) disease and heart disease. During your cleaning, our hygenist will remove any plaque and tartar build-up and polish your teeth, giving you a healthy, bright smile.

COSMETIC DENTISTRY Through Dr. Pollack s artistic eyes, we’ll create that beautiful smile you have always wanted. Dr. Pollack uses natural-looking alternatives including porcelain veneers and crowns, metal-free bridges, and natural colored fillings. A bright white smile that shows straight teeth along with a healthy, gleaming, pink gumline, can take 10 years off your true age.

TEETH WHITENING Our teeth whitening procedures will turn yellow and stained teeth into a dazzling, bright white smile. Let us help you make a fantastic first impression. Our patients have been extremely pleased with their impressive results.

VENEERS Dr. Pollack uses thin pieces of porcelain that cover the front of your teeth that will change their color and/or shape. Veneers are used on teeth with uneven surfaces or teeth that are chipped, discolored, oddly shaped, unevenly spaced or crooked. Little or no anesthesia is needed. Veneers are highly resistant to permanent staining from coffee, tea, or even cigarette smoking.

PORCELAIN CROWNS Dental porcelain can be sculpted to closely replicate the look of natural tooth enamel, while providing strength and resilience. Porcelain crowns are an excellent choice when recreating the form and function of a damaged tooth since a crown basically replaces the entire external portion of the tooth down to the gum level. Dr. Pollack s crowns are designed to create an even better esthetic appearance.

COSMETIC BONDING Dental bonding is the process of bonding tooth-colored resin to the teeth to mask imperfections such as unsightly spaces and chipped or broken teeth. In addition to providing cosmetic relief, bonding can be used to fill cavities. Once the resin has been bonded to the tooth, it becomes permanent and very unlikely to fall off or break away.

TOOTH-COLORED FILLINGS Our composite filling is a tooth-colored resin and glass mixture used to restore decayed teeth. We also use composites for cosmetic improvements of the smile by changing the color of the teeth or reshaping disfigured teeth.

SNORING AND SLEEP APNEA APPLIANCES Dr. Pollack can help you treat excessive snoring and sleep apnea by creating a dental or oral device to use while you sleep. These appliances are useful in correcting mild to moderate cases of obstructive sleep apnea. Talk to Dr. Pollack about the available options and which device is best for your particular situation.

NIGHT GUARDS The action of grinding where your upper and lower teeth move back and forth with great force against each other causes your teeth to flatten. If untreated, grinding can cause temperature sensitivity, pain (because the wear gets down to the nerve), cracks in the teeth, receding gums and eventually lost teeth. Grinding can also cause migraine headaches and pain in the shoulders, neck, back and ears. Research has shown the most effective treatment for grinding is a night guard. Let Dr. Pollack talk to you about the options.

DENTAL IMPLANTS AND BRIDGES If you are missing several teeth, implant-supported bridges can replace them. Dental implants will replace both your lost natural teeth and some of the roots. Let Dr. Pollack explain the options and the treatment plans for these important procedures.

FULL MOUTH REJUVENATION A full-mouth rejuvenation, also referred to as a full-mouth reconstruction, involves several cosmetic dentistry procedures designed to improve the health and appearance of your smile. Rejuvenations are usually recommended for clients who have teeth that are damaged, worn down, or missing completely. Not only do damaged teeth negatively impact your smile appearance, they can also weaken your chewing ability and create bite imbalance. Make an appointment with Dr. Pollack for a complete consultation to get you back to a winning smile.
