The practice

Welcome to Indian Peaks Dental, where our commitment to quality care and community well-being has been a cornerstone of our practice since the early 2000s. In 2021, Dr. Cara proudly took the helm of this cherished dental office, continuing its legacy of excellence in general dentistry.

At Indian Peaks Dental, we believe in creating a welcoming and judgment-free environment for all our patients.

Giving back to our community is at the heart of what we do. Each year, we host a Free Dental Day, typically in April, to provide essential dental services to those in need. Be sure to follow us on social media for updates on this special event and other community initiatives.

We look forward to serving you and your family at Indian Peaks Dental, where your smile is our priority!

Our dedicated team is passionate about patient education, ensuring that you are informed and comfortable with your dental health decisions. We take pride in our work, striving for the highest standards in care and service.

Dr. Cara is passionate about providing comprehensive dental care to patients to improve their overall health. Since completing her Doctorate in Dental Surgery degree at Loma Linda School of Dentistry, she has made continuing education a priority throughout her career. She takes pride in pursuing high-quality continuing education to continually grow as a dentist and provide the best possible care…

Some of the renowned educators she has had the opportunity to learn from include Dr. Kois, with a focus on comprehensive care; Dr. Molis, with a focus on achieving predictable Invisalign results; and Dr. Soroush Zaghi of The Breathe Institute, with a focus on airway, breathing, and sleep. She is passionate about bringing this knowledge into her practice to better understand the connection between…

Dr. Cara is also a member of the American Dental Association and the Colorado Dental Association. Outside of dentistry, she loves spending time with her family. Her husband, Andrew, is a middle school teacher, and together they keep busy running after their two daughters. As a family, they love to hike and camp. Dr. Cara also enjoys cooking and sewing.
