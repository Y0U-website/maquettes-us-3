What Are Dental Implants in Beaverton?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots crafted from durable titanium. This material can integrate into the jawbone to preserve it and create the perfect foundation for replacement teeth (dental crowns) or a fixed denture. We may use up to six dental implants to secure a denture to the jaw.

Dental implant placement is safe because it is image-guided and meticulously planned. Your dentist will explain what to expect during treatment and after.
