0 Our Team The Dental Care Team at Millbrae Smile Center

Dr. Vasanth is native of India, born and raised, graduated from A. B. Shetty Institute of Dental Sciences. After moving to United States, she went on to receive her doctorate of dental surgery from Tuft’s University school of Dentistry, Boston.

She has been practicing dentistry since 2010 in Arizona. Having moved to Bay Area few years ago, she has been working in Foster city and Redwood city.

Dr. Vasanth understands how uneasy some patients may feel about their dental visits and how she can make a difference in providing a relaxing and positive experience.

She takes pride in providing her patients high quality dentistry in a most compassionate and gentle way possible.

Over the years, she has developed the love for travelling with exploration and passion for discovering new cultures and food.

Dr. Vasanth moved to Bay Area with her husband, Suraj and her adorable son, Neel from Scottdale. The beautiful and diverse California with its vast array of multi cultural cuisine has grown on her and she is excited to call Bay Area her home. When not practicing dentistry, she is mostly found baking or painting. Dr. Vasanth thrives on building lifelong relationships and loves seeing her patients smile!
