Technology

Orthodontic treatment is more comfortable, precise, and faster than ever before. At Leavitt Orthodontics, we use contemporary orthodontic technology tailored to your specific needs. From digital 3D imaging to advanced teeth-straightening options like Invisalign with iTero’s latest 5D scanner, we eliminate the need for messy, goopy impressions while capturing the most accurate images of your teeth. By combining cutting-edge technology with the latest appliances, we ensure your treatment is efficient, comfortable, and designed to give you the perfect smile you’ve always wanted.
