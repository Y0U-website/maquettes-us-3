How All-on-4 Dental Implants Work

The All-on-4 system uses four implants placed in areas of the jaw with the strongest bone support. These implants anchor a full arch of prosthetic teeth, often placed the same day as surgery.

As part of our comprehensive oral surgery services, every procedure is carefully planned for precision and long-term success.

  • Reduces the number of implants needed
  • Minimizes the need for extensive grafting
  • Shortens recovery time

Frequently Asked Questions

What are All-on-4 dental implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses four implants to support a fixed set of teeth, offering a permanent alternative to dentures.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With proper care, implants can last decades. The prosthetic teeth may need maintenance over time.

Is the All-on-4 procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure comfort. Most patients report mild to moderate soreness during recovery.

How much do All-on-4 dental implants cost in Iowa?

Costs vary depending on your case, including extractions and preparatory procedures. A consultation will provide a personalized estimate.

Am I a candidate if I have bone loss?

Many patients with bone loss still qualify. In some cases, bone grafting may be recommended to improve outcomes.
