Why Choose Healthy Smiles Dental?

  • Prevention of Cavities & Decay: Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, reducing the risk of cavities.
  • Professional Teeth Cleaning: Even with good oral hygiene, professional cleanings remove hard-to-reach plaque and stains for a healthier smile.
  • Gum Disease Prevention: Routine checkups help prevent gingivitis and periodontitis, which can lead to tooth loss if untreated.
  • Oral Cancer Screening: Dentists check for signs of oral cancer, increasing the chances of early detection and successful treatment.

What Are Partial and Full Dentures?

Partial Dentures replace one or more missing teeth and Full Dentures replace all the teeth that are missing on either the upper or lower arches. See video for Dr. McNees’ explanation.
