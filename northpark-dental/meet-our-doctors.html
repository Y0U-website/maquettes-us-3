Meet Our Team of Experienced Dentists

Northpark Dental was established in the mid-1960s by Dr. Arthur Jallo and was originally located next to Laddie Lake on Central Ave. In 1981, the practice was purchased by Dr. Paul Chadbourn, and five years later, it moved to our current Blaine location. Throughout the years, our team has continued to grow and now includes Dr. Chadbourn’s son, Dr. Andrew Chadbourn. Together, they can provide each patient with a personalized treatment plan–from routine cleanings to advanced restorative and cosmetic procedures. These include everything from routine cleanings, restorative and cosmetic procedures.

Dr. Paul Chadbourn, Family Dentistry

Dr. Paul Chadbourn is owner and president of Northpark Dental. After receiving a Bachelor of Arts degree from St. Olaf College, Dr. Chadbourn earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University Of Minnesota School of Dentistry. He has spent over 40 years practicing dentistry in the Blaine area and is extremely grateful for the continued trust that patients have shown in the staff at Northpark Dental. The most rewarding aspect of his career has been the development of lasting relationships with the families that he has had the privilege to treat over the past decades.

Along with Dr. Andrew, Dr. Chadbourn oversees the comprehensive treatment options in our office. He constantly updates his professional knowledge through continuing education programs and has been a life-long member of the Minneapolis District Dental Society, the Minnesota Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Andrew Chadbourn, Family Dentistry

Dr. Andrew Chadbourn earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in biology at St. Olaf College. He then attended Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2014. During his studies at Nova, Dr. Andrew provided dental care in community health clinics in the greater Miami-Fort Lauderdale area. He currently serves select weekends as a dental provider for the Minnesota Army National Guard.

Carrying on a tradition, he is proud to represent the fourth generation of his family to have earned doctorate degrees in the medical/dental professions. Dr. Andrew is interested in all areas of general dentistry and in providing concerned, trusted care to patients of all ages. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, and the Minneapolis District Dental Society.
