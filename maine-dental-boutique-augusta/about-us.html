About Us

Maine Dental Boutique caters to clients with busy lifestyles and who value prompt, efficient service and short-notice booking options.

Your Initial Visit will include a Dentist Exam and updated digital x-rays. Dental Cleanings are done either at this visit or at a later visit depending on availability.

Our patients appreciate our spa-like atmosphere and enjoy getting in and out in a timely manner.

Book with confidence with no high-pressure sales!
