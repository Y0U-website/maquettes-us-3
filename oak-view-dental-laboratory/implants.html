Custom Abutments

Custom Abutments

Our implant team fabricates high-quality titanium and zirconia custom abutments that will provide an ideal emergence profile and shape. Through the use of CAD/CAM technology, our team ensures a natural fit and a long-lasting solution. All our custom abutments are compatible with most major implant manufacturers.

Screw-Retained Crown

Screw-Retained Crown

Screw-retained crowns are an ideal option due to their elimination of cement from the operatory. The leading cause of implant failure is excess cement left below the margin during oral surgery. Screw-retained crowns are easily retrievable for maintenance and initial seating is efficient. Our team carefully fabricates our screw-retained crown restorations to ensure every case is fulfilled with precision and esthetics.

Overdenture

Overdenture

The overdenture from Oak View Dental Lab merges the high function and stability of an implant-retained restoration with the ease-of-use and removability provided by a traditional full denture prosthesis. We typically craft our overdenture as a full-arch solution, which is attached to a CAD/CAM bar fixed in the edentulous ridge. By distributing occlusal impact across the entire bridge, the overdenture increases stabilization. The overdenture is removable for easy access during cleaning and repairs.

Maryland Bridge

Maryland Bridge

The maryland bridge restorations from Oak View provide a more conservative solution for a missing anterior tooth. We utilize high-quality materials to craft this restoration to ensure a durable, long-lasting product.

FCZ Hybrid

FCZ Hybrid

The full-contour zirconia hybrid from Oak View Dental Lab provides a high-esthetic solution. Due to the high flexural strength of zirconia, this hybrid ensures longevity and durability. Our zirconia hybrid is milled from single blocks of monolithic zirconia.
