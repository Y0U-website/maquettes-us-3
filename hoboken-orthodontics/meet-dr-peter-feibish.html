Meet Dr. Peter Feibish

MEET DR. PETER FEIBISH

I am beyond grateful to have realized a lifelong dream of being an orthodontist. I cherish the opportunity to support our patients, young and old, to enhance their self-image through improving their smile. I approach each patient interaction with care and joy to ensure an amazing treatment experience.

Education and Continuing Education

Born and raised in New Jersey, I attended Rutgers University, where I earned my Bachelor of Science in Biology. I was an awarded member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honors Society at Temple University School of Dentistry, where I graduated with my Doctorate of Dental Medicine. With a commitment to learning from world renowned professors, I attended Columbia University School of Dental and Oral Surgery, where I earned by Certificate of Orthodontics and my Master of Science degree in Orthodontics.

My passion for the art and science of orthodontics keeps me eager to learn about the ways that new technology and protocols can enhance my practice of orthodontics. You will often find me participating and leading orthodontic educational experiences for my peers and I take great pride in my commitment to being a lifelong student of my profession. In this way, our office is postured to provide the most advanced treatment alternatives available.

Outside the Office

In line with my commitment to serve people and bolster self-esteem in the world, I have dedicated my time as a co-founder of a non-profit that supports personal transformational experiences. It has been one of my life’s greatest achievement to serve my community in this way. I have a devoted meditation practice and am passionate about traditional hot yoga and volunteering efforts in the community.
