The practice

Dental hygienists work in dental offices, public health clinics, educational settings, and other healthcare environments while helping patients maintain lifelong oral health.

Explore dental hygiene programs at Missouri Southern State University. Whether you are preparing to become a licensed dental hygienist through the Associate of Science in Dental Hygiene or advancing your education through the Bachelor of Science in Health Science Dental Hygiene Option, MSSU offers pathways designed to support your professional goals in oral healthcare.

The Missouri Southern State University (MSSU) Dental Hygiene Program provides the education and clinical experience needed to prepare graduates for licensure as dental hygienists.
