What to expect

Communication

Your visit begins with clear, one-on-one communication. Dr. Moon will listen to your concerns, review your dental history, explain your periodontal or dental implant needs, and answer your questions so you feel informed before treatment begins.

Comfort

Our Montvale office is designed to feel calm, private, and welcoming. From gentle care to sedation options when appropriate, our team helps you feel supported during consultations, periodontal treatment, and dental implant visits.

Tailored Solutions

Whether you have complex or simple concerns, every smile is special and treating it that way is our specialty.

Technology

We use advanced diagnostic and treatment technology, including digital X-rays, 3D imaging, intraoral cameras, and laser dentistry when appropriate. These tools help Dr. Moon evaluate gum health, bone support, implant needs, and treatment options with greater precision

Convenience

We make your visit easier with online appointment requests, insurance guidance, and flexible payment options. Our team helps coordinate the details so you can focus on improving your oral health, function, and confidence.
