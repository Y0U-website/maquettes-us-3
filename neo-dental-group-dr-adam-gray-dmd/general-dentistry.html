General Dentistry

  • Cleanings During your dental cleanings, we focus on the removal of plaque, tartar, and stains from your teeth. This helps prevent tooth decay, gum disease, bad breath, and will help assure your long-term oral health.
  • Fillings When cavities or decay are detected, we use fillings to restore and protect the affected teeth. The decayed portion is removed, and the cavity is filled with materials such as composite resin, porcelain, or amalgam.
  • Teeth Whitening We offer various teeth whitening procedures to enhance the appearance of discolored or stained teeth. We offer both in-office treatments or take-home whitening kits, depending on your preference.
  • Root Canal Therapy When the pulp inside a tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay or injury, a root canal treatment may be necessary. The infected pulp is removed, the tooth is cleaned, and then sealed to prevent further infection. In order to restore your bite and appearance of your smile, we will discuss the option of replacing the affected tooth with a crown.
  • Extractions In cases where a tooth is severely damaged or impacted, extraction may be required. Our priority is to ensure your comfort during the extraction procedure to remove the affected tooth, and provide appropriate aftercare instructions.
  • Dental Implants Dental implants are a permanent solution to replace missing teeth. They involve the surgical placement of an artificial tooth root (implant) into the jawbone, followed by the attachment of a custom-made dental crown.
  • Orthodontic Treatment Orthodontic services focus on aligning and straightening teeth and correcting jaw misalignments. Treatments like braces, clear aligners, like Invisalign, or other orthodontic appliances are used to achieve optimal tooth and jaw positioning.
  • Periodontal Care Periodontal services are aimed at preventing, diagnosing, and treating gum diseases, such as gingivitis and periodontitis. Treatments may include scaling and root planing (deep cleaning), gum surgeries, and maintenance procedures.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry Cosmetic dental procedures focus on improving the aesthetics of your smile. We provide services that are tailored to your smile goals like porcelain veneers, dental bonding, gum contouring, and smile makeovers.
  • Oral Health Education Knowing how to maintain a healthy smile plays a crucial role in long term oral health. We educate patients about proper oral hygiene practices, including brushing, flossing, diet, as well as guidance on maintaining good oral health and preventing dental problems.
  • Dental X-Rays and Imaging We use dental X-rays and other imaging techniques to diagnose and monitor dental conditions that may not be visible during a routine examination.
  • Emergency Dental Care We are equipped to handle dental emergencies such as toothaches, knocked-out teeth, chipped or fractured teeth, and other urgent dental issues.
