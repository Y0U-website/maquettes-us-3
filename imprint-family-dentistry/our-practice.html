Modern Tech For Your Comfort

Intraoral iTero Scanners

Dentistry doesn’t have to be uncomfortable. Intraoral scanners have changed the game with more comfortable, accurate, and effective digital scanning. Forget about messy impressions and waiting weeks for a lab to create your restorations. This innovative tool is more precise than traditional putty, so you can expect improved treatment outcomes.

Intraoral Cameras

See things from our view! Capturing these detailed images helps us to convey potential issues and educate you to make the best decisions for your dental health.

3D Printers for Same-Day Appliances

No more waiting weeks to restore your teeth or receive your appliances. Our 3D printer allows us to print a multitude of restorations and appliances in-office, so you can get back to smiling!

3D CBCT

CBCT, or Cone Beam Computed Tomography, is a quick and comfortable scan that captures high quality 3D data and allows us to view comprehensive vital information for your procedures.

Digital X-Rays

Compared to traditional film X-rays, digital X-rays offer a higher resolution image with vastly less radiation exposure. Images are captured immediately and displayed on a computer screen within seconds, providing a clear and detailed view of your teeth and surrounding structures.
