About Dr. Mark Oxley

Dr. Oxley has been a lifelong resident of Vidalia, Georgia. After graduating from Vidalia Comprehensive High School in 2009, he earned an Associate of Science Degree at Middle Georgia College. He continued his education at Georgia Institute of Technology, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology.

Dr. Oxley’s passion for helping people, his dexterity for close precision work, and his interest in photography are traits he observed as a very young boy in the life and work ethics of the adults in his family. After seeing the results of an extensive aptitude test, Dr. Oxley realized that through a career in dentistry, he could use his skills and passion to help better the lives of the people in his community. He has returned to live and work in his hometown — Vidalia, Georgia.

Dr. Oxley maintains membership in the American Dental Association (ADA), the Georgia Dental Association (GDA), the Central District Dental Society (CDDS), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) and the American Association for Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD). He is licensed and holds certificates in Invisalign Clear Braces and Basic Life Support (BLS).
