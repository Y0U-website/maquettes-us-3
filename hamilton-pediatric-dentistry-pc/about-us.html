0 About Our Doctor DR. VERONICA HAMILTON Dr. Veronica Hamilton is a dedicated pediatric dentist committed to providing exceptional dental care to infants, children, and adolescents. With a strong educational background and extensive experience in organized dentistry, Dr. Hamilton brings expertise and compassion to her practice.

Dr. Hamilton earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Michigan Honors College in 2004 before pursuing her Doctorate of Dental Surgery with Distinction from the University of Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2009. Following dental school, she completed a residency program at The Ohio State University in 2011, where she obtained a Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry and a Masters Degree in Pediatric Dentistry, serving as chief resident. She is a Diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, demonstrating her commitment to excellence in her field.

In addition to her clinical work, Dr. Hamilton is actively involved in organized dentistry. She has served as the Past President of the Michigan Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Kent County Dental Society, and has chaired the Michigan Dental Association Reference Committee. Dr. Hamilton has also represented her peers as a delegate to the Michigan Dental Association for multiple years.

Dr. Hamilton’s dedication and contributions to dentistry have been recognized with the Matt Uday New Dentist Award in 2016. She is a Fellow of prestigious honor societies including the American College of Dentists, the International College of Dentists, and the Pierre Fauchard Academy.

Dr. Hamilton is a member of several professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the Michigan Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is fluent in Spanish, allowing her to effectively communicate with a diverse patient population.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Hamilton enjoys staying active by running marathons and participating in triathlons. She is an avid fan of Carolina Basketball (Go Tarheels!) and finds relaxation by spending time on Lake Michigan. Dr. Hamilton grew up in Ada, Michigan, and is deeply committed to providing access to dental care for patients from infancy through adolescence.

With her comprehensive education, dedication to organized dentistry, and passion for patient care, Dr. Veronica Hamilton is a trusted advocate for children’s oral health in the Grand Rapids community and beyond.
