Comprehensive General Dentistry Services

At James W. Rehm DMD, LLC, we believe in the power of preventive dentistry. From the first tooth’s arrival, we recommend that every patient come in twice yearly for dental cleanings and check-ups. Why? Because prevention is key to maintaining a healthy smile for life.

Why Twice Every Year

Maintaining Overall Health

A healthy mouth is vital to maintaining overall health. Routine professional teeth cleanings and examinations should be part of everyone’s oral health plan.

Thorough Cleaning

During regular cleanings, our hygienist will remove plaque and tartar-harboring bacteria that can’t be eliminated by even the most extensive brushing and flossing.

Early Detection of Issues

Visiting the office twice per year lets us stay updated on any changes in your oral health. He can look out for any problem areas, such as decay, so they can be treated before they worsen.

Prevention of Advanced Procedures

Regular check-ups help identify issues early, potentially avoiding the need for more extensive treatments like fillings or crowns.

Last Resort Options

If a tooth is so badly damaged or decayed that it cannot be saved, we can discuss options such as tooth extraction.

Personalized and Detailed Care

Our team looks forward to getting to know you and your entire family. We believe in building lifelong relationships with our patients based on trust, respect, and personalized care. You’ll be greeted with warmth and compassion when you walk through our doors.
