The practice

Our gentle, modern techniques restore both function and confidence so your child can eat, speak, and smile with ease.

We offer safe, effective sedation options that help your child feel relaxed, comfortable, and completely at ease during their dental appointment.

As both a pediatric dentist and a mother, she treats each patient like family. She prioritizes comfort, trust, and long-term oral health, combining clinical expertise with a gentle approach to create a calm, reassuring environment where children feel safe and valued.

Born in the Philippines and raised in New Jersey, Dr. Brown earned her bachelor’s degree from The College of New Jersey before completing her Doctorate in Dental Medicine at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. It was there that she discovered her passion for working with children a natural extension of her lifelong love of being around kids. She went on to complete her pediatric dental residency…

Today, Dr. Brown is proud to provide pediatric dental care to families in Howell, NJ and the surrounding communities. Outside of the office, her faith and family remain central to her life. She enjoys spending time with her husband, their two children, and their beloved cats, finding joy in life’s simple moments.
