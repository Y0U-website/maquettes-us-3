Dr. Amy Thompson strives to maintain and uphold the highest level of excellence in her work as a dentist, and believes that patient satisfaction is the most important part of her job.

Dr. Amy has also completed advanced hands on training at the acclaimed Bioclear Learning Center on the Bioclear Method, a minimally invasive approach to cosmetic dentistry and tooth rejuvination procedures. She is certified in Advanced Anterior, Core Anterior and Core Posterior and has treated everything from small cosmetic procedures to full mouth make overs.
