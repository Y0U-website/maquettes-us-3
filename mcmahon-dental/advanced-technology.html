Precision Through Innovation

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

No temporaries. No waiting. Using advanced 3D scanning and in-house milling, we design and place custom ceramic crowns in a single visit with accuracy down to the micron.

CBCT 3D Imaging

Our 3D cone beam scanner gives us a complete view of your teeth, jaw, nerves, and airway helping us plan everything from implants to sleep apnea therapy with remarkable precision.

Pearl AI Diagnostic Technology

We integrate artificial intelligence into our imaging software for real-time diagnostics and accuracy ensuring early detection and confident treatment planning.

Primescan Digital Impressions

Say goodbye to goopy molds. Our digital scanner, the CEREC Primescan, captures detailed 3D models of your teeth in seconds for restorations, aligners, and smile design.

3D Printing & On-Site Lab

Our in-house 3D printer allows us to fabricate models, guides, and temporary restorations quickly improving accuracy and turnaround time for every treatment.

Digital Smile Design

Preview your future smile before treatment begins. We use imaging and modeling software to plan every detail of your restoration or cosmetic transformation.
