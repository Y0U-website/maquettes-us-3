JONATHAN K. DAVIS, DDS

Dr. Davis graduated from Versailles High School in 1995. He attended the University of Findlay and graduated Magna Cum Laude in 1999 with a Bachelors of Science degree. Dr. Davis went on to earn his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the Ohio State University in 2003.

Serving as a Captain with the U.S. Army Dental Corp. from 2003 to 2007, Dr. Davis was the 1st Brigade Dental Surgeon for three years and served a twelve-month tour-of-duty in Iraq where he was awarded The Bronze Star. While in the Army and after Honorable Discharge, Dr. Davis received extensive training in General Dentistry and its specialties. His resume includes comprehensive coursework at the Center for Occlusal Studies under Dr. Jay Gerber. Since then, he has also completed 20 hours of Continuing Education in Conscious Sedation Dentistry.

Dr. Davis is honored to have been the recipient of the 2006 Old Main Award from the University of Findlay, and he continues to stay active in the Findlay community. He currently serves on the Board for the Arts Partnership and is a member of the Findlay chapter of the Veterans of Foreign Wars. Dr. Davis is also a member of the Knights of Columbus and an active parishioner at St. Michael’s church in Findlay. He resides outside of Arlington with his lovely wife, Ashley, and his four sons,

Jonathan Jr. ( Deuce ), Keith, Colton, and Luke.
