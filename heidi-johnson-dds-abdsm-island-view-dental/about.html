About Us

About Us Meet Dr. Johnson Professional Dr. Heidi Johnson brings over 20 years of dental experience, with specialized expertise in Dental Sleep Medicine and TMJ. As a Diplomate of the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, she provides comprehensive care for patients of all ages.

Having come from a family with severe dental fears, Dr. Johnson is extremely empathetic and ensures quality and comfort for every patient. She has a special gift for making dentistry fun for children and helping anxious patients feel at ease.

Dr. Johnson is passionate about ongoing education and remains an active member of the Spear Study Club, American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, Snohomish County Dental Society, Washington State Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Johnson began her career as a dental assistant in Bellevue, where she fell in love with the art and science of dentistry. She earned her Bachelor of Science in general biology from the University of Washington, then completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Washington School of Dentistry—one of the world’s leading dental schools.

Throughout her career, Dr. Johnson has received rigorous mentorship from highly skilled dentists and prosthodontists in the greater Seattle area, staying abreast of the latest dental techniques and continuing education.

Dr. Johnson lives in Mukilteo with her family, including 8-year-old twins. She is dedicated to contributing to the community through volunteer work and is passionate about providing individualized, professional care with warmth and compassion.

She has always dreamed of continuing the tradition of compassionate, skilled, and ethical dentistry with her own practice, where every patient receives the personalized attention they deserve.
