Learn More About Dental Implants

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as artificial roots for replacement teeth. The jawbone bonds with the titanium, providing a sturdy foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. This process helps prevent bone loss in the jaw, maintains facial structure, and offers a durable solution that can last a lifetime with proper care. Dental implants are highly effective for replacing individual teeth or supporting larger dental prosthetics.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Choosing dental implants offers numerous advantages over other dental restorations. They closely resemble natural teeth in look, feel, and function, and they help prevent bone loss in the jaw, which other options cannot. Implants are long-lasting, often for a lifetime, and provide stability for adjacent teeth. Although initially more expensive, they are the most cost-effective over time due to their durability and the high success rate, which exceeds 90 %.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Almost anyone can be a candidate for dental implants. Candidates for dental implants typically have sufficient jawbone density and no serious medical conditions that could impair healing. Both young adults with missing teeth and older adults with partial or full tooth loss can be good candidates. It’s essential to have healthy gums and enough bone structure to support the implant.

Which are better – implants or dentures?

Whether implants or dentures are better depends on individual needs. Dental implants are more durable, closely mimic natural teeth, and help prevent bone loss, making them a long-term solution. Dentures, while less expensive upfront, may require adjustments over time and don’t prevent bone loss. Implants provide stability and comfort but require sufficient bone structure and a more involved procedure. Dentures are non-invasive but may not offer the same functionality or comfort. For a more detailed comparison call our office.

What are Teeth-In-A-Day?

Teeth-In-A-Day is a procedure where dental implants and restorations are done on the same day, often immediately after tooth extraction. This approach offers immediate teeth, faster healing, and eliminates the need for temporary dentures. It’s ideal for those needing multiple tooth extractions or who have experienced sudden tooth loss.
