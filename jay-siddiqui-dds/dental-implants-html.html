How we place your implant

Every implant case at our Chevy Chase office is digitally planned before the first incision. That means smaller procedures, more predictable outcomes, and restorations that fit on the first try.

Consult & 3D scan

A CBCT (cone beam) scan gives us a true 3D view of your bone, nerves, and sinuses. We combine it with an intraoral scan to plan in both anatomy and final crown shape.

Digital planning

Dr. Siddiqui designs the implant position virtually — choosing size, angle, and depth that optimize bone support and crown aesthetics before anything physical happens.

Guided placement

Using XNav dynamic navigation or a 3D-printed surgical guide, the implant is placed within fractions of a millimeter of its planned position. Local anesthesia, typically 60–90 minutes.

Healing & integration

With the modern implant system we use, healing can sometimes be as quick as 8 weeks — faster than the traditional 3–4 month timeline. Your bone biologically fuses to the titanium (osseointegration) during this window. In select cases, a temporary crown can be placed the same day.

Final crown

Your implant is restored with a fully custom abutment and crown — designed in Exocad and milled in-house on our DOF Craft 5X. Every case gets a custom abutment (never stock), designed, fit, and delivered in one visit.

Why the tools matter

Not all implant placements are equal. Freehand placement relies on the surgeon’s feel — which is fine in easy cases and risky in hard ones. Our digitally guided workflow removes that guesswork.

CBCT 3D imaging

We capture a complete 3D volume of your jaw so we can see every nerve and sinus before we plan — not during surgery.

XNav dynamic navigation

Real-time GPS-style tracking of the drill inside your jaw during surgery. Sub-millimeter accuracy, without the 2-week wait for a printed guide.

In-house milling

The final crown is designed in Exocad and milled on our 5-axis DOF Craft mill. No outsourcing, no sloppy margins, no re-dos.

Single-tooth implant restoration

A lower molar with a failing post-and-core restoration — extracted, grafted, and restored with a CBCT-planned implant and custom in-house crown. Designed, milled, and delivered without ever leaving our office.
