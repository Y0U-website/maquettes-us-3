Dr. Taimur Khan

Dr. Khan is a compassionate and highly trained dentist proudly serving the Bay Area, CA community with advanced cosmetic and restorative dental care since 2012.

He began his academic journey at the University of Toronto before earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the prestigious Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. To further refine his skills, Dr. Khan completed an intensive Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), with a focus on complex cosmetic and restorative procedures.

As a top-rated cosmetic dentist in Martinez , Dr. Khan believes in a conservative approach, preserving as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while delivering long-lasting, aesthetically pleasing results. His patient-first philosophy means he listens deeply, crafts personalized treatment plans, and prioritizes comfort at every step.

When he’s not transforming smiles, Dr. Khan enjoys hiking and exploring the outdoors with his wife and three kids. During the COVID-19 pandemic, he discovered a love for coffee and taught himself to create intricate latte art—turning every cup into a moment of creativity. He’s also an avid fan of the Golden State Warriors and Manchester United.

As a proud local resident, Dr. Khan regularly participates in community events and supports local charities—reflecting his passion for giving back to the Bay Area community he calls home.
