Looking To Discreetly Align Your Smile?

Clear aligners can gradually shift your teeth into the strong, straight smile of your dreams — without anyone noticing. If you’re looking for a metal and bracket-free approach to treatment, talk to your dentist about our clear aligner options.

Types of orthodontics for adults

Clear Aligners Clear aligners are made from durable, medical-grade plastic designed to gently shift teeth into place. They can address a wide range of orthodontic needs, from mild to more complex misalignment. Many adults choose clear aligners because they offer a subtle, nearly invisible way to achieve a straighter smile over time.

Metal Braces Traditional metal braces are one of the most common and affordable options to correct mild to severe tooth and jaw misalignment. These braces are composed of durable, medical-grade stainless steel brackets and archwires to produce a natural, straight smile.

How it works

Consultation

Consultation

To begin your consultation, your dentist will conduct a thorough examination of your jaw, teeth, and gums to address any misalignment or bite problems. We offer various smile alignment solutions, so your dentist can carefully select a custom treatment outline that best fits your unique needs.

Digital Imaging

Digital Imaging

X-rays, photographs, mouth measurements, and digital models will be taken so that your dentist can properly diagnose the problem and create a tailored treatment plan just for you. After the data has been gathered and assessed, your dentist will then design a custom dental appliance for your smile.

Placement and Fitting

Placement and Fitting

After the dental appliance is created, your dentist will place it in your mouth and make necessary adjustments to ensure proper fitting and comfort. Your dentist will then provide you with care and maintenance instructions to keep your appliance and smile in tip-top shape.

Next Steps

Next Steps

Visit us every 4-8 weeks to track progress, adjust your appliance, and follow up with any questions or concerns. While every orthodontic journey is unique, treatment can last anywhere from eighteen months to three years.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs that I may need orthodontic treatment?

If you’re struggling with overlapping, crowded, or gapping teeth, you may be a candidate for orthodontic treatment. Jaw issues such as crossbites, overbites, and underbites are also signs that orthodontic treatment may be right for you. If you’re looking to straighten your teeth for aesthetic reasons, you are more than welcome to schedule a complimentary consultation so we can get started on crafting the smile you’ve always wanted.

What are the benefits of orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatment is extremely beneficial for patients who are looking to smile with confidence and reduce the risk of future dental problems. With the newest advances in orthodontic technology, treatment is now less noticable and more comfortable than ever. Many dental appliance options are designed to minimize their appearance so you can continue to lead your lifestyle uninterrupted. While our dental health tends to naturally decrease with age, orthodontic treatment can prevent significant gum disease, tooth loss, and tooth decay later in life.

What causes orthodontic problems?

Many adult orthodontic problems are inherited and gradually worsen through the teenage years into adulthood. Other problems such as gum disease, childhood thumb sucking habits, early loss of baby or adult teeth, or even dental accidents may have significantly impacted your jaw or teeth alignment. Regardless of the cause, your dentist is dedicated to finding a tailored treatment solution so you can have the straightened smile you’ve always wanted.

Is it too late for me to receive orthodontic treatment?

It is never too late for you to receive the orthodontic care you deserve. Today, one in four orthodontic patients is an adult. Untreated misaligned teeth may lead to other problems later in life such as tooth decay, gum disease, or difficulty chewing. While many patients look to orthodontic solutions for aesthetic reasons, getting treatment is in your best interest for a lifetime of strong oral health.

How much does it cost?

A multitude of factors come into play when determining the cost of your adult orthodontic treatment. The cost varies depending on the severity of your misalignment, as well as appointment visits, insurance type, and more. Our team recommends scheduling a consultation for more personalized information.
