About Dr. G

Christopher Glynn, DDS

Dental care built on genuine relationships, delivered with excellence and comfort, and grounded in what’s proven — for every patient, every visit.

Professional photos coming soon Our Story How We Practice Meet the Team Contact Us Our Story A Practice Built on Doing Things Right Dr. Glynn began his career in 2007 under the mentorship of the practice’s founder, Dr. Jeffrey Aagaard. The most lasting lesson from that time was simple: treat the person, not just the patient. He served as an adjunct instructor at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry from 2011 to 2015, and that drive to learn and grow hasn’t slowed down.

Today, Dr. G continues to build on that foundation — modernizing the practice, assembling a team of compassionate and skilled people who share his standards, and expanding what we’re able to offer. The goal isn’t just excellent dentistry — it’s excellent dentistry delivered by people who genuinely care about the person in the chair.

Our Philosophy

One Dentist, Every Patient You’ll see Dr. G. He knows your history, your concerns, and what matters to you. That ongoing relationship is how we deliver care that’s truly personal.

Perfectionism Meets Practicality Care that meets the highest clinical standards while making sense for your life and goals. We offer a wide range of treatment and work closely with trusted specialists when a case calls for it.

The Same Standard, Every Time A simple filling gets the same focus as a full smile makeover. The complexity changes, the standard doesn’t.

Thorough, Not Rushed We schedule enough time to do things right — and to make sure you’re comfortable. Complete exams, thorough hygiene appointments, and time to talk. We don’t rush, and our patients feel the difference.

You’re Part of the Process We encourage patients to be active participants in their care. You’ll be educated and informed at every step — what we’re finding, what we recommend, and why.

Private Practice on Purpose No corporate office. No shareholders. No production quotas. The only thing driving our decisions is delivering great care — and we wouldn’t have it any other way.
