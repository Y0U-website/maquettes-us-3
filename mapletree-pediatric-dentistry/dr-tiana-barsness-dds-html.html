Dr Tiana Barsness DDS

Dr. Tiana Barsness grew up in Glenwood, MN and received her Bachelor of Science in Biology and Health Sciences from the University of Minnesota, Crookston. She then went on to obtain a Graduate Certificate in Public Health Education before completing her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota. She earned a certificate in Pediatric Dentistry from Hennepin Healthcare in Minneapolis, MN and is a Board Eligible Pediatric Dentist. She is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, and the Minnesota Dental Association.

Dr. Tiana and her high school sweetheart have two dogs whom they love to walk together. She enjoys exercise, exploring new cities and restaurants, game nights and summer on the lake.

I am passionate about prevention, education and positive dental experiences. I want to be an advocate for all patients and strive to continually provide the highest level of care for your child in a safe, comfortable and compassionate environment.
