What can I expect with all-on-x implants?

Your tooth-replacement journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. Using digital scans and X-rays, we will assess your oral health and determine if All-on-X is appropriate for your needs.

Getting All-on-X

We’ll keep you informed, explaining what the procedure involves and the steps to expect, including:

Precise Planning Using sophisticated 3-D imaging, we’ll map out the optimal placement of your implants and create a surgical guide to ensure precise results and long-term implant success.

Implant Placement First, we will carefully extract any remaining teeth, if necessary. Next, the dentist will expertly place the pre-determined number of implants in your jawbone. We offer sedation options for your complete comfort, including nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation.

Temporary Denture We attach a temporary denture to the implants, so you don’t have to go without teeth and can leave our office the same day with a complete smile.

Final Restoration Over the next several months, the implants fuse with your bone. This process is called osseointegration . We will monitor your progress and, when healing is complete, attach a custom-made permanent denture to complete your smile transformation.

How do dentists decide which teeth can be saved?

An experienced dentist can determine whether a tooth can be saved. Criteria may include the nature of a tooth break (split vs. straight across) and the extent of damage. It’s also difficult or impossible to save a tooth broken below the gumline.

If we can save a tooth, we typically treat it and then cap it with a dental crown to restore strength. In some cases, a same-day dental crown may be appropriate.

When Your Tooth Cannot Be Saved

The dentist will assess your tooth carefully and take measures to save it when appropriate. However, if your tooth cannot be saved, we strongly encourage tooth replacement.
