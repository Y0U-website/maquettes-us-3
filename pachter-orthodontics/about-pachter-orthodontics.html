Welcome to Pachter Orthodontics

We’re Spreading POsitive ℠ Vibes.

Our mission is to make every person in our community smile by spreading POsitive℠ Vibes. But what does this mean exactly?

Well, first of all, it means treating every single person we encounter with warmth and respect. It means getting to know our patients on a human-to-human level. By understanding their fears, doubts, hopes, and dreams, we can build a healthy relationship that will not only deliver the best treatment results but create a friendship that lasts a lifetime.

We are a kind yet energetic culture (so get ready to laugh, and maybe dance). Both inside and outside of the office, we’re dedicated to inclusivity and anti-bullying. We believe that we have the ability to spread POsitivity ℠ into every nook and cranny of our community and this will play a role in what makes our corner of the world really shine.

Our POsitive℠ Values

At Pachter Orthodontics, it goes without saying that we value our patients and their smiles. But at the core of who we are, what are the principles that guide us every day?

1) R.E.S.P.E.C.T.! – (Thank you, Aretha) We believe in facing every situation with an empathetic and relatable mindset where we are inclusive and open to all opinions, thoughts, beliefs, and concerns – no matter how different they are from our own.

2) Do the Right THANG – No matter who is looking, we believe in always doing what is right. Our decisions are always made with the best interest of the patient and their entire situation.

3) Spice it Up – If Fun could be our middle name, we’d totally make it. We are passionate about our vibrant and inviting culture and we use high energy to fuel our workday and our patients’ POsitive ℠ experiences.

4) Give’em More – We are dedicated to giving our time, talent, and resources to better ourselves and our community as a whole.

POsitive℠ Office Space

Positivity goes past our smiley faces and cheerful greetings; we want you to feel at ease just being in our office. From our waiting room to the treatment area, our experience is carefully curated to make sure that you feel warm, welcomed, and comfortable.
