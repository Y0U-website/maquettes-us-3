FAQs About Implant-Supported Dentures

  • What are implant-supported dentures, and how do they work? Implant-supported dentures are a type of overdenture that’s anchored by dental implants instead of just resting on the gums. Titanium implants are surgically placed in the jawbone, and the denture attaches to them for added security. This setup improves bite strength and eliminates the slipping often associated with traditional dentures.
  • How many implants are needed to support a full denture? Most full-arch implant-supported dentures require four to six implants per arch. The exact number depends on your bone density, oral health, and the type of denture system used (fixed or removable).
  • Does Panneton Dental Group offer implant-supported dentures with advanced technology? Yes. Panneton Dental Group uses the latest digital imaging, guided surgery techniques, and high-quality implant systems to ensure precision, comfort, and lasting results for their implant denture patients.

More Questions About Implant Dentures

  • Are implant-supported dentures more comfortable than traditional dentures? Yes. Because they are anchored to implants rather than resting on the gums, these dentures don’t shift or rub, resulting in less irritation and a more natural feel. They also restore better chewing function and prevent bone loss in the jaw.
  • What’s the difference between fixed and removable implant dentures? Fixed implant dentures are permanently attached and can only be removed by a dentist, offering the most stability and natural feel. Removable versions snap onto implants and can be taken out for cleaning. The choice depends on lifestyle, budget, and oral health factors.

Panneton Dental Group regularly helps patients transition from loose, uncomfortable dentures to secure, custom-fit implant-supported options using modern tools and personalized treatment plans.

The Benefits of Implant Dentures for Long-Term Health

Implant-supported dentures aren’t just about comfort they help preserve your jawbone, restore your bite function, and boost confidence in social settings. Unlike traditional dentures, which sit on the gums and accelerate bone loss, implants stimulate the jaw like natural teeth. This prevents facial collapse and maintains a youthful appearance. Implant dentures are also more stable for eating and speaking, and they often last longer with proper care.
