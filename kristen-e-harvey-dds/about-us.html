Meet the Team at White Park Dental

Kristen E. Harvey, DDS, MAGD

After dental school, Dr. Harvey completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) program at the Audie L. Murphy Veterans Affairs hospital in San Antonio, Texas. This year-long residency offered advanced training in implant placement and restoration, Invisalign orthodontic therapy, sleep apnea appliances and conscious sedation. She also became proficient in cosmetic diagnosis and placement of all-ceramic crowns and veneers. After residency, she performed comprehensive dentistry in private practice for five years in Pennsylvania before becoming established in New Hampshire.

In her free time, Dr. Harvey enjoys outdoor activities, including camping, hiking, kayaking, snowboarding, and spending time with her husband and daughter. She is excited to have chosen Concord, New Hampshire to establish herself, being a New England native from Maine. She looks forward to getting to know you on an individual basis in order to help you achieve your optimal oral health and confident smile.

  • National Board of Dental Examinations and North East Regional Board of Examinations
  • American Dental Association and New Hampshire Dental Association
  • Academy of General Dentistry, Mastership status

Katrina, RDH

Katrina’s passion for the dental world started at the pediatric dental office where she went as a child. Going to get her teeth cleaned was always exciting for her if you can believe that. Those happy memories are what drives Katrina to do her best work and to ensure patient comfort at White Park Dental.

Katrina graduated with a bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene from the University of New England. While at UNE, Katrina was a member of the cross country and track teams.

Prior to White Park Dental, Katrina worked in Brattleboro, Vermont. While there, she learned how to handle difficult scaling and root planing cases, how to use the iTero scanner for Invisalign cases, as well as finding an interest in products like MI Paste and GC Dry Mouth Gel.

Katrina has appreciated the White Park Dental team’s welcoming atmosphere and is eager to meet new patients! In her spare time, the Westmoreland native enjoys running, hiking, skiing, reading, and knitting. When time allows, her favorite place to relax is at her family’s cottage in Sheepscot, Maine.

Olivia

Born and raised in Central New Hampshire, Olivia graduated from Saint Anselm College in May 2025 with a degree in Neuroscience and a minor in Chemistry. As a pre-dental student, she focused on the link between oral and brain health, particularly neurodegenerative diseases. She presented her research at conferences throughout New England and served as captain of the women’s varsity tennis team.

Olivia has received several honors, including the Delta Dental STEM Award and the Women in Leadership Award through the Miss America Organization. As a passionate advocate for the Alzheimer’s Association, she has helped write and promote legislation to support patients and caregivers across New Hampshire, contributing to public health efforts at both the New Hampshire State House and on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C.

She is currently attending the Northeast Collaborative School of Dental Assisting and is thrilled to be part of the White Park Dental team. Olivia is committed to providing compassionate, personalized care while continuing to pursue her long-term goals in dentistry.

Meghan

Meghan was born and raised right here in Concord, NH. She attended schools in the Concord School District to then become a student at the University of New Hampshire. There, Meghan majored in Psychology with a minor in Sociology.

Prior to this practice, Meghan had no dental experience whatsoever. But it was Meghan s love for people that drew her to this field. Meghan came from a nine-year career with Nike and wanted to apply her customer relationship skills in a different way. She is passionate about helping people and loves making connections with new patients!

Meghan is very close with her family and loves to spend time with her grandparents, parents, brother, and extended family and friends. She is happiest in the summertime, especially near a lake, where she can camp, kayak, fish, or simply relax with some close family and friends. When it comes to Sundays in the fall, you will find Meghan in a jersey in front of the TV! Meghan is happy to watch any game during the NFL season, but having grown up with a Patriots-loving father and a Raider-fanatic mother, she is most passionate about these two teams.
