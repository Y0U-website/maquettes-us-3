Meet The Doctors

Dr. Francis’s vision is to provide comfortable, compassionate, and comprehensive care to all her patients.

She is a graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in Columbus, Ohio. As a proud Buckeye, Ohio State holds a special place in her heart after spending eight years there. She earned her undergraduate degree in Biology, served as a student instructor during dental school, and was an active leader among her peers.

Dr. Francis is passionate about organized dentistry and is a current member of the American Dental Association , Ohio Dental Association , and the Western Ohio Dental Association . She previously served on the Subcouncil on New Dentists for the Ohio Dental Association.

While attending Ohio State, Dr. Francis was actively involved in clinical research at the James Cancer Hospital and Solove Research Institute , where she developed a deep appreciation for lifelong learning. She is committed to staying current with the latest research and advancements in dentistry to provide the highest quality care for her patients.

Family is a core value in Dr. Francis’s life, which is why returning to western Ohio to care for her community like family was a natural decision. She values the multigenerational relationships that dentistry creates and looks forward to helping patients of all ages achieve healthy, confident smiles. Her compassionate nature and commitment to clinical excellence help put even the most anxious patients at ease.

Originally from Fort Loramie, Ohio , Dr. Francis now resides in Russia, Ohio , with her husband, Trent, and their three children. Outside the office, they enjoy spending time with their large extended families, cooking, gardening during the summer, skiing in the winter, and visiting National Parks whenever they have the opportunity to travel.
