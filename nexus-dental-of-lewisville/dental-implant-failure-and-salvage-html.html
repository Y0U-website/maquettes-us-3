Why Do Dental Implants Fail?

Dental implant failure can often be attributed to one of the following issues:

  • Infection: Harmful bacteria can attack the tissues around your implant posts, causing them to deteriorate over time. This can ultimately weaken your jawbone and gum tissues to the point where they can’t properly support your dental implants anymore.
  • Failed Osseointegration: Once dental implants are inserted into your jaw, a natural process known as osseointegration allows them to bond with the bone around them. If osseointegration fails to take place, the result is an unstable dental implant.
  • Physical Trauma: Getting hit in the face or mouth could result in your dental implant breaking. It could also damage your gum and bone tissues, causing the implant post to lose its support.

Symptoms of Failed Dental Implants

You may be experiencing dental implant failure if you have noticed any of these warning signs:

  • The area around the implant posts is in serious pain.
  • One or more of your implant posts feels loose or has fallen out.
  • You are experiencing bleeding and swelling of the gums (which could mean that an infection is present).
  • You have had a hard time chewing your food recently.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

Step one of the dental implant salvage process is for our team to thoroughly examine your dental implants and the area around them. Once we’ve identified the problem, we can take appropriate measures to treat it. For example, if there’s an infection, we may be able to address it with a deep cleaning.

Sometimes it may be possible for you to keep your current dental implants. In other cases, the implant posts might need to be removed, but you could be eligible to have new ones placed once your mouth is healthy again.
