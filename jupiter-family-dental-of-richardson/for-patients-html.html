We Welcome Medicaid and CHIP

Our practice welcomes Medicaid and CHIP. If your family has either one of these types of coverage, we invite you to get in touch with us to learn how you can use your benefits in our office. We will do all we can to help you understand your plan and use it in the most advantageous way possible.

Cherry Financing

We’re proud to offer Cherry Financing to help make dental care more affordable and accessible. Cherry provides flexible monthly payment options, allowing patients to move forward with recommended treatment without having to pay the full cost upfront. With a quick application process, Cherry makes it easier to fit quality dental care into your budget.

CareCredit Financing Available

When multiple services are required or your dental insurance company will not pay for a specific treatment, you have the option of enrolling in a low or no-interest payment plan through CareCredit. With no surprise fees, you can make life much easier by breaking up the cost of your treatment into monthly payments that allow you to stay within your budget.

Sunbit Payment Plan

If you wish to pay for treatment over time, instead of all at once, Sunbit enables you to finance the total and divide it into monthly payments that are easier on your wallet. To set you up, Sunbit will make a soft inquiry for your credit report, which means it shouldn’t negatively impact your score. Are you worried that you don’t have good credit? No worries! Sunbit approves about 90 percent of applicants and responds quickly so you don’t have to wait to begin treatment!
