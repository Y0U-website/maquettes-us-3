Dr. Michael Layton, DMD - Dentist

Dr. Layton was born in Alabama and grew up in Orlando. He received his Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration from the University of Central Florida and his DMD degree from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 1987. He opened Lakeshore Dental Care in 1989.

He loves the art and science involved in dentistry and regularly improves his skills and knowledge through continuing education.

Dr. Layton enjoys spending time with his family and pets, likes landscaping and participates in High Performance Driving events on road courses in the Southeast.
