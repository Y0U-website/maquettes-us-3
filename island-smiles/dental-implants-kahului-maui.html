What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Procedure?

Receiving a dental implant is a multi-step process designed to ensure a successful and lasting restoration of your smile.

  • Consultation: We conduct a thorough examination, including X-rays, to determine your eligibility for implants.
  • Surgical Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as the root for your new tooth.
  • Healing Period: A healing period follows, allowing the implant to integrate with the jawbone for stability.
  • Abutment Placement: Once healed, an abutment is placed on the implant to support the crown.
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made crown is attached to the abutment, completing the restoration.

Aftercare Tips for Dental Implants.

To ensure a smooth recovery after your dental implant procedure, follow these tips:

Our team is always available to answer any questions and ensure a successful recovery.

  • Rest and Recover: Take it easy for the first few days.
  • Mind Your Diet: Stick to soft foods and avoid hard or crunchy items.
  • Maintain Oral Hygiene: Gently brush and floss to keep the implant site clean.
  • Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water to aid healing.
  • Watch for Signs: Contact us if you notice any swelling, pain, or discomfort.
