The practice

Dr. Talia Just, board-certified by the American Board of Orofacial Pain, graduated magna cum laude from Southern Illinois School of Dental Medicine in 2023. She then went on to complete a research-based residency in Orofacial Pain at the University of Minnesota, where she was chief resident.

She dreamt up Just TMJ & Sleep Center after seeing patients carrying around years of stress and tension, impacting their entire lives. She is passionate about empowering her patients to share their unique stories and using this information to create a treatment plan tailored to each individual.

What sets Dr. Just apart is not only her deep expertise in orofacial pain, but also her commitment to staying at the forefront of her field. She continuously explores innovative treatment modalities to provide the best possible pain management for her patients. Under her care, I received a specialized botox injection protocol that significantly helped manage my pain, and I always felt confident…

Dr. Just is the complete package: brilliant, compassionate, and dedicated to her patients’ wellbeing. She is simply outstanding, and I felt fortunate to have her as my provider.

Chronic pain can cause people to feel isolated, but we want you to know you’re not alone. Just TMJ & Sleep Center is here to listen and equip you with the tools you need to live a fuller life.

Dr. Just is a highly experienced and research-driven orofacial pain specialist with a passion for precise diagnosis and individualized treatment. Dedicated to providing expert care for patients suffering from orofacial pain, TMJ disorders, and sleep-related conditions, she passionately believes that each patient is an individual and should be treated as such.

In her spare time, Dr. Just loves to read, go to yoga or barre classes, and try new restaurants. She is married to her wonderful husband, Ryan, who serves in the United States Air Force. They both love to be involved in their local community, and you may even see Ryan coaching lacrosse or CrossFit!
