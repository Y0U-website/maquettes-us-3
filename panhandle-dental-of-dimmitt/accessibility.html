Accessibility Statement

Panhandle Dental is committed to making its digital information and services welcoming and usable for everyone.

Our accessibility goal

We aim to provide a website that aligns with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA to the greatest technically achievable extent. Accessibility is an ongoing practice, and we continue to review the experience as content and technology change.

Need assistance?

If you experience a barrier, please contact the office nearest you. We will make a reasonable effort to provide the information or service through an accessible alternative.
