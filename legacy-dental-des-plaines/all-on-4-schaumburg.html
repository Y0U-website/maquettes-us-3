What All-on-4 / All-on-X actually means.

Many cases allow a temporary fixed set of teeth on the same day as surgery, with the final restoration delivered after healing. The exact timeline depends on bone quality, healing, and the materials used for the final teeth.

Who is a good candidate.

Adults who have lost most or all of the teeth in an arch, or who are facing extensive extractions and want a fixed solution rather than dentures, are typical candidates. Patients with healthy enough bone to support implants can usually be treated; those with severe bone loss may need grafting first.

The only way to know whether All-on-X is right for you is an in-person examination with 3D imaging. We will tell you honestly - no online promises.

Why a written plan matters more than a same-day promise.

Advertising sometimes promises a finished, polished smile in 24 hours. The honest version: many cases deliver a temporary fixed set of teeth quickly, but the final restoration - the one you will keep - is delivered after healing.

We will not promise an outcome before examining you. What we promise is a written plan with timeline, sequence, and estimate at the consultation, so you can decide whether to move forward with full information.

What it costs in Schaumburg.

Full-arch implant costs vary widely depending on the number of implants, bone work needed, materials in the final restoration, and whether one arch or both are being treated. We share the full estimate at the consultation.

Most dental insurance plans cover only a small portion of full-arch implant treatment. We review your specific benefits before treatment begins and discuss financing options if needed.
