Key differences between All-on-4 implants and All-on-X dental implants include:

  • Same-day function – temporary teeth are usually placed the same day or within 24 hours.
  • Avoidance of traditional dentures – both options use implants instead of adhesives or bulky acrylic bases, restoring confidence and function.
  • All-on-4 implants use exactly 4 implants, while All-on-X uses 4-6 implants customized to the patient s needs.
  • All-on-X implants are better suited for complex cases involving bone loss or lack of support/stability. - All-on-4 is for less complex cases for patients with adequate bones in the front and back of the jaw.
  • All-on-4 implants are typically cheaper and have quicker surgical and recovery time than all-on-x due to fewer implants being placed.

When is All-on-X Recommended Over All-on-4 Implants?

  • Increased bite force and load distribution – Additional implants (All-on-5 or All-on-6) allow chewing forces to be distributed across more implant units, enabling patients to eat a broader range of foods.
  • Improved bone stimulation – Implants transmit forces into the jawbone, stimulating bone and helping prevent resorption; more implants engage more bone and may slow bone shrinkage.
  • Greater prosthetic support – More implants provide a wider base for the prosthesis, reducing the risk of wear or fracture and potentially extending the lifespan of the restoration.
  • Adaptability for compromised bone – Patients with significant bone loss, uneven bone quality, or a history of implant failure may benefit from extra implants that increase stability and reduce the need for bone grafting.
  • Future proofing – Additional implants can "future proof" the prosthesis; if a single implant fails, the remaining implants may still support the bridge, reducing the need for full replacement.

Am I a Good Candidate for Full Mouth Dental Implants?

You may be a good candidate for All-on-4 or All-on-X if:

  • You are missing most or all of your teeth in the upper or lower jaw.
  • You have worn dentures and are looking for a more permanent solution.
  • You have adequate jawbone to support the implants (or can avoid bone grafting with angled placement).
  • You are in good general health and do not smoke or are willing to quit.

The All-on-4 and All-on-X Full Mouth Dental Implant Procedure

The process starts with a detailed consultation and digital imaging to plan your custom treatment. On the day of your surgery, if available/elected, your dentist will administer sedation to ensure you are relaxed during your procedure. Your dentist will then place the implants into your jaw. In most cases, a temporary set of teeth is attached the same day.

The entire process is efficient and minimally invasive. Most patients return to daily activities within a few days.

Depending on your treatment plan, after a few months of healing, when the implants fully integrate with your bone, your dentist may recommend a final zirconia prosthesis be placed to complete treatment.
