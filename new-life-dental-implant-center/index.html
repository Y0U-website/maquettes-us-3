The practice

Bone health, tissue health and systemic health need to be considered when performing implant surgery to ensure long-lasting success. This is how what makes us different at New Life Dental Implant Center.

Every Restoration is custom and unique to each individual. The final restoration results will vary based on individual unique anatomy, dimensions, prosthetic needs and preferences.

Bone Grafting We perform bone grafting not only to fix voids but to rebuild and replace anatomy that had been lost over the years. This also ensures full coverage of all dental implants for long-term success.

6 implants vs. 4 implants We place more implants which gives us more primary & long-term strength. 4 implants leaves no room for failure. Studies show long-term survival is highest with 6 implants compared to 4.

Anesthesiologist We work with anesthesia providers to perform the dental sedation. Our surgeons focus entirely on surgery while an MD Medical Anesthesiologist provides safe sedation. Our patients can enjoy sleeping through the entire procedure.

One Price The entire procedure is one price from start to finish. There is no extra fees. Because we have an in-house laboratory, we can compile all fees together and provide you a set fee regardless of what may be required through the process.
