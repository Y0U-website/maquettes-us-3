A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap that covers a damaged tooth to restore its shape, size, strength, and appearance. Crowns are recommended for:

Protecting a weak or cracked tooth Restoring a broken or worn tooth Covering a tooth after root canal Holding a dental bridge in place Covering a dental implant Improving the appearance of discolored teeth Premium Materials We Use Emax Crowns Made from lithium disilicate ceramic, Emax crowns offer exceptional aesthetics and strength. Zirconia Crowns

Extremely durable and strong, zirconia crowns are ideal for back teeth (molars) that endure heavy chewing forces. They&#x27;re biocompatible, stain-resistant, and can last 15-20 years or more.
