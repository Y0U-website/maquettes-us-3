Dental Implants

  • Durability : With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, offering a reliable solution for tooth replacement.
  • Improved Oral Health : Implants help maintain jawbone structure, preventing the bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.
  • Enhanced Comfort and Function : Implants allow for comfortable chewing and speaking without the slipping or discomfort of dentures. ‍
  • Preservation of Facial Structure : By preventing bone loss, implants help retain the natural shape of the face.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants have healthy gums and adequate bone density to support the implant. In cases where bone density is insufficient, our team may recommend bone grafting to strengthen the jaw and prepare it for implant placement.

During your consultation, we will perform a thorough evaluation using advanced imaging technology, such as the i-CAT™ Cone Beam Scanner , to assess bone health and determine the most effective treatment plan.

Types of Dental Implant Procedures

  • Single Tooth Implants : For patients missing a single tooth, a single implant can be placed to hold a crown, restoring the appearance and function of the natural tooth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges : If you are missing multiple teeth in a row, implant-supported bridges can replace them without needing separate implants for each missing tooth, minimizing impact on neighboring teeth. ‍
  • All-On-4 Dental Implants (Teeth-In-A-Day) : For patients needing a full arch replacement, All-On-4 Dental Implants provide a stable, natural-looking set of teeth in a single day. This innovative solution places four implants that support an entire arch, offering full functionality and aesthetics.

The Dental Implant Process

To further enhance healing, we may use Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) , which uses the patient’s own natural growth factors to accelerate tissue regeneration and reduce recovery time.

  • Consultation and Planning : Our team will conduct a comprehensive consultation, including imaging and treatment planning, to determine the best approach for your dental implant needs.
  • Preparation : If bone grafting or other preparatory treatments are necessary, we will address these first to ensure a solid foundation for the implant.
  • Implant Placement : Using the latest techniques and technology, our surgeons precisely place the titanium implant into the jawbone. We offer Anesthesia & IV Sedation to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.
  • Healing and Integration : Over the next several months, the implant will integrate with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a durable base for the replacement tooth.
  • Final Restoration : Once healing is complete, an abutment and crown are attached to the implant, restoring full function and aesthetics to your smile.
