December 2015 - A great time was had by all at the Volunteer Banquet.

Volunteers participate in community outreach

The Lord calls us to serve and if you have a desire to become a committed member of the Healing Hands volunteer family we would love to have you join us on the local mission field. The Clinic is in need of Physicians, nurse Practitioners, Physician Assistants, Front Office Personnel, support Staff and Prayer Warriors. MEDICAL TRAINING IS NOT A REQUIREMENT TO SERVE AT HEALING HANDS. We encourage the youth of the area who may be considering a career in the medical field to serve in the Clinic. It is a wonderful opportunity to gain experience and to give back to the community.

If you would like to become a volunteer, please stop by the clinic during regular office hours and see our Volunteer Coordinator Sandy Sharrock for an application. Once she has received your completed application, she will contact you for an interview.
