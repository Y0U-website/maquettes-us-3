Our Dental Implant Process

We also offer bone grafting and tooth extractions in-office, if needed before placement.

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging We assess your jaw health and bone density to determine your candidacy.
  • Surgical Placement The implant is gently placed in the jawbone. Healing typically takes 3–6 months.
  • Restoration Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached—designed to match your natural teeth.
