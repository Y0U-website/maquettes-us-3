New Patients

Your comfort and well-being are our top priorities, and we’re dedicated to providing you with personalized care tailored to your unique needs. Whether you’re visiting us for a routine check-up or addressing a dental concern, our team is here to ensure your experience is positive and stress-free. We’re excited to have you join our dental family!

Financing & Insurance

At Grey Oak Dental, we understand that navigating dental expenses and insurance can be complex. That’s why we strive to make the financial aspect of your dental care as seamless as possible. Our team is here to assist you in understanding your insurance coverage and exploring available financing options. We believe everyone deserves access to quality dental care, and we’re committed to helping you achieve optimal oral health while respecting your budgetary needs. Feel free to reach out to us with any questions or concerns regarding financing or insurance—we’re here to help.

In-House Membership

Introducing our GO Plan, a comprehensive in-house dental insurance option designed to provide our patients with substantial discounts on a wide range of dental treatments. With coverage spanning from routine cleanings to intricate procedures, our plan offers reduced rates, ensuring accessible and high-quality oral healthcare without financial burden.

Emergencies

We recognize the anxiety and discomfort that dental emergencies can bring, but please know that we’re here to help. Dental emergencies can involve various situations, such as a knocked-out tooth, problems with dental procedures, or persistent toothaches. If you’re unsure whether your situation constitutes a dental emergency, it’s best to contact us promptly, as your well-being is our top priority, and we’re committed to providing the care you need when you need it.
