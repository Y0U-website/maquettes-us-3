Technology

Dr. Joshua Bagley believes investing in modern dental technology improves patient experiences and superior treatment outcomes. By integrating state-of-the-art equipment with his patient-centered approach, he can deliver more comfortable, efficient, and precise care. Explore the advanced technologies that help make your visits to Morris Plains Center for Dentistry as pleasant and effective as possible.

Intraoral Cameras

This pen-sized digital camera allows Dr. Bagley to capture detailed images of your teeth and gums, instantly displaying them on a screen you can view together. This technology transforms how we communicate about your oral health showing you exactly what concerns need addressing. Patients appreciate seeing their own teeth, which leads to a better understanding of treatment recommendations and more informed decisions.

Digital Impressions

Say goodbye to uncomfortable, gag-inducing putty impressions! Our digital scanning technology creates precise 3-D models of your teeth without messy materials. Used for crowns, bridges, clear aligners, and other restorations, digital impressions improve comfort while delivering superior accuracy. This technology reduces appointment time, eliminates retakes, and enables faster fabrication of custom dental work for better-fitting results.

Laser Teeth Whitening

Our advanced Zoom! laser whitening technology accelerates the bleaching process, producing dramatic whitening results in just one visit. The specialized light activates the professional-strength whitening gel, breaking down stubborn stains from coffee, wine, and tobacco.

This safe, efficient system brightens teeth by up to eight shades in one sitting while minimizing sensitivity. Fast laser whitening is perfect for patients seeking immediate results for special events like weddings, reunions, or job interviews.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation (Laughing Gas)

Also known as "laughing gas," nitrous oxide is a mild sedative that creates feelings of relaxation and comfort without putting you to sleep. Inhaled through a comfortable nose mask, this safe sedation option works quickly, wears off rapidly, and allows you to drive yourself home afterward. Perfect for patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing longer procedures, nitrous oxide helps make dental visits stress-free.

Digital Records

Our paperless, secure digital record system ensures your complete dental history, treatment plans, and insurance information are instantly accessible and protected. Digital records improve coordination of your care, eliminate lost files, and reduce wait times.

This environment-friendly approach also allows Dr. Bagley to share visual information during appointments, enhancing your understanding of treatment recommendations.

Digital X-Rays and Sensors

Dr. Bagley uses advanced digital x-ray technology that provides detailed images instantly, while reducing radiation exposure by up to 90 percent compared to traditional film. With exceptional clarity, these high-resolution images help detect cavities, infections, and bone loss.

Used during examinations and treatment planning, digital x-rays deliver more precise diagnostics in less time while prioritizing your safety and minimizing environmental waste.

TVs in Treatment Rooms

Our treatment rooms feature televisions with access to Netflix and Roku streaming. This amenity provides a welcome distraction during procedures, helping reduce anxiety and making time pass more quickly. Whether you re in for a routine cleaning or a longer procedure, enjoying your favorite show creates a more relaxing, comfortable experience at Morris Plains Center for Dentistry.

Noise-Canceling Headphones

Our noise-canceling headphones eliminate the sounds of dental equipment that many patients find unsettling. Combined with your choice of music or streaming content, this technology creates a cocoon of comfort during procedures. These headphones are especially beneficial for patients with dental anxiety, sensitive hearing, or those undergoing longer treatments. They transform the traditional dental experience into a more peaceful one.
