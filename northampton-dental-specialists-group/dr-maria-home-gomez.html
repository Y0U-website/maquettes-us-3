Meet Our Northampton, MA Dentist, Doctor Maria Home-Gomez

Dr. Maria Home-Gomez

Dr. Maria Home-Gomez

Dr. Maria Home-Gomez is a dedicated and compassionate associate dentist who earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. With a strong foundation in restorative dentistry, she is passionate about improving her patients’ oral health through personalized, evidence-based care.

Before dental school, Dr. Home-Gomez graduated summa cum laude from Saint Peter’s University with a Bachelor’s degree in Biology and a minor in Chemistry. Her academic excellence and dedication to science laid the groundwork for her thoughtful, evidence-based approach to dentistry.

Dr. Home-Gomez believes in creating a comfortable and collaborative environment where patients feel empowered to take an active role in their dental care. Her approach emphasizes overall well-being, patient education, and a gentle touch that helps even the most anxious patients feel at ease.

She serves as vice president of the Colombian Chapter of the American Academy of Women Dentists (AAWD). She is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Massachusetts Dental Society (MDS), and she remains committed to ongoing learning and service. Outside the office, Dr. Home-Gomez enjoys volunteering in her community and cherishing time with her family.
