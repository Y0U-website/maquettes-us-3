Services

Preventative Dental Care

The best way to treat dental problems is to never have them in the first place. At Paradise Valley Family Dental, we love healthy smiles.

Full Mouth Reconstruction & Smile Design

At Paradise Valley Family Dental, you can trust all your family’s oral health needs can be cared for under one roof.

Dental Implants

Dental implants serve a variety of functions. From improving the look of your smile, to helping you eat and speak.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is a safe and effective way to brighten your smile.

Invisalign®

A set of custom made comfortable clear aligners that gradually straighten your teeth.

Crowns and Bridges

Crowns restore teeth to its natural shape and size. Bridges replace teeth that are missing.

Dentures and Partial Dentures

Complete dentures replace all teeth and partial dentures replace a few missing teeth.

Oral Hygiene and Gum Therapy

Oral hygiene is important for overall health. Gum therapy treats periodontal disease.

Porcelain Crowns

Veneers cover the front surface of teeth with a layer of porcelain custom made for you.

Oral Surgery (Wisdom Teeth)

We can perform in-house oral surgery for wisdom teeth removal.

Root Canal Therapy (Endodontics)

Root canal therapy can save a tooth when the nerves are diseased and damaged.

Fillings (Composite resin fillings)

Composite fillings are bonded to your teeth for a strong natural look.

Oral Sedation

We offer oral sedation so you can relax during your appointment.

Mouthguards

Custom fit night guards and sport mouthguards protect your teeth.

Electric Toothbrush

Oral-B® electric toothbrush removes 100 % more plaque than a regular brush.

Periodontal Therapy

Laser-Assisted Periodontal therapy is an innovative treatment for periodontal disease.

Ultrasonic Scalers

The latest generation Cavitron Plus scalers for improved patient comfort.

Oral-ID Cancer Screening

The dreaded disease of cancer is much more treatable if it’s caught early.

IntraOral Camera

The camera delivers clear, high-resolution images that can be shared with patients.

Low Radiation Digital X-rays

In Dentistry we need to X-ray jaw bones and tooth structures, Lower Radiation = Safer.
