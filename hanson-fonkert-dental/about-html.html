About us

Hanson and Fonkert Dental was started in 1991 by Mike Hanson and Jodi-Fonkert Hanson. The married team have expanded their clinic from regular dentistry to now include a variety of specialty services, which include cosmetic dentistry and much more. They have 2 girls, Sam and Jessa and reside in Spicer, MN. They enjoy spending time with family and friends on the water and boating.
