Meet the Team at Hamlin Dental

Welcome to Hamlin Dental! Our dental practice was started in 1984 by Dr. Robert Lang, and we ve proudly been serving patients in Hamlin, NY and the surrounding communities of Brockport , Murray, and Hilton ever since. In 2019, Dr. Nicholas Garcia joined us, and after four years of mentorship with Dr. Lang, he now leads our team.

Our team loves caring for patients, and many of us have worked together for years! Our common goal is to deliver exceptional dentistry and an excellent experience for every patient each time they visit us.

Get to know more about us below, and we look forward to getting to know you when you visit Hamlin Dental.

Dr. Nicholas Garcia

Advanced Dental Training

Dr. Garcia is committed to bringing his patients the most advanced technology and techniques. As a result, he invests hundreds of hours in completing continuing education with CEREC Doctors (CDOCS), Spear Education, and the Resnik Implant Institute. He is a CEREC Doctors mentor and a member of the Spear Study Club , American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, New York State Dental Association, and Seventh District Dental Society.

Although he enjoys the technical part of dentistry, Dr. Garcia s favorite thing about being a dentist is getting to know his patients and their families.

When he s not caring for patients, Dr. Garcia enjoys playing soccer, running, biking, and cooking with his wife Morgan. They frequently travel to visit her family in Lawton, Oklahoma.
