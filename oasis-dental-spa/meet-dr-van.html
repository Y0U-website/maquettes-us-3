Meet Dr. Van

DR. NI VAN

Dr. Van graduated from Midwestern University-Illinois, where she obtained her Doctor of Dental Medicine and Master in Biomedical Sciences degrees. She was born in Denver, Colorado, but she now resides in Lemont. She met her husband Dr. Woo in dental school. They both found a passion to help patients attain better dental health and overall health associated with their dental needs. She enjoys doing general dentistry and has a particularly strong interest in cosmetic dentistry.

The bond and connection that Dr. Van gets to develop with each patient is truly rewarding, and she hopes to help all types of patients from every background. Dr. Van tries to make each dental appointment a positive one, and she strives to give each patient the best possible care. She loves being in a family type dental office and hopes to continue giving patients the great care they received from Dr. Whittingham.

Dentistry is our profession, but patients are our focus.
