Become a patient of NEVHC

Our certified enrollment counselors can answer your questions about our services and help you enroll in health coverage that meets your needs. Once you have been enrolled in health coverage, you may qualify to make NEVHC your home for primary care. Speak to a certified enrollment counselor today to learn more.
