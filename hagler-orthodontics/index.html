At Hagler Orthodontics, our treatment process is tactical and strategically planned. In an ideal situation, we’d like to see patients as early as age 7. By starting treatment early, we are able to recognize potential problems and correct them before they become more serious. Starting treatment early may also shorten the length of time you will need to be in braces.
