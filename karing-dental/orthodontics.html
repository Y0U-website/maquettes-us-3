Prevention of TMJ and Jaw Discomfort An uneven bite can place excessive strain on the temporomandibular joints and the surrounding facial muscles. By properly aligning your teeth, orthodontics helps prevent chronic headaches, jaw clicking, and the painful symptoms associated with TMJ disorders.

Clearer Speech and Articulation The position of your teeth plays a critical role in how you form sounds. Correcting significant gaps, overcrowding, or overjets can help resolve certain speech impediments, such as lisps, and improve your overall clarity and vocal confidence.

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of the case, but most patients complete their treatment in 12 to 24 months. Technologies like the Damon System can often shorten this timeframe.

Modern orthodontics are designed for comfort. While you may feel some pressure or tightness after an adjustment or when switching to a new Invisalign tray, significant pain is rare.

The frequency of your appointments depends on your specific treatment. For traditional or Damon System braces, we typically see you every 6 to 10 weeks to monitor progress and make adjustments. If you are using Invisalign, your visits may be even less frequent, as you will be switching to new aligners at home every week or two.
