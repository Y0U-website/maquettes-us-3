Peak Plan Membership

Introducing the Peak Plan

Lone Peak Dental cares about your smile! If you are without insurance and in need of dental care, our Peak Plan was designed with you in mind. This is an in-house membership program that gives members access to Lone Peak Dental s comprehensive, quality dental care. For a reasonable annual cost, our members receive standard cleanings, bitewings & x-rays, fluoride treatments, complete doctor s exam, and oral cancer screenings - 100 % covered, twice yearly. In addition, plan members also receive generous discounts on our other dental services, cosmetic procedures, and periodontal treatments.

NO DEDUCTIBLES

No hidden fees, co-pays, or deductibles. You will receive all the benefits of this plan immediately.

NO PRE-AUTHORIZATIONS

Get the comprehensive and consistent care you need without any extra wait or pre-approvals.

NO MAXIMUMS

No need to worry about maxing out your dental benefits and missing out on coverage for services when you need them.

NO SUBMITTING CLAIMS

No wasting time submitting claims. Everything is done right in our office and there are no surprises.

Our Peak Plan is simple and straightforward. No hidden fees, no insurance hassles, and best of all, no waiting! Once you ve enrolled, your benefits are available immediately. It s ideal for individuals, couples, students, and families that do not have access to dental insurance through their employers or schools. See if the Peak Plan is right for you!
