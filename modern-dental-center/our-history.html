Our History

By combining international resources with localized personal support, Modern has created the ideal way to work in our rapidly changing environment. Our goal is to become the total solution for you and your patients, delivering excellent restorations and more smiles.

  • What services and support can I expect from a Modern Dental Center? Modern Dental Centers are your resource for: Consultation & Case Planning
  • Continuing Education
  • Custom Shade Matching
  • Digital Products
  • Digital Support Services
  • Does Modern Dental provide assistance with case planning? The knowledgeable and experienced staff at your local Modern Dental Center will work directly with you to develop a detailed treatment plan for your case. Our team of experts will continue to support you throughout the entire process to ensure that the final restoration exceeds your expectations.
  • What is the timeline for a case? The standard turnaround time for digital restorations is 5-6 business days in the lab; for all other products, it is 10-12 business days. To assist you in managing shipping logistics and holiday schedules, we will provide you with the Modern Dental Return Date Calendar—an easy reference for establishing the return date based on when your case is received at your local Modern Dental Center.
  • What is the timeline for minor adjustments, such as contact additions? Our local Modern Dental Centers are fully equipped to manage minor adjustments in-house. The turnaround time is 1-3 business days.
