Over 100 years of Experience in Grand Haven, Michigan

Harbor Lights Dental Care is one of the oldest, continuously operating dental practices in Michigan. After graduating from the University of Michigan in 1898, Dr. John Meiras officially started his Grand Haven dental practice in 1901. For many years, he practiced dentistry from the second floor of the historic Peoples Savings Bank, located (at the time) on the southeast corner of Washington Ave. and Third St. Grand Haven, Michigan. Dr. Jack Leitner joined Dr. Meiras in 1950, taking over the practice after Dr. Meiras retired in 1955.

Dr. Leitner served hundreds of patients over a 40-year period, leading up to his eventual retirement in 1990, at which point his son, Dr. John Leitner, took over. Like his father, Dr. John Leitner served the people of Grand Haven and surrounding areas for nearly four decades. Now under the helm of Dr. Colin Pellegrom and his all-star team, the legacy continues with Harbor Lights Dental, a beacon of oral health and hygiene for the lakeshore communities of West Michigan!

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

We recommend getting a dental cleaning every six months. For some patients who have a higher risk for bacterial infections, Dr. Pellegrom may recommend more frequent appointments.

Do you take dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most dental insurance plans at our office and we work with Delta Dental.

Do you offer payment plans?

We partner with CareCredit to offer convenient options for breaking up your bill into affordable monthly payments. In some cases, you can even finance your dental treatment interest-free.

Do you charge for missed appointments?

If patients don’t notify us 48 hours in advance of any changes or cancellations to your appointment, a fee of $50.00 will be charged to your account. We do reserve the right to waive the fee when there are extenuating circumstances, however.
