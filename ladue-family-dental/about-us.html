Meet the Team at Ladue Family Dental

At Ladue Family Dental, our goal is to combine exceptional customer service with caring, compassionate dentistry; we strive to treat others like we would want our own family members to be treated. That’s a big part of what makes us different.

As soon as you walk into our modern, welcoming dental office, we’ll do everything possible to exceed your expectations. We want your dental visits to be relaxed and stress-free, making it easier to maintain regular preventative visits and optimal oral health. We will do whatever it takes to help you and your loved ones keep their natural teeth for a lifetime.

We look forward to welcoming you to our dental office and building long-term relationships with you, your children, and even your parents! Please take a moment to get to know our experienced team members, and feel free to call us if you have any questions or concerns.

Dr. Sarah Spring

Dr. Sarah Spring graduated with honors and various accolades from the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry. Upon graduation, she completed a fellowship program where she trained and mentored dental students as a part-time faculty member. Dr. Spring is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, a prestigious award that only six percent of dentists obtain after dedication to continuing education and implementing best in class dental care.

In her free time away from dentistry, Dr. Spring loves spending time with her husband, Gordon, and two children, Isaac and Edith, as well as traveling.
