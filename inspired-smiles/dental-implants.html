How Long Do Tooth Implants Last?

When properly cared for, dental implants can last 20 years or more, and in many cases, a lifetime. Their longevity depends on several factors, including home hygiene habits, overall health, and attending regular dental visits. Routine cleanings and examinations at Inspired Smiles allow our team to monitor your implants and ensure they remain strong and healthy for years to come.

Do Dental Implants Look Like Real Teeth?

Yes, dental implants are designed to look completely natural. Each crown is custom-made to match your surrounding teeth in shape, color, and texture. Because the implant is anchored securely into your jawbone, it functions just like a real tooth, maintaining your smile’s symmetry and your bite’s stability.

Patients often find that no one can tell the difference between their implants and their natural teeth.

What Happens if I Don’t Replace a Missing Tooth with a Dental Implant?

Leaving a missing tooth untreated can lead to more serious dental problems over time. Neighboring teeth can shift out of place, your bite may become misaligned, and the jawbone beneath the missing tooth will begin to shrink. This bone loss can make future restoration more difficult and affect your facial appearance.

By choosing dental implants, you protect your oral health, maintain your jaw structure, and prevent long-term complications.

How Long Does Healing Take for a Dental Implant?

The healing process, known as osseointegration, usually takes between four to six months as the implant fuses naturally with the jawbone. Some patients may require additional healing time if bone grafting or multiple implants are placed.

Thanks to our precise digital planning and gentle techniques, most patients experience minimal discomfort and smooth recovery. Our team provides personalized post-operative care instructions and ongoing support to ensure a successful outcome.

How Do I Care for Dental Implants After Placement?

Caring for your dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Consistent daily brushing and flossing, along with regular dental checkups, are the best ways to maintain your investment. Avoid chewing extremely hard foods or using your teeth as tools, and quit smoking if applicable.

At Inspired Smiles, we’ll create a maintenance plan tailored to your needs and schedule follow-up visits to help your implants stay healthy and beautiful.
