Is it right for you?

Clear aligners treat many common concerns: crowding, gaps, and mild to moderate bite issues. During your consultation, your dentist will examine your teeth and bite and let you know honestly whether aligners are the best option for your goals, or whether another approach would serve you better.

Caring for your aligners

For the best results, aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours a day and removed only to eat and clean your teeth. Because they’re removable, you keep brushing and flossing normally — which makes them easier to keep clean than braces. We’ll guide you through care and check your progress at regular, short visits.
