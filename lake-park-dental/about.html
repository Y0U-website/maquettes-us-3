Our Dentists

Beau Schneiss, DMD

OWNER / DENTIST Bringing advanced clinical expertise and refined patient care standards to Milwaukee’s East Side, Dr. Schneiss has returned home after practicing for the past 8 years in the Chicago area. A Wisconsin native, he graduated from Midwestern University Illinois College of Dental Medicine and completed his hospital-based residency training at the Veterans Affairs Medical Center and the University of Oklahoma.

Dr. Schneiss holds Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry and Associate Fellowship in the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, distinctions earned by only 6 % of dentists in the United States. Known for his collaborative, ethical approach, he prioritizes clear communication and thorough, efficient appointments. Having been a patient and gone through surgeries himself, he knows what it is like to be on the other end of care and wants patients to feel comfortable, safe and heard.

Outside the office, he is passionate about health and fitness, skiing, fly fishing, carpentry and home projects, exploring Milwaukee’s coffee scene, and is on a quest to visit all the national parks.

Jessica messerschmidt, DDS

Dr. Messerschmidt began her professional career as a kindergarten teacher, spending six years in classrooms across Wisconsin and North Carolina before pursuing dentistry to combine her love of people, art, and science. She earned her dental degree from Marquette University and now practices general dentistry in the greater Milwaukee area. A member of the ADA, WDA, and Greater Milwaukee Dental Association, she is committed to continuing education and helping patients understand the “why” behind their care.

Outside the office, she enjoys time with her husband, Brian, and their daughter, Evelyn, tackling home renovations, and exploring Wisconsin’s best hikes. Dr. Messerschmidt believes strong patient relationships are the most rewarding part of her work and looks forward to welcoming you.
