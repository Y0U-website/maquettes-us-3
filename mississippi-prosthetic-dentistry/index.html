Les soins

Dental bridges literally bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth. This dental prosthetic consists of one or more false teeth held in place by two crowns attached to teeth on either side of the empty space.

Implant overdentures and overpartials are removable dental prosthesis that can be placed over dental implants.
