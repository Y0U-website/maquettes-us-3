Benefits of Women&#x27;s Health Care at HANDS Clinic

Preventive care is the most powerful tool women have for protecting their long-term health. Regular exams help detect issues early — when they&#x27;re most treatable. Our providers offer personalized, respectful care that helps women in St. Lucie County stay informed and in control of their health.

Why HANDS Clinic

Since 2010, HANDS Clinic has been a trusted resource for women in St. Lucie County who need quality healthcare without the financial burden. Our volunteer providers bring expertise and compassion to every appointment. With 13 service areas under one roof, we make it easier to get the care you need — all in one place.

Have a question?

  • Can I get my prescriptions through HANDS Clinic? Yes. Prescription management is part of many of our services, and our Prescription Assistance Program can help eligible patients obtain approved medications at no cost. Talk to your provider at your visit to find out what&#x27;s available for your specific medications.
  • Do I need insurance to be seen at HANDS Clinic? No. HANDS Clinic specifically serves adults who do not have insurance. You do not need to have — or get — insurance to receive care here. Our eligibility requirements are based on income and residency, not insurance status.
  • Do you offer services in Spanish? Yes. HANDS Clinic serves a bilingual community and has Spanish-speaking staff available. Key communications and patient-facing materials are available in both English and Spanish. If you have a language preference, let us know when you contact us.
  • How do I know if I qualify for care at HANDS Clinic? Eligibility is based on four factors: income level, St. Lucie County residency, age (19–64), and insurance status. The easiest way to find out is to fill out our eligibility form — it only takes a few minutes and will determine whether you qualify for care.
  • Is care at HANDS Clinic really free? Yes. Services at HANDS Clinic are provided at no cost to eligible patients. There are no copays, no fees, and no surprise bills. Most services are completely free; some specialty referrals may be at a discounted rate. Your care team will always be upfront with you about what&#x27;s included.
  • Is my women&#x27;s health visit confidential? Absolutely. All patient information is private and protected. Your care is between you and your provider — we take confidentiality seriously and maintain the highest standards of patient privacy.
