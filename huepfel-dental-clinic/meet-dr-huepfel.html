MEET DR. HUEPFEL

Family & Cosmetic Dentist in Houlton, Wisconsin

Dr. Huepfel has over 26 years in the practice and is a graduate of the University of Minnesota. He has completed educational courses at the Mayo clinic in endodontics or root canal treatment. He spent two years of post graduate curriculum in esthetic dentistry through the prosthedontic department at the University of Minnesota, has extensive training in Lumineer placement, Invisilign orthodontic teeth straightening and has had dental treatment appear in Bride magazine.

More about Dr. Huepfel

Spring Valley High 1979, University of Minnesota 1989 BS, DDS 1989

Numerous classes on general and esthetic dentistry, invisalign, lumineers, and University of Minnesota esthetic dentistry core curriculum post graduate program.

My dentist, Dr. Fast, was a very kind and gentle man. He encouraged me to study dentistry. Now I’ve been practicing for over 20 years.

I do volunteer dentistry at Pine Ridge Reservation in South Dakota, and work at Union Gospel Mission family patient clinic. I also coach kids in baseball and football, and help with kids hockey.

I enjoy my time with my kids who are active in dance, football, swimming, hockey, and baseball. I also like to ski and spend time in Ely, Minnesota with my in-laws.
