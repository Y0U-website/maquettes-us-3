Dr. Ryan Lembke

Dr. Ryan Lembke was born and raised in Chaska, Minnesota. He received a bachelor’s degree from St. John’s University in Collegeville, MN. Then, he graduated with honors from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Dr. Lembke appreciates all aspects of general dentistry, including tooth-colored fillings, same-day crowns, root canals, oral surgery, and implant restoration and placement. He is motivated to get to know patients individually, working with you to achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dr. Lembke, a true Minnesotan at heart, enjoys spending time outdoors with friends and family. His hobbies include running, fishing, camping, pickleball, and cross-country and downhill skiing.
