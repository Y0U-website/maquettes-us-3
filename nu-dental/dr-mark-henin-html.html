Get to Know Dr. Mark Henin

Dr. Mark Henin has been practicing for several years and prioritizes helping patients improve their overall quality of life through dentistry. He values the ability to make a meaningful difference for patients and approaches care with a calm and patient-centered mindset. Feel free to read about his education and background before meeting him during your visit.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

During his undergraduate studies, Dr. Henin knew he wanted to work in a field where he could help people directly. A summer internship at New Jersey Medical School exposed him to both medicine and dentistry, and that experience helped guide his decision to pursue dentistry as a career.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Henin completed his undergraduate studies at Rutgers University in New Brunswick and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Columbia College of Dental Medicine in Manhattan. He went on to complete a residency with Northwell Health GRP Residency and continues to pursue ongoing continuing education every year. He is also a current and proud member of the American Dental Association and the New York Dental Association.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever he isn’t at the office, Dr. Henin spends time at church, plays basketball, studies, and enjoys being with his family all of whom are hardworking individuals. One interesting fact about him is that he has undergone four major orthopedic surgeries.
