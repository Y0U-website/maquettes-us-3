Dr. John T. Chen, DMD

Dr. John Chen grew up in Queens, NY and then moved to Boston, MA for 20 years. He attended Boston University School of Dentistry and later Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. Before practicing at Graham Family Dental Care, Dr. Chen worked in general and sedation dentistry. In our practice, he is known for combining function and aesthetics to improve your overall health. Dr. Chen is committed to giving his patients a soothing dental experience from beginning to end. He looks after his patients by listening to their needs and making sure they feel comfortable and relaxed.
