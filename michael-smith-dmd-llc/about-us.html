Meet the Team at Michael Smith DMD LLC

We have been serving patients in Belleville and surrounding communities with quality oral health care for 40 years. Dr. Michael Smith has established a warm and friendly environment staffed with an amazing team that puts your needs first.

Our staff has decades of combined experience in dentistry, each with unique talents, and many have been with us for more than 15 years. We work hard together behind the scenes to bring you a seamless, stress-free dental experience. Above all, we know how to make your visits pleasant and enjoyable; we love sharing a laugh, a smile, or listening to what your kids have been up to lately. We treat teeth, but most importantly, we treat people.

We all look forward to welcoming you to the office of Michael Smith, DMD, and getting to know you and your dental needs. Please call us at (618) 235-5141 to schedule an appointment to meet us, but until then, please get to know the wonderful people who will be serving you.

Dr. Michael T. Smith

Dr. Michael Smith was raised in New Melle, a small town in Missouri. He attended St. Louis University High School, Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, and finally SIU School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 2011. Dr. Smith joined Dr. Feder in 2013 as an employee and decided to invest his future in Belleville by purchasing the office in 2017.

He joined the Illinois Army National Guard in 2009 and is currently a Lieutenant Colonel serving as the Illinois State Dental Officer. During the summer of 2022, he deployed overseas to Poland to provide dental care to US soldiers stationed throughout the country. Currently, Dr. Smith spends his duty weekends in Springfield, Illinois screening soldiers from across the state for dental problems and administrating the national guard’s statewide dental health program.

Dr. Smith enjoys spending time restoring his 1860s home, where he lives with his wife and children. He is also interested in fitness, healthy eating, woodworking, automotive repair, and meeting the office’s IT needs. He recently rekindled his passion for wrestling both by coaching and training for competition!

Dr. Nicholas Swanson

Dr. Nicholas Swanson was raised in Mascoutah, Illinois, and remained in the local area to attend SIUE for college. He first entered the dental field in 2019 as an assistant to Dr. Smith, and later graduated from the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in 2024.

Initially, he joined the Air National Guard right after high school in 2013, but he is currently serving as a Captain in the Dental Corps of the Illinois Army National Guard, stationed at Camp Lincoln in Springfield, Illinois.

In his personal time, Dr. Swanson enjoys spending time with his wife, hosting events with family and friends, and trying to find the right hobby to immerse himself in.

Dr. Steven Newbold

Dr. Steven Newbold began his academic journey at SIU-Edwardsville, where he majored in Biology and Chemistry. He then went on to attend Washington University School of Dental Medicine and graduated in 1977. Dr. Newbold then embarked on a fulfilling career, practicing dentistry in New Athens, IL. for nearly 25 years, in addition to serving at the Illinois Department of Corrections starting in 1985. During his tenure, he held the positions of staff dentist and Dental Director at the Menard Correctional Center, until his retirement in 2018.

In 2022, Dr. Smith was deployed to Poland prompting the request of Dr. Newbold to oversee limited responibilities in his absence. Dr. Newbold agreed, and has found immense pleasure from his office work, hence his decision to extend his career with us to three days a week.

Dr. Newbold and his wife have four children and eleven grandchildren. He has been active in his community, having served on the School Board and is currently on the Village Board as a Trustee. He enjoys travelling, cooking, golf, and attending numerous grandkids sporting events.
