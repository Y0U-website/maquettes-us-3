Insurance Accepted

Navigating your particular dental insurance coverage shouldn’t have to be a headache-inducing event. Instead, let our staff make this process as simple as possible. Our practice is an in-network provider for Delta Dental Premier, and we will file claims for all other major plans on the patient’s behalf for maximum convenience. Better yet, our knowledge of the system will help us maximize the benefits available to you.

Financing Available

If you do not currently have dental insurance, the prospect of pursuing your desired dental care may seem intimidating financially. Thankfully, there are other options that our practice is happy to work with Cherry and CareCredit. Cherry offers flexible payment plans that allow you to split the cost of your dental care into manageable monthly payments.
