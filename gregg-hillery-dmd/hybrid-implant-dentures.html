Hybrid Implant Dentures

Benefits of Hybrid Implant Dentures

Hybrid implant dentures provide several significant benefits, making them an ideal choice for full-mouth restoration

Enhanced Stability Secured by implants, hybrid dentures provide a strong, secure fit that doesn’t shift or move.

Natural Appearance Custom-made to look like natural teeth, hybrid dentures improve the aesthetics of your smile.

Improved Functionality Hybrid dentures allow you to eat, speak, and smile confidently without worry.

Our team at Central City Dentistry ensures each set of hybrid dentures is designed to fit comfortably, providing a durable, long-term solution.

What to Expect During the Hybrid Denture Process

The process for receiving hybrid implant dentures involves several key steps to ensure a perfect fit and comfortable experience

Consultation We assess your dental health and discuss the best options for full-mouth restoration.

Implant Placement Titanium implants are surgically inserted into the jawbone to serve as secure anchors for the dentures.

Denture Fitting After healing, custom dentures are attached to the implants, providing a secure, natural-looking restoration.

This structured process allows us to deliver a solution that restores both functionality and aesthetics to your smile.
