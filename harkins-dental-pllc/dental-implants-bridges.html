Dental Bridges: Restoring Multiple Teeth

A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that bridges the gap between missing teeth. It consists of replacement teeth (pontics) attached to crowns placed on the healthy teeth flanking the gap. Bridges are typically made from porcelain fused to metal (PFM) or zirconia, offering strength and aesthetics.

Benefits of Dental Bridges:

  • Restores Functionality: Bridges effectively restore chewing and speaking ability for patients with multiple missing teeth.
  • Cost-Effective Option: Compared to implants, bridges may offer a more affordable solution for replacing several teeth.
  • Natural Appearance: Bridges are designed to mimic natural teeth, providing a cosmetically pleasing restoration.
  • Relatively Short Procedure: Bridge placement typically requires fewer appointments than implant surgery.
