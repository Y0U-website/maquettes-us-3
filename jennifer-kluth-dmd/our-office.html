Whether you wish to improve or simply maintain the beauty and health of your smile, you have come to the right place. Our restorative and cosmetic dentist, Jennifer Kluth DMD, and her staff are pleased to offer some of the most advanced technology in modern dentistry, allowing them to offer a comprehensive range of dental services designed to meet the needs of patients of all ages. We are proud to offer you world-class restorative and cosmetic dentistry right here in historic Schenectady.

Dr. Jennifer Kluth

Dr. Kluth’s finely honed dental skills and warm, down-to-earth demeanor allow her to provide dental care to patients of all ages.

She understands that each patient presents a unique case and, therefore, should be treated as an individual. During examinations, she takes the extra time to ensure that not only the immediate problems are treated, but ensures that your entire mouth is healthy. Whether you require orthodontic treatment with clear aligner braces or general gum care, Dr. Kluth wants you and every member of your family to feel at-ease when visiting our practice and will do everything he can to make your dental experience a positive one.
