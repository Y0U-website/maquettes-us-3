Understanding Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a specialized field of dentistry focused on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. These treatments not only enhance the aesthetic appearance of your smile but also improve overall oral function, including chewing and speaking. Common causes of alignment issues include genetic factors, jaw growth discrepancies, and overcrowded teeth.

How Orthodontics Can Help

At Mequon Smile Design, our team uses advanced technology, such as detailed X-rays, to assess your dental and jaw alignment. Based on your unique needs, we create a personalized treatment plan to address both cosmetic concerns and functional problems.

Orthodontic treatments can help with:

  • Straightening crowded or overlapping teeth for a more attractive smile.
  • Correcting bite alignment to restore proper chewing function.
  • Closing gaps between teeth for a more uniform appearance.
  • Reducing wear and tear on misaligned teeth by repositioning them.
