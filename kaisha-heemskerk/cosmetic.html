Veneers

Veneers are custom-made coverings that fit over the front surface of your teeth, improving the appearance of cracks, chips, stains, and other imperfections. They restore your teeth’s shape, function, and appearance for a natural-looking result that still looks like you.

  • Corrects cracks, chips, stains, and other cosmetic imperfections
  • Custom-designed to match the size, shape, and color of your surrounding teeth
  • Durable and stain-resistant with proper care
  • Can last 10–15 years or longer with regular checkups

Do Porcelain veneers look natural?

Porcelain veneers are one of the most natural-looking cosmetic treatments available. High-quality porcelain reflects light similarly to natural enamel, and each veneer is custom-designed to complement your features and blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth.

What is the process for veneers?

The process starts with a smile consultation and detailed records to design your personalized smile. At your next visit, teeth are carefully prepared and impressions or digital scans are taken — you’ll leave with temporary veneers. Once your final veneers are handcrafted by a skilled ceramist, they’re precisely placed and bonded for a strong, comfortable, natural-looking fit.

Are Veneers like regular teeth?

Porcelain veneers look and feel like real teeth but do require some care. They’re durable and stain-resistant, but habits like nail-biting or chewing ice can cause damage. For patients who grind, we recommend a night guard to protect your investment. With proper care, veneers can last 10–15 years or longer.

Whitening

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental treatment that brightens your smile by reducing stains and discoloration. At Waukee Dental, we offer customized take-home whitening options that safely and effectively enhance the natural brightness of your teeth. Professional whitening can address common staining caused by coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and aging. It is a simple, non-invasive way to refresh your smile and boost your confidence.

  • Reduces stains caused by coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and aging
  • Custom whitening trays designed to fit your smile comfortably and evenly
  • Simple, non-invasive treatment with no downtime
  • Membership plan patients receive 50 % off custom whitening trays

How long do results last?

Teeth-whitening results can last anywhere from six months to two years, depending on your habits. Limiting stain-causing foods and beverages, such as coffee, tea, and red wine, practicing good oral hygiene, and attending regular dental cleanings can help keep your smile brighter for longer.

Does teeth whitening causing sensitivity?

S ome patients may experience temporary sensitivity during or after whitening. This is normal and subsides within a few days. Your provider can customize your treatment to reduce sensitivity and recommend desensitizing products as needed.

How many shades lighter can patients expect?

Results vary based on your natural tooth color, the type of staining, and how consistently you use your custom whitening trays. Many patients notice their teeth become several shades lighter after completing treatment. Your Waukee Dental provider will give you personalized instructions to help you achieve a safe, natural-looking result.

Composite Bonding

Dental bonding is a conservative cosmetic treatment that uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair minor chips or cracks, close small gaps, conceal discoloration, or improve the shape and symmetry of uneven teeth. The material is matched to your natural tooth color, sculpted to the desired shape, and hardened with a specialized light — all in a single appointment with minimal alteration to your natural tooth.

  • Minimal removal of tooth structure
  • Cost-effective alternative to veneers or crowns
  • Natural-looking, seamless results
  • No anesthesia needed in most cases

How long does Composite Bonding last?

Dental bonding can last 5-10 years depending on placement and habits. Regular checks up and avoiding nail-biting or chewing hard objects can extend its lifespan.

Can bonding be repaired if it chips or cracks?

Yes. Bonding can be easily repaired in a single visit. The resin can be reshaped to restore both appearance and function.

SureSmile (Clear Aligner Therapy)

SureSmile clear aligner therapy offers a discreet, comfortable way to straighten your teeth without traditional metal braces. Using advanced digital technology, your provider creates a customized series of clear, removable aligners that gradually move your teeth into their ideal positions. SureSmile may help correct crowding, spacing, and certain bite issues — and because the aligners are removable, you can eat normally and maintain your regular brushing and flossing routine.

  • More comfortable alternative to conventional braces
  • You are dealing with crowding, gaps, or bite issues
  • You prefer removable aligners for easy eating and cleaning

How does SureSmile work?

SureSmile clear aligner therapy uses advanced technology to gradually shift your teeth into their position. The process begins with a digital scan of your mouth, creating a custom treatment plan that maps out your movement throughout treatment.

How long does SureSmile treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case and your individual smile goals. Most patients complete SureSmile clear aligner therapy within 6 to 18 months. At your consultation, Dr. Kaisha will evaluate your needs and provide an estimated treatment timeline.

How many hours per day should I wear my aligners?

SureSmile aligners are typically worn for 20 to 22 hours each day and changed every one to two weeks, as directed by Dr. Kaisha. Aligners should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing. Consistent wear is important for keeping your treatment on track.

Will I need to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Wearing a retainer is essential for maintaining your new smile and helping prevent your teeth from shifting over time. At the completion of treatment, you will receive a custom retainer. Your Waukee Dental team will provide personalized instructions on how often and how long to wear it.

Botox

We offer Botox for TMJ and therapeutic purposes, providing relief from jaw tension, teeth grinding, and chronic facial pain. In addition to its therapeutic benefits, Botox can also address certain aesthetic concerns.

  • Provides relief from TMJ-related jaw pain and tension
  • Can reduce symptoms of teeth grinding (bruxism)
  • Minimally invasive with no downtime and temporary, natural-looking results

How long does BOTOX last?

BOTOX results typically last 3-6 months, depending on the area treated and individual factors like metabolism.

Are there any side effects?

Common side effects, such as redness, swelling, or bruishing at the inection site, are typically mild and temporary. In rare cases, patients may experience headaches, eyelid drooping, or temporary muscle weakness. These effects usually resolve within a few days.

Can I return to work after BOTOX treatment?

Yes, BOTOX requires minimal downtime. Most patients can resume normal activities after treatment. However, it’s best to avoid strenuous exercise and lying down for a few hours post-treatment to prevent the product from migrating to unintended areas.

When will I see the results from BOTOX treatment?

The results from BOTOX treatment typically appears within 3-5 days after treatment, with the full effects visible within 1-2 weeks. The treatment gradually softens muscle activity, resulting in a smoother appearance.
