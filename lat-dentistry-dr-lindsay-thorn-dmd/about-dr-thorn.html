About Dr. Lindsay Thorn, Family…

0 Meet Dr. Lindsay A. Thorn, DMD Foundation. Dr. Lindsay Thorn is a Salem, Virginia native, Artist, Equestrian, and Family Dentist. She and her husband, Brad, share a family of pets together- 2 dogs, 1 cat, a horse, and a turtle! She has an undeniable love for animals and enjoys competing/riding horses. She loves all things outdoors and is passionate about seeking creative ways to support our environment . Caring for our community has driven her to become passionate about achieving overall health while improving the environment necessary to maintain wellness.

“I have always felt that dentistry is the perfect combination of my love for art with my background in microbiology and chemistry. In my work, I not only have to understand the research and science behind the work I am performing, but also I get to use my artistic hand to achieve beautiful, life changing results for others.”

Focus.

“Dentistry is such a wonderful profession because of the relationships you develop, the connection you feel to the unique community around you, and your ability to have an impact on all of it.” -Dr. Thorn
