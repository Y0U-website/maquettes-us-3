The practice

Patients come from across Western North Carolina, North Georgia, and Southeast Tennessee. Many are referred by family, friends, or local dentists.

The focus is on results that last and making sure patients feel confident about their smile long-term.

Treatment plans built individually, with direct doctor involvement

There’s a reason people keep coming back and why they send others here too.

This is an independent practice, and that shapes how we operate. Decisions are made within the practice, and always with the patient in mind.

Technology is adopted purposefully. New tools are used when they support better outcomes.

Treatment is planned carefully and adjusted as things change over time.
