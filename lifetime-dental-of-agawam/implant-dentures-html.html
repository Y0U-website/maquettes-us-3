What Are Implant Dentures?

While similar to traditional ones, implant dentures do not rest atop your gums or rely on natural suction to remain in place. Instead, they’ll be attached to dental implants that are strategically placed in your jawbone, offering superior stability for a fully rebuilt smile. This treatment typically comes in two forms depending on a patient’s situation and preferences. Our team will work with you to decide on which option is best for your oral health. Here are the two categories of implant dentures to choose from:

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

These implant dentures are meant to remain in your mouth all day, every day without any issues. The only one who should be able to remove them is your dentist whenever they need to provide treatment or perform deep cleaning. You won’t have to worry about your new teeth falling out of place at inconvenient times.

Removable Implant Dentures

While these implant dentures offer incredible stability and comfort like fixed ones, you’ll be able to take them off and put them back on whenever you choose. Sometimes this option can utilize your previous/existing dentures, as long as they’re able to be adjusted to work with implants. You’ll need to take these prosthetics off when cleaning, and you won’t require as much jawbone mass to undergo the treatment.

The Implant Dentures Process

You’ll need to visit us for an initial consultation to discuss your oral health and walk you through the treatment process. If you determine that this procedure is right for you, we can begin by embedding dental implants into your jawbone at the exact positions necessary for your dentures.

Once you’ve recovered from this process and osseointegration is successful, you’ll return to our office so we can design your new teeth. We’ll use impressions of your natural smile to create your dentures so that they’ll match correctly with your mouth and facial shape, that way you can enjoy a comfortable, functional, and beautiful smile.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

To determine if you’re eligible for implant dentures, we’ll need to assess your oral health and lifestyle habits. You may be a good candidate for the treatment if you:

Even if you aren’t currently eligible, we can work with you and provide treatments such as bone grafting or gum disease therapy to get you on track for a successful procedure.

  • Have sufficient jawbone density to support the implants
  • Do not smoke or chew tobacco products
  • Are free of oral/overall health issues that can cause your implants to fail
  • Want to upgrade from traditional dentures to a more reliable, comprehensive, comfortable, and longer-lasting solution

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Here are some of the advantages of combining dentures with dental implants:

  • Implant dentures can last several decades before needing replacements, whereas traditional ones will need to be replaced every few years.
  • Dental implants offer incredible stability that you won’t find with traditional dentures, meaning you won’t have to worry about your dentures slipping or moving out of place when eating or talking.
  • Removable implant dentures can be cleaned easily like traditional ones, but you’ll also be able to preserve your jawbone due to the metal posts.
