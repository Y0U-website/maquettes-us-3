Dr. Noreen Goldwire - Your Trusted Dentist

Dr. Goldwire received her DDS from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2001. She is extensively trained in a wide range of dental services including extractions, root canals, restorative implants, crowns, veneers, and dentures. Additionally, Dr. Goldwire is a certified Invisalign®️ and Candid ®️ provider.

Dr. Goldwire believes in practicing conservative dentistry. She gives patients a variety of treatment options, so they always feel in control of their care. Dr. Goldwire offers Invisalign®️ and Candid®️ using our iTero scanner, which can be used to give our patients a simulated look at what their smile can look like after treatment. We also use iTero scanners in place of most impressions in our office, eliminating the goopy, uncomfortable impression material you may be used to.
