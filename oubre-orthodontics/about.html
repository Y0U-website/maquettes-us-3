About Us

About Us Our Mission We are an orthodontic service for children and adults, utilizing advanced technology provided by a team committed to caring and excellence. Our expert orthodontists and dedicated staff work together to create personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

With a focus on patient comfort and satisfaction, we ensure a welcoming environment where everyone feels at ease. We strive to deliver outstanding results and help you achieve the smile you’ve always dreamed of!

Meet Dr. John Oubre, D.D.S.

Dr. John Oubre has been practicing orthodontics for 42 years and throughout his career he has maintained the highest possible standard of excellence, compassion and clinical proficiency in an effort to provide the best possible treatment to his patients. With a commitment to staying on the forefront of the profession and upholding these standards, Dr. Oubre strives to never stop learning and implementing new technologies that will help deliver the best possible treatment.

Dr. Oubre is married to his wife, Jeannie Oubre, who also has a dental degree. Together, they have five children: Michael, Matthew, Thomas, Olivia, and James. He is also the grandfather to four wonderful grandchildren. When he’s away from the office, Dr. Oubre enjoys playing golf and participating in his children and grandchildren’s many activities.

Meet Dr. Matthew Oubre, D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Matthew is married to Stephanie Vu, who also has a dental degree. Together, they have two sons and a daughter. While away from the office, he enjoys playing with his children, golfing, traveling, graphic design, and rooting for the Saints, Tigers, and Cajuns in the fall!
