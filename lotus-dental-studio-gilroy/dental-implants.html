Our dental implant process

At Lotus Dental Studio, we offer a supportive, step-by-step approach to replacing missing teeth with dental implants. Whether you’re just exploring your options or ready to begin treatment, we’re here to guide you with clarity and care. The process typically involves three main stages:

Personalized Consultation

During your exam or extraction appointment, we’ll walk you through all your options — from doing nothing to partials, bridges, or implants — so you feel fully informed and confident in your next step. If you choose dental implants, we’ll assess your jawbone health and plan a treatment timeline tailored to your needs.

Implant Placement Surgery

In a minor surgical procedure, a small titanium post (the implant) is placed in your jawbone to act as a sturdy, artificial tooth root. To keep you comfortable, we offer local anesthesia and additional sedation options upon request. After surgery, the implant is left to heal beneath the gum with a healing cap in place.

Healing & Restoration

Over the next few months, your implant fuses naturally with the surrounding bone in a process called osseointegration. Once healing is complete, we’ll attach your final restoration — a custom crown, bridge, or denture — designed to match your natural teeth in shape, shade, and function. You’ll be able to eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small, biocompatible titanium post that’s surgically placed into the jawbone to act as a root. It supports a crown, bridge, or denture for a natural-looking and stable restoration.

K L Am I a candidate for dental implants?

If you have healthy gums and sufficient bone, you may be a great candidate. Even if you’re not sure, we’ll evaluate your options — including bone grafting or alternatives — during your consultation.

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last 15 years or more — often a lifetime.

K L What if I’m not ready to decide after an extraction?

No problem. We’ll explain all your options at the time of your extraction and guide you on preserving your bone and timing if you want to consider an implant in the future.
