Implant-supported dentures are a type of overdenture that is anchored to dental implants instead of resting solely on the gums. Depending on your specific needs, they can be designed to be: Removable – Snaps on and off for easy cleaning
  • Fixed – Permanently attached for maximum stability

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Compared to traditional full or partial dentures , implant-supported options offer significant functional and lifestyle advantages:

For patients frustrated with loose dentures or struggling with sore spots, implant-supported solutions offer welcome relief and renewed confidence.

  • Improved chewing ability and bite strength
  • Greater stability — no slipping or shifting
  • Enhanced speech clarity
  • Preservation of jawbone density and facial structure
  • More natural appearance and feel
  • Long-term durability and reliability
  • Less irritation to gums

Who Is a Candidate?

You may be a good candidate for implant-supported dentures if:

During your consultation at Mizner Family Dental, we will assess your oral health, take digital scans , and coordinate any needed pre-treatment, such as bone grafting or extractions.

  • You are missing most or all teeth in the upper or lower arch
  • You have sufficient bone density in your jaw to support implants
  • You want a more secure alternative to removable dentures
  • You are in generally good health and a non-smoker
  • You are committed to proper oral hygiene and follow-up care

Our Approach to Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Consultation and Planning Dr. Mizner will evaluate your current oral condition and determine if implant-supported dentures are right for you. If you’re currently wearing traditional dentures, we’ll review how your needs can be better met with implants.
  • Implant Placement (Referral) We coordinate your care with a local oral surgeon who places the dental implants. Healing typically takes several months as the implants integrate with the jawbone.
  • Impression and Design Once healed, we use digital impressions to design your custom overdenture for a secure fit and natural appearance.
  • Attachment and Final Fitting Your new denture is securely attached to the implants using precision attachments, either as a snap-on (removable) or fixed option.
  • Follow-Up and Care We’ll walk you through how to care for your new restoration and schedule regular dental exams to maintain your results long-term.

Snap-On vs. Fixed Dentures

We offer two styles of implant-supported dentures based on your goals and preferences:

Removable (Snap-On)

  • Attaches securely to implants with locator attachments
  • Can be removed daily for cleaning
  • Easier maintenance for some patients
  • Typically requires fewer implants (2–4 per arch)

Fixed (Non-Removable)

Dr. Mizner will help you choose the right option based on your lifestyle, budget, and oral health.

  • Attached permanently to implants and not removed at home
  • Feels and functions more like natural teeth
  • Requires more implants (4–6 per arch)
  • Requires professional cleaning and maintenance

Caring for Implant-Supported Dentures

Whether you have a fixed or removable denture, caring for your new restoration is essential for long-term success:

With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last 10+ years or more.

  • Brush and clean around the denture and implant abutments daily
  • Use water flossers or special tools for hard-to-reach areas
  • Visit us for regular cleanings and implant checks
  • Avoid smoking or tobacco use, which can interfere with implant health
  • Address any soreness or changes in fit promptly

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant-Supported Dentures

Question text goes here

Are implant-supported dentures better than regular dentures?

They provide greater stability, comfort, and function than traditional dentures. Patients often report better chewing, clearer speech, and more confidence with implant-supported options.

How many implants will I need?

It depends on whether you choose a fixed or removable denture. Snap-on styles usually require 2–4 implants, while fixed options may need 4–6 for full support.

Is the procedure painful?

Implant surgery is performed under anesthesia by a specialist. Discomfort after the procedure is typically mild and short-lived.

How long does the process take?

The full process may take several months from implant placement to final restoration, allowing for healing and integration of the implants.

What if I already wear dentures?

You may still be a candidate. We can often retrofit or redesign a new denture to fit over implants, improving retention and comfort.
