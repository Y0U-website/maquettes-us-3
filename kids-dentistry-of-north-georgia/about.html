About Us

Big smiles begin at Kid’s Dentistry of North Georgia. We know children can feel anxious about going to the dentist, but we believe going to the dentist can be fun and worry-free!

Our Practice

We strive to make seeing the dentist a fun and pleasant experience for all. That’s why everything we do – from our positive and fun-filled atmosphere to our specially trained pediatric dental team – is structured to help you and your children enjoy a stress-free experience. Our goal is that your child’s positive dental experiences now leads to a lifetime of great oral health.
