Dental implants require advanced training and precise placement to ensure long-term success. Choosing an experienced implant dentist like Dr. Kohler helps reduce complications and improve outcomes.

How long does the dental implant process take? Treatment timelines vary based on your oral health, bone support, and the complexity of required care. Some cases take just a few months, while others may require additional healing time.

Are dental implants safe? Dental implants are a safe, proven solution for replacing missing teeth when placed by a skilled professional. Dr. Kohler performs an exceptionally thorough assessment, including a review of your health history and any factors that may affect healing or success.

Is getting dental implants painful? Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the process feels with sedation and streamlined techniques. While mild soreness or bruising is normal, many people return to normal activities within a day or two.

Can smokers get dental implants? If you smoke, we recommend quitting before surgery and not resuming until your implants have fully integrated. Tobacco use narrows blood vessels, which reduces essential blood flow, oxygen, and nutrients to the surgical site, while increasing the risk of peri-implantitis and implant failure.
