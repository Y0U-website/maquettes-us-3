Professional Cleaning

Our registered dental hygienists are experts in providing gentle and thorough cleanings for children. We use specialized tools and techniques to remove plaque and tartar buildup, leaving your child’s teeth sparkling clean.

  • What to Expect Your child will receive a gentle and thorough cleaning using kid-friendly instruments. We’ll also polish their teeth to remove any surface stains and make them shine. Our hygienists will take the time to show your child the proper way to brush and floss at home, empowering them to take an active role in their oral health.

Regular Exams

Regular checkups are an essential part of maintaining good oral health. During these exams, our dentists will carefully examine your child’s teeth, gums, and jaw to identify any potential issues early on.

  • What to Expect Our dentists will conduct a comprehensive examination of your child’s mouth, checking for any signs of decay, gum disease, or orthodontic concerns. We’ll also discuss your child’s oral hygiene habits, diet, and any other factors that may be affecting their oral health. You’ll receive personalized advice and recommendations for maintaining a healthy smile.

Digital X-rays

Digital X-rays are a safe and effective way to get a complete picture of your child’s oral health. They allow us to see beneath the surface of the teeth and detect hidden problems that may not be visible during a visual exam.

  • What to Expect We use digital X-ray technology, which minimizes radiation exposure and provides high-quality images. The process is quick and comfortable, and our team will explain everything to your child in a way they can understand. Digital X-rays help us diagnose and treat dental problems early on, ensuring your child’s smile stays healthy and strong.
