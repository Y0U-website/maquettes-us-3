Cosmetic dentistry encompasses various dental treatments that are designed to enhance the aesthetics of your smile. These treatments aim to improve the overall beauty of your smile, boost your self-esteem, and give you the confidence to smile freely. Cosmetic dentistry can be a suitable solution if your smile is affected by imperfections such as:

  • Teeth Whitening
  • Stained or discolored teeth
  • Misaligned or crooked teeth
  • Chipped, cracked, or broken teeth
  • Gaps or spaces between teeth
  • One or more missing teeth
  • Uneven or worn-down teeth
  • Irregular shaped teeth
  • Excessive gum tissue or a “gummy” smile

All-on-X

The All-on-X method involves placing four to six implants in each arch (top and bottom) of your mouth. These titanium screws provide a stable foundation for prosthetic teeth. It takes about 3-6 months for the implants to fuse with the bone through osseointegration.

Teeth in a Day With the All-on-X method, a fixed bridge will be placed on the same day as your surgery. Only patients with ideal conditions are good candidates for this option.
