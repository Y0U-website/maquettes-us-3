About

Dr. Jonathan Klineman

Dr. Jon was born in Cleveland and went to The Ohio State University College of Dentistry graduating in 1995. Dr. Jon practiced in Pittsburgh and Chicago for several years as an associate dentist. He was an adjunct instructor at the University Of Pittsburgh College of Dental Medicine.

With his wife and 3 sons, Dr. Jon enjoys RV trips and warm weather. He is a passionate fan of the Cleveland Indians and you will find him at the games throughout the season. Dr. Jon and his wife also spend hours in leadership positions of multiple non-profit organizations and together have raised millions of dollars to help Cleveland based non-profits.
