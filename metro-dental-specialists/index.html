The practice

At the Metro Dental Center in Detroit, MI, we have the technology, know-how, and experience to keep your smile in perfect shape. Let our dedicated staff care for your dental, cosmetic, orthodontic, and periodontal needs —all under one roof.

When you visit the Metropolitan Dental Center, you’re getting the best of the best. We offer top-of-the-line technology and efficient service, without sacrificing warm, friendly service. We rarely need to refer patients elsewhere for treatment—we do it all.

General Dentistry Our practice boasts the latest dental technology, including a digital scanner to create impressions (no more messy molds!), and an in-house dental lab. We specialize in dental implants, dentures, emergency dental services and much more.

Teeth Whitening Metropolitan Dental Center is excited to offer GLO G-Vial® Teeth Whitening Gel for convenient, at-home teeth whitening!

On-Site Dental Lab At Metro Dental Specilists, we have an in-house lab to make treatment more efficient and cohesive.

Les soins

Periodontal Services Gum disease is a serious issue that needs to be diagnosed and treated as soon as possible to avoid permanent damage. Proper care can prevent further complications.

Oral Surgery Our general dentists are quite capable of performing extractions whether they are simple, surgical, or impacted.

Cosmetic Dentistry Whether your teeth are discolored, cracked, or showing signs of wear—we’ve got you covered. Services include tooth-colored fillings, porcelain veneers, crowns and bridges.
