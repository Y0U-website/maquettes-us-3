0 Meet Dr. Kathryn Strassner Dr. Strassner is passionate about creating a positive, comfortable experience for every patient while delivering high-quality, comprehensive dental care. She believes that building strong relationships and taking the time to truly listen are key to helping patients feel confident in their smiles and their treatment.

Originally from Pittsfield, Massachusetts, Dr. Strassner moved to Wellington, Florida during her childhood. She later attended the University of Florida for her undergraduate studies and remains a proud Gator fan. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2003, and went on to complete an oral surgery fellowship to further expand…

Dr. Strassner is committed to staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry and regularly participates in continuing education, with a focus on cosmetic dentistry and Invisalign treatment. She is a member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, Fourth District Dental Society, and the American Academy of Clear Aligners.

Dr. Strassner has been part of the Saratoga Springs community since 2007, where she lives with her husband, Gillian, and their two daughters, who keep life busy with school and extracurricular commitments. In 2024, she was excited to take over Dr. David Parsons’ practice and continue caring for the community.

Outside of the office, Dr. Strassner enjoys staying active and spending time with her family. Whether she’s skiing, hiking, gardening, or getting in a Peloton workout, she values balance and making the most of her time both in and out of the practice. She also loves spending time with their golden retriever, Jack.

Dr. Strassner looks forward to welcoming you to the practice and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile!
