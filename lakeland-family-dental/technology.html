Technology

The technologies used to deliver dental care have consistently improved over the years, making diagnosis faster and more accurate and treatments more comfortable with a higher rate of predictability.

Both Dr. Eric Ruiz and Dr. Sam Ruiz keep an eye out for technology that provides evidence-based improvements to the care we provide. We want to give you the best because you deserve it!

These are just a few of the technologies you ll find at Lakeland Family Dental.

Our BIOLASE Epic X Diode Laser is the only laser FDA cleared for laser bacterial reduction (LBR). Laser light helps decontaminate gum tissues by decreasing the amount of harmful bacteria and removing diseased tissue. Most bacteria that cause gum disease are dark in color, and the laser is attracted to these specific bacteria instead of healthy bacteria. The laser also disinfects the root surface, which provides a more receptive surface for gum tissue reattachment and healing.

Laser therapy is a non-invasive, non-toxic alternative to pharmaceuticals that reduces inflammation, pain, pathogenic bacterial levels, and stimulates wound healing while leaving healthy tissue intact. The goal of laser therapy is to eliminate the bad bacteria that causes gum disease through its antibacterial properties, and to return the gum tissues to a state of health through its anti-inflammatory effects. That is why the laser is so effective in helping to treat gum disease and gum inflammation!

3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) Imaging

At Lakeland Family Dental, we ve invested in Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) 3D Imaging so we can provide the best possible care to our patients. While traditional x-rays are very useful, they do have their limitations as 2 dimensional representations of an area. With cutting edge 3D CBCT technology, we can visualize a tooth or surgical site in full dimension. This allows us to complete root canals, tooth extractions, implant placement, and other procedures with more precision than ever!

Digital X-Rays

X-rays are one of the most important diagnostic tools we have as dentists, and digital x-rays allow us to diagnose faster and more accurately while exposing patients to dramatically less radiation. Did you know that digital x-rays expose patients to 90 % less radiation than traditional film x-rays?

Since the x-rays are transmitted electronically, we can view them almost instantly without having to wait for film to develop. We view the images on computer monitors, making it easier to zoom in for a better view and explain any concerns we have to you much more easily. This makes digital x-rays a valuable patient education tool.

Unlike traditional film x-rays, digital x-rays don t require hazardous chemicals to develop, and they are stored electronically in your digital file. This means digital x-rays are less wasteful and a much better choice for the environment!

Today, x-rays are simpler and safer than ever before with the help of digital radiography!

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous oxide, which you might know as "laughing gas," has been around for a long time. But it s a simple, effective, and safe way to help patients relax during dental appointments. In fact, it s so safe that we can even use it for children!

Dental fear and anxiety is a common problem. If you feel anxious about visiting the dentist, don t worry. You are certainly not alone.

Unfortunately, however, dental anxiety often causes people to avoid regular dental visits, opening the door for bigger dental problems requiring more complex treatments. Nitrous oxide helps to dull the sounds and sensations of the dental office so you can feel calm and relaxed. Best of all, unlike other sedation options, the effects of nitrous oxide don t linger. You ll be able to drive yourself home and resume your regular level of activity right away!

Digital Records

Digital records make it easier for us to deliver fast and accurate care. We can access your history with just a click of the mouse so we can easily track any developing conditions or changes in your health. Additionally, digital x-rays can be stored in your record for fast retrieval, comparison, and sharing with specialists, unlike traditional physical x-rays that deteriorate over time. By using digital records, we use far less paper, which is much more environmentally friendly!
