What to Expect

A typical cleaning visit is comfortable, quick, and leaves your mouth feeling great.

Check-In & X-Rays

We update your records and take any needed digital X-rays to check for hidden concerns.

Hygienist Cleaning

Your hygienist removes plaque and tartar buildup, then polishes teeth to a smooth, clean finish.

Doctor Exam

The dentist reviews X-rays, checks for cavities, gum health, and any other concerns.

Home-Care Review

We discuss your personalised brushing and flossing routine and schedule your next visit.
