Dr. Katie Background Personal Dr. Katie attended the University of St. Thomas in St. Paul for college where she studied Biology and competed on the varsity tennis team. She later pursued a career in dentistry, graduating from the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor. Dr. Katie then attended Temple University in Philadelphia for her orthodontic residency, graduating with a Master’s Degree in Oral Biology and a Certificate in Orthodontics. Dr. Katie is proud to be a Board-Certified Orthodontist, signifying her commitment to a high standard of care and lifelong learning.

Dr. Katie loves being an orthodontist and being active in the community by supporting many local organizations. Dr. Katie is proud to have published her Master’s Thesis on adult orthodontic care and continuously strives to stay ahead of the curve through diverse professional affiliations. When not in the office, she loves spending time with her lovely daughters and husband, Dr. Matt.

Dr. Matt Background Personal Dr. Matt attended the University of Michigan, graduating Summa Cum Laude in Material Science and Engineering. Later he got his dental degree at the University of Minnesota. The field of orthodontics greatly appealed to him, as it integrated both his dental education and the mechanical principles he learned in engineering. He completed his three-year orthodontic residency at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Dr. Matt is a Board-Certified Orthodontist committed to providing lifelong quality patient care.

Dr. Matt spends the majority of his time at Larson Orthodontics but feels that giving back to the profession is also important. Hence, he teaches as an associate clinical professor at the University of Minnesota. Outside his office, he is a loving husband and a father to 2 beautiful daughters. He enjoys running, rowing, cross-country skiing and learning how to match (his wife) Dr. Katie’s tennis skills.

Meet our Team

At Larson Orthodontic Specialists, our team is the heart of everything we do. We’re a group of dedicated professionals who genuinely love creating confident, healthy smiles and making the journey just as enjoyable as the result. From your first visit to your final appointment, you’ll be greeted by friendly faces who take the time to listen, answer questions, and tailor your experience to you.

We pride ourselves on being patient-focused in every detail, combining advanced technology with a personal touch to ensure care that’s efficient, comfortable, and customized. Our team brings energy, positivity, and a little fun to each day, helping patients of all ages feel relaxed and right at home. We believe orthodontic treatment should be something you look forward to and we’re here to make that happen every step of the way.
