Why Start Early?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends establishing a dental home by your child&#x27;s first birthday or when their first tooth appears. These early visits build positive associations with dental care, allowing us to monitor oral development while preventing future dental anxiety. By starting early, we create a strong foundation for lifelong oral health while giving parents essential education and support for their child&#x27;s dental journey.

When should my child first visit the dentist? The first visit should occur by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth appearing. Early visits help establish good habits and prevent future problems.

How often should my child have dental checkups? We recommend visits every 6 months for most children. Some may need more frequent visits based on their oral health needs and development.

What makes a pediatric dentist different from a regular dentist? Pediatric dentists complete an additional 2-3 years of specialized training in child development, behavior management, and treating young patients with special healthcare needs.
