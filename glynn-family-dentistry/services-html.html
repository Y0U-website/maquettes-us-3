Dental Services

What we do for you.

From routine prevention to advanced restorative and cosmetic care, every case is held to the same standard. We involve every patient in understanding their care — what we’re doing, why it matters, and what to expect. When a case benefits from specialized expertise, we coordinate with trusted specialists and stay closely involved — but most of what our patients need, we take care of right here.

Exams, Cleanings & Preventive Care

Preventive care is the foundation of everything we do. Most dental problems are easier, less expensive, and less invasive to treat when they’re caught early. That’s why we invest the time in thorough exams and comprehensive hygiene appointments — not the rushed 30-minute variety.

Comprehensive Dental Exams

A full evaluation of your teeth, gums, jaw joints, bite alignment, and soft tissues. We check for decay, gum disease, oral cancer, and anything else that needs attention. If X-rays are needed, we’ll explain exactly why before we take them.

Professional Cleanings

Our hygiene appointments are a full hour. We do thorough periodontal charting, remove plaque and tartar buildup above and below the gumline, polish your teeth, and give you honest feedback on your home care. No rushing, no shortcuts.

Digital X-Rays

Digital radiographs produce high-quality images with up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film. Images are available instantly, and we’ll show them to you on screen and walk you through exactly what we’re seeing.

Oral Cancer Screening

Every comprehensive exam includes a visual and tactile screening of the lips, tongue, cheeks, floor of the mouth, palate, and throat. Early detection of oral cancer dramatically improves outcomes. It takes just a few minutes and could save your life.

Dental Sealants

A protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where decay most commonly starts. Sealants are especially effective for children and teenagers, but adults with deep grooves benefit from them too.

Our approach: We believe prevention is about education, not just procedures. We’ll always take the time to explain what we’re finding, answer your questions, and give you practical guidance on how to take care of your teeth between visits.

Cosmetic & Aesthetic Services

Cosmetic dentistry isn’t just about vanity — it’s about feeling good when you smile. Whether it’s a subtle improvement or a more significant change, we’ll walk you through your options honestly, including what’s realistic, what it costs, and how long it lasts.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Several whitening options that deliver significantly better results than over-the-counter products. We’ll evaluate your situation, discuss your goals, and recommend an approach tailored to your specific needs — because what works well for one patient isn’t always the right fit for another.

Porcelain Veneers

Thin shells of porcelain bonded to the front surfaces of teeth to improve shape, size, color, or alignment. Veneers are custom-designed to look natural and can dramatically transform a smile. We’ll discuss both the benefits and the long-term commitment involved.

Composite Bonding

A tooth-colored resin material applied directly to teeth to repair chips, close gaps, reshape edges, or cover discoloration. Bonding is a conservative, affordable option that can often be completed in a single visit, frequently without anesthesia — though we’ll always offer it if you’d prefer.

Smile Makeovers

A comprehensive approach that may combine multiple treatments — whitening, bonding, veneers, crowns, or orthodontic referral — tailored to your specific goals. We’ll work with you to develop a plan that fits your timeline and budget.

Crowns, Bridges & Fillings

When a tooth is damaged by decay, fracture, or wear, the goal is to restore it to full function and appearance as conservatively as possible. We use modern, tooth-colored materials and take the extra time needed to get the fit, contour, and bite exactly right.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Composite resin fillings that match the natural color of your tooth. These restorations allow for a more conservative approach — preserving healthy tooth structure rather than removing it — and can help reduce the risk of cracks and fractures that are common with older filling materials. Placed in careful layers and sculpted to restore the tooth’s original form.

Ceramic Crowns

A crown covers and protects a tooth that’s too damaged for a filling alone. We use a variety of metal-free ceramic materials that can be customized to properly balance strength and aesthetics based on the location and demands of each tooth. Digital impressions mean no goopy trays — just a comfortable, precise scan.

Fixed Bridges

A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring a prosthetic tooth to the natural teeth on either side. Bridges restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile naturally. We’ll discuss bridges alongside other options like implants so you can make an informed choice.

Inlays & Onlays

Sometimes a tooth needs more than a filling but less than a full crown. Inlays and onlays are custom restorations that fit precisely into or onto the tooth, preserving more of your natural tooth structure while providing excellent strength and durability.

What to expect: We always explain what we’re finding, present your options, and tell you honestly what we’d do if it were our own tooth. If something can wait, we’ll tell you. If it can’t, we’ll explain why.

Dental Implant Restoration

Dental implants are the closest option available that look and function like natural teeth. A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone where it integrates with the bone over several weeks, then a custom crown is attached on top.

Implants are an excellent option for patients who are missing one or more teeth due to injury, decay, or periodontal disease. Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering the teeth on either side. Unlike dentures, they don’t slip, shift, or require adhesives.

The implant process typically involves two phases: surgical placement of the post (which we coordinate with a trusted oral surgeon or periodontist when needed) and the restorative phase, where we design and place the final crown. We use digital impressions and work with skilled dental labs to ensure natural-looking results.

Not every patient is a candidate for implants — factors like bone density, overall health, and gum condition all play a role. We’ll give you an honest assessment and walk you through all of your options, including the timeline and cost involved.

Modern Dental Technology

We invest in technology that produces better results and a better experience — not to chase trends or check boxes. The tools we use support precise diagnosis, thoughtful planning, and outcomes our patients can see and feel.

3Shape Trios Digital Scanner

A handheld intraoral scanner that creates a precise 3D digital model of your teeth in minutes — no more biting down on trays full of impression material. But the scanner does far more than replace the goop. It opens digital capabilities that simply aren’t possible with physical impressions: digital records, diagnostic aids, smile design, complex treatment planning, greatly simplified orthodontic workflows, and streamlined remakes in the event of a lost or broken restoration. It saves time, ensures accuracy, and makes the entire experience more comfortable.

3D Printing

An emerging technology that’s changing how dentistry is delivered. In-office 3D printing allows us to fabricate dental appliances and diagnostic models using fully digital workflows — producing highly customized, precisely fitting results that weren’t possible with prior generations of technology.

Digital Design & CAD/CAM

Digital scans feed directly into computer-aided design software, allowing us to plan and visualize restorations before anything is fabricated. This means better-fitting crowns, more precise implant placement, and the ability to show you what we’re planning before we start.

Digital Radiography

Digital X-rays produce detailed images with a fraction of the radiation exposure of traditional film. Images are available instantly on screen, making it easy to show you exactly what we’re seeing and explain our findings in real time.

Panoramic X-Rays

A single image that captures the entire mouth — all teeth, upper and lower jaws, sinuses, and jaw joints — in one shot. Essential for evaluating wisdom teeth, jaw pathology, implant planning, airway assessment, and overall dental development.

Intraoral Camera

A small camera that captures high-resolution close-up images of individual teeth and soft tissue. We use it to document findings, show you exactly what a problem looks like, explain treatment recommendations, and track changes over time.

AI-Assisted Diagnostics

We utilize artificial intelligence tools that assist in analyzing radiographs and identifying potential findings — from early decay to bone loss to periapical pathology. AI doesn’t replace clinical judgment, but it adds another layer of detection that helps ensure nothing is missed.

Soft Tissue Laser

A dental laser used for precise soft tissue procedures — including gum recontouring, treatment of minor soft tissue lesions, and enhancing access during restorative work. Laser treatment is often more comfortable than traditional methods, with less bleeding, less swelling, and faster healing times.

Digital Patient Records

Your complete dental record — charting, images, X-rays, treatment history, and correspondence — is stored securely in the cloud. This means your information is always accessible, always backed up, and always available when you need care, whether it’s a routine visit or an emergency.

Our philosophy on technology: We don’t adopt new technology just because it exists. Every investment we make has to answer one question: does this help us take better care of our patients? If the answer is yes, we invest. If it’s just a shiny object, we pass.

Root Canal Therapy

The words "root canal" have a worse reputation than the procedure deserves. Modern root canal therapy is a straightforward way to save a tooth that’s infected or damaged at the nerve — and most patients find it a much easier and more comfortable experience than they expected.

When the soft tissue inside a tooth (the pulp) becomes inflamed or infected — from deep decay, a crack, repeated procedures, or trauma — a root canal removes the damaged tissue, cleans and disinfects the canals, and seals the tooth to prevent further infection. In most cases, a crown is placed afterward to protect and restore the tooth.

We use modern anesthetics that work well, take the time to make sure you’re fully numb before we start, and walk you through each step so you know exactly what’s happening. Most patients are genuinely surprised at how comfortable the experience is.

When a case requires specialized care — complex anatomy, retreatment of a previous root canal, or surgical endodontics — we’ll refer you to an endodontist we trust and coordinate your care closely.

Signs you might need a root canal: Persistent tooth pain, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, darkening of a tooth, swelling or tenderness in the gums near a tooth, or a persistent pimple on the gums. If you’re experiencing any of these, call us — we’ll get you in quickly.
