The practice

His journey took him to Bellarmine University in Louisville, Kentucky. He dove into biochemistry and molecular biology— where he met his wonderful wife. Together, they headed north to Michigan for the next chapter. There, Dr. Bourget earned his doctorate degree from the University of Detroit Mercy, pouring his heart into learning how to help people smile with confidence.

Dr. Bourget has since cared for patients full-time at Mansker Creek Dental for many years. He’s passionate about creating a gentle, comfortable experience for everyone who walks through the door.
