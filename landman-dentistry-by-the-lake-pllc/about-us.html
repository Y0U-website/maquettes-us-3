What to Expect at Your First Visit

Warm welcomes are what we’re all about. When you step into our office, you’ll be greeted by our kindhearted staff and a fresh, relaxing space where you’ll feel right at home. During your first visit, Dr. L will focus on getting to know you and learning your dental goals. We’ll gently treat your teeth to a thorough preventive cleaning and exam to ensure brilliant oral health. By the end of your appointment, you’ll walk away with an individualized treatment plan and a happier, healthier smile!

CBCT Technology

Cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) is a special x-ray that captures detailed images of your teeth, gums, and facial bones in just a single scan. This allows Dr. L to diagnose your oral health with maximal speed, safety, and accuracy.

CBCT Technology

Digital Intraoral Scanner

Say goodbye to goopy impressions. We use digital intraoral scanners to take the discomfort out of dental work. Plus, this mighty little camera allows our team to keep you engaged and educated when it comes to treatment planning.

Digital Intraoral Scanner

Dental Milling Machine

Enhancing your experience is at the core of what we do. With our best-in-class dental mill, we can scan your smile for a crown and have a high-quality, tailor-made solution ready to go in as little as one hour!
