The Professional Side

Dr. Anvy Francis is a compassionate general dentist who believes in making every patient feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their smile. She brings a thoughtful, gentle approach to dentistry—blending advanced clinical training with a genuine passion for helping others.

After earning her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in India in 2018, Dr. Francis continued her education in the United States, completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2024. She is committed to delivering high-quality, evidence-based care that supports lasting oral health and beautiful, natural-looking results.

Whether relieving pain, restoring function, or simply helping patients feel more confident, Dr. Francis approaches each visit with kindness and personalized care.

The Personal Side

Outside the office, she enjoys exploring the outdoors, hiking scenic trails, and traveling to new places—all of which inspire a fresh perspective and creative energy she brings back to her practice.
