Advanced Dental Technology

At our practice, we are dedicated to providing the highest standard of care by incorporating the latest advancements in dental technology. Our cutting-edge tools and techniques ensure precise diagnostics, efficient treatments, and exceptional patient experiences. Discover how our advanced technology can transform your dental care journey.

iTero Digital Scanning

Our iTero Digital Scanning technology offers a seamless and comfortable way to capture detailed 3D images of your teeth and gums. This state-of-the-art scanner can often replace the need for traditional dental impressions, providing a faster and more accurate representation of your oral structures . With iTero, we can create perfectly fitted crowns and Invisalign aligners, ensuring optimal results for your dental treatments.

Virtual Smile Preview

Curious what a smile makeover could look like on you? With Virtual Smile Preview, you don’t have to imagine it. Using our digital scanner, we take a quick scan of your teeth along with a short 10-15 second video of you talking and smiling. The software, the same technology behind Invisalign, then merges the two to show a preview of your new smile right on your own face.

Intraoral Cameras

Intraoral cameras are small, high-resolution cameras that allow us to take detailed images of the inside of your mouth. These images help us to diagnose dental issues with greater accuracy and to explain treatment options more clearly . Patients can see what we see, enabling a better understanding of their oral health and the recommended treatments. This transparency enhances patient education and trust.

CBCT 3D Dental Imaging

Our CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) 3D Dental Imaging system provides comprehensive, three-dimensional views of your oral structures. This advanced imaging technology is essential for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning , especially for complex procedures such as dental implants and orthodontics. With CBCT, we can visualize bone density, nerve pathways, and sinus locations, ensuring precise and safe treatments.

BlueSky Bio for Guided Implant Surgery

For optimal results in implant dentistry, we utilize BlueSky Bio software for planning and guiding implant surgery. This powerful tool allows us to create a detailed 3D model of your jaw and plan the exact placement of each implant. By simulating the surgery beforehand, we ensure that each implant is positioned with pinpoint accuracy, reducing the risk of complications and improving the success rate of the procedure . Guided implant surgery with BlueSky Bio provides a predictable and minimally invasive solution , enhancing both the outcome and the patient experience.
