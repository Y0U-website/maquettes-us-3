The practice

At our practice, the patient experience is the number-one priority. Whether you need a gentle routine cleaning or complex restorative work, we ll work closely with you to develop a treatment plan that addresses all your dental needs and goals, leaving you with a beautiful, functional smile you ll feel proud to share.

We provide a range of treatments that can improve the function and appearance of your teeth. We love working with young patients as well, so we hope to become the dental home for your entire family.

Routine Exams Dental Hygiene Dental Fillings Emergency Dentistry Dentures & Partials

We offer complete dentures, metal & flexible partial dentures, and flippers. Our staff will work with you to find which option best fits your needs!

We love to give you a glowing smile! We offer options of in office whitening as well as take home kits.

Our team loves to see your whole family! We have exams that start as soon as the first tooth comes in to get your child on the right track to a healthy smile.

Our office offers many options for crowns and veneers. Our team will work with you and help you decide which option is best for you!
