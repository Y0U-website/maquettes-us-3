Dr. Alex Ness

Dr. Alex hails from right here in Minot, and he completed his undergraduate degree at North Dakota State University, majoring in zoology with a minor in chemistry. He then earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Oregon Health and Science University, which is where he met and married Dr. Stephanie. He is currently a member of the American Dental Association, the North Dakota Dental Association, the Minot Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. He enjoys having the ability to help people live a healthier life.

Dr. Alex’s hobbies include spending time outside, biking, sports, computers, and enjoying relaxing TV shows with Dr. Stephanie. He actually won a state swim meet for the backstroke when he was 10!
