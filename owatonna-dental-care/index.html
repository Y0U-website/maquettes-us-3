Owatonna Dental Care is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for the entire family. Our experienced and friendly team strives to ensure your dental visit is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. We offer a wide range of dental services, including professional cleaning, x-rays, cavity fillings, tooth extractions, and root canals.

Regular professional cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral health. Our dental hygienists use state-of-the-art equipment to remove plaque and tartar buildup, which can lead to gum disease, cavities, and other oral health issues. During your cleaning, we will also check for any signs of tooth decay or gum disease and offer advice on how to improve your oral hygiene routine.
