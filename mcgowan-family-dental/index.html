The practice

Dr. Kelly McGowan received her bachelors degree in Biology from Loyola University Chicago in 2012. She then continued on to receive her degree in Dental Medicine from Midwestern University, CDMI in 2016.

Dr. Eileen McGowan received her bachelors degree in Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology from The University of Washington in 2018. She then continued on to receive her Masters of Biomedical Sciences at Midwestern University in 2020. Completing her professional education, she continued on to receive her Doctorate in Dental Medicine from Midwestern University, CDMI in 2024.

In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Eileen enjoys cosmetic dentistry and Pediatrics. Outside of work, Eileen runs with her dogs, Bowie and Clifford, and loves being with her nephew and nieces as often as possible!
