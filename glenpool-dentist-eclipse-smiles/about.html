Meet Our Doctor

We are committed to providing genuine care in a compassionate environment. Everyone who walks through our doors is treated like family.

Chris Andrus, DMD, FAGD

As a former Marine, businessman, and dentist, Dr. Andrus brings his discipline, focus, expertise, and compassion that patients appreciate. He is truly dedicated to crafting a personalized and modern dental experience for each patient while creating lasting and trusting bonds with them.

Chris Andrus, DMD, FAGD

Dr. Christian Andrus is a highly-trained and experienced dentist who is committed to providing the best possible dental experience for his patients. He has earned the prestigious designation of Fellow in the Academy of General Dentistry, which requires exceptional dedication to continuing education and the completion of a rigorous program of study. In addition to his advanced training, Dr. Andrus is an active member of several professional organizations and is trained in advanced life support techniques.

At his practice, Dr. Andrus takes a co-diagnostic approach to treatment and works with his patients to determine the best course of action for their individual oral health goals. He also offers two levels of sedation to help those with dental phobia overcome their fear. With his extensive education and experience, as well as his commitment to excellence and compassion, Dr. Andrus is the ideal dentist for anyone seeking high-quality dental care.

In addition to his extensive education and training, Dr. Christian Andrus is also a Scholar from the world-renowned Dawson Academy. The Dawson Academy is a leading provider of advanced education in the field of complete dentistry, which focuses on understanding the relationships between the teeth, bite, and muscles of the jaw. The Academy&#x27;s comprehensive curriculum covers a wide range of topics, including occlusion, temporomandibular disorders, esthetics, and comprehensive care planning.

By completing the Dawson Academy&#x27;s rigorous program, Dr. Andrus has demonstrated his commitment to staying at the forefront of the latest developments and techniques in dentistry. This advanced training allows him to provide his patients with the highest level of care and support, helping them to achieve optimal oral health and function.

Aside from his professional achievements, Dr. Andrus is also a dedicated family man who enjoys spending time with his wife and children. In his free time, he enjoys running, weightlifting, and playing outdoor sports. As a former Marine, he is a life member the local VFW chapter in Glenpool. Dr. Andrus also volunteers his time and expertise to provide dentistry to those in need throughout the year.

Dr. Jordan Dixon, DDS

Dr. Jordan Dixon’s passion for dentistry comes from his desire to make a positive impact on others’ smiles while educating patients about the important connection between oral health and overall health. He finds it especially rewarding to go above and beyond his patients’ expectations and be part of a team that creates healthy, confident smiles that keep on giving.

Dr. Jordan Dixon, DDS

Originally born in West Palm Beach, Dr. Dixon grew up in Titusville, Florida, home of the Kennedy Space Center. He attended Florida A&M University, earning a bachelor’s degree in biology and a master’s degree in biomedical sciences, before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. He is especially passionate about cosmetic dentistry and Invisalign.

Dr. Dixon’s philosophy is simple: every patient’s smile should be better than when they first sat in his chair. He understands that being in the dental chair can feel vulnerable, so he approaches every patient with compassion, integrity, and a willingness to listen. He wants patients to feel heard, comfortable, and confident in the care they receive. Joining Eclipse Smiles was an easy decision because of the team’s shared commitment to always putting patients first.

Outside the office, Dr. Dixon enjoys reading and admits he spends a little too much time at Barnes & Noble. He and his fiancé, Craig, enjoy working out, traveling, and attempting to play tennis. They also have a cat named Zola, who believes she is a dog. One of Dr. Dixon’s favorite quotes is, “You can have anything you want—just not all at once.”

Dr. Richard D&#x27;Avanzo DDS

Dr. D&#x27;Avanzo is a Board Certified Orthodontist and the owner of Belle Mead Orthodontics. He graduated from Albright College with a degree in biology and then obtained his DMD degree from Fairleigh Dickinson School of Dental Medicine. He also obtained his Master of Science degree in oral biology from Temple University School of Dentistry with a specialty in orthodontics.
