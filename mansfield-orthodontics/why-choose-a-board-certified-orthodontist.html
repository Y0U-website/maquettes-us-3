Why Choose a Board

Recognized by the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) and the National Commission on Recognition of Dental Specialties , the ABO is the only nationally recognized certification board for orthodontics.

Dr. Mindy didn’t pursue certification because it was required—she did it because you deserve the very best. She combines her extensive training with a warm, empathetic approach to help you feel confident every step of the way.

  • Passing both written and clinical examinations through the American Board of Orthodontics (ABO)
  • Renewing certification every 10 years to remain up-to-date with the latest orthodontic techniques

Why it Matters to You

Choosing an ABO-Board Certified orthodontist means you’re working with a specialist who is:

  • Focused on excellence, clinical expertise, and ongoing education.
  • Dedicated to the highest standard of care in diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up.
  • Passed the ABO written and clinical examinations.
  • Trained in handling complex cases using up-to-date techniques.
