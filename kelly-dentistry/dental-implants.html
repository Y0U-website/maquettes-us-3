A Collaborative Approach to Dental Implants

Dental implant treatment often involves more than one provider. In many cases, a trusted oral surgeon or specialist places the implant, while Kelly Dentistry remains actively involved in planning the case, designing the final restoration, restoring the implant after healing, and helping maintain it over time.

This coordinated approach helps patients move through implant treatment with a clear plan, consistent communication, and a final restoration designed to support both function and aesthetics.

Types of Implant Restorations We Help Plan and Restore

Single-Tooth Implants

A single implant can replace one missing tooth without relying on neighboring teeth for support.

Multiple-Tooth Implant Restorations

Implants may be used to support a bridge or restoration when several teeth are missing.

Implant-Supported Dentures or Prosthetics

For patients missing many teeth, implants may help support a more stable denture or prosthesis.

What to Expect

  • Implant Evaluation: We evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, bone support, and overall goals.
  • Digital Imaging and Planning: 3D CBCT imaging helps us understand your anatomy and plan the restorative outcome.

Dental Implant FAQs

Does Kelly Dentistry surgically place dental implants?

Kelly Dentistry focuses on the restorative and treatment-planning side of implant care. When surgical implant placement is needed, we coordinate with trusted local oral surgeons and specialists.

Why does the final restoration need to be planned before surgery?

Planning the final restoration first helps guide where the implant should be placed for proper function, appearance, and long-term support.

What does Kelly Dentistry do during the implant process?

Our team evaluates your smile, uses digital imaging for planning, coordinates with the surgical specialist, restores the implant with a custom crown or prosthesis, and provides long-term follow-up care.

Can one implant replace one missing tooth?

Yes. A single dental implant can often be used to replace one missing tooth with a custom crown designed to look and function naturally.

How do I know if I am a candidate for a dental implant?

The first step is an implant evaluation. Kelly Dentistry will review your oral health, bone support, bite, and goals before recommending next steps.
