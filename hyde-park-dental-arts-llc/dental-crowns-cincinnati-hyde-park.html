Cincinnati Dental Crown Procedure

We know that going for any kind of dental surgery or treatment can feel a bit stressful.

Which is why our team here at Hyde Park Dental Arts prides itself on offering a comfortable environment during the procedure while maintaining full transparency with all our patients about what the procedure entails.

So, in the spirit of that transparency, here’s an overview of the steps you can expect from our procedure:

During your first visit, your dentist will numb your jaw to ensure your comfort before preparing your tooth for the crown. This involves removing damaged enamel and carefully reshaping the tooth to create a precise fit for the crown.

Next, we’ll take detailed impressions, photographs, and scans to note down your exact measurements and ensure that your crown is built to be a perfect fit. Temporary crown While your measurements are sent to the dental lab and your custom crown is crafted, your dentist will cap your existing tooth with a temporary crown to protect it from further damage.

After a brief healing period, your dentist will bond your newly created custom crown securely to your tooth with the use of strong dental cement.

Your dentist will ensure the crown fits perfectly and seamlessly blends with your natural teeth. They will then polish it to achieve a flawless, natural-looking finish.

How Much Does a Dental Crown Cost Here In Cincinnati?

The cost of a dental crown depends on several factors, including the material used, the area of the mouth, and your insurance coverage. At our clinic, we use only the highest-quality materials: Zirconia , known for its strength and durability, and Porcelain , which offers a highly aesthetic, tooth-colored finish.

On average, dental crowns can range from $1000 to $2500 . The exact cost depends on the material chosen, the location of the crown, and whether insurance coverage is available.

Dental Crown FAQ’s

The average cost of a dental crown will be around $500 to $3000 for each tooth. To provide more specific estimates, metal crowns cost $600 to $2500, and all-porcelain crowns can cost $800 to $3,000. How long do crowns last? Dental crowns are known for their longevity, typically lasting 10 to 15 years or longer, depending on the amount of care and upkeep given to them. Which dental crown is best? The best options for a dental crown are zirconia and porcelain crowns, as they are the most commonly used and highly effective.

Both materials closely mimic the appearance of natural teeth, matching their shape, size, and color. Additionally, zirconia and porcelain are biocompatible, ensuring they are safe and gentle on your gums and surrounding tissues, making them ideal for long-lasting, natural-looking restorations.
