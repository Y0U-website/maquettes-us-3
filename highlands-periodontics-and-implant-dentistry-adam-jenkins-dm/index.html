What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone, and they are designed to function like natural tooth roots. They support crowns, bridges, or dentures, offering a long-term solution to tooth loss. With dental implants you can eat, speak, and smile with confidence, knowing your teeth are stable and natural-looking.
