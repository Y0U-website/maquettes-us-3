Our Story

Dr. Richard Duong

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Duong is passionate about being a pediatric dentist and loves entertaining children. Interacting with his patients is his favorite part of the job as he is a child at heart with a very vivid imagination. He strives to make every dental visit an enjoyable and pleasant experience for his pediatric patients by drawing upon his love of children’s stories and fairytales. Dr. Duong is committed to providing the best possible patient care and endeavors to provide an exceptional experience for children and adolescents that is as entertaining as it is educational.

When Dr. Duong is not providing care for his patients in Milpitas, he loves to travel the world with his wife and son, especially to areas of Europe where many of the fairy tale stories he loves originated. He continues to enjoy his hobby of reading classic children’s books and fairytales with his son to this day.

What Makes Us Different

At Milpitas Children’s Dentistry, it is our goal to help your child along their journey to lifelong dental wellness. We do this by creating a fun environment that kids look forward to visiting while educating them about their oral health. We empower our patients to be the heroes of their own stories and encourage them to take ownership of their at-home care. This sets a foundation for making good choices and establishing healthy habits that will last a lifetime.

Our Values

Integrity

At Milpitas Children’s Dentistry, we will never give you a sales pitch and only recommend the necessary treatments to keep your child happy and healthy. We believe in providing an honest, straightforward, and transparent approach to dentistry and strive to keep you informed every step of the way.

Quality

We take pride in our work and strive to go above and beyond to make sure every treatment is as effective, efficient, and comfortable as possible. From simple cleanings to restorative treatments, you can rest assured knowing your child will be receiving top-notch, kid-friendly care.

Having Fun

We love to see our patients’ smiles! Sharing a laugh and getting to know our patients on a personal level allows us to provide individualized dental care in an office you and your family actually look forward to visiting.
