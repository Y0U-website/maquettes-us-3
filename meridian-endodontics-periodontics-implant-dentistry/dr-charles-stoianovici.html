Dr. Charles Stoianovici

Dr. Charles Stoianovici Periodontist & Implant Surgeon

Dr. Stoianovici is a Board Certified Periodontist and Dental Implant Surgeon. His passion is to provide clinical excellence through modern and innovative techniques, as well as provide compassionate care for his patients in an educational, enriching, and transparent environment.

Through his distinctive research, Dr. Stoianovici was given the opportunity to present at various Local and National meetings and research competitions, being awarded third place in the Billy Pennel Basic Science Competition from the Southern Academy of Periodontics, and as a finalist at the Osteology Foundation. Dr. Stoianovici will also be presenting his research in the highly prestigious Orban Competition at the American Academy of Periodontology.

When not providing excellent periodontal service to his patients, Dr. Stoianovici enjoys fishing at Lake Pewaukee and other nearby fishing spots, the outdoors, riding motorcycles, health & fitness, and traveling.
