Invisalign

Aligners are much more comfortable than traditional braces. Since there are no brackets or wires the patient does not need to worry about getting any painful nicks or cuts in their mouth. The aligners are smooth and typically do not irritate the mouth. The number of emergency visits due to a poking wire or broken bracket is essentially eliminated.

Patients can remove their aligners when brushing, flossing, eating or drinking which is a significant advantage to traditional braces. It allows you to practice better oral hygiene and to eat foods that are not recommended with traditional braces. You will not have to worry about food particles getting stuck which commonly happens with traditional braces.

Aligners require minimal maintenance. They can get discolored over time, however, our trained staff will advise you on how to keep your aligners as clean as possible until you are ready to use your next set of trays.

Aligners are clear, making them a lot less noticeable than traditional braces. Most people cannot notice when aligners are being worn. That means you can feel free to smile and will not be self conscious. Is Invisalign treatment right for you? Are you a candidate?
