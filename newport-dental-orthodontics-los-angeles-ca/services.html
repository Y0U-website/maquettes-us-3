Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry is a proactive approach to oral health that emphasizes routine cleanings, early detection, and daily habits to stop dental issues before they develop. The goal is simple: preserve your natural teeth and avoid unnecessary treatments.

Curodont™ For years, the standard for early-stage cavities was to "wait and see." Meaning you were often told to watch a small spot grow until it was finally big enough to drill and fill. At Newport Dental & Orthodontics, what once seemed impossible is now a reality! Curodont can treat early cavities by supporting the tooth’s natural repair process. No needle, no stress!

Dental Sealants Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings that adhere to the chewing surface of back teeth. Research has shown they reduce the risk of tooth decay by nearly 80 % in molars. This is especially important for your child’s dental health.

Dental X-Rays X-ray imaging is a cornerstone of modern dentistry. It gives a dental professional a comprehensive view of the patient’s oral health and reveals what the naked eye cannot see.

Fluoride Treatment Fluoride is a natural mineral found in the earth’s crust, but it’s also present in some foods and water. In the context of dental health, fluoride plays a pivotal role, as it helps strengthen tooth enamel and prevent tooth decay.

Oral Exam Oral exams are very thorough examinations of your entire oral health, not just your teeth. Oral exams help your dentist collect more information than a dental cleaning or checkup and establish a baseline to compare the progress or decline of your oral wellness.

Teeth Cleaning Preventative teeth cleanings are a cornerstone of lasting oral health. Professional cleanings are important to helping reduce the risk of tooth decay and gum disease.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry helps patients regain the full function, appearance, and health of their teeth. Whether you’re dealing with missing teeth, decay, or damaged dental work, our team offers personalized restorative dentistry procedures designed to restore your smile and your confidence.

Abscess Tooth Treatment A dental abscess, also known as an abscessed tooth or tooth abscess, occurs when a pocket of pus forms in or around an infected tooth root. Abscessed teeth can happen to anyone, but a history of gum disease, untreated cavities, and cracks in teeth can increase your risk of developing one. A dental abscess is typically very painful and cannot be resolved without treatment from a dentist.

Cavity Treatment A cavity is a common dental issue that affects people of all ages. If you’re seeking effective cavity treatment, you’re in the right place. This article will guide you through the essential aspects of cavity treatment near you, from understanding what cavities are to exploring the best treatment options available.

Dental Bridge A bridge is a dental appliance that replaces one or more natural missing teeth, thereby "bridging" the space between two teeth. If you are missing any teeth and are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene practices, you may be a good candidate for a bridge.

Dental Checkup Regular dental exams are a cornerstone of oral health. They play a crucial role in maintaining a healthy smile and preventing dental issues.

Dental Crowns A crown, also known as a cap, covers a tooth to restore it to its normal shape and size, thereby strengthening and improving its overall appearance.

Dental Fillings To treat a cavity, your dentist will remove the decayed portion of the tooth and then "fill" the area on the tooth where the decay was. Fillings are also used to repair cracked, broken or injured teeth and teeth that have been worn down from nail-biting, tooth grinding or other misuse.

Dental Implants Dental implants are replacement tooth roots. Implants provide a strong foundation for fixed (permanent) or removable replacement teeth that are made to match your natural teeth.

Dentures Replace your missing teeth with cost-effective dentures to restore your smile, feel confident and enjoy your favorite foods again.

Endodontic Treatment Endodontic treatment is a specialized area of dentistry that focuses on the health of the dental pulp and tissues surrounding the root of a tooth.

Prosthodontics Prosthodontics, a specialized field of dentistry, that offers a range of solutions for damaged and missing teeth. A prosthodontist not only restores the appearance of your smile but also preserves your oral health.

Root Canals A root canal is a treatment used to save a tooth that is badly decayed and/or infected.

Tooth Extractions When tooth decay or traumatic injuries compromise the restorability of your tooth, it’s typically best to remove the entire tooth to prevent pain or recurring infections that put your other teeth at risk.

Toothache Relief Toothaches can result from tooth or oral tissue damage and can also be a sign of inflammation or infection. Only a dentist can determine the exact cause of your toothache, but we discuss some possible causes and methods for temporary relief.

Wisdom Teeth Removal Sometimes, wisdom tooth removal is required do to the impacted teeth causing you pain, swelling, or a bad taste in your mouth.
