Composite Bonding

Composite bonding is a fast and conservative procedure done to repair chipped or broken teeth, fill gaps between the teeth, or cover exposed tooth root surfaces. Composite bonding is minimally invasive and often requires no preparation of the tooth. The highly skilled dentists at Jackson & Jackson Dental usually complete this type of procedure in one visit.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Similar to composite bonding, we use composite resin fillings to seamlessly blend our restorations with the patient’s tooth by exactly matching the shade of the tooth. If a patient is concerned about the appearance of old failing amalgam or silver fillings, the dentists at Jackson & Jackson Dental can replace those old fillings with highly esthetic bonded composite resin restorations. This type of restoration is a great option for patients who have small fillings that are failing or have minimal decay on a tooth.

Gum Recontouring

The size and shape of the teeth play an important role in the overall esthetics of a beautiful smile. For patients with a “gummy smile”, their teeth may appear short or irregularly shaped. Using the latest in laser dentistry technology, our dentists can reshape the patient’s gums to create a more natural balance between the teeth and the gums.

Tooth Reshaping & Recontouring

Tooth reshaping is the process by which our skilled dentists sculpt the tooth to remove rough or sharp edges that can be caused by excessive wear from the patient grinding their teeth. At Jackson & Jackson Dental, tooth recontouring is an artistic technique that we use to develop and accentuate the natural anatomy of the teeth to produce the reflective qualities of a beautiful natural looking smile. These procedures are one of the most conservative ways to quickly and effectively enhance the patient’s smile.
