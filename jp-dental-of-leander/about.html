Meet Dr. Jae Pi

A Heart for People, A Mind for Precision

Dr. Jae Pi’s journey to dentistry began with a simple but profound motivation: a genuine desire to care for people. While he possesses a highly analytical background, he chose dentistry because it allows him to build personal connections and improve lives through direct, hands-on care. Known for his warm personality and gentle approach, Dr. Pi strives to ensure that every patient feels heard, comfortable, and truly cared for from the moment they walk through our doors.

Expertise & Education

Dr. Pi brings a unique perspective to oral health, combining the meticulous problem-solving skills of an engineer with the artistic precision of a dental surgeon.

Doctor of Dental Surgery: Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry of USC

Graduate Studies: Civil and Environmental Engineering, UC Berkeley

Comprehensive Care with a Personal Touch

With over 10 years of experience as a general dentist, Dr. Pi provides a wide range of treatments for patients of all ages. His extensive background allows him to handle everything from routine family dentistry to complex restorative work.

Furthermore, his additional two years of specialized orthodontic training enables him to offer a comprehensive approach to dental health. He doesn’t just look at a single tooth; he considers the overall harmony of your smile and long-term oral function.

Our Philosophy

"I became a dentist because I wanted a career dedicated to helping and caring for others. My goal is to combine technical excellence with a gentle, compassionate touch so that your visit is as pleasant as it is effective."
