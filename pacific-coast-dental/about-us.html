Dr. Katherine Kieu

Dr. Katherine Kieu earned her Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from UCLA. She then attended UCSF, receiving her DDS and completing her orthodontic residency at the University of Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, in 2009. She has worked with Pacific Coast Dental since 2020. As a board-certified orthodontist, she aims to help patients achieve healthy and beautiful smiles by straightening their teeth.

What Dr. Kieu enjoys about being an orthodontist is building relationships and friendships with the team while positively impacting her patients lives. She helps make our Pacifica dental office a place where we treat every person who walks through the door like family.

Dr. Kieu also loves helping people become more confident versions of themselves as their smiles are transformed through orthodontic treatment. She entirely agrees with the expression, "when you re smilin , the whole world smiles with you."

In her spare time, Dr. Kieu enjoys reading, traveling, and trying new restaurants. She is also an aspiring children s book author. Another interesting detail about Dr. Kieu is that on her first and only fishing trip, she caught a leopard shark, which after a quick photo, she released back into the ocean.

Ann Marie Acosta

Ann Marie Acosta earned her Bachelor of Science in Managerial Economics from the University of California Davis. She was gaining her credentials as her husband, Dr. John Acosta, received his dental training. This timing made Ann the most qualified to keep the office side running smoothly so he could focus on the practice’s clinical side. Ann and John have a great partnership, maintaining a professional and pleasant atmosphere free from drama.

Ann’s leading role is managing the business, coordinating with patients, and supporting the team by addressing computer issues and financial and marketing concerns. Anywhere our team needs help, Ann is always ready to jump in, putting her 12 years of experience in dentistry to work for every patient every day.

What Ann loves about her job is the Pacific Coast Dental team. She finds them the hardest working, most patient, supportive, and the kindest people she’s ever had the pleasure of knowing. The team lacks any air of entitlement. Instead, everyone is willing to help where needed. We love showing patients just how great their visit can be. We aim to help our patients realize that the dental office is not a scary place. Our patients are open and approachable and appreciate the hard work that goes into every appointment.

Knowing that we are helping patients with their overall health and keeping their teeth, in the long run, makes all our hard work worthwhile. We especially enjoy teaching young kids to love the dental office. The best stories are when parents share how their little one begs to come to the dental appointment with them. When patients regularly tell us they enjoy seeing us (they hate the shots but love us), we know we are doing something right.

Ann loves to read, write, dance, and play music on the piano or ukulele when not serving patients. She also enjoys time with her children and traveling. Ann also used to be a youth missionary and is comfortable giving inspirational talks in front of large crowds and directing creative productions and performances like dances, skits, and team-building exercises.
