Dental implants are small posts that are placed in the jawbone to act like artificial tooth roots. Once the implant has healed and bonded with the bone, it can support a custom restoration such as an implant crown , bridge, or denture.

Unlike removable appliances that sit on top of the gums, implants are designed to create a stable foundation for replacement teeth. This can help restore chewing strength, support a natural-looking smile, and protect the shape and function of your bite.

For many patients, implants are part of a larger restorative dentistry plan. That plan may include replacing one missing tooth, rebuilding several teeth, or improving the fit and stability of a denture.

A tooth implant works by replacing the root structure of a missing tooth. After the implant post is placed, the surrounding bone gradually heals around it. This process helps create the strength needed to support the final restoration.

Once the implant is ready, a restoration is attached. This may be a single implant dental crown, a bridge for multiple missing teeth, or a denture designed to connect to implants for added support.
