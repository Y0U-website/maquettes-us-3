Orthodontics / Clear Braces

Clear Braces, a relatively recent innovation is a system, of straightening teeth without the use of conventional braces. A series of clear plastic aligners are utilized to create tooth movement. A computer program carefully guided by the dentist can generate a series of aligners that move teeth. The Clear Braces trays are virtually undetectable, easy to use and comfortable to wear. Clear Braces is recommended for orthodontic situations with mild to moderate spacing or crowding.
