Osceola Community Health Services

Mission - Osceola Community Health Services is committed to improving the health of every community we serve by delivering compassionate, high-quality, and affordable healthcare. Vision - We are building a healthier future one patient at a time by providing high quality accessible patient centered services to the communities we serve. Osceola Community Health Services health centers are strategically located throughout the county. If you are looking for doctors in Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Poinciana, or BuenaVentura Lakes (BVL) Osceola Community Health Services offers accessibility.

"When you come to one of our health centers we want you to feel special, because you are." We want you to know how much we care, because we do."

- Dr. Belinda Johnson-Cornett, President & CEO
