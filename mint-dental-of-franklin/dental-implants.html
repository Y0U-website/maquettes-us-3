FAQ about Dental Implants

We create your individualized treatment plan during your initial visit, including discussing your smile goals, costs, and timeline.

After your examination, we use our digital scanner, Cerec Primscan, to design and create your dental impressions. It makes a 3D impression of your tooth without the need for messy bite impressions saving you time.

We also offer All-on-Four dental implants.

Instead of replacing every tooth individually, All-on-Four dental implants support the entire arch of your top or bottom teeth. The replacements look and feel identical to natural teeth.

  • How do dental implants work? Dental implants are used to restore functionality and treat cosmetic problems, and individuals who have: Undersized teeth
  • Missing teeth
  • Chipped, cracked, or misshapen teeth
  • Step 1: A titanium post is fused with the jaw and serves as your new tooth root.
  • Step 2: After healing, a custom-created ceramic crown caps the post.
  • Step 3: Enjoy eating, laughing, and smiling with your new and natural-looking teeth!
