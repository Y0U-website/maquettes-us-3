Manhasset Office

Happy KiDDS is dedicated to providing children with excellent dental care and has been an active member of the community since opening in 2006. Our modern, kid-friendly office makes every child feel relaxed, so relaxed that most children don’t want to leave at the conclusion of their appointment! It is not uncommon for parents to tell our doctors, “My child could not wait to come to see you!”

We Look Forward to Meeting You.

800 Community Drive, Suite 200 Manhasset, NY 11030 (516) 869-9500 Come see what all the children are talking about. Happy KiDDS’ Manhasset office at 800 Community Drive, Suite 200 is conveniently located off Exit 33 of the Long Island Expressway. Ample parking is available and the building is handicap accessible. Every room is appointed with TVs and video games to entertain your child during his or her visit. All charting and record-keeping are paperless for minimal impact on the environment and digital radiographs are used to reduce exposure to your child.
