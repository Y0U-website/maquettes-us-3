Wisdom Teeth Removal in Eugene OR

Why Should I Have My Wisdom Teeth Removed?

Impacted wisdom teeth can grow sideways, only partially emerge from the gumline, or remain stuck beneath the bone and gum tissue. These awkward positions can lead to significant oral health problems:

  • Bacterial infections from partially erupted teeth
  • Swelling, pain, and jaw stiffness
  • Disruption in natural or orthodontic tooth alignment
  • Jawbone damage due to tumor or cyst formation

Oral Examination and Consultation

Using advanced digital imaging and a thorough oral examination, our board-certified oral surgeons—Drs. Hammon, Allender, and Tucker—can assess whether removal is necessary. We often begin evaluations during the teenage years, either via a referral from a dentist, orthodontist, or during routine dental exams.

Early intervention often results in better outcomes, less discomfort, and a quicker recovery period.
