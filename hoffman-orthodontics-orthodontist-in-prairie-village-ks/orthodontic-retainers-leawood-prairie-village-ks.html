What Are Orthodontic Retainers ?

Orthodontic retainers are custom orthodontic appliances worn after braces or clear aligner treatment to help keep teeth in their corrected positions. They are used during the retention phase, which begins once active tooth movement is complete.

Hoffman Orthodontics provides retainers as part of ongoing orthodontic care in Leawood and Prairie Village, KS. There are different types of retainers, and the right option depends on the smile, the bite, and the treatment plan.

The Importance of Retention After Orthodontic Treatment

Retention matters because teeth can gradually shift over time after braces or clear aligners if they are not supported in their new positions. Wearing retainers as directed helps protect the alignment achieved during treatment.

This phase plays an important role in maintaining long-term smile stability and preserving the results patients have worked to achieve. Hoffman Orthodontics offers retention care in Leawood and Prairie Village, KS to help patients maintain those results after treatment.

Fixed Retainers

Fixed retainers are retainers that stay in place instead of being removed throughout the day. They are commonly positioned along the inside of certain teeth and may be recommended when steady, ongoing support is especially helpful.

Because they remain in place, fixed retainers provide continuous retention without relying on daily removal and reinsertion. For some patients at Hoffman Orthodontics, that consistency can make them a practical part of long-term retention care.

Bonded Retainers

Bonded retainers are a specific type of retainer attached behind the teeth so they stay in place at all times. In practical terms, they work quietly in the background to help maintain alignment while remaining out of direct view.

This type of retainer may be used when a more secure, built-in form of retention is preferred. Patients in Leawood and Prairie Village, KS who receive bonded retainers at Hoffman Orthodontics are given guidance on what to expect with a retainer that remains attached as part of everyday life.
