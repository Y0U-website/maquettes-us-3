Orthodontic Expanders in Yukon, OK

Palatal Expanders

We offer palatal expanders as part of our comprehensive orthodontic treatment options. This innovative approach supports optimal jaw development, alleviates crowding, and can reduce the need for tooth extractions.

Expanders work by broadening the palate, which not only enhances dental alignment but also increases airway volume. This improvement can positively impact overall health, particularly for patients dealing with sleep-disordered breathing issues such as sleep apnea, snoring, and related concerns.

In addition to supporting better breathing, expanders can help address common concerns like night terrors, sleepwalking, mouth breathing, and bed-wetting, promoting better sleep quality and overall well-being.
