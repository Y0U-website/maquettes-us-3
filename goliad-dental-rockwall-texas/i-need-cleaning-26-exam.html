Preventive Care

At Goliad Dental, we believe prevention is the key to a healthy smile. As your trusted family dentist in Rockwall, TX, we provide professional dental cleanings, routine dental exams, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants to help prevent cavities and gum disease. Regular preventive dentistry visits allow us to detect problems early, protect your oral health, and keep your smile looking its best. Schedule your dental cleaning today and let our team help you maintain a healthy, confident smile for years to come.

Dental X Rays Dental radiographs, or X-rays, are essential preventative tools that provide us with a detailed view of your oral health that isn’t visible during a standard clinical exam. By utilizing this technology, our team can safely and accurately detect hidden abnormalities to create a precise, personalized treatment plan for you.

* Issues located inside a tooth or below the gum line

Early detection is the most effective way to protect your smile. By identifying dental concerns early, we help you save time and money while preventing unnecessary discomfort.

Dental Exam During your initial visit and routine check-ups, our skilled dentists and hygienists provide a thorough evaluation to ensure your smile remains healthy and strong. Our examination process includes:

Diagnostic X-rays: Essential for identifying decay, cysts, bone loss, and tooth or root positioning.

Oral Cancer Screening: A careful check of your face, lips, tongue, throat, and gum tissues.

Gum Disease Evaluation: Monitoring the health of the bone and gums surrounding your teeth to detect early signs of periodontal disease.

Cavity Detection: A detailed inspection of all tooth surfaces using specialized instruments.

Restoration Review: An assessment of the integrity of your current fillings, crowns, and other dental work.

Professional Dental Cleaning Our professional dental cleanings, or prophylaxis, are performed by our skilled Registered Dental Hygienists to ensure your smile stays healthy and bright. Each appointment includes a comprehensive dental exam and the following treatments:

Removal of Calculus (Tartar) : We use specialized instruments to safely remove hardened plaque buildup from above and below the gum line.

Removal of Plaque : Our team clears away the sticky film of bacteria and food debris that produces toxins and leads to gum inflammation, helping to prevent gingivitis and periodontal disease.

Teeth Polishing: We finish your cleaning by removing surface stains and remaining plaque, leaving your teeth feeling smooth, clean, and refreshed.
