Travis Howey, DDS

Travis M. Howey, DDS | Dentist in Klahanie, WA practicing since 2005

Dr. Howey graduated from the University of Washington, School of Dentistry , and received many honorary awards, including the American College of Prosthodontics Achievement Award. Since graduation he has worked in several pediatric and general dentist offices in Eastern and Western Washington, as well as in his home town of Las Vegas, Nevada. Over the last 10 years, Dr. Howey owned and worked at two practices: one in Sammamish and one in Ellensburg, but has recently decided to focus his time on the Sammamish location.

Dr. Howey is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) , Washington State Dental Association (WSDA) , and the Seattle King County Dental Society (SKCDS) . Along with his dental society memberships, he has been a mentor to dental students through the University of Washington’s mentor program.

Outside of the office, Dr. Howey enjoyed being a part of the community by working with the youth and coaching little league and soccer when his kids were younger. Throughout his childhood, Dr. Howey played soccer on a competitive level and still enjoys playing. He also likes to snow ski in the winter, and water ski in the summer.
