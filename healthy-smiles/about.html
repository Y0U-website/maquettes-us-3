Meet your Doctor

Dr. Steven Oxner

As a native Memphian, Dr. Steven Oxner grew up only one stoplight away from the office. He attended Christ Methodist Day School, White Station High School, Mississippi College, and The University of Tennessee College of Dentistry, where he graduated with his Doctorate in Dental Surgery. His local connections and years of experience give him a strong understanding of the people and community he serves.

‍ Since 2017, Dr. Oxner has had the opportunity to serve the Memphis metropolitan area in both private and non-profit capacities. He is a member of the ADA, TDA, and Memphis Dental Society. Over the years, he has enjoyed helping patients with a wide variety of dental needs, from relieving pain to supporting oral health goals to creating cosmetic changes that help patients feel more confident.

‍ He enjoys the variety that comes with general dentistry and especially appreciates the blend of science, art, and construction involved in his work. For patients, that means care from a doctor who values both precision and practical results.

He and his wife, Abbey, first met during his third year of dental school while they were both attending Collierville First Baptist Church. Ten years later, they have four children ranging from 7 to 1 year old. They have chosen the great challenge and adventure of homeschooling their sweet kiddos. In their free time, the Oxners love to go on long walks and read as a family. Abbey loves to bake, and Dr. Oxner enjoys home renovation projects.

The Oxners are active members of Living Hope Church in Piperton, TN. Dr. Oxner and Abbey have always considered their profession to be a component of their ministry as a couple. They want Healthy Smiles to be a joyful environment where patients feel valued and comfortable.

Modern technology for comfortable care

Healthy Smiles combines a warm, personal approach with modern dental technology to help make care more efficient and convenient. Our office is equipped with tools that support precision, clear communication, and streamlined treatment. One of the biggest advantages for patients is the ability to complete certain treatments, including same-day crowns, without unnecessary delays.

Digital x-rays

At Healthy Smiles, we use digital x-rays as part of our modern approach to care. Digital imaging supports clear diagnostics and helps the team evaluate your dental health with efficiency. It also allows for a more streamlined experience during your visit.

Scanning

Digital scanning technology helps our team gather detailed information while improving the flow of treatment planning. It supports a more modern patient experience and plays an important role in services like same-day crown fabrication, clear aligner therapy, and nightguard fabrication. Our patients benefit from technology that helps care feel more precise and efficient.

Same-day crowns

Our office features same-day crown fabrication using a digital scanner and milling unit. This technology helps make the process more convenient by allowing crowns to be created in a single visit. For patients, that can mean fewer appointments and a more efficient treatment experience.
