About Us

Dr. Souha Hanna Dr. Souha Hanna has been dedicated to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles for over 20 years. After earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from the New York University College of Dentistry in 2000, she gained valuable clinical experience before opening her own practice in 2008. Known for her gentle approach and attention to detail, Dr. Hanna is passionate about providing personalized, high-quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

She is committed to continuing education and stays current with the latest advancements in dentistry, to ensure her patients receive the best possible care.

Fluent in both English and Arabic, Dr. Hanna enjoys building lasting relationships with patients and making every visit a positive experience. Outside the office,

she is a devoted wife and proud mother of three,

bringing the same compassion and dedication to her practice that she does to her family.

Dr. Andrew Hanna Dr. Andrew Hanna is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-focused dental care with an emphasis on comfort, education, and long-term oral health. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2023 and is passionate about delivering modern, high-quality dentistry using the latest techniques and technology.

Dr. Hanna believes in building lasting relationships with his patients by taking the time to understand their needs and helping them feel informed and at ease throughout every visit. He is committed to continuing his education and staying current with advancements in dentistry to provide the best possible care. Schedule your appointment today and meet Dr. Hanna. He looks forward to welcoming you to the practice and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile with personalized, compassionate care.

Our Staff Our team is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable, welcoming environment. We believe that great dentistry is about more than just treating teeth—it’s about building lasting relationships with our patients and making every visit a positive experience.

Our caring and knowledgeable staff works together to provide personalized care for every patient, from routine preventive visits to cosmetic and restorative treatments. We take the time to listen, answer questions, and make sure you feel comfortable and confident in your care.

We’re proud to be part of the community and look forward to welcoming you and your family to our dental family.
