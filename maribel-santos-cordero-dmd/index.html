The practice

Each child is a unique individual with a specific set of needs. From infancy through the teenage years, children will need different approaches to managing their dental growth and development, their behavior, and other particular needs. Baby & Toddler Oral Wellness Program Nurturing early oral health to support healthy smiles.

Airway Health & Sleep Trouble sleeping? Take our sleep breathing assessment today.

Integrative Orthodontics A dental specialty focused on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws for improved function and aesthetics.

Lip and tongue-tie treatments address restricted oral tissues that can impact feeding, speech, and overall oral function, helping to restore mobility and improve quality of life.

Oral Myofunctional Therapy is a specialized treatment approach focused on correcting improper oral and facial muscle functions to enhance breathing, swallowing, and overall oral health.

At Dentistry for Children & Adolescents, we believe that a dentist and a patient become a team for treating an individual’s dental needs. A child who loves going to the dentist will be an adult who has no anxiety or hesitation about receiving oral health care.
