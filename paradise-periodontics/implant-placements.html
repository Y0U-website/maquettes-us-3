Extraction With Immediate Implant Placement

If a tooth is requiring extraction and an implant is desired, in certain specific instances, the implant may be performed at the same time as the extraction. Your periodontist will evaluate whether you meet the criteria to have this performed.

  • Approximately 1-2 hours with the use of a local anesthetic.
  • First, the tooth is extracted and socket debrided. If your periodontist feels the implant may be placed, a series of drills will expand your bone and allow for implant placement. A bone graft will fill the surrounding surgical site.
  • A post-operative visit to check on your healing and to remove any remaining sutures will take place approximately two weeks following your surgery date. The implant will heal for 4 months prior to crown placement.
