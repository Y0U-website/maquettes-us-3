What to Expect: Adult Treatment Process

  • Consultation & Evaluation – We’ll review your dental and medical history, evaluate alignment and bite, and determine the best adult-appropriate plan.
  • Treatment Planning – You’ll receive a personalized plan outlining your options (braces vs aligners), timeline, expected outcomes, and cost.
  • Appliance Placement – Whether you choose braces or aligners, we provide step-by-step guidance for your lifestyle.
  • Monitoring & Adjustments – Adult treatment may include fewer visits but attentive care to track progress and comfort.
  • Completion & Retention – After your active phase, we’ll transition to ongoing retention (see Retainers page) to secure your smile long-term.
