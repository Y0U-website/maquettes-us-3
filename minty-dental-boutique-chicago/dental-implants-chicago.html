Types of Dental Implant Solutions We Offer

Every smile is unique, and so are implant treatments. Dr. Marjan designs custom plans that match your goals and budget, including:

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Ideal for replacing one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Perfect for patients missing several adjacent teeth—supported by two or more implants for maximum stability.

Full-Arch Dental Implants

Replace an entire upper or lower arch with four to six implants supporting a full, fixed bridge or denture (“All-on-4” or “All-on-6” concept).

Implant-Retained Dentures

Snap-in or removable dentures anchored by implants for better comfort and chewing ability.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

If bone loss has occurred, Dr. Marjan may recommend a bone graft or sinus lift to rebuild strength for successful implant placement. Each case is evaluated individually to ensure safe, predictable outcomes.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have healthy gums and adequate jawbone
  • Don’t smoke or are willing to quit during healing
  • Are seeking a long-term, fixed tooth replacement

Our Dental Implant Process

1. Consultation and 3D Evaluation

Your journey begins with a digital consultation and CBCT 3D scan , allowing our team to assess your bone structure and map out the most precise placement plan.

2. Guided Implant Placement

Using computer-guided technology, your implant is placed gently into the jawbone. We offer oral sedation by prescription to help you stay relaxed and comfortable during the procedure.

3. Healing and Integration

Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a durable foundation for your restoration. Temporary crowns can be placed if desired for aesthetic comfort.

4. Final Restoration

Once healed, your custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis is attached—crafted to blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

Recovery and Aftercare

Recovery is typically smooth. Mild soreness or swelling can last a few days but is easily managed with over-the-counter medication. Most patients return to normal routines within 24–48 hours.

With consistent care, dental implants can last 20 years or longer .

  • Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush
  • Floss gently around the implant area
  • Visit us every six months for cleanings and exams

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene, implants can last 20 years or more —many patients enjoy lifelong results.

Are dental implants painful?

Implant procedures are performed with local anesthesia, and sedation is available. Most patients report minimal discomfort after treatment.

Can I get same-day dental implants?

In some cases, yes. If your bone and gum health allow, immediate-load implants may be possible. Dr. Marjan will confirm during your consultation.

How long is the recovery time?

Initial healing takes a few days, while full integration can take 3–6 months depending on bone density and health.

Will insurance cover dental implants?

Some PPO plans cover portions of implant restoration. Our team will verify your benefits and help maximize your coverage.
