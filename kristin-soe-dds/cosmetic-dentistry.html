Who Benefits From Professional Teeth Whitening

Composite fillings can blend seamlessly with the natural color of teeth, whereas amalgam (silver) and gold fillings are more noticeable. They are the perfect choice for patients with metal allergies.

Additionally, composite fillings require minimal enamel removal compared to gold and silver fillings. In contrast, silver fillings are less durable than composite options and may expand or contract due to temperature fluctuations, which can lead to tooth fractures.
