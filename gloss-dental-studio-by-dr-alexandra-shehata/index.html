The practice

Dr. Shehata Kress is a Cincinnati native known for her calm, approachable style and commitment to personalized care. She earned her DDS from The Ohio State University and continues advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, and facial aesthetics.

Patients appreciate her clear communication and gentle approach. She believes patients make the best decisions when they feel informed and supported.

We provide a full range of dental services designed to support both oral health and smile aesthetics.

Gloss Dental Studio is a privately owned, patient-centered dental practice located in Milford, Ohio, proudly serving patients throughout Cincinnati’s east side including Mason, Blue Ash, Montgomery, Indian Hill, Terrace Park, Loveland, Madeira, Hyde Park, Oakley, Mariemont, Mount Lookout, Anderson Township, and Kenwood.

Many patients travel a short distance for care because they value a practice that focuses on relationships, personalized treatment plans, and modern dental technology.
