Dental implants offer a reliable solution for individuals who have lost a tooth, addressing the various challenges that come with tooth loss, such as altered chewing patterns, speech issues, and discomfort. These implants consist of a dental implant body, which is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a substitute for the tooth’s root. Attached to the implant body is the dental implant abutment, typically secured with an abutment fixation screw, which extends through the gums into the mouth.
