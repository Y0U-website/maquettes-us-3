What Clear Aligners Can Correct

Clear aligner therapy is effective for a wide range of orthodontic issues:

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gapped or spaced teeth
  • Rotated or tilted teeth
  • Overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Teeth that have relapsed after prior orthodontic treatment

Is Invisalign as effective as traditional braces?

For the vast majority of orthodontic cases, including crowding, spacing, and mild to moderate bite issues, Invisalign and Spark aligners are equally effective as traditional braces.
