About Us

Bayport, Baytown, Lake Elmo, Mahtomedi, Maplewood,

North St. Paul, Oakdale, St. Paul, Stillwater,

West Lakeland Township, White Bear Lake, Woodbury

With a commitment to high-quality service and personalized care, McGann Family Dental offers a wide range of dental treatments, from routine cleanings and basic restorations to advanced cosmetic and major restorative procedures including Dental Implants. Whether you re looking for family dental care or specialized treatments, McGann Family Dental is dedicated to helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.

With Zoom! whitening, you can achieve a brighter smile in just one hour, and our intraoral camera lets you see exactly what we see during your exam.
