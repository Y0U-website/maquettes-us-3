Dr. Sam Grizzard III, DDS

Dr. Sam Grizzard III, DDS is from Prince George County, Virginia and graduated VCU School of Dentistry in 2019. Before entering dental school, Dr. Grizzard taught middle school English in Prince George County, Virginia and holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in English from James Madison University and a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from VCU.

Dr. Grizzard enjoys watching major-league baseball (go Dodgers!); JMU football (go Dukes!); and spending as much time as possible with his family.

Dr. Grizzard comes from two generations of healthcare professionals, and prides himself on providing a level of care and connection with patients that is hard to find in the current healthcare environment.
