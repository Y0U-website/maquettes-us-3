Dr. Sanford Dentistry

  • Why did Dr. Sandford choose biological dentistry? Her transition to a mission-driven model was inspired by her personal experience as a cancer survivor and as the mother of a child affected by mercury poisoning.

  • Is she recognized in the community? Yes. She has been featured on KXAN, K-EYE, and in Austin Woman Magazine, and is a four-time recipient of the St. David’s Foundation Theo Project Awards.
  • How many years of experience does she have? Dr. Sandford has more than 30 years of clinical experience, demonstrating long-standing reliability and expertise in patient care.
