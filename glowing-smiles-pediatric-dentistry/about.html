HOW TO PREPARE

Establishing a positive attitude towards at-home oral hygiene is the best way to prepare your child for the dentist. Your child’s everyday dental routine can feel more like a party by playing some tunes and brushing your teeth together! You can also set expectations about their visit with kid-friendly books or videos all about the dentist!

WHAT TO EXPECT

Our top priority is getting to know you and your little one, and empowering your family with the tools and resources your child needs for smiling success! We’ll take your family on a tour around our space, introduce your child to our welcoming team, and learn their unique needs with a gentle, informative exam.

WHAT TO BRING

For a smooth, stress-free visit, please remember to bring your child’s medical history, dental insurance card, and any questions you may be wondering for Dr. Garber. We also invite your child to take along their favorite toy, snuggly stuffed animal, or cozy blanket for that added boost of comfort!
