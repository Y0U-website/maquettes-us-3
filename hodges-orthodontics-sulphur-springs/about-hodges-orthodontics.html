Our Office

And Community

Our practice is rooted in legacy, community, and a commitment to compassionate care.

In 1977, Dr. McNew opened his orthodontic practice in Greenville and Sulphur Springs, quickly earning a reputation for delivering quality, personalized care and genuine relationships with patients.

In 2010, as he prepared for retirement, Dr. McNew entrusted the practice to Dr. Hodges, confident that his patients would continue receiving care grounded in compassion, excellence, and integrity. Dr. Hodges practiced in both Greenville and Sulphur Springs from 2010 to 2022 under HSC Orthodontics, before focusing exclusively on Sulphur Springs and launching Hodges Orthodontics in 2023.

Today, Dr. Hodges proudly continues the legacy of creating beautifully healthy smiles for the community he calls home. He is deeply committed to maintaining a welcoming, family-friendly atmosphere where every patient is treated like a family member.
