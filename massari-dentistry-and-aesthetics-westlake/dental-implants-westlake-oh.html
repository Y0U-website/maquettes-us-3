Our Westlake Dental Implant Restoration Process

Your journey towards a beautiful smile with dental implants starts with an initial consultation with Dr. Jessica Massari. During this visit, she will carefully examine your oral health, discuss your concerns and expectations, and determine if you are a suitable candidate for dental implants.

If you are a good candidate, the next step is to schedule your implant surgery. Dr. Jessica Massari will first numb the treatment area and make a small incision in your gum tissue to access your jawbone. The titanium post will then be placed into your jawbone, and the incision will be closed with stitches.

After the surgery, it takes about 3-6 months for the implant to fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Once this is complete, you will return to our office to have the abutment attached to the implant and an impression of your teeth made for the dental crown.

Lastly, your custom-made dental crown will be placed on top of the abutment, completing your new and natural-looking smile.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restoration

  • Improved Appearance: Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, giving you a confident and beautiful smile.
  • Long-Term Solution: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, unlike other tooth replacement options.
  • Preserves Jawbone Health: The titanium post in dental implants stimulates the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.
  • No Dietary Restrictions: Unlike with dentures or bridges, there are no food restrictions with dental implants. You can enjoy all your favorite foods without worrying about damaging your restoration.
  • Easy Maintenance: Dental implants can be cared for just like natural teeth, with regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups.
