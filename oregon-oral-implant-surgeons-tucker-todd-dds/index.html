Dental implants are tiny titanium posts that are surgically inserted into the jawbone where teeth are missing. These posts act as tooth root substitutes. Once placed, the bone naturally bonds with the titanium in a process called osseointegration, establishing a solid foundation for artificial teeth commonly referred to as crowns or prostheses.
