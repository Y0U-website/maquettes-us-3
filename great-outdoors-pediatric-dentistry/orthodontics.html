Who Is a Candidate for Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are typically recommended for older children and teens who can follow instructions and wear their aligners as directed. They are effective for treating mild to moderate alignment concerns.

During your visit, our pediatric dentist in New Hampshire will evaluate your child’s smile and determine if clear aligners are the right solution.

  • Crowded teeth
  • Spacing between teeth
  • Mild bite issues
  • Teeth that have shifted over time

What to Expect During Treatment

Clear aligner treatment begins with a detailed evaluation and digital planning process. Each aligner is custom designed to gradually move your child’s teeth into proper alignment.

Your child will wear each set of aligners for a specified period before progressing to the next set. For best results, aligners should be worn most of the day, with removal only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.

Regular checkups allow us to monitor progress and ensure treatment stays on track. As part of our children’s dental care in New Hampshire, we make the process simple, clear, and comfortable for both parents and patients.

Caring for Aligners and Teeth

Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential during orthodontic treatment. Because aligners are removable, your child can continue brushing and flossing normally, which helps reduce the risk of cavities during treatment.

  • Brushing teeth after meals before reinserting aligners
  • Cleaning aligners regularly
  • Avoiding sugary drinks while wearing aligners
  • Following all instructions provided by your kids dentist
