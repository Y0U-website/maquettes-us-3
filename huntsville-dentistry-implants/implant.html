Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How much do dental implants cost in Huntsville, TX? The cost of a single implant varies depending on your individual situation, including whether bone grafting is needed and the type of restoration placed on top. We offer free consultations so you can get an accurate estimate based on your specific needs. We also offer flexible payment plans to make implants accessible — there’s no reason cost alone should keep you from a permanent smile.

Does getting a dental implant hurt? Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the process is. We use local anesthesia during placement, and sedation options are available for patients who want to be more relaxed throughout the procedure. Post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients return to normal activity the following day.

How long do dental implants last? With proper care — regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits — dental implants can last decades and often a lifetime. The implant itself, which fuses to your jawbone, is designed to be permanent. The crown on top may eventually need replacement after many years of wear, but the implant foundation remains intact.

What is the difference between a dental implant and All-on-X? A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth. All-on-X (sometimes called All-on-4) is a procedure designed for patients who are missing all or most of their teeth on one or both arches. Using just four to six strategically placed implants, we can anchor a full arch of beautiful, permanent teeth — all in a single day. It’s a life-changing alternative to traditional dentures.

How long does the implant process take? A single implant typically takes a few months from placement to final crown, as the implant needs time to fuse with the jawbone. All-on-X is different — you leave our office the same day with a full set of functioning teeth. During your consultation we’ll give you a clear timeline based on your specific treatment plan.
