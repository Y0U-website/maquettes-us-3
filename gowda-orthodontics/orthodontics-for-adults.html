Why adults choose orthodontics Health benefits. Aligned teeth are easier to clean and can reduce wear, chipping, and gum stress.
  • Bite comfort. We address crowding, spacing, over/underbites, crossbites, and relapse from past orthodontics.
  • Smile aesthetics. Subtle improvements or full smile makeovers on your terms and timeline.
  • Restorative readiness. Orthodontics can create ideal spacing for veneers, crowns, or implants in collaboration with your dentist.

Discreet options that fit your life

We’ll recommend the approach that best fits your goals, biology, and schedule.

  • Clear aligners — Nearly invisible trays (e.g., Invisalign®, Angel Aligners, Spark) that you remove to eat and brush.
  • Clear braces — Ceramic brackets that blend in with your teeth for a low-profile look.
  • Metal braces — Small, smooth brackets for precise control and predictable results.

Modern planning, comfortable care

No goopy impressions! Your journey starts with a quick digital scan. We pair high-quality photos with modern imaging to plan efficiently. Advanced 3D diagnostics are used only when they meaningfully improve safety or accuracy. In the office, ergonomic chairs, easy check-in, and a friendly team keep visits relaxed and on time.

What treatment looks like

  • Consult & records. Tell us your goals; we capture photos, a digital scan, and any needed X-rays.
  • Plan & options. We review timelines, investment, and choices (aligners or braces).
  • Start smart. Get your aligners or braces with clear instructions for home care.
  • Track progress. Short in-office visits (and periodic virtual check-ins when suitable).
  • Protect results. Retainers keep your new smile stable long-term.

Costs, insurance & payments

We work with major insurance plans and offer flexible monthly payments, including third-party financing options. You’ll get a transparent estimate before you begin, and our team helps you maximize any benefits.

FAQs: Orthodontics for Adults

Am I too old for braces or aligners?

There’s no upper age limit. Healthy gums and bone are what matter most. Many of our patients are in their 30s–60s and beyond.

Braces vs. aligners? What’s better for adults?

Both work. Aligners are nearly invisible and removable; braces offer “always-on” consistency. We’ll match the option to your goals, lifestyle, and bite.

How long will treatment take?

Most comprehensive adult cases take 12–24 months; limited cosmetic alignment can be faster. We’ll outline a personalized timeline at your consult.

Will treatment affect my speaking or daily routine?

Expect a short adjustment period. Aligners have minimal impact on speech. With braces, you may notice a brief adaptation, then it feels natural.

Can I straighten teeth if I have crowns, implants, or missing teeth?

Often, yes. We routinely coordinate with your dentist to sequence orthodontics with implants or restorative work for the best final result.

What if I had braces before and my teeth shifted?

That’s common. We offer targeted retreatment (often with aligners) to correct relapse and set you up with a durable retainer plan.

Is orthodontics safe if I have gum recession or periodontal history?

With your dentist or periodontist’s input, we can plan gentle tooth movement that respects gum and bone health.

How much does it cost, and do you offer payment plans?

You’ll receive a clear estimate up front. We accept major insurances and provide flexible monthly payments, including third-party financing options.

Do I really need retainers after treatment?

Yes. Teeth naturally shift over time. Wearing retainers as directed protects your investment and keeps your smile looking its best.
