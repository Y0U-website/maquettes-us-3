Dental FAQs

Have a question that was not answered here? Our Blacksburg office team is happy to help. Reach out online or give us a call and we will respond promptly.

  • Is your practice accepting new patients and families? Yes, we welcome new adult patients and families from Blacksburg, Christiansburg, Radford, and the surrounding New River Valley. While we offer family dentistry, we do not specialize in pediatric care but are happy to see older children. As a second-generation practice, we value building long-term relationships. All visits are by appointment only, so please call or request a visit online to reserve your first visit.
  • How do you help patients with dental anxiety or pain concerns? Our team specializes in providing a comfortable, pain-free experience. We offer nitrous oxide to help ease anxiety and ensure that you feel at ease in the chair. Dr. Huff and our staff take time to listen and explain every step, creating a warm, supportive environment. Patient comfort is a top priority, whether you are here for a cleaning, TMJ treatment, or emergency care. Many patients mention how our relational approach makes dental visits less stressful.
  • Do you treat TMJ, jaw pain, or frequent clicking and popping? Yes, Dr. Huff has extensive experience treating TMJ symptoms, including jaw pain, lockjaw, and clicking or popping noises when opening or closing your mouth. We provide thorough evaluation, diagnosis, and non-surgical management options right here in our Blacksburg location. If you are experiencing chronic jaw discomfort or headaches, schedule a consultation to find out how we can help reduce your symptoms and improve your quality of life.
  • What insurance plans do you accept and are payment plans available? We accept many major dental insurance plans, but coverage varies. Our team will help you understand your benefits and file claims on your behalf. We also offer options for patients without insurance, including CareCredit financing, to make quality care accessible. For specific insurance questions or to discuss payment plans, please contact our office directly so we can review your needs and provide clear guidance.
  • What should I do if I have a dental emergency in the New River Valley? If you have a knocked-out, broken, or severely painful tooth, call our office right away. We reserve time for urgent cases and aim to provide same-day care for existing and new patients in Blacksburg, Christiansburg, Radford, and nearby communities. If you call after hours, leave a detailed message and we will return your call promptly during the next business day. For life-threatening injuries or uncontrolled bleeding, please visit the nearest emergency room.
  • What are your office hours and appointment policies? Our office is open Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed Friday through Sunday. All visits are by appointment only to ensure each patient receives focused care. Please call or use our online request form to schedule. If you need to reschedule or cancel, let us know as soon as possible so we can offer your slot to another patient.
