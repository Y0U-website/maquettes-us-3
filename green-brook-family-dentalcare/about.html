Dr. Dennis Petrini, DMD

Dr. Dennis Petrini founded Green Brook Family Dentalcare in 1987 with a commitment to providing exceptional, patient-centered dental care to the community.

With decades of clinical experience, he has earned the trust of generations of families through his compassionate approach and dedication to excellence.

Dr. Petrini earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Michigan in 1976 and received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 1979.

  • University of Michigan, Bachelor of Science, 1976
  • University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, DMD, 1979
  • Member, American Dental Association
  • Member, Central Dental Society of New Jersey
  • Founded Green Brook Family Dentalcare in 1987

Dr. Peter Tsambazis, DMD

Dr. Peter Tsambazis is a highly respected periodontist with extensive experience in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease, as well as advanced dental implant therapy.

Since joining Green Brook Family Dentalcare in 1996, he has helped countless patients restore their oral health through specialized periodontal care.

Dr. Tsambazis earned both his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and specialty training in Periodontal Surgery from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 1992.

  • University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, DDS, 1992
  • Specialty training in Periodontal Surgery, UMDNJ, 1992
  • Member, American Dental Association
  • Member, New Jersey Dental Association
  • Member, International Congress of Oral Implantologists
  • Member, Northeast Society of Periodontists
  • New Jersey Specialty License No. 4024

Dr. Kathleen M. Hargaden, DMD, MS

Dr. Kathleen Hargaden is dedicated to creating healthy, confident smiles through personalized orthodontic treatment for children, teens, and adults.

She combines years of clinical expertise with a caring, patient-focused approach to help every patient achieve outstanding results.

She earned her Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Pennsylvania in 1985 before completing her specialty training in Orthodontics at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 1994. Dr. Hargaden is the recipient of the American Association of Orthodontists Award and joined Green Brook Family Dentalcare in March 2020.

  • University of Pennsylvania, Bachelor of Arts, 1985
  • Orthodontics, University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, 1994
  • American Association of Orthodontists Award recipient
  • Joined Green Brook Family Dentalcare in March 2020
  • New Jersey Specialty License No. 3912

Dr. John Dibling, DMD

Dr. John Dibling has been providing comprehensive dental care for more than three decades, with expertise in cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, restorative dentistry, Botox, and conscious sedation.

He is known for his gentle approach and commitment to helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.

Dr. Dibling earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry in 1985. He completed a General Practice Residency at Overlook Hospital from 1985 to 1986, followed by an advanced fellowship in Oral Implantology and Biomaterials at Brookdale Hospital in Brooklyn, New York from 1986 to 1988. He is also licensed to provide conscious sedation for qualified patients.

Dr. Dibling has proudly served patients at Green Brook Family Dentalcare since 1991.

  • Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry, DMD, 1985
  • General Practice Residency, Overlook Hospital, 1985-1986
  • Advanced fellowship in Oral Implantology and Biomaterials, Brookdale Hospital, 1986-1988
  • Licensed to provide conscious sedation for qualified patients
  • Conscious Sedation Permit No. 50
  • Serving Green Brook Family Dentalcare since 1991
