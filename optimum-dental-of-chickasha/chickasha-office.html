Same-Day Services

Busy Chickasha lifestyles demand efficient dental care. The office provides urgent evaluations, fillings, and other same-day options when clinically appropriate and scheduling allows.

Dental Implants

Implant treatment can replace one tooth, several teeth, or support a full-arch restoration after careful imaging and treatment planning.

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges, root canal treatment, and comprehensive planning help rebuild damaged teeth and restore everyday function.

Meet your Chickasha dentists.

Get to know the dentists who bring thoughtful, personalized care to the Chickasha community.

Dr. Braden Hill

Dr. Hill is a first-generation dentist whose interest in dentistry began after he broke his front teeth as a child and saw how confidently a dentist could restore them. That experience inspired him to help others regain health, function, and confidence in their smiles.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry and has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education. As a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, Dr. Hill has a special interest in implant and restorative dentistry.

Dr. Hill speaks Spanish and makes it a priority to create a relaxed, welcoming environment where patients feel heard and understand their options. Outside the office, he enjoys cheering on his children, date nights with his wife, Carly, and an occasional round of golf.

Dr. Skyler Peacock

Dr. Skyler Peacock grew up in southern Utah and completed his undergraduate studies at Dixie State University. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Glendale, Arizona. Before joining Optimum Dental, he practiced in Midwest City, Oklahoma, and Idaho.

Dr. Peacock married his high school sweetheart, Ashley. They have three children and enjoy reading, hiking, camping, and watching college football together.

Dentistry gives Dr. Peacock the opportunity to work with his hands and meet new people. He is committed to helping patients have a comfortable, positive experience and continues his education across a broad range of dental topics.
