The practice

Mount Pleasant Dental Assistant School is an institution developed by Dr. Rehan Shahid and Dr. Husnain Shahid. Dr. Rehan and Dr. Husnain recognized the need for well-trained, efficient chairside dental assistants and found difficulty in attracting good assistants throughout their years in practice. Mount Pleasant Dental Assistant School will help prepare interested persons to become part of the…

🕒 How Long is the Course? Dive into an enriching 13-week program comprising 81 hours of engaging lectures and labs, coupled with a 50-hour internship in a dentist’s office.

📅 When Can You Enroll? Secure your spot in the January, May, September batches to kickstart your dental assistant career.

⏰ Enrollment Deadline? Mark your calendars! The last business day before the commencement of the first class is your deadline.

🎓 Admission Requirements 📜 Must have a High school transcript, GED, or equivalent before starting class

❓ Can I Miss a Day? What Happens? 📅 Saturday classes allow for missing no more than 2 class sessions. Any class sessions missed, must be made up. Reach out to instructors for makeup options.

📚 How Many Tests? Brace yourself for 8 tests, 13 quizzes, 15 lab tests, and a comprehensive final exam.

👕 Dress Code and Scrubs? Students bring their own Royal Blue scrubs, worn at every Saturday class session.
