Joanna K. Roulston, DDS.

Joanna Roulston is the owner and practicing Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at Tulsa Dental Center. Joanna is a graduate of the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where she received her DDS. She completed her Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Science from the University of Oklahoma in Norman, OK.

Dr. Roulston was awarded the Oklahoma Academy of General Dentistry scholarship based on her work ethic and commitment to dentistry. She is currently a member of the Oklahoma Association of Women Dentists, the Oklahoma Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Roulston previously coordinated the Good Shepherd Mission Clinics in Oklahoma City. She currently participates with several charities, including OK Mission of Mercy, the Mobile Dental Unit, and the Red Dirt Relief Fund. She regularly treats patients referred by Eastern Oklahoma Donated Dental Services and Women in Recovery (WOR).

A native of Tulsa, OK, Dr. Roulston graduated from Union High School. Her hobbies include traveling, cooking, and spending time with her two children. Dr. Roulston said I am so grateful to be able to treat patients in such a wonderful community.
