Technology that helps your dentist see more, and helps you understand why.

Digital tools are most useful when they make care clearer. Our workflow combines three-dimensional records with specialist judgment to explain options and coordinate treatment.

CBCT + 5D planning

RAYPreMiere creates three-dimensional records of dental and facial anatomy. Its 5D planning tools help clinicians visualize key structures and explain the plan with greater clarity.

  • Focused scan areas based on clinical need
  • Supports implant, endodontic, and orthodontic assessment
  • Visual records for clearer treatment conversations

Intraoral scanning

Aoralscan Elite and Medit i700 create digital records of teeth and bite, reducing the need for conventional impressions when clinically appropriate.

  • Detailed records for treatment planning
  • Visual review of scans with your dentist
  • Supports implant and restorative workflows

3D facial scanning

RAYFace brings facial scans, intraoral records, and CBCT data together in a 3D view that supports planning and smile-design conversations.

  • Facial context alongside dental records
  • More visual treatment discussions
  • Connected data across the digital workflow

CAD/CAM laboratory coordination

Digital records move from scanning to design and laboratory fabrication with fewer manual handoffs, supporting coordinated restorative care.

  • Digital design for restorative workflows
  • Clearer review between clinicians and laboratory
  • Timelines based on treatment and fabrication needs

Safety built into every clinical workflow.

We invest in certified German and Italian medical infrastructure to guarantee 100 % sterile instruments, pure medical-grade air, and active cross-contamination protection you can trust before treatment.

E10 Class B sterilization

Deep sterilization with the Pro System workflow and traceable records for instruments prepared for each patient.

  • Class B vacuum sterilization cycles
  • Cleaning, packaging, and protected storage
  • High-level protection against bacteria, viruses and other microorganisms

Medical-grade air

The Tornado compressor supplies dry, hygienic air without oil for the dental units used during care.

  • 100 % oil-free compressed air
  • Membrane drying technology
  • Clean air for clinical procedures

Hygienic anti-retraction suction

Tyscor suction helps prevent negative pressure and the backflow of contaminated fluids through the suction line.

Want to understand how these systems support your appointment? Our patient coordinator can explain the protocols that apply to your visit.

  • Anti-retraction protection
  • Continuous, controlled airflow
  • Designed for patient protection
