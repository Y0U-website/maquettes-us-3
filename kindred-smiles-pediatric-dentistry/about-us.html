Meet Dr. Jackie Dr. Jackie St. Pierre completed her dental degree from Boston University and spent the first few years of her career treating patients of all ages in community health centers in Boston. She focused on increasing access to care for children and also completed her Master’s Degree in Public Health. She realized that she both enjoyed and had the greatest impact working with the pediatric population so she decided to complete her pediatric training with NYU Langone in Western Massachusetts and served as chief resident in her final year.

Dr. Jackie is a big advocate for the age one dental visit and focuses on helping her patients develop healthy habits. She strives to make the dental office a positive place for children of all ages and abilities and also has experience treating patients in a hospital setting.

In her spare time, you may find Dr. Jackie on walking trails, baking or watching movies with her family and friends.
