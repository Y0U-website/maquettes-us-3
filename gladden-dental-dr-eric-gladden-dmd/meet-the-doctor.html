Meet Dr. Eric Gladden Dentist Savannah

MEET THE DOCTOR

Dr. Eric Gladden was born and raised in Savannah, GA. He attended Armstrong State College for two years prior to being accepted into the University Of Georgia College Of Pharmacy. Following completion of his Bachelor of Science and Doctorate of Pharmacy degrees at the University of Georgia, Dr. Gladden completed a 1-year postdoctoral residency in nutritional support in Winter Park, FL. After practicing as a pharmacist for five years, Dr. Gladden attended the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry where he completed his Doctorate of Medical Dentistry Degree in 2002.

Dr. Gladden met his wife Anne while she was completing her pharmacy degree at the University of Georgia. They feel very fortunate to make their home in Savannah with their 2 children, Will and Megan & their Boykin Spaniel, Grace. When away from the office, Dr. Gladden enjoys spending time with his family and sharing his love of the water, especially recreational coastal activities including boating, fishing and sailing.
