Invisalign

Invisalign isn’t just for adults – it’s for image-conscious teens too! With a custom-made series of see-through aligners, we can nudge your child’s teeth closer and closer to their ideal positions without any metal at all. Plus, there are other considerable perks, like no dietary restrictions, fewer check-in visits, and shorter average treatment timelines. So, if your child’s smile would benefit from braces, but they are hesitant because they don’t want to draw unwanted attention to their smile, ask us if they are a candidate for Invisalign.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces have been used for decades to address common orthodontic problems in children. In fact, one of the reasons they are so popular is because they can address even more severe issues, like significant overbites and overcrowding! The entire treatment process will start with a consultation with one of our dedicated Levittown pediatric dentists . Once they assess the development of your child’s jaw, the position of their teeth, and the alignment of their bite, they’ll be able to determine if traditional braces are the best orthodontic treatment.

Want to learn more about the orthodontic treatments we offer? Then don’t hesitate to reach out to our team so we can schedule a time for your child to come in for a consultation!
