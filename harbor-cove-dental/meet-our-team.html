Meet Our Team

MEET OUR TEAM At Harbor Cove Dental, we’re proud to not only offer patients an excellent team of dentists but a friendly, dedicated staff who go out of their way to exceed expectations every day!

Angela Donovan, CDPMA – Office Manager

Angela Donovan is a Certified Dental Practice Management Administrator through the Dental Assisting National Board. She has over 20 years of dental practice administrative experience. She is the President of the New England Dental Office Managers Study Group, and serves as a committee member for Delta Dental of Massachusetts Office Managers Advisory Council (OMAC) and DenteMax OMAC. She is also an active member of the Association of Dental Implant Auxiliaries.

In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her husband, Dennis and their dog, Millie.

Amy Prime, RDH

Amy is a Registered Dental Hygienist. She graduated from the University of New England in 2010 with a Bachelor’s Degree of Science in Dental Hygiene. She prides herself on getting to know her patients and understanding how to meet their individual needs for the best quality care possible. Amy is very passionate about dentistry and oral health.

In her free time, she enjoys spending time with her husband Adam, family, and friends. She also loves live music, being outside, living on the North Shore, and of course her cat named Taco.

Elizabeth Nichols, RDH

Liz is a dental hygienist with over 30 years experience in the dental field. She is a graduate of the Forsyth School for Dental Hygienists. In her spare time, Liz enjoys exploring local history, boating, and spending time with family and friends.

Stephanie J. Bakland – Dental Assistant

Stephanie started her career in dentistry over 20 years ago. She has developed many specialized dental skills from her extensive training at the Pankey Institute, MA Dental Society and the Schuster Center for Professional Development. She is devoted to patient care and always goes above and beyond to make patients feel comfortable during their visit to our office.

Stephanie lives in Gloucester with her husband, Leif. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her family, traveling and sailing, as well as baking and crafts.

Samantha Rines – Dental Assistant

Samantha is a dental assistant who prides herself on patient comfort and care. She graduated from Essex Technical High School’s Biotechnology Program and continues to advance her education and career by pursuing her DANB certification.

In her spare time, Samantha enjoys spending time with her family, riding her ATV, and hiking with her dog, Max.

Kerri Wilson – Patient Care Coordinator

Kerri Wilson has a Bachelors Degree in Psychology which assisted her as the director of a boys group home for adolescent troubled teens. She counseled teens and provided a safe environment for kids to heal and cope with issues such as substance abuse, physical abuse and other family issues causing hardships in their lives. After many years working in that field, she decided to change careers and pursue her passion for dentistry. Kerri loves to interact with our patients and make them feel welcome. We love having her as part of our team.

Kerri lives in Hamilton, MA with her husband and two daughters. In her spare time, she volunteers in the community. She also enjoys photography and has been hired to take pictures at weddings and birthday parties.
