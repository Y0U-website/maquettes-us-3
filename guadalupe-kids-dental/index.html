The practice

We make dental visits fun and stress-free for kids while building healthy habits that last a lifetime.

At Guadalupe Kids Dental, our mission is to deliver compassionate, family-centered dental care with a commitment to prevention.

When treatment is necessary, we believe in taking the most minimally invasive approach possible that respects the unique needs of each child, prioritizing their comfort and long-term oral health.

Our team is dedicated to educating families and children about the importance of prevention, helping them maintain healthy smiles through personalized care plans that minimize the need for extensive procedures.

We support and encourage every patient so that they leave confident in their dental experience and are excited to return to Guadalupe Kids Dental.

Your baby’s first dental visit, recommended by age one, checks early oral health and covers teething, brushing, fluoride, and thumb-sucking, ensuring a strong foundation for lifelong healthy smiles.

Healthy smiles begin with strong preventive care . Regular checkups, sealants , fluoride treatments , dental X-rays, and brushing education help protect kids from cavities and gum disease.

Guadalupe Kids Dental is experienced with dental emergencies ranging from knocked-out teeth, severe pain, or injury. Please call contact us immediately, as quick action ensures the best outcome for your child’s oral health.
