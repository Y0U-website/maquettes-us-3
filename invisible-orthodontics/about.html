State-of-the-art Orthodontic Office in Hingham Welcome to Invisible Orthodontics! We are one of the largest providers of orthodontic care in Hingham, Massachusetts, and utilize digital workflow throughout the practice. Dr. Nick continues to attend conferences to keep abreast of the latest research and technology in orthodontics to ensure that each patient receives personalized, comfortable, and efficient orthodontic care.

Dr. Nicholas P. Zafiropoulos, DMD Approach Credentials Outside Office Dr. Nick loves building long-term relationships with the families in his practice, looking after generations of families in Hingham. He is always happy to answer questions and concerns from parents and patients. He is passionate about providing top-notch treatment for all his patients by combining personalized attention and the latest technology in orthodontics.

Dr. Nick completed his undergraduate education at Tufts University in 1988 with a BA in Biology. After graduating Valedictorian of his dental class at Tufts Dental School in 1994, he continued to the prestigious Tufts Postgraduate Orthodontic Program, where he spent an additional two years of full-time training in the art and science of orthodontics. He is President of the Executive Board of the Massachusetts Association of Orthodontists and a past president of the Tufts Association of Orthodontists.

When not perfecting smiles in his office, Dr. Nick enjoys the beaches of the Cape, boating, basketball, golf, and traveling. He resides in Mashpee with his amazing wife and his son Payi. Dr. Nick also frequently attends meetings and conferences to stay updated with the latest technology in orthodontics and be able to provide superior care for our patients.
