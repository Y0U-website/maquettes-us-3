Why Choose Dental Implants?

Missing teeth shouldn’t hold you back. At Hometown Dental Howell, we provide advanced dental implant solutions to replace single teeth, multiple teeth, or even a full arch.

A Complete Smile Restoration

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges that rest on the gums or rely on neighboring teeth, dental implants replace the entire tooth structure, from the root up. A small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a strong, permanent foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Implants restore the natural look, feel, and function of your teeth — helping you eat, speak, and smile with total confidence. Furthermore, because the implant acts like a natural tooth root, it stimulates the jawbone, preventing the bone loss and facial sagging that typically occurs after losing teeth.

Our Dental Implant Solutions

Whether you need one tooth replaced or a full smile reconstruction, our experienced team guides you through every step.

Single Implants

Replace a single missing tooth with a standalone implant and custom crown. This option restores your smile seamlessly without altering or damaging the healthy neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Say goodbye to slipping dentures and messy adhesives. We use strategically placed implants to securely anchor your dentures in place, providing incredible stability and comfort.

Full-Arch (All-on-X)

A permanent, life-changing solution for severe tooth loss. We can replace an entire upper or lower arch of teeth using just 4 to 6 implants, often providing you with fixed teeth in a single day.

The Implant Process: What to Expect

We ensure predictable, long-lasting results through careful planning and advanced technology.

Consultation Evaluation

We perform a comprehensive assessment of your oral health and jawbone using 3D digital imaging to determine if you are a good candidate for implants.

Implant Placement

During a comfortable, minimally invasive procedure (with sedation options available), the titanium implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone.

Healing Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration, creating a rock-solid foundation.

Final Restoration

Once healed, we attach a custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture to the implant, fully restoring your smile’s function and aesthetics.
