By visiting your dental professionals on a regular basis, many problems can be avoided. We provide a number of services aimed at preventing dental problems. These services include: routine cleanings (prophalaxis)
  • deep cleanings (scaling and root planing)
  • digital x-rays
  • fluoride treatments
  • sealants
  • custom mouth guards
  • night guards

Family Care

While we strive to prevent any dental problems from occurring in the first place, we are here to take care of you when they do arise. Our services include:

  • fillings (tooth-colored and silver)
  • crowns and bridges
  • implant crowns
  • root canals
  • partial dentures
  • complete dentures
  • extractions
  • mild sedation
