Accessibility Statement

  • Operable: User interface components and navigation are operable, including keyboard accessibility and sufficient time to interact with content.
  • Understandable: Information and the operation of the user interface are understandable, with readable text and predictable behavior.
  • Robust: Content is robust enough to be interpreted by a wide variety of user agents, including assistive technologies.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

  • Semantic HTML structure with appropriate heading hierarchy
  • Descriptive alt text for images throughout the site
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios between text and background colors
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • ARIA labels on interactive components where appropriate
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and zoom levels
  • Form fields with associated labels for screen reader compatibility
  • Skip navigation links for keyboard users
  • Descriptive link text that makes sense out of context

Known Limitations

We are aware that some areas of our website may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We are actively working to identify and address these gaps. Known areas we are working to improve include:

  • Ensuring all third-party embedded content (such as maps) meets accessibility standards
  • Enhancing keyboard focus visibility across all interactive elements
  • Adding captions to any future video content

Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers including NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver
  • Speech recognition software including Dragon NaturallySpeaking
  • Screen magnification software
  • Keyboard-only navigation

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may contact the relevant accessibility authority in your jurisdiction. In the United States, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division regarding ADA-related matters.
