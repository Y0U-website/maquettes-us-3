Common Concerns

Should I replace my old silver fillings? If your silver fillings are cracked, leaking, or showing decay around the edges, we recommend replacement. Amalgam expands with temperature, which can eventually fracture the tooth.

How long will a composite filling last? With good oral hygiene, they typically last 7–10 years. We check the integrity of the &#x27;seal&#x27; during your six-month cleanings.

Will my tooth be sensitive after the filling? Some mild sensitivity to cold is normal for 24–48 hours as the nerve settles. If it feels &#x27;high&#x27; when you bite down, a simple 2-minute adjustment is all you need.

Is the procedure BPA-free? Yes. We use modern, high-grade resins that are free from BPA and other endocrine disruptors, ensuring a safe restoration for your body.

Catch Decay Early. A small filling today prevents a root canal or crown tomorrow. Our conservative approach saves your natural tooth structure and your wallet.
