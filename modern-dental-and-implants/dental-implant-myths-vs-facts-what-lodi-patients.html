Dental Implant Myths vs. Facts: What Lodi Patients Actually Need to Know

Dental implants are the most advanced tooth replacement solution modern dentistry offers — and also one of the most misunderstood. Despite decades of clinical evidence and millions of successful placements, a set of persistent myths continues to prevent patients in Lodi and the greater Stockton area from pursuing a treatment that could genuinely transform their oral health and quality of life.

At Modern Dental & Implants, dental implant placement and restoration is one of our core specialties. Dr. Ben Udas and Dr. Mohammad Ahmed — who specializes specifically in full mouth reconstruction and implant-supported restorations — work with implant patients at every stage, from initial evaluation through final restoration. They hear these myths regularly. Here, they set the record straight.

Myth: Dental implants are too painful to be worth it.

Myth: Dental implants are only for older adults.

Myth: I don’t have enough bone for implants.

Myth: Implants require too much maintenance.

Myth: Implants look fake or obviously dental.

Fact: A well-placed, well-restored implant is indistinguishable from a natural tooth in function and appearance. The crown that sits atop the implant is custom-fabricated to match the color, shape, and translucency of your surrounding teeth. The result is a restoration that integrates naturally into your smile — not something that announces itself as a replacement. Patients who were self-conscious about the appearance of a denture or a visible gap are frequently surprised by how natural their implant restoration looks in photographs and in daily life.

Myth: Implants are too expensive.

Myth: Implants frequently fail.

Myth: I can wait until the gap bothers me more.

The best time to pursue an implant consultation is as soon as possible after a tooth is lost or identified as needing extraction.
