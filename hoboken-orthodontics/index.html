Les soins

Orthodontics (also referred to as dentofacial orthopedics) is a specialized form of dentistry that focuses on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of dental and facial abnormalities.

We offer several treatment options, including metal and clear braces , TADs and palatal expanders , Invisalign® and Invisalign for Teens® clear aligners, Damon™ Smile self-ligating braces and Damon Clear™ tie-less brackets, Insignia™ .
