Get Started

Get Started First Visit At Hometown Orthodontics, our goal is to make your first visit informative, comfortable, and easy to navigate. We’ll begin with digital photos and a 3D scan to get a clear picture of your teeth, bite, and overall dental health. Dr. Tuttle will then review your records, discuss your goals, and explain any recommended treatment options in a way that’s easy to understand.

We believe in providing patients with the information they need to make informed decisions, without pressure or confusion. Whether you’re exploring care for yourself or your child, you’ll leave with a personalized plan and a clear idea of what to expect moving forward.

Insurance & Financing

At Hometown Orthodontics, we understand that investing in your smile is a big decision, and we’re here to make it happen! Whether you’re from Richfield, Delta, Ephraim, or a neighboring town, we’ll help you navigate your insurance benefits clearly and handle the paperwork, so you don’t have to.

We also offer flexible payment plans tailored to your needs. Our goal is to make high-quality orthodontic care accessible to the families in our Central Utah communities, without cutting corners or adding unnecessary stress. Whether you’re just getting started or planning ahead, our team is here to help you find a solution that suits your needs and budget.

Patient Forms

To keep your first visit simple and stress-free, we’ve made our new patient forms available online. Just fill them out at your convenience, and we’ll be ready to welcome you the moment you walk through the door.
