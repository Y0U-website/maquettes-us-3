Dental Implant Surgery requires Good Jaw bone!

Implant surgery replaces the tooth roots with metal posts that look and feel like teeth and replaces missing or damaged teeth with artificial ones that look and feel like natural teeth.

Implant surgery is a great alternative to dentures and bridges that don’t fit. In addition, it can be an option when natural roots aren’t available for building dentures or replacing teeth.

How the procedure is done depends on the type of dental implant and the condition of your jawbone. Implant surgery may involve multiple procedures. Implants can provide a solid foundation for your teeth and are a great solution. However, the healing process around the implant must occur first.
