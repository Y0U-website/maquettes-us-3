FAQs

How much are dental implants?

A single dental implant typically costs between $3,000 and $5,000, including the implant, abutment, and custom crown. Factors affecting price include necessary preparatory procedures, case complexity, and regional cost differences. MacPhail Signature Smiles offers various payment solutions to make this valuable investment more accessible.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically positioned in the jawbone beneath the gums. The implant connects to an abutment, which supports a custom-crafted crown designed to match your natural teeth. This three-part system becomes permanently integrated with your oral anatomy, providing stability and function that is indistinguishable from that of natural teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants often last a lifetime, though the crown portion may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear. Success factors include home care habits, professional maintenance, tobacco use, and overall health. Patients of Dr. MacPhail, DDS who maintain regular checkups and good oral hygiene tend to experience the best long-term outcomes.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

In order to ensure that your dental implant procedure will be successful, it is very important that we assess a few factors. Ideal candidates are in good overall health and need to have enough jaw bone to support the implant. It is also imperative that we address the health of your gum tissues and remedy any signs of periodontitis prior to placing dental implants. Dr. MacPhail does a detailed exam and uses a CBCT (which takes a 3D-image of your head and neck) to guide her in proper implant placement.

Additionally, it is ideal for candidates to be non-smokers or at least be able to commit to not smoking for a time before and after treatment. If you are a smoker, that does not necessarily disqualify you from being a candidate for implants, so always ask Dr. MacPhail first. first. If there are issues with your jaw bone or your sinuses, there are treatment options for both of those that can make you a candidate for dental implants.

Implant-Supported Bridges or Dentures

If you are missing all of your teeth or large sections of teeth, we can place a few implants, then secure your bridge or dentures to the implants. These are long-lasting options that can be a great solution for multiple missing teeth as they help prevent bone loss and are more reliable than adhesive-placed dentures.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

Coverage varies significantly among providers, with many traditional plans offering limited reimbursement for implant procedures. Some policies partially cover certain components, like the final restoration or preliminary procedures. Our administrative team can verify your benefits and explore financing options to make treatment more affordable.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients report less discomfort than anticipated, as the procedure is performed under appropriate anesthesia with additional sedation options available. Post-operative symptoms typically include mild swelling and soreness for several days, which can be effectively managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain relievers. Most individuals resume regular activities within 24-48 hours after the procedure, while internal healing continues as the implant integrates with the jawbone.
