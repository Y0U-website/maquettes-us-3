Monali Patel DDS

Born in Kansas City and raised in Houston, Texas, Dr. Monali Patel (Mona) developed a natural affinity for children as the eldest in a bustling family. Her journey to pediatric dentistry began early, working at a Pediatric Dental office in Houston during her student years.

After graduating from Baylor College of Dentistry in 1996, Mona pursued her passion by joining the Pediatric Residency Program at the same institution. Under the guidance of mentors like Dr. Jon Ousley, she honed her skills and knowledge, eventually becoming a vital part of the Just For Kids Dental team.

In 2012, Dr. Patel took the helm at Just For Kids Dental, embarking on a new chapter as the proud owner. Alongside her husband, Dr. Yogesh Patel, Mona enjoys family time with their two boys, Kuran and Dilen, and indulges in her love for travel and cooking.

A member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Patel continues to make smiles brighter and childhood memories sweeter with her unwavering dedication to pediatric dentistry.

As a pediatric dentist, my mission is to make every child’s dental visit a fun and positive experience, paving the way for a lifetime of healthy, happy smiles. Dr. Monali Patel
