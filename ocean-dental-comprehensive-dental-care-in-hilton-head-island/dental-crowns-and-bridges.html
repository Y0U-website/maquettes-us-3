Dental Crowns and Bridges on…

  • Cracked or fractured teeth
  • Weakened teeth after root canal treatment
  • Missing teeth (one or more in a row)
  • Severely worn-down teeth
  • Teeth with large, failing fillings
  • Misshapen or discolored teeth that can’t be improved with whitening

Your Concerns, Our Focus

Missing or weakened teeth do more than affect your appearance — they can make eating difficult, cause neighboring teeth to shift, and put extra strain on your jaw over time.

At Ocean Dental, Dr. Sugg and her team address these concerns with custom crowns and bridges crafted from high-quality, tooth-colored porcelain. Each restoration is shade-matched and shaped to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth, restoring full function while protecting your long-term oral health.

By filling gaps and reinforcing weakened teeth, crowns and bridges also help prevent the bone loss and alignment changes that can follow tooth loss.

what To Expect

1. Restoration Preparation

Dr. Sugg and her team begin by numbing the area with local anesthesia so you feel comfortable throughout. The tooth is then gently reshaped to create the ideal fit for your new crown or bridge.

2. Custom Fabrication

A detailed impression of your tooth is taken and sent to a specialist dental laboratory, where your permanent crown or bridge is custom-fabricated from durable porcelain. In the meantime, we place a temporary crown to protect your tooth and keep you comfortable while you wait — typically one to two weeks.

3. Installation

At your final appointment, we remove the temporary crown and carefully check the fit, bite, and shade of your permanent restoration before cementing it securely in place. We make any necessary adjustments to ensure it feels completely natural before you leave.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bridges And Crowns

Are bridges and crowns painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should feel pressure but not pain during treatment. Some patients experience mild sensitivity or soreness for a few days afterward, which is normal and typically resolves on its own. If discomfort persists beyond a week, contact our office.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups with Dr. Sugg and her team, crowns and bridges typically last 10–15 years — and many last significantly longer. Avoiding habits like chewing ice, grinding your teeth, or using your teeth to open packaging will help extend their lifespan.

Do bridges affect chewing or speaking?

No — in fact, most patients find the opposite. A bridge restores normal chewing function and can actually improve bite efficiency and comfort compared to living with a missing tooth. Speech is rarely affected, and any minor adjustment typically resolves within a few days as you get used to your restoration.

Will people notice I have a bridge or a crown?

Very unlikely. Every crown and bridge at Ocean Dental is custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. Most patients tell us that even they forget which tooth has a crown after a short time.

How do I care for my bridge or crown?

Care is straightforward. Brush twice daily and floss carefully around the crown or under the bridge using floss threaders or an interdental brush — this area is particularly important to keep clean. Avoid chewing on very hard foods like ice or hard candy. Come in for your regular checkups so Dr. Sugg and her team can monitor the restoration and catch any issues early.

Can I get a crown or a bridge if I have a missing tooth?

Yes. A crown is used to restore a damaged tooth that is still present, while a bridge is specifically designed to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the healthy teeth on either side — or to dental implants. During your consultation, Dr. Sugg and her team will assess your situation and recommend the most appropriate restoration for your specific needs.
