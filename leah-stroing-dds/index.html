The practice

We are Dr. Leah Stroing and Dr. Megan White, sisters and Red Bluff natives who are passionate about helping people get quality dental care. It is our team’s mission at Stroing and White Dental to make sure our patients have a smile they can be proud of every day.

Dr. Stroing and Dr. White have been in the dental field since high school. While attending Red Bluff High School, both started as sterilization technicians and dental assistants. They worked their way through college and dental school, and upon finishing decided to return to their hometown of Red Bluff to practice. Les soins

Making sure that your teeth are healthy with comprehensive exams is a great habit to build. We want to be a part of the effort to keep your smile as healthy as possible.

With the impressions we took of your teeth, a dental crown is fabricated in an outside dental lab. A few weeks after the placement of the abutment, we can attach your dental crown which completes your restoration.

For this step, we partner with a trusted oral surgeon or periodontist. Under local anesthetic, they&#x27;ll make a small incision into your gums and place the titanium implant post into your jaw bone. The gum is then sutured.

Everyone deserves a smile they can be proud of. Ask about our cosmetic dentistry options and how we can help you improve the aesthetics of your smile.
