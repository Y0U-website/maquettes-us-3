How Our Process Works

Enrolling in a Sopris Wellness Plan is one of the simplest things you can do for your long-term oral health. There’s no application to wait on, no coverage to verify, and no claim forms to file, just clear benefits you can use from your very first appointment. Here’s what to expect:

1. Schedule a Consultation

Contact our office to set up a visit or a quick conversation with our front desk team. We’ll help you identify which plan fits your age, oral health history, and care goals.

2. Review Your Options

We’ll walk you through the specific benefits of each plan and answer any questions before you make a decision. Transparency is important to us, so you’ll know exactly what you’re getting before you commit.

3. Enroll and Get Started

Once you’ve chosen your plan, you’ll complete a straightforward enrollment form. Your benefits are active immediately, and there’s no delay between signing up and scheduling your first covered appointment.

4. Stay on Track Year-Round

Your plan runs on an annual basis. We’ll track your included services throughout the year and send you a renewal reminder when the time comes, so nothing falls through the cracks.

We’re in this with you for the long haul, not just for a single appointment. Frequently Asked Questions

Our Sopris Wellness Plans are straightforward by design, but we know you may still have questions. Here are answers to some of the most common we hear.

Can I use my membership plan if I already have dental insurance?

Our Sopris Wellness Plans are designed primarily for patients without traditional dental insurance, and plan discounts cannot be combined with insurance benefits. That said, some patients use our plans to help manage costs on treatments not covered by their insurance or after they’ve exceeded their annual maximum. Our team is happy to walk through your specific situation.

Is there a waiting period before I can use my benefits?

No, one of the biggest advantages of our in-house plans is that there are no waiting periods. Your benefits are active the day you enroll, and you can schedule your first covered appointment immediately.

What if I need treatment that goes beyond what’s included in my plan?

All of our plans include a 15 % discount on most additional treatments performed at our office. Whether you need a filling, a crown, a cosmetic procedure, or an emergency dental appointment , your membership ensures you have access to reduced rates on virtually everything we offer.
