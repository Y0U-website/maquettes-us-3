MEET OUR OFFICE TEAM

I was born in Surry County and graduated from North Surry High School. After working for eighteen years, I decided to go back to school and graduated with honors from Surry Community College in 2003.

I have worked at Dr. Gravitte’s office since he opened in 2004, and each day I enjoy meeting new patients and making every patient feel comfortable and at ease. Working with Dr. Gravitte and his staff has been an exciting learning experience that I truly enjoy!

I am a Mount Airy native and graduated from The College of Charleston. My position with Dr. Gravitte and our team allows me to grow new partnerships in our great community and facilitate educational and outreach opportunities to help people of all ages understand the importance of oral health. We always emphasize that oral health equals overall health, and that prevention and education are vital to the health of our community! Not to mention, the future of dentistry and dental technology is a fascinating career for our local youth, too!

I love working with patients and helping them in so many different ways! Being a part of the front office team is so rewarding, especially when it’s always "Dentistry with Heart" for our patients!

I love welcoming patients into our office and being the first introduction to our "Dentistry with Heart" practice! Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my fiancé, my family and exploring new places!
