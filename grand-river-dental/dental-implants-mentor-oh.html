Which Implant Solution Is Right for You?

Whether you’re replacing one tooth or your entire smile, Grand River Dental has an implant solution for you.

Single Tooth Implant

The most common solution. One titanium post with a porcelain crown replaces a single missing tooth — looking and functioning just like your natural tooth. No impact on neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

When multiple adjacent teeth are missing, two implants can anchor a bridge spanning the gap — a fixed, permanent solution that’s more stable than a traditional bridge.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Snap-on or fixed dentures anchored to 2–6 implants. No more slipping, adhesive paste, or dietary restrictions. The most comfortable and confident denture experience possible.

Full Arch Restoration

Replace an entire arch of teeth with as few as 4 implants. A cost-effective, permanent alternative to full dentures — with the stability and confidence of natural teeth.

Bone Grafting

If you’ve lost jawbone density from a missing tooth, a bone graft builds up the site before implant placement. We handle this in-office for a streamlined, convenient experience.

Same-Day Implant Crowns

In select cases, we can place the implant crown the same day as the implant post — reducing your overall treatment time and giving you a functional smile faster.

What to Expect at Grand River Dental

Dental implant treatment is a multi-step process — but we make every stage as smooth and comfortable as possible.

1. Consultation

We evaluate your oral health, take 3D X-rays, and discuss your goals. You’ll receive a detailed treatment plan with clear pricing before anything begins.

2. Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia, the titanium post is placed in the jawbone. Most patients are comfortable within a day or two. The healing phase takes 3–6 months.

3. Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone — creating a stable, permanent foundation. A temporary restoration keeps things looking good while you heal.

4. Crown Placement

Once healed, we design and place your permanent crown — custom-matched to your natural tooth color. The result looks and feels completely natural.

Dental Implant FAQs

A single implant at Grand River Dental typically ranges from $3,000–$5,000 including the post, abutment, and crown. Costs vary based on complexity, number of implants, and whether bone grafting is needed. We’ll review your insurance benefits and offer CareCredit 0 % interest financing to make implants accessible.

Some dental insurance plans cover part of the implant crown and may cover preparatory procedures. Coverage varies widely. Grand River Dental will check your benefits before treatment and maximize what your plan covers.

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are excellent candidates. Sufficient jawbone density, healthy gums, and no uncontrolled systemic conditions are key factors. Patients with bone loss may be candidates after a bone graft. Schedule a consultation and we’ll assess your candidacy.

From placement to final crown, the complete process typically takes 4–8 months. The majority of that time is the healing phase where the implant fuses with bone — your active appointment time is just a few visits.

The procedure is done under local anesthesia — you won’t feel pain. Afterward, most patients experience only mild soreness for a few days, managed with OTC pain relievers. Most patients are genuinely surprised by how straightforward the recovery is.
