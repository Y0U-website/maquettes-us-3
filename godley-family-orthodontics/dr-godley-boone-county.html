Press & Recognition

Dr. Danielle Godley , owner and orthodontist at Godley Family Orthodontics , has been featured in several local publications recognizing her work as an orthodontist, small business owner, and community leader in Zionsville, Indiana. Below are highlights from The Scout Guide Hamilton County and Boone County Magazine, with links to each full interview and feature.

Boone County Magazine: Modern Technology, Genuine Care (October 2025)

Boone County Magazine featured Godley Family Orthodontics in its October 2025 issue, highlighting how the practice pairs modern orthodontic technology with genuine, personalized patient care. The article explores Dr. Godley’s philosophy that excellent treatment goes beyond straightening teeth — creating healthy, functional bites and positive experiences for children, teens, and adults across Zionsville, Lebanon, Whitestown, and the surrounding Boone County communities.

Read more about the feature in our full post , or view the digital issue: Boone County Magazine October 2025

The Scout Guide Hamilton County: Orthodontic Health Month (October 2024)

October is recognized annually as Orthodontic Health Month, a time to raise awareness about the positive impact orthodontic treatment can have on oral health and overall well-being. To celebrate, The Scout Guide Hamilton County asked Dr. Godley for her insights on orthodontic health and the benefits of orthodontic treatment. She discusses common orthodontic issues, how treatment has evolved with modern technology, advice for patients and parents, and more.

Read the full article: Embrace Orthodontic Health Month: Insights from Dr. Danielle Godley of Godley Family Orthodontics

The Scout Guide Hamilton County: Women’s Small Business Month (October 2024)

In honor of Women’s Small Business Month, The Scout Guide Hamilton County featured Dr. Godley as a local business owner to know. The Scout Guide (TSG) publishes printed city guides and online directories that connect communities to local businesses and highlight the people behind them.

A passionate advocate for both her patients and her community, Dr. Godley has built a modern, innovative, and caring orthodontic practice. With a commitment to personalized treatment and a genuine, welcoming environment, she has redefined what it means to be a leader in healthcare. To celebrate, TSG asked her a few questions about her dedication to empowering women, her patients, and her family.

Read the full interview: Women’s Small Business Month Featuring Dr. Danielle Godley of Godley Family Orthodontics

Boone County Magazine: Women in Business (October 2024)

Each October, Boone County Magazine celebrates Women in Business, and this year the magazine chose Dr. Godley to feature in its October issue. She was highlighted alongside other wonderful female business owners, including Dr. Katelyn Brauer of Poplar Pediatric Dentistry, Gwendolyn Rogers of The Cake Bake Shop, Sam Harnish and Alyssa Burke of Balanced Friends, the Community Foundation of Boone County, and Mel McMahon of Indiana Owned.

Read the digital issue: Boone County Magazine October 2024

The Scout Guide Hamilton County: Orthodontic Care and Your New Year’s Resolutions (January 2024)

[Image suggestion: photo from the TSG article or a smiling patient photo. Alt text: Dr. Danielle Godley of Godley Family Orthodontics shares why orthodontic care belongs on your New Year’s resolutions list, The Scout Guide Hamilton County, January 2024 ]

To kick off the new year, The Scout Guide Hamilton County sat down with Dr. Godley to discuss why orthodontic care can be a transformative New Year’s resolution — for your smile, your confidence, and your overall health. In the Q&A, she shares how even small changes in tooth positioning can make a big impact, walks through today’s range of treatment options from classic metal braces to clear aligners and hidden braces, and offers practical tips for keeping teeth healthy during treatment.

Dr. Godley also breaks down what determines treatment timelines — from case complexity to aligner wear — and shares her top advice for anyone considering treatment: start with a complimentary consultation with a board-certified orthodontist.
