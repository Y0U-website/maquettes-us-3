These can often be adapted to your current denture to make it "snap" securely into place.

The Mini Implant system consists of a miniature titanium implant that acts like the root of your tooth and a retaining fixture that is incorporated into the base of your denture.

It is a one step procedure that generally takes just over an hour and involves minimally invasive surgery with no sutures. Placement of the implants is quick and easy, performed in the dentist’s office - usually in one day .
