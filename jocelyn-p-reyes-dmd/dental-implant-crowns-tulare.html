Why the crown matters as much as the implant

An implant is only as good as the tooth built on top of it. A well-designed implant crown distributes bite forces properly, seals neatly at the gumline, and blends with your natural teeth in color and shape. With over 20 years of restorative experience , Dr. Reyes sweats those details, that’s the difference between an implant you notice and one you forget is even there.

Common Questions

We focus on the restorative side: the crown, bridge, or denture that attaches to the implant. The surgical placement of the implant post is done by a trusted local specialist, and we coordinate the whole process so your care feels seamless from start to finish.

A dental implant has two main parts: a titanium post placed in the jaw (the artificial root) and the crown attached on top (the visible tooth). The implant crown is custom-designed to match your natural teeth in shape and color, so it looks and works like the real thing.

We use our Medit 3D digital scanner to capture a precise digital model of your implant and surrounding teeth, no messy impression trays. That precision means a crown that fits accurately, feels natural, and bites comfortably.
