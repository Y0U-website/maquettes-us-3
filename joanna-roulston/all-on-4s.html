How All-on-4 Works

All-on-4 technology strategically places four dental implants to support an entire arch of replacement teeth. Dr. Roulston positions these implants at calculated angles to maximize contact with existing bone structure, often eliminating the need for bone grafting procedures that can delay treatment for months.

The process begins with careful evaluation of your jaw structure using advanced imaging technology. This allows precise planning of implant placement to achieve optimal stability and support for your new teeth.

Innovative Implant Positioning

Unlike traditional methods requiring multiple implants, All-on-4 uses an innovative angled approach that takes advantage of your strongest bone areas. The two front implants are placed vertically, while the back implants are angled to avoid sensitive areas like sinuses while maximizing bone contact.

This strategic positioning creates a solid foundation that can immediately support temporary teeth, allowing you to leave our office with a complete smile on treatment day.

Premium Materials for Lasting Results

Your final restoration will be crafted from high-grade zirconia, representing the pinnacle of dental material technology. Unlike older acrylic materials, zirconia provides exceptional strength while maintaining the translucent properties that create natural-looking teeth.

Ideal Candidates for All-on-4

This treatment works exceptionally well for patients facing various dental challenges. You may be an excellent candidate if you’re dealing with multiple tooth loss, failing dental work, or uncomfortable dentures that interfere with daily life.

Many patients who’ve been told they need extensive bone grafting find that All-on-4’s angled approach makes treatment possible without these additional procedures. The technique often works even when traditional implants aren’t feasible due to bone limitations.

Age alone doesn’t determine candidacy we evaluate each case individually to ensure All-on-4 provides the best solution for your specific situation.

  • Extensive tooth loss in one or both arches
  • Failing bridges or dental work requiring replacement
  • Uncomfortable or unstable dentures
  • Moderate bone loss without desire for grafting procedures
  • Need for immediate tooth replacement

Your Treatment Experience

Consultation and Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation where Dr. Roulston examines your oral health, discusses your goals, and determines if All-on-4 meets your needs. Advanced imaging helps plan precise implant placement for optimal results.

Treatment Day

The procedure typically requires 4-6 hours, depending on your specific case complexity. You’ll receive sedation for comfort throughout the process, and leave with secure temporary teeth attached to your new implants.

Healing and Integration

Over the following 3-4 months, your implants integrate with surrounding bone tissue while you wear temporary restorations. This healing period ensures optimal stability before creating your permanent teeth.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we’ll create your custom zirconia teeth through a series of appointments spanning several weeks. This process ensures perfect fit, function, and aesthetics for your permanent smile.

Common Questions About Teeth in a Day

Will I be able to eat normally afterward? You’ll receive functional temporary teeth immediately, but initially need to follow a soft diet to support proper healing. As your mouth heals and implants stabilize, you’ll gradually return to enjoying all your favorite foods.

How much does All-on-4 treatment cost?

Pricing varies based on individual needs, but typically ranges from $25,000-$35,000 per arch nationally. We provide detailed cost breakdowns during consultation and offer financing to make treatment accessible.

How long does the actual procedure take?

Expect 3-4 hours per arch for the surgical procedure. Full mouth treatment naturally requires a longer appointment to complete both upper and lower restorations.

Will the procedure be painful?

Anesthesia ensures you feel no discomfort during treatment. Post-operative pain is typically manageable with prescribed medications and usually peaks within the first 48 hours before gradually improving.

How long does recovery take?

While individual healing varies, most patients experience their most significant discomfort in the first few days. Complete healing and adaptation to new teeth can take several months.

Can All-on-4 work with limited bone?

Often, yes. The angled implant technique frequently enables treatment even when patients have been told they lack sufficient bone for traditional implants. Multiple placement strategies can achieve successful outcomes.

Can I get All-on-4 the same day as extractions?

Absolutely. Same-day extractions and implant placement actually represent the ideal scenario, as you’ll have maximum bone volume available on extraction day.
