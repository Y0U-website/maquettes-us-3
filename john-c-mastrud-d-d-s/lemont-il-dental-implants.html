Single And Multiple Dental Implants

At our office, we restore single, multiple, and full sets of teeth implants. In a multiple dental implant procedure, a bridge can replace three or more missing teeth. This can help prevent further tooth loss because the other teeth shift to compensate for the lost space.

An alternative option to removable dentures is fixed bridge dentures anchored to the jawbone by multiple implants. These implant-supported dentures offer many advantages over traditional dentures. These include no messy denture adhesives that stay securely in place, making eating much more manageable, and you can brush your teeth normally.
