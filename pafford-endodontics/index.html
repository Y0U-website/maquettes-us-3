The practice

We are dedicated to delivering endodontic services at the highest level. Each endodontic case is unique, and every patient we treat deserves excellent care. Our promise is that we will work with your dentist to determine a treatment approach that is right for you.

Dr. Pafford and his assistants use the microscope throughout every dental procedure. Our Carestream 9600 3D scanner allows us to take high-quality 3-dimensional images of your condition, thereby providing you with more targeted and effective treatment.

Dr. Pafford and his staff believe that the patient’s needs come first. Dr. Pafford will thoroughly explain his findings and make recommendations that are in your best interest. We are always striving to make our office a comfortable and pleasant place to be.
