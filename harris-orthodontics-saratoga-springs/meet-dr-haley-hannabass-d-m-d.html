Meet the doctor

Dr. Haley Hannabass, D.M.D.

Hi, I’m Haley! I grew up in Daytona Beach, Florida, where I spent most of my time riding horses and surfing, so I’ve always loved being active and outdoors. I went on to attend the University of Florida (Go Gators!) before earning my dental degree from the University of Pittsburgh, where I graduated valedictorian and was inducted into the OKU Honor Society. I completed my orthodontics residency at the University of Alabama at Birmingham.
