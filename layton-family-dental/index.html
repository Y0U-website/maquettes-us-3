The practice

Crowns, or sometimes referred to as caps, are full coverage dental restorations.

omposite fillings, or white colored dental fillings, are a resin based esthetic material that is used to restore cavities. Although silver/amalgam fillings are still used on occasion.

Dentistry has come a long way from the analog waxing and casting of dental metals and adding ceramic by hand. Most new things in dentistry are made via a digital workflow from dentures to crowns

Not all cleanings are the same in dentistry. If you haven’t been to the dentist in a long time, tartar can build on your teeth and cause the bone around your teeth to melt away

Implants are a great option to replace missing teeth but some circumstances make placing implants difficult and a high chance for failure. Bridges are a great alternative to implants.

Complete dentures and partial dentures are some of the most tried and true ways to replace some or all of someone’s missing teeth.
