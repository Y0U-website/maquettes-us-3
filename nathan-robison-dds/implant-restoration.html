What is Implant Restoration?

Dental implant restoration refers to the process of replacing missing or damaged teeth with dental implants and attaching custom-made prosthetic teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures, to restore the appearance and function of natural teeth. Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. Once the implants have fused with the jawbone (a process called osseointegration), the implant restoration process begins.

Benefits of Implant Restoration

Apart from restoring your smile, implant restorations can offer a plethora of benefits. For example, it can enhance your chewing ability, improve speech, and prevent bone loss that commonly occurs after tooth loss. Another essential advantage of implant restorations is that it helps to distribute pressure while eating, thus avoiding pressure points on your other teeth. Additionally, the implant itself looks and feels like a natural tooth, so you can smile and eat confidently.

The Implant Restoration Process

When you visit West Dental Implants & Prosthetics, we will assess your oral health and recommend the best treatment plan. The implant restoration process typically involves several steps. First, Dr. Robison will surgically place the implant into your jawbone. During implant restoration, we will take impressions to create precise, natural-looking prosthetic teeth that match your existing teeth in color, shape, and size. These prosthetic teeth are then securely attached to the implants, providing stable, durable, and aesthetically pleasing replacements for missing teeth.

Implant Restoration Aftercare

It’s essential to take good care of your implant to ensure its longevity and overall oral health. After the restoration, we will give you instructions on caring for the implant. By following these simple steps, you can enjoy the benefits of your dental implants for many years to come.

One of the most important things you can do to maintain your dental implants is to prioritize oral hygiene. You should brush and floss the implant just like your natural teeth and visit us regularly for checkups or treatment if you notice any issues with your implant.

And if possible, use an electric toothbrush. Electric toothbrushes can provide a more effective cleaning than regular toothbrushes, which can help to keep your dental implant in optimal shape. Try to avoid baking soda and other abrasives which can damage the implant surface.
