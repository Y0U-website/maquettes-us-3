Insurance & Billing

Pay Your Bill Hassle-Free

We accept cash, checks, and most major cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discovery, and American Express). Payment is due at the time of service unless prior payment arrangement were made. If you don’t have dental insurance, we can accommodate by helping you file for benefits and can accept assignments if coverage is verified on the service date. It’s important to remember that regardless of insurance coverage, you are responsible for all fees charged at our office. For questions about billing and your remaining balance, please contact us.
