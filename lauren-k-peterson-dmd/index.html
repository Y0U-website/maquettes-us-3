Choosing a dentist who cares about your family’s oral health needs is essential. Even more important is choosing a dentist who truly cares about you and your family as individuals.

The practice

At Hälsa Dental, Dr. Peterson and her team will take the time to get to know you and your family. When you visit our dental office, you know you’re among friends. Dr. Peterson and the rest of our friendly, accommodating and experienced dental team know how to keep your family’s teeth & mouth healthy so that you can enjoy happy, beautiful smiles for life.

Request an appointment online , call or visit our dentist office in Wheat Ridge. Schedule your or your family member’s next visit with Dr. Peterson and discover how personalized, health-centered dental care can improve your smile!

We understand that the mouth is the gateway to the rest of your body, which means that your oral health directly affects your overall health.

Dr. Peterson and Dr. Dougherty use some of the latest dental equipment, including digital radiography, as well as Same Day Crowns to detect, diagnose, and treat oral health problems quickly and effectively so that you can maintain optimal oral and overall health. Together they have the knowledge and experience to help you understand how improving your oral health through consistent home care and regular visits to the dentist will not only enhance and improve your smile, but your overall health as well.

Dr. Lauren Peterson is a 2009 graduate (Magna Cum Laude) from the Boston University School of Dental Medicine, where she received her Doctorate in Medical Dentistry. Her undergraduate studies in History were completed at UCLA. Shortly after receiving her DMD, Dr. Peterson moved to and began practicing in Colorado.

As a dentist, Dr. Peterson aims to improve the overall health and appearance of her patients in a warm and inviting environment.

In addition, Dr. Peterson is a participant in a local Spear Study Club, a group of skilled dentists and specialists, who consult on complicated cases and attend continuing education at the Scottsdale Center in Arizona. She has attended advanced courses in facially-generated treatment planning, worn dentition, anterior cosmetic dentistry, occlusion and airway prosthedontics.
