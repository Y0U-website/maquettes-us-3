Your questions, answered simply

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are posts made of titanium which are implanted into the jaw bone. The bone grows around and integrates with the post, creating a replacement "root." This provides a structure around which a restoration can be built to replace a single tooth, or to which a bridge or denture can be affixed to replace multiple teeth.

Which are better implants or dentures?

Dental implants provide a permanent tooth replacement and help preserve jawbone by stimulating it like a natural tooth root. In contrast, traditional dentures must be routinely altered due to changes in the mouth as unused jawbone dissolves. Dentures can also be cost-effectively supported on four or six implants per arch.

How much do dental implants cost?

The final cost of your implant depends on several factors, including the location of the desired implant, the number of implants required, and any preparatory work needed to prepare the site for a successful placement. Overall, a dental implant is often the most cost-effective tooth replacement option over the long term.

What your implant journey actually looks like

Everyone asks the same first questions: Can I even get an implant? What’s involved? How long does it take? Here’s the honest version.

The consultation

We start by understanding where you are and where you want to be your health, your function, your goals, your budget and lay out the options, from a single implant to implant-supported teeth that don’t come out.

The surgery one visit when possible

Whenever it’s safe to, Dr.Guy prefers to do it all in one surgery remove what needs removing and place the implants together and to be as minimally invasive as possible, rather than dragging you through separate procedures months apart.

Healing the part marketing skips

Implants aren’t glued in like a hip replacement. Your bone actually fuses to the implant, and that takes time usually about three to four months depending on your health. You won’t go without teeth while you heal; there are always options to keep you smiling in the meantime.

Your final teeth

Once biology has done its job, we craft and customize your final smile. On ’Teeth-in-a-Day’ and All-on-X cases, the teeth you leave with that day are usually a beautiful provisional the final restoration comes once you’re fully healed.

Nervous about surgery? That’s normal here and we plan for it.

A lot of implant patients are anxious, and we completely get it. For longer surgical visits, conscious sedation is the go-to most people say it felt like they took a nap and barely remember being there. For lighter visits, nitrous (laughing gas) takes the edge off, and you can drive yourself home. And for patients with serious health history, Dr.Guy can bring in an anesthetist or treat in a hospital setting. The point is simple: your comfort and safety set the plan.
