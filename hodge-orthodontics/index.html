Welcome to the office of Dr. John Hodge, where we provide exceptional orthodontic care to the residents of East Mississippi.

The practice

Using proven state-of-the-art technology to enhance your experience, we maintain the most advanced procedures, appliances and equipment available to orthodontists, including 3D X-rays, advanced computer graphics, Clarity™ Advanced ceramic braces, Victory Series™ braces and 3M Clarity Aligners™ clear aligners to ensure that every patient achieves a healthy and beautiful smile.

Our special interest is the detection of problems at an early age so that we can provide interceptive treatment. This can reduce the need for extracting teeth and improve growth problems that affect profiles and facial shapes as well as jaw functions.

Dr. Hodge earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Mississippi and a Master’s degree in Orthodontics from the University of Oklahoma.
