Most advanced, versatile and lifelike option to replace missing teeth.

Dental implants are one of the most advanced and versatile options available for replacing missing teeth. Dental implants in Peoria, IL can provide a stable, natural-looking foundation for replacing one tooth, multiple teeth, or even a full arch of teeth.

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. After placement, the living bone gradually fuses with the implant through a process called osseointegration. Once healing is complete, the implant can provide a strong, stable foundation for a dental crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture.

Because implants are secured to the jawbone, they can help restore your ability to chew, speak, smile and laugh with greater confidence without the movement that can sometimes occur with traditional removable dentures.

Are you a candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants may be an option for many adults who have one or more missing teeth and are looking for a long-term tooth replacement solution. Good candidates generally have healthy gums and enough healthy jawbone to support the implant.

Certain factors, such as bone loss, gum disease, medical conditions, smoking, or other oral health concerns, may affect candidacy or require additional treatment before implants can be placed.

During your consultation, Dr. Meiss will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals and determine whether dental implants are an appropriate option for you. In some cases, additional procedures may be recommended before implant placement to create the proper foundation for successful treatment.

All-on-4 and full-arch dental implants

For patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, implant-supported full-arch solutions can provide another option for restoring function and appearance.

The All-on-4 concept uses strategically placed dental implants to support a full-arch prosthesis. Depending on your individual needs, several implants may be used to provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth.

In appropriate cases, patients may be able to leave their appointment with temporary replacement teeth shortly after implant placement while the implants heal and integrate with the surrounding bone. Your treatment timeline will depend on your oral health, bone quality and individual treatment plan.

What does dental implant treatment cost?

The cost of dental implants varies from patient to patient. Factors that can affect the overall cost include the number of teeth being replaced, the type of restoration needed, the condition of your existing teeth and gums, and whether additional procedures are required before treatment.

Rather than providing a one-size-fits-all price, we recommend scheduling a consultation so Dr. Meiss can evaluate your needs and explain your treatment options and associated costs.

Frequently asked questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular professional dental visits, dental implants can provide a long-term solution for missing teeth. The implant itself is designed to be durable, while the crown, bridge or denture attached to the implant may eventually need repair or replacement due to normal wear.

Maintaining good oral hygiene and keeping regular dental appointments are important for protecting the health of your gums, surrounding bone and dental implant.

Are dental implants painful?

Dental implant placement is performed using local anesthesia to keep you comfortable during the procedure. It is normal to experience some soreness, swelling or tenderness following treatment as your mouth heals.

Dr. Meiss will provide instructions for caring for the treatment area and help you understand what to expect throughout the recovery process.

How do I care for dental implants?

Dental implants should be cared for much like your natural teeth. Daily brushing and cleaning between the teeth are important for maintaining healthy gums and protecting your investment.

Routine dental examinations and professional cleanings are also important so your dental team can monitor the health of your implants, gums and surrounding teeth.

What is the difference between dental implants and dentures?

Traditional dentures are removable appliances that rest on the gums and can replace multiple missing teeth or an entire arch. Dental implants, on the other hand, are anchored into the jawbone and can be used to support individual replacement teeth, bridges or implant-supported dentures.

For some patients, implant-supported teeth can provide greater stability and a more secure feel than traditional removable dentures. The best option depends on your oral health, goals, bone structure and overall treatment needs.
