Dentures and Partials

The effects of missing teeth on your oral health can be worrisome. Gaps left by a missing tooth tend to cause the teeth on either side to move, which in turn, means your bite may be affected. How? The teeth next to the space can lean into the gap and change the way your upper and lower teeth bite together. At Mercy Seat Dentistry, we offer a wide range of options to help replace missing teeth: plastic/cast partial dentures, fixed bridges and dental implants. Get in touch and let’s figure the best option for you.

Looking for an alternative to metal braces? Look no further; Invisalign (Clear) Braces are the way to go!

Peridontal (Gum) Disease Treatment

Gum disease is no joke. This inflammation of the gums can spread and affect the bone that supports your teeth. Symptoms like swollen/red/tender/bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, loose teeth or visible pus around your teeth and gums could mean you have a gum disease. The good news is that gum disease can be treated and controlled through a maintenance program. If you experience any of the mentioned signs, get in touch with us quickly so we can put together a plan of action.

Root Canal Therapy

If you have a tooth that is painful, sensitive to hot and cold and biting, or if it keeps you up at night, the nerve of your tooth might be affected. To save the tooth, a root canal might be recommended - we simply clean the nerve of the affected tooth and fill up the space created.
