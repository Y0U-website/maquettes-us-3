Tooth Extractions

Sometimes a tooth needs to be removed because of infection, severe wear, fracture, or other conditions that cannot be corrected another way. We perform extractions in a safe, supportive environment and help patients understand what options may come next for restoring their smile.

If needed, we can also discuss what replacement options may follow an extraction, including dentures or implant-based solutions.

  • Ability to choose same day tooth removal
  • Non-traumatic extractions that preserve bone and gum tissue
  • Pain relief
  • Removal of foul taste and/or breath
  • Extractions starting at $122
