Invisalign for Adults

  • Comfortable Fit Made from smooth, BPA-free plastic, these aligners are designed for comfort and are less likely to cause irritation compared to metal braces.
  • Removable Convenience The aligners can be removed for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.
  • Fewer Visits to the Office Invisalign treatment generally requires fewer visits to the orthodontist compared to traditional braces, which is more convenient for busy adults.
  • Effective and Efficient Invisalign for Adults is designed to address a variety of orthodontic issues such as crowding, gaps, and bite problems, leading to a healthier and more attractive smile.

Our Invisalign for Adults Process

Initial Consultation and Assessment

Your journey to a straighter smile begins with a thorough consultation at Helotes Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics. Our orthodontists will evaluate your dental health and discuss whether Invisalign is the best option for you.

Customized Treatment Plan

We use advanced 3D imaging technology to create a detailed treatment plan that maps out the precise movements of your teeth. This ensures that the aligners are tailored to your specific needs for optimal results.

Using the Aligners

You will receive a series of aligners, each to be worn for approximately two weeks. These aligners will gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions. Regular check-ins with our orthodontists will ensure that your treatment is progressing as planned.

Maintaining Your Progress

We provide detailed instructions on how to care for your aligners and maintain excellent oral hygiene throughout your treatment. Periodic visits to our office will help monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments. Our teeth cleaning services ensure your teeth remain healthy throughout the process.
