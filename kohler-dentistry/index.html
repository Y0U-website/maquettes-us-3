Craig S. Kohler, DDS, MBA, MAGD, has practiced dentistry on the North Shore of Chicago for over 40 years. In addition, he stays abreast of new technology through mentoring and training other dentists and teaching at Spear Education in Scottsdale, Arizona.

After completing his residency, Dr. Kohler earned his Fellowship and Mastership from the Academy of General Dentistry by completing 1,600 hours of continuing education. His use of state-of-the-art technology and ongoing pursuit of advanced education allow him to provide excellent patient care.

To give patients access to more thorough and consistent at-home brushing, Dr. Kohler invented the MOTSY ® Light . This first-of-its-kind LED multi-attachment oral care toothbrush helps patients see and remove hidden plaque that other brushes may miss.

Dr. Kohler provides comprehensive dentistry to patients of all ages, combining general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry with a gentle touch to support healthy smiles at every stage of life.
