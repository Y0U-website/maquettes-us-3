Specialty-Level Training, Applied Every Day

Dr. Bhakti Jain holds an MDS in Prosthodontics with implantology training in addition to her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, earned with honors from UCSF. That advanced training — typically pursued after dental school specifically to master complex restorative and implant cases — shapes how she approaches every patient, not just the most complicated ones. Routine exams benefit from the same careful eye for bite alignment, gum health, and long-term tooth structure that guides full-mouth rehabilitation cases.

Family Dentistry Without Compromise

Many practices ask patients to choose between a family-friendly office and one equipped for advanced restorative and implant care. We built Montague Dental Excellence so families do not have to make that trade-off. Children can get their first cleanings here, teens can be monitored through the years their adult teeth come in, and adults can address everything from a simple filling to implant-supported dentures, all with the same team who already knows their history.

Decades of Roots in Milpitas

Our practice has served the Milpitas community, along with neighboring Fremont and San Jose, for many years. That kind of longevity is not an accident — it comes from patients returning visit after visit, referring family members, and trusting us with care that spans decades of their lives. We take that trust seriously, and it shows in how we communicate, follow up, and plan long-term care rather than treating each visit in isolation.

Modern Technology in Service of Better Care

Digital scanning, advanced imaging, and up-to-date treatment planning tools are not novelties in our office — they are part of how we deliver more predictable, more comfortable care. Better imaging means more accurate diagnoses. Digital scans mean fewer uncomfortable impressions. Thoughtful treatment planning software means you see a clearer picture of your options before committing to a plan. Technology, used well, should make your experience easier, not just more advanced on paper.
