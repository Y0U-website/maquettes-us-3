Laurel Rolnick, DDS

Dr. Laurel Rolnick always knew she wanted to work with children, ever since she was a little girl. Her main goal is provide exceptional dental care in a safe and loving environment — ensuring children develop a positive relationship coming to the dentist.

She earned her B.S in Psychology from Hofstra University , then went on to complete her DDS degree from New York University. She completed a general dentistry residency at One Brooklyn Health- where she earned the prestigious Resident of the Year title, a testament to her commitment to her patients.

Driven by her love for working with kids, Dr. Laurel entered a pediatric residency at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center in Brooklyn.

Throughout her journey, Dr. Laurel has stood out with her unwavering commitment to her patient’s well-being, constant pursuit of knowledge, and sincere care for children. Dr. Laurel’s promise is to make your child’s dental experience both enjoyable and safe, ensuring they leave her care smiling.

In her free time, she loves to spend time with her husband, two children and family.
