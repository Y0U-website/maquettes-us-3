How do dental implants work?

Dental implants require surgery to place them into your bone. After the surgical placement, we need to allow time for them to heal. Most patients tell us they are surprised by how little discomfort they experience and how quickly they heal.

The healing process usually takes three to six months. This time allows the dental implant post to fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This process creates a sturdy base to support your dental crown. Dental implants in Apex, NC replace tooth and root, making them similar to natural teeth.

Once healing is complete, your implant dentist in Apex, NC customizes a porcelain dental crown for your implant. We will match the shade and shape to your adjacent teeth so that no one notices that you have an implant. After a while, you’ll probably not notice either!

Dental implants require no special treatment other than the care you would provide for natural teeth. You will also not have to deal with adhesives, realigns, or other inconveniences.

What is bone grafting?

Has someone told you before that you were not a good candidate for dental implants? Perhaps you suffered from advanced gum disease, and you may not have the bone needed to support a dental implant. The good news is that a bone graft procedure may be the solution you need to allow you to sustain a dental implant, and Dr. Prusik can help with oral surgery in Apex, NC.

A bone graft builds up your bone by placing donor or synthetic bone material onto your jawbone, which encourages regeneration. Your jawbone will grow and integrate with the material until it s strong enough to support a dental implant. This takes a few months, and then Dr. Prusik can place a dental implant into your jawbone, which will integrate with your bone to take the place of your root. This happens naturally because titanium is biocompatible and will fuse with your bone.

Dental Implant Placement

After the tooth site is sufficiently healed, implant placement surgery can be performed. The crown will need to be custom-made for your implant and then placed in the mouth. These steps usually take several weeks to complete.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Apex, NC, are tooth root replacements surgically implanted into the jawbone. These roots integrate into the bone, creating a stable underpinning for your replacement tooth. Four to six implants can also secure a denture if you have complete or significant tooth loss.

Dentists recommend implants because they are like natural teeth and the only replacement option that restores your tooth and its missing root. By replacing both, you help preserve jawbone health and guard against deterioration. Additionally, dental implants prevent migrating teeth, which can lead to oral health complications.
