The practice

Dr. Brendan Krueger (left), Dr. Gail Callaghan (center), and Dr. Raffi Belian (right)

Dr. Belian trained at the Zimmer Institute in California, the 3I Institute in Florida, the Global Dental Implant Academy (GDIA) and the ILAPEO Institute in Brazil. He aims at being an expert in his field and takes pride in keeping up to date with the latest surgical techniques.

At Modern Dentistry, we are committed to offering the highest standard of care by investing in the latest technology. Our state-of-the-art CBCT Scan allows us to view dental anatomy in three dimensions, providing unparalleled precision in diagnostics and treatment planning.

Additionally, we utilize CAD/CAM technology (CEREC) to produce same-day crowns, ensuring you receive quick and accurate restorations without the need for multiple appointments.
