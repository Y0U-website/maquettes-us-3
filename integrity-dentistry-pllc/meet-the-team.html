​Integrity Dentistry: Redefining Your Dental Experience with Compassion and Innovation

At Integrity Dentistry, the mission is simple yet powerful: On a good day, we save a smile. On a great day, we save a life. This philosophy is at the heart of the practice, led by founder and CEO Carolyn Buckley a trailblazing dental professional with over 1​5 years of experience as a dental hygienist and a career as a national dental consultant. Carolyn combines expertise, innovation, and a passion for patient care to transform the traditional dental experience.

Carolyn’s journey to founding Integrity Dentistry was inspired by her upbringing as the daughter of an F-16 Air Force fighter pilot. The discipline, integrity, and strong work ethic instilled in her by her father shaped the foundation for her career and vision for Integrity Dentistry. These values are reflected in every patient interaction, as Carolyn strives to empower patients, prioritize transparency, and ensure compassionate, personalized care.

Her experiences as a dental hygienist and national consultant exposed her to gaps in care rushed treatment plans, inadequate infection control, and impersonal experiences. These observations motivated her to create a practice built on transparency, compassion, and innovation. Today, Integrity Dentistry prioritizes treating patients as individuals with unique needs, focusing on personalized, preventive care and addressing the oral-systemic connection.

As a practice owner, Carolyn is breaking through the antiquated dental hierarchy and reimagining what dentistry can be. Integrity Dentistry is built on the principle that oral health is deeply connected to overall systemic health. The practice emphasizes customized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique health needs, recognizing the critical role oral health plays in preventing and identifying early signs of systemic conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, autoimmune disorders, cancer, ​alzheimer s, COPD, sleep apnea, and more.

Carolyn is also deeply committed to supporting local businesses and counteracting the influx of large corporate buyouts that have flooded the dental industry often leading to impersonal care and systemic challenges for both patients and professionals. By staying locally owned and focused, Integrity Dentistry ensures personalized care and genuine relationships with every patient. Her mission is to save patients significant time and money ​over their lifetime while enhancing their quality of life through personalized, preventive-focused care.

Oral health is vital not only for a confident smile but for overall well-being. Integrity Dentistry offers services that extend beyond the standard dental visit, including advanced diagnostics, custom mouthguards, and prescription fluoride treatments that can reduce cavity risk by up to 75 %. With an emphasis on prevention, the practice focuses on early detection and collaborative care to support overall well-being through proactive oral health strategies.

Whether you’re visiting for preventive care, advanced diagnostics, or specialized treatment, the goal is the same: to improve your quality of life and ensure your smile reflects health and happiness.

Kimberly Wilkins, Lead Dentist

Hi, I’m Dr. Kimberly Wilkins and we are glad you re here.

I grew up in Lawrenceburg, Kentucky and attended college at the University of Kentucky. From there, I went on to dental school at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry and graduated in 1994. After that, I went into private practice in Nicholasville, Kentucky.

I then moved my practice to Lexington, Kentucky and in the early 1990’s, became the first female dentist to place dental implants in my practice. Since then, my interests have branched out to cosmetic dentistry and facial esthetics such as administering Botox and facial fillers. I enjoy all aspects of General Dentistry including fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, periodontal therapy, implants and cosmetic dentistry. I am a member of the American Dental Association, Kentucky Dental Association, and the Bluegrass Dental Society.

My passion for dentistry started from loving to go to the dentist growing up. While in undergraduate school I attended HCOP, the Health and Careers Opportunity Program at the University of Kentucky. It focused on students interested in Pharmacy School, Dental School and Medical School. It was there that I found out I truly enjoyed working with my hands while providing dental care. Improving patients dental care while creating beautiful smiles keeps my passion for dentistry going after 30 years!

I recently moved back to my hometown of Lawrenceburg. I am married with two children. I have a dog and a grand dog. In my spare time I love to bake, walk, read and attend continuing education classes. I love that Dentistry is full of new technology to learn about and enjoy caring for all my dental patients!
