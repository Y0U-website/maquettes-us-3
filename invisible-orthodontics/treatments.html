Orthodontic Solutions

Personalized orthodontic care for all ages! Children Teen Adult According to the American Association of Orthodontists, children should have their orthodontic evaluation at age 7. During this time, permanent teeth start to come out of the mouth already, and it is the perfect time to catch potential problems before they become real issues. We understand that children need specialized care, attention, and management. That’s why Dr. Nick and our team ensure that we give every child a fun, comfortable, and positive orthodontic experience at all times.

Children go through many changes during their teenage years – and it is also the busiest! But it is also the best time to have orthodontic treatment because most or all of the permanent teeth are present in the mouth. And because your teens are still in their growing phase, having treatment in early adolescence is often shorter and can avoid the need for teeth extractions and jaw surgery in the future. Let your teen enjoy a stunning, confident smile that will last a lifetime with our orthodontic solutions.

Braces aren’t for kids anymore! More adults seek orthodontic treatment to achieve healthy, stunning smiles that will let them enjoy eating, speak confidently and improve their overall health. If misaligned teeth are left untreated, you may develop dental problems such as jaw pain, teeth wear, and difficulty cleaning your teeth. Here at Invisible Orthodontics, we offer a variety of esthetic orthodontic treatments to suit every adult’s needs, concerns, and lifestyle.

Invisalign ®

Are you thinking of straightening your teeth without anyone noticing? Invisalign may be the perfect solution for you! It is one of the world’s most innovative ways to straighten teeth with removable, clear aligners. These aligners are discreet and virtually invisible. Discover more about Invisalign!

Braces Metal Damon Clear Unitek Metal braces have been the most common orthodontic treatment offered for decades. With orthodontic advancements, metal brackets are now smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever before. It is an excellent option for patients who prefer something fixed or those with complex teeth misalignment. You can also choose fun, colored rubber elastics to resonate with your personality or show creativity during holidays.

We are now proud to be offering Damon braces that provide better results in less time. It is called self-ligating braces because, unlike traditional metal braces, it uses a system similar to doors which opens and closes to hold the archwire inside the bracket. And because it doesn’t use colorful elastic bands, it is more comfortable, easier to clean, and requires fewer adjustments. As a result, your appointments can be spaced out further and will take less time.

Clear braces are an aesthetic alternative to metal braces. It is the treatment of choice for complex cases wherein clear aligners may be unsuitable. The components are the same, but the brackets are tooth-colored or translucent, which blends with your natural tooth color, making it less noticeable.

Unitek braces are manufactured by the world’s biggest dental industry innovator – 3M. Unitek brackets are designed to shorten orthodontic treatment time and improved efficiency. It offers several types of brackets to suit every individual’s case and preferences.

Sophisticated orthodontic technologies for superior comfort

iTero Digital Scanner

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable rubber impressions with our digital scanner. The iTero scanner captures teeth and other structures with superior accuracy and increases patient comfort in just minutes.

3D iCat Imaging

I-CAT 3D X-Ray Imaging Machine creates high-resolution 3D images of patients’ teeth and jaws to help us thoroughly analyze structures that can help Dr. Nick plan your treatment efficiently. It also reduces scan time and radiation by 75 % over traditional x-rays.

Protect your orthodontic investment!

Your orthodontic treatment doesn’t end the day we remove your braces or on your last clear aligner trays – it is just the beginning of your retention phase. You worked hard for your stunning smile, so protect it by wearing your retainers. Retainers will ensure that your teeth stay in place while your teeth, bones, and gums heal entirely.

Sleep Apnea Did you know that obstructive sleep apnea is far more prevalent and can be treated by an orthodontist using a removable appliance? Snoring happens when the structures inside your mouth, nose, or throat fully or partially block your airways. If you frequently wake up fatigued or your partner complains about loud snoring, Dr. Nick will be glad to help you sleep better!
