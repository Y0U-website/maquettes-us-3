FAQ’s

When will I see the results? Results typically begin to appear within 3-7 days after treatment. Full effects are usually visible about two weeks post-treatment.

How long do results last? Effects generally last 3-4 months. Individual results may vary based on treatment area, dosage, and personal metabolism.

Is there any downtime or side effects? There’s typically no downtime required. Common side effects may include temporary redness, swelling, or bruising at injection sites, usually resolving within a few days.

What should I do before and after Botox® treatment? Before: Avoid blood-thinning medications for about a week. After: Stay upright for several hours, avoid touching treated areas, and refrain from strenuous exercise for 24 hours.

What can I expect during Botox® treatment? The provider will inject the product into specific muscles using a fine needle. The process usually takes 10-20 minutes; most patients report minimal discomfort.
