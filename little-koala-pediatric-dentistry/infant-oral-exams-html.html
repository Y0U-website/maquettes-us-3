Infant Oral Exams

Following the recommendations of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Lee, Dr. Amy, and Dr. Grant suggest infants should come in for their first oral exam between the ages of six months to 1 year old.

Dr. Lee, Dr. Amy, and Dr. Grant will review proper diet, age-appropriate hygiene, and go over pacifier use to make sure your child’s oral health starts strong and stays strong.
