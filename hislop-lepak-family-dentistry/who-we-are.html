Meet Our Hislop & Lepak Family Dentistry Team

Hislop & Lepak Family Dentistry , located in Commerce Charter Twp, MI, is your trusted family dentist. With a name that reflects our commitment to the importance of family, we prioritize a hands-on approach to patient care . Our dedicated team not only takes care of our patients but also takes care of each other, fostering an atmosphere akin to a second family. When we aren’t busy taking care of patients, our team enjoys spending time with each other. We firmly believe that establishing trust and nurturing strong relationships form the bedrock of exceptional dental care.

Our History

With a history dating back to the 1960s, Hislop & Lepak Family Dentistry has become a cornerstone of dental care in our community. Dr. Hislop acquired the practice in 2021 and is committed to carrying on this legacy and embracing the future. While constantly introducing new technology, we remain steadfast in upholding our high standards of quality and timeless practices.

Dr. Hislop’s hands-on approach and desire to make a positive impact in people’s lives contribute to the well-being of the community and our patients. Always asking ourselves, What can we do to make the world a better place? has served as a cornerstone of our success in our practice and in our lives.
