Orthodontist in Hercules

OrthoWorks is a full-service orthodontic practice in Hercules, serving the Bayfront and the wider West Contra Costa community from our office on Alfred Nobel Drive. We treat children, teens, and adults with braces, Invisalign, Phase I interceptive care, and surgical orthodontic coordination, all under the care of nine board-certified orthodontic specialists.

Orthodontic Care for Hercules Families

Hercules is a family-centered community framed by the San Pablo Bay, and our care reflects that. Families bring kids in for early evaluations around age 7, return for full treatment in the teen years, and often complete their own care alongside their children. That multi-generation rhythm fits how Hercules families live, and our team welcomes it.

Every orthodontist at OrthoWorks completed two to three additional years of full-time orthodontic residency after dental school and treats only orthodontic cases. That focus matters in mixed-dentition kids whose growth must be tracked carefully, in adult cases that involve restorative or periodontal complexity, and in surgical cases that require careful coordination with oral surgery. We see patients from Hercules Middle & High School, the Ohlone, Hanna Ranch, and Lupine Hills Elementary zones, and from Pinole, Rodeo, and the wider West Contra Costa Unified School District footprint.

What to Expect

Your first visit is a complimentary orthodontic consultation. We complete a full exam, take photos and digital scans, and present every viable treatment option. For young kids, that might mean a monitor-and-grow plan instead of treatment. For teens and adults, we present braces, Invisalign, or a combination, with timelines and investment in plain numbers. Treatment visits run every 6 to 10 weeks. Every case ends with custom retainers and a long-term retention plan.
