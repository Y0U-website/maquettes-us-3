Meet Dr. Peter Durbin

Peter Durbin, D.D.S.

Dr. Peter Durbin completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Notre Dame , his dental degree at the University of Michigan , and recently graduated from the University of Illinois Orthodontics program , one of the oldest and most prestigious programs in the country. He has trained under some of the brightest minds in our field and he will bring a fresh perspective to the practice. Dr. Durbin’s addition will allow us to continue to grow and better serve our community.

Dr. Durbin’s treatment philosophy revolves around a patient-centered approach, which includes keeping the patient informed of the different aspects of orthodontic treatment. He hopes to make genuine connections with our patients to maximize the trust they have in him and our team.

In his free time, Dr. Durbin loves cooking, traveling, and spending time with his wife, Casey, his dog, Larry, and his family. He is an avid sports fan, particularly the Cubs and Bears, and loves movies and music.
