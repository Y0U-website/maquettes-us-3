Exceptional dental care for your child

We specialize in dentistry for infants, children, adolescents and those with special health care needs. We deliver our care in an environment created out of our love for children.

The practice

Our office, from its open-concept design to state-of-the-art equipment, has been created with your child’s experience in mind.

Our goal is for every patient to have a fun, educational and positive dental visit. We strive to foster a trusting relationship with parents and children in a compassionate and safe environment, and we hope to build long-term relationships with our patients and their families.

Your child’s beautiful and bright smile starts here. Our primary goal is to ensure your child develops and maintains good oral health while promoting lifelong, positive dental habits.

Our goal is to always perform sedation-free dentistry first. However, we recognize that some children may need additional assistance to get comfortable and relax. We offer laughing gas and general anesthesia.

We repair your child’s cavities with the safest, non-toxic dental materials – all of our fillings are BPA- and mercury-free. We will color-match and find the right filling that will blend in with your child’s teeth. No one will notice your child’s fillings.
