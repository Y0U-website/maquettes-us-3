New Patients: What to Expect…

Come on in! We look forward to meeting you and serving your dental care needs.

As soon as you walk into our beautiful new office, you will know: This is not the dental office of yesteryear. Our office is bright, state-of-the-art, and designed for comfort .

We truly put the “care” into dental care, and go above and beyond to treat not only your teeth and gums, but your overall health. And that should make you smile!

We’re dedicated to making you feel valued and validated, with your questions answered, your anxieties eased, and your concerns met. We make every effort to make you feel at home. We’re not only skilled technicians but a family who genuinely cares and is committed to putting you at ease.

We want our family-friendly office to be a sanctuary from the hustle and bustle of the outside world. You are encouraged to notify us of any special preferences before you arrive for your appointment so we can try to accommodate them.

We also love children and have the patience, personality and simple tricks of the trade to make your little ones appreciate the dentist from their earliest experiences.

When you smile, we smile. So make your appointment today! And thank you for entrusting us with your care.
