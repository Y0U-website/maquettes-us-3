Frequently Asked Questions

What is dental care?

Dental care is a fundamental aspect of our overall health and wellbeing. It refers to the maintenance of oral hygiene and the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of oral diseases and conditions.

Oral health plays a critical role in our lives, impacting our ability to eat, speak, and socialize comfortably. Moreover, poor oral hygiene can lead to a variety of medical conditions such as heart disease, stroke, and diabetes. This highlights the importance of maintaining good oral hygiene and seeking dental care when needed.

Types of Dental Care

There are different types of dental care that individuals may require depending on their oral health needs. Some of the most common types of dental care include:

  • Preventive Care: This type of dental care focuses on preventing oral diseases and conditions through regular checkups and cleanings. Preventive care typically involves routine dental exams, cleanings, and fluoride treatments to protect teeth from decay and cavities.
  • Restorative Care: Restorative dental care refers to procedures that repair or replace damaged or missing teeth. Common restorative procedures include fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures.
  • Endodontic Care: Endodontic care involves the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the inner pulp of teeth. Root canal therapy is a common endodontic procedure used to treat infections and inflammation in the tooth pulp.
  • Periodontal Care: Periodontal care focuses on the prevention and treatment of gum disease, which is a common oral health condition that affects the gums and supporting tissues of the teeth.
  • Orthodontic Care: Orthodontic care is used to correct misaligned teeth and jaws. This type of dental care typically involves the use of braces, retainers, and other orthodontic appliances to straighten teeth and improve bite alignment.
  • Oral Surgery: Oral surgery involves procedures that treat a variety of conditions such as impacted teeth, cysts, and tumors. Common oral surgery procedures include extractions, dental implants, and jaw surgery.

Benefits of Dental Care

Dental care is a critical aspect of our overall health and wellbeing. It involves the maintenance of good oral hygiene and the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of oral diseases and conditions. By seeking dental care when needed and maintaining good oral hygiene practices, we can improve our overall health, appearance, and quality of life.

  • Improved Oral Health: Regular dental checkups and cleanings help prevent oral diseases and conditions, such as cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer.
  • Improved Overall Health: Good oral health is linked to improved overall health. Maintaining good oral hygiene may reduce the risk of certain medical conditions, such as heart disease, stroke, and diabetes.
  • Improved Appearance: Dental care can improve the appearance of your teeth and smile. Procedures such as teeth whitening, orthodontics, and veneers can enhance the appearance of your teeth and improve your confidence.
  • Increased Comfort: Dental care can alleviate oral pain and discomfort caused by conditions such as cavities, gum disease, and misaligned teeth.
  • Cost Savings: Preventive dental care can save you money in the long run by preventing costly dental procedures.

How do I find a good dental insurance plan?

Before selecting a dental insurance plan, it’s essential to assess your oral health needs. Consider factors such as how often you visit the dentist, if you have any pre-existing conditions or require any specialized treatments. This will help you determine the type of dental insurance plan you need, such as a plan that covers preventive care or a more comprehensive plan that covers major dental procedures.

It’s essential to understand what services are covered by the dental insurance plan. Most plans cover preventive care, such as routine checkups, cleanings, and X-rays. However, coverage for restorative and cosmetic procedures, such as fillings, crowns, braces, or teeth whitening, may vary depending on the plan. Make sure to review the coverage carefully and understand any limitations or exclusions.

Dental insurance plans typically offer a network of providers who have agreed to accept the insurance plan’s fees. Choosing an in-network provider can help you save money on dental care as they have agreed to discounted rates for services. Make sure to check if your preferred dentist is in the network and confirm the availability of the services you need.

Dental insurance plans typically have an annual maximum benefit, which is the maximum amount the plan will pay for dental services during a year. Make sure to check the maximum annual benefit for the plan you are considering and ensure it aligns with your dental care needs.

The cost of dental insurance plans varies depending on the level of coverage, the network of providers, and the geographic location. Make sure to compare the costs and premiums of different plans to find the one that offers the best value for your budget. Keep in mind that some plans may have deductibles, copayments, or coinsurance fees, which can affect the overall cost.

Reading reviews and ratings from current or past plan members can provide valuable insights into the quality of service provided by the insurance plan. You can check online reviews or ratings from reputable sources such as Consumer Reports or the National Association of Insurance Commissioners.

Selecting the right dental insurance plan requires careful consideration of your dental care needs, the plan’s coverage, provider network, annual maximum benefit, and cost. Take the time to assess your needs, review the coverage and costs of different plans, and check for in-network providers. Reading reviews and ratings can also help you make an informed decision. With these tips, you can find a good dental insurance plan that meets your needs and helps you maintain good oral health.

What is some practical dental care advice?

Maintaining good dental hygiene is essential to our overall health and wellbeing. Poor oral health can lead to a range of health problems, including gum disease, tooth decay, and even heart disease. Fortunately, there are several practical dental care tips you can follow to maintain healthy teeth and gums.

Brushing your teeth twice a day is essential to maintaining good dental hygiene. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste to gently clean your teeth and remove plaque and food particles. Brush for two minutes each time, making sure to reach all surfaces of your teeth, including the front, back, and chewing surfaces.

Flossing is an essential part of dental hygiene that helps remove plaque and food particles from between your teeth and gums. Use a length of floss about 18 inches long, and wind it around your middle fingers. Use your thumbs and forefingers to gently guide the floss between your teeth and wrap it around each tooth in a C-shape. Move the floss up and down to remove any debris, and then move on to the next tooth.

Using mouthwash can help kill bacteria and freshen your breath. Choose a mouthwash that contains fluoride to help strengthen your teeth and prevent tooth decay. Swish the mouthwash around your mouth for 30 seconds before spitting it out.

Sugary and acidic foods can damage your teeth and lead to tooth decay. Limit your intake of sugary and acidic foods, such as soda, candy, and citrus fruits. If you do consume these foods, make sure to brush your teeth or rinse your mouth with water afterward.

Drinking water can help wash away food particles and bacteria from your mouth, reducing the risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Make sure to drink plenty of water throughout the day, especially after eating or drinking sugary or acidic foods.

Regular dental checkups are essential to maintaining good oral health. Visit your dentist at least twice a year for a checkup and cleaning. Your dentist can identify any dental problems early and recommend treatment to prevent them from becoming more severe.

Incorporating these practical dental care tips into your daily routine can help you maintain healthy teeth and gums. Brush and floss regularly, use mouthwash, limit sugary and acidic foods, drink plenty of water, and visit your dentist regularly. By following these tips, you can keep your teeth and gums healthy and prevent dental problems from developing. Remember, good dental hygiene is essential to your overall health and wellbeing.

What does an oral and maxillofacial surgeon do?

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are dental specialists who specialize in the diagnosis and treatment of various conditions and injuries related to the mouth, jaw, and face. They are highly trained and skilled professionals who perform a wide range of surgical procedures to improve the function and aesthetics of the oral and facial structures.

What is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon?

An oral and maxillofacial surgeon is a dental specialist who has completed four years of dental school followed by a four-year surgical residency program. During their training, they gain extensive knowledge and experience in the surgical treatment of the face, mouth, and jaw. They are trained to perform a variety of surgical procedures, including dental extractions, bone grafting, orthognathic surgery, facial trauma reconstruction, and implant surgery.

What Does an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon Do?

An oral and maxillofacial surgeon performs a range of surgical procedures to treat various conditions and injuries related to the mouth, jaw, and face. Some of the most common procedures performed by oral and maxillofacial surgeons include:

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are trained to perform dental extractions, including wisdom teeth removal. They use specialized instruments and techniques to extract teeth safely and efficiently, minimizing pain and discomfort.

Bone grafting is a surgical procedure in which a surgeon takes bone from another part of the body or a donor source and places it in the jaw to support dental implants or to replace bone loss due to trauma or disease. Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are skilled in performing bone grafting procedures.

Orthognathic surgery is a surgical procedure that corrects jaw misalignment or other deformities of the jaw or face. This procedure can improve the function and aesthetics of the mouth, jaw, and face.

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are trained to perform facial trauma reconstruction procedures. These procedures involve the repair of facial fractures or other injuries to the face, including lacerations, contusions, and burns.

Dental implant surgery is a procedure in which an artificial tooth root is implanted in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or bridge. Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are trained in this procedure and can place implants safely and efficiently.

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are also trained to diagnose and treat oral cancer. They can perform surgical procedures to remove cancerous tissue and reconstruct the affected area of the mouth or jaw.

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons play a vital role in the diagnosis and treatment of various conditions and injuries related to the mouth, jaw, and face. They are highly trained and skilled professionals who can perform a range of surgical procedures to improve the function and aesthetics of the oral and facial structures. If you are experiencing any issues related to your mouth, jaw, or face, consider consulting with an oral and maxillofacial surgeon to explore your treatment options.

What type of dentist performs root canals?

If you’ve been told that you need a root canal, you may be wondering what type of dentist you should see. Root canals are a common dental procedure that is performed to treat infected or damaged dental pulp. While general dentists can perform root canals, there is a specialized type of dentist that is trained specifically in this area: endodontists.

What is an Endodontist?

An endodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of dental pulp and root canal problems. Endodontists are required to complete an additional two to three years of specialized training beyond dental school to become certified in this field. During this training, they learn advanced techniques for diagnosing and treating complex dental issues, including root canal procedures.

What is a Root Canal?

A root canal is a procedure that is performed to treat an infected or damaged dental pulp. Dental pulp is the soft tissue that is located in the center of a tooth. When the pulp becomes infected or inflamed, it can cause pain and other symptoms. During a root canal procedure, the endodontist will remove the infected or damaged pulp, clean out the root canals, and fill the canals with a special material to prevent further infection.

Why See an Endodontist for a Root Canal?

While general dentists are trained to perform root canal procedures, endodontists are specialists in this area and have a higher level of expertise. Endodontists are trained to diagnose and treat complex root canal issues, including cases that may require surgery or retreatment. Endodontists also have specialized equipment, such as microscopes and digital imaging technology, which can help them identify and treat even the smallest root canal issues.

When Should You See an Endodontist?

If you have been referred to an endodontist by your dentist or have been experiencing severe tooth pain, swelling, or sensitivity, you should schedule an appointment with an endodontist. The endodontist will perform a thorough examination and recommend the appropriate treatment, which may include a root canal procedure.

While general dentists can perform root canal procedures, endodontists are dental specialists who are specifically trained in this area. Endodontists have a higher level of expertise and specialized equipment to diagnose and treat complex root canal issues. If you have been told that you need a root canal, consider seeing an endodontist for the best possible care. With their advanced training and expertise, they can provide the necessary treatment to restore your dental health and alleviate your symptoms.

Why are dental crowns recommended after a root canal?

What is a Dental Crown?

Why Are Dental Crowns Recommended After a Root Canal?

  • Protect the Tooth: During a root canal, the infected or damaged pulp inside the tooth is removed, leaving the tooth hollow and weakened. Placing a dental crown over the tooth helps to protect it from further damage, including cracking or breaking. The crown provides a strong, protective barrier that helps to keep the tooth intact and functional.
  • Improve the Appearance: After a root canal, the tooth may appear discolored or slightly misshapen. Placing a dental crown over the tooth can help to improve its appearance, making it look more natural and aesthetically pleasing. Crowns can be customized to match the color and shape of the surrounding teeth, providing a seamless and natural-looking result.
  • Restore Functionality: Because a root canal can weaken the tooth, placing a dental crown over the tooth can help to restore its functionality. The crown helps to provide additional support and stability to the tooth, allowing it to function properly when chewing and biting.
  • Long-Term Durability: Dental crowns are designed to be durable and long-lasting. They can withstand the pressure and wear and tear of everyday use and can last for many years with proper care and maintenance. By placing a dental crown over a tooth that has undergone a root canal, patients can enjoy long-term protection and durability.
