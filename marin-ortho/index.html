The practice

Our team of Board Certified doctors, Dr. Jasmine Gorton, Dr. Christina Sola, and Dr. Bill Schmohl work together interchangeably on all of our patients, providing the San Francisco Bay Area with high-quality, innovative orthodontics and outstanding doctor availability.

Our use of advanced technology helps to shorten treatment time and makes the orthodontic process seamless for our patients. We are the top Invisalign® provider in the area and a leading expert on using Invisalign® for early treatment on growing children. We use the latest in digital orthodontic technology to give our patients a truly next level experience.

Our focus: Comfortable, patient-centered treatment plans that work with the body’s natural potential to achieve a “WOW Smile” .

Over the past 20 years, we have been able to consistently offer our patients the most modern treatment options in Marin County (See “ What Sets Us Apart ”), thanks to a strong commitment of our doctors to continuing education.
