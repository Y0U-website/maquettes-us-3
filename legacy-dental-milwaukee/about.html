Expertise in Dental Care

Let&#x27;s Meet the Doctors and the team!

JOON JAE PARK, DDS A 2016 honors graduate of the Marquette University Dental School, Dr. Park is dedicated to providing the highest quality comprehensive care to ensure that his patients have optimum oral health. To achieve this goal, he is committed to continuing education and lifelong learning.

Dr. Park, who moved to Wisconsin from South Korea in 2004 as an exchange student, is fluent in both English and Korean.

I graduated from MATC in 2003. Shortly after graduation, I joined Dr. Zabkowicz’s practice and have been proud to serve our patients for the past 23 years.

One of the most rewarding parts of my career has been building long-term relationships with patients and their families. Many of the people I care for today are individuals I’ve known since they were young, and it is a privilege to continue providing dental care throughout the different stages of their lives. I also enjoy educating patients about their dental health and making sure they feel comfortable and relaxed when they’re in the office.

Outside of the office, I enjoy spending time with my husband and our two dogs, Buck and Midge. You’ll often find me at our cabin up north, taking walks with my dogs, or trying out new restaurants. I am also a proud parent of two children who are currently attending college.

I joined Dr. Zabkowicz’s practice in 2022 and have loved being part of the dental field ever since. Since then, I have enjoyed my job and the opportunity to work with such wonderful patients every day. I am cross-trained in chairside assisting, front desk reception, and dental insurance, which allows me to help patients throughout their entire visit and answer questions along the way.

My favorite part of the job is getting to know our patients and building relationships with them over time. I also enjoy learning about and participating in different dental procedures, making every day a little different and exciting.

Outside of work, I enjoy bowling, spending time with friends and family, and traveling as much as I can.
