Dr. Scott Martin is a Wyckoff native, and opened Martin Orthodontics in October 1999. His mission is to provide exceptional orthodontic care to children and adults, and to make sure every patient has a fun and memorable experience while they build a smile to last a lifetime.

Dr. Martin studied chemistry as an undergraduate then continued his education in dental medicine and finished at the top of his class at the University of Medicine & Dentistry of New Jersey in 1996. Before opening his practice, he completed a three year orthodontic residency at Montefiore Medical Center in the Bronx, NY.
