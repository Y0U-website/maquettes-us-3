About Lilac Family Dental

General and Family Dentist in East Rochester

Lilac Family Dental is a general and cosmetic dentistry practice that will make your smile bloom with happiness. Our services include teeth whitening, same-day crowns, inlays and onlays, veneers, dental implants, tooth removal and immediate dentures, cleanings, exams and periodontal exams, and Invisalign invisible braces.

At Lilac Family Dental, our goal is to provide each patient with a comfortable dental experience in a relaxing and inviting environment. Every day we provide compassionate and friendly dentistry. Our patient-centered approach will put even the most anxious patients at ease.

Over 40-50 % of children will be affected by tooth decay before age five. Yet with with proper preventative care, such as regular cleanings, checkups, brushing & flossing, fluoride & dental sealants, the risk of dental disease can be greatly reduced.
