About Us Dr. Marc Morrell Approach Dr. Marc Morrell takes a personalized and patient-centric approach to orthodontic care. He believes in providing individualized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique needs and goals.

Driven by a passion for creating healthy and confident smiles, he ensures that every patient receives the highest level of care and attention throughout their orthodontic journey. No detail is too small.

Dr. Marc Morrell’s impressive credentials speak to his dedication and expertise in the field of orthodontics. He graduated at the top of his class from Rutgers University with a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree (DMD) and further pursued three years of specialized training in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics at Montefiore Medical Center as well as completing an additional dental residency, allowing for complex diagnosis and treatment planning of multidisciplinary orthodontic cases.

He achieved the esteemed status of Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, representing his unwavering commitment to orthodontic excellence. He is also a member of prestigious professional associations such as the American Dental Association, the American Association of Orthodontics, the Middle Atlantic Society of Orthodontists, New Jersey Dental Association, and OKU Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Morrell appreciates the beauty of outdoors and often finds solace at the beach. Staying active is also his priority, as he believes in maintaining a healthy and balanced lifestyle.

Inspiring Smiles Beyond Our Practice

We are committed to inspiring smiles beyond our practice. We are proud to partner with Smiles Changes Lives, an incredible community service organization dedicated to transforming lives through orthodontic care. By collaborating with Smiles Changes Lives, we actively participate in initiatives that bring confident smiles to those who need it most.

We are excited to extend our involvement beyond our practice into the community, schools, and social events. If you have a community initiative or event that could benefit from our support or sponsorship, please reach out to one of our team members. We look forward to working together to create brighter smiles and make a meaningful difference in our community!
