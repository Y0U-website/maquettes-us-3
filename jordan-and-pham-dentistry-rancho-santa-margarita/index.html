After preparing your tooth for restoration, we use a state-of-the-art digital 3-D camera to scan your mouth. This scan replaces the need for physical impressions.

Computer Assisted Design (CAD) software allows us to design every precise detail of your restoration with total accuracy. The software allows our team to customize the shape of the crown to fit your specific clinical needs.

Your exact design specifications are sent to our on-site milling machine to create your crown, milled from a single piece of porcelain. Your new crown is then fitted, polished, and bonded. ALL IN A SINGLE VISIT!!
