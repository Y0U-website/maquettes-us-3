Dr. Joseph

Dr. Spencer Joseph was born and raised in the Detroit Metropolitan area before leaving to obtain his Bachelor’s degree and Doctorate of Dental Surgery in Nashville TN at the Tennessee State University and the Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry.

Upon graduation, Dr Joseph completed a General Practice Residency at The Brooklyn Hospital Center in Fort Greene NY. Dr. Joseph then proudly and honorably served 4 years in the US Army providing dental care for our troops.

After completing his service, Dr. Joseph began his Orthodontic training in Washington DC at the illustrious Howard University College of Dentistry. Upon graduating Dr. Joseph began working side by side with his father Dr. Wayne Joseph who has two orthodontic practices in the Detroit area. He joined our team in Fall 2021.

Dr. Joseph has been married to his lovely wife Eboni for 2 years and together they have 2 beautiful children. In his free time, he loves to binge watch tv, kickboxing, and just being a Dad!
