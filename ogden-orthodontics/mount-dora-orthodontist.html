Braces, Aligners, and Honest Advice About Which One

Every option below is available at the Mount Dora office. Which one fits depends on your bite, your age, and your patience for rubber bands, and Dr. Ogden will tell you plainly which he would choose for his own family.

Traditional Metal Braces

Still the workhorse for complicated bites and for kids who would lose a removable aligner by Tuesday. Smaller and more comfortable than the ones parents remember.

Clear Braces

The same mechanics as metal with brackets that blend into the tooth. A frequent pick for teens and adults who want fixed treatment without the flash.

Invisalign and Clear Aligners

Removable, nearly invisible, and effective for many bites when worn as prescribed. Dr. Ogden will tell you honestly whether yours is one of them.

LightForce 3D-Printed Braces

Brackets printed for your teeth specifically, which typically means fewer visits and shorter appointments. Useful when your drive to Donnelly Street is not a short one.

Early Treatment for Kids

A first check around age seven, per AAO guidance. Usually the visit ends with a growth plan and no hardware. Monitoring visits are free.

Adult Treatment

Crowding that returned, a bite that never got fixed, or teeth you have quietly wanted to straighten for twenty years. It is not too late, and you will not be the only adult in the chair.

Do you offer Invisalign at the Mount Dora office?

Yes. Dr. Ogden treats with Invisalign and other clear aligners at all three offices, including Mount Dora. Aligners work well for many teens and adults, but not for every bite. At your consultation Dr. Ogden will tell you plainly whether aligners can get the result you want or whether braces would do the job better. Pricing, timelines, and what treatment looks like week to week are on our Invisalign in Mount Dora page .

What age should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first visit around age seven. That visit is usually a growth check, not a start date. Dr. Ogden monitors many Lake County kids for a year or two before recommending any treatment, and for some the recommendation is to wait. Growth checks at our office are free.

Where is your Mount Dora office?

We are at 2110 N Donnelly St, Suite 700, about a mile north of downtown Mount Dora. The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. Families reach us in a few minutes from Tavares and Eustis on US-441, and from Sorrento and Mount Plymouth on SR-46.

Do you treat adults?

Yes, and more of them every year. Adults from Mount Dora, Tavares, and Eustis come in for crowding that has crept back since their teenage braces, bites that make chewing uncomfortable, or teeth they have simply wanted to fix for decades. Clear aligners and low-profile braces make treatment easy to live with at any age.
