The practice

LSC dental hygiene students provide services including cleanings, x-rays, and fluoride treatments as part of their academic learning program. Additional treatment options (sealants, mouth guards, and whitening) are available. They are supervised by dental hygiene faculty.

LSC’s Dental Hygiene Clinic does not provide the following services: Exams, diagnosis or restorative services. Dental Hygiene Clinic visits are not intended to take the place of a dentist appointment.
