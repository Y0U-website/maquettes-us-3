The practice

Our team will take the time to consult with patients to pinpoint treatment options that will meet their specific needs. Those procedures will be executed in our comfortable office.

Dr. Rohr was born and raised here in Hastings and has had a passion for dentistry since he attended Hastings High School. Dr. Rohr was a 3 sport athlete playing Football, Baseball, and Wrestling.

Dr. Rohr currently serves as the head coach of the Hastings Wrestling Club where he coaches kids ages PreK-8th grade. Dr. Rohr s hobbies include exploring the outdoors especially being out on the rivers and lakes, coaching, and golfing.

He is very excited to meet every one of the great patients at Hastings Family Dental Care and continue to serve patients with the highest quality and compassionate care possible.
