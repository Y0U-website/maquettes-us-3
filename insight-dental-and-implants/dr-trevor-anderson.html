Dr. Trevor Anderson, DMD

Dr. Trevor Anderson brings a strong commitment to patient-centered general dentistry, combining hands-on care with a focus on building long-term relationships with patients and their families. Originally from Fort Smith, Arkansas, Dr. Anderson earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Central Arkansas and completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in May 2025.

After graduation, he began practicing in Oklahoma City, where he spent six months refining his skills in comprehensive general dentistry. Guided by the Golden Rule, treating every patient the way he would want to be treated, Dr. Anderson is dedicated to helping each patient optimize their oral health and achieve their personal dental goals in a comfortable, trusting environment.

Passion Beyond the Chair

Dr. Anderson believes great dentistry starts with connection. He prioritizes clear communication, trust, and personalized treatment planning so every patient feels heard, respected, and confident in their care.

Outside the office, Dr. Anderson enjoys restoring classic cars and spending quality time with his family—interests that reflect his patience, attention to detail, and appreciation for craftsmanship.
