Our custom titanium abutments are made from high-quality titanium, a material known for its strength, durability, and biocompatibility. These abutments are precision-milled using state-of-the-art technology to ensure a perfect fit and optimal support for the final restoration. With their excellent mechanical properties, titanium abutments provide long-lasting stability and reliability.

If you’re looking for a more aesthetically pleasing option, our custom zirconia abutments are an excellent choice. Zirconia is a ceramic material that offers exceptional strength and natural-looking aesthetics. Our zirconia abutments are carefully crafted to match the color and shape of the surrounding teeth, resulting in a seamless and beautiful smile.
