0 Emergency Dental Care at Green Valley Dental At Green Valley Dental we pride ourselves in putting patients first. No one can ever predict a dental emergency, but when it does happen, be assured that we will be ready for you. At your emergency visit, we will do our best to find you a same day appointment. Our flexible schedules and appointment times will allow for you to be seen in a timely manner…many days we can get you in immediately.

Because of the predictable unpredictability of an emergency, emergency appointments are designed to address your primary concern. A typical ememrgency visit is scheduled for a thirty minute appointment to access, evaluate and create an action plan but most of all to get you out of pain today. If our schedule permits we will provide treatment the same day.
