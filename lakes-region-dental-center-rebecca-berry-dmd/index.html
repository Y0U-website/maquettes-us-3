The practice

Many times there can be several solutions to a dental concern or problem. I will work with you to fully explain all possible options and help you make a fully informed decision on what is best for you and your overall health.

At our practice, your appointment time is reserved especially for you so you can receive the undivided attention of myself or our hygienists. We strive to provide a pleasant dental experience and take pride in getting to know our patients as individuals.

I look forward to meeting you and helping you reach your oral health goals!

Lakes Region Dental Center is the dental practice of Rebecca Berry, DMD located in Oakland, Maine which is conveniently located near Waterville. Dr. Berry performs all aspects of general dentistry including whitening, dental extractions, dental implants, crowns (caps), dentures, pediatric dentistry.
