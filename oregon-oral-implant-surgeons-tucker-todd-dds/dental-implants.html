What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are tiny titanium posts that are surgically inserted into the jawbone where teeth are missing. These posts act as tooth root substitutes. Once placed, the bone naturally bonds with the titanium in a process called osseointegration, establishing a solid foundation for artificial teeth commonly referred to as crowns or prostheses. Stable anchor points are created with small posts that emerge from the gums, allowing new teeth to be mounted securely.

Implants not only enhance your appearance and functionality but also help preserve facial structure by preventing bone deterioration that accompanies tooth loss.

While our Eugene-based oral surgeons handle the surgical components—implant placement, bone grafting, and extractions—your restorative dentist will design and place the final crown, bridge, or denture.

What Types Of Prostheses Are Available?

Depending on the number of teeth missing, patients have several prosthetic options:

Fixed Prosthesis: Permanently attached and can only be removed by a dentist

  • Single Prosthesis (Crown): Replaces one missing tooth and attaches to one implant.
  • Partial Prosthesis (Fixed Bridge): Replaces multiple teeth using two or more implants.
  • Complete Dental Prosthesis: For patients missing all upper or lower teeth; may be fixed or removable, depending on needs.
  • Removable Overdenture: Anchored with a bar or ball-and-socket attachment for easy removal.

Why Dental Implants?

Dental implants are not just a cosmetic enhancement—they’re a functional, permanent solution . Whether you’ve just lost a tooth or have been living without teeth for years, implants enable you to eat, speak, and smile without worry. They also help preserve your facial appearance by preventing jawbone shrinkage.

Dr. Per-Ingvar Brånemark, the Swedish scientist and orthopedic surgeon, introduced the concept more than 40 years ago. His innovation has since changed the lives of over a million people worldwide, offering them renewed comfort, self-confidence, and oral health.

Advantages Over Traditional Restorations

Why sacrifice healthy teeth to anchor a bridge when implants can stand independently? Traditional dentures can slip, cause discomfort, and may need to be removed at night. Implants eliminate these concerns, delivering superior stability, comfort, and aesthetics .

What Type of Anesthesia Is Used?

Most dental implants and associated procedures can be performed in-office using local anesthesia. General anesthesia or sedation is also available based on your comfort level or health history.

Post-Implant Care & Maintenance

Once in place, your dental implants can last for decades with proper care. That includes daily brushing, flossing, and regular visits to your dentist. Maintaining good oral hygiene is key to long-term implant success .
