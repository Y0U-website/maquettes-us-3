The Timeline

Phase 1 treatment typically lasts 9 to 12 months. After that, most children enter a resting phase where they wear a retainer and are monitored while any remaining permanent teeth come in. When it&#x27;s time, Phase 2 treatment begins. Because of the work done in Phase 1, Phase 2 is often shorter and less complex.
