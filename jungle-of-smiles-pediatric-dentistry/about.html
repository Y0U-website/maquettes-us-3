About Jungle of Smiles: Sarah I Mathias, DDS, MS

At Jungle of Smiles, we’ve created a safari-themed atmosphere that helps kids feel excited and entertained during their visit. Our board-certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Sarah, and her team offer safe and effective treatments that utilize the latest techniques in children’s dentistry.

We are proud to be the premier provider of pediatric dental care serving Laguna Hills, CA, and the surrounding Orange County area. Our team of dedicated dental professionals is committed to making every visit a fun and stress-free experience for your little ones.

Meet Dr. Sarah

I have always had a connection with children, which is why I chose to specialize in pediatric dentistry. Prior to becoming a dentist, I worked as a camp counselor and nanny. The honesty of children, both the good and bad, never fails to surprise me. It’s been an incredible journey to see my little patients grow into young adults, head to college, and sometimes return to the practice with their own children.
