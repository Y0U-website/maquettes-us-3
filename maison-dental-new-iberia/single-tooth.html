Single-Tooth Implant in…

  • Implant placement: The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Sedation dentistry options are available for patients who prefer additional comfort during this visit.
  • Osseointegration: Over the following three to six months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone. A temporary restoration may be placed during this healing period.
  • Abutment placement: Once integration is confirmed, the connector piece that links the implant post to the crown is attached.
  • Crown delivery: A custom ceramic or porcelain crown is fabricated to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth and permanently secured to the abutment.

Caring for a Single-Tooth Implant

One of the most appealing qualities of a single-tooth implant is how naturally it fits into a daily oral hygiene routine. It is brushed and flossed just like a natural tooth, with no special removal or adhesives required. Maintaining consistent general dentistry checkups allows our team to monitor the health of the implant, the surrounding bone, and the gum tissue to catch any concerns early.

Implants are durable, but they are not immune to damage from excessive force. Patients who grind their teeth are advised to discuss a nightguard to protect the restoration. With proper care, a single-tooth implant can last a lifetime, making it one of the most cost-effective tooth replacement options available over the long term.
