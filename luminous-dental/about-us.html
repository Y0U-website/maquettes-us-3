Get To Know Dr. Leah Divito

Dr. Leah Divito grew up in Boardman, OH. She developed an interest in dentistry at an early age when she got her braces off and saw a straight, bright smile. She couldn’t believe the impact straightening her teeth could have on her overall confidence and well-being. That’s when she knew she wanted to help others feel the same way. Because she excelled at both math and science and loved the creativity of art, dentistry was the perfect fit. Now, she can combine her talents with artistry and science to improve people’s lives — one smile at a time!

Dr. Divito received her bachelor’s degree from John Carroll University and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Ohio State University College of Dentistry. After completing a general residency at St. Elizabeth’s Hospital in Youngstown, OH, Dr. Divito began her career in public health.

While she loved the impact she made in people’s lives, she realized that she could flourish and grow as a dentist in her own practice. Dr. Divito regularly takes advanced training courses in Invisalign, implant placement, Botox, and cosmetic restorations.

When she isn’t caring for patients, Dr. Divito loves to read, bake, and explore the outdoors with her husband and their daughters.

Helping Girls Explore Careers in Science

Community Outreach Partnership

When Dr. Divito was in 7th grade, she won a scholarship to B-Wiser Science camp. She spent a week with other girls at The College of Wooster, where she was exposed to all areas of science through the counselors, who were women in college pursuing careers in STEM. Dr. Divito believes that having the opportunity to spend a week with like-minded girls being led by women in science, gave her the confidence to explore a career in science too. To help other girls pursue their dream, Dr. Divito offers scholarships to 7th-grade girls to attend the B-Wiser Science camp.
