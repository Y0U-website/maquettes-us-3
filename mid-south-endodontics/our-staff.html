Dr. Michael Lowery

Michael Lowery, D.M.D.

Dr. Michael Lowery has been in solo practice in Southaven, MS since 2006. After realizing that there was a need for an Endodontic practice in Germantown, TN he took a leap of faith and opened Mid South Endodontics in April of 2019.

Originally from Tupelo, MS, Dr. Lowery attended college at Birmingham-Southern College in Birmingham, AL where he graduated with a B.S. in Business then Dr. Lowery attended The Else School of Business at Millsaps College where he received his Masters of Business Administration. After business school he was accepted into The University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry where he graduated in the top 5 % of his class. Following dental school, Dr. Lowery was immediately accepted into the Endodontic Program at Tufts University in Boston, MA.

While in his residency, Dr. Lowery was thoroughly trained in non-surgical endodontics with a strong emphasis in microscopic endodontic therapy

Upon graduation from Tufts University, Dr. Lowery achieved Diplomate status becoming a Board Certified Endodontist.
