New and Returning Patients

✓ Making an Appointment

Scheduling is easy—just call (610) 444-8744 and our friendly team will assist you. If you need to cancel, please give 24 hours’ notice so we can accommodate others.

✓ Initial Examination

Your first visit lasts about an hour. We’ll review your health, clean your teeth, and provide personalized recommendations to help you care for your smile and improve your oral health.

✓ Cancellation Policy

We understand that life happens! If you need to cancel, please give us 24 hours’ notice. No-shows and late cancellations are subject to a fee of $55.00 for the hygiene side and $100.00 for the doctor’s chair

For Best Results: Stop by Every Six Months

Experience personalized care, advanced dental services, and a warm, welcoming community—where your smile, comfort, and connection truly come first at every visit. We’re here to make every step of your dental journey easy and enjoyable.

Getting your teeth cleaned every six months helps prevent cavities and gum disease, removes buildup brushing can’t, and allows us to catch issues early—keeping your smile healthy, fresh, and bright.

We offer cosmetic services like veneers and professional teeth whitening to help enhance your smile. These safe, effective treatments can improve confidence and deliver noticeable, natural-looking results in just a few visits.

Bring a Rooting to Our Propagation Station

Our propagation station is a fun way to grow connections—literally. Patients are invited to bring plant clippings, root new ones, and take one home, creating a shared, green space for our community.
