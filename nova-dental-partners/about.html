About Nova Dental Partners

Welcome to Nova Dental Partners, an exceptional family , cosmetic , and implant dental practice in Alexandria, Woodbridge and Fairfax, Virginia. Our skilled dental team provides patients with a lifetime of healthy, confident, and beautiful smiles. Our compassionate providers blend art with science to create beautiful smiles.

Excellence in dental care is the foundation of Nova Dental Partners, as patients’ beautiful smiles are our top priority. Our experts assist them in achieving and maintaining long-term dental health by personalizing superior treatments.

At Nova Dental Partners, we provide gentle care to patients of all ages using modern dentistry and the latest technological advancements.

Excellence in dental care is the foundation of Nova Dental Partners. Our strong family values set us apart, as we treat patients like family with care, kindness, and respect. Our skilled providers also volunteer, providing community outreach for Mission of Mercy (MOM) projects in underserved areas of Virginia to address the dental needs of the community.

We welcome new and existing patients to Nova Dental Partners. Schedule an evaluation by calling our office or booking an appointment online today.
