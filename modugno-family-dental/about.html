Always Growing

At Modugno Family Dental, we are continuously evolving to meet the needs of our patients. Dr. Justin Modugno and his dedicated team are committed to providing consistent, personalized care that grows with you. Unlike corporate dental offices, where you may see a different provider each time, we pride ourselves on offering a consistent, one-on-one experience with the same trusted provider at every visit. This personalized approach allows us to develop lasting relationships and provide more tailored treatments, ensuring that your unique dental needs are always met.

Proud Locals

As a Hoover High School graduate, Dr. Modugno has deep ties to the North Canton community and a personal understanding of what it means to grow up here. Our practice reflects the values of personalized, family-friendly care, which sets us apart from corporate offices. We focus on familiar faces and a comforting environment, ensuring every patient, from children to seniors, receives the highest quality dental care with the utmost respect for their individual needs.
