Meet the Doctor

Dr. Scott A. Martinka

Modern dentistry is ever-evolving, and new technology is constantly shaping the field as we know it. Dr. Scott Martinka takes pride in knowing that his practice provides the highest level of dental care in his hometown of Willmar, MN.

After graduating from Willmar High School, Dr. Scott Martinka earned his bachelor’s degree at St. John’s University. He completed his studies at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. Dr. Martinka has an extensive background in dental technology, including CEREC® CAD/CAM technology, digital cone beam computed tomography, and 3D imaging. His background and passion for dental technology have transformed the way he performs cleanings, fillings, dental implants, and other traditional dental practices.

Today, Dr. Scott is happy to be back in his hometown practicing dentistry and serving his community. He’s married with two young children and enjoys cooking, playing hockey and golf, and spending time with his family at the lake.
