Welcome to O2 Dental Group — Fayetteville’s Patient-First Dental Practice

The practice

Fayetteville is one of the most diverse cities in North Carolina, home to military families, long-time residents, and a vibrant Hispanic community. Our team reflects that diversity: we have bilingual doctors and staff who speak both English and Spanish, and we’re proud to serve every patient with the same level of care and respect. ¡Se habla español!

And if dental anxiety has kept you away from the dentist — you are not alone, and you are absolutely welcome here. We see patients who haven’t been in years, and we approach every visit without judgment, at your pace, with a team that genuinely cares about how you feel.

O2 Dental Group on Skibo Road is one of the most conveniently located dental practices for active-duty service members, veterans, and military families near Fort Bragg. Dr. Julio Morales served with the U.S. Army’s 82nd Airborne Division right here in Fayetteville before earning his dental degree — he understands military life from the inside out.

We welcome Tricare and offer flexible scheduling designed to work with demanding military schedules. If you’re relocating with the military, we make it easy to transfer your dental records and get established as a new patient quickly — our team has helped hundreds of military families get connected with great dental care from day one of their assignment.

Beyond the post, we serve patients from across Fayetteville and the surrounding region — Haymount, Cross Creek, Westover Hills, Bordeaux, Cliffdale, and Kings Grant — as well as Hope Mills, Spring Lake, Raeford, Eastover, Stedman, Gray’s Creek, and all of Cumberland and Hoke counties, easily reached via All American Freeway, US-401, Raeford Road, Cliffdale Road, Bragg Boulevard, Ramsey Street…

Tooth trouble never picks a good moment, so we keep room on the books. Call the Skibo Road office and we’ll aim for the same day — open Monday through Thursday until 6:00 PM and Fridays until 4:00, which is more runway than most practices in town give you.

Most major plans work here, TRICARE included for the military families we see every day. Uninsured? The O2 Advantage Plan drops the price on routine and repair work with no deductible and no nasty surprises, and there’s monthly financing for the bigger jobs.

Our doctors and staff include native Spanish speakers, so the conversation stays clear in whichever language fits your family. Serving one of the most mixed communities in the state is something we’re proud of.

Les soins

Checkups, cosmetic , restorative, emergency, kids’ visits, specialty work — it all lives on Skibo Road. Nothing gets punted across town and nobody has to chase records between offices; your care simply stays put from the first appointment forward.

We put money into the tools that earn it: crowns finished in a single sitting, cone-beam 3D scans, digital denture workflows, and decay checks with no radiation. Clearer answers, quicker fixes, fewer return trips.

At O2 Fayetteville, implant evaluation begins with on-site 3D cone beam CT imaging — the modality that accurately maps bone volume, density, and three-dimensional anatomy before any treatment is planned. Patients told elsewhere that they aren’t candidates…

Straighter teeth without the look of metal brackets — Invisalign is a discreet way to fix crowding, spacing, and bite concerns for both adults and teens. O2 Dental Group of Fayetteville is an Invisalign provider and offers orthodontic evaluations.
