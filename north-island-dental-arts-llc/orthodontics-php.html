How do I decide between braces and other teeth straightening procedures or methods?

The best way to choose between braces and other teeth straightening processes is to schedule your appointment with our experts as soon as possible. How long does it take to straighten your teeth with braces?

The treatment time can vary from person to person. Some people need braces for as little as 1 year while some need to wear braces for up to around 2 years. It all depends on the severity of the misalignment of your teeth. When you come in for your appointment, we will provide you with our comprehensive assessment and let you know our recommendation for your smile goals.

Do I need orthodontics if my teeth are straight but there is an issue with my bite?

Orthodontics can correct issues that are not necessarily crooked teeth including overbites and under bites. Healthy bites eliminate lots of problems like jaw pain later in life. If you are concerned that you have a bad bite, it is important to speak to one of our specialists and set up an appointment for you to come in to our Long Island office in New Hyde Park. We will assess how your bite needs to be corrected and give you the best treatment options available.

There are thousands of adults who are now seeking to correct bite issues and problems with their smile that were never addressed earlier in life.
