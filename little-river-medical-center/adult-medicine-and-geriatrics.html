Geriatric care for older adults

Care changes as we age. For patients 65 and older, we focus on managing more than one condition at a time, reviewing medications to avoid harmful interactions, watching for changes in memory and mobility, and completing the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit that keeps your care plan current. If you or a parent needs help staying healthy and independent, we build care around that goal.

Medicare Annual Wellness Visit

If you are a new Medicare enrollee, you are entitled to a “Welcome to Medicare” visit to meet with your health care provider to discuss and update your personal care plan. There is no charge for this visit and the Part B deductible does not apply.

Some of the topics addressed during this visit:

This visit must take place in the first 12 months you are enrolled in Medicare Part B.

As a Medicare beneficiary you receive this benefit each year and it is classified differently from your annual physical. Your subsequent wellness visit appointments will cover topics that your provider established as a baseline during your Welcome to Medicare visit.

  • A review of your medical and family history
  • Height, weight, and blood pressure measurements
  • A review of necessary screenings, vaccinations or other possible tests needed
  • A simple vision test
  • A review of falls risks and safety
  • Screenings for hearing impairments, dementia and depression
  • Discussion of advanced directives

Chronic Care Management

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need insurance to see a primary care doctor at LRMC?

No. We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and most commercial plans, and we see uninsured patients on a sliding fee scale based on household size and income.

Where do you offer primary care in Horry County?

At our Little River, Loris, Carolina Forest, Aynor, South Strand, and Myrtle Beach locations. Call 843-663-8000 to find the one closest to you.

What is a Medicare Annual Wellness Visit?

It is a yearly visit covered by Medicare Part B to review your health, update your care plan, and set prevention goals. It is not the same as a head-to-toe physical, and our team can walk you through what it includes.

Can I get all my care in one place?

Yes. Medical, dental, behavioral health, and pharmacy services are part of the same organization, so your care is coordinated.
