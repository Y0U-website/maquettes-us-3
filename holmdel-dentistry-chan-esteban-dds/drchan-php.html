Meet Dr. Esteban Chan

Your General, Cosmetic, And Emergency Dentist In Holmdel

Dr. Esteban Chan grew up in Edison, New Jersey and graduated from John P. Stevens High School. He graduated from Rutgers University, Cook College, where he majored in Biology and Nutrition. His keen interest in Science solidified his desire to become a dentist. He was especially motivated by his family and friends.

Dr. Chan completed his dental schooling and training at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He is a member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and the Chinese Dental Association. Dr. Chan is also a proud member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and is working on obtaining his fellowship with the AAID, as well.

Dr. Chan prides himself in being meticulous about his work, because ultimately, his patients’ confidence and comfort are what matters most. He understands how a person’s self-esteem can be transformed through quality dentistry.

As the dentistry field changes through improvements in technology and innovations, Dr. Chan maintains current through constantly attending training seminars. He is constantly looking for ways to improve his patients’ comfort and success in dentistry.

In his spare time, Dr. Chan enjoys snowboarding, cycling, traveling and visiting with his friends. He loves to spend time with his 3 nephews, attending their sporting events and school activities. Since becoming a father, he cherishes every minute spent with his 2 sons. He looks forwards to when his sons can join him to play golf, his favorite sport.
