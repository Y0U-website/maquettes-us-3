The practice

Periodontal care is also known as periodontal maintenance or deep cleaning. It involves the process of thoroughly cleaning the teeth to prevent the development of periodontal disease.

A dental filling is a procedure where a dentist removes decayed tooth material and fills the cavity with a restorative substance, such as composite, amalgam, or gold, to restore tooth function.

Crowns and bridges restore damaged or missing teeth, improving function and appearance. Crowns cover weakened teeth, while bridges replace one or more teeth.

Botox in dentistry is used to treat TMJ disorders, bruxism, and facial pain. It relaxes muscles, reduces clenching, and enhances aesthetics by smoothing wrinkles around the mouth and jawline.

Clear aligners are transparent, removable dental devices used to straighten teeth. They gradually shift teeth into proper alignment without the visibility of traditional braces.

Les soins

Tooth extraction is the removal of tooth from its socket in the bone. It is usually performed due to tooth decay, infection, or crowding, and can be done surgically or non-surgically.

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth’s root, providing support for a crown, bridge, or denture, ensuring stability and function.
