Your First Visit

Children welcome!

Dr. Freedman strongly believes that a child should have their first dental examination in our practice by age one. This gives us the opportunity to evaluate their teeth, provide information on proper diet and home care, and help the child develop a sense of comfort with our practice and staff. These visits are fun visits. We allow the child to play with the equipment, meet the staff, and receive a present.
