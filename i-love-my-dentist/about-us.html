Dr. Krizner-Meza

Dr. Melissa Krizner-Meza, affectionately known as Dr. K, is originally from Pittsburgh and grew up in South Florida. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Communications from the University of Miami before spending ten years working in the hospitality industry. Inspired to pursue a career in healthcare, Dr. K completed post-baccalaureate studies at Florida Atlantic University before obtaining her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

The University of Florida also happens to be the alma mater of Dr. Ramkissoon and Dr. Weiss, and the three even shared some of the same professors during their training.

What Dr. K enjoys most about dentistry is meeting new people and building meaningful, long-term relationships with patients and their families. As she puts it, I love that dentistry connects me with people and communities. It provides me a way to give back.

One of the qualities that truly stands out about Dr. K is her strong commitment to serving others. She is passionate about giving back to the community and has dedicated time to providing dental care to underserved populations through organisations such as the WeCare Clinic in Gainesville, Mission of Mercy, FIMRC Medical Mission to Peru, Remote Access Medical, AAWD, Super Sealant Saturday Dental Children’s Program, and the Summer Health Professions Education Program.

Outside of the practice, Dr. K has a creative side and enjoys all types of crafting, especially knitting. She also loves spending time outdoors with her husband and their two dogs, Cora and Lola Rose.

Lisa

Lisa is the lead hygienist and a certified registered dental hygienist (CRDH) at I Love My Dentist. In her role, Lisa provides thorough dental cleanings, scaling and root planing, and periodontal care. She is passionate about helping patients improve their oral health while feeling comfortable and confident every step of the way.

Lisa completed her dental hygiene education at South Florida State College in Avon Park, Florida, after completing her prerequisites at Polk State College in Winter Haven. Her dental career began as a dental assistant from 1999 2001, and after completing hygiene school in 2007, she practiced in Winter Haven, Florida for over 16 years. Lisa joined the I Love My Dentist team in August 2023, where she continues to bring both skill and warmth to every appointment.

What Lisa enjoys most is getting to know her patients and coworkers on a personal level and helping each person leave the office feeling healthier, refreshed, and proud of their smile. Her passion for dentistry began early, inspired by her own orthodontic journey and the life-changing results she experienced.

Outside of work, Lisa loves traveling and spending time outdoors, especially kayaking, boating, and soaking up time at the beach. Fun fact: before becoming a dental hygienist, she wore many hats, including working as a veterinary assistant, a patient care assistant in a nursing home, and even a realtor.
