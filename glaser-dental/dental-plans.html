Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as dental insurance?

No.

This is a membership program offered directly by our office. It does not involve an insurance company, premiums, claims, or reimbursement — just a flat annual fee paid to Glaser Dental.

Can I use this program along with my dental insurance?

No.

This program cannot be combined with dental insurance or any other discount or promotional offer. You may choose to use either your insurance benefits or your Wellness Program membership for a given visit, whichever works best for you.

Does the discount apply to things like toothpaste or mouthwash I buy at the office?

No.

The membership discount applies only to restorative treatment.
