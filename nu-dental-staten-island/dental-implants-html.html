What Are Dental Implants?

These prosthetic tooth replacements are composed of small cylindrical posts made from titanium a material that is designed to integrate with organic bone and tissue. After they are inserted into the jaw via oral surgery, they become part of the bone through a process called osseointegration. Once they’re placed, implants are strong enough to hold dental restorations entirely on their own, just like real tooth roots hold natural teeth.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are more complex than other tooth replacements, so getting them often takes a few months. That said, you needn’t worry; these posts are worth the extra time and effort! Dr. G himself performs the implant treatment in-house, having placed over 2,000 dental implants in his career. You can trust him to make your restored smile look and feel amazing! In fact, he and our team will oversee your consultation, implant surgery, osseointegration, and delivery of the final restoration.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

You’ll consult Dr. G as you start your dental implant process. Through this talk, he’ll learn about your treatment goals, oral health background, and similar key factors. This feedback helps us see if dental implants suit you, allowing us to tailor your treatment plan accordingly.

If you don’t qualify for dental implants at first, don’t worry. You can become a good candidate later through preliminary dental work tooth extractions, bone grafts, sinus lifts, etc. Such procedures would strengthen your mouth and jaw enough to handle implant posts.

Dental Implant Surgery

You’ll undergo dental implant surgery if your consultation goes well. At that point, we’ll continue to handle your treatment instead of referring you to outside specialists. This approach ensures you (and your smile) benefit from Dr. G’s extensive dental implant experience.

The actual dental implant surgery will involve the following:

  • Anesthesia Dr. G will numb your mouth with anesthesia before surgery starts. That way, the placement work will remain painless; it won’t cause any aches or pains.
  • Minor Incisions Once your anesthesia kicks in, we’ll make an incision in your gums. This cut will make room in your mouth for your implant post.
  • Implant Placement Dr. G will use advanced tools and methods to place your implant post, ensuring your new tooth looks and works wonderfully.
  • Gum Closure We’ll quickly (and safely) close your gum incision after the placement. From there, Dr. G will set a cap over your new implant.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Shortly after your surgery, your implant post will start fusing with nearby bone tissue. This fusion process osseointegration lasts 4-6 months and helps your new tooth take root in your jaw. To that extent, it makes your dental implant a permanent part of your mouth.

You’ll receive an additional surgery once your implant fuses. For this procedure, we’ll place an abutment a small metal connector on your base post. Lab workers will then craft your final restoration while you recover from the abutment work.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

At the end of treatment, you’ll receive your final restoration. This prosthetic will vary based on how many teeth you lost or had replaced. Depending on the final number, it could be a dental crown, a dental bridge, or even an implant denture.

It won’t take us long to fit your final restoration. Once we receive it from the dental lab, we’ll apply special cement to your abutment, set the device, and make some final adjustments. You should then be ready to enjoy your restored smile!

Dental Implant FAQs

Can I Take Dental Implants Out?

No, you cannot take dental implants out yourself. They are designed to merge with the jawbone and essentially become a part of your own anatomy. Once this fusion process is complete, only an implant dentist on Staten Island can remove a dental implant. That being said, if you’re getting an implant denture, you can choose to have your prosthetic fixed in place or be removable for cleaning purposes.

Do Dental Implants Feel Natural?

Yes! After your implants have gone through the osseointegration process, they will feel exactly like the roots of your real teeth.

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Decades of clinical research have proven dental implants to be an effective and safe tooth replacement, provided they are placed by a trained professional. Even after 10+ years, more than 95 % of dental implant patients are still enjoying their new teeth to the fullest.

The only thing to keep in mind is that patients with certain health conditions, such as low jawbone density, gum disease, diabetes, or cancer, may have an increased risk of complications after dental implant surgery. That’s why Dr. G will be sure to discuss your medical history and how we can reduce your risks beforehand.

Can Dental Implants Get Cavities?

Unlike real tooth enamel, the materials that dental implants are made from do not break down when exposed to acids produced by harmful oral bacteria. As a result, dental implants cannot get cavities. However, you’ll still need to brush and floss them; poor oral hygiene can lead to gum disease, which may cause your implants to fail.

Are Dental Implants an Eligible FSA Expense?

Because dental implants are usually considered a restorative solution that improves your oral health, they are often eligible for FSA reimbursement. However, most FSAs place a limit on the amount of money that you can use each year. Our team can look over the details of your FSA and help you find ways to make dental implants on Staten Island affordable for you.
