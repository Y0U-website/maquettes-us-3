Dr. Stephanie Ness

Dr. Stephanie is originally from Beaverton, Oregon, which is a suburb of Portland. She graduated from Seattle Pacific University with her Bachelor’s in Biology and a minor in Chemistry before moving on to pursue her Doctor of Dental Medicine at Oregon Health and Science University. She is currently a member of the American Dental Association, the North Dakota Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry. Her favorite part of her work is meeting new people and being a part of such a great community.

Dr. Stephanie actually married Dr. Alex two days after their dental school graduation! She also has plenty of family on the West Coast. Outside of the office, her hobbies include cooking, traveling, cross-stitching, and singing with the Minot Chamber Chorale.
