Cosmetic Dentistry

We can work with a patient’s request to improve the appearance of their smile, gums and/or bite. This is done by addressing their concerns in color, position, shape, size, alignment, or overall smile appearance.

Teeth Whitening Designed for You

At Northside Family Dental, we’re here to help you get the smile you’ve been dreaming of. We offer several whitening options and will work with you to find the one that fits your goals. Our team is happy to walk you through each choice and help you feel confident every step of the way.

You’ll see us for one session (75-90 minutes long) and one of our hygienists do the whitening in the office. Then, you will do some follow-up at whitening at home for 4 days following this treatment. We will show you what todo and send you home with everything you need. This way you see instant results and have less to do at home.

Opalescence Go This ready-to-use option is perfect for anyone looking for a fast and easy whitening boost. No impressions, no molds just pre-filled, disposable trays you wear for 30 60 minutes a day over 10 days. Pop them in, brighten up, and toss them when you’re done!

Opalescence PF We will make you custom trays designed specifically for your mouth and send you home with eight syringes of Opalescence PF whitening gel. You will be able to keep and reuse the trays for years to come and purchase touchup syringes whenever you need them. Bleach Pens This is an affordable and portable whitening solution that can be used independently or as a complement to trays/in-office whitening.

Teeth Straightening with SureSmile

SureSmile aligners offer a comfortable, low-key way to straighten your teeth no wires, just a clear, custom-fit solution designed to work with your life. From your first fitting to your final results, we’ll be here to guide you every step of the way.
