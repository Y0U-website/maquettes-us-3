How Many Hours Per Day Should I Wear Clear Aligners?

For the fastest and most predictable Invisalign treatment results, wear your clear aligners 20 to 22 hours per day.

The 22-Hour Standard

Align Technology, the maker of Invisalign clear aligners, recommends 20 to 22 hours of daily wear for predictable orthodontic treatment results. Wear your aligners at all times, except while eating, drinking (anything other than cool water), brushing, or flossing.

How Patient Compliance Affects Tracking

Per the American Association of Orthodontists , teeth move through bone only when continuous, gentle force is applied to the periodontal ligament, the connective tissue that anchors each tooth to the jaw. Remove your aligners for extended periods and your teeth drift back, the aligners lose tracking (their precise fit against your teeth), and your Invisalign treatment timeline extends. For the full biology, read Dr. McComb’s article on consistent aligner wear and tooth movement .

No Food Restrictions with Removable Aligners

The best part of Invisalign clear aligners is no food restrictions. You can enjoy the foodie scene across Culver City, West Hollywood, and Santa Monica without worrying about breaking a metal bracket. Simply remove your aligners, enjoy your meal, brush, and put them back in.

Can I eat popcorn, nuts, and sticky foods with Invisalign?

Yes. Because Invisalign clear aligners are fully removable, there are no dietary restrictions. Unlike traditional metal braces, which prohibit crunchy, sticky, and hard foods, you can eat whatever you want. The only rule: remove your aligners before eating anything, and rinse or brush before putting them back in. Per Align Technology’s clinical guidelines, eating with aligners in can stain the plastic and trap sugar against your enamel, accelerating cavity risk.

Will Invisalign affect my speech? Will I have a lisp?

You may notice a slight lisp for the first 24 to 48 hours as your tongue adjusts to wearing Invisalign clear aligners. This is one of the shortest adjustment periods of any orthodontic treatment.

Adapting to Invisalign Clear Aligners

This is temporary. Most patients adapt completely within a day or two of wearing their aligners. Consistency helps — the more consistently you wear them, the faster your tongue recalibrates.
