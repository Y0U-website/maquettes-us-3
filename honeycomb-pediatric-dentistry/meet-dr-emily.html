Meet Dr. Emily Rockne, Pediatric Dentist in Ozark, MO

A pediatric dentist who believes every child deserves to feel safe, seen, and cared for.

A calling that started with kids

Dr. Emily Rockne has always had a natural connection with children. That connection led her to pediatric dentistry — a specialty that requires additional training beyond dental school, focused entirely on the unique needs of infants, children, and teens.

Specialized pediatric training

After dental school, Dr. Emily completed advanced pediatric training and served as a dentist in the U.S. Navy — an experience that shaped her calm, prepared, and detail-oriented approach to care.

A local family, rooted in the Ozarks

Dr. Emily lives in the Springfield/Ozark area with her husband and three children. When she&#x27;s not caring for little smiles, you&#x27;ll find her hiking, camping, swimming, and soaking up outdoor adventures with her family.

Why Honeycomb

Dr. Emily created Honeycomb to be a nurturing place where kids feel safe, welcomed, and free to be playful — and where parents feel truly heard. Every detail, from the space to the language we use, is designed with your child in mind.

"People matter most. When kids feel loved and parents feel informed, everything else falls into place."
