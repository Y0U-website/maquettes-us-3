How often do I need my teeth cleaned?

We recommend having your teeth cleaned twice a year.

Is an adult too old for orthodontics?

You are never too old for orthodontics! There is a variety of new teeth adjustment techniques available to day.

Do you take payment plans?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express. We even have additional financing available through Care Credit.

What can facial fillers do?

A lip enhancer can increase the volume of your lips which will make the lines around your mouth disappear. Facial fillers are a beautiful complement to your teeth and smile.

Do you offer teeth bleaching?

Yes! We offer various bleaching techniques including at-home and in-office whitening.

What is a cosmetic Lumineer?

Cosmetic Lumineer are a beautiful, less invasive veneer.

Do you see children? At what age?

Yes, children can be seen as early as 2 years old. We recommend you bring your child in during your annual tooth cleaning so they can meet our hygienist.

What type of X-Rays and techniques do you offer?

We use the most updated techniques including digital X-Rays.
