Free Consultations

At Gulf Coast Orthodontics, we offer a complimentary first consultation. This initial visit includes digital photographs of your mouth and 3D X-rays to thoroughly assess what lies beneath your smile. Following a detailed oral exam, our orthodontist will provide an initial diagnosis and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to optimally enhance your smile.

Custom Braces

Dr. Laboda and Dr. Brao at Gulf Coast Orthodontics are dedicated to making your orthodontic experience as comfortable and efficient as possible. Utilizing the latest advancements in custom designed braces and clear aligner technology, we ensure your treatment feels effortless and effective.

Flexible Payment Options

We offer a variety of financing options designed to accommodate your budget. This includes in-house 0 % payment plans, and discounts for payments made in full and multiple family members.

Additionally, we accept dental insurance. Simply provide your insurance details before your first visit, and we’ll verify your orthodontic benefits in advance. If your policy includes orthodontic coverage, we’ll handle the filing of any claims on your behalf.
