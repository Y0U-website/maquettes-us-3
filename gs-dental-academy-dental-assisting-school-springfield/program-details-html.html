​ Tuition

The subjects below are covered in depth from both a didactic and a practical clinical experience. The morning lecture is followed by an afternoon of clinical, “hands on” training in our state-of-the-art real life practicing dental office. During the course of program, the students learn the operation of all dental equipment, instrumentation, and materials commonly used in a general dental practice. Our program covers all aspects of Dental Assisting and Dental Radiography..

The tuition covers all costs for the course as outlined below:

We offer several methods for tuition financing. Please call our office at 973-988-0008, for more details.

  • Textbooks.
  • All training and visual aids, materials, and dental supplies used in the clinical training.
  • There are no hidden costs or expenses once you get started.
  • Dental Radiography training in accordance with the New Jersey State regulations.
  • Training in all phases of General Dentistry: Including Endodontics, Crown & Bridge, Cosmetic Bonding, Amalgam Restorations, Impressions, Periodontics, 4-handed dentistry, front desk.
  • Real-Life dental clinical experience.
  • All training is done by highly trained dental professionals in a real life practicing dental office..
  • Upon graduating from the program, a Certificate of Completion in Dental Assisting, and Dental Radiography will be awarded.
