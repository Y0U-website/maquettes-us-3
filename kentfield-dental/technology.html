The technology behind better dentistry

We invest in the latest dental technology so your care is more precise, more comfortable, and often done in a single visit. Explore each one — and how it compares to the old-school way.

Our dental technology

Same-Day Crowns CEREC lets us design, mill and place a permanent ceramic crown in one visit — no goopy molds, no temporary, and no waiting weeks for an outside lab.

3D Printing We print your nightguards, retainers, surgical guides and temporaries right here in our Kentfield office, often the same day, instead of mailing your case off to an outside lab.

3D CT Imaging A low-dose 3D scan that shows your jaw in full — bone, nerves, sinuses and roots — so we can plan your care with precision instead of guesswork.

Handheld X-Rays A portable, low-dose X-ray taken right at your chair, so your images are faster, more comfortable, and Dr. G’s team can stay in the room with you.

Digital X-Rays Sharp, instant x-rays that appear on the screen in seconds — with notably less radiation than the old film many offices still use.

Intraoral Scanner A small wand builds a precise 3D model of your mouth in minutes — with no goopy putty and far less to trigger gagging.

Intraoral Cameras A tiny camera that puts a close-up photo of your own tooth right on the screen, so you can see exactly what we see and decide with us instead of just taking our word for it.

Cloud Records Your charts, x-rays and scheduling live on secure, encrypted cloud software — backed up automatically, accessible when your care needs it, and safer than a paper file or an aging office server.

Comfort & Sedation From the quiet of our electric handpieces to three levels of sedation, every detail is designed to keep your visit calm and comfortable.
