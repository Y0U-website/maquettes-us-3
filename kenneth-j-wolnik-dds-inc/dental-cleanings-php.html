Dental X-Rays

Dental x-rays allow us to view areas of your teeth that are more difficult to examine. With an x-ray, we can check your teeth for cavities, bone loss, malignant or benign masses, and more.

X-rays are an essential piece of maintaining optimal oral health. At our office, we use Planmeca ProMax, an advanced digital radiograph unit that allows us to take your dental x-rays extraorally. That means we can take your x-rays without placing instruments in your mouth. This process uses the lowest radiation dose possible to achieve the proper diagnosis. Our system is easy and comfortable for you and all members of your family, including children.

Dental x-rays are taken once a year, or as needed.
