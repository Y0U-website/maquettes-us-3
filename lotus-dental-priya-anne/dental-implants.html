Dental Implants Q & A

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are tiny metal posts. They’re usually made of titanium and form an artificial tooth root. They’re set into your jawbone, where they gradually fuse with the bone. Once they’re secure, Dr. Anne attaches replacement teeth. Permanent choices are the most stable and feel the most like natural teeth.

Dental implants replace missing teeth, restoring the appearance of your smile. At Lotus Dental, Dr. Anne uses implants to give you a natural look and feel, whether you’re missing one tooth or several. They can even be used to support dentures, helping you regain function and confidence.

Who is a candidate for dental implants?

The best candidates for dental implants are nonsmokers with good oral hygiene, healthy gums, and no signs of periodontal (gum) disease. Strong jawbone support is necessary. If you meet these requirements, there’s a good chance implants will work for you.

Should you lack sufficient jawbone, you’ll need to undergo a bone graft before having implants. This involves strengthening your existing bone with synthetic material or bone from another part of your body.

If you’re missing just one tooth, Dr. Anne uses a single implant with one crown. If you’re missing several teeth in a row, she can use a few strategically placed implants to hold a permanent bridge. For patients who have lost all their teeth, a set number of implants can secure a full denture, providing a comfortable, stable fit.

How do dental implants compare with conventional dentures and bridges?

Unlike dentures and bridges, implants are fixed directly into the bone so that they don’t slip or shift when you eat or speak. They also don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support, which helps protect the health of your remaining natural teeth.

Implants provide a stronger bite and look more natural. They can last decades even a lifetime with proper care. Bridges typically last 7-10 years.

How do I care for dental implants?

Treat your implants like your natural teeth. Brush and floss at least twice a day and attend regular checkups and professional cleanings.
