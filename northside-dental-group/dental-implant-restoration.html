Dental Implant Restoration Options

When it comes to options available for dental implant restoration, you will be glad to learn that the dental industry has advanced technology that offers a variety of implants. Some of the general dental implant options include:

Dental crowns are tooth-shaped caps placed over the damaged parts of the tooth, and when the tooth structure is gone, an implant can serve as the new root for the crown.

Bridges, on the other hand, replace one or more missing teeth, and implants can anchor the bridge securely in place.

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth and surrounding soft tissues. When many or all teeth are missing, implants can provide the stability and retention needed for dentures, enhancing comfort and functionality.

Dental Implant Restoration Benefits

The benefits of dental implant restoration go beyond just regaining your confidence, a natural-looking smile, and improved oral health. Other benefits include:

Losing teeth can affect the way you pronounce words. With a dental implant restoration, this problem is solved, and you can speak more clearly.

Dental implants are easy to clean and maintain since they act and feel like natural teeth.

Because they are permanent and securely anchored, there is no risk of slipping or shifting and no need for messy adhesives.

Tooth loss can cause your face to sag or appear older. But dental implant restoration can preserve your facial structure and give you a youthful appearance.
