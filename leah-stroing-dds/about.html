Formerly known as Johnson Street Dental, this office has beneficially impacted the lives of local families in the Red Bluff community for over 40 years. It is our mission at Stroing and White Dental to continue to serve our neighbors in this well known office. We feel comfortable treating patients of all ages, and can’t wait to meet you and your families! ‍ We work with a large network of insurers to aid in your specific dental needs. We aim to create lifelong relationships between our office and the community so patients can achieve and enjoy the best possible dental health.

What to expect at your first visit

Our priority is to make sure you feel comfortable and heard.

Stroing and White Dental is located in a convenient and accessible location on Johnson Street in Red Bluff, CA. Our reception area is bright and modern, and when you enter the office, you will be welcomed by our friendly and knowledgeable receptionist. We will always be conscientious of your schedule by seating you as close as possible to your appointment time. It’s our top priority to provide you with the best care possible that successfully addresses your personal needs.
