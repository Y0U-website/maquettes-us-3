Dental Implants

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, our dental implants in Auburn offer a permanent solution to tooth loss, mimicking the natural tooth structure in a way that surpasses their counterparts in both durability and appearance.

Please schedule a consultation with our team! We’ll help you unravel the complexities behind dental implants and provide you with in-depth knowledge about this innovative procedure.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post designed to replace the root portion of a missing tooth. First, we securely anchor it into the jawbone beneath the gum line, creating a strong and stable foundation for attaching an artificial tooth or multiple teeth. Over time, the implant naturally integrates with the bone through a process called osseointegration, ensuring long-term stability and durability.

The Function and Purpose of Dental Implants

We customize dental implants to offer stable support for artificial teeth. The key benefit of dental implants is that they do not rely on neighboring teeth for support, as with bridges. This independence allows for a more natural and comfortable experience while preventing jawbone density loss that typically occurs after tooth extraction.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Aesthetic Improvements and Enhanced Confidence

For individuals who have lost teeth, dental implants offer an excellent solution to restore their natural smile and boost their self-esteem. Not only do they provide a secure and durable replacement, but they also mimic the look and feel of natural teeth. As a result, you can speak clearly, eat comfortably, and smile with confidence, enhancing both your appearance and quality of life

Implants restore the ability to chew food correctly, enhancing diet and overall health. Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip or cause discomfort, implants function just like natural teeth, offering a considerable boost in functionality.

Maintaining jawbone health is essential for preserving facial structure and preventing the collapse that often follows significant tooth loss. Fortunately, dental implants help by actively stimulating the jawbone, which not only prevents bone loss but also maintains the natural contours of your face, ensuring a healthier and more youthful appearance

Dental Implant Restorations

North Auburn Dentistry focuses on providing comprehensive dental care to our patients, including high-quality restorative treatments such as crowns, bridges, and implant-supported prosthetics. While we don’t perform the surgical placement of dental implants in-house, we collaborate closely with skilled oral surgeons or periodontists who specialize in implant surgery. Once the implants have been successfully placed and have undergone the necessary healing period, we take over the restorative aspect of the treatment.

  • Abutment Placement and Impression Making : We attach abutments to the implants, which serve as the connection point for the final prosthetic restoration. Impressions of the implant sites and surrounding teeth are then taken to create custom-fabricated restorations.
  • Custom Restoration Fabrication : Our skilled dental laboratory technicians carefully use the impressions to fabricate custom crowns, bridges, or implant-supported prosthetics. By paying close attention to every detail, we ensure that the restorations not only fit perfectly but also blend seamlessly with the patient’s natural teeth in terms of color, shape, and function.
  • Final Restoration Placement and Adjustment : Once the final restorations are ready, we carefully and securely attach them to the abutments using dental cement or screws. Afterward, we conduct a thorough assessment of the fit, function, and aesthetics to ensure they align perfectly with the patient’s bite and smile. If necessary, we make precise adjustments to enhance comfort, improve durability, and achieve a seamless, natural-looking appearance.
  • Ongoing Maintenance and Care : After the restoration process is complete, we provide patients with guidance on proper oral hygiene practices and schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor the health and stability of their dental implants. Our goal is to help patients maintain their restored smiles for years to come through comprehensive dental care and personalized treatment plans.

Maintenance and Long-Term Care

Good oral hygiene is essential for the long-term success of your dental implants. Regular brushing, flossing, and use of an antimicrobial mouthwash will help to maintain the health of your gums and prevent peri-implantitis—a condition similar to gum disease.

Visiting us for routine check-ups and cleanings is vital. These appointments allow us to assess the health of your dental implants and to detect any issues early on.
