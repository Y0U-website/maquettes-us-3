Healing takes about 3 to 6 months, depending on how your bone responds to the implants. Temporary dentures can be worn during this period for comfort and function.

Proper care is essential for the long-term success of your denture retention system:

Maintaining your denture attachment system helps prevent soft tissue inflammation and prolongs the life of your implants and dentures.

Are Implant-Retained Dentures Permanent or Removable ?

Yes—implant overdentures are removable, but the implants anchoring them are permanent. This means you can take the denture out daily for cleaning while still benefiting from the stability that implants provide.

These are different from fixed hybrid dentures, which are screwed in and can only be removed by a dental professional.
