How to Maintain Good Dental Hygiene at Home

Good oral hygiene doesn’t end after you visit our office. Maintaining a healthy smile requires a daily commitment to proper care at home. Here are some key tips our team at North Dental recommends to keep your teeth and gums healthy between professional cleanings:

If you’re unsure about your technique or need guidance on choosing the right products, our hygienists are always available to offer personalized advice during your next visit.

  • Brushing : Brush your teeth at least twice a day using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Make sure to brush all surfaces of your teeth, including along the gumline, to remove plaque.
  • Flossing : Flossing once a day helps remove food particles and plaque from between your teeth and along the gums, where a toothbrush may not reach. This simple practice is key to preventing gum disease and cavities between teeth.
  • Using Mouthwash : An antimicrobial or fluoride mouthwash can help reduce plaque and bacteria, providing extra protection for your teeth and gums.
  • Diet and Oral Health : A balanced diet is also important for maintaining good oral health. Limiting sugary foods and drinks can help reduce the risk of cavities and tooth decay.

Advanced Hygiene Services for Gum Health

Gum health is a critical aspect of overall dental hygiene. If you’re experiencing signs of gum disease, such as bleeding gums, swelling, or persistent bad breath, we may recommend advanced hygiene treatments to restore your gum health.

  • Deep Cleanings (Scaling and Root Planing) : For patients with signs of gum disease, our hygienists may perform deep cleaning , also known as scaling and root planing. This procedure involves cleaning beneath the gumline to remove plaque and tartar from the roots of the teeth, helping to reduce gum inflammation and prevent further damage.
  • Periodontal Maintenance : After initial treatment for gum disease, regular periodontal maintenance is essential to keeping your gums healthy. Our hygienists will work with you to schedule follow-up appointments and ensure that your gum disease is kept under control.

Preventive Care with Fluoride and Sealants

In addition to cleanings, we offer a range of preventive treatments designed to protect your teeth from decay and damage.

  • Fluoride Treatments : After your cleaning, we may recommend a fluoride treatment to strengthen your tooth enamel and provide added protection against cavities. Fluoride is especially beneficial for children, but adults who are prone to cavities can also benefit from this treatment. ‍
  • Sealants for Added Protection : For children and adults with deep grooves in their molars, we may recommend dental sealants . Sealants are a protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, which helps to block out food particles and bacteria, reducing the risk of cavities.

FAQs About Dental Hygiene

What are the golden rules of dental hygiene? ‍

Brush twice daily, floss every day, use mouthwash, limit sugar, and visit your dentist regularly.

What should you avoid before a hygienist appointment? ‍

Skip foods or drinks that stain (like coffee), avoid mouthwash, and don’t overbrush.

What are essential dental hygiene precautions? ‍

Brush gently with a soft-bristled brush, replace it every three months, and avoid hard, sticky foods.

Where do dental hygienists earn the most? ‍

Hygienists typically make the most in states like Alaska, California, and Washington.
