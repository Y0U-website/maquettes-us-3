The practice

At Metro Dental, we make dental care comfortable, convenient, and stress-free. Our compassionate dentists use advanced technology and offer sedation options to help ease dental anxiety.

We proudly serve patients throughout the DMV, accept most PPO insurance plans, and offer zero-down, zero-interest financing for qualified patients.

We provide general dentistry services to kids and adults. This includes everything from a simple cleaning to more advanced procedures like dental implants, root canals, crowns, etc

Dental Implants Our goal is to save your teeth; however, if you happen to lose them, we provide implants, bridges, and dentures and solutions to missing teeth

Cosmetic Dentistry Procedures that fall under this category include, teeth whitening, veneers, enamel bonding and complete smile make-overs

Do you think you need an extraction? In many cases, the tooth can be saved. Fill out the form below and we will call you to discuss your symptoms

Grossly Damaged Tooth a tooth may need to be extracted multiple attempts at saving it through a root canal procedure are not successful.

Orthodontic Purposes We may also recommend extraction of one or more teeth in a jaw to create room for the alignment of teeth during orthodontic treatment.

Les soins

General dentistry includes preventive dental procedures such as cleanings, sealants, etc, as well as restorative procedures like fillings, root canals, dental crowns, extraction, gum disease treatment , and more

Invisalign Invisalign is a technologically advanced treatment that uses plastic aligners to straighten misaligned or crowded teeth.
