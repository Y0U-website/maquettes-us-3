The practice

We believe dental care should be built on trust, compassion, and long-lasting relationships. We’re not just your dentist—we’re your dental home. From your child’s first tooth to your grandparent’s dental implants, we’re here to care for your entire family under one roof.

Whether you’re coming in for a routine cleaning, cosmetic enhancement, or restorative work we’re committed to making your experience comfortable and stress-free.

Our office is designed with your comfort in mind. Enjoy our relaxing waiting area with WiFi, TV entertainment in treatment rooms, and convenient parking. We create a welcoming space where even anxious patients feel at ease.

Life doesn’t stop for dental emergencies. We offer convenient weekday hours (Monday-Thursday, 8am-5pm) and emergency appointments available by calling our emergency line. When you need us, we’re here.

Easy to find and easy to reach. Our office at 5460 Lilburn Stone Mtn Rd offers ample parking and wheelchair accessibility. Serving families throughout Lilburn and surrounding communities with dental care that’s close to home.
