Three Decades of Trusted Expertise & National Recognition

With over 30 years of dedicated experience in his practice, Dr. Kulits has guided thousands of patients through their smile journeys with care, expertise, and a little bit of humor along the way. His exceptional history of success is best summarized by the trust placed in him: he is now proudly treating the second generation of patients—former kids who are returning with their own children for care.

Dr. Kulits is a distinguished Board-Certified Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics (ABO) . This voluntary certification signifies his commitment to going above and beyond the standard requirements, demonstrating clinical excellence and lifelong dedication to the highest standards of patient care.

Inspired by a Smile

Dr. Kulits’ interest in orthodontics began when he experienced braces himself as a teenager.

Seeing the transformation in his own smile—and the confidence it created—inspired him to pursue a career helping others experience the same change.

That early experience continues to shape the thoughtful, patient-centered care he provides today.

Leading the Field & Rooted in the Community

Dr. Kulits remains committed to advancing orthodontic care through continued education and collaboration with respected colleagues. Through organizations such as the Schulman Group, he works with leading orthodontists across the country to refine treatment approaches and stay at the forefront of the specialty.
