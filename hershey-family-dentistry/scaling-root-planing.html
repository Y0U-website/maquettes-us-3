Scaling and Root Planing | Hershey Family Dentistry

This procedure is typically recommended for patients with moderate to severe gum disease and is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort.

Aftercare: Following scaling and root planing, our team will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to ensure proper healing and maintenance of your gum health. We may also recommend more frequent dental cleanings to prevent the recurrence of gum disease.

We are committed to providing you with the highest standard of periodontal care. If you have symptoms of gum disease or if it’s time for your regular check-up, please contact us to schedule an appointment. Let us help you achieve and maintain healthy gums for a lifetime of smiles.

  • Root Planing: Smoothing the root surfaces of your teeth to help the gums reattach and prevent further plaque buildup.
  • Halts the Progression of Gum Disease: By removing plaque and tartar, scaling and root planing help stop the infection and inflammation of the gums.
  • Prevents Tooth Loss: By treating gum disease early, scaling and root planing can prevent the loss of teeth that can occur as the disease progresses.
  • Improves Oral Health: Healthy gums are essential for overall oral health. This procedure helps restore the health of your gums and supports the longevity of your teeth.
