General Dentistry

Our staff ensures every patient receives the highest level of service and highest quality of dental work. Maintaining good oral health is key to maintaining good overall health and we are here to help you by providing the proper team and equipment to take care of all your dental needs.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Your mouth is the entryway to the rest of your body and the start of your overall health. At your 6 month cleanings, we will do screenings for oral cancer.

Root Planing

When plaque and tartar accumulate below the gum line, we clean and smooth this area to ensure it does not become irritated with bacteria. This will prevent further dental and health problems!

Dental Crowns

A crown is designed to cover a damaged tooth and can strengthen the tooth. Crowns are made to improve the appearance and alignment of the tooth.

Bondings & Filling

If you have a discolored tooth or have chipped/cracked your tooth, bonding can give you the confidence to smile again in a cost-effective manner. Fillings are done to repair cavities or a cracked or broken tooth due to teeth grinding.

Preventative Care

To ensure a good overall healthy mouth, the American Dental Association recommends visiting your dentist every 6 months.

Smile Makeover

Laurel Dental Care offers a variety of cosmetic procedures that can give your smile a total aesthetic transformation. Depending on your individual needs and goals, a smile makeover can treat discoloration, missing or chipped teeth, overcrowding, misalignment, excessive wear, and more. When complete the result is a healthy smile that you’re proud to put on display.
