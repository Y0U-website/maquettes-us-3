Our treatment process

Make an Appointment

Scheduling an appointment is easy with our online booking option or call and speak with one of our friendly staff and schedule a time that works best for you!

Consultation and Check-Up

Whether you schedule with one of our Hygienists for a cleaning or a general exam, you will meet with one of our experienced Dentists who will perform a comprehensive exam to assess your oral health.

Treatment Plan and Follow Up

A treatment plan will be developed and discussed. One of our office experts will be ready to assist you in determining your insurance coverage or payment options and will schedule you for follow up treatment.
