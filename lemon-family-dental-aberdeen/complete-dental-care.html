Dental Bridge

Dental Bridge

First Visit: Preparation

Preparation involves re-contouring the anchor teeth to prepare for the bridge. We remove a portion of the enamel on these teeth to allow room for a bridge to be placed over them. Next, digital impressions of the teeth are taken and sent to the digital lab. Dr. Lemon will make a temporary bridge to protect the exposed teeth and gums while the permanent bridge is being made.

Second Visit: Placement

During the second visit, your temporary bridge will be removed and the new permanent bridge will be checked and adjusted, as necessary, to achieve a proper fit. Close

Veneers

Veneers

If traditional teeth whitening procedures are not enough to produce the smile you desire, you may want to consider veneers. A veneer is a thin shell that covers your natural tooth in order to improve the appearance of your smile. Veneers are about the width of a contact lens, and are made of a tooth colored porcelain that is customizable to your desired shade.

Veneers can correct a variety of issues including teeth that are: Stained or discolored Chipped or broken Worn down Uneven, unsightly, crooked, or irregularly shaped Spaced oddly, creating gaps

Dentures

Dentures

Full Dentures:

Full dentures are needed when all of the teeth are missing. You can choose between conventional and immediate full dentures. Conventional dentures are made after your teeth have been removed and your gums have healed. It usually takes about 12 weeks. Immediate dentures are made beforehand so that you can have your dentures as soon as your teeth are removed.

Partial Denture:

Partial dentures are custom made to fit with your remaining natural teeth. The remaining teeth help keep the partial denture secure, without using messy adhesives. Close
