Caring For Your Dental Implants

Another advantage of dental implants is how easy they are to care for. Whether you have one implant or a mouthful, you can simply brush and floss as you normally would. Unlike dentures, permanent implants are not removed for cleaning.

Your dental implants cannot develop tooth decay, but it’s still crucial to come to our Norfolk, MA, dental office twice each year for examinations and cleanings. Even with dental implants, you can still get periodontal disease, so it’s important to maintain good gum health.

One of the most common complaints we hear from patients at our Norfolk, MA, dental practice is that they have food restrictions with their dentures. With dental implants, patients can eat all of their favorite foods—without restriction. People who have gotten dental implants can chew confidently, knowing that their implants are stable and will not shift as do dentures.

Options for Patients with Low Bone Density

If your bone quality isn’t good, that doesn’t rule out the possibility of dental implants. If additional procedures are necessary, such as bone grafting to improve bone stability, we will discuss these with you. It is our intention to deliver the most reliable and long-lasting dental implants possible so that you can enjoy a healthy, comfortable smile.

Surgical placement of the dental implant into the jawbone is why dental implants are such an effective way to replace natural teeth. The dental implant replaces the root of the tooth and stimulates healthy bone growth.

Because the bone contains few nerves, the surgery is relatively mild. Some swelling and bruising are common but typically manageable with an anti-inflammatory medication.

Osseointegration for Dental Implants

Dental implants are designed to fuse with your natural bone, which is why they are so stable and reliable. Made of titanium or other biocompatible metals, dental implants will actually become part of your bone structure over the course of three to four months.

If you smoke, your dental implant may take longer to integrate. We will discuss what you can expect and closely monitor your progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants a permanent solution for tooth loss?

Dental implants in Norfolk, MA , are a time-tested solution for tooth loss and the option our implant dentists most recommend.

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root made of solid titanium. A dentist surgically implants this root into your jawbone and allows for a healing or integration period, usually several months. When integration is complete, your dentist caps your implant with a dental crown . The custom crown matches your real teeth for a natural-looking finish.

Dental implants are considered a permanent solution to tooth loss because the implant is technically the replacement root. And this root, with proper care, can last for many years or even for life. Even if you have to replace your crown, your tooth root is often still viable and ready for its replacement crown.

Remember, your gum health plays a critical role in your implant health and longevity. Other factors that may influence dental implant health include tobacco use.

  • Brushing and flossing daily—at least twice or following every meal
  • Seeing your dentist regularly for exams and teeth cleanings
  • Reporting any oral health issues in a timely manner
  • Talking to your dentist if you suspect bruxism (teeth grinding and clenching)

Are dental implants painful?

Typically, you will not experience pain during dental implant surgery because of the powerful local anesthesia. However, you may feel pressure.

After implant placement, and when anesthesia wears off, you may experience mild discomfort and swelling, but it is not long-lasting. You can take an over-the-counter pain medication, such as ibuprofen, to ease any discomfort.

It’s also important to follow all aftercare instructions provided by your Norfolk dentist to help ensure a straightforward recovery.
