Medical History

Inform us of any medications you’re taking and make sure we have an up to date medical history on file.

Questions

FAQ

Do you take my insurance?

Yes, we are in-network with major providers like Delta Dental, United Concordia, United Healthcare, and Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Dakota. Even if we’re out-of-network with your plan, we’ll file claims on your behalf to help you maximize your benefits.

What can I expect during my first visit?

At your first appointment, we’ll take the time to get to know you, discuss your dental history, and perform a thorough exam. Our goal is to make you feel comfortable, answer your questions, and create a personalized plan for your dental health.

What services do you provide?

We offer a full range of dental services, including preventive care , restorative treatments (like same-day crowns and root canals), cosmetic dentistry , and oral surgery . Check out our Services Page for a full list!

How do you make visits more comfortable?

We offer a Comfort Menu with amenities like ceiling-mounted TVs, noise-canceling headphones, and streaming services (Netflix, Disney+, YouTube TV). We also provide options like nitrous oxide and oral sedation to ease de ntal an xiety.
