Covid-19 Prevention

  • We ask our patients to avoid bringing companions to their appointments unless it is absolutely necessary.
  • Body temperature will be taken for all patients and companions. Temperature is also recorded daily for all staff members.
  • New scheduling protocols have been implemented to reduce waiting times, and to limit the number of patients in the reception area at any one time. You may be asked to wait in your car before entering the building.
  • Patients and companions will wear a mask all the time while not receiving dental care.
  • All patients and visitors will be asked to use hand sanitizer upon entering the office, and before and after entering treatment rooms.
  • Our waiting room will no longer offer magazines or reading materials since those items are difficult to disinfect.
  • All common areas will be sanitized periodically during the day.

Air Quality - Prevention of Potential Airborne Transmission

  • Use an antimicrobial rinse to reduce bacterial and viral concentration in the mouth prior to procedures.
  • Modification of the intraoral high volume vacuum/suction system to capture aerosols during procedures.
  • An extraoral suction device will be used to add a third layer of protection during aerosol-producing procedures.
  • Foggers will be used to deliver an environmentally friendly disinfectant effective against harmful bacteria and COVID-19 in between patients.
  • Implementation of electrostatic sprayers for sanitation and disinfection of our entire facility. The electrostatic delivery system allows us to more efficiently and effectively disinfect treatment rooms and common areas.
