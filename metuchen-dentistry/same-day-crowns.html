Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a CEREC Crown?

CEREC crowns are same-day dental crowns created using advanced CAD/CAM technology. Instead of taking messy physical impressions and placing a temporary crown while waiting weeks for a lab to fabricate the final restoration, CEREC allows your Metuchen Dentistry to design, mill, and place your permanent ceramic crown in a single appointment.

The process begins with a digital scan of your tooth. That scan is used to design a custom ceramic crown that fits your bite precisely. The crown is then milled from a high-quality ceramic block right here in our Metuchen dental office. After shaping and polishing, it is bonded permanently to your tooth.

Because treatment is completed in one visit, CEREC crowns are commonly referred to as “same-day crowns.”

Why Are Same-Day Crowns So Popular in Metuchen?

Patients today value convenience. Traditional crowns require two visits spaced weeks apart. During that waiting period, patients must wear a temporary crown that can loosen, break, or cause sensitivity.

With CEREC same-day crowns at Metuchen Dentistry, everything is completed in one visit. You leave with a permanent restoration immediately. There is no need for a temporary crown, no return appointment for placement, and no waiting on a dental lab.

For busy professionals, families, and commuters in Metuchen, NJ, this convenience is a major benefit.

How Long Do CEREC Crowns Last?

One of the most common questions we hear is, “How long does a CEREC crown last?” In most cases, a CEREC same-day crown lasts between 10 and 15 years, and many last much longer.

The lifespan of any dental crown depends on oral hygiene, bite forces, and regular dental checkups. Patients who brush and floss consistently and attend regular dental cleanings at Metuchen Dentistry often see their crowns last well beyond a decade.

When properly placed and cared for, CEREC ceramic crowns are comparable in durability to traditional lab-made crowns.

How Long Should a CEREC Crown Last?

A realistic expectation is 10 to 15 years, although it is not uncommon for same-day crowns to last 15 to 20 years with proper care. Longevity improves when patients avoid chewing ice, limit extremely hard foods, and wear a nightguard if they grind their teeth.

Routine dental exams in our Metuchen office allow us to monitor the integrity of the crown and ensure the surrounding tooth remains healthy.

What Are the Disadvantages of CEREC Crowns?

While CEREC same-day crowns are an excellent solution for most patients, they are not perfect for every situation.

In extremely complex cosmetic cases or very large restorations, a traditional laboratory-fabricated crown may provide additional layering options for advanced aesthetics. Patients with severe teeth grinding may also require additional protection to extend the life of any ceramic restoration.

Another consideration is cost. CEREC crowns can sometimes cost slightly more than traditional crowns due to the advanced technology involved. However, many patients find that eliminating multiple visits and temporary crowns makes the investment worthwhile.

At Metuchen Dentistry, we evaluate every case individually to determine whether a same-day crown is the best option for your specific dental needs.

What Problems Can Occur With CEREC Crowns?

The potential problems associated with CEREC crowns are similar to those of traditional crowns. These may include bite adjustments, sensitivity after placement, or rare bonding issues.

Because we use precise digital imaging and advanced milling technology, CEREC crowns often achieve extremely accurate fits. Proper placement technique and careful bite adjustment significantly reduce complications.

When placed by an experienced dentist in Metuchen, NJ, CEREC crowns have high success rates and excellent long-term outcomes.

Why Do Dentists Recommend Dental Crowns?

Dentists recommend crowns when a tooth is too damaged to be restored with a filling alone. Large cavities, cracked teeth, broken cusps, and teeth that have undergone root canal therapy often require crown protection.

A crown covers the entire visible portion of the tooth, restoring its strength, shape, and function. Without a crown, a weakened tooth may fracture further, leading to more extensive treatment.

At Metuchen Dentistry, we only recommend dental crowns when they are necessary to protect your long-term oral health.

What Is the Healthiest Type of Crown?

Many patients ask about the healthiest crown material. All-ceramic crowns, including CEREC crowns, are highly biocompatible and metal-free. They closely mimic the appearance and translucency of natural enamel while providing excellent durability.

Ceramic crowns do not contain metal alloys and are well tolerated by gum tissue. For patients seeking a natural-looking and biologically friendly restoration, CEREC same-day crowns are an excellent choice.
