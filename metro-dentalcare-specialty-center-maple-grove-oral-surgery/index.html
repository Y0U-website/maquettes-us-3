The practice

Stay relaxed during your treatment with our sedation options. We have nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation available.

We are a trusted partner for patients and referring doctors in Maple Grove and surrounding areas. We handle a variety of procedures, from biopsies and tooth extractions to bone grafting and treating orofacial trauma.

At Metro Dentalcare Specialty Center Maple Grove Oral Surgery, you will find a professional team devoted to your oral health goals. Whether you have minor issues that need treatment or a complex case that requires multiple appointments, you and your family are in safe hands at our practice.

Our team performs biopsies to accurately diagnose oral conditions so that patients can obtain treatment in a timely manner.

We take a gentle approach to extractions, including wisdom teeth, to minimize patient discomfort.

Socket preservation helps patients to maintain the integrity of their jawbone after tooth removal.

Restore lost bone and ensure a solid foundation for future dental implants with bone grafting.

We’re here to answer any questions you may have about our services.
