Meet Dr. Melissa Nevid

Call (410) 695-4603 Tap to call (410) 695-4603 MENU ☰ Dr. Melissa Nevid DDS, FICOI, FAGD Dr. Melissa Nevid is committed to helping every patient feel comfortable, heard, and confident in their smile.

With over 16 years of experience, she blends clinical excellence with a compassionate approach to care. Her priority is creating a supportive environment where patients feel at ease, whether they’re visiting for a routine checkup or a more advanced procedure.

Dr. Nevid earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry and holds a Bachelor of Science in Psychology from the University of Maryland. Her educational background allows her to understand not just dental health, but the human side of dentistry, too.

She is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics, and the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Nevid is also a proud member of the American Dental Association and actively participates in outreach programs that bring care to communities near and far.

Outside the office, Dr. Nevid enjoys live music and spending quality time with her husband, daughter, and their dog Nathan.
