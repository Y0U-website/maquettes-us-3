All-On-4 Dental Implants in Levittown & Long Island, NY

Timelines and exact steps vary by case. Our team works closely with oral surgeons, prosthodontists, and dental technicians to ensure the best possible outcome for every patient.

  • Treatment Planning — Based on your evaluation, we create a customized plan covering optimal implant placement, prosthesis design, and any preparatory treatments needed.
  • Preparatory Treatments (if needed) — Some patients require tooth extractions or bone grafting beforehand to ensure adequate bone density and volume to support the implants.
  • Implant Placement — On surgery day, local anesthesia or sedation ensures your comfort while we position four implants in the jawbone using guided placement techniques, angled precisely to maximize stability.
  • Temporary Prosthesis — A temporary full-arch prosthesis is attached right after placement, so you leave with functional teeth while your implants heal.
  • Healing & Osseointegration — Over the following months, the implants fuse with the jawbone. We&#x27;ll see you for regular follow-ups to monitor healing.
  • Final Prosthesis Placement — Once osseointegration is complete, your temporary prosthesis is replaced with a custom, permanent one matched to your natural teeth.
  • Follow-Up Care — We continue monitoring your oral health and prosthesis fit with ongoing follow-up visits.

Long-Term Outcomes & Maintenance

  • Stability and Functionality — Designed to function like natural teeth, All-on-4 implants let patients bite and chew comfortably across a full range of foods.
  • Aesthetics — Your full-arch prosthesis is custom-made to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size for a natural-looking smile.
  • Durability — Made from biocompatible titanium resistant to decay and gum disease, these implants can last decades with proper care.
  • Maintenance — Long-term success depends on: Oral hygiene — Brush twice daily, floss, and use an antimicrobial mouthwash, paying close attention to the area around implants and prostheses.
  • Regular check-ups — Routine visits for professional cleanings let us monitor your implants and catch issues early.
  • Avoiding harmful habits — Steer clear of chewing hard objects, teeth grinding, and smoking, all of which can damage implants over time.
  • Prosthesis adjustments — Over time, your prosthesis may need adjustment or replacement due to normal wear — regular visits help us catch this early.

Considerations Before Treatment

  • Bone Density — Adequate jawbone density is required; patients with significant bone loss may need bone grafting first.
  • Oral Health — Existing issues like gum disease or tooth decay should be treated before implant surgery.
  • Overall Health — Certain medical conditions or medications can affect implant success — we&#x27;ll discuss these with you during your consultation.
  • Cost — All-on-4 is a meaningful investment. We&#x27;ll help you explore insurance coverage and financing options to find a plan that works for you.

Is All-On-4 Right for You?

All-on-4 dental implants give patients in Levittown and across Long Island a permanent, natural-looking solution to extensive tooth loss — restoring the ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. If you&#x27;re dealing with gum disease alongside missing teeth, we may also recommend periodontal treatment as part of your care plan. For patients needing multiple types of restoration at once, All-on-4 is often part of a broader full mouth rehabilitation plan.

Ready to find out if All-on-4 is right for you? Schedule a consultation at our Levittown office and let our team build a personalized treatment plan around your needs.

Overall, the purpose of All-on-4 dental implants is to provide patients with a permanent, stable, and aesthetically pleasing solution to extensive tooth loss. By restoring their ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently, All-on-4 implants significantly improve a patient’s quality of life and oral health.

The All-on-4 technique is an excellent solution for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth and desire permanent restoration. It offers numerous benefits, including:

Stability All-on-4 implants fuse with the jawbone through osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation for the prosthesis. This stability allows patients to confidently bite, chew, and speak without worrying about their teeth shifting or slipping.

Aesthetics The full-arch prosthesis attached to the All-on-4 implants is custom-designed to resemble natural teeth. The result is a beautiful, natural-looking smile that enhances facial appearance and boosts self-confidence.

Functionality All-on-4 implants restore the full functionality of the teeth, allowing patients to eat a wide variety of foods without restrictions. With the support of the implants, they can comfortably enjoy their favorite meals and experience improved digestion.

Improved Oral Health Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 implants do not impede oral hygiene. Patients can brush and floss their teeth as they would with natural teeth, ensuring proper oral hygiene and reducing the risk of gum disease or other oral health issues.
