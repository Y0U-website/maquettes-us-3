What Is the All-on-X Teeth Replacement System?

All-on-X represents an approach to restoring your smile, using 4–8 dental implants that support an entire permanent denture. This innovative treatment is crafted to offer a permanent, functional solution for missing teeth.

Dr. Martin will plan exactly where your implants should go in your arch, accounting for face shape and optimal security for your dentures. We may use a variety of dental implants to make this possible.

All-on-X permanent dentures often require shorter recovery methods and can permanently anchor your teeth for optimal function.

We design your dentures to complement your face and fill out your cheeks to prevent facial sagging. Once you have your permanent dentures, you will immediately notice an improvement in your entire face’s aesthetic appearance.

Our Unique Approach to All-on-X Dental Implant Placement

Unlike the conventional method that typically utilizes four dental implants, Dr. Martin believes in over-engineering the support structure. He tends to recommend six implants for most cases in order to provide greater long-term stability, strength, and peace of mind for patients.

Dr. Martin goes further than other dentists who will just create an implant placement plan that is industry standard but nothing more. Instead, Dr. Martin places as many implants as possible and intentionally over-engineers each case to maximize security and success.

Think of it like a chair: four legs may be enough, but if just one weakens or breaks, the entire structure becomes unstable. With six implants, even if one were to weaken in the future, the remaining implants provide full support and a secure foundation.

This philosophy isn’t just about having extra implants; it’s about enhancing the durability, longevity, and success rate of your dental restoration. It’s our commitment to keeping your smile strong and functional by preserving your jawbone health and protecting your natural teeth.
