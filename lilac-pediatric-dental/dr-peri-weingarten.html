Meet Dr. Peri Weingarten

Dr. Peri Weingarten, DDS

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Peri Weingarten was born and raised in the Midwest and earned her Bachelor of Arts from Indiana University, focusing on chemistry and biology. She continued her education at the Indiana University School of Dentistry, where she received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Peri completed a general practice residency at Jacobi Medical Center in the Bronx, caring for patients of all ages in a hospital setting. She then pursued specialty training in pediatric dentistry at St. Barnabas Hospital, where she served as chief resident.

For the past 10 years, Dr. Peri has practiced in both hospital and private practice settings. As a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist, she is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also certified by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments and holds CPR and Pediatric Advanced Life Support certifications.

Passionate about community outreach, Dr. Peri has volunteered both locally and internationally, including in Guatemala and Israel. Her goal is to provide high-quality, compassionate dental care that helps children feel comfortable and confident. In her free time, she enjoys exercising, exploring new restaurants, and spending time with her husband and daughter.
