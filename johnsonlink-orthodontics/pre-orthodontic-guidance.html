Pre-Orthodontic Guidance for Kids…

  • How often will we come in? Observation visits are typically scheduled every 6–9 months, depending on your child’s needs.
  • Is the program really free? Yes! Our observation visits are completely complimentary.
  • Will my child need braces later? Not always. The purpose of the program is to determine the ideal timing and treatment, if any treatment becomes necessary.

Tongue Thrusting

Misplaced pressure from the tongue can shift teeth & affect jaw growth.

You’re part of the Team

We encourage parents and guardians to attend every observation visit. Together, we’ll answer questions, review your child’s growth, and make sure you always understand what we’re seeing and why.

Early Monitoring

We carefully track your child’s dental development so we can identify concerns before they become bigger problems.

Growth Guidance

As your child grows, we monitor jaw development and tooth eruption to determine the ideal timing for treatment.

Confidence

Regular checkups give families confidence knowing their child’s smile is developing just as it should.

Healthy Smiles for Life

Every child develops differently. We create recommendations based on your child’s unique growth.

Personalized Care

Thoughtful monitoring today helps build the foundation for a healthy, confident smile in the years ahead.

Better Timing

Treatment works best when it’s started at the right time, not too early and not too late.
