First Appointment & Checkups

0 First Visit & Checkups Age-appropriate cleanings We will share our special tips and tricks. We’ve got a few up our sleeves for tots, toddlers, tweens, and teens.

Diet-based prevention

Diet plays a big role in cavity prevention (possibly the biggest). Our diet review will enable your child to make smart snacking choices.

Digital X-rays (taken as needed)

We follow the AAPD recommendations. Tap here to see the AAPD’s recommendations for X-rays based on age and individual risk for cavities. Ta p here to better understand radiation exposure related to dental X-rays ( Here’s an additional resource from Today’s RDH ).

Vitamin and mineral supplement counseling

Based on your child’s needs and your preferences, we will recommend products that can help keep your kiddos cavity free.

Growth and Development monitoring

We will watch their smile grow and change, and recommend intervention if needed.

Mouthguard and Trauma counseling

Young walkers and athletes are especially at risk for trauma. We will go over what to do if you find yourself in a situation involving trauma to the mouth or teeth. Some young athletes may benefit from a mouthguard as well.
