Meet Dr. Nidhi

Dr. Nidhi Agarwal, or " Dr. Nidhi " as she is known to her patients, always wanted to be a dentist, ever since she was a kid sitting in her orthodontist s treatment chair!

After graduating from Boston University with Honors and receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine, Dr. Nidhi completed a 2-year residency program in Pediatric Dentistry. During this time she received training on various aspects of pediatric dentistry designed specifically to fulfill the needs of young patients including child development, behavior management, restorative and preventive dentistry, trauma management, conscious oral sedation dentistry, dental rehabilitation under general anesthesia. She also spent a lot of time with patients with special health care needs.

Dr. Nidhi is a Board-certified Pediatric Dentist and recipient of Richard C. Pugh award-a national award achieved by only few pediatric dentists each year.

Beyond the walls of her dental home, you can catch Dr. Nidhi being a mom of two and exploring new recipes with her little sous chefs and playing outdoors. Dr. Nidhi is more than excited to help guide little patients on their path to top-notch oral health, and she can’t wait to meet you!

Dr. Nidhi has been involved in organized dentistry since dental school and is a strong believer in lifelong learning. She is an active member in the following organizations:

Dr. Gursimran Ahluwalia (Dr.Sim) received his D.M.D. Degree from Boston University. Following graduation from dental school, Dr. Sim worked in private practice for four years. During four years of private practice, Dr. Sim worked extensively with children and adolescents and decided to specialize in pediatric dentistry.

He completed his 2-year residency in advanced education in Pediatric dentistry at Boston University. During his 2- year training, Dr. Sim received specialized training in dentistry for children, adolescents, special needs, sedation, and Hospital dentistry at Franciscan Children’s hospital and Boston Medical center.

Dr. Sim has a strong commitment to meet the individual needs of each and every child, including those with special needs.

He wants every experience to be a positive one- a philosophy he brings to Killingly Kids Dentist.
