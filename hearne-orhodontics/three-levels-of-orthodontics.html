Level Two Orthodontics: Appearance and Bite

Most patients will require more than level one orthodontics to achieve a healthy smile. Level two orthodontics addresses aesthetics and occlusion (the way your teeth fit together when you bite down).

The majority of patients who come to see us want straight teeth. They want this addressed first and foremost because they notice the problem every day, whether it be crooked, overlapped, crowded, or rotated teeth. Although functional issues may be less noticeable, they can cause significant damage over time. When you address function as well as aesthetics, you can enjoy:

  • A balanced bite
  • Less tooth erosion
  • A reduced risk of bone atrophy, gum recession, and TMJ issues
  • Optimal stability
  • A decreased chance of teeth grinding, or bruxism

Level Three Orthodontics: Appearance, Bite, and Jaw Alignment

The third level of orthodontics involves aesthetics, bite, and jaw alignment. This is the most comprehensive level of care possible in the field of orthodontics. By assessing and treating jaw joints, facial muscles, and all oral and maxillofacial structures, our doctors can provide an unparalleled level of care. In addition to straight, beautiful teeth, you can also address more serious concerns related to conditions such as sleep apnea or TMJ disorder. With all elements working together in harmony, you can enjoy a healthy, vibrant smile for years to come.
