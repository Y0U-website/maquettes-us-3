Meet the Team

Dr. Scott Beck

Originally from Hastings, NE, Dr. Beck was drawn to dentistry by his grandfather and great-grandfather, both of whom were dentists in the state of Nebraska. Dr. Beck attended the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry where he received a Regent’s scholarship, graduating top of his class.

Fascinated by the ever-changing landscape of oral health, Dr. Beck consistently seeks opportunities to gain skill and knowledge in modern dental techniques. In 2016, Dr. Beck became one of 1,800 dentists worldwide certified in the Internationally Acclaimed Pinhole Surgery Technique (PST).

A father of three teenagers, Dr. Beck and his wife Montica are busy with sports and activities. When not on the road, Dr. Beck enjoys working on his property and assisting with the family ranch.

Dr. Jason Zitterkopf

Originally from Scottsbluff, NE, Dr. Zitterkopf is proud to have followed in the footsteps of multiple generations who attended the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry, including his grandfather who himself practiced in Scottsbluff.

Dr. Zitterkopf has served in the Nebraska Dental Association’s House of Delegates for multiple years and plans on staying active in organized dentistry. In addition, he continues to seek out opportunities to stay abreast of modern dentistry technologies and procedures.

When not chasing around their two beautiful children, Dr. Zitterkopf and his wife Amber are active in their church and enjoy raising bees and chickens.

Dr. Kaitlin Wasserman

Dr. Kaitlin Wasserman grew up across Minnesota, Nebraska, and Illinois. Her journey into dentistry began during high school while working at her orthodontist’s office, where she saw the powerful impact dental care can have on a person’s confidence and well-being. Combined with her own positive experiences as a patient, this inspired her to pursue dentistry as a career.

She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln and went on to receive her dental degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. Her education strengthened her passion for providing compassionate, high-quality care and creating positive experiences for her patients.

Dr. Wasserman and her husband, Matt, now call Scottsbluff home and are excited to welcome their first child, a daughter, this fall. Outside of dentistry, she enjoys attending a local Bible study, working in her garden, especially growing treats for her guinea pig and watching movies she missed the first time around and catching up on the references her friends have been making for years. She also finds joy in keeping a peaceful home aquarium.
