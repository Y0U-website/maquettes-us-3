Strategic Plan - Good Neighbor Health Clinic & Red Logan Dental Clinic

Strategic Plan 2026 2028

Welcome to our Strategic Plan! We’re proud to share this document as a guide for our organization over the next several years, put together through the work of our community members, stakeholders, volunteers, patients, staff, and more! For the past 30 years, our patients have been at the center of all that we do. We are excited to continue growing our organization to best support the needs of those we serve!

Good Neighbor Health Clinic (GNHC) is a non-profit organization providing cost-free medical, dental, and mental healthcare to uninsured and underinsured adults living within 30-miles of our clinic in White River Junction, Vermont. Good Neighbor Health Clinic provides care for adult residents in 22 Vermont and 16 New Hampshire towns.

This document outlines the strategic planning process and details the strategic focus areas identified as critical for GNHC’s continued growth and impact.

The following focus areas reflect the thoughtful input and insights gathered throughout the strategic planning process and are designed to help clarify and realign the organization’s impact within the Upper Valley to guide the work done over the next 3-5 years:

Goal 1: Strengthen Workforce Capacity and Sustainability

Goal 2: Enhance and Expand Patient Care Services

Goal 3: Maintain and Improve Our Facility

Goal 4: Increase Visibility and Financial Support

Volunteer With Us

Our ability to provide needed care to our neighbors is built on a vibrant network of volunteers. We’re delighted that you’re interested in being a part of our team! To learn about current volunteer opportunities, visit our volunteer page.

At this time, we DO NOT have opportunities available for individuals without clinical licensure. In order to be a clinical volunteer, we require Vermont licensure. Volunteer roles are based on current needs and may change as new opportunities arise.
