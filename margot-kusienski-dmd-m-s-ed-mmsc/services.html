Technology

Our goal is to deliver the most up-to-date endodontic treatment supported by the most current endodontic scientific literature. We, as a team, are passionate about endodontics and caring for you as a person. Throughout our office you’ll find:

  • Digital imaging (x-rays)
  • CBCT (3D imaging)
  • Surgical microscopes (LED light and magnification)
  • Ultrasonics
  • Electronic apex locating devices (helps measure length of the root)
  • Nickel titanium rotary instrumentation (helps to clean inside the root canal system)
  • Electronic medical records
  • High speed electric hand piece (decreases noise)
  • Most up-to-date endodontic dental materials such as bioceramics and mineral trioxide aggregate.

Patient Care

Your comfort and well being are of the utmost importance to our team. We strive to make your dental visit as pleasant as possible. Amenities available during your visit with us are:

  • Aromatherapy eye, neck, back, hands
  • Blanket, neck pillow, physical therapy balls, worry stones
  • Safety glasses (tinted or not) to protect your eyes
  • Nitrous oxide therapy ( laughing gas ) to provide additional relaxation
