The practice

We believe that each patient deserves time and attention. That’s why our experienced team takes the time to listen to your concerns, answer your questions, and ensure you never feel rushed. Our goal is to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile through compassionate, patient-centered care.

We offer a full range of dental services for every member of your family. From routine cleanings and exams to emergency care, we are here to support your oral health every step of the way.

Dr. Daniel Durance, DDS, is a 2017 graduate of the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry, who specializes in preventive and restorative family dentistry. Born and raised in Midland, MI, he graduated from Midland Dow High School in 2009 and then attended Albion College, where he received his undergraduate degree in Biochemistry and Cell Biology.

Dr. Jennifer McCarty was born and raised in Lake Orion, MI. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from Central Michigan University, then continued her education at the University of Michigan, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.
