What Are Dental Implants?

While traditional methods sit on top of the gums, dental implants in Green Hills are the only solution that recreates the tooth’s root structure as well as the visible crown. The implant itself is a small titanium post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone to mimic the root of a lost tooth. Because titanium is biocompatible, it will eventually fuse to the jawbone, basically becoming a part of you. Afterward, Dr. Kirby will place a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture (depending on how many teeth you’re missing) to complete your smile.

How Dental Implants Can Change Your Life

Thanks to their unique placement inside the jaw and their ability to recreate the entire structure of a lost tooth, dental implants can accomplish many things that other replacement teeth cannot. Here are some of the benefits of dental implants in Green Hills that you can look forward to:

Day-to-Day Benefits

Dental implants are able to restore chewing power nearly equal to natural teeth, so you won’t have to worry about diet restrictions as you would with traditional dentures. Another advantage they have over dentures is increased security. Because your new teeth are anchored onto posts that are placed directly in your jaw, your prosthetic should not slip out of place at awkward times.

Not only will you be able to smile confidently with the knowledge that your teeth won’t fall out or move around, but your pearly whites will also look perfectly natural. Your implant dentist in Green Hills will make sure your restorations match your original teeth in size, shape, and color. In short, dental implants are able to look, feel, and function almost exactly like your natural teeth did.

Health Benefits

Without the roots of your teeth, your jawbone loses density, which can create wrinkles around your lips and on your face. Because dental implants stimulate the jawbone, they will help you maintain a younger-looking facial structure. They’ll also keep the jawbone strong enough to reduce the risk of multiple oral health problems, such as further tooth loss, gum disease, and cavities.

Dental implants greatly improve your oral health in general. Unlike traditional dental bridges, implants don’t require any existing healthy tooth structure to be worn-down. Also, unlike dentures alone, implants shouldn’t irritate your gums. Since they prevent these problems, dental implants in Green Hills are by far the healthiest way to replace missing teeth.

Long-Term Benefits

In addition to being the healthiest option, dental implants also are the most successful of all of the tooth replacements available today. In most cases, dental implants have a success rate of 95 % or higher, even after more than a decade. Although dental implant failure is possible, it’s also largely preventable if you take the right precautions (i.e., avoiding tobacco and practicing good oral hygiene).

Also, dental implants are very easy to care for. For the most part, you can just brush and floss them like you would natural teeth. You shouldn’t have to spend time and money on additional cleaning supplies, like water flossers or denture cleanser.

Lastly, dental implants are the most cost-effective solution in the long run. Admittedly, they cost more up-front than dentures or bridges, but it should just be a one-time payment that can last for several decades. Traditional options must be repaired frequently, and replaced entirely every five to ten years on average. Over the years, the costs of these repairs and replacements may compound their original lower price.

By investing in dental implants, you’re making a decision that can benefit your smile, your health, and even your wallet!

Advanced Dental Implant Procedures

Based on the results of your initial consultation, you may not be a candidate for dental implants at first, but that doesn’t mean you can’t become one with additional dental implant procedures. Often, patients don’t have enough bone density to support implant posts. If bone loss is preventing you from getting dental implants, we can create the foundation they need to thrive. No matter if you need bone grafting or a sinus lift, we can help you replace your missing teeth with dental implants.

Bone Grafting

A titanium implant post replicates the root of your tooth. As a result, it stimulates your jaw to encourage new bone growth. Through a process called osseointegration, your bone fuses to the post, allowing it to stay in place forever with the right care. Unfortunately, if you have lost density in your jaw, there won’t be enough bone to support the post, which will ultimately lead to implant failure if the issue isn’t corrected.

Bone grafting is a simple surgical procedure to add thickness to an area where the jawbone has been compromised. Bone from another area of your body or a donor is grafted onto the location to make it stronger. After it’s in place, it will integrate with your jawbone over the next several weeks.

Sinus Lifts

You have a maxillary sinus located on either side of your nose near your back molars. Sometimes, there isn’t enough space to place implant posts in the back of the mouth if the sinuses sit too low or you’ve lost bone in the area. If the post is placed without increasing the height, the post will protrude into the sinus cavity.

A sinus lift is a surgical procedure to raise the sinus. A small hole is cut into the upper jawbone to gain access to the sinus. The sinus is lifted upward, and a bone grafting material is inserted into the hole to hold it in place. Your gum tissue is sutured closed, and you’ll spend the next several weeks healing.

Maintaining & Caring For Your Dental Implants

Making Oral Hygiene a Priority

Maintaining a good oral hygiene routine is important no matter if you have dental implants or not. However, if you are living with these permanent prosthetics, you need to be diligent about brushing, flossing, and rinsing just like you do your regular teeth. This helps to minimize tooth decay, gum disease, and even bad breath. Allowing these problems to linger will only result in serious oral health issues that can negatively impact your dental implants in Green Hills.

Eating a Healthy Diet

Leafy greens, lean proteins, fruits, vegetables, and dairy are all excellent sources of nutrients that your body and smile need to remain healthy. Too much sugar and starch can lead to harmful bacteria and acids building inside your mouth and causing tooth decay to form on your natural teeth as well as gum disease. Should your soft tissues become inflamed, you can be left with needing periodontal therapy to try and avoid dental implant failure. By choosing healthier food options, your real and artificial teeth will look and feel better than ever.

Breaking Bad Habits

Everyone has bad habits, but if you are living with dental implants and you smoke, chew on pens and pencils, eat ice, bite your fingernails, or use your teeth as tools, your worthwhile investment can quickly lead to more timely and costly procedures. Avoiding these practices will better protect your natural teeth and dental implants from serious damage. While it may not be easy to break these habits, it’s best to seek cessation resources to kick the habit or even keep sugar-free chewing gum on hand to minimize the temptation to smoke or chew on inanimate objects.
