Early Orthodontic Treatment (Phase 1) in Redding, CA

As a parent, you want to give your child every advantage in life—including the confidence to smile freely and the comfort of a healthy bite. What many don’t realize is that orthodontic care doesn’t have to wait until the teen years. In fact, the best time to begin may be much earlier.

At Shasta Orthodontics, we believe in the power of early intervention.

Our Phase 1 orthodontic treatment offers a gentle, proactive approach to support your child’s dental development, correct harmful habits, and help prevent more serious problems down the road. This early care lays the foundation for a strong, healthy smile—and often makes future treatment easier and more effective.

This isn’t just about straight teeth. It’s about supporting your child’s comfort, self-esteem, and lifelong well-being.

The Lasting Impact of Early Orthodontics

Our early orthodontic treatment in Redding, CA , offers powerful benefits that can shape not only your child’s smile—but their confidence, comfort, and overall growth:

  • Gently guides jaw development so there’s room for permanent teeth to come in naturally
  • Helps break habits like thumb sucking or tongue thrusting that could affect future alignment
  • Reduces the risk of painful impactions by creating space before adult teeth erupt
  • Improves facial balance and function, supporting natural development
  • Can shorten or simplify future treatment, reducing the need for more complex orthodontic care later on

Common Questions About Phase 1 Orthodontics

We understand that early orthodontic treatment is a big decision. Here’s what many parents want to know:

  • What Is Phase 1 Treatment? It’s orthodontic care done while your child still has baby teeth, using appliances like expanders and braces to guide healthy development.
  • How Do I Know If My Child Needs It? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an evaluation by age 7. Signs your child may benefit include early crowding, jaw shifts, or noticeable bite issues.
  • What Happens After Phase 1? Once early treatment is complete, we monitor your child’s growth. Phase 2, often involving full braces, usually begins during the early teen years.
