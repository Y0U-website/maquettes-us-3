CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics) is an innovative technology that allows us to craft and place your porcelain or zirconia dental crown in a SINGLE visit. CEREC utilizes advanced technology to measure your tooth and oral structures digitally.

The digital impressions are sent to an on-site milling machine, where your dental crown will be precisely made and then permanently cemented, within an hour or two. You don t need 2 injections!

On your dental implant day, when you walk out of our office, your dental implant will be in place and you will have a temp tooth there if available! It will be just like a regular tooth.

With the most advanced technology, we will have your surgical guide ready before you come in after computerized simulation, your surgery time will be minimal!

Most importantly, your dental implant will be where it should be to support your crown the best!
