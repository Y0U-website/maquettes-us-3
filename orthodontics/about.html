About Us

Dr. Jennifer Sipkin

Dr. Sipkin is a top graduate of Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and was accepted into NYU’s orthodontic residency program immediately after graduation. She feels honored to have studied under some of the greatest orthodontists and craniofacial specialists New York has to offer. She has even been featured on CBS news for her expertise in Invisalign treatment. Her most recent publication, which can be found in The Angle Orthodontist journal, focuses on the efficacy of Invisalign treatment for patients with periodontal disease.

Dr. Sipkin is a highly skilled orthodontist and dedicated community leader. She has been involved in numerous community service fundraisers and serves on the board of Project Broadway, an organization that brings arts education to underserved communities. She is a mom to 2 beautiful daughters and spends her free time taxiing them around to their various sports and arts activities. In addition to her practice, she has also taught dental students at NYU and Columbia and has conducted research on a variety of scientific topics, ranging from craniofacial disorders to periodontal disease.

Dr. Daniella Davis

Dr. Davis is passionate about providing the highest level of care to each of her patients and works tirelessly to give them a smile they deserve. She is extensively trained in the most advanced technologies and appliances. She is always seeking out creative solutions to orthodontic problems. Dr. Davis is dedicated to staying up-to-date on the latest research and advancements in the field and is an active member of various orthodontic societies. Her commitment to excellence and attention to detail make her a valued member of the Sipkin Orthodontics team.

Born and raised on Long Island, Dr. Daniella Davis is proud to serve the community as a skilled orthodontist at Sipkin Orthodontics. After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry and being inducted into the prestigious OKU Honors Society, Dr. Davis completed her specialty training in Orthodontics and a Masters in Dental Science at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in New Jersey. With a deep understanding of the latest techniques and technologies in the field, Dr. Davis is able to offer her patients the most advanced and effective orthodontic treatments available.

When she’s not in the office helping patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles, Dr. Daniella Davis enjoys spending time with her husband and children. Her hobbies include baking, painting, and all things Disney, and she loves to take on fun craft projects. With an outgoing and energetic personality, Dr. Davis enjoys getting to know each and every patient and building lasting relationships with them. Her commitment to outstanding patient care extends beyond the office, as she is dedicated to supporting her patients and their families in all aspects of their lives.

Dr. Alexandra Stein

Dr. Stein combines advanced clinical expertise with a warm, approachable style that puts patients of all ages at ease. She takes the time to understand each patient’s goals and tailors treatment to fit their unique needs. With a background in academic research and a passion for lifelong learning, Dr. Stein stays up to date on the latest techniques to ensure the highest standard of care.
