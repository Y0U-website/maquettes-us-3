Excellence in Cosmetic Dentistry

Tooth Whitening We offer home-use bleaching to brighten teeth that are discolored, stained or darkened from injury.

Porcelain Veneers These thin, custom-made, tooth-colored shells are designed to cover the front side of the teeth where spaces, permanent stains, poorly shaped or slightly crooked teeth have occurred. The procedure requires just a few appointments.

Smile Makeovers This service combines a variety of techniques, which might include bonding, straightening, tooth colored fillings, crowns or porcelain veneers to create the patient’s ideal smile. We also offer tooth colored porcelain onlays as well as tooth colored fillings.

Excellence in Dental Health Care

We promote preventative care and stress regular dental checkups, which can do more than brighten your smile. They give your dentist an opportunity to check for many things, including periodontal (gum) disease, oral cancer, TMJ or misaligned bite relationships and more. Our office also performs root canals, bridges, implants and extractions as well as sealants and fluoride treatments. Teeth are meant to last a lifetime, but the warning signs that they are in jeopardy can often be difficult to recognize. Allow us to help you by checking regularly to ensure you maintain excellent dental health.

Our hygienists provide superior patient education, soft tissue management and home care instructions. You can be assured that our staff members share a personal commitment to your health.
