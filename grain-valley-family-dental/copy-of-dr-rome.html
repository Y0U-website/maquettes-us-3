Dr.Camille Pellegrino DDS

Camille R. Pellegrino graduated Summa Cum Laude from Missouri Southern State University in 2011 with a bachelor’s degree in BioChemistry. She then received her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Missouri Kansas City in 2015.

In 2021 Dr. Pellegrino continued in public health but moved back to Missouri while working at McCoy Samples Mattingly out of Chillicothe. Most recently, she has been serving the population of Independence as a part of Swope Health Services.

Dr. Pellegrino is thrilled to be given the opportunity to work with the amazing teams at Grain Valley Family Dental and Odessa Family Dental in serving their community of patients. She enjoys her free time in the many local restaurants, music venues and parks this gorgeous city has to offer.
