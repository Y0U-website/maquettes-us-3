What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. It is precisely placed into the jawbone, where it naturally bonds with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a strong, stable foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

The result is a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth—allowing you to smile, eat, and speak with confidence for years to come.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation & Comprehensive Evaluation

Every implant journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation to create a personalized treatment plan. Using advanced 3D CBCT imaging, the OSYNTRA team evaluates bone quality, anatomy, and the ideal implant position to ensure precise, predictable outcomes. If additional bone support is needed, bone grafting may be recommended before implant placement.

Bone Grafting (When Needed)

A strong foundation is essential for long-term implant success. If bone has been lost due to tooth extraction, periodontal disease, or natural resorption, bone grafting may be recommended to rebuild the implant site. Because these procedures are performed in-house, your care remains seamless from consultation through recovery.

Precision Implant Placement

Once your treatment plan is complete, your implant is placed with meticulous precision using advanced surgical techniques and digital planning. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, with sedation options available for added comfort, and most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the experience is.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next three to six months, the implant naturally bonds with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable, long-lasting foundation for your new tooth. A temporary restoration may be provided when appropriate to maintain your appearance and function during healing.

Final Restoration

After healing is complete, your restorative dentist places a custom crown, bridge, or implant-supported restoration designed to blend naturally with your smile. Throughout every phase, OSYNTRA works closely with your restorative dentist to ensure a seamless process and exceptional long-term results.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants.

You may be a candidate if you have:

Adequate bone support,

or are a candidate for bone grafting to rebuild the implant site.

Healthy gums,

or periodontal disease that can be treated before implant placement.

Good overall health,

with any medical conditions or medications carefully considered during treatment planning.

A commitment to good oral hygiene,

which is essential for long-term implant success. While non-smokers generally have the highest success rates, many smokers can still be successful candidates with appropriate management.

Every patient is unique. The best way to determine whether dental implants are right for you is through a personalized consultation. At OSYNTRA, we take the time to understand your goals, evaluate your oral health, and develop a treatment plan designed to provide predictable, long-lasting results.

Implant Services

Single Tooth Implants

Replace a Missing Tooth with a Natural-Looking Solution

A single dental implant is the gold standard for replacing an individual missing tooth. A small titanium implant is placed into the jawbone to replace the tooth root, where it naturally bonds with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration. Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached, restoring a tooth that looks, feels, and functions like a natural one. Unlike a traditional bridge, a single implant does not require altering neighboring healthy teeth and helps preserve the jawbone after tooth loss.

Multiple Tooth Implants

Restore Multiple Missing Teeth with Confidence

When several teeth are missing, dental implants can support a fixed bridge without replacing every tooth with an individual implant. This approach restores chewing function, improves appearance, and provides a stable, long-term alternative to removable partial dentures.

Immediate Implant Placement

Replace Your Tooth Sooner

In many cases, a dental implant can be placed immediately after a tooth is removed, reducing treatment time while helping preserve the natural bone and gum contours. This approach may also minimize the number of surgical procedures needed during treatment.

Using advanced 3D CBCT imaging, we carefully evaluate each extraction site to determine whether immediate implant placement is appropriate. When conditions allow, this technique provides an efficient path toward restoring your smile while maintaining excellent esthetic and functional results.

Full-Arch Dental Implants

A Stable Alternative to Traditional Dentures

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, full-arch dental implants provide a secure, fixed alternative to removable dentures. Four to six strategically placed implants can support a complete arch of replacement teeth, restoring comfort, function, and confidence.

At OSYNTRA, every full-arch case is customized using advanced digital planning and 3D CBCT imaging. We carefully evaluate your bone, bite, and overall oral health to determine whether you’re a candidate for treatments such as All-on-4®, All-on-6®, or same-day temporary restorations.

Peri-Implantitis Treatment

Protecting Your Dental Implants

Peri-implantitis is an inflammatory condition that affects the gums and bone surrounding a dental implant. If left untreated, it can lead to bone loss and, in severe cases, implant failure.

We diagnose peri-implant disease using advanced imaging and comprehensive clinical evaluation. Treatment may include specialized cleaning, laser therapy, regenerative procedures, or surgical treatment to eliminate infection, restore healthy tissues, and protect the long-term success of your implant.
