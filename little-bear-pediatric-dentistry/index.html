The practice

At Little Bear Pediatric Dentistry, we believe every child deserves compassionate care, every parent deserves support without judgment, and every visit should feel welcoming, personal, and fun.

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist | Sensory-Friendly Care | Special Needs Expertise | Serving Malvern, the Main Line, and Chester County families.

You can call us anytime. If we’re unavailable, your call will be answered by our answering service .

We care for children of all needs and abilities throughout Chester County and the Upper Mainline, focusing on the best treatment plan for each individual. Our local hospital privileges also allow us to provide the highest quality care to medically-compromised patients and children with specific healthcare needs.

Bringing your child in for regular dental exams is one of the best ways to establish good habits and lifelong oral health. In our Malvern office your child will receive an individualized oral health assessment followed by guidance on oral healthcare at home and a preventive plan in the dental office that will suit their needs.

We’re here for you and your child when life throws unexpected events your way. If your child is dealing with a dental emergency, we’ll help get them out of pain and prioritize their care with same-day appointments whenever possible.

Our connections with local medical centers enable us to provide services and support for children of all needs and abilities. From the routine to the complex, we’re here to address your child’s dental needs and help them achieve the healthy teeth and gums they deserve.

We believe visiting the dentist should feel easy and welcoming, not stressful.
