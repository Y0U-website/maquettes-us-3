Emergency Dentist

  • Root Canals: Infections can cause intense tooth pain. Our root canal treatments remove the infection and save your tooth.
  • Tooth Pain Relief: We diagnose the cause of your tooth pain and offer immediate solutions to bring you relief.
  • Cracked Tooth Repair: Cracks can lead to serious complications if not treated promptly. We provide effective repair solutions to prevent further damage.
  • Broken or Knocked-Out Teeth: Accidents happen. We offer urgent care to repair or reattach broken or knocked-out teeth.
  • Lost Fillings or Crowns: Losing a filling or crown can be distressing. Our emergency dental services ensure swift replacements to protect your tooth.
  • Abscess Treatment: Dental abscesses are painful and potentially dangerous. We treat infections to prevent them from spreading.

Why We Might Offer Nitrous Oxide

Your comfort is maintained throughout dental procedures with nitrous oxide sedation as an option for relaxation. It helps patients during their treatment or for patients who have dental anxiety.

  • Dental Anxiety
  • A Fear of Visiting the Dentist
  • An Overly Sensitive Gag Reflex
  • A Fear of Needles (Aichmophobia)
  • Extreme Teeth Sensitivity
  • Feelings of Claustrophobia While in the Dental Chair
  • Decreased Sensitivity to Local Anesthesia
  • Difficulty Controlling Movements
  • Special Needs (Including Physical, Cognitive or Behavioral)
