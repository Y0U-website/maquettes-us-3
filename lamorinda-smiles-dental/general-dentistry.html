Our first comprehensive appointment allows us to create a custom oral health plan to keep your teeth healthier.

Pediatric Dentistry

We love to see kiddos of all ages! Set up your child for a lifetime of excellent oral health. At Lamorinda Smiles, we work to ensure your child has a fun, comfortable, and positive experience. From teethers to teenagers, we provide exceptional care for your exceptional child.

Crowns and Bridges

Crowns and bridges are an effective way to repair damaged teeth and replace missing ones. Using our CEREC milling machine, we can even make your crowns while you wait!

Implants
