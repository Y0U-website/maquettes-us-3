What are the procedures needed for care?

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, and in some cases, a lifetime. Regular dental checkups and cleanings, along with good oral hygiene habits, can help ensure the longevity of your dental implants.

What are dental bridges?

Dental bridges are artificial teeth that fill the gap of one or more missing teeth. They are anchored to the remaining natural teeth or dental implants on either side of the gap.

How long do dental bridges last?

Dental bridges can last for 5-15 years or longer with good oral hygiene. However, the lifespan of a dental bridge depends on several factors, including the materials used, the location of the bridge, and the patient’s oral hygiene habits.
