Full-Service Implant Dentistry

From Surgical Placement to Final Restoration

Monroe Family Dentistry offers comprehensive implant care all under one roof. You won’t need to bounce between specialists—we take care of you from start to finish.

We begin with a thorough exam, digital imaging, and a conversation about your goals. If bone loss has occurred, we may recommend a preparatory procedure like bone grafting or a sinus lift to ensure long-term implant success.

The implant—a small titanium post—is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, it will fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a sturdy foundation for your new tooth.

Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment (connector piece) and custom-fabricate your restoration. This may be a dental crown, implant-supported bridge, or full-arch denture—depending on how many teeth you&#x27;re replacing.

Bone Grafting & Sinus Lifts

Some patients need additional support before receiving dental implants. If you’ve experienced bone loss due to missing teeth or periodontal disease, bone grafting or a sinus lift can help rebuild your jaw structure.

These procedures enhance bone volume so your implant can fuse properly, improving your long-term outcomes.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to last 25 years or more with proper care—many last a lifetime. The restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) may need to be replaced every 10–15 years due to wear, but with good hygiene and regular checkups, your smile can stay strong for decades.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with sufficient bone density and no active gum disease are candidates. If you&#x27;ve been told you&#x27;re not a candidate before, don’t give up—modern techniques like bone grafting and mini implants have expanded options for many patients.

The best way to find out is to schedule a consultation at Monroe Family Dentistry. We’ll assess your oral health, review your medical history, and create a treatment plan that fits your goals.
