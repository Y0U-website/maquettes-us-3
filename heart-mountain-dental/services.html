Our Dental Services

Comprehensive Care for Every Stage of Life

At Heart Mountain Dental, we believe in providing high-quality, personalized dental care that evolves with your needs. From your child’s first checkup to advanced dental treatments later in life, we’re here to support your smile every step of the way. Below are the core services we provide—all delivered right here in Cody, Wyoming, by a team that truly cares.

Preventative Dentistry

Protecting Your Smile Starts with Prevention

Preventative care is the foundation of lifelong oral health. Our preventative services include regular cleanings, dental exams, x-rays, fluoride treatments, and oral cancer screenings. These routine visits help catch small issues before they become bigger problems—saving you time, money, and discomfort in the future.

Whether you’re coming in for a six-month cleaning or your child is due for their first exam, we make each visit as comfortable and informative as possible. Prevention is key and it’s never too early or too late to start.

Restorative Dentistry

Bringing Function, Comfort, and Confidence Back to Your Smile

When damage, decay, or missing teeth affect your ability to eat, speak, or smile, our restorative dentistry services can help. We offer fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures tailored to restore both the appearance and functionality of your teeth.

Our team takes time to walk you through every step, offering solutions that fit your lifestyle and budget. Whether it’s repairing a chipped tooth or replacing several, our goal is always the same: helping you feel confident and comfortable again.

Surgical Dentistry

Need a tooth extraction or another oral surgery? We offer in-office surgical services so you don’t have to travel far for the care you need. From simple extractions to more complex procedures, our approach is always gentle, precise, and focused on your comfort and recovery.

We understand that surgery can be intimidating. That’s why we take extra time to answer your questions, explain your options, and ensure you feel supported every step of the way.

No matter your dental needs, you’ll find trusted, experienced care at Heart Mountain Dental. Call today to schedule your appointment and take the first step toward a healthier smile.
