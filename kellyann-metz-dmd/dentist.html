Dentist | Tenafly, NJ

I became a dentist because I want to have a personal impact on the lives of people by helping them look and feel their best.

Instead of joining a large, impersonal dental practice. I bought a home-based practice and took root in a local community.

My husband and I sent our daughter to our terrific public schools. I became involved in civic organizations and am a huge supporter of our first responders.

Now, almost 30 years later (boy, how did that happen!), I’m lucky to have patients who are multiple generations of the same family. Moms, police officers, brides, even tattoo artists are finally getting the smile of their dreams.

Even though my office is laid back and homey -- all the treatments are super high tech. I hold certifications in many of the latest innovations.

Without going to an expensive specialist, you and your family can receive the latest dental treatments in a highly personalized atmosphere. And to make sure you get the best care you need, I accept most insurance plans.

If you are a fearful patient or have not been to a dentist in a while, no worries. Let me and my team surprise you with some TLC. You can even get a complementary massage.

I hope to see you soon -- in the meantime, be sure to check out some of our videos at the bottom of this site!.
