Let Us Introduce Ourselves

The dental practice team at Joy for Dentistry, LLC is proud to provide you with expert consultation and treatment.

A 2004 graduate of University of Pennsylvania Dental School, Dr. Lyou has been practicing in the Delaware Valley for 20+ years. Dr. Lyou is a firm believer that healthy attractive smiles can make a big impact in her patients’ lives. She uses the latest techniques taught by some of the most respected clinicians in the world. She specializes in making every smile a masterpiece.

Dr. Lyou is an active member of the Lower Bucks County community and frequently visits local schools to educate children on the importance of dental hygiene.

Tefta is our caring member of our practice. Her contagious personality and gentle technique make your routine dental visit an enjoyable experience.

An integral member of Joy for Dentistry. Sunny can calm the most nervous patient and believes a positive first visits to a dental office can create a lifetime dedication to good oral health.
