About Us

Dr. Aida Padron

Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry

Dr. Aida Padron is dedicated to treating each patient as a unique individual, not just another appointment on the calendar. She recognizes that every person who walks through her doors brings their own story, concerns, and experiences. With a compassionate ear and genuine care, Dr. Padron takes the time to listen attentively.

With over 40 years of dental expertise, she delivers personalized care tailored to the specific needs and worries of each patient. Unlike some practitioners who may rush to treatment, Dr. Padron prioritizes understanding your concerns, ensuring you feel comfortable and confident throughout your visit.

Visiting the dentist doesn’t have to be a stressful or daunting experience. Dr. Padron strives to create a welcoming, supportive environment where you are treated like family respected, understood, and cared for every step of the way.

Education

Dr. Padron is a highly accomplished dentist with extensive training in multiple dental specialties. She completed a Dental Sleep Medicine residency at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston and holds a fellowship in Implants Prosthodontics from the American Academy of Implants Prosthodontics. Additionally, she pursued a fellowship in Craniofacial Orthodontics at the University of Illinois Medical Center. Dr. Padron also completed advanced education in general dentistry at New York University College of Dentistry.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University and holds a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Central University of Venezuela. Furthermore, she received specialized training in interceptive and preventive orthodontics in Caracas, Venezuela. This diverse and comprehensive background allows her to provide expert care across a broad range of dental disciplines.
