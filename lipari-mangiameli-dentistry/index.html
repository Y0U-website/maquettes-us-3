The practice

Dr. Richard Lipari, known to many patients as Dr. Rich , earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from NYU College of Dentistry before completing an advanced residency at Montefiore Medical Center with a focus on cosmetic & implant dentistry . During his residency, he was recognized for his excellence in aesthetic dentistry and thoughtful approach to patients with anxiety.

Today, Dr. Lipari practices in the heart of Chappaqua, NY, where he combines advanced clinical expertise, meticulous artistry and personalized care to create healthy, natural-looking smiles. His comprehensive approach encompasses cosmetic, restorative and general dentistry, with treatment tailored to each patient s individual goals and facial aesthetics.

Utilizing state-of-the-art digital technology, including digital scanning and 3D printing, alongside the expertise of world-class ceramists, Dr. Rich is known for transforming worn, stained and misaligned teeth into smiles that are beautiful, balanced and remarkably natural .
