About Magnolia Family Dental in Sandy Spring, MD

Dr. Reese Ruder’s passion for dentistry runs in the family, as his father, brother, and numerous other family members are also dentists. Dr. Reese attended the University of Florida (Go Gators!) for his undergrad and obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Having completed extensive implant training in the U.S. and internationally, Dr. Reese is dedicated to strengthening his neighbors’ smiles with the same level of compassion and skill he’d give his own family.

Dr. Reese understands the importance of creating an environment that is comfortable, convenient, and nonjudgmental, where the whole family can thrive. He provides a full range of general dentistry treatments, from preventive care like mouthguards and family dentistry to whitening services, restorative solutions such as white fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures. Dr. Reese’s experience also includes treatments like root canals and tooth removals, ensuring all your dental needs are met in one familiar place.

Outside the office, Dr. Reese finds his greatest joy in the simple moments of family life alongside Dr. Tamaskar, their two adorable kids, and the family dog. An enthusiast for an active lifestyle, he is also a dedicated tennis player who enjoys the focus and energy of a match on the court.

Dr. Sarika Tamaskar

Credentials & Memberships

Dr. Sarika Tamaskar was born and raised right here in Olney. Her deep community roots allow her to build lasting relationships grounded in local trust, shared values, and a genuine investment in her neighbors’ well-being. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Maryland, College Park, and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Dr. Tamaskar then expanded her expertise through a residency in Advanced General Dentistry at the V.A. Medical Center in Dayton, Ohio.

Dr. Tamaskar is dedicated to creating a welcoming and compassionate environment for patients of all ages. As an experienced family dentist, she offers a full range of general dentistry services for adults and children, including white fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, teeth whitening, night guards, and more. She has also completed extensive additional training to be able to offer specialty procedures, such as Invisalign® orthodontics and treatment for snoring, sleep apnea, and TMJ/jaw pain.

In her spare time, Dr. Tamaskar enjoys spending time with Dr. Reese, their two young kids, and their goldendoodle. She loves taking her family on new adventures, including trying new restaurants in the area and traveling.
