Meet Dr. Gandhi

Dr. Jigar S. Gandhi earned his doctorate from the State University of New York at Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine in 2009. He graduated top of his class and was one of four selected from his graduating class to be inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon. This is a National Honorary Fraternity of Dentistry where membership is based upon excellent scholarship, exemplary traits of character, and potential qualities of future professional growth and attainment.

Dr. Gandhi’s exceptional care extends beyond dental health; he is committed to nurturing the well-being of the entire individual. His approach is characterized by a compassionate, gentle, and attentive manner, fostering an environment where patients feel heard. He is dedicated to not only perfecting smiles but elevating overall health.

He is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), International Congress of Implantologists (ICOI), American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, New York State Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), Global Institute for Dental Education, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Fraternity. He was Chief Resident at James J. Peters VA Medical Center in The Bronx, New York.

Dr. Gandhi also practices biological dentistry . To learn more, visit his website www.toothandbody.com .
