Misti Raman, DMD, was born and raised in the greater Columbia area. After graduating from Brookland-Cayce High School , she proceeded to earn her bachelor’s degree from the University of South Carolina and her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the Medical University of South Carolina s College of Dental Medicine. Her postgraduate education has focused on occlusion and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Misti’s dedication to serving others and exceeding patients’ expectations form the foundation of the extraordinary Irmo Smiles patient experience.

Dr. Misti has two beautiful children, Lexi and Jimmy, and enjoys traveling and spending time on Lake Murray. She also appeared on CBS’s The Amazing Race in the fall of 2014.
