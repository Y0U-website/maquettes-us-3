The practice

Dr. Nina Capretta brings a unique perspective to dentistry, beginning her career as a dental hygienist before earning her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. With years of experience in private practice, she combines technical expertise with a deep commitment to patient-centered care.

A lifelong learner, Dr. Capretta is actively involved in continuing education and study clubs, ensuring she stays at the forefront of dental advancements. She is a proud member of the ADA, NYSDA, the Ninth District Dental Organization, and is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality care for every patient.
