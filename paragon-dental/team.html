0 Meet the Team Matthew Carter, DDS Patients’ goals are unique to each patient, so there’s no “one size fits all” standard that can be applied to everyone across the board. Treatment plans are tailored to each person’s lifestyle, attitude, goals, and his offerings as a dentist.

They wanted a practice that would address improving the patient’s health needs and lives in order to bring about a positive healing and advancement. In this environment, patients learn about how an individual’s lifestyle pattern has put them where they are today, and what needs to be done to create an environment of improvement. Dr. Carter believes that when health is fully achieved, it means less dental work (which is what everyone wants!).

Dr. Carter feels that his purpose is fulfilled when a patient puts their health first and is able to maintain their dental care without needing his assistance.

Coatlicue Torres-Carter, DDS

Dr. Coatlicue Torres-Carter is a California Native from San Diego.

Creighton University Dental School brought her to the Midwest where Dr. Torres-Carter found the love of her life, made a home, and built a dental practice dedicated to individualized care. Both she and her husband Dr. Carter serve as the “dynamic duo” of Holistic Dentistry, sharing their passion for patient-first, total body healing.

Together, they have begun their amazing search and journey to combine world-class Bioesthetic Restorative Dentistry with Biological Dentistry, to include biological compatible materials with respect for a deep understanding of approaching dental disease and complications from a holistic (whole body) approach.

Like her husband, Dr. Coatlicue Torres-Carter (referred in practice as Dr. Torres), shares a love of the education environment. She prides herself in creating a space where staff and patients alike can learn about oral health in a safe, open-minded space.

She is a firm believer in patients having all the information available so they can make the best choice for themselves. This core value has lead her on an extensive journey around the world seeking the best practices, best materials, highest-quality treatments, and techniques- right down to the toothbrushes patients receive at cleanings.

Her primary focus in-practice is providing patients with treatments to help with their airway and bite. Her passion for education comes from her deep love for providing exceptional, individual care to each patient. Her goals are to truly uncover our patient’s desires for health, function, appearance, and long-term desires and create an environment for the body that promotes healing.

Outside of Dentistry, Dr. Torres-Carter loves to cook! She is currently working on building her own cook book to create delicious, well balanced food meant to help heal the body through nutrition.
