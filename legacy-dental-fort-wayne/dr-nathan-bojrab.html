Dr. Nathan Bojrab

Experienced Fort Wayne Dentist

I grew up in Fort Wayne. As a child, I imagined I would play professional sports as a career. However, as a student at Bishop Dwenger High School, I went on a dental mission trip to Honduras. This experience – seeing firsthand how improved oral health changes lives – shaped my passion for the field of dentistry.

After dental school, I was thrilled to get back to my roots and practice dentistry in the city that I love. My daily goal at Legacy Dental is to build lifelong relationships with patients, as well as create a comfortable and enjoyable environment for them at every office visit.
