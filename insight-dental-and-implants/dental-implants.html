Dental Implants in Oklahoma City (OKC), OK

  • What is the recovery time after getting dental implants? Recovery time varies, but most patients can resume normal activities within a few days. Full healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone, a process called osseointegration, typically takes three to six months.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Coverage for dental implants depends on your insurance provider. Some plans cover portions of the procedure, such as extractions or restorations, while others may offer no coverage. Contact your insurance provider for details, or let us help you explore financing options.
