About Us in Des Plaines, IL

Her journey in the field began in 2009, following her General Practice Residency at Loyola University Medical Center and her education at Tufts University in Boston for both college and dental school. Dr. Paula further honed her expertise through a two-year Pediatric Dental Residency at the University of Southern California. During this time, she received additional training at a Level 1 Trauma Center, Children’s Hospital Los Angeles, further solidifying her commitment to providing the highest standard of pediatric dental care.

A Joyful Approach to Pediatric Dentistry

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Paula finds genuine joy in working with children, hearing their stories, and making them feel at ease. Her role allows her to help children find comfort in their smiles and ensure they leave each appointment wanting to come back. Dr. Paula’s personalized approach is not just professional but also fun, making the dentist’s office an enjoyable experience for her patients.

Her patients appreciate the exceptional care she provides, making every visit to the dentist a positive and cheerful one. Being a mom herself, she knows firsthand that each child is unique and needs specialized care and attention!

Life Beyond the Clinic

Outside of her professional life, Dr. Paula is an advocate for a healthy lifestyle, enjoying activities like Pilates and yoga. Her love for rescuing animals has brought two wonderful dogs, Izzy and Wrigley, into her life.

As a dedicated mother of two sons, she actively participates in their lives, coaching her son’s soccer team and engaging in various activities, including sports and cub scouts.

Passion That Shines Through

Dr. Paula’s enthusiasm for her role at Kind Kids Pediatric Dentistry makes every day, even the most challenging ones, a source of fulfillment and joy.

At Kind Kids Pediatric Dentistry , we are more than just a dental clinic—we are a caring, fun, and child-friendly space where kindness is at the heart of everything we do.
