At Implant and Sedation Dentistry of Charleston, we understand the importance of not just getting dental implants but maintaining them properly for their longevity. Located in the heart of Goose Creek, SC, our practice, led by Dr. Ryan Gilreath, DMD, is dedicated to providing comprehensive care for your dental implant needs.

Dental implant maintenance is crucial for the long-term success and health of your implant-supported restorations. Unlike natural teeth, dental implants are not susceptible to cavities, but they can still suffer from peri-implant diseases if not properly cared for. Regular maintenance ensures that your implants remain a healthy and stable foundation for your prosthetic teeth.
