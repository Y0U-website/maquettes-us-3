The practice

Restorative dentistry helps maintain overall and dental health even when the health of one or more teeth or the surrounding areas is compromised. The goal of restorative dentistry is to restore the function and integrity of the tooth structure, function, and health, while maintaining a natural appearance.

Our Westport dental office specializes in cosmetic dentistry. This means that we not only pay attention to the health and dental care of your teeth, but we also pay close attention to aesthetics. We want you to maintain great oral health but we also want you to have healthy teeth, a beautiful smile and the confidence to smile often!

We offer comprehensive general and cosmetic dentistry services, including:

General Dentistry – General dentistry is the preventive and diagnostic care needed to keep your teeth and gums healthy. We offer comprehensive exams, professional dental cleanings, root canals, digital radiographs, and extractions.

Cosmetic Dentistry – If your teeth are healthy, but you’re not happy with their appearance, we offer a wide range of cosmetic dentistry procedures that can correct aesthetic imperfections and help you achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. These include veneers, cosmetic bonding, teeth whitening, and Invisalign.
