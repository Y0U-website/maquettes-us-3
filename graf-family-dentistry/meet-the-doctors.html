Meet The Doctors

Dr. Graf spent a year in private practice after graduating from UK. He then entered the Indian Health Service and provided dental care to the people of the Rosebud Lakota Sioux Indian Reservation in South Dakota. Afterward, he practiced in a community health center in Missouri for several years. Dr. Graf is excited to have returned to Louisville and is committed to providing quality dentistry to patients in his hometown, less than a mile from the house he grew up in.

Dr. Graf and Dr. Wilson-Graf have two wonderful children — a son, John, and a daughter, Emerson. When Dr. Graf is not spending time with his family, he enjoys golfing, reading, and watching sports. Dr. Leigh Wilson-Graf Dentist Dr. Wilson-Graf hails from Lexington, Ky where she attended Tates Creek High School. She received her Bachelor of Science from the University of Kentucky. Dr. Wilson then completed her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University’s College of Dentistry.

After dental school, Dr. Wilson-Graf spent a year in a corporate dental practice. Following that year, Dr Wilson-Graf joined the Indian Health Services, and along side her husband, served the people of the Rosebud Lakota Sioux Reservation. After her time in South Dakota, Dr. Wilson-Graf spent several years providing care at a community health center in Missouri.
