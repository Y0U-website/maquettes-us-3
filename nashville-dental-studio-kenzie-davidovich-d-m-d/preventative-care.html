Regular teeth cleanings and dental check-ups every six months are essential for preventive maintenance. After your initial hygiene appointment, our expert team, including a hygienist and a dentist, will offer personalized guidance on the optimal interval for your needs. In some instances, more frequent recall visits may be required, especially if there are concerns regarding periodontal health or oral hygiene. Be assured that our professional team is dedicated to providing personalized attention and guidance tailored to your individual dental needs.
