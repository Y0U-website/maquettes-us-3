How Long Does It Take to Get an Implant?

Getting dental implants is a process that occurs over several months. The process begins with discussing your goals and expectations, followed by a thorough exam and x-rays to ensure you are a good implant candidate.

The next step is placing the titanium implant post surgically in your jawbone. This procedure is performed comfortably under local anesthesia.

After placement, the implant heals and integrates with the jawbone. This process typically takes 3 6 months. During this period, we fit you with a temporary restoration so you can continue to smile confidently.

The final step is placing the crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile and restore function.

Dental Implants: Long-Term Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are a popular and effective choice for many patients. They consist of titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, which act as a stable foundation for replacement teeth.

Over time, the posts fuse with your bone in a process known as osseointegration , providing strength and stability that mimics natural tooth roots. Dr. Mowlajko s expertise in placing and restoring implants means you receive cohesive, expert care in one convenient location in Anderson, SC.
