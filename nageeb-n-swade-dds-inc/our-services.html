Cleanings (Prophylaxis) and Scaling and Root Planing (Deep Cleaning)

In addition to daily brushing and flossing, annual visits to the dentist for cleanings are important to remove any residual plaque and to assess the state of both the teeth and the gums. In some cases where there is a lot of plaque below the gum line, a deep cleaning may be needed. Plaque that builds up below the gumline can lead to periodontal disease where such concerns as bone loss or inflammation of the gums may occur. A deep cleaning will remove such buildup, help reduce inflammation and help lead to a healthier mouth.

Fixed Partial Dentures (Bridges) and Dental Implant Restorations

Both fixed partial dentures (bridges) and dental implants are viable treatment options to replace a missing tooth. With bridges, a portion of the two adjacent teeth are shaved with a multi-unit fixed prosthesis fabricated to replace the missing tooth. With dental implants, a portion is placed in the bone with a resulting crown that restores the tooth in the mouth. In our office, we fabricate bridges and restore dental implants.
