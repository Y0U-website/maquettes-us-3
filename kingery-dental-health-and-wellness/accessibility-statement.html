Accessibility Statement

We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of content on our website, but please do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or require assistance.

If you have trouble seeing web pages, the US Social Security Administration may have resources that will help you. You can also use the Accessibility features of your computer and/or software to:

– Use the keyboard to navigate screens – Increase text size – Magnify your screen – Change background and text colors – Make your mouse pointer more visible

If you are looking for mouse and keyboard alternatives, speech recognition software such as Dragon Naturally Speaking may help you navigate web pages and online services. This software allows the user to move focus around a web page or application screen through voice controls.

We are committed to ensuring we meet all of your needs. If you require assistance with any of the content on our website, please call our office at (630) 789-0900 and we will do everything in our power to assist you. Thank you!
