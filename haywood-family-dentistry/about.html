At Haywood Family Dentistry, we provide affordable, quality dental care for all who need it, specializing in working with patients with dental anxiety and sensory sensitivities. We take extra care to create a calm, patient-centered environment where trust and empowerment are at the heart of everything we do.Whether you’re new to the area, haven’t seen a dentist in a while, or are a long-term patient we’re thrilled to care for you!

A patient- centered office for your oral care.

At Haywood Family Dentistry, we understand the dental chair isn’t everyone’s favorite place to be, but we’re dedicated to making your experience as comfortable and enjoyable as possible. Our job is to meet you where you are, and help you get where you’d like to go. Whether you have dental anxiety, sensory processing needs, or just looking for routine cleanings and care, we’re here for you!

Mountains are places of divine encounters and revelations.

Okay, no, we can’t quite make your visit to the dentist “divine.” However, at Haywood Family Dentistry we’re dedicated to making your experience as comfortable, informative, and stress-free as possible. We take the time to listen to your concerns, prioritize your goals, and create a comprehensive plan to get you where you want to be with your oral health.
