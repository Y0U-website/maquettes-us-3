Office

Dr. Wallach will take the time to listen to your personal oral health concerns and goals to be sure you get the smile you want. Our general dentistry consultations and routine dental appointments include a comprehensive examination of the teeth, mouth, gums, jaw joints, and other support structures. Dr. Wallach will look for signs of wear and instability, which are often the cause for cosmetic concerns.

Our hygiene program is the cornerstone of our practice. As partners in your health, our hygienists provide comprehensive preventative care and in depth education to identify potential issues early and maintain optimal oral health. We will help you establish a program specific to your individual needs. Many recent studies have demonstrated that maintaining healthy gums is essential not only to your oral health, but your overall health as well.

  • Comprehensive examination
  • Thorough teeth cleaning
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Periodontal (gum) disease screening
  • Advanced diagnostic technology
