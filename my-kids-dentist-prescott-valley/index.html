Hannah Peterson, RDH, and Dr. Brown founded the My Kids Dentist Affiliate Dental Hygiene Practice. This branch of My Kids’ Dentist, along with our other Registered Dental Hygienists, provides cleanings, x-rays, sealants, and screenings at most schools in the Quad Cities area and donates a portion of those services to kids in need.
