About Us

Dr. Claire Stickler

Dr. Claire Stickler, or "Dr. Claire” as her patients call her, is known for her caring touch and upbeat personality, making even the most nervous patients comfortable. It has been her lifelong dream to have a practice of her own where she can serve her community with compassion, honesty, and quality dentistry. Her goal is to make every dental experience a positive one – changing the status quo of being miserable at the dentist! She has created an environment focused around being kind, relaxed, and non-judgemental.

Dr. Claire graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a History degree before moving to Ann Arbor (Go Blue!) to complete her dental degree at the University of Michigan. She has completed continuing education courses in oral surgery, and Invisalign.

After making the move from St.Joe, Dr. Stickler is already feeling right at home in the Sturgis community, and is eager to meet new faces and to welcome her patients into a newly renovated space.

Dr. Stickler loves exploring new places and even met her husband while studying abroad in Athens, Greece. While not at the office, she enjoys cooking, being on the water, and chasing after her bernedoodle puppy!
