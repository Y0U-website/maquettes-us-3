Get to Know Dr. Amanda Hyland

As our lead dentist, Dr. Hyland can say that she truly looks forward to coming to work each day. She and her talented team work hard and have fun doing it, which is why they can’t wait to welcome in new patients. Interested in learning more about her? Just keep reading below.

Why Did You Want to Become a Dentist?

For Dr. Hyland, the decision to become a dentist was an easy one. She wanted to improve people’s lives, and oral healthcare is a great vehicle for that type of care. This passion is also what led her down the rabbit hole of wanting to provide TMD treatment, orthodontics, and sleep apnea therapy to her patients.

As a young, fit woman, Dr. Hyland regularly wondered why her jaw would click as she ate, why she didn’t have much of a chin, and why she snored at night. Through her post-graduate studies, she learned that it all came back around to the position of her lower jaw, which is a factor that’s normally overlooked but can cause so many serious side effects, including constant migraines, snoring, and more. Being able to decrease chronic facial pain can be a life-changing treatment for so many people, which makes her proud to be a general dentist who focuses on much more than just teeth alone!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Hyland majored in biology (with a minor in business management) at Luther College in Decorah, IA before pursuing her dental doctorate at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry. Since then, she has completed over 200 hours of focused continuing education regarding TMD treatment, orthodontics, and sleep apnea therapy in combination with general dentistry.
