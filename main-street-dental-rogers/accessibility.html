Accessibility

Here are some optimization steps you can with your computer and/or web browser to enhance your online experience.

You may want to consider mouse and keyboard alternatives, such as speech recognition software, which may help you improve your navigation of websites. This webpage includes information on some speech recognition software options .

Deaf and hard-of-hearing users may want to take advantage of closed captioning for videos on websites. Learn how to turn captioning on and off in YouTube . You also have the ability to adjust your device’s volume and the media player’s volume for optimal listening.
