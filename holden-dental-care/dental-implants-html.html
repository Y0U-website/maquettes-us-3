What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants refer to small posts that are surgically placed beneath a patient’s gumline, directly into their jawbone tissue. Made from biocompatible materials, these rods can fuse with bone matter and effectively function as replacement roots for missing teeth. Once they’ve healed, a custom-made dental restoration can be added into the mix to fully restore any number of missing teeth, no matter where they’re located inside the mouth.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Dental Implant Consultation Your dental implant journey begins with a consultation at our office, where Dr. Ma will determine if you’re a good candidate for implants by examining your smile, reviewing your dental wellness, and ensuring your jawbone tissue is healthy.
  • Dental Implant Surgery We’re proudly partnered with some of the best, locally-renowned dental implant experts in Holden, ensuring that your dental implant procedure is tailored precisely to your specific needs.
  • Dental Implant Osseointegration/Abutment Following your dental implant surgery, the posts will need several months to heal and fuse with your jaw tissue. From there, abutments can be attached, which eventually connect your replacement teeth.
  • Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s) Once everything has healed, your custom-made dental restoration can be attached, which fully restores your oral functionality and lets you navigate daily life with a complete set of lifelike, durable teeth.
