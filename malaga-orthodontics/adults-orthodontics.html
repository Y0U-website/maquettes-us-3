Adult Braces Lancaster Ohio

It is never too late to get the beautiful healthy smile you’ve always wanted. We offer full treatment or smile touch-ups with clear aligners or braces.

Believe it or not, teeth continue to shift beyond the teenage years. Previously straight teeth can move, causing jaw pain, brushing difficulties, or dissatisfaction with your smile. Receiving orthodontic care will help prevent dental issues such as tooth decay, gum disease, and abnormal tooth wear. Dr. Malaga uses the latest braces and aligner technology to address these dental issues and more, while giving you the beautiful, healthy smile you’ve always dreamed of.
