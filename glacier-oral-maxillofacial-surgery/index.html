Wisdom Teeth Extractions Wisdom teeth, often called “third molars” are the very last tooth in your jaw. There are many people that are able to keep their wisdom teeth and use them just like any other tooth. However, for many people this is not the case. Sometimes wisdom teeth can cause pain, dental cavities, or even infections. If this is the case, it is often recommended to have them removed.

Bone Grafting Bone grafting is the term for a set of surgical procedures that are used to rebuild or augment bone in the jaw. It is most commonly used to prepare the jawbone for dental implants. During the procedure, the site is prepared and the graft material is carefully placed at the site needing enhancement. This promotes new bone growth, ensuring a solid foundation for future dental implants.

Full Mouth Dental Implants Full mouth dental implants, often called “All-on-X” or even “Full Arch” dental implants, offer a permanent and effective solution for those with multiple missing or damaged teeth. Dr. Noblitt has unrivaled experience in full mouth dental implants - he has placed thousands of these implants and trained many surgeons on the latest techniques.
