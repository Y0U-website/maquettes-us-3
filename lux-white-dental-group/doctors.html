DOCTORS

Dr. Lux attended Butler University where she received her undergraduate degree in Biology. She obtained her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Marquette Dental School in May, 2011. She completed her residency in the graduate program for Prosthodontics at Marquette Dental School in 2014 where she received a masters in Prosthodontics.

Dr. Lux maintains active membership in the American Academy of Prosthodontics, American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics, American Dental Association, and Wisconsin Dental Association. She is a past President of the Burlington Dental Society. Currently, Dr. Lux is a region 4 trustee for the Wisconsin Dental Association and is serving as a delegate to the American Dental Association House of Delegates.

Dr. Lux is published in the Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry:

Lux, Laura H., "A Comparison of Two Methods to Articulate a Maxillary Cast with Lateral Cephalometry" (2014). Master’s Theses (2009 -). Paper 252. http://epublications.marquette.edu/theses_open/252

Thompson GA, Casey N, Franciso P, Lux LH, Wiens JP, 2021. Comparison of two arbitrary cast transfer systems with kinematic facebow for mounting a maxillary cast on a semi-adjustable articulator, The Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry, ISSN 0022-3913, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prosdent.2020.12.023.

She was also an adjunct faculty member at Marquette University School of Dentistry, but continues to Lecture to graduate programs on the topics of implant restoration and prosthetic dentistry. For a complete list of credentials please email us!

Dr. Molly Conlon graduated from Northwestern University in 2013. She went on to complete her master’s degree in Oral Health and dental training at Boston University, graduating with her DMD in 2019. She was heavily involved in leadership and community service roles throughout her time at BU, serving as a member of the American Student Dental Association Board of Trustees and on several educational committees. She recently completed a one-year internship at Froedtert Hospital and Medical College of Wisconsin.

Dr. Conlon is a member of the Council on Advocacy for Access and Prevention for the ADA, as well as the current President of the Burlington Dental Society.

Her areas of interest include esthetic dentistry, special needs, and oral and maxillofacial surgery.

In her free time Dr. Molly Conlon enjoys biking, yoga, exploring museums, and playing with her Labrador, Brodie.

Dr. Molly Conlon is passionate about patient-centered comprehensive dental care and maintaining smiles for a lifetime.

Dr. White is a Lake Geneva native and has been in practice since 1977. He attended the University of Wisconsin- Madison for undergraduate studies and obtained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Marquette University Dental School in 1977.

Dr. White maintains active memberships in the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Burlington Dental Society.

Dr. White taught in the complete denture and fixed prosthodontic courses at Marquette University School of Dentistry from 2007-2014.

He regularly attends continuing education. For a complete list of credentials please email us!
