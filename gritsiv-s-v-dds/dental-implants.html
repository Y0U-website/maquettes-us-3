The entire procedure usually takes 3 steps.

Surgical Implantation After your exam and once it has been determined that Dental Implants are the right choice for you. You will be scheduled for your placement appointment. At this appointment Dr. Gritsiv will surgically place the implant into the jawbone. It replaces the missing tooth root and ensures that your dental restoration is stable.

Healing Next, we will allow the bone around the implant to heal. This process is called osseointegration and it’s this process that makes the implant such a strong, long-lasting option. Your jawbone grows around the implant, bonding to the biocompatible post and holds it in place. Every patient is different so the length of time for this process varies. Some patients might need to wait until the implant is completely integrated, which can be several months, before we can attach your custom-made implant crown to the implant.

Restoration The final step is the placement of your custom-made implant crown or crowns. This crown will be designed to blend in with your other teeth. In some cases, if we are replacing more than one tooth a custom-made bridge or denture will be fit to your implants. **Often while we are fabricating your custom-made crown, bridge, or denture we will give you a temporary to use until your permanent replacement is available.

The entire implant process generally takes six to eight months. Most patients do not experience a disruption to their daily lives while the implant is integrating into their bone.

Am I a Good Candidate for a Dental Implant?

Do Dental Implants Require Special Care?

No, dental implants do not require any additional maintenance than your natural teeth. It is essential that you brush and floss regularly, as well as visit your dentist at least twice a year for dental cleanings.

Will People Know I Have a Dental Implant?

Not unless you tell them! Dental implants are not noticeable. They are designed to look and feel just like your natural teeth.

The health of the gums is vital to maintaining lasting implant therefore it is imperative that patients continue with regular professional cleanings/check-ups and patient home care. Each patient is different, and success relies upon many factors but many dental implants last a lifetime.

Dentures cannot be made into implants. However, it might be possible to have implants placed beneath existing dentures to aid in the stabilization and support for those dentures.

Are Dental Implants Removable Like Dentures?

No, dental implants are placed into your jawbone and over time the bone actually fuses to the dental implant.
