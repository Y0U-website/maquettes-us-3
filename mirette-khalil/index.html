Our mission here at OC Pearl Family Dental is to provide you and your family with the highest quality dental care in a comfortable setting. Under the skilled guidance of Dr. Mirette Khalil, our compassionate team provides high quality comprehensive dentistry in a state-of-the-art facility. We pride ourselves on continually learning the latest techniques and use only the highest-quality materials to ensure our patients have a dental experience unlike any other.

We believe that whatever your dental needs may be, you will be treated with the highest quality dentistry available and with the compassion, the respect, and the care you deserve.
