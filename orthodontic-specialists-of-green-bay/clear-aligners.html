The Clear Aligners treatment process From the beginning straight to the end

1. Your first consultation

At your initial consultation, we will discuss your orthodontic needs and evaluate whether clear aligner treatment is right for you.

2. Custom your treatment plan

We will use a 3D digital scanners and cutting-edge software to create fast, precise 3D digital images of your teeth. Then we will map out a precise treatment plan, including the exact movements of your teeth and how long your treatment will be. You’ll even get to see how your teeth will move and preview your new smile.

3. Your clear aligners

Your custom-made aligners will be made using our in-house 3D printing lab supported by the uLab systems cutting-edge software and materials. The best part, you’ll receive aligners in a higher quality control with quicker turnaround times. They’re virtually invisible, so most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them during your teeth straightening. Wear your invisible aligners 20 to 22 hours a day for the best results, and take them out only to eat, brush, and floss.

4. Your progress

As you wear each set of aligners, your teeth will gently and gradually shift into place. We may recommend that you begin wearing a new set of aligners every week and schedule checkups every six weeks or so.

FAQs About Clear Aligners

How do custom clear aligners straighten my teeth?

Clear aligners use a series of virtually invisible, custom-molded plastic trays to gradually and gently shift your teeth into alignment. Each tray is worn for a specific period (usually 1 to 2 weeks) before moving on to the next set in your customized digital plan.

How can I take care of my teeth and appliances if I’m wearing a clear aligner?

ALWAYS remember to brush your teeth after every meal and floss at least once a day. Make sure to use toothpaste that contains fluoride. When you take out your aligner to eat, make sure you brush your teeth, floss, and remember to keep it safe in its container so that it does not get lost or broken. Keep it clean, too, by brushing it gently with a toothbrush.

How many hours a day do I need to wear my clear aligners for treatment to work?

For your treatment to stay on track and be successful, you must wear your clear aligners for 20 to 22 hours every single day . They should only be removed when you are eating meals, drinking liquids other than plain water, or brushing and flossing your teeth.

Can I accelerate my clear aligner treatment at OSGB?

Yes! At Orthodontic Specialists of Green Bay, we can combine your clear aligner therapy with advanced acceleration technologies like OrthoPulse® or VPro™ to safely speed up bone remodeling and help you achieve your results more efficiently.

Can I drink coffee, tea, or soda while wearing my clear aligners?

No, you should always remove your clear aligners before drinking anything except plain, room-temperature water. Hot liquids like coffee or tea can warp the medical-grade plastic of the aligners, while dark or sugary drinks (like soda, red wine, or juice) can stain the plastic and trap cavity-causing sugars directly against your teeth.
