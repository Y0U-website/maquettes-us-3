Same-Day Dental

At our office, we recommend using mini dental implants for their numerous benefits and same-day stability.

  • More accessible: Mini implants are small in diameter, allowing them to fit even in minimal bone structure. For patients with deteriorated jaws, a mini implant is a perfect alternative to traditional implants. Additionally, these slim titanium posts fit into smaller spaces, allowing patients to easily replace front teeth, which can be challenging with larger implants.
  • Less expensive: With fewer components, fewer appointments, and fewer additional procedures, mini implants are about half the cost of traditional implants!

Frequently Asked Questions

At our office, we prioritize your comfort and satisfaction. Because we use a local anesthetic to numb your mouth before the procedure, most patients report little to no discomfort!

Additionally, with their minimally invasive procedure, mini implants heal much faster, resulting in less discomfort after placement. If you experience any pain or discomfort, it’s usually short-lived and easily managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

The cost of your same-day dental implants depends on a lot of factors.

Because we use mini implants, rather than traditional dental implants, you’re already saving time and money. However, the number of implants you require will influence the price of your procedure. A single crown may require one implant, which is relatively inexpensive. A roundhouse bridge, however, may require 10 to 12 implants, which can significantly impact the cost.

Fortunately, we make your dental care needs affordable with flexible financing solutions! With manageable monthly payments, patients can afford to restore their smiles, even on a budget.

Same-day dental implants last as long as you take care of them!

Just like your natural teeth, implant restorations require a good oral health routine and frequent dental visits. Brushing, using a water flosser, and visiting us at the Mini Dental Implant Center of America in Springfield, MO, are great ways to keep your smile lasting as long as possible!

With the proper care and maintenance, your implants can last you a lifetime.
