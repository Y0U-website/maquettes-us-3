Meet Dr. Brady Ellis

Dr. Ellis was raised locally in Colfax, WA. While he had to move far away for dental school, he and his wife are excited to be back in the area to join our dental family! Dentistry allows Dr. Ellis to do what he loves: work directly with people to improve their lives and live in this area that means so much to his family. Dr. Ellis can’t wait to meet you and give you the best dental experience you’ve ever had!

The Professional Side

EDUCATION

It was during his time in dental school that Dr. Ellis set aside sports and discovered new interests, especially a love for designing and building. He especially enjoys building furniture and working on houses. As much as he loves carpentry, Dr. Ellis realized he misses the impact of working directly with people.

​​​​​​​Being a dentist brings his two passions together: creativity and working with people in a meaningful way. Dr. Ellis is excited to be back on the Palouse and to join our dental community! Brady is a dedicated and valued member of the Jonny Fisher team, and makes a real difference in the lives of all his Patients.

The Personal Side Dr. Ellis was born in Spokane and raised in Colfax, WA. He kept busy playing football, basketball and baseball throughout high school. Dr. Ellis met his future wife, Katie, during his junior year. They both grew up locally and love the area and are thrilled to be back on the Palouse to raise their growing family.
