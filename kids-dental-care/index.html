Our pediatric dentistry team specializes in providing gentle, compassionate care tailored to children, including advanced sedation services for a stress-free experience. We prioritize creating a safe and comforting environment to ensure every child receives the highest quality dental care.

If your child has a cardiac, intellectual, behavioral health, or other condition that prevents them from being treated in an outpatient office setting, Dr. Ammar Idlibi is here to help. We proudly partner with Connecticut Children’s Medical Center (CCMC) and Hartford Hospital to provide exceptional care and support.

We are dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for children with special needs, ensuring their unique requirements are met with compassion and expertise. Our team creates a welcoming and supportive environment to make every visit a positive and comfortable experience.
