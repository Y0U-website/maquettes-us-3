Oral Surgery

  • What is Oral Surgery? Oral surgery refers to any mouth operation. It includes procedures done on your teeth, gums, tongue or any other area inside your oral cavity. Some of the most common include: Wisdom tooth removal to treat or prevent pain and infection
  • Tooth extractions to remove decayed or damaged teeth
  • Dental bone grafts to increase the volume and density of your jawbone
  • Dental implants to replace missing teeth

What Conditions are Treated with Oral Surgery?

There are many reasons why you might need to come see us but some reasons your dentist may recommend a consultation are:

Abscessed teeth Broken teeth Bone loss Extensive tooth decay Impacted teeth Loose or missing teeth Oral cancer Noncancerous lumps Sleep apnea TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) disorders

What are the Benefits?

Oral surgery can correct conditions for you that nonsurgical treatments can’t.
