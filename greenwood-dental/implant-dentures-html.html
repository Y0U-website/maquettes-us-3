Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

A reassuring thought is even if you think you are not eligible for dental implants, the truth is that most adults suffering from complete tooth loss can enjoy these permanent prosthetics. You don’t need to settle for less when you can embrace the possibility of a fully functional, longer-lasting, life-changing tooth replacement solution.

Your consultation with Dr. Greenwood will include a full examination of your oral cavity. Considered the following factors, he will determine if you’re ready to move forward with dental implant surgery:

  • Your oral health and if you show signs of gum disease
  • Your overall health and if you’re in good shape to undergo minor oral surgery
  • Your jawbone density and if you need a bone graft to create a more stable foundation

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

The benefits of combining traditional dentures and dental implants will leave you feeling more confident than ever before. By bringing these two treatments together, you will discover:

  • You can eat the foods you want without worrying about your teeth falling out or slipping
  • You can embrace a better quality of life
  • You can enjoy a more youthful appearance because your jawbone remains stimulated, and your cheeks appear fuller because of your new teeth
  • You can easily maintain your smile each day without additional equipment or cleaning supplies
  • You can reduce your risk for systemic issues caused by tooth loss
