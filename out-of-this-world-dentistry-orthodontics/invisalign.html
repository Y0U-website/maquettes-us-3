Invisalign

  • Removable convenience: Take aligners out for eating, brushing, and special occasions.
  • Comfortable edges: Smooth plastic reduces irritation compared with brackets and wires.
  • Predictable planning: Digital 3D planning shows estimated tooth movements.
  • Hygiene friendly: Easier brushing and flossing may support gum health during treatment.
  • Fewer adjustment visits: Many visits are quick check-ins to monitor progress.

How Invisalign Works

While every plan is customized, most Invisalign journeys follow similar steps:

  • Consultation And 3D Scan: A clinical exam and digital impressions capture your teeth for planning.
  • Custom Plan: A computer model maps staged tooth movements and estimates the treatment timeline.
  • Aligner Series: You receive numbered trays. Most patients change to the next set every 1–2 weeks.
  • Attachments And Aids: Small tooth-colored bumps, elastics, or minor reshaping may enhance control.
  • Progress Checks: Visits every 6–10 weeks verify tracking and comfort.
  • Refinements If Needed: Extra aligners fine-tune results if minor adjustments are required.
  • Retention: After active movement, retainers maintain your new tooth positions.

Your Day-To-Day Experience

Most people adapt to aligners within a few days. It is common to feel light pressure for 24–72 hours after switching to a new set. Speech may sound different briefly, then normalize as your tongue adapts.

Wear aligners 20–22 hours daily, removing them only for eating, drinking anything other than water, and oral hygiene. Rinse aligners when you remove them and clean them daily with a soft brush and cool water. Avoid hot water, which can warp plastic. Keep aligners in their case to prevent loss or damage.

Average treatment time ranges from about 6 to 18 months, depending on goals and case complexity. More complex movements and significant bite corrections can take longer and may require more appointments or refinements. Consistent wear is the most important factor in staying on schedule.

Invisalign and braces both straighten teeth effectively. Invisalign offers removability and a discreet look, while braces can be advantageous for certain complex tooth movements. An in-person evaluation helps determine which option best fits your bite and lifestyle.
