About

0 Where Every Smile Tells a Story At New Chapter Dentistry, we believe that exceptional dental care is about more than just teeth—it’s about people, relationships, and the stories we help our patients write with their confident smiles.

Our Vision & Values New Chapter Dentistry was founded on the principle that dental care should be a positive, transformative experience. Our core values are:

We aim for smiles all around: At the heart of our dental practice is the mission to create smiles—both literal and figurative—that radiate joy, confidence, and satisfaction.

Excellence is our motivator: Excellence drives us to deliver the highest standard of dental care, pushing our team to innovate and exceed expectations.

Communication nurtures success: Effective communication fosters trust, clarity, and collaboration among patients, staff, and providers.

Compassion is in our roots: We create a safe, supportive environment where patients feel understood, staff feel fulfilled, the community feels supported, and our care goes beyond teeth to touch lives.

A New Chapter in Dental Care

New Chapter Dentistry was created with a vision: to transform the way people experience dental care. Drawing from his experience in the field and commitment to continuing education, Dr. Vanfleteren has built a Bay City dental practice that puts patient comfort and exceptional results at the forefront of everything they do.
