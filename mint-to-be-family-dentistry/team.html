Alexandra Fushi, DMD, MPH

When not in the office, Dr. Allie works at Cleveland Children’s Surgery Center treating pediatric and special needs patients in the OR under general anesthesia performing everything from cleanings to full mouth rehabilitation.

She has authored and contributed to several research papers, articles, presentations, and guidelines in conjunction with the American Dental Association for which she won several research awards. Dr. Allie was also the Midwestern University’s College of Dental Medicine winner and national representative for an article she wrote on pediatric dental care and the importance of baby teeth in the annual American Dental Association’s Health Literacy Contest.

Due to her public health background, Dr. Allie is passionate about working with the underserved having done so both domestically and abroad. She has participated in several mission trips to Honduras and Samoa in the South Pacific, where she both assisted and provided oral health care to remote patient populations.

A lifelong learner, Dr. Allie is committed to continuing post-doctorate education, and is actively working toward her Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry. Committed to organized dentistry, she has served as an Alternate Delegate for the Greater Cleveland Dental Society on the ODA House of Delegates. She is also an active member of The American Dental Association, The Ohio Dental Association, The Greater Cleveland Dental Society, and The Academy of Sports Dentistry.

In January 2025, Dr. Allie was featured in the Academy of General Dentistry’s magazine, Impact , as one of 10 Dentists To Watch. A major achievement, highlighting a dentist’s dedication, innovation, and promise to shape the future of thoughtful, quality dental care.

Dr. Allie enjoys traveling, and spending time with family and friends, especially her rescue dog, Elvis.

Elvis
