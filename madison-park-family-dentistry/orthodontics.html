Our orthodontic services

Invisalign

Invisalign is a trusted clear aligner system that straightens teeth using a series of custom-made, removable trays. Each set of aligners gently guides your teeth into place, with no metal brackets or wires. Most patients wear their aligners 20–22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, brush, and floss. You’ll come in for regular check-ins to make sure everything is on track, and our dentist will oversee your care from start to finish. It’s a discreet, flexible way to improve your smile and your bite, without interrupting your routine.

OrthoFX

OrthoFX aligners are designed to shift your teeth while you sleep — worn for just 9 to 12 hours a day. Perfect for adults or teens who want less daytime wear, this system gives you the freedom to remove your aligners during work, school, or social events without slowing down your progress. Treatment includes weekly aligner changes, check-ins with our team, and access to OrthoFX’s remote support system. You’ll get consistent, personalized care with the added convenience of a low-maintenance schedule — ideal for busy lives.

Answers to your questions

What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is the area of dentistry that focuses on aligning your teeth and bite. It can improve function, make cleaning easier, reduce wear, and support long-term oral and overall health.

Why would I need orthodontic treatment? K L

You might benefit from Torrance orthodontics if your teeth are crowded, spaced out, crooked, or your bite feels off. Straightening your teeth can improve appearance, but it can also help with jaw comfort, chewing, speech, and preventing future dental issues.

Orthodontic treatment can work at almost any age, and more adults are choosing to straighten their teeth than ever before. For kids, we typically recommend an initial evaluation around age 7 to catch any developing issues early.

What’s the difference between clear aligners and braces? K L

Clear aligners are removable and nearly invisible, while braces are fixed to your teeth. Both can straighten your smile effectively, but aligners offer more flexibility and discretion, especially for adults and teens with busy schedules.

At Madison Park Family Dentistry, our clear aligner treatments are fully dentist-led and handled in-house. If your case is more complex or requires additional support, we’ll connect you with a trusted local specialist.
