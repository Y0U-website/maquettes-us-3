Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you need to replace anywhere from one to all of your teeth, dental implant restoration may be the right treatment option for you. Teeth that are severely decayed or damaged and need to be extracted can be replaced with natural looking prosthetics supported by dental implants.

If you have one or more teeth that have been missing for a while, the bone that supported those teeth may have deteriorated over time. You may not be a candidate for dental implants if the bone is not thick enough for the implant to be placed. However, with some preparation such as bone grafting, dental implant restoration may still be an option for you.
