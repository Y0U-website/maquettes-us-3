Frequently Asked Questions

How long does All-on-4 treatment take?

Timelines vary depending on individual healing and treatment complexity. Many patients receive a temporary fixed restoration shortly after implant placement.

Is All-on-4 permanent?

The implant foundation is intended to be long lasting. The final prosthesis may need maintenance or replacement over time.

Will I need bone grafting?

Some patients may require grafting, but the angled implant design often reduces the need for extensive bone augmentation.

Is the procedure painful?

Implant placement is performed with appropriate anesthesia to ensure comfort. Post-surgical soreness is typically manageable.
