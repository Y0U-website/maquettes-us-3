It’s a revolutionary way to replace an entire set of missing teeth, making life much easier for those who suffer from tooth problems, gum diseases, or injuries.

The practice

With only four very strong implants, a fixed set of teeth can be held in place, eliminating the need for removal. This treatment places the implants in strategic spots, with two at the back of the mouth for strength and two at the front for stability. This method is better for the patient’s bone density and means that future treatments to increase bone density are unlikely.

All-on-four dental implants are a vast improvement over traditional dentures or individual implants. They look and feel natural and are easy to care for, resulting in a better quality of life for patients. Additionally, the procedure is quick and uncomplicated compared to traditional implant operations, and can be completed in just one visit.
