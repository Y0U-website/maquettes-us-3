The Benefits of Cleanings & Exams

Protect Your Oral Health Routine cleanings and exams remove harmful bacteria and plaque buildup that daily brushing can’t always reach, reducing your risk of cavities, gum disease, and more serious dental concerns. A healthier smile starts with preventive care!

Catch Problems Early Dental exams allow our team to identify potential issues, like tooth decay or gum inflammation, before they become painful or expensive to treat. Early intervention can save you both time and money while preserving your natural teeth.

Boost Your Confidence Enjoy the feeling of a fresher, cleaner smile after every visit. Professional cleanings not only improve your oral health but also brighten your teeth, leaving you feeling more confident about your appearance.

What to Expect From Cleanings & Exams

A Warm Welcome & Discussion

A Warm Welcome & Discussion

Your visit begins with a friendly welcome from our team. Dr. Juste will take the time to learn about your dental history, concerns, and goals, so your care is tailored specifically to you. This is your opportunity to ask questions and share any anxieties, you’ll always be met with understanding and support.

Professional Cleaning

Professional Cleaning

Using state-of-the-art equipment, including the AIRFLOW Prophylaxis Master, we’ll gently and thoroughly clean your teeth. This innovative technology is part of the GBT (Guided Biofilm Therapy) approach to teeth cleanings that we use at our office. The Prophylaxis Master uses a mixture of air, water, and fine particles to remove plaque and tartar from your teeth both precisely and comfortably.

Comprehensive Exam & Personalized Advice

Comprehensive Exam & Personalized Advice

After your cleaning, Dr. Juste will perform a detailed examination, checking for signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns. X-rays may be taken, if needed. Based on Dr. Juste’s findings, she’ll provide personalized recommendations for maintaining or improving your oral health. Before you leave, we’ll ensure all your questions are answered and that you feel confident about your next steps.
