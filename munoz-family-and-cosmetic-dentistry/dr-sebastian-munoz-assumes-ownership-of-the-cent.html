Dr. Sebastian Munoz assumes…

Dr. Sebastian Munoz has purchased the dental practice of Dr. David Brusky and his Green Bay clinic, The Center for Dental Excellence, effective February 1. Brusky is retiring from dentistry after 27 years in the profession.

Munoz has been part of the Green Bay dental community for over 3 years. He grew up in the Fox Valley, graduating from Berlin High school and the University of Wisconsin-Oshkosh before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry.

“I firmly believe that all patients should be treated as if they are family,” Munoz says. “I am excited to provide my patients with the best possible dental care while building lifelong relationships.”

Munoz is a member of the Wisconsin Dental Association (WDA) and the American Dental Association (ADA). He is an avid fan of Wisconsin sports teams, and when not in the office enjoys spending time with his wife Katrina and their two daughters. You can also find him doing things outdoors or on the golf course.

The Center for Dental Excellence is located at 410 Security Blvd., behind the United Health Group building (formerly AMS). For more information or to schedule an appointment, call (920) 662-1440 .
