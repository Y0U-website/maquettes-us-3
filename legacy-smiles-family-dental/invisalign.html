Is Invisalign Right for You?

Invisalign may be a good option for patients with mild to moderate crowding, spacing, overbite, underbite, or crossbite concerns. Ideal candidates have healthy teeth and gums, realistic goals, and a commitment to wearing aligners as directed.

Some complex orthodontic needs may require braces or additional treatment options. During your consultation, our team will evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and goals to determine whether Invisalign clear aligners are the right approach.

Your Invisalign Journey

Consultation and Digital Planning

Your Invisalign consultation includes an exam, digital scan, and discussion about your smile goals. Your dentist will review your oral health, evaluate your bite, and explain whether aligners are appropriate for your needs.

Aligner Fitting

Once your aligners are created, you will receive instructions on how to wear and care for them. Some patients may need small tooth-colored attachments to help the aligners move teeth more precisely.

Active Treatment

Most patients switch to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks. Progress visits allow our team to check how your teeth are tracking and make sure treatment is moving as planned.

Retainers After Invisalign

After active treatment is complete, retainers help maintain your new alignment. Wearing retainers as directed is an important part of protecting your results long term.

What to Expect Day to Day

  • Wear aligners 20 to 22 hours per day
  • Remove aligners for meals and drinks other than water
  • Brush and floss before reinserting aligners
  • Expect mild pressure or tenderness when starting a new set
  • Clean aligners as directed and avoid hot water, which can warp the trays
