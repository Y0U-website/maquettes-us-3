What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first visit starts with a warm welcome from our team and a tour of our comfortable office environment. After reviewing forms and insurance information, we’ll guide you to a private exam room where we gather detailed diagnostic records.

We use advanced technology, including our i-CAT FLX Cone Beam Scanner and iTero Element digital scanner, to capture highly accurate images and digital impressions. These tools allow us to evaluate tooth position, jaw relationships, and overall oral health while creating a detailed 3D model of your smile.

Once we review your records, we’ll discuss your treatment goals, explain your options, and answer your questions. We believe every patient should understand the benefits and considerations of each treatment choice before moving forward. Most first visits take less than an hour and provide a clear roadmap for your orthodontic journey.

Innovative Orthodontics: What Are My Options?

Braces remain an important part of orthodontic treatment, but they are only one of many treatment options available. Our philosophy is that every patient deserves a solution that fits their individual needs, lifestyle, and treatment goals.

For younger patients, Phase 1 Orthodontics often focuses on growth modification and jaw development. These treatments typically occur between ages 6 and 10 and help create healthier jaw relationships as children grow. Many young patients benefit from maxillary expansion, which can reduce crowding, improve facial balance, and encourage proper jaw development. During this stage, we can also evaluate tongue function, speech patterns, and oral habits. When appropriate, we collaborate with speech pathologists and myofunctional therapists to help support long-term stability and healthy function.

As patients move into their teenage years, treatment often focuses on correcting bite concerns such as overbites while guiding adult teeth into ideal positions. We believe that healthy function and beautiful smiles go hand in hand, and our goal is always to achieve both.

Treatment Options We Offer

At Granite Coast Orthodontics, innovative orthodontics means offering a variety of treatment options rather than a one-size-fits-all approach. Every system ultimately works by applying gentle, controlled forces that allow teeth to move safely into healthier positions. The best choice depends on your goals, lifestyle, and clinical needs.

Every treatment option has advantages and limitations, and we openly discuss these factors during your consultation so you can make an informed decision.

  • Clear aligners for discreet treatment, especially popular among adults.
  • Radiance Clear Braces for a more esthetic braces option.
  • Metal (traditional) braces for reliable and effective treatment.
  • Gold braces for patients seeking a unique appearance.
  • LightForce Brackets with custom 3D-printed technology for precise tooth movement.
  • Lingual braces that fit behind the teeth and remain virtually invisible.
  • Phase 1 Orthodontics focused on growth modification and jaw development.
  • Orthodontic treatment for teens and adults.

Who Benefits Most?

Orthodontic treatment can benefit patients of nearly every age. Children often benefit from early evaluations that identify growth concerns before they become more significant. Teens appreciate treatment options that help them feel confident throughout school and social activities. Adults frequently pursue orthodontic treatment to improve both oral health and appearance while maintaining busy work and family schedules.

Our office is designed with convenience in mind. We offer private treatment rooms, a computer station, free Wi-Fi, and comfortable amenities that help make appointments easier to fit into your day. For patients who travel frequently or maintain demanding schedules, we create treatment plans that minimize disruption while keeping progress on track.
