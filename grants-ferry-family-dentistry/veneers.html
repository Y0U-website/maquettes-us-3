Veneers

  • How long do veneers last? With proper care and maintenance, porcelain veneers typically last 10-15 years. Routine dental visits, excellent oral hygiene, and the use of a protective nightguard can all extend the life of your veneers.
  • Does getting veneers hurt? The process of getting porcelain veneers usually involves the removal of a thin layer of the tooth’s enamel. Your dentist will ensure that you are comfortable and that your mouth is numb to make for a pain-free experience. After your veneers are placed, some people may experience mild sensitivity that usually resolves over the course of 1-2 weeks.
  • How much do veneers cost? This depends largely on your specific needs, such as the number of veneers needed and the health of the natural tooth. Generally, veneers cost about $1000-$1500 each. Dental insurance, discount plans, and our in house membership plan may help reduce the cost of veneers, although most insurance plans do not cover veneers. We understand that the cost of veneers can be a concern. We’ll work with you to explore payment options, and our finance team can discuss financing plans to make veneers more affordable.
  • Am I a good candidate for veneers? Veneers are a good option if your teeth are generally healthy but would benefit from minor cosmetic improvements, such as chipping, staining, or slightly misaligned teeth. Teeth with a heavy amount of decay or damage may be better suited with dental crowns rather than veneers.
  • How do I know I will like how my veneers look? There is no “one size fits all” in dentistry. Each person, each smile, each tooth is unique, and our mission at GFFD is to help you find the smile that best fits you and makes you proud. Dr. Green has dedicated countless hours to perfecting the craft of beautiful, natural looking veneers. She will work with you to be sure you are pleased with your smile.
