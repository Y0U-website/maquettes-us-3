The practice

All aspects of General and Cosmetic Dentistry are performed at our office. We believe that our patients’ time is important and we take pride in our “on time” appointments.

Dr. Gregory Perez has provided quality general dentistry in his hometown of Nutley, N.J. since April 1990. After graduating from Nutley high School, Dr. Perez received his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Boston College and his Doctorate of Medical Dentistry from Fairleigh Dickenson University.
