What Are the Downsides of Dental Implants?

Dental implants have many advantages; however, as with every dental treatment, there are a few downsides.

In the majority of cases, the benefits of dental implants greatly outweigh any disadvantages. Your dental implant dentists in Troy, IL, will happily speak with you about the risks of dental implants and put your mind at ease.

  • Dental implants can cost more upfront than other tooth-replacement alternatives
  • You may need to see your dentist for several appointments
  • A small procedure is required in which a local or general anesthetic is used
  • There is a low risk for complications such as teeth, blood vessels, and nerve damage
  • Dental implants are not suited for those who: Smoke
  • Those who are generally unhealthy
  • Those with a health condition that affects bone mass
  • Patients with a smaller jaw structure

What Are the Alternatives to Dental Implants?

If you do not qualify for dental implants because of costs or oral health issues, your dental implant dentists at our Troy office will consult with you to provide the best alternative treatment plan to replace your missing teeth.

Below are the alternatives we have to offer our patients:

  • Mini Dental Implants
  • Implant bridges
  • Snap-on dentures
  • Bridges
  • Dentures

What Are the Costs Of Dental Implants?

Prices for dental implants can vary depending on the individual situation; A single-tooth implant costs anywhere from $2,000 up to $6,000. Multiple dental implants may be required. However, the cost of each tooth will be lower as some costs are only applied once.

Dental implant costs depend on your unique case and treatment plan. For example, if you need complicated bone grafting, this will increase the cost of your treatment. Give our office a call to schedule a consultation and discuss which dental implant treatment is best for you as well as what the cost will be for your unique situation.
