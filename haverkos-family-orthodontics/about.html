About Us Our Mission Our mission is to build confidence by creating a comfortable, caring space where both kids and adults feel great about their smiles. We want everyone to leave our office with a boost of self-assurance and the smile they desire!

We’re dedicated to staying on top of the latest orthodontic techniques to give you the best care possible. As lifelong learners, we’re always mastering the newest technologies to give you the best treatment possible!

Dr. Stephen Haverkos, DMD, MSD

As a board-certified orthodontist, Dr. Haverkos takes a personalized, high-quality approach to your orthodontic care. There are many different approaches to creating your dream smile. Some patients prefer traditional braces while others prefer clear aligners. Dr. H and team will take the time to get to know you and listen to your concerns during your smile transformation. He will use the most advanced technology and technique to personalize a treatment plan that is best for you.

Steve and his wife Stephanie live on the west side with their three children and three rescue dogs.
