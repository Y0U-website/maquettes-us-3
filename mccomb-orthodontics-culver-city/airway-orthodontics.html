Can adults still expand the palate, or is it too late?

Adults can still expand the palate. The approach depends on how fused the palate has become. Children ages 7 to 10 use a simple rapid palatal expander. Teens and adults with a fused suture use MARPE, a mini-implant assisted expander that widens the jaw without surgery. Fully fused adult palates may need surgical SARPE, a coordinated procedure with an oral surgeon. For how adult MARPE works, who is a candidate, and what it costs, see our MARPE page .

What is MARPE and how is it different from SARPE?

MARPE, mini-implant assisted rapid palatal expansion, widens a fused adult palate at the bone without surgery. SARPE is the surgical version, used when the palate is fully fused and an oral surgeon releases the bone first. Both are ways to widen an adult jaw that a childhood expander can no longer move. We cover how MARPE works, who is a candidate, and exactly what it costs on our MARPE page .
