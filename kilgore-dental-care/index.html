We also want to provide excellent dental care to people without dental insurance. Our office can work with you to develop some payment options so you can receive the care you need. Our main goal is to help you have healthy teeth and gums for life.

Dr. Odhiambo was a military kid until the age of 16. While stationed in North Carolina, she began seeing her dentist, Dr. Gilliam, and by the age of 10 decided that she too wanted to be a dentist.

Dr. Odhiambo graduated with a Bachelor of Arts Degree in Psychology from Creighton University in 2006. Dr. Odhiambo graduated from the Creighton University School of Dentistry in 2010. Then in 2011, Dr. Odhiambo made the decision to move to East Texas and she has been here ever since. She is thankful to be practicing in Kilgore, TX and welcomes patients of all ages.

Dr. Odhiambo enjoys all aspects of dentistry and finds satisfaction in helping patients realize their dental dreams! Delivering comprehensive and optimal dental care is her goal.

When Dr. Odhiambo is not in the office, she enjoys reading and cooking.
