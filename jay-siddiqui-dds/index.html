Dr. Jay Siddiqui, DDS

Lead dentist and founder of Radiant Dental Care. Dr. Siddiqui built this practice around a simple idea — that precision implant and cosmetic dentistry should feel like…

The practice

You deserve a doctor who knows your name — not a number in a corporate system. Every case I take on, I plan personally, place personally, and restore personally.

Dr. Siddiqui trained in the hospital model — case conferences, cross-specialty planning, and the kind of rigor that produces results you can count on. Radiant Dental Care brings that discipline to a private setting where every patient gets the attention of one committed clinician.

After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, Dr. Siddiqui completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Virginia Commonwealth University, working alongside faculty at the forefront of dental research and digital technology. That training shapes how he practices today — he treats the whole mouth, not just the tooth in front of him, using the most current…

He’s placed more than 1,200 implants, built full-arch reconstructions for patients who had given up on their teeth, and restored thousands of single-tooth and cosmetic cases with in-house CAD/CAM milling. Early in his career he was an associate at larger practices — experiences that confirmed what kind of dentistry he wanted to build, and what kind he didn’t.

In 2020, Dr. Siddiqui opened Radiant Dental Care in Chevy Chase to practice the way he always wanted to — one doctor, one team, one standard. No associates rotating through. No upselling. No five-minute appointments. Every consult, every surgery, every final delivery is done by Dr. Siddiqui himself.

The practice invested early in the technology that actually moves the needle on outcomes: a CBCT 3D scanner for bone-level surgical planning, XNav dynamic navigation for millimetric implant placement, and in-house CAD/CAM milling so crowns and restorations are designed, fabricated, and delivered under one roof.

Dr. Siddiqui lives in the DMV with his family and spends his weekends cooking, travelling, and following (too) much basketball. He keeps his continuing education heavy — surgical and prosthetic courses, materials science, and the digital workflows that are rapidly changing what’s possible in private practice.

Dr. Siddiqui holds advanced training in the specific surgical and digital disciplines that matter most for implant and cosmetic dentistry.

Les soins

Your implant is restored with a fully custom abutment and crown — designed in Exocad and milled in-house on our DOF Craft 5X. Every case gets a custom abutment (never stock), designed, fit, and delivered in one visit.

The failing tooth is removed using techniques that preserve the bone socket — critical for implant success.

Every implant case at our Chevy Chase office is digitally planned before the first incision. That means smaller procedures, more predictable outcomes, and restorations that fit on the first try.

In-house design and milling of crowns, veneers, and full-arch prosthetics.
