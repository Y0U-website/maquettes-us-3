Frequently Asked Questions

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign uses a series of nearly invisible plastic aligners customized to your smile. Each aligner gently shifts your teeth a little closer to their final position, and you progress through the series until treatment is complete. Dr. Jaurequi designs your plan with specialized software so you can see how your teeth will move from day one.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time depends on how much your teeth need to move and how consistently you wear your aligners. Dr. Jaurequi reviews your progress every 4 6 weeks, and treatment finishes when you ve worn through your prescribed aligners and your smile looks straight and beautiful. Wearing your aligners about 22 hours a day keeps treatment on schedule.

Is Invisalign suitable for teenagers?

Yes Invisalign is a great option for many older teens, especially those who want a discreet alternative to metal braces. Success depends on wearing the aligners consistently, so it s a good fit for responsible teens ready to follow Dr. Jaurequi s wear instructions.
