Accessible Care Financial Assistance We believe every child deserves great dental care, regardless of financial circumstances.

Sliding Scale Fee Policy Kids2Dentist will see all patients regardless of the ability to pay. We offer a sliding fee discount based on family size and income. Discounts are available to patients with incomes less than 200 % of the Federal Poverty Guidelines.
