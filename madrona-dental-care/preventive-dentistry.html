Diagnosis and Prevention

We get it, it can be a bit too easy to put off your biannual dental exam and we don&#x27;t blame you, life gets busy! But remember, regular preventive care plays an important role in helping us monitor your oral health, identify changes early, and reduce the likelihood of more complex concerns over time.

For many patients, visits at least twice a year are appropriate. Others may benefit from a different recare schedule depending on their gum health, cavity risk, bacterial levels, medical history, or previous dental concerns. Dr. Gray will help determine the timing that makes the most sense for your individual needs, so your care is based on your health rather than a one-size-fits-all schedule.

Cleaning & Hygiene

Regular teeth cleanings and hygiene appointments are the foundation of preventive dental care. During your cleaning, we will thoroughly cleanse your teeth and gums, removing any plaque or debris from every surface in your mouth. You’ll even be able to reverse minor stains to keep your smile bright and beautiful. We recommend scheduling routine cleanings and exams every six months for most patients.

However, good oral hygiene shouldn’t stop when you leave our office. Keeping up with your at-home brushing and flossing routine is equally as important as attending professional cleanings, and is the best way to keep your teeth and gums healthy. Use a fluoride-rich toothpaste and a soft-bristled toothbrush to brush your teeth at least twice a day for two minutes at a time; and don’t forget to floss! Flossing at least once every day, ideally before going to bed, helps to get rid of food particles, plaque, and bacteria that can’t be removed with brushing alone.

Guided Biofilm Therapy

Guided Biofilm Therapy is a gentle, advanced approach to professional dental cleanings. Using airflow technology, warm water, and fine powder, we can remove plaque, biofilm, and surface stains more comfortably than traditional scraping alone. This helps us clean hard-to-reach areas with greater precision while protecting your teeth, gums, and dental work. Guided Biofilm Therapy can be especially helpful for patients with sensitive teeth, gum inflammation, implants, orthodontics, or a history of buildup.

Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays are safe, quick, and comfortable. Using the latest technology, we can create crisp, high-resolution images of your mouth’s structure, so we can make accurate diagnoses and treatment plans. Digital x-rays use only a fraction of the radiation as traditional film x-rays, so you can rest easy. Most patients receive one or two full sets of x-rays every year, but we may recommend additional imaging in the case of a dental emergency, or if we find an oral health issue that we need to investigate further.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments are one of the best ways to keep dental decay at bay. A simple, non-invasive procedure, fluoride treatments help to keep your enamel strong. After your cleaning, we’ll apply a fluoride-rich varnish or gel; we may ask you to refrain from eating or drinking anything for 30 minutes or so following your visit. Fluoride helps strengthen the enamel, making it more resistant to acids from plaque, bacteria, and sugary foods that cause cavities.

CariFree Gel

CariFree gel is a simple, supportive way to help protect your smile between visits. This professional-strength toothpaste is designed to strengthen enamel, support a healthier oral pH, and make it harder for cavity-causing bacteria to thrive. It can be especially helpful for patients who are more prone to cavities, have dry mouth, wear orthodontic appliances, or want extra support after restorative treatment. When recommended, we’ll walk you through how to use it at home as part of a personalized prevention plan.

Periodontal Care

We focus on keeping your gums healthy through personalized periodontal care designed to prevent, detect, and treat gum disease. From routine cleanings and thorough evaluations to advanced treatment options, we’re here to support every stage of your oral health. By combining patient education with a whole-health approach, we help you maintain not just a healthy smile—but overall wellness.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer screenings are part of every preventive visit and are an important way to monitor changes in your oral health over time. Consistent exams can help us identify concerning signs early, when additional evaluation and treatment may be more effective.

Our team will examine your gums, tongue, cheeks, lips, throat, and the other soft tissues in your mouth, looking for lesions, lumps, discoloration, or other abnormalities. If we notice anything concerning, we may recommend a biopsy or referral to a specialist for further evaluation. Regular oral cancer screenings help us stay proactive and give you greater peace of mind about your health.

Night Guards

Unconscious teeth grinding and clenching, known as bruxism, is a common issue that tends to happen at night while patients are asleep. However, night guards can help patients alleviate symptoms of bruxism by creating a barrier between your teeth that protects them from grinding-related damage. Depending on the severity of the case, night guards can be made from several different materials with varying degrees of softness.
