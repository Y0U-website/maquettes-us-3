Success Starts with Smart Habits

Top 6 Tips for Treatment Success

These are the most important things you can do

Never Miss an Appointment

Regular adjustment appointments are crucial for keeping your treatment on track. Missed appointments can extend your treatment time.

Brush After Every Meal

Food gets trapped easily in braces. Brush for 2 minutes after eating to prevent cavities and keep your braces clean.

Use Your Elastics as Directed

If prescribed rubber bands, wear them exactly as instructed. Elastics do important work moving your teeth and jaw!

Carry an Orthodontic Care Kit

Keep a travel kit with a toothbrush, toothpaste, floss, and wax for on-the-go care, especially at school or work.

Protect Your Investment

Avoid hard, sticky, and crunchy foods. One piece of hard candy can undo weeks of progress by breaking a bracket.

Communicate with Your Orthodontist

If something doesn’t feel right, hurts, or breaks, call us! Don’t wait until your next appointment.

Avoid These Common Mistakes

Learn from others and keep your treatment on track

❌ Skipping Elastics

Why it’s a problem: Elastics are crucial for correcting your bite. Not wearing them can significantly extend treatment time.

✓ Solution: Set hourly reminders on your phone until wearing elastics becomes automatic.

❌ Eating Prohibited Foods

Why it’s a problem: Hard or sticky foods can break brackets, requiring repair appointments and delaying progress.

✓ Solution: Keep a list of foods to avoid on your phone or fridge as a reminder.

❌ Poor Oral Hygiene

Why it’s a problem: Inadequate cleaning can lead to permanent white spots, cavities, and gum disease.

✓ Solution: Set a timer for 2 minutes when brushing. Make it a non-negotiable part of your routine.

❌ Missing Appointments

Why it’s a problem: Your teeth need regular adjustments to move properly. Missed appointments delay results.

✓ Solution: Schedule your next appointment before leaving the office. Add it to your calendar immediately.

❌ Not Reporting Problems

Why it’s a problem: Small issues can become big problems. A broken bracket left unfixed can halt tooth movement.

✓ Solution: Call the office immediately if anything feels loose, broken, or painful.
