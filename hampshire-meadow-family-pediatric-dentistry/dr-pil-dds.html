General Dentist Hadley, Amherst MA

Dr. Gregory Pil DDS

Dr. B. Gregory Pil graduated from Middlebury College with a Bachelor of Arts in Neuroscience with High Honors. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University College of Dentistry and completed a General Practice Residency at the VA Northern California Health Care System in Sacramento, where he provided care for medically compromised patients. During his residency, he gained advanced training in comprehensive care, including implantology, oral surgery, and complex restorative procedures.

Dr. Pil is dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered care with a gentle and thoughtful approach. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys exploring new restaurants (always looking for recommendations!) and staying active by playing tennis.
