Dr. Assabi A. Isaac , DDS

Dr. Isaac is a highly-trained oral and maxillofacial surgeon with a broad scope of practice and board certification from the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS).

Born and raised in California, she received her B.A. in Biological Sciences from California State University, Sacramento, and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland Baltimore School of Dentistry.

Following her residency at Temple University Hospital&#x27;s Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Program, Dr. Isaac served as an attending surgeon at St. Luke&#x27;s Hospital for 5.5 years performing extensive trauma surgery.
