Surgical Orthodontics in…

At One Orthodontics, we evaluate each case comprehensively and determine whether treatment should include joint care with a surgeon or if simpler orthodontic solutions will suffice.

  • Facial asymmetry or imbalance
  • Severe bite dysfunction (crossbite, open bite) that cannot be corrected by braces or aligners alone
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking, or airway/breathing issues related to jaw alignment
  • Relapse of previous orthodontic treatment with jaws now out of alignment

Our Surgical Orthodontics Process

Throughout the process, our team at One Orthodontics leverages advanced digital orthodontic technology such as CBCT (Cone Beam CT) imaging and 3D treatment planning software to ensure precise diagnosis and predictable results. Every surgical case is carefully coordinated to ensure your orthodontic and surgical stages work seamlessly together, minimizing downtime and optimizing recovery.

Post-surgery, we monitor healing and bite stability closely to make sure your results are long-lasting and comfortable. Many of our patients notice improved confidence, speech clarity, and facial balance within just a few months of recovery.

The entire journey may span 12–24 months or longer depending on complexity, and healing time varies. Rest, diet modification and follow-up visits are essential.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation & Scan – We begin with a full exam, digital imaging, and assessment of jaw, tooth and facial structure to determine whether surgical orthodontics is indicated.
  • Pre-Surgical Orthodontic Preparation – Before surgery, your teeth are aligned using our Braces or Clear Aligners services so that post-surgery your bite will fit naturally.
  • Surgery Coordination – Working with an experienced oral and maxillofacial surgeon, your jaw(s) are repositioned in the hospital setting under anesthesia.
  • Post-Surgical Orthodontic Refinement – After surgery, your orthodontic treatment continues to fine-tune tooth position, bite function, and aesthetic alignment.
  • Retention & Long-Term Stability – Once active treatment is complete, we provide retention (see our Retainers page) and maintenance to safeguard your results.
