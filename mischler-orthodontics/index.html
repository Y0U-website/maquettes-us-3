Welcome to Our Practice Welcome to Mischler Orthodontics, where we create confident smiles throughout Southern Indiana and the greater Louisville area.

For over 40 years, Dr. William Mischler has built our practice on a foundation of precision and genuine care. Now, joined by Dr. Alec McDonald, that commitment continues. Both doctors share the same University of Louisville School of Dentistry training and a philosophy of perfecting the details while never losing sight of the person behind the smile.

Whether you re considering treatment for your child or yourself, the right orthodontic care can improve bite function, make teeth easier to clean, and give you a boost of self-assurance.

At Mischler Orthodontics, we’ll ensure that you understand your treatment, feel involved in every decision, and always leave feeling confident and informed. Our approach blends small-town warmth with sophisticated care, providing the personal attention of a close-knit practice, backed by advanced techniques and technology.

Everything we do is intentional. From the initial consultation to the day your braces come off, we re precise, transparent, and genuinely invested in your outcome. This attention to detail shows up in results that patients tell us exceed their expectations.
