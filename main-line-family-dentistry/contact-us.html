Contact Bryn Mawr PA Dental Office

Welcome to Main Line Family Dentistry in Bryn Mawr, PA. Our dental care team welcomes children and adults, providing complete general, cosmetic and restorative dental services. From disease prevention to smile makeovers and implant dentistry, we are your dental care home on the Main Line. Our dental team serves patients in Bryn Mawr, Haverford, Bala Cynwyd, Wynnewood, Havertown, and the surrounding communities. Schedule a visit with one of our dentists to maintain, restore or enhance your natural smile.
