We’re here to provide a full range of dental services and help you get the care you need. At Marin Community Clinics, we are committed to treating our patients with the compassion and respect you deserve. We believe in providing comprehensive treatment and skilled dental services that address both your short-term and long-term dental care needs in an honest, judgment-free environment where it all comes down to great care.

Marin Community Clinics has taken all precautionary steps to ensure the safety of our patients, visitors, and staff during the COVID-19 pandemic.

We are here for you. It is normal to feel stress during this time. It is important for you to have the tools to feel less anxious. We are here to help.

We have the support services you need. Marin Community Clinics has on-site pharmacies, labs, and radiology in San Rafael and Novato. Enrollment Counselors are available to help you with your health insurance forms.

Health Hubs are open twice per week for free food distribution in San Rafael on Wednesdays and Novato on Thursdays.

Additional food resources (including pop-up pantries) can be found by visiting SF-Marin Food Bank Food Locator: https://www.sfmfoodbank.org/find-food/
