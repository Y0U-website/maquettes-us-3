Comprehensive Pediatric Dental Practice in Beaverton and Hillsboro, Oregon

Hi5 Dental offers friendly, personalized, and expert pediatric oral health care for children of all ages in Beaverton and Hillsboro, Oregon.

The practice

We believe that a positive first dental experience for kids paves the way for a lifetime of healthy smiles and happy visits.

We prioritize building trust and confidence with every child through patience and understanding.

Our kid-friendly waiting area and treatment rooms are designed to create a positive and enjoyable experience.

We use simple language to explain procedures and answer questions, ensuring everyone feels informed and at ease.

Pediatric Dental Services We offer a full range of pediatric dental services to make sure your kids’ great smiles start from a positive experience.

From first visits to regular checkups, we provide comprehensive care to keep your child’s smile healthy and cavity-free, while teaching them good oral hygiene habits for life.

We offer a variety of restorative and cosmetic treatments to address any dental concerns and ensure your child’s smile is both healthy and beautiful.

We provide early orthodontic evaluations and interventions to guide proper jaw development and tooth alignment, and offer custom-fitted mouthguards for sports activities.

Les soins

At Hi5 Dental, we believe that prevention is key to a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our primary and preventive dental care services are designed to keep your child’s teeth strong, healthy.
