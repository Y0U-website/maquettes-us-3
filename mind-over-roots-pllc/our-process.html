Appointment Day

We will begin our journey with a consultation visit. This is time for you to get to know us and talk to us about your previous experiences and what has been bothering you. At this point, we will need to take routine radiographs to help us diagnose your pain. Once we have gathered all of the data we need, we will discuss treatment options with you and answer any questions. We will tailor your treatment experience to your needs.

Depending on our plan, we can proceed to treatment on the same-day, if preferred, or schedule for a time that will fit with your schedule. Treatment may not always be completed during one visit; we will always prioritize what will be more comfortable and best for you.

Patient Forms

We would like to be well prepared before you even walk through our doors. Prior to your first visit we will send you an in-depth new patient form to know how we can best serve your needs and address any concerns you may have.

We accept and are In-Network with Most Major Dental PPO Insurances :

We do understand how financially daunting medical and dental treatment can be, and we will do our best to provide affordable yet high quality services.

We will assist you with your dental plan benefits by checking your eligibility/verification to have an estimate prior to your visit, submitting charges to your insurance company on your behalf, and accepting payment directly from them.

We ask patients to pay an estimated percentage on the day of services rendered based on the most accurate information we receive from your insurance company, however, upon receipt of your company’s dental plan benefit, if there is a difference, we will refund you or may need to bill any remaining balance to you.

Please be patient and cooperative with us during the process.
