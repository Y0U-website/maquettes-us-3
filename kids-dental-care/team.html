Dr. Ammar Idlibi, DMD

Owner & Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Idlibi has been certified as Diplomate’ of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 1994 and has since maintained his annual Board certification. A Board Certified Diplomate’ is a pediatric dentist whose credentials have been verified, who has successfully completed voluntary examinations, and who demonstrates specialized knowledge and skill. A Diplomate is dedicated to providing exceptional patient care through a voluntary commitment to lifelong learning.

Source: American Board of Pediatric Dentistry website. Who is a Diplomate?

To find a Board-certified pediatric dentist in your area, click here and enter your zip code.

Dr. Idlibi is a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. The term Fellow in a professional association often refers to a person who has distinguished themselves above the standard norm, either by publications or contributions to the profession. AAPD Member resources; Fellow. https://www.aapd.org/resources/member/ .

Dr. Idlibi has had operating privileges at Connecticut Children Medical Centre (CCMC) since 2003 and had provided specialized dental care to thousands of children under general anesthesia.

Dr. Idlibi primary focus is on preserving the integrity of children’s psyche through employing behavior management techniques, conscious sedation, and general anesthesia. Treating children’s teeth is important, but protecting their delicate psyches is far more important.

Dr. Idlib’s approach is what he calls Total Patient Care. This approach addressed the overall wellbeing of a child rather than the child’s teeth only. Based on this approach, Dr. Idlibi had referred many of his pediatric patients to other subspecialized experts such as phycologists, endodontists, periodontists, oral surgeons, TMJ specialists and others.

Dr. Idlibi receives hundreds of referrals each year from many general dentists who trust him with providing specialized care to their own children patients.

Dr. Silviu Barzoi, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Barzoi received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry from Stony Brook University in Long Island, New York graduating with Magna Cum Laude and a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the world-renowned New York University.

Dr. Barzoi worked as a Navy Dental Corps Officer honing his clinical skills and furthering his dental education with courses taught at the Naval Post Graduate School, a part of the famous Walter Reed National Military Medical Center in Bethesda, MD.

Dr. Barzoi has extensive training in all phases of dentistry and keeps up with the latest advances in dental materials and procedures. From non-invasive and drill less cavity treatment procedures to computer aided, designed and manufactured all ceramic crown and esthetic veneer restorations, root canals performed with endodontic microscope and utilizing the latest technology and materials, removable or fixed, tooth or implant supported dentures, atraumatic simple or surgical extractions including symptomatic wisdom teeth.

Dr. Barzoi has the experience and compassionate nature to effectively and predictably treat patients of all ages. Dr. Barzoi is married, has two children, and loves spending time with his family and dog doing all sorts of outdoor activities.
