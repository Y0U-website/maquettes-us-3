Meet Dr. Kate Schaefer

Dr. Kate (Katrina) Schaefer began her career in the biotechnology industry. Although it was a challenging and fulfilling career, she missed having direct and positive interactions with people. So after two years working in advanced medical research labs, she applied for and was accepted into the prestigious University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine.

Her broad experience has accumulated through her work and studies in major medical centers from Boston to Washington, DC to Virginia (before moving to Texas in 2012). These moves – from dental school to two advanced residencies to private practice – have underscored one common theme: whether on the East Coast or the Gulf Coast, patients are not excited to get root canal therapy! Dr. Schaefer recognizes this, but also knows how they can enjoy a lifetime of benefits if they are comfortable enough to proceed with endodontic treatment.

Dr. Schaefer moved to the Clear Lake area in 2012. When not working, she enjoys spending time with her husband and three children.

This is why Dr. Schaefer founded League City Endodontics.

At League City Endo, Dr. Schaefer is combining advanced technology with the best practices and approaches that she has gained over more than two decades of experience and from across the country. In doing so, she has created an endodontic option that offers patients the choice of root canal therapy in a comfortable, modern, and patient-centric setting. She also can offer her “tiny hands with a big heart,” qualities which have past patients still calling for treatment from thousands of miles away!
