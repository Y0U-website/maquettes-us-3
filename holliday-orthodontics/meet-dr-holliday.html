ORTHOMASTERCLASS: A GROWING VISION FOR INNOVATIVE LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES

The idea for OrthoMasterClass began in 2014 amongst doctors throughout Western America and Japan. With the expanding interest from the orthodontic community, we officially launched OrthoMasterClass in 2016. As a Top 1 % Invisalign provider and Aligntech Faculty member, Holliday Ortho has treated over 6,000 clear aligner cases. Having coached numerous immersion courses throughout the years, my team and I have helped build innovations and growth amongst practices worldwide.
