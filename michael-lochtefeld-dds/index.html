Meet Dr. Lochtefeld

From dental bridges to crowns, our dental office has a variety of general dentistry treatments and procedures to keep your mouth healthy and smiling.

The practice

Dr. Lochtefeld began working at this office location in April of 2017 after moving back to Ohio from South Carolina. In October of 2019, Dr. Lochtefeld decided to make this office his dental home, as he had grown close to his patients being the only doctor working at this location for nearly 3 years.

He purchased the practice from Dayton Comprehensive Dentistry and renamed the practice Michael Lochtefeld DDS. For Michael, the best part of being a dentist is helping people look and feel their very best.

"I had to find a new dentist after 30 years. I was hoping to find a dentist that I liked as well as my previous dentist. The office staff and dentist did not disappoint. They were very friendly and I am happy with the choice I made ."

Les soins

From dental bonding to Invisalign, at Michael Lochtefeld DDS, we are committed to helping you regain confidence in your smile through our many cosmetic dental services .
