Permanent teeth replacement that feels like your own

League City dental implants are a popular and effective dental service used to replace missing teeth. They are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth, providing a long-lasting solution for those who have lost one or more teeth.

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth or bridges. They are made of biocompatible materials, typically titanium, and are designed to fuse with the jawbone over time.

Once the implants are securely anchored in place, they can support a variety of dental restorations, including single implants, full-mouth implants, and implant-supported bridges.

Types of Dental Implants

Single Implants

Single-tooth implants are used to replace one missing tooth. The implant is placed into the jawbone and allowed to fuse with the bone over a period of several months. Once it has fused, an abutment is attached to the implant and a custom-made crown is placed on top, creating a natural-looking, functional replacement tooth designed to last for many years.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are used to replace all of the teeth in the upper or lower jaw. This more extensive procedure typically involves placing multiple implants throughout the jawbone. Once they have fused with the bone, they support a full arch of replacement teeth, offering a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for those who have lost all of their teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Implant-supported bridges replace several missing teeth in a row. The bridge is supported by two or more dental implants placed into the jawbone, providing a stable anchor for a restoration designed to look and function like natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Improved Appearance

Implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth, providing a natural-looking, aesthetically pleasing solution for missing teeth.

Restored Function

Designed to function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Durability

Durable and long-lasting; with proper care, they can last a lifetime.

Conservative

A conservative solution to replacing missing teeth, as they do not require shaving down the adjacent teeth, which keeps a one-tooth problem a one-tooth problem.

Preservation of Jawbone

Implants can help preserve the jawbone and prevent the bone loss that can occur when teeth are missing.

Restore Your Smile with a Long-Lasting Solution

Implant treatment can provide a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for missing teeth. Whether you need a single implant, full-mouth implants, or implant-supported bridges, they offer benefits including improved appearance, restored function, durability, and preservation of the jawbone.

Implants are also a common part of a larger restorative dentistry plan or a complete smile makeover . If you need dental restorations, talk to Dr. Ng to see if implants are right for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good candidate for League City dental implants?

Most adults who are missing one or more teeth may be candidates. During your consultation at Hometown Smiles Dentistry, we evaluate your oral health, jawbone support, and overall dental condition to determine if implants are the right solution for your smile.

How long does the dental implant process take? K L

The treatment typically happens in stages. After the implant is placed in the jawbone, the area needs time to heal and integrate with the bone. Once healing is complete, we attach the final restoration, such as a crown or bridge.

What happens if I don’t have enough bone for implants? K L

In some cases, patients may not have enough jawbone density to support an implant right away. In these situations, we may recommend a bone grafting procedure to rebuild the lost bone and create a stronger foundation for the implant.

Do implants look and feel like natural teeth? K L

Yes. They are designed to closely mimic natural teeth in both appearance and function. Once the final restoration is placed, most patients find their implant blends naturally with the rest of their smile.

They are designed to be a long-term tooth replacement option. With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can remain strong and functional for many years.

We prioritize patient comfort throughout treatment. The implant placement is performed with local anesthesia, and most patients report only mild soreness during the healing period, similar to other routine dental procedures.

Can sedation be used during the procedure? K L

Yes. For patients who feel anxious about surgery, we may recommend sedation options to help you stay relaxed and comfortable during your procedure.

How do I get started with implants in League City? K L

The first step is scheduling a consultation with our team at Hometown Smiles Dentistry. We’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine whether implants are the best solution for replacing your missing teeth.
