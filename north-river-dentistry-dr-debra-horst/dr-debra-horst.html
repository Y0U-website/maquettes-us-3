Dr. Debra Horst

Eastern Mennonite University article about Dr. Horst in 2011: http://emu.edu/now/news/2011/07/dentist-alum-honored-at-mcv/

Prior to dental school, Dr. Horst was an assistant in a local dental practice for seven years. She also worked with the Harrisonburg-Rockingham Free Clinic as an assistant and then volunteered as a dentist for two years until it closed in 2013.. She became interested in dentistry when her mother suggested she do a mentorship during high school at a local dentist office. She quickly knew where her future would lead, and pursued a dental degree. She enjoys using her skill to help patients achieve oral health.

In 2009, Dr. Horst married her husband Mark, who is also from Harrisonburg. They are the proud parents of their three children: Violet, Rye, and Azalea. Dr. Horst’s hobbies include tennis, golf, traveling, and spending time with her family.
