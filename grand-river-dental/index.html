Les soins

When multiple adjacent teeth are missing, two implants can anchor a bridge spanning the gap — a fixed, permanent solution that’s more stable than a traditional bridge.

Root Canal Therapy Modern root canal treatment to save an infected tooth — far less uncomfortable than its reputation suggests, and often the only alternative to extraction.

Missing a tooth — or several? Dental implants are the gold standard for permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement. At Grand River Dental, we offer single implants, implant-supported bridges, and full-arch solutions for patients in Mentor and Lake County.
