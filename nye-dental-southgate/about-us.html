Meet Dr. Zaid Anbaki

Clinical Director

Our chief Clinical director Dr. Anbaki, has been working in the dental field since 2002. He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania; top of his class.

Dr. Anbaki used his Ivy League education and excelled his dentistry skills while working in multiple dental offices through out the country. After opening both of his dental practices, he brought all his experiences as well as hundreds of hours of continue education here in Michigan.

Dr. Anbaki is proud to be a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Maryland Dental Association, Detroit Dental society, and academy of general dentistry. Dr. Anbaki will provide his patients with the best care possible and loves to see his patients leave with a big smile.
