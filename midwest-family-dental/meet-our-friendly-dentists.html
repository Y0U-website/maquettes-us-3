Dr. Gayatri Satam, DMD ( Chicago, IL )

Dr. Gayatri Satam is a native of the Rogers Park/Edgewater neighborhoods of Chicago. She received her DMD from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry where she was also awarded the Dr. Kamlesh Amin Scholarship for academic achievement and inducted into the national dental honor society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon.

Dr. Satam is a member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, and Chicago Dental Society. She uses the latest techniques in dentistry to provide you and your family with healthy and beautiful smiles. She is gentle, patient, and thorough and will make your experience comfortable and leave you with a better understanding of your oral health. She attends continuing education classes to further broaden her knowledge and dental services.

While Dr. Satam is not practicing dentistry, she enjoys trying different restaurants, baking desserts, and exercising.

Dr. Akosua Boatemaa, DDS ( Waukegan, IL )

Dr. B earned her Bachelor of Arts degree from Dartmouth College and majored in neuroscience in 2008. Following her undergraduate degree, Dr. B received her master’s in Biomedical sciences at the University of North Texas Health Science Center in Forth Worth. She then continued her education at Texas A&M Baylor College of Dentistry, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree in 2018.

“Dentistry affords me the unique opportunity to help people of all ages achieve optimal oral health all while relieving them of immediate pain or infection and giving them the healthy smiles they can truly be proud of. I understand the importance of building trusting relationships with each patient I treat, and strive to deliver exceptional dental care that is comprehensive, gentle, and compassionate."

Dr. B is committed to providing a gentle, relaxing, and happy dental office environment where she works closely with each patient to personalize treatment according to their specific needs and wants.

To stay up to date. Dr. Boatemaa participates in continuing education courses covering all aspects of dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the National Dental Association.

When Dr. B is not at the office she enjoys cooking, traveling, meditation, sports, attending church events, brunching, and spending time with her family and friends, particularly her loving husband and son and brand new daughter.

Dr. Nada Marei, DMD ( Chicago, IL )

Dr. Nada Marei is a compassionate and dedicated general dentist committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Born and raised in Chicago, Dr. Marei is of Egyptian and Turkish background and is fluent in both English and Arabic. Dr. Marei earned her Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and French and Francophone Studies from the University of Illinois Chicago Honors College. She then went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry in 2025.

During dental school, Dr. Marei was highly involved in leadership, research, community service, and organized dentistry. She served as Secretary of the DMD Class of 2025, President of the UIC Dental Student Council, President of the Association of Muslim Dental Students, Vice President of the Middle Eastern Dental Student Association, Secretary of the American Student Dental Association, and held several other leadership roles. She is also involved with the National Arab American Medical Association and the Chicago Dental Society West Side Branch.

Her dedication to clinical excellence, leadership, research, and service has been recognized through several honors, including the Golden Scaler Award from the Department of Periodontics, the Dr. Walter E. Dundon Award for Clinical Excellence and Leadership, the International College of Dentists Student Humanitarian Award, the Illinois State Dental Society Foundation Scholarship, the Dr. Lina K. Tharp Award, and research awards from UIC College of Dentistry.

Since graduating, Dr. Marei has been practicing general dentistry and has gained valuable experience in comprehensive patient care, including restorative dentistry, crowns, root canal therapy, extractions, dentures, partials, periodontal treatment, cosmetic dentistry, and molar root canal treatment. When she is not in the office, Dr. Marei enjoys spending quality time with her family and cats, playing tennis, and discovering new movies and shows.
