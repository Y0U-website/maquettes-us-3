Meet Dr. Yang

Dr. Yang was born in Alaska and raised across the West Coast, spending his early years in California, Oregon, and Washington . It was in Bellevue, WA, where he met his future wife, Dr. Rachel Yang , in high school — he was the band member, and she was the cheerleader.

The two continued their education on the East Coast, with Dr. Yang attending Tufts University, where he graduated Magna Cum Laude with a major in Philosophy. His academic journey took him to the University of Pennsylvania for dental school, where he also earned a Master’s degree in Bioethics and graduated in the top 10 of his class. He was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society .

Dr. Yang then returned to California for medical school and oral surgery residency at UCSF, completing extensive training in general surgery and anesthesia. After residency, he successfully earned board certification from ABOMS.

Today, Dr. Yang lives in the Bay Area with his wife Dr. Rachel Yang , a breast cancer surgeon and breastfeeding medicine specialist. Together, they are raising their family and enjoying life in the vibrant Bay Area. Outside the office, Dr. Yang enjoys food, music, and travel .
