Comfort and convenience are the hallmarks of Martin Dental’s office operations. To ensure your convenience, below is the information you need about our hours, location, appointment scheduling, insurance acceptance and billing.

Appointments

Our office will remind and confirm your dentist appointment in Altoona via email, text, or telephone depending on your preference. In addition, a phone call reminder is typically done 1-2 days prior to your appointment. We respectfully request that you provide us 24 hours notice if you need to reschedule your appointment.

Insurance and Billing

We accept all dental insurance plans and are happy to help you understand your benefits. We accept cash, checks, and credit/debit cards. We also offer SmileMore a dental savings plan and Care Credit a flexible payment plan.

Financing Options

SmileMore Dental Savings Plan

With the SmileMore Dental Savings Plan for Martin Dental, you don’t need dental insurance to receive the highest quality of care at affordable prices. For a low annual fee of $69, you’ll enjoy substantial savings on dental services provided by Dr. Tony Martin and his dental hygiene team.

CareCredit is available to help you pay for treatments and procedures your insurance doesn’t cover. CareCredit offers Deferred Interest* financing or low minimum monthly payment options so you can complete your necessary or elective care. You can even use CareCredit for your entire family.

With three simple steps, including an instant approval process, it’s easy to apply for CareCredit. After you’re approved, you’re free to use CareCredit for the services you choose including LASIK, veterinary, dentistry, cosmetic, hearing aids and more.

CareCredit is endorsed by some of the most credible organizations specific to each healthcare profession.

For more information or to apply online, visit carecredit.com .
