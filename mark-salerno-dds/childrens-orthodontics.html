Our Difference

Invisalign

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten your child&#x27;s teeth without the brackets and wires of traditional braces, making it a comfortable, flexible option for teens and adults who want effective orthodontic treatment on their own terms.

Space Maintainers

When a baby tooth is lost early, a space maintainer holds the gap open and guides incoming permanent teeth into the correct position, preventing future alignment issues before they have a chance to develop.

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a tried-and-true orthodontic solution that effectively corrects a wide range of alignment and spacing concerns, giving your child a reliable, time-tested path to a healthy, confident smile.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces work just like traditional metal braces but feature tooth-colored brackets that blend naturally with your child&#x27;s smile, making them a great option for kids and teens who want effective orthodontic treatment with a more subtle, confident look.

Phase 1/Early Interceptive Treatment

Phase 1 orthodontic treatment addresses developing alignment and spacing concerns while baby teeth are still present, giving us the opportunity to guide your child&#x27;s jaw and tooth development before small issues have a chance to become bigger ones.

iTero Intraoral Scanner

With our iTero intraoral scanner, we can quickly and accurately create 3D images of your child’s teeth, gums, and bite to make treatment planning a breeze.

How it works: The children’s orthodontics process Consultation At your consultation, Dr. Erin will determine if treatment is necessary and beneficial for your child. She will examine your child&#x27;s jaw, dental arches, and teeth to check for early signs of gaps, crowding, and misalignment, and address any oral habits like nail biting, tongue thrusting, and thumb sucking that may be affecting their development. Dr. Erin will also share helpful brushing and flossing techniques to support good oral hygiene and set your child up for a successful treatment outcome.

Impressions and Digital Imaging

Dr. Erin will then take a series of X-rays and impressions to document your child&#x27;s facial profile as well as any tooth or bite issues, helping her determine if extractions or removable appliances will be necessary, such as a space maintainer, palatal expander, or limited braces. Each case is unique, so while some children benefit from a device used to maintain or move teeth and jaw positions, others see better results when some baby teeth are removed — and Dr. Erin will walk your family through every option so you always feel confident in the path forward.

Appliance Placement or Extraction Treatment

After gathering data and deciding on the best orthodontic treatment for your child, Dr. Erin will securely place either a custom dental appliance or administer any necessary tooth extractions. Once your child&#x27;s appliance is in place, she will check for proper fit to ensure maximum comfort and make any adjustments needed before you leave the office.

Next Steps

After treatment begins, Dr. Erin will schedule follow-up appointments every 4-8 weeks to monitor your child&#x27;s progress and make sure everything is moving in the right direction. Children&#x27;s orthodontic treatment can often be completed in 12-18 months or less, setting your child up to enjoy a strong, healthy smile for life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

What are the signs that my child may need orthodontic treatment?

If your child shows one or more of the following signs and symptoms, they may be a good candidate for orthodontic treatment:

Our team is happy to work directly with you and your child to assess these symptoms before they become severe issues over time

  • Underbites
  • Crossbites
  • Crowded teeth
  • Excessively gapped teeth
  • Extra or missing teeth
  • Excessive thumb, finger, or pacifier sucking

What are the benefits of children’s orthodontics?

Early treatment can be in a patient’s best interest if their problem is one that could worsen over time and cause severe dental issues in adulthood if left untreated. The goal of children’s orthodontics is to recognize and eliminate these problems as early as possible, so a child’s teeth and jaw can properly grow and maintain space for incoming adult teeth.

What causes orthodontic problems in my child?

While many orthodontic problems are inherited, some are caused by common habits over time, such as thumb sucking, finger sucking, or excessive pacifier use. Other factors such as mouth breathing, dental disease, abnormal swallowing, poor dental hygiene, or early or late loss of baby teeth, accidents, and poor nutrition can alter your child’s teeth and jaw alignment. No matter the cause, our team will cater to your child’s unique needs and teach them healthy habits for a lifetime of optimal oral health.

What age should my child be seen by an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that your child’s first check-up should take place before the age of 7 or any time an orthodontic issue is noticed before then. By the age of 7, your child’s mouth holds enough permanent teeth for an orthodontist to gain a strong understanding of your child’s mouth and jaws as they develop. This provides your orthodontist with plenty of information to give your child a tailored treatment.

How much does children’s orthodontics cost?

The cost of children’s orthodontics is different for each patient. Factors that may affect the cost include the severity of your child’s teeth misalignment, appointment and visits, insurance type, and more. The best way to find out how much you’ll pay for your child’s care is to schedule a consultation with your orthodontist.
