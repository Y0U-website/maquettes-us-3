The Benefits of Teeth Cleanings and Hygiene

Fresher Breath and Brighter Smile Professionally cleaned teeth look and feel better. Removing plaque and surface stains leaves your smile whiter and your breath noticeably fresher, something brushing alone can&#x27;t always achieve.

Early Detection of Dental Problems During your cleaning, Dr. Hill checks for early signs of tooth decay, gum disease, and oral cancer. Catching these issues early means simpler, more effective treatments with better outcomes.

Improved Overall Health Good oral hygiene reduces inflammation and lowers the risk of illnesses linked to oral bacteria, including cardiovascular disease and diabetes. Regular cleanings boost both oral and whole-body health.

The Teeth Cleanings and Hygiene Treatment Process Comprehensive Oral Exam Before the cleaning begins, Dr. Tristan Hill performs a detailed oral exam to assess your teeth, gums, and soft tissues. This step ensures that there are no underlying problems, like cavities or gum disease, that need immediate attention. He may also review your dental and medical history to customize your care. We also recommend yearly X-rays to look for hidden dental issues.

Professional Scaling and Polishing

One of our skilled dental hygienists uses specialized tools to gently remove built-up plaque and tartar from areas you can&#x27;t reach with normal brushing. This process, known as scaling, is followed by polishing your teeth to eliminate surface stains and smooth enamel. The result is a cleaner, shinier smile and a reduced risk of gum irritation or decay moving forward.

Fluoride Treatment and Home Care Guidance

For added protection, especially against cavities, a fluoride treatment may be applied after your cleaning. This strengthens your enamel and helps keep your teeth resistant to erosion. Dr. Hill also reviews proper brushing and flossing techniques, offering customized tips that fit your lifestyle. He may suggest specific products or habits to maintain your oral hygiene between visits.
