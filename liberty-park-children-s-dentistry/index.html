The practice

At Liberty Park Children’s Dentistry, we strive to help every child establish a dental home and cultivate a lifelong relationship with the families of our patients. We believe in establishing a partnership among our entire staff, the parents or caregivers, and the child to provide quality dental care in a safe and fun environment. We are proud to serve the dental needs of children in Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, Irondale, Birmingham, Leeds, Moody and the surrounding communities.

MEET OUR TEAM Family photos courtesy of Megan Hand Photography

We complete and update a caries risk assessment on every child, which will enable us to tailor realistic dietary advice, oral hygiene instructions, and preventive plan specific to the family of the child.

Our dental products are mercury-free and BPA-free. In addition to the tried-and-true stainless steel crowns, we also offer white, zirconium crowns (pending case selection).

Our practice is completely digital allowing parents and patients to complete forms and make payments online. We also electronic charting and digital radiography with very low exposure.

We offer treatment with nitrous oxide or "laughing gas" as well as treatment under general anesthesia at Children’s of Alabama.

We offer a desensitization program to acclimate and help children with special needs accept dental treatment in a dental setting. We have a special room with dimmable lighting, noise cancelling headphones, bean bag, and a task-oriented social story specific to the appointment.
