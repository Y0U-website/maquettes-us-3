Dr. Michael Cooper

For more than 25 years, Dr. Michael Cooper has been providing gentle yet advanced treatments for healthy, beautiful smiles. He has dedicated his career to providing patients with the most comfortable and effective dental care possible. As a skilled general, cosmetic, and restorative dentist, Dr. Cooper serves Russellville, Little Rock, and other Arkansas communities with a comprehensive range of advanced dental treatments at his practice. He is proud to be in the position to help his patients look and feel their best, meeting their dental needs with care and precision.

After graduating high school in 1973 from Trumann, Arkansas, Dr. Cooper attended Arkansas State University where he became a Student of Distinction. He graduated in 1979 and then went on to attend the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry. As a College of Dentistry student, Dr. Cooper was selected to the Dean’s Honorary Odontological Society and he also received a Certificate of Merit in recognition of high scholastic achievement throughout the entire dental curriculum.

Dr. Cooper founded his practice in 1984 and has been providing outstanding dental care to individuals from throughout Arkansas and the entire Southeast since that time. He is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD) and as a volunteer for Give Back a Smile, Dr. Cooper helps restore smiles for victims of domestic abuse. He is also a supporter of the Smiles for Life Foundation, an organization that benefits children who are facing serious health conditions and educational deficiencies.

Dr. Cooper has been married to his wife Paulette for over 30 years. They have two children, Alisha Huddleston and Chris Cooper. In his free time Dr. Cooper is an avid baseball fan and he attends the St. Louis Cardinals Legends Camp every year.

Dr. Michael Cooper is proud to be a member of the Crown Council, an alliance of dentists from around the world who share a commitment to be leaders in their profession and in their communities. To become a member of the Crown Council, a dentist must display a strong dedication to promoting oral health, fighting oral cancer, and serving others through volunteer work. Crown Council dentists are recognized for their leadership, innovation, and technical skills. Only one out of every 175 dentists in the United States achieves this prestigious membership.
