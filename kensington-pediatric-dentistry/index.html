The practice

From gentle, child-centered care to advanced comfort technology, discover the Kensington Pediatric Dentistry difference, where every visit is designed to make children feel safe, confident, and excited about their smiles.

Unlike general dentists, pediatric dentists complete years of additional, specialized training focused exclusively on children and patients with special needs. This advanced education emphasizes child development, behavior management, and the treatment of growing teeth, allowing for care that is both highly skilled and compassionate.

Board certification represents the highest level of expertise in pediatric dentistry. Our board-certified pediatric specialist has completed advanced training and rigorous examinations beyond standard requirements and continually trains our team, ensuring every child receives exceptional, personalized care.

We believe great pediatric care starts with listening. We take time to understand your child’s needs, personality, and comfort level so families feel supported and confident at every visit.

Our team uses calm guidance, encouragement, and patience to create stress-free visits. Children are never rushed, pressured, or made to feel uncomfortable or judged.

Each child’s dental experience is tailored to their stage of growth, emotional needs, and oral health goals. We focus on building trust while delivering the right care at the right time.

We use modern tools such as digital intraoral scanners and the SOAN by NuSmile system to make treatments smoother, quicker, and more comfortable for children.

From our kid-friendly environment to our thoughtful amenities, everything in our office is built to help children feel relaxed, safe, and excited about their dental visits.

Les soins

Gentle cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments designed to keep little smiles healthy and strong while helping children build positive dental habits from an early age.

White crowns and fillings that restore damaged teeth with natural-looking, durable results while protecting your child’s comfort, confidence, and long-term oral health.
