Giving Back To Our Community

Whaley Children’s Center

We are proud supporters of Whaley Children’s Center. We donate a portion of the profit from each new patient visit to Whaley. Their mission is to provide care, support, and services to at-risk foster children in Flint and the surrounding areas. Hartland Pediatric Dentistry is always open to new opportunities for partnerships with local charities, too. If you have an outreach idea for us, please feel free to reach out to our team.
