COVID-19 Update

Dr. Thomas Loe adheres to all Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and California Dental Association guidelines in an effort to minimize the coronavirus spread.* Dr. Loe recognizes his obligation to promote the health and welfare of patients, and he closely monitors the news and health advisories to keep his patients and staff safe and healthy.

* Any patient who has a fever or is exhibiting COVID-like symptoms will be politely asked to reschedule their appointment.

Dr. Loe and his support staff have been vaccinated against COVID-19 in accordance with the July 2021 California Public Health Order for Health Care Workers. The doctor and his dental assistant wear personal protective equipment during exams and dental procedures.

The dental office undergoes frequent cleanings. The exam room is cleaned after each patient. Good sanitation has long been important to the doctor and his staff. If you have questions or wish to discuss your specific situation, please don t hesitate to request a phone consultation.
