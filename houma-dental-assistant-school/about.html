About Us

Empowering the Next Generation of Houma Dental Assistants

Houma Dental Assistant School is a partnership between Zollege and local dental offices — you learn where the work actually happens, on real equipment, with working professionals.

Zollege was founded in 2010 as Vandelay Education by Dr. Thomas Ince, a dentist who couldn’t find well-trained dental assistants to hire. Rather than wait for traditional programs to change, he built the one he wished existed: shorter, taught hands-on in working dental offices, and structured to be paid for without a loan.

Success in Healthcare Begins at Houma Dental Assistant School

Dr. Ince’s brother Ryan, a physician, ran into the same problem on the medical side. The two collaborated on a medical assistant program built the same way — the same accelerated, in-office approach, rebuilt for medical assisting.

A Faster Path Into Healthcare

Dental assisting is one of the fastest-growing roles in healthcare. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects 6 percent employment growth between 2024 and 2034 and reports a national median wage of $47,300 a year for dental assistants.

A traditional path to a healthcare credential can mean years of school and a loan you carry long after you finish. Houma Dental Assistant School takes 12 weeks, and you pay for it a week at a time.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook
