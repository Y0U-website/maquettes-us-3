Dr. Jamie Elizabeth Sands is recognized as one of Los Angeles’ premier cosmetic dentists. Her technical precision, artistic vision, and patient-centered approach have earned the trust of discerning patients, including high-level executives and members of the entertainment industry.

Dr. Sands earned her Bachelor of Science degree from the University of California, San Diego (UCSD) and her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of the Pacific (UOP) Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, where she developed the clinical foundation and artistic philosophy that continue to define her practice today.

With over 27 years of experience, Dr. Sands specializes in dental implants, porcelain veneers, direct resin veneers, and Invisalign. She has built a reputation for delivering exceptional, ethical cosmetic dentistry with results that are both beautiful and natural. Her expertise has been featured on television programs including The Doctors, Fox News, and TLC’s 10 Years Younger.

Known for her meticulous attention to detail and conservative treatment philosophy, Dr. Sands specializes in cosmetic bonding, veneers, and complex full-mouth rehabilitation. Every treatment plan is thoughtfully tailored to enhance both oral health and aesthetics while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

In addition to advanced cosmetic dentistry, Jamie Sands, DDS provides comprehensive preventive and restorative care, including cleanings, crowns, and implant dentistry. The practice is committed to delivering an exceptional patient experience while creating healthy, confident smiles that last a lifetime.
