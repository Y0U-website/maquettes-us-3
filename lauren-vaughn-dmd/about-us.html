She embarked on her dental adventure at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry after graduating from Alice Lloyd College. She is a University of Louisville School of Dentistry graduate in 2016, she is not just a graduate but a perpetual student of the art and science of dentistry.

With a fervor for lifelong learning, Dr. Vaughn has undertaken numerous additional continuing education courses, all aimed at enhancing the well-being of the local community and patients. She attended University of Florida’s mini-residency in pediatric/special needs. Also, she has taken many courses in all aspects of dentistry. Her mission is clear: To ensure patients are not just treated but aim to be treated comfortably and efficiently.

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Lauren, is happily married to her high school sweetheart, Dr. Andrew Vaughn, MD. Their family includes the lively Evelyn, precious Rowan and three fur babies. When not perfecting smiles and helping with patients’ oral health, you’ll find her cooking up a storm in the kitchen, discussing NFL thrills, and exploring new places with her family.

For Dr. Lauren Vaughn, it’s not just about dentistry; its about creating a harmonious blend of professional expertise and a passion for life. And yes, every adventure begins has to begin with her essential cup of morning coffee!
