Meet Jesus Mendez, DMD

Dr. Mendez is a General Dentist with clinical training and experience in dental implants. Originally from Miami, Florida, Dr. Mendez attended dental school in Massachusetts. He holds a DMD degree from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, where he graduated Magna Cum Laude. Additionally, Dr. Mendez earned a Master of Science in Oral Health Sciences from the same institution and a Bachelor of Arts in Chemistry from Florida International University.

Dr. Mendez can provide treatment in all areas of general dentistry, including, but not limited to, crowns, bridges, extractions, dental implants, root canal therapy, and dentures. Dr. Mendez focused most of his practice, while in South Boston, on full arch rehabilitation including overdentures (implant dentures) and All-On-X. He is passionate about ethical patient care, open communication, and patient education.

Outside the office, Dr. Mendez enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling, and spending time outdoors.
