Less to Deal With, Same Results

ClearCorrect is a clear aligner system that moves your teeth into position without brackets or wires. Custom-made from digital scans of your teeth, each set of aligners does its job, and you move on to the next.

The positives are that they are nearly invisible when worn, removable when you eat or brush, and built specifically for your teeth using digital scans rather than guesswork.

Sound Like You?

Misaligned teeth aren’t just a cosmetic issue. They affect how you chew, how well you can clean them, and how your jaw feels. Left alone, small problems tend to become bigger ones. Here’s what to look out for:

  • Teeth overlapping or shifting
  • Gaps that catch food
  • A bite that feels off when you close
  • Wear on certain teeth
  • Difficulty keeping areas clean

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Most cases take between 6 and 18 months. It depends on how much movement your teeth need.

Do clear aligners hurt?

You may feel some pressure when switching to a new set, but it is usually mild and temporary.

Can I eat normally with clear aligners?

Yes. You remove them to eat, so there are no food restrictions.

What happens if I break an aligner?

Call us. In some cases you can move to the next set, but we will guide you on the safest option.

Am I a candidate for clear aligners?

If your case is mild to moderate, you likely are. The best way to know is to come in for a consultation.
