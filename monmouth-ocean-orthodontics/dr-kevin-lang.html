Meet Dr. Lang

Dr. Kevin Lang

Dr. Lang is passionate about the orthodontic field and it shows through his work! He loves having the opportunity to create second and even third generations of family smiles. Seeing former patients bring in their own children and grandchildren is one of his great joys and speaks volumes of the quality of his orthodontic treatment.

When not working on his patients’ smiles, Dr. Lang enjoys golfing and spending time with his wife, Sue, a health and fitness instructor, and two children. Dr. Lang is also an animal enthusiast and loves spoiling his pets including his two dogs, three cats, and six turtles.
