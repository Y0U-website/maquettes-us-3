Our Practice

For over two decades, Oliveira and Cardo has been one of the most sought after dental practices in the lower Westchester region with outstanding commitment to dental care and periodontics.

Our Mission

We are a state-of-the-art dental practice that caters comprehensively to the dental needs of residents all over Westchester Country. We provide patients with the full suite of preventative, restorative and cosmetic dentistry services, as well as specialist dentistry such as endodontics and periodontics.

Whether you are looking for a new smile or simply want a friendly, compassionate dental office to care for your oral health and that of your children, parents and grandparents, then let Oliveira and Cardo P.C. be your first choice. We maintain a fun and inviting environment and even offer sedation or “twilight” dentistry to the more fearful of patients. Your wellbeing and comfort is our priority, second only to your excellent oral health and beautiful smile aesthetics.

We look forward to treating you as a patient and discussing your dental health.At Oliveira and Cardo we accept most PPO dental insurances and offer in-office dental plans for the uninsured. Check our Insurances page to know more.

Oliveira and Cardo is a patient-centered dental practice known for delivering comprehensive care with a strong emphasis on long-term oral health. Their philosophy is rooted in building lasting relationships with patients, ensuring that each individual receives personalized attention in a comfortable and modern clinical environment. By combining advanced technology with a compassionate approach, the practice creates a setting where patients feel confident, informed, and actively involved in their treatment decisions.

A cornerstone of Oliveira and Cardo’s approach is their commitment to preventive dentistry. They believe that the best dental outcomes begin with early detection and proactive care, which is why they prioritize routine exams, professional cleanings, and patient education. Their team works closely with patients to develop customized prevention plans, helping to reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease, and more serious oral health issues. By focusing on prevention, they not only protect patients’ smiles but also help minimize the need for more complex procedures in the future.

In addition to prevention, Oliveira and Cardo excel in restorative dentistry, offering a full range of treatments designed to repair and enhance oral function and aesthetics. Whether addressing damaged teeth, replacing missing teeth, or restoring overall oral health, their clinicians utilize high-quality materials and evidence-based techniques to achieve durable, natural-looking results. Their restorative philosophy goes beyond simply fixing problems—they aim to rebuild confidence and improve quality of life, ensuring that every patient leaves with a healthier, stronger smile.

We believe that every patient deserves personalized attention, clear communication, and access to the latest advances in digestive health care. Our team works together to create a supportive environment where patients feel comfortable and confident in their care.
