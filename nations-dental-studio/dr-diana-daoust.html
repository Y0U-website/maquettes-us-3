Diana D Aoust, DMD

Dr. D Aoust is originally from Rochester, NY. She earned both her undergraduate and Doctor of Dental Medicine degrees from Boston University. Following dental school, Dr. D Aoust moved to the Washington, D.C. area with her husband, Matt, where she worked as an associate in private practice before moving to Nashville in 2017.

Since moving to Nashville, Dr. D Aoust has joined the Nashville Dental Society, volunteered at the Interfaith Dental Clinic, and worked as an associate in East Nashville and Columbia, TN. Dr. D Aoust prides herself in her "chairside manner" and strives to create a transparent, informative, and welcoming patient experience.

As a proud resident of The Nations, Dr. D Aoust is committed to the growth and development of the community. She can often be found spending time with her husband and two daughters at their favorite local coffee shops, restaurants, concerts, and sporting events. If you see Dr. D Aoust out and about, say hello!
