Is this a dental emergency? Here’s what we treat

Severe tooth pain or swelling

Sudden or worsening tooth pain can signal an infection, abscess, or nerve damage. We’ll diagnose the cause quickly and provide relief.

Knocked-out or partially dislodged tooth

Time is critical: a knocked-out tooth has the best chance of being saved if you seek care within 30–60 minutes. Keep the tooth moist (in milk or saliva), call us immediately, and come in right away.

Broken, chipped, or cracked tooth

A cracked or broken tooth can worsen quickly if left untreated. We’ll assess the damage and repair or protect the tooth before it becomes a bigger problem.

Lost filling, crown, or dental restoration

A lost crown or filling leaves your tooth exposed and vulnerable. We’ll restore function and comfort with quick, effective repairs.

Dental abscess or infection

Signs include throbbing pain, facial swelling, fever, or a pimple-like bump on your gum. We treat abscesses urgently to stop the infection and relieve your pain.

Gum, lip, or soft tissue injuries

Lacerations or injuries to your gums, lips, or cheeks can bleed heavily and require prompt care to prevent infection and promote healing.

Emergency dentistry FAQs — answered by your Alexandria dentist

What counts as a dental emergency?

A dental emergency is any situation involving severe pain, bleeding, a knocked-out tooth, a broken tooth, swelling, or a suspected infection. If you’re not sure whether your situation qualifies, call us — we’d rather help you figure it out than have you wait in unnecessary pain.

K L How quickly can I be seen for a dental emergency in Alexandria?

We do our best to see emergency patients the same day you call. If you’re in severe pain or have a knocked-out tooth, please call our Alexandria emergency dentist immediately at (888) 537-3275 . The sooner you come in, the more options we have to help you.

K L What should I do if I have a dental emergency after hours?

Call our office at (888) 537-3275 and follow the instructions for after-hours emergencies. For life-threatening situations, such as difficulty breathing, severe facial swelling, or uncontrolled bleeding, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately.

K L How much does an emergency dental visit cost?

Our emergency exam starts at just $49, which includes a problem-focused exam, necessary x-rays, and a consultation on your treatment options. If additional treatment is recommended (such as a filling , a crown , or extraction), we’ll walk you through the costs before proceeding. We also accept most major dental insurance plans.

K L Can I go to the ER for a dental emergency?

Hospital ERs can treat dental infections with antibiotics and manage severe pain, but they typically can’t fix the underlying dental problem. For urgent tooth-related issues, seeing an Alexandria emergency dentist is always the better option for actual relief and treatment. Call us first; we’ll get you in fast.
