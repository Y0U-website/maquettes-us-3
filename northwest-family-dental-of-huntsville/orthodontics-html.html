Invisalign Clear Braces

Adults and teens alike can straighten their teeth without cumbersome metal braces thanks to Invisalign. These smooth plastic aligners are barely visible when worn, so you’ll be able to smile confidently throughout the course of your treatment. You can also remove the aligners whenever you’d like, allowing you to brush your teeth as normal and eat practically any food on your plate, including the ones that you can’t chew with traditional braces.

Invisalign Teen

Clear aligners continue to gain popularity as a more comfortable and discreet alternative to traditional orthodontics among teens and adults alike. But for this treatment to be effective, patients must adhere to the provided wear-time instructions and then switch to their new trays when directed to continue the forward motion of their teeth.

If your teen requires treatment but you’re worried that they’ll fall off track with their treatment plan, they might be a good candidate for Invisalign Teen. Continue reading to learn more about what sets this option apart!

How Is Invisalign Teen Different?

In many aspects, Invisalign Teen functions just like the standard trays. Your child will receive custom-fitted trays that fit snugly over their arches to shift their teeth to their ideal places. They must wear them for 20 to 22 hours daily to prevent relapse, then switch to the next pair in their series after about a week or two.

But these particular trays include a small blue dot that gradually fades as your child wears their aligners, serving as a visual indicator that it’s time to switch to the next set. This can help absent-minded or preoccupied teens stay on track with their anticipated treatment timeline.

Is Invisalign Right for Your Teen?

Before proceeding, your teen must consult Dr. Grill to determine whether they’re a good candidate. He’ll examine your child’s teeth and gums, and treat any pre-existing concerns, such as cavities or gum disease, that could negatively affect the outcome.

He’ll also discuss your teen’s dental concerns, goals, and habits. For instance, this service is often ideal for mild to moderate gaps, overcrowding, or bite misalignments, but your child must demonstrate that they can brush and floss twice daily to help maintain their dental health.
