What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to serve as permanent tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth like crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike traditional dentures, implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Why Choose Implants Over Other Options

Implants restore full chewing and speaking ability like natural teeth.

They look and feel like real teeth for a seamless smile with dental implants near Lady Lake.

Implants are long-lasting and provide a stable solution compared to dentures or bridges.

Implants are cared for just like natural teeth with regular brushing and dental checkups.
