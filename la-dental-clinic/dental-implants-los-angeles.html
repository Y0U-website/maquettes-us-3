When is the ideal time to get a dental implant?

  • Immediately after tooth extraction: In some cases, if you have healthy gums and sufficient bone density, an implant can be placed on the same day as the tooth extraction. This can shorten the overall treatment time.
  • Early placement (2-3 months after extraction): This allows the gum tissue to heal. 1 Placing the implant at this stage often occurs before significant bone loss happens.
  • Delayed placement (more than 3 months after extraction): This is common, especially if there was infection or bone loss around the extracted tooth. It allows the bone to heal and may require bone grafting to ensure enough support for the implant.
