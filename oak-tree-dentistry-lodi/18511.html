Frequently Asked Questions

How do I take care of my dental implants?

You don’t have to do anything extra to keep your dental implants viable, functional, and beautiful. Of course, dental implants can’t decay, but you want to keep your gum health in check to avoid complications that can cause implant failure, namely gum disease.

Caring for Your New Dental Implants

The best way to care for your dental implants in Lodi or Elk Grove is to treat them like they are not implants! In other words, brush and floss them just like your biological teeth. You should also continue seeing the dentist twice yearly for comprehensive checkups and teeth cleanings.

You should talk to your dentist if you suspect nighttime teeth grinding, and always wear the proper face equipment or mouthguard if you’re playing sports.

Finally, report oral changes to your dentist as soon as possible, including red or irritated gums, loose crowns, or other changes.

Why chose implants over other options like dentures or a bridge?

Dental implants are the most natural-looking and natural-functioning replacement for a missing tooth. Unlike dentures, implants don’t slip or click on daily activities like laughing, talking, or eating.

When compared to the cost of dentures or bridges, you might not see the big difference you expect. Dr. Gill performs the entire dental implant procedure here in our Elk Grove dental office, including any additional necessary treatments such as bone grafting or sinus lifts that make dental implants possible for patients with bone loss.

Implants help in maintaining healthy jawbone, hence keeping the facial structure intact. They also hold your healthy teeth in place by preventing the shifting and tilting that are common when you leave a gap in your smile.

Many more insurance companies are recognizing the long-term benefits of dental implants, and while it is unlikely they will completely cover the dental implant procedure, you may receive some benefits when choosing this tooth-replacement option.

We also offer patient financing to help spread the costs of dental implant treatment over time. We will help you find a solution to achieve the healthy, attractive smile you want even when facing tooth loss.

What is the recovery time for dental implants?

You may experience some swelling and mild soreness for a few days, but it shouldn t affect your daily activities. Because the bone where Dr. Gill places the dental implant does not have the same nerve structure as teeth and gums, most patients report that dental implant surgery was much easier than they anticipated.

Once the dental implant is in place, Dr. Gill will cover it with a healing cap during the time it takes for your implant to integrate (fuse) with the bone. He will monitor your healing closely to make sure your treatment is on track.

Implants usually take about 12 to 14 weeks to integrate with your jawbone. After your implants have healed, you are ready for the final phase of dental implant treatment, and your crown can be placed.

Unlike dental crowns on natural teeth, we will not need to prep the dental implant. Instead, we will place it on a tiny attachment, called an abutment, which secures it to the dental implant post.

You can care for single dental implants in much the same way that you care for your natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, dental exams, and professional dental cleanings will improve the longevity of your dental implant and your natural teeth.
