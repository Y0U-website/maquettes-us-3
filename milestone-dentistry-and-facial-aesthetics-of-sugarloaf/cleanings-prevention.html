Why Regular Dental Cleanings Are Important

Regular dental cleanings play a vital role in maintaining optimal oral health. They effectively eliminate plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing alone may not adequately address. These harmful deposits can accumulate in hard-to-reach areas of the mouth, ultimately leading to gum disease, tooth decay, and other dental issues. Professional dental cleanings are recommended every six months, during which a dental hygienist will use specialized tools to remove plaque and tartar, polish teeth, and check for dental problems.

Milestone Dentistry and Facial Aesthetics has skilled dental hygienists who use the latest techniques and tools to provide a thorough cleaning and personalized advice on maintaining good oral hygiene practices at home. We will ensure that your visit is comfortable and stress-free, with plenty of time for questions and answers. We understand that good oral health goes beyond teeth cleaning, so our dentists always take time to discuss your overall health and lifestyle habits that may be impacting your oral hygiene.
