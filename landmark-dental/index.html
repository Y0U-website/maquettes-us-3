The practice

Whether you just moved to Rochester and need to find dental care, or you haven’t been to the dentist for years because of past experiences or anxiety, Dr. Mapes and his experienced team want to show you that going to the dentist can be an enjoyable experience.

We believe that preserving your smile is one of the most important gifts you can give to yourself and your loved ones, and we’d love to be part of helping you achieve optimal oral health.

We strive to educate our patients so they can make confident, informed decisions about their oral health. We achieve this by focusing on preventive dental care, which includes routine exams and checkups every six months. Regularly scheduled dental visits enable us to understand your unique dental needs and foster long-term relationships.

Combined with a good brushing and flossing routine at home, our goal is for every patient to maintain healthy teeth and gums for a lifetime, avoiding tooth decay, gum disease, and other dental issues.
