Recall visits are very important to maintain the overall health of your teeth and gums. When we catch problems early on, we can offer conservative treatments that have desired outcomes. Routine visits include a review of your medical history, an oral exam, an oral cancer screening, x-rays, a periodontal (gum) evaluation, and cleaning.

If you have a toothache, swollen gums, a dislodged tooth, a cracked tooth, a missing filling, or a lost crown, please give us a call immediately.

You will need a dental crown if you have a cavity that is too large for a filling, a tooth that has worn down or weakened, or a large filling that has cracked. A dental crown is also needed if you had root canal treatment in the past. Dental bridges are replacement teeth that fill the space where one or more teeth are missing.

During root canal treatment, the inflamed or infected pulp is removed, and the inside of the tooth is cleaned and disinfected, then filled and sealed. Afterwards, the tooth is restored with a crown or filling for protection. Modern root canal treatments are very similar to having a routine filling and is usually completed between one to two appointments.
