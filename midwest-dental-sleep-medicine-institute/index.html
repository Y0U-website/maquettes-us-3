Untreated sleep apnea does more than disrupt your sleep; it’s been linked to high blood pressure, daytime fatigue, and other long-term health risks. Diagnosing and treating the underlying sleep disorder is the first step toward feeling like yourself again.

Ready to treat your sleep disorder without a mask? Learn how Dr. Gotsis can help.

Oral appliance therapy is a mask-free, custom-fit alternative to CPAP.

Your appliance is built specifically for your bite and airway, so it’s comfortable enough to wear every night. Many patients turn to oral appliance therapy after struggling with CPAP, and stick with it because it actually fits their life.

Because it’s small, quiet, and travel-friendly, oral appliance therapy fits into daily life in a way CPAP often doesn’t — no power outlet, no cleaning a mask and hose every morning. Just a device you put in before bed and take out when you wake up.
