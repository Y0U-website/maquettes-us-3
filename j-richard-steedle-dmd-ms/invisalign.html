Reidsville Orthodontics

We will provide you with the braces that fit your needs. Whether you want to show off with colors or prefer discrete braces or invisalign we will provide you with the options you desire.

Metal Braces

Get your braces with all the colors you can imagine! Our metal braces utilize the most modern technologies in the field of Orthodontics to help make sure you have the most efficient treatment possible.

Ceramic (Tooth-Colored) Braces

Ceramic Braces offer a more discrete option for treatment. With the clear "tooth colored" braces, it is easy to have braces even if you aren’t interested in the traditional braces look.

Clear Aligners

Clear Aligners are custom designed to your teeth and can give you the perfect smile you are looking for without any braces or wires. Even if you have been told you may not be a candidate for Clear Aligners in the past, we stay up to date with all the most modern improvements and would be happy to reevaluate if aligners are right for you.
