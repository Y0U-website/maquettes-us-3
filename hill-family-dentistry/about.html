Dr. Tristan A. Hill

Welcome to Hill Family Dentistry! I am Dr. Hill, a second-generation dentist who has been local to the Memphis area my whole life. I grew up in Collierville, running around my parents&#x27; dental practice, and fell in love with the profession. Running into patients at the grocery store or ball fields made it seem like one big family. And that&#x27;s the exact same feel I want to have here at Hill Family Dentistry.

I went to St. George&#x27;s and MUS, where I played soccer, baseball, and basketball. I then attended Rhodes College on an academic scholarship, where I earned a degree in Biochemistry. Then it was off to my parents&#x27; alma mater, the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in Memphis, where I met my wife, Amy. We are blessed with two young boys, Francis and Walter, and they are our entire world! When we&#x27;re not in the office, we like to take the boys to the zoo, the Home Depot kids’ workshops, and Tigers basketball games.

Your First Visit

The Full Experience At your first visit, you&#x27;ll be greeted by our friendly and welcoming team and get to experience the comforting amenities of our cozy office.

Next, you will meet with one our wonderful hygienists or assistants to address any immediate concerns before receiving a comprehensive oral exam and cleaning , complete with any necessary x-rays. We always spend time addressing the current state of your dental health before outlining a unique treatment plan tailored to your needs. Before leaving, we can also schedule any future visits that fit your busy life.

What to Bring To make sure your first visit goes as smoothly as possible, be sure to bring the following items to your appointment:

Insurance Card (if using) and/or Form of Payment

If you have any other questions on what you should bring to your first visit, give us a call and one of our team members can assist you.
