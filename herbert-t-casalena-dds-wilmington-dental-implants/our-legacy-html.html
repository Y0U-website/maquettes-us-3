Our Legacy: Dr. Herbert T.…

Dr. Herbert T. Casalena, DDS practiced dentistry in Wilmington, Delaware for nearly sixty years. A name Delaware knew. A practice generations of families called their dental home. This page is for his patients, for the people who knew him, and for everyone searching for the dentist they have always trusted on Pennsylvania Avenue.

A Wilmington Institution

Dr. Casalena founded his Wilmington dental practice in 1965. Over six decades, he became one of the most recognized dentists in Wilmington and across New Castle County, treating multiple generations of the same families with patience, precision, and the kind of personal care that is rare today.

His office at 2300 Pennsylvania Ave #6AB, the same location Wilmington Dental & Implants occupies today, became a fixture of the Trolley Square neighborhood. Children who first sat in his chair came back as parents, then as grandparents, bringing the next generation to the same waiting room. Patients did not just respect Dr. Casalena’s dentistry. They counted on him.

Dr. Hina, DMD with Dr. Herbert T. Casalena, DDS.

The Continuation

Dr. Casalena and Dr. Hina, DMD shared a mutual passion for dentistry and a commitment to serving their country. Dr. Hina was called to active service as a dentist in the United States Army Medical Unit. Dr. Casalena has since passed away.

His Wilmington practice is now continued by Dr. Hina at the same Pennsylvania Avenue address. Patient records, clinical protocols, the team, and the relationships that define a sixty-year practice are all preserved here.

Fate placed the responsibility of introducing Dr. Hina to Dr. Casalena’s patients in her hands, and she accepts it with both humility and conviction. The trust Dr. Casalena built, patient by patient, does not end with him. It continues here, at the same address, with the same familiar faces at the front desk.
