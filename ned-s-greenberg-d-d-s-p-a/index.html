Before implant treatment, Dr. Greenberg will assess the health of your teeth, gums, and surrounding tissues using CBCT scans. The high-resolution images provided by the CBCT scanner can determine if you require a tooth extraction or gum disease treatment or if other concerns about your oral health should be addressed before placing implants. For the initial phase of treatment, Dr. Greenberg places one or more titanium implant posts into the jaw bone. We will give your mouth a healing time of 3 to 6 months after oral surgery, during which the titanium post fuses with the bone tissue. After the healing period, Dr. Greenberg covers the post with an abutment. Once the abutment is secured, he will cover the abutment with a dental crown .
