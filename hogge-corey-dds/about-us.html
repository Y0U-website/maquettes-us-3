The Team

At All About Teeth, we hold our patients in high regard. Our dentist and entire dental staff are committed to providing people of all ages and all walks of life with excellent dental services and true kindness. We firmly believe that the better informed you are about your oral health and the procedures available to improve the condition of your teeth and overall smile, the more comfortable you will be deciding how and when to begin.

We’re very proud of the team we have built and the work they do to make dental care available to patients throughout the Logan, UT area. From your first contact with our office, we want you to know that we care about you and will help you look and feel your best! Contact us any time and we can answer any general dentistry questions you have.

MEET Dr. Corey Hogge

Dentist

Dr. Corey Hogge attended Utah State University where he received his bachelor’s degree in nutrition. Upon graduating, he went on to Creighton University where he received his master’s degree in human anatomy, followed by a doctorate in dental surgery. An outdoorsman at heart, Dr. Corey enjoys spending time with his family, riding four wheelers, working on projects with his boys, and Jeeping in the mountains. In 2000, Dr. Corey married his high school sweetheart, Melissa. The couple has three energetic kids: Ethan, Autumn, and Carter.
