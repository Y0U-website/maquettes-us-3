Same-Day Appointments

Need to get your smile back on track? We are here for you. Dental emergencies can’t be planned, and we recognize that. To get your smile back to health, we accommodate patients with same-day appointments. Unsure if you are having an emergency? We’ve got you. Simply give us a call and we can work it out together.

Dental Sealants

The easiest way to prevent cavities is to protect them in the first place! Think of dental sealants as an extra protective layer. They coat your teeth and protect them from harmful bacteria and plaque in hard-to-clean places. It’s an easy, seamless way to save you from an uncomfortable, damaging cavity.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Stay healthy with our head & neck and oral cancer screenings. If we see anything suspicious during your exam, our team is equipped with in-depth tools that detect issues and help us to determine appropriate next steps. Screenings include an examination of the head & neck, jaw muscles, all tissues inside your mouth, and digital X-rays that reach beneath the surface. Keeping up with your regular exams ensures that signs of oral cancer are found at an early stage.

Dental Fillings

Restore your tooth from decay and get back to smiling. Dental Fillings include a quick removal of the damaged area, filling the area with resin, and restoring your tooth to its previous shape, function, and appearance. So, breathe in and breathe out, cavities happen but you are in good hands.
