If you notice these signs of impacted wisdom teeth, an exam and updated x-rays can help determine what is happening below the surface.

  • Swollen or tender gums near the back teeth
  • Jaw stiffness or difficulty opening your mouth fully
  • Bad breath or a bad taste that does not go away
  • Headaches or ear discomfort related to jaw tension

What happens if impacted wisdom teeth are left untreated

Leaving impacted wisdom teeth untreated can lead to discomfort and complications. An infected impacted wisdom tooth can cause swelling that spreads to the jaw or face.Other complications may include damage to nearby teeth, shifting of your bite, or the formation of cysts around the impacted tooth.

Early evaluation allows us to identify concerns before they become painful emergencies . Even if you are not experiencing severe symptoms right now, an exam can provide peace of mind and a clear plan moving forward.
