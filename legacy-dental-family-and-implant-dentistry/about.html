About Our Dental Practice

Advanced technology and Comfortable amenities

We combine the latest dental technology and patient-centered amenities to provide care that’s accurate, efficient, and as comfortable as a cozy day at home. Experience the difference innovation makes for your smile at Legacy Dental.

Nervous about dental care? Nitrous sedation offers a calming option to help you feel at ease during your treatment, making every visit more relaxed and worry-free.

Our advanced imaging tools, like CT scans and 3D printers, enhance both precision and efficiency, offering you state-of-the-art diagnostics and custom solutions in less time.

Say goodbye to uncomfortable impressions! Our digital scanners capture precise images of your teeth and bite for better-fitting restorations and a more comfortable process.

Enjoy cozy blankets, pillows, TVs in treatment areas for education, and a refreshment station; thoughtful touches designed to make your time with us as pleasant as possible.

At Legacy Dental, we are deeply committed to supporting the community we love. From free extraction days to partnering with United Tables, a local nonprofit combatting food insecurity, our outreach programs demonstrate our dedication to making a difference. By choosing Legacy Dental, you join hands with us in spreading hope and wellness throughout Crossville.
