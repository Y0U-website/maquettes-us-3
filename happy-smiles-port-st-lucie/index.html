The practice

Four commitments shape every appointment at Happy Smiles Dental.

Diagnosis you can see Intraoral photography and 3D imaging on every treatment plan, so you understand the reasoning before you agree to anything.

Cosmetic-minded restoration Even a single back molar is shaped and shaded to look like it belongs. Function first, artistry always.

Appointments that respect time Longer blocks, fewer double-bookings, and a schedule built so you are seen close to the time on your card.

No-pressure planning Written estimates, insurance breakdowns, and staged options. You decide the pace of your care.

Dr. Zadov founded Happy Smiles Dental to practice the way she believed dentistry should be practiced: diagnose thoroughly, treat conservatively, and explain everything. Her work spans preventive and restorative care as well as cosmetic smile makeovers, and she invests heavily in continuing education so patients benefit from current technique rather than habit.
