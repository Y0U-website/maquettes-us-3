The practice

COSMETIC DENTISTRY Cosmetic dentistry is all about improving the appearance of your smile. At Homer Dental Center, we provide many cosmetic dentistry services, including teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and dental fillings.

SAME-DAY CROWNS Dental crowns are tooth-colored caps that cover teeth to improve the look and function of the tooth. With same-day crowns, we can restore a damaged tooth in one visit. We match the crowns to the rest of your smile.

Dr. Adamson welcomes you to Homer Dental Center, located in Homer, Alaska. She is a highly educated dentist, with training from KOIS and SPEAR. Her office provides many treatments that cannot be found anywhere else in Homer. You can trust her team to be welcoming and honest about your oral health and diagnoses.

INVISALIGN CLEAR BRACES Want a beautiful, uniform smile without metal braces? Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners to re-align your teeth. With Invisalign, you can achieve a straight smile without the discomfort of traditional orthodontics.
