When Shana lost her dental insurance, she couldn’t afford the $300 monthly premium to keep it. As a divorced mother of two teenagers working two jobs while caring for her 90-year-old grandmother, dental care had to wait. While searching online for an affordable dentist, she found Healing Hands Health. Her first visit was in December 2021.

"I walked in expecting a dental appointment, but I found something more," Shana said. "The front office staff were smiling and genuinely happy. By the time I finished checking in, I was smiling too."

After her work hours were cut, Susan lost the health insurance she had depended on for years. Overnight, routine doctor visits became unaffordable, and prescription medications she needed were suddenly out of reach. Like so many people facing financial strain, Susan delayed care and hoped nothing serious would happen.

A lifelong outdoorsman, Steven describes himself as “a simple man.” But after losing his wife, life became anything but simple. He had struggled with heart issues for 20 years, but his health began to further decline. He suffered chronic chest pain, shortness of breath, and “blood issues” left him too weak to work or enjoy the things he loved.

His first appointment at HHH was in April 2024. He was uninsured and hadn’t seen a dentist in years. Steven needed extensive care that included replacing restorative work, treating decay, and extracting teeth.

“They explained the long-term plan: better-fitting dentures, fillings, and some other work. They cleaned my teeth, showed me what to watch for, and explained how oral health connects to heart health. I’d never heard that before.”
