Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer many advantages over other options for tooth replacement, such as:

Our dentists will carefully assess your oral health and determine whether implants are the right choice for you. They will then design a customized treatment plan that considers your unique needs and aesthetic goals.

  • Natural Appearance: Dental implants look and feel just like natural teeth. They enhance the aesthetics of your smile.
  • Improved Function: Implants allow you to eat your favorite food without worrying.
  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate jawbone and prevent bone loss, which is common after tooth loss.
  • Durability: Implants can last for a lifetime with proper care. They are a long-term cost-effective solution.
  • Comfort: Implants eliminate discomfort and slipping, unlike removable dentures.
  • Improved Speech: Implants help to maintain natural speech patterns by securely anchoring the replacement tooth.

The Dental Implant Process

At our Hammond, IN office, we can help you achieve the smile you want. Let’s go over the steps of the process.

We provide detailed instructions on how to care for and recover from each stage to ensure a smooth healing process.

  • Initial Consultation: We conduct a thorough oral examination, take 3D pictures, and review your health history. This allows us to plan the placement of implants precisely.
  • Treatment Planning: We create a custom plan based on your anatomy and dental goals.
  • Implant Placement: A titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone while using local anesthesia to keep you comfortable during the procedure.
  • Healing: The implant will fuse with your jawbone over the next few months to create a stable base.
  • Abutment Placement: An abutment is then attached to your implant after it has healed to connect it with the replacement tooth.
  • Crown and Prosthesis Attachment: At this point, the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to restore your smile fully.

Bone Grafting and Preparatory Procedures

Some patients may require additional procedures to achieve a successful implant outcome. Bone grafting is an effective method for rebuilding the jawbone lost due to gum disease or tooth loss, ensuring solid support for implant placement.

Soft tissue grafts and sinus lifts may also be necessary to improve the implant site and enhance overall results. Our dentists will assess your condition and discuss these options with you during your consultation if needed.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants require the same dedicated care as natural teeth. Let’s go over the basics.

With proper care, your implants will remain healthy, functional, and comfortable for many years.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss daily, including around the implant site.
  • Use antimicrobial mouth rinses as recommended by your dentist.
  • Maintain regular dental visits for professional cleanings and exams.
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption to support healing and implant longevity.
