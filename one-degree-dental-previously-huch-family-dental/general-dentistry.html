General Dentistry

Preventing dental issues before they arise is the key to maintaining a healthy smile. While some problems may be unavoidable, regular visits to your general dentist play a crucial role in early detection and prevention. At One Degree Dental, we provide comprehensive preventive care, including dental exams, X-rays, and professional cleanings, to keep your oral health on track. The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends seeing your general dentist at least twice a year for optimal dental health.

General Dentistry Services Include:

  • Periodontal Care
  • Preventive Dentistry
  • Night Guards
  • Retainers
  • Bonding
  • Crowns & Fillings
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • TMJ Disorder Treatment
