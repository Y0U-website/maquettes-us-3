Making an Appointment

It is easy to make an appointment for your initial examination and dental treatments. Simply call (626) 287-1153 and one of our friendly team members will be happy to assist you.

What to Expect

Enter our lobby to be greeted by our friendly team. You will receive new patient paperwork, including a health questionnaire, insurance information request, and contact details. We will also provide information about our office and patient privacy practices.

Initial Examination

Your initial dental exam at Niko Niko Dental will take about one hour. Our team will discuss your oral hygiene routine, listen to your health concerns, and demonstrate proper dental care. The hygienist will clean your teeth, and Dr. Thalmann will complete the exam and provide treatment recommendations.

Pain Concerns

We strive to make our patients comfortable by minimizing pain. Before any procedure, Dr. Thalmann will discuss your pain tolerance and create a pain management plan. Together, we aim to treat your dental health issues with minimal discomfort.

Dental Insurance

We accept a variety of insurance providers to accommodate our patients’ requests. Please call our office at (626) 287-1153 to see if we are currently working with your insurance provider.

Financial Policy

Niko Niko Dental provides high-quality dental care to San Gabriel families. We understand every patient has different financial needs and will work with you to find a solution, with or without insurance . Call us to learn about our payment options.
