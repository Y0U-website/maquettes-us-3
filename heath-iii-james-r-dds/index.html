Apply as a non-degree-seeking (NDS) student if you wish to take classes at Oregon Tech but don’t plan to pursue a degree. This application is for students looking to take a single course, a few courses for professional or personal development, or pursue a certificate program.

Apply for readmission if you have enrolled at Oregon Tech but have not taken classes for more than four terms and you wish to resume your studies. No application fee is required.

If you are trying to find SPE 111, MATH 243, or COM 225 new Common Course Numbers were approved by the Transfer Council in December 2022. These courses have been re-numbered, and you will also notice that some MATH and WRI courses now end with a Z, due to this new statewide change. For more information, please visit: https://www.oit.edu/registrar/common-course-numbering

Tuition and fees for future terms are subject to change as new rates are approved for the upcoming academic year.
