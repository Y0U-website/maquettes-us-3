GET TO KNOW DR. SKUJA

WHAT TO EXPECT

At your child’s first visit we’ll introduce you to Dr. Skuja and our staff, give you a brief tour of our office, and start your child’s exam! We like to get to know each of our families and what their child’s individual preferences, needs, and goals may be. We prioritize communication, compassion, and positivity—even a bit of playfulness—to help you and your child have the most successful appointments possible.

A Dental Office Designed for Kids

We use technology that enhances the patient experience, from digital records and an electronic portal for communication to our state-of-the-art tools that help us deliver minimally invasive treatments and keep your child comfortable. We also aim to create a soothing environment with Montessori-style learning activities in our waiting room that facilitate calm, independent play.

CONVENIENT LOCATION We’re conveniently located near the heart of Malvern in Uptown Worthington retail center. With plenty of parking and generous walkways, you can schedule your appointments while on a visit to Wegmans, Target, or the upcoming Nemours medical center.

MONTESSORI-STYLE PLAY AREA While we utilize top-of-the-line dental technology in our exam rooms, we prefer to keep our waiting room tech free. Montessori play is a wonderful way to enhance development in children by fostering imagination, emotional strength, and cognitive skills.

TVS IN OPERATORIES We know that a little distraction can go a long way during a dental exam. We provide TVs in the dental operatories for entertainment and as a distraction tool giving our patients diversion during a demanding procedure.

WARM, SOOTHING ENVIRONMENT Little Bear Pediatric Dentistry aims to provide a welcome change to the stereotype of sterile corporate dentistry with our natural light, soothing colors, and plenty of calm and engaging Montessori-style toys for kids in the waiting room.

DIGITAL PATIENT PORTAL Communication is easier than ever with your personal patient portal, where you can communicate with us and coordinate care for your child.

GIRL SCOUNT OF EASTERN PENNSYLVANIA

Little Bear Pediatric Dentistry is proud to give back to the Girl Scouts of Eastern Pennsylvania, an umbrella group which includes several troops that cater to different age ranges. Dr. Skuja has seen the difference the Girl Scouts have made in her own daughter’s life and is inspired by their mission to help girls “grow courageous and strong through a wide variety of enriching experiences, such as field trips, skill-building sports clinics, community service projects, cultural exchanges, and environmental stewardships.”
