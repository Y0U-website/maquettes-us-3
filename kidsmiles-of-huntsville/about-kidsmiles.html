About KidSmiles Texas

Every Child Deserves a Dental Home They Love

We Make It Fun

Kids love coming here! Our pediatric dental offices are designed just for children—with friendly faces, games, fun murals, and smiles around every corner.

We Make Time

Life is busy. That’s why we offer convenient appointment times and do everything we can to make visits fit your family’s schedule.

We re Here to Help

We understand dental care can feel overwhelming. Our team helps families find flexible options and ensures your child’s care never feels out of reach.

Why We Love What We Do

We know parents want a pediatric dentist they can trust—one who makes every visit positive and stress-free. That’s why KidSmiles exists. At our offices in Lake Conroe and Huntsville, we create a dental experience where kids feel safe, cared for, and confident about their smiles. Our pediatric dentists and orthodontists take time to know each child, ease fears, and celebrate healthy habits that last a lifetime. Because when children love their smiles, parents can relax knowing they’re in the best hands.

We promise to care for your child as if they were our own. From the first visit to every check-up after, we’ll be your partners in creating a positive dental experience your child will actually enjoy. As they grow, we’ll continue helping them build confidence, healthy habits, and a smile that lasts a lifetime.

Guiding Principles That Put Families First

Every parent wants their child to feel safe, confident, and cared for at the dentist. That’s why our team at KidSmiles is here — to make every visit positive, every question answered, and every smile something to celebrate.

Community

You’re part of something bigger when you walk through our doors. We love being involved in the same schools, teams, and neighborhoods as the families we serve — because healthy smiles start with strong communities.

Relationships

Your child deserves a dentist who knows them by name and makes them feel at ease. We take time to connect, celebrate their courage, and cheer them on as they grow into confident, healthy adults.

Commitment & Integrity

You should always know what to expect — and why. We’re here to guide you with honest recommendations, transparent care, and the peace of mind that your child is in safe hands.

Innovation & Safety

As a parent, you want the best for your child — and so do we. From advanced technology to safety standards that go beyond what’s required, we’re always improving the way we care for your family.

KidSmiles’ Vision is to make going to the dentist something kids love and parents trust. When families leave smiling, we know we’ve done what we were called to do.
