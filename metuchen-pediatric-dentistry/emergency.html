Knocked-Out Tooth

​ If part of your child s tooth has fallen out or if the tooth is completely knocked out, this is a pediatric dental emergency. Your child will need to see us the same day. While you are waiting for your dental appointment, rinse your child s mouth with warm salted water and put some pressure on the injury to reduce or stop the bleeding.

Chipped, Cracked, or Fractured Tooth

If your child s tooth is chipped or broken, whether it s a baby tooth or a permanent tooth, it is important to call Metuchen Pediatric Dentistry right away. If it s after business hours and appears severe, go to your nearest hospital emergency room.

Toothache

If your child has a toothache, call us right away. While you are waiting for your appontment, have your child gently rinse their mouth with warm water. Check for food trapped between teeth and use a cold compress to minimize swelling. You may also give over-the-counter pain relievers as needed and as directed by Dr. Sodhi.

Lost Fillings

​ If a child loses a filling, call Metuchen Pediatric Dentistry right away. If your child is experiencing pain, use over-the-counter pain relievers as needed and according to the directions of Dr. Sodhi.

Dental Abscess

An pediatric abscessed tooth is an emergency and needs to be treated by right away to prevent the spread infection to other parts of your child s body.
