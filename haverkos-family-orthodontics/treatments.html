Braces

Traditional

Traditional braces are a tried-and-true method for straightening teeth and creating stunning smiles! With metal brackets and wires, they work efficiently to align your teeth and improve your bite, making them a great option for all ages.

Ceramic

For those who prefer a more subtle look, ceramic braces are an excellent choice! These clear or tooth-colored brackets blend in with your teeth, providing the same effective treatment as traditional braces while keeping your smile looking stylish throughout the process.

InBrace

InBrace is an orthodontic treatment system that places braces behind your teeth, offering a completely invisible way to straighten your smile. Unlike traditional braces, InBrace works from the inside, so no one will know you’re wearing them, making it a perfect choice for those who want a discreet solution.

In addition to being invisible, InBrace is designed for comfort and efficiency, using advanced technology to precisely move your teeth into place. With fewer adjustments and a smoother experience, you can enjoy your orthodontic journey with less hassle while achieving a beautifully aligned smile!

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a great alternative to traditional braces, offering a discreet way to straighten your teeth. Made from smooth, transparent plastic, these aligners fit comfortably over your teeth and are virtually invisible, allowing you to smile confidently throughout your treatment.

Not only are clear aligners aesthetically pleasing, but they also provide the convenience of being removable. This means you can enjoy your favorite foods and maintain your oral hygiene routine without any hassle, making them a popular choice for both teens and adults seeking a flexible orthodontic solution!

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a popular cosmetic treatment that helps brighten your smile by removing stains and discoloration. Whether it’s caused by coffee, wine, or age, our professional whitening options can effectively lighten your teeth, giving you a fresh, confident look.

Our teeth whitening treatments are safe, effective, and tailored to your individual needs. With both in-office procedures and at-home kits available, you can choose the option that fits best into your lifestyle while achieving that dazzling smile you’ve always wanted!

Technology

The iTero scanner is a cutting-edge tool that allows us to take detailed digital impressions of your teeth without the need for messy molds. This quick and comfortable scan provides a 3D image of your mouth, helping us plan your treatment with accuracy and giving you a sneak peek at your future smile!

With our in-house lab equipped with advanced 3D printing technology, we can design and produce custom orthodontic appliances with exceptional speed and precision. This allows us to provide you with highly personalized care in less time, ensuring a treatment experience tailored to your unique needs.

Our digital X-ray technology offers a safer and more efficient way to capture detailed images of your teeth and jaw. With reduced radiation exposure and instant results, we can quickly assess your dental health and create a personalized treatment plan while keeping your safety in mind.

We use high-quality digital photography to document your smile transformation at every stage of treatment. These clear, detailed images help us track progress and make adjustments as needed, while also giving you a visual record of your orthodontic journey!
