Services We Offer

At Jones Dentistry we offer comprehensive dental care and a personalized treatment plan for each patient. Our goal is to educate every patient on ways to have a healthy and beautiful smile. We believe in making your experience enjoyable, comfortable, and personally tailored to your needs.

Preventative Care

Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry or Smile Designs

Restorative dental procedures are used to help improve the function of teeth that have been affected by dental decay, trauma, or other damage. Through fillings, porcelain crowns, or porcelain bridge work we are able to restore those teeth back to their full glory. Have a missing tooth? No worries! With the use of implant restorations, porcelain bridge work, or partial dentures we can assist in giving you your smile back. Through a comprehensive exam we are able to create a treatment plan specifically for you and discuss all of your treatment options!
