Restore Your Smile With Restorative Dentistry

Damaged or missing teeth can impact your oral health and confidence. At L tz Family Dentistry in Lawton, OK, we offer restorative dentistry services to repair and rejuvenate your smile.

Root Canals

Root canal therapy can save an infected or damaged tooth, relieving pain and preventing further complications. We use advanced techniques to make the procedure as comfortable as possible.

Extractions

When a tooth is severely damaged or decayed, extraction may be necessary. Our team performs extractions with care and precision, ensuring your comfort throughout the process.

Dentures

In restorative dentistry, dentures play a crucial role in replacing missing teeth and restoring essential oral functions. Whether full or partial, dentures help patients regain the ability to chew, speak clearly, and maintain proper alignment of the remaining teeth. They also support facial structure, preventing the sunken appearance that often results from tooth loss. By restoring both function and form, dentures are a reliable, non-invasive solution for improving oral health and quality of life.

Amalgam Fillings

Amalgam fillings are a safe and reliable option for restoring teeth. Recognized by the American Dental Association, they provide long-lasting durability.

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings are a durable, natural-looking solution for repairing cavities. Made from tooth-colored resin, they blend seamlessly with your smile.

Partials

In restorative dentistry, partial dentures are used to replace missing teeth and restore functionality to the mouth. These custom-made appliances fit comfortably around the remaining natural teeth, filling in gaps caused by tooth loss. By improving chewing ability and preventing the remaining teeth from shifting, partials help maintain proper bite alignment and overall oral health. They are an effective solution for patients who still have some natural teeth but need replacements for others.

Nitrous Oxide Conscious Sedation Available

For patients with dental anxiety, we offer nitrous oxide sedation to help you relax during your treatment.
