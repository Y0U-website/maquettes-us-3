Restorative Dentistry FAQs

Restorative dentistry involves procedures that repair or replace damaged teeth, improving both function and appearance.

What is restorative dentistry?

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing or replacing damaged teeth to restore their function and appearance, using treatments like fillings, crowns, and bridges.

How long does a restorative procedure take?

The duration varies depending on the complexity of the procedure. Simple fillings may take about an hour, while crowns or bridges might require multiple visits.

Is restorative dentistry painful?

We prioritize patient comfort by using local anesthesia to minimize pain during procedures. Post-treatment discomfort is usually mild and manageable.

How do I care for my teeth after a restorative procedure?

Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing twice daily, flossing, and attending regular dental check-ups. Follow any specific aftercare instructions provided by our team.

Are restorative procedures covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by insurance plan. We recommend checking with your provider for details on what restorative treatments are included.

What materials are used in restorative dentistry?

We use high-quality materials like composite resins, porcelain, and metal alloys to ensure durable and natural-looking results.

Can restorative dentistry improve my smile s appearance?

Yes, restorative treatments not only enhance function but also improve the aesthetics of your smile, boosting your confidence.

How do I know if I need restorative dentistry?

If you have damaged, decayed, or missing teeth, a consultation with Dr. Ezzati can determine if restorative dentistry is right for you.

What is the recovery time for restorative procedures?

Recovery time varies by procedure. Simple treatments may require minimal downtime, while more complex restorations might need a few days for full recovery.
