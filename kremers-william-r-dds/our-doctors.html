Personalized, Transparent Care

from Day One

From the moment you step into our office, you’ll be greeted by name and welcomed like family. Genuine hospitality is the cornerstone of our care, and that starts with a friendly face at the front desk. We&#x27;ll walk with you to your treatment room, and your assistant will personally introduce you to your doctor after you&#x27;re seated and we&#x27;ll walk you through what to expect.

No two smiles are the same, and neither are our appointments. Your first visit is designed around you—your concerns, your goals, and your comfort. Our team takes the time to listen, build rapport, and understand your dental history, so we can provide the best possible care for your needs.

Your first exam is typically one-hour. This is where we take a deep dive into your oral health, ensuring no stone goes unturned. We use advanced digital scans, x-rays, and photos to assess every tooth, check your bite, and evaluate your gum health. Nothing gets overlooked, so you leave with a clear picture of your dental health and a plan tailored just for you.

We believe transparency is key to building trust, and we don’t take that lightly. Before you leave, your doctor will walk you through any recommended treatments, answer all your questions, and help create a plan that fits your lifestyle and budget. Whether you&#x27;re here for routine care, cosmetic enhancements, or restorative treatments, you’ll feel confident knowing exactly what comes next.

Cutting-Edge Technology, Optimal Results

We&#x27;ve invested in state-of-the-art technology to make treatments more comfortable, precise, and personalized. Experience dental care tailored to deliver your best smile.

No more messy impressions. Our iTero digital scanner creates a high-resolution 3D model of your teeth, allowing us to show you exactly what’s happening in your mouth and map out potential treatments with precision.

Skip multiple appointments on your busy schedule. Our CEREC system allows us to design, craft, and place custom, high-quality crowns in a single visit. Leave with a permanent solution in just a couple hours.
