Cavities and Bonding

Cavities are one of the most common dental problems. They are created by bacteria that cause decay on the tooth surface.Cavities are most commonly found on the chewing surface or between the teeth. Dr. Crystal utilizes evidence based dentistry to treat decay at the proper stage with the best materials. And if you’re one of the unlucky many to have chipped your front tooth, we’re that happy to repair that tooth with highly aesthetic bonding, matching the colour and shape precisely.

Crowns (Caps)

The closest chance we’ll have to being royalty! Crowns (commonly caps) are one of the most effective dental treatments. Today, crowns are made entirely of tooth colored porcelains and are exquisitely strong and aesthetic. Additionally, Dr. Crystal works in a completely digital environment which means no more goop! The digital scanner allows for optimal patient comfort, improved fit, and quicker fabrication. A crown may seem intimidating, but it has become a far more comfortable procedure.

Cleanings

Even though you brush twice daily and floss twice annually, you probably need some help getting those pearly whites looking good. Semi annual cleanings are the norm, with some patients needing more and some needing fewer per year. So let’s see you every 6 months to make you shine and check out the teeth and surrounding anatomy. This is really the best way to keep your mouth (and you!) healthy — we look at more than just those white things. Nothing feels better than a fresh mouth!

Veneers

Veneers are hands down the most profound way to change your smile. It all starts with consults and custom smile designing. Taking the time to design and plan your smile up front leads to optimal aesthetics and removed the unexpected. Once we have the perfect plan, we can reshape the teeth to accommodate porcelain laminates and transform your smile in just a few visits. Working on your front teeth is a huge responsibility and one not taken lightly by Dr. Crystal.

Clear Aligners

Arguably one of the best innovations in dentistry in the last few decades, clear aligners such as SureSmile or Invisalign allow patiens to comfortably and discreetly straighten their teeth. These treatments are effective, efficient, and will rapidly transform your mouth — a better smile, easier to clean, and an even bite. Set up your consultation today to see what can be done.
