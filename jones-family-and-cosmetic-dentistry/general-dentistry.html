Our Sedation Options

At Jones Family and Cosmetic Dentistry, we prioritize your comfort and relaxation during dental procedures. Therefore, our practice offers a variety of sedation options to fit your individual needs. Trust our team for a comfortable and anxiety-free dental experience.

Nitrous Oxide

Also known as laughing gas’, nitrous oxide is a safe and effective sedation option that helps you feel at ease throughout your visit. We’ll gently place a mask over your nose and tell you to breathe deep. It only takes a few moments for the laughing gas to start working. Unlike other types of sedation dentistry, laughing gas is quick to wear off. This means that you’ll be able to drive yourself home or return to work.

Oral Sedation

We also offer oral sedation to create a soothing experience for those who may feel uneasy about dental procedures. Dr. Jones will prescribe you an oral sedation pill that is taken prior to your appointment. Since oral sedation makes you feel drowsy, you’ll also need someone to drive you to and from our office. Our skilled team ensures a calm and relaxed state throughout your visit.
