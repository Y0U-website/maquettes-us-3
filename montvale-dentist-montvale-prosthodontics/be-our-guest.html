Your First Visit: What to Expect

At our office, we focus on education-first dentistry -- meaning we prioritize giving you the information you need to make the best decisions for your smile. Our goal is to provide a thorough evaluation and discuss your options openly, empowering you to make informed decisions about your care.

A Guide to Your First Appointment

Finances & Insurance

We believe in dental freedom. We treat based on needs, not insurance, but do work with many insurance companies. We will send claims on your behalf as a courtesy. If you do not have dental insurance, you are in good company. About 60 % of our patients do not have dental insurance. We have multiple financing options that will fit your lifestyle & goals. Our team would be happy to answer any questions about the process.

Referring Doctors

We love collaborating with other specialties for the benefit of our patients. Seeking excellence, we work with top tier labs and the highest standard materials to deliver unmatched results.
