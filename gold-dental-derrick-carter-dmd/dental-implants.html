Frequently Asked Questions

What are Dental Implants:

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone to act like the root of a natural tooth. Once the implant goes through the process of osteointegration (which generally takes anywhere from 3-6 months), a custom dental crown, bridge or denture is attached to complete your smile. They are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural appearance and feel
  • Improvement of the restored ability to chew and speak
  • Long-lasting durability
  • Support for jawbone health
  • Prevention of shifting teeth
  • Greater comfort and stability versus removable dentures
  • Improved confidence in your smile

Caring for Dental implants

Dental implants should be cared for just like natural teeth. Brushing at least twice daily, flossing daily (water-flossers are a plus!), and routine implant maintenance cleanings with your dental offices’ hygienist. All of these habits help ensure the maintained health to the investment you’ve made with your dental implant/implants.

What can Dental Implants replace

  • A single missing tooth
  • Multiple missing teeth
  • A full arch of missing teeth
  • Greater stabilization compared to loose or uncomfortable removable dentures

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Many patients are good candidates for dental implants. Ideal implant candidates generally have:

  • Healthy gums
  • Adequate jawbone support
  • Good overall oral hygiene
  • A commitment to regular dental care and hygiene appointments

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation and Evaluation
  • We begin with a comprehensive exam, digital imaging, and discussion of your treatment goals to determine if dental implants are right for you.
  • Implant Placement
  • The implant post is carefully placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Restoration
  • Once healing is complete, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant for a secure, natural-looking result.
