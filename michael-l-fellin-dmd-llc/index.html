Michael L. Fellin, DMD

Dr. Michael L. Fellin practices Family and General Dentistry in Lebanon, PA. He has been a dentist for nearly 15 years and dental office owner in Lebanon County for over 10 years.

The practice

Dr. Fellin earned his Bachelor of Science from Millersville University in 2007 and his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Temple University Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2011. He is the acting Vice President of the Lebanon County Dental Association and a member of the American Dental Association and Pennsylvania Dental Association.

Dr. Fellin provides patients with high-quality, consistent care. You can look forward to seeing him at every office visit.
