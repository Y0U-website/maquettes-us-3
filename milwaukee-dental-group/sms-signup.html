Messages you may receive

Message frequency

Frequency varies with your schedule and account; on average a patient receives 0 5 messages per month.

Cost

We do not charge for these messages. Standard message and data rates may apply per your wireless carrier.

Opt out & help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time; you will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the information above.

Your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send the messages described here and to log your replies to your patient record. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in / consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions .
