About Park Dental Care

We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the best care in Long Island.

Dr. Jihyun Park, D.M.D.

Dr. Jihyun Park is a dedicated dentist committed to providing exceptional oral health care. She graduated from Ewha Womans University in Seoul, Korea in 1992, before completing her master’s program at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Dr. Park pursued a doctorate in Neuroscience at the University of Pittsburgh before transitioning her career to dentistry after a mission trip where she served in dental care.

Dr. Park achieved her lifelong dream by graduating from Tufts Dental School in 2002 and completing a rigorous residency program at Flushing Hospital. Her patient-centric approach emphasizes personalized care, compassion, and staying abreast of the latest advancements in dentistry.

Dr. Park is an accomplished practitioner with specialized expertise in implant dentistry, having honed her skills at NYU in both surgical placement and restorative aspects of dental implants.

Certified as an Invisalign provider, Dr. Park offers patients a discreet and effective way to achieve beautiful smiles.

Patients appreciate her gentle demeanor, dedication to their overall well-being, and her ability to provide comprehensive dental solutions.
