Comprehensive Dental Services for you and your family

Maintaining your oral wellness is a journey that shouldn’t be taken alone. At Harbor Lights Dental Care, we’re here to offer you professional support for all your dental needs and ensure that your smile remains strong, healthy, bright, and whole.

Preventive Care

Tired of dealing with dental problems that could’ve been avoided? That’s where preventive care comes in. Our team focuses on stopping issues before they start—with a simple oral exam, regular teeth cleaning, and honest guidance. We make it easy to keep your smile healthy and strong.

Restorative Care

Whether it’s a simple tooth repair or a complete full mouth restoration, we make your smile feel brand new. We use strong, natural-looking options like bioactive fillings, inlays and onlays, and a dental crown when needed—all designed for your comfort and confidence.

Periodontal Care

Noticing bleeding gums, bad breath, or loose teeth? These are signs of periodontal disease—and ignoring them can lead to bigger problems. Our experienced dentist and staff offer gentle, effective gum disease treatment to protect your smile.

Surgical Care

Need a tooth extraction or planning for dental implants? We’re here to make it simple. Our oral surgery team handles everything from routine surgical care to advanced procedures like ridge preservation, bone grafts, and sinus lift surgery to make sure your jaw is strong enough for long-term results.

Cosmetic Care

Not happy with your smile? Let’s change that. Our cosmetic care focuses on simple fixes and stunning results. From teeth whitening to porcelain veneers and dental bonding, we help you boost your confidence fast. Want to fix a chipped tooth or do a full smile makeover? We’ve got you.

TMJ Therapy

If you experience constant jaw pain, popping or clicking when you chew, or frequent migraines and headaches, it cound indicate an issue with your temporomandibular joints (TMJ). TMJ therapy can help you reclaim your life from unnecessary pain. Contact us today for more information and treatment options.
