Membership Plan: Flexible Dental Care Options at MVP Dental

We understand the challenges of not having dental insurance and how it can prevent patients from receiving regular preventative care. That’s why we’re excited to offer our Membership Savings Plan (MSP) as an alternative to dental insurance. Our goal is to see you for routine cleanings rather than emergencies, helping you maintain the healthiest mouth possible!

Our plan is straightforward and budget-friendly. For an annual fee of just $250, members receive bi-annual preventative care, including two dental cleanings, dentist exams, and X-rays, at no additional cost. Plus, you’ll enjoy discounted prices on all our procedures, such as fillings, root canals, and crowns.

Say goodbye to the stress of dental care costs and hello to a brighter, healthier smile with our Dental Membership Savings Plan!
