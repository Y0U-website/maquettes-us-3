All-on-X Dental Implants in…

  • Implant placement: The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Sedation dentistry options are available for patients who prefer additional comfort during this visit.
  • Osseointegration: Over the following three to six months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone. A temporary restoration may be placed during this healing period.
  • Abutment placement: Once integration is confirmed, the connector piece that links the implant post to the crown is attached.
  • Crown delivery: A custom ceramic or porcelain crown is fabricated to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth and permanently secured to the abutment.

Life with All-on-X Implants

Day-to-day life with All-on-X implants is remarkably close to life with natural teeth. The fixed arch does not come out, does not shift, and requires no adhesives. Patients brush twice daily with a soft-bristled brush and use a water flosser or specialized floss to clean beneath the arch. Routine general dentistry visits allow our team to inspect the implants, assess the health of the surrounding gum tissue, and make any necessary adjustments to the prosthetic over time.

Most patients report that the ability to eat comfortably, smile freely, and speak without hesitation is among the most meaningful aspects of their recovery. For those who have spent years managing failing or missing teeth, All-on-X is often described as life-changing.

Maison Dental Studio works with patients to navigate financing and insurance options and make full-arch implant treatment accessible. Our team walks through every financial detail during the consultation so there are no surprises along the way.

The Dental Crown Process at Maison Dental Studio

Most dental crown placements are completed over two appointments. During the first visit, Dr. Murray or Dr. Mullen will evaluate the tooth, prepare it by reshaping the surface to make room for the crown, and take precise impressions. These impressions are sent to a dental laboratory where a custom crown is fabricated to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth. A temporary crown is placed to protect the prepared tooth while the permanent restoration is being made.

At the second appointment, the temporary crown is removed and the permanent crown is checked for fit and color before being bonded securely in place. We encourage patients to ask questions throughout the process so they feel fully informed and comfortable before, during, and after treatment.

For patients who experience anxiety around dental procedures, we offer sedation dentistry options to help make the experience more relaxed and comfortable.
