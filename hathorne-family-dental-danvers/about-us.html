Welcome to Hathorne Family Dental

At Hathorne Family Dental, we are committed to providing top-quality, personalized dental care to our patients. Our experienced team of dental professionals is dedicated to ensuring your comfort, satisfaction, and relaxation during every visit. We offer a wide range of services, including routine check-ups, teeth cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants, and cosmetic dentistry. Our goal is to make your dental experience exceptional, stress-free, and affordable.

Serving the Danvers, Massachusetts, community since 1990, we have been providing exceptional dental care for over 20 years. Our long-standing commitment to excellence and patient-centered care has made us a trusted choice for generations of families.

From general dentistry to specialized procedures like orthodontics, periodontics, and oral surgery, we are here to help. Our state-of-the-art dental office is equipped with the latest technology, including digital X-rays and intraoral cameras, to ensure accurate diagnoses and precise treatment. We strive to create a warm, welcoming, and anxiety-free environment where you feel comfortable and confident in the care you receive. Whether you’re a longtime patient or just starting your dental journey with us, we’re here to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful, and radiant smile.
