SureSmile vs. Traditional Braces

How does SureSmile compare to traditional braces? In many ways, SureSmile is superior to braces. SureSmile is more comfortable, discreet, and convenient. It also causes less irritation to the gums and mouth.

Traditional braces provide an excellent “tried and true” way of straightening teeth. But the silver braces can stand out on your teeth when you talk or smile. With SureSmile, others likely won’t notice you are wearing them when you speak, take pictures, or even during your biggest, widest smile.

Not only is SureSmile comfortable and discreet, but you can smile with confidence while wearing them, knowing that your teeth are getting straighter and straighter all the time. SureSmile clear aligners are made from a more durable material, making them less likely to break or become damaged.

How Do I Care For SureSmile Aligners?

The ease of caring for your clear aligners is an advantage over other orthodontic options. Traditional braces, for example, are not as challenging to clean as you might expect, but it’s much easier to clean clear aligners.

To clean your SureSmile aligners, simply brush them with mild soap and water. It would be best if you also rinsed them after each meal or snack. You can remove your clear aligners when you need to eat or drink. It’s essential to keep your aligners clean so that they can effectively straighten your teeth and so that you don’t experience a buildup of excess plaque on your teeth.
