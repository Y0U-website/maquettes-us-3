All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implant replacement teeth are ideal for candidates who already wear dentures or are missing multiple teeth. For an evaluation, please call Louis Napolitano DMD to schedule an appointment.

Restore Your Smile With All-on-4 Dental Implants

There are several ways in which All-on-4 implants are better than dentures:

Staff is extremely kind and efficient. Dr. Napolitano is a compassionate dentist and does wonderful work.

  • You can eat the same range of foods that you can eat with natural teeth. You’ll be able to bite with 70 % more force than you can with dentures.
  • There are no wires, brackets, or adhesives.
  • The replacement teeth are not excessively sensitive to temperatures.
  • No one will be able to tell you’re wearing a dental prosthetic.
  • They prevent the bone deterioration and resorption in your jawbone that leads to the sagging facial features common in denture wearers.
  • Patients take care of All-on-4 implants in the same way they care for their natural teeth by brushing each day.
  • The implants also operate in the same way as natural teeth, so the patient does not have to be cautious of what he or she eats. Along with the fact that patients do not have to remove All-on-4 implants when eating or cleaning the teeth, there is also no need for uncomfortable wires or brackets to hold the implants in place. Even more importantly for patients, the dental implants allow anyone to eat what the heart desires. You do not have to worry about hot or cold affecting your dentures. You also can bite down with more force, which allows you to eat the foods that you love.
  • They look exactly like your natural teeth. No one will be able to tell that you are wearing a dental prosthetic.
  • From a dental health perspective, it is important to note that when you have All-on-4 implants, it is going to prevent bone deterioration in the jawbone and ultimately restore your facial features by fighting resorption. The process of resorption involves the implants stimulating the jaw to send a message to the body that the jaw is supporting teeth once again. Resorption will help the jawbone to regain some of the mass that it has lost previously.
