About Us

Pediatric dentist office in Mt. Pleasant, WI Welcome to Paramount Pediatric Dentistry. We are pediatric dental providers proudly serving Southeastern Milwaukee. Our two locations in Hales Corners and Oak Creek are conveniently located near the Hales Corners, Milwaukee, Franklin, Muskego, New Berlin, Greendale, Oak Creek, South Milwaukee and Cudahy communities.

As specialists in children’s dentistry, our doctors and staff are experienced in treating children from infants to teens, as well as those with special health care needs. We strive to provide each child with a unique experience by offering a fun, positive, nurturing environment.

It is our mission to offer superlative comprehensive oral health care to each child while establishing a positive rapport with dentistry. Please view our site and contact us with any questions. We look forward to meeting you soon.

Dr. Alexis Belisle

Dr. Alexis Belisle was born and raised in Saint Paul, Minnesota. She attended Loyola University Chicago where she earned her BS in biochemistry. After four years of undergraduate studies, Dr. Alexis took some time off from her studies to live in Thailand and travel around Southeast Asia. She then moved to Milwaukee to attend Marquette University School of Dentistry, where she earned her doctorate of dental surgery. Dr. Alexis received her postgraduate education and training specializing in pediatric dentistry at the Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin in Milwaukee.

Dr. Alexis is currently on staff and holds privileges at Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin.

Dr. Alexis received her board certification in pediatric dentistry becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also an active member of the American Dental Association, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Wisconsin Dental Association, the Greater Milwaukee Dental Association and the Wisconsin Society of Pediatric Dentists.

Dr. Alexis met her husband, Peter, in dental school at Marquette and they were happy to stay in Wisconsin. They have two sweet daughters- Layla and Liliana- who keep them busy and always on their toes. As a family, they love snuggling their dog Sadie, cooking together, and playing outside.

Dr. Megan Byers

Dr. Megan Byers grew up in Menomonee Falls, WI. She completed the Pre-Dental Scholars program at Marquette University, where she earned both her undergraduate and dental school degrees. She then spent two years at the University of Illinois at Chicago where she gained additional specialty training in pediatric dentistry as well as a master’s degree in Oral Sciences.

Dr. Megan is currently on staff and holds privileges at Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin.

Dr. Megan is a board certified Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also an active member of the American Dental Association, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Wisconsin Society of Pediatric Dentists.

Dr. Megan and her husband, Ben, live in Milwaukee with their yellow lab, Agnes, and will be welcoming a daughter this summer. She is an avid volleyball player, is constantly trying to improve her golf game, and also enjoys playing the piano, traveling, cooking, and baking.

Dr. Damon Thielen

Damon Thielen was born and raised in Pierre, South Dakota. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of South Dakota, earning a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. Dr. Thielen obtained his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, a Master of Public Health degree and a certificate in Dental Public Health from Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health. He completed a pediatric dental residency at Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin, where he was Chief Resident.

Dr. Thielen spent almost ten years in private practice in South Dakota and was extremely active in helping to promote the oral health of the children of the state. He is founding member and Director of Sioux Empire Smiles, a free dental clinic helping under-served children in southeastern South Dakota. He was awarded Young Dentist of the Year by the South Dakota Dental Association in 2017.

Dr. Thielen is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and holds hospital privileges at the Children’s Hospital of Wisconsin. He is a member of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Wisconsin Society of Pediatric Dentists.

Dr. Thielen enjoys helping to shape the face of organized dentistry and has been a Board Member, Trustee and past President of district/state dental associations.

In his free time, Dr. Thielen enjoys playing tennis, collecting sports cards, golfing with the in-laws and spending time with his wife Jessica and their two children Vada and Vance. The Thielen’s are excited to be back to Wisconsin!

Dr. Lauren Murray

Dr. Lauren Murray is originally from Minneapolis, Minnesota. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Sciences at Marquette University. She continued her education at Marquette University School of Dentistry where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery. Dr. Lauren continued her postgraduate education at an advanced education in general dentistry at the Milwaukee VA gaining more experience in hospital dentistry and caring for medically complex patients.

Dr. Lauren worked as a general dentist at Children’s Wisconsin at the Midtown Clinic finding her passion for pediatric dentistry where she fulfilled her National Health Service Corps commitment. She then completed her pediatric dental residency at Children’s Wisconsin and served as the Chief Resident.

Dr. Lauren is a current board candidate for the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is a member of the Wisconsin Dental Society and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Lauren and her husband Tyler live in Wauwatosa with their cat Fresh. In her free time she enjoys traveling, reading, spending time on the lake and cheering on the Milwaukee Bucks.
