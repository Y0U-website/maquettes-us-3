Doctor

Aristo Shyn

Dr. Aristo Shyn attended the University of North Florida and graduated summa cum laude with a bachelor’s degree in Biology and a minor in Public Health. He was distinguished as a published researcher and was the sole recipient of the Academic Achievement Award in Biology from his graduating class.

He completed his dental training at Nova Southeastern University and received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree by the age of 23. Dr. Shyn has received doctoral and postdoctoral training in nearly all aspects of comprehensive dentistry.

Patients have hailed from all over Georgia and even from out-of-state to receive dental treatment at his practice. Dr. Shyn consistently exceeds the minimum number of continuing education requirements for his licensure and is focused on innovating the patient experience.

Before calling Georgia his permanent home, Dr. Shyn grew up in Alaska and spent 10 years along the Florida coast. He is thrilled not to have to deal with grizzly bears or tropical storms.

When out of the office, Dr. Shyn enjoys golf and supporting local causes.
