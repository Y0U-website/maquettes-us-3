Dental Implants The Gold Standard of Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are an excellent option for replacing missing teeth. After the implant is placed, a custom-made ceramic crown is attached to restore both the appearance and functionality of your tooth. The crown is designed to look and feel like a natural tooth, helping you smile, eat, and speak with confidence.

What makes dental implants stand out is their ease of maintenance. They are simple to clean, and the ceramic crown is resistant to staining, making it easy to maintain your implant just like your natural teeth. Regular check-ups are essential, and our team will guide you on how to care for your implant to ensure its longevity.

Dental Implants for Function and Aesthetics

Dental implants have many benefits over other tooth replacement options.

Whole Tooth Replacement

Instead of just filling the gap in your smile, implants replace the entire tooth from the root to the crown. This is beneficial for maintaining healthy bone structure and the appearance of the face.

Enjoy an Unrestricted Diet

Because dental implants are rooted in the jawbone, you do not have to avoid chewy or crunchy foods. You can exercise the same common sense care as you do with your natural teeth.

A Long-Lasting Solution

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth. Once your dental implant integrates with your jawbone, your dental implant has the potential to last a lifetime with the proper care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most patients are (or can become) excellent candidates for dental implants. At Natural Smiles Family Dentistry, we believe that anyone who is missing teeth and in good overall health can benefit from this long-lasting solution. However, to ensure a successful and predictable outcome, your mouth needs to be free from certain conditions that may impact the healing and success of the implant.

For example, gum disease can affect implants in much the same way it affects natural teeth, so it’s essential to have healthy gums before undergoing the procedure. Untreated infections that affect the bone can also delay healing and may need to be addressed beforehand.

In some cases, patients may have insufficient bone to securely hold the dental implant in place. The good news is that this can often be corrected with a bone grafting procedure, which restores lost bone and provides a stable foundation for the implant.

Additionally, certain lifestyle factors, such as smoking or uncontrolled diabetes, may affect the success of dental implants. Smoking can hinder healing, while diabetes can affect the body s ability to recover after surgery. At Natural Smiles Family Dentistry, Dr. Luz will evaluate your individual situation and work with you to overcome any obstacles to ensure the best possible outcome.
