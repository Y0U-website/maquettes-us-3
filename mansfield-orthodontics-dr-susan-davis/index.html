The practice

Communications are easy: you can call, text or email and someone will be able to help you. You can schedule your own Consultation appointment online. All our forms are online, and appointment reminders are automatically emailed and texted to your cell phone.

High-tech personalized treatment with digital workflow. Dr. Davis will craft a customized modern and efficient treatment plan specifically for your orthodontic needs. You’ll spend less time waiting, more time with the doctor.

Our remote digital monitoring will potentially allow you to go longer between visits. The result? Fewer visits, some of which can be from your own home through your mobile phone. Less time driving, missing school, work, or activities means more time for you and your family!

We have payment plans with flexible down payments and monthly payments to suit your personal financial situation. We accept insurance, and are In-Network with Cigna and Delta.

We are centrally located in Mansfield at the junction of Route 6 and Route 195, close to the East Brook Mall. We are 6 miles from the University of Connecticut main Campus. We serve all the communities in and around Mansfield and Storrs.

We use digital 3D scanning technology to eliminate those impressions that no one likes!

Treatment is easier to tolerate, with shorter treatment time, gentle tooth movements and less discomfort than older types of braces.
