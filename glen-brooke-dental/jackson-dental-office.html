Meet Your Dentists

Skilled and Compassionate Professionals Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Matteen Kashef

Background

Dr. Kashef is a second-generation dentist whose passion for dentistry began at an early age. Growing up, he spent afternoons helping at his mother’s dental office, where he developed an appreciation for the meaningful relationships and positive impact dentistry can have on patients’ lives.

He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Nutritional Sciences with a minor in Health Disparities in Society from the University of Florida before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Throughout dental school, Dr. Kashef dedicated select Saturdays to providing volunteer dental care to underserved communities in North Florida. He also co-led a weekly mentorship group for middle school students centered on moral development, community service, and building meaningful friendships.

Dr. Kashef values creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, heard, and empowered in their oral health journey.

Dental Style

Dr. Kashef believes that exceptional dentistry begins with trust. He values taking the time to understand each patient’s goals, concerns, and experiences so that treatment decisions feel collaborative and personalized. His approach is rooted in patient-centered care—creating an environment where patients feel comfortable asking questions, understood in their priorities, and empowered to make informed decisions about their oral health. Through open communication and genuine relationships, Dr. Kashef strives to provide care that supports both long-term oral health and overall well-being.

Fun Facts

Outside the office, Dr. Kashef enjoys spending time with his wife, whether that means trying new recipes together, exploring hiking trails, discovering local favorites, and watching Milwaukee Bucks basketball with family and friends.
