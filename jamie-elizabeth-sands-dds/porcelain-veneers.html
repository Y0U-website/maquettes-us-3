Frequently Asked Questions

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With proper care, porcelain veneers typically last 10–20 years. Avoiding habits like nail-biting, ice-chewing, and using your teeth as tools will help extend their lifespan.

Are veneers painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel discomfort during treatment. Some patients experience mild sensitivity for a few days after placement, which resolves quickly.
