Our Mission

We are committed to provide you with excellent care, through the use of state-of-the-art procedures, using premium materials, and to accomplish it in a stress-free environment. Our mission is to solve your issues by trying to do the most for you with the least amount of procedures and to do so at a competitive and fair fee.

Meet the Doc

Dr. Richard Wood was born in Trinidad & Tobago where he attended the University of the West Indies, St. Augustine Campus. After three years, he received his Bachelor’s Degree in Chemistry and Physics in 1984, and became a certified teacher.

In 1988, he moved to Florida to attend the College of Dentistry at The University of Florida at Gainesville. In 1992, he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree with high honors, later moving to the Pensacola area where he worked as a dental practice associate until 2007. He then acquired a practice in Avon Park, and has kept busy providing exceptional dental care for his patients since then.
