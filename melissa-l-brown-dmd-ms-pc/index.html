The practice

At Dr. Brown’s office, our mission is to provide excellence in orthodontics while creating an experience for our patients and their families in which they feel valued, well-cared for and appreciated - like they’re part of Dr. Brown’s family!

We strive to create confident smiles and experiences that last a lifetime while providing the personal touch and individualized care that we all desire to receive from our healthcare providers!

We look forward to hosting you at our office where our brand new building, state-of-the-art technology, and qualified team ensure that you receive the most efficient, comfortable, and quality care possible.

Advanced Technology From digital impressions & 3D printing to advanced treatment planning modalities, our practice utilizes cutting-edge technology for efficient, precise, and seamless treatment experiences.

Team of Experts Dr. Brown and her team are committed to learning the latest technologies in orthodontics and evaluating how and if they should be implemented into the practice.

Family Oriented We pride ourselves on being a family-oriented practice dedicated to creating a comfortable and friendly environment for patients of all ages.

Dental providers can use our secure digital referral form to send orthodontic treatment details directly to our team. We will review the information and follow up with next steps. Patients do not need a referral to schedule an orthodontic consultation and may contact us anytime to get started.
