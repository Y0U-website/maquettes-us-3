The practice

We provide comprehensive care tailored to meet the needs of individuals across the lifespan. From children and adolescents to adults and seniors, our dedicated team offers personalized treatment plans designed to address a wide range conditions.

Since 2000, Dr. Laura Glicksman has been working with patients to provide an exceptional orthodontic experience from the moment you walk into our office until the big day braces are removed.

Dr. Glicksman’s experience in orthodontics is coupled with her genuine concern for patients. All of our staff is dedicated and we strive to maintain a comfortable, relaxed office atmosphere with an outgoing and friendly personality.

All our professionals at Laura B. Glicksman, MS DMD PC, maintain the highest levels of accreditation and pursue ongoing education to stay abreast of the latest trends in orthodontics.

Dr. Glicksman is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the North East Society of Orthodontists, and the Massachusetts Association of Orthodontists. She is a member of the American Dental Association, and is very active in the Massachusetts Dental Society where she served as a former chair of the Standing Committee on Women and Leadership, and as a Guest Board Member.
