Women’s Health at Healthy Connections

Our Approach to Women’s Health

When you choose Healthy Connections as your partner, you’re getting the highest quality care and protection of your physical health and emotional wellbeing. We offer:

Prenatal & Obstetrics Care

Your pregnancy journey is one of the most important times in your life. We’re here to provide expert prenatal and obstetrics care, guiding you every step of the way to ensure a healthy and happy pregnancy.

Routine Well-Woman Exams

Routine exams, including breast and pelvic screenings, are essential for maintaining good health. Our comprehensive well-woman exams help you stay proactive about your health.

Hormone Replacement Therapy

We offer advanced bioidentical hormone replacement therapy, helping you achieve hormonal balance and enhance your quality of life.

Infertility Evaluation and Treatment

If you’re experiencing challenges with fertility, we provide thorough evaluations and personalized care to help address your concerns and guide you toward the right solution.

Minimally Invasive Surgeries

Our team specializes in expert pelvic floor repair and other minimally invasive procedures to improve your pelvic health and overall well-being.

Contraceptive Management & HPV Vaccination

We offer a full range of birth control options, including Mirena and Implanon, along with HPV vaccinations to protect your reproductive health.

Adolescent Gynecology

Young women require specialized care, and our adolescent gynecology services are designed to meet their unique needs with sensitivity and expertise.

Urinary Incontinence Diagnosis & Treatment

If you’re experiencing urinary incontinence, we offer comprehensive diagnosis and medical treatment to help improve your quality of life.

Surgical Services Our advanced surgical services include:

  • Vaginal, laparoscopic, and supracervical hysterectomy
  • Reconstructive vaginal surgery
  • Urinary incontinence surgery
  • Myomectomy (fibroid removal)
  • Office-based procedures like Essure tubal occlusion and Novasure endometrial ablation
  • Advanced laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgeries
