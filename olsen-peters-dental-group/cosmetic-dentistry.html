cosmetic dentistry

Your smile is your quickest first impression, so we know you want it to be a good one! Our staff is dedicated to making sure that you have a beautiful smile. Some of our most popular cosmetic procedures include:

Composite restoration: to restore a damaged tooth to natural shape and function Veneers: porcelain facing on the front of the tooth used mostly to improve appearance of a patient’s smile, or sometimes to improve function Bridge or dental implant : both excellent solutions to replacing a missing tooth Clear aligners: custom fitted, clear plastic alignment trays help discreetly align your smile
