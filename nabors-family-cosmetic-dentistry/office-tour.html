At Nabors Family and Cosmetic Dentistry, our goal is to provide personalized care to every patient who walks through our door. We always strive to build lasting trust and friendships, because those qualities can make your visit simple and stress-free.

When you see us for an appointment, you’ll be greeted by a friendly team and a clean environment. Our instruments are sterilized, and surfaces are disinfected after each appointment. We exceed all Occupational Safety and Health Administration recommended procedures.

Our office has six enclosed treatment rooms and three hygiene rooms for privacy. Unlike most dental offices, we want this space to feel comforting, so you can sit back and relax while the dentist works . From pleasant music to natural light and comfortable chairs, you might not even want to leave!
