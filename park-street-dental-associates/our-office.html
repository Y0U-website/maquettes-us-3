Payment Options

We accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover credit cards as well as cash and personal checks. In addition, financing options such as CareCredit and in-house financing are available for major dental work done in our office. Our billing coordinator will be happy to discuss any insurance or billing questions you may have.

Insurance Plans

Our office participates with Delta Dental Premier and Cigna PPO. We can also file claims for most other insurance plans. Please call us with your insurance information prior to your visit, and we will be happy to do a complimentary benefit check for you.

Convenient Location

Our office is located at 83 Park St., Montclair, NJ 07042 with parking available in our lot. We are accessible by NJ Transit buses #11, 28, and 29, and the train to Walnut Train Station. Please enter your zip code or city, state below for door-to-door directions.
