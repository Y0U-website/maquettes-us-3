Restorative Dentistry

In our Restorative Dentistry practice at All About Teeth, we focus on restoring the functionality and aesthetics of teeth that have been compromised due to decay, injury, or disease.

Dental Fillings: One of our primary restorative treatments involves the use of Dental Fillings. These are essential for repairing teeth that have been damaged by decay, restoring them to their normal function and shape. We offer a variety of filling materials to suit different needs and preferences, including amalgam, composite, and porcelain. Each material has its benefits, and we discuss these options with our patients to find the best solution for their specific situation.

Crowns and Bridges: We also specialize in Crowns and Bridges. Crowns are used to completely cover or “cap” a damaged tooth or an implant. They are designed to protect and strengthen the tooth structure while also improving its appearance. Bridges, on the other hand, are utilized to fill the space left by one or more missing teeth. They consist of two or more crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap, with a false tooth or teeth in between. These restorations not only restore the appearance and function of the teeth but also prevent the remaining teeth from shifting out of position.

Root Canal Treatments: Another crucial aspect of our restorative services is Root Canal Treatments. This procedure is employed to treat teeth that are badly decayed or infected. During a root canal, the infected or inflamed pulp inside the tooth is removed, and the inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and disinfected, then filled and sealed. This treatment is vital for preserving the natural tooth and preventing the spread of infection, thereby maintaining the integrity of the patient’s oral health.

At All About Teeth, our goal in Restorative Dentistry is not only to repair or replace damaged teeth but also to ensure that our patients regain their confidence in their smile and maintain optimal oral health. We use advanced techniques and materials to ensure the best outcomes for our patients.
