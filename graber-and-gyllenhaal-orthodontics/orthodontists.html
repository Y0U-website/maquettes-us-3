Personal

“In college, I was originally a technical theatre major because I really enjoyed the planning aspect of putting together a production with a team and working with my hands. However, I had also always been fascinated with science courses because they were extremely interesting. I was drawn to the dental profession because it is a wonderful combination of art and science!

It was an honor to work with my father, for whom I have so much personal and professional respect. I’ve learned a lot from him, most notably the benefits of friendly, one-on-one care that makes you feel like an important part of our family.”

When she isn’t at the office creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Katie likes spending time with her husband and their son, Mikey, and daughter, Bre. Dr. Katie’s hobbies include reading, traveling, and exercising; she is also a big fan of the Chicago Cubs.

Personal

Dr. Leslie is thrilled to be serving patients in the community where she grew up. Her dad, who was a Periodontist, greatly motivated her desire to be part of the dental profession, and her brother felt that same influence as he is a dentist as well!

Dr. Leslie values a patient-centered approach and understands that each person is unique in their treatment goals and needs. She truly believes that being an Orthodontist is the best profession and feels thankful to have a career where she can positively affect those around her by creating beautiful healthy smiles and help build confidence while doing so.

When she is not in the office, Dr. Leslie can be found spending time with her husband and two young sons . She has always been interested in health and fitness and enjoys trying different group fitness classes and anything Peloton! After attending two Big Ten universities, Dr. Leslie loves watching any and all college sports games but will always be loyal to her Wisconsin Badgers.
