Full Mouth Dental Implants

  • Implants are the strongest and longest-lasting option for tooth replacement.
  • Implants do not require alteration to the structure of adjoining teeth, like dental bridges do.
  • Implants help preserve the bone mass in the jaw, which is a major factor for maintaining long term oral health. They prevent the collapsed, wrinkled facial look caused by the bone loss that occurs because of missing teeth.

The Full Mouth Dental Implant Procedure

With our specialized training in implant placement and the creation of new teeth, the experts at Karmazin Dental know how the new teeth should look and function. The crowns, bridges or dentures that will attach to the implants are custom fabricated using a certified dental lab, which only uses high-quality materials to ensure long-lasting comfort and fit.

Our team uses advanced technology to ensure the accuracy of your implant treatment. Using a state-of-the-art 3D CT scanner, we capture detailed 360-degree views of your entire mouth and jaw, including nerves and bone density. Special 3D software allows us to map out the exact location and angle of each implant, simulating the entire procedure. If needed, we can create a surgical guide to use during the procedure to aid in implant placement. The use of this 3D imaging and planning makes the procedure efficient and minimally invasive.

When we have your personalized plan developed, we will first administer a local anesthetic to numb the immediate area. If you might experience some anxiety, we can discuss the addition of a dental sedation. Then the implants, which looks like tiny screws or cylinders, are surgically placed into your jaw. The incision is closed with sutures and now we wait. Over the next two to six months, the titanium implants and the bone are allowed to bond, grow, or fuse together to form anchors for your artificial teeth. During this time, a temporary teeth replacement option will be designed for you.

Now a second step is usually necessary to uncover the implants and attach extensions or abutments. These temporary healing caps, along with the connecting devices that allow your multiple crowns restoration to attach to the implants, complete the foundation on which your new teeth will be placed. Your gums need a couple of weeks following this procedure to heal.

Depending upon the number of implants placed, the connecting device that will hold your new teeth can be tightened down on the implant. Karmazin Dental may recommend a ball type snap-on retaining fixture or a bar type that follows the curve of your arch. The final restoration will snap on either foundation.

Finally, full bridges or full dentures will be custom created for you and be placed on to your retaining system. After a short time, you will experience restored confidence in your smile and your ability to chew and speak.

Every case is different, and some of these steps can be combined when conditions permit. Our team will work with you to determine the best treatment plan.
