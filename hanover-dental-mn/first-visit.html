Find information pertaining to dental benefits and payment options below to prepare you for your first visit to Hanover Dental. We are happy to serve you—let us know what we can do to make your experience exceptional!

No Insurance? Not a Problem.

We accept most insurance plans. Our goal is to help you maximize your benefits. Text , call, or use the form below to confirm your benefits at Hanover Dental. Unfortunately, we are not contracted with state insurance plans (MA).

Did you know 1 in 3 adults don’t have any form of dental insurance? Don’t let that be a barrier to receiving dental care. We offer an in-house savings plan as an economical alternative to dental insurance. Other financing options are also available. Contact our office to learn more.

What to Expect

We make sure all of our new patients feel welcome, safe, and taken care of. After reserving your appointment, we will email you a link to New Patient Forms. To make your appointment quicker, please fill them out before your first appointment. You can also access them with the button located here .

Your first visit usually consists of two main parts: 1) a complete exam and 2) a cleaning. During the exam, Dr. Tyler will review your dental and medical history, examine your teeth, and review x-rays and photos of your mouth. From there, he will explain any issues and help you choose options for treatment. Most often, one of our awesome hygiene team members will complete a cleaning.
