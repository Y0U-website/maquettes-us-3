The practice

You’ve heard about root canals before, and probably not in a good way. But why are root canals so important? They restore the health and function of your tooth. You won’t have to be in pain while enjoying your favorite foods and drinks.

Think of retreatment like adding another coat of paint on a wall. You painted it and let it dry, but some spotty patches remain. Retreatment is like that. You had a root canal but still, feel some discomfort well after the procedure.
