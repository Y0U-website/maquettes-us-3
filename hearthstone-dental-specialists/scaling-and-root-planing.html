  • Fresher breath: Removing plaque and tartar from the teeth can help eliminate bad breath caused by bacteria.

What to Expect

Before the procedure, the periodontist will examine the teeth and gums to determine the severity of the gum disease. They may also take X-rays to check for any underlying issues. Apart from removing buildup, they may also smooth out the roots to help the gums reattach to the teeth. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort.

Aftercare and Recovery

After scaling and root planing, patients may experience some mild discomfort, swelling, or bleeding. It is essential to follow the aftercare instructions provided by the periodontist to ensure proper healing. Patients should avoid smoking, eat soft foods, and brush and floss gently for a few days after the procedure. It is also recommended to schedule follow-up appointments with the periodontist to monitor healing and ensure optimal oral health.
