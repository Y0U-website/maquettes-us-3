Dental Implants in Alhambra, CA

Implants are easy to care for, too – simply continue to follow a diligent oral hygiene routine as you always have. With proper oral hygiene and regular exams and cleanings, your implants may last a lifetime.

  • Dental implants resemble natural tooth structure
  • With dental implants, your teeth will remain stable and secure so you can enjoy all your favorite foods.
  • Titanium implants can be used to improve the stability of dentures, by preventing slipping and shifting.
  • The American Academy of Dental Implantology indicates that dental implants have an amazing success rate of up to 98 %.
