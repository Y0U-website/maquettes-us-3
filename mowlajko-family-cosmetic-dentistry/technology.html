Technology

Mowlajko Family Dentistry blends advanced technology with compassionate care to elevate your dental experience. Our state-of-the-art equipment, ranging from digital x-rays to TVs in treatment rooms, is selected with your comfort and health in mind, ensuring precise treatments and a relaxing visit every time.

Digital X-Rays & Sensors

Digital x-rays and sensors at Mowlajko Family Dentistry offer a safer and more efficient way to examine your oral health. This advanced technology uses electronic sensors rather than traditional film. This creates a more comfortable experience and produces detailed images of your teeth.

Using digital x-rays also significantly reduces radiation exposure by as much as 80 percent compared to conventional x-rays. Some uses for digital x-rays include detecting cavities, evaluating jawbone health, and planning for procedures like implants, extractions, and orthodontics.

Panoramic X-Rays

Panoramic x-rays offer a comprehensive view of your mouth, including all your teeth, jawbones, and surrounding facial structures in one complete image. This technology is beneficial because it shows a broad perspective essential for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

The benefit of panoramic x-rays is a more comfortable and less invasive experience than traditional x-rays. Panoramic x-rays help us plan orthodontic treatment, evaluate jaw structure, and detect cysts, tumors, or bone abnormalities. Since these x-rays are taken outside the mouth, they also benefit children, adults with a strong gag reflex, or patients with special needs.

Nitrous Oxide or "Laughing Gas"

Nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas, is a mild sedative that ensures you have a comfortable and anxiety-free dental experience. This sedation method involves placing a small breathing mask over your nose and administering a nitrous oxide and oxygen mixture.

As you inhale, you will feel the immediate effects and experience a feeling of relaxation and well-being. Nitrous oxide s effects are mild and wear off quickly after removing the mask, allowing you to return to your usual activities soon after your appointment. We commonly use nitrous for various dental procedures, from routine cleanings to more complex treatments like crowns and dental implant placement.

Digital Records

This modern approach to record-keeping ensures the accuracy, efficiency, and security of your information. Digital records allow our team members to quickly access your dental history, treatment plans, and x-rays for seamless communication and personalized care. Digital recordkeeping also supports environmental sustainability by reducing paper use.

Rotary Endodontics

Rotary endodontics is an advanced form of root canal therapy used at Mowlajko Family Dentistry. This technology eliminates the need for manual steel files used for traditional root canal therapy. It is electrically powered for a smooth, seamless procedure that eliminates uncomfortable sensations commonly experienced during a root canal. Rotary endodontics also allows for more efficient cleaning and shaping of the root canals, leading to more effective treatments.

For you, using rotary endodontics means a quicker, quieter, and more comfortable root canal experience with reduced treatment time.

Intraoral Cameras

An intraoral camera is a small, handheld device used to capture detailed images of the inside of your mouth. It allows for accurate diagnosis with clear, close-up views of your teeth and gums, revealing issues that might not be visible to the naked eye.

The images are displayed on a computer monitor so you can see what the dentist sees and better understand your dental condition. This transparency helps educate and empower you and leads to a more informed approach to your dental care.

TVs in Treatment Rooms

This simple yet effective amenity allows you to watch your favorite shows or movies during dental procedures for a welcome distraction and a more relaxing experience. The presence of TVs helps ease anxiety and makes the time spent in the dental chair more enjoyable and less stressful for patients of all ages.

Digital Impressions

Digital impressions have revolutionized the way we capture the precise details of your mouth. This leading-edge technology uses a digital intraoral scanner to create a highly accurate 3-D digital model of your teeth and gums. We also use the resulting scans to track changes in your dental health from one visit to the next.

For you, digital impressions mean a more comfortable experience with no need for the unpleasant taste or discomfort of messy mold materials. The detailed nature of the impressions also allows for exceptionally precise measurements, leading to better-fitting restorations and appliances.
