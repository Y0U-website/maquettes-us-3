Professional Dental Cleanings

Even the most diligent home care routine cannot remove all plaque and tartar from your teeth. Tartar also called calculus is hardened mineral deposits that form when plaque is not fully removed. Once tartar develops, only a professional cleaning can remove it safely. Our hygienists use specialized instruments to clean thoroughly along and below the gum line, in spaces between teeth, and on tooth surfaces that brushing misses.

Most patients benefit from a professional cleaning every six months. Patients with a history of gum disease or higher cavity risk may need more frequent visits. After your cleaning, your teeth will be polished and your mouth will feel noticeably fresher.

Comprehensive Dental Exams

Every hygiene visit at Hawkins Dentistry includes a thorough exam with Dr. Kent Hawkins. He checks each tooth for signs of decay, evaluates your gum health, examines your bite, and screens for oral cancer. Digital X-rays are taken at appropriate intervals to evaluate what is happening inside and between teeth, below the gum line, and around the roots.

Catching problems early makes them far easier to treat. A small cavity found at a routine exam means a simple filling. A small area of gum inflammation addressed early rarely becomes serious. Regular exams are one of the most valuable investments you can make in your health.

Gum Disease Screening

During your hygiene visit, our team measures the depth of the pockets around each tooth a key indicator of gum health. Healthy gum tissue fits snugly around the tooth. As gum disease develops, these pockets deepen, allowing bacteria to accumulate below the gum line. Identifying early gum disease allows us to intervene with professional cleaning and improved home care before the condition advances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule a dental hygiene appointment?

Most patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months. Some individuals with gum disease, a history of frequent cavities, or other risk factors may need visits every three to four months. Dr. Kent Hawkins will recommend the schedule that best supports your oral health.

What does a dental hygienist do during a visit?

Our dental hygienist removes plaque and tartar buildup from your teeth, polishes your tooth surfaces, checks for signs of gum disease, takes X-rays when needed, and provides personalized oral health guidance. Dr. Kent Hawkins then performs a comprehensive exam to evaluate your overall oral health and address any concerns.

Why are dental X-rays important?

X-rays allow our team to see inside and between teeth, around roots, and beneath the gum line areas that cannot be evaluated visually. They help us detect cavities, monitor bone levels, check the status of existing restorations, and identify issues before they become symptomatic. We use low-radiation digital X-ray technology for clear, comfortable imaging.

Can dental hygiene visits help prevent serious health problems?

Yes. Research has linked poor oral health to increased risk of heart disease, stroke, diabetes complications, and other systemic conditions. Removing bacteria-laden plaque and tartar through regular professional cleanings reduces the bacterial load in your mouth and supports your overall health, not just your oral health.

What should I do to maintain my oral health between visits?

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, floss once a day, drink plenty of water, and limit sugary and acidic foods. If Dr. Kent Hawkins has recommended specific products or tools like a water flosser, prescription fluoride, or a specific toothbrush use them consistently. And keep your six-month appointments at Hawkins Dentistry.
