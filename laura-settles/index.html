The practice

Dr. Settles grew up in Bristol, Virginia, and is a graduate of Virginia High School. Laura then pursued her undergraduate studies at Bluefield State College in West Virginia. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from West Virginia University.

Dr. Settles has been a long time volunteer in dental outreach in Appalachia. She loves the people and culture of the region. She brings with her an eagerness to advance the skills she acquired at WVU. As a result, we know she’ll provide our patients with the best treatment options to restore confidence and function.

Dr. Settles has earned many awards, including the Academy of General Dentistry Award, Academy of Esthetic Dentistry Award, Tobacco Cessation Award, and Academy of Operative Dentistry Award.

She and her husband Shawn now live in Wytheville with their 3 beloved pets, 2 cats and a German Shepherd dog.

Implants work best when you have adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate whether you’re a candidate or if preparatory procedures like bone grafting are needed first.

Dr. Jones, Dr. Davenport, or Dr. Holston examines your mouth and takes 3D imaging to assess bone quality and plan precise implant placement.

We surgically place titanium implant posts into your jawbone under local anesthesia (sedation available). The implant integrates with your bone over 3-6 months.

Les soins

Once healed, we attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Your new teeth are crafted to match your natural tooth color, shape, and bite.

Most dental practices refer implant cases to oral surgeons or periodontists in larger cities. At Wytheville Dental Group, our prosthodontists handle the entire process—from implant placement through final restoration—right here in Wytheville.
