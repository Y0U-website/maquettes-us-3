Who Is a Candidate for Implants?

Many patients can benefit from dental implants. Ideal candidates generally have:

If you’re unsure whether implants are right for you, our team will perform a thorough evaluation and discuss your options.

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums and good overall oral hygiene. If improvements are needed, our team can provide care and guidance to help you get ready for treatment.
  • Adequate bone support in the jaw (or are eligible for bone grafting if needed)
  • No uncontrolled medical conditions that could affect healing

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation & Planning

We take 3D imaging and intraoral scans, using cutting edge technology, to design a personalized treatment plan.

Extraction and Bone Grafting

If a damaged tooth is still present, it will be gently extracted and a bone graft will be placed to strengthen the jaw and create a stable foundation for future implant.

Implant Placement

A titanium post is surgically and gently placed into the jawbone, where it will act as the new tooth root.. Most patients experience only minor discomfort afterward, similar to having a tooth extracted.

Healing & Integration

Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. While your implant heals, you may receive a temporary crown or denture for function and appearance.

Restoration

Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant(s), completing your smile.

Caring for Dental Implants

Dental implants are designed to last for many years, but just like natural teeth, they require proper care. To keep them healthy:

With the right care, dental implants can provide a permanent, reliable solution for missing teeth for a lifetime.

  • Brush and floss daily to prevent gum disease around the implant
  • Visit your dentist regularly for checkups and cleanings
  • Avoid smoking, which can interfere with healing and long-term success

Frequently Asked Questions

Do implant dentures hurt?

Most patients report only mild discomfort after implant surgery, similar to having a tooth extracted. Any soreness typically resolves within a few days, and we provide personalized instructions for a smooth recovery.

How long do implant dentures last?

With proper care, the implants themselves can last decades — often a lifetime. The denture portion may need adjustments or replacement over time due to normal wear, but overall longevity is far greater than traditional dentures.

Will I be without teeth during the process?

No. In most cases, patients are provided with a temporary denture to wear during healing, so you’ll never have to go without teeth.

Can I eat normally with implant dentures?

Yes! Implant dentures provide significantly stronger bite force compared to traditional dentures, allowing you to enjoy a wider variety of foods with more comfort and confidence.

Does insurance cover implant dentures?

Coverage varies depending on your plan. Some policies may cover extractions, grafting, or part of the denture cost. Our team will help review your benefits and financing options.
