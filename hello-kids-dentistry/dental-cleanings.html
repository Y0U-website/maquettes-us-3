Dental Cleanings

Our team explains each step in kid-friendly terms to help ease anxiety and build trust. We believe in empowering children to feel confident and informed about their oral health!

  • Flossing Between Each Tooth: We carefully floss between teeth to remove any remaining food particles and plaque that could cause decay.
  • Tooth Polishing: We use a special flavored polish to clean and smooth the surfaces of the teeth, leaving them shiny and fresh.
  • Fluoride Treatment (if recommended): A quick fluoride application helps strengthen enamel, protect against cavities, and support overall tooth health especially for kids who are still learning to brush effectively.

Parent Partnership

Your role in your child’s dental journey is so important and we’re here to support you every step of the way. At each cleaning visit, we offer parents clear, practical guidance to help maintain strong dental habits at home between visits.

We provide customized advice based on your child’s unique needs, including:

We love working together with parents to create a strong foundation for lifelong dental wellness. It’s more than just a cleaning it’s a step toward a future full of healthy, happy smiles.

  • Brushing and Flossing Techniques: Learn age-appropriate strategies to help your child brush and floss effectively, even through those tricky toddler and tween stages.
  • Smart Nutrition for Healthy Teeth: We’ll guide you on tooth-friendly snacks and meals, and how to reduce sugary foods that can increase the risk of cavities.
  • Fluoride and Mouthwash Use: We’ll discuss whether your child needs fluoride supplementation or mouthwash and how to use them safely at home.
