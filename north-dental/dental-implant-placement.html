Dental implant placement

If you’re considering dental implants, Dr. Moorad will thoroughly assess your oral health to determine if you’re a candidate and explain the benefits in detail.

  • Natural Appearance and Feel: Implants mimic the look and function of natural teeth, providing a seamless appearance that feels completely natural.
  • Preserve Bone Structure: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, helping to prevent bone loss that often accompanies missing teeth.
  • Stability for Eating and Speaking: Unlike removable dentures, implants are securely anchored, providing stability for speaking and chewing without shifting.

The Dental Implant Placement Process at North Dental

Placing a dental implant requires several stages, each designed to ensure optimal integration with the jawbone and a secure fit for the final restoration. Here’s what you can expect during the dental implant placement process at North Dental:

1. Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your dental implant journey begins with an in-depth consultation. Dr. Moorad will evaluate your oral health, review any relevant medical history, and take digital X-rays to assess the bone structure. This comprehensive examination helps us develop a personalized treatment plan, including the type of implant that best suits your needs.

If you require preparatory procedures, such as bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone, we’ll incorporate these into your treatment plan for the best possible outcome.

2. Implant Placement Surgery

On the day of your implant placement, we ensure you’re comfortable by using local anesthesia and, if needed, sedation options to ease any anxiety. During the procedure, Dr. Moorad carefully places the titanium implant post into the jawbone, serving as an artificial tooth root. This surgical step is performed with precision to ensure a stable and long-lasting result.

Following implant placement, we’ll provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to support healing.

3. Osseointegration and Healing

After the implant is placed, a natural process called osseointegration begins, where the jawbone gradually fuses with the titanium post. This phase typically takes several months, and it’s crucial for creating a strong foundation for the final restoration. During this period, we may place a temporary restoration to maintain aesthetics and function.

4. Abutment and Final Restoration

Once osseointegration is complete, Dr. Moorad will attach an abutment, a small connector piece that holds the crown or other restoration in place. The custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture is then secured to the abutment, completing the process and restoring the function and appearance of your smile.

Aftercare and Maintenance of Dental Implants

Proper aftercare is essential to ensure the long-term success of your dental implant. Our team provides thorough instructions and support throughout the healing process:

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing a stable, beautiful smile.

  • Maintain Good Oral Hygiene: Just like natural teeth, implants require regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings to stay healthy and functional.
  • Regular Checkups: Routine exams allow us to monitor the implant and ensure that it remains securely integrated with the jawbone.
  • Avoid Certain Habits: To protect your implant, avoid hard foods, refrain from smoking, and wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth.
