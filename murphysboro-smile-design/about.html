Dr. Yun S Kim DDS

Dr. Kim graduated from the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry, completing an accelerated six-year program. With 30 years of experience, he is passionate about providing high quality dental care and building strong, trusting relationship with his patients through personal attention and compassion. Dr.Kim remains committed to lifelong learning through continuing education. Outside the office, he enjoys playing golf and traveling with his family. Dr. Kim is married and has two sons who are currently attending dental school and aspire to follow in his footsteps.

Dr. Stroud-Martin DMD Dr. Stroud-Martin is a graduate of Waltonville High School, Rend Lake College, Southern Illinois University Carbondale, and Southern Illinois University Edwardsville. She is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Southern Illinois Dental Society, American Association of Implant Dentists, and the American Association of Women Dentists,.

"My biggest reasons for starting my own practice were my staff and my patients. Starting my own practice would not have been so successful without Gods guidance. I found an awful looking building that “luckily” (God) the owners were willing to completely rehab it for a dental practice. Everything was refinished. I picked every tiny aspect of it and designed it with patient and staff ease and comfort in mind. I’m really proud of it now and the journey has been great."

She enjoys making her patients and staff feel like they are part of our family. Dentistry is stressful for everyone involved so we try to make it less stressful with different types of music and a family type atmosphere.

" I personally love all varieties of music. My husband Anthony and I have seven children 4 sons aged 14,13,12 and three daughters aged 3,2, and 1. I enjoy music, owning my own business, and doing continuing education. I am addicted to challenges, learning, and goals."

Anna Mae Hawkins is a registered dental hygienist who has proudly called Pinckneyville home her entire life. She graduated from Pinckneyville High School before earning her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene from Southern Illinois University in 2006.

Anna Mae and her husband, Wayne, continue to live in Pinckneyville, where they’re raising their three children, Clay, Dax, and Peyton, along with a variety of farm animals. Outside the office, she enjoys cheering on her kids at their sporting events, spending time on the water, boating, reading, and playing golf whenever she can. One of her favorite places to unwind is Orange Beach, Alabama.

What Anna Mae enjoys most about dentistry is building relationships with her patients and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles. She loves meeting new people and is committed to making every patient feel comfortable, cared for, and welcome during their visit.
