DR. YURY BERSHADSKY

Dr. Bershadsky is more than committed to dental perfection. In addition to creating the natural beautiful smile you’ve always dreamed of, you will also receive attentive and responsive care. No question will go unanswered and every concern will be addressed assuring you the most pleasant and thorough experience possible. At L.A. Dental Arts, our goal is to provide top quality dental care to our patients, from that first toddler’s check-up to adult care.

EXTENSIVE EDUCATION

Dr. Bershadsky finished his undergraduate medical studies at the University of Illinois at Chicago, and was accepted to Northwestern University Dental School; becoming one of the youngest dental school attendees in the history of Northwestern University. After graduating with honors in 1995, he became a part of a prominent prosthodontic dental group affiliated with Northwestern University Dental School, where he stayed for two years gaining additional training and experience in advance prosthodontics, implant, cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

In addition, Dr. Bershadsky has also been certified in orthodontics, Invisalign and TMJ therapy through a 2 year intense residency program. Other continuing education includes certification by the California Implant Institute for advanced bone grafting, surgical placement, and restoration of dental implants; as well as extensive training in Cosmetic rehabilitation and full mouth reconstruction utilizing implants and aesthetic modalities in modern dentistry.

Dr. Yury Bershadsky is also a trained and certified CEREC 3D practitioner, utilizing the latest technology in fabricating porcelain crowns, veneers and onlays in a single appointment.
