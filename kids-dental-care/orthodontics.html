It’s not uncommon to associate those awkward childhood years with braces, retainers, rubber bands, and the like. That’s because dental experts understand the importance of addressing issues with teeth alignment early as a child’s mouth is still developing. WHAT EXACTLY IS ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT?

HOW CAN IT HELP MY CHILD?

Orthodontic treatment focuses on using braces, retainers, rubber bands, and other tools to assist in forcing your teeth into the desired, healthy position. Correcting issues with tooth placement or alignment is essential in maintaining the health of your mouth.

An orthodontist will perform an assessment to first fit your mouth with it’s initial treatment. Traditional braces use a special grade cement to affix the brackets and wires in your mouth, whereas invisible braces will require you to wear a mold that’s fabricated from your mouth shape.

Over time as your mouth adapts to the treatment, your orthodontist will make periodic adjustments to tighten your braces and continue to force them into the optimal position.
