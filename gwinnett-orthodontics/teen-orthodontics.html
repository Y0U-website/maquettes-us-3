Orthodontic Consultation

Treatment

When your custom digital braces or aligners are ready, we’ll carefully place your brackets/wires or trays and make sure everything fits comfortably. We’ll also walk you through how to care for your braces, so you feel confident and prepared from day one. Our goal is to make your transition into treatment feel smooth, supported, and easy to manage.

Check-Ins with Gwin Monitoring

We will monitor your progress through Gwin Monitoring , in which we remotely monitor your progress week-by-week to minimize your in-office visits while maintaining high-quality care! You will only need to come in when it is time for the next phase of treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Treatment time depends on your specific needs, but most patients wear braces for about 12 to 24 months. With digitally planned treatment, we’re able to guide your progress more efficiently while keeping your results precise and lasting.

You may feel some soreness when braces are first placed or adjusted, but it’s usually mild and temporary. Today’s braces are smaller and more comfortable, and most patients adjust within a few days.

You’ll want to avoid hard, sticky, or chewy foods that can damage your brackets or wires, like popcorn, caramel, and hard candies. We’ll guide you on what to eat and how to care for your braces so your treatment stays on track.

Brushing and flossing are still essential, just with a bit more attention to detail. We’ll show you the best techniques and tools to keep your teeth and gums healthy throughout treatment.

Metal braces are often the most effective option for more complex cases and offer consistent, reliable results. Invisalign is a highly effective but nearly invisible option. During your consultation, we’ll help you decide whether braces or clear aligners are the best fit for your smile and lifestyle.

Most patients come in every 6 to 8 weeks for adjustments, but with digital planning and tools like Smile Monitoring, some visits may be reduced. We’ll keep your treatment progressing smoothly while respecting your time and schedule.

The cost of orthodontic treatment varies based on your individual needs, the type of treatment you choose, and how long your care will take. Because every smile is different, the best way to understand your investment is through a consultation where we can evaluate your goals and create a personalized plan.

At Gwinnett Orthodontics, we believe treatment should feel manageable as well as effective. We offer flexible, interest-free financing and accept most PPO insurance plans, and our team will walk you through all of your options so you can choose what works best for you and your family.
