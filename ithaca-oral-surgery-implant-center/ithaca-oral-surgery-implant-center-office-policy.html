Ithaca Oral Surgery Implant Center Office Policy

Our office is committed to providing you with the best possible care. If you have dental insurance, we are anxious to help you receive your maximum allowable benefits. In order to achieve these goals, we need your assistance and your understanding of our payment policy. We participate with select dental and medical insurance plans. Please check with the receptionist to see if your plan is included among them.

Certain insurance plans require a written referral. It is the patient’s responsibility to present a properly dated referral at the time of the office visit. Co-payments for all insurances are due at the time services are rendered. The exceptions to having a referral of course is if you do not have a current dentist, or it is a sudden dental emergency.

We will gladly discuss our proposed treatment and answer any questions relating to your insurance. You must realize, however, that:

If a surgical procedure is required, we bill your insurance company for our surgeon’s fees only. Any services rendered outside of our office (i.e., pathology, labs, etc.) will be billed separately by each facility. We deal with many outside facilities; therefore, we cannot guarantee that any outside facility participates with your insurance or that all costs will be covered under your particular contract.

We must emphasize that as health care providers, our relationship is with YOU and not your insurance company. While the filing of insurance claims for surgery charges is a courtesy we extend to our patients, all charges are your responsibility from the date the services are rendered .

If you do not have insurance, payment in full is expected prior to service . We offer Care Credit to help make payments easier. Ask about this service if it may help.

If you have any questions about the above information or any uncertainty regarding insurance coverage, PLEASE don’t hesitate to ask us. We are here to HELP YOU.
