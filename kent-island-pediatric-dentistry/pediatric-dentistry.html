Pediatric Dentistry

  • Restorative Dentistry Fillings and dental crowns can help restore the appearance and function of natural teeth. Pulpotomies and extractions are sometimes necessary. More About Restorative Dental Services for Children
  • Sedation Options We want everyone to feel comfortable with the treatment plan and aware of all options available for your child to have a positive experience with any needed dental work. Nitrous oxide and mild oral sedation are available when needed. More About Sedation Options for Children

We Proudly Offer Laser Dentistry with Solea!

For many patients, a dental laser is a more comfortable way to treat many dental issues. In many cases, no anesthetic is needed. A laser makes it fun for kids to have their teeth fixed!
