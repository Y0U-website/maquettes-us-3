Meet the Team at McCall Family Dentistry

Welcome to McCall Family Dentistry, where our family can’t wait to welcome your family to our practice!

Drs. Ryan McCall, Dr. Mira Egbaria, and Dr. Patrick Brambert have decades of experience between them, and we’ve been building our reputation for quality dental care in Geneva for over 40 years.

We utilize state-of-the-art equipment and a warm office atmosphere to create a dental experience you won’t find anywhere else. With a wide range of services, we strive to meet all of your family’s dental needs here, and you can rest assured that you’ll receive dental care, treatment, and restorations that you can rely on and trust.

Our practice is built on family values, and our team is filled with many family members. Learn more about our team here before you visit us, and contact us to schedule you next visit.

Dr. Ryan McCall, DDS, FAGD

Dr. Ryan McCall, or "Dr. Ryan" as he is known to our patients, is a Geneva native who has strong ties to the tri-cities communities.

He attended Illinois State University and earned his bachelor’s degree in Chemistry. Although studying the periodic table provided hours of entertainment, he knew he had to try his hand at something that would allow him to give back to the community. He decided to follow in his father’s footsteps.

Dr. Ryan moved back to the big city, where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Illinois: College of Dentistry. Shortly thereafter, Dr. Ryan decided he wanted a taste of the Northwest, so he packed up his bags and moved to Portland, OR. While there, he completed a general practice hospital residency at the VAMC in 2005 and provided dental care for a large group practice in the area. After three years of enjoying mountaintop views and hiking through the great outdoors, Dr. Ryan decided to return to Geneva to provide dentistry for the community in which he was raised.

Like his father, Dr. Ryan plays guitar and has a passion for music. He is still awaiting the reunion of his high school band, Natural Disaster. He currently resides in Geneva with his wife, Lindsey; daughter, Lyla; and son, Sidney.

Dr. Mira Egbaria, DDS

Dr. Mira is gentle, patient, and caring. She strives to provide the best care possible to her patients in a comfortable environment. Through her residency at Northwestern Memorial Hospital , Dr. Mira achieved advanced training and education in comprehensive general dentistry. She gained experience treating a wide variety of patients and is adept in all aspects of dentistry and loves to focus on prevention and patient education.

When not practicing dentistry, she enjoys spending time with her husband and daughter and watching all Michigan athletic events (Go blue!)

Dr. Patrick Brambert, DDS

Dr. Brambert was born and raised in the west Chicago suburbs and knew he wanted to be a dentist since he was 12.

He went to Elmhurst University for his bachelor’s degree in biology. While at Elmhurst, he was an honors program scholar and published cellular cancer research. His research won first place in the Natural Science and Mathematics category at the National Collegiate Honors Council.

After graduating from Elmhurst, he attended the University of Iowa for his dental degree. At Iowa, he published research regarding tooth whitening, served as class Vice President, and earned a distinction in public health by taking on extra course loads. He capped off his education with an externship at a special needs clinic in Iowa. This was followed by an externship where he worked as a dentist at the Ojibwe tribe reservation in Cass Lake, MN.

Dr. Brambert currently serves as a member of the Army National Guard. He serves as a dental officer, company chief medical officer, and deployed to Iraq in support of Operation Inherent Resolve.

Outside of work, Dr. Brambert enjoys doing improv and acting and has appeared on multiple TV shows. Most of all he loves going on adventures with his wife and two kids.
