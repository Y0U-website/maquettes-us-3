The practice

When your child is sick, the Community Health Worker in your school can arrange a Virtual Visit with a Jordan Valley provider using a TytoCare exam kit. When you pick up your sick child, they will have a diagnosis and, if necessary, a prescription waiting for pickup at the pharmacy of your choice.
