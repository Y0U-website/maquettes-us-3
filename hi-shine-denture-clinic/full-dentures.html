What Are Complete Dentures?

Complete dentures are what most people picture when they think of dentures a prosthetic device that fits into your mouth to replace your teeth. They are made from a flesh-coloured acrylic base that fits over the gums and is supported by the jawbone. Artificial teeth are arranged on top of this. Complete dentures can be supported by implants to provide extra stability. If you have questions about implant dentures, we can provide all the information you need to ensure you feel confident about your decision.
