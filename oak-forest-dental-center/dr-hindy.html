Dr. Mohamed Hindy

Dr. Hindy’s passion about teaching and practicing dentistry made him work on advancing his career and education to achieve excellence in both academia and clinical practice side by side.

Dr. Hindy obtained his Master Degree in Clinical and Translational Sciences and pursued Advanced Education in the field of implant dentistry following the American Academy of Implant Dentistry Credentialing Track.

Dr. Hindy is board certified by the American Board of Oral Implantology and is a Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. His active role in the academy and his annual attendance at the AAID meetings keep him updated with the most advanced treatment modalities in the field of implant dentistry.

Dr. Hindy enjoys spending time with his family and enjoys playing soccer and tennis.
