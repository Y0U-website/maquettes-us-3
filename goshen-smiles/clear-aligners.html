Clear aligners are custom-made trays that gently shift your teeth into better alignment over time. Worn for 20 to 22 hours a day, these removable trays are virtually invisible and can be taken out to eat, brush, and floss. Clear aligners are ideal for: Mild to moderate crowding
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Minor bite issues
  • Relapsed orthodontic cases
  • Adults and teens seeking discreet treatment

Clear Aligners vs. Invisalign

Invisalign is a leading brand of clear aligners and the system Dr. Fugate has used since 2007. While all Invisalign trays are clear aligners, not all clear aligners are Invisalign.

We may also provide other trusted systems for cases that are better suited to alternative aligner technologies. During your consultation, we’ll determine which clear aligner system is right for your needs, goals, and budget.

What to Expect During Treatment

Step 1: Consultation and Digital Scans

We begin with a full dental exam and take digital impressions of your teeth to create your customized treatment plan.

Step 2: Custom Aligner Fabrication

You’ll receive a series of clear aligners, each worn for about two weeks. Each set makes small adjustments until your teeth reach their final alignment.

Step 3: Regular Check-Ins

You’ll return to our Goshen office every 6 to 8 weeks to check progress and receive new aligners. Most patients complete treatment in 6 to 18 months, depending on their case.

Step 4: Retainers

Once treatment is complete, you’ll wear a retainer at night to maintain your new smile.

Are Clear Aligners Right for You?

Clear aligners work best for teens and adults with mild to moderate orthodontic needs. If you have significant bite issues or complex alignment problems, traditional orthodontics may still be required.

If you&#x27;re also dealing with damaged or chipped teeth, we may recommend dental bonding or ceramic crowns in combination with aligners for the best functional and cosmetic results.

Frequently Asked Questions About Clear Aligners

How long do I need to wear clear aligners each day? ‍

Aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours per day for best results. Only remove them when eating or brushing your teeth.

Are clear aligners painful? ‍

Mild pressure or tightness is normal when starting a new set of aligners, but most patients find them more comfortable than metal braces.

What if I lose an aligner tray? ‍

Contact our office immediately. We may instruct you to wear your previous set or move ahead depending on your treatment progress.

Can I get clear aligners if I’ve had braces before? ‍

Yes. Clear aligners are often used for orthodontic relapse cases to realign teeth that have shifted after previous treatment.

Will I need a retainer after aligners?

‍ Yes. Retainers are essential to maintain your new tooth position and prevent relapse. We’ll provide a custom retainer and instructions after treatment.
