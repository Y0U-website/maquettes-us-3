The practice

We understand the power of a confident smile and believe everyone deserves one they love. That s why we take the time to get to know each patient personally, developing individualized treatment plans that address their unique aesthetic and functional needs. From diagnosis to completion, we’ll guide you through every step of the process with clear communication and compassionate care.​

Our philosophy (and mission statement) is simple: we treat every patient like family. We only recommend treatments we would wholeheartedly provide to our own loved ones, ensuring you receive the highest quality of care in a warm and welcoming environment.​

Whether you visit our inviting office in Petoskey or our convenient location in Gaylord, you’ll find the same dedicated team and unwavering commitment to excellence. We eagerly look forward to welcoming you and your family to Great Lakes Orthodontics!
