The practice

At Maple Glen Dental, our mission is to deliver exceptional dental care with a focus on family-friendly service utilizing modern practices and ensuring every patient feels confident and cared for.

Our newly designed office, featuring a modern open-concept layout, is crafted to provide a welcoming and comfortable environment from the moment you arrive.

As a general dentistry practice, we offer a comprehensive range of services, including routine cleanings, check-ups, fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions, implants, and more.

Our preventive care keeps your smile healthy and bright ADA recommends a dental check up and cleaning at most every 6 months.

At Maple Glen Dental, we provide personalized and effective restorative solutions tailored to your needs. We believe in offering you a range of treatment options, including the choice of no treatment . Our goal is to educate and inform you, empowering you to make the final decision regarding your dental care.

Discover the benefits of our modern family dental care. Our newly built office brings new technologies such as intraoral digital impressions (no gooey molds), guided surgeries, 3D CBCT scans, 3D printers and crowns that are fabricated in a full digital workflow.

Out team at Maple Glen Dental brings decades of dental experience.
