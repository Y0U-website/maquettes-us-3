The practice

Herron Regional Dental Center is proud to be your local, family-friendly dental office. We have two offices in Pittsburg & Columbus, KS, and care for families from the surrounding communities of South East Kansas.

Our skilled, compassionate team, under the guidance of Dr. Robert Herron, manages everything from regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to braces, Invisalign®, Invisalign Teen®, crowns, children’s dentistry, and restorative services.

We are passionate about creating healthy, beautiful smiles. Our practice offers exceptional preventive and restorative dental treatments in a professional, honest, and friendly manner. We have an incredible team who personally greets every patient as they come through the door.

Dr. Herron spends time with you to understand, diagnose, and treat your individual needs so that when your care is complete, you have the perfect smile and healthy teeth you’ve always desired.

Behind each smile is a highly skilled yet compassionate dental professional who looks forward to serving the needs of their patients. Our goal is to treat everyone as we would want our own family to be treated.

Dr. Robert Herron is passionate about helping people feel better and look better through high-quality dental care. He has been proudly serving the SEK area for 33 years and he looks forward to many more years of building relationships with and beautiful smiles for everyone he meets.
