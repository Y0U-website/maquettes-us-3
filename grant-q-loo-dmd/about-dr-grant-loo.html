About Dr. Grant Loo

For decades, Dr. Grant Loo has been a trusted provider of dental services for the Augusta, Georgia community. He maintains an unwavering commitment to delivering top-tier dental care while continuously nurturing a passion for lifelong learning and community service. His approach seamlessly integrates his extensive experience with a gentle touch, ensuring that you and your family receive nothing but the best in dental care.

Born and raised in the city of Augusta, Dr. Grant Loo embarked on his academic journey at the Academy of Richmond County. Continuing his pursuit of knowledge, he attended Augusta College before ultimately earning Bachelor of Science degrees in Microbiology and Pharmacy from the University of Georgia.

During his academic journey, he diligently worked as a pharmacist to support his path through dental school. This dedication led him to graduate from the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry in 1990, where he obtained his Doctorate of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Loo’s commitment to the field of dentistry extends beyond his practice. He is an active member of several dental associations, including the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Eastern District Dental Society, Augusta Dental Society, and the Thomas P. Hinman Dental Society. His dedication to these organizations culminated in his presidency of both the Eastern District and Augusta Dental Societies.

He has been honored with fellowships in the Pierre Fauchard Academy and the International College of Dentists, as well as an Honorable Fellowship from the Georgia Dental Association, recognizing his service to the organization. Notably, he was recently awarded the Lifetime Membership Award from the Georgia Dental Association.

Education holds a pivotal role in Dr. Loo’s career. He has actively participated in the School of Dentistry’s Building Imperative Steering Committee for the new College of Dental Medicine building. Presently, he is actively involved with the Dental College of Georgia Continuing Education Committee and serves as a board member of the School of Dentistry Alumni Association Board.

Mentoring aspiring dental professionals brings him great satisfaction, as he guides students contemplating a career in dentistry. Additionally, he serves as a preceptor for Augusta University dental hygiene students and Augusta Technical School dental assisting students, providing invaluable support along their educational journeys.

Dr. Loo extends his commitment to service to community involvement. He is honored to have served as Vice Chair of the Board of Trustees of Augusta Preparatory Day School and currently serves as a Board Member of the Chinese Consolidated Benevolent Association of Augusta. He is affiliated with First Baptist Church of Augusta, where he has attended since childhood.

Outside the dental clinic and community activities, Dr. Loo values quality time with his wife, Shuishan, and their daughters, Mackenzie and Ashley. They cherish creating lasting memories with family and friends, whether it’s watching movies, embarking on exciting travels, or sharing the thrill of cheering for the University of Georgia football team during fall weekends.

Dr. Grant Loo’s active engagement in dental associations and unwavering commitment to ongoing education ensure you’ll receive the most up-to-date and comprehensive dental care, complemented by a deep dedication to community service and integrity. Whether you need a regular check-up, specialized treatment or just some friendly dental advice, he is available for all your dental needs.
