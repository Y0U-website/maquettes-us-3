What Are Braces?

Braces are an orthodontic treatment that uses brackets and wires to guide teeth into better alignment over time. As the teeth move, braces can help improve the way the smile looks and how the bite comes together, creating a more balanced foundation for long-term oral health.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a trusted option for guiding teeth into better positions with consistent, controlled movement. They are commonly used for a wide range of orthodontic needs and give patients a clear treatment path from start to finish.

For many families, metal braces are familiar, dependable, and easy to understand. Mindful helps patients know what to expect throughout treatment, from adjustments to daily care at home.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces work like traditional braces, but they use tooth-colored or less noticeable brackets for a softer appearance. They may be a good option for patients who want the structure of braces with a more subtle look.

This option can be especially appealing for teens and adults who want orthodontic treatment to feel less noticeable in photos, school, work, or social settings. Mindful can help patients understand whether ceramic braces are a good fit for their smile and treatment needs.

The Role of Braces in a Healthier Bite

A healthier bite depends on how the teeth are positioned and how the upper and lower teeth meet. Braces can help improve crowding, spacing, rotated teeth, and bite alignment by guiding the teeth into positions that work better together.

FAQs About Orthodontic Braces

How long do braces usually take?

Treatment time depends on the patient’s age, alignment needs, bite concerns, and how the teeth respond to movement. Mindful will explain the estimated timeline before braces begin.

Do braces hurt?

Braces can cause soreness or pressure after they are first placed or adjusted. This usually means the teeth are responding to movement, and the feeling often becomes easier to manage after the first few days.

How often are braces visits?

Most patients come in for regular adjustment visits throughout treatment. These visits help Mindful monitor progress, make needed changes, and keep the smile moving in the right direction.

What foods should patients avoid with braces?

Patients with braces should avoid very sticky, hard, or chewy foods that can damage brackets or wires. Mindful will review food guidance before treatment starts so patients and families know what to expect.

How do patients care for teeth during braces?

Brushing and flossing take extra attention during braces because food and plaque can collect around brackets and wires. Mindful will show patients how to keep their teeth clean throughout treatment at home.
