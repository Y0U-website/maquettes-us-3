Meet Dr. Jake Beus

Welcome to Main Street Dental & Implants, where we believe that changing lives starts with a smile. Our founder, Dr. Jake Beus, is a devoted father and dedicated dentist who deeply understands the importance of a beautiful smile. His driving passion and goal is to help you achieve the smile of your dreams.

Growing up in a small town in Utah, Dr. Jake always knew he wanted to help people in a medical capacity. During a two-year mission in Honduras, he learned the importance of communication and compassion, which stayed with him as he pursued a career in dentistry, graduating from Roseman University College of Dental Medicine in 2015. He believes that small towns and the people associated with them are the heart of America.

But for Dr. Jake, dentistry is about more than just fixing cavities and root canals. It’s about restoring confidence, banishing embarrassment, and changing his patients’s daily lives. He loves to see people smile; it breaks his heart when someone hides their smile because of their teeth. That’s why he’s centered his practice on providing advanced, life-changing dental procedures such as dental implants, same-day crowns, and invisible aligner packages at an affordable cost.

As a family man, Dr. Jake embodies the virtues of commitment and involvement. He’s married to his beautiful wife, Danielle, and they have two talented daughters who keep them busy. On most weekends, he cheers for his daughters at their gymnastics and dance competitions. When not cheering, Dr. Jake and his family enjoy spending time together boating and traveling, making memories, and exploring new places in the great Colorado outdoors.

Dr. Beus’s love for his family and his community is what drives him to invest in himself and his practice. He’s constantly learning and improving his techniques to provide the best care possible for his patients. Through the advancements in dental implants and implant-secured bridges and dentures, his personal passion has become restoring the smiles of patients who’ve lost one or more teeth. With an in-house full-service dental lab, he can create perfect smiles more efficiently and at a lower cost than his competitors.

At Main Street Dental & Implants, we know that visiting the dentist can be intimidating. That’s why we’ve created a warm and welcoming atmosphere where patients feel comfortable and well cared for. And we believe that everyone deserves access to quality dental care, regardless of their financial situation. That’s why we accept most insurance plans, including Medicare and Medicaid.

If you’re looking for a dental practice that’s committed to changing lives, look no further than Main Street Dental & Implants. Dr. Jake and our team are here to help you achieve the smile of your dreams. Whether you need a routine cleaning or a full smile makeover, we’re here to guide you every step of the way.

So don’t wait any longer – schedule an appointment with us today and experience the Main Street Dental & Implants difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Main Street Dental and Implants offer?

At Main Street Dental and Implants, we offer a comprehensive range of dental services, including cosmetic dentistry, restorative treatments, oral surgery, and more. From routine checkups and cleanings to full-mouth restorations, we’re here to help you achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

What sets Main Street Dental and Implants apart from other dental practices?

At Main Street Dental and Implants, we’re committed to providing personalized care that’s tailored to your unique needs and goals. We believe in taking the time to get to know our patients and understand their concerns, so we can provide the best possible care. With state-of-the-art technology and a compassionate team, we’re dedicated to making your dental experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Does Main Street Dental and Implants accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most insurance plans, including Medicaid. We believe that everyone should have access to high-quality dental care, regardless of their insurance carrier. If you have any questions about your insurance coverage, our team is here to help.

What should I expect during my first visit to Main Street Dental and Implants?

During your first visit, you can expect a comprehensive oral assessment and a thorough discussion of your dental goals. We’ll take the time to get to know you and your dental history and perform a thorough examination to assess the health of your teeth, gums, and mouth. From there, we’ll work with you to create a personalized treatment plan that meets your needs and goals.

Does Main Street Dental and Implants offer sedation dentistry?

Yes, we offer oral sedation to help our patients feel more relaxed and comfortable during dental procedures. While we don’t offer intravenous or gas sedation, our oral sedation is a safe and effective option for patients who experience dental anxiety or fear.

Can Main Street Dental and Implants treat children?

Unfortunately, because Main Street Dental and Implants is focused on advanced cosmetic and restorative dentistry, we do not treat children at our practice. However, we are happy to refer you to a reputable pediatric dentist who can provide specialized care for your child’s dental needs.
