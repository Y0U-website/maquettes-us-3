Meet Dr. Cruz

Dr. Taimy Cruz

Diplomat of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Taimy Cruz Hondares was born and raised in Cuba. Early in her teen years, inspired by her

beloved family dentist, she discovered her passion for dentistry. She obtained her DDS in 2008 from the Higher Institute of Medical Science of Havana-Cuba. She then completed a 2-year General Practice Residency in Cuba. After practicing in Cuba for 6 years, she moved to United States.

Dr. Cruz chose to become accredited in the United States by attending and graduating

from Western Health Education Northside Center in Youngstown, OH in 2017, where she

She loves connecting with her patients and giving them the confidence to overcome their

dental fears. She is fluent in Spanish and is fully licensed in 2 countries. She looks forward to becoming an active member of the community.

Dr. Cruz went beyond residency to complete her board certification in pediatric dentistry and is a Diplomate of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She earned the Richard C. Pugh Award for scoring in the top 3 % nationally on the qualifying exam awarded by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. This optional and rigorous certification displays her commitment to excellent patient care and the field of pediatric dentistry.

She loves to spend time with her family including her daughter and son, Isabella and Robert Matthew. She enjoys positive reading, watching movies and swimming.
