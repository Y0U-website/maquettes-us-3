The Benefits of Phase 1 Orthodontics

Promote Proper Bite Development Phase 1 orthodontics plays a crucial role in addressing jaw issues or a narrow palate, which can hinder the development of a proper bite. Without early intervention, these problems can lead to difficulties such as speech impediments, premature tooth wear, or complications when permanent teeth erupt.

Support Proper Tooth Eruption Early treatment helps guide adult teeth into their correct positions, reducing or even eliminating misalignment. This can improve your child’s smile and oral health frequently allowing for a better result when treating with full braces down the road. In some cases, comprehensive treatment may be rendered unnecessary by early treatment.

Encourage Healthy Growth Early intervention can improve oral function, support clear speech, and make it easier to maintain healthy teeth and gums. In some cases it can play a role in improving airway function. While not every child needs Phase 1 treatment, it can be a proactive step to address specific concerns and lay the foundation for a healthy, confident smile.

Functional Appliances

For certain bite problems, we use functional appliances to help guide the way the upper and lower jaws fit together as your child grows. When used at the right stage of growth, these appliances can improve the bite and help create a healthy, lasting smile.

GOODBYE, GOOPY IMPRESSIONS!

Instead of using goopy impressions, we take a quick, precise digital scan to fit for appliances. This modern approach makes the process faster, cleaner, and more comfortable.

Airway conscious orthodontics

We take an airway-minded approach to orthodontic care, recognizing the important relationship between tooth alignment, jaw development, and breathing. When properly diagnosed—often in collaboration with an ENT—certain orthodontic treatments may help support healthy airway function and long-term wellness.

PERSONALIZED TREATMENT PLAN

Every child’s smile is unique, and their treatment plan should be, too. We take the time to evaluate each patient’s individual needs and create a customized plan to achieve the best results. There’s no one-size-fits-all approach here—just thoughtful, tailored care designed to fit your child’s particular needs.

PATIENT-FOCUSED CARE

At Nic Orthodontics, we take the time to support every child’s unique needs. Using the Tell-Show-Do approach, we explain, demonstrate, and guide them through each step to help them feel comfortable. For children who are especially nervous, we offer Comfort Visits so they can get familiar with our team and office before starting treatment.
