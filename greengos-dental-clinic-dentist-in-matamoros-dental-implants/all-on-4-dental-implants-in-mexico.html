What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a modern technique used to replace an entire arch of missing teeth with only four implants.

These implants are strategically placed in the jawbone to support a full set of fixed prosthetic teeth. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 restorations remain securely attached and function like natural teeth.

This treatment is often recommended for patients who:

Have lost most or all of their teeth

Currently wear dentures

Want a permanent tooth replacement solution
