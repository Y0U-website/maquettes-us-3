The practice

The high-resolution imaging capabilities provided by 3D Imaging enables Dr. Yudkin to make more accurate diagnoses and treatments. This breakthrough technology increases the efficiency of your visit allowing for the highest degree of success and with less post-operative symptoms.

Since Dr. Yudkin opened his office in 1999, he has worked diligently to build a practice that’s distinguished by excellence in endodontic treatment using the most advanced technology. His practice features sophisticated equipment for treating even the most complex root canal cases.

Dr. Yudkin is dedicated to providing patients with the best treatment possible. To assist in his diagnoses and contribute to a healthier, happier community, Advanced Endodontics of NJ is among the first in the local area to add a CS 9600 extraoral imaging system to its office.

The system revolutionizes patient treatment by capturing highly detailed 2D and 3D radiographs, or x-rays. It also uses 3D facial scanning and 3D model/impression scanning for complex diagnostic, surgical and endodontic procedures. Dr. Yudkin can obtain all the images he needs in the office without referring patients to outside imaging centers, which leads to fewer visits and faster treatment.…
