The practice

Special Health Resources provides affordable, comprehensive healthcare for your entire family. We have convenient locations in Tyler, Longview, Paris and Texarkana, TX. We accept Medicaid, Medicare and most private insurance plans. We also offer self-pay fee discounts based on income and household size.

Over the past two decades, SHR has grown into a comprehensive healthcare system in Northeast Texas, providing affordable care for the whole family. With clinics in Tyler, Longview, Paris and Texarkana, TX, as well as a fleet of mobile units to serve more rural areas, SHR provides healthcare to families in all 23 Northeast Texas counties. Learn More About SHR

Our services include adult and pediatric primary care, adult and pediatric dental care, women’s health, immunizations, STD and HIV/AIDS screening and treatment, as well as mental health services and substance abuse treatment.

SHR is proud to bring quality healthcare to all 23 counties of Northeast Texas with convenient locations in Tyler, Longview, Paris and Texarkana, TX, as well as mobile clinics that serve the rural areas of Northeast Texas. Whatever healthcare needs you have, SHR has friendly doctors and medical providers nearby to serve you.

SHR began as AIDS Inc., the community response to the AIDS epidemic late 80s. Initially, AIDS Inc. provided social support to HIV positive individuals and their families, but soon, the organization assumed a leading role in providing medical care, emergency assistance and case management and other supportive services to HIV positive individuals across 23 counties of Northeast Texas.
