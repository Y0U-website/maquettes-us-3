The practice

At Hill Country we aren’t constrained by borders, boundaries, or rules. We join you on your unique adventure to a smile that’s YOU, even if it means traveling uncharted territories and making new discoveries along the trail.

Our passion is to transform lives, one smile at a time. We are dedicated to ensuring that every patient, no matter what age, can achieve the best smile possible by creating simple, safe, and secure experiences so patients and their parents look forward to their visit.

Your journey is as unique as your smile which is why we aim to INSPIRE you along the paths to persevere and take control of your dental health with confidence. No journey is complete without exploring and discovering the unknown which is why we commit to sharing our expertise and INTELLIGENCE along the way to make your journey smart and cutting-edge.

Just like a road trip, braces have come a long way which is why we believe that it’s never too late to join the journey of achieving your best smile. Our practice boasts of INNOVATIVE ways to overcome your dental detours and delays so that it is easy for you to let your journey begin with us!

We understand that life can be busy and stressful so we make visiting our practice as easy and stress-free as possible.

Advanced technology, a highly trained staff, and educating our patients is our map to helping our patients successfully arrive at an amazing smile.

We are committed to helping children follow the best journey! When kids are inspired to take ownership of their oral care, they form great habits. By providing a simple path, we challenge our patients to value the benefits of regular, preventative oral care. This begins the moment patients and their parents enter one of our offices.
