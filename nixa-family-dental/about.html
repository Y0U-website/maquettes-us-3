Meet Your Doctors

Dr. Jacob McLauchlin, DDS was born and raised in Nixa where he graduated from Nixa High School. He earned his bachelor’s degree at Drury University, then went on to graduate from University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry with endodontic (root canal) honors. Dr. McLauchlin served as the founding vice president of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD) at UMKC. He is proud to be rooted in the community he was raised in and is excited about serving the great people of the Ozarks.

Dr. McLauchlin is married to his high school sweetheart Janae. They live in Nixa and have four beautiful children, Mac, Miller, Boone, and Bayler. They are actively involved and serve at James River Church. They are family oriented and are always looking forward to fun adventures whether in their backyard or on the road. Dr. McLauchlin’s hobbies include just about anything outdoors, including sports and especially golf! Dr. McLauchlin and Janae are honored to apply their family focused mindset to Nixa Family Dental and provide you and your family with full service, comprehensive dental care!
