The practice

We are Drs. Rob and Alyssa Kievit, a husband and wife duo providing personalized dental care. It is important to us that each of our patients feel heard, understood, and are fully engaged in their dental care. Our patients are active participants in their treatment decisions so that collectively we can achieve your goals. No smile is the same and therefore no treatment plan is the same.

We want you to love your smile. We are highly trained in tooth whitening, porcelain veneers, implant restorations and more to create the smile you have always wanted.

We treat patients as young as one year old to over 100 years old. We value the relationships we build with our patients and love that we get to know you and your entire family.

Our team will be happy to check any insurance benefits for you before your visit. We also offer options for financing your care.

For our patients without traditional dental insurance, our membership plan is a cost effective way to pay for your annual exams, cleanings, treatment, and more.

We can’t wait to meet you and help you achieve a smile you love!

Les soins
