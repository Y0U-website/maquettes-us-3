Life with Dentures

How long does it take to get used to new dentures? It typically takes 2 to 4 weeks for your tongue and cheek muscles to learn how to keep the denture in place. We schedule follow-up adjustments to ensure there are no &#x27;sore spots&#x27; during this transition.

Will I be able to eat normally? Yes, but it requires a learning curve. We recommend starting with softer foods and cutting items into smaller pieces. Implant-supported dentures offer the closest experience to natural chewing.

How often should I replace my dentures? On average, dentures should be replaced or &#x27;relined&#x27; every 5 to 7 years. Over time, your jawbone changes shape, which can cause the denture to lose its fit even if the teeth are still in good condition.

Do I still need dental checkups if I have no natural teeth? Absolutely. We perform oral cancer screenings, check the health of your gum tissues, and evaluate the fit of your denture to prevent bone loss and irritation.
