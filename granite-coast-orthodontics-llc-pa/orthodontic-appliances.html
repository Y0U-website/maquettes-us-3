Orthodontic Appliances

Every day, our laboratory technician, Sierra, works closely with Dr. Doug to fabricate custom retainers, aligners, and orthodontic appliances that meet each patient’s individual needs. Their collaboration combines clinical expertise with skilled craftsmanship to create appliances that fit comfortably, function effectively, and support successful treatment outcomes.

  • Hawley retainers and clear removable retainers to maintain treatment results.
  • Palatal expanders used during Phase One treatment, which can be made in our office or custom 3D printed by our orthodontic colleagues in Boston.
  • Night guards to protect teeth from grinding and clenching.
  • TMJ splints designed to help reduce jaw discomfort and improve bite function.
  • Distal Jet appliances that help create space by moving upper molars efficiently.
  • Mesial Slider appliances for specific tooth movement and bite correction.
  • Bite plates that help correct deep overbites and improve tooth positioning.
  • Forsus™ appliances that address skeletal discrepancies and overbites while minimizing the need for patient-dependent rubber bands.
  • Additional fixed appliances that guide tooth movement with less day-to-day maintenance than removable elastics.

Benefits For Kids And Adults

Custom appliances help prevent unwanted tooth movement, preserve space for developing permanent teeth, improve bite development, and protect orthodontic treatment results. For children, early intervention may reduce the need for more extensive treatment later. For teens and adults, retainers and other appliances help maintain healthy, stable smiles. Throughout appliance treatment, our goal is to provide solutions that are comfortable, durable, and tailored to your individual needs.

  • Preserving space so permanent teeth erupt properly.
  • Supporting healthy jaw and bite development.
  • Reducing the likelihood of extractions or prolonged treatment.
  • Helping correct bite discrepancies more efficiently.
  • Protecting orthodontic results for years to come.
