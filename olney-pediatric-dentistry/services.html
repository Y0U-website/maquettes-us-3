Our Services

At Olney Pediatric Dentistry, your child’s comfort and happiness are at the heart of everything we do. Our warm, welcoming, and fun environment helps every child feel safe, supported, and right at home. Whether it’s a routine cleaning, cavity treatment, or a second opinion, we personalize each visit to meet your child’s unique needs, making dental care a positive and stress-free experience.

We’re also big believers in prevention and education. From brushing and flossing tips to guidance on healthy nutrition, we empower families with the tools to support lifelong oral health.

Comprehensive Dental Services

Infant Oral Health

Your baby’s first dental visit is a key milestone in their oral health journey. We create a gentle, welcoming environment to introduce your little one to the dentist. During this visit, we’ll assess their oral development, provide teething tips, and guide you on proper gum and tooth care for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Preventive Dentistry

We prioritize excellent preventive care to keep your child’s smile healthy and bright. Our services include thorough exams, gentle cleanings, and digital X-rays for early detection of dental issues. We follow the ADA’s recommendation for biannual cleanings and exams to maintain optimal oral health.

Minimally Invasive Dentistry

Our approach to dental care focuses on preventing and treating tooth decay while preserving as much of the natural tooth structure as possible. Using minimally invasive techniques like Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF), the Hall Technique, and the SMART Technique, we provide effective, evidence-based solutions with little to no discomfort—minimizing the need for traditional drilling or anesthesia whenever possible. This gentle approach is especially ideal for young children, anxious patients, and those with special healthcare needs.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative care at our practice focuses on restoring both the function and aesthetics of your child’s smile. Based on your child’s individual needs, we will create a customized treatment plan to treat their dental issues.

Emergency Dentistry

Whether it’s pain, infection, a broken tooth, or sudden injury, we’re here to offer immediate attention and relieve your child’s discomfort. We’ll assess the situation, offer a solution tailored to your child’s needs, and take steps to prevent further complications.

Special Needs Dentistry

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extractions are sometimes necessary to preserve your child’s oral health. Whether due to severe decay, infection, or crowding, we ensure minimal discomfort and a smooth recovery. Our team is dedicated to keeping your child comfortable throughout the process and providing compassionate, supportive care.

Sedation Dentistry

Space Maintenance

When a child loses a baby tooth too early, a space maintainer appliance helps prevent future dental issues by ensuring enough space for adult teeth to grow in properly. We offer effective space-saving solutions that preserve alignment and promote healthy dental development.
