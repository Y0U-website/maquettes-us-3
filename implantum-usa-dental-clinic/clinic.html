Advantages

The clinic has a permanent highly qualified staff. All doctors have honed their skills and ability to find an individual approach to each patient over the years of practice. Doctors have taken master classes, advanced training courses (including those in leading foreign clinics), received dozens of certificates and certificates of merit, and have hundreds and thousands of happy patients.

Implantum specializes not only in general dentistry: implantologist, orthodontist, maxillofacial surgeon, and pediatric dentist are also ready to help our patients.

In addition to wide range of dental services, the clinic also has a cosmetology department. Experienced cosmetologists will masterfully provide any cosmetic procedure, from facial cleansing and peeling to injectable rejuvenation.

The clinic will ensure not only healthy, snow-white smiles, but also perfectly well-groomed appearance.
