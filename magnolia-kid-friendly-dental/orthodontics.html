FAQ about Kids Orthodontics

Straight teeth greatly improve confidence while making it easier to brush and floss for better hygiene and overall health. A straight smile is one of the greatest gifts you can give your child because it’s the gift that keeps on giving!

  • What is orthodontics for kids? Orthodontics for kids focuses on the smiles of children and their unique needs. The specialty focuses on the monitoring, prevention, and correction of facial abnormalities related to the teeth and jaw. Many problems are much easier, faster, and less expensive to fix while your child is young and the jaw is still developing. Common orthodontic services for kids include: Space maintainers
  • Palate expanders
  • Braces
  • Aligners
  • Habit correction
  • Are braces important for kids? Yes, braces are important for kids who require orthodontic intervention, but they’re not the only option! Today’s modern technology and techniques give kids and parents more options for treatment that fit their lifestyle and budget. Our Mitchellville, MD dental office also offers clear aligners in addition to metal braces. Braces and aligners can correct a variety of issues: Protruding teeth
  • Crossbite, underbite, overbite
  • Spacing or crowding
  • Is Invisalign better than braces? Invisalign works as well as braces for many types of malocclusion in children. We’ll recommend the best teeth straightening system after performing an evaluation and discussing your needs. Magnolia Kid Friendly Dental offers the ClearCorrect system, a popular alternative to Invisalign . ClearCorrect uses a series of clear plastic trays to straighten teeth and set the foundation for a comfortable, beautiful smile.
