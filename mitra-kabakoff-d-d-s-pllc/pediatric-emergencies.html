When To CALL for emergency care

We encourage you to call our office at the first sign that something is wrong so that we can help your child feel happy and healthy again. From toothaches to chipped teeth, we are here to provide emergency care for your little one.

If a baby tooth is knocked out (avulsed), do not try to place it back in the mouth. Replanting a primary tooth can lead to the risk of damaging the developing permanent tooth.

Unlike a baby tooth that is knocked out, adult teeth should be placed back into the socket as soon as possible. Touch only the crown of the tooth (not the root!) to minimize injury to the root. If the tooth is dirty, gently rinse the tooth with water only. Do not scrub or dry the tooth, and keep the tooth hydrated at all times. If it can&#x27;t be replaced in the socket, keep the tooth in milk, in your mouth next to your cheek, or in an emergency tooth preservation kit (such as Save-a-Tooth).

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Board certification shows a commitment to providing excellent pediatric care. Not only does this voluntary process require a rigorous exam, but board-certified pediatric dentists undergo specialized training to become true experts in pediatric medicine and dental care.
