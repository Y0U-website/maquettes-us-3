Dr. Marianna Zadov, DDS

An experienced dentist whose work spans general, cosmetic, and restorative care, with an emphasis on clear communication and personalized treatment planning.

Dentistry built around understanding, not urgency

Dr. Zadov is the Clinical Director and lead dentist at Happy Smiles of Fort Myers, where she provides cosmetic, restorative, and preventive dental care.

Her practice is shaped by a simple belief: patients make confident decisions when they can see what is happening in their own mouth, understand the options in plain language, and choose without pressure. Appointments are paced to leave room for questions and for reviewing images together.

That philosophy carries through the whole practice. Every recommendation is tied to a specific goal for the patient, whether that is comfort, function, appearance, or simply keeping a healthy smile healthy.

You can also read more about her professional work at MariannaZadov.com .

Doctor of Dental Surgery, University of Toronto

Dr. Zadov earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Toronto. Since then, her professional experience has spanned general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry.

Working across those areas has shaped how she plans treatment: a cosmetic result has to hold up functionally, and a restorative result has to look natural. Both are considered together rather than in isolation.

Clear communication and a long-term plan

Care begins with listening: what you want from your smile, what has shaped your past dental experiences, and what would make treatment feel manageable. Findings are explained with your own photos and scans, in plain language.

From there, treatment planning is personalized. Options are presented with their trade-offs, including the option to monitor rather than treat when that is reasonable, and the least invasive predictable approach is favored.

The goal is long-term care rather than one-time fixes, so decisions are made with an eye on how a smile will function and look years from now.

General, Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

The range of care Dr. Zadov provides

Three connected areas of dentistry, planned together so results are both healthy and natural-looking.

General dentistry

Routine examinations, cleanings, and everyday restorative work that keep teeth and gums healthy over the long term.

Cosmetic dentistry

Treatment planning for the appearance of a smile, including whitening, veneers, and comprehensive smile makeovers.

Restorative dentistry

Rebuilding function and structure with crowns, implant restorations, and root canal therapy when a tooth needs saving.
