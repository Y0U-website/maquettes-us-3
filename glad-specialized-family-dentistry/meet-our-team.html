29 Hudson Rd. Sudbury, MA 01776 Meet Our Team Dr. Liliana Gomez-Infante DMD, CAGS in Pediatric Dentistry Dr. Gomez-Infante received her dental degree from The Central University of Venezuela. In 1998, she came to the United States to attend Boston University: Goldman School of Dental Medicine, where she earned her D.M.D, as well as a Certificate of Advanced Graduate Study (CAGS) in Pediatric Dentistry. In 2000, Dr. Gomez-Infante was awarded with The Clinical Research Award.

Dr. Gomez-Infante has written articles published in the Massachusetts Dental Society Journal, and the Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She has been a faculty member at Franciscan Hospital for Children in Brighton, and has been in private practice for more than twenty years. Dr. Gomez is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), The Massachusetts Dental Society, and the Hispanic Dental Association. In her spare time, Dr. Gomez enjoys spending time with her husband and twin boys. She is also a huge Red Sox Fan!

Dr. Gustavo J. Infante

DMD, CAGS in Prosthodontics

Dr. Gustavo Infante is an experienced Prosthodontist who has been practicing dentistry since 1998. He received his dental degree from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine, as well as, a Certificate of Advanced Graduate Study (C.A.G.S.) in Prosthetic Dentistry. In 2023 he completed Dental Implantology training at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in NJ. In 2001 he was awarded "The David J. Barraban Award for Clinical Excellence."

Dr. Infante has been a Clinical Instructor in the Operative Dentistry Program, the A.E.G.D. Program and the General Dentistry Department at Boston University, where he also held a Faculty Practice appointment for more than four years.

Dr. Infante combines an exceptional education, extensive clinical experience, and long-standing personal dedication to improving people’s dental health allowing him to bring outstanding functional health and beauty to his patient’s smiles. The goal of his practice is to treat each person as an individual and to provide the highest level of care possible to his patients.

Among other procedures, he emphasizes on restorative dentistry, implant dentistry, full mouth reconstructions, all porcelain crowns, onlays, and porcelain veneers, and all aspects of prosthetic dentistry and complex restorative rehabilitations.

Dr. Infante is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. Dr. Infante and his wife Liliana, enjoy spending free time outdoors and keeping up with their twin boys.

Dr. Saam Bozorg

DMD, Orthodontist

Dr. Saam Bozorg (Dr. B) earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and completed his Orthodontic Certificate at the University of Connecticut, where he also pursued advanced training through an Invisalign Aligner Intensive Fellowship. He believes orthodontic care is about much more than straightening teeth; it’s about building trust, nurturing confidence, and creating healthy habits that last a lifetime.

He has extensive experience caring for children and teens in pediatric-focused settings and makes it a priority to explain treatment in a reassuring, age-appropriate way - so young patients feel comfortable, confident, and excited about their progress.

In addition to clinical care, Dr. B is the co-founder of Lura Health, an innovative healthcare company developing technology that integrates health monitoring into orthodontic appliances. His work has been supported by multiple national research grants and patents but at heart, he remains most fulfilled by one-on-one patient care and the relationships built along the way.

Outside the office, Dr. B enjoys skiing, surfing, tennis, and basketball. He is also learning to play flamenco guitar!

Dr. B considers it a privilege to care for your child. He looks forward to earning your trust, answering your questions, and partnering with you to create a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Natalia Acosta-Farhadi

DDS, MS, Periodontist

Dr. Acosta-Farhadi earned her Bachelor of Science in Microbiology and Cell Science and Master of Science in Health Education and Behavior from the University of Florida, where she also conducted research in oral biology and pediatric dentistry. She earned her dental degree from the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry, where she served as President of her class.

She completed her specialty training in Periodontology at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, earning a Master of Science and Certificate in Periodontology. Her training included advanced surgical and implant procedures, including dental autotransplantation. She received the Derek T. Turner Research Award for her research involving virtual reality haptic-based systems and was actively involved in teaching dental students at UNC.

She is a member of the American Academy of Periodontology, American Dental Association, Massachusetts Dental Society, Worcester District Dental Society, and International Team for Implantology.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Acosta-Farhadi loves spending time with her husband and their dog and cat. They enjoy traveling, trying new restaurants, and exploring new places. She also enjoys baking, reading, and solving puzzles. If you’ve recently traveled somewhere amazing, discovered a great restaurant, or read a book you loved, she’s always happy to hear your recommendations!
