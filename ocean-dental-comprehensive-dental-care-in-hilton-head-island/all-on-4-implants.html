Your Concerns, Our Focus

All-on-4 implants replace missing or failing teeth with a full, fixed set of natural-looking restorations anchored by just four implants. This treatment restores full function, stability, and confidence.

All-on-4 implants restore more than just the look of your smile, but also eliminate discomfort, slipping, or dietary limits often caused by dentures or damaged teeth. It’s a long-lasting solution that helps you eat, speak, and smile freely again.

How it Works

Strategic Implant Placement

Four dental implants are placed at precise angles in the jawbone to maximize support and stability for a full-arch prosthesis.

Prosthetic Attachment

A custom-designed dental bridge or arch is securely fixed to the implants, providing immediate function, a natural appearance, and long-lasting durability.

How it Works

Once completed, patients typically leave the office with a fully functional and natural-looking smile. With proper care and routine dental visits, all-on-4 implant restorations can last for many years, providing a long-term solution for missing teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Implants

Is the procedure painful?

Local anesthesia and sedation options make the procedure comfortable. Most patients report mild soreness during healing.

How long does the All-In-4 treatment take?

Finalized treatment may take a few months to complete. Implants require time to integrate allowing them to support a prosthesis. The patient may wear a temporary denture during the waiting period so they never are without teeth. The final permanent denture requires a few appointments to ensure an accurate fit and attractive smile.

Are All-In-4 implants removable?

No. The prosthetic is fixed to the implants for stability, unlike traditional dentures.

How long do All-in-4 implants last?

With proper care, implants can last decades. Routine dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential.

Who is a good candidate for All-In-4 implants?

Ideal for patients missing most or all teeth in an arch, or those with failing teeth who want a fixed, long-term solution. Bone health will be assessed during a comprehensive evaluation to ensure proper implant support.
