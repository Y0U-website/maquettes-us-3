Meet Dr. Ziyad Effendi

A Favorite Pediatric Dentist for Darien Families

Dr. Ziyad Effendi became a pediatric dentist because he believes every child deserves a dental visit filled with smiles rather than stress. His passion for healthcare and working with children made pediatric dentistry a natural fit. After earning his dental degree, Dr. Ziyad completed advanced training in pediatric dentistry. His experience includes routine dental care, helping nervous children feel comfortable, and supporting patients with special healthcare needs. His goal is to create positive visits and healthy smiles that can last a lifetime.
