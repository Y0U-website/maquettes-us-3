Pediatric Digital X-Rays

We use digital x-rays to get a clear look at your child’s dental health—quickly, safely, and only when necessary. Digital x-rays use very low radiation and are considered safe for children. Typically, x-rays are recommended once a year, though children at higher risk for oral disease may need more frequent scans.

Pediatric Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child&#x27;s mouth healthy and happy!

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) is a safe, effective, and minimally invasive treatment (no drills or injections) that helps prevent small cavities from worsening. When applied directly to the affected teeth, SDF strengthens enamel, guards against further decay, and is particularly beneficial for young patients or hard-to-treat teeth, supporting oral health while avoiding more invasive procedures.

Special Needs Dentistry

Special needs dentistry focuses on caring for children who may need extra support during dental visits due to developmental, physical, behavioral, sensory, or medical conditions. Every child deserves comfortable, compassionate dental care — and sometimes that means a personalized approach.

How We Care in a Pediatric Dental Office

Pediatric dentists receive advanced training in child development and behavior guidance, allowing us to adapt each visit to your child’s unique needs. In our office, that means:

- Personalized appointments tailored to your child’s pace and comfort level

- Sensory-friendly options like dimmed lights, reduced noise, and clear, simple explanations

- Positive behavior guidance to build trust and confidence

- Safe sedation options, when appropriate, to ensure treatment is completed comfortably

- We work closely with parents and, when needed, your child’s medical team to create a supportive plan that prioritizes safety and emotional well-being.

- Our goal is simple: help every child feel safe, respected, and proud of their smile.

Pediatric Sedation

Our dental practice offers pediatric sedation to help children feel calm, comfortable, and at ease during their dental visits. Sedation can be especially helpful for kids who experience anxiety, have trouble sitting still, or need more involved treatment. By tailoring sedation options to each child’s age, comfort level, and medical history, we’re able to provide gentle, stress-free care while ensuring your child’s safety every step of the way.
