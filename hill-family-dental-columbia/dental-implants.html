What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Columbia have become a popular and advanced solution for replacing missing teeth. Essentially, they involve a titanium post that is implanted into your jawbone, serving as a replacement root for your lost tooth. This post is a stable foundation for a custom-made crown that looks and feels just like your natural teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges , which can shift or become uncomfortable, dental implants are fixed in place and can be cared for just like your regular teeth.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

One of the primary benefits of dental implants is their durability. With proper care, they can last many years, providing a long-term solution to tooth loss. They also help preserve jawbone health. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone can begin to deteriorate over time; dental implants stimulate the bone, helping to maintain its strength and structure.

Another significant advantage is the improvement they bring to your quality of life. Dental implants in Columbia allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. You won’t have to worry about slippage or discomfort, which can sometimes occur with dentures or other alternatives. Plus, when you choose dental implants at Hill Family Dental, you opt for a solution that looks and feels natural.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants in Columbia?

Most adults in good general health and have sufficient bone density in their jaw are suitable candidates for dental implants. If you’ve lost teeth due to injury, decay, or gum disease, it’s worth considering this option. During your consultation at Hill Family Dental in Columbia, TN , our knowledgeable team will assess your oral health, review your medical history, and determine if dental implants are the right choice.

The Dental Implant Process The journey to getting dental implants involves several steps, but don’t worry; our team at Hill Family Dental is with you every step. Initial Consultation : This is where we get to know you and your dental needs. We’ll take X-rays and create a treatment plan tailored just for you.

Implant Placement : Once we determine you’re a good candidate, the titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This procedure is typically done under local anesthesia, ensuring your comfort.

Healing Period : After the post is in place, a healing period of a few months is necessary. During this time, the implant will fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration, making it a solid foundation for your new tooth.

Crown Placement : Once healing is complete, we’ll attach a custom-made crown to the implant. This crown matches your natural teeth in shape and color, ensuring a seamless look.

Follow-Up Care : Our commitment to your dental health doesn’t end here. We’ll schedule follow-up appointments at Hill Family Dental to monitor your implant and overall oral health.
