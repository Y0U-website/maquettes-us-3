Early Orthodontic Care for Kids…

If you notice any of these patterns, it is a good reason to bring them up at your child’s next checkup. Our team is here to help you sort through what matters and what can wait.

  • Bite concerns : Upper teeth that sit too far in front of the lower teeth, or lower teeth that close in front of the upper teeth
  • Mouth breathing : Chronic open-mouth breathing, especially during sleep, can affect how the jaw develops over time
  • Thumb sucking or pacifier use past age 2 : These habits, when continued beyond toddlerhood, can shift how teeth grow in and how the palate forms
  • Early or late tooth loss : Losing baby teeth much earlier or later than expected can affect the spacing available for permanent teeth

What Happens During an Early Orthodontic Screening?

An early orthodontic screening is built into the care we provide during your child’s regular dental exams and cleanings at Guadalupe Kids Dental. Dr. Bennion will take a look at how your child’s teeth are erupting, how the jaw is developing, and whether any habits or patterns are worth watching. If X-rays are needed for a clearer picture, we will discuss that with you beforehand.

What Are the Possible Outcomes?

After the evaluation, there are generally three directions things can go. No action may be needed at all, and your child is simply monitored at future visits. Treatment may be something to plan for later as your child grows. Or, if early intervention would genuinely benefit your child, we can talk through options and timing that make sense for your family.

Why Choose a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist for Early Orthodontic Care?

Not every provider who offers orthodontic screenings has specialized training in child development. Pediatric dentists complete additional years of residency training beyond dental school, specifically focused on children’s oral health from infancy through adolescence. Dr. Bennion completed his pediatric dental residency at the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia and the University of Pennsylvania and holds board certification in pediatric dentistry.
