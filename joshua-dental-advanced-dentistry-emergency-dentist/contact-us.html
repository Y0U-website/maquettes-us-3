Do you accept new patients?

Yes — new patients are always welcome at Joshua Dental. Whether you are scheduling a routine cleaning or coming in to discuss a more complex concern, we will take care of you from your very first visit. You can get a head start by reviewing our Patient Information page before you arrive.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us at (626) 243-7999 right away. We offer same-day emergency appointments and will do everything we can to see you as quickly as possible. Visit our Emergency Dentistry page to learn more about the situations we treat and what to do before you reach the office.

Can I book an appointment online?

Yes. Our online scheduler is available any time at flexbook.me/joshuadent/1 — simply choose a date and time that works for you. If you would rather speak with someone, call (626) 243-7999 and our team will be glad to help.
