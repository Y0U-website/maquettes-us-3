FAQ

We’re all curious! Here’s the basics:

How long does Invisalign take? It is our goal to design your case as efficiently as possible. With the average timeline of alignment being 9-12 months, your journey may vary depending on your needs and goals.

Is Invisalign as effective as metal braces? Invisalign trays are effective in treating all the same issues that traditional braces address. They are a more convenient way to achieve the same result, especially for adults with busy lives or patients that are wanting to uphold a certain look.

How do I know if it’s working? The Invisalign system comes with sophisticated software to help us plan and track your treatment precisely. You’ll check in with us every 6-8 weeks and we will be able to show you and discuss your progress.

How much does Invisalign cost? No two smiles are alike, so the cost of your personal treatment will depend on your exact needs. On average, Invisalign can cost from $7,500 or more.
