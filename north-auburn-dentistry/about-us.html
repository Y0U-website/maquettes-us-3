About Us

About North Auburn Dentistry

At North Auburn Dentistry , we offer the comforts and conveniences of home so you always feel welcome and relaxed. From complimentary refreshments to pillows and blankets, we go out of our way to make you feel comfortable.

We also provide our patients with flexible financing options for dental care . We don’t want you to stress over dental care expenses . If you have dental insurance, we will contact your provider and work out payment with them before you even come in for your dental appointment. We also offer in-house payment plans, and we accept CareCredit ℠ . We remove the obstacles standing between you and great dental care.

Clean and Safe Dental Practice

Kid-Friendly Dental Office

Positive dental experiences during childhood are the foundation for a lifetime of good oral habits and healthy smiles. Our team at North Auburn Dentistry understands that children can feel anxious about dental visits , and that our younger patients have different needs for treatment and communication than our older patients. Dr. Dever and our team always take the time to talk children through their dental procedures so they feel at ease and relaxed in our care. Our mission is to make your child’s dental visits easy, educational, and fun!

Meet Dr. Brandon Dever, DDS

Dr. Brandon Dever and his wife grew up in the Sacramento, California area. In middle school, Dr. Dever discovered two interests — human biology and art. By the 8th grade, he knew he wanted to be a dentist .

Dr. Dever received his bachelor’s degree in human biology from Brigham Young University and went on to complete his dental training at Case Western School of Dental Medicine in Cleveland, OH in 2004.

Dr. Dever ’s passion for making his patients comfortable has led him to incorporate many relaxing and patient-friendly techniques and technologies into his practice. These techniques allow Dr. Dever to minimize patient anxiety. His gentle and reassuring manner help all of our patients feel at ease and experience a pleasant dental visit . He also believes the best doctor-patient relationships are built on trust and respect. During your dental consultation, Dr. Dever will get to know you on a personal level and thoroughly discuss your dental needs and goals before designing your treatment plan.

When he’s not helping patients create healthy and beautiful smiles at North Auburn Dentistry, Dr. Dever enjoys spending time with his wife and children. They like going camping, exploring and learning about new things, and serving in their church.
