FAQ About CEREC

  • What is CEREC? CEREC is an innovative system that creates dental crowns directly in the dentist’s office using 3D imaging technology and an in-office milling machine. The crowns are made of the highest quality porcelain or zirconia. Unlike traditional crowns, CEREC same-day crowns are made on-site and can be fitted that same day.
  • Is CEREC worth the money? CEREC is worth the money if you’re looking for a high-quality tooth restoration or crown replacement in one day. The convenience of just one dentist visit, plus the ability to instantly restore your mouth’s function, is a considerable value that traditional crowns made at exterior labs can’t provide.
  • How long do CEREC crowns last? CEREC crowns may last 10-15 years or even longer, the same as a laboratory made crown, depending on the crown’s location, its use, and dental care. Ways to help a crown last longer include: Avoid grinding teeth and wear a nightguard if needed
  • Visit the dentist for regular checkups and cleanings
  • Maintain quality dental hygiene at home
