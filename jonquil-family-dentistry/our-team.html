Meet the Jonquil Crew!

Daniel Seay, DMD, PhD

A distinguished graduate of the Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA, Dr. Seay brings a high standard of clinical expertise to every appointment.

Beyond his professional life, Dr. Seay is a proud husband and a father of two amazing children. When he isn’t in the office helping patients achieve their best smiles, he can often be found exploring the local culinary scene, catching up with friends, or mowing our very own lawn!

Joanne - RDA

With over 30 years of experience as a Registered Dental Assistant, Joanne brings a wealth of clinical expertise and a gentle touch to the Jonquil Family Dentistry team. Originally from Alabama, she has dedicated her career to providing high-quality patient care and ensuring every individual feels at ease during their visit. Her extensive background in restorative and preventive dentistry makes her an invaluable asset to our clinical operations.

Outside of the office, she leads a vibrant and active life. She cherishes every moment spent with her grandson and can often be found at local music festivals or exploring Nashville’s ever-evolving food scene. An avid lover of photography, she enjoys capturing the beauty of the world around her. Her warm personality and decades of dedication to her craft help foster the welcoming environment our patients have come to love.

Samantha - Front Office Coordinator Samantha, who goes by Sam, has lived in South Nashville for majority of her life and is proud to call the community her home.

In her role, she serves as a scheduling coordinator, manages insurance verification, and oversees the office’s social media accounts. Outside of work, she enjoys exploring different types of food, camping outdoors, and spending time with her friends, her fiance, and their cats, KoRnbread & Chili. She is a passionate fan of horror movies and Led Zeppelin, and she considers David Bowie and Obi-Wan Kenobi to be her personal heroes.

It is a privilege for her to work for Dr. Seay and she is very happy to be part of the team in the heart of her hometown!

Christine - RDH

Originally from South Carolina, Christine, is a dedicated dental hygiene professional with over a decade of experience in patient care and oral health education. She earned her degree in Dental Hygiene from Trident Technical College in Charleston 2012.

In 2014, Christine relocated to Nashville, where she continued to grow both personally and professionally. Known for her approachable demeanor and commitment to patient comfort, she takes pride in helping individuals achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

Outside of the office, Christine enjoys staying active through soccer and values spending time with her friends. She has a passion for exploring Nashville’s vibrant food scene and is always eager to try restaurants and experiences!

Olivia - RDH

Olivia is a dedicated dental hygienist at Jonquil Family Dentistry who joined the practice in mid-October, 2025. She is deeply committed to patient education, helping individuals understand the nuances of their oral health while building meaningful, long-term connections. Olivia takes great pride in creating a comfortable, positive experience for every patient, ensuring they leave the chair feeling both well-cared for and confident in their smile.

Outside of the office, Olivia enjoys an active lifestyle and values her personal time. She loves playing with her dog and frequently catches the latest releases at the movies with her friends. Her warm personality and enthusiasm for life make her a wonderful addition to both the clinical team and the Nashville community.
