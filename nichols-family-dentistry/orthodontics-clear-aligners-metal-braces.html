Caring for Your Smile During Treatment

Daily habits can make a significant difference.

If aligners are not worn as recommended, teeth may not move as planned.

  • Brush after meals using a soft toothbrush
  • Floss daily (floss threaders help with braces)
  • Rinse with water after snacks
  • Avoid sticky or very hard foods if wearing braces
  • Wear aligners consistently

FAQs About Orthodontics In Grand Rapids

Treatment time varies depending on your specific alignment and bite concerns. Braces often take between 12 and 24 months, though minor spacing issues may take less time. While clear aligners may take less time. During your consultation, we provide a personalized estimate for you. Book yours today by giving our team a call!

Clear aligners are highly effective for many mild to moderate alignment issues when worn as directed each day. Braces may provide more precise control for complex bite or rotation concerns. We evaluate your smile carefully and recommend the option most appropriate for your goals and dental needs.

Braces are not designed to cause sharp pain during placement, but mild pressure or soreness can occur after adjustments. This feeling usually lasts a few days and gradually improves. Soft foods and common pain relievers can help during this time. Most patients adjust quickly as their mouth becomes accustomed to treatment. If you’re experiencing severe discomfort, call us ASAP!

Orthodontics in Grand Rapids can be appropriate for patients of all ages. As long as teeth and gums are healthy, adults can successfully straighten their smiles. Many adults appreciate clear aligners for their discreet appearance, while others choose braces for more complex corrections or specific bite adjustments.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children receive an orthodontic evaluation by age seven. Early visits allow providers to monitor growth and identify developing concerns. Not every child needs immediate treatment, but early assessment helps determine ideal timing. Schedule your child’s visit via our Contact page today.
