Pediatric Silver Diamine Fluoride

When a cavity appears in your child&#x27;s tooth, Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) might be one option Dr. Greg discusses with you. This treatment is quick and doesn&#x27;t require any drilling or numbing shots - it&#x27;s simply painted onto the affected tooth to help stop decay from progressing. While SDF can be effective for certain situations, it&#x27;s not right for every child or every cavity. Dr. Greg will evaluate whether this conservative approach makes sense for your child&#x27;s specific needs and explain all available options so you can make the best decision together.

Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by Dr. Greg. Typically, x-rays are recommended periodically, though children with a higher risk of oral disease may need them more frequently.

Following new guidelines released in 2023, we no longer use lead aprons or shields during x-ray exams. More than 50 years of research has demonstrated the risk of harm is very small or even zero, making these protective measures unnecessary. Dr. Greg is always happy to discuss any questions or concerns you may have about your child&#x27;s x-ray needs.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear sealant on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Nutritional Counseling

We believe in a "whole health" approach, which includes making smart food choices that support your child&#x27;s dental and overall health. It&#x27;s not always easy to change eating habits, but it&#x27;s definitely worth it. Following our dietary recommendations - including avoiding frequent snacking and grazing throughout the day - benefits not only your child&#x27;s smile but their overall wellbeing. Let us help guide you in creating tooth-friendly eating patterns that the whole family can embrace!

Oral Habits

Non-nutritional sucking habits like thumb sucking, pacifier use, or lip sucking are completely normal for young children. However, if these habits continue beyond certain ages, they can sometimes affect your child&#x27;s tooth alignment or jaw development. Dr. Greg understands that breaking these habits can be challenging for both children and parents, which is why he provides gentle, supportive guidance tailored to your child&#x27;s age and individual needs. With patience and the right approach, we can help your family navigate these transitions in a way that feels positive and manageable.

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to help prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride varnish may be applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called "remineralization," strengthening teeth with essential minerals. Dr. Greg evaluates each child individually to determine whether fluoride treatment would be beneficial for their specific needs, as not every child requires this additional preventive measure.

Pediatric Emergencies

Kids will be kids, and sometimes that means unexpected bumps, falls, or sudden tooth pain! When dental emergencies happen, Dr. Greg and our team are here to help get your child back to feeling like themselves again. Whether it&#x27;s a toothache that&#x27;s keeping them up at night, a chipped tooth from playground adventures, or any other urgent dental concern, we&#x27;re ready to jump into action.

Not sure if what&#x27;s happening with your child&#x27;s tooth counts as an emergency? Give us a call anyway - we&#x27;d rather hear from you and provide peace of mind than have you worry at home. Our team can quickly assess the situation, give you helpful guidance, and get your child scheduled promptly if needed. Our goal is simple: help your little one get back to playing, laughing, and being a kid as quickly as possible.
