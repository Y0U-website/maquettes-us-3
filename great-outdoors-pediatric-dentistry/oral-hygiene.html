Understanding Plaque and Cavity Prevention

Plaque is a sticky film made up of bacteria, food particles, and natural debris that forms on teeth throughout the day. If not removed, plaque produces acids that weaken enamel and lead to cavities. Over time, plaque buildup can also contribute to gum disease, which may affect the tissues and bone supporting the teeth.

The best way to prevent these issues is through proper oral hygiene and routine visits for children’s dental care in New Hampshire.

Brushing helps remove plaque from the surfaces of the teeth and along the gumline, while flossing reaches areas that a toothbrush cannot. Together, these habits form the foundation of a healthy smile.
