Implant-Supported Denture

Keeping the implant site clean and free of plaque and bacteria is essential. Be sure to brush and floss carefully around the implant and abutment. You should also see our team regularly for checkups and cleanings.

Caring for Your Implant-Supported Dentures Implant-supported dentures require similar care as regular dentures. Be sure to brush them daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and nonabrasive denture cleanser. And during your checkups with us, we can check the fit of your dentures and make any necessary adjustments.

  • Improved speech: implant-supported dentures will not slip or move when you talk, which can help you speak more clearly.
  • Improved comfort: implant-supported dentures are more comfortable than traditional dentures because they do not rest on the gums.
  • Enhanced appearance: implant-supported dentures look natural and can help you regain confidence. They are designed just like a natural tooth and tooth root, extending into the jawbone instead of resting on top of your gums like traditional dentures.
