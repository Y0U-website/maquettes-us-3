The practice

We have been offering dental implant services for over 20 years and Dr. Hopkins has achieved Diplomate status with the American Board of Oral Implantology. We offer full-scope implantology and can replace single, multiple and full arches of teeth.

TeethXpress is an advanced implant procedure and can give you a whole new set of teeth in just one day. Imagine being able to eat, speak and smile with confidence again. Our doctors are highly skilled in implantology and give you the results you deserve to live your best life.
