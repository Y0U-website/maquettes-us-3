Dr Madi Garrity Dental…

Dr. Madi Garrity earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine before completing a three-year Dental Anesthesiology residency at Stony Brook University Hospital. Her extensive hospital-based training includes providing anesthesia care for children, adults, and medically complex patients.

Dr. Garrity is passionate about helping patients receive the dental care they need in a safe, comfortable, and compassionate environment. She takes an individualized approach to anesthesia care, with careful preparation and continuous monitoring throughout each procedure.

Originally from Duxbury, Massachusetts, Dr. Garrity is excited to return home and serve families throughout New England. Through Newbury Anesthesia Partners, she works closely with our dental team to provide safe, personalized anesthesia care for patients who may benefit from additional comfort and support during dental treatment.

Outside of her professional life, Dr. Garrity enjoys spending time with family and friends and exploring everything New England has to offer.
