Dr. Chin Park

Family Dentist in Stone Mountain, Georgia

Chin Park, DMD, is a general family dentist and founder of Park Family Dentistry in Stone Mountain, Georgia. He has over a decade of experience in dentistry and enjoys helping patients of all ages achieve beautiful white smiles.

Dr. Park graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in microbiology from the University of Florida in Gainesville. He completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University School of Dental medicine. Dr. Park also served in the Korean Army as an intelligence and interpretation operator in Afghanistan as part of Operation Enduring Freedom.

He treats patients suffering from dental decay or teeth that are crooked, loose, infected, missing, misshapen, or discolored. Dr. Park and his team offer crowns, fillings, full and partial dentures, root canals, dental implants, teeth whitening, preventive care, extractions and more. He also specializes in pediatric dentistry and emergency dentistry.

In his free time, Dr. Park enjoys playing tennis, golf, and basketball. He participates in various tournaments and leagues and loves traveling and playing the piano. Dr. Park enjoys advancing his craft in dentistry and being part of a neighborhood community.

He maintains his credentials with continuing education to provide the most advance, up-to-date dental care available. Dr. park warmly welcomes patients of all ages to Park Family Dentistry and looks forward to serving them.
