Meet Your Green Hills Dentist, Dr. James Kirby

After graduating in 2008, Dr. Kirby made the journey back to Nashville to practice dentistry. Over the last decade, he has worked in both private practice and corporate dentistry. He is very excited to make the transition to Green Hills area! He enjoys building relationships with his patients, serving them with care and compassion and treating them as he would want to be treated. Dr. Kirby enjoys all aspects of dentistry, but he especially enjoys cosmetic dentistry and dental implant restoration.

When he isn’t busy in the office, Dr. Kirby’s hobbies include reading, listening to music, playing racquetball, and spending time in the great outdoors. He is an avid fan of both the Tennessee Titans and the Nashville Predators, and you can regularly find him at professional sports games.

Dr. Kirby and the staff are greatly looking forward to meeting you, and they want you to know that everyone here can’t wait to make you smile!
