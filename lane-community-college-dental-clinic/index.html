The practice

Our clinic provides friendly, comprehensive, and low-cost* dental hygiene care for the community, while giving our dental hygiene and dental assisting students practical, hands-on experience under careful supervision. Built in 2024, our new clinic brings bright, welcoming, state-of-the-art facilities to the community, all generously funded by Bond -voters.

Call 541-463-5206 or fill out the appointment webform to book your appointment today!
