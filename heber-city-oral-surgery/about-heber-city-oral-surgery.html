Jerry Sorensen DDS, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Sorensen was born in Logan, Utah. He completed his undergraduate education at Brigham Young University where he majored in Chemistry and Physics. In 1969 he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of California San Francisco Medical Center Dental School. He was Senior class President. Dr. Sorensen has been the recipient of numerous awards including the Dean Willard Fleming Student Award for academic excellence.

After graduating from dental school he practiced general dentistry for 7 years and received a Fellowship in American Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD). He was then accepted into the Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery residency program at the University of California San Francisco Medical Center- Fresno Valley Medical Center (UCSF-Fresno) where he completed his OMFS residency in 1980.

Dr. Sorensen is a fellow of the American Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS), Diplomate of the American Academy of Pain Management. He is a member of the Utah Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery (UAOMS), the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS) the American Dental Association (ADA), Utah Dental Association (UDA) and several other organizations on a local, state and national level.

Dr. Sorensen and his wife, Rebecca reside in Heber City, Utah. They have 8 children and 30 grandchildren.

Why Choose Heber City Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery?

Care and Commitment

From the front office to the exam room, our practice is made up of professionals who take your oral health very seriously. Using state-of-the-art equipment designed with safety and efficiency in mind, we are able to offer top-notch care. COVID precautions are in place. As you walk through our office, you will notice that everything is clean, comfortable, and thoroughly sterilized. Your safety and comfort are our priority.

Honesty and Affordability

We know that the cost of healthcare is a major consideration. In addition to offering a number of in-office payment options to fit your budget, we also give you up-front cost information on your treatment ahead of time. We are members of a variety of insurance plans. We are happy to help you navigate your dental insurance and alternative payment options.

Easy Access to Oral Surgery Care and Information

We know that your time is valuable to you, and that’s why we offer extended office hours on select days- (Sat & Sun and after-hours Oncall by phone), appointment reminders, and quick turnaround time on calls and appointment requests. We are happy to answer any questions or concerns in the office, over the phone, by teledentistry, or via email.

Comprehensive Oral Surgery Care in One Location

At our practice, we are qualified to handle all aspects of your Oral and Maxillofacial care, so you are always surrounded by people that you trust. If we must refer out, we promise to send you to carefully vetted colleagues who apply the same professional principles in their office that we do in ours.

Safety, Comfort and Confidence

We respect and understand that many patients experience dental anxiety. At our office, we do everything we can to make sure that your experience is as enjoyable as possible. By communicating clearly what you can expect during your treatment, we are often able to alleviate dental fear. We encourage you to bring headphones and music to listen to during your treatment and ask us about sedation options.

At our practice, you aren’t just a patient. We take an interest in you and your oral health needs. We think you’ll find a friend in your oral surgeon during your visit!
