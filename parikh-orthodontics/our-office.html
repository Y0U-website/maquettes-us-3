0 modern + warm We believe orthodontic offices should be modern and inviting. We completed an office “refresh” in the summer of 2019, providing a fresh look to an already beautiful space.

Our office employs above-standard sterilization and disinfection protocols for all equipment, appliances, and spaces.

digital technology

As one of the first offices in Marin to introduce 3D imaging, we employ some of the most up-to-date technology in diagnosis and treatment planning.

We utilize digital records on all patients hosted on a HIPAA-compliant, cloud-based patient management system.

comfortable + convenient

Our office is conveniently located on Miller Avenue on the way to downtown Mill Valley. During appointments, you can relax in our waiting room, which features complimentary WiFi, bottled and sparkling water, and Nespresso coffee (to keep you going through the day)!

Our office has been proud to serve the orthodontic needs of Mill Valley and Marin since 1967. Started by Dr. Earl Johnson, this practice was built to serve a community of families focused on attention to detail and exemplary skill. The practice evolved further with the introduction of 3D imaging and a focus on whole-health orthodontics. Dr. Sachee Parikh joined in 2014 and has been excited to merge new technology with the established foundation of providing excellent care to our patients.
