Are you missing a tooth or several teeth? Dental implants are a relatively painless way to replace missing teeth.

We are happy to offer this unique and specialty service to our community because we know travel outside of the area can be difficult for some.

Many of our patients have reported very little to no discomfort during and after having an implant placed. We pair the implant placement with our Cerec technology making a seamless transition from the placement of the implant to restoring it with a crown.
