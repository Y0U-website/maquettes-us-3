What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants refer to titanium posts that are surgically placed beneath the gumline in order to serve as new tooth roots that support custom-made replacement teeth. Since titanium is biocompatible, the posts fuse with the jawbone to form a sturdy, permanent foundation that perfectly mimics nature, leading to a result that looks and feels like your teeth were never gone in the first place.

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

The dental implant treatment process is an involved one, but it will be well-worth it to achieve a complete and comfortable smile. Rest assured, your implant dentist in Forest Grove will be with you every step of the way. Our team will make sure you have all of the information you need to have a positive experience and will be happy to answer any questions or concerns you might have.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The first step towards getting dental implants in Forest Grove is to schedule a consultation with one of our friendly dentists. They’ll examine your mouth, take detailed X-rays, and review your health history to help determine the best path forward. Some patients can start their dental implant treatment immediately or soon after their consultation. Others have to complete preliminary treatments before they can begin to take care of issues like gum disease and jawbone loss.

Dental Implant Surgery

Once we’ve confirmed the details of your treatment plan with you and wrap-up any necessary preliminary treatments, we’ll prepare for your dental implant surgery. The expertise of our dentists means we can complete this step in our very own office with our very own team no referrals needed!

We’ll start your surgery by making sure you’re comfortable. In other words, your implant dentist will administer a local anesthetic and any dental sedation requested. Then they’ll make an incision in your gumline so they can access your jawbone and place your titanium implant posts. Your implant dentist will suture the incisions closed, and our team will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions before you leave the office.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

For the next three to six months, your mouth will be recovering from dental implant surgery, and your jawbone and implant posts will begin the process of osseointegration. This is when organic bone material fuses with compatible inorganic materials like titanium.

When we’re confident your implants and jawbone are properly fused together, we’ll attach small connector pieces (known as abutments ) to the posts. They’ll help us place your final restoration and keep it fixed to your dental implants.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

It can take some time for our partners at the dental lab to finish customizing your dental implant restoration. As soon as we receive it, we’ll have you return to our office for your final fitting! Your implant dentist will secure your restoration to the abutments and make sure everything looks good and functions properly. Then all that’s left for you to do is go out and enjoy your new smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

When you opt for dental implants, you’re not just replacing lost teeth you’re also gaining access to numerous advantages that traditional restorations, like bridges or dentures, can’t match:

  • They can be used to replace any number of missing teeth, regardless of where they were lost, and they’re designed to perfectly recreate your natural teeth in terms of look and feel.
  • They restore the majority of your original biting force (much more compared to traditional dentures and bridges), meaning that you’ll be able to eat virtually all types of foods with ease.
  • With the right care, dental implants have the potential to last for decades or even a lifetime, making them the only truly permanent tooth replacement solution.
  • Dental implants can prevent the bone loss that commonly occurs in the jawbone following tooth loss since the posts stimulate the tissue by acting as replacement tooth roots.

Missing One Tooth

Whether you’re missing a front tooth or a back molar, we have good news: we can replace it with a single dental implant! Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants are self-supporting, so the teeth surrounding the gap don’t need to be altered. We just need to insert the implant into your jawbone, allow the two to fuse together, and secure a custom dental crown on top. Then, the entire structure will be restored.

Missing Multiple Teeth

We always have the option of replacing each individual tooth with a dental implant. However, that usually isn’t the most convenient option. For example, if you are missing four teeth consecutively, it’s much easier to fill the gap in your smile with an implant bridge. Since the implants are secured to your jawbone and the restoration is custom-made with your dental needs and aesthetic goals in mind, you’ll enjoy a strong bite and renewed confidence!

Missing All Teeth

What if you are missing all of your teeth? Does that mean your only option is traditional dentures? Fortunately, the answer is no. With implant dentures, we can secure several dental implants throughout the arch before securing the custom restoration in place. Not only does this lead to added stability and durability, but it also helps prevent bone loss and preserve your youthful face shape.
