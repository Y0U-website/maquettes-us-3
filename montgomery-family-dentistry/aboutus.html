About Us

Dr. Faith Hayden

Dr. Hayden was raised in Cincinnati, OH. She attended and graduated from Indian Hill High School. Her undergraduate education was at Howard University in Washington, DC where she received her Bachelor of Science degree in 2005. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Howard University College of Dentistry in 2014 where she still sits on the admissions committee. Dr Hayden completed her General Practice Residency program at Miami Valley Hospital in Dayton, Ohio.

Dr. Hayden is very committed to serving her local city. She has donated her time and services to several schools in the Cincinnati area since 2014. She attends numerous continuing education courses each year to stay at the forefront of her specialty so she can provide the best and most up to date care for her patients. She is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Ohio Dental Society, National Dental Association, and the Women’s Dental Study Group.

Dr Hayden is an active member at Crossroads Community Church. She loves to travel, spend time with family, on her time off she enjoys a great glass of wine, and enjoys a good book in her free time.
