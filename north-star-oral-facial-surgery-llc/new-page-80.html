WHY CHOOSE

NORTH STAR ORAL & FACIAL SURGERY?

CARE AND COMMITMENT

From the front office to the exam room, our oral surgery practice is made up of professionals who take your care very seriously. Using state-of-the-art equipment designed with safety and efficiency in mind, we are able to offer the best personalized care.

We know that the cost of care can be confusing. We give you up-front cost information on your treatment ahead of time. We are in network with a variety of insurance plans, and we offer dental financing options in time frames that fit your needs. We are happy to help you navigate your dental and medical insurances and alternative payment options.

COMFORT

We respect and understand that many patients experience dental anxiety. At our office, we do everything we can to make sure that your experience is as comfortable as possible. By communicating clearly what you can expect during your treatment, we are often able to alleviate your fear and anxiety. Please ask us about different sedation options.

PERSONALIZED SERVICE

At our practice, we focus on personalized care. We take serious interest in you and your surgical needs.
