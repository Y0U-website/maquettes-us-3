During your first appointment, your hygienist will complete the necessary X-rays and oral cancer screening and address any concerns you may have. Your oral health will determine the type of cleaning you will need and, if able, will be completed the same day, followed by a comprehensive exam with the dentist.

Our goal is to provide the very best oral care and ensure your oral health stays on track by getting you scheduled for your treatment needs as quickly as possible. We are always available to answer any questions or concerns you may have. We look forward to working with you.
