Expect a better dental experience.

What are the things that are most important to you in a dental relationship? Each dental practice provides a unique experience based on the priorities that are most important. Our goal has been to create an ideal dental experience for our patients that focuses on the following priorities:

Superior quality Dr. Butterfield prides herself on her passion for quality and attention to detail. Her staff was hand picked because they share her same passion to ensure treatments are done right in the best way possible. You will feel the difference quality makes, as though you have had the most thorough treatment you have ever received. Additionally, through continuing education, Dr. Butterfield and her staff are consistently improving their skill-set and knowledge base. If quality matters to you, you will love Dr. Butterfield.

Technology Technology improves diagnosis, delivers better treatments and speeds recovery. It is another way to show that we care about each patient’s experience.

Patient Communication We carefully consider each treatment plan and educate you in a manner that empowers you to make confident decisions regarding your oral health. We ensure you have the time to ask questions and have those questions answered in a meaningful way.

Sincere Care We value you as a patient and strive to build meaningful relationships based on trust. We respect you, your time, health and the emotions associated with your dental experience. We treat every patient as a part of our family and give each patient individualized care.

We are a full service dental office that serves the entire family. In addition to our general procedures, we provide implant restorations, cosmetic restorations and whitening. To ensure you always receive the best care we refer to specialists as well.

Meet Our Team

Meet Dr. Pam Butterfield

Dr. Butterfield is a native of Seattle. She received both her Bachelor of Arts and Doctor of Dental Surgery degrees from the University of Washington.

Dr. Butterfield was a clinical instructor at the University Of Washington School Of Dentistry. She has been in private, family dental practice since 1984. She has participated in several study clubs including the Pacific Northwest Study Club and the Comprehensive Dental Study Group.

Dr. Butterfield is predictable and prefers long term relationships. This defines her approach to dentistry, her patients, staff and family. She gets to know her patients, responds to their needs and respects their values. She offers personalized, preventive, restorative and cosmetic dental care. Families trust her to assist them make informed decisions about healthy, cost effective treatment.

Her office is filled with friendship, good cheer and genuine care.
