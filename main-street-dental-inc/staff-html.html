Chetna Kansangra

Hi, my name is Chetna Kansangra; I have been a Clinical, Consultant Dietitian since 1989. I was born in Kenya, studied in England and moved to USA in 1995. Travelling through three continents allowed me to gain clinical experience working with multiracial groups in different hospitals and communities.

My Dietetic work in the USA involves working mainly with residential agencies, consulting mentally and physically handicapped clients, and teaching the residential staff on providing healthy foods and maintaining optimal health. I also see private clients for individual personalized consultations. At Main Street Dental, I use my Dietetic knowledge to educate dental patients on the increasing awareness of the relationship between healthy teeth and healthy life.

I hope throughout my life I can make a difference to all my clients’ lives by increasing awareness of the choices they have, educating, motivating and encouraging a positive attitude to behavioral change. I truly believe life is a choice and every day we all have to make choices that determine the outcome of our health.
