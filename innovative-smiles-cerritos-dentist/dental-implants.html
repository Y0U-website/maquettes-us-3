Our Cerritos Dental Implant Process: A Journey to a New Smile

At Innovative Smiles, we have streamlined the dental implant process to be as comfortable and efficient as possible. Your journey is personalized to your specific needs, ensuring the best possible outcome.

1. Comprehensive Consultation Your path to a new smile begins with a thorough consultation at our Cerritos office. Our implant dentist will perform a detailed examination, including advanced 3D CBCT imaging, to assess your oral health, bone density, and suitability for implants. We will discuss your goals, answer your questions, and develop a customized treatment plan.

3. Healing and Osseointegration Over the next few months, a remarkable process called osseointegration occurs. The jawbone naturally fuses with the titanium post, creating an incredibly strong and stable foundation for your new tooth. We will provide a temporary restoration so you never have to go without a tooth.

4. Abutment and Final Restoration Once healing is complete, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant post. We then take final impressions to create your permanent, custom-made crown. This crown is expertly designed to match your natural teeth perfectly.

5. A Lifetime of Confident Smiles Your final crown is securely attached to the abutment, completing your restoration. You can leave our office ready to smile, eat, and speak with renewed confidence, knowing your new tooth is strong, secure, and beautiful.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

1. Are dental implants painful? The implant placement procedure itself is not painful. We use effective local anesthesia to completely numb the area. Most patients report feeling only minor pressure. Any post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication, similar to what you might feel after a simple tooth extraction.

2. How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental check-ups at our Cerritos office, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant post itself is made of a biocompatible material that becomes a permanent part of your jaw. The crown may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear, but the implant foundation remains secure.

3. Am I a good candidate for dental implants? Most adults who are missing one or more teeth and are in good general health are excellent candidates for dental implants. The primary requirement is having sufficient jawbone density to support the implant. If you have experienced bone loss, a bone grafting procedure can often make implant placement possible. A full evaluation with our implant dentist will determine your candidacy.

4. How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on several factors, including the number of implants needed and whether any preparatory procedures like bone grafting are required. While implants can be a significant investment, they are also the most durable and long-term solution. At your consultation, we will provide a transparent, detailed breakdown of all costs and discuss our flexible financing options.

5. How do I care for my dental implants? Caring for your dental implants is as simple as caring for your natural teeth. You should brush twice a day, floss daily (using special floss to clean around the implant), and maintain regular professional cleanings and check-ups at Innovative Smiles. Excellent oral hygiene is key to ensuring the long-term health and success of your implant.
