The practice

At Madison Square Family Dental, we are proud to have a team of highly skilled and compassionate dental professionals who are dedicated to providing the best possible care to our patients. Our team is led by Dr. Bo Ding.

Dr. Ding is an experienced and compassionate dentist. He goes above and beyond to provide quality and personalized care in a relaxing and modern facility.

Dr. Ding grew up in Atlanta, GA. After earning his Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience and Behavioral Biology at Emory University, he went on to complete his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Roseman University of Health Sciences in Salt Lake City, UT. While in dental school Dr. Ding was involved in many extracurricular activities and volunteer events in the community.

Dr. Ding understands that continuing his education is important to stay up to date with the latest techniques and procedures in dentistry.
