Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You may be a good candidate for implant dentures if:

If you are not sure whether you qualify for implant dentures, visit us for a consultation. Most adults with missing teeth can get this procedure.

  • You are missing all or most of your teeth throughout one or both of your dental arches.
  • Your jawbone is strong enough and large enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease and other conditions that might negatively affect your implants.
  • You have good habits that will keep your risk of dental implant failure to a minimum.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures can provide some truly remarkable benefits:

  • Stability and strength: Implant dentures provide a much stronger bite force than regular dentures, so you can look forward to enjoying a wide variety of tasty and delicious foods.
  • Jawbone health benefits: Dental implants prevent the jawbone from deteriorating. In contrast, traditional dentures might actually accelerate bone loss.
  • Beauty: We carefully design implant dentures so they look very natural.
  • Longevity: Dental implants are designed to stand the test of time! Traditional dentures have a much shorter life expectancy and usually need to be replaced every 5 7 years.
