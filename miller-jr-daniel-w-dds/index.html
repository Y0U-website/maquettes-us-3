Partials in dentistry are removable dental appliances designed to replace one or more missing teeth, helping restore function and appearance. They are custom-made to fit comfortably in the patient’s mouth and are often secured with clasps that attach to the natural teeth.

Bonding in dentistry is a procedure where a tooth-colored resin material is applied and hardened with a special light to restore or improve a person’s smile. It is commonly used to repair chipped, cracked, or discolored teeth, and can also help close gaps or change the shape of teeth.
