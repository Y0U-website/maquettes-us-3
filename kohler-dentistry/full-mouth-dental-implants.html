Streamlined Implant Protocols Enable Placing Same-Day Teeth

Thanks to modern advancements, replacing an entire arch of missing teeth is quicker, more precise, and more comfortable than in the past. Unlike traditional implants, which require months of healing before you get your new teeth, this approach lets patients enjoy a confident new smile the same day in many cases.

Two or more implants per arch are placed vertically in the front, while two or more are angled at the back to take advantage of the naturally densest bone. This innovative method typically bypasses the need for preliminary bone grafting by delivering maximum stability even with minimal bone volume.

What to Expect During Your Full-Arch Restoration Journey

Personalized Consultation & Planning Your journey to a new smile begins with a thorough one-on-one consultation with Dr. Kohler, where he carefully evaluates your oral health, bone structure, and overall candidacy for full-mouth implants. Implant Placement Once you’re resting comfortably under sedation , Dr. Kohler meticulously places four or more implants per arch in your jawbone. Same-Day Smile Transformation In many cases, a temporary set of implant-supported dentures in Wilmette, IL is attached the same day as surgery. Final Restoration Once healing is complete, your custom-crafted permanent dentures are placed. Your final restoration is designed for durability, aesthetics, and a natural feel—giving you a smile that looks and functions like real teeth.
