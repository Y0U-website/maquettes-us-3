Dr. Roland Nishioka, DmD

Dr. Nishioka has been practicing dentistry in his Pearl City practice for over three decades. He received his dental degree from Oregon Health & Science University in Portland, OR.

Dr. Nishioka has four children, two of which have followed in his footsteps and are now practicing dentists in Hawaii. His son and daughter can now be found working in the office as well.

In his free time, Dr. Nishioka enjoys stand-up paddle surfing and taking care of his mango trees and pineapple plants. He also frequently visits his younger son, Tanner, who plays minor league baseball on the East Coast.

Dr. Travis Nishioka, DMD

Dr. Travis followed in his father’s footsteps and also received his Doctorate in Dentistry from Oregon Health & Science University. Upon graduating, he immediately moved home to join his father’s practice. Dr. Travis believes in what his father started: a family practice that is centered around being able to build strong relationships with his patients and providing them with personalized care.

In his free time, Dr. Travis enjoys playing board games such as Sequence and Catan with his fiancé. He also can be frequently found at the golf course, working on improving his swing.
