Lakeview Smiles Family Dentistry: Covid 19 Policy

Your safety is our top priority.

Pre-screening

Our office will communicate with you beforehand to ask some screening questions. You’ll be asked those same questions again when you are in the office.

Temperature Reading

When you come in the office, we will take your temperature and ask you a few-health related questions.

Sanitization

We have hand sanitizer that we will ask you to use when you enter the office. You will also find some in the reception area and other places in the office for you to use as needed.

Mask Policy

We require everybody to wear a personal mask when visiting us.

Staggered Visits

We have reduced our schedule and staggered appointments to allow for social distancing between patients. It is important that you be on time for your appointment to help us coordinate this spacing.

Social Distancing

We will do our best to allow greater time between patients to reduce waiting times for you, as well as to reduce the number of patients in the reception area at any one time.
