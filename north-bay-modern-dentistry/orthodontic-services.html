Orthodontic services

At NorthBay Modern Dentistry, Dr. Marina Shamoun is dedicated to creating healthy, balanced smiles through personalized orthodontic care. Our practice offers a range of orthodontic services tailored to each patient’s needs, using the latest advancements in treatment to ensure effective, comfortable, and lasting results.

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a reliable and effective solution for correcting various dental misalignments. Comprising high-quality stainless steel brackets and archwires, they apply consistent pressure to guide teeth into their proper positions over time. Modern advancements have made metal braces more comfortable and less noticeable than ever before. Patients can also personalize their braces with colored elastics, adding a fun and individualized touch to their treatment.

Invisalign

Invisalign aligners offer a nearly invisible way to straighten teeth. Made from clear, medical-grade plastic, these custom-fit aligners are designed to gradually guide teeth into alignment. They are removable, making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene and enjoy meals without restrictions. Invisalign is an excellent option for patients seeking a more discreet alternative to traditional braces

Expansion & Early Intervention

For younger patients, we provide expansion and early intervention treatments. These services help guide the growth of a child’s jaw and teeth, often preventing or reducing the need for more extensive orthodontic work in the future. By identifying issues early, Dr. Shamoun can create a foundation for healthier dental development, ensuring young patients can grow into their natural smiles with confidence.

Our orthodontic services are available for children, teens, and adults, with treatments customized to support each individual’s unique goals and lifestyle.
