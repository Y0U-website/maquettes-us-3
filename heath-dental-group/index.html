The practice

PHONE HOURS: Mon.–Thurs.: 8 a.m. – 12 noon and 1 p.m. – 3 p.m.

Our experienced, caring dental team has been serving Santa Barbara County families with patient-centered dental services for more than 30 years. Heath Dental Group takes the time to listen to your needs and work with you to maintain excellent oral health and improve your best smile.

Dr. Bill Heath — a lifelong resident of Lompoc and practicing dentist for more than 30 years — is not only a respected member of the Spear Dental Faculty Club, but is committed to the health and vibrancy of the local community through service and civic involvement.

A beautiful, healthy smile can improve your confidence, self-esteem, and overall health. Heath Dental Group offers a variety of cosmetic and reconstructive dental solutions including porcelain dental veneers, cosmetic bonding, teeth whitening, dental implants and more.

Regular, twice-yearly professional dental cleanings and exams are essential for maintaining a healthy mouth. We’ll provide you with high-quality professional dental cleanings and thorough preventive exams.

Your child should visit the dentist within six months after the presence of the first tooth or by his or her first birthday. Early oral examination is essential to maintain good oral health.

We know that when accidents and dental emergencies happen, you need access to a dentist quickly. Heath Dental Group is here to handle client’s unexpected dental emergencies.

At Heath Dental Group, our goal is to give you the smile of your dreams. Every smile is different, so our solutions are customized to fit your individual needs and may include whitening, veneers, crowns, aligners or other treatments.
