Services

  • Fluoride Fluoride is a natural mineral that is often referred to as nature’s cavity fighter. This amazing mineral strengthens the tooth’s enamel, making it more resistant to tooth decay. Using fluoride toothpaste and drinking fluoridated water are great practices to ensure you recieving a sufficient amount of fluoride. Professional fluoride treatments are also an effective method to fight against tooth decay, strengthen enamel, and help children develop healthy teeth.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Early detection and treatment of oral cancer can significantly increase your chances of a quick and complete recovery. At every hygiene visit, we examine all the tissues of the mouth, including gums, cheeks, lips, and tongue. If we suspect any unusual changes in the mouth tissue, we may suggest a biopsy and microscopic analysis by a qualified lab.
  • Sports Mouthguards It’s estimated that over 20 million children take part in one after-school sport or another. With that amount of children engaging in sports and other activities like skiing, gymnastics, and martial arts, there is a higher chance of injury. Did you know that up to 20 % of these injuries are due to fractured or otherwise damaged teeth? Our team can create a sports mouthguard for your child to protect their smile. After an impression of your child’s teeth is taken, they will fabricate a new mouthguard molded to custom-fit their unique smile.

Oral Condition Treatment

Conservative dental care for optimal wellness

  • Pediatric Restorative Dentistry If your child needs restorative treatment, we will create a custom treatment plan for your child and review all treatment options with you before treatment begins. Our team will ensure that your child stays comfortable throughout their appointment and provide the best care possible to them.
  • Fillings If tooth decay does sneak into your child’s smile, fillings are a minimally invasive procedure to eliminate decay and restore little smiles to full health. In this routine treatment, we will inject an anesthetic near the tooth to numb the area. Then, we will use a dental drill to remove any decay present in the area and fill in the carved-out space with a dental restoration material.
  • Crowns Dental crowns can restore its strength and appearance if a child’s tooth is damaged or weakened. In this procedure, we’ll gently prepare the tooth by removing any decay or damaged areas. After numbing the area with a local anesthetic, we’ll shape the tooth and place a custom-made crown over it, protecting the tooth and reinforcing your child’s smile with a durable, natural-looking solution.
  • Periodontal Hygiene Cleaning Pediatric periodontal hygiene cleanings are essential for maintaining healthy gums and preventing more serious issues for children at risk of gum disease. During this gentle treatment, we’ll thoroughly clean your child’s gums and teeth, removing plaque and tartar buildup that brushing alone can’t reach. By keeping their gums healthy and free from infection, we help protect your child’s smile and overall well-being, setting the foundation for lifelong oral health.
  • Frenectomies A frenum or frenulum is a small piece of tissue that attaches either the upper lip to the gum tissue or the tongue to the floor of the mouth. In some children, this frenum or frenulum is attached too tightly and can pull on the lips or gums causing tongue-tie, problems nursing, speech issues, and other concerns. During a frenectomy, we make a small incision in the frenum or frenulum, allowing for greater movement of the lips. Results are instant.
