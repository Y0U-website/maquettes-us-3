Your Community

Scholarships

Our commitment to serving our patients and community goes beyond creating beautiful smiles. We strive to build a better community by supporting others. The Hatala Orthodontic Scholarship award recognizes our local graduating seniors who display outstanding achievement and share our commitment to this community.

Field Trips

Field trips are a fun way for local school classes to learn about oral health and orthodontics. We also love to host Brownies in Broome County to meet a health helper and achieve a badge. Our hygienists use learning stations and hands-on activities to provide instruction on proper dental health and function, care of teeth and gums, and how braces work to create healthier smiles. Please contact us if you are interested in a field trip opportunity.

Groundhog Day Shadowing

At Hatala Orthodontics we love having visitors and sharing our passion and knowledge of the orthodontic field. Each year on Groundhog’s Day, we invite local high school seniors to learn why we love orthodontics and provide inspiration for their dreams of a future dental career.

Car Washes

Need a fun idea for a group fundraiser? Ask us about hosting a car wash for your local group or club. If you have driven down Riverside Drive on a Saturday in the summer you have probably seen our patients hosting car washes and bake sales for their school organizations. You may have even stopped in yourself for a wash or some baked goods. Contact us if you would like to host one this year.
