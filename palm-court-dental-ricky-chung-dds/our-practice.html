Dental Care Made Simple

At Palm Court Dental, our mission is simple—provide the highest quality service at an affordable price. Our team is dedicated to making your dental care as convenient as possible.

All our procedures are performed in-house by our skilled dentist, Dr. Chung. Dr. Chung and his team pay close attention to even the smallest details to produce stunning, long-lasting results.

Our office is conveniently located across the street from the Palm Court Shopping Center and provides a clean, professional environment to fulfill all your dental needs.

Dr. Yu-Chi Ricky Chung, DDS

Dr. Chung practices with the main belief of “treating people the way you want to be treated.” His gentleness and patience have helped both children and adults find a relaxing and comfortable experience at the office.

Dr. Chung makes it his goal to treat patients with kindness, compassion and honesty so they can have healthy mouths, confident smiles and comfort in their dental visits. With the use of Invisalign® clear aligners, dental implants, metal-free crowns, whitening and other cosmetic procedures, you can have the smile and confidence you have always wanted and deserved.

Dr. Chung is originally from Southern California and received his Bachelor of Science in biology from New York University in 2003. He completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at New York University College of Dentistry in 2006. Upon finishing his education in New York, he became licensed to practice in both Nevada and California.

The beginning of his career took him to a general dentistry office in Las Vegas, Nevada, where he honed his clinical and patient management skills for 10 years.

When Dr. Chung is not dedicating his time to his patients and dentistry, you can find him enjoying his time with his wife and two daughters.

Dr. Chung is a member of the American Dental Association®, California Dental Association, Tri-County Dental Association, and American Academy of Clear Aligners.
