Your Questions Answered

We accept all forms of credit and debit cards including: Discover, Mastercard, Visa and American Express.

Is there a fee to join a Membership Club?

We require a $99 initiation fee that will also cover the first month payment.

How do I know if I need the Periodontal Membership Club?

Following a new patient exam a recommendation will be made by our doctor to establish which Membership Club works best for you. For exisiting patients - if you are currently on periodontal maintenance this is the club for you.

After signing up there is a one year contract before any cancellation.

What information is required for me to sign up for my club membership?

Your full name, address, phone number, date of birth, email address and billing information will be required in order to sign up. If you are interested in signing up for one of our Membership Clubs simply call us, Email Us, or come into the office to fill out a sign up form.

When can I start using my Membership Club?

Your Membership Club is effective immediately upon signing up.
