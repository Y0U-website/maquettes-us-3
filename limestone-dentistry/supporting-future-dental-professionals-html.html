The Dental Professionals of Tomorrow

Dr. Thornton has years of experience in both dentistry and teaching others about dental care. In addition to caring for families at Limestone Dentistry, he often speaks at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry on a range of advanced topics.

However, Dr. Thornton knows that learning doesn’t stop once students leave the lecture hall! That’s why our team works with students pursuing dental careers so they can get invaluable hands-on experience. We proudly partner with local schools like:

These partnerships help provide students with meaningful exposure to the dental field and open doors to future careers in dentistry.

  • Hosa Future Health Professionals
  • Spalding University

Why We Believe in Supporting Future Dental Professionals

At Limestone Dentistry, Dr. Thornton and our team believe that giving back to our community means more than providing quality care today. It also means creating a solid foundation for tomorrow. By supporting and mentoring future dental professionals, we’re passing along our knowledge, experience, and passion for dentistry to the next generation.

Through hands-on training, real-world opportunities, and lasting partnerships, we are helping students take their first steps toward rewarding careers in dental care. In doing so, we’re not only shaping the future of dentistry, but also helping ensure that brighter, healthier smiles are part of our community for generations to come!
