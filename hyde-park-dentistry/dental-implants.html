What are Dental Implants? Dental implants are biocompatible, hypoallergenic restorations that mirror the design and function of a tooth root. Once installed, your bone naturally fuses to the surface (a process we call “osseointegration,”) permanently anchoring it into place. After an implant is completely integrated, it is capable of supporting a fixed restoration and can last for the rest of your life.
