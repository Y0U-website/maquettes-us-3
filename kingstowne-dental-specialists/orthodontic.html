Why Would I Need

To See An Orthodontist?

You may need to see an orthodontist for several reasons, including the following:

  • You have a bad bite or jaw problems – A bad bite, such as an overbite, underbite, crossbite or open bite, can negatively affect your appearance and your self-esteem. Not only that, but bad bites can also cause issues with speech, or even with chewing and eating. If left untreated, serious bite problems can also contribute to jaw issues like temporomandibular joint disorder (TMJ/TMD).
  • Your teeth are extremely crooked – If your teeth are very crooked and misaligned, you may have problems speaking and chewing properly. Crooked or crowded teeth that do not fit together properly can make it hard to eat, and can become worn down prematurely. If your teeth are crooked enough to affect your oral health, you definitely need to see an orthodontist.
  • You don’t feel confident about your smile – Even if your bite and your smile are relatively healthy and are not causing oral health problems, you may still feel bad about your smile due to issues like gaps in your teeth, slightly-misaligned teeth, and uneven teeth. Orthodontists at Kingstowne can use Invisalign in Alexandria or braces to correct these cosmetic issues, and ensure that you feel great about your smile.
