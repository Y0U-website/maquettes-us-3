Dr. Tyler Leavitt

Dr. Tyler Leavitt was born in Pocatello and grew up in Blackfoot, and happy to still call this area home. He has a passion for getting to know people as individuals-making them laugh, treating them like family and making them smile.

He is also passionate about orthodontics and the confidence a great smile brings to patients. He created Leavitt Orthodontics as a place where he could combine these passions and to give YOU a reason to smile.

EDUCATION AND CREDENTIALS At BYU-Idaho, he earned his Bachelors of Science degree. He was accepted into the Idaho Dental Education Program (IDEP) where he completed his dental education at Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska. He graduated with the highest honors-Summa Cum Laude and inducted into Alpha Sigma Nu Honor Society and Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honor Society.

After receiving his Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS), he completed a specialty residency training in orthodontics as well as a Masters degree in Dental Science at St. Louis University, Center for Advanced Dental Education in Missouri.

HOMEGROWN HERE IN IDAHO

Dr. Tyler Leavitt was born in Pocatello and raised in Blackfoot and graduated from Blackfoot High School, Dr. Leavitt’s childhood was filled with many activities. His senior year of high school, he was All-Conference in both football and baseball. After his education and training taking him all over the country, Dr. Leavitt is glad to be back home.

FAMILY AND HOBBIES

He met his wife, Leslie at BYU-Idaho and they have three children: Patrick, Henry, and Molly. While not in the office, Tyler is taking his boys fishing and spending as much time as possible with family and friends and enjoys giving back to the community he loves.
