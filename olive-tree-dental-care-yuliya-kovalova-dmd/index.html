The practice

We take a minimally invasive, evidence based approach and use modern technology to provide care tailored to you. Our pricing is transparent, and your well being always comes first.

We provide care in English, Russian, and Ukrainian to better serve our local community and help every patient feel comfortable and understood.

Dr. Yuliya Kovalova is a dedicated dentist known for her precise clinical skills and a calm, thoughtful approach to care. She earned her bachelor’s degree in chemistry with a minor in biology from the University of North Georgia, and went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University — one of the nation’s top dental schools.

Dr. Kovalova’s journey brought her from Atlanta, Georgia to North Port, Florida in 2021, a place she now proudly calls home. She enjoys being part of the local community and is dedicated to providing dental care that is transparent, evidence-based, and tailored to each individual. Fluent in Ukrainian and Russian, Dr. Kovalova connects with a diverse range of patients in a way that feels both…

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Kovalova enjoys spending time with her family, relaxing at the beach, traveling, and taking on home improvement projects.

We are an out-of-network provider, so you can use your dental insurance benefits only if your plan includes out-of-network coverage. Full payment is required at the time of service. After your treatment, we will submit a claim to your insurance on your behalf, and any reimbursement will be sent directly to you according to your insurance’s fee schedule.

We understand that not everyone has dental insurance. To support our uninsured patients, Olive Tree Dental Care offers discounted pricing and flexible payment plans, to make quality dental care accessible for all.
