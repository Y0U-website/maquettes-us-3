The practice

Shaun Whitney, DDS Dr. Whitney was born and raised in Spokane, Washington, and graduated from Gonzaga University. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska.

He completed advanced specialty training at the University of Nebraska Medical Center, including a three-year residency in periodontics and implant surgery, followed by an additional two-year residency in endodontics.

Dr. Whitney is also a member of the American Dental Association, the American Association of Endodontists, and the American Academy of Periodontology. He is a board-eligible Endodontist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology.

As the only dedicated periodontal practice in the Coeur d’Alene, ID area, we’re committed to the long-term health and wellness of your smile. Every treatment we provide is designed to save teeth, prevent disease, and promote lifelong oral health.

Through advanced endodontics and GentleWave technology, we treat infection deep within the tooth to preserve your natural smile and prevent future pain, all with remarkable comfort and precision. As a dual-specialist surgical expert, Dr. Whitney can provide comprehensive solutions, including apicoectomy and root amputation , ensuring you can keep as many of your own teeth as possible.

Our soft tissue treatments correct gum irregularities, restore balance to your smile, and improve both function and aesthetics. These treatments help maintain healthy gum tissue, keeping your smile in its best possible shape.

As a dual-trained periodontist and endodontist , Dr. Whitney provides comprehensive care designed to preserve your natural teeth, protect your oral health, and restore lasting comfort and confidence!
