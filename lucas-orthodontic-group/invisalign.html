Invisalign FAQ

How does Invisalign work? Invisalign works by applying gentle pressure to move your teeth into their desired positions. Every set of aligners is slightly different from the one before it, allowing your teeth to shift over time. You’ll wear each set for about a week before switching to the next, gradually progressing through your personalized treatment plan.

How long does treatment with Invisalign take? The length of your Invisalign treatment depends on your specific needs. On average, most patients complete their treatment in 12 to 18 months. However, minor cases may be corrected in as little as six months, while more complex cases may take longer.

Does treatment with Invisalign hurt? Invisalign is generally more comfortable than traditional braces, but you may experience mild discomfort when you start wearing a new set of aligners. This is normal and a sign that your teeth are shifting. The discomfort typically goes away after a few days.

How often do I need to wear my aligners? For the best results, you should wear your Invisalign aligners for 20-22 hours a day. You can remove them for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, but it’s important to wear them consistently to ensure your treatment stays on track.

Can I eat whatever I want with Invisalign? Yes! Since the aligners are removable, you can eat all your favorite foods without any restrictions. Just be sure to take your aligners out before meals and clean your teeth before putting them back in.

How do I care for my aligners? To keep your aligners clean and clear, rinse them regularly with lukewarm water and gently brush them with a soft toothbrush. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the aligners. You can also use Invisalign cleaning crystals or a retainer cleaner to keep them fresh.

How much does Invisalign cost? The cost of Invisalign varies depending on the complexity of your treatment. At Lucas Orthodontic Group, we offer flexible payment options, including in-house financing, to make Invisalign accessible. We’re also happy to assist with filing your insurance to maximize your benefits.

Will I need a retainer after Invisalign treatment? Yes, once your Invisalign treatment is complete, you’ll need to wear a retainer to maintain your results. Retainers help keep your teeth in their new positions and prevent them from shifting back over time.
