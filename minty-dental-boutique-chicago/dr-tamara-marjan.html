About me

Dr. Tamara Marjan, a distinguished graduate of the UIC Advanced Dental Program, has been dedicated to providing exemplary dental care since completing her studies. Known for her expertise in general dentistry, she is also a respected member of the Illinois Academy of General Dentist, Chicago Dental Society, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Marjan’s first dental degree was earned from the University of Jordan, showcasing her diverse educational background and commitment to her field.

Her practice is not just her profession but also her passion. Dr. Marjan is a devoted family person, balancing her professional life with her personal life.

She continuously strives to enhance her skills and knowledge, regularly participating in advanced training courses to stay abreast of the latest developments in dental care.

Dr. Marjan’s services encompass a broad spectrum of dental treatments, ensuring that all your oral health needs are met with the highest standards. Whether you need routine check-ups, advanced dental procedures, or cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Tamara Marjan’s commitment to her patients’ wellbeing makes her a trusted choice for dental care.
