The practice

At Lakeside Dental we take pride in recognizing that each individual has different needs and desires. We customize our treatment plans to fit the goal of each patient and will work with you to obtain the goals that you set for your oral health.

We welcome new patients. We realize you have a choice when it comes to your dental office and our goal is to provide you with conservative, predictable treatment options, and the education to make the right choice for you.

We understand going to the dentist can be a scary experience for many people. Sedation options are available for our anxious patients. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas), and Dr. Gandhi has advanced training in deeper sedation options.

We accept all dental insurance plans that allow you to choose your own dentist, even if we are not a contacted provider. We will electronically file these claims for you. We will be happy to verify your insurance eligibility and benefits before your appointment.
