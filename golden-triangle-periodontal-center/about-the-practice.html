About the Practice

Golden Triangle Periodontal Center sponsors annual dental Hi-Tech continuing education courses for dentists and hygienists in the area and also sponsors hygiene study club courses. Dr. Starr has been practicing periodontics and dental implants in Columbus and Starkville for over 30 years.

He and his wife, Lee Ann, live in Columbus and have two daughters.
