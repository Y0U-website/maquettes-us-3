Dr. Hawley is originally from Excelsior, Minnesota. He completed his undergraduate education at St. Cloud State University in 1998 by receiving a B.S. degree in Medical Microbiology. He then relocated to Minneapolis, Minnesota, where he earned his DDS degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2003.

Following graduation from dental school, Dr. Hawley completed a 1 year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) and then served as a dental officer in the United States Navy for an additional 7 years. In 2008, Dr. Hawley received an M.S. degree and a Certificate in Endodontics after completing two years of rigorous training at the Naval Postgraduate Dental School Endodontics.

Dr. Hawley is a member of the American Association of Endodontists, American Dental Association, and South Dakota Dental Association. He has been published in the Journal of Endodontists.
