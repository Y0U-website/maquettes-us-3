Timing It Right

Early Interceptive Treatment

Phase 1 orthodontics, also called early interceptive treatment, is a limited course of treatment performed while a child still has a mix of baby and permanent teeth, generally between ages 7 and 10. It addresses problems that become more difficult to correct later, including severe crowding, crossbites, protruding front teeth at risk of injury, and jaw growth discrepancies. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Most children evaluated at that age do not require immediate treatment and are monitored until the appropriate time.

With gentle care and expertise, we make this early phase of orthodontic care a positive, engaging experience for your child, easing any anxieties and making visits something they can look forward to.

Braces

Braces are a cornerstone of orthodontic treatment, offering a solution for correcting various dental misalignments and bite issues. At Park Ridge Orthodontics, we provide expertly tailored braces treatments for patients of all ages.

Metal Braces Braces are a fixed orthodontic appliance consisting of brackets bonded to each tooth and connected by an archwire that applies continuous gentle pressure to move teeth into planned positions. They are available in stainless steel and in clear ceramic, which blends with natural tooth color. Treatment generally lasts 6 to 24 months. Park Ridge Orthodontics uses an indirect bonding system in which bracket positions are planned digitally on a 3D model and transferred with a custom 3D-printed guide, improving placement accuracy compared with placing brackets individually by eye.

Clear Braces For those seeking a more discreet orthodontic solution, our clear or ceramic braces are an excellent choice. They offer the same effectiveness as traditional metal braces, but with a more subtle appearance. The brackets blend in with the natural color of your teeth, making them less noticeable.

Invisalign®

Discover the modern way to straighten teeth with Invisalign®.

Invisalign® Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using a series of clear, removable plastic aligners rather than fixed brackets and wires. Each aligner is worn 20 to 22 hours per day for roughly one to two weeks before advancing to the next in the series, and each moves the teeth a small, planned increment. Treatment typically takes 12 to 24 months depending on complexity. At Park Ridge Orthodontics in Park Ridge, NJ, the aligner series is planned from a 3D digital scan taken with our iTero Lumina scanner.

Invisalign Teen® Invisalign Teen is designed specifically for the unique needs of teenagers and adolescents. Perfect for kids involved in sports or music, this option offers comfort without interfering with activities.

Invisalign First® Invisalign First introduces younger patients to orthodontics with a gentle approach. These aligners are specially designed for children’s growing mouths, addressing issues early on. Invisalign First helps guide the development of young jaws and teeth, setting the foundation for a healthier smile.

Retainers

A retainer is an orthodontic appliance worn after active treatment to hold teeth in their corrected positions. Retainers are either removable, such as clear thermoplastic or wire-and-acrylic Hawley designs, or fixed, meaning a thin wire bonded permanently behind the front teeth. Because teeth have a natural lifelong tendency to shift, retainer wear is generally recommended indefinitely on a nighttime schedule rather than for a fixed period. Park Ridge Orthodontics fabricates retainers on site using an in-house 3D printer.

Our commitment extends beyond aligning your teeth; we guide you in preserving your smile’s beauty with the right retainer choice to meet your individual needs.

Corrective Jaw Surgery

Corrective jaw surgery addresses complex jaw misalignments beyond the scope of braces alone. This surgery is ideal for severe bite discrepancies and structural issues, improving oral function and facial aesthetics.

Our approach involves a thorough evaluation and personalized treatment plan. We collaborate with skilled oral surgeons to address severe jaw misalignments, overbites, underbites, and facial asymmetry. This combined effort ensures optimal results, harmonizing orthodontic treatment with surgical expertise for a balanced, functional, and beautiful outcome.
