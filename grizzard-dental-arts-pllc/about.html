About Grizzard Dental Arts

Comprehensive dentistry with a hometown feel.

Why Grizzard Dental Arts?

Because excellent dentistry requires both clinical expertise and a sculptor’s talent. Dr. Grizzard has built his career on mastering these skill sets, enabling him to provide dental care of the highest standards — all delivered with a comfortable and easy chairside manner.

Small Team, Big Impact

Grizzard Dental Arts’ value is in the patient-centered approach provided by a dental team that truly cares not only about the patient’s dental health outcomes, but what’s going on in a patient’s life.

Dr. Grizzard enjoys being each patient’s dedicated dentist, and our hygienists’ attention to detail carries through in their care and concern for each patient.

Philosophy

Dr. Grizzard’s approach is to keep natural teeth as healthy as possible; the less work he does on a natural tooth, the better it is for the patient. However, if a tooth is no longer healthy and requires a restoration (filling), endodontics (root canal), extraction, implant, crown, bridge, or prosthodontic (denture), he will provide the patient with their options and educate the patient on the risks and benefits of each option.
