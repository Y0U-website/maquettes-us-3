An occlusal guard, also known as a night guard or bite guard, is a custom-made oral appliance worn while you sleep. It prevents damage caused by clenching or grinding (bruxism) and helps reduce jaw tension and related discomfort. Common signs you may need a night guard include: Waking up with jaw pain or tightness
  • Frequent headaches or facial soreness
  • Worn-down or flattened teeth
  • Cracked or chipped enamel
  • Clicking or popping in the jaw joint

What Is a Sports Mouth Guard?

A sports guard is a custom-fitted appliance worn during contact or high-impact sports to protect the teeth, gums, and jaw from injury. Unlike bulky store-bought guards, professional sports guards from Goshen Smiles offer a precise fit and improved comfort.

Sports guards are recommended for athletes of all ages involved in:

Proper protection can help prevent tooth loss, soft tissue damage, and jaw fractures. For children and teens with orthodontic appliances like Invisalign or retainers, we provide specialized guards to accommodate their dental needs.

  • Football
  • Basketball
  • Soccer
  • Wrestling
  • Martial arts
  • Skateboarding or BMX
  • Any contact or risk-prone activity

Frequently Asked Questions About Occlusal & Sports Guards

How long do night guards last? ‍

With proper care, custom night guards can last 3 to 5 years. We’ll check the fit during your regular exams .

Can I use a night guard from the drugstore? ‍

Over-the-counter guards often fit poorly and may not protect effectively. A custom guard offers superior comfort and coverage.

Do mouth guards prevent TMJ problems? ‍

They can help reduce strain on the jaw and may prevent TMJ-related symptoms when used early and consistently.

At what age should kids start wearing sports guards? ‍

We recommend sports guards as soon as your child begins participating in high-risk sports, typically around age 6 or 7.

Can I wear my aligners and a guard at the same time? ‍

No. If you&#x27;re in clear aligner treatment, we’ll recommend a sports guard specifically designed to fit over your trays, or delay night guard fabrication until treatment is complete.
