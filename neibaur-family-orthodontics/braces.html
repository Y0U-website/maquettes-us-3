Soreness Caused by Braces

You will probably feel some soreness and discomfort the first few days after receiving braces and after any adjustments. These sensations are normal. You can gargle with warm salt water (one teaspoon of salt in about eight ounces of water) or take a mild pain reliever to alleviate discomfort.

Physical Activity

You can participate in all your typical sports or physical activities while wearing braces, but we strongly recommend wearing a mouth guard. A mouth guard will keep your teeth and soft tissues safe during sports. Please get in touch with our orthodontist or your family dentist about receiving a custom sports mouth guard.

Dental Hygiene with Braces

During your braces treatment, continue brushing at least twice daily and floss daily. Consider using a water pick or other device to help flush food particles and debris from your mouth and between the wires of your braces.

You should also continue to visit your family dentist regularly for dental cleanings. Doing so will help keep your mouth healthy and free from decay and disease. For more information on life with braces and to schedule your consultation with our caring orthodontist, please call our practice today.
