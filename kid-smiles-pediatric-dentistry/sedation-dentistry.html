Sedation Dentistry

A few of our younger patients find that administering a sedative prior to or during treatment is helpful. The doctor might be concerned that the child is unable to sit still or follow the instructions. It is possible that the child will not tolerate normal anesthetics. These children may not be able to receive regular dental care. The right sedation is crucial for their oral health.

Dr. Michel is certified and experienced to provide light sedation with nitrous oxide.

A parent or guardian should accompany a child who is receiving moderate or deep sedation. Contact us for more information about our sedation services and to schedule a consultation concerning care for your child.
