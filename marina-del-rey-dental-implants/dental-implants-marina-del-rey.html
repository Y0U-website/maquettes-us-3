Tooth loss can be an inevitable occurrence for numerous patients. Various causes exist that can lead to this problem, including injury, disease, or natural aging.

At Dental Implants of Marina Del Rey, our expertise in dental implants can save the visual and functional aspects of your smile with the utmost quality. Dental implants are a natural and fundamental restoration for smiles.

They consist of a titanium screw which is surgically placed onto your jawbone. After the screw has successfully integrated with your bone tissue, an artificial tooth is then fabricated to complete the restoration.

The procedure starts with an impression that is made from the structure of your smile. This process is important in determining the correct spacing and shape for the dental implants.

Experience is key to success in your dental implant procedure.

Our Surgeons Have Placed Thousands of Implants

Once the screws have been created, they are inserted onto your jawbone using the most efficient and precise surgical techniques. It may require up to six months for the screws to be firmly integrated in your jawbone.

Once this process has concluded, the prosthetic tooth (crown) is designed. The crown can be polished and shaped to match the natural features of your teeth.

It is then bonded onto the surface of the dental implant and optionally supported by additional abutments. After the crown is secured on the dental implant, your smile is fundamentally restored in beauty, health, and function!
