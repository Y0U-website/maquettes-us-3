Ken Cory, DMD

Dr. Cory was raised in Las Vegas, Nevada, and graduated from the UNLV School of Dental Medicine earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine. After dental school, Dr. Cory lived in Oregon working with underserved patients. Dr. Cory has lived in Montrose, CO since 2008. Dr. Cory is a member of the American Dental Association, Colorado Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry.

In his free time, Dr. Cory enjoys the outdoors and spending time with his sons and his two labs. He is an avid skier, fisherman and loves exploring the local mountains and trails on his adventure motorcycle.

Dr. Cory is committed to continuing education and averages 100 hours a year of advanced training in implants, dentures, same day crowns, cosmetics, six-month braces and many other areas of dentistry. Dr. Cory is skilled in dental implant placement and is a member of the International College of Implantologists which advances the study of dental implant surgery and restoration.

Dr. Cory’s main goal is for you to have the smile you deserve. Dr. Cory takes great pride in his work and will always treat you like family.
