What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The All-on-4 Dental Implants procedure involves securing a complete arch of replacement teeth using just four strategically placed implants. This technique eliminates the need for individual implants for each tooth, reducing surgery time and promoting faster recovery.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants:

  • Immediate Results : Enjoy a fully restored smile after a single procedure.
  • Enhanced Stability : The implants provide a secure foundation for your new teeth.
  • Bone Preservation : Prevents bone loss commonly associated with missing teeth.
  • Cost-Effective : Achieve full restoration with fewer implants.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

What are All-on-4 Dental Implants? All-on-4 Dental Implants provide a full-arch restoration using just four strategically placed implants. This innovative technique securely supports a complete set of replacement teeth, offering a durable and natural-looking solution.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take? The All-on-4 procedure typically allows for same-day restoration, meaning you can leave with a fully functional set of temporary teeth immediately after implant placement. Permanent teeth are placed after the healing period.

Who is a good candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants? This procedure is ideal for patients missing most or all teeth in an arch, seeking a permanent alternative to dentures, and having sufficient jawbone density to support the implants.

What are the benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants? All-on-4 implants offer immediate results, enhanced stability, prevention of bone loss, and a cost-effective solution for full-arch restoration compared to individual implants for each tooth.
