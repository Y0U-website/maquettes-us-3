Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants means investing in your health, your appearance, and your quality of life. Here’s what makes them so valuable.

Natural Look and Feel

Modern dental implants are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. The crown is carefully crafted to match the size, shape, and shade of your surrounding teeth, giving you a seamless smile. Because they’re anchored directly into your jawbone, implants feel stable and secure—you’ll regain the confidence to laugh, smile, and eat without hesitation.

Long-Term Durability

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post integrates with your jawbone, creating a foundation that’s as strong as a natural tooth root. While the crown may eventually need replacement after many years of use, the implant itself is designed for permanence. This makes implants one of the most cost-effective long-term solutions available.

Preservation of Jawbone and Facial Structure

When you lose a tooth, the bone that once supported it begins to deteriorate—a process that can lead to a sunken facial appearance and shifting of surrounding teeth. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone just like natural tooth roots do, preventing bone loss and maintaining the structural integrity of your face. This not only preserves your appearance but also protects your remaining teeth from misalignment.

Types of Dental Implants We Offer

At Living Art Dentistry, we tailor our implant treatments to your specific situation. Here are the main types we provide:

Single Tooth Implant

If you’re missing just one tooth, a single implant is the ideal solution. We place one titanium post and top it with a custom crown, giving you a replacement that looks, feels, and functions exactly like your natural tooth—without affecting the neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Missing two or more adjacent teeth? An implant-supported bridge uses two or more implants to anchor a series of connected crowns. This approach provides exceptional stability without requiring us to alter healthy teeth, as traditional bridges do.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

For patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, full mouth dental implants offer complete restoration. We strategically place multiple implants throughout your upper and lower arches to support a full set of permanent teeth. The result is a fully functional, beautiful smile that doesn’t require removal or special maintenance.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 is an innovative technique that uses just four implants per arch to support a full set of teeth. By angling the implants strategically, we can often avoid the need for bone grafting, making this an efficient and cost-effective option for full-arch restoration. Many patients appreciate the faster treatment timeline and reduced surgical complexity.

Our Dental Implant Procedure in Brooklyn

We believe in transparency. Here’s exactly what you can expect when you choose Living Art Dentistry for your dental implants.

1. Initial Consultation and 3D Imaging.

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation. We’ll examine your oral health, discuss your goals, and use advanced 3D imaging technology to assess your jawbone density and structure. This allows us to plan your treatment with precision and determine if any preparatory work—such as bone grafting—is needed.

2. Implant Placement Surgery

Once your treatment plan is finalized, we’ll schedule your implant placement. During this procedure, we carefully insert the titanium posts into your jawbone. We offer multiple sedation options to ensure your comfort, and most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. The surgery is typically completed in one visit, though complex cases may require additional appointments.

3. Healing and Osseointegration

After placement, your implants need time to integrate with your jawbone. This healing period, called osseointegration, usually takes three to six months. During this time, the bone grows around the implant, creating a stable foundation. We may provide you with temporary teeth so you can maintain your smile and function while you heal.

4. Abutment and Crown Placement

Once your implants have fully integrated, we’ll attach the abutments and place your custom-made crowns. We take precise impressions to ensure your new teeth fit perfectly and match your natural bite. After a final fitting and adjustment, you’ll walk out with a complete, functional smile. Throughout the entire process, our team provides thorough aftercare instructions and remains available to answer questions and address any concerns. Your comfort and successful outcome are our top priorities.
