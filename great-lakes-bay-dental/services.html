Restorative Dentistry ▼

At Great Lakes Bay Dental, we understand the importance of a healthy and functional smile. Dr. Daniel Clement, our skilled dentist, is dedicated to providing comprehensive restorative dentistry services that help restore the beauty, strength, and functionality of your teeth. Whether you’re dealing with a missing tooth, tooth decay, or other dental concerns, we offer a range of effective solutions tailored to meet your specific needs.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are an excellent option for replacing one or more missing teeth. These custom-made prosthetic devices consist of artificial teeth that are anchored to neighboring teeth or dental implants, creating a seamless restoration that fills the gap and restores your ability to bite and chew properly.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns, also known as caps, are versatile restorations used to repair severely damaged, decayed, or weakened teeth. Crafted from high-quality materials, such as porcelain or ceramic, dental crowns restore the shape, size, strength, and appearance of your teeth while ensuring long-lasting durability.

Dentures

Dentures are removable prosthetic devices that replace missing teeth and restore your ability to speak and chew comfortably. Whether you need full dentures to replace all of your teeth or partial dentures to fill in gaps, Dr. Clement will work closely with you to create custom dentures that fit securely and look natural.

Implant-Retained Crowns

Dental implants provide a stable and permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. Dr. Clement offers implant-retained crowns, where dental implants are surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. These implants provide a strong foundation for the attachment of custom-crafted dental crowns, resulting in a functional and aesthetically pleasing restoration.

Root Canal Therapy

If you’re experiencing severe tooth pain or have an infected tooth, root canal therapy can alleviate your discomfort and save your natural tooth from extraction. Dr. Clement uses advanced techniques and modern technology to perform precise and comfortable root canal treatments, removing infected pulp and restoring the tooth’s health.

Advanced Dentistry ▼

We prioritize your comfort and strive to provide a relaxed and stress-free environment, especially when it comes to advanced dentistry procedures. We understand that some patients may experience anxiety or fear when undergoing complex treatments. To ensure your peace of mind and to help you feel at ease, we offer the following options:

Nitrous Oxide Sedation

Nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas, is a safe and effective sedative option that helps you relax during dental procedures. Administered through a comfortable mask placed over your nose, nitrous oxide induces a gentle euphoric feeling while keeping you conscious and able to communicate with our dental team. The effects wear off quickly, allowing you to resume your daily activities following the appointment.

Night Guards

If you suffer from teeth grinding or clenching, also known as bruxism, we can provide custom-made night guards to protect your teeth from excessive wear and prevent associated complications. Night guards are worn while sleeping and create a barrier between your upper and lower teeth, reducing the negative effects of bruxism and promoting better oral health.

TMJ Therapy

Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders can cause jaw pain, headaches, and difficulty with jaw movement. Dr. Clement is experienced in diagnosing and treating TMJ issues. Through a comprehensive evaluation, he will develop a personalized treatment plan that may include therapies, exercises, lifestyle modifications, and other techniques to alleviate your TMJ symptoms and improve your overall jaw function.

Periodontal Dentistry ▼

We understand the significance of periodontal health in maintaining overall oral well-being. Dr. Daniel Clement, our experienced dentist, specializes in periodontal dentistry and offers a range of services aimed at treating and managing periodontal disease, a progressive condition that affects the gums and supporting structures of the teeth.

Periodontal Maintenance

Regular periodontal maintenance is essential for individuals diagnosed with periodontal disease. Our skilled hygienists perform thorough cleanings to remove plaque and tartar that have accumulated above and below the gumline. This crucial step helps prevent the progression of the disease and promotes gum health.

Scaling and Root Planing

Also known as deep cleaning, scaling and root planing is a non-surgical procedure designed to treat the early stages of periodontal disease. During this meticulous treatment, our dental team removes plaque, tartar, and bacterial toxins from the root surfaces of the teeth and smoothens them to discourage further plaque buildup. This process aids in reducing gum inflammation, preventing bone loss, and promoting the healing of gum tissues.

At Great Lakes Bay Dental, we take a comprehensive approach to periodontal dentistry. We emphasize the importance of early diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and patient education to effectively manage and control periodontal disease. Our team is dedicated to providing a comfortable and supportive environment for patients undergoing periodontal treatments.

We believe that prevention is the key to maintaining optimal periodontal health. Dr. Clement and our dental professionals will work closely with you to develop a personalized maintenance plan that includes regular check-ups, professional cleanings, and guidance on at-home oral care practices. By partnering with us, you can take proactive steps towards preserving the health and integrity of your gums and teeth.

If you are experiencing symptoms such as gum inflammation, bleeding, or persistent bad breath, it is important to seek professional care. Contact Great Lakes Bay Dental today to schedule an appointment with Dr. Clement and learn more about our effective periodontal dentistry services.
