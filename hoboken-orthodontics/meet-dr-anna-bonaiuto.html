Meet Dr. Anna Bonaiuto | Orthodontist in Hoboken

MEET DR. ANNA BONAIUTO

Dr. Bonaiuto is a board-certified orthodontist who’s intensely committed to high standards of care and always striving for the ultimate aesthetic and functional result. She likes to keep appointments light and fun, and prides herself in establishing a natural rapport with patients, who she views as her extended family.

EDUCATION

Dr. Bonaiuto attended Fordham University where she graduated summa cum laude. She then went on to earn her Doctorate of Dental Medicine with honors from UMDNJ, now Rutgers Dental School, where she completed her residency in orthodontics.

Outside the office

Born and raised in Brooklyn, Dr. Bonaiuto currently lives in Hudson County with her husband, Arthur, daughter Maddalena, son Louis, and their dog, Bear. In her time out of the office, she values time spent with family and enjoying the waterfront. Her passions include music, food, and definitely not working out.

Hoboken Ortho is a leading orthopedic practice dedicated to providing top-notch care and treatment for musculoskeletal conditions in Hoboken and the surrounding areas. With a team of highly skilled and experienced orthopedic surgeons, physicians, and physical therapists, Hoboken Ortho is committed to helping patients recover from injuries, manage chronic conditions, and improve their overall quality of life. The practice specializes in a wide range of orthopedic services, including sports medicine, joint replacement, spine surgery, hand and wrist surgery, and pediatric orthopedics.

Whether you’re an athlete dealing with a sports-related injury or someone suffering from arthritis, Hoboken Ortho offers personalized treatment plans tailored to meet your specific needs. The practice is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including advanced diagnostic imaging and minimally invasive surgical techniques, ensuring that patients receive the most effective and cutting-edge care available.

In addition to surgical interventions, Hoboken Ortho places a strong emphasis on non-surgical treatments and rehabilitation. Their team of physical therapists works closely with patients to develop customized rehabilitation programs designed to restore mobility, strength, and function. Hoboken Ortho also offers educational resources and support to help patients understand their conditions and make informed decisions about their care.

With a patient-centered approach, Hoboken Ortho strives to create a comfortable and compassionate environment where patients feel supported throughout their treatment journey. Whether you need preventive care, surgical intervention, or rehabilitation, Hoboken Ortho is dedicated to helping you achieve optimal orthopedic health and return to your active lifestyle.
