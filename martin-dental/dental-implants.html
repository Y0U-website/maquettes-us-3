DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants are becoming a more common treatment option for many patients missing one, multiple, or all of their teeth. Dental implants are the standard of care in tooth replacement. The good news is that not every tooth needs to be replaced with a dental implant. It could take as few as four implants to support a full row of teeth.

There are multiple types of Dental Implants...

Replace a Single Tooth: One dental implant is used to replace the root of the tooth and a crown is then attached to the top to simulate the tooth.

Replace Multiple Teeth: The technique for replacing multiple teeth in a row is to use multiple dental implants, which are then used as anchors for a multiple tooth bridge.

Replace All Teeth: Dental implants can be used one of two ways for full tooth replacement. Dental implants can be used as anchors for a full set of dentures or can replace an arch of upper or lower teeth that are permanent and fixed.

Dental implants can last decades and are a safe, reliable method for tooth replacement. Most patients experience very little discomfort after the procedure, report being pleased with the results, and enjoy new confidence that a full, beautiful smile gives them!
