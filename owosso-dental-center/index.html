The practice

Dr. Sue Dinh is originally from Houston, Texas. She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Houston before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in 2018. She took over Owosso Dental Center practice in January 2022 from Dr. Timothy Glowniak when he was retiring.

Dr. Dinh is married to her husband Sean, and they welcomed their daughter in July 2022 and son in February 2025. The family has called Owosso home since June 2022. Dr. Dinh is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care to patients of all ages in a warm and welcoming environment.
