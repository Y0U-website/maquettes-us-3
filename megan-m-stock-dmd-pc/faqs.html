Frequently Asked Questions

Although this page may help answer some of the most frequently asked questions, we encourage you to call the office with any questions that you may have.

I am very fearful of the dentist. What can you do to help me?

If you have the freedom to choose your provider through your insurance (for example, PPO plans), we will gladly submit claims on your behalf to maximize your insurance benefits as an out-of-network provider. We offer a number of flexible financing options and discounts. We accept cash, check and all major credit cards. You are encouraged to contact our financial coordinator to discuss your specific case.

Every effort is made to see a patient experiencing pain on the same day that they call while still respecting our regularly scheduled patients. If you experience a dental emergency during non office hours, please call the office at (724) 935-0160 and the recording will provide you with a number at which to reach Dr. Stock. Once you speak with Dr. Stock, if it is determined that you need treatment immediately, she will gladly meet you at the office.
