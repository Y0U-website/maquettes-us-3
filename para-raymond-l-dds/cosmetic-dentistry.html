Teeth Whitening

Whitening procedures offer a way to change the color of your teeth, brightening your smile. If your teeth and gums are healthy, various whitening methods can remove deep stain from the tooth structure that has built up over time. These procedures have been thoroughly researched over many years and have proven to be safe and effective with no long-term negative effects on the health of your teeth or gums. After thorough examination, we can determine which whitening procedure would be best for you.

Bonding

Bonding refers to the use of tooth colored resin material to correct esthetic or functional problems with front teeth. Many problems can be corrected with bonding, including:

  • Dark or stained teeth
  • Chipped or fractured teeth
  • Misaligned teeth
  • Gaps between teeth

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers may be used to transform the color, shape and alignment of your teeth. The front surfaces of the teeth to be treated are minimally reduced and a thin veneer of porcelain is bonded to the prepared surface. The result is a beautiful, long lasting smile. Our office has been improving smiles with veneers for more than 25 years. Schedule a consultation visit to determine if veneers are right for your smile.
