Meet Dr. Stephen Clark

Family Dentist in Landover Hills, MD

We’re delighted to introduce Dr. Stephen Clark, a dedicated dentist with a passion for improving the health and well-being of his patients. A New England native, Dr. Clark brings a warm, caring approach to dentistry, making sure each patient feels comfortable and well-cared for.

Dr. Clark earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Hampton University before continuing his education at Howard University College of Dentistry, where he honed his skills and knowledge to become a top-tier dental professional. His commitment to dentistry stems from a deep desire to improve people’s health and quality of life. He believes that the mouth is not only a vital part of the body but also a gateway to overall health. By focusing on the health of the oral cavity, Dr. Clark knows that he can help patients feel better, live better, and function more effectively in their daily lives.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Clark enjoys staying active and engaged with life. A fan of sports, travel, and trying new foods, he enjoys broadening his horizons. He also loves to read, often diving into books that expand his knowledge and inspire his practice.

Dr. Clark’s goal is to make every visit a positive experience, offering exceptional care with a personal touch. Whether you’re here for a routine check-up or a more advanced procedure , you can trust that Dr. Clark and the team at Landover Hills Dental Care are committed to helping you achieve your healthiest smile and feel your best.
