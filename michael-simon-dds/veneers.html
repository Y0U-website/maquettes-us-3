Veneers

We Like Bringing Smiles Back to Faces

Do you hide your smile because of broken, discolored, or gapped teeth? Would you like to have a celebrity’s “million-dollar smile”, but without painful procedures? If so, you may be a perfect candidate for Veneers — thin, custom-made shells of ceramic porcelain material that are bonded to the front of your teeth. They match the strength and color of your existing tooth enamel for seamless fit and function.

Veneers can restore your smile and solve several cosmetic issues simultaneously, while drastically improving the appearance of your smile in a natural way. They are long-lasting, and require only the same home care you would give your natural teeth. With veneers, bringing the smile back to your face could not be easier.
