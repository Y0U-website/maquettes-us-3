The practice

At Niko Niko Dental, we offer a full range of dental services to keep your smile healthy and confident. From routine cleanings and checkups to cosmetic enhancements like veneers and teeth whitening, we provide care tailored to your needs. We also offer implant restorations, partials and dentures, root canals, extractions, and emergency dental visits.

As we continue to grow, we’re incorporating advanced technology like lasers and digital scanners to enhance your experience. Whether you’re here for preventive care or specialized treatment, our goal is to provide exceptional dentistry in a welcoming, comfortable environment.
