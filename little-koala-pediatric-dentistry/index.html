The practice

Why should you choose a pediatric dentist for your child? Learn more about our office and why we are different.

Dental emergencies happen. Thankfully, the team at Little Koala Pediatric Dentistry is ready to help your child when a crisis strikes.

Our dedicated and compassionate team cares for children from Union City, CA, and the surrounding communities of Fremont, Newark (the Tri-City area), Hayward, Castro Valley, and San Lorenzo.

From cleanings and exams to special needs dentistry, fluoride treatments, silver diamine fluoride (SDF) treatment, and tooth-colored crowns on baby teeth, we care for your child’s every dental need under one roof!

Our mission is to create healthy and happy smiles one child at a time.

To help our patients have the most pleasant experience, we offer sedation dentistry using nitrous oxide (laughing gas), in-office IV sedation, and general anesthesia.

At Little Koala Pediatric Dentistry, we provide specialized quality care for children and adolescents in a “child-friendly” environment. As pediatric dentists, we focus on preventive care to help each child have a healthy smile that will last a lifetime. Our office has been serving infants, children, teens, and children with special health care needs in the community for almost four decades.

Dr. Lee is excited to share her passion as a pediatric dentist in this office. She is known for her patience and compassion. Dr. Lee is dedicated to providing quality care.
