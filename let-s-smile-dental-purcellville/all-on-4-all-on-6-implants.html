All-on-6 Dental Implants:

All-on-6 dental implants are a variation of the All-on-4 concept, providing additional support and stability with the use of six dental implants. This technique is often recommended for individuals who may have a bit more bone loss or require extra support for the prosthetic arch. Here are some key aspects of All-on-6 dental implants:

  • Six dental implants: As the name suggests, six dental implants are used to support the prosthetic arch. The additional implants distribute the chewing forces more evenly and offer greater stability.
  • Versatility: All-on-6 implants can accommodate various prosthesis designs, including fixed or removable dentures, based on the patient’s specific needs and preferences.
  • Improved bone preservation: The extra implants help in preserving and stimulating the bone in the jaw, which can be beneficial for overall oral health and facial aesthetics.
  • Enhanced support for biting and chewing: All-on-6 implants offer even more robust support, enabling patients to bite and chew with greater confidence and strength.
