Common Questions about General Dentistry What is general dentistry?

General dentistry encompasses a wide range of dental services aimed at maintaining, improving, and restoring oral health. It includes routine checkups, cleanings, fillings, preventive care, and other essential treatments that help prevent dental issues and address any that arise. By focusing on the overall well-being of your teeth and gums, general dentistry supports a healthy smile and lays the foundation for long-term oral health.

  • Preventative Dental Care - Depending on what we find during your exam, we may recommend one or more preventive treatments. Fluoride treatments and varnishes applied directly to your teeth will help strengthen the enamel and ward off cavities. Tooth sealants are thin plastic coatings applied to the biting surfaces of certain teeth to form a barrier against bacteria that can cause cavities and disease.
  • Gum Disease Treatment - If your gums are suffering, we can perform a “deep cleaning” treatment using scaling and root planing. We’ll remove bacteria from around and below the gumline, then smooth the tooth roots to help keep bacteria from adhering to them. For severe cases of gum disease, we may also use antibiotics to restore your gum health.
  • Custom Athletic Mouthguards - Did you know that athletes who don’t wear mouth protection are 60 percent more likely to suffer tooth and mouth injury than athletes who do wear protection? We can make you or your child a custom-fit mouthguard that will provide superior performance and longevity over store-bought mouthguards.
  • Dry Mouth Treatment - After an evaluation to help determine the cause of your dry mouth, we may recommend a product from the CariFree line of specialty oral care products. These products help with both dry mouth symptoms and cavity prevention.

  • How often should I get a cleaning? To keep your smile looking its best, come in for a routine checkup twice a year.
  • Why should I go to a general dentist? General dentistry focuses on preventive care designed to keep your smile as healthy as possible by stopping issues like cavities and gum disease. So if you want your smile to look and feel good, general dentistry is essential. Regular exams and cleanings are the cornerstone of preventive care.
