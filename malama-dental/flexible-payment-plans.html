Flexible Payment Plans for Your Dental Care

At Malama Dental, we believe everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care without financial stress. That’s why we offer flexible payment options and financing plans to help you receive the treatment you need when you need it. Whether it’s preventive care, cosmetic treatments, or restorative procedures, our goal is to make your dental care affordable and convenient.

We understand that every patient’s financial situation is different. Our friendly team will work closely with you to find a payment solution that fits your budget, so you never have to delay important dental treatments.

Cherry Financing

At Malama Dental, we offer Cherry financing to make dental care more affordable and stress-free. Cherry allows you to split the cost of your treatment into easy monthly payments, so you can get the care you need without worrying about large upfront costs.

Whether you need preventive, cosmetic, or restorative dental treatments, Cherry provides flexible payment options, including no-interest plans for qualified patients. The application process is quick and simple, and our Malama Dental team is here to help you every step of the way.

CareCredit® Financing

At Malama Dental, we proudly accept CareCredit® to help make your dental treatments more affordable. CareCredit® is a healthcare credit card that allows you to pay for your dental care with convenient monthly payments, so you don’t have to delay important treatments.

Whether you need preventive, cosmetic, or restorative dental services, CareCredit® offers flexible financing options, including promotional interest-free plans for qualified patients. The application process is quick and simple, and our Malama Dental team is always here to guide you and help you receive the care you need without financial stress.

Whether you’re planning cosmetic, restorative, or advanced dental treatments, Proceed Finance gives you the freedom to move forward without financial stress. The application process is simple and transparent, and our Malama Dental team is here to help you choose the financing option that best fits your needs.
