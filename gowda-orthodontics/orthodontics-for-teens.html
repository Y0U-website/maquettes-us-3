Treatment options for teens

We’ll recommend the option that fits your teen’s goals, lifestyle, and clinical needs.

  • Metal braces — small, smooth brackets designed for comfort and control.
  • Clear braces — a more discreet ceramic option that blends with teeth.
  • Clear aligners — nearly invisible trays (e.g., Invisalign®, Angel Aligners, Spark) for flexible, removable treatment.

How the process works

  • Consultation & digital records. Easy photos, digital scan (no goopy impressions), and necessary X-rays.
  • Personalized plan. We review options, timelines, and costs. No pressure.
  • Start confidently. Our team fits braces or delivers aligners, then teaches care, hygiene, and what to expect.
  • Regular check-ins. Short visits or virtual touchpoints to keep things on track.
  • Smile hold. Retainers protect the result: simple, proven, and teen-friendly.

Teen-friendly technology & comfort

Comfort matters. We use modern imaging and digital scanning for precise planning and a better fit. 3D scans and advanced imaging are used when they add real value to diagnosis or treatment. In the office, ergonomic chairs, an easy check-in, and a welcoming lounge help every visit feel low-stress.

Life with braces or aligners

  • Sports & band: Mouthguards keep athletes safe; braces and aligners are both instrument-friendly.
  • Food & habits: With braces, avoid very hard/sticky foods. With aligners, remove to eat, then brush and pop them back in.
  • Hygiene: Daily brushing/flossing + simple tools (floss threaders, water flossers) keep gums healthy and breath fresh.
  • Photos & events: Clear options are camera-ready; braces can be subtle or colorful with elastics for school spirit.

Costs, insurance, and flexible payments

FAQs: Orthodontics for Teens

How long does teen orthodontic treatment take?

Most comprehensive teen treatments run 12–24 months, depending on goals, growth, and how consistently the plan is followed.

Braces or aligners? What’s better for my teen?

Both work well. Braces are always “on,” which helps with consistency. Aligners are nearly invisible and removable, but require wearing them 20–22 hours/day.

Will it hurt?

You may feel pressure or tenderness for a few days after getting braces/aligners or after adjustments. Over-the-counter pain relief and a soft diet help.

Can my teen play sports or a wind instrument?

Yes. A sports mouthguard protects smiles during contact sports. Musicians adapt quickly; aligners can be removed for practice if needed.

What foods are off-limits with braces?

Avoid hard, sticky, or very chewy foods (ice, hard candy, taffy, popcorn kernels). With aligners, remove them to eat, brush, and reinsert.

How often are appointments?

Typically every 6–10 weeks for braces. For aligners, visits may be spaced out and sometimes combined with virtual check-ins.

What if an aligner is lost or a bracket breaks?

Call us. We’ll advise the next steps and schedule a quick fix or order a replacement tray to keep treatment on track.

Are retainers really necessary?

Yes. Teeth naturally drift over time. Retainers protect your teen’s investment and keep the smile looking great.
