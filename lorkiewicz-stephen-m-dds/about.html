Get to Know your Dentist, Dr. Ryan Harrington Ryan P Harrington DMD…

In 2016, Dr. Harrington and his wife, Rachel, moved back to Massachusetts to be closer to their families. Upon returning to MA, he worked as an associate dentist for five years at Innovate Dental in Leicester (formerly known as Leicester Dental Associates). He has a high regard for the dental practice that Dr. Lorkiewicz has built, and is excited to continue in Dr. Lorkiewicz’s tradition of providing excellent family dentistry to patients in the Webster area.

Dr. Harrington is a member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society and the Worcester District Dental Society. He takes continuing education very seriously and has participated in many courses to stay up to date on modern dentistry. He prides himself on offering a wide range of dental services to his patients including endodontics (root canal therapy), surgical placement and restoration of dental implants, as well as SureSmile clear aligner orthodontic treatment.

On a personal note, the Harrington family welcomed their first child, Lukas, on Christmas morning, 2020. Julian, Luke’s golden retriever older brother, has been very patient with his new little brother. This young family is excited to immerse themselves into the community of Webster, where they hope to remain for a very long time.
