Catherine Zybak, DMD

Dr. Catherine M. Zybak graduated First Honor graduate in her class from the Medical University of South Carolina College of Dental Medicine in 1989. Her academic achievements include awards in Operative Dentistry, Periodontics, Prosthodontics, and Oral Surgery. She was also elected to a national Dental Honor Society. Dr. Zybak spent some time in a General Practice Residency at the Veteran’s Administration Hospital in Omaha, Nebraska. She has been practicing in Sumter since 1998.

Dr. Zybak is married to James Bowman; they reside in Sumter with their son Jack and twins Cat and Maggie. They also have three dogs and a cat.

Dr. Zybak is a member of the American Dental Association and a firm believer in dentists’ continuing education. She is a caring and gentle practitioner dedicated to quality dentistry and the maintenance of oral health. Dentistry is her passion, and her patient’s satisfaction is her primary goal.
