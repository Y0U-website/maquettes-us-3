What Is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontics is a specialized field in dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting issues with tooth and jaw alignment. Properly aligned teeth not only look better but also function more efficiently. Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease, while a balanced bite helps prevent uneven tooth wear and jaw joint stress.

At Glow Dental, all orthodontic cases are personally treated or overseen by our board-certified orthodontist, ensuring you receive the highest standard of specialized care. We believe orthodontics extends far beyond aesthetics – it’s an essential part of maintaining lifelong oral health.

Orthodontic Treatment for All Ages and Stages of Life

Orthodontics for Children (Early Treatment / Phase I Orthodontics)

The American Association of Orthodontics (AAO) recommends that children receive their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7, even if their teeth appear straight. At this stage, our orthodontist can assess the jaw growth and development of permanent teeth to determine whether early (Phase I) treatment is beneficial.

Phase I orthodontics is a proactive approach intended to guide growth and tooth alignment, often preventing more complex problems later in life. Treatment may include braces or appliances such as space maintainers, palatal expanders, or habit correctors.

Benefits of early orthodontic treatment:

  • Creates adequate space for incoming permanent teeth
  • Guides proper jaw growth and bite alignment
  • Reduces the risk of injury to protruding teeth
  • Decreases the need for permanent tooth extractions later in life
  • Addresses harmful habits, such as thumb-sucking or tongue thrusting
  • Simplifies and shortens future orthodontic treatment

Signs your child may need an early orthodontic evaluation:

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Mouth breathing or snoring
  • Jaw shifting or asymmetry when closing
  • Thumb or finger sucking
  • Crowded, blocked, or misaligned teeth
  • Teeth that do not meet properly (open bite or crossbite)

Orthodontics for Teens and Adolescents (Phase II Treatment)

The teen years are the most common time to begin comprehensive orthodontic treatment, as most permanent teeth have erupted and the jawbone is still developing. We offer teen-friendly options, including metal braces, clear ceramic braces, and Invisalign clear aligners. Whether your teen is starting orthodontic care for the first time or continuing from Phase I, we will design a personalized plan that delivers lasting, beautiful results.

  • Align teeth for a confident smile during formative years
  • Correct bite issues such as overbites, underbites, and crossbites
  • Establish a strong foundation for lifelong oral health

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

The length of orthodontic treatment varies for every patient and depends on several factors, including age, case complexity, and patient cooperation. On average, most treatments take 12 to 36 months to complete.

Factors that can affect treatment time include:

  • Severity of Misalignment : The amount of correction needed plays a major role in determining overall treatment length. Patients with minor crowding or spacing may finish treatment in about a year, while those with more complex alignment issues or surgical orthodontic cases may require two to three years.
  • Patient Age & Biology : Younger patients may see slightly faster movement, while adults benefit from precise, predictable results with today’s advanced technology. Modern braces and aligners make achieving a beautiful, healthy smile easier than ever at any stage of life.
  • Patient Cooperation – Carefully following your orthodontist’s instructions – such as wearing elastics or aligners as prescribed and maintaining excellent oral hygiene can significantly shorten treatment time and ensure the best possible outcome.
