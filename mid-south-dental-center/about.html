What is the Mid-South Mission of Mercy?

History

Since 2016, we have provided over 14 million dollars worth of dentistry to more than 16,000 patients in the greater Memphis area with the help of more than 16,000 volunteers.

The America’s Dentist Care Foundation (ADCF) was founded in 2008 to provide dental equipment, technical support, and clinic expertise to groups that hold Mission of Mercy (MOM) dental clinics. The first MOM project was held in 2000 with the goal of providing free dental care to individuals who are under served; due to being uninsured, under-insured, or simply having limited access to dental care. All MOM projects provide care to both adults and children, on a first come, first serve basis.

While the ADCF has been helping states organize MOM events for years, the first MidMOM event took place in 2016.
