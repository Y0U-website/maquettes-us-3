Adult Orthodontic Treatment at Gold River Orthodontics

Growing Popularity Among Adults

Orthodontic treatment is no longer just for teens. A growing number of adults are choosing to undergo orthodontic procedures, driven by advances in technology that offer discreet and efficient solutions. At Gold River Orthodontics, we understand the unique needs of adult patients who desire a better smile without disrupting their professional lives.

Discreet Treatment Options

To accommodate the aesthetic concerns of our adult patients, we offer several advanced orthodontic options:

Clear Braces:

These feature ceramic brackets that blend in with the color of your teeth, making them far less noticeable than traditional metal braces.

Invisalign®

A popular choice for adults, these clear aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed for eating, brushing, and special occasions.

Surgical Orthodontics

Some adult orthodontic cases involve complex jaw misalignment that cannot be corrected with braces or aligners alone. In these cases, surgical orthodontics may be recommended. This treatment combines orthodontic care with jaw surgery (orthognathic surgery) to improve bite alignment, facial balance, and overall oral function. Dr. Amanda Chen works closely with oral surgeons to ensure comprehensive and coordinated care for patients requiring surgical intervention.

Benefits of Adult Orthodontic Treatment

Choosing to undergo orthodontic treatment as an adult can have numerous benefits:

Improved Oral Health

Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean and less likely to suffer from tooth decay and gum disease.

Enhanced Self-Esteem and Confidence

A straight, attractive smile can significantly boost your confidence, impacting personal and professional relationships.

Customized Treatment Plans

Recognizing that adults have different schedules and responsibilities than younger patients, we tailor each treatment plan to fit seamlessly into your lifestyle. During your initial consultation, we use advanced imaging techniques to assess your dental structure and discuss your aesthetic goals. This allows us to create a personalized treatment plan that aligns with your personal and professional life, ensuring you receive the most effective and convenient treatment.

Correcting Past Issues

Whether you did not receive orthodontic treatment as a child or your teeth have shifted over time, adult orthodontics can address crowding, gaps, and misalignment.
