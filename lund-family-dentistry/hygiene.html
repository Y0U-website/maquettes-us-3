Hygiene at Lund Family Dentistry

We personalize each visit to your oral health needs, making sure you feel informed, empowered, and cared for.

  • Detect dental issues early—before they become serious
  • Maintain fresh breath and a brighter smile
  • Support your overall health, including heart and immune health
  • Keep dental care affordable by reducing the need for complex procedures

What to Expect During Your Cleaning Appointment

  • Gentle Scaling: Removal of plaque and hardened tartar using specialized instruments
  • Teeth Polishing: Elimination of surface stains and smoothing tooth surfaces
  • Fluoride Treatment (if needed): Helps strengthen enamel and protect against decay
  • Comprehensive Exam: Your dentist checks for cavities, gum health, oral cancer, and signs of wear or damage
  • Tips for Home Care: Customized advice to improve brushing, flossing, and diet habits

Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend cleanings every six months. However, if you have gum disease or other conditions, you may need more frequent visits.

While a cleaning doesn’t bleach your teeth, it removes surface stains and plaque, often making your smile look noticeably brighter.

Most dental insurance plans cover two routine cleanings per year. We’re happy to help you check your benefits and file claims.
