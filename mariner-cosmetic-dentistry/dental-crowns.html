Dental Crowns

Restore your smile with dental crowns

A dental crown is a cap that completely covers a damaged or decayed tooth to restore its shape, size, and strength. Crowns are often used to protect weak or broken teeth, improve their appearance, and ensure proper tooth function. At Mariner Dentistry, we offer custom dental crowns made from high-quality materials like porcelain, ceramic, or metal to provide a natural, durable solution for your dental needs.

Protect and restore with dental crowns

The process of getting a dental crown typically requires two visits. First, your dentist will prepare the damaged tooth by removing any decay or old fillings, shaping the tooth, and taking an impression. The crown is then custom-made to fit your tooth perfectly. During your second visit, the crown is cemented into place, providing a strong and durable restoration that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Crowns

Dental crowns restore the strength, function, and aesthetics of your teeth, providing a long-lasting solution for damaged or weakened teeth.
