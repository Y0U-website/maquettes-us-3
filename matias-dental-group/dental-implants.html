What is the Dental Implant Surgical Procedure Like?

During your procedure, a small incision is made into the gums. The titanium post is placed into the jawbone, and the tissue is closed over the implant. You are then sent home to heal.

At home, the jaw bone integrates with the titanium post in an osseointegration process. The bone slowly grows securely around the implant, eventually making it part of your body and the most robust base for your new teeth. The healing process takes anywhere from two to six months. Some patients ask for a temporary crown until the process is complete.

When healing is done, the abutment is attached to the implant post. The new crown is fitted to the opposite end of the abutment. Once secured tightly, your brand-new tooth should instantly feel comfortable and natural. You are sent home with aftercare instructions.

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Those with missing teeth often sacrifice confidence and the ability to smile or laugh without hiding their mouth in shame. So the most significant benefit is greater enjoyment of life. But the benefits go way beyond this:

  • Improved appearance: With tooth loss comes bone loss. Without bone support, your facial structure changes shape, making you appear older and gaunt.
  • Easy care & convenience: Implants don’t require sticky glues, cleaning tablets, or strange flossers. Oral hygiene is the same as natural teeth. Just brush normally!
  • Improved oral health: Dental implants can’t get cavities. Nor do they need to be attached to neighboring teeth like bridges , improving oral health significantly.
  • Easier speaking: Removable dentures tend to slip and slide in the mouth, causing embarrassing slurring and mumbling.
  • Easier eating: Implants are so secure in your jaw that they allow you to bite with the same force as natural teeth. You can finally enjoy all the foods you love once again.
  • Bone loss prevention: Missing teeth equals bone loss in the jaw. Bones need constant stimulation from teeth to not deteriorate. Dental Implants provide jaw bone stimulation.
  • Durability: From time to time, other tooth replacement options need repairs or be replaced entirely. If properly cared for, implants can last a whole lifetime.
