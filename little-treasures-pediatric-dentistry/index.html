The practice

It’s important to us that each patient feels at home and receives one of the best care experiences while achieving and maintaining long-term health and a beautiful smile. That’s why our staff has spent years developing expertise to assist each patient. We want every visit to Little Treasures Pediatric Dentistry to be enjoyable, and we aim for each patient to leave with a brighter smile.

After several years of practicing general dentistry, I realized my true passion was treating children. But I didn’t want to just “learn on the job”, I wanted to be trained by specialists to become a specialist. So, I went back to school to receive formal training. I had the privilege of completing my postgraduate training at the prestigious Maimonides Medical Center, in New York, where I was…

I then went on to pass additional and optional exams, to become board certified and receive my status as a Diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.
