Patient Information

Our office is open Monday - Thursday from 8:00am - 5:00pm and on one Friday a month. Earlier appointments are available in special circumstances. Please call 408-842-6660 with any questions or to schedule an appointment today.

If you have pain or an emergency situation, please call the office at 408-842-6660 and we will make every attempt to see you the same day.

If this is a serious or a life-threatening emergency, call 9-1-1

Our office accepts most all insurance policies except for HMO plans where you have to choose your dentist from a list of providers. Many that we accept include Delta Denta, Cigna, and MetLife. Please call and speak to our Insurance Coordinator to ensure we accept your insurance policy. Accepted payment methods include American Express, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and CareCredit.

Please make sure to inform the office staff of any health conditions you may have as well as any changes that have occurred with your health such as diabetes, heart disease, etc... Make sure to also inform the office staff of any medications that you are currently taking or changes in your regular medications. Your correct health history is a vital component of you receiving the best treatment options possible.

Our advanced treatment options such as digital imaging, nitrus oxide, and infection control will keep you safe and comfortable, but we need your accurate health information to ensure that we are taking the correct steps in your treatment.

We like to first see children between the ages of two and three years old. Keep in mind, they should already be brushing their teeth (with Mom and Dad’s help) twice daily.

We recommend that children come in during Mom or Dad’s appointments to watch and feel comfortable with the office. We encourage the family to talk about the visit beforehand or read books about their first visit to the dentist. During their first visit, we will try and examine their teeth, clean their teeth, and possibly take X-Rays. We would like to keep their visit as positive as it can be so if they aren’t ready for more, we will stop and try and get a little farther on their next visit to ensure they will have a lifelong positive experience of visiting the dentist.

We always welcome new patients. Dr. Laveroni, and every member of his staff, is eager to help you perfect your smile, so schedule an appointment today by calling 408-842-6660 .
