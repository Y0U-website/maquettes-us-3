CEREC® Same Day Crowns

Laurel Dental Care is proud to offer this service to our patients! Receiving a CEREC restoration is accomplished in one visit using a digital picture. After the image is captured, the tooth appears on the computer screen for you to see! The CEREC machine will mill the restoration in about 15 minutes! Schedule Your Visit Today! What are the benefits of CEREC® Crowns? Minimally Invasive Patients no longer have to take gooey, gross impressions for crowns. The slim camera wand takes a detailed picture instantly, so there are no gag reflexes and a more accurate impression to fit your tooth!

No Waiting

Previously patients had to make numerous visits to get a permanent crown. CEREC® allows our patients to get a permanent restoration in one visit!

Looks and Feels Natural

CEREC® technology creates durable restorations that last for years to come, allowing you to smile with confidence knowing your tooth looks and feels natural!

Safe and Sturdy

CEREC® uses materials similar to your tooth’s natural enamel, which makes this process safe and effective. In many cases, these materials are stronger than your natural teeth.
