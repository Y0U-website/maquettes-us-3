  • Porcelain Veneer A thin layer of porcelain bonded to the surface of a front tooth, either to improve the aesthetics of a tooth or to protect a damaged tooth surface.
  • Periodontal Scaling Also known as a deep cleaning, is completed for patients who have chronic inflammation and infection of the gums and bone surrounding the teeth. This is the process of removing calculus and plaque from the root surfaces of the teeth.
  • Crown Restoration which completely caps or encircles a tooth or dental implant to restore the tooth to it’s original shape. Crowns are often needed when a large cavity or fracture threatens the ongoing health of a tooth. They are bonded to the tooth using dental cement. Crowns we offer can be made from porcelain fused to gold, full gold or full porcelain.
  • Bridge Also known as a fixed partial denture, a bridge is a dental restoration used to replace a missing tooth by joining permanently to adjacent teeth or dental implants. They are bonded to the teeth using dental cement. Bridges we offer are made from porcelain fused to gold, full gold or full porcelain.
  • Root Canal Therapy A dental operation to save a tooth by removing the nerve of the tooth to eliminate infection and filling the cavity with a protective substance to permanently seal it. After receiving root canal therapy, the tooth becomes brittle and needs a crown to protect it from fractures.

Next, the medium aligner initiates movement with improved efficiency. Last, the hard aligner completes the movement similar to a finishing wire with braces. This results in a more comfortable and timesaving solution to straightening your smile.
