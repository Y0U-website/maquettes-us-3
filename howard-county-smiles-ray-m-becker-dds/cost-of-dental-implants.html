  • Durability: After your implant posts heal, they will permanently stay in your mouth. While you may need to replace a crown, bridge, or denture over time, your implants are permanent.
  • Jaw Support: After losing permanent teeth, you can lose natural jaw bone. Bone loss can lead to facial sagging, which can age you. Bridges and dentures do not support the jaw because they lie on the gums. We place implant posts into the jaw bone, and the bone grows around this post. With implants, you can improve your facial structure and jaw health.
  • Easy Maintenance: You can brush and floss implant-supported restorations like natural teeth. Removable dentures and traditional bridges need special care. These restorations also need special cleaners or soaks. Dental implants also do not need constant rebasing or relining.
