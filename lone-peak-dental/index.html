The practice

Having the very best team is vital to providing superior patient care. Learn more about Dr. Lorscheider & our experienced dental team!

For over 20 years, we have proudly served the communities of Cedar Hills, American Fork, Highland, Alpine, Pleasant Grove, and Lehi. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has made us your trusted dental choice in northern Utah County.

Our dental team welcomes you to Lone Peak Dental of Cedar Hills! During your time at our office, we will provide you with the exceptional and gentle dental care in a relaxing, welcoming atmosphere. We are dedicated to accommodating the specific needs our patients and welcome all members of the family.

With over 25 years of dental experience, Dr. Lorscheider is committed to advancing his knowledge and skills in dentistry. He works closely with each patient to understand their individual oral health needs and help guide them through their treatments to ensure that they are comfortable and confident that they re in good hands.

Our compassionate, qualified team specializes in family dentistry. Dr. Lorscheider is highly skilled at working with children and special needs patients; providing a calm, gentle, and positive approach to help put both the patient and care-giver at ease.

We have a dedicated children’s waiting area with toys, movies, and books to help our younger patients feel more relaxed, making it easier for you to bring your children. Look no further than Lone Peak Dental to know that your family is getting the care they need in an environment that is pleasant and welcoming for all. We invite you to be a part of our Lone Peak Dental family!
