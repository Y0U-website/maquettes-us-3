Meet Your Dentist in Cartersville, GA

Jonathan M. Owens, DMD

Dr. Owens is a second-generation dentist, born and raised in Georgia. He is very passionate about all aspects of dentistry, spending numerous hours each year continuing his education, allowing him to provide optimal care to his patients. Dr. Owens takes the necessary time to explain treatment options to each patient, helping them understand the best treatment available.

Doctor of Dental Medicine Medical College of Georgia Augusta

Bachelor of Exercise Science Brigham Young University

Dr. Owens is a member of the Georgia Dental Association , the American Dental Association , the LDS Dental Academy , and the American Society of Implant and Reconstructive Dentistry . Locally, he is a member of the Bartow County Chamber of Commerce.

Dr. Owens is a member of the American Society of Implant & Reconstructive Dentistry (ASIRD). ASIRD is committed to improving the quality of care for dental implant patients by elevating the standards of surgical and restorative dental education and practice. Not every dentist routinely restores and maintains dental implants, and not all placers of dental implants are surgeons. By using an ASIRD team, rest assured that your dentist routinely restores and maintains dental implants and that only a trained dental implant focused oral surgeon will place your implant. Learn more about ASIRD .

Dr. Owens has been married since 2003 and has four boys.
