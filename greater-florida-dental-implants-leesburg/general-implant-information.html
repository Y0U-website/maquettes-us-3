FAQ About Dental Implants

  • How many appointments are needed for implants? Most implant plans involve at least two visits. The first visit is for placing the implant post, followed by a healing period that is often three to six months. After the bone heals, you return for placement of the final crown, bridge, or arch restoration. We will explain your timeline clearly based on your needs and your treatment plan.
  • Can I get an implant right after an extraction? In many cases, yes, and planning early can help protect jawbone support. When a tooth is not replaced, the jawbone can recede because there is no root to stimulate the tissue. If bone loss has progressed, additional procedures may be needed. During your consultation, we will evaluate the site and explain whether immediate placement, delayed placement, or grafting is recommended.
  • Will I need a bone graft before implants? Not every patient needs grafting, but it may be recommended if the jawbone has receded after tooth loss. Implants require stable bone for long-term success, so rebuilding the area can improve support and predictability. We may discuss grafting or a sinus lift depending on the location and the amount of bone available. Your evaluation will confirm what is needed.
  • How much do implants cost? Implant pricing varies because every treatment plan is different. Total cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, the type of restoration you choose, whether preparatory procedures are needed, and what is included in the overall package. During your consultation, we will review your options and provide a clear breakdown of costs and financing.
  • What is the cost for a single tooth implant? The cost for an implant tooth depends on the complexity of the case and what is included, such as imaging, implant placement, healing components, and the final crown. If an extraction, gum care, or a graft is needed, that can affect the total. During your visit, we will explain what your treatment includes and what factors are driving the final estimate.
