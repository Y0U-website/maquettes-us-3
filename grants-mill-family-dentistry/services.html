Dental Fillings

Fillings are performed when there are cavities present in the mouth. A filling involves numbing the tooth, then removing the decay and replacing the tooth structure with a resin (tooth-colored) material. The resin is bonded in place. These restorations are given to allow the tooth to look, feel, and perform like natural teeth.

Dental Fillings

Fillings are one of the most commonly performed procedures done in a dental office. Your tooth will be numbed, the decay is removed from inside the tooth, and the filling is placed to replace tooth structure. Typically, there are no restrictions and you are free to resume normal activities following the procedure.

Crown & Bridge Restorations

If there is significant decay present, a full-coverage restoration (rather than a filling) is indicated. A crown fits onto the tooth like a “cap,” giving it strength and preventing future fractures or pain. The tooth will be modified so a crown will properly fit, then a quick scan is made of the tooth using our intraoral scanner to send to the dental laboratory. Patients love this added technology because there are no more goopy impressions!

Crown & Bridge Restorations

Extractions & Dentures

We always encourage our patients to save and restore teeth before electing to remove them. However, sometimes teeth become unrestorable and must be extracted to remove pain or infection. Extractions involve numbing the area and gently removing the tooth, then allowing the area to heal. We then make a plan to restore the missing space in order to prevent the shifting of the other teeth.

Implant Restorations

Implants are a wonderful way to restore missing teeth. This is a surgical procedure that will be performed by an oral surgeon. After the implant has properly healed it is restored with a crown in our office. We would love to provide more information on why implants are an amazing option for restoring teeth.

Implant Restorations

Placing the implant itself is a simple, surgical procedure performed by an oral surgeon. After a period of healing, Dr. Wiley will restore the implant with a crown. You would take care of the implant as you would a natural tooth.

Other Enhancements

Night Guards

Many people grind their teeth or have jaw-clenching habits. Also known as bruxism, this is an abnormal condition that can damage teeth, as well as cause pain and sensitivity. While it mostly occurs at night during sleeping, it can happen also due to misaligned teeth or stress. Nightguards are an excellent remedy to protect natural teeth as well as the dental restorations you’ve invested in. Custom night guards fit in your mouth perfectly and can be fabricated from a simple dental impression.

Injectable Flowable Composite

Injectable Flowable Composite is a more conservative veneer option. This is essentially known as "bonding," and is basically a bonded composite veneer. Unlike traditional veneers, it requires no shaving of your teeth, no anesthetic, and is completed within one office visit.
