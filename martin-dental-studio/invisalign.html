The Benefits of Invisalign

They’re Removable Pop out your aligners anytime you need to brush, floss, or enjoy your favorite foods. When you’re done, just put them back in to stay on track.

Exceptional Comfort Invisalign aligners are crafted from smooth, BPA-free plastic that feels gentle on your teeth and gums. They’re designed to fit so comfortably, you may even forget you’re wearing them.

A Convenient Choice With Invisalign, you can keep living life as usual. Work, eat, and socialize without the inconvenience or appearance of traditional braces.

Goodbye Goopy Impressions!

Creating your custom Invisalign trays has never been easier thanks to digital scanning. Say goodbye to goopy impression trays and hello to your best smile yet!

The Invisalign Treatment Process Initial Consultation & Exam Your Invisalign journey starts with a personalized consultation. You’ll chat with your doctor about your goals, have your teeth and bite evaluated, and learn whether Invisalign is right for you. This visit is the perfect time to ask questions and explore your options.

Aligner Fitting & Pick Up

Once you’re ready to begin, we’ll take precise digital scans or impressions of your teeth. These ensure your aligners are made just for you. When your custom trays arrive, you’ll stop by the office to get them and begin treatment. Some patients may also receive small, tooth-colored attachments that help keep aligners securely in place.

Check-ins

Every six weeks or so, you’ll visit us for a quick check-in to monitor your progress and pick up your next set of aligners. Each visit brings you one step closer to the smile you’ve been dreaming of.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign works well for many mild to moderate alignment concerns. For more complex orthodontic needs, your doctor may recommend traditional braces. The best way to know for sure is to schedule a consultation so we can evaluate your smile and go over your options.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most patients find Invisalign more comfortable than braces. You may feel slight pressure or tenderness when starting a new set of aligners — that means your teeth are moving! If discomfort persists, we suggest Tylenol rather than ibuprofen, which can interfere with tooth movement.

How should I wear my aligners?

Wear your aligners for 20–22 hours each day. Remove them only for meals, snacks, brushing, and flossing. Staying consistent ensures your treatment stays on schedule.

What should I do if I crack my aligners?

If your aligners crack or go missing, contact us right away. Delaying a replacement can slow down your progress. We’ll get you a new set quickly so you can stay on track.

Can I use my Invisalign trays as a nightguard?

No — Invisalign trays aren’t designed to withstand grinding or clenching. If you grind your teeth, you may need a custom nightguard. If you notice damage on your aligners in the morning, let your doctor know as soon as possible.

What are Invisalign attachments?

Invisalign attachments are small, tooth-colored bumps made of dental composite that are temporarily placed on certain teeth during Invisalign treatment. Their purpose is to help the aligners grip the teeth more effectively, allowing for greater control and precision. By providing this added leverage, attachments enable more complex tooth movements—such as rotations, vertical shifts, and root control—making Invisalign function more like traditional braces when treating more challenging alignment issues.

Can you see Invisalign when you’re wearing them?

Invisalign aligners are crafted from advanced SmartTrack® material, designed to be virtually invisible when worn. The thin, clear plastic conforms closely to your teeth, making them difficult to detect during normal social interactions. To maintain their clarity, avoid consuming anything besides water while wearing aligners, and clean them regularly to prevent staining. Some patients have small tooth-colored attachments that may be slightly more noticeable, but these are still far less visible than traditional braces.

How are Invisalign trays made?

Your treatment starts with digital scans that create a detailed model of your mouth. This model is sent to a specialized lab, where your aligners are designed and produced using advanced CAD/CAM technology. Once they’re made, they’re sent back to your doctor for your fitting appointment.
