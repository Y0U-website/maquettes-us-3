Helping you SMILE again

At Nelson Dental, we understand that everyone has different dental needs. Bad breath, bleeding gums, chipped or cracked teeth, crooked smiles – we’ve seen it all, and we’re here to help.

If you’re experiencing any oral discomfort, pain, or unusual symptoms, schedule an appointment with us. Our team will assess your condition and recommend the appropriate treatment for you.

Sensitive Gums

Red, swollen, or bleeding gums may be signs of gum disease, which can lead to tooth loss if untreated. Regular cleanings and exams are essential for gum health.

Tooth Pain

Persistent tooth pain can signal a cavity, infection, or other serious issue. Early treatment can prevent further complications and save your tooth.

Cracked/Broken Teeth

A cracked or broken tooth can affect both your smile and oral health. Restorative treatments like crowns or fillings can restore function and appearance.

Stained Teeth

Discolored teeth can make you feel self-conscious about your smile. Professional take-home whitening trays can brighten your teeth safely and effectively.

Teeth Grinding

Grinding your teeth at night can cause jaw pain, headaches, and tooth damage. A custom night guard can protect your teeth and reduce discomfort.

Missing Teeth

Missing teeth can make eating and speaking difficult, while also impacting your confidence. Solutions like implants, dentures, or bridges can fully restore your smile.

Jaw Pain

Chronic jaw pain, clicking, or difficulty chewing could be signs of TMJ disorder. Custom treatments like night guards or bite adjustments can help relieve discomfort and restore proper jaw function.

Dental Anxiety

Fear of the dentist prevents many from seeking care. Our Comfort Menu, sedation options, and gentle approach can help make every visit stress-free.

Snoring & Sleep Apnea

Chronic snoring or sleep apnea can disrupt your rest and affect your overall health. Custom appliances can improve airflow and help you sleep soundly.
