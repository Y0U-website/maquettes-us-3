Technology

Our dental office is equipped with the latest technology to streamline your treatment and keep you comfortable. We want you to have a relaxed dental experience, but we know how valuable your time is, so efficiency is also vital.

Keep reading to learn more about the technology at Ladue Family Dental.

CBCT Cone Beam Technology

Panoramic X-Rays

Instead of relying on an x-ray film placed inside your mouth, a panoramic x-ray rotates around your head. It projects a beam through your mouth to capture a detailed image, including your upper and lower jaws, teeth, nasal area and sinuses, and temporomandibular joints (TMJ).

We use panoramic x-rays to plan treatment for clear aligners, dental implants, and extractions, as well as to monitor tooth decay and gum disease. They’re a fast and safe diagnostic tool in our dental office.

Digital Records

We use digital records to improve patient communication and record-keeping. Our digital records system lets us track your entire family’s dental history, making it easier to create treatment plans and procedures.

We can also easily send your dental records to other dentists, specialists, or your insurance company. Your records are safe and available when you need them.

Digital X-Rays and Sensors

We place small electronic sensors into your mouth to capture detailed digital images of your teeth and gums. We can then display these images on a computer screen in real-time to diagnose dental concerns such as tooth decay, bone deterioration, or impacted teeth.

Compared to traditional x-rays, digital x-rays offer less radiation exposure and are a more comfortable way to analyze your dental needs and plan for treatment. We can even adjust the brightness and zoom in, allowing us to see subtle dental concerns that traditional x-rays might miss.

Intraoral Camera

With an intraoral camera, you can see what we see! We place a small wand inside your mouth to capture detailed images of your teeth and gums; it allows us to see all areas of your mouth and explain dental conditions to you. This visual representation makes it easier to understand what’s going on with your oral health.

We use an intraoral camera to create treatment plans for tooth-colored fillings, dental crowns, clear aligners, and cosmetic procedures.

Rotary Endodontics

Using an electrical handpiece instead of traditional dental tools, we can remove infected tissue and clean and reshape your tooth’s root canals when performing root canal therapy. This handpiece is made from nickel titanium and is much more flexible and precise than other methods, making your procedure as fast and efficient as possible.

Rotary endodontics allows us to easily navigate the curves inside your teeth without damaging your tooth structure.
