Complete Dental Care

Dr. Smestad and the staff at North Star Dental Care can provide a complete range of general and family dental services. We also work closely with several dental specialists including endodontists, periodontists, oral surgeons, orthodontists and pediatric dentists. Our general dentistry services include:

  • Dental exams and x-rays, preventive dental cleanings, fluoride treatment and sealants
  • Tooth-colored fillings, cosmetic bonding, teeth whitening, veneers and porcelain crowns
  • Restorative crowns, bridges, root canal therapy and surgical tooth extractions
  • Dental care for children
  • Periodontal gum therapy, dental implants and dentures
