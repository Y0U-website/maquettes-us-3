The practice

Following dental school, Dr. Lookingbill completed a hospital based general practice residency program at the Baltimore VA Medical Center. He continues to expand his dental education by attending multiple local study groups and seminars and is an active member in the American Dental Association and the Maryland State Dental Association.

Dr. Lookingbill, his wife Megan, and their two children, Sean and Alyssa, reside in Lutherville. He enjoys running, playing soccer and spending time with his family.

We enjoy seeing children of all ages and our family oriented staff has a very comforting and calming effect on even the most nervous of patients.

Les soins

We perform services across a broad spectrum of cosmetic and family dentistry, including root canals, extractions, crown and bridge and implant dentistry using a caring and gentle approach in our Parkville, Maryland location.
