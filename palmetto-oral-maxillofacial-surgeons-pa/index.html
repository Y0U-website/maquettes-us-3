The practice

With decades of combined experience, Dr. Doug Oliver, Dr. Michael Morgan, and Dr. Matthew Lee bring advanced training and modern surgical techniques to every procedure. We’re proud to serve Florence, South Carolina, and the Pee Dee community with exceptional care tailored to each patient’s needs.

At Palmetto Oral, we offer a full range of surgical procedures focused on improving oral health, comfort, and quality of life. From dental implants and wisdom teeth removal to biopsies and bone grafts, our surgeons combine specialized expertise with the latest technology to deliver precise, compassionate care.

Replace missing teeth with strong, natural-looking implants that restore your smile and confidence.

Experience safe, comfortable wisdom teeth removal with advanced technology for a smooth recovery.

We perform precise oral biopsies to diagnose concerns early and ensure the best course of care.

Receive safe, precise dental extractions focused on comfort and smooth healing.

During your initial visit, our team will take time to review your medical history, discuss your treatment options, and answer every question you may have.
