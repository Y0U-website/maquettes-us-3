Dr. Shahram Karimi attended Arizona State University for his undergraduate studies and earned his D.M.D. dental degree at Tufts School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. He began practicing dental care in 1995 while living in Arizona.

While spending summer vacations on Martha’s Vineyard, he identified the need for a practice offering cosmetic dentistry and specialty procedures, and he founded Paradise Dental Group, Martha’s Vineyard, in 2010. Dr. Karimi opened Paradise Dental on Martha’s Vineyard and became a year round resident. Today, Paradise Dental provides the Island’s only full service Cosmetic and Family Dentistry…
