What are the Benefits of Dental Implant Placement?

Dental implant placements offer numerous benefits that make them a preferred option for patients with missing teeth. They are durable, long-lasting, and highly successful with proper care and maintenance. They also look and function like natural teeth and help prevent further bone loss, which can occur with missing teeth.

Am I a Good Candidate for Implants?

Knowing if you’re a good candidate is essential if you’re considering dental implants as a solution for missing teeth. An excellent way to determine this is by scheduling a consultation with our dental professional. Factors impacting your candidacy include oral health, jawbone density, and overall health.

If you are not a good candidate for dental implant placement, there are alternative solutions, such as dentures or bridges, that your dentist can suggest. However, these solutions have drawbacks and may not offer the same long-term benefits as dental implants.

Don’t let the fear of the unknown keep you from exploring dental implant options. Take the first step and schedule a consultation to see if you’re a good candidate for this life-changing treatment.

How to Prepare for the Procedure?

Preparing for dental implant placement can seem daunting, but it doesn’t have to be. With the help of Northside Dental Group, you can feel confident and prepared every step of the way. If you’re considering getting dental implants, preparing beforehand is essential. Fortunately, Northside Dental Group is here to help guide you through the process. We will create a personalized treatment plan and provide instructions on preparing for the implant placement procedure. This may include abstaining from smoking, maintaining good oral hygiene, and avoiding certain medications that can thin the blood.

What to Expect During and After the Procedure?

The dental implant placement procedure typically takes one to two hours, depending on the case’s complexity. We can use local or general anesthesia to keep you comfortable during the process. Afterward, you may experience some discomfort, swelling, and temporary bleeding, which you can manage with pain medication and ice packs. Following your dentist’s instructions for proper oral hygiene and diet during the healing period would be best. Once the implant fully integrates with the bone tissue, your dentist will attach an abutment and a dental crown to complete the restoration.
