The practice

While completing his specialty training, Dr. Seung’s research in bioactive materials was published in the Journal of Endodontics. He is also the recipient of a competitive research grant awarded by the Foundation of the American Association of Endodontists (AAE).

Dr. Seung is certified by the American Board of Endodontics (ABE) and is a diplomate of the ABE, joining an distinguished group of endodontists who have demonstrated their dedication to professional growth and providing the highest quality of patient care. He also serves as a clinical professor in the Endodontics department at his alma mater.

Dr. Seung is currently a trustee on the board of the Foundation of the American Association of Endodontists. He holds membership in the American Dental Association (ADA), American Association of Endodontists, Maryland State Dental Association (MSDA), Maryland Korean Dental Association, and the Edward C. Penick Endodontic Study Club.

A passion for personalized care guides Dr. Seung in his practice. He believes that everyone deserves access to top-quality endodontic surgery as needed and that services should always be provided in a warm and personable manner.

Patient education is woven into every visit to help patients take control of their dental health and wellness.
