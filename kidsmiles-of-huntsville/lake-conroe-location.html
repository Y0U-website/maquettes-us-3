Carl E. Pittman, DDS

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Pittman graduated from the University of Texas at Austin and received his dental degree at the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston, graduating as a member of the dental honor society Omicron Kappa Upsilon. He then completed his pediatric dental specialty training through the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston and Hermann Hospital. Dr. Pittman is Board Certified in the specialty of Pediatric Dentistry and is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and numerous other pediatric and dental professional organizations.

He is a native Houstonian and is married to Dr. Kara Froelich, who practices pediatrics in Conroe, Texas. They have two children. In his leisure time, he enjoys drumming, photography, and fishing.

Dr. Pittman looks forward to meeting you and your child at KidSmiles of Lake Conroe.
