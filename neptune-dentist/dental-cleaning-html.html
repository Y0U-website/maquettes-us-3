A dental cleaning, also know as a teeth cleaning is a routine, preventative procedure done in a healthy mouth. Our dental hygienist will update your medical history to see if there have been any changes in your medical health and evaluate if you may need to have x-rays taken. Bite-wing x-rays are taken every 12-18 months of your anterior, premolar, and molar teeth to determine if you have cavities in these areas of your mouth. A full series of x-rays which shows us all your teeth and surrounding bone will be done at your initial visit, or every 3-5 years depending on your needs.

Our dental hygienist will then make recommendations for taking better care of your teeth and gums at home.

Dr. Musheyev will then perform a full examination of your mouth. This includes examining your teeth, gums, and the rest of your mouth for signs of disease. Dr. Musheyev will also do a head and neck/oral soft tissue cancer screening. If any decay or conditions warranting treatment are found, Dr. Musheyev will explain your treatment options, and our staff will help you schedule your treatment in our modern and comfortable Neptune Dentist office.

How often is a Dental Cleaning needed?

It is recommended for most people to schedule a dental hygiene visit, or "teeth cleaning" twice a year. However, some people need to follow a different schedule. If you have a high risk of dental disease, suffer from conditions such as diabetes or other things that compromise your immune system, or have suffered from periodontal disease, Dr. Musheyev may recommend more frequent visits. People in high-risk groups may need to see our hygienist for a dental cleaning every three or four months.

High-risk groups include people who smoke, people with extensive dental work or dental implants, people with gum disease, and people who tend to get a lot of cavities. Pregnant women, diabetics, or people with weakened immune systems may also need more appointments. Make sure Dr. Musheyev knows about your health conditions so he can recommend an appropriate dental cleaning schedule.
