Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

Do you treat kids, teens, and adults at the same office?

Yes. We see families across all ages and can often coordinate visits to simplify schedules.

Braces or aligners? How do I choose?

Both work well. Braces stay on and don’t rely on wear time; aligners are discreet and removable but require consistent wear. We’ll recommend what fits your goals and routine.

How long will treatment take?

Most comprehensive plans finish in 12–24 months . Limited cosmetic alignment can be shorter. Your timeline depends on biology, goals, and appliance choice.

Are scans safe? Do they replace X-rays?

Digital scans use light (no radiation) and model teeth/bite for planning. X-rays/CBCT are used only when they add important diagnostic information for safety or accuracy.

What happens after treatment?

Retainers keep your result stable. We’ll tailor a plan, clear, fixed/bonded, or both, and show you simple care tips and replacement options.
