How to Contact LCH

Our business hours vary across our services and locations, but our Call Center is open 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. Monday through Thursday and 8:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m. Friday.

Call us at (610) 444-7550 and our Patient Services Team will connect you. If your condition is an emergency and you need medical attention right away, please call 911 or go to your nearest Emergency Room for care. You do not need to call us first.
