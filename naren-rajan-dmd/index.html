The practice

To schedule an appointment at our Mendham, NJ dental practice, contact us today at 973-543-5700 .

We offer a full range of dental services at Dentistry of Mendham, including:

General Dentistry – Our general dentistry services encompass all of the routine preventive care needed to keep your teeth and gums healthy for a lifetime. In addition to dental evaluations and teeth cleaning, we provide root canal treatments and extractions.

Restorative Dentistry – When a tooth has suffered damage or decay, restorative dentistry can be used to restore its strength, function, and appearance. Dr. Rajan provides composite fillings, inlays and onlays, dental crowns , dental bridges , and dentures .

Cosmetic Dentistry – Sometimes your teeth are healthy, but you’re just not happy with the way they look. Cosmetic dental treatments like teeth whitening, dental veneers, cosmetic bonding, and micro abrasion can give you a smile you’re proud to share with the world.

Periodontal Procedures – You can’t have healthy teeth without healthy gums, which is why Dentistry of Mendham offers periodontal treatments to treat gum disease and repair the damage it causes.

Obstructive Sleep Apnea Treatment – If you have sleep apnea, you may be surprised to learn that there’s an effective alternative to CPAP therapy—an oral appliance from your dentist. These appliances keep your airway open while you sleep so you can rest easy.

Our goals as a practice are to help patients achieve dental health using the latest digital techniques combined with our care, skill and judgement in a comfortable environment.
