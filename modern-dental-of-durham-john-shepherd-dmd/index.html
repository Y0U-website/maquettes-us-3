The practice

After receiving his dental degree at NSU’s College of Dental Medicine , Dr. Shepherd worked as an associate dentist in Las Vegas and Hawaii. During the years as an associate, he was able to gain experience in many avenues of dentistry, ranging from dental implants to Invisalign.

After he developed his knowledge, he invested in his vision as a provider and opened his first practice which was located in Southern California, where he was raised.

Dr. Shepherd and his wife were inspired to move to North Carolina after visiting friends who lived here and seeing how amazing it would be to raise their son and continue his career in dentistry.

Dr. Shepherd works with quality in mind, and his integrity as a provider and team leader is unparalleled!
