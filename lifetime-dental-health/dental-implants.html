What are dental implants?

A prosthetic screw-like device that’s implanted into your jaw bone , dental implants provide the foundation for a dental crown , and ensure that your prosthetic tooth stays in place while also stimulating jaw bone growth.

Can anyone have a dental implant placed?

Before we can confirm that you’ll benefit from an implant, you’ll need to have your teeth, gums, and jaw bone evaluated by our team to ensure your candidacy. Dental implants require healthy gums and adequate amounts of jaw bone for support, and while some patients who lack these criteria may not be candidates for this procedure, others with thin or soft bone levels may be able to undergo bone grafting to rebuild bone loss and maintain an implant.

What happens during the implantation process?

Once we’ve exposed the jawbone in the treatment area, a hole will be drilled in order for the implant to be inserted. Depending on your unique oral health situation, we may place a temporary removable prosthetic immediately, or you may need to wait for the crown to be crafted and attached at a future date.

Following the implantation process, you can expect to experience varying levels of discomfort, and that the site will feel swollen for several days. Patients should rinse with salt water often, gently brush the teeth surrounding the implant, and consume a soft diet while the area heals.
