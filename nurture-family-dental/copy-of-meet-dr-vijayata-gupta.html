Lauren Bedro, DDS

Dr. Bedro earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Human Biology from Michigan State University in 2018. Afterward, she completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in 2022.

She is passionate about dentistry because it allows her to positively transform someone s life. A smile can greatly influence a person s confidence, and when she can help enhance that confidence, it brings her the greatest sense of fulfillment!

As a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association and Central District Dental Society, Dr. Bedro remains committed to staying current in her field. During her dental education, she provided care to underserved communities through outreach programs, including the Grand Rapids and Wayne County Health Centers. She also took part in the Titans for Teeth Mobile Clinic, offering dental care to elementary and middle school students.

Originally from Plymouth, Dr. Bedro now lives in Howell with her husband, Jake. Outside the office, she enjoys skiing trips with her family, getting lost in a good book and cheering on Detroit sports teams and the Michigan State Spartans!
