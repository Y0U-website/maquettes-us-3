For healthy teeth and gums, follow the 2/2 rule. Brush for 2 minutes, twice a day with a soft-bristled brush. You can always brush more often, but a minimum of two times a day is recommended.

Regular dental checkups are an essential part of maintaining a healthy smile. Invest in yourself by making an appointment once every 6 months.

Get the maximum benefit out of your oral health routine by flossing every day. Flossing helps prevent gum disease and tooth decay.
