Dr. Penny Resnick-Graulich Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Penny has become synonymous with high quality, loving children’s dentistry in Westchester County. She established her pediatric dental practice in 1984 after graduating from University of Pennsylvania Dental School and New York University Dental School with a specialty in pediatric dentistry. Dr. Penny loves to make you laugh and her office is more fun than a barrel of monkeys!!

Dr. Penny has a wonderful family. Irwin, her loving husband, is president of an advertising agency. Her daughter, Helen, is married and living in Montana with her three adorable children. Scott is working in real estate in New York City. And, Daniel is a lawyer practicing in Washington, DC.

Dr. Penny loves gardening, reading and traveling. She has become an incredible floral designer after taking classes at the New York Botanical Gardens. She says that her cute little dog, Princess, helps her come up with some unique floral ideas! With her great sense of humor and fantastic dental skills, Dr. Penny has made going to the dentist fun for parents and children alike. Stop by to say hello and meet the incredible Dr. Penny!
