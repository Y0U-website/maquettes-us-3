Early Orthodontic Care for Kids…

  • Space Maintainers . When a baby tooth is lost early, we hold the space so adult teeth come in straight, which can reduce the need for braces later.
  • Interceptive guidance. We advise on habits and timing that shape a healthy bite as your child grows.
  • Invisalign for teens. For older kids who need alignment, clear aligners are a discreet option we offer in-house.
  • Coordinated referrals. If your child needs full orthodontic treatment, we connect you with a trusted orthodontist and stay involved so care feels seamless.

Common questions from parents

When should my child first see someone about braces?

Around age 7 is the general guideline for a first orthodontic check, but you do not need to wait for that. We watch your child&#x27;s bite at every routine visit and will tell you if an earlier look makes sense.

Does my child actually need braces, or just monitoring?

Many kids just need monitoring, not treatment. The goal of early care is to catch the small number of issues that are easier to fix early, and to reassure you about everything that is developing normally.

Can a pediatric dentist handle orthodontics?

We handle the early stages: watching development, holding space, guiding habits, and offering Invisalign for teens. For full braces or complex cases, we coordinate with a trusted orthodontist so your child&#x27;s care stays connected.

Is Invisalign a good option for my teen?

For many responsible teens, yes. Clear aligners are removable and hard to notice, which teens appreciate. We will evaluate your teen&#x27;s smile and tell you honestly whether it is the right fit.
