FAQs

Still have questions? We’re here to help, contact us today or give us a call!

  • How does the SMART protocol protect me during mercury filling removal? The SMART protocol uses advanced suction, protective barriers, and air filtration to limit mercury vapor exposure. This process meets the highest safety standards for both patients and staff during amalgam removal procedures.
  • Do you accept dental insurance for biological or cosmetic procedures? Yes. We are insurance-friendly and help patients use their benefits for qualifying procedures. Our team works with most plans and assists you in maximizing your coverage for biological and cosmetic dental care.
  • What is the connection between oral health and systemic conditions like heart disease? Oral bacteria and inflammation can trigger or worsen systemic health issues such as heart disease, stroke, and diabetes. Our hygiene visits and treatment plans are designed to support both your oral and overall health.
  • What options do you offer for sleep apnea and airway problems? We provide non-surgical airway expansion using the DNA appliance system, Homeoblock, and WatchPAT 300 sleep studies. These options help open the airway naturally and are suitable for adults and children near Lakeway and Bee Cave.
  • Are your cosmetic restorations like veneers and crowns safe for sensitive patients? Absolutely. We use biocompatible materials for all restorations and offer personalized compatibility testing. Our veneers are crafted by an award-winning ceramist for both beauty and systemic safety.
  • How do you help patients who feel anxious or embarrassed about dental visits? Compassion is at the heart of our practice. We welcome patients who may have avoided dental care for years and provide a gentle, judgment-free environment focused on comfort and trust.
  • Do you treat children for orthodontic or airway issues? Yes. We offer Healthy Start and growth guidance orthodontics for children with mouth breathing, tongue thrust, or sleep issues. Early care can help prevent future airway and jaw problems for families in the Lake Travis area.
