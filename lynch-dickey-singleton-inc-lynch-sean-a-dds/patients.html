Mission Statement

Welcome To Our Practice We strive to be a caring office providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable environment for you and your family. We are confident our services and friendly staff will make you feel at home.

Quality Care When you have dental problems or concerns, you need to turn to a dentist who listens and responds. Informed Patients We feel that a successful relationship begins with mutual trust and understanding. We also believe that informed patients are better prepared to make decisions regarding their health and well being. We try to blend a mix of personal care, high technology and continuing education, to maintain a commitment to our patients in every aspect of our practice.

We stand behind our advice, decisions and work.

Being well-prepared for your appointment will ensure that the doctor has all of the needed information to provide the best possible care for you. It also will help relieve any unnecessary anxiety you may be feeling. Educate yourself on your symptoms by reviewing the content on this website . Also, take some time to review our staff page and familiarize yourself with the doctors. We look forward to your first visit.

We are a preferred provider for Delta Dental Premier, Aetna PPO, and Cigna PPO but can file with other insurance carriers as well. At this time we do not accept or file with Medicaid. We will file insurance claims but the patient is responsible for any non-payment from the insurance company.

Payment, insurance deductibles, and co-pays are due when services are rendered. We offer a no-interest, no-fee payment plan through CareCredit for individuals who qualify regardless of their insurance coverage. It takes only three simple steps, including an instant approval process, to apply. After approval, you are free to use CareCredit for our services and other medical services including LASIK, veterinary, cosmetic, hearing aids, and more. Our friendly staff will be more than happy to discuss this option. For more information or to apply online, visit carecredit.com .

Please bring your dental insurance information to each visit to ensure we have your most current information.

For payments, we can accept cash/check, credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, and CareCredit.
