Am I a candidate for Invisalign® instead of braces?

Many adults are excellent candidates for Invisalign. Clear aligners can correct crowding, spacing, and certain bite issues.

However, more complex cases may be better treated with braces for greater precision.

Your dental history, gum health, and alignment goals all play a role in determining the right approach. We work closely with your general dentist to ensure long-term success.
