Our Team

Annette Dufour, DDS

Dr. Annette Dufour was born in Sault Ste. Marie, Michigan. She moved to Escanaba with her family at the age of five. Dr. Dufour graduated from Escanaba High School in 1984 and earned her associate’s degree in Dental Hygiene from Ferris State College in 1987.

In 1992, she received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She was awarded the Senior Student Award by the Academy of General Dentistry and the Academy of Operative Dentistry Award, as well as membership in the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honorary Dental Fraternity upon graduation.

After dental school, Dr. Dufour attended a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency program at the University of Michigan’s Operative Department.

Krista Gorenchan, DDS

Dr. Krista Gorenchan, formerly Krista Ison, moved with her family to Escanaba as a child. She and her husband, Tim, both graduated from Escanaba Area High School in 1996. After high school, Krista attended Northern Michigan University, where she obtained her Bachelor of Science degree in biology. She was then accepted to the University of Michigan School of Dentistry and earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree in May 2004.

Shortly after getting married in July 2004, she began her dental career in the Detroit area. In 2005, an opportunity in the public health field allowed Dr. Gorenchan and her husband to move back to the Upper Peninsula to be closer to their families.

In 2006, Dr. Gary Bradley and Dr. Annette Dufour hired Dr. Gorenchan at Great Northern Dental Associates, where she is now a partner in the practice.
