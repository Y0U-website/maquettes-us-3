Meet Our Doctors

At Guerrino Dentistry and Associates, we are proud to have passionately served our community for decades. Our founder, Dr. Paul Guerrino, leads his skilled team of doctors who provide a vast range of advanced services, with Dr. Guerrino handling periodontics and implants. We are dedicated to helping every patient who walks through our doors enjoy healthy, vibrant smiles. Our doctors look forward to meeting you and your loved ones so we can forge a trusted bond that serves your needs for a lifetime.

Learn more about our motivated team of dentists below!

Afshan Ahmed, DDS - General and Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Afshan Ahmed is a 2003 graduate of New York University College of Dentistry.

Paul Guerrino, DDS - Periodontics and Implantology

Paul J. Guerrino, Dentist-Implantologist, (New York University) has over 35 years of professional experience.

Joseph Guerrino, DDS - General Dentistry

Dr. Joseph Guerrino graduated from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine (ranked 7th best dental school nationally and 11th internationally).

Peter Chen, DDS - Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Dr. Peter Chen is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon with a focus on wisdom teeth and dental implant surgery.

Bobak Bakhsoudeh, DDS - Endodontics

Dr. Bobak Bakhshoudeh was born and raised in Long Island, NY where he received his Bachelor in Science degree at SUNY Stony Brook University.

Vasso Ziotis, DDS Orthodontist

Dr. Ziotis is a passionate and dedicated orthodontist with a commitment to creating beautiful, confident smiles.
