Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer a range of benefits that can significantly improve your oral health and overall quality of life. One of the key advantages of dental implants is their success rate, providing improved confidence in your smile. Other advantages include the ability to eat without restrictions and a natural feel akin to your own teeth. Further by preventing jawbone shrinkage (loss of bone) and preserving the health of surrounding bone and gums, implants help maintain the integrity of the jawbone.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

1. Brush & Floss Daily

Just like natural teeth, your implants need daily brushing and flossing to remove plaque. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and low-abrasive toothpaste to avoid scratching the implant crown.

2. Use Interdental Brushes

Interdental brushes or water flossers can help clean around the implant post and gum line, where regular floss might not reach.

3. Schedule Regular Cleanings

Regular dental cleanings and checkups are vital for implant maintenance. Your dentist will check for any signs of complications, such as infection or gum recession.

4. Stay Hydrated

5. Limit Smoking

Smoking impairs the healing process and can lead to implant failure. If you smoke, talk to your dentist about ways to quit for optimal oral health.

6. Address Teeth Grinding (Bruxism)

If you grind your teeth at night, wearing a night guard can protect your implants from excessive pressure and potential damage. Talk to us about potential solutions.

7. Watch for Signs of Infection

If you notice redness, swelling, pain, or discharge around your implant, contact your dentist immediately. Early intervention can prevent more serious complications.
