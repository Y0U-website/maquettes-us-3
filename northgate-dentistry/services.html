Our Services

Routine Exams & Teeth Cleanings

Regular dental exams and teeth cleanings are essential for good oral and dental health. During your appointment, we will remove any calculus or plaque buildup from your teeth. In addition, we will check for any signs of oral cancer, gum disease, or other irregularities. At the close of the visit, we will floss and polish your teeth.

Pediatric (Children’s) Dentistry

Single Visit CEREC Crowns

We are proud to have incorporated single visit CEREC crowns into our practice. These types of crowns allow us to fabricate and place your new crown in one visit, saving you time and ensuring your crown is produced and placed by the same practice and not an outside lab. Dental Implants Replacing missing teeth is crucial to your dental and overall health. Oral Sedation

We will work with you to determine what is causing your TMD disorder and which treatment option best fits your needs. Treatments include custom-fit oral appliances, physical therapy, relaxation techniques, and more. Snoring Appliances If you have been diagnosed with sleep apnea or have a snoring problem, a custom-fit oral appliance can help alleviate your symptoms. Worn like a retainer or mouth guard, an oral appliance will support your jaw and help you maintain an open airway.
