What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are an innovative and efficient way to replace an entire arch of teeth using just four strategically placed implants. Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 is a type of dental implant that provides a permanent, non-removable solution that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.
