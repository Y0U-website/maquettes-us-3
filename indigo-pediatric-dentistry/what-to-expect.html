Our Promise

Along with providing you the highest level of dental care, we promise to be a compassionate healthcare partner whose happiness resides in each tear we prevent, in every fear you overcome, and in the heart of each unique and awe-inspiring child that walks through our door.

Our Services

Indigo is a privately-owned pediatric dentistry office that provides an inspirational environment for children as young as newborns up to the age of 19. We offer:

  • Complimentary services for our Itty Bitty Indigos (children under 2)
  • Prevention and education
  • Maintenance checkups: dental exam, x-ray, cleaning and fluoride treatment
  • Composite fillings
  • Zirconia crowns
  • Stainless steel crowns
  • Extractions & space maintenance
  • Pediatric partials
  • Maxillary and lingual frenectomy procedures for newborns and children diagnosed with ankyloglossia
  • In-office sedation
