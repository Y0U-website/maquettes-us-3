The practice

With your healthy, beautiful smile, you’re ready to take on the world. Orthodontic care creates a lasting, positive difference in your life, and we’d love to help you transform yours! Dr. Clay Lisenby and the Lisenby Orthodontics team offer attentive, individualized care that’s tailored to you.

At your complimentary consultation, Dr. Lisenby will ask about your goals and concerns. Following your exam, he’ll create a treatment plan that uses leading-edge techniques and state-of-the-art technology. Your orthodontic experience will be efficient, affordable, and comfortable. Best of all, you’ll have a lasting, confident smile you’ll love!

Ours is a patient-centered and family-friendly orthodontic office. We are proud of the smiles we create and treasure the friendships we grow with our patients. We’d love to help you achieve your best smile while becoming your friend.

Our Macon, GA orthodontic office serves Warner Robins, Forsyth, and nearby communities. Please call and schedule your complimentary consultation today.
