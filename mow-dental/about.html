About

0 Dr. Kyle Mow Dr. Mow was born and raised in Elk Grove, CA. He completed his undergraduate studies in Biological Sciences from the University of California, Irvine. He then received his Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences before obtaining his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University in Glendale, AZ.

After graduating from dental school, Dr. Mow returned to Southern California where he practiced in Downey prior to purchasing his first practice in Lomita.

Dr. Mow has taken numerous courses in implant and cosmetic dentistry in order to ensure his patients receive the high quality care they deserve. He is excited to meet you and help you have the smile you have always wanted!
