New Patients Welcome

Your First Visit

We will reserve about an hour for your first visit. A member of our front office team will greet you and review your paperwork when you arrive. For your convenience, our NEW PATIENT FORMS are available online. You can fill out your papers online and bring them with you to your appointment to save time. After your paperwork has been completed, Dr. Chapman or Dr. Puderbaugh will see you promptly.

Your Comprehensive Exam

After your exam, Dr. Chapman or Dr. Puderbaugh will thoroughly explain his or her findings and recommend options for treatment.

Financial Arrangements

We accept cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, and personal checks (for existing patients with an established payment history). As an added service to our patients, we offer Care Credit.

CareCredit is a simple way to consolidate and manage certain out-of-pocket healthcare expenses, because it is a credit card with a revolving line of credit that can be used repeatedly and for any family member.
