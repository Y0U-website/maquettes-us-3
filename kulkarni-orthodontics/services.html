Braces & Invisalign Services

Our Services

Personalized orthodontic solutions for children, teens, and adults across the Miami Valley.

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

A nearly invisible way to straighten teeth using a series of custom-made, removable aligners. Ideal for teens and adults who want discretion and comfort.

Modern Braces

High-efficiency metal and ceramic braces designed for precise tooth movement. A reliable choice for simple to complex orthodontic cases.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Guiding jaw growth and tooth eruption in children can prevent more serious issues later. We recommend an initial evaluation by age 7.

Teen Orthodontics

Comprehensive treatment plans tailored to busy teens, with flexible scheduling and options that fit school and extracurricular life.

Adult Orthodontics

It&#x27;s never too late for a healthier, more confident smile. We offer discreet solutions designed around professional and personal lifestyles.

Retainers & Post-Treatment Care

Custom retainers and ongoing monitoring help protect your investment and keep your smile looking its best for years to come.
