Meet Dr. Sandor

Dr. Allan Sandor completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Florida in 1998. He received his dental degree from Marquette University, College of Dentistry in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. After graduation Dr. Sandor joined the United States Navy as a dental officer and completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the Marine Corps Recruit Depot at Parris Island, South Carolina. After residency Dr. Sandor served a two-year tour with the 2nd Dental Battalion in support of the 2nd Marine Logistics Group out of Camp Lejeune, North Carolina.

After practicing general dentistry for three years, Dr. Sandor was selected by the Navy for out-service training in Endodontics. He received his certificate in Endodontics from the University of Iowa, College of Dentistry in 2008. In his last tour of active duty, Dr. Sandor served as the Regional Staff Endodontist at Naval Air Station Jacksonville supporting the sailors, soldiers and marines stationed in Northeast Florida, Southern Georgia and Cuba. Dr. Sandor has presented research at various dental meetings and is published in the Journal of Endodontics.

Dr. Sandor is a member of the American Association of Endodontists, American Dental Association, and the South Dakota Dental Association. He has been published in the Journal of Endodontists.
