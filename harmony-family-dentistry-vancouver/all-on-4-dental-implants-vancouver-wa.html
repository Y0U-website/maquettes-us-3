What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a modern solution for patients missing several teeth. Instead of replacing each tooth individually, we employ four titanium posts to support an entire row of prosthetic teeth. This method restores your smile, improves chewing, and feels much like natural teeth.

Are You a Good Candidate for All-on-4?

All-on-4 implants work well for many people, but they are not right for everyone. The best candidates are those who are missing most or all of their teeth in one or both arches and want a permanent solution. You should be in good general health and have enough jawbone to support the implants.

During your consultation, Dr. Julie Jang examines your mouth, reviews your medical history, and takes X-rays to determine if all-on-4 dental implants near you are the best option for you.

Why Choose All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 implants have become very popular because they offer a simple solution for complex dental problems. Instead of replacing each tooth with a separate implant, we can replace an entire row of teeth with only four implant posts. For a full mouth restoration, you need just eight implants total.

Our dentist near you numbs your mouth during the procedure so that you won’t feel pain. After the numbness wears off, you may have some soreness and swelling for a few days. We’ll provide pain medication and care instructions to keep you comfortable during recovery.

Caring for all-on-4 dental implants near you is simple. Brush them at least twice a day and floss daily, just like natural teeth. Regular dental cleanings and checkups are also important to keep your implants and gums healthy. What’s the difference between All-on-4 implants and dentures? All-on-4 implants are permanently fixed to your jawbone and don’t come out. Dentures are removable and can slip around in your mouth. All-on-4 implants feel more natural, allow better chewing function, and help preserve your jawbone, while dentures can lead to bone loss over time.
