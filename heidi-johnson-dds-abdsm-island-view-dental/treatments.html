Treatments Sleep Apnea Sleep apnea occurs when the airway becomes blocked during sleep. This may lead to pauses in breathing that can include snoring, fatigue, and even long-term health concerns.

At Island View Dental, we understand how exhausting sleep apnea can be. That’s why Dr. Johnson offers gentle and effective treatment options designed to help you breathe easier and sleep more soundly. Our custom-fitted oral appliance can keep your airway open comfortably throughout the night, without the need for bulky machines or masks.

With improved sleep comes more energy, better focus, and a healthier you. Get started on your sleep apnea treatment today at Island View Dental.

Pediatric Airway and Myofunctional Therapy with Myobrace

Healthy nasal breathing and proper oral muscle function can support jaw development, sleep quality, and long-term dental health.

Led by Dr. Johnson, a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine, our Myobrace approach pairs habit training with comfortable oral appliances to encourage better breathing, tongue posture, and growth guidance.
