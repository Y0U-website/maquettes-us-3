0 About Us Dr. Kristina Fernandez Come Meet Dr. Kristina Fernandez was born and raised in Southern California. She knew she wanted to become a dentist at the age of four after following her mom (dental assistant) around the pediatric clinic at Loma Linda university. She relentlessly pursued her calling and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS), at Loma Linda University school of Dentistry in 2019. She received her Bachelor’s in science degree at California Baptist university in 2014.

Having established a strong clinical practice, Dr. Fernandez and Rocio Jaimes (EF) opened Jefferson Dental in April of 2024.

Dr. Fernandez is passionate about building strong patient rapport through empathy, honesty, and good quality dentistry. She enjoys spending chair side time with her patients and demonstrating that a visit to the dentist can be enjoyable.

She lives in Redlands and spends her time running marathons, biking, practicing yoga, and making jewelry.

Rocio Jaimes EF Come Meet Rocio Jamie’s has worked in the dental field for over 25 years. She received her RDAEF degree from UCLA in 2006 and completed her RDAEF II degree from Loma Linda university in 2022.

She is extremely talented and passionate about dentistry. Her years of experience and skill in private practice have made her a tremendous asset and popular among all patients. Her knowledge in the field makes her top tier. The love and quality she pours into her work speaks for itself.

There would be no Jefferson Dental without Rocio. She is truly magnificent.

Rocio and Dr. Fernandez have worked together since 2019. Their shared vision and passion for dentistry prompted them to open a family owned dental office in April of 2024. This outstanding EF and DDS duo take dentistry to another level.

With love, empathy, and kindness this pair creates beautiful dentistry.
