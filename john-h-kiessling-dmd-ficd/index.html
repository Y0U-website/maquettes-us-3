The practice

Give us a call or text if you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment.

Kiessling Family Dentistry has been serving Harrisburg; Middletown; Highspire; Hummelstown; Enola; Mechanicsburg; Camp Hill; Hershey; and Wormleysburg, Pennsylvania with gentle dental care since 1988.

Our talented and caring team of dental professionals is committed to providing patients with the highest quality dental treatments and procedures, ranging from routine exams and cleanings, to fillings, implants, crowns and veneers. We specialize in creating beautiful smiles.

At Kiessling Family Dentistry, our mission is to create a dental experience of excellence and comfort. We will create the treatment plan best for you.
