Financial & Dental Insurance Options

We recognize that financial concerns can sometimes prevent individuals from receiving the dental care they need. At Kaloko Dental, we want to ensure that your family’s dental health is not neglected, which is why we offer various payment options to accommodate your needs.

Effective February 1, 2024, Kaloko Dental will be an out-of-network status with all dental insurances. Please note that we will continue to submit claims on your behalf for potential reimbursement through your dental insurance.

To streamline our processes, we kindly request that all payments be made at the time of service. Subsequently, we will promptly submit insurance claims to expedite the reimbursement procedure. We recommend contacting your insurance provider in advance to confirm your coverage and understand any specific requirements. This will help ensure a smooth experience when filing your claim.

If you have any questions or need further information regarding our payment options or scheduling your first visit with Dr. Kristopher Devers, please don’t hesitate to call us at 808-326-7755. Our friendly team members are here to assist you and get you started on your dental journey. We prioritize your oral health and are dedicated to providing you with exceptional care.

no insurance? no problem!

Not everyone has dental insurance coverage, and we strive to make high-quality dental care accessible to all patients. That’s why we are pleased to offer our in-house Kaloko Dental Savings Club Program specifically designed for patients without insurance.

Our Kaloko Dental Savings Club Program provides a range of benefits and discounts on dental services to help you maintain your oral health and receive the necessary care at affordable prices. By joining our savings club, you can enjoy savings on preventive treatments, restorative procedures, and other dental services offered at our practice.

If you’re interested in learning more about our Kaloko Dental Savings Club Program or have any questions, please contact our office at 808-326-7755. Our team will be happy to provide you with all the details and assist you in enrolling in the program. We believe that everyone deserves access to excellent dental care, and we are committed to helping you achieve optimal oral health without the need for traditional insurance coverage.
