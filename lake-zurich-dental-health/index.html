Preventative care includes dental cleanings, routine x-rays, and periodic examinations. These services will allow you to maintain optimum oral health.

Same day crowns provide you with the benefit of having your crown conveniently fabricated in our office and cemented in a single visit. Therefore eliminating the need for a temporary crown to be worn for a couple of weeks.

Teeth whitening is a process where you lighten your teeth to achieve a brighter, whiter smile. Whitening can be achieved with either a same day Zoom procedure, with home trays, or both.

Inlays and onlays are stronger than fillings but more conservative than full crowns. You have the benefit of a longer lasting restoration which can be completed in a single visit.
