Ashley Dinh, DDS

As an Air Force Reserve Dentist, Dr. Dinh participated in humanitarian missions to Sri Lanka to provide emergency dental care to displaced tsunami residents throughout four remote villages. She was helicoptered into each village to extract hopeless teeth under austere conditions. Dr. Dinh also provides community services to Give Kids a Smile and has volunteered her time to the Northern Virginia Dental Clinic & Free Dental Day.

Continuing Education & Professional Memberships

Dr. Dinh is dedicated to pursuing advanced education and training, including endodontics, periodontics, soft tissue grafts, pedodontics, orthodontics (Invisalign, traditional braces, Myobrace, and interceptive orthodontics), cosmetic dentistry, and dental implant restorations and placements. Dr. Dinh is certified in IV Sedation. She is a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry and Academy of General Dentistry, as well as a fellow of the American Dental Implant Association.

Dr. Dinh and her husband, Hiep enjoy traveling, back-country camping, skiing, biking, hiking, and live theatre. With their two beautiful children, Sydney and Mason, they are now focused on local tennis and golf tournaments throughout Northern Virginia. She is a native Texan and brings her southern hospitality to Virginia.
