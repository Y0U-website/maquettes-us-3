Associate Member Application

Interested in becoming an Associate Member of the Medina County Dental Society?

Please submit the online application below and a member of our board will be in touch with you shortly to confirm whether or not your application has been accepted. If accepted, a membership fee of $75.00 can be made online here .

  • Cart 0 Back Associate Member Application MCDS Leadership Member Portal News Back Events Volunteer Opportunities ASSOCIATE MEMBER APPLICATION Associate Members share many of the benefits of our regular members. Often, individuals may choose to apply for Associate Membership if they fit into any of the following categories: Ethical dentists whose primary practice locations are not located in the jurisdiction of this society, but who are members in good standing of their component societies and who are members in good standing of the ODA and the ADA
  • Allied health professionals (i.e. Dental Hygienists, Dental Assistants, Dental Lab owners and personnel, etc.)
  • Non-dentists who are contributing to the advancement or teaching of dentistry
