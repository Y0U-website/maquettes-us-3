Our preventive dentistry services

Cleanings & Exams

These visits are the foundation of great oral health. We take our time to look closely, clean thoroughly, and explain clearly. Our goal is to protect your smile and catch issues early, before they become problems.

Digital X-Rays

Our low-radiation digital x-rays give us a clear view of what’s happening beneath the surface, from bone levels to hidden decay. It’s a fast, safe, and essential tool for building your personalized care plan.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Nightguards for Grinding

Chronic clenching or grinding can lead to worn teeth, jaw pain, and long-term damage. We custom-make nightguards that protect your smile while you sleep.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride helps strengthen enamel and reduce the risk of cavities. It’s especially helpful if you’re prone to decay or have sensitivity. Think of it as a little extra defense for your teeth.

Biolase Epic X Laser

This technology makes gum treatments more gentle. It allows us to treat soft tissue with precision, which means less bleeding and swelling, and greater comfort during and after your visit. It can also provide relief for minor mouth sores or jaw discomfort.

We often use the Biolase Epic X Laser after scaling and root planing to help eliminate lingering bacteria beneath the gumline. Disinfecting the treated areas, it supports healing and encourages healthier gum tissue. The result is a more comfortable recovery and stronger long-term gum health.

Answers to your questions

What’s the point of a dental exam if nothing hurts?

Most dental problems don’t cause pain right away, and by the time they do, they’re often more complicated (and expensive) to treat. Regular exams help us catch issues early and keep your smile healthy and low-maintenance.

How often do I really need a cleaning? K L

For most people, twice a year is ideal. If you have gum disease or buildup that forms quickly, our Torrance preventive dentist may recommend more frequent visits to stay ahead of any issues and protect your long-term health.

A typical preventive visit includes a dental cleaning, a full-mouth exam, and digital x-rays if needed. We’ll also check your gum health, screen for oral cancer, and take time to talk through any changes or concerns.

Can preventive care really affect my overall health? K L

Absolutely. Gum disease has been linked to heart disease, diabetes, and other health issues. By taking care of your mouth, you’re also supporting your immune system, digestion, and whole-body wellness.

What can I do at home to prevent cavities and gum disease? K L

Great question! Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and avoid frequent snacking or sugary drinks. Staying consistent with your home care (and your checkups) goes a long way toward avoiding more complex treatment later.
