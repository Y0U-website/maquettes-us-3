Dentist Macon, GA

Howard D. Booth, Jr., DDS, MAGD

General and cosmetic dentist Dr. Howard "Doug" Booth graduated from Emory Dental School in 1977. He served four years as a general dentist in the U.S. Army and moved to Atlanta in 1982 to begin private practice. He relocated his practice to Macon in 1983.

Dr. Booth has extensive training in the most advanced dental treatment services available in all facets of dentistry, including cosmetic dentistry, implant restoration and reconstructive procedures. He has expanded his clinical practice to include oral appliance management of mild to moderate sleep apnea and the use of Botox and dermal fillers for facial esthetics and pain relief from TMD (jaw joint) disorders.

Through his active memberships in the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Georgia Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, American Academy of Facial Esthetics and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Dr. Booth maintains an active continuing education calendar and stays involved in the advancement of the dental profession.

Dr. Booth and his best friend and wife, Maya have two adult children. Dr. Booth is an active triathlete and participates in local and national competitions; he also enjoys boating and spending time at the ocean.
