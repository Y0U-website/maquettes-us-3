How Do Clear Aligners Work?

Clear aligner treatment uses a series of custom-made, removable trays to gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions. Each tray is worn for a set period before advancing to the next in the series. Dr. Vibhakar uses digital impressions — no traditional molds or goopy material — to map your teeth precisely and design a treatment plan tailored to your alignment goals. Progress check-ins are scheduled throughout treatment to monitor your results and make adjustments as needed.

At Higher Standards Dental Studio, we treat basic to moderate alignment cases in adults. This includes crowding, spacing, mild bite issues, and general misalignment. Once treatment is complete, a retainer is provided to maintain your results long-term — an important step that we make sure every patient understands before finishing treatment.
