Common Orthodontic Emergencies

Orthodontic emergencies can range from noticeable appliance damage to smaller issues that still affect comfort or treatment progress. Hoffman Orthodontics provides emergency orthodontic care in Leawood and Prairie Village, KS for patients who run into these kinds of concerns during treatment.

Broken Brackets and Loose Bands

A broken bracket or loose band means part of the appliance is no longer secured the way it should be. When that happens, the braces may not work as intended, and the affected area can become more uncomfortable or harder to manage.

Poking Wires and Appliance Irritation

Wires can shift during treatment, and appliance parts can sometimes rub against the lips, cheeks, or gums. These problems are a common source of orthodontic discomfort and are often easy for patients to recognize because they create irritation in a very specific spot.

Lost Spacers, Rubber Bands, or Other Orthodontic Parts

Smaller orthodontic parts such as spacers, rubber bands, or other components can come loose, fall out, or go missing during treatment. Even though they may seem minor, these pieces often play an important role in how the appliance functions and how treatment moves forward.
