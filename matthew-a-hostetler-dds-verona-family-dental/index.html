Les soins

• Dental Sealants (Coatings applied to the chewing surface of permanent molars and pre-molars to help protect them from decay.)

Implants are titanium structures that are placed into the jaw bone and act as an artificial tooth root. Implants can be restored in several ways: A single tooth, part of a multi-tooth bridge, or they can be used to attach to a denture to hold it into place…

At this time we are not taking on new Orthodontic cases however we are happy to refer you for a local Orthodontic consultation!

Veneers are permanent, porcelain restorations bonded to the surface of anterior teeth. They provide the color, shape and overall appearance you desire for your teeth and are a great long-term solution to many cosmetic concerns.
