Les soins

We provide a full line of pediatric dental services, including preventive and diagnostic, aesthetic, and restorative care.

Preventive and diagnostic: Preventive and diagnostic dentistry is designed to head off future dental problems, and to catch any emerging issues as soon as possible. It is most important to prevent problems and catch them early.

Interceptive orthodontics: Early orthodontic evaluation and treatment can improve the relationship between the upper and lower jaws, and can help create space for the permanent teeth to come in. It can also prevent complicated oral surgery later in life.

Teeth will naturally lose their brightness over time. Drinking coffee or tea, or using tobacco, as well as certain childhood medications can cause teeth to discolor or darken even faster than normal.
