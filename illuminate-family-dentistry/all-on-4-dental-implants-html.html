  • You have good habits that will support the long-term success of your new smile.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 treatment offers some amazing benefits:

  • A strong bite. You can expect to be able to eat all of your favorite foods, including tough and chewy items that are off-limits with traditional dentures.
  • A beautiful smile. We carefully design All-on-4 dentures with the goal of making them as attractive and natural-looking as possible.
  • All-on-4 dentures are permanently attached to the implants that support them. In many ways, they can be treated just like natural teeth.
  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and can prevent it from deteriorating over time.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

There are a few key differences between All-on-4 treatment and traditional dental implants:

Our team can help you decide which type of treatment is best for your unique situation.

  • All-on-4 is faster and less invasive. All-on-4 usually requires just one surgery, whereas traditional implants may require two or more.
  • All-on-4 may be more cost-effective. The streamlined treatment process is more economical in some cases than full-arch tooth replacement with regular implants.
  • Traditional implants are more versatile. Traditional implants can replace any number of teeth, whereas All-on-4 is exclusively for full-arch replacement.
  • Traditional implants may be stronger. Using more implants may lend greater stability and strength to a denture.
