Our Implant Services

All-on-4® / All-on-6 / All-on-X Full-Arch Implants

If you’re tired of failing teeth, constant dental work, or loose dentures, this treatment can completely change your life. All-on-4 replaces an entire arch of teeth with a full, fixed, beautiful smile — secured on as few as four implants.

Benefits:

  • Teeth in one day (temporary fixed teeth)
  • No more slipping, clicking, or adhesive
  • Strong, secure, and natural-looking
  • Restores chewing power
  • Prevents bone loss
  • Long-lasting full-arch solution

Single Dental Implants

Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, dental implants are the gold-standard replacement option. Unlike bridges or partials, implants replace the entire tooth — root and crown — for unmatched stability and longevity.

Why patients choose single implants:

  • Natural look and function
  • Permanent solution
  • Protects surrounding teeth
  • Prevents bone loss
  • Easy to care for

Snap-In Dentures (Overdentures)

A secure, more affordable alternative to fixed implants. Snap-in dentures use dental implants to anchor a removable denture, giving you a stable fit without the slipping or discomfort of traditional dentures.

Benefits of snap-in dentures:

  • Far more stable than traditional dentures
  • Improved chewing ability
  • Less bulk, more comfort
  • No adhesive needed
  • More affordable than full fixed arches

Dental Implant FAQs

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how smooth the experience is. With gentle techniques, anesthesia, and optional sedation, you’ll feel relaxed and comfortable during the procedure. Afterward, you may feel mild soreness — similar to a routine extraction — but it typically improves quickly. Many patients go back to normal activities within a day or two.

How long do implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. With good home care and routine checkups, they can last decades — often a lifetime. They’re one of the most reliable and durable treatments in dentistry.

What’s the difference between All-on-4 and dentures?

All-on-4 gives you a fixed, secure set of teeth that stay in place 24/7. They don’t slip, click, or require adhesive. Dentures rest on the gums, which can make them less stable and limit what you can comfortably eat. Most patients find All-on-4 feels more natural, more confident, and far closer to having their real teeth again.

Can I get implants if I’ve worn dentures for years?

Yes, absolutely. Many long-time denture wearers are excellent candidates for implants. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, modern techniques — such as bone grafting, PRF, or ridge augmentation — often make implants possible. A quick 3D scan will help us determine the best plan for you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We partner with several financing providers so you can choose a comfortable monthly payment plan. Our team will walk you through all options with full transparency — no pressure, no surprises.
