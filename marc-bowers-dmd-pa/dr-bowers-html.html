Dr. Bowers is originally from Heath Springs, SC where he attended Andrew Jackson High

School and graduated in 1991. He attended the College of Charleston, Charleston, SC from

1991-1995 where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He attended the

Medical University of South Carolina, Charleston, SC from 1996-2000, where he earned his

Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.Following graduation from the Medical University of South

Carolina, he was accepted into the General Practice Residency Program at Palmetto Health

Alliance affiliated with Graduate Medical Studies at the University of South Carolina, Columbia,

SC from 2000-2001. After being in private practice in Charleston, SC for one year he attended

the Post-Graduate Residency Program in Endodontics at Boston University, Boston, MA.

While in residency at Boston University he attended numerous courses of study at the

surrounding universities, to include Tufts University and Harvard University. He completed the

program at Boston University in 2004 where he earned a Certificate of Advanced Graduate

Studies in Endodontics. Dr. Bowers has been in private practice in Easley, SC since December

of 2004 and in Seneca since August of 2012. Dr. Bowers, his wife, Margot, and three sons,

Carson (17) Hayes (13), Wells (12) reside in Greenville, SC. Dr. Bowers enjoys reading,

traveling, dining, water sports, snow skiing, hiking, salt-water fishing and spending time with

friends and family. Dr. Bowers and his family are members of Westminster Presbyterian Church
