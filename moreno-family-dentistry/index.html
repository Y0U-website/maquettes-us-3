The practice

Moreno Family Dentistry has been proudly serving our Kansas City Community since 2004. It is our mission to provide high quality dentistry in a modern, family friendly environment. Our focus is on providing dental services to the entire family from ages 0 to 100 plus. The practice offers many modern amenities such as digital radiography and intraoral photography.

Professional dental cleaning helps to remove the debris left in teeth that can cause dental problems down the road. During this session, the dentist can check for any signs of dental decay or gum disease

Cosmetic dentistry refers to various dental treatments that are performed to enhance the appearance of your teeth. There are many procedures that fall under cosmetic dentistry, including whitening, bonding, and veneers.

Pediatric dentistry focuses on the oral health of children. Our dentist has experience in treating children’s teeth, gums, and mouth. We work with children, making their visits more comfortable and less stressful. .

A crown is a "cap" that is placed over a tooth that needs repair. A crown can be used to restore a severely broken or decayed tooth, protect a weak tooth from fracturing, or cover a dental implant.

An emergency is when you are experiencing severe pain or bleeding, broken or loose teeth, or an object caught between your teeth.

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone. The jawbone must grow around these implant posts in a process called osseointegration, allowing the implant to be strong and sturdy.
