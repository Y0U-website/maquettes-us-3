Invisalign Adult

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses clear removable aligners to straighten your teeth without the use of metal braces. These custom-made aligners fit over your teeth and gently adjust their position over time. Invisalign braces for adults are a great orthodontic solution, especially considering that the aligners are virtually invisible, so most people won’t even realize you have them on!

Invisalign Treatment Process

Invisalign treatment starts with a consultation with our amazing Invisalign-trained Dentists. We will then create advanced 3D images of your teeth using the iTero® Digital Scanning System. This allows us to map out a precise, customized digital treatment plan that details the step-by-step transformation of your smile as well as the length of your treatment.

Your custom-made aligners will be created using Invisalign’s SmartTrack® material, which is virtually invisible. Once you have your aligners, you will wear each set for 20-22 hours a day, changing to a new set every 1-2 weeks. Each set of aligners will gently and gradually shift your teeth into place, following your treatment plan until you reach your perfect smile!
