The practice

Olympia Hills Junior Pediatric Dental provides laser dentistry on most restorative services. Why laser dentistry? Because it allows our team of dental professionals to treat your child without needles, drilling, or numbing! Call our office today or click here to learn more on why laser dentistry is the Gold Standard of pediatric care.

We believe in nurturing your child’s smile right from the start. Regular oral exams help ensure their whole mouth teeth, gums, and everything in between is healthy and developing properly. Our exams are interactive, helping your child learn about their teeth in a fun way while giving you peace of mind that their smiles are staying healthy.

We specialize in gently treating lip and tongue-ties, a condition where the lip or tongue is tightly attached to the mouth floor or gums. This affects basic activities like speaking or eating. Our use of a dental laser to release the tethered soft tissue for tongue and lip ties can dramatically improve children’s lives.
