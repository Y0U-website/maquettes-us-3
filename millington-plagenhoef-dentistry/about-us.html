About Our Dental Practice

Millington Plagenhoef Dentistry is a state-of-the-art dental practice located in Trussville, Alabama. We are a family practice that offers a variety of cosmetic procedures, digital x-rays, and laser technology. The purpose of Millington Plagenhoef Dentistry is to spread the love of Jesus Christ while preserving and restoring oral health in a patient-driven manner. We strive to develop lifelong relationships with our patients by combining a professional and compassionate staff with the latest in dental technology. The result is a beautiful, healthy smile that lasts a lifetime!

Meet Our Doctors

J.R. Millington, D.M.D.

J.R. was born and raised in Birmingham and graduated from Hewitt-Trussville High School in 2001. After high school, J.R. attended Auburn University where he received his Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Science. He then went on to receive his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the UAB School of Dentistry.

J.R. is married to his high school sweetheart, Amy, and they have four children: Olivia, Anna Kayt, Trip, and Brigs. J.R. and his family live in Trussville and enjoy investing back into the community. J.R., his family, and the office are supporters of local non-profits, NO LONGER and SERVING YOU MINISTRIES.

Alex Plagenhoef, D.D.S.

Dr. Plagenhoef joined our practice in August 2020, and became Partners with Dr. Millington in 2023. He is a native of Dothan, AL, and graduated from Providence Christian School in 2012. After completing his B.S. in Biomedical Sciences at Auburn University, he moved to Houston, TX, where he received training from the UTHealth School of Dentistry and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Alex met his wife, Alexandra, at Auburn University and they have been happily married since 2018. They have 2 beautiful little girls, Blair and Collins. He enjoys spending time with his family, being outdoors, hunting, fishing, snow-skiing and serving his church and community.

Dr. Plagenhoef is a member of the American Dental Association, along with the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics.
