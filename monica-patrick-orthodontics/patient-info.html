Patient Info

Patient Information Patient Information First Visit at MPO Your first visit with Dr. Monica Patrick is an exciting step toward achieving a healthier, more confident smile. During this consultation, we will evaluate your health, discuss your goals, and explore the best treatment options for you.

We will also take digital scans or X-rays, if needed, to get a clear understanding of your dental structure. Our goal is to ensure you feel informed and comfortable as we begin your orthodontic journey together!

Finance and Insurance

At MPO, we proudly accept most major insurance plans to help make your orthodontic care as affordable as possible. Our team will work with you to maximize your benefits and ensure you understand your coverage.

We also offer flexible financing options because we believe the financial aspect shouldn’t be a barrier to receiving the treatment you need. Whether through payment plans or coordinating with your insurance, we are committed to making your care accessible and stress-free.

Patient Forms
