How Dental Implants Work at Harmony Smiles

Dr. Munoz performs a comprehensive evaluation using digital X-rays and 3D imaging to assess your jawbone density, gum health, and overall oral condition. You’ll discuss your goals and receive a detailed treatment plan that outlines every step, timeline, and cost involved.

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone during a comfortable, carefully managed procedure. Over the next several months, the implant fuses with the bone through osseointegration, creating a strong, stable foundation. A temporary restoration may be placed during this healing period so you’re never without a tooth.

Once the implant has fully integrated, Dr. Munoz attaches a custom-made crown that is color-matched and shaped to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. The result is a restoration that looks, feels, and functions exactly like the real thing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental implants are a significant investment in your oral health, and we want you to feel completely informed before making a decision. Here are the questions we hear most from patients at Harmony Smiles.

Most adults with good overall health are candidates for dental implants. Key factors include having enough jawbone density to support the implant and healthy gums free from active periodontal disease. During your consultation, Dr. Munoz will use 3D imaging to evaluate your bone and determine the best approach. If additional bone support is needed, options like bone grafting may be discussed.

The full process typically takes three to six months from implant placement to final crown, with most of that time dedicated to healing and osseointegration. The actual surgical procedure is often completed in about an hour per implant. Dr. Munoz will give you a clear timeline during your consultation based on your individual situation.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won t feel pain during placement. Most patients report that the discomfort afterward is manageable with over-the-counter pain medication and fades within a few days. We also offer nitrous oxide and our full comfort menu to help keep you relaxed throughout the process.

With proper care including regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups dental implants can last 20 years or more. Many patients enjoy their implants for a lifetime. Avoiding smoking and maintaining good oral hygiene are the best ways to protect your investment.

Coverage varies by plan, but many dental insurance policies cover a portion of implant treatment especially the crown portion. Harmony Smiles is an insurance-based practice and our team will verify your benefits before treatment begins. We also offer flexible financing options so you can move forward with care regardless of your coverage.
