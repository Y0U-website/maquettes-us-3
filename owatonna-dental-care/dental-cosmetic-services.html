Cosmetic Dental Care

Professional Cosmetic Dental Care in Owatonna, MN

Owatonna Dental Care is committed to helping you achieve your dream smile. Our cosmetic dentistry services are designed to enhance the appearance of your teeth and boost your confidence. Our experienced team of dental professionals uses the latest techniques and technology to provide you with the best possible care.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a popular cosmetic dentistry service that can brighten your smile by removing stains and discoloration caused by food, drink, and lifestyle habits. Our treatments are safe and effective and can be completed in as little as one visit.

Clear Liners

Clear liners, also known as clear braces, are a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. They effectively straighten teeth without metal brackets and wires. Our team will work with you to create a personalized treatment plan that meets your needs and goals.

Bonding

Bonding is a cosmetic dental treatment that can repair chipped, cracked, or stained teeth. It involves the application of a tooth-colored resin to the affected tooth, which is then hardened with a special light. Bonding is a quick and affordable way to improve the appearance of your smile.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells placed over the front of your teeth to improve their appearance. They can correct a variety of cosmetic dental issues, including chipped, stained, or misshapen teeth. Our team will work with you to create a personalized treatment plan that meets your specific needs and goals.
