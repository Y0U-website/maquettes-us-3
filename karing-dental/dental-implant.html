Most adults in good general health who are missing one or more teeth are candidates for implants. Sufficient jawbone density is important, and patients with conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or who smoke may require evaluation. We perform a thorough consultation and imaging to determine the best path forward for your specific situation.

The process typically spans several months from implant placement to final crown attachment. After the titanium post is placed, a healing period of 3–6 months allows the implant to fuse with the bone, a process called osseointegration. The final restoration is then custom-fabricated and attached for a seamless result.

Most patients report that the implant placement procedure is far more comfortable than anticipated. Local anesthesia is used throughout, and any post-procedure soreness is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team can also discuss sedation options if you experience dental anxiety.
