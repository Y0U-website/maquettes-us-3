Benefits of Our Dental Implant Services in Frankfort, IL

Dental implants offer functional and cosmetic advantages for patients dealing with missing teeth. Many people choose implants because they provide a stable and natural-looking tooth replacement option.

Implants fill gaps left by missing teeth and help create a more complete and balanced smile.

Missing teeth can make eating certain foods more difficult. Dental implants help restore biting and chewing function.

Implants stimulate the jawbone similarly to natural tooth roots, helping reduce bone loss in the area of the missing tooth.

Some patients require extractions before orthodontic treatment to create additional space for tooth movement and alignment.

With proper care and regular dental visits, implants can remain durable and functional for many years.

Replacing missing teeth can help reduce movement of nearby teeth and maintain better alignment within the smile.

Frequently asked questions

  • 1. What are dental implants? Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots and hold replacement teeth securely in place.
  • 2. How long do dental implants last? With good oral hygiene habits and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for many years.
  • 3. Can implants replace more than one missing tooth? Yes. Dental implants may be used to replace single teeth, multiple teeth, or full arches of missing teeth.
  • 4. Do dental implants look natural? Yes. Implant restorations are customized to match surrounding teeth in color, shape, and size for a natural-looking appearance.
  • 5. Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Many adults with healthy gums and adequate jawbone structure may qualify for dental implants after a dental evaluation.
