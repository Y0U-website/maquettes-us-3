Invisalign ®

Straight teeth without an unsightly metal smile.

Change your smile without changing your lifestyle. Invisalign uses a series of clear aligner trays to straighten teeth virtually invisible. And because they are removable, you can eat and drink whatever you desire. Brushing and flossing teeth is also a breeze while on Invisalign treatment because there are no metal brackets and wires to clean, helping patients have satisfactory gum health throughout their orthodontic treatment. Dr. Nick is a Platinum Plus provider and has perfected several smiles with Invisalign clear aligners.

Invisalign for teens. Schedules of your teenagers can get quite busy, especially during school days. Invisalign Teen is made specifically for teens with one added feature – the compliance indicator. The blue dot turns white when the aligners are worn for at least 20-22 hours daily. It is very helpful for parents to keep track of their teen’s compliance in wearing their aligners.

Invisalign for adults. Invisalign is a popular treatment for working professionals who want to straighten their teeth without getting attention while they speak or talk. Invisalign aligners are clear and almost invisible to the naked eye – no one will notice you’re wearing anything until your new smile is revealed. There are no metal brackets and wires, making the experience more comfortable.
