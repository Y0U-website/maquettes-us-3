Can All-on-4® help with chewing and digestion?

All-on-4 or implant-supported dentures can aid in many things, including speaking and chewing. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 will not rock or tilt while chewing. This means you can add more foods to your diet. In fact, you’ll have no dietary restrictions.

Imagine enjoying all the foods you used to love. Imagine munching on carrot sticks and celery for a healthy snack.

Eating Healthy Foods with All-on-4

The foods you eat contribute to your oral and physical health. For this reason, we encourage our patients to choose dental implants when replacing missing teeth. Implants are like starting over with an intact bite force and a level chewing field.

All-on-4 means your Lodi or Elk Grove, CA, dentist uses approximately four to six dental implants to secure a custom denture to the jaw. You’ll not have to concern yourself with adhesives, relines, or other maintenance. You’ll be free to enjoy your smile and add the foods you love to your diet.

What Are All-on-4 Dentures?

All-on-4 uses four or more dental implants to anchor a fixed denture in place. To qualify for all-on-4 dentures, you must have adequate bone (either naturally or with bone grafting) to support the denture and the load from chewing.

Types of Sedation Dentistry We Offer

Our team monitors you closely during dental sedation, and we take a health history to ensure your candidacy.

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous oxide is a safe gas administered through a nasal cannula during your procedure. You feel detached and melancholy with nitrous, but you’re still aware. This sedation method means you’ll feel less anxious during your procedure, but there are no lingering effects. You can drive yourself home or back to work.

Oral Sedation

Oral sedation is a pill given before treatment. With this sedation method, you’ll feel completely relaxed and may not recall the finer details of your treatment. You’ll need a friend or loved one to drive you. We also recommend resting for the day and not making any life-altering decisions.
