Preventative Care

Preventative care is key to a healthy smile. During a dental exam, Dr. Low can assess the health of your teeth and your specific needs.

Dental Exams, Check-ups, and Consultations

A good dental care routine and regular check-ups will keep your teeth and mouth healthy and prevent inflammation, infection, decay -- and tooth loss. Dental exams typically should take place every six months.

Dental prophylaxis is a cleaning treatment performed to throughly clean the teeth and gums. It is an important dental treatment that can stop the progression of gingivitis and periodontal disease. Plaque and tartar removal is key to good oral health and tooth preservation.

Dr. Loe provides custom-made night guard to help alleviate jaw tension and headaches.

Dr. Loe provides custom-made snore guards to help your loved ones get a more restful night of sleep.

Restorative Dentistry

Dental treatments can give you a brighter and healthier smile, and Dr. Loe has vast experience revitalizing teeth and gums.

Dr. Loe will work to preserve and restore a tooth damaged by decay to get it back to its function.

Dr. Loe can assess whether a root canal is the best option to relieve dental pain. Root canals are often necessary to save a tooth with inflammation or infection in the roots.

Dr. Loe will consult with patients when deciding whether to salvage a tooth or extract it.

Prosthodontics

With more than 30 years of experience, Dr. Loe is highly skilled in the design and manufacture of crowns, implant crowns, bridges, and replacements for teeth, including dentures and partial dentures.

Dr. Loe can assess whether a crown is needed to restore a damaged tooth, bringing it back to its normal shape, size and function. A crown can protect a tooth and improve its appearance.

Not all teeth can be restored. Dr. Loe might recommend a dental implant crown to replace a tooth. With this procedure, an implant is inserted into your jawbone. An implant crown is then attached to the implant.

Dr. Loe can replace missing teeth with dental bridges. A dental bridge is constructed with a false tooth or teeth, which are held in place by the abutment teeth on either side of the gap.

Dr. Loe can build comfortable-fitting dentures to replace missing teeth. Partial dentures are used when some natural teeth remain.

Cosmetic Dentistry

If you would like to boost your appearance through cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Loe is here to help.

Dr. Loe can repair chipped, cracked, and uneven teeth with a cosmetic procedure known as dental bonding. This procedure corrects minor imperfections. A composite resin is applied to fill gaps and reconstruct the tooth, making it look as good as new.

Dr. Loe can apply veneers, which are thin permanent coverings placed over the front (visible) part of the tooth. Veneers, which are shaped to look natural, can be used to correct a variety of dental issues, including stubborn stains, crooked teeth, chipped or worn teeth or gaps between teeth.

Some patients opt for Zoom bleaching , an in-office treatment to lighten discoloration of the tooth enamel and dentin. Before determining whether Zoom whitening is best for you, Dr. Loe will conduct a comprehensive examination of your teeth and gums. The Zoom whitening procedure takes about an hour. Following the procedure, Dr. Loe can provide an at-home touch-up kit, including custom-made whitening trays.
