About Our Dental Practice

After 5 years living in New York and four years practicing in the Bay Area, Dr. Patel and his wife have settled in the Central Valley. In his spare time, he enjoys playing ice hockey, snowboarding, improving his cooking skills and cheering on the Bay Area sports teams. He has a passion for helping those in need, and has volunteered with the Program for Survivors of Torture, providing dental care to refugees given asylum in the United States.

Dr. Yara Abdelnabi

Growing up in a dental family, Dr. Yara found her passion for dentistry at a very young age. Dr. Yara appreciates the arts and nature, and always strives to combine her love of art with her dental career, with a greater purpose of serving the community.

Dr. Yara grew up in Alexandria, Egypt. She always knew she wanted to further her education abroad and travel the world. She recently relocated to California with her husband. In her free time, Dr. Yara loves working out, reading, hiking, and watching sitcoms.
