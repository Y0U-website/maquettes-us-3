Results and Aftercare

If you’re in good general health and practice oral hygiene on a daily basis, dental implants are often successful. Smoking, poor dental hygiene and damaging habits such as chewing hard food or ice may damage your crown just as it would a natural tooth.

Who May Have Dental Implants?

Certain factors can make them less suitable for some individuals. These factors may include:

  • Being under age 18, because teeth and jaws are still growing and developing.
  • Smoking or using tobacco products.
  • Having untreated gum disease or dental infections.
  • Having low density and volume of the jawbone.
  • Poor oral hygiene.
  • Certain health conditions, such as autoimmune diseases.
