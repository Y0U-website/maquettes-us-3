Aftercare Caring for Clear Aligners Wear them 20–22 hours a day. Remove them to eat or drink anything except water. Clean them daily with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water. Keep them away from hot water, which can warp the trays. Store them in their case, not a napkin. New trays can feel snug for a day or two – gentle pressure means the plan is working, and it settles quickly. There are no wires or brackets to irritate cheeks, and no adjustment appointments to recover from.
