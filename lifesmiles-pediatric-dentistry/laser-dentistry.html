Laser Dentistry Lifesmiles Pediatric Dentistry

Dental lasers makes fine, precise incisions. This method preserves the healthy tooth and reduces discomfort often associated with traditional procedures. Unlike drills, lasers do not cause irritating sensations like vibrations and pressure, making for a more comfortable dental experience.

Lasers assist with numbing by confusing the nerve fibers of the tooth and eliminating the sensation of pain. Dr. Gail can reduce discomfort in patients without the need for shots. If your child can’t bear needles, or if you’re searching for a gentle alternative to shots, our laser dentistry techniques will be an ideal solution for your family.

  • Removing inflamed gum tissue
  • Performing biopsies
  • Removing the frenulum, correcting tongue-tied patients
  • Treating cavities
  • Treating infections
