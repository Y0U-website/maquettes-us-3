Keeping Up Your Oral Health

Dental visits is essential to maintaining your oral health. They can help identify and fix problems with teeth or gums, and also assess the general condition of your mouth. They can also help point out potential trouble spots and offer suggestions for preventive care. In order to keep your teeth clean, free of disease, healthy gums, and fresh breath, it is important to maintain a daily oral hygiene routine. This should include the following:

A major goal of contemporary dentistry is to help you keep your natural teeth for your entire life. By maintaining a structured program of oral hygiene, you have the best possible chance of achieving this goal.

  • Brush and Floss: You should brush twice every day and floss every day to help prevent the formation of plaque, a bacteria-laden biofilm, on the surface of your teeth. Some bacteria can assist in the development of gingivitis and other gum diseases.
  • Get enough fluoride: Fluoride is vital for tooth regrowth and decay prevention in both children and adults. Fluoride paste can be applied directly to the tooth during an office appointment.
  • Limit between-meal snacks: Sugary snacks can cause tooth decay by feeding the bacteria that produce acid. To help prevent tooth decay, limit sugary snacks to mealtime.
  • Use a mouth rinse: Therapeutic rinses are more than just temporary odor or taste masks. In addition to temporarily masking bad breath and tastes, they may help to boost your overall oral hygiene. Over-the-counter products typically offer only cosmetic benefits, while therapeutic rinses also include bacterial- and cariogenic (cavity-fighting) ingredients.
  • Quit tobacco: If you use tobacco, you are at a greater risk for oral cancer, gum disease, and tooth decay. Quitting now is the best way to reduce your risk. Ask us how we can help you quit today.
  • Examine your mouth regularly: If you establish a regular oral care routine, you will be able to quickly identify any changes in your mouth, such as chipped teeth, red or swollen gums, or unusual sores. If you find anything of concern, please let us know so that we can provide early treatment, which offers the best chance for remedying many problems.
