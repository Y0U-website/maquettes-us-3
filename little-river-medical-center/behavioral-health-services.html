Care built into your medical home

Many people never follow through on a referral to an outside therapist. Keeping behavioral health inside the same organization as your primary care removes that gap. Your doctor and your counselor can share a record and a plan, which matters most for conditions like depression and diabetes that affect each other.

Medication assisted treatment

For patients working to recover from opioid or alcohol use, we offer medication assisted treatment that pairs medication with counseling and support. Learn more on our Medication Assisted Treatment page.

Coverage and getting started

We offer individualized care plans for adults, teens and children; and we encourage family participation.

The goal of our integrated behavioral health services is to improve your overall well-being by helping you develop techniques to resolve health or mental health issues affecting your daily life.

You must be a LRMC patient to receive integrated behavioral health services.

We accept Medicaid, Medicare, and most commercial insurance, and we offer a sliding fee scale for uninsured patients based on household size and income. Behavioral health services are for established LRMC patients, so the first step is to become a patient or talk with your current LRMC provider about a referral.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take Medicaid for behavioral health?

Yes. We accept Medicaid and Medicare, most commercial plans, and offer a sliding fee scale for uninsured patients.

Do you see children and teens?

Yes. Our team provides behavioral health care for children, teens, and adults.

Do I have to be an LRMC patient?

Behavioral health services are generally available to established LRMC patients. If you are not a patient yet, you can register first or ask your current provider for a referral.

What should I do in a mental health emergency?

Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline any time, or call 911 if someone is in immediate danger.
