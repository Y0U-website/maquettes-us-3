A historic home for exceptional dentistry.

Located in the heart of downtown Ball Ground, our practice occupies a beautifully restored historic Mercantile building — a landmark on Ball Ground Main Street.

Warm, calm, and nothing like a dental office.

From the moment you walk in you will notice this is not your average dental waiting room. The waiting area is anchored by a golden tree mural, an antique fireplace, boutique armchairs, and warm natural light from our large street-facing windows. We want you to feel at home before your name is even called.

Help yourself to water, take a seat, and relax. We run on time because we respect yours.

Clinical precision in a home-like setting.

Our treatment rooms combine the clinical capability of a full-service prosthodontic practice with the warmth and calm of a warm, home-like space. Exposed red brick walls, large windows with natural light, slate-blue dental chairs, and warm hardwood floors create an environment that feels very different from a traditional dental office.

Because we believe the space you sit in matters just as much as the care you receive in it.
