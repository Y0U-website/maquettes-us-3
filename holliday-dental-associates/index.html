Dr. Fairchild “Faira” Holliday Payne grew up at Holliday Dental Associates. Since graduating from MCG’s Dental College of Georgia in 2015 she has practiced in 4 private practices and taught future dentists full time at the Dental College in Augusta for 3 years. We are thrilled to have her back home!

“I am honored and excited to be here, and be able to continue my father’s and grandfather’s legacy of bringing compassionate, gentle dentistry to Macon.” -Dr. Faira

Dr. Holliday has been practicing Gentle Dentistry on Spring St in Macon for a very long time. He is very active in the local community and government and is a wonderful philanthropist. Click the button below to learn more about his memberships and community activities.
