  • This course provides dental auxiliary clinicians with essential knowledge and clinical skills for the safe and effective administration of local anesthesia. Topics include neuroanatomy, pharmacology, patient assessment, injection techniques, pain management, and emergency protocols. The curriculum integrates lectures, independent study, clinical training and hands-on practice to develop competency in preparation, administration, and management of local anesthesia.
  • Upon successful completion of this course, students will receive a Certificate of Completion and will meet the educational requirements to apply for either the Oregon Local Anesthesia Functions (LAF) Certificate administered by the Dental Assisting National Board (DANB) or the Local Anesthesia Endorsement for Dental Hygienists.

FAQs for Local Anesthesia Functions

The course is 6 weeks, and is a hybrid model. There are 6 weeks of online lecture learning material and in-person labs. The labs will be spread out over 4 Fridays. The first Friday is a full day, 8-5, and the following three Fridays are half days (8-12).

At our dental assisting school located on 5690 Main St Springfield, OR 97478. The school shares a building with McKenzie Dental Center.

The course is Oregon Board of Dentistry approved, and follows a strict syllabus. We cover all the same materials as a dental hygiene course. Anatomy, injection techniques, medical emergencies, pharmacology, neurophysiology and neuroanatomy.

We provide all materials for the anesthesia labs. Students will need their own textbook, Liability Insurance, and a recommended laptop/computer/tablet for the online portion.

Assistant Eligibility: Applicants must hold either an Oregon Expanded Function Dental Assistant (EFDA) Certification OR an Oregon Certified Anesthesia Dental Assistant (DAANCE) Certificate, have at least three years of recent dental assisting experience, and maintain a current BLS for Healthcare Providers certification (or equivalent). Hygienist Eligibility: Applicants must hold a current Oregon Dental Hygiene License and maintain a valid BLS for Healthcare Providers certification (or equivalent).

The course ends with a two-part final, in which students must pass the multiple-choice exam with a 75 % or better, and complete an injection portion where they must pass a PSA injection and an Inferior Alveolar injection practical. After successfully completing all requirements, the student is awarded a certificate.

PSA, MSA, ASA, AMSA, GP, NP, mental/incisive, Long Buccal, Gow-Gates, Inferior Alveolar nerve block, and infiltrations.
