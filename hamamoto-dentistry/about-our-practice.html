Expert Dentists in Bothell

Our doctors bring years of experience and a deep passion for personalized care.

Dr. Stefanie Hamamoto

A Bothell native and University of Washington alumna, Dr. Hamamoto combines advanced training in esthetic dentistry, Invisalign, and soft-tissue surgery with a warm, communicative approach. Her goal is to make patients feel seen, heard, and cared for at every stage of life.

Dr. Garret Yamaguchi

With expertise in restorative and implant dentistry, Dr. Yamaguchi provides precise, detail-oriented care for patients needing everything from fillings to full-mouth rehabilitation. He is dedicated to ongoing education and delivering conservative, long-lasting results.
