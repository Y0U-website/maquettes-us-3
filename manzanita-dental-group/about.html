Background and Training

Dr. Sandhu earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Howard University College of Dentistry in Washington, D.C. in 2012. He began his career in State College, Pennsylvania, where he co-owned and operated a multi-provider general dental practice for several years before relocating to California. He has been practicing dentistry for over 13 years, serving patients across Pennsylvania and California, and he remains committed to continuing education to stay current with advances in general and restorative dentistry.
