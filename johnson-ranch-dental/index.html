The practice

Initial exam: The dentist will examine your teeth, gums, and mouth to assess your oral health. This may include taking x-rays to get a better look at your teeth and mouth.

Cleaning: The dentist or dental hygienist will clean your teeth to remove plaque and tartar buildup. This is called a professional cleaning or prophylaxis.

Treatment plan: If the dentist finds any issues during the initial exam, they may recommend a treatment plan. This may include fillings, crowns, extractions, or other procedures to address any problems.

Education: The dentist may also provide education on how to properly care for your teeth and gums at home, including proper brushing and flossing techniques.

Follow-up: The dentist may recommend that you come back for regular check-ups to maintain your oral health. These visits will help the dentist monitor your progress and address any issues that may arise.

It’s important to remember that every person’s experience at the dentist is different, and your visit may be different from someone else’s. If you have any concerns or questions, be sure to ask the dentist or dental staff for more information.
