Accessibility Statement

for Koczarski Family & Aesthetic Dentistry

Koczarski Family & Aesthetic Dentistry is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all patients and visitors, including those with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Accessibility Standards We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which provide guidance on making web content more accessible for individuals with a wide range of disabilities.

Ongoing Efforts Our team is actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website. We regularly review our site and make improvements to enhance accessibility and ensure a better experience for all users.

Third-Party Content While we strive to ensure accessibility across our website, some third-party integrations (such as scheduling tools or chat features) may not be fully accessible. We encourage users to contact us if they encounter any issues.

We will do our best to provide the information or services you need in an accessible format.

Feedback We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. Please let us know if you encounter accessibility barriers so we can address them promptly.
