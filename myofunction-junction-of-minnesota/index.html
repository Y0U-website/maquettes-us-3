Myofunctional therapy focuses on how the tongue, mouth, and facial muscles function together to support proper breathing, chewing, swallowing, and speech. When these patterns are not functioning as they should, they can affect the way the teeth, jaw, and airway develop over time.

Through targeted exercises and guided therapy, these patterns can be improved to support better function, comfort, and long-term stability.

Supports proper breathing patterns and helps maintain a healthy, open airway.

Encourages proper tongue positioning and addresses habits that affect function and development.

Supports more restful sleep while reducing strain on the jaw and surrounding muscles.
