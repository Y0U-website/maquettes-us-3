The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends visiting the dentist at least twice a year. These checkups, along with professional cleanings, can help detect dental problems in their early stages. From there, if an issue such as gingivitis is present, a comprehensive treatment program can be tailored to save time and money, preserving one of your greatest assets- your smile.

Preventative services include comprehensive cleanings and exams, scaling, X-rays, and other diagnostics designed to prevent conditions like gum disease and address them before they become problematic. It’s critical to visit the dentist at least twice per year as part of a prevention program, even if you don’t currently have dental issues.
