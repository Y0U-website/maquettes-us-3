The Dental Implant Restoration Process

At Limberakis Family Dentistry , we are committed to providing seamless and effective implant restoration treatments. Here’s what you can expect during the process:

1. Comprehensive Evaluation

We begin with a thorough examination of your dental implant to assess the condition of the crown, abutment, and implant post. Digital X-rays may be used to evaluate the integrity of the implant and surrounding bone structure.

2. Customized Treatment Plan

Once the issue has been identified, we’ll create a personalized restoration plan. This may involve repairing the existing crown or replacing it entirely with a new, custom-made crown that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

3. Restoration Procedure

Depending on the extent of the damage, we will either repair the affected components or replace them. Our team uses high-quality materials to ensure that your restored implant looks and functions just like a natural tooth.

4. Follow-Up Care

After your implant restoration, we’ll provide instructions for maintaining your dental implant and ensuring its longevity. Regular check-ups at our office will help keep your restored implant in top condition.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restoration

Restoring your dental implant ensures that your smile remains both functional and beautiful. Some of the key benefits include:

  • Improved Appearance : A well-maintained implant blends seamlessly with your natural teeth, restoring your smile’s aesthetic appeal.
  • Enhanced Function : A properly restored implant allows you to chew, speak, and smile with ease.
  • Long-Term Durability : With proper care and maintenance, your restored implant can last for many years.
  • Preservation of Oral Health : Addressing any issues with your implant promptly helps prevent further damage to surrounding teeth and gums.
