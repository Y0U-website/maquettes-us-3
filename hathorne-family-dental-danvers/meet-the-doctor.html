Dr. Keerthy Chilakamarry, DMD

Dr. Keerthy Chilakamarry is a dedicated and compassionate dentist with a passion for providing exceptional patient care, with 10 years of experience in the field. The doctor has honed her skills in all aspects of dentistry, from routine cleanings and fillings to complex restorative procedures.

Dr. Keerthy earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery DMD degree from BU and has since remained committed to staying at the forefront of the latest dental techniques and technologies. She is a member of the ADA, the AGD, and the MDS.

The doctor is deeply committed to her patients’ well-being and takes the time to listen to their concerns and develop personalized treatment plans that meet their unique needs. Her warm and gentle approach has earned her a reputation as a trusted and caring dentist, and she looks forward to welcoming you to her practice.
