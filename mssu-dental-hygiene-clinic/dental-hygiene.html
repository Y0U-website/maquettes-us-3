Career Outlook for Dental Hygienists

Dental hygienists play an important role in preventive oral healthcare and are in demand across a variety of healthcare settings.

Dental hygienists work in dental offices, public health clinics, educational settings, and other healthcare environments while helping patients maintain lifelong oral health.

  • Median annual wage: $94,260 (May 2024)
  • Projected job growth: 7 % from 2024 2034, faster than the average for all occupations
