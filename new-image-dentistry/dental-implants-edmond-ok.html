FAQs

  • Will getting dental implants be painful? While the idea of undergoing dental surgery may seem daunting, our team at New Image Dentistry P.C. prioritizes patient comfort throughout the process. We utilize local anesthesia to ensure that you feel little to no discomfort during the procedure. Additionally, we offer options for sedation dentistry to help patients relax during treatment. After the procedure, any discomfort can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. The titanium posts used in dental implants are biocompatible and fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable foundation for the restoration. However, it’s essential to maintain good oral hygiene and attend regular dental check-ups to ensure the longevity of your implants.
  • Are dental implants suitable for everyone? While dental implants are a highly effective option for most people with missing teeth, certain factors may affect candidacy. These include the quality and quantity of jawbone available to support the implants, overall oral health, and any underlying medical conditions. During your consultation at New Image Dentistry P.C., our dentist in Edmond, OK, will evaluate your specific situation and discuss whether dental implants are the right choice for you.
  • How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants can vary depending on several factors, including the number of implants needed, any additional procedures required, and the type of restoration used. During your consultation, we’ll provide you with a detailed treatment plan, including a breakdown of costs. While dental implants may require a higher initial investment than other tooth replacement options, their longevity and benefits often make them a cost-effective solution in the long run.
