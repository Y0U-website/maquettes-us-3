What to Expect After Reporting

We will acknowledge your report and work to provide a response within five business days. Where a resolution requires technical changes, we will communicate a timeline for remediation. In the meantime, we will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or functionality you need through an alternative method.

Third-Party Content

Our website may include content or tools provided by third parties — such as online scheduling tools or embedded maps. These third-party elements may not be fully within our control, and their accessibility may vary. We encourage third-party providers to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and will raise accessibility concerns with them when identified.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility concern, you have the right to contact the relevant regulatory authority in your jurisdiction. In the United States, accessibility complaints related to websites may be directed to the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division. For additional information about your rights as a patient, please review our Privacy Policy or visit our Patient Information page.
