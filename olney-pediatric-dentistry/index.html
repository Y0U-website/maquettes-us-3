At Olney Pediatric Dentistry, we take great pride in being a family-owned and operated practice. Founded by Dr. Jenny Rhee, our team is passionate about creating a nurturing environment where your child can feel completely at ease while receiving exceptional pediatric dental care. Thoughtfully designed with little ones in mind, our practice ensures that every visit is not only enjoyable but also…

Dr. Jenny is a proud native of the DC metropolitan area and a board-certified pediatric dentist with over a decade of experience. She has dedicated her career to providing exceptional dental care for infants, children, adolescents, and individuals with special needs.

For Dr. Jenny, the most rewarding aspect of her work is building lasting relationships with families while enhancing their children’s oral health. She takes great joy in watching her youngest patients grow up in her care and develop healthy, confident smiles.

After years of practicing in various counties across Maryland, Dr. Jenny is thrilled to bring her expertise to her local community with the opening of Olney Pediatric Dentistry, where she lives and raises her family. She considers it a privilege to care for children and looks forward to providing exceptional dental care that fosters lifelong oral health.

At Olney Pediatric Dentistry, your child’s comfort and happiness are at the heart of everything we do. Our warm, welcoming, and fun environment helps every child feel safe, supported, and right at home. Whether it’s a routine cleaning, cavity treatment, or a second opinion, we personalize each visit to meet your child’s unique needs, making dental care a positive and stress-free experience.

Your baby’s first dental visit is a key milestone in their oral health journey. We create a gentle, welcoming environment to introduce your little one to the dentist. During this visit, we’ll assess their oral development, provide teething tips, and guide you on proper gum and tooth care for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

We prioritize excellent preventive care to keep your child’s smile healthy and bright. Our services include thorough exams, gentle cleanings, and digital X-rays for early detection of dental issues. We follow the ADA’s recommendation for biannual cleanings and exams to maintain optimal oral health.

Les soins

We’re also big believers in prevention and education. From brushing and flossing tips to guidance on healthy nutrition, we empower families with the tools to support lifelong oral health.
