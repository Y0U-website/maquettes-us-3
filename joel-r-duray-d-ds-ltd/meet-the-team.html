Meet The Team Duray & Kovar DDS…

Dr Joel Duray

Dr. Joel was born and raised in East Grand Forks. He graduated with his undergrad at Concordia College Moorhead. He continued his education receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. Dr. Duray spent 1 year in a general practice residency in the Army. He then continued to serve for 2 years in Germany providing dental care. When not working Dr. Duray enjoys spending time on the water with his wife, and Golfing.

When not working Dr. Kovar enjoys spending time with his wife Hannah, Daughter Nora. He enjoys reading, exercising and rock climbing.
