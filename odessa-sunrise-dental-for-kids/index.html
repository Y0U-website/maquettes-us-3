The practice

Odessa Sunrise Dental for Kids started with a passion to create meaningful experiences that help children feel confident and unafraid in the dental chair, taking time to educate, build trust, and invite parents into the conversation. So, settle in and smile big – Dr. Brenda can’t wait to welcome you in!

We provide gentle, effective care for cavities with services like composite fillings, lab-made crowns, and pulpotomies, all tailored to meet your child’s needs without unnecessary stress.

Early dental care lays the foundation for a lifetime of healthy habits. We offer tailored appointments for patients three and under to help families get started on the right foot with age-appropriate guidance and gentle care.

Every child deserves a great experience, from the first hello to the goodie bag when they leave. We go the extra mile to build trust, reduce fear, and create a place kids want to return to (and equip them with the tools they need to maintain healthy oral habits at-home too).

A space created just for Young Patients From ceiling TVs in treatment rooms to a fun mural in the lobby and our scoop machine, our office is designed to make visits fun, positive, and kid-friendly from the moment your family walks in.

Family-friendly visits Dr. Manzanares is a family-friendly dentist with extensive experience treating children. We’re here to provide high-quality care backed by the latest techniques in children’s dentistry.

Focused on education and prevention We empower both kids and parents with the knowledge and guidance they need to keep smiles cavity-free and avoid unnecessary treatments down the line.

Compassion-led care that builds trust Every child is different, which is why we tailor our approach to keep patients calm and confident using creativity, communication, and empathy to make every visit as smooth as possible.
