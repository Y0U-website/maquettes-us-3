Recognizing that our dentists and dental specialists may be facing delays in securing PPE, Medina County Emergency Management is graciously sending our members surplus face masks to be used in their practices.

Medina County Dental Society would like to welcome our newest incoming board member, Dr. Chris Sinick. A General Dentist, Dr. Chris Sinick runs his Medina-based practice, Sinick Family Dental , with his wife, Dr. Jessica Sinick. We’re thrilled that Chris has agreed to join the board as the incoming Secretary at the end of May 2020. This means he’ll be slated to lead the Medina County Dental Society as President by 2023.

We know this is a difficult time for all our members as they navigate the spread of COVID-19 and its impact on their families, friends, practices, and careers. We wish you all the best as you ride out this time of uncertainty.
