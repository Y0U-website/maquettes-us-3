For Individuals Who Are Deaf or Hard of Hearing

Closed Captioning. Closed captioning provides a synced, on-screen transcript of the audio for video content. This helps individuals who are deaf or hard of hearing, as well as anyone watching in a noisy environment. Most of our video content includes captions — you can learn how to turn captions on or off on YouTube.

Volume Controls. Your computer, tablet, or mobile device has built-in volume controls, and most video and audio players include their own volume settings as well. Adjusting both can help you find the listening experience that works best for you.
