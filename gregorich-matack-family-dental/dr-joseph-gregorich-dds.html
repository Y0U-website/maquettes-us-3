Dr. Joe Gregorich has been practicing dentistry in his hometown of Duluth, MN since 2008. Growing up in Duluth Heights, his education journey began at Holy Rosary School until high school where it continued at Duluth Central. He left the area for 8 years to earn a bachelor’s degree at Saint John’s University then his doctorate at the University of Minnesota. Dr. Joe considers dentistry a passion, a hobby he absolutely loves, and considers it a privilege to practice.

Although Dr. Joe performs most general dental procedures, he has carved out a niche in implant dentistry and finds it to be his favored. Taking several advanced courses prior, he began incorporating implants into his practice in 2011.

Dr. Joe takes a high level of pride in his work. He assigns personal commitment to complete each procedure to the best of his ability and stands behind it once complete.

Dr. Joe is a family man. He and his wife, Stacy, have 3 children (1 son and 2 daughters). They enjoy traveling together, especially to their favorite place on earth”, the family cabin in Ely. Dr. Joe enjoys working out, checking things off his “adventures untaken” list, and is most at home in the outdoors.

Dr. Joe’s quote alignment is one from Erma Bombeck: “When I stand before God at the end of my life, I would hope that I would not have a single bit of talent left, and could say, ‘I used everything you gave me.” This is the driving force he uses during patient treatment – he treats his patients in a committed and passionate way, not settling for less than an excellent outcome.
