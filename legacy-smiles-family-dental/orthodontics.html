What Can Orthodontic Treatment Address?

Orthodontic care is designed to correct malocclusion, which refers to problems with how the teeth and jaws fit together. Common concerns that may benefit from orthodontic treatment include crowding or overlapping teeth, spacing or gaps, overbite, underbite, crossbite, open bite, uneven tooth wear, and jaw discomfort related to misalignment.

Improving alignment can support better brushing and flossing, reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease, protect teeth from uneven wear, and help patients feel more confident in their smiles.

The Orthodontic Process

Consultation and Planning

Your orthodontic consultation includes an exam, digital imaging, and a discussion about your goals. Your dentist will evaluate tooth positions, jaw relationships, and oral health to create a personalized treatment plan.

Active Treatment

During treatment, teeth are gradually moved into better positions through braces adjustments or aligner changes. Routine progress visits allow our team to monitor movement and make adjustments as needed.

Retention and Long-Term Stability

After active orthodontic treatment is complete, wearing retainers as directed helps maintain your results. Teeth have a natural tendency to shift, so long-term retainer wear is an important part of protecting your investment in a healthier smile.

Orthodontic Care for All Ages

Orthodontic treatment is not limited to children and teens. Adults with healthy gums and bone support can also benefit from alignment correction. Early evaluation for children can help guide jaw growth and identify developing issues before they become more complex.

Our team works with each patient to recommend the right timing and approach based on their individual needs.
