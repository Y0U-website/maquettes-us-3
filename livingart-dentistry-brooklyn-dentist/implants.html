Implants

  • Refresh your smile with a natural-looking, beautiful tooth replacement
  • Restore the ability to chew properly and speak clearly
  • Protect the health of the surrounding teeth and bone
  • Require no additional cleaning rituals or maintenance
  • Preserve facial structure for a more youthful appearance
  • Replace a single missing tooth with a full arch

Your Implant can be completed in Five to Six visits

Appointment 1:

Appointment 2:

After carefully planning your implant, Dr. Levingart is ready to insert the screw into the bone. The area will be thoroughly numb with local anesthesia and Nitrous Oxide may be offered to allow the most comfortable experience. The implant will be carefully placed by the doctor with bone grafting material and closed tightly with sutures.

Appointment 3:

This is a three-week follow-up appointment to ensure healing is going well!

Appointment 4:

Three months after the placement of the screw, you will be ready for the uncovering of the implant and preparation for the custom abutment and crown for the area! A 3D scan will be taken again to evaluate healing. Impressions will be taken. A healing screw will be placed into the uncovered implant for further healing while the final abutment is being made in the lab.

Appointment 5:

The custom abutment is placed and prepared. A shade for the crown is chosen with our patient and sent to the lab for final fabrication.

Appointment 6:

The implant crown is fit, occlusion is checked, and a final x-ray is taken to evaluate the ideal fit.
