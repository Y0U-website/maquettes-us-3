Expert Orthodontists

Our team of orthodontists aims to solve your child’s orthodontic problems early on while they are still growing. We offer a variety of treatment options for kids, including:

Our orthodontists can also treat adults! We offer a range of options to correct and enhance your smile, from touch-ups to full orthodontic treatment:

Our orthodontists are ready to help you straighten your smile. Book an appointment with us now!

  • Clear and metal braces: We offer both clear ceramic braces and metal braces that are used to align your kiddo’s teeth and correct their bite.
  • Invisalign: Using a series of custom-made, clear, removable aligners, your child can straighten their teeth and change out aligners for a new set every two weeks.
  • Oral appliances: Our oral appliances help protect against sports-related tooth damage and concussions, and can also treat the causes and effects of teeth grinding and sleep apnea.
  • Traditional orthodontia: We offer all the options you need, including braces, to get the straight smile you deserve.
  • Invisalign Lite: Designed specifically for adults who have previously had orthodontic treatment, Invisalign Lite involves a series of clear and removable custom-made aligners. Your teeth will typically be straightened in six to nine months.
  • Clear braces: Clear ceramic braces will straighten your teeth and correct your bite in about six to nine months.
  • Teeth whitening: We offer take-home teeth whitening kits, which are an easy and safe way to brighten your smile.
  • Oral appliances: These appliances offer protection against tooth damage and sports-related injuries.

Dental Service Types

No matter what your kiddo may need, we’re here to keep them smiling from first tooth to teens.

Pediatric Dentistry

We offer a variety of pediatric dentistry services, including preventative care, sealants, teeth x-rays, fluoride treatments, dental exams, pediatric tooth extractions, and much more. At Kids Care Dental, we aim to educate as well. We offer brushing and flossing lessons so children can develop life-long positive oral health habits. We’re eager to help your kiddo learn how to care for their teeth.

Orthodontics

Whether you need clear or metal braces, Invisalign®, or oral appliances, we’ve got you covered. Kids Care Dental offers orthodontics for both adults and children, and we accept most insurances as well as DentaQuest, so you don’t have to stress about perfecting your smile.

Oral Surgery

If your kiddo requires wisdom tooth extraction or surgical molar removal, we’ve got you covered. We offer pediatric oral surgery services that can correct any dental issues your child may be experiencing.

Surgery Center

In some cases, your child’s pediatric dentist may recommend completing their dental treatment at a surgery center—medical facilities equipped to administer general anesthesia. This allows our team to safely complete care while your child rests in a deep sleep, similar to the sleep experienced during procedures like ear tube placement or tonsil removal.
