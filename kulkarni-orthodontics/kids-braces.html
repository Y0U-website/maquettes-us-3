Braces for Kids & Teens

  • Cover the loose bracket with orthodontic wax to protect the cheek.
  • If a wire is poking, use wax or a clean pencil eraser to gently push it flat.
  • Call us the next business day so we can schedule a repair.

Knocked or displaced tooth

  • Find the tooth and hold it by the crown (top), never the root.
  • Rinse gently with milk or saline; do not scrub or dry it.
  • If possible, place the tooth back in the socket and bite down on gauze.
  • Call us immediately — or go to the nearest emergency dentist / ER.

Soreness or pain

  • Rinse with warm salt water to soothe irritated gums.
  • Floss gently around the painful area to remove trapped food.
  • Use a cold compress on the cheek and an age-appropriate pain reliever.
  • Call if pain is severe, swollen, or lasts more than 48 hours.

Need help right now?

Call your home office. For life-threatening injuries, severe facial trauma, or bleeding that won&#x27;t stop, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency room first.

  • • Severe facial trauma or jaw fracture
  • • Uncontrolled bleeding after 10 minutes of pressure
  • • Trouble breathing or swallowing
  • • Knocked-out permanent tooth — time sensitive
