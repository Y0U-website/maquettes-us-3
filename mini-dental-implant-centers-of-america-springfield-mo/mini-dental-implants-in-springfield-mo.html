The Mini Dental Implant Procedure: An Overview

The Mini Implant Consultation

At this visit, we will also take precise 3D CBCT scans to evaluate your jawbone structure, oral health status, and specific restorative needs. We’ll also take digital impressions so the dental lab can create your custom restoration. These steps are crucial for customizing your treatment plan and ensuring the best possible outcomes with mini dental implants.

Mini Dental Implant Placement

At the beginning of your procedure, we’ll numb the treatment site with local anesthesia to keep you completely comfortable. Then, we’ll create a small pilot hole through the gums and precisely position the implant in the jawbone.

Once your mini implant is in place, we’ll usually attach a temporary restoration the same day , so you never have to go without teeth. While you enjoy your restored smile, our dental lab will craft your custom, permanent tooth or teeth. When ready, we’ll replace the temporary with your final restoration.

This simple, minimally invasive process means less downtime, so you can get back to your normal routine quickly, with a smile you’ll love.

Post-Procedure Care

Due to their differences in size, mini implants and traditional implants differ significantly in their aftercare and recovery processes. Mini implants, being smaller and less invasive, have a much quicker and easier recovery period. Patients often experience minimal discomfort and can usually resume their routines shortly after the procedure, typically within a day or two.

However, just like their traditional counterparts, the longevity and success of mini dental implants depend largely on the quality of your oral hygiene routine. You should brush twice daily for at least two minutes each time, and floss and rinse daily to prevent plaque from building up around your implants or restoration.

At your regular six-month cleanings, we will evaluate the condition of your implants and restoration, making any adjustments or recommendations as needed.

The Benefits of Mini Dental Implants

Mini dental implants offer big benefits in a smaller, more versatile design:

  • They facilitate quicker healing: Thanks to their slim form, mini implants eliminate the need for incisions, stitches, and surgical procedures like bone grafting. As a result, patients often recover within 48 hours.
  • They have broader applications: Their narrow design makes mini implants suitable for patients with less jawbone volume. Many people are ineligible for conventional implants because they have lost too much bone, a natural process after tooth loss. Fortunately, mini implants do not require nearly as much space, making them an ideal solution.
  • They reduce risks and complications: The one-piece design of mini implants makes them easier to keep clean, lowering the chance of infection around the implant. Because they’re minimally invasive, they also disturb the tissue less, which helps prevent healing issues.
  • They are more cost-effective: Mini implant placement is faster and more straightforward, reducing the treatment duration and costs.
