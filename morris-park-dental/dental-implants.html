What is a dental implant?

Morris Park Dental provides affordable dental implants in the Bronx. Dental implants can improve your quality of life by helping you gain back a healthy, functional and beautiful smile.

Implants can replace individual or multiple missing teeth. Implants blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, and you will experience the confidence that comes from having your smile complete again. Dental implants are durable and long lasting, and they allow you to eat all the foods that are required in a healthy, balanced diet.

Who’s a good candidate for dental implants?

Anyone healthy enough for tooth extractions and oral surgery may qualify for dental implants. You’re an ideal candidate if:

Chronic diseases such as diabetes or heart disease, a history of radiation therapy or chemotherapy, and a history of tobacco use may affect healing after implant placement and should be discussed with your dentist at your consultation appointment prior to proceeding with implant treatment.

  • You have at least one severely damaged tooth
  • You aren’t comfortable with dentures or are unable to wear them
  • You have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the implants
  • You are dedicated to good oral hygiene and routine checkups after the procedure

Explore Our Dental Implant Solutions in the Bronx

Implant Overdentures Are you frustrated with removable dentures? Dental implants in the Bronx can be used to stabilize your denture providing an immediate and long-term improvement in comfort, chewing ability, and quality of life.

Loose and ill-fitting dentures can be uncomfortable and embarrassing. Some patients find that they are unable to tolerate conventional dentures at all.

With conventional dentures, patients often need to use denture adhesive, which can be messy and make dentures difficult to clean. Dentures can also cover taste buds resulting in a decreased sense of taste.

The addition of dental implants may result in a denture that is less bulky and does not cover the entire roof of the mouth, like conventional dentures, thus enhancing sense of taste and preventing gagging.

Dental implants placed in the bone can be used to stabilize existing or new dentures. Implant overdentures are removable and easy to clean, but must be removed every evening for proper hygiene.

All-On-4 All-on-4 is a fixed bridge supported by just 4 implants. The bridge does not rest on the gums or cover the roof of the mouth at all, like an implant overdenture or conventional denture would. In addition, the implants and bridge can be placed the same day that teeth are taken out.

The All-on-4 fixed bridge is screwed into the dental implants and can only be removed by your dentist. It is easy to clean using a waterpik and toothbrush.

Our All-on-4 here in the Bronx proves that this fixed bridge is a cost-effective option for replacing all of your missing teeth for patients who are unhappy with their dentures or have teeth that cannot be saved.
