Patient FAQs

Q: When and where is the next MidMOM clinic? A: The next MidMOM clinic will be held Jan 22-23, 2027 at Bellevue Baptist Church in Memphis, TN. Please continue checking back on our website, Facebook, and Instagram for more information.

Q: Do I need an appointment to receive care? A: No, all patients will be seen on a first come, first serve basis. There are no tickets, vouchers, or appointment spots to be purchased or reserved.

Q: Do I need a driver’s license to receive care? A: No, patients are not asked for any type of identification or paperwork to receive treatment. There are no eligibility requirements.

Q: Can I receive care if I don’t live in Tennessee? A: Yes, there aren’t requirements or restrictions regarding who is eligible to receive treatment. We see patients on a first come, first serve basis until we reach capacity for the day.

Q: What treatment does MidMOM provide? A: We offer fillings, cleanings, and extractions based on the most immediate need. We are able to do a limited number of other procedures each year (excluding dentures, braces, crowns/capping teeth, extractions/procedures that require sedation). You will receive an exam from one of our volunteer dentists to determine what can be done at MidMOM in the time available and with the resources available.

Q: Can I get a partial at MidMOM? A: Possibly, if the doctor determines you are a candidate.

Q: What services are NOT provided at MidMOM? A : Unfortunately, we are unable to provide dentures, braces, crowns/capping teeth, extractions/procedures that require sedation.

Q: Is there anything that would prevent me from receiving treatment? A: Yes. You’ll receive a medical exam to ensure you’re able to receive treatment. Some things may prevent you from receiving treatment include, but are not limited to: fever; uncontrollable, high blood pressure; history of bleeding; certain medications (immunosuppressants, blood thinners, etc.).

Q: Will food and water be provided? A : We will provide water during the clinic day.

Q: What should I bring? A: We recommend bringing a list of your current medications and a one day supply of all medications. If you have children with you, please bring the appropriate supplies (diapers, bottles, food, etc.) for the day as these items will not be available through the clinic. You may want to bring a snack in case there are long wait periods. Additionally, if you are diabetic, please bring appropriate snacks that you might require throughout the day.

Q: What kind of time frame should I expect when being seen at MidMOM? A: You should expect to spend the majority of your day with us. By the time you wait in line, have your medical screening, dental screening, and treatment completed, it could be a 6-8 hour (or longer) day. Please be prepared for that amount of time.
