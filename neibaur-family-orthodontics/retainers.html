Dental Retainers

  • Removable Retainers: Often the precursor to fixed appliances, they’re crafted from plastic and come in assorted sizes. As the name suggests, they’re easily removable for cleaning.
  • Essix Retainers: These transparent plastic structures resemble mouth guards and cater to minor dental corrections like crowding or spacing.
  • Hawley Retainers: Crafted with an acrylic body, they sport a wire front connected to your molars, and a horseshoe-shaped back to ensure a comfortable fit.

The Role of Retainers Post-Orthodontic Treatments

Neibaur Family Orthodontics emphasizes the importance of wearing retainers post-orthodontic treatment to sustain the achieved results. With time, teeth tend to shift naturally; hence, wearing a retainer prevents reversion. It also ensures the newly sculpted smile remains intact, giving time for the surrounding tissues to adapt to the dental changes.

Duration of Retainer Use

Post-treatment at Neibaur Family Orthodontics, while braces or Invisalign might be a thing of the past, retainers become an essential nightly companion. Depending on individual needs, retainers might be a short-term or long-term commitment to ensure the teeth remain corrected. Some individuals with retainer-dependent occlusions may need lifelong nightly use.

Consequences of Neglecting Retainers

The Neibaur Family Orthodontics team observes that patients neglecting their retainers might see their teeth gradually revert. While it doesn’t nullify the orthodontic procedure, it does mean undergoing the correction process again. Inadequate retainer use can also lead to complications like speech impediments, chewing problems, and jaw discomfort.

Hawley Retainers: Preserving Your Perfect Smile

After the commitment of undergoing orthodontic treatment, ensuring the longevity of your newly straightened teeth becomes paramount. Enter the Hawley retainer, a dental device tailored to ensure your teeth remain in their desired positions after braces. This removable retainer, made of clear plastic, comfortably encompasses your teeth, acting like a shield against undesirable shifts.

What Is a Hawley Retainer?

Akin to a mouthguard, a Hawley retainer is a transparent device crafted from acrylic or medical-grade plastic. We customize this retainer for every patient, molding it meticulously to ensure it fits snugly around your teeth and gum line. While Hawleys are slightly similar to Invisalign aligners, their primary function leans towards retention rather than alignment.

The Role of Hawley Retainers

Often referenced as fixed space maintainers, these retainers step in post-brace removal to maintain the pristine gap between teeth. They become an essential nighttime companion, mitigating the risks of any inadvertent tooth movements during sleep.

While they don’t offer the luxury of removal during meals like clear aligner trays, their design allows easy removal during daily dental hygiene routines. Initial weeks post brace removal may require continuous wear, ensuring teeth stability. As a rule of thumb, post-brace care generally involves wearing Hawley retainers for a few years, with the exact timeframe tailored to individual cases.

When Is a Hawley Retainer Appropriate?

Although primarily a post-orthodontic treatment device, there are instances where we may introduce a Hawley during treatment. Potential scenarios include managing underbites, rectifying crossbites, or addressing crowded teeth. Its inherent stability makes it a valuable asset for those needing alignment-focused interventions before transitioning to permanent retainers or other solutions.

Maintaining Your Hawley Retainer

Daily care involves immersing the appliance in denture cleaner, preventing plaque build-up. We recommend a gentle brushing session with a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste. Note that whitening pastes aren’t friendly to these retainers; they could degrade the plastic material.

Essex Retainers: The Subtle Guardians of Your Smile

Ensuring the longevity of dental procedures, be it implants, bridges, or orthodontics, is imperative for the health and aesthetics of your smile. The Essex retainer emerges as a trusted ally, designed with precision to hold teeth in their rightful places post-treatment and prevent inadvertent movements.

Understanding Essex Retainers

While typically associated with post-treatment care, the versatility of the Essex retainer allows it to stand as an independent solution for maintaining teeth alignment. Each Essex retainer is crafted to snugly embrace the dental arch, with adjustments for optimal patient comfort. Depending on the specific needs, some patients may have these retainers permanently bonded, ensuring lifelong teeth positioning.

Acquiring Your Essex Retainer

The path to acquiring an Essex retainer begins with an initial visit to the dentist’s chamber, primarily to capture the unique mold of your teeth. From this mold, a bespoke Essex retainer is crafted over subsequent weeks, ensuring a tailor-made fit for the individual. Once ready, a fitting session verifies its comfort and appropriateness. After this confirmation, patients typically begin their retainer-wearing routine, extending day to night, fortifying teeth positioning and alignment.

The Multiple Advantages of Essex Retainers

For many, dental implants present the pinnacle of teeth replacement solutions. Yet, they aren’t a one-size-fits-all answer. Especially for those grappling with periodontal disease-induced tooth loss, removable dental aids like Essex retainers shine through. Their design lets them overlay existing teeth, preserving jaw alignment and preventing untoward tooth shifts. This protection ensures your smile retains its radiance for extended durations.

Furthermore, the preventive nature of Essex retainers can often negate the necessity of more intrusive dental procedures in the future, like crowns, bridgework, or even fillings. They act as a protective measure, helping retain your natural teeth and potentially ward off the need for advanced treatments.
