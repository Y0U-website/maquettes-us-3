Dr. Nurit Bittner

Dr. Nurit Bittner completed her dental education at the Central University ofVenezuela in 2003, graduating as valedictorian with magna cum laude honors.She then obtained a Certificate of Graduate Study in Prosthodontics and a Master of Science in Prosthodontics at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in2008. Subsequently, she earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree fromthe same institution in 2011.

Dr. Nurit Bittner served as the program director of the Postgraduate Prosthodontics program at Columbia University from 2013 to 2022. She currently holds the position of Clinical Director of the Postgraduate Prosthodontic Program at NovaSoutheastern University and has been a faculty member since 2008.

She is a Board Certified Prosthodontist and has been a Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics since 2010. Additionally, she is a fellow of the AmericanCollege of Prosthodontics and the Greater New York Academy of Prosthodontics.

Dr. Bittner was also a member of the Board of Directors for the American College of Prosthodontists New York Section.She has conducted and made significant contributions to research studies and has delivered lectures on a national and international level in the fields of implants and esthetic dentistry. Dr. Bittner has authored a book chapter, published in respected peer-reviewed journals, and contributed to editorials related to Prosthodontics.

Dr. Bittner holds licenses to practice dentistry in both New York and Florida. She has dedicated several years to practicing dentistry with a focus on dental prostheses, implants, and esthetic dentistry. She is always looking to learn new technologies and updates to provide to her patients the highest quality of care.
