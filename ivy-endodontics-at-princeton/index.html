Ivy Endodontics proudly serves the Central Jersey community with advanced, microscope-guided root canal therapy. Led by Dr. Franco DeMayo, a board-certified endodontist, our team combines 3D digital imaging with a gentle, patient-first approach so you leave with your natural tooth, and your smile, intact.

Apicoectomy (Periapical Microsurgery) A precise microsurgical procedure that removes the root tip and any surrounding infected tissue. An effective solution when root canal therapy alone isn’t enough to fully restore your oral health.

High-Definition 3D Scanning Using the Carestream CS 9600, we capture ultra-high-resolution 3D scans that reveal hidden canals and fractures invisible on a traditional X-ray for a more accurate diagnosis and a more predictable outcome.

Emergency Endodontic Care Severe tooth pain doesn’t wait, and neither do we. Ivy Endodontics prioritizes dental emergencies in Plainsboro and surrounding Mercer, Middlesex, and Monmouth County towns, with same-day appointments to get you out of pain fast.
