Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants are a safe and well-established treatment. They are perhaps the most reliable long-term solution for tooth loss.

How long does the entire process take?

The entire process, from implant placement to final crown placement, can take several months, largely due to the healing time needed between procedures.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Patients are usually surprised at how minimal the discomfort is, especially with local anesthesia and optional sedation. Post-operative pain is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.
