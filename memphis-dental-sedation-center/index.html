The practice

Our practice specializes in predictable, comfortable, and efficient removal of problematic teeth. Services include:

Wisdom Teeth Removal: Expert consultation, surgical planning, and safe removal of impacted or erupting third molars.

Fear and anxiety can be major barriers to dental care. At Memphis Dental Sedation Center, IV sedation (twilight sleep) is central to our approach.

Efficiency: Allows our oral surgeon to perform complex procedures effectively while patients remain fully relaxed.

Safety First: Our oral surgeon is trained to safely administer and monitor sedation, keeping vital signs stable.

Zero Anxiety: Eliminate stress, fear, and discomfort associated with oral surgery.

Located in Bartlett and proudly serving the entire Memphis area, Memphis Dental Sedation Center, is dedicated to providing the highest standard of oral surgery care with a focus on patient comfort. We specialize in all types of tooth extractions and wisdom teeth removal, ensuring every procedure is stress-free and pain-managed through expert sedation.
