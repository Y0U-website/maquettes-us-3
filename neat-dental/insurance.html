0 Insurance & Payment Options At Neat Dental , we believe that high-quality dental care should be accessible to everyone. That’s why we accept a wide range of insurance plans and offer flexible payment options to fit your needs.

We Accept Most PPO Insurance Plans

We proudly accept most Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) insurance plans. Our team will work with your provider to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs. If you’re unsure whether your plan is accepted, feel free to contact us, and we’ll be happy to verify your coverage.

How We Help: ✔ We handle insurance claims and paperwork for you. ✔ We provide a detailed breakdown of your benefits before treatment. ✔ We help you understand your coverage and treatment options.

Accepting State Husky Dental Insurance!

We are committed to making dental care accessible to as many patients as possible. That’s why we proudly accept State Husky dental insurance to provide affordable, high-quality care to individuals and families.

✔ Comprehensive Coverage: Includes preventive care, restorative treatments, and more. ✔ Easy Process: Our team will guide you through using your Husky benefits. ✔ No Hidden Costs: We ensure complete transparency in treatment costs.

If you have Husky A, B, C, or D , we can help you understand what services are covered and ensure you receive the best care possible.

No Insurance? No Problem!

For patients without insurance, we offer flexible payment solutions, including:

✔ Affordable Self-Pay Rates – Competitive pricing for all services. ✔ Financing Options – Monthly payment plans to fit your budget. ✔ Care Credit – Offers flexible payment plans for dental treatments, making care more affordable.

Contact us to learn more about your payment options!
