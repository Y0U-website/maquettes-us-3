The practice

We believe in early intervention and providing orthodontic care to children starting at the age of 7. By addressing issues such as crowding, spacing, and misaligned bites early on, we can guide their dental development and set them up for a lifetime of confident smiles.

We are committed to helping teens achieve the smiles that will boost their confidence and their oral health. With a range of treatment options, including braces and Invisalign, we can find the perfect solution for your teen to create a smile they’ll be proud to show off.

We understand that adults have unique orthodontic needs and concerns. Whether you’re seeking orthodontic treatment for the first time or looking to enhance your previous orthodontic work, our personalized approach ensures that we address your specific goals and desires.

At Papasikos Orthodontics your smile will be specifically customized by our doctor to compliment your unique facial features, making you look your absolute best.

We use the latest state-of-the-art bracket technology to make treatment more efficient, comfortable, and precise. Our advanced braces system are designed to reduce treatment time while achieving the best possible results, giving you a straighter, healthier smile faster than ever before

For patients looking for a more discreet option, we offer clear and ceramic braces that blend seamlessly with your teeth for enhanced esthetics. These advanced brackets provide the same effective treatment as traditional braces while reducing visibility, so you can smile with confidence throughout your orthodontic journey.

Although braces are great for some teens, many prefer to go metal-free and use the most popular aligners on the market, Invisalign Teen. Invisalign Teen takes approximately the same amount of time as braces and, if worn properly, gets the same results.

At Papasikos Orthodontics , we recognize how important it is to make orthodontic care accessible and affordable for everyone. We’re dedicated to ensuring that financial concerns don’t prevent you from achieving the smile you deserve. Let’s collaborate to explore flexible financing options and ease any financial worries throughout your orthodontic journey.
