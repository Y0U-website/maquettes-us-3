The absence of teeth is directly related to a low quality of life and is an obstacle to enjoy it in its fullness. At Orthodontics Dental Care, we have a specialized operating room with a sterile circuit and the extensive experience of Dr. Alvaro Beltrán in regenerating and solving very complex cases. We always bet on quality treatments and materials, more complete, with more stable and lasting results over time.

When teeth are missing in the mouth, the teeth around them tend to move towards the empty space, which can cause problems of occlusion or bite, problems of TMJ (temporomandibular joint), increase in the appearance of caries and gum problems.

In the past, the only solution to this problem was removable dentures, but today it is possible to replace teeth with fixed teeth using a dental implant. There is a wide range of qualities in implants, in Orthodontics Dental Care, we move away from the whole low cost range in implants and only work with brands that assure us the highest quality.
