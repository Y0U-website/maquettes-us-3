The practice

Dr. Priscilla Jolly and her team take a family-first approach to orthodontic care. Whether you’re exploring braces for your child, considering Invisalign for yourself, or just want to know if treatment is right for you, we’ll take the time to listen, explain your options, and build a treatment plan tailored to your goals and your budget.

We offer complimentary consultations for every new patient, with no referral needed. Dr. Jolly personally performs a full exam at every consultation so you leave with a clear understanding of your treatment options.

Traditional Metal Braces Reliable and effective for patients of all ages. Today’s braces are smaller and more comfortable than ever.

Clear & Ceramic Braces Tooth-colored brackets that deliver the same results as metal braces with a more discreet appearance.

Invisalign Clear Aligners Nearly invisible, removable trays that straighten your teeth without brackets or wires. Certified Invisalign provider.

Early Treatment The AAO recommends children be evaluated by age 7. We offer interceptive treatment when needed, free monitoring when you don’t.

Adult Orthodontics It’s never too late for a confident smile. Multiple options and plans designed for busy adult lifestyles.
