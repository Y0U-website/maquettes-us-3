About Us

Dr. Lynn A. Travis Dr. Travis came to dentistry from the patient’s side of the chair. As a teenager she needed corrective jaw surgery, and rather than putting her off, the experience left her genuinely curious about the work. She took a job as a dental assistant to see more of it and was hooked from that point on.

She grew up on Long Island and stayed on as an assistant throughout college at Hofstra University, where she graduated top of her class with a degree in psychology. From there she went to Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine, receiving her DDS in 1999, and then completed a residency at Stony Brook University Hospital, treating patients whose dental care was complicated by other health conditions. She has practiced here on Long Island since June of 2000.

Those early years working chairside still shape how she practices today. She takes a conservative approach to treatment and to X-rays, recommending them when they will change what she does next rather than because a certain amount of time has gone by. She talks patients through what she is seeing and why before any work begins. A good number of her patients arrive anxious about dental visits, and most find that understanding what is about to happen makes a real difference.

Dr. Travis attends continuing education seminars regularly and takes part in several professional study clubs. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Association and the Suffolk County Dental Society.

Dr. Travis lives nearby with her husband and their three children. Outside the office she spends her time with family and friends, at the beach, in the garden, and at her children’s sporting events.
