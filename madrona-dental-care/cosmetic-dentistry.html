KöR At-Home Teeth Whitening

KöR at-home whitening offers a professional, convenient way to brighten your smile on your own schedule. Using custom whitening trays and a powerful refrigerated whitening gel, KöR is designed to lift deep stains while helping minimize sensitivity. This option is a great fit for patients who want gradual, noticeable results with guidance from our team. We’ll make sure your trays fit comfortably, walk you through how to use the gel, and help you whiten safely and confidently from home.

KöR In-Office Teeth Whitening

KöR in-office whitening is designed for patients who want a brighter smile with a more guided, treatment-focused experience. During your visit, our team applies professional-strength whitening gel and carefully monitors your comfort and results throughout the process. KöR is known for its ability to address deeper discoloration, including stains that may not respond as well to traditional whitening options. For many patients, in-office whitening can also be paired with at-home trays to help enhance and maintain your results over time.

Dental Bonding

If you&#x27;re looking for a more conservative way to transform your smile, dental bonding may be right for you. Your dentist will apply a color-matched composite resin to your teeth, shaping and sculpting it to blend seamlessly with the rest of your smile. Then, they&#x27;ll cure it with a special blue light to complete the process. Bonding doesn&#x27;t require any invasive prep, and can cover up minor chips, cracks, and stains, often in a single visit. Schedule a consultation today to find out if dental bonding can help you achieve your dream smile!
