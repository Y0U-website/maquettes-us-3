What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. They consist of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a strong foundation for the replacement tooth or dental crown. Implants not only restore the appearance of your smile but also improve your ability to bite and chew.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

  • Improved Function : Dental implants function and feel like natural teeth, allowing you to eat and speak with confidence.
  • Enhanced Aesthetics : Implants are designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a natural and beautiful smile.
  • Preserved Bone Structure : When a tooth is lost, the surrounding bone begins to deteriorate. Dental implants stimulate bone growth, helping to maintain your facial structure and prevent further bone loss.
  • Long-lasting Solution : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a durable and cost-effective tooth replacement option.

Our Implant Dentistry Services

At Panda Dental Care, we offer a comprehensive range of implant dentistry services, including:

  • Single Tooth Implants If you are missing a single tooth, a dental implant can be placed to replace it. Our skilled implant dentists carefully evaluate your situation and customize a treatment plan to ensure a seamless and natural-looking replacement tooth.
  • Multiple Teeth Implants For patients missing multiple teeth, implant-supported bridges or dentures are an excellent solution. Dental implants provide a stable and secure foundation for a bridge or denture, enhancing comfort and chewing efficiency.
  • All-on-4® Dental Implants All-on-4® dental implants offer a revolutionary solution for restoring an entire arch of missing teeth. This technique allows for the placement of a full set of replacement teeth with only four strategically positioned dental implants.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures Implant-supported dentures provide a more reliable alternative to traditional dentures. By anchoring the dentures to dental implants, we can improve stability, eliminate slippage, and enhance both functionality and comfort.
