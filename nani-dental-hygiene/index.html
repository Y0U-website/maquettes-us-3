ON-SITE DENTAL CARE Receive treatment in the comfort of your own home

On-site dental hygiene care to residents of licensed facilities and patients receiving in-home care from family or licensed caregivers. Using mobile dental equipment and universal standard infection control protocols, we provide individualized care and treatment for each resident we visit.

Nani F. Bezuayehu, a Registered Dental Hygienist in Alternative Practice, has been providing dental care in general and periodontal dental offices since 1989. As a paramedic, she has provided health care services as an emergency room technician since 2003. With RDH and emergency care services, Nani provides safe and urgent care needs.

Nani has lived in the Bay Area since the 1990s and is committed to providing accessible quality care to all people. Beyond work, Nani is passionate about green building, cooking and her community.
