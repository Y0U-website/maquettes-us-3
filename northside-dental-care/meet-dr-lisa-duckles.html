Commitment to Patient Care

Dr. Duckles is committed to providing the same high level of care that patients at Northside Dental Care have come to expect.

Her goal is to create a comfortable and welcoming environment where patients feel supported and confident in their dental care.

She feels honored to serve the Bellingham community and help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Education and Continuing Training

Dr. Duckles believes that great dentists never stop learning.

She spends many hours each year completing continuing education courses to stay up to date with the latest dental techniques and technologies.

She has completed the core curriculum at the Pankey Institute , a respected dental education organization known for advanced training in comprehensive dentistry.

Dr. Duckles is also proud to serve on the Pankey Institute Board of Advisors and continues to attend advanced training courses regularly.
