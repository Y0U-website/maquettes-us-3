Dr. Logan Connor, DMD

Committed to comfortable and stress free Denistry

Dr. Connor is passionate about dentistry and providing exceptional care. He advocates for preventative care and uses the latest advancements in dentistry. Dr. Connor combines his skills in cosmetic dentistry to help you achieve your desired smile. With digital dentistry and AI software, he can provide same-day crowns, allowing you to leave your appointment with your permanent crown in place.

Dr. Connor was born and raised in Minnesota. He grew up in Saint Peter, MN, attended Saint John’s University, and earned his master’s degree in Arizona. After that, Dr. Connor moved to Illinois and completed his education at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine.

Following dental school, he returned to Minneapolis to pursue his passion for dentistry and to be a part of the Twin Cities community.

In his spare time, you can find him soaking up the sun on a lake, shanking golf balls into the woods at a local golf course, or enjoying a brewery with some friends. He’s slowly becoming a foodie and loves finding new foods to cook and restaurants to indulge in. Let him know your recommendations!

Dr. Connor’s goal is to make your dental experience comfortable and stress-free. Dentistry can be pretty complex and overwhelming, so Dr. Connor likes to take the time to explain everything about your smile and create a customized plan to keep you healthy and smiling. He’s excited to work with you!
