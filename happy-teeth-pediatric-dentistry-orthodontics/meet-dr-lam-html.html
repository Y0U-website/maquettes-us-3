Get to Know Dr. Anna Lam

Nothing makes Dr. Lam happier than helping kids feel comfortable and confident at the dentist and then seeing their progress over time. Watching them meet various milestones with bright, healthy smiles is always rewarding, as is getting to be a part of families’ lives and building connections that endure from the cradle to the graduation stage.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

It was Dr. Lam’s own pediatric dentist that set her on the path she still walks today. She remembers feeling comfortable and cared for during those initial dental visits, which shaped how she viewed dentistry from a young age. That impression stayed with her, and later through shadowing and clinical exposure, she realized she wanted to create that same kind of positive experience for her own patients.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Lam began her college career at the University of Massachusetts- Amherst before going on to graduate from the prestigious Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She then completed her pediatric dental residency at NYU Langone in Providence, RI.

Always eager to learn and improve, Dr. Lam maintains active memberships in the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Lam and her husband (a fellow dentist she met at Tufts) were married in 2025, and they spend their quality time playing pickleball, running, and trying new restaurants together. Fun fact: Dr. Lam is a HUGE Taylor Swift fan and can name almost any song after just 3 seconds!
