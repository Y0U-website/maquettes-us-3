The practice

I m Dr. Rich Facko, and I ve been practicing pediatric dentistry in Palos Heights for over a decade. I m also a dad of four, so when you bring your child in, I understand what s at stake from both sides of the chair.

Our goal isn t just healthy teeth. It s to give your child an experience positive enough that they ll actually want to come back. That starts with going at their pace, keeping things calm and low-pressure, and making sure you always know what s happening and why.

At most dental offices, parents drop their kids off at the door and wait for a summary on the way out. That s not how we work.

Good dental health habits start as young as possible and can last for your child’s whole life.

Some kids are easy. Some kids need more time, more patience, or a different approach. Some have sensory sensitivities, developmental differences, or dental anxiety from a past experience.

We ve built our practice around handling all of it. From our first baby tooth visits through the teenage years, we adjust our approach to fit your child, not the other way around. And when treatment becomes necessary, we explain every option clearly so you can make the decision that s right for your family.

At Palos Pediatric Dentistry, we understand how critical it is to provide a comfortable and minimally invasive environment for our young patients. For children with anxiety, we may utilize sedation dentistry or general anesthesia to aid Dr. Facko during a dental procedure.
