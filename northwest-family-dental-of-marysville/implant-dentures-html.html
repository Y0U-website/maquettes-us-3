What Are Implant Dentures?

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

A fixed implant denture is made to stay in place at all times. It can only be removed by a dental professional. In most scenarios, four to six strategically placed dental implants are placed to hold a permanent denture. This is a preferred option for many patients because they act more like real teeth.

Removable Implant Dentures

Removable implant dentures, sometimes called snap-on dentures, are also held into place using dental implants. The difference is that they use clips or ball sockets to attach to them. This way, patients can take their dentures out as needed. This type of implant denture needs to be removed throughout the day for cleaning.

The Implant Denture Process

During your consultation, we will determine whether or not you are a good candidate for implant dentures. At this point, we can create a treatment plan that meets your needs. For some patients, preparatory procedures are needed before getting dental implants to improve the probability of success.

The dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Then, they fuse to the surrounding bone tissue via the osseointegration process. This can take between three and six months. Next, metal connectors, called abutments, are placed on the end of the implants. Your final denture is designed and crafted according to your needs and secured into place by your dentist.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

The majority of healthy adults can get implant dentures. During your consultation with us, we will discuss your needs and options in detail. To make a good candidate, you must be:

  • Healthy enough to undergo minor surgery
  • Free of existing oral health issues
  • Able to maintain excellent oral hygiene
  • Someone with sufficient jawbone density
