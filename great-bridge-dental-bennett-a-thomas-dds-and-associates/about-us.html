Meet the Team at Bennett A. Thomas, DDS and Associates

Dr. Bennett A. Thomas

Dr. Bennett A. Thomas earned a bachelor s degree in zoology at Ohio University in 1983 and went on to attain his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from West Virginia School of Dentistry in 1987. To further advance his skills, Dr. Thomas did a General Practice Residency at Charleston Area Medical Center in West Virginia. After working with a dentist in Virginia Beach for a time, he founded his own practice, Dr. Bennett A. Thomas and Associates, (also known as Drs. Thomas, Thomas, and Holwick) in 1989.

Dr. Thomas is committed to providing personalized, comfortable dental care in a warm and caring environment. He has over 30 years of experience in helping his patients achieve excellent oral health. His commitment to learning about the latest advances in dentistry ensures that you always receive the highest quality, most up-to-date dental care possible. He has a thirst for knowledge, and attends so many continuing education seminars that he always surpasses the number of hours required by the state per year for licensure!

A native of St. Albans, WV, Dr. Thomas enjoys golfing, surfing, deep water fishing and writing poetry in his spare time. He also loves visiting the local elementary schools and reading to the young students, who appreciate his sense of humor!

Dr. R. Todd Holwick

Dr. Robert "Todd" Holwick attended St. Bonaventure University in Allegany, NY and received his bachelor’s degree in biology. With a passion for helping others and a love of science, Dr. Holwick attended Georgetown University School of Dentistry where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree.

Dr. Holwick joined Bennett A. Thomas DDS & Associates in 1997. He enjoys getting to know our wonderful patients and providing solutions that help them achieve excellent oral health. Dr. Holwick is experienced in all areas of dentistry and is certified in soft-tissue laser treatments.

A native of Williamstown, MA, Dr. Holwick enjoys hiking and competing in triathlons. He also helps with designs and special effects for the charity Halloween Haunted Attraction in his free time.

"I enjoy working with patients of all ages in all aspects of dentistry, from simple fillings to full mouth reconstruction including implant crowns. I like to stay on top of the latest technology that improves our patients’ comfort. Having worked here for over 20 years, I especially enjoy the camaraderie with the other dentists and dental professionals on our team."
