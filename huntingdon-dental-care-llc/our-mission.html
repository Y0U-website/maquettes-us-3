Our Mission

At Huntingdon Dental Care, LLC our goal is to provide quality comprehensive dentistry to you and your loved ones. Services provided include oral cancer screenings, comprehensive exams, extractions , implants , crowns , bridge , fillings as well as root canals .

Dr. Wong also believes in constantly training to hone his craft by taking over six times the required training for Pennsylvania dentists. For his commitment to excellence in dentistry, Dr. Wong achieved Fellowship. Read below to find out what a Fellow from Academy fo General Dentistry means.

FAGD stands for Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry. Earning Fellowship takes dedication to dental continuing education (CE) beyond graduation. Only 6 percent of general dentists in the U.S. and Canada are AGD Fellows. When you see a dentist with FAGD after their name, you can rest assured that they care about knowing the latest techniques and best practices in dentistry. AGD Fellows practice these techniques in a classroom multiple times yearly — not every dentist does this.

To become an AGD Fellow, a dentist must:

Complete over 500 credit hours of approved dental education after dental school. Earn 350 of those 500 credits in live hands-on training. Pass an exam equal in difficulty to board certification exams.
