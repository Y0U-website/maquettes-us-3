Lasting Impressions Supports the Community Through Various Initiatives

At Lasting Impressions, we believe in strong families and healthy communities. We support many local organizations in our community that work every day to improve the quality of life for many in the Capital Region.

The Quest for Grace Foundation, a local non-profit providing assistance to foster children in our community, is strongly supported by Lasting Impressions, and every year Dr. Mark Polsinello hosts a golf tournament to raise funds to help the organization. You can read about the Quest for Grace Foundation below, and visit their website for more information

Attend a fundraising event Attending our annual fundraising event or golf outing is fun and provides crucial financial support. For more information on the Quest for Grace Foundation, please visit www.questforgrace.c om

Dr. Polsinello organizes and helps sponsors the annual Whiffle Ball Tournament for St. Lukes. The proceeds from the tournament help fund the Recovery Resource Center. Our annual Whiffle Ball Tournament will be held on June 15th, 2018. For additional information, please contact our office or visit: www.stlukesonthehill.org
