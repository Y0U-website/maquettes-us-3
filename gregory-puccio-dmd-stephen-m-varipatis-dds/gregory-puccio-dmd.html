Gregory Puccio, DMD

Meet Your Parkville, MD Dentist

Dr. Puccio grew up in Old Tappan, New Jersey, where he graduated from Don Bosco Preparatory High School. In 2009, he received his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from Loyola College in Maryland.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (formerly known as The University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey). He completed his year-long general practice residency at Hackensack University Medical Center, where he received advanced training in oral surgery, endodontics (root canal therapy), cosmetic dentistry, and comprehensive restorative treatment.

Dr. Puccio values providing a gentle and comfortable dental experience. He is committed to continuing his education and takes many advanced courses each year to enhance his skills and better serve the dental care needs of his patients.

In his free time, Dr. Puccio enjoys hiking, traveling, and spending quality time with his wife.
