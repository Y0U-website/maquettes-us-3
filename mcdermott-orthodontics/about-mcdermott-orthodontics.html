Frequently Asked Questions

Do you treat adults as well as children? Yes. We offer orthodontic treatment for patients of all ages, including adult braces and Invisalign.

Is the consultation really free? Yes. Your initial consultation is complimentary and includes an examination, discussion of treatment options, and a personalized recommendation.

Do you offer Invisalign? Yes. We are an Invisalign provider and offer both Invisalign and Invisalign Teen as alternatives to traditional braces.

What age should my child first see an orthodontist? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial screening by age seven. Early evaluation allows us to monitor growth and intervene only when necessary.

Do you have payment plans? Yes. We offer flexible financing and payment options to help fit orthodontic treatment into your budget.
