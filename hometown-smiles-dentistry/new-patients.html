What to Expect on Your First Visit

Warm Welcome

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by our friendly staff. We’re here to answer any questions and make you feel at home.

Dr. Ng will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health. This includes a detailed evaluation of your teeth, gums, and overall oral hygiene. If needed, digital x-rays will be taken to provide a complete picture of your dental health.

Following the examination, Dr. Ng will discuss his findings with you. He will explain any issues, answer your questions, and create a customized treatment plan tailored to your needs and goals.

Our focus is on preventive care to keep your smile healthy. We’ll provide professional cleaning, fluoride treatment, and advice on maintaining optimal oral hygiene at home.

We believe in open communication and want you to feel comfortable discussing your dental concerns. Our team is always here to listen and provide the information you need to make informed decisions about your dental care.

Hometown Smiles Dentistry is a family-friendly practice. We strive to make dental visits enjoyable for patients of all ages, from young children to grandparents.
