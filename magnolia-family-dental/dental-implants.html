FAQs about dental implants in Sandy Spring

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth. With proper care, they can last many years. Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits help maintain their condition. The crown on top may need replacement over time, but the implant itself often remains stable.

Dental implants are considered a trusted restoration and common procedure when performed by a trained dentist. The materials used are compatible with the body and designed to support healing. A full dental exam can help ensure you are a good candidate before treatment begins. Schedule your personalized exam today by calling our team!

The procedure is usually performed with local anesthesia to keep you comfortable. Some patients may feel mild discomfort after the procedure, which often improves within a few days. Our dentist provides guidance to help manage healing and reduce discomfort during recovery. If you experience any ongoing discomfort, call our team ASAP!

Healing time varies, but it often takes a few months for the implant to bond with the bone. This process is important for stability. During this time, follow-up visits can help monitor progress and ensure proper healing before placing the final restoration.

Not everyone is a candidate for implants. Patients need healthy gums and enough bone support. A dental exam is designed to help determine if this option is suitable based on your oral health and overall condition. Book your consultation through our Contact page today.

Caring for implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups are important. Keeping the area clean can help prevent gum issues and supports long-term success. Our dentist may recommend specific tools for cleaning around the implant.

Alternatives include dental bridges and dentures. These options are also designed to replace missing teeth but may not provide the same level of stability. A dental consultation helps compare options and determine which treatment fits your needs and lifestyle.
