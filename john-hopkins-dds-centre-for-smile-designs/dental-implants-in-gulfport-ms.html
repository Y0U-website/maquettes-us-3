Benefits of Mini Implants Compared to Traditional Dental Implants

Like any dental procedure, mini dental implants have their benefits and drawbacks. However, for most patients, the pros far outweigh the cons.

Promote Jawbone Health

When mini dental implants are inserted, they stimulate the jawbone and promote a healthy bone structure. These metal posts can also prevent patients from developing the appearance of a sunken face, which often happens to those who choose dentures for long-term tooth replacement.

Resemble Natural Teeth

Mini dental implants mimic a natural tooth in appearance and performance. Because of this benefit, more patients are investing in these tooth replacement options. They improve your mouth’s function and enhance your smile.

Only One Visit Required

Because of their smaller size, the surgery can be completed in a single visit to the Centre for Smile Designs. This factor makes the process faster and easier compared to traditional dental implants.

A Permanent Tooth Replacement Solution

Mini dental implants are not temporary. When you practice proper home care and keep your routine appointments with Dr. Hopkins, you can expect them to last for years.

No Bone Grafting Required

Smaller implants are more convenient for patients with less jawbone tissue. Most patients do not need an additional bone grafting procedure, which will save you money.

Lower Costs than Traditional Dental Implants

Mini dental implants cost significantly less than regular dental implants, making them more appealing to many patients.

Drawbacks of Mini Dental Implants

If you grind your teeth, dental implants may not be a good choice for you. When this problem is unresolved, the new teeth will wear down, and the metal posts can become damaged.

If your mini dental implants support a denture, they will have rubber O-rings that fit around the balls on the metal post. These rings can wear out and become loose, requiring you to replace them. Frequently taking your dentures out can lead to more frequent replacement of the O-rings.

Because of their size, two mini dental implants may be required in places where a single conventional implant would work. In some cases, standard dental implants might have a longer life span because they can distribute weight more evenly, taking stress off the jawbone. They also provide a larger surface area and a stronger chewing force.

The Cost of Mini Dental Implants Explained

Many patients already know that dental implants are a significant investment. One conventional implant can cost over one thousand dollars, but not mini dental implants. However, the cost varies between patients because every case is different.

Mini dental implants don’t require bone grafting, further reducing the cost of the procedure.

Your mini dental implant cost includes the local anesthetic administered to numb the area, the metal post, and the permanent restoration.

  • The type of restoration being used for the artificial teeth or tooth
  • The size of the post
