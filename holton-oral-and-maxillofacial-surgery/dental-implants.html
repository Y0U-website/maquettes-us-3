Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants offers a range of advantages:

If you’ve experienced bone loss, ask about bone grafting to prepare your jaw for implants.

  • Enhanced Functionality: Implants restore your ability to chew and speak naturally.
  • Improved Aesthetics: Custom restorations blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.
  • Jawbone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss.
  • Long-Lasting Results: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Our Dental Implant Process

At Holton Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, we guide you through every step of the dental implant process:

  • Initial Consultation: We begin with a comprehensive evaluation, including advanced imaging like 3D CT scans , to assess your oral health and determine your candidacy for implants.
  • Preparation: If needed, preparatory procedures like tooth extractions or bone grafting are performed to ensure a strong foundation for your implant.
  • Implant Placement: Dr. Holton uses cutting-edge techniques to precisely place the titanium implant into your jawbone. This step is performed under local anesthesia or IV sedation for your comfort.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your jawbone, creating a stable base for the restoration.
  • Final Restoration: Once healing is complete, your dentist will attach the custom crown, bridge, or denture to your implant for a natural-looking finish.
