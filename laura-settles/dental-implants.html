Dental Implants in Wytheville, VA​

Permanent Tooth Replacement That Looks, Feels, and Functions Like Natural Teeth

Most dental practices refer implant cases to oral surgeons or periodontists in larger cities. At Wytheville Dental Group, our prosthodontists handle the entire process—from implant placement through final restoration—right here in Wytheville.

When Dental Implants Are the Right Solution

Our Wytheville dentists recommend implants for patients dealing with:

Implants work best when you have adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate whether you’re a candidate or if preparatory procedures like bone grafting are needed first.

  • Single missing tooth: An implant-supported crown replaces one tooth without affecting adjacent teeth
  • Multiple missing teeth: Implant-supported bridges restore several teeth with fewer implants than individual replacements
  • Full arch tooth loss: Implant-supported dentures or fixed bridges replace an entire upper or lower arch
  • Loose dentures: Implants stabilize existing dentures, eliminating movement and adhesives
  • Failing teeth: Teeth with severe decay, fractures, or bone loss that can’t be saved

The Dental Implant Process

Dr. Jones, Dr. Davenport, or Dr. Holston examines your mouth and takes 3D imaging to assess bone quality and plan precise implant placement.

We surgically place titanium implant posts into your jawbone under local anesthesia (sedation available). The implant integrates with your bone over 3-6 months.

Once healed, we attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Your new teeth are crafted to match your natural tooth color, shape, and bite.

Types of Implant Restorations

One implant supports one crown, replacing a missing tooth without touching adjacent teeth.

Two or more implants support a bridge replacing multiple missing teeth—more stable than traditional bridges.

Removable dentures that snap onto 2-4 implants per arch, providing stability while allowing removal for cleaning.

Fixed Implant Bridges (All-on-4/All-on-6)

Permanent full-arch replacements attached to 4-6 implants. These don’t come out—you brush and care for them like natural teeth.

Advantages of Dental Implants

Implants function like natural tooth roots, maintaining bone density and preventing the sunken facial appearance associated with long-term tooth loss.

Traditional bridges require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth. Implants stand independently, preserving your remaining natural teeth.

Implants don’t slip when you eat or speak. No adhesives, no clicking sounds, no worrying about embarrassing moments.

With proper care, dental implants can last 25+ years or even a lifetime.
