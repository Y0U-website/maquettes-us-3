The practice

Oak Road Dentistry is your hometown dentist in Snellville, GA. To us, each of our patients is like a member of the family and we treat you as such. We combine state-of-the-art technology with gentle, compassionate care, all in a comfortable office right in the heart of Gwinnett County. Our family dentistry services include:

Restorative Dentistry – Sometimes, even with regular dental visits, you might suffer tooth decay or damage from an injury. In these cases, we use restorative dentistry to restore function, strength, and appearance to the affected tooth. Restorative dental procedures include dentures, dental crowns, dental bridges, and fillings.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation – At Oak Road Dentistry, we specialize in skilled full mouth rehabilitations. Your treatment plan is tailored to your unique needs and every restoration is carefully crafted with your facial proportions, treatment goals, and budget in mind.

Dental Crowns – Dental crowns are most commonly used to repair a tooth that has been damaged by deep decay or dental trauma. We use the latest technology and materials to design crowns that look natural and have the strength to stand the test of time.

Dental Bridges – A dental bridge is used to replace either a single missing tooth or multiple adjacent teeth. From durable implant-supported bridges to traditional, we have an option that’s right for you.

Teeth Whitening – With professional teeth whitening treatments, you can achieve a dramatically whiter smile in under an hour.

Dr. Patrick French – A native of Snellville, Dr. French attended South Gwinnett High School before going on to Auburn University for his undergraduate degree and the Medical College of Georgia to earn a degree in Dental Medicine. Dr. French is a member of the American Dental Society, Georgia Dental Society, and the Hinman Dental Society in Atlanta.

Dr. Clancy Poore – Dr. Poore grew up in Augusta, Georgia and attained his Dental Medicine degree from the Dental College of Georgia before completing a residency to specialize in IV conscious sedation, implant dentistry, and treating medically complex patients. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and Georgia Dental Society.
