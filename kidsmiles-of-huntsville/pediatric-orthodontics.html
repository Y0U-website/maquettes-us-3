Orthodontic Treatments Designed for Kids & Teens

Phase One Treatment (Ages 5–12)

Early orthodontic care focuses on jaw symmetry, tooth spacing, breathing, and habits that affect bite and smile development. Starting treatment at the right time—often before all permanent teeth arrive—can reduce or eliminate the need for braces later. Families from Huntsville to Montgomery count on us for proactive, preventive care.

Phase Two Treatment (Age 12+)

Once your child’s permanent teeth are in, we assess how their teeth and jaw work together and create a plan to align everything properly. Options include metal braces, clear braces, or Invisalign. Early Phase One care often shortens Phase Two treatment time significantly.

Questions About Pediatric Orthodontics

When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early visits allow Dr. White to identify issues with jaw growth and tooth spacing before they become bigger problems potentially saving time and cost down the road.

How much do braces cost in Huntsville or Lake Conroe, TX?

Treatment costs vary depending on the complexity of your child’s case and the type of treatment recommended. At your consultation, we’ll provide clear pricing, check for orthodontic insurance benefits, and walk you through payment options designed to fit your budget.

Do braces hurt?

Today’s braces are more comfortable than ever. Smaller brackets and advanced wires minimize discomfort. Any soreness after placement or an adjustment typically resolves within a few days.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length depends on each child’s needs. The national average for braces is around 24 months, but early Phase One treatment can shorten that timeline significantly. Dr. White will give you a realistic estimate at your consultation.
