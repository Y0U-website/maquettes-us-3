Your Financing Options

At Papasikos Orthodontics , we recognize how important it is to make orthodontic care accessible and affordable for everyone. We’re dedicated to ensuring that financial concerns don’t prevent you from achieving the smile you deserve. Let’s collaborate to explore flexible financing options and ease any financial worries throughout your orthodontic journey.

Affordable Payment Plans

We’ll work with you to develop a customized payment plan that suits your treatment needs. We offer interest-free financing and affordable monthly payment options to make your orthodontic care as manageable as possible

Interest Free Financing

We provide interest-free financing without the need for credit checks when setting up your payment plan. Whether you’re interested in custom braces or Invisalign, we’re here to make orthodontic care both accessible and affordable for you and your family.

Low Down Payment

We offer flexible payment options to make your orthodontic treatment more affordable. With down payments as low as $250, we strive to ensure that high-quality care is accessible to everyone. Our team works with you to create a payment plan that fits your budget, so you can start your journey to a beautiful smile without financial stress.

Insurance Plans

We understand the importance of making orthodontic care as affordable as possible. That’s why we accept most insurance plans to help cover the cost of your treatment. Our team will work with you to maximize your benefits, ensuring that you get the most out of your coverage. We’ll also assist with the paperwork, making the process as seamless and stress-free as possible. Whether you have dental or orthodontic insurance, we’re here to help you navigate your plan and get the care you deserve.

We are in-network with most insurance plans.
