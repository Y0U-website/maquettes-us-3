The practice

Providing great dentistry is more than placing the perfect restoration in the most comfortable way. Making a meaningful connection with each and every one of my patients is important to me. Dr. Ahmed

Originally from Tampa, Florida, Dr. Ahmed completed her undergraduate degree at the University of Miami. She left sunny Florida for Ohio State University College of Dentistry, which makes her a Hurricane Buckeye! Upon graduating in 1998, she went on to complete the Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) program at Miami Valley Hospital where she served as chief resident.

Dr. Ahmed’s husband is originally from Springfield and together they are raising two boys. In her free time, Dr, Ahmed enjoys fitness, traveling, and cooking. In fact, she is known to make a mean guacamole and is always encouraging her children and staff to sample new foods.
