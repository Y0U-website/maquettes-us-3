The Right Treatment at the Right Time

Dr. Ogden does not start treatment just because a patient showed up. He starts it when the timing gives you the shortest path to a stable result. Here is what that can look like at the Clermont office.

Braces

Traditional brackets, self-ligating brackets, and custom 3D-printed LightForce braces, all placed with indirect bonding, which means positioning is planned digitally before anything touches your teeth. Precise placement up front is a big part of why treatment finishes on schedule.

Invisalign

We are an award-winning Invisalign office, and Dr. Ogden plans every aligner case himself. If your bite would finish better in braces, he will say so at the consultation instead of selling you aligners anyway.

Early Treatment for Kids

We check kids around age seven, and most of them do not need anything yet. When growth guidance helps, tools like expanders do the work while your child is still growing. When it does not, we watch and wait instead of rushing your child into appliances.
