Meet the Doctor

Meet Dr. Kameron Tauber, DDS, MS

Dr. Kameron Tauber grew up in Georgia, where his passion for dentistry began at a young age when he was inspired by his mother, a dental hygienist. Hearing those stories sparked Dr. Tauber’s curiosity showed him that dentistry is about much more than teeth, it’s about people.

As his education progressed, Dr. Tauber discovered his true calling in pediatric dentistry. He loves helping kids feel comfortable at the dentist, mentoring them, and creating an environment where oral health feels approachable and even fun. Every visit is an opportunity to create a positive, empowering experience that shapes how a child views dental care for years to come.

Outside the office, Dr. Tauber enjoys staying active and spending time outdoors. A lifelong sports enthusiast, he grew up playing competitive soccer and still enjoys following the game today. He can often be found hiking, running, or exploring new trails with his Aussiedoodle, Bandit. When indoors, he enjoys cooking, collecting vinyl records, and discovering new music.

For Dr. Tauber, pediatric dentistry is a way to serve his community, build trust with families, and help every child grow up with a healthy smile and the confidence to share it.
