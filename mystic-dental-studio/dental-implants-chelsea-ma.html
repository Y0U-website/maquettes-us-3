Dental Implants | Chelsea, MA

Understanding each stage can help patients feel more at ease and prepared throughout their care.

  • Treatment Planning: A personalized plan is created based on your imaging results, bone density, and overall dental health.
  • Implant Placement: The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone by a qualified provider, where it will begin to integrate with the bone over time.
  • Healing Period: The implant is given time to bond with the surrounding bone. This phase can vary in length depending on how each individual heals.
  • Crown Placement: Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing the restoration for a natural look and feel.
