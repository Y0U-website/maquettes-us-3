Radiology Safety Course

$695

This course is designed to meet the standards of the California Dental Board for the operation of radiographic equipment.

This course includes 32 hours of didactic, laboratory, and clinical application of x-ray safety, digital imaging, and evaluation.

This combination class, is generally offered 3x a week. This course is ideal for dental assistants and dentists who would like their staff to be x-ray certified .

Why Lincoln Dental Assisting Academy?

Lincoln Dental Assisting Academy provides students with a detailed education in modern dental practices, complete with hands-on training from practicing dental professionals in real-life dental offices.

Financial Aid and Affordable Payment Options are available. With a 100 % Pass Rate you could be starting your new career soon.

True Hands-on Experience Assisting a Real Dentist With Real Patients.

Every one of our students will assist a licensed dentist one-on-one in various dental procedures on real patients – not just on manikins.

Clinical practice is performed on other students and real patients to enhance the learning experience all under the watchful eyes of our instructors.
