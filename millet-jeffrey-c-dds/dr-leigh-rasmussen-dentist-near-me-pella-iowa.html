MEET DR. RASMUSSEN AGD Mastership Dr. Leigh Rasmussen joined our practice in 2009. She is a native of Pella, attended Iowa State University, and then dental school at the University of Iowa. She completed her Masters of The Academy of General Dentistry in 2018.

She emphasizes patient education and preventive care, and enjoys practical aesthetic restorations.

Dr. Leigh’s professional focus is family dentistry, especially for people with dental anxiety.

Dr Leigh loves quiet time at home with her family, reads A LOT and supports the arts in Pella.
