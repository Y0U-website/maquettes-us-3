What Are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures are replacement teeth that attach to small metal posts placed in your jawbone. Unlike regular dentures that sit on your gums, these dentures connect to dental implants. This connection makes them much more stable and secure.

Think of implants as artificial tooth roots. Our dentist near you places these titanium posts into your jaw through a simple procedure. Once they heal and bond with your bone, we attach your dentures to these posts. The result is a set of teeth that feels almost natural.

Who Can Get Implant Dentures?

Most adults who are missing multiple teeth can get implant dentures near you. You need enough jawbone to support the implants and healthy gums. Certain health conditions, like uncontrolled diabetes or recent radiation therapy, might affect your treatment.

During your consultation, our dentist will review your medical history and examine your mouth. We’ll discuss whether implant dentures are right for your situation.

Caring for Your Implant Dentures

Taking care of implant dentures is straightforward. Remove them daily and brush them with a soft toothbrush and denture cleaner. Clean the implant posts and your gums with a separate soft brush.

Visit our dentist in Vancouver , WA regularly for checkups. These appointments help us make sure your implants stay healthy, and your dentures fit properly. With good care, your implant dentures will serve you well for many years.
