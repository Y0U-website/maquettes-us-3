Implant overdentures are removable dentures that connect to dental implants for improved retention and function. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums and rely on suction or adhesive, overdentures snap onto dental implants using attachments. Depending on your needs and anatomy, overdentures can be used to replace: A full arch of upper or lower teeth
  • A partial set of missing teeth (less common)

Benefits of Implant Overdentures

Implant overdentures combine the best of both worlds: the stability of dental implants with the convenience of removable prosthetics. Benefits include:

If your current dentures are uncomfortable or difficult to keep in place, implant overdentures may offer a life-changing improvement.

  • Enhanced stability — less shifting or rocking
  • Improved chewing efficiency
  • Reduced sore spots and gum irritation
  • Easier speech compared to traditional dentures
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • Can be removed for easy cleaning
  • Often more affordable than full fixed implant bridges

Are You a Candidate for Overdentures?

You may be a candidate for implant overdentures if:

We’ll perform a thorough evaluation and take digital scans to assess your bone structure and oral health. If you are a candidate, Dr. Mizner will create a personalized treatment plan and collaborate with a local oral surgeon for implant placement.

  • You are missing most or all teeth in one or both arches
  • You currently wear full dentures but struggle with poor fit or stability
  • You have sufficient jawbone for implant placement (or are open to bone grafting if needed)
  • You want a cost-effective alternative to full fixed implant restorations
  • You’re in generally good health and committed to good oral hygiene

What to Expect: Implant Overdenture Process

Getting implant overdentures involves several coordinated steps:

  • Initial Consultation We begin with a full exam, medical and dental history review, and digital imaging to determine if overdentures are right for you.
  • Implant Placement (Referral) We refer you to a trusted oral surgeon for implant placement. Most overdentures use 2–4 implants per arch for proper support.
  • Healing Period Healing and integration of the implants (called osseointegration) typically takes 3–6 months. During this time, you may wear a temporary denture.
  • Denture Design and Fitting Once your implants have healed, we take precise impressions and design your custom overdenture. Special attachments are placed to connect the denture to the implants.
  • Delivery and Adjustments You’ll try in the final denture, and we’ll make adjustments as needed for comfort, function, and appearance.

How Do Overdentures Differ from Other Options?

It’s helpful to understand the difference between implant overdentures and other restoration options:

Compared to Traditional Dentures:

  • More stable, secure fit
  • Fewer issues with movement or sore spots
  • Does not require adhesives
  • Better preservation of bone and facial structure

Compared to Fixed Implant-Supported Dentures:

  • Removable by the patient for easier cleaning
  • Requires fewer implants
  • More affordable in many cases
  • Slightly less natural in feel than fixed restorations

Caring for Implant Overdentures

Daily care is simple and important for long-term success:

With proper care, overdentures can offer 10+ years of dependable function.

  • Remove and rinse your overdentures daily
  • Clean the underside and attachment areas thoroughly
  • Brush your gums and any natural teeth gently
  • Soak the dentures overnight in a recommended solution
  • Keep up with routine exams and cleanings for implant checks and maintenance
  • Let us know if you notice any looseness or discomfort

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Overdentures

Question text goes here

How long do implant overdentures last?

The denture itself may last 7–10 years or longer with good care. The implants that support it, however, can last a lifetime with proper maintenance.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes. That’s the key feature of this treatment — you can remove them for easy daily cleaning, while still enjoying a secure, stable fit during the day.

Do overdentures hurt?

No. In fact, most patients find them much more comfortable than traditional dentures. Any discomfort during the initial adjustment period is typically minimal and short-lived.

How many implants are needed?

Most overdentures use 2–4 implants per arch. This varies based on your bone health, bite, and whether the denture is upper or lower.

Can I convert my current denture to an overdenture?

In some cases, yes. If your current denture is in good condition, we may be able to retrofit it to snap onto implants. We’ll evaluate your existing denture and determine what’s possible.
