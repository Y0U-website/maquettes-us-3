Meet The Doctor

A healthy mouth is the foundation for a healthy lifestyle

A resident of Mission, Texas, born and raised in Amman, Jordan, Dr. Al-Manisir received his BDS from the University of Jordan’s School of Dentistry.

He then attended Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in California, where he graduated Summa Cum Laude.

Dr. Al-Manasir moved to the RGV back in 2013 where he fell in love with the area and decided to settle down and open Iman Dental Center.

Dr Al-Manasir is a member of the National Honor Dental Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honorary Dental Society, Clinical Master Series and American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and is on the path to become an accredited Cosmetic Dentist in the RGV.

He enjoys traveling and spending time with his wife Patricia and their daughters who have been the source of joy and endless adventures. Please feel free visit the before and after smile gallery to see a few of the artistic smiles designed by Dr. Al-Manasir

Mohammad Khasawneh, DDS | Mission , Texas

Dr.Mohammad Khasawneh has been a RGV resident for more than 9 years , He graduated from Dentistry school in 2007 before completing his Masters degree in dental prosthodontics in 2014 from Jordan university of science and technology.

After that he was accepted and graduated dentistry from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio UT Health San Antonio in 2016 .

With more than 14 years of dental experience from working in multiple practices in many countries, Dr.K’s mission is to provide comprehensive dental services to all patients in a safe,comfortable and pleasant environment and is always looking forward to addressing all your dental needs.

Outside of the office, he spends most of his time with his wife and beautiful daughter enjoying the outdoors and everything the Rio grande valley and its amazing community has to offer.
