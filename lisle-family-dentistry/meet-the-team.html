Our hand-picked team brings the expertise you deserve for quality dental treatments. We designed our services and office with your comfort and health as our highest priority – including cutting-edge technology, preventive solutions, and emergency care.

Dr. Neha Kapila

Her patients feel at ease with her as a person and clinician. She loves spending time with her patients not only professionally, but personally, and takes pride in caring for them as family members. In 2020, she founded DentistsCoach, where she mentors international Dentists and Pre-dental students in their journey to Dental Schools in the USA.

She feels grateful that she can fulfill her passion of giving back to this wonderful community and amazing people in so many ways. Dr. Kapila also serves as Pre-Clinical Faculty at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, Illinois. Dr. Kapila is a member of several professional organizations, including: American Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, Illinois State Dental Society, and Will County Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kapila loves spending time with her two lovely kids, friends, trying cuisines from different cultures, watching Marvel movies, listening to music, and making reels on Instagram!
