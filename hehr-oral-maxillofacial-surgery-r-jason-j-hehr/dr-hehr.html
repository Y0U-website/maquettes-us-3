Meet Dr. Jason Hehr

From the Ice Rink to the Operating Room

Long before founding one of the region’s most respected surgical practices, Dr. Hehr cultivated the grit and teamwork that now define his approach to patient care. Originally from Western Canada, he was awarded a hockey scholarship to Northern Michigan University, where he earned a degree in biochemistry while playing Division I hockey. That same dedication carried him through a semi-professional hockey career—including five seasons with the South Carolina Stingrays, where he eventually settled into the Charleston community he now serves.

A Career Inspired by a Personal Journey

Dr. Hehr&#x27;s path to oral surgery began as a teenager, following a facial injury sustained during a hockey game. After traditional medical care fell short, it was the dental team that stepped in to restore his smile—and plant the seed for a future in oral health. That firsthand experience shaped his desire to help others through restorative and surgical dentistry.

Extensive Training and Surgical Expertise

After retiring from hockey, Dr. Hehr pursued his dental degree at the Medical University of South Carolina, graduating in 2004. He continued his education with a rigorous oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at MUSC, completing the program in 2008. The following year, he opened his private practice in Charleston with a mission to bring advanced surgical solutions to the Lowcountry in a comfortable, patient-focused setting. Today, Dr. Hehr performs a wide range of procedures including:

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth

Surgical Full-scope procedures including bone grafting, jaw correction, and trauma care

Sedation Dentistry I.V. sedation and general anesthesia options for a calm, stress-free experience

Technology & Other 3D imaging, oral pathology evaluation, and TMJ treatment using modern diagnostic tools

Facial Aesthetics Cosmetic and facial rejuvenation treatments, performed in a clinical setting with a surgeon’s precision.

Serving the Community Beyond the Chair Dr. Hehr proudly serves as the Official Oral Surgeon of the South Carolina Stingrays, maintaining a close connection to his athletic roots. He also partners with organizations such as Smile Today and Zimvie, aligning his practice with leaders in restorative and implant dentistry. Outside of surgery, Dr. Hehr continues to advocate for patient education, comfort, and confidence. His modern practice includes an aesthetics division offering skin rejuvenation and non-surgical facial treatments to help patients look and feel their best.

Dr. Hehr&#x27;s facility is fully equipped with advanced diagnostic tools, digital imaging, and a surgical suite designed for optimal safety and outcomes. From the first consultation to final follow-up, patients can expect attentive care that reflects the values Dr. Hehr built his career on—integrity, innovation, and excellence.

Meet Megan Dolby, Medical Esthetician

We are also home to an advanced facial aesthetics division, led by Megan Dolby, a Certified Licensed Medical Esthetician with years of experience in skin health and rejuvenation therapies.

Personalized Skincare, Proven Results

Megan, a Charleston native, holds an undergraduate degree from Michigan State University and completed her formal esthetician training at Trident Technical College. Since earning her license in 2015, she has worked with hundreds of clients across the Lowcountry, offering evidence-based skincare treatments tailored to individual skin types and goals.

Driven by Wellness, Backed by Science

Megan’s commitment to medical aesthetics stems from a deep understanding of how healthy skin contributes to overall wellness and confidence. She combines clinical-grade technology with a personal approach, helping each patient achieve visible, lasting improvements in their skin&#x27;s texture, tone, and vitality. Her knowledge and experience have also played a key role in expanding our practice’s cosmetic service offerings. Through her guidance, we now offer advanced procedures such as Pico Resurfacing & Tattoo Removal, Hydrafacials, and Virtue RF Microneedling with PRP.

Her specialties include:

Laser Skin Resurfacing Microneedling with PRP Chemical Peels Dermaplaning Customized Facials Obagi and SkinMedica Skincare Protocols
