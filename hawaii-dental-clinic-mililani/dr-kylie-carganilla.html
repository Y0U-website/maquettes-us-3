Born and raised on O‘ahu, Dr. Kylie Carganilla earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Oregon Health & Science University after completing her undergraduate studies in Arizona. Dr. Carganilla is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable and confident in their smiles. She values patient education, gentle care, and creating positive dental experiences for patients of all ages. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Carganilla enjoys exploring new restaurants and spending time with her husband and dog, hiking and adventuring around the islands.
