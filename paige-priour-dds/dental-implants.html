Why not having missing teeth is so important

When you have missing teeth, it can be difficult to eat and speak properly. This can make it hard for you to enjoy your favorite foods, and it can also make it harder for you to talk to your friends and family. Your teeth are also an important part of your smile, and when you have missing teeth, it can make you feel self-conscious and unhappy.

Your teeth are also important for keeping your mouth healthy. When you have missing teeth, it can cause your other teeth to shift out of place, and your jawbone to deteriorate, which can lead to a whole host of other problems. It’s important to keep all of your teeth healthy and strong so that you can eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing or damaged teeth. Modern dental implants are small titanium screws that replace and function like natural tooth roots. Implants not only improve the appearance of your smile, but they also restore dental function and promote dental health.

Many people choose dental implants because they closely resemble natural teeth and do not require altering neighboring healthy teeth like traditional bridges or dentures can. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
