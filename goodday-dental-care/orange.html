Meet Dr. Elies Kim

Dr. Elies Kim leads the Goodday Dental Care team with over 13 years of experience and a patient-first philosophy. She is fluent in English and Korean, and the practice provides Spanish-speaking support for Orange County’s diverse community. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the UCLA School of Dentistry and a master’s degree from UC San Diego, and serves as junior faculty for implant seminars with Biotem and the Cando implant course.
