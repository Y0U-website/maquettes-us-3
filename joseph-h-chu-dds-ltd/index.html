Aloha from Kauai Endodontics. Ever since we opened our office in 2010, our mission has been to spread the spirit of "Aloha" through exceptional quality care and treatment. We strive to bring compassion and excellence to your experience. From our sunny smiles to our gentle touch, we re here to ensure your journey with us is filled with the warmth of Kauai. Mahalo for choosing Kauai Endodontics…

Dr. Chu s visionary leadership led to him establishing Kauai Endodontics in 2010, after graduating from New York University Dental School and specializing at Lutheran Medical Center. He has served as President of the Honolulu County Dental Society and the Kauai County Dental Society, showcasing his expertise and commitment to the dental community.

Dr. Chu is dedicated to professional advancement, volunteering as an advisor for the University of Hawaii Pre-Dental Club and teaching at esteemed institutions. Currently, he contributes to the development and regulation of dental practices as a member of the Hawaii State Board of Dentistry.

Beyond our local community, Dr. Chu s impact extends through meaningful medical missions, providing vital dental care in the Philippines, Nepal, and Burma.

​Dr. Marin is a board-certified endodontist, holding Diplomate status with the American Board of Endodontics, and brings a wealth of expertise in advanced endodontic care. Dr. Marin earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Loma Linda University, graduating cum laude, followed by specialized training in Endodontics at New York University College of Dentistry.
