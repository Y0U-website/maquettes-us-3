Dental Implants: An Innovative Solution for Tooth Replacement

If you’ve heard of dental implants but are not sure what they are or how they work, we can tell you that they are a remarkable solution for replacing missing teeth!

Dental implants are a permanent tooth replacement that closely resembles the structure of your natural tooth. We have been using dental implants for many years to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or most of a patient s smile.

How Dental Implants Work

A dental implant is a very small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over time, the cylinder bonds completely with natural bone and tissue in the jaw.

This process forms a stable foundation for the dental restoration that will be attached to it. Once treatment is complete, the dental implant will look and function just like a natural tooth.

If you’re healthy enough for oral surgery, you will most likely not have a problem with having a dental implant placed. However, not everyone is a good candidate for implants, and if this is the case, we will work with you to make the best decision about the treatment that is right for you.

The Importance of Replacing Missing Teeth

Replacing your teeth is good for both your overall health and your appearance. It’s important to understand that a missing tooth is not just a cosmetic problem.

If not replaced, the space left by a missing tooth leads to a change in the structure of your jaw. This in turn affects your dental bite and your ability to chew and speak properly. Dental implants also help to prevent loss of bone in the jaw, which occurs when there is no root left to stimulate regrowth of bone tissue.
