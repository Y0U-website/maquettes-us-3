How the Process Works

Comprehensive Consultation – We assess your oral health, bone density, and treatment goals using advanced 3D imaging.

Treatment Planning – A personalized plan is created to place 4–6 implants in the optimal positions for long-term stability.

Implant Surgery – Implants are surgically placed in the jaw. In many cases, a temporary full-arch prosthetic can be placed the same day.

Healing Phase – Over a few months, the implants integrate with your bone (osseointegration).

Final Restoration – A custom-made, permanent full-arch prosthetic is secured to the implants for a flawless, functional result.

Benefits of Full Arch Implant Therapy

Full Arch Implant Therapy is one of the most life-changing procedures we offer—delivering a smile that’s strong, secure, and beautifully functional.

  • Permanent solution – Unlike dentures, implants are fixed in place and don’t shift or slip
  • Restores natural function – Eat, speak, and smile with full confidence again
  • Bone preservation – Stimulates the jawbone to prevent bone loss over time
  • Natural look and feel – Custom-crafted teeth that look like your own
  • Improved quality of life – No adhesives, no dietary restrictions, no embarrassment
  • Fewer implants needed – Just 4–6 implants can restore an entire arch of teeth
