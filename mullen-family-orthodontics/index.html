The practice

We get it – teenagers sometimes lose or stop wearing their retainers. Our Smile Guarantee will make sure they enter adulthood with a stunning smile – no questions asked.

We offer free consultations to educate each patient and family in a friendly, no-pressure environment. We accept all major dental insurance plans and offer zero-interest, flexible payment plans.

Our preference whenever possible is to consolidate child/adolescent treatment into one comprehensive phase to save you time and money.

We never want finances to dissuade patients from getting the treatment that is most ideal for their particular smile, which is why we provide our aligner treatment for the same price as braces.
