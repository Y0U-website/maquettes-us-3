Accessibility Statement for Palmer Family Dental

Palmer Family Dental is committed to providing a website that is accessible and usable for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We work continuously to improve the accessibility of palmerfamilydentalpa.com and strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

We regularly monitor and test our website using automated tools and periodic human review to identify and address accessibility issues. As we implement improvements, some areas of the website may not yet fully comply with the WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards, but we are actively working to enhance accessibility across all pages. Our website may also include third-party content or integrations that we do not control, which could present accessibility challenges. We evaluate these tools and seek accessible alternatives whenever possible.

If you experience difficulty accessing information on our website, we are happy to provide assistance. Upon request, we can offer information, documents, or services by phone or email.

We welcome feedback about how we can improve accessibility. If you encounter any barriers or have suggestions, please contact us at:

Email: palmerfamilydental@aol.com Phone: 412-531-7770 Mailing Address: Palmer Family Dental, 1721 Cochran Rd, Pittsburgh, PA 15220

When contacting us, please include the webpage address and a brief description of the issue. We aim to respond to accessibility-related inquiries within 3–5 business days.

We are committed to maintaining an accessible website and will review and update this statement periodically to reflect ongoing improvements.
