Provisions of the Program:

This is not a dental insurance. Enrolling in Lillington Family Dentistry’s "In-House" Dental Savings Program gives you the opportunity to obtain your dental treatment at a reduced fee. Our In-House offer cannot be combined with dental insurance.

  • Yearly enrollment fees are non-refundable.
  • Initial fee is nonrefundable.
  • Enrollment fees are due at the first appointment for the year. No exceptions.
  • The two exams and cleanings must occur within the year of enrollment and cannot be rolled over to the next year.
  • By enrolling, the patient accepts responsibility for making and keeping his/her own appointments.
  • There is a $50.00 fee for broken appointments without 24hour notice.
  • Our program is not transferable to other patients
  • Participation cannot be combined with any other offer.
  • This program applies to patients who are eligible for regular cleanings. Please inquire about the separate program for patients with periodontal/gum disease.

Patients who participate in our Dental Savings Plan, experience:

  • No pre-authorization
  • No limitations for pre-existing conditions
  • Free consultations
