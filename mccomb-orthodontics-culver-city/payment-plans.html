Orthodontic Treatment

McComb Orthodontics prices Invisalign and traditional braces identically. Treatment cost is only a reflection of case complexity. Case complexity is not decided by treatment duration, but can be estimated by it.

  • Short case (6 to 10 months): $4000 to $5400
  • Standard case (12 to 16 months): $6000
  • Complex case (18 to 24+ months): $6600 to $7100
  • 7.5 % discount for patients paying the full treatment cost at the start of treatment.

In-House Financing

Every patient gets the same terms: 0 % interest, no credit check, no application.

Financing duration is tied to treatment length: 12-month cases finance over up to 24 months, 16-month cases over up to 24.

For PPO insurance patients, the lifetime benefit, typically $1,000 to $2,000, is applied at the consultation. The remaining balance is financed at 0 % interest.

In-House vs Third Party Financing

In-House Financing

  • No credit check and no credit score requirement for financing approval
  • No external application: payment agreement is directly with McComb Orthodontics
  • Payment adjustments available after treatment starts with no questions asked

Third-Party Financing

  • Credit check and approval required before financing is confirmed
  • Application submitted to an external lending company; terms set by the lender
  • Payment schedule is typically fixed by the lender with limited ability to adjust

Does McComb Orthodontics charge interest on the payment plan?

No. The in-house payment plan carries 0 % interest from the first payment to the last. There is no deferred-interest clause and no promotional period that expires. The total treatment cost you agree to at the consultation is the total you pay regardless of how long the financing runs.

Do I need good credit to finance orthodontic treatment at McComb Orthodontics?

No. McComb Orthodontics does not run a credit check for in-house financing. Approval is not based on credit history or credit score. Any patient can access the payment plan. Applying for in-house financing at McComb Orthodontics does not affect your credit score in any way.

What is the minimum down payment to start treatment?

Does dental insurance cover orthodontic treatment at McComb Orthodontics?

Yes. McComb Orthodontics is in-network with all PPO dental insurance providers. Most orthodontic insurance plans carry a lifetime benefit of $1,000 to $2,000. That benefit is applied to the treatment cost at the consultation, and the remaining balance is financed in-house at 0 % interest. Delta Dental and MetLife patients receive an additional contracted-rate benefit: approximately $700 less for Invisalign treatment compared to out-of-network pricing.

What happens to my payment plan if my dental insurance lapses mid-treatment?

If your dental insurance cancels or changes during active treatment, you are personally responsible for any remaining amount your insurance has not paid. We can adjust your in-house payment plan to ensure the balance owed is spread over the course of your treatment. What it means practically: McComb Orthodontics will no longer receive quarterly payments from your insurer for the remaining benefit, and the patient is responsible for those installments going forward. Our financial coordinators will walk through the specific adjustment at the consultation or any time during treatment.

What happens if I miss a payment?

McComb Orthodontics handles missed payments directly and without penalty. Our financial coordinators will work with you to help resolve the issue. There are no late fees in the in-house plan. If you are going through a difficult period, the right move is to call (310) 299-8894 and speak with our financial coordinators directly.

Can I use an HSA or FSA for orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic treatment is an eligible expense for both Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) under IRS guidelines. Patients who use HSA or FSA funds apply them toward the treatment cost at the consultation, and the remaining balance is financed in-house at 0 % interest. Are refinements included in the treatment fee?

Yes. All Invisalign refinements are included in the original treatment fee for compliant patients. A refinement is a standard part of finishing treatment, similar to adjusting a wire in braces. It is not an unexpected add-on or an extra charge. The only exception: patients who are serially non-compliant with tray wear may incur additional costs if Invisalign begins charging the practice for replacement tray sets. Compliant patients do not pay extra for refinements under any circumstance.

What if I need to stop treatment before it is finished?

If a patient discontinues treatment before completion, refunds are calculated case-by-case by the treatment coordinator and Dr. Ryan McComb based on the time elapsed and the treatment stages completed. Patients are not charged for work that has not been performed. The calculation accounts for clinical work done, lab fees incurred, and any insurance benefits already received. Patients who need to discontinue treatment for any reason should contact the practice directly at (310) 299-8894 to discuss the options.
