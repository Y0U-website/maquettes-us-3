Meet the Doctors

Matthew Bender, DMD

I grew up in North Carolina and have lived in Utah since attending BYU as an undergraduate. I have been providing comprehensive dentistry in the area since graduating from Roseman University: College of Dental Medicine and I love what I do.

I believe that high-quality dentistry includes not only a focused and methodical medical approach but also an artistic element. In that regard, I continually study and attend leading seminars to stay abreast of the latest trends in dental techniques and technologies, including the latest offerings in cosmetic dentistry and dental implants. I have seen countless patients dramatically improve their confidence and, in turn, their lives with beautiful new smiles that they never imagined they could have.

When not working, I enjoy spending time with my wife, Ashley, and our new baby girl. I also enjoy playing sports, hiking, camping, spending time with friends and family, and doing home-renovation projects.

I served an LDS mission in Bahia Blanca, Argentina and enjoy using Spanish to serve a greater number of people, including through some incredible humanitarian trips to Central and South America providing free dental care to those in need.

I love creating relationships with my patients, and I work hard to provide the highest quality of dental care. I look forward to meeting and serving each of you and your families. Together with Dr. Lewis and the excellent staff at Meadowbrook Dental, I am excited for what the future holds. If there is any way that I can help you, please don’t hesitate to ask.

Scott Lewis, DMD

I have grown up around dentistry and always knew I wanted to do something that involved working with my hands, problem solving, and working with people. There were many careers that involved the first two criteria but it was the interaction with others that made dentistry appealing to me. It is still my favorite part of dentistry, giving it variety and keeping things exciting.

I find many benefits for myself and my patients by being involved in organized dentistry and up to date research. I am a current member of the Utah Dental Association and American Dental Association and keep up on new techniques through CE courses and peer-reviewed journals. Advanced courses in implants, root canals, oral surgery techniques, and cosmetics are the courses I enjoy taking and look forward to helping you and your family and friends with the techniques I’ve learned.
