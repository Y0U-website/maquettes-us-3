Common Dental Emergencies

Severe Toothache

Severe toothache can be a sign of an underlying problem, such as decay, abscess, or infection. Ignoring pain can lead to severe complications. Hessville Smiles Dentist strives to identify the root cause of your pain and offer the best treatment for your situation.Often caused by gum disease or infections, toothaches can be very painful. We determine the cause and offer appropriate treatment for pain relief

Knocked Out Teeth

Time is critical if you have knocked a tooth out. Rinse it gently, and then try to put the tooth back into its socket. Avoid touching the root. If this is not possible, you can keep the tooth moist by placing it in a container with milk or saliva. Hessville Smiles Dentist can provide instructions and emergency treatment. The chances of saving a tooth are significantly increased if you act quickly. Time is critical if a permanent tooth has been knocked out. Keep the tooth moist and seek immediate care from our emergency dentist. If treated quickly, we may be able to re-implant the tooth.

Loose or Dislodged Teeth

Trauma can cause a tooth to become dislodged. If your tooth is loose, you must seek immediate treatment. Dr. Umar Haque will reposition and stabilize it to avoid further damage, increasing the chances of saving it. Please contact our office immediately for assistance.

Dental Abscess

An abscess in the mouth can be a serious infection. It can cause severe pain, swelling, and pus. Untreated, the infection can spread to other areas of the body, causing severe health problems. Hessville Smiles Dentist ’s team will treat the abscess immediately to eliminate the infection and restore oral hygiene.

Broken or Cracked Teeth

A cracked or broken tooth is susceptible to infection and further damage. Dr. Umar Haque will assess the extent of the problem and recommend the best treatment. This may include bonding, crowns, or, in more severe cases, extraction. It is important to seek immediate treatment to avoid complications. Accidents can cause teeth to break, chip, or crack. Treatments may include bonding, root canals, or placing dental crowns, depending on the severity of the issue

Damaged Dental Work

It is vital to repair a lost filling or crown as soon as possible to avoid further damage. Hessville Smiles Dentist offers its services in repairing damaged dental work.

Oral Cavity Injuries or Bleeding Gums

Due to the abundance of blood vessels in this area, injuries to the gums and other soft tissues may result in excessive bleeding. If bleeding persists, treat your situation as a dental emergency and seek immediate help.

Loss of Crowns or Fillings

A lost dental crown or filling can expose the tooth to further damage. We provide same-day restorations that restore the function and appearance of your tooth.

Soft Tissue Injuries

Injuries of the lips, gums, or tongue may cause discomfort and bleeding. We offer immediate treatment to help manage the injury and promote recovery.

Preventing Future Dental Emergencies

There are ways to reduce the risk of experiencing a dental emergency:

Following these preventive measures will help keep your teeth healthy and reduce the chances of an unexpected emergency.

  • Good Oral Hygiene: Brush and floss regularly to prevent cavities and gum disease.
  • Protective Gear: Use nightguards and mouthguards if you grind your teeth or play contact sports.
  • Regular Dental Check-Ups: Visit our office regularly for cleanings and exams to catch potential problems early.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: Be cautious with hard foods that can chip, crack, or break your teeth.
