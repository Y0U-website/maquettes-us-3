Who is Dr. Kelley Fisher?

Dr. Fisher’s Approach To Dentistry

After earning her Doctorate from Creighton University School of Dentistry, Dr. Fisher advanced her studies at the prestigious Dawson Academy, where she mastered the nuances of occlusion and full-mouth rehabilitation. Her commitment to excellence extends beyond her practice—she is the co-author of Teledontics and Telegnathics and an international speaker, sharing her insights on advanced smile restoration and the integration of holistic principles in modern dentistry.

Dr. Fisher proudly serves discerning patients in Sammamish and throughout the greater Seattle area, offering an experience defined by comfort, beauty, and total-body wellness. She maintains active membership with the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Dental Association, Seattle-King County Dental Society, Washington State Dental Association, Dentists for Oral Conscious Sedation, and the Eastside Comprehensive Study Club.

Why Choose Us?

“A healthy, beautiful smile is a powerful thing. People notice a great smile. In my practice, I have seen the life changing benefits it has had for my patients. I have felt the tremendous improvement in my own health and well-being by having a healthy bite. I invite you to see for yourself how transformative a new smile can be. By changing smiles, I get to change lives everyday!”

I often went to bed with a migraine and woke up in the morning with jaw pain. Through my own experience, I began to seek out advanced dental training. I was fortunate enough to learn more than just “tooth” dentistry. I learned how the muscles, joint, and teeth of the head and neck work together in harmony. I learned that form (beauty) and function are intimately linked which led me down a path of becoming the expert in cosmetic dentistry and smile makeovers in Sammamish and the greater Seattle area.
