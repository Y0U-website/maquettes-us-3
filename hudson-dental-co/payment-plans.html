How do I apply for financing?

Applying for financing is simple. Please use the following links to apply with Cherry or Proceed Finance. Most applications take just a few minutes to complete, and you&#x27;ll typically receive an approval decision right away.

What treatments can be financed?

Our financing options can be used for virtually all dental treatments we offer, including preventive care, restorative procedures, cosmetic dentistry, and emergency treatments. Our team will help you determine the best financing option for your specific treatment plan.

Can I combine insurance and financing?

Yes! Many patients use financing to cover the portion of treatment not covered by their insurance. We will assist all patients with getting out-of-network insurance benefits from their carrier and find a financing solution for any remaining balance.
