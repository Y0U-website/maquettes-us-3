The practice

Dr.Claire’s key focus is on providing patients with high-quality dental care in a comfortable environment. She believes that building a strong rapport with her patients is essential to providing the best possible care. She takes the time to listen to her patients’ concerns and works with them to develop a personalized treatment plan that meets their unique needs.

Whether you need a routine check-up or a more complex dental procedure, you can trust Dr. Claire to provide you with the personalized care and attention you deserve.

The Lake State Dental Discount Plan is an annual, reduced fee dental plan that allows individuals and families—just like yours—to receive quality dental services at lower prices. The Membership Plan is a great option for those who do not have dental insurance, so you can maintain great oral health at an affordable price.

Your Lake State Dental Discount Plan provides you with two teeth cleanings per year, 4 bitewing X-rays, two examinations, fluoride treatment, and oral cancer screening.

For all other necessary dental services, your membership also includes you a 10 % reduction in fees, so long as such fees are paid at the time of service, and you have had a cleaning within the last six months.
