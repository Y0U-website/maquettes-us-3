Orthodontics FAQs

Most clear aligner treatments take 6–18 months, depending on how much movement your teeth need. Small cosmetic adjustments may take only a few months, while more detailed alignment may take a little longer. During your consultation, we’ll use digital scans to show you a preview of your expected timeline — so you know exactly what to expect before starting.

You’ll remove them to eat, drink anything besides water, and brush your teeth. Other than that, they stay in place and work quietly in the background while you go about your day.

Yes — retainers keep your new smile stable and prevent teeth from shifting over time. We provide custom retainers and will review how often to wear them. Most patients transition to nighttime-only retention once teeth are fully settled.

Clear aligners are known for being gentle and comfortable. You may feel mild pressure when switching to a new tray (every 1–2 weeks), but this usually fades within a day. Most patients describe the feeling as “tight” rather than painful — and far easier than braces. If you’re sensitive to pressure, we’ll give you tips to make transitions even more comfortable.
