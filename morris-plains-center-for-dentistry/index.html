The practice

For over 30 years, our practice has served the Morris County community. Dr. Bagley continues this tradition of excellence with the philosophy of treating our patients as he would his own family. We believe in building trust through honest care and never over-treating.

Dr. Bagley only recommends the dentistry needed to maintain your oral health. He educates you about your dental needs, explains treatment options openly, and ensures you feel heard and valued throughout your visit.

Dr. Joshua Bagley’s journey into dentistry started at a young age, inspired by his father, who owned a dental laboratory. He spent hours watching the process of crafting restorations and was fascinated by the blend of science, artistry, and precision that dentistry requires.

As he explored career paths, Dr. Bagley knew he wanted to work in healthcare, but he also loved problem-solving and working with his hands. Dentistry provided the perfect combination, allowing him to help people, relieve pain, and create beautiful, healthy smiles. What he enjoys most about being a dentist isn’t just the technical side it’s the relationships he builds with his patients. He understands that trust is the foundation of great dental care and takes the time to listen, explain treatment options, and ensure every patient feels comfortable and informed. His goal is simple: to provide exceptional care while making every visit a positive experience.

Morris Plains Center for Dentistry welcomes all ages, from toddlers to seniors. Our complete range of services is designed to meet the unique needs of every family member so you can receive all the care you need in one warm and friendly location.

Morris Plains Center for Dentistry proudly welcomes patients from throughout Morris County, including Morris Plains, Parsippany-Troy Hills, Denville, Randolph, Morristown, Dover, and Rockaway.

We value your time and offer evening appointments on Wednesdays and Thursdays to accommodate your busy schedule. Our office accepts most PPO insurance plans, flexible payment options with Care Credit, and offers an in-house membership plan to make quality dental care accessible for everyone.
