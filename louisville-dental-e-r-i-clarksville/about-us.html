Who We Are

We are a group of dental professionals that noticed their is a large gap between traditional dental care, and emergency dental care. By definition Emergencies happen when they are least expected, so you can’t plan for them. That’s why we offer a seven day a week schedule from 8 am to 8 pm.

What We Do

The simple answer is we take care of people’s smiles. We offer all forms of dental treatment, with a focus on emergency dental care. This is the kind of care people don’t know they need until they absolutely need it. When you find yourself there, we will be there for you.

Why We Do It

The reason we do what we do is we have a passion for helping people take care of their smiles. Not only are we dentist’s, but we are also neighbors, fathers, mothers, sons, and daughters. We love seeing people smile, and if we can help you be proud of the smile you have, we are good with that.
