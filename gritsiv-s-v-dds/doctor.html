Meet the Doctor

After graduation Dr. Gritsiv worked in a multifaceted dental practice in Atlanta where he honed his professional skills. When it was time to settle and transition into owning a practice, Dr. Gritsiv decided to relocate to the Gainesville area and be part of this growing community. Dr. Gritsiv has made North Georgia his home and values the relationships that he, and his staff, have developed with his patients and realizes that is the key to making dentistry rewarding.

Dr. Gritsiv is a member of the Georgia Dental Association, American Dental Association,

Academy of General Dentistry, Northern District Dental Society, and the Hinman Dental Society. He is committed to continuing his dental education by completing more dentistry courses than is required in order to ensure quality care to his patients.

When he is not helping his patients at Oakwood Smiles, Dr. Gritsiv enjoys spending time with his family (including his dog, Zha-Zha), watching football, soccer, and hockey. He and his wife get the most joy from spending time with their daughter, Riley.
