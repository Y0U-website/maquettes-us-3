Technology

At North Seattle Smiles, we invest in technology that will enhance your experience by making treatment more comfortable, efficient, and productive. Please browse through the technology we currently offer, and don t hesitate to call if you have questions or need to schedule an appointment.

Digital X-Rays & Sensors

Digital x-rays and sensors offer a detailed view of your dental structure with significantly less radiation exposure than traditional x-rays. They provide instant, high-quality images, allowing for quicker diagnosis and treatment planning. This technology is essential for a wide range of services, including cavity detection, root canal therapy, and implant placement, enhancing patient care through precision and efficiency.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

Nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas, is a safe and effective sedative that helps ease your anxiety during dental procedures. It s administered through a small mask over the nose, and as you inhale it, it relaxes you without putting you to sleep. The benefits include a calming effect and quick recovery after treatment. At North Seattle Smiles, we use nitrous oxide for a variety of services, from simple cleanings to more complex procedures like root canals to ensure a more comfortable experience.

Laser Dentistry

Soft tissue lasers revolutionize treatments involving gums and other soft tissues and are a minimally invasive alternative to traditional surgery. This technology results in less discomfort, reduced bleeding, and faster healing times. At North Seattle Smiles we use dental lasers for gum reshaping, treating periodontal disease, and in some cases, canker sores and cold sore removal, for a more comfortable and effective treatment experience.

Intraoral Cameras

Intraoral cameras provide real-time, detailed images of the inside of your mouth, which enhances your education and involvement. This technology allows us to detect oral problems in their early stages. Since you can see what we see, it also gives you a better understanding of your oral health needs. We use intraoral cameras across various procedures, from routine exams to complex procedures, to enable precise diagnostics and empower you to make informed decisions about your treatment options.

Digital Records

Digital records modernize patient care by providing secure, immediate access to dental histories and treatment plans. This technology streamlines appointments and enhances communication between you and your dentist. With digital records, we ensure accuracy, improve continuity of care, and allow for quick updates, which all contributes to more personalized and efficient dental care.
