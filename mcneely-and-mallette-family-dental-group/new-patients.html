Your First Visit

From the moment you walk through our doors, your comfort comes first. Your initial visit includes digital X-rays, a comprehensive exam, and dedicated time with your dentist to ask questions, discuss concerns, and create a treatment plan tailored just for you.

Finances & Insurance:

We believe exceptional dental care should be within reach for every family. That’s why we verify your insurance benefits before your visit, helping you understand your coverage and avoid surprise costs. We’re in-network with several major PPO plans and proudly accept HSA, FSA, Sunbit Financing and CareCredit financing. Our goal is to make your experience smooth, stress-free, and affordable; so you can focus on your health, not the paperwork.
