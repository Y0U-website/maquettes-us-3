Interdisciplinary Orthodontic Care

Collaborative Planning for Comprehensive Dental Health

At Lowe Orthodontics, we understand that many patients—especially adults—have dental needs that go beyond orthodontics. Achieving the best esthetic and functional outcomes often requires the combined expertise of multiple dental professionals. In these cases, we take an interdisciplinary approach, collaborating closely with your general dentist and any necessary specialists to develop and sequence a customized treatment plan tailored to your overall oral health goals.

This team-based strategy is especially effective in complex cases where a single provider may not be able to address all aspects of care. As a patient, you benefit from the combined knowledge and skill of several experienced clinicians working together to deliver a cohesive, high-quality result.

Pre-Restorative Orthodontic Treatment

Before undergoing restorative dental procedures such as crowns, veneers, or bridges, it’s essential that the underlying structure and alignment of your teeth are optimized. During your consultation, our orthodontists will evaluate your bite, spacing, and alignment to identify any issues that may impact the longevity and success of your restorations.

By completing orthodontic treatment first, we help ensure your dental work fits properly, functions well, and lasts as long as possible.

  • Creating space to rebuild worn or damaged teeth
  • Correcting bite alignment to prevent future wear on new restorations
  • Improving esthetics by aligning teeth before cosmetic restorations

Pre-Implant Orthodontic Treatment

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth, but proper placement requires adequate space and alignment. In many cases—especially in adults who have had missing teeth for a long time—the surrounding teeth may have shifted, compromising the space needed for implants.

Because dental implants are fixed and cannot be moved once placed, orthodontics should often be completed prior to implant placement to ensure the best outcome.

At Lowe Orthodontics, we are experienced in coordinating care with restorative dentists, periodontists, prosthodontists, and oral surgeons. Our goal is to provide seamless, integrated treatment that results in a healthy, beautiful smile—and a long-lasting result you can feel confident about.

  • Reopen space where teeth have drifted or collapsed
  • Align surrounding teeth to allow for ideal implant placement
  • Ensure long-term stability of the implant by optimizing bite forces
