The practice

Regular dental exams help prevent dental problems from developing. Routine dental appointments involve an exam by the dentist along with education from the dental hygienist on optimal oral health. Medical history review, digital xrays, a periodontal assessment to determine the best care for you and an oral cancer screening are completed during the exam.

Composite fillings restore a tooth to shape and function while blending to the natural tooth color. Composite fillings are made up of ceramic, glass and silica particles added to a resin base that are then bonded and light cured to the tooth.
