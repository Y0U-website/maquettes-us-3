Advantages of Dental Perio Scaling and Root Planing

Undergoing dental scaling and root planing in Prattville, Alabama, offers numerous benefits, including:

  • Improved Gum Health: Removal of plaque and tartar enables gums to heal and reduces inflammation, halting the progression of gum disease .
  • Fresher Breath: Elimination of bacterial buildup helps combat persistent bad breath stemming from periodontal infections.
  • Potential Tooth Preservation: Early intervention in gum disease reduces the risk of tooth loss and minimizes the necessity for extensive dental procedures .
  • Enhanced Overall Health: Periodontal disease has been associated with various systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes. Treating gum disease can contribute to improved overall health.
