General dentistry is a field of dentistry that focuses on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of oral health issues. It includes preventive dental care, such as brushing, flossing, and regular checkups, as well as more advanced services, such as fillings, crowns, and bridges.

Cosmetic dentistry is a field of dentistry that focuses on improving the appearance of a person’s teeth, mouth, and smile. It can involve a variety of procedures such as teeth whitening, dental implants, veneers, crowns, bridges, and more.

Preventative dentistry is a branch of dentistry that focuses on the prevention of oral health problems such as tooth decay, cavities, gum disease and other dental issues. Preventative dental care includes regular checkups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, sealants, and other treatments that are designed to keep your teeth and gums healthy and prevent future problems from occurring.
