How do I clean my dentures?

Cleaning your dentures is the best way to ensure their long life. Here are a couple tips for keeping your dentures in clean, excellent condition.

  • Brush your dentures under warm, running water with a soft toothbrush.
  • Use a cleanser, such as Bright Now or Sparkle denture cleaners, which are both available at Ontario Dental Arts.
  • Soak your dentures in a cleaning solution daily for best cleansing results, and rinse thoroughly before use.
  • Brush your gums daily with a soft toothbrush and toothpaste. This will eliminate food buildup and promote a healthy mouth.

What do I avoid?

  • Avoid dropping your dentures. Most breakage occurs during cleaning. A good strategy to protect your dentures is to clean them over a sink filled with water or a towel.
  • Hold your dentures in your fingers, not in the palm of your hand. This will help to keep from grasping and bending or breaking if grasped too hard.
  • Never use toothpaste on your dentures. Abrasives in toothpaste will harm your dentures with repeated use.
