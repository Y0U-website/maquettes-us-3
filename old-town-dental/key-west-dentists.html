We have a better way

At Old Town Dental, we’re all about teeth – young ones, old ones, shiny ones, crooked or chipped ones. That’s why you can think of us as your “Dental Home.” Because no matter what your tooth issue, we’re here to resolve it.

Our general dentists, specialists and hygienists are all under one roof. Which means that treatment for implants, or periodontal care can take place in the same office. Panoramic x-rays, oral surgery, restorative work requires a visit to only one location. And families are welcome – from young children to senior citizens – because we have specialists for every age. That can save you time and expense – plus assure you of quality care by one familiar, cohesive team.

The beautiful weather and the spectacular fly fishing were what first attracted Dr. Mullen to Key West, but it was the Old Town Dental team and the state-of-the-art facility that convinced him and his wife, Rosanna, to settle in Key West permanently.
