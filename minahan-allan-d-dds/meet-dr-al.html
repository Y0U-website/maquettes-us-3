Allan Minahan, DDS

I am constantly taking continuing education courses in order to stay on top of current ideas, procedures, and treatments. These courses are a great way to explore new equipment, evaluate techniques, and expand my knowledge and skills. Ultimately, continuing education is about enhancing the patient experience and delivering ever better results.

I am active in local and state dental organizations, attend many meetings, lectures, and seminars each year, and participate in dental study clubs. I’m also a Clinical Participant in testing and analyzing new dental equipment and volunteer as a mentor for prospective dental students.

In the Community

I am involved in local mentoring programs for youth interested in the dental profession. It’s been a pleasure to mentor kids who have a passion for dentistry and oral healthcare, and have helped many pursue their goals.

I also volunteer in our church, supporting many community events and projects including feeding the less fortunate, free dental treatment days, and holiday events. Additionally, I volunteer at my kids’ school, coaching their sports teams and offering dental health lessons.

I love being an active member of the Kenmore community. It’s rewarding to get out and meet people, and to give back to the community that mentored and supported me!

Beyond the Practice

I grew up here in Kenmore and returned to the area after finishing studies at Pepperdine University in Southern California. I now live ten houses away from where I grew up!

My wife Jennifer works in insulin pump medical device sales with Medtronic. We have two amazing kids, Owen and Emmarie, who are active in every sport they can work into their busy schedules. Depending on the time of year, you’ll find our family at local fields, courts, pools, or golf courses!
