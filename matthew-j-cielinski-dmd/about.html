About Cielinski Family Dental in Keene, NH

Cielinski Family Dental has been delivering results in the Keene area since 2003. We fell in love with the Keene area and the office space and decided it was a perfect place to raise our new family. Our goal is to provide a superior customer experience and tremendous value for our customers.

Our Office

We use modern dental equipment to provide our patients with quality dental care in a comfortable environment. To improve sterilization and infection control we have recently installed a state-of-the-art sterilization station and placed HEPA filters in our treatment rooms. Our treatment rooms and reception area overlook the Ashuelot River making it a pleasant setting for your dental experience.

The reception area has a comfortable, living room-like atmosphere. We play an eclectic mix of music from classical to popular rock to help make your visit enjoyable.

Dr. Matthew J. Cielinski, DMD

Dr. Cielinski, known as Dr. C by many of our patients was raised in a quiet town in Maine. His parents never thought he’d finish school because he first graduated from the University of Maine at Orono with a BA in Zoology, and then obtained a PhD in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Massachusetts.

He actively pursued a career in dental-related research with a postdoctoral fellowship at the prestigious Forsyth Dental Center in Boston. Dr. Cielinski graduated from the Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University with a DMD degree and multiple honors, including the Altshuler Family Student Award for high ethical standards and Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Cielinski began his practice in Keene in 2003. He is a member of the American Dental Society, the New Hampshire Dental Society and the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

He participates in a variety of community services such as Dental Lifeline/Donated Dental Services. He resides in Keene with his wife and two children. Dr. Cielinski enjoys photography, travel, fishing, reading, music and spending time with his family.

Our Staff

Our friendly and experienced staff works hard to make every dental visit as pleasant as possible. This begins with our administrative team, who will help you schedule your appointments and understand your financial responsibilities. For those patients with dental insurance, we strive to help you use and understand your insurance benefits. If needed, we will also help you negotiate with your insurance company to ensure you receive all the money you are entitled to.

Our hygiene team is dedicated to making your cleaning appointments efficient, informative, and friendly. They strive to be thorough in their technique while keeping you apprised of your progress towards optimal dental health.

Our staff works hard to keep up with the latest technological advances and complete continuing education courses to ensure you receive the best possible dental care.
