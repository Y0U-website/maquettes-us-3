Restorative care

When teeth are damaged or decayed, we use conservative, tooth-colored materials to restore your smile’s strength and natural appearance.

  • Composite (white/tooth-colored) fillings
  • Porcelain and zirconia crowns
  • Dental bridges to replace missing teeth

Root canal therapy

A root canal can save a tooth that would otherwise need to be extracted. Modern techniques make the procedure far more comfortable than most patients expect.

  • Relieves tooth pain and clears infection
  • Preserves your natural tooth
  • Step-by-step explanation throughout

Gum disease treatment

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. We treat early-stage and advanced gum disease with deep cleaning procedures and ongoing maintenance.

  • Scaling & root planing (deep cleaning)
  • Periodontal maintenance visits
  • Personalized home-care coaching

Tooth replacement

Missing teeth affect your ability to chew, speak, and feel confident. We’ll help you explore every option and choose a solution that suits your lifestyle and budget.

  • Full and partial dentures
  • Fixed dental bridges
  • Implant-supported crown restorations

Cosmetic dentistry

Small changes can make a big difference. We’ll help you choose cosmetic options that look natural, last well, and fit your budget.

More services Additional dental services we offer Dental Bridges Fixed, natural-looking tooth replacement that anchors to adjacent teeth, no removal, no adhesive. Porcelain and zirconia options available.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Dental bonding (chips, gaps, discoloration)
  • Smile refresh with natural-looking crowns

Dental Bonding

Repair chips, close small gaps, and correct minor discoloration in a single visit, no anesthesia needed in most cases. One of our most affordable cosmetic options.

Emergency Dentistry

Toothaches, swelling, broken teeth, and lost crowns need prompt attention. We offer same-day urgent care and do our best to see you the day you call.

Digital X-Rays

Our digital imaging system uses up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film, produces instant results, and delivers sharper images for better diagnosis.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening delivers dramatically brighter results in a single visit, far more effective and uniform than over-the-counter strips.

Dental Crowns

A crown covers and protects a damaged, cracked, or heavily filled tooth, restoring its full shape, strength, and appearance with a natural-looking porcelain or zirconia cap.

Dental Implants

The most permanent solution for a missing tooth, a titanium root topped with a crown that looks, feels, and functions exactly like your natural tooth.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth can’t be saved, gentle removal is the next step. We make extractions as comfortable as possible and always discuss replacement options before you leave.
