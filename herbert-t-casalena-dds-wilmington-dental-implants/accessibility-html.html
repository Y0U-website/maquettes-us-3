Accessibility Statement

  • Back to Wilmington Dental & Implants Accessibility Statement Last updated: April 14, 2026 Our Commitment Wilmington Dental & Implants is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We strive to comply with applicable accessibility standards, including the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines. Website Accessibility We are continuously working to improve accessibility and usability. Our efforts include: Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Sufficient color contrast
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Semantic HTML structure
  • "Skip to content" functionality
  • Accessible forms with labels and ARIA attributes
  • Responsive design across devices
  • Readable typography standards

Third-Party Content

Some third-party content or features may not be fully accessible. We encourage users to contact us if accessibility issues arise.

Accessibility Tools

Our website may include an accessibility tool to enhance usability. While helpful, it may not address all accessibility needs.

Physical Office Accessibility

Our office is designed to comply with applicable ADA accessibility requirements and includes:

  • Ground-level accessible entrance
  • Accessible parking
  • Wide doorways and treatment areas
  • Integration within a medical campus environment

Alternative Access

If you experience difficulty accessing any content on our website, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or services you need through alternative methods.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review and update our website to improve user experience for all visitors.

Feedback
