Lavoie Pediatric Dentistry is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all patients and families, including individuals with disabilities.

We strive to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to enhance usability.

Accessibility Standards

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards wherever possible.

Ongoing Efforts

We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website by:

  • Improving navigation and usability
  • Enhancing compatibility with assistive technologies
  • Reviewing content for clarity and accessibility

Third-Party Content

Some third-party integrations may not fully meet accessibility standards. We encourage users to contact us if they encounter any issues.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, please contact us:

📞 508-233-3341 🌐 www.lavoiepediatricdentistry.com

We will do our best to provide the information or assistance you need in a timely manner.
