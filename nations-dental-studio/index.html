The practice

The office is at 4900 Centennial Blvd in The Nations, an independent, woman-owned practice in West Nashville.

Dr. Diana D’Aoust is originally from Rochester, NY. She earned her DMD from Boston University School of Dental Medicine , practiced in DC and East Nashville, and opened Nations Dental Studio in 2021. She lives in The Nations with her husband and two daughters.

She’s a certified Invisalign provider, a member of the ADA and Nashville Dental Society, and holds advanced certifications in facial aesthetics from the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics. She built Nations Dental Studio around a simple idea: a calm environment, clear communication, and enough time to actually get to know her patients.
