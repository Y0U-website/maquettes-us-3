Patients Frequently Ask

Is the procedure painful?

The All-on-4 teeth in a day procedure is often less painful than most patients expect. The reason for this is that there are no nerve endings inside the bone, which means that you won’t feel pain in that area. Most of the discomfort or soreness comes from the manipulation of the gum tissue, where the nerves are located. In fact, the soreness typically lasts for just a few days, not weeks.

The majority of our patients find that over-the-counter (OTC) medication is sufficient to manage any discomfort. It’s important to note that nerves are primarily inside the teeth, not within the bones. On average, patients may need pain medication for the first 2-3 days after the procedure, but they are usually off pain medication within a week or even sooner. So, while there may be some soreness, the procedure is generally not as painful as one might imagine.

What is the Process Like?

The All-on-4 dental procedure offers a remarkable way to transform your smile in just one day. This process starts with a surgical procedure where you receive a beautiful set of temporary’ teeth in a single day. Over the next few months, the bone in your jaw integrates with the titanium implants, and your gum tissue settles, possibly requiring minor adjustments. The All-on-4 procedure is highly customizable, allowing you to address both upper and lower jaws in one session or one at a time, depending on your unique needs.

One of the key advantages of this procedure is its ability to eliminate the need for traditional dental work like crowns, bridges, or fillings, simplifying your dental care routine. Patients often find that their diet and overall quality of life improve significantly with their new teeth, as they can comfortably enjoy a wider variety of foods.
