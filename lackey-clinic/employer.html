Employer Referral Program

Employees with access to affordable health & dental care are more likely to take less sick leave and have increased productivity and job satisfaction.

We serve uninsured adults in Virginia, who meet income requirements.

If you have any questions, please call us at 757.886.0608 ext. 103 (Se habla Español). Thank you!

Services We Provide

Lackey Clinic provides the following services for uninsured adults in Virginia:

Primary and Specialty Medical Care | Dental Care | Better Vision Eye Care | Free and Low-Cost Medications | Behavioral Health Counseling | Telehealth Services | Spiritual Care

We ask for a contribution of $25 for medical, eye care, and behavioral health appointments if patients are able to contribute.

Starting October 1st, 2025: For dental fees, we will charge $40 per appointment (including exams, cleanings, fillings, extractions, and scaling). Fees are due at time of booking. If a patient no shows for their dental appointment or are more than 15 minutes late, there will be no refund. (Crown and root canal fees vary.)

Services NOT Available

Chronic Pain Management | Pre-natal Care | Substance Abuse Treatment | Treatment for Severe Mental Illness | Disability physicals and Workmen’s Compensation physicals

Your Employee’s Eligibility Requirements

Your employee’s yearly income, based on family size, must be within the range on the chart.

If applying for medical services, your employee must not have medical insurance.

If applying for dental only services, your employee must not have dental insurance.

Your employee must live in Virginia.

Your employee must be 18 years of age or older.
