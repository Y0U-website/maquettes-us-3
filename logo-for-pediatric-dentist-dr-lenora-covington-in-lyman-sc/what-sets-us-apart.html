What Sets Us Apart

  • We provide useful, practical parent and child education, with a focus on prevention, nutrition, and establishing good oral hygiene habits.
  • We offer a safe and positive environment, where children are free to be themselves. Using common words, a Tell-Show-Do approach, and encouraging patients to talk with us about their worries, we establish comfort in the dental office.
  • We recognize the uniqueness of each child, designing visits and interactions to their specific needs, always mindful of their comfort and offering praise and support.
  • We actively involve families in their child’s treatment and care, explaining diagnoses and discussing treatment options. Informed parents are able to make knowledgeable decisions and are engaged advocates for their little one’s health and wellbeing.
