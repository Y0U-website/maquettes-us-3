Why People Get Dental Implants

People get dental implants in order to replace a missing tooth or missing teeth in their mouth. Dental implants can effectively be lifelong solutions if they are properly taken care of, which is why they are so beneficial when compared to other artificial tooth solutions like crowns and bridges , which will eventually need to be replaced over time even with proper care.

Another benefit of dental implants is that they allow for the continuation of normal chewing and in some cases are aesthetically beneficial, especially when they are used to replace missing teeth that are closer to the front of the mouth.
