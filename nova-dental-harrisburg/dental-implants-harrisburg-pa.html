About Dental Implants

Treatment

Assessment

We conduct a detailed exam and digital imaging to assess your oral health and ensure your jawbone is ready for implant placement.

Placement

A small titanium post is carefully inserted into your jawbone. This post will act as a secure root for your future tooth restoration.

Healing

Over the next several weeks, your implant will naturally fuse with your jawbone (osseointegration), providing a strong foundation.

Restoration

Once healing is complete, we place a custom-made crown (or bridge/denture), giving you a fully restored, functional, and natural-looking tooth.

Dental Implants Q&A

Dental implants are permanent prosthetic devices designed to replace missing teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they integrate over time to provide unmatched stability.

The result? A tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth - allowing you to smile, eat, and speak with complete confidence.

Dental implants offer numerous advantages, including:

Strong, stable bite - chew your favorite foods with ease Natural appearance - blends seamlessly with your smile Improved speech - no slipping or clicking Preserves jawbone - prevents bone loss Long-lasting durability - implants can last a lifetime with proper care Convenience - no adhesives, no soaking - brush and floss like natural teeth

First Visit - Implant Placement After a complete evaluation, we numb the area and carefully place the titanium implant post into your jawbone. You’ll receive detailed aftercare instructions and any necessary medications.

Healing Period - 3 to 4 Months During this time, your implant will integrate with your jawbone, providing a strong, lasting foundation for your new tooth.

Second Visit - Impressions Once healing is complete, we take precise digital impressions to create your custom crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture.

Final Visit - Restoration Placement Your new restoration is securely attached to the implant post. The result? A fully functional, beautiful smile that looks and feels completely natural — with no downtime required after this visit.
