Protecting Your Teeth

Routine Services for Lasting Oral Health

Comprehensive Care General Dentistry Services Cleaning and Prevention Regular cleanings and preventive care are vital for maintaining a healthy smile. At Nielsen Dental Studio, we offer thorough dental cleanings to remove plaque and tartar, combined with comprehensive exams to detect potential issues early. Our goal is to keep your teeth and gums in optimal condition, preventing problems before they start.

Fillings

Fillings are essential for preventing tooth decay and correcting cosmetic damage, helping our dentists preserve your oral health. Before placing a filling, any decay is carefully removed. We provide local anesthesia and sedation options to ensure your comfort during the procedure. There are two types of fillings available: amalgam (silver fillings) and composite resin, which can be color-matched to your natural teeth.

Tooth and Wisdom Tooth Extractions

Tooth and wisdom tooth extractions are sometimes necessary to maintain your oral health. At Nielsen Dental Studio, we perform extractions with care and precision, ensuring your comfort throughout the process. Whether it’s a damaged tooth or impacted wisdom teeth, our experienced team is here to provide safe and effective removal to preserve your overall dental well-being.

3D X-Rays

Our advanced 3D X-ray and imaging technology provides detailed views of your teeth, bones, and soft tissues. This state-of-the-art imaging allows for precise diagnosis and treatment planning, ensuring the highest level of care for your dental needs. Experience the benefits of enhanced accuracy and better outcomes with our cutting-edge 3D imaging services.
