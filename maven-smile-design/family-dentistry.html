What is Family Dentistry?

Family dentistry is a branch of dentistry that focuses on providing comprehensive oral care to patients of all ages, from young children to seniors. Family dentists are trained to diagnose, treat, and prevent various dental issues, from routine check-ups and cleanings to more complex restorative and cosmetic procedures.

Our Family Dentistry Services

Dr. Ralston is passionate about helping his patients achieve optimal oral health and a beautiful smile. He takes the time to listen to his patient’s concerns and goals and works with them to develop a customized treatment plan that meets their individual needs. As a family dentist, Dr. Ralston provides a wide range of dental services for patients of all ages. Whether you need a routine check-up or more complex restorative or cosmetic work, he and his team are here to help. Some of our services include:

Regular check-ups and cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral health. During your routine dental exam, our team will check for signs of tooth decay, gum disease, and other dental issues. We will also provide professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup, leaving your teeth looking and feeling clean and healthy.

If you have a cavity or other dental issue, we offer a range of restorative treatments to help restore your teeth to their natural function and appearance. Our team will work with you to determine the best treatment, whether a simple filling or more complex restorative work like a crown or bridge.

Dental implants are an excellent option for restoring your smile if you have missing teeth. Our team has the experience and expertise to provide high-quality dental implant treatments that look and function like natural teeth.

If you have a severely damaged or infected tooth, root canal therapy can help save your tooth and prevent the need for extraction. Our team uses the latest technology and techniques to provide comfortable and effective root canal treatments.

Teeth Whitening and Other Cosmetic Procedures

If you want to enhance your smile, we offer a range of cosmetic treatments, including teeth whitening, veneers, and more. Our team will work with you to achieve the smile of your dreams.

If you experience anxiety or fear when visiting the dentist, we offer sedation dentistry to help you feel relaxed and at ease during your appointment. Our team will work with you to determine the best sedation option.

Once you and doctor ralston have determined that the fit of your bridge feels right, they will use powerful dental cement to attach it permanently to your abutment teeth, restoring your smile and your bite.
