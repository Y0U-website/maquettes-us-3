Braces

At Legacy Orthodontics, we understand the impact of a beautifully aligned smile on your confidence and oral health. Braces have a transformative impact on both dental health and personal confidence. By gradually shifting teeth into proper alignment, they may improve bite function, reduce the risk of tooth decay and gum disease, and alleviate jaw pain.

Braces for Teens

Early Intervention

Braces during adolescence can correct dental issues before they worsen, often leading to shorter treatment times and better results.

Permanent Teeth Alignment

Since teens’ mouths are still developing, braces can guide teeth into place more efficiently, preventing future complications.

Boost in Self-Esteem

Teens often become more confident with straighter teeth, which can positively affect their social interactions and self-image during a formative time.

Braces for Adults

It’s Never Too Late

Braces can be effective at any age. Whether you missed the opportunity for braces as a teen or your teeth have shifted over time, we offer solutions to help you achieve a straight, healthy smile.

Improved Oral Health

Correcting misaligned teeth reduces the risk of gum disease, tooth decay, and jaw pain, contributing to better long-term dental health.

Aesthetic Benefits

A straighter smile can enhance appearance and boost confidence, which is important for personal and professional interactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will I need to wear braces?

The duration of your orthodontic treatment with braces varies based on your individual needs and the complexity of your case. On average, most patients wear braces for 18 to 24 months. During your initial consultation, our Drs. Hansen and deSa will provide a personalized treatment plan and a more accurate timeline based on your specific situation.

Do braces hurt?

It is common to experience some discomfort when braces are first applied or after adjustments. This discomfort is usually mild and temporary, lasting only a few days. Over-the-counter pain relievers and orthodontic wax can help alleviate any soreness. Our team will guide you on how to manage discomfort and ensure your experience is as comfortable as possible.

What foods should I avoid while wearing braces?

While wearing braces, it’s important to avoid foods that can damage the brackets and wires. These include hard foods like nuts and hard candies, sticky foods like gum and caramel, and chewy foods like bagels and licorice. We recommend eating softer foods and cutting harder foods into smaller pieces to protect your braces. Our team will provide you with a detailed list of foods to avoid and tips for maintaining a braces-friendly diet.
