About Natural State Dental

Our Story

At Natural State Dental, Dr. Brumley and our team are dedicated to more than just great dentistry — we’re committed to building genuine relationships with every patient who walks through our doors. We believe dental care should feel personal, and we take the time to get to know each patient and understand their unique needs. Dr. Brumley is passionate about alleviating pain and making patients feel completely at ease, whether they’re coming in for a routine cleaning or something more complex. To us, this practice is more than a business. It’s a family.

Our office is designed to feel more like a spa than a dental clinic, complete with comfortable seating, soothing music, a refreshment bar, and sedation options for patients who need a little extra reassurance. From your very first visit, you’ll find a team that genuinely cares about helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile in the most comfortable way possible.

Dr. Abbey Brumley

Dr. Abbey Brumley is not just passionate about dentistry; she cherishes the relationships she builds with her patients. For Dr. Brumley, dentistry is more than treating teeth; it’s about connecting with people on a deep level and fostering friendships. She finds joy in the artistry of dentistry and the profound satisfaction that comes from alleviating someone’s pain. Dr. Brumley aims to make patients feel completely at ease with their treatment, ensuring a comfortable and positive experience.

When she’s not transforming smiles, Dr. Brumley loves spending quality time outdoors with her husband and daughter, whether it’s going for walks, hiking, enjoying the lake, or traveling to new places. An avid reader, she also finds solace in books. Dr. Brumley is an active Cross Church member, serving in the nursery, giving back to her community, and nurturing her spiritual life

Continuing Education Committed to staying at the forefront of dental innovations and techniques, Dr. Brumley pursues at least 25 credits per year in continuing education, focusing on Orthodontics, Botox, and Implants. This dedication ensures that Natural State Dental offers the most advanced and effective treatments available.

Professional Affiliations Dr. Brumley is proud to be associated with prestigious organizations such as the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Arkansas State Dental Association (ASDA), reflecting her commitment to excellence in dentistry.
