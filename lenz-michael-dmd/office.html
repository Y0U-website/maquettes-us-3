Dental Insurance Information

For your convenience, we are pleased to file your treatment claims on your behalf to your insurance company. We accept all major PPO dental insurances including MetLife , Guardian , and more. Traditional benefit ( indemnity ) plans do not stipulate where you can go. You can see any dentist (including us) and receive the full benefits of the policy. Delaware Medicaid accepted for children up to the age of 21.
