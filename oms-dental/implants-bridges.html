What are bridges and how they work?

Dental bridges are fixed prosthetic devices used to replace one or more missing teeth by bridging the gap between natural teeth. At OMS Dental , bridges are anchored in place using crowns on adjacent teeth or dental implants for support. They restore function, appearance, and alignment , helping maintain a natural bite and preventing surrounding teeth from shifting.
