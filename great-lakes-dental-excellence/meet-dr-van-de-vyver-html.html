Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

In his free time, Dr. Van de Vyver enjoys creating things with his hands, whether this involves using his 3D printers at home to make toys and fidgets for the kids or working on computers. He is also really into board games and puzzles, even competing in local library puzzle competitions. Fun fact: Dr. Van de Vyver played trumpet in high school and could have gone to university on a music scholarship, but he felt science and engineering was his true life path.
