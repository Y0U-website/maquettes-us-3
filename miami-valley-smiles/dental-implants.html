Implant-Supported Bridge

When two or more neighboring teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge may be an excellent alternative to a traditional dental bridge. Dental implants are used to support the replacement teeth, reducing the need to use healthy neighboring teeth for support.

Implant-supported bridges can restore several missing teeth while providing a secure, comfortable, and natural-looking result. They can also help restore chewing function and prevent the gaps left by missing teeth from affecting your smile.

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients who are missing most or all of their teeth, dental implants can be used to provide additional support and stability for dentures. Implant-supported dentures attach to strategically placed implants, helping reduce slipping and movement while eating, speaking, and smiling.
