The practice

We have over 30 years of providing high-quality, personalized dental care to friends and neighbors of all ages, and we can’t wait to welcome you! After just one visit, you’ll see why patients in Chesapeake, Great Bridge, Deep Creek, Grassfield, Hickory, Northeast North Carolina, and surrounding areas have chosen us to serve their dental needs.

Many of our team members grew up in this area and have been working with us for decades. They are professionals who love what they do, and their mission is to treat every patient with the kindness and respect they deserve. We understand that you are someone’s father, mother, child, spouse, or sibling, and we strive to care for you as we would our own loved ones.

In dentistry, there’s often more than one way to address a problem, and we will always explain all your options. We will tell you why we might recommend one treatment over another, but you are always in control of your dental care. The dentists of Great Bridge Dental are here to guide and inform you, but the ultimate decision about treatment is always up to you!

Are you tired of visiting large group dental practices where you see a different dentist every time? During your first appointment with us, we will assign one of our dentists and hygienists to you. They will be your oral health care partners as long as you’re a patient at our practice. When your smile is as healthy as it can be, we can talk to you about correcting any cosmetic dental flaws like chipped teeth or broken teeth, tooth staining, or wide gaps and uneven spacing.

Are you nervous about visiting the dentist? Let us pamper you with a neck pillow and blanket so you can sit back and relax while you listen to your favorite tunes. If you need a little more help to relax, ask us about comforting sedation with nitrous oxide. You can get the dental care you need, and it has no lingering after-effects.

Going to the dentist can be stressful enough for some people, so we do everything we can to make it easy for you to get the essential care you need. We will work with your financial needs because you deserve the highest quality dental care regardless of your budget.

We are in-network with Delta Dental Premier and the Anthem BC/BS FEP Blue, Complete and Grid+ Networks. We accept PPO policies that allow you to go out-of-network. You would only be responsible for the difference between what we charge and what the insurance pays.
