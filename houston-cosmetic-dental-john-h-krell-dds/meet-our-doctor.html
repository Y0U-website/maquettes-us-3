Community Leadership

Beyond the practice, Dr. Krell served on the Board of Directors of the Greater Southwest Houston Chamber of Commerce for six years, where he developed and chaired the healthcare committee. His volunteerism earned him the Distinguished Director Service Award for his long-term service to the community.

He has sponsored and hosted numerous fundraisers, supporting the Fund for Animals and the SPCA, organizing Toys for Tots drives, and even playing Santa Claus for Covenant House. Giving back to Houston remains close to his heart.

Beyond the Office

Dr. Krell is devoted to his profession, but his two children, Alexandria and Johnny, are the loves of his life. And when the winds and tides are just right, you might find him wading through the South Texas marshes in search of trout or redfish — still hoping, one day, to land a tagged redfish or a world-class trout.
