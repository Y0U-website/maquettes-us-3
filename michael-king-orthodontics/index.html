Dr. Michael King received his Bachelor of Science in Biology with minors in both Chemistry and Art from Westminster College, Salt Lake City, Utah, graduating in 2004. He then went on to graduate top of his class, Summa Cum Laude in 2009 from the University of Nevada Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine. After dental school, Dr. King furthered his education by attending Louisiana State University’s…

Dr. King has become involved in many professional Orthodontic and Dental Organizations. Dr. King is Board Certified and holds the title of Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. He is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Midwestern Society of Orthodontists, and the Alumni Associations at UNLV and LSU.

Dr. King is a hard working and dedicated individual who is motivated by challenges and feels great satisfaction in finding creative solutions. He enjoys working with his hands and concentrating on details and was drawn towards orthodontics when he saw the opportunity to develop a personal working relationship with his patients.

In June 2009, Dr. King married the love of his life, Kelly. Together, they enjoy spending time with their son Roman, family, friends and their dog Banjo.
