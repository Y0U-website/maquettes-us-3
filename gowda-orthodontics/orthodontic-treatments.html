Clear (Ceramic) Braces

For those seeking a more discreet option, clear ceramic braces are a great choice. These braces blend with the color of your teeth, making them less noticeable compared to traditional metal braces. Ceramic braces work just as effectively but with a more refined aesthetic.

How they work: Just like metal braces, clear braces use small brackets and a wire to gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. The only difference is the color of the brackets, which are made of a tooth-colored ceramic material.

  • A more discreet treatment option
  • Ideal for teens and adults who want to maintain a professional look during treatment
  • Effective for moderate to severe cases

Clear Aligners: Invisalign®, Angel Aligners™, Spark™

For those who want a more subtle and flexible option, clear aligners are the way to go. We offer the latest in clear aligner technology, including Invisalign® , Angel Aligners™ , and Spark™ . These systems are designed to be virtually invisible, making them a popular choice for adults and teens who don’t want to wear traditional braces.

How they work: Clear aligners are removable, allowing you to eat and drink what you like, and brush and floss normally. Aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth and gradually shift them into the desired position. You wear each set of aligners for a specific period (usually 1-2 weeks) before moving on to the next set.

  • Virtually invisible and easy to wear
  • Removable for eating, drinking, and cleaning
  • Comfortable with fewer office visits and adjustments
  • Ideal for mild to moderate alignment issues

Early Treatment (Phase 1) for Children

Phase 1 treatment is designed for children in their early years, typically between the ages of 6 and 10, to guide their jaw growth and ensure that there’s enough room for adult teeth to come in properly. Early treatment can help prevent the need for more extensive treatment later on.

How it works: Phase 1 may involve the use of space maintainers, expanders, or partial braces to address issues like crossbites, crowding, or jaw alignment problems. The goal is to address the problem early to ensure a healthier and more comfortable bite as they grow.

  • Prevents more complex issues from developing later
  • Helps with spacing and jaw alignment
  • Sets a solid foundation for the eruption of adult teeth

TADs (Temporary Anchorage Devices) for Precise Movement

In certain cases, TADs (Temporary Anchorage Devices) may be used to achieve more precise tooth movements. These tiny, titanium screws are placed in the bone to serve as stable anchor points for braces or aligners. TADs are used when additional anchorage is needed to move teeth effectively without affecting neighboring teeth.

How they work: TADs are placed in a minimally invasive procedure, and they remain in place for the duration of treatment. After they have helped achieve the desired tooth movement, they are removed quickly and easily.

  • Provides precise control for complex movements
  • Reduces the need for additional appliances
  • Shortens treatment time for certain cases

Retainers: Maintaining Your New Smile

After your treatment is complete, retainers help maintain your new smile and prevent teeth from shifting back to their original position. Retainers are custom-made to fit your teeth and can be either removable or fixed .

How they work: Removable retainers are typically worn for a specified number of hours each day or just at night. Fixed retainers are bonded to the back of your teeth and provide continuous support.

  • Keeps your smile stable after braces or aligners
  • Custom-made for a comfortable fit
  • Simple, long-term maintenance for your results

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do you offer braces for kids, teens, and adults?

Yes, we offer treatment for children, teens, and adults with a variety of options, including traditional braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners.

What treatment option is best for me?

It depends on your goals and the complexity of your case. We’ll recommend the best option based on your needs during the consultation.

How long does treatment typically take?

Treatment typically lasts between 12–24 months , depending on the type of treatment and your individual goals.

Are aligners a good option for teens?

Yes, clear aligners can be an excellent option for teens who want a discreet and flexible treatment. We offer several aligner systems, including Invisalign®.

What happens after treatment is complete?

You’ll wear a retainer to maintain your results, and we’ll provide follow-up visits to ensure your smile stays perfect for the long term.
