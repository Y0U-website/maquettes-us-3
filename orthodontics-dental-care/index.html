Orthodontics is much more than moving teeth for aligning them. The masticatory system is formed by muscle and tendons, teeth, bones, gums and a pair of joints. A good orthodontic treatment requires studying all the elements of this system in depth, so that everyone works in harmony without wearing, without inflammation and without pain.

Many complex issues in adults could had been prevented by consulting pediatric dentist in childhood and even adolescence. This specialist can make a whole analysis of habits, nutrition and masticatory system’s performance and stimulate a healthy mouth while the growing.

The success of many dental treatments such as orthodontics, implantology and aesthetics depends on the hygiene and health of the gums. Periodontics is responsible for maintaining healthy gums and tissues that support the teeth, which is why periodontics is one of the fundamental pillars of any treatment.

Commonly known as killing the nerve, endodontics is the specialty of conservative dentistry that takes care of all dental nerve treatments. Nowadays, with the use of computed tomography to diagnose and the use of magnifying glasses or dental microscope in this treatments, success and precision are greater.
