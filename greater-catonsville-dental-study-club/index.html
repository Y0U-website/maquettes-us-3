The practice

A great way to meet colleagues and stay abreast of the current techniques and new technologies.

For more than thirty years, the Greater Catonsville Dental Study Club has existed in the Baltimore area to bring together members of the local dental community. The group members are representative of dentists as a whole, with members from six different specialties.

Through educational seminars, speaker presentations, and social events, the Study Club fosters an atmosphere of learning and networking. The Greater Catonsville Dental Study Club is a proactive group of dentists who take the initiative to improve the dental community and enhance the business climate.
