Ontario Dental Arts offers a full range of denture services.

Snap-In Dentures

Flexible Partials

Many patients are concerned about a metal clasp showing with the use of partial dentures. Flexible partials eliminate that concern. Made of a slightly flexible material that does not show a metal clasp, these dentures make your smile seamless.

Full Dentures

Are you missing all of your teeth on the top, bottom, or both arches of your mouth? A full set of dentures are the right fit for you.

Partial Dentures

If you are missing some or most of your teeth, Ontario Dental Arts can help make your smile complete. Partial dentures fill in a small number of missing teeth, allowing you to keep your natural teeth as long as possible.

Reline

A denture reline is typically necessary between two to five years. Your gum line changes quite a bit over time, and a reline will refit your existing denture to your gum-line now.
