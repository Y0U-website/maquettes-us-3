Dental Implants in Pacific Palisades, CA

Missing teeth can affect your confidence, oral health, and overall quality of life. At the dental practice of Monica Valencia, D.D.S. in Pacific Palisades, CA , we offer dental implants as a permanent, natural-looking solution to replace missing teeth. Dental implants not only restore the appearance of your smile but also provide the stability and function of natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible titanium, which are surgically placed into the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or bridge. The implant integrates with the bone over time, creating a strong and stable foundation for the artificial tooth. This process, known as osseointegration, allows the implant to function like a natural tooth root, providing exceptional durability and strength.

Dental implants are the most advanced and effective solution for replacing one or more missing teeth, offering a long-lasting and aesthetically pleasing result.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer numerous benefits compared to other tooth replacement options, including:

  • Natural Appearance: Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth, blending seamlessly with your existing teeth.
  • Improved Oral Health: Implants do not require the alteration of adjacent teeth, as is necessary with dental bridges. This helps preserve the health of your surrounding teeth and jawbone.
  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur after a tooth is lost. This helps maintain the natural shape and structure of your face.
  • Long-Term Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective solution for tooth replacement.
  • Enhanced Functionality: Dental implants restore full chewing and speaking function, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and speak clearly without worry.
  • Increased Confidence: With dental implants, you can smile confidently, knowing that your replacement teeth are secure and natural-looking.

Types of Dental Implants

At our Pacific Palisades practice, we offer a range of dental implant options to meet your specific needs:

  • Single-Tooth Implants: Ideal for replacing a single missing tooth, this implant is topped with a custom crown that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: For patients missing multiple teeth, implant-supported bridges provide a stable and durable solution without the need to alter adjacent teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: For those missing an entire arch of teeth, implant-supported dentures offer a secure and comfortable alternative to traditional dentures, providing superior stability and function.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Proper care is essential for the longevity of your dental implants. With good oral hygiene, including regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups, your implants can last a lifetime. Dr. Valencia and her team will provide you with personalized instructions on how to care for your implants to ensure their long-term success.
