What is similar

Both models may use CBCT imaging, digital planning, and a team approach. Both may offer fixed implant bridges for patients replacing an upper arch, lower arch, or both arches.

What is different

At Radiant, the same doctor-led relationship can continue from consultation through surgery planning, provisional teeth, final teeth, repairs, hygiene, and maintenance. That continuity matters because full arch dentistry is not finished the day implants are placed.

  • Local follow-up instead of a surgery-center-only experience
  • A plan that can be phased when appropriate
  • Integration with general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry
  • Digital workflows for scans, printed prototypes, and final design review

Technology is only part of the answer

CBCT imaging, guides, xNav navigation, 3D printing, and in-house digital workflows help. But full arch success also depends on diagnosis, bite design, hygiene access, patient expectations, and maintenance.

How to compare treatment plans

Ask what is included: extractions, grafting, number of implants, temporary teeth, final material, maintenance visits, repair policy, sedation options, and how emergencies are handled after treatment.
