Financing

At Hometown Orthodontics, we understand that orthodontic treatment can be expensive and overwhelming. For this reason, we offer a variety of flexible financing options, low down payments, interest free payment plans, and are in-network with most insurances. We also offer a paid-in-full discount and several corporate discounts.

We strongly believe that treatment should be available to ALL who wish to change their life through orthodontics, and we are committed to working with you to find a plan you can afford. Most importantly, we’re proud to be one of few orthodontic practices within our area to accept Medicaid, and are always accepting new Medicaid patients. Whether you travel near or far, rest assured, your orthodontic treatment is covered!
