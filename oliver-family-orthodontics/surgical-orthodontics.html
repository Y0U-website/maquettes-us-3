At Oliver Family Orthodontics, we specialize in surgical orthodontics, also referred to as orthognathic surgery. It treats more serious jaw issues that can’t be fixed with braces alone. This treatment combines the expertise of both orthodontists and oral surgeons to enhance the function of your jaw and the appearance of your face.

What Is Surgical Orthodontics?

Surgical orthodontics corrects jaw problems that affect your ability to chew, speak, or breathe comfortably. It’s usually recommended for adults whose jaws have finished growing and who have noticeable issues with how their jaws line up.

Surgical orthodontics is used in conjunction with traditional braces to treat improper bites or aesthetic facial concerns. The braces shift a patient’s teeth while oral surgery places the jaw in proper alignment.

Good news for you! We have an expert eager to curate your healthiest, happiest smile. After determining how best to reposition or augment the structure of your jaw or facial bones to boost function, performance, and aesthetics, our team will get to work.

What Problems Can It Fix?

  • Severe Malocclusions: When the upper and lower teeth don’t meet properly, making it hard to eat or speak.
  • Jaw growth issues: Such as overbites, underbites, or open bites that affect your face and bite.
  • Obstructive Sleep Apnea: Structural jaw issues block your airway during sleep.
  • Facial asymmetry: When one side of the face looks different due to jaw misalignment.
