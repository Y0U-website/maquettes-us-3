Pediatric Dentistry Team in Methuen and Lowell

Our Philosophy

Dr. Mona and our friendly team are committed to providing children of all ages with exceptional care in a gentle and fun environment.

We believe in conservative and preventative dentistry, and use kid-friendly language to help children understand what we are doing. We believe in working with families to help them develop realistic habits that can promote a healthy smile for life. At Kangaroo Smiles we know how stressful dental treatment can be for both children and parents. For that reason, our top priority is to ensure that you are completely comfortable with all aspects of your child’s dental visit.

Dr. Yi Ju (Jessie) Tsai

American Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Originating from Taiwan, where she earned her first dental degree, Dr. Jessie further pursued her passion for dentistry by acquiring her Doctor of Dental Medicine at the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine.

With her deep compassion for working with kids, she furthered her expertise with an International residency of Pediatric Dentistry at New York University; a fellowship at University of Maryland Baltimore and a two-year advanced education in Pediatric dentistry at the University of Alabama Birmingham. She was a faculty member at NYU Langone educating and mentoring future pediatric dentists. Her commitment to excellence and dedication to the field is evident through her extensive training and experience.

Renowned for her warm and caring personality, Dr. Jessie provides a fun, comfortable, and educational experience for children and parents. She effortlessly creates a vibrant, comfortable, and enlightening environment for both children and their parents. Her fervor lies in cultivating a positive dental mindset among children and teenagers, steering them towards a lifetime of healthy smile. Dr. Jessie believes in a comprehensive approach to pediatric dentistry, ensuring that each child receives the individualized care they need to achieve optimal oral health.

Currently, Dr. Jessie serves as a Clinical Instructor in the Department of Pediatric Dentistry at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Outside of the office, you can find Dr. Jessie treasuring quality time with her wonderful husband, and their sweet baby boy Ryan.
