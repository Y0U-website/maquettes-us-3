The practice

The entire staff at Mallette Dental is passionate about providing the best service possible to our patients. Examining your teeth and gums, providing a comprehensive cleaning, and restoring you to good health is just one part of what we do.

Drs. Mallette are certified members of the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association, the Stark County Dental Association, and the practice is an A+ Accredited Business with the Better Business Bureau.

Of course, you should expect great care and expertise for all primary dentistry services like exams, cleaning, fillings. But where Mallette Dental truly shines are the more technical services that require experience, precision and knowledge.

Drs. Mallette are highly skilled in the art of implants , in the practice of orthodontics and in the applications of cosmetic dentistry . They use 3D imaging to ensure a full understanding of your case before implementing a solution. They work with the best dental suppliers to provide a wide range of options that fit your lifestyle, your preference and your budget.

Infection control protocol and guidelines as put forth by the American Dental Association will always be strictly followed in our office. We strictly adhere to all OSHA guidelines for sterilization of the treatment rooms, equipment and staff for your protection as well as ours. We will continue to make improvements for your safety as they are updated.
