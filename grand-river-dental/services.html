Family Dentistry

From your toddler’s first tooth to grandma’s implants — we care for every generation in one warm, welcoming office.

Kids & Teens Gentle, fun care starting from the very first tooth. We build positive dental habits early and make every visit something kids look forward to.

Adults & Parents Comprehensive care for busy adults — routine cleanings, cosmetic upgrades, or complex restorations, all scheduled around your life.

Seniors Maintaining oral health through every decade — from implants and dentures to gum care and dry-mouth solutions tailored for older patients.

Preventive Care

The best dental problems are the ones that never happen. Our preventive services keep your smile in peak condition.

Routine Exams & Cleanings Comprehensive oral evaluations and professional cleanings every 6 months — the cornerstone of lifelong dental health.

Digital X-Rays Low-radiation digital radiographs that give us a complete picture of your oral health — quickly and comfortably.

Fluoride Treatments Strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities with professional fluoride applications — especially important for younger patients.

Sealants Thin protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to guard against decay — great for kids and teens.

Children’s Dentistry Gentle, fun, age-appropriate care that sets children up with healthy habits and a positive relationship with dentistry.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile’s appearance with our range of cosmetic treatments, tailored to your unique goals.

Porcelain Veneers Ultra-thin shells of porcelain bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps, or uneven surfaces.

Dental Bonding Tooth-colored resin applied and sculpted to repair chips, cracks, or discoloration in a single visit.

Gum Contouring

Reshape and even out your gumline for a more balanced, attractive smile using gentle laser techniques.

Smile Makeovers A personalized combination of cosmetic treatments designed together for a complete smile transformation.

Tooth-Colored Fillings Natural-looking composite resin fillings that match your tooth color and blend seamlessly with your smile.

Restorative Dentistry

When teeth are damaged or missing, our restorative treatments bring back both function and appearance.

Dental Implants The gold standard for replacing missing teeth — titanium posts that fuse with the jawbone to support natural-looking crowns.

Crowns & Same-Day Restorations Need a crown? Skip the temporary and the second appointment. We design, mill, and place permanent crowns in-office in a single visit — so you walk in with a damaged tooth and walk out with a fully restored one.

Dental Bridges Fixed prosthetics anchored to adjacent teeth to fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth — restoring your bite and smile.

Root Canal Therapy Modern root canal treatment to save an infected tooth — far less uncomfortable than its reputation suggests, and often the only alternative to extraction.

Dentures & Partial Dentures

Removable full or partial dentures custom-fitted to replace multiple missing teeth and restore your natural bite and appearance.

Tooth Extractions

Simple and surgical extractions performed gently with local anesthesia, including wisdom tooth removal, when preservation is not possible.

Specialty & Wellness Services

We go beyond routine dentistry to address whole-body health — from airway function and sleep to muscle therapy and more.

Sleep Apnea Appliances Custom oral appliances that gently reposition the jaw during sleep — a comfortable, non-invasive alternative to CPAP for mild to moderate sleep apnea.

Airway Management Comprehensive evaluation and treatment of airway-related concerns, including tongue tie, mouth breathing, and structural factors that affect breathing, sleep, and overall health.

Myofunctional Therapy Appliances and therapy with exercises to retrain the muscles of the face, tongue, and mouth — aiding in arch development to reduce orthodontic need, airway development, nasal breathing and sleep quality. Also corrects improper swallowing patterns, tongue thrust, and breathing habits.

TMJ & Bruxism Treatment Custom nightguards and targeted therapies to relieve jaw pain, headaches, and tooth damage caused by clenching or grinding.

Tongue Tie Treatment Gentle frenectomy procedures to release restricted tongue or lip ties — improving feeding, speech, and airway function for children and adults.
