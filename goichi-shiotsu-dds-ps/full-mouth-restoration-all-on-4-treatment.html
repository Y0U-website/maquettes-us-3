Are Full Mouth Dental Restorations and Smile Makeovers the Same?

While full restorative dentistry and smile makeovers can overlap, they are generally a bit different.

A smile makeover focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth by addressing chipped teeth, stains, or gaps. This treatment might include procedures like dental crowns, veneers, or tooth-colored fillings to enhance the look of your smile.

On the other hand, full mouth restorative dentistry is a restoration dental treatment that also focuses on improving the function of your teeth. This may include treating problems like tooth decay and bone loss.

It can also involve replacing missing teeth with All on 4 dental implants. These All on Four dental implants restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile confidently.
