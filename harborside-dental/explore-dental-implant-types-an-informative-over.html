How Is an Endosteal Implant Placed?

This sequenced approach ensures stable integration and aesthetics.

  • Consultation & Imaging: 3D scans assess bone dimensions.
  • Surgical Placement: The titanium screw is inserted into a prepared osteotomy.
  • Osseointegration: A 3 4 month healing allows the implant to fuse with bone.
  • Abutment & Crown: An abutment is attached, then a custom crown restores function.

What Is the Subperiosteal Implant Procedure?

First, a gum incision exposes the jawbone, where a custom metal framework is placed under the periosteum. After soft tissue healing, posts protrude through the gums to support prostheses. Recovery typically spans 4 6 weeks before loading with dentures.

How Is the All-on-4 Implant Surgery Performed?

The All-on-4 protocol involves placing two straight anterior and two angled posterior implants in a single session, often followed by attaching a temporary full-arch prosthesis the same day. After 3 4 months of healing, the permanent bridge is secured to the integrated implants.

What Is the Mini Dental Implant Process?

Mini implants are inserted through the gum in a one-visit procedure using a small pilot drill, often under local anesthesia. Immediate attachment of a denture or crown is common, and patients enjoy functional improvement within 24 48 hours.

How Are Zygomatic Implants Surgically Installed?

Under sedation, long zygomatic fixtures are anchored in the cheekbone via the maxillary sinus approach. Healing spans 4 6 months before prosthesis attachment, offering a graft-free solution for severe upper jaw atrophy.

What Should You Expect During Dental Implant Recovery and Aftercare?

Recovery protocols vary by implant type but generally focus on hygiene, diet modifications, and follow-up visits to ensure successful healing and long-term maintenance.

How Long Is the Recovery for Each Implant Type?

  • Endosteal: Osseointegration spans 3 4 months before final restoration.
  • Subperiosteal: Soft tissue healing in 4 6 weeks.
  • All-on-4: Provisional prosthesis same day; final bridge in 3 4 months.
  • Mini Implants: Functional within 48 hours; full healing in 6 8 weeks.
  • Zygomatic: Healing over 4 6 months before prosthesis placement.

What Are the Best Practices for Dental Implant Aftercare?

Gentle oral hygiene with a soft toothbrush and antimicrobial rinse protects healing sites. A soft-food diet for two weeks minimizes stress, and avoiding smoking promotes faster osseointegration. Regular check-ups at Harborside Dental monitor progress.

How Does Harborside Dental Support Patients Post-Procedure?

Our team schedules follow-up visits at one, three, and six months to assess healing, adjust prostheses, and reinforce home care techniques. We offer emergency phone support and local referrals for any concerns.

What Are Common Risks and How Are They Managed?

Minor swelling and discomfort are managed with ice packs and prescribed analgesics. Infection risk is minimized through prophylactic antibiotics and strict hygiene protocols. In rare cases of implant failure, revision surgery with bone grafting restores readiness for replacement.
