About

As part of our holistic mission, we provide additional services to help you achieve your personal health goals.

Our Offerings include: Biological Surgery, Ceramic Implants, SDS Implants, Covered Socket Residuum, Safe Mercury Removal, and Full Mouth Rejuvenations, as well as cleanings, exams, and whitening.

Paragon Dental is dedicated to a more natural, body-safe experience for all of our patients, tall and small.

Your journey starts here

In addition to holistic dentistry, Paragon Dental offers a Skincare Wellness Journey. Whether your skin care goals are to look younger, resolve texture concerns, manage acne, or just a quick pick-me-up, we have you covered!

Much like our dentistry, our approach to aesthetics is completely customized to the person. We take away the guesswork when it comes to what kind of facial you need since every facial is tailored to the individual.

We also offer advanced exfoliation treatments that include chemical peels, diamond microdermabrasion, and dermaplaning to accelerate your skin care goals.

Take your journey to the next level with our Collagen Induction Therapy treatments as well!

Whether you’re receiving a check-up, surgery, or skin care treatment, Paragon Dental is here to help you feel and look your best.

Build a strong foundation

As an education-forward environment, we want our patients to understand the foundations that build a great smile and how they can work towards feeling their best. During your initial visit, we go into detail about how we can best support you on your health journey. We like to work from “dirty to clean,” starting with infections that may be present and working all the way up towards your general health.
