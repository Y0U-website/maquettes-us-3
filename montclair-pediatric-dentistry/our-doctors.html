Our Doctors

Meet Dr. Lina Paek

Dr. Paek is a board-certified pediatric dentist who combines clinical expertise with a gentle, individualized approach to care. At Montclair Pediatric Dentistry, she partners closely with families to support each child’s oral development from their very first tooth through their teen years. With special interests in prevention, minimally invasive treatments, and care for children with special health needs, Dr. Paek is committed to creating a welcoming, eco-conscious environment where every child can thrive.

Meet Dr. Rebecca Hsieh

Dr. Hsieh is a board-certified pediatric dentist at Montclair Pediatric Dentistry who brings warmth, compassion, and clinical excellence to every visit. With a strong foundation in public health and a special interest in minimally invasive care, she partners with families to create positive, educational dental experiences that support lifelong oral health.
