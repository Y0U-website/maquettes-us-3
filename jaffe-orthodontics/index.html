Teenage years are full of growth, discovery, and social pressures — the last thing your teen needs is to feel self-conscious about metal braces. Invisalign Teen offers all the benefits of clear aligner therapy with special features designed specifically for adolescent patients.

Invisalign Teen is a newer technology specifically engineered for younger mouths that may not have all permanent teeth fully erupted. The aligners can actually accommodate teeth that haven’t come in yet, making it an excellent option for many teenagers.
