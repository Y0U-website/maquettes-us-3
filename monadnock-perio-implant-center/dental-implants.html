Frequently asked questions

1. What are dental implants, and how do they work?

Our team at Monadnock Perio and Implant Center in Keene & Peterborough, New Hampshire, describes dental implants as very small titanium posts that serve as replacement tooth roots. They are surgically placed into the jawbone by our experienced dentists. Once the implants have fused with the jawbone, a process referred to as osseointegration, they provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth and restorations, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

2. Are dental implants a safe treatment?

Dental implants are regarded as a secure and dependable option for substituting lost teeth when executed by qualified experts. The dentists at our office have extensive experience and utilize today’s advanced technology to ensure a smooth and successful procedure. Dental implants have an extremely high success rate, and with proper care, they last for decades.

3. What are the benefits of quality dental implants compared to other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants offer numerous advantages compared to other options, such as dentures or dental bridges. They provide a natural look and feel, improved chewing ability, and prevent any bone loss in the jaw. They also do not rely on the neighboring teeth for foundational support, preserving your natural tooth structure. At Monadnock Perio and Implant Center, the team is dedicated to helping patients enjoy these life-changing benefits and determine their candidacy.

4. How much do most dental implants actually cost?

The cost of dental implants and dental implant restorations varies due to several factors, including the number of dental implants placed, the complexity of the procedure, and any additional treatments required. While they may have a higher upfront cost compared to other options, their long-term durability and benefits often outweigh the initial investment. Monadnock Perio and Implant Center provides a personalized estimate and discusses payment options with you.

5. Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage for quality dental implants depends on your specific insurance plan. Some plans cover parts of the procedure, such as the crown, while others might consider implants as an elective treatment. The friendly staff at Monadnock Perio and Implant Center is here to assist you in understanding your coverage and exploring financing options if needed.

6. Who is considered a good candidate for dental implants?

Most of our patients with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density are considered candidates for dental implants. Maintaining good oral and overall health is crucial for ensuring successful healing. The dentists at Monadnock Perio and Implant Center perform a thorough examination and evaluation to determine if implants are the right choice for you.

7. How long does the actual process of getting dental implants take?

The dental implant process will typically take a few months, as it involves multiple steps. After the implant is placed during oral surgery, the jawbone needs time to both heal properly and integrate with the implant, a process that might take several months. Once the healing process is complete, the replacement tooth is attached. Our team guides you through each step and ensures you are comfortable throughout the process.

9. Do dental implants require special care?

Caring for your new dental implants is similar to maintaining your natural teeth. Brushing twice a day, flossing at least once a day, and visiting your dentist regularly are essential for maintaining the health of your implants. Our team consistently emphasizes the importance of proper oral hygiene practices and guides to ensure the longevity of your implants.
