The practice

Your search for a Woodinville or Seattle dentist ends with a simple call to our friendly team today!

We love serving patients of all ages. Our team enjoys helping even your youngest family member grow into a healthy adult. Welcome to a dental home for your and your whole family.

Dr. Koczarski maintains a constant dedication to the finest advanced dental education. You can be confident in his unmatched ability to create impressive results with modern dentistry.

Your teeth, muscles, and jaw joints function together to play a vital role in your overall health. Dr. Koczarski blends the harmony of your chewing system for ultimate function and comfort.

A Northwest native, Dr. Koczarski graduated first in his class at the University of Washington School of Dentistry. He’s an honorary member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Society, which recognizes the top 5 percent of dentists in the country.

Dr. Koczarski is also a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry, and was recently recognized as a fully Accredited Member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

He’s co-director of the Hornbrook Group Center of Advanced Dental Education and former co-director of the University of Pacific Dental School in San Francisco. Dr. Koczarski has also been extensively published in a variety of academic journals.

In his spare time Dr. K stays active by golfing, competing in triathlons, running with and spending time with his three boys, Corey, Christian and Dylan.
