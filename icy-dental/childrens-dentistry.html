Children’s Dentistry

When should my child go visit the dentist?

We recommend that your child goes to the dentist when the first tooth appears and no later than his/her first birthday.

What should I expect for my child’s First visit?

The exam is short and simple. We may ask you to sit in the dental chair with your child and hold your child if they are an infant or young toddler. For most younger children, this helps to provide them with security they need. For older children that can already talk, you may be asked to wait in the reception area for part of the visit so that a relationship of trust can be built between your child and dental staff.

What happens during my visit?

Depending on the child’s age, x-rays may be taken to help identify decay and to check on the progress of your child’s permanent teeth under the gums. We will then gently examine your child’s teeth and gums. A cleaning is then performed and if your child is eligible for dental sealants, your dentist will let you know. Lastly, an application of topical fluoride is left on the teeth to help strengthen them and protect them against decay.
