How the Implant Process Works

Most patients are surprised at how straightforward the process is. Here&#x27;s what to expect from start to finish.

Consultation + 3D CBCT Scan

We take a comprehensive look at your jaw bone, gum health, and any existing teeth. The CBCT 3D scan tells us exactly where to place the implant for optimal long-term success. We discuss your options and quote the full cost upfront.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jaw bone under local anesthesia (with oral sedation available if you&#x27;d like to be more relaxed). Most patients describe it as easier than they expected — comparable to having a filling done.

Healing + Osseointegration

The implant fuses with your bone over the next few months — a process called osseointegration. During this time, you can usually wear a temporary tooth so there&#x27;s no visible gap. We check in periodically to make sure things are healing well.

Abutment + Crown

Once the implant is fully integrated, we place the abutment (connector) and design your custom crown. The crown is color-matched to your other teeth — most people can&#x27;t tell which tooth is the implant.

Long-Term Care

Brush and floss like a normal tooth, see us for regular 6-month cleanings, and your implant should last decades. We follow up at 1, 3, and 12 months after placement, then on your regular hygiene schedule.
