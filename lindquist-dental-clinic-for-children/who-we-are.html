Our History

Lindquist Dental Clinic for Children (LDCC) was founded in 1936 and was originally named after Carl E. Lindquist, a Tacoma banker and philanthropist. The original Carl E. Lindquist School Clinic was supported by the Tacoma School District and The Federation of Social Agencies (now the United Way of Pierce County) and served students in the Tacoma School District.

In 1975, the clinic moved to Bates Technical College and then Clover Park Technical College, serving the community as an educational clinic. In 1998, the demand for low-cost children’s dental care increased and the clinic began operating in a rented five-chair dental clinic in Parkland, south of Tacoma. At that time, the organization transitioned from a teaching clinic with limited service hours to being entirely professionally staffed with full-day service hours of 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

About Us

Lindquist Dental Clinic for Children (LDCC) is a private, nonprofit 501(c) 3 organization. We provide quality dental services to children and youth (ages 0 thru 20) throughout the Puget Sound, regardless of their family’s ability to pay.

Since 1936, LDCC has grown to a 15-operatory clinic along with our community and school-based dental services outreach program. Our dedicated staff, volunteers, and board of directors embrace the LDCC vision that all children deserve access to oral health care.

Lindquist Dental Clinic for Children’s (LDCC) mission is, “To provide accessible, compassionate, and effective dental care to children in need.”

LDCC is a thriving, state-of-the-art practice, which through the collaborative efforts of its staff, board, and compassionate members of the community, ensures that all Puget Sound children in need are provided the opportunity to achieve optimal oral health.
