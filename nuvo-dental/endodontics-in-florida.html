The Root Canal Procedure at Nuvo Dental

Diagnosis

Using advanced imaging, we identify the source of your pain and confirm the need for a root canal.

Cleaning and Shaping

The infected pulp is carefully removed, and the root canals are cleaned and shaped to prepare for sealing.

Filling and Sealing

The cleaned canals are filled with a biocompatible material to prevent future infection, and the tooth is sealed.

Restoration

A crown or filling is placed to restore the tooth’s function and appearance.
