Insurance Accepted

If you are a Massachusetts resident and are 18 or over, you are now required to have health insurance. You may qualify for a number of low-cost health insurance programs for people who live in Massachusetts. Our Financial Counselor can help explain the choices that may be available to you. They can also assist you with the process of enrolling. To make an appointment, please call 617-296-0061.
