Questions Minot Patients Ask About Dental Implant Restoration and Repair

Can a damaged implant crown be repaired?

In many cases, yes. Depending on the extent of the damage, the crown may be repaired or replaced while preserving the implant itself.

What causes implant restorations to become loose?

Loosening may occur because of normal wear, bite forces, component wear, or mechanical issues involving the restoration.

Does a loose implant mean the implant has failed?

Not necessarily. Many cases involve the restoration or connecting components rather than the implant itself. Dr. Olson evaluates the situation to determine the cause.

How often should implant restorations be checked?

Implants should be evaluated during routine dental visits to monitor the restoration, surrounding tissues, and overall implant health.

Can old implant restorations be replaced?

Yes. Many implant-supported crowns, bridges, and dentures can be replaced when they become worn, damaged, or outdated while retaining the underlying implant.
