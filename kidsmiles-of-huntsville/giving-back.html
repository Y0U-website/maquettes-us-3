Giving Back

Pro Bono Offerings As another way to give back to our communities, we always desire to offer our services to those who may not have the means to care for their oral health. Oral health is important in keeping the rest of the body healthy. When cases arise, we do our best to offer pro bono or discounted rates. If you know anyone in our Lake Conroe or Huntsville communities who is in need, please send them our way, and we will determine the best way to help them.

Pay It Forward Program We have created a pay-it-forward program, too. When we offer discounted rates or pro bono work, we ask that the recipient pay a good deed forward to someone in the community. One young lady created an anti-bullying campaign in her school. Our hope is that when we do good, others follow our lead.

  • Presbyterian Day School
  • Children’s Lighthouse
  • Good Shepherd Learning Center
