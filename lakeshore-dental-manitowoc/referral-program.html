Great Patient Referral Program Lakeshore Dental would not be the dental practice it is without the support it receives from the community around us. Your referral of a friend or family member* is the greatest compliment we can receive.

We hope you are so excited about your dentist and hygienist that when someone mentions needing a new dentist you tell them all about Lakeshore Dental and the positive dental experience you had, not to mention the adorable office labrador, Kenai. Your referral will get you rewarded.

There is no limit on the number of referrals you can make! As long as the referral results in a New Patient appointment, you will receive the referral program reward.
