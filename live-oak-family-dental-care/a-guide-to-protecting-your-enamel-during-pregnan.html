Ah, the joys of pregnancy! But did you know morning sickness can make your enamel more vulnerable to damage? Let’s explore the why and how without the technical jargon.

During morning sickness, your mouth turns acidic. Enamel, beware! The acidity from acid reflux or vomiting can dissolve enamel, paving the way for potential decay. Let’s shield that smile by removing plaque efficiently and using fluoridated toothpastes and rinses that will create a better ‘shield’. Waning: Do not brush your teeth right after vomiting which is when your enamel is at its softest state. Brushing can take some of that layer away rather, rinse with tap water and swish thoroughly with fluoridated rinse. You are safe to brush one hour later in the episode.

Gagging during brushing? It’s a common challenge, especially in early pregnancy. Fear not! Discover tricks like using a pediatric toothbrush for effective cleaning without the gag reflex. Another option although less effective is a waterpik which forces water and sweeps plaque.

Navigate the toothbrush battleground with ease. Opt for a smaller toothbrush, go slow, and create a relaxing atmosphere. Your teeth will thank you, and so will your sanity!
