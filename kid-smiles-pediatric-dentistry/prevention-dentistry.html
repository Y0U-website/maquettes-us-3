Prevention Dentistry in Yonkers, NY

Our goal is to help your child preserve and protect their smiles for the rest of their lives. We educate our patients about how to properly care for their teeth and prevent them from getting damaged or decaying.

These steps include regular cleanings and exams, proper brushing and flossing at-home using the right products, and protecting their teeth while playing and doing other activities. They can reduce the need for root canals, fillings or extractions by taking good care of their teeth.

To help your child preserve their teeth for life, we offer these preventative dentistry services:

Exams Your child’s first birthday should be the beginning for dental care. They should also schedule their examinations at least every six months. We can help your child avoid having to have fillings, root canals, or any other treatment by giving them complete exams twice a year.

X-Rays Our state-of-the-art system is more efficient and exposes patients to lower radiation doses. We can show your child the images, and tell you exactly what treatment is required.

Cleanings Regular cleanings should be scheduled at least twice a year to maintain good dental health. We will clean the teeth and polish them until they sparkle.
