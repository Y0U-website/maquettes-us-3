Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces remain one of the most reliable and effective ways to create a healthy, beautifully aligned smile. Using high-grade stainless steel brackets and wires, braces gently guide your teeth into their ideal positions over time with precision and consistency.

Thanks to modern advancements, today’s braces are smaller, smoother, and more comfortable than ever before, making treatment more efficient while still delivering highly predictable, long-lasting results.

Clear braces function similarly to traditional metal braces but are made from a ceramic material designed to blend more naturally with the color of your teeth. This makes them a more discreet option while still delivering the same reliable, effective tooth movement.

They are a great choice for patients who want a more subtle treatment option without compromising results. Because the ceramic material requires a bit more care, they are often best suited for older teens and adults who are able to maintain good oral hygiene and handle them with care.

What Are Braces?

Braces use a system of small brackets and wires that work together to gently guide your teeth into their ideal positions over time. Each adjustment is carefully planned and monitored by our doctors to ensure steady progress and long-lasting results. Braces are a great option for patients of all ages and are especially effective for more complex orthodontic cases.
