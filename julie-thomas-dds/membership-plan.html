How It Works

Through the Empower platform, you can sign up for our membership plan with a simple annual or monthly fee. There are no deductibles, no waiting periods, no annual maximums, and no claims to file. It is dental care on your terms.

Why Empower

Empower makes managing your membership simple and transparent. The platform handles enrollment, payments, and plan details so you always know exactly what is covered. It is designed specifically for dental practices like ours to give patients a better alternative to traditional insurance.

Who Is It For

The Membership Plan is ideal for patients who:

  • Do not have dental insurance through an employer or marketplace
  • Are retired or self-employed
  • Want a predictable, budget-friendly way to maintain dental health
  • Have aging out dependents who need their own coverage
