Dr. Michelle Parzatka Jennings Native to the area, Dr. Jennings grew up in Hinsdale and graduated from Hinsdale Central High School.

After completing her undergraduate degree at Western Michigan University, she completed her dental school degree in 1989 at the University of Illinois College of Dentistry. She furthered her education at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, receiving her specialty degree in Periodontics in 1991. Since her graduation in 1991, Dr. Jennings has been in private practice at LaGrange Periodontics for over 30 years. Dr. Jennings is also a past Clinical Professor in Periodontics at the University of Illinois College of Dentistry.

Dr. Jennings attends regular dental meetings and specialty conferences in order to ensure she is providing the most advanced and modern treatment available. In addition, she has lectured for numerous dental and periodontal societies and serves on various dental committees.

Besides her devotion and dedication to periodontics and treating her patients, she also takes great pride in being a mother of two wonderful children. When not involved with dentistry, Dr. Jennings enjoys running, fitness, sports and traveling. She is blessed with supportive family and friends.

Dr. Farah Shakir Dr. Farah Shakir grew up in Wilmette, a suburb of Chicago. She completed her B.S. in Biology and Neuroscience from the University of Illinois. She also achieved her master’s degree in biotechnology from Rush University before going on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, Certificate in Periodontics, and Master’s degree in Oral Science and earned her master’s on Peri-Implant disease from the University of Illinois.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Periodontology, Chicago Dental Society and Illinois State Dental Society.

She specializes in the most current non-surgical, surgical periodontal and dental implant services. Her interest are bone augmentation in preparation for implant, sinus grafting and soft tissue procedures. She is also certified in Intravenous conscious sedation.

In her free time, she enjoys traveling with her husband, raising her two young children, and working out. She is fluent in Arabic and Spanish. Dr. Shakir is also a Clinical Assistant Professor at the University of Illinois College of Dentistry.
