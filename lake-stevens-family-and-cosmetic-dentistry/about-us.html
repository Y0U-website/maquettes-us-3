Dr. Nam Nguyen

Dr. Nam Nguyen (Dr. Nam) holds both a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and a Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Washington.

Dedicated to offering cutting-edge dental care, Dr. Nam consistently seeks out advancements to ensure his patients receive the highest level of comfort and treatment. He has received specialized training in Implantology, Botox Injection for TMJ and Facial Esthetics, and Fillers. To ensure comprehensive care for his patients, he has also participated in multiple seminars with Spear, renowned for their excellent dental continuing education.

Originally from Vietnam, Dr. Nam relocated to the United States at the age of 14, where he developed a profound appreciation for the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest. Beyond his practice, he generously volunteers with Medical Team International and the Adventist Medical Evangelism Network, extending free dental care to those in need.

When he’s not transforming smiles, Dr. Nam cherishes moments spent with his wife and daughters. He delights in exploring the diverse culinary offerings of the local area, as well as indulging his passion for outdoor adventures like hiking and snowboarding.

Dr. Nguyen is committed to providing patient-centered care and remaining current with best practices in dentistry. In 2021, she received her Fellowship award through the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD); only 6 % of general dentists in the U.S. and Canada have earned their AGD Fellowship. This award demonstrates her long-term commitment to going above and beyond continuing education and licensing requirements and dedication to learning, growing, and staying current in the field so that she can provide the best oral care to her patients and their families.

Dr. Patricia Nguyen takes a gentle, caring approach to providing dental care for all her patients. She believes in educating patients about their oral health and treatment options, empowering them to make informed decisions for their smiles. Dr. Nguyen loves meeting people from all walks of life and building lasting relationships with them. She also loves being able to make a positive difference in one’s smile, confidence, or quality of life.

Dr. Nguyen and her family have called the Pacific Northwest their home since 2015. When not practicing dentistry, she enjoys skiing, dispersed camping, discovering different restaurants, watching movies, and travelling. She and her husband also recently welcomed their first child, a baby girl, who is keeping them very busy!
