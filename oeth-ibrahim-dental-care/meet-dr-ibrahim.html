Meet Dr. Ibrahim

Dr. Peter Ibrahim, DMD

Dr. Peter Ibrahim has proudly called Florida home after moving from Egypt in 2005. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Bradenton, Florida, and enjoys all aspects of dentistry, with a special focus on implant dentistry and clear aligner therapy.

Dedicated to advancing his skills, Dr. Ibrahim actively pursues continuing education and multiple fellowships. He is affiliated with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, MISCH Implant Institute, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, and Florida Dental Association.

Dr. Ibrahim takes pride in providing exceptional, patient-focused care and looks forward to helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile.
