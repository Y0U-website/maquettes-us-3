Jiyeong Oh, LD, CDT

Licensed Denturist l Certified Dental Technician A decade of hands-on denture and dental lab experience

Oasis Denture Clinic is led by Jiyeong Oh, LD, CDT, a licensed denturist and certified dental technician with a decade of experience in removable prosthetics, denture fabrication, and dental lab craftsmanship.

Jiyeong earned her Diplomate in Denturism from Bates Technical College in 2016 and later furthered her education through the Advanced Curriculum in Denturism at American Denturist College. This additional training expanded her skills in digital dentistry and deepened her knowledge of implant-supported and complex prosthetics. Her background combines clinical denturism with hands-on dental technology experience, allowing patients to receive care from someone who understands both the clinical and laboratory aspects of denture treatment.

What Makes Oasis Denture Clinic Different

In-House Precision Lab

Oasis Denture Clinic is backed by Oasis Dental Lab, an established dental laboratory with over 15 years of experience. This close connection allows the clinic and lab to work together more efficiently, helping improve communication, consistency, and control throughout the denture process.

Advanced Digital Denture Workflows

We leverage advanced 3D digital workflows, supported by high-precision intraoral scanners, 3D printers, and milling machines, to support accurate planning, efficient fabrication, and consistent results.

More Efficient Communication and Workflow

Because the clinic and lab work closely together, important case details can be reviewed and refined with fewer communication gaps, helping each denture case move more efficiently and consistently from the first appointment to the final appliance.

Denture Options for Different Needs and Budgets

We offer denture options for different needs and budgets, including a tiered pricing structure for complete dentures, so patients can choose the level of care that best fits their goals, preferences, and budget.

Our Approach to Denture Care At Oasis Denture Clinic, denture care is never treated as one-size-fits-all. Each patient’s comfort, appearance, function, oral health, and budget are considered when discussing treatment options. The goal is to provide honest guidance, practical solutions, and carefully made dentures that support confidence in daily life.

By combining clinical care, advanced digital dentistry, and in-house lab craftsmanship, Oasis Denture Clinic provides a more personal denture experience for patients who want quality care from a locally operated denture clinic.
