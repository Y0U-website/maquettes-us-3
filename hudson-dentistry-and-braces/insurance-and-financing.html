In-Network Insurance

If we are in-network with your insurance provider:

Please note that insurance plans often do not cover all procedures in full. If insurance payment is not received within 30 days, any outstanding balance becomes the responsibility of the patient.

Balances over 90 days may be subject to interest charges.

  • You and your insurance company share responsibility for fees
  • Your estimated portion is due at the time of service
  • We will submit claims on your behalf
  • We will assist in estimating your coverage

Out-Of Network Insurance

If we are out-of-network with your plan:

Insurance benefits are based on the contract between your employer and your insurance company.

  • Payment is due at the time of service
  • We can provide documentation to assist you in submitting claims
  • Reimbursement is determined by your insurance provider
