Meet Dr. DiLisio

Dr. Abra DiLisio is a distinguished board-certified anesthesiologist with roots in Michigan. Alongside her husband, she moved to New York to advance her career in anesthesiology at the renowned Mt. Sinai Hospital in Manhattan. This move marked a significant step in her professional journey, bringing her expertise and compassionate care to one of the nation’s leading medical centers.

Following her residency, Dr. DiLisio transitioned into private practice as a mobile dental anesthesiologist, serving patients across the tri-state area. With a commendable track record of safely treating over 1000 patients with IV sedation, her primary goal is to make dental care accessible and comfortable, especially for those with dental anxiety or complex dental needs. Her commitment to patient care and alleviating dental fears highlights her dedication to her profession.
