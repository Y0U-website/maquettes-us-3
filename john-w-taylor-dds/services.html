What We Do

Cosmetic Dentistry

Each Cosmetic Dentistry service is designed to restore strength and appearance. We can resolve stains, decay, fractures and other tooth damage with a solution that presents an all-natural appearance. Our cosmetic dentistry is sure to give you a smile that speaks volumes!

Dental Hygiene

Dental hygiene is a necessity in proper care of your teeth and mouth. A regular visit to the dentist for standard cleanings preserves your teeth for years to come. Nitrous sedation is available allowing you to sit back and relax while we work!

Orthodontic Treatment

‍Rockwall Gentle Dental offers traditional and modern orthodontic treatment. Whether you prefer braces or invisible aligners, Dr. Allen will work with you to get your teeth where you want them.

Emergency Dentistry

In the circumstance damage has already been done, Rockwall Gentle Dental is prepared to care for you immediately. Dr. Allen and the staff will work to provide options to relieve pain, repair damage, improve your bite and get you back to normal function and appearance as soon as possible.
