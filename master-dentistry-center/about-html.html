Comfort comes first

Dental anxiety is real, common, and nothing to be embarrassed about. Tell us how you’re feeling and we’ll adapt — slower pacing, extra numbing, breaks whenever you need them, and sedation options for surgical procedures. Many of our happiest patients are people who avoided the dentist for years.
