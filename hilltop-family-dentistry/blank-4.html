Meet the Team

We want you to have the best dental experience possible, and most of all, we want to help you keep your teeth.

We treat you like you re family...

I’ve lived in Fayetteville for over 20 years, first arriving while stationed at Fort Bragg during my years in the army. I love this area and quickly decided to make it my forever home.

I entered the Army as a Blackhawk Pilot and I loved it. However, 16 years ago, I went back to school to become a dentist when I realized THIS is my true calling. I’ve experienced a lot of dentistry as both a dentist and a patient. I love dentistry and I am honored to be able to help people with their teeth and health.

I m grateful to be continuing the tradition of quality compassionate care established by Dr. Skipper Smith who started this office in 1989.
