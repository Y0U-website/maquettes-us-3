Some of the benefits of choosing metal braces include the following:

  • The Advantages of Metal Braces
  • Brackets – These are cemented to the fronts of the patient’s teeth.
  • Archwire – This thin wire runs through each of the metal brackets and puts pressure on the teeth in order to guide them into their proper place.
  • Elastics or Rubber Bands – These are colored ties that are used to hold the archwires to the brackets.
  • Metal braces are a time-proven method of shifting a patient’s teeth into proper alignment.
  • They’re better at treating more complicated orthodontic problems than other forms of treatment.
  • Metal braces generally offer a quicker treatment time.
  • Since they’re cemented onto your teeth, you’ll never have to worry about forgetting to wear them.
  • They’re less expensive than other treatment options.
