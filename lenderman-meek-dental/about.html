ABOUT LENDERMAN & MEEK DENTAL

Creating Happy, Healthy Smiles Since 2008

Compassionate, Relational Care

When Dr. Lenderman opened Lenderman Dental in 2008, he had a vision of providing quality, professional care with a focus on building relationships in a clean, comfortable environment. Since then, his practice has grown tremendously. In 2022, Dr. Kyle Meek joined Dr. Lenderman as a partner in his mission, under the new name Lenderman & Meek Dental and continues to provide the patient-first care you know and love.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Randy Lenderman, DDS

Dr. Randy Lenderman is a family and cosmetic dentist serving Conway, Arkansas, and the surrounding area. After attending the University of Arkansas, Dr. Lenderman received his doctorate from the Louisiana State University School of Dentistry and began his career in northwest Arkansas before opening Lenderman Dental in Conway in 2008. In his pursuit to provide families with the best oral health care services possible, he has participated in hundreds of hours of continuing education programs. His love for dentistry is evident in the passion and care he provides for his patients.
