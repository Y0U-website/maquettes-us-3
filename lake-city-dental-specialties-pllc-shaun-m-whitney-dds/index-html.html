What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Coeur d’Alene, ID, permanently restore a fully functional bite with strong, beautiful tooth replacements. Implants mimic the structure of a natural tooth with three parts: a post to replace the tooth root, a restoration that’s crafted to blend in seamlessly with your smile, and a connector piece called an abutment to bring it all together.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

  • Restore full chewing strength
  • Enable you to eat a variety of healthy foods
  • Prevent bone loss and preserve facial structure
  • Support long-term oral health by protecting surrounding teeth
  • Look, feel, and function just like natural teeth
  • Can last significantly longer than other tooth replacement options
  • Improve speech and overall comfort compared to removable dentures
  • Versatile options, including full-mouth dental implants and single-tooth replacements

A Versatile Solution for Any Number of Missing Teeth

Dental Bridges

If you’ve lost several teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can restore strength and balance to your bite with just two implant posts. Instead of relying on neighboring teeth for support, dental implants in Coeur d’Alene, ID, serve as stable anchors for the bridge, protecting your remaining teeth from unnecessary wear and tear. The result is a secure, natural-looking restoration that feels comfortable and stable when chewing or speaking. It’s an ideal option for filling larger gaps while maintaining long-term oral health.

Implant-Supported Dentures

If you’re tired of struggling with loose or uncomfortable dentures, implant-supported dentures offer a secure, life-changing alternative. Your removable prosthesis will snap securely into place on several strategically placed implant posts, supported by specialized abutments.

This design makes it easier to eat, laugh, and speak with confidence again, without the discomfort of uncomfortable adhesives. You’ll enjoy a stable, natural feel and improved comfort that traditional dentures simply can’t match.

All-on-X Full-Mouth Dental Implants

Full-mouth dental implants are as close as it gets to having a complete set of healthy teeth again. Using as few as four to six implant posts per arch, we can restore an entire set of natural-looking, fixed teeth that stay securely in place.

Full-mouth dental implants in Coeur d’Alene, ID, provide excellent stability, full chewing function, and a beautiful smile that feels like your own. It’s a long-term, reliable solution designed to enhance both your oral health and quality of life.

What Is the Process of Getting Dental Implants in Coeur d’Alene, ID?

Consult & Planning Like every treatment with Dr. Whitney, your completely personalized experience starts with a comprehensive examination. We’ll listen to your concerns, evaluate your oral and medical health, and explain all your options. From there, we’ll create a plan that’s tailored to your unique needs and smile goals.

Procedure We’ll use safe and effective pain control techniques to ensure you’re completely comfortable throughout your treatment, including any necessary supportive surgeries . Then, we’ll place your implant posts in the jawbone using precision imaging and advanced surgical techniques. Whenever possible, we’ll also provide a temporary restoration so you can smile with confidence while your implants heal.

Follow-Up Appointment Our team will be with you every step of the way. Follow-up appointments allow us to closely monitor your healing process, address any concerns you may have, and make any necessary adjustments.

Healing & Final Restorations Over the next several months, your implant posts will fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. When they’ve achieved full strength, we’ll attach your permanent restorations, crafted from beautiful, durable materials that look entirely natural. Get ready to celebrate your new smile!
