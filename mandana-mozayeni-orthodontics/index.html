Child and teen orthodontics is an orthodontic subspecialty that treats bite abnormalities and other alignment issues in people 18 and younger.

Orthodontic treatment uses custom oral appliances, like braces, clear aligners, and orthodontic retainers, to place mild pressure on your child’s teeth and jaw. This pressure moves the teeth or jaw into proper alignment, improving the appearance of your child’s smile while supporting their oral health.
