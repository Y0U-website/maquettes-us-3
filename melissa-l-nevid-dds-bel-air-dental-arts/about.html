She grew up above her dad’s dental practice in New York, so she’s seen firsthand how powerful a personal touch can be. With a background in psychology and a calm, gentle approach, she helps patients—especially those with dental anxiety—feel more comfortable and in control of their care.

Care Without Compromise

We believe you shouldn’t have to choose between advanced dentistry and a compassionate experience. At Bel Air Dental Arts, our vision is simple: deliver high-quality care in a setting that feels calm, thoughtful, and fully centered around each patient. You get the best of both worlds—modern tools and old-fashioned attentiveness.

Modern Dentistry That Feels Personal

We’re here to change the way people feel about going to the dentist. That means less clinical, more connection. Less waiting, more listening. We combine leading-edge technology with one-on-one care that’s tailored to you, not just your teeth. Our mission is to make dental visits feel more human, every single time.

To Help You Feel Comfortable Putting Your Health First

We know many patients come to us with anxiety, past dental experiences, or just a lot of questions. That’s okay. Our promise is to meet you where you are—with clear communication, gentle care, and a supportive team that helps you feel safe taking the next step toward better health.

Join Our Dental Family

We’re proud to care for patients from Abingdon, Bel Air, Aberdeen, and nearby communities. Whether it’s been six months or six years since your last visit, we’re here to help without judgment. When you step into our office, you’ll find a space built for comfort and care that meets you with kindness.
