Endodontist Services

Ivy Endodontics at Princeton Advanced Procedures

When tooth pain strikes, you deserve expert care. Endodontists are dental specialists dedicated to saving your natural teeth through advanced root canal treatment and endodontic procedures.

Endodontists undergo an additional three years of rigorous specialized training beyond dental school to master the complexities of endodontic care. Dr. Franco DeMayo completed a comprehensive three-year residency program, developing advanced expertise in treating diseases and injuries affecting the tooth’s interior specifically the pulp and root canal system. This intensive, focused training enables Dr. Franco DeMayo to deliver better outcomes for even the most challenging tooth problems, providing you with the specialized care your tooth deserves.

Dr. Jean Kang graduated from Columbia University’s School of Dental and Oral Surgery and completed her endodontics residency at the University of Pennsylvania, where she gained expertise in both conventional and surgical endodontics. She is a clinical instructor at Penn, teaching endodontic residents and dental students. Since 2000, Dr. Kang has practiced in central New Jersey and has been voted Top New Dentist/Endodontist by her peers every year since 2012. She is committed to evidence-based dentistry and believes success is measured by long-term patient and referring dentist satisfaction.

Root canals have a reputation for being painful, but the truth is root canal therapy relieves pain. Our endodontists use modern anesthetics and pain management techniques to ensure your comfort throughout treatment. Most patients report that the procedure is no more uncomfortable than having a filling placed.

Losing a tooth affects your ability to chew, changes your facial structure, and can impact your self-esteem. Endodontists specialize in saving teeth that might otherwise be extracted. Keeping your natural tooth is always the best option whenever possible.

Thanks to their specialization and advanced tools, endodontists typically complete treatments in fewer visits and with greater efficiency than general practitioners, getting you back to normal faster.

Endodontists take time to thoroughly evaluate your condition, explain treatment options, and answer all your questions. You’ll understand exactly what’s happening with your tooth and why treatment is recommended.

Specialized training and equipment allow endodontists to work with precision and care, minimizing trauma to your tooth and surrounding tissues. This gentler approach promotes faster healing.

Severely curved roots, calcified canals, traumatized teeth, and other challenging situations are routine for endodontists. Cases that general dentists might hesitate to treat are exactly what we’re trained to handle.
