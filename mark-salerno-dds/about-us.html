Meet your pediatric dentist

Dr. Viet Tran

Dr. Tran&#x27;s path to pediatric dentistry wasn&#x27;t just academic, it was personal. Growing up, he experienced firsthand how a difficult dental visit could leave a lasting mark, and those memories never left him. After earning his DMD from Boston University School of Dental Medicine and completing his General Practice Residency at New York Presbyterian Hospital, he pursued advanced training in pediatric dentistry at Stony Brook University Hospital, drawn by a deep desire to make dentistry different for the next generation.

With over 14 years of clinical experience, Dr. Tran built Smile First on a philosophy rooted in empathy: meeting every child exactly where they are, going at their pace, and refusing to rush an experience that deserves care and intentionality. When he had the opportunity to take over the practice of his mentor, Dr. Mark Salerno — a beloved figure in Long Island pediatric dentistry — he saw it not just as a career milestone, but as a responsibility and a privilege. That legacy continues today across three locations.

Dr. Tran is a Clinical Assistant Professor at Stony Brook University Hospital, where he helps train the next generation of pediatric dental professionals. His academic involvement keeps him at the forefront of the latest techniques and research, which directly benefits every patient who walks through the Smile First doors. He was an early adopter of Overjet AI-powered radiograph analysis and incorporates tools like iTero digital scanning to give families a more accurate, transparent look at their child&#x27;s oral health.

Beyond the clinical side, Dr. Tran is known for his ability to connect with kids, parents, and the broader community. His team reflects his values: honest, compassionate, growth-minded, and genuinely proud of the work they do together. He believes that a judgment-free environment, clear communication, and a little humor go a long way in changing how a child feels about the dentist — and how they&#x27;ll feel about their health for the rest of their life.

When he&#x27;s not in the office, Dr. Tran enjoys golfing, beach vacations, biking, exploring new destinations, rooting for the NY Giants, and spending time with his nephew.

Meet your pediatric dentist

Dr. Gabrielle Fusco brings warmth, creativity, and a genuine love of children to her work at Smile First. Growing up in Franklin Square, NY as the oldest of four siblings, she always had a natural connection with kids — and built her career around turning that into something meaningful. She believes pediatric dentistry is a journey, and she&#x27;s committed to partnering with families at every stage — keeping communication open, questions welcome, and the environment as comfortable and joyful as possible.

Outside the office, Dr. Gabby loves traveling with her family, spending time outdoors with her daughter, and baking. She can&#x27;t wait to welcome your family to Smile First and help build a lifetime of healthy, confident smiles.

Meet your orthodontist

Dr. Erin Diamantakis has been dedicated to orthodontics since childhood. Outside the office, she loves spending time with her husband Dr. Manny Diamantakis and their two children, Leo and Kalli. A first visit designed to put your whole family at east

Your child&#x27;s first visit

From the moment you call to schedule, the Smile First experience is designed to feel easy and reassuring. The team walks you through what to expect before you ever arrive, verifies your insurance benefits ahead of time to reduce any surprises at checkout, and makes sure someone is with your child every step of the way.

Patient Experience At your child&#x27;s first visit, we&#x27;ll ask what brought you in and what matters most to your family, because your goals shape the care we provide. Our doctors review findings with you in plain language, use Overjet AI-powered analysis to walk through X-rays clearly, and involve you fully in every step of treatment planning. Before you leave, you&#x27;ll have your next appointment scheduled and a clear picture of what to expect going forward.

What to Bring To help us make the most of your child&#x27;s first visit, please bring the following:

Completed new patient forms (available online through CareStack prior to your visit)

List of any current medications your child takes

Any relevant dental records or X-rays from a previous provider, if applicable

Modern tools and thoughtful touches that make every visit better

At Smile First, we invest in the technology and the little details that make a real difference, because great dental care is about more than what happens in the chair. Every element of our office is designed with your family&#x27;s experience in mind.

Overjet AI-assisted diagnostics

Overjet is an FDA-cleared artificial intelligence platform that analyzes dental X-rays in real time, helping our doctors detect decay, bone levels, and other findings with greater accuracy and consistency.

iTero digital scanning

The iTero scanner creates precise 3D digital impressions of your child&#x27;s teeth without the mess of traditional molds. It&#x27;s faster, more comfortable, and gives our in-house orthodontist the detailed imaging needed to plan orthodontic treatment, including Invisalign, with confidence.

Digital X-rays

Our digital X-rays use significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays and produce instant, high-resolution images that our doctors can review with you chairside. This means faster appointments, clearer explanations, and a more comfortable experience for your child.

In-house orthodontics

With Dr. Erin on the team, your child can move from pediatric dental care into orthodontic treatment without ever leaving the practice they already know and trust. Whether your child needs early interceptive treatment, traditional braces, or Invisalign, it all happens under one roof.

In-house general anesthesia and OR privileges

Smile First offers in-house general anesthesia and OR privileges, a level of access very few pediatric practices in the area can offer, so anxious patients, special needs patients, and those requiring more extensive treatment can receive safe, comfortable care without being referred elsewhere.

Prize vending machine

After every visit, patients get to pick a prize from our old-school vending machine. It&#x27;s become one of the most talked-about parts of the Smile First experience, and for a lot of kids, it&#x27;s something they look forward to from the moment they walk in.

CareStack online scheduling

Booking an appointment at Smile First is simple. CareStack&#x27;s online scheduling lets you request an appointment anytime from any device, and new patient forms are available digitally so you can complete everything before your first visit.
