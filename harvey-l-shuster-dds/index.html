The practice

Our dental implant services provide a permanent solution for missing teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics to your smile. We use Straumann implants, renowned for their quality and reliability.

Bone grafting is crucial for patients with insufficient jawbone density. Our advanced grafting procedures help rebuild and strengthen the bone, creating a solid foundation for dental implants.

For complex procedures or anxious patients, we offer general anesthesia. Our team is trained in administering and monitoring anesthesia to ensure your safety and comfort throughout your treatment.

When suspicious oral lesions are detected, our dental biopsy services provide crucial diagnostic information. Our experience in oral pathology ensures accurate sampling and interpretation of results.

Our CT scanning technology allows for detailed 3D imaging of your oral structures. This advanced diagnostic tool aids in precise treatment planning for implants, extractions, and other procedures.

We specialize in the safe and efficient removal of wisdom teeth. Whether impacted or causing crowding, we provide extraction services to prevent complications and alleviate discomfort.

We bring 40+ years of experience in dental implantology to the Eastchester, NY community. Our philosophy centers on combining clinical excellence with compassionate patient care.

Our patients are always invited to reach out with any questions. For common questions on insurance and wisdom teeth removal, we invite you to view our FAQs first.
