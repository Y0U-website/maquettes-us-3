Marigold Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics provides comprehensive pediatric dental care in Maplewood, NJ. Our services include:

Preventive and Diagnostic Services – Preventive care is at the heart of what we do! Not only do these services keep your child’s teeth and gums healthy, they also lay the foundation for a lifetime of good oral hygiene habits.

Restorative Dentistry – When a tooth is damaged from decay or injury, restorative dentistry helps restore its strength, function, and appearance. We offer composite dental fillings, pulp treatment, and pediatric dental crowns, along with gentle tooth extractions when a restoration is not an option. Cosmetic Dentistry – We want your child to feel good about their smile, but sometimes an injury or discoloration can create cosmetic imperfections that cause kids to become self-conscious. In these cases, we offer cosmetic bonding and safe teeth whitening treatments.

Sports Guards – A sports guard designed by a pediatric dentist is nothing like the premade options you find in stores. Our custom-sports guards are comfortable because they’re molded to your child’s teeth and jaw—and a comfortable sports guard is one that will be worn!
