Dr. Tara Groff is a Massachusetts native, born and raised in Dedham. Life has taken her to several places, including Baltimore, MD, where she earned her undergraduate degree, Tufts University in Boston, where she received her Doctor of Dental Medicine, and a stop in the United States Air Force, where she served as a general dentist and earned the rank of Captain.

Dr. Groff’s love of children’s dentistry is what led her to Ann Arbor, MI. There she completed her Master of Science in Pediatric Dentistry.

Now she has made her way back to her home state and has established Hanover Pediatric Dentistry. Here she can’t wait to meet your family and help use her vast knowledge in pediatric dentistry to help your child have the healthy smile they deserve! Serving the South Shore area.
