Restorative Dentistry

Crowns and Bridges

When several teeth are missing in a row, it can affect more than just your smile — it can impact your ability to chew comfortably, maintain proper oral health, and feel confident in everyday moments. Dental crowns and bridges provide a long-lasting solution by restoring strength, balance, and function to your bite. Because they are securely attached to your teeth, they stay firmly in place and won’t shift like removable options. Even better, this treatment typically doesn’t require surgery, allowing you to restore your smile and get back to daily life with minimal downtime.

Dental Implants

Transform your smile—and your life—with dental implants that look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. Imagine enjoying the simple things again: biting into a crisp apple, laughing with friends, or speaking with total confidence, all without worrying about your teeth. Our skilled team blends compassionate care with advanced technology to make your implant experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible. Dental implants offer a permanent, seamless solution that integrates naturally with your existing teeth, so you can stop hiding your smile and reclaim the confidence you deserve.

Partial Dentures

Missing just a few teeth? Partial dentures may be the right choice for you. Partial dentures look natural, are removable, and can be a great solution to restoring the function of your teeth and jaw. Make the first step in restoring your smile by booking a consultation .

Oral Conscious Sedation

For patients who feel anxious about dental treatment, we offer oral conscious sedation to help create a more relaxed and comfortable experience. This option involves taking a prescribed medication before your appointment, allowing you to remain awake but deeply at ease throughout your procedure. Many patients feel calm, drowsy, and less aware of time, making treatment feel quicker and more manageable. With careful monitoring and a focus on safety, our team ensures you feel supported every step of the way so you can receive the care you need with greater confidence and comfort.

Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, your dentist may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Tired of your dentures slipping and sliding around in your mouth? Implant-retained dentures, also called overdentures or snap-on dentures, can replace all of your teeth, just like traditional dentures. However, instead of relying on suction or adhesive, they are secured in place with several dental implants. These removable appliances make it easy to keep up with your oral hygiene, and are made from a combination of acrylic and metal materials for enhanced durability.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns restore damaged teeth by covering and protecting what remains of your natural tooth. When you have a cracked or broken tooth, these custom caps not only shield the root but also improve how your teeth work together when you bite. If you&#x27;ve had a root canal or dental implant, your dentist will typically recommend a crown to strengthen the treated tooth. With several material options available, crowns help both your dental health and appearance, giving you back a smile you can feel good about.
