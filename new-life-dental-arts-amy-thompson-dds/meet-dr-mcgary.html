Meet Dr. McGary

Dr. Julia McGary met Dr. Amy in 2011 when they both became founding members of a local study club. After she retired from her practice in Marysville, she started working at New Life Dental Arts in March 2023 as an interim hygienist and dentist. With her incredible skills, knowledge, and exceptional care for her patients, she was an excellent fit from the start.

Dr. Julia grew up in California and went to hygiene school in Hayward, California, where she later returned to teach. Her father was a dentist, so dentistry was a natural fit for her and turned out to be a career she thrived in with her strong desire to grow and learn. Not surprisingly, Dr. Julia went on to dental school 7 years later at the University of Pacific in San Francisco, and graduated in 2002.

Now semi-retired, Dr. Julia still enjoys being an active member our local dental community. Always willing to help, she is the perfect addition to NLDA, working as both a hygienist and dentist as the need arises. Our patients and team adore her, and we are finding any and every way we can to keep her around for as long as possible. When she is not at the office, you will find her quilting at home with her adorable rescue Beagles!
