Dental Services for Kids & Teens

Gentle dental care for kids, teens & special needs patients from routine visits to orthodontics, sedation and oral surgery.

The practice

Kids Dental Castle provides pediatric dentistry and orthodontic care in Pomona, CA, in a fun, comfortable, and welcoming environment. Our team provides preventive and restorative dental care, braces and Invisalign , special needs dentistry, sedation dentistry, and oral surgery all conveniently available in one office.

We focus on making every visit easier for children and their families, from a child’s first dental visit through the teenage years and beyond. Our team takes the time to understand each patient’s individual needs and create a positive dental experience.

Pediatric dentistry focuses on the oral health of infants, children, and teens, providing gentle, specialized care to keep growing smiles healthy, strong, and cavity-free.

Orthodontics helps straighten teeth and align jaws using braces, clear aligners, and other treatments creating healthy bites and confident, beautiful smiles that last a lifetime.

Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners to gently straighten teeth, offering a comfortable, discreet alternative to braces for kids, teens, and adults.

Nitrous oxide, or laughing gas, is a safe, gentle sedation option that helps children and adults feel relaxed and comfortable during dental treatments.

IV sedation and general anesthesia provide safe, effective options for deeper relaxation or complete sleep, ensuring comfort and ease during more complex dental treatments.

Wisdom teeth extraction removes impacted or overcrowded third molars to prevent pain, infection, and alignment issues helping protect long-term oral health and comfort.

Oral surgery addresses conditions like impacted teeth, infections, or jaw issues with safe, precise procedures to restore comfort, health, and a confident smile.
