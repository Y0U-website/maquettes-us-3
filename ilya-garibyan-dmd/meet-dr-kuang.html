About Dr. Tiffany Kuang

After growing up in a household where she experienced firsthand what it’s like to care for a medically complex patient long-term, Dr. Kuang developed a love for patient care and medicine. She became an oral surgeon to care for others who have complex needs and restore their oral function. As a surgeon, she is able to manage advanced surgical cases and provide patients with the comprehensive treatment they need while ensuring their safety.

Dr. Kuang and Dr. Garibyan were co-residents during their residency and founded Premier Center for Oral, Dental Implant & Facial Surgery . They wanted to work in a collaborative setting where they could provide exemplary care while creating a fun environment for staff and patients.

Dr. Kuang understands the nervousness that often accompanies surgical procedures. Because she grew up taking care of her mother, she is able to connect with patients and empathize with their situation. Dr. Kuang helps patients feel at ease and takes the time to thoroughly review their anesthesia options and their upcoming oral surgery procedure. She is always available post-operatively to answer questions or concerns to make sure every patient feels confident and comfortable.

In addition to her work at Premier Center for Oral, Dental Implant & Facial Surgery, Dr. Kuang is on staff at Elliot Hospital in Manchester, NH, where she accepts facial trauma cases.

Education & Residency

Dr. Kuang studied biochemistry and psychology at New York University and earned her DMD from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2013. She completed a general dentistry residency at Hackensack Meridian Mountainside Medical Center in 2014, an oral and maxillofacial surgery internship at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2015, and her oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at St. Joseph’s University Medical Center .

Certifications

Dr. Kuang is a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons ( AAOMS ) and the Massachusetts Society of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons ( MSOMS ). She is board certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery ( ABOMS ), a testament to her commitment to patient safety and comfort.

Hobbies & Interests

Outside of work, Dr. Kuang enjoys anything outdoors, such as hiking, running, and kayaking. She likes to try new cuisines, travel, and get lost in unfamiliar cities for new adventures.
