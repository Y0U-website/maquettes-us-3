Planned Giving

  • DAF Direct makes it easier to support KinderSmile Foundation by giving directly from your donor-advised fund. Learn more here.
  • For Other DAF Providers: If your DAF provider is not listed, contact them directly to initiate a grant recommendation to KinderSmile Foundation.

Gifts of Stock & Securities

Donating appreciated stocks, mutual funds or bonds to charity is an efficient way to contribute that can provide you with significant tax benefits.

Retirement Assets & Beneficiary Designations

Name KinderSmile Foundation as a beneficiary of an IRA, 401(k), or other retirement account.

Charitable Gift Annuities & Trusts

Support KinderSmile Foundation through Charitable Remainder Trusts (CRTs) and Charitable Gift Annuities (CGAs).

Matching Gift & Employer Giving Programs

Check with your employer to see if your contribution qualifies for a match. Many employers offer matching gift programs that double or even triple the impact of charitable donations.
