Comfortable. Caring. Compassionate.

In addition to his extensive formal education, Dr. Grisanti has participated in over 900 hours of continuing education since graduation from dental school. He is an alumni of the Dawson Academy, a premier organization providing advanced dental education. In 2009 he was awarded his Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry, an honor only 7 percent of AGD dentists have achieved. Dr. Grisanti has also been selected by his peers to join the prestigious Southwest Academy of Restorative Dentistry, and has been selected as a Texas Monthly magazine Super Dentist, 2009.

Dr. Grisanti remains active in organized dentistry, serving as past president of the Dallas Academy of General Dentistry and the Rockwall County Dental Society. He is an active member in the American Dental Association, the Texas Dental Association, the American Orthodontic Society, The Chicago Dental Society, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, Southwest Academy of Restorative Dentistry, and the Academy of General Dentistry. He currently serves on the board of directors for the Dallas component of the AGD.

It is Dr. Grisanti’s philosophy to “treat each patient with the highest level of care and compassion while providing the most ideal, technically advanced treatment which will help keep your teeth stable, functional, and esthetic for a lifetime.”
