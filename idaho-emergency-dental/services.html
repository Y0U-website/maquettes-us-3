Our Services

Specialized emergency and urgent dental care to get you out of pain and back to your life. If our clinic is not open, we will try to provide a referral or guidance to get you the help you need. Also, ask us about dentures and implants on a case-by-case basis.

Emergency and Urgent Care Focus Please note that Idaho Emergency Dental is not a full-service dental clinic. We specialize in emergency and urgent dental needs, providing immediate relief and treatment for acute dental problems. For routine cleanings, cosmetic procedures, and ongoing preventive care, we recommend establishing a relationship with a general dentist in your area. We are happy to provide referrals to help you find your dental home.

Emergency Dental Exams Comprehensive emergency examinations to quickly diagnose the source of your dental pain or issue. We use advanced diagnostic tools including digital X-rays to identify problems and develop an immediate treatment plan.

When infection reaches the inner pulp of your tooth, a root canal can save the tooth and relieve severe pain. Our experienced team performs root canal therapy efficiently and comfortably, often completing treatment in a single visit.

Dental Implants Replace missing teeth with permanent, natural-looking dental implants. Implants provide a strong foundation for replacement teeth and help preserve jawbone health. We offer consultations and implant placement services.

Dentures and Repairs Restore your smile and ability to eat comfortably with custom-fitted dentures. We offer full and partial dentures, as well as denture repairs and adjustments for existing dentures that no longer fit properly.

Same-Day Restorations When fillings or teeth break, you can trust us to restore your smile and bite. We offer same-day fillings for chipped front teeth and same-day CEREC crowns/onlays for fractured or cracked back teeth.
