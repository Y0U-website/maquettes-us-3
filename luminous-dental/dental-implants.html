Dental Implants

Missing teeth can cause additional oral health issues, such as bone loss, shifting teeth, and difficulty chewing. Dental implants address these concerns, providing aesthetic and functional benefits to your oral health.

  • You wear dentures or bridges but find them uncomfortable or inconvenient.
  • You want to improve your ability to eat and speak.
  • You wish to enhance your smile and boost your confidence.

Benefits of Dental Implants in Hudson

Dental implants offer a range of benefits that go beyond aesthetic improvements:

  • Natural Look and Feel: Dental implants are customized to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. They look and feel just like your original teeth, giving you a flawless smile.
  • Long-Lasting Solution: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Unlike traditional restorations, they aren’t prone to decay or need frequent replacements.
  • Improved Comfort: Say goodbye to the discomfort of ill-fitting dentures. Dental implants sit naturally in your jaw, indistinguishable from real teeth.
  • Restored Functionality: Enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. Implants restore full chewing power, allowing you to eat, laugh, and speak confidently.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area tends to weaken over time. Dental implants stimulate the bone, preventing loss and preserving your facial structure.

The Process of Getting Dental Implants in Hudson

Here’s what to expect if you choose to get dental implants at Luminous Dental Hudson:

Consultation

It all begins with a personalized consultation with Dr. Leah Divito. Using state-of-the-art technology like digital X-rays and intraoral cameras, we’ll assess your oral health to determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants.

Placement of the Implant

Once ready, we will surgically place the implant post into your jawbone. We carefully tailor the procedure to your anatomy for maximum precision.

Healing & Osseointegration

After the implant placement, a healing period of several months is necessary. During this time, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process known as osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation.

Applying the Restoration

Once the implant has fully integrated, we’ll attach a customized abutment and the final restoration. This phase ensures your new tooth looks and functions like the real thing.

Aftercare for Dental Implants

Proper aftercare is essential to ensure the long-term success of your dental implants. Follow these tips to maintain your new smile:

The team at Luminous Dental Hudson is always available to provide tips and support for maintaining your implants.

  • Brush and floss regularly, just like your natural teeth.
  • Schedule routine dental check-ups at Luminous Dental Hudson.
  • Avoid hard or sticky foods that could damage the crown.
  • Quit smoking, as it can interfere with the healing and osseointegration process.
