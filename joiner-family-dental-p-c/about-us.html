Our Story

Joiner Family Dental exists for several reasons. But at the heart of it all is a simple question we ask ourselves with every patient that walks through our doors. “If this was my friend or family member, how would I want them to be treated?” This leads us to have an uncompromising set of goals when it comes to the core of what we do.

At Joiner Family Dental:

  • We are dedicated to ethical and honest communication.
  • We believe that the best way to foster oral hygiene is not just through treatment but also through patient education.
  • We carry the deep conviction that your resources of time and finances, are personally entrusted to us and therefore ought never to be taken advantage of.
  • We are aware that making a difference in the lives of our patients is directly connected to making a difference in the life of our Roscoe community.

Come and see for yourself why patients everywhere are making this the last time they’ll ever search for a new dental office.

Community Involvement

We firmly believe taking care of patients also means that we take care of our community. That’s why we are always looking for different ways to give back to our town of Roscoe, Illinois and the families that reside there.

Partnering with Caritas, Paper Angel Closet, sponsoring trips to Timberlee outdoor camp, donating finances to the Roscoe Fall Festival, and giving financial support for students going on Missions Trips and Class Trips, are just a few of the fun and exciting ways we’ve been able to show our support and give back to the bigger picture.

We can’t wait to hear about what outreach or organizations are important to you and to see how we can partner with you on your mission!
