What Is All-on-4?

All-on-4 is a full-arch tooth replacement system a complete set of upper or lower teeth, 10 to 14 teeth permanently anchored to just four precisely placed implants. You come in missing teeth; you leave with a fixed, non-removable smile.

The name comes from the engineering insight that four implants, when angled strategically in the strongest parts of the jaw, can support an entire arch. This is different from replacing each tooth individually, and it’s different from dentures. The result functions like natural teeth: you eat steak, bite into apples, speak clearly, and smile without hesitation.

For patients who have been told they lack enough bone for traditional implants, All-on-4 is frequently still possible, as the angled posterior implants reach deeper, denser bone that standard vertical implants can’t access, often eliminating the need for bone grafting.

How Complete Dental Implant Solutions Work

Replacing the top or bottom arch of teeth with standard dental implants would require an implant for each tooth. Alternatively, All-on-4 uses just four implants to replace the top or bottom row of teeth. The treatment works even if the patient has no teeth at all. The four implants support the entire arch of replacement teeth. Also, this treatment does not require bone grafting. The implants will fuse with the existing bone and become part of the jaw. Because some of the implants are set at an angle, they are longer and offer more support.

All-on-4 implant treatment is similar to getting regular implants. However, the procedure and recovery time is faster. First, the doctor will take impressions and X-rays and send those to a lab. At a subsequent appointment, our staff will use a local anesthetic to numb the patient and may have to remove any remaining damaged teeth. Then, the dental professional will insert screws into four places in the upper and lower jaw. These titanium anchors will help support the artificial teeth throughout the mouth.

The Dental Implant Placement Process

Our team will send you home with specific aftercare instructions that you should follow exactly for proper healing and prevention of infection. Instructions commonly include brushing with a soft bristle toothbrush, sleeping with your head elevated and eating only soft foods. Immediately following the procedure you may be sore, have swelling and light bleeding. As always, call our office immediately if these symptoms get worse rather than subside.

Patients should maintain proper care of the implants and their oral health. Just as with natural teeth, patients need to continue brushing and flossing daily. Regular dental checkups are essential. A dentist should routinely examine the implants and artificial teeth to ensure that everything looks the way it should.

The Top Benefits of All-On-4

  • Durability: Fixed prostheses are stronger and longer-lasting than traditional dentures, which often require frequent repairs or replacements. With proper oral care, permanent implants can provide many years of reliable function.
  • Convenience: These implants offer superior stability and comfort, supported securely by artificial roots. This allows you to speak clearly and enjoy all your favorite foods with ease, making them feel just like natural teeth.
  • Quick Recovery: Because only four implants are typically needed for full-mouth restorations, the procedure is less invasive and recovery is faster. At Oxford Smiles, our dentists ensure the surgery is efficient, helping you heal quickly and comfortably.
  • Immediate aesthetic results: Whether using All-on-4, 5, 6, or 8 prostheses, new teeth can often be placed at the earliest. This means you can leave with a confident, beautiful smile without waiting months as required with traditional methods.
  • No Need for Bone Grafting: When jawbone quality or quantity is limited, bone grafting is usually necessary for traditional implants. However, with the All-on-4 technique, implants are placed at strategic angles-two at the back at 60 degrees-where bone loss is less severe, eliminating the need for grafts in most cases.
