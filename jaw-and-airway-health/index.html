The practice

Our approach is comprehensive and conservative : detailed evaluation, evidence-based planning, and personalized treatment designed to last.

Why Choose Us Non-surgical, functional therapy. Our approach comprises of custom appliance therapies that allow for joint decompression, myofascial relief, and long-term TMJ stability and airway competency — day and night.

Chronic TMJ symptoms are often influenced by muscular compensation and nervous system stress. That’s why we incorporate neuro-muscular reflex testing as part of a comprehensive evaluation. It helps identify patterns of compensation and guides a conservative plan that supports successful long term outcomes.

Your first visit is usually about two hours and fully focused on you—allowing time for a comprehensive evaluation, clear answers, and a personalized plan.
