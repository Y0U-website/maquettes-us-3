Full Arch Dental Implants in Huntington Beach — Complete Smile Restoration for Upper or Lower Jaw

Full arch implant restoration replaces every tooth in your upper or lower jaw with a fixed prosthesis anchored to 4–6 implants. Permanent, functional, and indistinguishable from natural teeth.

Full Arch Implant Restoration: Complete Tooth Replacement

Full arch dental implants represent the pinnacle of tooth replacement technology. Rather than relying on suction, adhesives, or pressure against gum tissue the way dentures do, a full arch implant restoration is anchored permanently into your jawbone via 4–6 titanium implants. The prosthesis does not move, does not come out, and does not require messy adhesives. It feels and functions like natural teeth in every meaningful way.

Full arch implant treatment is appropriate for patients who are missing all teeth in one or both arches, patients with severely failing dentition requiring comprehensive extraction and restoration, and patients who are dissatisfied with conventional dentures and want a permanent fixed solution. It is also considered for patients undergoing full mouth reconstruction .

The specific implant protocol — whether All-on-4 , All-on-6 , or a different configuration — is determined by 3D bone analysis and treatment planning at Huntington Beach Prosthodontics. In most cases, same-day temporary teeth are provided, meaning you leave your surgical appointment with a complete set of teeth already in place.

Cost of Full Arch Implants in Huntington Beach

Full arch implant restorations range from $20,000–$35,000 per arch in the Huntington Beach / Orange County area. The cost depends on the specific implant protocol (All-on-4 vs. All-on-6), the prosthetic material (acrylic-on-metal hybrid vs. monolithic zirconia), whether extractions are included, and whether bone grafting is required.

While significant, this investment is often compared favorably to the 20-year cost of conventional dentures — which require relining, replacement, and adhesive — and to the cumulative cost of treating and eventually losing failing dentition tooth by tooth. Most patients find the quality-of-life improvement substantial.
