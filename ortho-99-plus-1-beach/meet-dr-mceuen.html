Meet Dr. Kurt McEuen

Services Patient Information Financial Contact Schedule Schedule Meet Dr. Kurt McEuen Bartram Park Location Dr. McEuen, a native of sunny Southern California, is a relatively recent newcomer to Florida. His academic and professional journey through Utah and Texas has enriched his experience and honed his skills, and ultimately brought him to the Sunshine State.

Football to Orthodontics

Dr. McEuen’s professional journey began at Brigham Young University, where he balanced academics and athletics as a Quarterback for their football team. His tenacity and discipline on the field parallel his dedication to his patients in providing excellent orthodontic care. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Roseman University in Salt Lake City, Utah, setting the foundation for his dental career.

Pursuing His Passion

Having served as a general dentist in the United States Army for four years in San Antonio, Texas, Dr. McEuen discovered his true calling: orthodontics. The ability to transform smiles and boost confidence led him to further his studies, choosing Jacksonville University for his specialized training and residency in orthodontics.

A Transformative Approach to Orthodontics

Dr. McEuen’s mission goes beyond just correcting teeth alignment; he’s dedicated to enhancing smiles, uplifting spirits, and building confidence in his patients. His approachable demeanor and top-notch orthodontic care have made him a cherished member of our dental team and a favorite among patients.

Active Family Life

When he’s not crafting beautiful smiles, Dr. McEuen is busy making memories with his wife and four energetic children. An avid sports enthusiast, he loves traveling with his children’s sports teams and coaches various sports within the community, spreading his love for teamwork and discipline.

With Dr. McEuen, you’re not just getting an accomplished orthodontist; you’re also gaining a friend. His warm and friendly approach to orthodontic care ensures an excellent patient experience and a beautiful, confident smile.
