Les soins

A periodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease, as well as procedures like dental implants and gum grafting to restore oral health.

For patients missing more than one tooth, our clinic in Duncanville offers multiple dental implants as a fixed alternative to partial dentures. If you have two or more adjacent missing teeth in a row, we can replace them with a custom-designed implant bridge.

With advanced regenerative techniques, we rebuild lost jawbone caused by tooth loss or any periodontal disease. Having strong and healthy bones is important for dental stability and future implant placement.
