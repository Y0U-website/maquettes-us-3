Meet Dr. Phillip Moorad

Dr. Moorad: Personalized Care for Healthy Smiles

A Rich Background in Dentistry

Dr. Phillip Moorad’s journey in dentistry began with a passion for helping people achieve optimal oral health and improved quality of life through compassionate, expert care. After earning his undergraduate degree in zoology from Oklahoma State University in 1992, Dr. Moorad pursued his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) at Oklahoma University College of Dentistry, graduating with honors in 1996. His academic excellence and dedication to learning are reflected in the top-tier care he provides to his patients at North Dental.

Early in his career, Dr. Moorad gained valuable experience practicing as an associate dentist in Arizona, where he further developed his skills in general dentistry and cosmetic dentistry . His passion for environmentally sustainable practices led him to establish a unique, eco-friendly dental practice, earning a Gold Certification from the Eco-Dentistry Association. This achievement placed him among the few dentists in the U.S. to receive this prestigious distinction.

Dr. Moorad’s commitment to continuous learning is evident in his completion of more than 700 hours of continuing education, with specialized training in implant restorations , Invisalign , and dental lasers. He is also a proud graduate of esteemed postgraduate dental institutions, including the Kois Institute and the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Learning.
