Meet Dr. Kevin G. Smith

DMD, FASDA, FADSA, Diplomate ADBA

Dr. Kevin G. Smith hails from the Great Northwest. He was born and raised in Portland, Oregon. After pursuing a Biology Major in college and a two-year mission for his church in Seoul, South Korea, he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Oregon Health and Science University.

After residency, Dr. Smith and his family moved to Texas. He provided in-office anesthesia services for pediatric dentists throughout the Lone Star state for over a decade.

Happily, new opportunities have brought Dr. Smith and his family to the North Star State Minnesota! Dr Smith’s general warmth, gentleness, professionalism, and concern for others make him a favorite with patients in the setting of dental anesthesia. He has been privileged to offer anesthesia services specifically to over 15,000 pediatric dental patients and considers himself very much at home with the younger population in the dental community.

Dr. Smith is a board certified diplomate of both the American Dental Board of Anesthesiology and the National Dental Board of Anesthesiology. He is also a fellow in anesthesiology of both the American Society of Dentist Anesthesiologists and the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology. In addition, Dr. Smith is a member of the American Society of Anesthesiologists and the International Anesthesia Research Society.
