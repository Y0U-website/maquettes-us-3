Fillings

When a cavity strikes, our dentists expertly remove the damaged portion and replace it with a durable filling material.

Dental Prophylaxis

Preventing dental issues begins with proper home care and continues with our professional efforts. Our team promotes, restores, and maintains your oral health through biannual dental exams, including oral cancer screenings and regular X-rays for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

Scaling & Root Planing

For active gum disease, we perform thorough scaling and root planing procedures to restore gum health effectively.

Partials & Dentures

Replace missing teeth affordably with our precision-crafted removable complete or partial dentures. Multiple appointments ensure the best fit.

Root Canals

Save your tooth with our successful root canal therapy, which is ideal for decay, infection, or trauma affecting the nerve. A sealed crown protects against chewing forces.

Crowns & Bridges

Utilize porcelain’s strength and beauty with our non-removable bridges and crowns, cemented to surrounding natural teeth for stability.

Extractions

Sometimes, a tooth needs removal despite our efforts. Dr. Samad skillfully performs stress-free extractions, while complex cases may be referred to an oral surgeon.

Implants

Durable titanium implants are a reliable solution for tooth replacement, either completed in-house or referred for specialized treatment.

Veneers

Enhance your smile with thin porcelain veneers, conservatively altering tooth shape and color. Dr. Samad evaluates candidacy and may recommend protective measures.

ZOOM! Whitening

Night Guards
