The practice

We offer free consultations. Please feel free to give us a call or come in and enjoy our office amenities while you wait, such as television, and coffee.

What We Believe We feel there’s only one way to practice dentistry: with concern, clear communication, clinical excellence, and honesty. Our responsibility is to help you plan for a future of good dental health. We can only do that with your help. If you have a concern or need additional information, please ask. Let us know your thoughts about our practice. We want to keep you smiling.

Comfortable Dentistry Many people feel uneasy about dental treatment. But if you haven’t been to the dentist in a while, you may be pleasantly surprised. Our gentle approach to care will make your visit a positive experience. We help you to feel as comfortable as possible.

Appreciation Thank you for visiting our site. Any time you have a question or concern, do ask us via email or phone or fax at any time. We think candid communication-especially when it comes to your health-is evidence of quality care and mutual respect.
