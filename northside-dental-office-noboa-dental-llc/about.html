About the Doctor

Meet Dr. Angela T. Noboa, DDS

Dr. Angela T. Noboa is a general dentist committed to providing comprehensive and personalized dental care in a comfortable, welcoming environment. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Universidad Nacional Pedro Henríquez Ureña in the Dominican Republic in 1999.

After continuing her professional development in the United States, Dr. Noboa completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the Florida Institute for Advanced Dental Education in Miami in 2022.

Her approach combines careful clinical evaluation, clear communication and respect for each patient&#x27;s individual needs and goals. Dr. Noboa takes time to explain treatment options and strives to build lasting relationships grounded in trust, honesty and compassionate care.

Fluent in English and Spanish, she welcomes Miami&#x27;s diverse community to Northside Dental for preventive, restorative, cosmetic and general dental care.
