Forty years ago, Paramount Dental Studio opened its doors and began providing high-end restorations to doctors in Orange County and the Los Angeles area.

We are a little larger nowadays but our work culture and attitude of making it right every time remain the same. We stand behind every one of our restorations. In the years since 1981, we have added a number of team members, including one of the largest veteran work forces of any national dental laboratory, moved to a state-of-the-art facility and invested heavily in the latest digital technology.

We are now one of the largest independent dental laboratories in the United States and employ a global workforce to serve doctors across the country.
