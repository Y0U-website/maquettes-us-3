What are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The All-on-4 implant treatment involves the strategic placement of just four dental implants per arch to support a complete set of teeth. Unlike traditional dentures that sit on the gums and may require adhesives for stability, All-on-4 implants are anchored securely into the jawbone, providing unparalleled strength and functionality. These implants fuse with the natural bone over time, creating a solid foundation for the prosthetic teeth that not only restores the appearance of a natural smile but also promotes better oral health.

When are All-on-4 Implants Recommended?

  • Extensive Tooth Loss: All-on-4 implants are ideal for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both dental arches. Whether due to decay, trauma, or other dental issues , All-on-4 implants can provide a comprehensive solution to restore a full set of teeth.
  • Unstable Dentures: Patients who struggle with loose or ill-fitting dentures may benefit from All-on-4 implants. Traditional dentures can shift or slip during eating or speaking, causing discomfort and embarrassment. All-on-4 implants offer superior stability, eliminating the need for adhesives and providing a secure fit.
  • Bone Loss: Individuals with reduced bone density in the jaw may still be candidates for All-on-4 implants. Unlike traditional implants that require significant bone volume for successful placement, All-on-4 implants utilize strategic angulation to maximize contact with available bone, often eliminating the need for bone grafting procedures.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Enhanced Stability: Unlike traditional dentures, which may slip or shift during eating or speaking, All-on-4 implants remain firmly in place, allowing patients to enjoy their favorite foods and speak confidently.

Improved Functionality: With All-on-4 implants, patients can regain full chewing function, allowing them to eat a varied diet without restrictions. The implants ensure your replacement teeth stay in place during chewing, providing a natural and comfortable eating experience.

Natural Appearance: All-on-4 implants are designed to closely resemble natural teeth, both in appearance and function. They are custom-made to match the shape, size, and color of the patient’s remaining teeth, creating a seamless and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Preservation of Bone Structure: All-on-4 implants help preserve the jawbone by stimulating bone growth, fusing with the surrounding bone, providing stability, and preventing further deterioration of the bone structure.

Long-Term Solution: All-on-4 implants offer a permanent solution for tooth replacement, with the potential to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. Traditional dentures may need to be replaced or adjusted periodically, All-on-4 implants offer a long-term solution for missing teeth.

How Long do All-on-4 Dentures Last?

With proper care and maintenance, All-on-4 implants can last for many years and, in some cases, a lifetime. The longevity of implant-supported dentures depends on factors, including the patient’s oral health, habits, and lifestyle. To prolong the durability of All-on-4 implants, you should follow recommended oral care practices, including regular brushing and flossing, as well as using antimicrobial mouth rinses. Also, routine dental visits for professional cleanings and examinations allow us to monitor the health and stability of your implants over time.

RESTORATIVE RELATED:

DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants are artificial replacements for missing teeth. They are typically made from titanium and surgically inserted into the jawbone to provide a secure, long-lasting solution for tooth replacement. Dental implants can be used to replace a single missing tooth or multiple teeth or to support dentures.

CROWNS & BRIDGES

Crowns and bridges are restorative dental treatments used to repair decayed, broken, or missing teeth. A crown is a custom-made artificial tooth that covers the entire visible part of a natural tooth, while a bridge is a prosthesis consisting of one or more artificial teeth held together by two crowns that are attached to healthy teeth on either side of the gap. Crowns and bridges can be made from different materials, such as porcelain, ceramic, or metal alloys.

DENTURES

Dentures are removable prosthetic teeth used to replace missing teeth. They usually consist of an acrylic base that fits over the gums and artificial teeth made from porcelain or plastic. Dentures can be full or partial and may need to be adjusted or replaced as your mouth changes with age.
