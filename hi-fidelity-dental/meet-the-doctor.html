Meet Dr. Anthony Bianco, DMD

Meet the doctor Personalized care for a lasting smile. No matter where you are on your dental journey, Dr. Bianco is here to listen, guide, and help you build the healthy, confident smile you deserve.

A non-traditional path that led to a calling for connection .

Dr. Bianco was born in Chicago and grew up in Glen Ellyn. After graduating from Glenbard West High School, he earned his bachelor&#x27;s degree in Italian from Washington University in St. Louis.

His path to dentistry wasn&#x27;t a straight line. After graduate school at the University of Chicago, he spent a decade in management with Trader Joe&#x27;s, a career built entirely around taking care of people, which turns out to be excellent training for a dental practice.

His desire to push further won out, and he embarked on his dental career in 2015. A graduate of Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine-Illinois, he treats everything from routine cleanings to full-mouth implant reconstruction using some of the most advanced technology available.

What patients notice first is the gentle touch and the easy, unhurried conversation. He genuinely likes people, hearing their stories, learning what they care about, and improving their health along the way. That&#x27;s the part he looks forward to every morning.

He never stopped being a student.

Dentistry moves fast. Dr. Bianco spends a serious portion of every year in continuing education with the people who set the standard, so the care you get in Elmhurst reflects where the field is going, not where it was.

Two boxers, a ball field, and a whole lot of family.

When he&#x27;s not in the operatory, Dr. Bianco is a devoted family man, and thoroughly outnumbered by dogs. Ask him about them and you&#x27;ll get the full story, probably with photos.

It&#x27;s the kind of thing that makes a dental visit feel less like an appointment and more like catching up with a friend who happens to be great at his job.
