Steven Deming, DDS

Steve Deming was born and raised in Oregon. More specifically, born on Cornell Road in Beaverton in the back seat of a Volkswagen to his parents’ surprise. Son of James and Elaine Deming, dentistry is in his blood. James and Elaine, now enjoying retirement, met while James was in dental school and Elaine was in dental hygiene school. James was a founding faculty member of the dental hygiene program at Portland Community College where he taught for over 30 years. After raising five children, Elaine returned to practicing dental hygiene in Beaverton for many years.

Currently a father of four, Steve practices dentistry in Dallas, Oregon. He has pursued additional training in the surgical placement of dental implants and orthodontic tooth straightening system know as Invisalign, which allows one to achieve straight teeth without braces. In his spare time, he enjoys coaching his children’s sports teams, practicing their weekly spelling words and volunteering as a Boy Scout scout master, middle school track coach and at the Salem free clinic.
