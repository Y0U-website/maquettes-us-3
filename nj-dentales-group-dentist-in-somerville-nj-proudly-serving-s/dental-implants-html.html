What to expect at NJ DenTales Group

Dr. Anand completed an Implantology Boot Camp in 2025 and places implants directly in our Somerville, NJ office — no referral out required. The process is typically:

  • Consultation & 3D imaging. We map your mouth in three dimensions to plan the ideal implant position.
  • Implant placement. A minor surgical procedure performed with local anesthesia — most patients describe it as easier than a tooth extraction.
  • Healing & osseointegration. Over 3-6 months, your jawbone fuses naturally around the implant post.
  • Custom crown placement. The final tooth is crafted to match your surrounding teeth exactly and secured to the implant.

Single implants, multiple implants, and All-on-4

We offer solutions for any extent of tooth loss — from a single missing tooth to full-arch restorations using four strategically placed implants (All-on-4). During your consultation, we will walk through the options, costs, and insurance coverage so you can decide with full information.

Cost & insurance

Implant pricing depends on the specific case, whether bone grafting is needed, and the crown material. We accept most PPO dental insurance, offer 0 % financing through CareCredit, and provide transparent pricing at the consultation — no hidden fees. Many patients are surprised to learn that implants cost less over 20 years than repeatedly replacing dentures or bridges.
