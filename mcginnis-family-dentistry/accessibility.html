Website Accessibility

McGinnis Family Dentistry is committed to providing a website experience that is usable by a broad range of visitors.

Our Accessibility Goal

This website is designed with keyboard navigation, readable contrast, descriptive headings, meaningful alternative text, responsive layouts, and clear form labels. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we test for it after every major design or content change.

Need Assistance?

If you have difficulty using this website or accessing information, please contact our office at 850-992-0202. Our team will work with you to provide the information you need through a reasonable alternative method.

Third-Party Services

Some scheduling, mapping, review, or patient-portal services on this website may be operated by third parties. If you encounter an accessibility barrier with one of these services, please let us know so we can help find an alternative.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. Please contact us at 850-992-0202 with any comments or concerns.

Questions?

Contact McGinnis Family Dentistry for help with website information or appointment requests.
