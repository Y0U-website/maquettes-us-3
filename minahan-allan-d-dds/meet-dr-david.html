David Minahan, DDS

The best care of our patients is our prime mission at Minahan Dental; ongoing education is the key to us staying current with evolving trends and techniques in dentistry and ensuring we’re providing the most up-to-date options.

I complete many hours of advanced study, in a broad range of dental topics, each year. I take courses and attend lectures and meetings locally, regionally, and nationally. I am also a 40 year member of a local management study club for dentists.

  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS), University of Washington School of Dentistry , 1975

Professional Memberships

Over the years I’ve been an active volunteer with the American Dental Association and the UW School of Dentistry. I’ve had the honor of serving as President of the Washington State Dental Association, as well as the UW Dental School Alumni and Dean’s Club groups. Currently I’m completing a four-year term as an ADA Council member, serving at meetings in Washington, DC.

Beyond the Practice

I was born in Sedro Woolley and grew up in the Burien/Des Moines area of Seattle. After graduating from dental school, my family and I settled in Kenmore, where I’ve lived for over 40 years.

My wife Susan and I have two adult sons, Greg and my partner in the practice, Allan, who live in the area. We’ve been blessed with two active young grandchildren, Owen and Emmarie. With all of us living close together, we are able to enjoy many family and youth sporting events.

When not hanging out with family, I’m often found at the golf course, pursuing my passion for the sport. Travel, tagging along at farmer’s markets and walks around Green Lake with my better half, and most spectator sports, occupy a lot of my free time.
