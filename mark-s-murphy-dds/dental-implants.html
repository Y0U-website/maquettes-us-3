Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer unparalleled advantages over other tooth replacement options:

For patients in Castro Valley and nearby areas, dental implants provide a reliable, long-term solution to missing teeth.

  • Natural Function and Appearance: Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
  • Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.
  • Convenience: Unlike dentures, implants are fixed and require no adhesives or special cleaning routines.

Dental Implant FAQs

How much do dental implants cost in Castro Valley?

The cost of dental implants depends on how many teeth you&#x27;re replacing and whether you need any preparatory treatment like a bone graft. During your consultation at Mark S. Murphy, DDS, we&#x27;ll give you a clear, personalized estimate and review your financing and insurance options.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general and oral health are candidates for dental implants. The best way to know is a consultation, where Dr. Murphy evaluates your jawbone, gums, and overall health to recommend single-tooth, multiple-tooth, or full-arch implants.

What&#x27;s the difference between a single tooth implant and full-arch implants?

A single-tooth implant replaces one missing tooth with an individual post and crown. Full-arch implants replace an entire row of teeth using several implants to support a fixed bridge or denture — ideal for patients missing most or all of their teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades and often a lifetime. The implant post fuses with your jawbone, and the crown on top typically lasts many years before it may need replacement. Routine cleanings and good home care are key.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Implant placement is done under local anesthesia, so you shouldn&#x27;t feel pain during the procedure. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward, easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief.
