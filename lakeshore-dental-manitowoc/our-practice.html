Technology

Advancements in modern dentistry, such as improved processes and technology, provide Dr. Lauren with a wide array of options for restoring the health, beauty and function of your teeth.

Since we believe you — our valued patient — deserve the best services available, our entire team at Lakeshore Dental is dedicated to continuing education, which keeps us on the leading edge of today’s technology and techniques.

Our team is highly trained in the use of our in-office advanced dental technology and state-of-the-art equipment. Intra-oral photos and digital images are taken on a regular basis. These techniques allow us to evaluate your dental needs while providing a visual tour of your mouth. Our laser-assisted periodontal therapy provides gentle, effective treatment. We use the most advanced, safe and effective materials to give you the results you desire.

The combination of our experience, passion and cutting-edge technology allows us to carry out your treatment comfortably and efficiently.
