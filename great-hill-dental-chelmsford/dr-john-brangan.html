John A. Brangan, Great Hill Dental

"From the first visit onward, everyone at Great Hill Dental is committed to educating patients on the connection between oral and overall health, delivering care that supports total well-being."

Dr. John A. Brangan is most passionate about restoring patients’ oral health in a low-stress environment. Treating anxious people who may have stayed away from the dentist for years and helping them become great dental patients is his goal.

He loves the practice of dentistry and enjoys giving exceptional care to all patients in a calm and caring environment. Dentistry has provided him and his family with a great life and gives him opportunities to meet new people every day.

Being active in the community is important to him, as it provides opportunities to give back. He volunteers at his children’s school and supports local charitable organizations.
