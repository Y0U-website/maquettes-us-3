Q: Do you accept insurance?

A: YES!

We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO and Cigna.

All insurances are welcome, but please note that you are responsible for any uncovered portions.

For those without insurance, please ask about our in-house membership plan.

Q:Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?

A: YES!

We understand that dental emergencies are never convenient, so we set aside time each day for urgent visits. If you’re experiencing pain, swelling, or a broken tooth, call us right away and we’ll make every effort to see you the same day.

Q: Do you do Invisalign?

A: Yes!

Invisalign is a discreet and effective way to straighten teeth and correct bite issues. We’ll assess your specific needs during your consultation and let you know if it’s the right treatment for you.
