Our Doctors

Dr. Mic: Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Mic always knew he wanted to become a dentist, but he was inspired to be a pediatric dentist after shadowing Dr. Norm several years ago and has always looked to him as a mentor throughout his dental education, associateship, and practice ownership. Dr. Mic has been trained in oral and I.V. sedation, as well as treating patients under general anesthesia and those with special needs. He stays up to date on his knowledge by attending many meetings and seminars throughout the year to be able to provide the highest quality, most updated care possible for all of his patients!

He is a member of the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Society, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the fifth District Dental Society, and the Oneida-Herkimer Dental Society. Dr. Mic is also Board Certified with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Mic is on staff at MVHS and is also an attending dentist at Dr. James Rozanski Dental Health Center where he teaches aspects of pediatric dentistry to general dentistry residents.

Dr. Mic resides in New Hartford, NY with his two daughters and wife Cynthia, who is an orthodontist and a native of Whitesboro. They met while attending Le Moyne College, and went on to attend dental school together as well. When they aren’t talking about teeth at the dinner table, they enjoy spending time with family in the Adirondacks, cheering on the Syracuse Orange, and running the Boilermaker every year!

Dr. Norm: Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Cognetto (a.k.a. Dr. Norm) has lived in central New York all his life, growing up and attending elementary school in Utica, NY and graduating from New Hartford High School in 1983. He earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Utica College and in 1991, a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Norm then completed a two year residency program specializing in pediatric dentistry at Women and Children’s Hospital of Buffalo.

Wanting to create an environment where he and his patients would feel relaxed and comfortable, in 1993 Dr. Norm returned to his hometown and opened his practice on Genesee Street. A place where any fears children might have had would be left at the door when they arrived. His practice became known as Kidsteethonly. In 2021 he sold Kidsteethonly to Dr. Micaroni, his "hand-picked" associate, and continues to work there to provide extraordinary care to the community he’s always served.

Dr. Norm continues to enhance his knowledge and skills, attending multiple classes, seminars and meetings each year in order to continue to provide his patients with the greatest level of care available. Dr. Norm recently completed a comprehensive course from Tongue-Tie Academy, in order to provide laser frenectomy for infants and children with diagnosed tongue-ties and lip-ties.

Dr. Norm is Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, NYS Dental Society and our local Oneida Herkimer County Dental Society. He is currently the Chairman of the Children’s Dental Health Month Committee. Dr. Norm is on staff at MVHS and is a past chairman of the dental department. Dr. Norm is also an attending dentist at the Dr. James Rozanski Dental Health Center where he teaches aspects of pediatric dentistry to general dentistry residents.

Dr. Norm currently lives in New Hartford with his wife Valerie. He has two adult sons, Nicholas and Christian who live in Buffalo and New Hartford respectively. He also shares his home with his rescue cat Clarabelle who is the topic of many stories shared with his patients. Dr. Norm is an avid outdoor enthusiast and enjoys cycling, running, triathlons, mountain climbing, bow hunting, fly fishing, and woodworking.

Dr. Patel: Board Eligible Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Patel was born and raised in Texas and is excited to explore the beauty of upstate NY! He spent his childhood in the suburbs of Houston, TX and then went on to earn his bachelors’ degree at the University of Texas at Austin. For dental school, Dr. Patel attended the UTHealth School of Dentistry in Houston. He was heavily involved in craniofacial research, and he even published a research article in Birth Defects Journal in 2024!

For his pediatric dentistry training Dr. Patel decided he wanted to move to NYC and experience the “concrete jungle where dreams are made of.” He obtained his pediatric dentistry certificate at Interfaith Medical Center at One Brooklyn Health, where he was given the role of Chief Resident and received rigorous training in general anesthesia and treating children with special healthcare needs. During this time he became passionate about working with children in foster care. He is currently Board Eligible by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Patel first became interested in pediatric dentistry when he went on a medical/dental mission trip in Antigua, Guatemala. During this trip he thought the dentist had the most impactful role! Dr. Patel believes that effective communication and being detail oriented is crucial for success in pediatric dentistry. He takes pride in high quality work, and aims to treat every family with empathy, kindness, and respect!

Dr. Patel resides in New Hartford and would love any chance be involved in local school and community events. Outside of the office, Dr. Patel loves to explore nature, travel, and workout. On some weekends you may find him in NYC visiting his partner and exploring a new museum exhibit or restaurant! In the winter months he will probably escape to Texas to spend some quality time with his family! Please extend a warm welcome when you see him in the office!
