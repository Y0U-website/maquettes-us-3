Meet the doctor behind the practice

Dr. Thomas Martin

Born in Chicago and raised in Indiana, Dr. Thomas Martin’s journey into dentistry was inspired by the people closest to him, a family friend who coached him through his days as a competitive runner and his uncle, a dentist whose patient-first philosophy left a lasting impact. That blend of discipline and mentorship pointed him toward a career where science, problem-solving, and compassion come together every day.

Dr. Martin earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from Indiana University. He went on to receive his Master’s in Biology from IUPUI, before completing his dental education at the Indiana University School of Dentistry. Today, he combines his love for science with hands-on care, helping patients rediscover their confidence through healthy, beautiful smiles.

A firm believer in lifelong learning, Dr. Martin regularly takes continuing education courses and stays up-to-date on the latest treatment methods and technologies. He particularly enjoys implant dentistry and cosmetic procedures like no-prep veneers, treatments that require both technical precision and artistic vision. For him, great results begin with meaningful conversations and a deep understanding of his patients’ goals.

Inside the office, Dr. Martin is known for his easy-going nature and clear communication. He doesn’t just direct care, he sees his role as an advisor, helping patients feel informed and empowered every step of the way. Whether it’s been six months or sixteen years since your last dental visit, you’ll be welcomed with kindness, not judgment.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Martin enjoys spending time with his family, including his wife, daughter, and their four rescue pets. He’s an avid runner, a lover of science fiction, and a fan of complex video games that offer a challenge and fresh perspective. No matter the environment, Dr. Martin brings thoughtfulness, focus, and curiosity to everything he does.

Here’s what your first visit will look like

Comfortable, clear, and completely judgment-free

A new dental office can feel intimidating, but not here. From the moment you walk into Martin Dental Studio, you’ll be met with warmth and clarity. We take time to get to know you, understand your goals, and review your treatment options without pressure or sales tactics. Our goal is to make sure you feel seen, heard, and truly cared for.

Patient Experience At your first visit, you’ll meet with our friendly team, discuss your dental history, and receive a thorough exam and digital imaging as needed. Dr. Martin will walk you through your oral health step by step, using visual tools so you can see what he sees. You’ll leave with a clear understanding of your dental health and personalized recommendations, always tailored to your comfort level and goals.

What to Bring Don’t forget to bring the following:

New patient forms (submitted digitally or filled out prior to arrival)

Referral paperwork if visiting from the VA

Technology and comforts designed to elevate your experience

We believe you deserve the best, and that includes advanced tools and thoughtful touches that make going to the dentist easy, informative, and even enjoyable. From top-tier scanning technology to relaxing amenities, we’ve designed your entire visit around comfort and clarity.

CBCT Scanner

This advanced 3D imaging tool gives us a precise look at your teeth, jaw, and bone structure to guide implant placements, diagnose issues, and ensure accuracy at every stage of care.

iTero Scanner

Say goodbye to gooey impressions. This digital scanner lets us take accurate, highly detailed images of your teeth, instantly, which we use for Invisalign, cosmetic planning, and more.

Intraoral Cameras & Digital X-Rays

We use small, high-resolution cameras and modern digital X-rays to show you exactly what’s going on with your teeth and gums in real time, perfect for curious minds and confident decision making.

Cozy amenities

We want your visit to feel more like self-care than a chore. That’s why we offer cozy blankets, pillows, noise-canceling headphones, lip moisturizer, and even a tooth pillow, all designed to help you relax and feel at ease.

Giving back to those who need it most

Community outreach that creates real impact

We believe everyone deserves quality dental care, especially those who are rebuilding their lives. That’s why Martin Dental Studio proudly partners with Cross Bridge, a local organization supporting people in recovery. Dr. Martin has provided free or significantly discounted care to dozens of individuals, helping them smile again with confidence and dignity.
