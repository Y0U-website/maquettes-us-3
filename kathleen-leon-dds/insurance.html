We Accept Medi-Cal/Denti-Cal for Children

Our primary focus is on pediatric dental care, and we proudly welcome children ages 2–12. Every child deserves a healthy smile, and we’re here to support families every step of the way.

To make care more accessible, we accept Medi-Cal/Denti-Cal for eligible children in the 2–12 age group. If your child is enrolled in this program, he or she is welcome here—and we’ll work with you to ensure your child receives the dental care needed in a warm and comforting environment.

Please note: At this time, we are not currently accepting new adult Medicaid patients. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and encourage you to contact us if you have any questions about your options.

Out-of-Network Care

Even if your insurance is not in-network, we offer out-of-network care with the same high-quality services. We will work with you to manage the costs and make sure you receive the treatment you need without the added financial stress.

Premium Insurance Options

No Insurance? No Problem!
