Get to know

Dr. Dallas

Dr. Dallas Ocrainiciuc is a graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry and completed a General Practice Residency at Cleveland Clinic Mercy Hospital in Canton, Ohio. Through her residency, she expanded her dental knowledge and provided advanced care in both clinical and hospital-based settings. She earned her undergraduate degree in Biochemistry from Marietta College before attending dental school. Her path into dentistry was shaped by a desire to build a career that was both deeply fulfilling and impactful, while allowing her to care for others and lead with intention.

Born and raised in Belmont, Ohio, Dr. Dallas grew up on a farm in a close-knit family with three brothers. Raised in a rodeo family, she learned the value of hard work, discipline, and community early in life, and those values continue to shape how she cares for patients today. Dr. Dallas has a passion for treating patients of all ages and providing exceptional care to those in need. She prides herself on creating a caring experience where patients can receive comprehensive treatment delivered with compassion and a genuine understanding of their wants and needs.

She is especially passionate about the oral-systemic connection and enjoys helping patients understand how oral health can affect overall health. Her approach is rooted in prevention, long-term wellness, and patient education so individuals can feel informed and confident in their care. Outside the office, Dr. Dallas enjoys spending time with family, staying active, and investing in personal and professional growth. Fun fact: she is a former trick rider and grew up doing acrobatic stunts on horseback.

Get to know

Dr. O

Dr. Mihail Ocrainiciuc is a graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. Prior to dental school, he earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Walsh University, majoring in Biology with a minor in Chemistry. After receiving his dental degree, Dr. O completed a General Practice Residency at Summa Health in Akron, Ohio, where he received advanced training in both clinical and hospital-based settings while collaborating with a diverse team of dental specialists.

Originally from Romania, Dr. O immigrated to the United States with his family at the age of five and grew up in North Canton, Ohio. He enjoys building lasting relationships with patients and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized, high-quality dental care.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. O regularly pursues continuing education to stay at the forefront of evidence-based dentistry. He has a particular passion for implant dentistry and is actively continuing his advanced implant education with the goal of becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. O enjoys exercising, playing golf, exploring specialty coffee, listening to music, and appreciating automobiles. Most importantly, he enjoys spending time with his children and family. A fun fact about Dr. O is that he is a former competitive chess player who earned numerous tournament trophies throughout his career.

Modern tech and thoughtful comforts

At Hometown Family Dental, we combine modern technology with patient-focused comforts to make visits more efficient, informative, and comfortable. Our tools help support clear diagnosis and treatment planning, while our amenities are designed to help patients feel more relaxed throughout their time with us. It is all part of creating a dental experience that feels elevated, welcoming, and centered around your needs.

CBCT

Our CBCT technology allows for detailed 3D imaging that supports more precise evaluation and treatment planning. It is especially valuable for advanced diagnostics and helps us better understand what may not be visible during a standard visual exam.

3D printer

Our in-office 3D printer supports a more advanced and efficient workflow. This technology helps us plan and deliver treatment with greater precision while supporting a modern patient experience.

Digital intraoral scanner

A digital intraoral scanner allows us to capture detailed images of your teeth and mouth without traditional impressions in many cases. This technology supports comfort, accuracy, and a more streamlined process for treatment planning.

Intraoral cameras

Intraoral cameras help patients see what we see during the exam. This makes it easier to understand your diagnosis, follow our recommendations, and feel more informed about your dental health.

TVs

TVs help create a more comfortable and relaxed environment during your visit. They are one of the many touches we use to make time in the chair feel easier and more enjoyable.

Beverage station

Our beverage station is part of the welcoming atmosphere we want every patient to experience. It is a simple comfort that helps make your visit feel more personal and inviting.

Headphones & Blankets

Headphones and blankets are available to help patients feel more relaxed, cozy, and comfortable during their appointments. These small touches add an extra layer of calm in the chair and reflect our commitment to making every visit feel thoughtful and supportive.

Neck Pillows & Scent Diffuser

Neck pillows and a gentle scent diffuser help create a more comfortable, calming experience from the moment patients arrive. With added support during treatment and a warm, less clinical atmosphere throughout the office, these thoughtful touches help every visit feel a little more relaxed.
