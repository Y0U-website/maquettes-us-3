Dr. Matthew Agnini

Dentist In Leesburg, FL | Focusing On Implants

Dr. Matthew Agnini, known as Dr. Matt to his patients, is the founder and owner of Greater Florida Dental Implants Leesburg. He graduated from the University of Maryland Dental School in 2002 and completed a General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Baltimore in 2003. This residency provided Dr. Matt with extensive training in both the surgical and restorative phases of dental implants.

In 2004, Dr. Matt joined his father and later his brother to form Agnini Family Dental in Lakeland, Florida, where he practiced for 13 years. In 2018, Dr. Matt relocated to Leesburg, Florida, where he specialized in extractions, dentures, and dental implants, focusing on full-mouth surgery and restoration with implants until 2024.

Dr. Matt earned a Fellowship in the Academy for Dental-Facial Esthetics in 2014 and has since received multiple awards for his work in full-mouth restoration with dental implants, commonly known as All on X.

Currently, Dr. Matt offers a range of services, including All on X, extractions, single-tooth implants, and denture services such as extractions with immediate dentures and snap-in dentures. Additionally, he has been a licensed sedation dentist in Florida since 2007, providing sedation for surgical dental procedures.
