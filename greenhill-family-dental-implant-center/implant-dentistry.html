Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants have become a preferred tooth replacement option for many patients because they provide several important advantages.

Natural-Looking Results

Implant restorations are customized to complement your surrounding teeth, helping create a natural-looking smile.

Stable Chewing Function

Because implants are supported by the jawbone, they provide excellent stability when eating.

Preserve Neighboring Teeth

A single dental implant can replace a missing tooth without requiring healthy neighboring teeth to be prepared solely to support a traditional bridge.

Support Jawbone Health

Natural tooth roots stimulate the jawbone. When a tooth is lost, the bone in that area can gradually shrink. Dental implants help provide stimulation to the surrounding bone.

Long-Term Tooth Replacement

With appropriate care and maintenance, dental implants can provide a durable foundation for replacement teeth.

Dental Implant Options

Implant dentistry isn’t limited to replacing a single tooth.

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

When one tooth is missing, an individual implant and custom crown can recreate the missing tooth without relying on adjacent teeth for support.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When several neighboring teeth are missing, strategically placed implants may support multiple replacement teeth.

This can eliminate the need to place an implant for every missing tooth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Dental implants can provide additional support for certain removable dentures, helping improve stability when eating and speaking.

Full-Arch Implant Restorations

Patients missing most or all of the teeth in an arch may be candidates for a full-arch implant-supported restoration.

A carefully planned number of implants can support an entire set of replacement teeth, providing greater stability than traditional removable dentures for appropriate candidates.

What Is the Dental Implant Process?

Dental implant treatment usually takes place in stages rather than during a single appointment.

Consultation & Treatment Planning

We’ll evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and missing tooth area. Diagnostic imaging may be used to assess the supporting structures and help determine your treatment options.

Implant Placement

The dental implant is surgically positioned within the jawbone.

When appropriate, Greenhill Family Dentistry may coordinate this portion of treatment with a trusted oral surgeon, periodontist, or other dental specialist.

Healing & Integration

The implant is given time to integrate with the surrounding jawbone. This process, known as osseointegration , creates the stable foundation needed to support the final restoration.

Creating Your Implant Restoration

Once the implant is ready for restoration, detailed records are used to design your implant crown, bridge, or other replacement teeth.

Final Restoration

Your completed restoration is attached to the implant and carefully evaluated for appearance, fit, and comfortable function.

The exact treatment timeline varies depending on your individual circumstances.

Dental Implants vs. Dental Bridges

For a single missing tooth, patients frequently compare a dental implant with a traditional bridge.

A dental implant replaces the missing tooth independently and generally does not require neighboring teeth to be prepared.
