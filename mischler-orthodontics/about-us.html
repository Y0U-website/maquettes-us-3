Meet the Team at Mischler Orthodontics

The Mischler Orthodontics Experience

When you re a patient at Mischler Orthodontics, you ll experience a warm and vibrant energy and a genuine sense that everyone here is happy to see you. That s not by accident; it s the practice culture we ve built over four decades.

Dr. William Mischler and Dr. Alec McDonald lead a team of professionals who ve been together for years. Some of our staff members have been here for over a decade. You’ll notice a familial quality where people know and genuinely look out for each other.

Small-Town Warmth, Big-City Sophistication

Our team values continuous learning, supporting one another, and creating an environment where quality and kindness come together.

Dr. Alec McDonald

Dr. McDonald is proud to continue the tradition of exceptional orthodontic care at Mischler Orthodontics. After joining the practice as an associate orthodontist in July 2025, he worked alongside Dr. Mischler to provide personalized, evidence-based care before assuming ownership of the practice in July 2026. This seamless transition reflects a shared commitment to the same clinical philosophy, patient experience, and high standard of treatment that patients and families have trusted for decades.

Originally from Indianapolis, Indiana, Dr. McDonald earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Indiana University Purdue University Indianapolis before attending the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. There, he completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD), Master of Science in Oral Biology (MSOB), orthodontic residency, and Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD).

During dental school and residency, Dr. McDonald developed a passion for orthodontic research. His research in orthodontic materials has been recognized with research awards, published in peer-reviewed literature, and presented at regional, national, and international scientific meetings. He received the American Association of Orthodontists Award for Exemplary Aptitude and Achievement in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, as well as the Award for Excellence in Oral Immunology and Infectious Diseases Research. Dr. McDonald is a proud member of the American Association of Orthodontists.

Outside the office, Dr. McDonald stays active through weightlifting, volleyball, and golf. His wife, also a dentist, is from Colorado and has been teaching him to ski, though he admits it involves plenty of falling!

A devoted Colts, Pacers, and University of Louisville fan, he loves attending sporting events and exploring new restaurants with friends. A former bodybuilding competitor, health and wellness remain central to his lifestyle. Fun fact: He s determined to audition for Big Brother until he makes it on the show!

Dr. McDonald is excited to get to know you and your family as he helps create the confident and healthy smiles you deserve.

Dr. William Mischler

Dr. Mischler completed all his education at the University of Louisville, earning his Bachelor of Science in Dental Science, his Doctor of Dental Medicine, and his orthodontic specialty certification. He began practicing orthodontics in 1981 and founded Mischler Orthodontics in 1988. After more than 45 years of caring for patients and building one of Southern Indiana s most trusted orthodontic practices, Dr. Mischler is beginning his retirement journey while continuing to work alongside Dr. Alec McDonald to ensure a seamless transition for patients and families.

Dr. Mischler s path to orthodontics began in sixth grade when he wrote a paper about his future career goals he wanted to be an orthodontist. That early inspiration, likely sparked by meeting his older sister s orthodontist, became his lifelong calling. His passion for creating beautiful smiles and working with great people has sustained him throughout his remarkable career.

Outside the office, Dr. Mischler enjoys boating, cycling, and tending to his yard. He s an avid fan of high school and college sporting events and loves tinkering with old cars. For over 20 years, he and his wife brought joy to their community by portraying Mr. and Mrs. Claus during the holiday season.

Dr. Mischler and his team look forward to serving your family with the same care and dedication that has defined our practice for over three decades.
