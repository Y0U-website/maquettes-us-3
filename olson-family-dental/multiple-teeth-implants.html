What Are Multiple Teeth Implants?

Implant-Supported Tooth Replacement in Minot

Multiple teeth implants replace several missing teeth using strategically placed dental implants that support custom restorations. Depending on the number and location of missing teeth, implants may support bridges or other fixed restorations. Because implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, they provide exceptional stability while helping preserve surrounding bone and oral structures.

Questions Minot Patients Ask About Multiple Teeth Implants

How many implants are needed to replace multiple teeth?

The number of implants depends on how many teeth are missing and the type of restoration being planned. Dr. Olson develops a customized treatment plan based on each patient’s needs.

Are multiple teeth implants better than removable partial dentures?

Many patients prefer implant-supported restorations because they offer greater stability, improved chewing ability, and help preserve jawbone health.

How long do multiple teeth implants last?

With proper care and routine dental visits, dental implants can last for decades and often for a lifetime.

Do implant-supported restorations look natural?

Yes. Restorations are custom-designed to match surrounding teeth and create a natural appearance.

Am I a candidate for multiple teeth implants?

Many adults with missing teeth are candidates for implant treatment. Dr. Olson evaluates bone levels, oral health, and medical history to determine the most appropriate treatment options.
