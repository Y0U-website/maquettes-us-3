A board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon, Dr. Sonia Bennett is known for clinical precision and for friendly, personable care.

Dr. Bennett obtained her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Indiana University School of Dentistry, then completed her surgical training in Oral Maxillofacial Surgery at Highland Hospital/University of the Pacific in the San Francisco Bay area.

She is thrilled to be back in the Chesterton, IN area, delivering a positive experience to patients throughout the community. Dr. Bennett provides patients with excellence in such procedures as dental implants, wisdom teeth removal, and bone grafting, using advanced technology to enhance safety and outcomes.

As you seek oral surgery solutions in Chesterton, IN, consider Glacial Sands OMS. Our practice performs a robust list of surgical interventions, including dental implants, All-on-4®, and other advanced methods of dental restoration. Additionally, we have a track record of safe and expedient wisdom teeth removals, and can provide surgical intervention to help with TMJ and related disorders.

Our oral surgery procedures include treatments for patients of all ages. Our pediatric services are marked by a commitment to keeping our youngest patients comfortable and safe at all times. We also offer reconstructive options, including corrective jaw surgery for those who have been injured in accidents.

We are proud to welcome patients to our world-class surgical facility, where we use standard-setting technology to create a smooth, seamless experience.

At Glacial Sands OMS, we take pride in offering custom treatments to meet each patient’s needs. We are pleased to offer both oral surgery and aesthetic services.
