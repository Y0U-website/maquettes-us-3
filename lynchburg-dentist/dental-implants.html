The technology: 3D planning before anyone picks up an instrument

The process, step by step

  • 1. Consultation and 3D scan. We examine your mouth, take the CBCT scan, and tell you plainly whether an implant is right, and what it will cost.
  • 2. Planning. Your doctor maps the implant position on your 3D scan. If bone grafting is needed, it gets built into the plan now, not discovered mid-surgery.
  • 3. Placement. The titanium post is placed under local anesthetic, usually in 60 to 90 minutes. Most patients are back to normal routines the next day.
  • 4. Healing. Over 3 to 6 months the bone fuses to the implant. This stage is where lasting implants are made, and we don&#x27;t rush it.
  • 5. Restoration. The same doctor who placed your implant attaches the abutment and your custom porcelain tooth, checks the bite, and you&#x27;re done.

Every way we replace missing teeth

  • Single tooth implant , one post, one crown, no touching the neighbors.
  • Multiple tooth implants , implant-supported bridges that replace several teeth without a removable partial.
  • Full-arch implants (All-on-X) , a complete upper or lower arch on four to six implants, staged for results that last.
  • Implant-supported dentures , snap-in stability for denture wearers tired of adhesive.
  • Bone grafting , rebuilding the foundation when bone has been lost.

Am I a candidate?

If you&#x27;re an adult missing one or more teeth, the honest answer is: probably, and the 3D scan will tell us for sure. Healthy gums and adequate bone are the two main requirements. Long-standing gaps often mean some bone loss, which grafting can rebuild. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and certain medications call for extra planning rather than an automatic no. Dr. Azevedo&#x27;s hospital-based training in treating medically complex patients means a health history that scares other offices usually doesn&#x27;t scare us. The consultation is where you get a straight answer, not a sales pitch.
