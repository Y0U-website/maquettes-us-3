Dr. Fialkoff is dedicated to a collaborative approach to your dental health. He believes in empowering patients through clear explanations of their conditions and a comprehensive discussion of all available treatment options, including their pros and cons.

He encourages you to actively participate in the decision-making process to create a restorative plan that aligns with your expectations, schedule, and lifestyle. From moving teeth to replacing missing ones with high-quality crowns, bridges, or implants utilizing advanced 3-D imaging, Dr. Fialkoff offers exceptional care across a wide range of dental needs.

His extensive training, coupled with his commitment to using the finest materials and labs, ensures high-quality results.

Dr. Fialkoff simultaneously completed his DMD from Rutgers University and a Masters in Healthcare Management from Columbia University

Outside of dentistry, he enjoys rock climbing, reading, and spending time with his wife, kids, and pets.
