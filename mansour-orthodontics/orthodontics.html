Treatments We Offer

Thanks to technology and industry progress, metal braces are no longer the only corrective option available. With new breakthrough treatments like Invisalign® , Empower® braces , and ceramic braces , straightening your teeth has never looked so good.

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

Invisalign® involves a series of clear plastic aligners that straighten teeth without the noticeable bulky materials associated with traditional braces. No one will know you’re improving your smile unless you tell them!

Braces

Empower® is a series of self-ligating braces from American Orthodontics that utilize lighter wires and lower friction brackets to move teeth, resulting in faster treatment times and greater overall comfort.

Adult Orthodontic Treatment

We offer a variety of adult orthodontic treatments designed for different age groups. Through the use of modern technology, we are able to reduce patient discomfort levels, frequency of visits, and treatment length. Book a consultation today!

Pediatric Orthodontic Treatment

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that orthodontic evaluation occur at the first sign of issues or no later than age 7. At this age, pediatric orthodontic treatment may not be necessary, but vigilant exams can anticipate and prevent complications.

iTero® Digital Scanner

The iTero® scanner captures a digital 3D impression of the teeth and gums. It is able to deliver clear and precise imagery without the discomfort of conventional impressions. This process allows us to evaluate your condition and which orthodontic appliances may work for you.

Corrective Jaw Surgery

Orthognathic or corrective jaw surgery treats and corrects abnormalities of the facial bones, specifically the jaws and the teeth. These abnormalities often cause difficulty associated with chewing, talking, sleeping, and other routine activities.
