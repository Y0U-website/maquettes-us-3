Implant Crowns in Nashville

| Dental Services in Nashville | Implant Crowns in Nashville

Missing a tooth changes how you eat, talk, and even smile. An implant crown brings back what’s gone, locking into place without the hassle of adhesives or shifting parts. Once it’s set, you’ll chew, speak, and smile with the same confidence you had before the gap appeared. This is something our Nashville patients appreciate every day.

The implant itself sits deep in your jaw, acting as a solid anchor. With the crown attached, you won’t notice any movement. It doesn’t lean on your gums or neighboring teeth for support. Instead, it stands on its own, handling daily pressure without slipping or shifting.

How We Secure Your Implant Crown

Getting a crown onto your implant isn’t a quick fix. Healing comes first, and only after the implant is fully settled do we move forward. This isn’t about speed. It’s about making sure every step lines up for a result that lasts.

Every phase matters. Rushing or skipping steps leads to crowns that don’t fit or last. We take the time to get it right, so you avoid extra visits and enjoy a crown that feels natural from day one.

  • Healing Check: We make sure the implant has fused with your bone before anything else happens.
  • Abutment Placement: A connector attaches to the implant, ready to hold the crown steady.
  • Impressions and Measurements: Detailed molds capture your bite and tooth shape for a seamless fit.
  • Custom Fabrication: The crown is crafted in a lab to match your teeth in color and contour.
  • Final Placement: We secure the crown, check your bite, and confirm comfort before you leave.

Choosing Materials That Last and Look Right

When it comes to implant crowns, durability and appearance go hand in hand. You want a crown that blends in and stands up to daily use. We match the material to your needs, focusing on both strength and a natural look for Nashville smiles.

Front teeth need to look seamless, while back teeth need to take on more force. We help you choose the right material for the job, so your crown holds up and looks right in every situation.

  • Porcelain: Delivers a lifelike appearance, matching the color and shine of real enamel.
  • Zirconia: Handles heavy chewing without chipping, making it ideal for back teeth.
  • Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal: Combines a strong metal base with a porcelain surface for a natural finish.

From Start to Finish, Here’s the Timeline

Healing sets the pace for the entire process. After the implant goes in, your bone needs time to bond with it. That phase can stretch over several months, depending on how your body responds. Once the implant is stable, the rest moves quickly. Impressions, lab work, and final placement usually wrap up in a few weeks.

Trying to rush the healing only invites trouble. We stick to a schedule that gives your implant the best chance to succeed. Most patients see the full process, from implant to finished crown, take three to six months. The wait pays off with a crown that feels and functions like a real tooth, especially for those in the Nashville area.

Protecting Your Implant Crown for the Long Haul

Even though implant crowns don’t decay, the surrounding gum tissue still needs attention. Bacteria can build up, leading to infection if you let your routine slide. Daily care keeps everything healthy and extends the life of your crown.

Stick to these habits, and your crown can last well over a decade. Neglect leads to gum trouble or crown damage, but a steady routine keeps everything in shape for Nashville patients and beyond.

  • Brush Twice a Day: Clean around the crown and gumline with a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss Daily: Remove food and plaque between the crown and neighboring teeth.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: Skip ice and hard candy to prevent cracks or chips.
  • Schedule Cleanings: Regular visits let us check the crown and keep the area spotless.
  • Wear a Night Guard if Needed: Protects the crown if you grind your teeth at night.

Why Our Implant Crowns Stay Reliable

Unlike bridges or dentures , implant crowns don’t depend on other teeth or adhesives. Once in place, they work like the tooth you lost. They are steady, comfortable, and built for daily life. We’ve restored smiles for people who lost teeth to injury, decay, or failed dental work, and the results hold up year after year.

Every crown we place is made to fit your bite and appearance. You get a solution that blends in and stands up to real use, not just a quick fix. We guide you through each step, making sure you know what’s happening and why, so you’re never left guessing.
