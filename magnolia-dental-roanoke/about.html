About

Family dentistry, the way it should feel.

Magnolia Dental Roanoke is a warm, welcoming dental practice on Airport Road NW — built around Dr. Amanda Saado’s belief that great dentistry starts with listening, and that every person who walks in the door deserves care delivered with real heart.

Our story

We chose to build Magnolia Dental Roanoke on Airport Road NW because this corner of north Roanoke is one of the most genuinely diverse, hardworking, and underserved communities in western Virginia. The Williamson Road corridor and the Airport neighborhood are home to families from every background — and every family deserves a dental home where they feel welcome the moment they walk in.

Dr. Amanda Saado leads the practice with a simple promise: clinical excellence, patient comfort, and personalized care — rooted in expertise and delivered with heart. We don’t just treat teeth; we care for the people behind every smile. That starts with how you’re greeted at the front desk and runs all the way through how a treatment plan is explained, scheduled, and paid for.

We’re open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm — five full days a week, because we know shift workers, service-industry folks, parents, and retirees all keep different schedules. And when insurance gets in the way of care, our in-house membership plan steps in. No insurance, no problem — that’s not just a tagline; it’s how the practice is actually built.
