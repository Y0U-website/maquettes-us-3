Stages of Surgical Orthodontics at Our Leander Practice

The process of surgical orthodontics requires multiple appointments at our Leander, TX, practice, since it is a complex treatment. Our team of orthodontic experts will guide you through the whole procedure, ensuring your comfort and satisfaction at every step. It has the following stages:

  • Consultation: First, we will perform an orthodontic examination to determine the severity of your issues. We will also record digital scans of your oral cavity to study your case and form a personalized treatment plan.
  • Pre-surgical orthodontic treatment: Prior to the surgery, you will wear braces to align your teeth and prepare them for the final jaw position. This phase may take 12 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of your needs.
  • Orthognathic surgery: The surgery takes place under anesthesia. We will make cuts in the jaw bones and move them into the desired position. The bones are then secured with plates, screws, or wires. The surgery may last for 2 to 3 hours.
  • Post-surgical orthodontic treatment: After the surgery, you will need to rest for a few weeks. You may experience minor pain, swelling, or bruising, which will gradually subside. You will continue wearing braces for a few more months to achieve the desired results.

How Long Does It Take To Recover After Orthognathic Surgery?

The recovery time after jaw surgery varies depending on the type and extent of the surgery, as well as your individual healing process. The initial healing period may require 6 to 7 weeks, but complete recovery can take up to 3 months. You may need to take two weeks off from work or school after your surgery and follow a soft diet. Avoid strenuous activities during this period and rest well to achieve better healing. We will schedule follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and advise you on the best practices for a smooth recovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good candidate for surgical orthodontics?

We typically recommend this option when jaw problems run too deep for braces alone to fix, such as a protruding jaw, receding chin, or difficulty speaking, eating, or swallowing.

Is there an age requirement for jaw surgery?

Your jaw must be fully developed before jaw surgery can be performed. For most girls, this is around age 16 or older, while for boys, it is typically 18 or older.

Will I need braces before my jaw surgery?

You will. Most patients spend 12 to 18 months in pre-surgical braces before they’re ready.

How long does the actual surgery take?

Two to three hours under anesthesia, depending on how much correction your jaw needs.

How long is the recovery after jaw surgery?

The first 6 to 7 weeks are the hardest part. Some patients aren’t fully healed for up to 3 months.

Will I still need braces after surgery?

A few more months of braces usually follow surgery, fine-tuning your bite once the bones have set.

What can I expect right after surgery?

You may experience minor pain, swelling, or bruising, which gradually subsides. Most patients take about two weeks off from work or school and follow a soft diet during the initial recovery.
