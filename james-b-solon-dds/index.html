The practice

Dr. James Solon has been providing exceptional dental care since 1980, bringing decades of expertise, precision, and a gentle touch to every patient he treats. He earned his undergraduate degree in Chemistry from Miami University of Ohio before continuing his education at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry.

Committed to offering the highest standard of care, Dr. Solon recently upgraded his practice in Willoughby, Ohio, expanding both the space and services available to ensure patients receive the best possible experience. Known for his meticulous approach and compassionate demeanor, he takes pride in helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles in a comfortable and welcoming environment.
