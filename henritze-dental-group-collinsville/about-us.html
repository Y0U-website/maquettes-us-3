The History of HDG

Meet Dr. Andrew Henritze, a native of Roanoke, Virginia, and a distinguished graduate from Virginia Tech and the Medical College of Virginia at Virginia Commonwealth University.

What began as a single practice in 1997 evolved into a remarkable story of growth and success. In 2017, recognizing the need for change, Dr. Henritze envisioned expanding beyond a traditional dental practice. Collaborating with colleagues, the vision for Henritze Dental Group (HDG) took shape. With dedication and perseverance, HDG transitioned from a single location in 2018 to an impressive network of twelve locations in Southwest Virginia within a few years.

Dr. Henritze’s passion for providing exceptional dental care fueled HDG’s growth, offering comprehensive services and elevating the patient experience. The commitment to serving the community remains unwavering, and Dr. Henritze takes immense pride in HDG’s success, humbled by the opportunity to serve Southwest Virginia. As HDG continues to expand, Dr. Henritze and the incredible doctors of Henritze Dental Group, remain dedicated to delivering outstanding dental care and fostering a legacy of excellence in oral health throughout the region.

State of the Art Technology

From advanced imaging systems for precise diagnostics to state-of-the-art treatment tools, we employ innovative technology to ensure unparalleled precision, comfort, and exceptional results in every aspect of your dental care. Discover how our modern technological solutions redefine dental excellence, providing you with the finest in oral health and personalized care.

Our Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) services offer detailed 3D imaging, enabling precise treatment planning for superior dental care. Experience the future of dental imaging for accurate diagnoses and personalized treatment, ensuring optimal outcomes and your utmost comfort.

Revolutionizing traditional impressions, this cutting-edge technology offers a comfortable, efficient, and precise digital solution for capturing detailed 3D images of your teeth and gums, essential for crafting personalized Invisalign treatment plans. Say goodbye to messy molds and hello to a seamless, accurate dental experience with our advanced iTero scanner.

Our laser dentistry services harness the power of advanced laser systems, ensuring minimally invasive procedures, faster healing times, and precise treatment for a range of dental needs. Experience the gentle touch and precision of lasers, offering a comfortable, efficient, and innovative approach to achieving optimal oral health. Discover the transformative potential of laser dentistry for a brighter, healthier smile.
