Insurance & Payment Options

Dental Insurance Coverage

Cielinski Family Dental accepts most dental insurances and we are a member of Delta Dental Premier. This means that fees are pre-determined, and every patient gets charged the same amount for each procedure regardless of their insurance carrier.

If you are unsure of your coverage, our staff can assist you in verifying your coverage limits. In addition, we will help you by handling all claim submissions. We will estimate your co-payments as accurately as possible based on your insurance company’s information.

We request payment at the time of service. We will kindly consider making payment arrangements only before service completion. We recommend contacting us ahead of time if you have questions about payment.

Payment Options

We accept cash, personal checks, and credit cards. We also offer extended payment plans through CareCredit . CareCredit allows interest-free financing upon credit approval. You may also make payments online.

Questions? Call Us!

Unsure about how to pay for your treatments? Feel free to contact us and we’ll be happy to walk you through the process. We can work with you to figure out your options for your situation to make sure you can get the care you need.
