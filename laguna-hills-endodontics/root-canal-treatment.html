When root canal treatment is needed

Severe Tooth Pain

Persistent, intense pain inside a tooth often signals pulp inflammation that requires professional root canal treatment.

Tooth Discoloration

A tooth that darkens or changes color may indicate dead pulp tissue that needs professional removal.

Prolonged Sensitivity

Sensitivity to temperature that lingers long after the stimulus is removed may indicate pulp damage requiring treatment.

Abscess or Swelling

A pimple-like bump on your gums or facial swelling can indicate a tooth with infection needing treatment.
