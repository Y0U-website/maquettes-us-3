Financing & Insurance

Affordability and accessibility are important to us here at Imagine Dental. That’s why we offer several financing options for patients including Cherry, CareCredit , and in-house membership plans. Whatever you choose, we can assist in the sign up process to make sure any instance involving payment is buttery smooth.

We also want patients to be aware that we are out of network with most major insurance companies although we will gladly file your claim on your behalf and still offer competitive prices for our procedures. This decision allows us to freely treat patients without the constraints imposed by insurance plans such as frequency limits and procedure restrictions. Our staff would be happy to discuss your specific situation with you and determine the best possible course of action.
