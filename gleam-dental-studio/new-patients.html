TEETH CLEANING

Along with daily brushing and flossing, it’s recommended that every 6 months you receive a professional cleaning to prevent tooth decay and damage. Clean teeth are crucial for overall oral health.

ORAL EXAM

Dr. Korkus and his team are very knowledgeable and understand the complexities of dentistry. Oral exams are essential for discovering any issues that may or may not cause pain or discomfort.

X-RAYS

Tooth positioning and growth can sometimes cause oral discomfort or pain. X-rays are the best way for our team to spot any abnormalities or issues that could be problematic.
