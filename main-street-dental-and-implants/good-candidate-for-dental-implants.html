The Importance of Oral Hygiene Post-Implant Surgery

When it comes to implant dentistry, the journey doesn’t end with the procedure. A significant part lies in maintaining oral hygiene after receiving your dental implants. Like natural teeth, they need care and attention.

Good oral hygiene is essential for the long-term success and maintenance of dental implants.

Maintaining Your Dental Implants: It’s More Than Just Brushing

Post-surgery care involves more than just brushing twice daily. While brushing is important, flossing is often overlooked but plays a crucial role in keeping harmful bacteria at bay.

  • Use soft-bristle toothbrushes specifically designed for dental implants to clean your implants daily.
  • Regularly use specialized flossers to prevent plaque buildup between your artificial teeth.
  • Visit your dentist for routine check-ups every six months or as recommended.
