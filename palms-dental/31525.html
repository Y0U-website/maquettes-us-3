What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work?

The Anatomy of a Dental Implant

A dental implant is a replacement tooth root inserted in the jaw and consists of three components: the implant post (fixture), the abutment, and the prosthetic tooth or teeth.

The implant post is titanium that naturally integrates with your jawbone, acting as natural anchors for your new teeth. The fixture sits below the gumline, hidden from view, and must be placed through a surgical procedure.

The fixture is made of titanium, a strong, biocompatible material clinically proven to provide long-lasting results. The abutment connects the new tooth to the post, and the prosthetic can be a dental crown, bridge, or denture.

The Dental Implant Procedure and Timeline

It’s important to realize that every dental patient is different, so the following timeline for dental implants can vary. With that being said, here is roughly what you can expect from traditional dental implants:

Consultation Your initial consultation will include a complete oral examination. This will take place during one trip to the dentist.

Pre-treatment surgeries If your dentist determines that you need a bone graft or tooth extractions before you get dental implants, this process can take three to twelve months, depending on your dental condition.

Implant placement Your dentist will place your implants directly into your jaw approximately one to two weeks after your consultation if you don’t require pre-treatment surgeries.

Implant healing Once your implants are in place, it will take three to six months or longer for your jawbone and the implant to bond. During this time, your prosthetic restorations will be created.

Abutment placement Approximately one to two weeks before your last appointment, your dentist will place abutments on your implants. These abutments will serve to connect your implants to your new teeth.

Final implant restoration At your last appointment, we will complete your implant procedure by adding a crown or other prosthetic teeth and giving you the smile you’ve always wanted.
