The practice

Dr. Elliot Kim leads our team with over a decade of dental expertise and a commitment to patient-centered care. Known for his friendly demeanor and deep understanding of modern dentistry, Dr. Kim ensures every patient receives personalized treatment tailored to their unique needs. His dedication to excellence and continuous learning makes him a trusted choice for your oral health.

Dr. Elliot Kim has dedicated his career to providing comprehensive dental care that prioritizes patient comfort and satisfaction. With a focus on continuous learning, he has adopted the latest advancements in dental technology, including CBCT 3D imaging, to offer precise and effective treatments.

Dr. Kim’s approachable and compassionate nature puts patients at ease as soon as they walk through the door. He believes in the importance of educating patients about their dental health to empower them with knowledge and confidence. From routine care to complex procedures, Dr. Kim’s expert touch and careful attention to detail ensure you and your family are in excellent hands at Ohana Dental.

We understand visiting the dentist can be overwhelming, so we’re here to guide you every step of the way. From your first appointment, our team will take the time to listen, answer questions, and explain procedures clearly. Expect a welcoming atmosphere with personalized care from start to finish.

Ohana Dental offers a wide range of dental services, including preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency care. Our specialties include CBCT 3D scanning and restorative treatments designed for precision and efficiency. With our expertise and modern approach, you can expect exceptional results tailored to your needs.

At Ohana Dental, we believe modern technology enhances patient care. Our advanced tools, like CBCT 3D scanning and digital X-rays, allow for more accurate diagnoses and treatments. These innovations ensure your experience is as seamless and effective as possible. Learn more about our Digital Dentistry practice.
