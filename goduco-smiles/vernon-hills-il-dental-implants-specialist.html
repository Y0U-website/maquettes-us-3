Is All-on-X Right for You?

Are Missing Most or All of Your Teeth All-on-X is specifically designed to replace an entire arch of teeth with a fixed, implant-supported restoration making it the gold standard solution for patients with extensive tooth loss.

Are Unhappy With Your Current Dentures If you’re struggling with loose, uncomfortable, or ill-fitting dentures that limit what you can eat or make you feel self-conscious, All-on-X offers a permanent, fixed alternative that eliminates those frustrations entirely.

Have Experienced Bone Loss One of the most significant advantages of All-on-X over traditional implants is its ability to work with reduced bone volume. By angling the posterior implants strategically, we can often place All-on-X without the need for bone grafting even in patients who have been told they don’t have enough bone for implants.

Want a Permanent, Low-Maintenance Solution All-on-X restorations are cleaned just like natural teeth brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups. No adhesives, no soaking overnight, no removal. For patients who want a permanent, hassle-free solution, it’s unmatched.

How All-on-X Works

Step 1: Comprehensive Consultation & Imaging Your journey begins with a thorough evaluation that includes 3D cone beam CT scanning, digital impressions, and a full assessment of your oral health, bone density, and facial structure. This allows us to plan your implant placement with pinpoint accuracy before a single incision is made.

Step 2: Custom Treatment Planning Using advanced digital planning software, we map out the precise angle, depth, and position of each implant to maximize contact with available bone even in patients who have experienced significant bone loss. This is what makes All-on-X possible for patients who were previously told they weren’t implant candidates.

Step 3: Implant Placement On the day of surgery, the dental implants are placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation. In most cases, any remaining failing teeth are extracted at the same appointment, eliminating the need for a separate procedure.

Step 4: Temporary Teeth Same Day In most All-on-X cases, a full set of temporary teeth is attached to the implants on the same day as surgery. You leave our office with a complete, functional smile not a gap.

Step 5: Final Restoration After a healing period of several months during which the implants fuse permanently with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration your temporary restoration is replaced with your final, custom-crafted permanent teeth. The result is a full arch of beautiful, natural-looking teeth built to last decades.

What are Bridges and am I eligible?

Implant-supported bridge

An implant-supported bridge replaces missing teeth without relying on adjacent natural teeth for support, which helps preserve your remaining tooth structure and overall oral health. The bridge uses an artificial tooth, or pontic, that is anchored by dental implants rather than natural teeth, and is permanently secured in place. Because it doesn’t depend on surrounding teeth, this option provides superior strength, stability, and a more natural look and feel making it an excellent choice for patients missing one or more teeth.

Tooth-supported bridge (traditional)

A tooth-supported bridge is the most common type of dental bridge and relies on the natural teeth on either side of the gap for support. To place the bridge, the adjacent teeth are filed down and fitted with crowns that anchor the artificial tooth, or pontic, restoring both function and appearance. This method is effective and long-lasting, but it does require the supporting teeth to be healthy and free of decay, since the process of altering them cannot be reversed once completed.
