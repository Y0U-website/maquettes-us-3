BRACES

Ortho Unlimited provides various options to help you achieve a smile without limits. Among our offerings, we specialize in both traditional metal braces and ceramic braces.

BRACES

METAL BRACES

The most commonly used braces today are traditional metal braces. Our orthodontists use a small self-ligating bracket system, which offers less friction and a more comfortable treatment for you. These high-quality braces are made of metal alloys and are smaller and more comfortable than ever before. Plus, you can add colored elastics (rubber bands) to your braces for a personalized and vibrant smile.

CERAMIC BRACES

For patients with aesthetic concerns, we offer ceramic braces made of clear materials that blend in with your teeth, making them less visible than metal braces. We use a state-of-the-art adhesive pre-coat ceramic bracket system that streamlines the bracket placement process and ensures your braces are more comfortable than ever. Our ceramic braces are typically recommended for teenage and adult patients who want a discreet, yet effective, orthodontic treatment option.
