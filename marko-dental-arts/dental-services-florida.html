CAD/CAM Restorations Marko Dental Arts currently accepts scans from 3Shape Trios, Cadent iTero, CEREC, as well as STLs and OBJs. Check in with our CAD Specialist today to see if our systems are compatible with yours.

  • Improved Treatment Planning: With detailed scans, our team can now better visualize your dental structure, aiding in more accurate treatment planning and personalized care.
  • Enhanced Communication: The Micro Mapper allows us to instantly share high-precision placement of implants with high accuracy to our designers to make a design.
  • Efficient Workflow: This advanced technology streamlines our workflow, reducing the time required for traditional impression-taking methods and ensuring faster turnaround times for your dental restorations.
  • Patient Comfort: Say goodbye to messy impressions! The Intraoral Micro Mapper provides a comfortable and non-invasive scanning experience, eliminating the need for gooey impression materials.
  • Improved Accuracy: By eliminating human error, the Micro Mapper ensures consistent and reliable results, leading to better-fitting dental appliances and restorations.

Fixed-Detachable Restorations

Marko Dental Arts offers full-arch, screw-retained hybrid prostheses. A fixed-detachable restoration allows for superior stability screw-retained over 4 or more implants. These lifelike restorations come with pink porcelain to mimic real gingiva.

Classic Ceramics Classic ceramics offer monolithic (solid) zirconia or lithium disilicate restorations at a more affordable value. With our in-house CAD/CAM milling system, our ceramics are digitally designed and milled, then hand-finished by our experienced craftsmen to produce great quality products with excellent contacts, occlusion & esthetics. Our classic ceramics are the ideal option for an economic price point without sacrificing lifelike details or esthetics.

Cosmetic Ceramics

We hold our cosmetic ceramic work to a high standard, because it’s what we do best. Paired with our cutting-edge digital technology to aid in the production sequence, our ceramists incorporate fine artistry and keen attention to detail to produce our cosmetic ceramics, which is what makes them ideal for the discriminating patient.
