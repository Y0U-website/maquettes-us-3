After growing up in Taylorsville, I graduated with a B.S. in Medical Biology from the University of Utah in 2000.

I earned my D.D.S. degree, with Honors, from the University of Washington in 2004.

I completed my Orthodontic residency in 2006 and earned a Masters of Science degree and Certificate of Orthodontics from St. Louis University.

My wife, Amy Vranes Mortensen, and I married in 1998, and together we have 4 children.

I began practicing orthodontics in Stansbury Park in 2010, in the same building I now occupy. Our space was small and becoming outdated. In 2022 I merged my practice with an outside group. In addition to gaining a new facility, I felt that our unique partnership would translate to better care for patients. Unfortunately, what I observed was that I lost complete control of the patient experience.…

I recently re-opened a new office at 263 Country Club in Stansbury Park. Here, we will return to what made us successful in the past.

Personalized Care. We will know your name and treat you like family.
