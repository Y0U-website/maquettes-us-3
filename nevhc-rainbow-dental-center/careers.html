Provider Benefits

Midlevel Provider Benefits

Medical: Medical coverage is offered through Kaiser Permanente (no charge for employee; company contribution towards premium for dependents) or PacifiCare HMO (no charge for employee; company contribution towards premium for dependents). A PPO plan is offered through United Health Care; this plan is not paid by the company. Dental: Dental services are offered through California Dental Network HMO (no charge for employee; dependents may be covered with a minimal premium).

Vision: Vision care is offered through EyeMed, which uses Lenscrafters/Eye Exam 2000 (no charge for employee, dependents may be covered with a minimal premium). Disability and life insurance benefits are available for employees who work 75 % time or more. These benefits are effective the first of the month following the successful completion of the 90-day “Get Acquainted” period.

Disability Insurance: NEVHC subscribes to a short- and long-term disability program that coordinates with State Disability and pays approximately 2/3 of the monthly salary during a period of approved disability. Life Insurance (Standard Life): NEVHC offers term life insurance and pays out at 1.5 times the annual salary to the designated beneficiary.

Life insurance is offered at $2,000/dependent child(ren) life and $5,500 for spouse life. The pension plans and flexible spending plans are available for employees who work 50 % time or more:

Pension Plan: NEVHC has a 403 (b) employer-sponsored pension plan available to regular employees. NEVHC contributes an amount equal to 5.5 % of the employee’s monthly base salary towards this pension plan. Employees may make voluntary contributions through payroll deductions on a “pre-tax basis” up to the limits imposed by the IRS.

Flexible Spending Plan: This plan allows employees to be reimbursed on a pre-tax basis for their out-of-pocket health care expenses (such as braces, co-payments on office visits, prescriptions, etc.) Pre-tax reimbursement is also available for dependent care expenses up to $5000 per year. There is no charge to the employee for this benefit.
