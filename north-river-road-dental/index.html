The practice

Our promise is to provide you with exceptional dentistry in a friendly and caring environment.

Dr. Abraham is a graduate of Loma Linda University and earned her degree, Doctor of Dental Surgery, in 1993. She practices as a general dentist and established her office in Keizer in 1997.

She stays current on best practices as a member of the Marion Dental Research Group and exceeds the hours required for continued education, for practicing dental professionals, to maintain her license.
