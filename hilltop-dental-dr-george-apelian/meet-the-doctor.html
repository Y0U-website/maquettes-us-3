George Apelian, DDS

Continue practicing dentistry at Hilltop Dental.

Continue to meet the challenging needs of my family.

Continue to thrive in making planet earth a better place for someone, each and every day.

With Honors, Completed four-year clinical program in three years.

Post-Doctorate, hospital residency V.A/UCLA affiliate hospital Sepulveda, California

Degree: Bachelors (BS & BA) Chemistry & Biology

Certificate – Southern California Implant Institute

2004 -2007 at Goleta’s Cottage Hospital. In an outpatient operating room setting, extensive oral rehabilitative procedures were performed, free of charge, for underprivileged and developmentally disabled adolescents and young adults.

Co-founder of the Dental Clinic at Devereux in Santa Barbara, California. The clinic rendered challenging and much-needed dental and oral health care to developmentally disabled children and young adults. The clinic operated from 2003-2008.

Founder and the sole sponsor of the dental clinic at the Salvation Center of Disabled Children in Shingavit, Armenia (www.Prkutyun.am). Since November 2005, the clinic provided free-of-charge, much-needed dental care to the very poor and the underprivileged. Over 15,000 families and individuals have been treated. The clinic still continues its mission to this day through the Paros Foundation.

2014 – Present: Founder of and currently practicing dentistry at Hilltop Dental. Agoura Hills, California.

1983 – 2013: Supervising dentist and comprehensive dental practitioner at private multidisciplinary Dental Practice. Tarzana, California.

Northeast Regional Board of Dental Examiners
