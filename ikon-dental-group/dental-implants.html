Dental Implants

Losing a tooth can be a distressing experience that can cause self-consciousness for many- but don’t worry! For patients who have lost a tooth or multiple teeth, dental implants can help to fill in the gap for a natural-looking smile.

What Are Dental Implants?

In the past, the only options to correct a missing tooth were bridges and dentures. However, today, dental implants are used to replace a missing tooth for a natural-looking smile. Dental implants are used to replace missing tooth roots and provide a strong foundation for replacement teeth that are made to look like your natural teeth.

What are The Benefits of Dental Implants?

While dental implants are used to improve the appearance of your missing tooth, they are also associated with many other benefits. These include:

  • Improved speech- Dentures that fit poorly can cause trouble with speech. Implants allow you to speak clearly without the worry of dentures slipping.
  • Improved comfort- Because implants are permanent, you don’t have to worry about denture discomfort.
  • Improved self-esteem- Dental implants can help you feel confident in your smile again.
  • Easier eating- Dentures can slide and make eating difficult. Dental implants can make eating easy!
  • Convenience- Because dental implants are permanent, you don’t have to worry about messy adhesives to keep them in place.

Who is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most people who are healthy enough to undergo oral surgery are candidates for a dental implant. Your gums should also be healthy, and you should have enough bone to hold the implant in place. The best way to see if you are a good candidate for dental implants is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Khan.

How is a Dental Implant Placed?

The first step to placing a dental implant is to create an individualized treatment plan. Once this plan has been drawn up, a tooth root implant will be placed into the bone socket of the missing tooth. This small root implant is a post made up of titanium. The jawbone will then heal around the implanted metal post, which can take 6-12 weeks.

Next, a small connector post called an abutment is attached to the post to hold the new tooth in place. This new tooth will be made through an impression, which creates a model of your bite. A replacement tooth, called a crown , is attached to the abutment. Dr. Khan will match the color of your new tooth to your natural teeth, so no one will be able to tell that you have a dental implant.

Are Dental Implants Painful?

Because local anesthesia is used during the dental implant procedure, most people experience mild discomfort from the procedure. After the procedure, mild soreness is common and can be treated with over-the-counter pain medications.

The process for dental implants takes place over several months:

It is important that you have adequate bone structure to secure the implant in place. Dr. Khan will create a personalized treatment plan.

Implantation The dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed into the bone serving as the root of the prosthetic tooth. Strategic planning is crucial to the long-term success and comfort of the final restoration, and unfortunately, this is overlooked by many inexperienced doctors. Sometimes the restoration of the implant can be performed right away, and other times it requires healing up to 4 months. If necessary, you will be provided with a temporary tooth (or teeth) during the implant healing phase.

Implant Restoration Once the dental implant has completely healed, your final restoration whether it is a single crown, multiple crowns, dentures, or fixed hybrid can be fabricated.

Do Dental Implants Require Special Care?

Dental implants require no more maintenance or care than your natural teeth. Implants, like your natural teeth, depend on healthy gums and surrounding tooth structures for support. It is essential that you brush and floss regularly and visit Dr. Khan at least twice a year for dental cleanings. Regular and diligent oral hygiene should keep your dental implant healthy for a lifetime of smiles.

Dental Implants Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do dental implants feel like normal teeth? Yes, dental implants are designed to feel and function like natural teeth. They are securely anchored into the jawbone, which provides a stable and comfortable feel, closely mimicking the sensation of natural teeth.

How many times can a dental implant be replaced?

While dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution, they can be replaced if necessary. However, with proper care and maintenance, the need for replacement is uncommon.

Are there any side effects of dental implants?

Dental implants are generally safe, but like any surgical procedure, there may be some side effects. These can include minor swelling, discomfort, and bruising in the area, but these symptoms typically resolve within a few days.

What is the lifespan of dental implants?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care and regular dental check-ups. Factors like oral hygiene, lifestyle, and the health of surrounding bone and gum tissues play a significant role in the longevity of implants.

How long do dental implants take to heal?

The healing process for dental implants usually takes several months, with the initial phase of osseointegration (where the implant fuses with the jawbone) taking about 3 to 6 months. Full recovery and restoration of the implant may take longer, depending on one’s individual health factors.

When is the best time to get a dental implant?

The best time to get a dental implant is soon after losing a tooth to prevent bone loss and other complications. However, implants can be placed even years after tooth loss with the proper preparation and treatment.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

A good candidate for dental implants is someone with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone to support the implant. Additionally, they should be in good overall health and committed to maintaining good oral hygiene.

Is a dental implant painful?

The dental implant procedure is generally well-tolerated and is performed under local anesthesia, so patients usually experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. Any post-operative pain is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain medications.

Are you put to sleep for dental implants?

Most dental implant procedures are done under local anesthesia, which numbs the area and allows the patient to stay awake but comfortable. In some cases, sedation or general anesthesia may be used, depending on the complexity of the case and patient preference.

Do dental implants become loose over time?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution and should not become loose over time if properly placed and cared for. However, factors like gum disease or insufficient bone support can potentially cause an implant to loosen, requiring prompt attention from a dentist.

How do dental implants affect speech and pronunciation?

While dental implants may initially affect how you speak or pronounce words, those issues will resolve the longer you have the implants. As you get used to the implants and you heal completely, those speech issues will no longer be a problem.

Can dental implants be covered by insurance?

Whether your dental implants are covered by your insurance depends on your individual dental insurance plan and your coverage. Most dental insurance plans don’t cover dental implants, but it’s worth it to check with your plan. We are more than happy to submit an estimate to your insurance.

Can dental implants be used in conjunction with other dental treatments?

Absolutely! Dental implants can be combined with dental bridges, braces, crowns, veneers, and more. To determine the right combination of treatments for your needs, our team at IKON Dental Group will work with you to build a customized treatment plan.

Can dental implants be used for children or teenagers?

Dental implants are not typically recommended for children or teenagers. This is mainly because their jaws are still growing, and receiving dental implants too early can lead to complications later on.

What role does nutrition play in the healing process of dental implants?

Nutrition plays a huge role in the dental implant healing process. Eating the proper foods can help reduce inflammation, promote better healing, prevent infection, and support the long-term success of your implants. We recommend eating plenty of protein, getting enough vitamin C, boosting your immune system with zinc-rich foods, and drinking plenty of water.

How do I care for dental implants after surgery?

For the first 24 hours after your dental implant surgery, Dr. Khan will advise you to stick to a liquid or soft diet. You’ll also want to avoid smoking or using straws. You can also use ice packs to help with any discomfort and swelling. Using a soft-bristle toothbrush is also recommended to clean the implant and the surrounding areas.

How much do dental implants cost, and what factors affect the price?

On average, a dental implant can cost anywhere between $3000-$6000, but many factors affect this cost. This includes the type of implant, the abutment, and dental crown that is restored on the implant. Additional costs include procedures such as bone grafting or sinus lifts if needed. The number of implants needed will also affect the price of your dental implant procedure.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Yes, it is possible for smokers to receive dental implants, but it is very important to understand that there are health considerations to keep in mind. Smoking can affect how successful the implant procedure is and how long an implant can last. Smoking increases the rate of implant failure due to diminished blood flow, which in turn slows the healing process, and increases the risk of an infection around the implant, known as peri-implantitis.
