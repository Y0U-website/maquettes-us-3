About Us

What Sets Our Menomonie Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Menomonie Dental is a general dental and specialty practice located in Menomonie, WI. Our clinic specializes in comprehensive dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, routine dental care, and teeth whitening. Book an appointment at our dental office today!

Meet Your Dentist

A Group of Skilled and Compassionate Professionals in One Dental Office.

Dr. Emily Barnes

Background

Dr. Barnes is originally from Eau Claire, WI. She earned her DDS degree at the University of Iowa and joins us at Menomonie Dental with a passion for making a difference in the lives of her patients, whether that means getting someone out of pain or making someone’s smile bigger.

Dental Style

Dr. Barnes’ passion for dentistry is rooted in creating positive changes for people. Dr. Barnes is an active member of the WDA, ADA, AGD, and helped start an onsite dental clinic at the Chippewa Valley Free Clinic where she was a board member.

Fun Facts

Dr. Barnes has a wonderful and active life outside of Dentistry. She has a passion for fitness including yoga, tennis, and running. She and her husband Brett also keep busy with their 3 children (including a set of twins!) and their dog Oscar. The Barnes family is also proud Badger and Packer fans.
