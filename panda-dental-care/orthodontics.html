Comprehensive Orthodontic Services At Panda Dental Care, we offer a wide range of orthodontic services to help you achieve a straighter, more beautiful smile. Our orthodontic services include, but are not limited to:

  • Traditional Metal Braces Metal braces are a popular orthodontic treatment that uses metal brackets and wires to gradually move your teeth into their desired position. They are effective, reliable, and can correct a wide range of orthodontic issues, including crooked teeth, crowded teeth, and gaps between teeth.
  • Ceramic Braces Ceramic braces are similar to traditional metal braces, but the brackets are made from tooth-colored or clear materials that blend in with your teeth for a more discreet orthodontic treatment option. Ceramic braces are ideal for individuals who want to achieve a straighter smile without noticeable metal braces.
  • Invisalign Invisalign is a popular orthodontic treatment that uses clear, removable aligners to gradually shift your teeth into place. Invisalign is a virtually invisible and comfortable alternative to traditional metal braces and is ideal for adults and teenagers who want a discreet orthodontic treatment option.
  • Retainers Retainers are used after orthodontic treatment to help maintain the position of your teeth and prevent them from moving back to their original position. Retainers are custom-made to fit your teeth and are designed to be worn regularly to ensure that your teeth remain straight and healthy.
  • Myobrace Unlike traditional braces, Myobrace uses a series of removable oral appliances to guide the growth of the jaws and encourage proper oral habits. This non-invasive treatment approach not only helps correct tooth alignment but also aims to promote a lifetime of oral health. Myobrace is suitable for children between the ages of five and 15, as it takes advantage of the growth and development that occurs during this crucial period.
