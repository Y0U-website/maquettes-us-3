Announcements and News

We are open and will continue providing you with the best possible basic primary care healthcare, just in a little different manner.

The practice

In order to help our wonderful patients and volunteer staff stay safe, we have changed the way in which we take care of you but, rest assured, you will receive care.

Please remember that Healing Hands is not a walk-in clinic-APPOINTMENTS ARE NECESSARY. We also do not have urgent care capabilities and we are unable to handle emergency situations. Our primary focus is on those who have simple issues and chronic conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, thyroid conditions and COPD.

You are not alone! We are here to help in anyway that we can. Please call, even if you just need direction or prayer. Stay home and stay safe. Know that we love you!

To help prevent infection, remember to wash your hands for at least 20 seconds before touching your face. Need info on COVID-19? Two great websites that are full of reliable information include the Center for Disease Control and the World Health Organization .
