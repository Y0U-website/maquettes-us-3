Meet Dr. Ryan Brown

Dr. Brown’s top priorities are your child’s comfort and satisfaction. He realizes that children deserve to experience a gentle, positive approach to their dental care, and has worked and trained extensively to build a practice that exceeds your expectations. More than anything, he knows what it means to be a concerned parent.

Dr. Brown was honored with multiple awards while in dental school, most special to him being the Dr. Solomon Averbach Humanitarian Award, which recognized his ability to demonstrate compassionate patient care. After completing his schooling, he remains active in the dental community as a member of the Arizona Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Brown is the only pediatric dentist in the Prescott area honored with the distinguished status of Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry; this nationally recognized status reflects his commitment to scholarship, leadership, and his dedication to giving back to his community. Having practiced dentistry in the quad cities area since 2007, Dr. Brown considers one of the most rewarding parts of his job to be seeing former patients bring their own children into the office to receive the same quality care.

Apart from offering trusted, expert, and effective treatment, Dr. Brown also enjoys being an active part of his community. As a former Roughrider soccer player himself, he currently enjoys volunteering as the Dentist of the Yavapai College Soccer Team. Dr. Brown still enjoys playing and coaching soccer and is very involved in the Yavapai Soccer League. In addition to soccer, he also enjoys participating in humanitarian aid projects.

Throughout his life, he has been fond of spending time in the great outdoors; camping, hiking, and mountain biking are among his favorite pastimes. With a life full of worthwhile projects, he finds his greatest joys come from time spent with his beautiful wife, Krista, and their four children.
