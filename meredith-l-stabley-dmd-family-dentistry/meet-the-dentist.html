MEET THE DENTIST

MEREDITH STABLEY DMD

Dr. Stabley grew up in Hershey, PA where she attended Hershey High School. She continued her education at Ursinus College in Collegeville, Pa earning her undergraduate B.S. degree with majors in Biology and French as well as a minor in Spanish. While at Ursinus, Meredith played field hockey as the starting midfielder for the 2006 DIII National Champion Ursinus bears. GO BEARS!

After college, she attended Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry to complete her DMD degree in 2012. While at Temple, Meredith was fortunate to lead the Temple Haiti club traveling to Haiti in both third and fourth years to provide dental care to underserved areas. Dr. Stabley was inducted into the prestigious OKU honor society and graduated ranked 2nd, at the top of her class.

To further her education, she completed a general practice residency at Lehigh Valley Health Network Muhlenberg in Bethlehem, PA before she entered private practice. After working in private practices in Colonial Park and Millersville, Dr. Stabley decided to transition to her own private practice where she could focus on comprehensive care dentistry and building lasting dental team and patient relationships. Meredith lives in Lancaster. In her free time, she enjoys making jewelry, sewing, gardening, exploring the local area, running, and hiking with her dogs Remi, Ozzie, Nico and Cosmo.
