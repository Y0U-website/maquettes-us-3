Dentists have advanced training in the muscles, nerves, and anatomy of the face, making them uniquely qualified to deliver precise, safe, and natural-looking injectable treatments. Botox Injections

As we age, skin elasticity diminishes causing lines and wrinkles. Botox temporarily improves the look of moderate to severe lines in the face giving you natural-looking results. This prescription-only injection is used to treat frown lines, forehead, crow’s feet, and vertical bands connecting the neck and jaw. Schedule a consultation with Dr. Atkinson today to discuss how Botox can help you look younger!

Fillers

Dermal fillers are injected beneath the skin around the eyes, mouth, and nose to smooth lines and plump skin. This nonsurgical cosmetic procedure can restore volume to your face with results that last for months or years.
