Meet Your Castro Valley Dental Team

A Father-Daughter Team with a Shared Passion for Smiles

Dr. Mark S. Murphy, DDS

Dr. Mark Murphy has served the Castro Valley and Hayward communities since 1981. He graduated from University of California at Davis with a Bachelor of Science degree in Zoology and later graduated from University of the Pacific School of Dentistry.

He values using the most advanced technology in order to provide patients with the best in health, esthetics, comfort, and convenience for restorative procedures. Dr. Murphy has utilized CEREC CAD/CAM technology since 1997, allowing patients to have single-unit crowns completed in just a few hours. He is active in various local computerized dental study groups and has helped instruct other dentists in the CEREC CAD/CAM technique. He is also skilled in placing and restoring implants.

Dr. Murphy is married with a daughter, Mackenzie, who he is proud to have joined him at the office. He enjoys nature photography that he shares throughout the office, as well as fishing, golf, barbecuing, and travel.

Dr. Mackenzie J. Murphy, DDS

Dr. Mackenzie Murphy is a Bay Area native who grew up in her father’s dental office, knowing at a young age that she wanted to pursue a career in dentistry. She is grateful for the amazing mentorship from her father and strives to continue his legacy of providing patients in the Castro Valley and Hayward areas with beautiful, healthy smiles using the most up-to-date technology and conservative approaches.

She graduated magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Biology from Williams College in Massachusetts. There she was a First Team All-American softball pitcher and first baseman, as well as an Academic All-American and Female Scholar Athlete of the Year for her college.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Murphy enjoys travel, golf, softball, baking, reading, and, of course, rooting for the Giants, Warriors, and 49ers.
