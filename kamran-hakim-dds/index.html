A dental implant involves two procedures. The first is the surgical placement of a titanium post below the gums into your jawbone. It takes several months for the bone to fully integrate with the post to prepare it for the second part of the procedure.

The next step involves mounting the replacement tooth or other prosthetic on top of the post. The end result is a strong, secure, natural-looking tooth.
