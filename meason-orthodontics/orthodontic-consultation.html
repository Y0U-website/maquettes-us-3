What to Expect at Your First Visit

When you arrive, our team will greet you, get you checked in, and make sure you’re comfortable. There’s no paperwork stress; we’ll handle the basics quickly so you can focus on what brought you in.

Step 3: An Honest, Plain-Language Conversation

Once Dr. Meason has a clear picture of your smile, he’ll sit down with you and walk through what he sees. He’ll explain things in everyday language and answer every question you have, no rushing, no jargon.

Who Should Schedule a Consultation?

The short answer: just about anyone. Orthodontic consultations are useful at almost any age, and the visit is free, so there’s no downside to coming in and seeing where your smile stands. Here’s a quick guide for each stage of life.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic visit around age 7. At this age, we can catch early signs of crowding, bite issues, or jaw growth concerns while there’s still time to gently guide things in the right direction. Even if your child doesn’t need treatment yet, an early visit gives us a baseline and helps your child feel comfortable in our office for future visits.

Learn more about early orthodontic treatment →

The teen years are the most common time for orthodontic treatment, whether that’s traditional braces or clear aligners like Invisalign or Spark. A consultation is a great way to talk through options, look at timing, and figure out what fits your teen’s school, sports, and social life.

Learn more about braces or Invisalign for teens

It’s never too late to invest in your smile, and more adults than ever are pursuing orthodontic treatment. Whether you’re fixing something that’s bothered you for years, finishing what you started as a teen, or addressing shifting that’s happened after old orthodontic work, a consultation is the best way to explore your options, including discreet treatments like Invisalign and Spark. If you had orthodontic treatment years ago and your teeth have shifted, or if you’re transferring from another orthodontist mid-treatment, we’d love to take a look. Bring any records you have, and we’ll walk through your options together.

Treatment, Costs & Financing Discussed at Your Visit

You’ll never be pressured to decide on the spot. We want you to leave with all the information you need to talk things over with your family and make the right call for your budget and lifestyle.

  • Whether treatment is needed at all. If your smile is healthy and on track, we’ll tell you that. No upselling, no unnecessary recommendations.
  • What your treatment options are. Including braces, Invisalign, Spark, in-house clear aligners, retainers, or early treatment for kids.
  • A clear cost estimate. We’ll walk through pricing for your specific situation so you know exactly what to expect.
  • What your insurance may cover. We accept most major insurance plans (no Medicaid), and we’ll help you understand your benefits.
  • In-house financing options. Flexible monthly payment plans we manage right here at the office, no outside lenders, no complicated applications.
