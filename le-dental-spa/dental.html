FAQ

Most frequent questions and answers about our dental services.

Why do I need to come in for regular dental check-ups? Many people leave it too late to come in for a check and clean. Dental problems often start without pain or even discomfort, and by the time you can tell something is wrong, you may need complex, expensive treatment. Coming in once or twice a year for a check-up gives us the best chance of alerting you to any issues before they become worse.

How will I know how often to get a cleaning? The best way to make sure you come in once or twice a year for your check-up is to let us remind you! If you give us permission, we can send you a reminder when you are due for your half-yearly check and clean. You can then book online easily for a time that suits you.

What will happen at my check-up? At your check-up and clean, our team will make you comfortable, and the dentist will check your teeth very thoroughly. We will take X-rays and digital images of your teeth so we can see if there are any changes at your next check-up. Your teeth will then be cleaned and polished, and a strengthening fluoride treatment will be applied.

What if the dentist finds a problem at my check-up? Depending on what we find, we may either be able to start treatment at your check-up, or we will put together a care plan for you. We will make sure you have all the information about the issue that needs attention, including treatment options and costs.

How much will my appointment cost? No two smiles are alike, so the cost of your personal treatment(s) will depend on your exact needs. We work closely with you on your personal costs and have membership plans available. If you are curious and would like an estimate we also offer consultations. All you have to do is schedule your appointment online to get started, we are happy to help!
