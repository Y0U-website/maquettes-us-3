The practice

Our mission at Lewisburg Family Dental Practice is to treat our patients as we would our family and friends, by providing the most current and comprehensive dental care available in a professional and warm environment.

We treat the person, not just the mouth. Your Smile is important to us!

Dr. Romano comes to the area from the state of New York. He earned his undergraduate degree in biology from City University of New York where he graduated with highest honors in 1998. He earned his D.D.S. degree from Columbia University’s School of Dental and Oral surgery in 2003. He then completed his General Practice Residency at Staten Island University Hospital in 2004.

Dr. Romano also attended Ringling School of Art and maintains a great interest in art. He enjoys his time off by painting portrait and landscape. You can view some of Dr. Romano’s artwork here .

We accept only the below listed insurance plans and will gladly process your claim. Please bring the necessary information with you on your first visit.

These plans generally cover only a portion of the total treatment cost. Unless other arrangements have been made, we ask that you pay your portion of the bill at the time of treatment. It is your responsibility to pay any balance not paid by your insurance company.

If you have insurance coverage from other companies, we will gladly fill out your form but it will be necessary for you to pay our fee in full. You will then be reimbursed by your insurance company up to the limits of your policy.

For your convenience, we also accept payments made with your Visa, Discover, and MasterCard.
