The braces process

Orthodontic treatment follows a clear sequence from evaluation to retention. Here is a step-by-step overview:

  • Consultation and exam: A clinical exam, photos, and digital scans or X-rays define your bite and tooth positions.
  • Customized plan: A plan outlines goals, estimated timeline, and the type of braces suited to your case.
  • Bonding appointment: Brackets are placed and the first archwire is secured with ties.
  • Active adjustments: Visits every 4–8 weeks allow wire changes and refinements as teeth respond.
  • Debonding: Brackets are removed when alignment and bite are corrected.
  • Retention: Clear retainers or fixed retainers help maintain results after treatment.

Your options with braces

Choosing among types of braces depends on your goals and lifestyle:

  • Metal braces: Durable and efficient for a wide range of movements.
  • Ceramic braces: Tooth-colored brackets blend in for a lower-profile look.
  • Lingual braces: Hidden behind the teeth for maximum discretion, often used in select cases.
  • Clear aligners: Removable trays that can be effective for mild to moderate issues when compliance is high.

What to expect

Timeline: Active treatment typically lasts 12–24 months, though simpler cases may finish sooner and complex cases may take longer. Consistency with visits and home care helps keep your plan on track.

Comfort: Mild soreness is common after adjustments and usually improves within a few days. Over-the-counter pain relievers and a soft diet can help.

Diet: Avoid hard, sticky, and very chewy foods that can loosen brackets or bend wires. Cut firm foods into small pieces to protect your braces.

Oral hygiene: Brush after meals with a soft brush and fluoride toothpaste. Floss daily using a floss threader or interdental cleaner. Consistent oral hygiene is essential to prevent white spots and gingivitis.

Sports and music: A mouthguard protects your braces during contact sports. Most wind and brass musicians adapt well after a short adjustment period.
