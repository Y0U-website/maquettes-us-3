What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant consists of a titanium root that is surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the missing tooth root. Over time, the bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation for a custom-made crown that resembles your natural teeth. This process restores both the function and aesthetics of your smile.
