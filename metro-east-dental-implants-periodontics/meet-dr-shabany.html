Meet Dr. Shabany

Khaled Shabany, DMD, MS

Dr. Shabany completed his undergraduate studies with a Bachelor of Sciences Degree in Biology at Southern Illinois University at Edwardsville. He received his dental training at Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine and graduated in 1996.

Following graduation, he completed three years of post-graduate studies at Northwestern University Dental School Earning his Masters degree from the Graduate Periodontics Program in Chicago, Illinois in 1999.

He has been engaged in clinical practice as well as teaching since completing his postgraduate program. He was an Assistant Professor at Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine for several years, and was involved at undergraduate and graduate levels. He is also involved with the Jamaica Dental Mission trips that include dental and pharmacy students providing care to communities in need.

Dr. Shabany is committed to continuing education and attends meetings and seminars every year to continue to be on the cutting edge in his field. He is constantly adding and modernizing treatment and therapy techniques to provide clients with the best possible outcome.
