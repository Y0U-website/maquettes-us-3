Advanced Technology in Endodontics

DELIVERING PRECISION, COMFORT, AND EXCELLENCE

At Live Oak Endodontics, we are committed to providing the highest standard of care by implementing the most advanced technology available in the field of endodontics. Our state-of-the-art equipment ensures accurate diagnosis, enhanced treatment precision, and a more comfortable experience for our patients.

We invest in top-of-the-line technology not just because it keeps us at the forefront of our specialty, but because it translates directly into better care for you. Our goal is simple: to make your experience as comfortable, efficient, and successful as possible.

Surgical Microscopes

We utilize surgical microscopes during every procedure, allowing for unmatched visualization of the finest details within the tooth structure. This level of magnification significantly improves accuracy, leading to better outcomes and long-term success.

Digital Radiographs

Our practice also utilizes digital radiographs , which provide high-resolution images with minimal radiation exposure. These images are instantly viewable, allowing for quicker diagnostics and real-time communication with patients.

3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)

As a standard of treatment, we rely on 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) technology. This advanced imaging gives us a comprehensive, three-dimensional view of the teeth, bone, and surrounding structures—ensuring precise planning and more predictable results, especially for root canal therapy and surgical procedures.

Sedation Options

Additionally, a number of sedation options are at our disposal to allow for more comfortable care. Ask us for more details!
