Interested in a Practice Valuation?

A dental practice valuation or appraisal gives you an up-to-date picture of what your practice is worth in the current market and can help you set an asking price when you are ready to sell your practice. Did you know all practice owners should have their practice valued every 12 months?

If you are interested in completing an appraisal for your practice or just have questions, please fill out the form and your local Henry Schein Transition Sales Consultant will contact you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a dental practice valuation inquiry?

  • Short Answer: A valuation inquiry is the first step in understanding the true market value of your dental practice, allowing you to connect with a transition specialist for a confidential, complimentary consultation.
  • Detailed Guidance: By submitting an inquiry, you gain access to expert guidance on the "transition lifecycle". Whether you are considering a sale in the next 12–24 months or simply seeking "valuation clarity" for future planning, this initial contact helps establish a baseline for your practice’s worth based on current market trends.

What happens after I submit my practice valuation inquiry?

  • Short Answer: Once you submit the form, a Henry Schein DPT transition consultant will reach out to schedule a discovery call to discuss your goals, review your practice’s high-level financials, and outline the appraisal process.
  • Detailed Guidance: Our process is designed to be "stress-free". Your dedicated consultant will guide you through the "preliminary evaluation" phase, which includes a review of your collections, equipment, and market potential. This ensures that any subsequent professional appraisal is grounded in "true operating value" rather than just surface-level revenue.

Is my valuation inquiry kept confidential?

  • Short Answer: Yes. Confidentiality is a cornerstone of our service to protect your legacy, staff, and patient relationships.
  • Detailed Guidance: We understand that even considering a transition is a sensitive matter. Henry Schein DPT maintains strict confidentiality protocols from the moment of your initial inquiry through the final closing. This ensures that your intention to evaluate or "sell your dental practice" remains private until the optimal time (determined by you) to share the news with your team and community.

Do I need to be ready to sell immediately to request an inquiry?

  • Short Answer: Absolutely not.. Many dentists inquire early in their career to get "valuation clarity" or to begin the 12–24 month preparation process required to maximize practice value.
  • Detailed Guidance: Driving "earlier engagement" is one of our primary goals. We encourage mid-to-late career dentists—and even emerging early-stage dentists—to inquire so they can begin "protecting their legacy" and financial security long before the actual sale date. Understanding your practice’s value now allows for strategic "load speed" and "technical optimizations" of your business operations over time.

Why should I choose Henry Schein DPT for my practice valuation?

  • Short Answer: As the largest dental practice brokerage firm in the U.S., we offer unmatched resources, skill and expertise and data-driven insights to ensure your appraisal is accurate and competitive.
  • Detailed Guidance: Our national footprint provides us with the industry data needed to track trends across the country. By choosing Henry Schein DPT, you benefit from a "full-service firm" you’re not working with a fly by night operation or one that doesn’t take you, your practice, dentistry and all of the data with the professionalism, respect and responsibility that each deserves.
