Preventative Dentistry

Flouride Treatment Fluoride helps make the tooth more resistant to the build up of plaque and sugars. Fluoride can be applied to the teeth as a gel, foam or varnish. According to the ADA, fluoride intake is often recommended for infants and children between the ages of 6 months and 16 years. During this time, primary and permanent teeth are developing. Fluoride becomes incorporated into the development of permanent teeth, making it difficult for acids to demineralize the teeth.

Adults can benefit from fluoride as well to fight tooth decay and strengthen teeth. Especially if any adult is susceptible to dry mouth, gum disease or is undergoing orthodontic treatment.
