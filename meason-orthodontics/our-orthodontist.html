Meet Dr. Kyle Meason

Board-Certified Orthodontist in Weatherford, TX

Hi there! I’m Dr. Kyle Meason. First off, thanks for taking the time to check out Meason Orthodontics. I’m excited to meet you in person and help you on your smile journey, but until then, let me give you a bit of my professional and personal background so you can get to know a little more about me.

A Texan Born & Raised

I’m a native Texan , born and raised in a farming family in the Lubbock area.

After graduating from New Deal High School , a small school just north of Lubbock, I attended Texas Tech University and earned a degree in architecture.

A Calling to Dentistry

A Passion for Making People Smile

During dental school, I truly discovered my passion for making people smile. And I felt that the best way to do that was through orthodontics. What’s better than helping someone achieve a healthy, beautiful smile? So, I went on to earn my Orthodontic Certificate and Master of Science in Dentistry at the University of Colorado. Now, I’m so excited to be back in the great state of Texas, and I am looking forward to making our wonderful community smile!

A Family Man

In addition to being an orthodontic specialist, I am also a husband, father, and bluegrass music enthusiast. I met my beautiful wife, Sarah, at Texas Tech, and we have three wonderful children, Kennedy, Hudson, and our newest addition, Audrey. We enjoy reading books, going to the zoo, and visiting museums as a family.
