What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a special branch of dentistry that focuses on correcting teeth and jaw alignment. When your teeth are crowded, crooked, or don’t fit together properly, it can affect your bite, cause discomfort, and sometimes even lead to other dental problems. Orthodontic treatment moves teeth into the right position over time, giving you a healthier, more functional smile.

Dentist near you use devices like braces, Invisalign, and retainers to guide teeth and jaw growth. Each treatment is tailored to your needs, age, and lifestyle, ensuring the optimal results while keeping you comfortable.
