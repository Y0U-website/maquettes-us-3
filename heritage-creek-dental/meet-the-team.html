Meet the Team

Heritage Creek Dental Team

Kristi- front desk

Kristi has been with Heritage Creek Dental since March 2023. Kristi lives in Norwalk with her husband, Todd, two boys and three dogs.

Kristi’s favorite thing about dentistry is seeing the change you can make in someone’s smile and how that can change their life.

In her free time, Kristi enjoys walking, watching the KC Chiefs and eating chocolate.

BrI - Dental Assistant, RDA

Bri is an Expanded Function Dental Assistant that joined the Heritage Creek Dental team in July 2025. She is originally from Panora, IA and attended DMACC, where she received her Dental Assisting Diploma in 2018.

Bri loves dentistry because of how rewarding it can be helping patients achieve a brighter and healthier smile.

Bri and her boyfriend, Pat, enjoy spending time with their fur baby Gronk. She also enjoys spending time with friends and family, being active, and unwinding with a good show or movie.

Macy - Dental Hygienist, RDH

Macy joined the Heritage Creek Dental team in November 2020. She is originally from Burnside, IA and attended Iowa Central Community College in Fort Dodge, where she received her Associates of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene.

Macy is licensed to administer local anesthetic and certified to provide nitrous oxide. Macy loves dentistry because of the relationship she builds with her patients and being able to teach the importance of good oral hygiene.

Macy and her husband, Trevor, have one son and several fur babies. In her free time, she loves spending time with friends and family and cheering on the Hawkeyes.

Mariah- Dental Hygienist, RDH

Mariah is a Registered Dental Hygienist and started with Heritage Creek in August 2017. She attended Kirkwood Community College in Cedar Rapids where she received her Associates of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene.

Mariah is licensed to administer local anesthetic and certified to provide nitrous oxide. She loves dentistry because she loves establishing relationships with patients and feels it is rewarding to help improve the oral health and overall health of her patients.

Mariah and her husband, Brandon, have two kids. They have a Chocolate Lab named Coco. In her free time, Mariah likes to spend time with her family and friends, go shopping and watch Judge Judy.

ALEXIS - DENTAL HYGIENIST, RDH

Alexis is a Registered Dental Hygienist and joined the Heritage Creek Dental team in February 2024. She is originally from the Mason City area and attended Des Moines Area Community College in Ankeny, where she received her Associates of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene in 2019.

Alexis is licensed to administer local anesthetic and certified to provide nitrous oxide. Alexis’ favorite aspect of dentistry is helping patients overcome their past dental trauma and changing their outlook on dental care.

Alexis and her husband, Rasim, have two sons and three fur babies. In her free time, she loves spending time with her kiddos, discovering new restaurants, or finding new movies to watch.
