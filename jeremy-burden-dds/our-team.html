Meet the Team

Dr. Jeremy H. Burden, DDs Since 2001, Dr. Burden has been focused on providing high quality dental services with genuine consideration and compassion for his patients. He is dedicated to providing the very latest in methods and materials that dentistry offers. Subsequently, he has developed a reputation for providing exceptional dental care for residents of Bath and surrounding communities.

A Maine native, Dr. Burden grew up in the Midcoast region. In 2001 he graduated with honors from the Loma Linda University School of Dentistry, attaining his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Following graduation, he returned to Maine to serve the needs of the community he grew up in.

Dr. Burden is dedicated to keeping up to date with the latest dental techniques and knowledge in order to deliver excellent results for his patients. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, Maine Dental Association, Casco Bay Study Club, the Spear Study Club, and Merry Meeting Bay Dental Society. He has also completed the core curriculum of advanced continuing education at The Pankey Institute.

Dr. Mary f. mcclintock, DMD With nearly a decade of experience, Dr. McClintock is dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-focused dental care. She believes in the power of preventive dentistry and strives to create a welcoming and comfortable environment where every patient feels heard and respected.

A Midcoast Maine native, Dr. McClintock proudly serves the community she calls home. She graduated at the top of her class from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2016 and has been an active member of the American Dental Association since dental school. She began her career in Western Massachusetts, working alongside exceptional mentors and developing a strong foundation in public health and private practice.

After returning to Maine, Dr. McClintock continued to expand her expertise, combining her knowledge of preventive care with a patient-centered philosophy. She is committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry to ensure her patients receive the best possible treatment.
