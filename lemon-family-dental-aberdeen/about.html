Digital X-Rays Digital X-rays are far superior and much safer for patients. They provide clearer images, are much more comfortable and less invasive than traditional X-rays. Additionally, they save time and expose patients to 80 % less radiation than typical X-rays.

Cutting Edge Lab Partner At Lemon Family Dental all lab cases are sent to ZIEMEK, a local, family-owned DAMAS certified dental lab. Only a small group of elite dental laboratories in the United States can achieve DAMAS certification. By using ZIEMEK labs, the outcome is a superior product and a beautiful smile.

Cloud Based Patient Software The commitment to modern technology doesn’t end at the office. Lemon Family Dental uses a cloud based software program to provide patients access to view educational videos, future appointments, and billing statements from any mobile device.

  • Exams are much less invasive and more cost effective.
