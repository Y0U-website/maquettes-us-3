Our Vision To be Maui’s most trusted dental practice, known for exceptional care, advanced technology, and a welcoming, patient-focused experience.

HI Precision Dental offers a full range of preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental services for patients of all ages. Our advanced technology ensures comfortable, precise, and lasting results.

Professional exams and cleanings detect cavities, maintain healthy teeth and gums, prevent oral issues early, and ensure long-term dental health for patients of all ages consistently.

Digital X-Rays Advanced digital X-rays provide accurate detection of cavities, bone health assessment, and oral conditions while minimizing radiation exposure for safe, precise, and efficient diagnosis every visit.

Gum Care Treatments Specialized periodontal care including deep cleaning, scaling, and gum treatment ensures healthy gums, prevents periodontal disease progression, and maintains optimal oral health for patients of all ages.
