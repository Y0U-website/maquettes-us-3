All-on-6: The Premium Full Arch Implant Solution

While All-on-4 has an excellent clinical track record, All-on-6 takes full-arch implant support one step further. By distributing the load of your complete prosthesis across 6 implants rather than 4, each individual implant bears less mechanical stress — potentially extending both implant longevity and prosthesis lifespan.

All-on-6 is particularly well-suited for patients with sufficient bone volume across the full arch, patients who are heavy grinders (bruxers), and patients choosing a larger, heavier final prosthesis material like monolithic full-zirconia. In the upper jaw, where bone is inherently less dense than the lower jaw, 6 implants often provide a more secure foundation.

The procedure timeline for All-on-6 is identical to All-on-4: same-day temporary prosthesis, 3–6 month osseointegration period, and final prosthesis delivery. The surgical procedure takes slightly longer due to the additional implants, but patients’ recovery experience is comparable. If you are considering a full arch restoration or exploring full mouth reconstruction , All-on-6 deserves serious consideration for patients with adequate bone.
