shuffled around vs. in-house.

how it works.

Four stages, all under one roof. The timeline varies per person we’ll map yours at the consult.

we plan it in 3D.

A digital scan and X-ray show us your bone, nerves, and bite. We map exactly where the implant goes before we touch anything.

we place the implant.

A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone the new root. The surgery is quicker and gentler than most people expect, done right here in Scottsdale.

we let it heal.

Over a few months the bone fuses to the post, creating a permanent anchor. We give you a temporary so you’re never walking around with a gap.

we place your crown.

Once it’s fused, we attach a custom crown — designed and fitted in-house color-matched to your other teeth. Done. A tooth that feels like yours.

is it right for you?

Implants work for most adults missing one tooth, several teeth, or all of them. The main requirement is enough healthy bone to anchor the post and if you’re short on bone, we can often build it back with a graft.

If you’re tired of a bridge, a gap, or dentures that move, a consult is the fastest way to know. We’ll tell you straight whether you’re a candidate right here at our Scottsdale office.

the questions.

how long does the whole process take?

Usually 3–6 months start to finish, most of which is healing time while the bone fuses. The actual appointments are short — you’re not in the chair for months.

does getting an implant hurt?

how long do implants last?

With good care, decades — often a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacing eventually, but the implant itself is built to stay.

what if i don’t have enough bone?

Common, and fixable. A bone graft rebuilds the area so the implant has a solid anchor. We’ll know from your 3D scan whether you need one.

does insurance cover implants?

Many PPO plans cover part of the crown or restoration, sometimes part of the surgery. Coverage varies a lot — we verify your exact benefit before you commit.

are implants better than a bridge or dentures?

For most people, yes. Implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth, don’t slip, and protect your jawbone. We’ll walk you through the tradeoffs honestly at the consult.
