Dr.Scarlett

Science-Driven. Patient-Centered. Conservative Care.

With over 32 years of clinical experience, Dr. Scarlett offers a unique perspective on oral health that blends rigorous scientific inquiry with a conservative, patient-first philosophy. Before earning his dental degree from the University of Florida in 1994, he completed a dual major in Chemistry and Physics at the State University of New York at Cortland.

From Engineering to Excellence

Dr. Scarlett’s path to dentistry was driven by a desire to merge an abstract scientific background with a hands-on, people-oriented profession. Having transitioned from an engineering foundation into dental medicine, he applies high-level technical precision to the art of patient care. This mindset ensures that every treatment plan is approached with meticulous detail and a focus on long-term structural integrity.

A Value-Based Philosophy

In an era of complex procedures, Dr. Scarlett remains committed to a conservative approach. He believes that the most successful outcomes occur when a clinician aligns their technical skill set with the patient’s personal values.

My role is to provide the options and the expertise, but the patient is always the priority. It isn’t about me; it’s about empowering the person receiving treatment to make the best decision for their life and health.

The Foundation of Oral Health

Dr. Scarlett is a firm advocate for patient education, emphasizing that the most critical dental work happens outside of the clinic. He believes that consistent, thorough home care—specifically dedicated brushing and flossing routines—is the irreplaceable foundation of a healthy smile.

Beyond the Clinic

While Dr. Scarlett maintains a stoic and focused professional demeanor, he is deeply driven by empathy and a commitment to the well-being of others. Outside of his practice, he is an accomplished photographer, an avid guitar player, and a student of music theory. He is also a passionate fitness enthusiast—when he isn’t with patients, you will likely find him in the weight room or logging miles on his bike, fueling the discipline and precision he brings back to his practice every day.
