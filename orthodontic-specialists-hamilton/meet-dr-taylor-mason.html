Dr. Taylor Mason

Education & Experience

Dr. Mason received his Bachelor of Science degree right down the road at Northern Kentucky University and his dental degree at the University of Louisville. He then joined the U.S. Army and completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency. While in the Army, Dr. Mason sent time in Colorado, upstate New York, and Afghanistan. He even got to perform dental work on the military service dogs.

Dr. Mason completed his orthodontic training at Indiana University and joined the Orthodontic Specialists team in 2022. He especially enjoys finding creative solutions to give patients the smile they’ve always wanted, including using specialized MARPE expanders to broaden smiles and improve breathing. He loves to learn, and he continues to enhance the depth and scope of his work by engaging in additional education courses.
