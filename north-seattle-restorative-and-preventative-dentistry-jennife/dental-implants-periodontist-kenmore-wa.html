Implants have been used for dental replacement teeth since 1950’s when the Anatomist Professor Per-Ingevar Branemark discovery that the optics he was using in rabbit fibulas were not removable, and he was the first to successfully secure a lower denture using intraosseous implants to replace a patient’s lower teeth.

Selection of the right quality of bone and angle for ideal tooth restoration is key. Patient overall health is also a factor. There are a myriad of choices your dentist is making along the way that can impact the results.

Torque to 15 Ncm / 35 Ncm

We to this day continue to use implants to secure full arch dental prostheses. This is a zirconia bar with multi-unit abutments. This is tightened into a patients mouth using a torque wrench 15 Ncm. This is secured with small 3mm screws which fit within 5mm larger screws to be secured in the mouth.
