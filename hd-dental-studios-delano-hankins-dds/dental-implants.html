When are dental implants necessary?

Dental implants are a reliable and effective solution for patients missing one or more teeth . Unlike dentures, bridges, or crowns, implants are placed directly into the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for artificial teeth. Additionally, implant-supported teeth look and feel more natural than other types of dental prosthetics .

Dental implants might seem better at first solely due to appearances, but they play a very important role in the functionality of your mouth as well. Dental implants support a healthy bite and keep each tooth properly in place while supporting a healthy jawbone and preventing bone deterioration. When a tooth is lost or pulled out, the jawbone will begin to deteriorate since it relies on the presence of teeth for its proper form. This usually happens because what preserves the jawbone are the stimuli of chewing which are provided by the teeth!

How Painful Are Dental Implants?

Many patients who have received dental implants say that there is little to no pain associated with the process. Local anesthesia can be used during it, and most dental implant recipients report that the procedure is less painful than a tooth extraction. If you experience any pain, excessive soreness, and discomfort after dental implant surgery is normal and typically goes away after a few days. How to Care for Dental Implants

After you have surgery, be cautious as you will be very sore. Once healed, treat your dental implants like you would a normal tooth. In order to take care of your dental implants, it’s recommended that you brush and floss at least twice a day regularly to prevent cavities and also rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash every morning.
