About Heath Dental Group

MEET DR. BILL

Dr. William Heath has been practicing dentistry for more than 30 years. A graduate of Brigham Young University and University of Washington School of Dentistry, Dr. Bill annually invests a minimum of 50 of hours annually in continuing dental education through his membership in the prestigious Spear Dental Faculty Club. Dr. Heath is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association and a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry.

A lifelong resident of Lompoc and Santa Barbara County, Dr. Bill serves a number of patients who have known him for decades. Committed to the health and vibrancy of the community at large, Dr. Bill has served as a youth sports coach, in his church’s leadership and as a member of the Lompoc School Board. In his free time, Dr. Bill enjoys hiking, golf, time with his family, baking pies and brewing homemade root beer.

“Dentistry is more than polishing, flossing, pulling and drilling — it’s about serving and treating each patient with focused attention to support their whole health. We make that a priority with every patient we serve.”

BILL HEATH, DDS
