Composite Bonding

Bonding is the quickest cosmetic fix we offer. We apply a tooth-colored composite resin directly to the tooth, shape it, and harden it with a curing light. The whole process usually takes 30 to 60 minutes per tooth, and there’s no lab work involved.

It’s less durable than veneers over the long term, but it’s also less expensive and requires little to no removal of your natural tooth structure. For many patients, that trade-off makes sense.

  • Small chips and cracks
  • Minor gaps
  • Reshaping uneven teeth
  • Covering stubborn stains on individual teeth

Tooth-Colored Fillings

If you need a filling — or want to replace an old metal one — we use composite resin that matches your natural tooth color. These fillings bond directly to the tooth, which means we can often preserve more of your healthy tooth structure than traditional amalgam fillings require.

They look better, they work well, and they’ve been the standard in our practice for years.
