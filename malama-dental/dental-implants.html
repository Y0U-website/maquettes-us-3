Dental Implants in Long Valley, NJ

Missing teeth can affect your smile, chewing, and confidence. Dental implants at Mālama Dental in Long Valley, NJ, provide a permanent, natural-looking solution. Implants restore your bite and prevent bone loss while blending seamlessly with your existing teeth. Our team works closely with you to design a custom treatment plan that lasts.

Process of Bad Breath Treatment

Initial Consultation: Includes a full exam, digital X-rays, and discussion of your treatment options and smile goals.

Implant Placement: The dental implant is precisely placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Healing and integration with the bone (osseointegration) takes several months.

Abutment and Crown: After healing, the abutment is attached, and impressions are taken to craft your final crown.

Final Fitting: The crown is secured and adjusted to ensure a comfortable, natural-looking result.

Next Steps

Follow-Up Care: Regular visits are essential to ensure the implant is healthy and stable.

Maintenance: We provide instructions on proper care, including brushing, flossing, and scheduling ongoing dental visits to protect your investment.
