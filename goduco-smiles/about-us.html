DENTIST IN VERNON HILLS

Dedicated dental office

You can receive advanced, personalized care at our dental office in Vernon Hills, IL. Dr. Philip V. Goduco is passionate about helping you and your family achieve beautiful smiles. He offers gentle, stress-free comprehensive examinations and cleanings as well as complex treatment to care for all of your unique needs.

What we do

Dr. Goduco truly loves his work. He finds deep fulfillment in helping his patients achieve lasting oral health and happy smiles. Dr. Goduco’s secondary goal is to change the way that people view dentistry. Many patients come to us with an unfavorable outlook on dental care because of previous experiences. Often, they believe that treatment will be highly unpleasant or embarrassing.

WE HAVE WHAT YOU NEED

A full range of treatments

Dr. Goduco and his team perform a complete range of dental treatments. While preventative care is a major focus of our practice, we also offer state-of-the-art restorative procedures. Dental implants are one of the most popular options. Dr. Goduco has an impressive background in implant dentistry. He can perform all aspects of your care here in our office, including bone grafting, implant placement, and restoration.

Similarly, Dr. Goduco also provides general treatments for the entire family. From gentle, reassuring care for your children, to dentures for our senior patients, we are able to treat the unique needs of every patient.

Dr. Goduco also proudly incorporates new, advanced techniques into his treatments to provide the best results possible. From the Chao Pinhole method to treat gum recession to advanced sleep apnea therapy, he can provide the complex care that you need. Dr. Goduco can also provide emergency services to help those who urgently require treatment.

Preventative care

Our team is dedicated to helping patients enjoy lasting oral health through compassionate preventative care and advanced procedures .
