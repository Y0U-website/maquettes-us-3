Signs You May Need Dental Implants

  • ✦ Single Gap — You lost one tooth to a fracture, decay, or an extraction, and you would rather not reshape the healthy teeth sitting on either side of it.
  • ✦ Unrestorable Tooth — A tooth is cracked below the gumline or too decayed to hold a crown, so replacing it beats spending more on a repair that is going to fail.
  • ✦ Loose Denture — An existing denture shifts around while you eat or talk, and implants can anchor it so that it stays where you put it every single day.
  • ✦ Shrinking Ridge — The jaw has begun narrowing where teeth were lost, which changes your profile and makes any future replacement considerably more complicated.
