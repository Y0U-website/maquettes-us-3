Step-by-Step Guide to Receiving Your Clear Aligners

Dr. Nickles will design your treatment plan custom to your needs and work with you to create a beautiful, healthy smile.

Here is the general plan to get you the smile you deserve:

Step 1: Schedule your consultation with Dr. Daniel Step 2: Pay for your treatment plan, financing is available; apply at carecredit.com (discounts available if you pay for your entire treatment in full) Step 3: Digital scan of your mouth, before, during, and after pictures will be taken, and send digital oral image will be sent to the lab Step 4: Your custom-made clear aligner straightening trays are made Step 5: Return in 2 weeks to receive your custom trays and start straightening your teeth Step 6: Visit Dr. Nickles for your regular appointments, and watch your smile transform

Take the first step by giving Dr. Daniel a call.
