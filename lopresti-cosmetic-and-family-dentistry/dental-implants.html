Dental Implants in Stamford, CT

  • How does the dental implant process work? Getting a dental implant begins with a comprehensive evaluation to determine bone health and overall suitability. Once approved, a small titanium post is surgically placed into the jaw to serve as a secure foundation. After a healing period that allows the implant to integrate with the bone, a custom abutment and crown are attached to restore function and appearance. Throughout the process, our team ensures clear communication, personalized care, and a comfortable treatment experience.
  • How much does a dental implant cost? Dental implant costs will depend on how many teeth you’re replacing, insurance coverage, and the complexity of each implant procedure. Typically, implants cost between $2,000 and $3,000 per tooth, excluding the abutment (the piece that connects the implant to the artificial tooth) and the artificial tooth itself. While out-of-pocket costs for patients with dental insurance vary depending on the particular plan, coverage, and limitations, we’re always happy to provide an estimate of cost based on those factors just after your consultation appointment.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Some dental insurance plans may cover a portion of the cost of dental implants, particularly if they are considered medically necessary. However, coverage will vary depending on your insurance provider and the specific details of your policy. Please verify your coverage with your insurance carrier.
