Meet Dr. Chen

Dr. Amanda Chen

Board Certified Orthodontist

Compassionate Orthodontist at Gold River Orthodontics

Educational Foundation and Specialized Training

Dr. Chen’s commitment to orthodontics is affirmed by her Board Certification from the American Board of Orthodontics and memberships in several professional organizations, including the American Association of Orthodontics , American Dental Association , California Dental Association , Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists , and the Sacramento Dental Society.

Dr. Chen and her team at Gold River Orthodontics prioritize patient comfort and communication. From initial consultations to follow-up care, they ensure each visit is informative and tailored to individual needs, fostering an environment where patients and families are actively involved in the treatment process. Gold River Orthodontics is equipped with digital solutions to patient care which further enhance quality of care.

Outside of her professional duties, Dr. Chen cherishes traveling and playing golf with her family, gardening, and actively participating in her daughter’s sports endeavors. These personal interests and family activities reflect her well-rounded character and commitment to family values and living with a purpose.

Dr. Amanda Chen, through her extensive education, specialized practice, and patient- first approach has established a standard of excellence at Gold River Orthodontics. Her professional achievements and personal hobbies intertwine to paint a picture of a dedicated, compassionate, and skilled orthodontist committed to her patients, family, and ongoing professional development.

Professional Recognition

International College of Dentists Special Recognition Award

Dr. Amanda Chen recently received a Special Recognition Award from the International College of Dentists (ICD) for her editorial work on The Nugget’s November/December 2025 issue, Mommy Brain. The Nugget is the award-winning journal published bimonthly by the Sacramento District Dental Society.

We are here to help you.

At Gold River Orthodontics, our mission revolves around you, the patient, and ensuring your optimal oral health and a confident smile.

We provide a variety of orthodontic solutions including traditional metal braces, esthetic ceramic braces, and Invisalign® clear aligners to meet the diverse needs and preferences of our patients. Besides traditional orthodontic treatment, we provide surgical orthodontic treatment for those who have jaw imbalances which could lead to a myriad of dental and/or functional dysfunction.

Typically, we schedule regular appointments every 4-6 weeks to adjust braces and monitor progress. However, the frequency can vary depending on your specific treatment plan.

If you experience any discomfort or a breakage with your braces or aligners, please call us immediately at (916) 942-9006. We will provide you with immediate advice and arrange an appointment if necessary.

Orthodontic treatment times vary depending on the complexity of the case, but most patients wear braces for about 1 to 3 years. Invisalign® treatments may be shorter, depending on the alignment issues being addressed.

Yes, we welcome you to schedule a consultation for your child. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic evaluation no later than age 7.

Yes, we offer various financing options to make orthodontic care accessible for all our patients. Our staff can help tailor a payment plan that fits your budget.

We provide orthodontic treatment for all age groups, from children and teenagers to adults. We specialize in treating patients with special needs such as Autism, ADHD, ADD, Cerebral Palsy, Down syndrome, sensory processing disorder, dental anxiety or apprehension, and other medically complexed health condition.

We will provide you with comprehensive care instructions for braces or aligners, including tips on brushing, flossing, and recommended dietary changes to protect your orthodontic appliances and maintain oral health.

Retainers are custom-made devices designed to keep teeth in their new position after braces or Invisalign are removed. A ll orthodontic patients are recommended to wear retainers to maintain their new smile for life.
