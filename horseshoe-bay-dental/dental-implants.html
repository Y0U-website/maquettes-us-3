Dental Implants in Horseshoe Bay, TX

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring your smile. An implant consists of a small titanium post that is placed in the jawbone, where it acts like a natural tooth root. Over time, the bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Dental implants can help improve chewing, speaking, and overall comfort while preventing bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth, helping you regain confidence and enjoy everyday activities with a healthy, complete smile.

FAQ

  • How long does it take to heal after dental implants? Most patients can return to normal activities within a few days after dental implant surgery, but complete healing and integration with the jawbone typically takes three to six months. Every smile heals differently, and your dentist will guide you through each step of the process.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for many years and may last a lifetime.
  • How many dental implants can be done at once? Dental implants can replace one missing tooth, several teeth, or even a full arch. Depending on your oral health and treatment plan, multiple implants may be placed during a single visit, helping restore your smile, comfort, and confidence more efficiently.
