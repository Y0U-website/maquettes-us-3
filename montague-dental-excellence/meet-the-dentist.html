What Is Advanced Training in Prosthodontics?

Advanced training in prosthodontics focuses on restoring and replacing teeth — including crowns, bridges, implant-supported dentures, implants, bite function, and full-smile rehabilitation.

How Dr. Jain can help

From routine care to complex smile reconstruction, Dr. Jain provides comprehensive treatment for patients at every stage. Whether you need a first exam and cleaning, a single crown, or a coordinated plan involving several implants and restorations, her training allows her to manage the full scope of your care directly, without needing to refer more complex cases elsewhere. This continuity means fewer providers to coordinate with and a treatment plan built around a complete picture of your oral health.
