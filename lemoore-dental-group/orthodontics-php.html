How Invisalign Works

3D Imaging

Advanced 3-D computer-imaging technology prepares your entire treatment plan from current positioning to your final smile.

Custom Aligners

Clear thermoplastic aligners are created, similar to teeth-whitening trays, custom made to exactly fit your teeth.

Progressive Treatment

You replace aligners with a new set about every two weeks and progress through treatment for about a year.

The Convenience Factor

Aligners are easily removable to eat and drink what you want, with no food restrictions. Keeping your teeth clean is just as easy as before treatment.

Boosting Your Confidence

No one will know you’re straightening your teeth because the clarity of the aligners renders them almost invisible! Comfortable and discreet treatment.

iTero™ Digital Scanning

Our iTero™ intra-oral scanner makes accurate digital impressions, eliminating uncomfortable tray and putty impressions for better accuracy.

Traditional Orthodontics

A skinny metal wire known as an archwire connects to each bracket. Using tension, our high-tech memory wires place pressure on your teeth to move them into place.

Colorful (or clear) elastic bands are stretched to hold the archwires to the brackets. As adjustments are needed, archwires of various diameters are tensioned and ligature elastics are changed.
