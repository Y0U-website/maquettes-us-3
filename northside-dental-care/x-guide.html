X-Guide

This technology helps guide the dental implant exactly where it should be placed.

  • See the position of the implant in real time during surgery
  • Place implants with improved accuracy

How X-Guide Technology Works

The process starts with 3D imaging of your jaw using a Cone Beam CT scan .

This scan creates a detailed view of your teeth, bone, and surrounding structures.

Using this information, our dentists can:

During surgery, the X-Guide system provides live guidance , showing the dentist exactly where the implant is being placed.

  • Plan the ideal location for the implant
  • Determine the correct angle and depth
  • Prepare for a precise implant placement
