The practice

We have more than two years advanced training (beyond dental school) to make us the perfect partner for your dentist to help save your teeth. We have over 50 years of experience combined. We are highly skilled in performing complex treatments and complete thousands of endodontic procedures a year.

We use state-of-the-art technology such as operating microscopes, CBCT (3-D imaging), digital imaging, and ultrasonic instrumentation, to treat patients quickly and comfortably.

We limit our practice to endodontic treatment so that we can be efficient and precise for our patients. This equates to positive experiences and faster healing. We are committed to helping maintain your natural smile, so we use advanced techniques to ensure comfortable and successful procedures.
