While attending dental school, I knew that I would eventually return home to Hamlet to practice dentistry. Although I loved living in Eastern North Carolina (Go Pirates!), I knew that my ultimate goal was to return to Hamlet so that I could care for the community that allowed me to become who I am today.

Being a dentist allows me to practice a career that I love every single day; however, my desire is to be more than a general dentist in my community. To me, my ultimate calling is to care for my patients while fostering trust and life-long relationships. Although I am proud to have earned the title of Dr. Henry, I also want my patients to know Kennedi, too.
