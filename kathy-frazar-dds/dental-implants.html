What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that mimic the natural structure of a tooth. Each implant consists of a post that replaces the tooth root and a custom crown or restoration that completes your smile. At The Houston Dentists , we use metal-free zirconia implants , which offer a biocompatible, highly aesthetic alternative to traditional titanium options. Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, implants provide a stable, long-term solution for a healthier, more complete smile.

Natural-Looking Results Zirconia implants are white, not gray—so there’s no risk of metal showing through the gums. This makes them ideal for the front of the mouth or patients with thin gum tissue.

Biocompatible and Metal-Free

Zirconia is hypoallergenic and resistant to corrosion, making it a safe choice for patients with metal sensitivities or those seeking a holistic approach to tooth replacement.

Long-Lasting Stability

Implants fuse with your bone to create a durable foundation that can last for decades. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth or adhesives.

Single Tooth Implants

If you’re missing one tooth, a single zirconia implant topped with a custom crown restores your smile without affecting the surrounding teeth.

Implant Bridges

For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can replace several teeth using just a few implants—eliminating the need for a removable partial denture.

Full-Arch Implant Restorations

If you’re missing all of your teeth in the upper or lower jaw, zirconia implants can support a full set of fixed teeth. This modern solution is more stable and natural-feeling than traditional dentures.
