Impressionless scanning is included in the cost of your orthodontic treatment. There is no additional charge for this comfortable alternative to traditional impressions. Our investment in this technology is part of our commitment to your comfort. Questions about insurance or pricing? Contact us !

5-Step Process for Getting Started With Impressionless Scanning in New Albany

Schedule Your Appointment: Reach out through phone, email, or our website to set up your scanning appointment.

Settle In: When you arrive, you’ll sit in one of our comfortable treatment chairs while our trained technician explains the simple process.

Watch Your Smile Take Shape: Our technician will use a small, wand-like scanner (no uncomfortable trays!) to capture images of your teeth from different angles. You can watch on the screen as your 3D smile model builds in real-time.

Quick and Easy: The entire scanning process typically takes just 3-4 minutes. Our technician will capture your upper teeth, lower teeth, and your bite pattern with smooth, practiced movements.

Automatic Processing: Your digital scan is automatically sent to our lab partner, who will use the precise 3D scans to create your custom retainers, aligners, or other orthodontic appliances.

How Impressionless Scanning Fits Into Your Treatment

Digital scanning isn’t a one-time event it’s a flexible tool we use at multiple stages to ensure the best possible outcome:

Initial Treatment Planning (Invisalign Patients)

If you choose Invisalign treatment , your first iTero scan captures the starting point of your smile. This scan goes to Invisalign, where Dr. Maple then creates your customized treatment plan using the precise 3D model.

Mid-Treatment Adjustments

If an aligner isn’t fitting quite right or your treatment needs adjustment, we can quickly rescan and order refinements. With Invisalign treatment, these rescans are typically included in your treatment package no additional scans needed, just better results.

Custom Appliances & Special Cases

For patients needing specialized orthodontic appliances, we can send your iTero scan to multiple labs, not just Invisalign. This flexibility ensures you get the right appliance from the right manufacturer every time.

Lost or Damaged Retainers

Because your scan is stored digitally, we can order replacement retainers without bringing you back in for new impressions though we recommend coming in to ensure your teeth haven’t shifted.

Retainer Creation

Here’s where digital scanning for braces really shines: we can scan your teeth before your traditional braces are removed. This allows us to potentially have your retainers ready the same day your braces come off, creating a seamless transition.

Frequently Asked Questions About Digital Scanning for Braces and Invisalign

  • Does digital scanning hurt? No, the scanning process is completely painless. A small handheld device simply takes pictures of your teeth without touching them.
  • How long does the scanning process take? The entire scanning process typically takes about 3-4 minutes to complete.
  • Is there any radiation involved in the scanning? No, there is no radiation involved. Unlike X-rays, the iTero scanner uses optical technology to capture images of your teeth.
  • How long until I receive my appliance after scanning? After your scan is complete, it typically takes 7-10 business days for retainers or other appliances to be ready.
  • Can I see my teeth during the scanning process? Yes! One of the benefits of digital scanning is that you can watch the 3D model of your teeth being built on the screen in real-time as the scanner captures images.
  • I have a strong gag reflex. Will I have problems with the iTero scanner? No! This is one of the biggest benefits of iTero scanning. The scanner wand doesn’t trigger gag reflexes the way traditional impression trays do. The wand stays away from the back of your throat, and you can breathe normally throughout the entire process. We’ve successfully scanned patients who could never tolerate traditional impressions.
  • How accurate is iTero compared to traditional impressions? Our process for iTero in New Albany is more accurate than traditional impressions. The digital technology captures details and dimensions that traditional impression materials often miss or distort. In our experience, the appliances created from iTero scans fit better and require fewer adjustments than those made from traditional impressions.
  • Do you ever still use traditional impressions? In rare situations where time is critical and a patient is comfortable with traditional impressions, we may offer it as an option to start treatment faster. However, we always give you the choice, and about 80 % of our impressions are now digital. Our goal is always your comfort and the best possible outcome.
