Meet our Doctors

Dr. Walter Machowski Jr. received his undergraduate degree from Wofford College in 1977. He later received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical University of South Carolina, College of Dental Medicine in 1982.

Dr. Walter has been serving as a General Dentist in Myrtle Beach, SC since 1982 in two practices with his wife, Dr. Sarah Godbold-Machowski, DMD. Prior to his election to the State Board of Dentistry, he taught senior Dental students from MUSC on performing restorative procedures for indigent patients for the James B. Edwards College of Dental Medicine at Horry Georgetown Technical College.

Dr. Sarah Godbold-Machowski graduated from University of South Carolina-Coastal Carolina University in 1978 with her undergraduate degree. She then went on to MUSC and received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 1984.

Dr. Sarah is a member of the American Dental Association, South Carolina Dental Association, and Grand Strand Dental Society. She is a Membership Chairman and a Delegate and Alternate Delegate of SCDA for the Pee Dee District. She also serves as a Chairman for the Pierre Fouchard Society and has been a Board Member for the SCDA Foundation since 2015.

Both Dr. Sarah and Dr. Walter have volunteered with SOS in Myrtle Beach treating emergency patients. As well as giving time to Give Kids A Smile, Christina’s Smile, Dental Access Days(DAD), and Donated Dental Services. Their practice has been restoring implants since 1988, using digital radiology for the past 15 years, and has been utilizing Cerec crown and bridge for the past 10 years.
