General & Family Dentistry

To properly combat tooth decay and gum disease, we advise having a professional dental cleaning at least once every six months. With thorough brushing and flossing at home, our skilled hygienists can remove plaque that is difficult to reach and accumulates over time, leading to long-term dental health problems.

Make sure you practice proper hygiene at home and use a fluoride-containing toothpaste and ultrasonic toothbrush for a better experience when visiting our office. We’ll notice, we promise!

Digital dental x-rays will be taken externally (extraoral) or intraorally (intraoral). We only use the best, latest equipment in-office. The most often utilized x-rays are intraoral digital dental x-rays, which offer a wealth of information regarding the state of your teeth right now.

The most common symptom of tooth decay is that dental cavities harm millions of people yearly. They appear as a result of bacteria that thrive in the plaque that builds up on our teeth feasting on the sugar that remains after our meals. The acid produced by this bacterium begins to erode at the enameled surfaces of teeth. Cavities are easily treated, but they can be prevented by brushing, flossing, and maintaining good oral hygiene.

Dental crowns might be your best alternative if you have teeth that require significant repairs due to severe cracks or damage. Artificial caps called dental crowns are shaped to fit over fractured teeth. Using a cast or a 3D image of your tooth, we may create a white dental crown from porcelain or metal that looks natural and harmonizes with the rest of your smile. We make all of our crowns in-house, so there’s less waiting.

Endodontics, commonly known as a root canal, is a typical dental operation when a tooth typically needs to be extracted due to severe tooth decay or dental trauma that destroys the soft tissue at the center of our teeth, known as pulp. The pulp is a highly sensitive bundle of blood vessels and nerves that, if exposed, can cause excruciating discomfort or even migraines. Millions of teeth are saved each year thanks to modern root canal procedures, which are identical to having a filling in terms of comfort.

A denture or partial is one of the many replacement options for missing teeth. Teeth replacement can significantly affect your facial esthetics and restore your ability to chew. You’ll immediately seem younger thanks to a full or partial denture, which can also help you feel more confident to smile.

Gleason Dental Clinic also offers some limited Orthodontics. Give us a call for details.

Cosmetic Dentistry

An alternative to conventional metal braces that effectively straightens teeth with removable bespoke plastic trays without the unsightly appearance of traditional metal braces.

Our premium porcelain veneers can fix various dental problems, such as chipped, crooked, misaligned, and cracked teeth, as well as any aesthetically unappealing deformation or discoloration that may be entirely out of your control. This will boost your confidence by giving you the impression of a completely redone and stunning smile.

Our high-quality, custom-built dental implants gently insert an artificial root into your jaw’s bone structure before supporting your replacement tooth. Your smile will be identical to your original teeth.

We provide economical professional in-office whitening procedures that can completely remove any surface stains from your teeth and make your smile more radiant.

Teeth contouring is one of the easiest, most affordable methods for improving chipped, uneven, or poorly aligned teeth to produce a more appealing smile. We frequently combine shaping or contouring teeth with a procedure known as bonding, which involves coating the teeth with resin to enhance their overall appearance.
