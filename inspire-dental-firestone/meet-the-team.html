Meet Dr. Nataliya Korman

Dr. Korman is a native of Ukraine and is a second-generation dentist. Dr. Korman worked in her mother’s dental practice and it’s there that she fell in love with dentistry and knew it was her chosen career path. Dr. Korman received her first doctorate dental degree in Ukraine followed by a dental degree (DDS) from University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine. She is a member of the American Academy of General Dentistry, Colorado Dental Association, Metropolitan Denver Dental Society.

Dr. Korman has over a decade experience practicing dentistry in Colorado, providing high-quality, personalized dental care to her patients. She recently acquired Inspire Dental where she looks to continue to create an environment that is built on personal relationships and quality dentistry with her patients. Dr. Korman prides herself on having a conservative treatment approach and is looking forward to sharing her gentle care and years of advance training with her patients.

In her spare time Dr. Korman enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, cooking and exploring Colorado.

Dr. Korman is proud to contribute to the local community. Her philosophy is simple: dentistry is not just about teeth—it’s about people, trust, and the joy of seeing someone smile with confidence.

Dr. Korman is looking forward to meeting you and your family!

Meet Christi Cook, Registered Dental Hygienist

I volunteer locally with Colorado PTA, KIND (Kids in Need of Dentistry), Give Kids A Smile Day, and the Colorado Mission of Mercy. I went on my first dental mission trip in 2019 with the Belize Mission Project, which was so humbling and transforming.

I am passionate about keeping up on new research and technology to provide the best possible care and prevention in recognizing the oral-systemic link to overall health. I believe it is important to provide comprehensive oral cancer screenings and periodontal assessments at dental hygiene appointments. I am also a stickler for following infection control protocols. My loyal patients have described me as “gentle, but thorough”.

I have had almost every procedure known to dentistry done in my mouth, so I can empathize and answer any questions!

Outside of the office, I enjoy crafting, decorating, and musicals. My husband and I have three grown children. They have recently blessed us with three grandsons, who are the joy of our life.
