Cosmetic Dentistry

At Goodwin Dental, we not only care for the health of your teeth but also how you feel about your smile. If you are not happy with how your teeth look we can help with a range of cosmetic options to help you achieve the smile you desire.

Enhancing your smile can be accomplished in several ways:

Teeth Whitening

Discolored or stained teeth can be brightened using in-house treatments or with take-home custom trays. Most patients see an improvement in color by at least two shades of white. We use professional grade Venus Whitening services.

Crowns & Veneers

Crowns are a cap placed over a tooth to improve its appearance or protect the rest of the tooth from further damage. Veneers shield discoloration and imperfections using thin, custom-made porcelain shells which cover the front of the teeth.

Dental Implants

Missing teeth are replaced with a custom artificial tooth and anchored in place by an implant. The implant will match the color and appearance of surrounding teeth giving you a natural smile.

Dentures

Dentures are personalized, artificial teeth and gums that are used to replace lost and removed teeth. They can easily be taken out for cleaning and you can have a full or partial set of dentures.

Bonding

Improve the appearance of chipped, broken, cracked, stained, or uneven spaced teeth by bonding a matching colored material to the surface of the tooth.

Tooth reshaping

Also known as dental contouring, the appearance of teeth can be improved by the modification or removal of some the tooth enamel. This treatment is often combined with bonding.

Crown lengthening

When there is more gum than tooth showing (“gummy smiles”) or an uneven gum line, excess tissue can be removed to expose more of the tooth’s crown, creating an appearance of longer teeth.
