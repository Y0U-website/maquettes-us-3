Innovative Dentistry In Your Neighborhood

Dr. Lemon started his practice in Aberdeen in 1999. With the help of his dedicated, friendly, and knowledgeable staff the Aberdeen office has grown to include all the modern technology you would expect in a large city right here in Aberdeen. Serving communities in Aberdeen, Hoquiam, Montesano, Ocean Shores, Pacific Beach, Elma, and Westport. Lemon Family Dental a hometown dentist in Aberdeen.

There are three elements at the heart of Dr. Lemon’s dental practice; his compassionate approach to patient relationships, his comprehensive range of dental services, and the modern technology he uses to ensure that every procedure he performs is as stress-free and comfortable as possible for his patients.

TA native Washingtonian, Dr. Lemon obtained his B.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Washington in 1990. After working as a systems analyst for Andersen Consulting and Nike, Inc., he pursued a career in dentistry and graduated from Oregon Health Sciences University in 1999.

Dr. Lemon started his private practice in Aberdeen in July of 1999 and strives to provide the best possible dental health care for his patients. With the help of his dedicated team, his practice has grown to include another office in Olympia. Dr Lemon is a member of the American Dental Association, the Washington State Dental Association, and the Thurston-Mason Counties Dental Society

Dr. Lemon’s main focus centers around his family: His wife, Patty of 27 years, and their three children, Fiona, Phoebe, and Kai. Their free time is filled with bike rides, fitness activities, and exploring cultural foods.
