Are full arch implants a good alternative to dentures?

Yes, full arch implants in Sweetwater are a good alternative to traditional dentures. They offer a permanent solution for people with missing teeth. Traditional dentures come with challenges. They may slip or shift while you are eating or laughing, and require dealing with adhesives. Many people who use dentures follow a more restrictive diet, as they are concerned about biting into hard or sticky foods.

How Full Arch Implants Work

A full arch implant involves your dentist using several strategically placed titanium posts, anchored directly into your jaw, to act as tooth roots. Over time, these posts fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration (which also promotes jawbone health ). Once you’ve healed after surgery, you’ll have a custom-designed restoration (denture) attached permanently to your implants.

This is a stable, fixed restoration that stays in your mouth permanently and offers excellent stability and functionality. You won’t have to worry about your dentures moving around when biting into an apple, and you’ll never have to remove them at night or use adhesive.

Will my speech change with my new full-arch hybrid dentures?

Your speech may change slightly after getting full-arch hybrid dentures in Sweetwater, but this is generally temporary, and most people adjust quickly . The adjustment period is different for everyone and depends on the particulars of each person’s oral health and the type of implant they receive. What is the Hybrid Denture Process Like?

Your journey to a permanent smile begins with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Moore and our experienced team.

Consultation & Pre-Op

We ll take photos, x-rays, and digital scans to collect all the records we need. We ll discuss your smile goals what you like or don’t like about your current teeth or dentures and create a clear vision of how you want your final smile to look. We ll also walk through finances in detail so that before you leave, you’ll know exactly what the process involves, how long it will take, what it costs, and how you can pay for it.

The Procedure: Get Your Temporary Smile in Days

Once you are ready, we ll schedule your surgery date. The procedure takes place over two days: On Wednesday, we place your dental implants, and on Thursday, we deliver your first screw-retained temporary hybrid denture. You’ll wear this temporary for a few months while your gums heal and swelling goes down.

Healing & Feedback

The healing period is crucial for bone integration, where your jawbone fuses with the implants to create a stable foundation. During this time, you can give us feedback on the look of your new smile things like tooth shape, length, and color so when we place your final hybrid denture (usually 4 6 months later), it’s exactly the way you want it.
