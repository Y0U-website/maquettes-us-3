0 Meet our team Dr. Matthew & Dr. Meredith McCracken Dr. Matt and Dr. Meredith McCracken are both Tuscaloosa natives, graduating from Tuscaloosa County High School in 1992 and from the University of Alabama in 1997. Both received their Bachelor of Science degree in biology and attended the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry, graduating in 2002. They have been in general practice together since 2002. Matt and Meredith have four kids, two sets of twins: Maxwell and Matthew, and Mitchell and Mary Elizabeth. The family are members of the First Methodist Church.

They have many pets. Dogs: Johnny, June, and Jolene; Cats: Dolly, Minnie Pearl, Emme Lou, Kenny, Waylon, Patsy, and Fatty Cat; Pacman frog: Jamal; and Gecko: Rango.
