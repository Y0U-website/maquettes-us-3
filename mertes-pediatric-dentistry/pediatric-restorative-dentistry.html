Pediatric Pulpotomies

If your child is experiencing tooth discomfort, the good news is that there are several ways Dr. Greg can help restore their comfort and preserve their smile. A pulpotomy is just one tool in our comprehensive treatment toolkit - sometimes it&#x27;s the perfect solution, and sometimes a different approach works better for your child&#x27;s particular situation.

What sets our practice apart is Dr. Greg&#x27;s commitment to really listening to both you and your child before suggesting any treatment. He&#x27;ll take the time to understand what&#x27;s been bothering your child, examine the affected tooth carefully, and then walk you through the options that make the most sense.

Pediatric Extractions

Nobody wants to hear that their child might need a tooth extraction, and honestly, neither do we. Dr. Greg&#x27;s first instinct is always to save teeth whenever possible - that&#x27;s why he&#x27;ll explore every conservative option before suggesting an extraction. But here&#x27;s the reality: sometimes removing a tooth is actually the kindest thing we can do for a child&#x27;s overall oral health and comfort.

Maybe a tooth has been severely damaged by trauma, or perhaps it&#x27;s preventing other teeth from coming in properly. Whatever the reason, if Dr. Greg recommends an extraction, it&#x27;s because he genuinely believes it&#x27;s your child&#x27;s best path forward. The good news? Kids are remarkably resilient, and with our gentle approach and comfort options, most children bounce back quickly and feel much better afterward.

Pediatric Frenectomies

That tiny piece of tissue under your baby&#x27;s tongue or lip might seem insignificant, but it can actually cause some pretty big challenges. If you&#x27;re dealing with breastfeeding difficulties, speech concerns, or other issues that might be related to a tight frenulum, a frenectomy could be a game-changer for your family.

Dr. Greg has helped many families navigate this decision, and he understands it&#x27;s not always clear-cut. During your consultation, he&#x27;ll assess whether your child&#x27;s frenulum is actually causing problems or if other factors might be at play. The procedure itself is quite straightforward when it&#x27;s needed, but the key is determining whether it&#x27;s truly the right solution for your child&#x27;s specific situation.

Pediatric Sedation

Some kids hop right into the dental chair without a care in the world, while others need a little extra help feeling comfortable during their visit. If your child falls into that second category, you&#x27;re definitely not alone - and there&#x27;s absolutely nothing wrong with needing some additional support.

Pediatric Dental Crowns

We know that your child being happy and healthy is the most important thing, and when they get little bumps in the road, it can be concerning! If your little one needs a solution for damaged or decayed teeth, pediatric dental crowns might be just what Dr. Greg recommends.

For front teeth, we offer beautiful white crowns that are specifically designed to restore those tiny teeth to their natural function and appearance. For back teeth, we have both stainless steel and white crown options available, and Dr. Greg will discuss which type makes the most sense for your child&#x27;s specific situation. With the right crown choice, your child can say goodbye to discomfort and enjoy a durable solution that will keep their smile functioning well until those baby teeth are ready to fall out naturally.

As always, Dr. Greg will thoroughly explain why a pediatric crown might be the best option for your child and walk you through all the details so you feel confident about the treatment plan.
