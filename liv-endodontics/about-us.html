Delivering expert, compassionate endodontic care to help you feel better, faster.

At Liv Endodontics, we believe that endodontic care can be both highly advanced and deeply personal. Our mission is to help patients return to life without dental pain while making the experience as smooth and supportive as possible.

With modern practices in Livingston and Summit, NJ, and a dedicated team of specialists and staff, we proudly serve patients across our local communities and beyond.

Our team

At Liv Endodontics, our team brings together highly trained endodontists and a caring support staff. We share a commitment to clinical excellence and patient-centered care. Whether you are here for a root canal or a more complex procedure, you will be treated with respect, compassion, and personalized attention every step of the way.

Our

Liv Endodontics was founded to change the way patients experience root canal treatment. Too often, patients associate endodontics with fear and discomfort. We are here to change that story by combining the best of modern clinical techniques with a warm, welcoming patient experience.

From a single location, our practice has grown to serve patients in both Livingston and Summit. We work closely with referring dentists throughout New Jersey, maintaining strong relationships and seamless communication to support your care journey.

We have invested in advanced technology such as CBCT imaging, operating microscopes, and EdgePRO laser therapy to ensure that every treatment we provide is precise, efficient, and gentle.

At Liv Endodontics, we understand that no two patients are alike. That is why we take time to listen, answer your questions, and tailor every aspect of your treatment to your individual needs.

Expertise that you can trust Our endodontists bring extensive training and clinical experience to every case. We are committed to delivering the highest level of care for every patient.

Same-day treatment for urgent care We know that dental pain can disrupt your life. Whenever possible, we offer same-day appointments to provide fast relief.

Technology that enhances outcomes Our practices are equipped with state-of-the-art tools, including 3D imaging, laser therapy, and microscopes in every treatment room, to ensure exceptional accuracy and results.

A focus on your comfort From your first contact with our office to the completion of your treatment, your comfort is our top priority. We take a calm, supportive approach and move at your pace so you can feel at ease throughout your care.
