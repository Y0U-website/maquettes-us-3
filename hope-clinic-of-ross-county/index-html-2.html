Our mission.

The mission of Hope Clinic is to share the love of Christ by providing basic, sound, quality medical care at no cost to those who have no health insurance in a climate that is accepting, authentic, and inviting.

Who do we serve?

Anyone who does not have health insurance and has an income that falls within 300 % of the federal poverty guidelines can visit the clinic. Children as well as adults are welcomed.

What we are not.

We are not to be a replacement for a primary care doctor or for services such as prenatal care, vaccinations, lab or X-rays, laceration repair, casting, emergency services, sexual assault, STD’s, trauma/MVA, acute psychosis/psychiatric disorders, overdoses or ingestions. We do not dispense narcotics.
