How is a Tooth Implant Done?

Each patient is unique and will receive a course of treatment designed specifically for their situation. What we describe here is one of several options available, and can be generally explained. Through an initial complimentary consultation with a dental implant dentist , we can better determine and explain the implant procedure that is specific to you.

Before the Implant Procedure

One of our Denver prosthodontists will examine your mouth and take one or more X-rays of the area. This will help him determine the optimal placement of the single dental implant and prepare for the procedure.

Tooth Implant Installation

A temporary tooth is provided, allowing you to normally eat and function almost immediately. Your implant will need a few months to heal and integrate with the jawbone before the permanent porcelain crown can be attached.

Dental Crown Attachment

The final step in the single-tooth implant procedure is the placement of the permanent ceramic tooth on the tooth implant. The new tooth is installed for life with no additional treatment needed.

Aftercare of Tooth Implant

Once your permanent crown is placed on the dental implant, you should expect the new tooth to fit, look, and function like a natural tooth. Your regular dental hygiene routine should be sufficient to keep the tooth and gum around it clean and avoid dental implant pain .

How Long Does a Tooth Implant Take?

The timeline for a dental implant for one tooth varies based on individual circumstances but generally involves three stages. The initial procedure, where the implant is placed, takes about 1-2 hours. After placement, healing takes approximately 3-6 months to allow for osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the jawbone. Once healing is complete, placing the permanent crown takes another 1-2 weeks. Overall, the one-tooth implant process typically takes 4-6 months, depending on your unique needs and healing time.

How Much Does a Single Tooth Implant Cost?

The cost of a single tooth implant can vary depending on several factors, such as the materials used, the complexity of the procedure, and any additional treatments required. On average, a single-tooth implant typically costs between $3,100 and $5,800. This estimate includes the implant, abutment, crown, and any necessary preparatory procedures, such as extractions or bone grafting.

Additional One Tooth Implant Costs

While the primary cost of the implant is for the implant, abutment, and crown, there may be additional expenses. These can include:

  • Tooth Extractions: $200 to $600 depending on the tooth position.
  • Bone Grafting: $200 to $3,000 based on complexity.
  • Other Procedures: 3D CT scans, anesthesia, and gum grafting for aesthetic improvement may also increase the cost.

Are Single Dental Implants Covered by Insurance?

Dental implants for one tooth are generally not fully covered by insurance as they are considered a cosmetic procedure. However, many insurance plans offer partial coverage, especially if the implant is medically necessary. You may find that your insurance covers part of the single implant procedure, such as the initial consultation, X-rays, or even the crown.
