Children Are A Treasure

At Little Treasures Pediatric Dentistry, you can be confident in the dental office you have chosen for your child. From your first visit, we want you to feel welcomed and well cared for.

Dr. Kalai and his staff promote preventative dental care including a healthy diet and great oral hygiene at every visit. Because of this, we love to have parents with their children during their first visit. This gives us the opportunity to educate you as well so you can help reinforce these healthy habits at home.

Our Mission

Meet Dr. Ronen Kalai

After several years of practicing general dentistry, I realized my true passion was treating children. But I didn’t want to just “learn on the job”, I wanted to be trained by specialists to become a specialist. So, I went back to school to receive formal training. I had the privilege of completing my postgraduate training at the prestigious Maimonides Medical Center, in New York, where I was honored as the “Chief Resident” for my graduating class.

I then went on to pass additional and optional exams, to become board certified and receive my status as a Diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

During my years of practicing pediatric dentistry, I have been fortunate to have practiced in various parts of the country, including New York and Florida. And after finally settling down back here in California with my wife and daughter, I realized how valuable my experiences have been with patients from all backgrounds and cultures.

Having the ability to communicate in Spanish, Hebrew, and Farsi, I often love to speak with my patients in those languages when preferable to them.
