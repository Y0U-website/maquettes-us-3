Insurance

We accept most dental insurance programs, including non-managed care, indemnity, and PPO out-of-network. Our team will prepare the forms but remember that your policy is an agreement between you and your insurance company. You are responsible for any costs your insurance does not pay. We can submit pre-treatment estimates to your insurance company so you can know if it will be covered and how much you may owe.

Kleer®

Kleer is a membership program designed for individuals who do not have dental insurance. With Kleer, patients can enjoy access to affordable dental care at participating dental practices with a simple monthly or annual fee.
