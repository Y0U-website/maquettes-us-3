The practice

We use the best technology available to provide the most comfortable and efficient care for our patients. We utilize innovations including Temporary Anchorage Devices, Dental Laser, self-ligating brackets and indirect bonding to minimize treatment time.

We strive to provide our patients with treatment that fits their budget. To this end, we offer payment plans with no-interest financing and monthly payments. We always discuss procedures and their fees with our patients before starting treatment.

Dr. Kothari has been practicing orthodontics in the Chicago area since 1984. He was a Clinical Professor of Orthodontics at the University of Illinois College of Dentistry from 2003 to 2013. He has spoken at orthodontic conferences around the world and his articles have been published in orthodontic journals.

We treat patients of all ages, from children and teenagers to adults. In fact, we have treated several patients as children who have later returned to our office for their children’s treatment! For our adult patients, we offer clear ceramic brackets which are less noticeable during their time in braces.
