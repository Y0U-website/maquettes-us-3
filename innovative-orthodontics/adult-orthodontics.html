Adult Orthodontic Treatment

Discreet & Modern Treatment Options for Adults We understand that our adult patients have unique needs and priorities. That’s why we specialize in aesthetic options that deliver incredible results without disrupting your life.

  • Improve Oral Health: Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. A properly aligned bite can also alleviate jaw pain and uneven wear.
  • Discreet Treatment Options: With modern solutions like clear braces and Invisalign®, you can straighten your teeth without the look of traditional metal braces.
  • Achieve Lasting Results: It’s never too late to achieve a healthy, functional, and beautiful smile that will last a lifetime.
  • Invisalign® Clear Aligners: A series of nearly invisible, removable aligners that straighten your teeth without anyone knowing. Enjoy the freedom to eat what you want and maintain your oral hygiene routine with ease.
  • Clear Braces: These ceramic brackets blend with the natural color of your teeth, offering the powerful, reliable results of braces with a much less noticeable appearance.

Your Journey to a New Smile

Our process is designed to be straightforward, comfortable, and respectful of your time. We combine expert care with a welcoming atmosphere to make your experience a positive one.

The first step is a simple conversation. We’ll discuss your goals, perform a thorough exam using our iTero® digital scanner, and outline the best treatment options for you.

Whether you choose Invisalign® or clear braces, our team will ensure a perfect fit and provide you with all the information you need for a successful journey.

You’ll have periodic check-ins with our team to monitor your progress. These appointments are efficient and ensure your treatment stays on track toward a fantastic result.
