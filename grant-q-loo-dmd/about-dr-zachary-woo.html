About Dr. Zachary Woo

Dr. Zachary Woo is an Evans, GA native, graduating from Greenbrier High School. He continued his academic pursuits at The University of Georgia, where he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology.

His passion for dentistry led him to the Dental College of Georgia at Augusta University, where he graduated in 2021. Following graduation, he embarked on a two-year General Practice Residency at DCG, specializing in a wide range of dental procedures, including IV sedation, wisdom tooth extractions, dental implants, and providing comprehensive care to medically compromised patients.

Dr. Woo is a member of several esteemed dental organizations, including the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the International Congress of Implantologists, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Georgia Dental Association, and the Hinman Dental Society. These affiliations reflect his commitment to staying current with the latest advancements in the field of dentistry and delivering the highest standard of care to his patients.

On a personal note, Dr. Woo resides in Augusta with fiancé, Summer, and their loyal four-legged companion, Cooper. Growing up as the eldest of five boys, family has been an integral part of his life. In his free time, he enjoys traveling with Summer, teeing off for a round of golf with his brothers, or cheering enthusiastically for the University of Georgia football team. These experiences highlight how important it is to have balance and happiness on his journey to becoming a top-notch dentist.

Your smile and oral health is Dr. Woo’s top priority, and we can’t wait to be part of your journey to achieving and maintaining excellent oral health.
