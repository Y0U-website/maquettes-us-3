The practice

New patients are invited to schedule a one-hour consultation with our team to discuss their orthodontic concerns and learn what treatment options are available.

Dr. Zach and Dr. Alex will provide details of what treatment method (Invisalign , braces, early interceptive Phase I treatment) would be best and answer any questions. Adults, teens, and children are welcome in our Fairlawn and Medina practice. Virtual consultations are also available.

Dr. Zach and Dr. Alex are in the TOP 1 % of Invisalign providers WORLDWIDE due to their extensive knowledge and experience using this aligner system. In their capable hands most patients can choose Invisalign treatment - even if they had been told they were not good candidates for Invisalign elsewhere.

Traditional braces - using brackets and wires - are a great option for giving you a wonderful smile. Patients of any age who prefer braces for their orthodontic treatment may choose from clear, silver, or gold brackets. Clear, silver, or gold braces all yield the same result while offering an aesthetic choice to our patients.

An early phase of orthodontic treatment - or an interceptive phase - may be recommended for younger children (ages 7-10) as a means to allow adult teeth to erupt properly and prevent more complicated treatment. Your child does not need a referral for an evaluation. Orthodontic evaluations are recommended at age 7 years old by the American Association of Orthodontists.
