Personalized Treatment Plans

Whether you need dental crowns and bridges or aligners, we can provide personalized treatment recommendations, including:

SureSmile Orthodontic Aligners

At James W. Rehm DMD, LLC, we provide orthodontic aligners by SureSmile. Using the latest technology and highest-quality materials, SureSmile aligners effectively fix teeth that need improvement in their position. Read More These clear aligners are custom-made trays that treat mild-to-moderate cosmetic issues.

Custom Home Whitening Trays

We offer safe and effective custom home whitening trays made from molds of your teeth and the most effective whitening gel available. The process is quick: after making molds of your teeth, the trays are ready within four days. Read More Simply fill the trays with gel and wear them for half an hour each day for a week. Avoiding staining agents like coffee, wine, and tea ensures optimal results.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are the solution if you have worn, misshapen, or uneven teeth but prefer to avoid braces. We bond these thin ceramic shells to the front of your teeth, providing a beautifully straight smile without the inconvenience of braces. Read More They’re durable, easy to care for, and can last many years with proper home care and regular check-ups.

Tooth-Colored Bonding and Fillings

Dental bonding offers an alternative to veneers and effectively restores chipped, cracked, discolored, or misaligned teeth. Our doctor uses composite resins matched to your natural tooth color to seamlessly repair your smile. Read More After lightly etching the tooth’s surface and applying bonding liquid, a plastic resin is sculpted to shape, trimmed, smoothed, and polished for a natural-looking finish. While durable, bonding may require repair every three to five years, as it’s not as strong as natural enamel.
