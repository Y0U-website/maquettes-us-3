Welcome To Highland Family Dentistry

Creating Healthy, Beautiful Smiles: Meet Dr. Brianda Culley

For Dr. Brianda Culley (formerly Hernandez), dentistry is more than just a profession—it’s a passion for combining advanced science with art to deliver life-changing results.

A Foundation of Excellence

After earning her bachelor’s degree in Biology (with minors in Chemistry and Spanish) from The University of Texas Pan-American in 2008, Dr. Culley pursued her dental education at the top-tier Texas A&M Health Science Center Baylor College of Dentistry , graduating with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) in 2013.

The Power of Advanced Training

Dr. Culley believes that continuous learning is essential for patient care. She has deliberately sought out cutting-edge, specialized training to bring the best techniques directly to you. This advanced commitment includes:

She consistently exceeds state requirements for continuing education, ensuring she is expertly versed in the newest technologies in cosmetic dentistry, restorative techniques, and high-tech imaging. When you choose Dr. Culley, you’re choosing a provider whose commitment to ongoing education means a smarter, safer, and more comfortable experience for your smile.

  • A 2-year post-graduate course through the Academy of GP Orthodontics.
  • Advanced certifications in Facial Esthetics and Laser Surgery from organizations like the American Board of Laser Surgery, learning directly from global leaders such as Dr. Robert Convissar.
