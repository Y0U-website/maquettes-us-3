About Us

Compassionate Dental Care for the Whole Family in Jackson, Tennessee!

Hub City Family Dentistry provides exceptional dental care to families and individuals alike. Our dedicated team understands the importance of a healthy smile, which is why we strive to create a welcoming environment that caters to patients of all ages. Committed to addressing a wide range of dental needs, from routine check-ups to advanced procedures, we prioritize patient comfort and satisfaction. At Hub City Family Dentistry, you can trust that we will handle your dental concerns with precision and care, allowing you to focus on enjoying your healthy, beautiful smile.

Meet Dr. Logan Schmidt

Logan Schmidt graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Biology degree from the University of Central Arkansas in 2013. He continued his education and graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S) from Texas A&M University, College of Dentistry in 2017.

Dr. Schmidt participates in continuing education courses for Oral Surgery, Endodontics, and Oral Pathology. He is a member of the the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Schmidt has been married to his wife, Chelsea since 2013 and they have 2 sons. In his free time, he enjoys hiking, reading, traveling, cooking, and relaxing with his family.
