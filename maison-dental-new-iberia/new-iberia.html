Questions About Our New Iberia Office

Are you accepting new patients at your New Iberia location?

Yes, we are currently accepting new patients at 600 Vicnaire St. You can call us at (337) 367-8910 to schedule, or use the contact form above and we’ll reach out to confirm your appointment.

Does your new location also offer sedation dentistry?

Yes. Both Dr. Murray and Dr. Mullen completed advanced training in IV sedation during their AEGD residencies. If you have dental anxiety or need a longer procedure, our sedation dentistry options can make your visit much more comfortable. Call us to discuss which option fits your situation.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We are in-network with Delta Dental and the Zelis umbrella network, which covers many common plans. We also offer CareCredit, Cherry financing, and an in-house membership plan. The fastest way to confirm coverage is to call (337) 367-8910 before your visit.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my appointment?

We understand schedules change and request at least 24 hours’ notice for cancellations or rescheduling. This allows us to offer your appointment time to other patients who may need care. Same-day cancellations may result in a fee, but we work with patients when emergencies or illness occur.
