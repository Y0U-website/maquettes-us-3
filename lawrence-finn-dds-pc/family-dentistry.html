Family Dentistry We love to treat families. Our services incorporate care for both adults and children.

TMJ Therapy Niteguard and bite therapy this treatment includes dysfunction of the jaw joint or muscles due to grinding, incorrect bite, and other factors. Biteguard therapy is usually involved.

Root Canals Root canals are procedures meant to repair a decaying or infected tooth. This procedure can help prevent the development of an abscess, which can create a number of harmful complications. You will be comfortable throughout the entire procedure.

Oral Surgery Oral surgery encompasses a wide range of procedures, including extractions and wisdom teeth removal, and most can be performed within our office during your visit.

Dentures • Partial Dentures • Bridgework • Implants These are procedures used to replace missing teeth, they come in many different designs, some removable, and others permanently affixed. We custom design your case according to what you feel is appropriate.

Specialty Dentures and Partial Dentures These can be special attachment dentures or partial dentures retained with implants or crowns and precision attachments. We can also use implants to help retain loose dentures and partial dentures.

Periodontal Treatment Gingivitis This entails treatment for mild to advanced gum disease. We treat all stages.

Pregnancy and Oral Health Care We take pride in keeping you and your baby healthy. Periodontal disease has been linked to preterm and low birth weight babies. Maintaining oral health is of prime importance.

Sterilization and Disinfection We strictly adhere to OSHA standards.

Snap-On Smiles We are an original provider of this service since its inception

Sensitive Teeth We provide relief for teeth that are sensitive to hot, cold and sweets. This problem can be eliminated or reduced.

Emergencies Immediate emergency care same day appointments.
