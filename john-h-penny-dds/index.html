Dr. John Penny graduated from Seminole High School, right here in Pinellas County, before attending the University of South Florida for his undergraduate education. He continued on to the Medical College of Virginia at Virginia Commonwealth University, earning his Doctorate of Dental Surgery there.

Dr. Penny is dedicated to keeping abreast of the ever-changing world of dentistry, as new technology emerges all the time. He understands the vital importance of keeping up to date, and is able to use the latest techniques and equipment to ensure that his patients have access to the latest diagnostic and preventive treatment methods.

Originally from Lake Charles, LA, Dr. Penny has lived in St. Pete Beach since 1976. He and his wife have raised a son and two daughters, and their home is now full with six dogs, three cats, a parrot, and three horses. Dr. Penny enjoys spending his free time outdoors, whether with his family or fishing, playing tennis, or scuba diving.
