Dr. Peri

Alexandra Peri, DDS

Patient care has always been extremely important to Dr. Peri, and that’s why she created MI-nt Dental. MI-nt Dental is a family practice where you can receive compassionate care in a boutique, spa-like comfortable atmosphere. With Dr. Peri’s passion for creating beautiful smiles for her patients, you can feel confident that you are receiving the best care at MI-nt Dental.

In her free time, Dr. Peri loves to spend time with her loved ones as family is very important to her. She is married to her wonderful husband, Brandon, and they share an adorable son, Joey. Their family is rounded out with some Siberian cats, Madison and Edison, and a Pomeranian, Teddy. In the summer, you can find Dr. Peri enjoying time outside at the lakes in her favorite state, Michigan.
