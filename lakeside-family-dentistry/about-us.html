About Us

Our practice strives itself in being the most comfortable dental experience for all of our patients. With staff that have worked at this location for over a decade, we know our patients well. We view and treat our patients and co-workers as family. Our goal is to ensure everyone leaves our office with a bigger smile than they came in with.

In her free time, Dr. Flowers enjoys spending time with her two young daughters, dog, and husband. She loves fishing and crabbing in local coastal watersheds, mountain biking, running, hiking, mushroom hunting and traveling.
