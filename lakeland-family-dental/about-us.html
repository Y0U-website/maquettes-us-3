Our Team

Made up of compassionate, friendly people who treat their patients like friends, the Lakeland Family Dental team is our greatest asset! Our team members have served our patients for many years, are CPR certified, and their passion for excellence shows in their beaming smiles and on the smiles of our patients’ faces. We seek ongoing training and professional development opportunities to keep our skills fresh. Most of all, we create a positive, fun environment that translates to happy patients.

Dr. Samantha Ruiz

Dr. Samantha Ruiz, also known as "Dr. Sam", knew she wanted to be a dentist from an early age.

She grew up in Kenosha and, after graduating high school, attended Marquette University for nearly a decade. She earned her biological sciences degree from Marquette in 2007 and her DDS in 2010.

Dr. Sam is a member of the American Dental Association, Wisconsin Dental Association, Waukesha County Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, and the Lake Country Spear Study Club. She is also a certified Invisalign provider. Dr. Sam has past experience serving on the InSession Planning Committee for the WDA, and currently serves in the ADA s Advisory Circle.

Professionally, Dr. Sam considers patient comfort and satisfaction during dental visits to be her top priority. One of her greatest enjoyments in practicing dentistry is witnessing a person s confidence blossom as they learn to show off a beautiful new smile, sometimes after years of being embarrassed about their teeth. Dr. Sam also places great importance on keeping her dental education up to date by attending continuing education courses.

Dr. Sam and her husband (Dr. Ruiz) live in Oconomowoc, and love everything about Lake Country. In her free time, Dr. Sam enjoys hiking, days out on the lake, traveling, and photography. You can see some of her photos adorning the walls at Lakeland Family Dental!

Dr. Eric Ruiz

Dr. Eric Ruiz ("Dr. Ruiz"), received his BA in molecular and cellular biology from the University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign. He went on to obtain his DDS from the Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2010.

Dr. Ruiz is a certified Invisalign provider and a member of the American Dental Association, Wisconsin Dental Association, Waukesha County Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, Lake Country Spear Study Club, and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Ruiz is passionate about serving his patients, whether it’s curing a painful toothache or restoring a beautiful smile. He truly enjoys interacting with so many unique people every day. He enjoys all aspects of dentistry and is always trying to further his knowledge with continuing education.

On a community level, he is a board member of the Oconomowoc Rotary as well as the Lake Area Free Clinic.

Originally from New Windsor, Illinois, Dr. Ruiz moved to Wisconsin to attend dental school, where he met his wife (Dr. Sam). After graduation, they moved to Oconomowoc, and have enjoyed calling it home ever since. Dr. Ruiz is a huge sports fan and enjoys running, biking, and playing guitar in his spare time.

Peggy

Originally from Prairie du Chien, Peggy has spent 3 decades in the dental field so far, with over 20 of those as a registered dental hygienist at our practice.

Elizabeth ("Liz")

Elizabeth, fondly known as "Liz" in our office, has a passion for dental hygiene!
