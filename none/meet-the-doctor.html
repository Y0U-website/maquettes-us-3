Dr. Richard Guzha, DDS

Dr. Richard Guzha has devoted a vast amount of time and effort into serving the greater area of Washington state.

Since 1998, he has established himself as a valued member of the community in Lakewood, Puyallup and Tacoma, Washington, where he strives to provide all of his patients with affordable dental care.

Dr. Guzha provides comprehensive care to meet the needs of each of his patients, and he utilizes the best resources at his disposal to ensure they receive high-quality care.
