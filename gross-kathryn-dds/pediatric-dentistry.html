Dentistry for Kids at New Horizons Dental

At New Horizons Dental, we believe that pediatric dentistry is about more than just healthy teeth. It’s about building confidence, creating positive experiences, and setting children up for a lifetime of good oral health. From the moment your little one’s first tooth appears to the time their permanent smile is fully in place, our team at New Horizons Dental is here to guide your family every step of the way.

Building Healthy Smiles from the Very Beginning

Pediatric dental care starts earlier than most parents expect. Even before teeth arrive, it’s a good idea to gently clean your baby’s gums with a clean, damp cloth after feedings. Once that first tiny tooth erupts, it’s time to introduce a soft-bristled toothbrush and a pediatrician-approved toothpaste.

Why does this matter so early? Because the health of baby teeth and gums has a direct impact on the adult teeth developing underneath. Healthy baby teeth help children chew, speak clearly, and hold space in the jaw for permanent teeth to come in properly. Here’s a general timeline of what to expect:

  • The two bottom front teeth typically erupt around 6 to 8 months
  • The four upper front teeth usually follow shortly after
  • The remaining baby teeth appear in pairs along the sides of the jaw
  • All 20 baby teeth are generally present by age 2.5
  • The first permanent teeth usually begin erupting between ages 5 and 6
  • Baby teeth gradually fall out and are replaced by adult teeth through the early teen years

Your Child’s First Dental Visit

We recommend scheduling your child’s first official dental visit shortly after their third birthday. The goal of this visit is simple: make it comfortable, positive, and low-pressure.

You can help prepare your child at home by incorporating regular brushing and flossing into their routine, reading books about going to the pediatric dentist Salina relies on, or even bringing them along to your own appointments so they can get familiar with our office, our team, and the friendly faces they’ll see at each visit.

The first appointment is typically short and relaxed. It usually includes a gentle examination of the teeth and gums, X-rays if appropriate to check on developing permanent teeth, a light cleaning if your child is comfortable, and a fluoride treatment to help protect against cavities.

Parents are welcome to stay with their child at the start of the visit, though we may gently encourage some one-on-one time between your child and our team. This helps children build independence and feel more at ease on their own during future visits.

Cavity Prevention and At-Home Care

Keeping kids cavity-free takes a team effort between our office and your home routine. One of the most effective tools we offer is dental sealants . These thin, protective coatings are applied quickly and painlessly to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, dramatically reducing the risk of decay in those hard-to-reach grooves.

During each visit, our hygienists work with children to review and reinforce proper brushing and flossing technique. We make it fun and age-appropriate so that kids walk out feeling motivated rather than lectured. We also discuss fluoride use at home to make sure your child is getting the protection they need between visits.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pediatric Dentistry

When should my child have their first dental visit?

We recommend a child’s first visit shortly after their third birthday, or earlier if you notice any concerns with the teeth or gums. Starting early helps establish a healthy routine and a positive relationship with dental care.

How often should kids come in for checkups?

Most children benefit from visits every six months. Regular checkups allow our team to monitor development, clean the teeth, apply preventive treatments , and address any small issues before they become bigger ones.

Are dental X-rays safe for children?

Yes. We use modern digital X-ray technology that significantly reduces radiation exposure compared to older film-based systems. X-rays are an important tool for checking on permanent teeth developing below the gumline and detecting decay between teeth.

What if my child is nervous about the dentist?

That’s completely normal, and we’re well-practiced at helping anxious kids feel comfortable. From our gentle approach to our welcoming team, we do everything we can to make each visit a good one. For children with significant anxiety, we also offer sedation dentistry options. Just ask us about what might work best for your child.
