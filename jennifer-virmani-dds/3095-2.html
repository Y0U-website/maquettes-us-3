Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

Before the Procedure Begins

How long does it take to heal from dental implant surgery?

Dental implants are custom-made to fit your mouth comfortably, so they look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. Contrary to popular belief, dental implant surgery is not painful because of the nerve-numbing anesthetic your dentist gives you before the procedure.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Dental implants are the only dental restoration option that preserves natural bone, actually helping to stimulate bone growth. The time the procedure takes depends on how many implants you need.

If you only need one implant, the procedure may only take about 30 minutes. A more complex surgery involving more than one implant may take around four hours.

The Dental Implant Recovery Timeline

It may take several months for the implant to heal properly and effectively fuse to your jawbone.

Four different factors can affect your dental implant recovery timeline:

  • Your current dental health
  • How many teeth need replacement
  • The location of the missing teeth
  • If you need an extraction before the procedure

What is a dental implant?

Tooth Replacement

A dental implant is a metal post inserted into the jaw bone to replace the root of a tooth. It is often the most ideal option for replacing teeth, as they provide the most natural tooth replacement option available. In addition to looking natural, dental implants are strong and have the same look and feel of your real teeth. They also are very safe, helping to prevent further tooth loss.
