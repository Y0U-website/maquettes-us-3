Six Month Smiles

Do you want a new, straighter smile but you do not want to spend a year or longer in aligners or braces? Six Months Smiles is designed for people who want to quickly straighten crooked teeth for an improved smile appearance. Our dental team at Liberty Lake Smile Source offers Six Month Smiles as one of our orthodontic treatments, available at our dental center in Liberty Lake, WA.

What is Six Month Smiles? This orthodontic treatment uses braces or aligners to adjust and straighten the visible teeth in about six months. The difference between this treatment and traditional aligners or braces is the teeth affected. Six Month Smiles focuses on the front teeth, not adjusting the back molars, which take longer to shift. Six Month Smiles is geared toward adults that want to straighten teeth for a more attractive smile, versus teens or adults with severe malocclusions or bite misalignments.

Straight Teeth In Six Months

Six Months Smiles offers a faster way to achieve a straight smile. The clear braces or aligners can fix gapped, crowded or crooked teeth, improving the appearance and health of your smile. The treatment is available for patients 16 years of age or older that have mild to moderate orthodontic issues affecting the front teeth. The braces or aligners are discreet, using clear plastic or components to make them almost invisible. The average treatment time is six months, so some people may get results in less or more than six months.

Six Month Smiles is a great option for many patients that want to improve their smile without years of wearing braces or aligners. Not only is the treatment time shorter, but it is usually more cost-effective, too.
