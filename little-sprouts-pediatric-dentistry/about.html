About Our Practice

Board-certified pediatric dentist

Dr. Jonathan McNair

Dr. Jonathan McNair&#x27;s path to pediatric dentistry is rooted in his childhood experiences in Ocean Springs, Mississippi. As the youngest of seven and an uncle to 10, he developed a knack for working with kids from an early age.

Growing up, Dr. McNair was interested in everything from sports and nature to science and math. His journey into dentistry began unexpectedly at 14, when a landscaping client became his mentor and inspired his future career.

Dr. McNair&#x27;s dedication to his craft is reflected in his extensive education: a Biology degree from Millsaps College, a Master&#x27;s in Biomedical Science, a D.D.S. from the University of Tennessee, and advanced training in Pediatric Dentistry at NYU Langone Health. He is proud to be a board-certified pediatric dentist, providing children with the highest level of care.

At Little Sprouts Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. McNair combines his expertise, passion for working with children, and warm personality to create positive dental experiences. For him, it&#x27;s not just about dental care – it&#x27;s about nurturing healthy smiles and confident kids for years to come.

Outside the office, Dr. McNair is an avid movie lover, artist, anime fan, and food enthusiast. He loves traveling, staying active, and spending time with family and friends. But what truly drives him is his commitment to continuous learning and finding better ways to serve his young patients.

We&#x27;ve designed your child&#x27;s first visit to be a fun, stress-free introduction to dental care. You&#x27;ll be greeted by our friendly team and given a tour of our office.
