1. What is cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry is a branch of the dental field that focuses on improving appearance by restoring function and health while maintaining your natural smile. Cosmetic procedures vary from simple whitening to porcelain crowns, bridges, or Invisalign ®.

Cosmetic dentistry is often confused with regular dental care, but the two are very different. Cosmetic procedures focus on improving your smile and appearance, while regular dental treatment focuses on maintaining healthy teeth and gums.
