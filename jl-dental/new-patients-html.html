New Patient Information

Welcome to JL Dental! Here, Dr. Jonathan and Dr. Laura Mears combine family-friend service and world-class dental care to ensure everyone in Arnold can have a healthy, beautiful smile. If you’re considering coming to see us, then you’ve come to the right place! Below, you’ll find all the information you need before scheduling your first appointment. If you have any questions once you’ve read through everything, be sure to give us a call. We can’t wait to meet you and your family!

Your First Visit

You’ll get to meet one of our highly-skilled hygienists who will give you a brief exam, and then they will take a set of x-rays as well as few images with an intraoral camera. They’ll then go over these images with you and let you ask any questions you might have. After a thorough cleaning, Dr. Jonathan or Dr. Laura will come in, do their own exam, and discuss any treatments you might need. If you come to see us with a pressing concern such as a toothache, however, we’ll try to do the treatment the same day if possible.

New Patient Forms

Because we understand the importance that convenience plays in family dentistry, we have our new patient forms here for you to print and fill out prior to your first visit. Please feel free to do so at your leisure, saving yourself the time and hassle in the waiting room during your appointment.

Dental Insurance Welcome

At JL Dental, we believe that excellent dentistry should also come at an excellent value. With us, you won’t have to sacrifice your wallet in the name of quality, because we’re more than happy to accept your dental insurance. While our focus is always on providing you the best possible care and not just the most cost-effective, our team of experts will always work to help you get the most out of your benefits at every visit.

Affordable Financing Available

Financing is also available to make your dental care even easier on your budget. We accept both CareCredit and CitiHealth Card. These are third-party financing options that offer various payment plans for flexibility and peace of mind.
