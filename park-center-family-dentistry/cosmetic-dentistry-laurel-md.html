Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are one of the most versatile tools in cosmetic dentistry. These thin, custom-crafted ceramic shells are bonded to the front surfaces of your teeth to correct a wide range of concerns, including chips, cracks, gaps, discoloration, and minor misalignment. Each veneer is individually designed and shade-matched to complement your facial features and create a harmonious, natural-looking smile. With proper care, porcelain veneers can provide beautiful results that last for many years.

Aesthetic Bonding

Dental bonding is a conservative and cost-effective way to improve the appearance of your teeth. Using a tooth-colored composite resin, Dr. Tran can repair chips, close small gaps, reshape uneven teeth, and cover stubborn stains — often in a single visit. The composite material is carefully sculpted and polished to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth, giving you a natural and refreshed look without the need for more extensive treatment. Aesthetic bonding is an excellent option for patients who want noticeable improvement with minimal preparation.
