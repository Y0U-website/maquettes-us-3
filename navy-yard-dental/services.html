Services

Dr. Jones and his caring team uses the latest technology to create beautiful and healthy smiles! We are pleased to offer our patients the below services in a friendly and comfortable environment.

The team at Navy Yard Dental has only one goal, and that has to do with the question How can we make you smile today? We help patients achieve and maintain great oral healthy through offering a variety of dental procedures to accommodate the entire family. Whether you are four or 104, you will find we can tailor our services to meet your needs.

All procedures are done in a clean, safe environment using sterilized tools. The Navy Yard Dental team genuinely cares about your oral health needs, making sure to pay special attention to any requests you have. We offer financing options and referral incentives, which our staff can explain in detail, and can help you choose which option best fits your situation.

In the event of a dental emergency, we have staff on standby to attend to your needs as they arise.

If you need a dentist who is committed to excellence and provides the highest level of care, give Navy Yard Dental a call today to schedule an appointment with Dr. Jones. We promise to make you smile.
