Our goal

We strive to make the first appointment comfortable for you and your family. At your complimentary consultation, we will happily provide a tour of the office, diagnostic records, and a thorough discussion with the doctor regarding treatment needs. If orthodontic treatment is recommended, we will walk you through the next steps regarding scheduling and payment options.

Regardless of the specifics of your appointment, you’ll always be in great hands. The Graber & Gyllenhaal Orthodontics team includes Academy of Orthodontic Assisting certified orthodontic assistants who are consistently completing continuing education. Together with our friendly Treatment Coordinators and administrative team, we’ll make your visit as enjoyable and informative as possible.

Have follow-up questions?

When treatment is recommended we customarily will have a second consultation to review your specific needs and indications. These consultations can also be done virtually to accommodate parents, guardians, or spouses who cannot be present in the office.

Financial & Insurance Information

At Graber & Gyllenhaal Orthodontics, we believe that every patient deserves high-quality orthodontic treatment and we do our best to make orthodontic treatment affordable and accessible to all. We offer a complimentary benefits check and a variety of payment options to fit your family needs so you can be on your way to a great smile.

Your Neighborhood Orthodontists

At Graber & Gyllenhaal Orthodontics we see a variety of patients. Those with personal dental goals, metal or plastic allergies and any other special needs are always welcome here. We’re proud to offer special programs like our Hero Program, honoring those who serve our community—teachers, military, firefighters, and police officers.

Come see us at either of our convenient Chicago-area locations in Vernon Hills and Glenview , Illinois.
