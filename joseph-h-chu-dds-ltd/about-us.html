Meet the Team

Dr. Chu s visionary leadership led to him establishing Kauai Endodontics in 2010, after graduating from New York University Dental School and specializing at Lutheran Medical Center. He has served as President of the Honolulu County Dental Society and the Kauai County Dental Society, showcasing his expertise and commitment to the dental community.

Dr. Chu is dedicated to professional advancement, volunteering as an advisor for the University of Hawaii Pre-Dental Club and teaching at esteemed institutions. Currently, he contributes to the development and regulation of dental practices as a member of the Hawaii State Board of Dentistry.

Beyond our local community, Dr. Chu s impact extends through meaningful medical missions, providing vital dental care in the Philippines, Nepal, and Burma.

​Dr. Marin is a board-certified endodontist, holding Diplomate status with the American Board of Endodontics, and brings a wealth of expertise in advanced endodontic care. Dr. Marin earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Loma Linda University, graduating cum laude, followed by specialized training in Endodontics at New York University College of Dentistry. His passion for patient care is reflected in his contributions to research, education, and leadership roles within the field.

With extensive experience in both civilian and military dentistry, including serving as Division Officer of Dental Specialties in Japan, Dr. Marin has demonstrated a commitment to excellence in endodontic treatment. Additionally, his dedication to public health shines through his involvement in outreach programs that provide vital dental care to underserved communities.

Dr. Marin’s expertise, credentials, years of experience and leadership are valuable assets to our clinic. He is here to serve, treat, learn, share, grow within our community and he is a wonderful addition to the care you receive here at Kauai Endodontics!.
