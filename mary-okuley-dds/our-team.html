Our Mission

Our mission is to provide specialized and comprehensive care for children from infancy through young adulthood, and those patients with special needs. We do our best to build trust and respect in a caring and nurturing environment. We strive to achieve optimum oral health through prevention and education first, and intervention, second. We want to make every experience a positive one so that a lifetime of good oral health may be obtained. Our practice philosophy is to treat and protect every child as if they were our own.

Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Mary Okuley

Dr. Mary Okuley is originally from northwest Ohio. She earned a bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from the Ohio University. She then attended the Ohio State University where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery. Dr. Mary’s lighthearted mannerism and passion for treating children led her to complete a 24-month residency in pediatric dentistry at Temple University Hospital. Upon completion of her specialization she made Greenville her home.

She is currently a diplomat* of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the South Carolina Dental Society, and the South Carolina Society of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Mary is committed to constant improvement through continued education in the application of the latest techniques. In her free time, Dr. Mary enjoys spending time with her husband and her young children.

*As a diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Mary has voluntarily proven she possesses the requisite knowledge, skills, and experience for delivering pediatric dental care at the highest level.
