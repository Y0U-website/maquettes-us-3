Aligners & Invisalign Fittings

  • Treatment Duration Typically, the Invisalign treatment at Neibaur Family Orthodontics spans between 6 and 24 months, depending on individual needs.
  • Dietary Freedom With the discreetness of Invisalign, you’re free to enjoy any food of your choice. Although, as your treatment progresses, it’s advisable to moderate certain foods and drinks.
  • Compared to Metal Braces While metal braces, known for their strength, cater to even the most challenging cases of misalignment, their visual appeal often becomes a concern. This makes many patients at Neibaur Family Orthodontics lean towards aesthetically pleasing options like clear braces or Invisalign.
  • Comfort with Invisalign The aligners, tailored to perfection, are made of smooth and comfortable plastic. Many patients at Neibaur Family Orthodontics have found Invisalign to offer greater comfort than traditional braces, primarily due to its removability.

Is Invisalign Right For Me?

The aligners are removable, so you can take them out to eat and drink what you want during treatment. And you can also remove the aligners to brush and floss as you normally would for fresh breath and good oral hygiene.

For the best results, you should wear your retainers as directed. Not wearing your retainer can lead to shifting teeth and potentially ruin your results. Remember to remove your retainer before brushing, and brush both the inside and outside of the retainer before placing it back in your mouth. When not in use, place your retainer in its case and keep it away from pets or children.
