Accessibility Statement

  • Keyboard Navigation: Our website can be navigated using a keyboard, enabling users who cannot use a mouse to access information and services.
  • Readable Content: We ensure that text content is readable and understandable, using clear fonts and contrasting colors to enhance readability.
  • Consistent Layout: Our website maintains a consistent layout and predictable structure, making it easier for users to find the information they need.
  • Captioning and Audio Descriptions: Videos and live audio content include captions and audio descriptions, ensuring that users with hearing or visual impairments can access our multimedia content.

Feedback and Continuous Improvement

We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our website and services. We welcome feedback from our users on how we can enhance their experience. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at (641) 424-9398. Your input is invaluable in helping us serve you better.
