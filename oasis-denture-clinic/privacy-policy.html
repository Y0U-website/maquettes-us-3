Health Information and HIPAA

If you become a patient of Oasis Denture Clinic, certain dental and health information may be considered protected health information under HIPAA and Washington State privacy laws. Protected health information is used and disclosed only as allowed by law, including for treatment, payment, healthcare operations, or when otherwise required or permitted by law.

Our Notice of Privacy Practices provides full details on how we handle protected health information. As required by HIPAA, our Notice of Privacy Practices is available at our clinic and upon request.

Website Forms and Email Communication

Please avoid submitting detailed medical or dental information through general website forms or standard email unless specifically instructed by our clinic. While we take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website, standard email and online communication may not be fully secure. If you prefer, you may contact us by phone.

Text Messages and Email

If you choose to communicate with us by text message or email, you understand that these methods may not be encrypted. We may use text or email for appointment reminders, scheduling, billing, and care-related communication if you authorize us to do so. You may provide authorization by checking the consent box on our website forms or by contacting our clinic directly.

You may update your communication preferences at any time by contacting our clinic.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or similar services) to understand how visitors use the site and to improve website performance. This information may include pages visited, time spent on the site, general location information, browser type, and device information. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that visitor behavior on a healthcare provider website may in some circumstances constitute consumer health data under Washington law. We do not use analytics data to infer health conditions or to share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help operate our website and clinic systems, such as website hosting, scheduling, billing, payment processing, analytics, software, and IT support. When required by HIPAA, we enter into Business Associate Agreements with service providers who may access protected health information. All providers must agree to protect patient information and comply with applicable privacy and security requirements.
