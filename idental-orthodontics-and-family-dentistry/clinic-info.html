0 Clinic Information Our Mission iDental Orthodontics and Family Dentistry, founded by Vivek Vasudeva , is a modern, multi-specialty dental practice serving Racine, Kenosha, Milwaukee, and Walworth counties in Wisconsin. Built on a mission to support underserved families and children across Southeastern Wisconsin, iDental is committed to delivering comprehensive, high-quality care that remains accessible and affordable.

We offer a full range of services under one roof, including orthodontics, general dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery and implants , as well as next-day dentures . We are dedicated to helping working families throughout Wisconsin with flexible options, including the iDental Premier Plan for patients without insurance and additional discounts based on household income.

Our Story

At iDental, we are dedicated to making a positive difference in the lives of our patients by providing high-quality dental care to our community.

Walk-ins are welcome Monday to Thursday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM. Fridays and Saturdays are by appointment only.

We are located in the former Racine Dental location and feature state-of-the-art operatories, fully digital systems, computers in every operatory, digital X-rays, Dentrix software, and 44 operatories to serve our patients efficiently and comfortably.

We also accept Medicaid patients and provide special discounts for elderly who have no dental Medicaid coverage. We provide special discounts for the uninsured, and under insured in our community through our Premier Plan .
