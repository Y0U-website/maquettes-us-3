Teeth Cleaning

  • How Long Does a Routine Teeth Cleaning Take? Most routine cleanings take between 45 and 60 minutes from start to finish. If X-rays are taken or if there is significant buildup to address, the appointment may run a little longer.
  • Is a Deep Cleaning the Same as a Regular Cleaning? No, a deep cleaning is a more involved procedure than a standard cleaning. It removes bacteria and tartar from beneath the gumline and is typically recommended when signs of gum disease are present, rather than as part of routine preventive care.
  • Does Teeth Cleaning Hurt? For most patients, a routine cleaning is comfortable and causes little to no discomfort. Some gum sensitivity is possible when there is heavy buildup, but the team at John D. Mainwaring, DDS, prioritizes patient comfort throughout every procedure.
