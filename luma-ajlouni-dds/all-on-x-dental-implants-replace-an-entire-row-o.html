What Is Involved With Fixed Dentures and Snap-in Dentures?

Initial Assessment

  • An in-depth evaluation of your current oral health, jaw anatomy, and bite using visual exams, x-rays, and bone density scans.
  • Consideration of alternative options, especially for those who might not have the bone density required for traditional implants.
  • Comprehensive discussion to ensure the chosen treatment aligns with your aesthetic and functional preferences.

Advanced Imaging

  • Utilization of cone beam CT scans to obtain detailed views of the jaw structure.
  • Computer simulations that determine precise implant positions for All-on-X
  • Digital tools ensuring accuracy and enhancing the overall efficiency of the planned procedure.

Procedure

  • Our skilled dental team meticulously places titanium implants into the jawbone, following the pre-determined plan.
  • Specific anchor points are established to allow for the secure attachment of the dentures.
  • Depending on the stability and the patient’s oral health, temporary crowns or dentures might be attached immediately post-procedure.

Outcome
