Dr. Mario Abdennour is a microscope trained endodontist that specializes in conventional root canal therapy, endodontic re-treatment as well as surgical endodontics.

During his specialty program he also trained in Surgical Endodontics at the Karl Franz University Hospital in Austria. Upon graduation from Harvard, he was the class recipient of the Krakow award for clinical proficiency in Endodontics. Dr. Abdennour also conducted endodontic research at the Forsyth Dental institute in Boston contributing to the patent (USPTO) of an endodontic therapeutic agent. This research was sponsored by the Krakow Endodontic Fund.

For the last sixteen years, Dr. Abdennour has held a faculty appointment as a clinical instructor at the Advanced Graduate Endodontic Program at Harvard School of Dental Medicine where he teaches on a part time basis, surgical and non-surgical Endodontics. He also lectures Harvard Dental students annually as well as local components of State Dental Societies in the areas of Endodontic Surgery and Non-Vital Intracoronal Bleaching. Presentations also include live endodontic surgery at the Endodontic Program of the West Virginia University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Abdennour has served as the President and Chairman of two states component district dental societies and currently is the President of the Massachusetts Association of Endodontists, term expiring in 2018.

As a faculty member of Real World Endo’s Board of Advisors, Dr. Abdennour conducts lectures and seminars in English, French and Spanish in the United States, Canada and the caribbean on Precision-Based Endodontics.

Memberships include the American Association of Endodontists, Massachusetts Association of Endodontists, American Dental Association, Massachusetts Dental Society, Harvard Odontological Society and the American Academy of Dental Sciences.
