Gilroy Dental Services

At our Gilroy office, we believe your dental care should be as comprehensive as it is convenient. That’s why we offer a wide range of services under one roof. You can get everything you need for a healthy, beautiful smile without the stress of juggling multiple offices.

General Dentistry

Routine checkups and preventive care form the foundation of lifelong oral health. From cleanings and exams to fillings and gum care, our general dentistry services keep your smile strong and catch problems early before they become bigger (and more expensive) concerns.

Oral Surgery

When your smile needs advanced care, our dentists provide precise oral surgery in a safe, supportive environment. Whether it’s wisdom teeth removal, tooth extractions, or other surgical treatments, we’ll help you feel comfortable and cared for at every step.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is one of the first things people notice—and we love helping patients feel proud of theirs. From teeth whitening and veneers to Invisalign® clear aligners, our cosmetic dentistry options are designed to enhance your natural beauty and boost your confidence.

Your first visit experience

Your first appointment at our Gilroy dental office is all about making you feel comfortable, cared for, and confident about your dental health. We’ll take time to get to know you, listen to your concerns, and give you a clear picture of your oral health.

By the end of your first visit, you’ll have a healthy smile and peace of mind knowing you’ve found a dental team you can rely on.

  • Gum evaluation: We’ll carefully check for signs of gum disease and ensure your foundation for a healthy smile is strong.
  • Digital x-rays: With advanced imaging, we can see below the surface for a complete, detailed view of your teeth and jaw.
  • Dental cleaning: Professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar.
  • Professional cleaning: Our hygienists will gently remove plaque and tartar, leaving your smile fresh and polished.
  • Personalized consultation: Your dentist will walk you through your oral health status, treatment options, and next steps. You’ll have plenty of time to ask questions so you feel fully informed about risks, benefits, and alternatives.

Insurance & savings options at our Gilroy office

We believe everyone deserves access to great dental care. That’s why we accept all PPO insurance plans and offer an in-house Smile Savings Plan for patients without coverage. From routine checkups to advanced treatments, we’ll help make your care affordable and stress-free.
