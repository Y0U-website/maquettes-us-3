Which categories do you fall into?

Having A Broken Front Tooth

It is very common to have one broken front tooth. Usually, it is a tooth that already has a root canal, a crown or both. Sometimes, it happens because of a fall. Regardless, it is frustrated because you can’t smile!

In most of these cases it is ideal to have the dental implant placed at the same day the broken tooth, for many reasons.

  • Little to no pain. You can go back to work on the same day.
  • You have a new tooth same day, and no one noticed that you just broke a tooth.
  • You do not have to wear a temporary tooth that moves when you talk and has to be removed when you eat.
  • The result looks and feels natural.

Having Multiple Broken or missing teeth

We understand the stress and the dread that come with having multiple missing and broken teeth, from difficulty chewing to embarrassment, and even fear of seeing a dentist or being judged by a dentist.

If you finally have enough and want a change, we are here to help.

Whether you want to maintain your remaining teeth and replace only the missing ones, or if you know that none of your teeth can be saved, and you do not want to wear dentures for the rest of your life, we have a dental implant solution for you.

  • We will restore your mouth to a healthy state
  • Little to no pain during the procedures

  • No more floppy dentures or covering your smile while talking, laughing, or eating.
