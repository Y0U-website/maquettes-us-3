Meet Our Specialist

DR. NIRAV PAREKH

Suffolk University – Masters in Health Administration Licensed in CT

Nirav attended Suffolk University where he earned his Masters Degree in Health Administration. He continued his education at New York University College of Dentistry and received his General Dental Degree in 2014.

Following dental school, he joined Reliable Dental Care as an Associate General Dentist. He has earned certifications in Nitrous oxide administration, Infection control, child abuse awareness and CPR.

Nirav enjoys reading, the sport of cricket and qualified to play in the Chess National Championship in India. He lives with his wife, Madhavi, a fellow dentist.
