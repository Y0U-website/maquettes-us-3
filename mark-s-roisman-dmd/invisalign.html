Creating Your Custom Treatment Plan

At Mark S. Roisman, DMD, we use advanced technology to create a custom treatment plan tailored to your unique orthodontic needs. Our team will take digital impressions of your teeth and use specialized software to create a 3D model of your smile. This model will be used to plan the movement of your teeth and create a series of custom-made Invisalign aligners. With our expertise and state-of-the-art technology, you can trust that your treatment plan will be designed to achieve the best possible results.

How Invisalign Orthodontic Treatment Works

The Invisalign process begins with digital impressions. Dr. Roisman uses these to create a 3D model of your smile and plan how your teeth should move throughout your treatment. Invisalign is particularly effective for realigning crowded teeth, which can hinder effective brushing and flossing, leading to potential issues like tooth decay and gum disease. This plan is then sent to Invisalign, where your clear aligners are fabricated.

Once you have your aligners, you will switch to the next set about every two weeks, or as instructed by Dr. Roisman. You’ll visit our office every six weeks to ensure that your teeth are moving as planned. When treatment is complete, you’ll receive a set of Vivera retainers to make sure your beautiful new smile lasts a lifetime.

Wearing and Caring for Your Invisalign Aligners

To get the most out of your Invisalign treatment, it’s essential to wear your aligners for at least 20-22 hours a day. You can remove them for eating, brushing, and flossing, but be sure to clean them regularly to prevent bacterial buildup. We recommend using a soft-bristled toothbrush and mild soap to clean your aligners. It’s also important to store them in a protective case when not in use to prevent damage or loss. By following these simple care instructions, you can ensure that your Invisalign aligners remain effective and comfortable throughout your treatment.
