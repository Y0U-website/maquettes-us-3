First Visit Information

Your first visit is designed to be relaxed, informative, and personalized. What To Do if You Experience a Problem During Treatment

If you have a question or concern during your orthodontic treatment, help is just a message away. Our practice uses Dental Monitoring to provide convenient, concierge-style support throughout your smile journey.

Through the Dental Monitoring app, you can communicate directly with our team whenever something doesn’t seem quite right. Whether you have a question about your braces or aligners, notice a change, or experience minor discomfort, simply send us a message and photos through the app.

Dr. McNeal and our team will review patient messages and address your concerns as soon as possible. In many cases, we can evaluate the situation and provide guidance remotely, helping you avoid an unnecessary trip to the office. If an in-person visit is needed, we’ll let you know and help you schedule the appropriate appointment.

Dental Monitoring is one more way we deliver the personalized attention and exceptional patient experience you can expect from McNeal Orthodontics.

Patient Resources Frequently Asked Questions

When will I receive my new patient forms?

After you schedule your complimentary consultation, we’ll send your patient forms electronically so you can complete them online before your appointment. Completing them ahead of time helps make your first visit check-in quick and convenient.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, a parent or legal guardian if the patient is under 18, any questions you may have about treatment, and your smile goals.

What if I have an orthodontic emergency?

If something doesn’t feel right during treatment, you can contact us through the Dental Monitoring app. Our team can help determine the best next step, and concerns are monitored 24/7 to make sure you can always reach someone for guidance.

Do you accept insurance and offer financing?

Yes. We accept and file all dental insurance plans (excluding DMO and HDMO participation) and offer flexible financing through OrthoFi, allowing you to choose a payment plan that works for your budget.

Can I contact the office if I have questions between appointments?

Absolutely. We want every patient to feel supported throughout treatment. If you have questions or concerns, simply contact our office, and our team will be happy to assist you.
