Dental Botox® Treatments in Needham, MA

Not just a cosmetic treatment, Botox® has many therapeutic purposes in dentistry. That’s why Harvey Family Dental is proud to make dental Botox® treatments available to our patients in Needham and surrounding communities.

Botox®, officially known as a botulinum toxin, is a natural and non-surgical treatment designed to relax muscles and eliminate tension. While many people know it can reduce fine lines and wrinkles making your skin look smoother and younger many are unaware it can also treat common dental concerns like jaw pain, grinding, and gummy smiles.
