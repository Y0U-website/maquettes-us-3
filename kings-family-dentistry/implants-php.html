What are Dental Implants

Dental implants act like real teeth, but they’re made from medical-grade titanium alloy—a metal your body gets along with really well. Our dentists place the implant post directly into your jawbone, where it stands in for the root of your missing tooth. After the post joins with the surrounding bone (that’s called osseointegration), we attach an abutment. This little piece connects the post to your new artificial tooth. Finally, our periodontist tops it all off with a custom dental crown, or sometimes dentures or a bridge, so everything looks and feels just like your natural teeth.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants work really well—about 95 % of the time or even better. That’s because the titanium posts actually bond with your jawbone, almost like they’re part of your own teeth. Dentists have used this technology for years, and it’s reliable. If you take care of your implant, it can last a long time—maybe even for life. So, instead of stressing over whether your tooth can handle yet another root canal, you get to enjoy a replacement that just works. You can eat, talk, and smile without worrying about your implant giving you trouble.
