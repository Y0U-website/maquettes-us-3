Silver Diamine Fluoride

At Little Bee Pediatric Dentistry in Hammond, we’re here to make dental care safer, easier, and less stressful for kids of all ages. One of our innovative treatments, Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF), is a quick, non-invasive solution to help stop cavities in their tracks.

Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. Typically, x-rays are recommended every year, though children with a higher risk of oral disease may need more frequent scans.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Pediatric dental sealants are a crucial preventive treatment designed to protect children&#x27;s teeth from cavities and decay. In Hammond, Little Bee Pediatric Dentistry offers this essential service to ensure the long-term oral health of young patients. Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, where most cavities in children are found.

Pediatric Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child&#x27;s mouth healthy and happy!
