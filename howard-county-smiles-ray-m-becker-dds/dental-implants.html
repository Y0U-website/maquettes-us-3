About the Dental Implant Procedure

Dr. Becker will create a personalized treatment plan to provide you with the best dental implants Columbia, MD, offers. He’ll perform the tooth implant surgery and complete the restoration in our Howard County dental office.

The dental implant placement is a simple procedure performed in the dentist’s office under local anesthesia. The implant typically takes three to six months to heal and fully integrate with the jawbone. In some cases, Dr. Becker will place the permanent dental crown during this visit. Other times, a temporary crown will be placed while the implant heals.

You can also get a CEREC® crown for an implant . If he examines your mouth and decides that you’re a candidate, Dr. Becker can provide same-day crowns to support your implant.

If you have multiple missing teeth, Dr. Becker will uncover the implant and attach the dental bridge or denture after you heal. Your restoration will look and function the same as your natural teeth. You can brush and floss normally. Routine dental visits will ensure the dental implant’s long-term health and function.

Dental Implant vs. Dental Bridges vs. Dentures

All three of these restorations replace missing teeth. However, a dental implant is the only one that replaces the actual root structure of a tooth. This means that the prosthetic tooth (or teeth) is permanently fixed in the mouth and doesn’t need any support from neighboring teeth or the use of adhesives.

For denture patients, dental implants can minimize speech impediments. They’ll also allow you to eat a healthy and varied diet and chew much more efficiently. You can live without concern over what foods you can and cannot eat.

What is the best choice for missing teeth?

You have many replacement options to choose from if you have one or more missing teeth. Dr. Becker will evaluate your oral health and find the best option for you. A dental implant is often the best choice for someone who wants a long-lasting and worry-free solution.

Dental implants have the highest success rate and patient satisfaction compared with any other type of dental treatment. They give patients the most lifelike replacement for missing teeth. Implant restorations can also help you eat, drink, and speak without restrictions.

Are you a good candidate for dental implants?

Dr. Becker will assess your teeth, gums, and jaw before treatment to see if you’re an ideal candidate. You must have enough bone in your jaw to support the implant, and the best candidates also have healthy gum tissue.

We offer periodontal therapy and bone grafting if you have concerns about your oral health. These treatments improve gum and bone structure to accommodate implants and ensure a stable result.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Just like natural teeth, implants need at-home oral care and regular dental visits. Continue to brush and floss to keep your new teeth clean. We may also recommend a water flosser to get hard-to-reach areas.

What should I eat after I get my dental implants?

After your dental treatment, temporarily avoid chewy, sticky, or hard foods. These include popcorn, chips, nuts, crackers, and candy. Some foods can get stuck in the incision site and cause micro-abrasions.

Once the soft tissues in your mouth have healed, you can resume your regular diet. Tooth implants are a convenient restoration because they improve the bite’s function so you can enjoy your favorite foods again.

How long does a dental implant last?

With proper care and maintenance, your dental implant can last a lifetime. The post is permanent. You may need to replace your crown, bridge, or denture over time. However, the restoration will last longer than if it didn’t have support from implant posts.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants are safe. They’re a common and reliable treatment for replacing missing teeth. The process involves placing a small titanium post into your jawbone, which then heals and fuses with the bone to support a new tooth.

While risks like infection or complications with healing are possible, they’re rare. With proper care and regular checkups, dental implants can be a safe and long-lasting solution.
