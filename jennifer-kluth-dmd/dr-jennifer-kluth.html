Meet Dr. Kluth

Dr. Kluth is a member of the American Dental Association, and the New York State Dental Association, and serves as the Fourth District Dental Society secretary and newsletter editor. She believes in supporting organized dentistry and enjoys getting to know her dental colleagues. She is also an active participant in Kois Center, Spear Education and Clinical Mastery Series continuing education groups.

She looks forward to meeting you and helping you achieve your oral health goals.
