Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant typically needs replacement every 10 to 15 years, depending on wear and care.
  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients report that the procedure is less painful than they expected, and discomfort can be managed with local anesthesia during the procedure and over-the-counter pain relievers afterward.
  • Can anyone get dental implants? Most people are good candidates for implants; however, patients should have healthy gums, adequate bone to secure the implants, and be committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental visits. Smokers or those with chronic disorders like diabetes or heart disease may need to be evaluated on an individual basis.
