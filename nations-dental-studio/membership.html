Get answers to your most common questions

Can I use a membership plan if I have dental insurance?

No. Membership plans are designed for patients without dental insurance and cannot be combined with any active dental insurance, including out-of-network plans. If you have insurance, visit our insurance and financing page to learn how your benefits work at our office.

What does every membership plan include?

Every plan includes two cleanings and exams per year with X-rays, plus a 15 % discount on most additional procedures. There are no claims to file, no waiting periods, and no annual maximums.

What ages qualify for the Child Plan?

The Child Plan covers patients ages 0 through 13. Patients 14 and older would enroll in the Adult Plan or Perio Plan depending on their dental needs.

Who is the Perio Plan for?

The Perio Plan is for patients who require periodontal maintenance rather than standard cleanings. If you have been diagnosed with gum disease or your dentist has recommended periodontal maintenance visits, the Perio Plan covers those visits.

Are there waiting periods or annual maximums?

No. There are no waiting periods and no annual maximums. Your benefits begin as soon as you enroll, and your 15 % discount applies to eligible procedures throughout the year with no cap.

What procedures does the 15 % discount apply to?

The 15 % discount applies to most procedures performed at Nations Dental Studio. Some exclusions apply. Please contact our office for exact plan details and exclusions.

Can I use the membership at other dental offices?

No. Membership plan benefits apply exclusively to services provided at Nations Dental Studio in Nashville, TN.
