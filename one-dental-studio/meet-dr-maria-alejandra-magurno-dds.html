Dr. Maria Magurno

Dr. Magurno earned her Doctorate in Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) from Central University of Venezuela in 1998 and her Masters in the Specialty of Prosthodontics in 2002 from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachussetts, where she received the Dr. Hitoshi Ito Excellence Award as well as awards from the American College of Prosthodontics.

Dr. Magurno is also a Moderate Sedation Provider certified in oral and IV sedation. This creates a relaxed and comfortable atmosphere for her patients, so they can receive all the dental treatment needed in one or two visits, while having a pleasant experience with a reduced level of anxiety. Dr. Magurno and her staff are committed to her patients well-being and will work as hard as possible to make them happy and walk out with a beautiful smile!

She has received certification in Invisalign orthodontics, and she is also certified as a Botox for cosmetics as well as for TMD (Temporomandibular Joint Disorder) provider.

During her Prothodontics residency Dr. Magurno pursued a wide range of advanced courses in cosmetic dentistry, including bonding, veneers, crowns, implants, as well as specialty focused fixed, removable, and maxillofacial prosthodontics programs. Also, Dr. Magurno received extensive training to develop expertise in full mouth rehabilitation and oral design, all of which provided her with a strong clinical background in prosthodontics.

Dr. Magurno has achieved many awards during her education. Among where are the Award and Recognition as a lecturer at the Venezuelan Society of Prosthodontists and the Venezuelan Society of Esthetic Dentistry’s meetings. She is also a member of the American College of Prosthodontics, the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, and Atlantic Coast Dental Association, where she has given multiple presentations.

“From my point of view there is not just one important specialty in dentistry, I have learned that the key to success in any dental treatment is the multidisciplinary work. That’s why at One Dental Studio we work together as a team of multi specialty doctors, creating a whole world of possibilities to resolve dental cases and treat patients the best way possible, giving function and esthetics a primary focus."
