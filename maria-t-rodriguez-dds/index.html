Les soins

Routine dental cleanings don’t just give you a fresh, confident smile—they also help protect your overall health. Research shows that gum disease and oral infections are linked to conditions like heart disease, stroke, diabetes, and pregnancy complications.

Deep cleaning helps treat gum disease by removing plaque and tartar from below the gumline. It gives your gums a fresh start and helps keep your teeth healthy long-term.
