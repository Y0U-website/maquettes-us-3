Meet the Team at Mahtomedi Family Dental

Dr. Brian Welle and his team are excited to welcome you to Mahtomedi Family Dental! Our dental practice in Mahtomedi, MN, has proudly served the community for over 25 years, and we would love to be your dental home. Dr. Welle and his team provide the full range of dental services our patients need to maintain their healthy, beautiful smiles, making it simple for families to receive their dental care in one convenient location.

Our dedicated team of professionals is passionate about your dental care. Our primary goal is to help you feel welcome and at ease when you visit our Mahtomedi dental office. Take some time to get to know our friendly team members before you arrive.

When he s not taking care of patients, Dr. Welle enjoys spending time with his wife and two kids. Dr. Bradley Hagen, DMD

Dr. Brad Hagen is passionate about providing gentle, personalized dental care to patients of all ages. A Moorhead native, he’s excited to be back in Minnesota and looks forward to growing roots in Mahtomedi.

Dr. Hagen attended Concordia College in Moorhead, where he earned his degree in Chemistry, before completing dental school at the University of Florida College of Dentistry. After graduation, he spent three years in Colorado working at a nonprofit dental clinic that served underserved and low-income populations. He then spent another three years at Fort Belvoir, Virginia, providing care to military families. These experiences deepened his commitment to making quality dental care accessible and comfortable for everyone.

Dr. Hagen believes in treating every patient like family. He takes time to listen, explain treatment options clearly, and help patients feel at ease in the dental chair.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Hagen enjoys spending time with his wife, Sarah (a veterinarian), and their two young sons. He’s a big fan of the Minnesota Wild, Vikings, and Twins, and also loves cheering on the Florida Gators. In his free time, you’ll often find him playing soccer, traveling, or relaxing at the lake swimming, having campfires, and enjoying time with family and friends.

Dr. Hagen is excited to be back home in Minnesota and looks forward to helping you and your family achieve healthy, confident smiles!
