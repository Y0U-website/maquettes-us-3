Technology

In order to provide the highest quality of endodontic care, our treatment areas are equipped with dental operating microscopes, digital x-rays, and ultrasonics. With advancements in imaging technology has come the advent of cone beam computed tomography (CBCT). Our CBCT machine will provide a three dimensional scan of relevant structures to aid in diagnosis and management of your unique situation. This helps prevent tooth loss, as well as unnecessary or unpredictable treatments.
