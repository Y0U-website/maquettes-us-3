Meet Dr. Gabriela Baltierres

Dr. Gabriela Baltierres is a general and cosmetic dentist serving patients in Joliet and the greater Chicago area. With more than 23 years of clinical experience, she has built a relationship-based practice focused on personalized, high-quality dental care. Known to many patients as “Dr. Gaby,” she is fluent in Spanish and is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their dental treatment.

Dr. Baltierres earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry. She resides in a Chicago suburb with her husband and has raised three daughters. Outside of dentistry, she enjoys road trips, camping, and spending time with her family.

Surgical Extractions

Surgical extractions are performed when a tooth cannot be removed with a simple procedure. Our team provides careful, comfortable treatment to safely remove problematic teeth and support proper healing.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. They restore function, stability, and appearance while helping preserve jawbone health.

Orthodontic Dentistry

Orthodontic dentistry focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and bite issues. Treatment options such as clear aligners help improve both oral health and smile appearance.

Dental Education And Certifications

University Of Illinois At Chicago DDS

Dr. Gabby received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Illinois in Chicago. Since becoming a dentist she has developed a strong, specialized skillset including installing dental implants, implementing Invisalign, and Orthodontic Dentistry.

American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) IMPLANT CERTIFIED

Dr. Gabby has completed the New York Maxicourse, recognized as one of the nation’s top programs – A 300 hour program providing comprehensive training in implant dentistry.

United States Dental Institute Orthodontic Training

In addition to completing Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine dental implant intensive, Dr. Baltierres is also certified through the US Dental Institute in Orothodontic Dentistry.
