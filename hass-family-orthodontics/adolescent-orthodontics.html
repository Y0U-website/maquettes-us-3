Adolescent Orthodontics

Frequently Asked Questions About Adolescent Orthodontic Services

What is the best adolescent orthodontic treatment?

Each patient has their own unique needs, preferences, and personality, which means there is no single treatment that is best for everyone. Dr. Hass will help you and your teen understand the different options available, along with their advantages and disadvantages.

Is 15 too old for braces?

You’re never too old for braces! Ideally, we would begin treatment soon after all of your child’s primary teeth have fallen out and the secondary teeth have started to erupt, but there is no upper age limit for braces.

How long is adolescent orthodontic treatment?

The length of adolescent orthodontics depends on whether your child had early orthodontic services. It typically takes 1 to 3 years for adolescent orthodontics, provided that your teen doesn’t miss their appointments, gets broken brackets replaced right away, and follows our instructions.

Do braces change facial appearance?

Yes, braces do change a teen’s facial appearance, but these changes are positive ones. Orthodontic treatment creates a beautiful smile with a more symmetrical face and balanced profile.

Is it okay for my child not to get braces?

While orthodontics may not be as urgent as filling a cavity or placing a dental crown, the risk of not getting orthodontic treatment when it’s recommended is that it can cause your child serious dental issues when they reach adulthood, which can be time-consuming and costly to fix.
