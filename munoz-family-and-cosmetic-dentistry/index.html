The practice

We believe oral health plays an important role in your overall well-being, which is why we provide you with the information you need to make informed decisions about every treatment option we recommend.

Dr. Sebastian delivers dental care in a gentle and compassionate manner, with your comfort as his top priority.

You can depend on the Center for Dental Excellence team to provide the preventive care and comprehensive dental services you need for long-term oral health.

Dr. Sebastian Munoz a Colombia native, grew up in the Fox Valley and graduated from Berlin High School. Dr. Sebastian continued his education at the University of Wisconsin-Oshkosh where he earned his undergraduate degree in Biology Healthcare with minors in Chemistry and Spanish. Dr. Sebastian then went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry…

Dentistry allowed Dr. Sebastian the opportunity to combine his passion for helping people and science. He firmly believes that all patients should be treated as if they were family and is excited to provide his patients with the best possible dental care while building lifelong relationships. Dr. Sebastian is a member of the Brown Door Kewaunee Dental Society, Wisconsin Dental Association (WDA)…

Dr. Sebastian is an avid Wisconsin sports fan and enjoys cheering his teams on, especially the Packers. When not in the office he enjoys spending time with his wife Katrina and their three children, outdoors, or at the golf course.

Dr. Sebastian Munoz has purchased the dental practice of Dr. David Brusky and his Green Bay clinic, The Center for Dental Excellence, effective February 1. Brusky is retiring from dentistry after 27 years in the profession.

Munoz has been part of the Green Bay dental community for over 3 years. He grew up in the Fox Valley, graduating from Berlin High school and the University of Wisconsin-Oshkosh before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry.
