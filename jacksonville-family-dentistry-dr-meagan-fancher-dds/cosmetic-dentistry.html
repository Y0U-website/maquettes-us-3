WHAT IS A

SMILE MAKEOVER?

This cosmetic undertaking addresses a number of issues affecting the appearance of a patient’s smile. It can include anything from teeth whitening to bonding, tooth-colored fillings, veneers, crowns or bridges. Our Cosmetic Dentistry Options

Veneers

Dr. Fancher places a custom-fit layer of composite or porcelain over teeth to make them a uniform size and shape. They can conceal anything from chips and cracks to teeth of varying lengths.

Smile Makeover

What kind of smile do you want to see in the mirror? Uneven, misshapen, misaligned or discolored teeth don’t need to keep you from flashing your pearly whites. Any of these conditions are easily fixed.

Digital Smile Design

Tapping into diagnostic software that displays a three-dimensional impression of the patient’s mouth, Dr. Fancher can open up the possibilities that are achievable and show them to patients during the consultation phase of treatment.

Clear Aligners

Offering both Invisalign and Clearcorrect, the Jacksonville Family Dentistry team can straighten and align teeth to perfection in less time and for less cost than traditional braces. Impressions are taken of the patient’s teeth and the clear aligners are created and sent to them. They wear each set for 20-22 hours per day. There are no brackets or wires. Dr. Fancher monitors the progress during treatment, which usually takes 10-12 months.

Teeth Whitening

ZOOM Whitening can take your smile to a brighter white, up to eight shades whiter. This non-invasive procedure involves a ZOOM light and hydrogen peroxide-based whitening gel to lift stains and discolorations. It lasts about 45 minutes.

Bonding/Composite Veneers

This procedure uses tooth-colored composite material to repair chips, gaps or change the shape or color of a tooth.
