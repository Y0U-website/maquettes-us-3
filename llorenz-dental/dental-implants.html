Book Consultation Call (928) 285-4591 What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that consist of a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a custom crown is attached, giving you a natural-looking and fully functional tooth.

Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. They look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20-30 years or even a lifetime. The titanium post itself is designed to be permanent. The crown on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear.
