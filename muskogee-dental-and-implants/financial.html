Financial FAQs

  • How much will my dental visit cost? Dental care costs depend on the treatment type, insurance coverage and your specific plan. We strive to offer affordable options, making quality dental care accessible to all patients. View Office Fees
  • Do you offer a membership plan for patients without insurance? Yes! We offer an in-house dental membership plan designed for patients without insurance, allowing you to access preventative services such as cleanings and exams, as well as discounts on restorative and cosmetic treatments such as crowns, fillings, implants and Invisalign ® . Discover Dental Membership Plan
  • Do you offer third-party financing for dental treatments? Absolutely! We partner with CareCredit ® and Sunbit to provide flexible financing options, allowing you to break payments into manageable installments. The application process is quick and simple, allowing you to get the care you need without delay. Explore Third-Party Financing
  • Can I use third-party financing with my insurance? Yes! Third-party payments can be applied to your balance in full, partially, or to cover any out-of-pocket costs not covered by insurance, providing a convenient and flexible way to manage your dental expenses.
  • Is the in-house membership plan applicable with third-party financing? Third-party financing may not be used to pay for services through the in-house membership plan. We accept cash, checks and personal credit cards for discounted services and treatments included in the membership plan.
