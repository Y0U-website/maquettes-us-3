Am I too old to have traditional braces?

There’s no upper age limit, but we do consider factors like bone loss or gum health before starting treatment. Any age is welcome for a consultation — we’ll take a close look and let you know if you’re a good candidate for braces.

Are traditional braces only for kids and teens?

No! Traditional braces are effective for patients of all ages, including adults.

What is the difference between clear trays and traditional braces?

Clear trays (like Invisalign) are removable and nearly invisible, while traditional braces use brackets and wires to move teeth. Braces are often better for complex cases and don’t rely on patient compliance, since they stay on 24/7.

Metal vs Clear?

Clear braces work the same way as traditional metal braces. But they consist of ceramic brackets and frosted wires to match the shade of your natural teeth. As a result, clear braces are less noticeable compared to metal braces. Clear braces aren’t as strong as metal braces.

When should I get a consultation for my child’s orthodontic needs?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an evaluation around age 7. That doesn’t always mean braces right away — early consultations help catch any issues and let us plan the right timing for treatment.

Why do I need orthodontic treatment?

Why get traditional braces?

  • “Set it and Forget It” – This tried-and-true concept applies to Traditional Orthodontics when it comes to looking for your teen child’s best outcome in the minimum amount of time without making your child worry about where their trays are or progress checks on their own.

How often do I need to visit for adjustments?

With self-ligating braces, adjustment visits are needed less often compared to traditional braces. Your orthodontist will schedule appointments as needed to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments.

How long does treatment with braces take?

Most treatments last 12–24 months, but it varies depending on the complexity.

What do the different colored bands on braces mean? Can I choose my colors?

Colored bands—also known as elastics or ligatures—help secure the archwire in place. They come in all sorts of fun colors, so yes, you can absolutely choose them! Want something subtle and professional or bold and expressive? It’s your smile, your vibe.

What happens at a typical adjustment appointment?

You’ll probably just get a new archwire and bands to keep your treatment progressing. We’ll replace any worn parts, check your comfort, and address any questions or changes. Each adjustment moves you a step closer to your ideal smile.

How long does it take to get used to braces?

Most people adapt within a few days to a week. You may feel some pressure or slight soreness at first, but you’ll become used to the sensation quickly.

What should I do if a bracket comes off while I’m away or on vacation?

Try to keep the bracket and bring it in. Call us—if it’s not causing discomfort or affecting your wire, we may ask you to wait until you’re back. But if it hurts or cuts you, come in sooner so we can fix it quickly.

Can I wait until my next appointment to fix a broken bracket?

If it’s not bothering you or shifting your treatment, yes, you can wait. But when in doubt—better to let us check sooner. Leaving it can slow progress or cause discomfort.

Are traditional braces painful?

While braces can cause some discomfort when they’re first placed or adjusted, it usually subsides within a few days.

What problems can traditional braces fix?

They can correct crooked teeth, overcrowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and more.
