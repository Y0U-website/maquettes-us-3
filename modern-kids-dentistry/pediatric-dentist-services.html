Pediatric Dentist Services

Specialized Pediatric Dental Services At Modern Kids Dentistry, we understand and celebrate the fact that every child is unique. We offer a host of specialized services to meet your child’s individual needs:

Lexington Office 4384 Clearwater Way, Suite 150 Lexington, KY 40515

  • Oral Health Examination
  • Digital X-Rays
  • Dental Cleaning
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Infant Oral Health Consulting
  • Orthodontic Evaluation
  • Sealants (BPA Free)
  • Nightguards & Sports Mouthguards
  • Mobile Digital X-Rays — a smaller sensor is more comfortable for young patients’ mouths, with immediate screen viewing to show a parent if need be, and no moving room to room keeps appointments running efficiently.
  • Topical Anesthetic “Best Topical Ever,” which numbs profoundly before injection.
  • Electric Handpieces are quieter and faster than air-driven handpieces. Minimal noise means reduced patient anxiety, and faster is better for a pediatric patient who has a short attention span.
  • Tooth-Colored Fillings (BPA free)
  • Silver Crowns
  • Tooth Colored (zirconia) Crowns
  • Root Canals (primary teeth – “baby teeth”)
  • Root Canals (adult teeth, to include molars)
  • Space Maintainers
  • Soft tissue laser frenectomy (to treat tongue tie, high lip frenums)
  • Laser therapy for cold sores.
  • Teeth Whitening (teenage patients)
  • Dental care for children with special medical needs.
  • Dentistry with nitrous oxide (laughing gas).
  • Dentistry under oral sedation.
  • Dentistry in the operating room setting.
  • 24-hour emergency phone service (for existing patients only).
