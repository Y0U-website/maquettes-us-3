The practice

Welcome to Mahtomedi Family Dental! Dr. Brian Welle and his team are honored that you re considering us for your dental home. Our family dental practice has been a part of the Mahtomedi community for over 25 years, and we look forward to serving you.

Patients of all ages are welcome at Mahtomedi Family Dental. Dr. Welle is also a father and loves helping children learn the importance of dental care and having healthy teeth. We make it simple and comfortable for families with busy schedules to take care of their smiles in one convenient location.

Dr. Welle uses his extensive training and advanced skills to provide our patients with a wide range of dental services.

Our primary goal is to help you achieve and maintain a healthy and beautiful smile that will last for your lifetime!

When you visit our Mahtomedi dental office, we want you to feel like you are part of our dental family. You ll receive a warm welcome from our team, and we love spending time getting to know you. Long-term relationships with our patients help us provide exceptional personalized treatment and make our jobs rewarding!

If you or a loved one feels nervous or anxious about visiting the dentist, Dr. Welle offers nitrous oxide, also called laughing gas, to help patients feel more at ease and relaxed in the dental chair without lingering side effects afterward.

Our goal is always to help our patients feel comfortable and well-cared for when they visit Mahtomedi Family Dental, so please let us know how we can improve your experience if you have suggestions.

We never want finances to keep you from achieving the smile we know you deserve, so we work with you to comfortably fit the dental treatment you need into your budget. We encourage you to call us with any questions about your treatment plan or payment options.
