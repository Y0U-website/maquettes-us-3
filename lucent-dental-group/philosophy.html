Philosophy

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

We provide community and family-centered care for a broad range of dental concerns. But more than dentistry, our team is dedicated to making them feel apart of a family that will genuinely care for them. Everything we do is focused on our patients because we believe they deserve only the best.

OUR APPROACH With a dental practice that focuses on prevention, early intervention, and the collective improvement of society’s health and wellness, we take a step towards a brighter future with happier and healthier smiles. We strive for this through compassionate and quality service with a sincere heart to serve.

OUR BELIEF PEOPLE OVER PROFITS. Putting our patients, staff and dentists first. 100 % Dentist owned and operated- No corporate partners and no private equity firms- no external influence, just dentistry based on ethics with the lone emphasis on Patient needs. We strive to create a no pressure setting for dentists to grow as clinicians.

PHILOSOPHY Lucent Dental Group aligns our vision and mission to our ultimate goal and this is to reach a level of dental excellence and service that positively influences the health of our patients . This is a mantra that permeates our company and reflects in the standard of dentistry we provide our patients and the environment we create for our team.
