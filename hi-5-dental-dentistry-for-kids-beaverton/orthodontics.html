Orthodontics

  • Available Treatments If early treatment is recommended, we’ll discuss the available options with you and develop a personalized plan for your child. Early orthodontic treatment may involve: Habit Correction We’ll work with your child to address habits like thumb sucking or tongue thrusting that can negatively impact their bite.
  • Growth Guidance We may recommend appliances to help guide the growth of the jaw and create space for incoming permanent teeth.
  • Space Maintainers If a baby tooth is lost prematurely, a space maintainer can help preserve the space for the permanent tooth to erupt correctly.
  • Limited Braces In some cases, early braces may be used to correct specific issues and prepare for more comprehensive treatment later on.
  • Full Braces Children who have all of their adult teeth are candidates for full Orthodontic Treatment.
  • Invisalign Using a series of clear, removable aligners, we can discreetly straighten your child’s teeth without the need for traditional metal brackets and wires.

Sports & Mouthguards

If your child plays sports, a custom-fitted mouthguard is essential for protecting their teeth from injury. Mouthguards help cushion the impact of blows to the face, reducing the risk of chipped or knocked-out teeth.

  • What to Expect We offer custom-fitted mouthguards that are comfortable and provide superior protection. The process is simple: We’ll take an impression of your child’s teeth.
  • A custom mouthguard will be fabricated in a dental laboratory.
  • We’ll ensure a proper fit and show your child how to care for their mouthguard.
  • Advantages Better Fit They are designed to fit your child’s teeth precisely, providing optimal comfort and protection.
  • Improved Breathability They allow for easier breathing and speaking while wearing the mouthguard.
  • Increased Durability They are made from high-quality materials that can withstand the wear and tear of sports activities.
