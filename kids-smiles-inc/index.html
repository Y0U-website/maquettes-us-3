The practice

Dr. Lewis is an active staff member at Cape Cod Hospital, an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), Diplomate status in the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry , member of the Northeast Regional Pediatric Dental Society , Cape Cod Dental Society , and the Massachusetts Dental Society , where she is a voting Delegate.

Dr. Lewis lives in Harwich with her husband Dan and three children.

At Kids Smiles, your child will receive quality dental care, enhanced in an environment designed solely with your child s needs in mind. Dr. Lorraine Lewis and our team strive to provide your child with the care to make each visit the best that it can be and to promote a strong foundation for a lifetime of good habits.
