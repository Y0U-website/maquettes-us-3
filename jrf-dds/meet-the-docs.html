Meet The Docs JRF DDS

Dr. Rubin received her DDS degree from Buffalo school of dental medicine in 1996. After graduating from school she went on to do a one-year post-residency at Albert Einstein College of Medicine.

2002 was a big year for Dr. Rubin as she have birth to triplets and moved to Long Island. During this time Dr. Rubin continued to practice on patients while raising her children. She gained experience in sedation, implants, Botox for TMJ pain and so much more!

In 2013 dr. Rubin decided she was ready to go out on her own and start her own practice.

“My favorite part of dentistry is the relationship that I have with all my patients. My patients are like family to me!

In her free time, Dr. Rubin likes to take hikes

Joining the ranks at JRFDDS is Dr. Unsa Malik, a talented and well-rounded dentist.

Dr. Malik graduated with Honors from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. During her training, she became a strong advocate for treating trauma, TMJ disorders, and patients with special needs. She then continued her education by completing a General Practice Residency at Northwell Health focusing on general, cosmetic, and surgical dentistry.

Dr. Malik is thrilled to be back on Long Island to serve her community. She strives to provide excellent patient care and create a positive experience at each visit. She has refined her knowledge and skills through continuing education courses allowing her to keep our practice at the cutting edge of the latest techniques in modern technology. Outside of work, Dr. Malik enjoys traveling, playing tennis, and exploring Long Island recreation with her family and friends.
