Outside the Office

Dr. Herron’s wife Lori has been a registered nurse for 34 years and currently works at Mercy Hospital in Joplin, Missouri. He has a married daughter, Andrea, who lives in Neodesha, KS with her husband Ty and two children, Kendall and Brett. Dr. Herron’s son Kurt is completing his medical residency program in Arizona. His youngest daughter Liesl is married to Brad. They reside in Carl Junction, MO with their two children, Brooks and Margo.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Herron enjoys spending time with his family as well as raising small animals and gardening. When it comes to being active in the community, he is involved as a lead triage dentist for the Kansas Mission of Mercy project and is proud supporter of all things PSU!
