Why Starting Early Makes a Difference

The earlier preventive care begins, the more effectively we can guide your child’s oral development. We recommend that children have their first dental visit around their first birthday or when their first tooth appears, whichever comes first. These first infant visits give us the opportunity to check on early tooth development, discuss feeding habits, and give parents practical guidance for caring for their child’s teeth at home.

Starting early also helps children grow comfortable with dental visits. When appointments are calm, positive experiences from the very beginning, children are much more likely to continue prioritizing their dental health as they get older. That sense of trust and comfort is something we work hard to build and protect at every visit.

What to Expect at Preventive Checkups

Preventive visits at our office are designed to be relaxed and family-friendly. We welcome parents back with their children throughout the appointment, and we take time to answer questions and explain what we are doing every step of the way. Here is what a typical preventive visit includes:

Every appointment ends with a clear picture of your child’s oral health and a plan to keep it on track. These visits are also a great opportunity to address any questions or concerns you have noticed at home.

  • A professional cleaning by one of our dental hygienists
  • A comprehensive oral exam by Dr. Bennion
  • X-rays when appropriate for your child’s age and dental history
  • Fluoride treatment to strengthen enamel
  • A personalized conversation about home care habits and nutrition

Preventive Care for Children with Special Needs

Every child deserves access to compassionate, high-quality dental care, including children who need a little extra support. Dr. Bennion has extensive training in treating children with complex medical histories and developmental differences, and his personal experience as the parent of a child with Down syndrome deeply informs how we approach care for every patient. We take the time to understand each child’s unique needs and adapt our environment, pace, and communication style accordingly.

Preventive care is especially important for children with special needs, who may face additional oral health challenges. We work closely with families to develop routines that are realistic, sustainable, and effective for their child’s specific situation.
