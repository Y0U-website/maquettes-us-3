New Patients

  • New Patients Welcome to our Pediatric Dental Practice At Olney Pediatric Dentistry, we’re honored to care for children of all ages and warmly welcome new patients into our practice. During your child’s first visit, you’ll meet our friendly team and get to know Dr. Jenny, who is dedicated to providing exceptional care in a supportive, calming, and kid-friendly environment. What to Expect at Your First Visit Personalized Care: Every child is unique. We take the time to understand their individual needs and create a positive, personalized experience that helps your child feel safe and supported.
  • Continuity of Care: Dr. Jenny will be your child’s pediatric dentist at every visit. She values building long-term relationships and watching her patients grow—all while laying the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.
  • Transparency: We believe in open, honest communication. From explaining clinical findings and treatment options to reviewing insurance benefits, we make sure you feel informed and confident every step of the way.

Insurance and Billing

We’re committed to making pediatric dental care simple and accessible. Please provide your insurance information when scheduling your appointment so we can verify your benefits in advance.

We are in-network with most major PPO dental plans and are happy to file claims on your behalf. Our team is here to help you understand your coverage and financial responsibilities with clarity and care.

No insurance? No problem. Ask us about our in-house membership plan, designed to make high-quality care more affordable. It includes savings on routine visits and treatments to help you manage your child’s dental care with ease.
