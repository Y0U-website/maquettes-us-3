What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth replacements that provide a permanent foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants replace both the visible part of your tooth and its root. A single dental implant consists of a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone, an abutment that connects to the post, and a custom-made dental crown that matches your natural teeth. This structure allows implants to function exactly like natural teeth, becoming a permanent part of your mouth so you can eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

Implant Solutions for Every Need

There are several implant solutions based on your specific needs. For a single missing tooth, an individual implant with a crown provides the perfect replacement without affecting adjacent teeth. If you’re missing several teeth, multiple implants can support bridges or partial dentures with superior stability.

For patients who have lost all their teeth on either jaw, implant-supported dentures offer remarkable stability. The All-on-4 approach uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth. Patients with insufficient bone density may benefit from mini implants, which are smaller in diameter and often don’t require bone grafting procedures.

The Benefits of Dental Implants in Clarksville, IN

Dental implants offer many advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. With proper care, they can last a lifetime, making them a wise investment in your oral health with a documented success rate of over 95 %. They look, feel, and function just like natural teeth, with no one able to tell you have implants unless you choose to share that information. When you lose teeth, your jawbone begins to deteriorate, but implants stimulate bone growth and prevent bone loss, maintaining your facial structure and preventing premature aging.

Unlike tooth-supported bridges, implants don’t require reducing adjacent teeth, which means more of your natural teeth remain untouched, improving your long-term oral health. Implants eliminate the discomfort and embarrassment of removable dentures by staying firmly in place so you can eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence. This stability means you can eat virtually anything, maintaining a healthy, balanced diet without limitations.

The Dental Implant Process at Beechwood Dental

Your dental implant journey typically spans several months, though timelines vary based on individual circumstances. It begins with a comprehensive examination where Dr. Li evaluates your oral health, takes digital X-rays, and discusses your treatment goals. Some patients may need pretreatments such as tooth extractions , bone grafting, or treatment for gum disease before implant placement.

During implant placement, the dentist precisely positions the titanium posts into your jawbone under local anesthesia, with sedation options available for your comfort. After placement, your implants need time to integrate with your jawbone over 3-6 months. During this healing period, you may wear a temporary restoration so you’re never without teeth.

Once integration is complete, we’ll take impressions to create your custom restoration. Whether it’s a single crown, dental bridge , or full denture, your new teeth will be precisely crafted to look natural and function perfectly. Caring for your new implants is straightforward. You can brush, floss, and get a routine dental check-up to keep your new teeth healthy.

Is Dental Implant Treatment Right for You?

Most adults with good general and oral health can be candidates for dental implants. Dr. Li will consider factors such as adequate bone density to support the implant, healthy gum tissue free of periodontal disease, good overall health, and commitment to good oral hygiene. Even if you’ve been told elsewhere that you’re not a candidate for implants, advances in techniques and technology mean Dr. Li may have solutions for you. Options like bone grafting, sinus lifts, or alternative implant types can make implants possible for many patients previously considered unsuitable.
