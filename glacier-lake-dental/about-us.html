Dr. Tracy Grasdal, DDS

Dr. Tracy Grasdal s journey into dentistry began at the College of St. Catherine, where she studied biology. She followed her passion for helping others at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, graduating with her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. She also spent time abroad studying at the University of Bergen in Norway. With an unwavering commitment to excellence, she has mastered advanced techniques in CEREC 3-D in-office restorations and cosmetic dentistry through the Spear Institute.

For Dr. Grasdal, the variety of people she meets and the ability to positively impact their lives are what she values most about her profession. Since entering the field in 1996 and especially since founding Glacier Lake Dental in 2002, she has found joy in dentistry s unique blend of science and interpersonal connections.

Beyond her professional commitments, Dr. Grasdal spends time with family, attending her children s sporting events and academic activities. She also enjoys reading and staying active by skiing, exercising, and exploring the outdoors. A unique fact about Dr. Grasdal is that she is fluent in Norwegian and can converse in German.
