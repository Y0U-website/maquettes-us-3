The practice

We provide a full range of dental treatments at our comfortable Glen Cove location. Some of our specializations include:

Dental Implants – Dental implants are the best solution for replacing missing teeth because they are the only solution that replaces the tooth both above and below the gum line. This prevents bone loss in the jaw and eventual facial collapse.

Same Day Crowns – In the past, when you needed a tooth restoration, you’d have to make a few trips to the dentist and wait weeks for your permanent crown to be fabricated. With Same Day Crowns, the entire process is condensed down to a single day.

Cosmetic Dentistry – Our cosmetic dentistry services include bridges, crowns, veneers, and dental bonding.

With all of our services, you can feel confident knowing that you’re receiving the very best care. In addition to our advanced dental equipment, we strive to provide a comfortable environment, with flat screen TVs, a soothing water wall, and nitrous oxide to keep you relaxed throughout your procedure.

Dr. Sami is recognized as one of the premier cosmetic dentists in the North Shore of Long Island. He has created countless new smiles for patients over the past several years using the most modern techniques and materials available.

Our office philosophy is firmly grounded in a conservative, comprehensive approach to treatment that involves restoring form, function and aesthetics and results in an overall sense of well-being. Whether it’s a total smile makeover or a simple exam, we are here to address all your dental care needs.
