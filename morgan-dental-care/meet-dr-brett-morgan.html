Meet Dr. Brett Morgan | Gorham Dentist

Proudly Independent Dentist in Gorham, ME

You deserve a dentist who truly listens and puts your comfort first. At Morgan Dental Care, in Gorham, ME, Dr. Brett Morgan, DMD, believes your smile matters, and so does your peace of mind. Every visit is focused on understanding your needs, addressing your concerns and helping you feel confident, comfortable and fully supported in your dental care.

Where Did Dr. Morgan Study Dentistry?

Dr. Brett Morgan brings years of advanced dental training and hands-on experience to every patient he serves. After earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree as part of the inaugural class at the University of New England College of Dental Medicine, he continued to expand his knowledge through ongoing education, including advanced training in oral sedation dentistry and cardiac life support, so you always benefit from the latest techniques and technology.

His approach is built on honesty, clear communication and gentle care. Dr. Morgan believes in helping you understand your options, so you feel confident and at ease with every decision about your oral health.

Why Did Dr. Morgan Become A Dentist?

Choosing dentistry was not the obvious path for Dr. Brett Morgan. Listen to him explain how a chiropractor friend pointed the way and why the work is still rewarding.

As simple as getting someone out of pain. As complex as giving them their confidence back, restoring their health and beauty. It’s an extraordinarily rewarding thing to do. Dr. Brett Morgan, DMD

Dr. Morgan became a dentist for one heartfelt reason: he loves serving people in a way that makes a real difference in their lives. Nothing means more to him than earning someone’s trust, especially those who’ve stayed away from the dentist because of past fears or difficult experiences.

His relationship with Morgan Dental Care runs deep. Long before becoming an associate dentist here in 2017, he knew this practice from every angle: as a patient, a front desk hygiene coordinator, a dental assistant and even as the one who swept the floors at night.

But the biggest influence on his love for dentistry began at home. Being raised by Dr. Ted Morgan, founder of Morgan Dental Care, showed him what it looks like to care for people with integrity, compassion and genuine heart. That example continues to guide him every day as he supports the Gorham community.

Get to Know Dr. Morgan Beyond Dentistry

Outside the dental office, Dr. Morgan finds joy in the world of music. He enjoys playing music in his free time, and he has a personal goal: when he turns 60, he plans to shred on the piano.
