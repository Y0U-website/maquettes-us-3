Early Orthodontic Treatment at Gold River Orthodontics

Importance of Early Evaluation

Early orthodontic evaluation is a crucial step in a child’s development and dental health. Gold River Orthodontics follows the guidelines set by the American Association of Orthodontists, recommending that children receive their first orthodontic evaluation by the age of seven. This early assessment helps in identifying potential orthodontic issues such as crowding, crossbites, or misaligned jaws before they develop into more complex problems.

Benefits of Early Orthodontic Treatment

Addressing dental issues at an early age can lead to more effective and less invasive treatments. Early intervention allows us to:

Guide Jaw Growth

Properly timed treatment can influence jaw growth, leading to improved facial symmetry.

Lower Risk of Trauma

Correcting protruding front teeth reduces the risk of them being damaged or injured.

Correct Harmful Oral Habits

Early treatment can help correct thumb sucking, tongue thrusting, and other habits that might affect the development of the mouth and teeth.

Improve Appearance and Self-Esteem

A straighter, more aligned smile can boost a child’s confidence.

Our Early Treatment Options

At Gold River Orthodontics, we offer a variety of early treatment options tailored to each child’s unique needs:

Growth Modification Devices

These appliances can modify the jaw’s growth pattern to improve the alignment of the jaw and bite, often reducing the need for more invasive treatments later.

Space Maintainers

If a child loses a tooth prematurely, a space maintainer is used to keep the space open until the permanent tooth erupts.

Early Aligners

Similar to Invisalign®, these clear aligners are designed for younger patients with emerging or early permanent teeth.

Our Approach to Early Orthodontics

Our team at Gold River Orthodontics is experienced in early orthodontic care and uses the latest technologies to ensure precise and comfortable treatment. We understand the importance of a gentle approach when working with young patients and strive to make their orthodontic experience positive and fun.

Consultation and Customized Treatment Plans

Each child’s orthodontic journey begins with a comprehensive consultation, including X-rays and a digital scan of their teeth, to create a detailed and personalized treatment plan. Our goal is to address orthodontic issues efficiently and effectively, setting the stage for a healthy and beautiful smile in adulthood.
