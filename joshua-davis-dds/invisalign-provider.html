Caring for Your Invisalign System

Make sure that you take good care of all of your Invisalign aligners. You must brush the aligners and use warm water to rinse them. Alternatively, Invisalign provides a cleaning kit that you can use to keep the aligners hygienic.

You should also continue taking care of your oral health while using the Invisalign system by removing your aligners several times a day to brush and floss your teeth. Brush your teeth each time you eat and before you put your aligners back in. Putting in your aligners before you brush your teeth could damage them.
