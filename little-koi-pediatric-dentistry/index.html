The practice

Our Walnut pediatric dentist established our practice to provide quality over quantity in pediatric dental treatment. We limit our daily schedule to ensure each child receives unhurried attention, thorough examinations, and personalized education about dental health.

Dr. Vi-Khoi Duong focuses on compassionate care that prioritizes your child’s long-term wellbeing rather than maximizing patient volume. By taking time to connect with families and going at each child’s pace, we create stress-free visits that build confidence and establish positive relationships with oral health from the very first dental appointment.

We use safe, durable materials to repair cavities and restore your child’s teeth, helping prevent further decay and maintain long-term oral health.

When a baby tooth is severely damaged or decayed, crowns protect and strengthens it. We offer stainless steel options as well as natural-looking porcelain or zirconia crowns for front teeth.

If tooth decay reaches the nerve of a baby tooth, a pulpotomy (also known as a “baby root canal”) can relieve pain and save the tooth until it’s ready to naturally fall out.

To help ease anxiety and make treatment more comfortable, we offer nitrous oxide sedation in a safe, child-friendly setting.

For children with complex treatment needs or severe dental anxiety, we offer general anesthesia in a fully monitored environment, so care can be delivered safely and effectively.

We are experienced in working with children who have medical, developmental, or behavioral conditions. Every child deserves compassionate, customized care, and we’re here to provide it.

Les soins

Routine checkups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants help prevent cavities and support lifelong oral health. Prevention is key, especially in growing smiles.

Sometimes a tooth needs to be removed to protect your child’s smile, whether due to infection, crowding, or trauma. We make extractions gentle and stress-free.
