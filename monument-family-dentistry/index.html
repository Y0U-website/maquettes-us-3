The practice

Monument Family Dentistry was built on a simple idea: dentistry should be personal, thoughtful, and centered on what’s truly best for the patient.

Patients first We believe every patient deserves honesty, respect, and clear communication. Our team takes the time to understand your goals, explain your options, and guide you toward the healthiest path forward.

Advanced technology With tools like the SOLEA laser, CEREC same-day crowns, and 3D cone beam CT imaging, we’re able to provide precise, efficient care that often means fewer appointments and greater comfort.

Boutique experience As a smaller, relationship-focused practice, we pride ourselves on creating an environment where patients feel genuinely cared for. From personalized music to cozy comforts like blankets, we’ve designed every detail to help you feel relaxed and at ease.

Insurance guidance Dental insurance can be confusing. Our knowledgeable team is here to help you understand your benefits, navigate your coverage, and make informed decisions about your care.

We understand that paying for dental care can feel overwhelming, but we’re here to help make it easier. Whether you have insurance, are considering our affordable membership plan, or need flexible financing options, we’ve got you covered.

We accept a variety of insurance providers to ensure excellent dental care is in reach.

Our Smart Dental Plan offers an simple, easy way to save on exams, cleanings, and treatments.
