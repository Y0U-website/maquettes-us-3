About Nelson Dental

Your Hometown Dentist

Nelson Dental ND is more than just a dental practice—it’s the realization of a lifelong dream for Dr. Evan Nelson and his wife, Darby. Proud Wahpeton natives and Huskies alumni, the Nelsons built their practice with a deep commitment to their community. After 14 years of leading a thriving practice in Sisseton, South Dakota, they made the heartfelt decision to return home to Wahpeton to be closer to family and provide exceptional care to the neighbors they’ve always cherished.

A Modern Practice with a Personal Touch

Opening in August after months of hard work and renovations, Nelson Dental has transformed a former dentist’s office into a warm, modern space designed to serve patients of all ages. The newly remodeled office combines state-of-the-art technology with an inviting, family-friendly atmosphere, complete with TVs on the ceilings, noise-canceling headphones, and a patient Comfort Menu. Every detail reflects the Nelsons’ dedication to creating a stress-free experience.

Meet The Team

85 years of combined experience

At Nelson Dental, you’ll find more than exceptional care; you’ll find a team that’s truly invested in your well-being. Join the Nelson Dental family today and experience dentistry where smiles and community come first.

Technology & Innovation for the Best Care

Nelson Dental takes pride in offering advanced dental technology to enhance patient care. From CEREC same-day crowns and CBCT 3D imaging to comprehensive restorative and cosmetic solutions, the practice combines innovation with a human touch.

Your Hometown Dentist

For the Nelsons, Wahpeton isn’t just where they work—it’s home. Whether cheering on their kids at Friday night football games or catching up with neighbors at the grocery store, they’re proud to be an active part of the community.

Building Trust, One Smile at a Time

Dr. Nelson’s philosophy is simple: provide high-quality dental care in a relaxed, welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable and heard. From routine checkups to advanced restorative procedures, the team takes pride in tailoring care to each patient’s unique needs.

“I want our patients to feel like they can trust us,” Dr. Nelson said. “We don’t oversell or push unnecessary treatments. Our goal is to build relationships and deliver care that truly makes a difference.”
