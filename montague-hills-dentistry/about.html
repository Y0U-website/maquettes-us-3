A Positive Experience with Every Appointment

We offer a high standard of excellence in patient-centered service that enables us to provide the quality of dental treatment you and your family deserve. With comprehensive treatment plans that include restorative and cosmetic procedures, as well as oral surgery and orthodontics, we can provide everything you need to achieve optimal dental health.

Our operatories are equipped with the latest technology, including a digital imaging system in each room where we can display and explain each procedure for better communication, interpretation, and understanding of your dental treatment plan. The incredible levels of detail this system provides makes identifying and diagnosing conditions faster and easier. We provide soft tissue laser therapy and ultrasonic water equipment for comfortable and effective professional cleanings. Our ergonomic seats will maximize your comfort as will the soft music and peaceful view of the hills.
