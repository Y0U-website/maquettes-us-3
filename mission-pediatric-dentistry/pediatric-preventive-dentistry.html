Pediatric Preventive Dental Services We Offer

Regular Dental Checkups Regular dental checkups help early detection of oral problems to avoid unnecessary complications in the future.

Professional Teeth Cleaning Professional teeth cleaning to remove stubborn plaque, tough stains, and hardened tartar to reduce the risk of tooth decay and gum diseases.

How often should my child have a preventive dental checkup?

Children should visit the dentist every six months for a checkup and cleaning, just like adults. These regular visits help us catch any issues early and reinforce good oral hygiene habits.

Are fluoride treatments safe for kids?

Yes! Fluoride treatments are safe and effective. They help strengthen your child’s tooth enamel and protect against cavities, especially in areas where brushing can’t always reach.

What are dental sealants, and does my child need them?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth. They help prevent food and bacteria from getting stuck in grooves, reducing the risk of cavities, especially for kids prone to decay.

How can I help my child maintain healthy teeth at home?

Encourage brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, flossing daily, limiting sugary snacks, and drinking plenty of water. We also recommend regular checkups to keep their smile on track.
