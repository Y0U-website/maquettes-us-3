Teenage (typically ages 11 15) is the "golden window" for orthodontics because the jaw is still developing and all permanent teeth have erupted. Leveraging growth spurts makes teeth more responsive to adjustments, allowing orthodontists to efficiently correct alignment, bite issues, and prevent future problems like tooth decay, trauma, and periodontal disease.

THE CONSULTATION Every patient’s journey begins with a free consultation where we determine the candidacy of the patient. Digital records including photographs, radiographs, and a quick two minute scan has replaced the need for messy impressions.

THE PLACEMENT The second visit will involve placing your new orthodontic appliance and will last anywhere from 10 to 90 minutes, depending on the solution. We will provide home care instruction and schedule ongoing visits.

ONGOING VISITS Every patient’s treatment plan is customized for the complexity of their situation and the solution determined. Ongoing visits range from every 4-12 weeks for a period from 6 months to up to 1-2 years.
