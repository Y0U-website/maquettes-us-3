Dr. Jeff Millet

MEET DR. MILLET Biology Degree & D.D.S. A native of Newton, Dr. Jeff attended Central College and received his B.A. degree in Biology. He then entered the University of Iowa College of Dentistry where he received his D.D.S.

Dr. Jeff met his wife Ashley while attending Central College and together they have a daughter Kiera and a son Joseph.

Professionally, Dr. Jeff brings over ten years of private practice experience.

He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Des Moines Dental District. He has completed additional training in endodontics (root canals), implants, esthetic dentistry, sleep apnea, and Invisalign.

While at Central College, Dr. Jeff had the privilege of competing on a national level with the men’s golf team. In his spare time, he is an avid golfer, and enjoys spending time with his family outdoors or boating.
