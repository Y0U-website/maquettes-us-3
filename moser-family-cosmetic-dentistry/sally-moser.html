Dr. Sally Moser, DDS

Dr. Sally Moser was born in Peru and was at the dentist frequently as a child. However, unlike most other children, she often found herself looking forward to going to the dentist, as she enjoyed the application of science combined with the service aspect of dentistry. At the young age of 13, she knew she was meant to be a dentist. After attending undergraduate and graduate programs in Peru, Dr. Moser graduated from the International Dental program at the University of Southern California in 2013.

Following her graduation from USC, Dr. Moser entered into a larger private practice, allowing to pursue her interests in her favorite aspects of dentistry and continue her education. Her hobby quickly became researching, training, and mastering new technologies and skills within the ever-changing field of dentistry. She is never one to miss out on an opportunity to further her education.

She moved to Texas in 2016 to begin working at a private practice until she found the perfect location to establish her very own. Today, she is thrilled to welcome patients to Moser Family & Cosmetic Dentistry. As the sole owner and practitioner, Dr. Moser takes pride in the quality of care she provides her patients. She has built a team that shares her dream and is as dedicated to providing exceptional care as she is. The level of care and dedication she provides cannot be matched by any other local practice, and her patients feel and understand this deeply.

Her Passion for Dentistry

Today, her passion for dentistry is fueled by helping her patients achieve the best oral health possible, getting them out of pain, and getting to truly know and serve her community. Dr. Moser’s focus on preventative care ensures that her patients not only receive excellent care but are educated and empowered to make the best oral healthcare decisions that suit them and their lifestyle.

When not at her practice or seeking out educational opportunities, Dr. Moser enjoys staying physically fit and active with her husband Michael and their two Boston terriers. She also stays active in the community as a member of the board of the Chamber of Commerce and engages in any community service-oriented opportunities available like Budas Brightside.
