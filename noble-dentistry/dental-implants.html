Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and routine dental care, implants can last many years—often decades.

Is the implant placement painful?

Local anesthesia keeps you comfortable during the procedure. Most patients report minimal discomfort afterward, similar to a simple extraction.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The process varies based on healing time and whether additional treatments are required. Many patients complete treatment within several months.

What if I am missing multiple teeth?

Implants can support bridges or dentures, making them an excellent solution for multiple missing teeth.
