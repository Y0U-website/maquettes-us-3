Dental Implants

What is a dental implant, and how does it work? A dental implant is placed, or implanted, into the jawbone. When this is done, it stimulates bone growth through osseo integration. The osseo integration process sets dental implants apart from other tooth replacement options. They integrate into the bone, providing the best stability and durability among tooth replacement solutions available at our office. With such a strong foundation, patients a reat ease knowing their implant will last a lifetime with proper care and attention!

How do I determine if I am a good candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants require patients to be proper candidates before moving forward with oral surgery to place them. Drs. Eliza Berris, Sophie Aquino, and Aravinda Shetty of La Habra, California’s Grace Dentistry, will evaluate you during your initial consultation to determine if you are a good fit. Dental X-rays are taken, and using these images, our team can decide if sufficient bone of the jaw is available to ensure a successful procedure. Patients with insufficient bone may find that preparatory procedures such as bone grafting can help them obtain dental implants in the future.
