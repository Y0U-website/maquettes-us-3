At Pacana Dental, our entire staff is committed to lifelong learning and continuous improvement, to provide you with the best treatment plans. As a result, we are always able to offer our patients the highest quality of dental care.

Dr. Mark Pacana, DDS

Dr. Mark Pacana is proud to serve the community that he loves.

He received a bachelor of science degree in Biology and minor in Philosophy at Loyola University Chicago. Following his tenure as a Rambler, he received the title of a doctor of dental surgery and graduated from the the University of Illinois at Chicago (UIC) College of Dentistry in 2000.

Shortly after, Dr. Pacana was invited to extend his training as an Oral Surgery Extern. After several years as a practicing associate, he moved to Algonquin, Illinois, and established his Pacana Family Dental in 2005.

Licensed dentists are required to meet a minimum number of continuing education courses each year, and Dr. Pacana takes great pride in being able to partake in several classes to help him become a more well-rounded dentist in order to enhance treatment. He is also a proud member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, and McHenry County Dental Society.

Outside of his dental practice, Dr. Pacana enjoys spending time with his family. He has two sons that keep him busy out of office hours, as they both play competitive hockey. Dr. Pacana also enjoys golfing and fishing. When he is not working hard on your teeth, he is out working hard on the ice as part of a men’s league hockey team.
