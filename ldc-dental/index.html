Leslie Flahaven, DDS, chose her path in grade school and didn’t look back. Her interest in dentistry started with her mother and her childhood orthodontist and the fascination grew from there.

Being the first person to go to college on her mother’s side, she knew her path was long and hard. She earned her first degree, a Bachelors of Science, from the University of Illinois @ Urbana-Champaign where she graduated with honors in 2001.
