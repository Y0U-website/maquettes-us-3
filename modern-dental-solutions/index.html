The practice

At Modern Dental Solutions our mission is to provide high-quality digital dental restorations and customer service that meet the unique needs of each patient. We strive to be a reliable and trusted partner for dental professionals, delivering consistent and accurate results every time.

Locally owned and operated by Marcus and Shana Faust. Both have 25+ years of experience in crafting fixed restorations. Marcus earned his CDT in 2007 and stays current with ongoing continuing education. We stay up-to-date on the latest technologies and techniques to ensure that we can provide the best possible service to our dentists and patients.

Our laboratory is equipped with the latest technology and equipment, allowing us to produce dental restorations and appliances of the highest quality. 3Shape Dental System is used for CAD design while Aidite Zirconia is milled on the Aidite AMD-500DC and sintered with the newest CSF-400Pro furnaces. 3D printing is done with the latest from Aidite’s new printer options the P1 & F1 printers.
