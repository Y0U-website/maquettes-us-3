Our Team

Doctors

Dr. Nishita Irukulla

DDS.

Dr. Irukulla went to New York University College of Dentistry, where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. An avid learner and a perfectionist with a penchant for constantly improving herself, Dr. Irukulla has continuously been studying and applying her knowledge to her practice. She also completed her Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry and is currently pursuing her Masters with the Academy of General Dentistry. Where the state board requires 30 CE credits in 2 years, Dr. Irukulla has obtained 800 educational credits so far and still counting.

When off-work, she loves listening to music and playing sitar, a stringed instrument. She enjoys cooking and spending time with her friends and family. Her other interest areas include reading books, playing board games, hiking, and going for long walks. Dr. Irukulla has also participated in many community services like going on an outreach program to Guatemala, providing free dental check-ups for the local school and providing Thanksgiving dinners to the local, needy families amongst others.

Dr. Rustam Shukurov

DMD

Dr. Shukurov goes out of his way to collaborate with patients and informs them at every step about the course of treatment he plans for them. His own first visit to a dentist was an unpleasant one, and thus, he does everything in his capacity to make the patient comfortable and at ease.

The customer-first approach at New Falls Dental is what resonates with Dr. Shukurov significantly. We build trust with our patients, which goes a long way and, at the same time, offer services that are much more affordable than many. He appreciates that there is a constant focus on skill up-gradation at New Falls Dental, and the focus we have put on the aesthetics of our office to create an exceptional experience.

In his free time, Dr. Shukurov enjoys spending time with his wife and young sons, hiking, playing video games, watching movies, and pursuing his interest in science and astronomy.

Administrative Team

Mitesh Shah

Manager

Mitesh is originally from India, earned his Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering from The Maharaja Sayajirao University in Baroda. After relocating to the United States in 2009, Mitesh shifted his focus toward business and management, quickly rising from a team member to a team leader within a few months.

He went on to serve as an office manager for over 15 years, successfully overseeing operations across five dental practices. His core belief in leadership is rooted in nurturing strong teams and growing practices while always prioritizing exceptional patient care.

Now residing in the suburbs of Philadelphia with his wife, Mitesh serves as our office manager, bringing a wealth of experience and passion to his role.

In his free time, he enjoys movie nights with his family and peaceful walks through Peace Valley Park. Mitesh is dedicated to building a well-rounded, knowledgeable dental team committed to advancing their skills and serving the community’s oral health needs.

He believes in working closely with patients to understand their goals and deliver outstanding, personalized care.
