Healthcare Services

Good health care enables us to live healthy lives and to contribute to our society. Northeast Valley Health Corporation (NEVHC) reaches out to thousands of people who may have been otherwise turned away from getting health care services due to lack of insurance. We are able to provide our patients with comprehensive primary health care services.

Our primary health care services include:

  • Healthcare Services - NEVHC SCV Dental & Wellness Health Center and Van Nuys Women’s and Reproductive Health Center NOW OPEN. For more information click here here.
