About Dr. Meiss

Kenneth D. Meiss, DMD, grew up on a family farm in Fairbury, Illinois, where he learned the value of hard work, responsibility and serving others. Those early experiences continue to influence the way he approaches dentistry and caring for his patients.

Dr. Meiss earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from Illinois Wesleyan University before earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in 1997.

A Patient-Centered Approach to Dentistry

Dr. Meiss believes good dental care starts with listening to patients, understanding their concerns and explaining their options clearly.

Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, he works with each patient to develop care based on their individual dental needs, goals and overall oral health. His goal is to help patients feel informed and comfortable with their treatment decisions while building long-term relationships based on trust.

Serving the Peoria Area

Dr. Meiss is proud to provide dental care to patients and families in the Peoria area. His connection to central Illinois began growing up in Fairbury, and he continues to value the relationships he has built with patients and families throughout the community.

Life Outside the Practice

Dr. Meiss and his wife, Marli, have three daughters, Elsa, Emma and Tessa, and a Siamese cat named Busy.

Ken and his family attend Bethany Baptist Church in Edwards, Illinois. His faith is an important part of his life, and he and his family believe their greatest calling is giving glory to God and their Savior, Jesus Christ.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ken enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling, hiking, running, downhill skiing, playing golf and helping his father on the family farm.
