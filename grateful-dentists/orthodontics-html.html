Invisalign Clear Braces

Invisalign utilizes customized, clear aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions. To stay on-track with your treatment timeline, you’ll need to wear your trays for at least 22 hours each day, only taking them out when you brush and floss or eat. Throughout the process, you’ll visit our dental office every six to eight weeks for regular checkups and to pick up your new series of aligners.

SureSmile

SureSmile also uses customized clear aligners to straighten teeth and address minor bite issues. We’ll start the process by capturing a digital scan of your mouth that we’ll send to our SureSmile laboratory. From there, we’ll be able to develop the best treatment plan to help you get the results you want. When your first set of trays arrive at our dental office, we’ll contact you so you can come in and learn how to use them.
