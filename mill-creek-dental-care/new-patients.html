New Patients

Welcome to Mill Creek Dental Care

As a new patient, your journey to optimal oral health begins with our comprehensive first visit at Mill Creek Dental Care. Dr. Skidmore personally oversees this initial appointment, conducting a thorough examination that includes a periodontal screening for gum disease and an oral cancer/head and neck examination. Additionally, we will take a full mouth set of digital radiographs, unless records have been transferred from a previous dental office, to establish a baseline view of each tooth.

Our approach is highly collaborative; we value your concerns and expectations as they shape the path to your customized treatment plan. Dr. Skidmore takes the time to complete the entire new patient appointment, allowing him to get to know you and your dental needs. Subsequent cleanings will be performed by our experienced hygienists, followed by a comprehensive examination with Dr. Skidmore.

Our Mission, Your Smile

Our goal is to steer you towards achieving optimal oral health using the least invasive treatments possible. We strongly believe in the power of clear communication and encourage you to ask any questions you may have—whether they pertain to treatments, insurance, or billing. If we don t have an immediate answer, you can trust that we will diligently find it for you. By eliminating any communication barriers, we can provide exceptional service and assist you in attaining a beautiful, healthy smile.

Getting Started

To make your first visit seamless, we ve made available a few forms for you to fill out in advance. This will help us understand your medical history and tailor our services to meet your unique needs.
