The practice

We use artificial intelligence to analyze dental X-rays with microscopic accuracy. This provides an objective "second opinion" to support confident, conservative treatment planning.

Our new 3D imaging provides detailed three-dimensional views of your dental anatomy. This is essential for comprehensive diagnostics and precise, safe dental implant planning.

Using the Primescan system, we can design and deliver permanent restorations in a single visit. Eliminating the need for traditional impressions or temporary crowns.

As our practice continues to grow, you may notice updates to our look and technology. What remains unchanged is our dedication to serving the communities of Union City, Hayward, Fremont, and Newark with integrity, transparency, and respect.

We proudly accept most PPO insurance plans and are committed to helping patients understand and maximize their benefits. We look forward to welcoming you and showing you how thoughtful care and modern dentistry come together at Nori Dental Studio.
