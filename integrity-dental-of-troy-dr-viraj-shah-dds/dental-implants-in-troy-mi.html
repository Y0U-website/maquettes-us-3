Question About Dental Implants

Who Is A Good Candidate For Dental Implants

If you have lost one or more of your natural teeth or want a permanent tooth replacement, our dental implants in Troy, MI, might be the right option for you. The primary requirements for this procedure are optimal bone thickness and good oral hygiene. During your initial evaluation, our dentist will assess your oral cavity to determine if you are eligible for this treatment.

We will also evaluate your dental and medical history to determine any factors that may affect the treatment’s success. If you do not have sufficient bone thickness, we may recommend additional treatments, such as bone grafts, to enhance your candidacy for this procedure.

Process of Dental Implants in Troy, MI

After your consultation and oral examination, we will devise a treatment plan. The first step of this procedure is implant surgery. Our team will first inject a local anesthetic to numb the treatment region and minimize pain. We will then place a small cut on the gumline and expose the underlying bone.

Our skilled dentist will create a small hole in the jawbone, equal to the size and thickness of the implant. After inserting the implant, we will close the incision and allow the implant to heal. The healing period may last 3 to 6 months.
