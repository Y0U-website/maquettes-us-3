ABOUT US

Dr. Fady Farid

DDS

Dr. Fady Farid moved to Tennessee when he was 17 years old and attended Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU).

Dr. Farid obtained his dental degree at Meharry Medical College in 2018. He graduated first in his class with several awards, including physiology, microbiology, neuroscience, and pharmacology. Dr. Farid furthered his surgical training in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Implantology by attending several continuing education classes.

Dr. Farid met his wife, Youstina, in 2017 and has two children, Natalie and Melana. Besides his passion for healthcare and education, Dr. Farid enjoys spending time with his family and friends.

Our Mission

We provide quality and affordable dental care for patients of all ages. In addition, we offer a wide range of dental services, including preventative, cosmetic, restorative and emergency dental care. As such, we pledge to provide dental services and products with professional quality that meet the standards of the American Dental Association (ADA). As well as the government agencies governing the dental profession. We pride ourselves on our patient-centered practice.

At Infinity Dental, we do everything possible to make dental visits pleasant and a positive experience for our patients, by providing world class amenities and customer service. As a result, we continually strive to earn the loyalty of our patients and expect that we will see second and even third generations. Therefore, our state of the art dental office is dedicated in providing high quality and affordable dental care.
