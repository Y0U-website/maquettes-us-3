Dr. Derek T. Dunlap

Meet Derek T. Dunlap, DMD

Doctor Dunlap practices the full scope of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, but his special interests include wisdom teeth , dental implants , and orthognathic surgery. In his spare time, he enjoys playing golf, being outdoors, and spending time with his wife, Renee, and their three beautiful children.

Professional Organizations

Dr. Derek T. Dunlap is a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology. He is also a member of the South Carolina Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the American Dental Association, and the South Carolina Dental Association.
