Our Invisalign for Teens Process

Initial Consultation and Assessment

The journey to a straighter smile begins with an in-depth consultation at Helotes Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics. Our orthodontists will assess your teen&#x27;s dental health to determine if Invisalign is the most suitable option. Learn more about our orthodontic services and how we can help your teen achieve their ideal smile.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Utilizing advanced 3D imaging technology, we create a comprehensive treatment plan that outlines the precise movements of your teen&#x27;s teeth. This ensures that the aligners are tailored to provide the best possible results. Our cutting-edge technology and modern techniques guarantee high-quality care.

Using the Aligners

Your teen will receive a set of aligners, each to be worn for about two weeks. These aligners will gradually move their teeth into the desired position. Regular check-ups with our orthodontists will monitor progress and make necessary adjustments. Our team is always available to answer any questions throughout the treatment process.

Ongoing Care and Maintenance

We provide thorough instructions on how to care for the aligners and maintain excellent oral hygiene during treatment. Periodic visits to our office ensure your teen&#x27;s progress is on track. Our cleanings and exams services help keep their teeth healthy throughout their Invisalign journey.
