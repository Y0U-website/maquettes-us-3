Frequently asked questions

They should notice you look well, not that you have new teeth. Natural translucency, surface texture, and length variation are what keep cosmetic dentistry from looking artificial.

A whitening and bonding case can finish in two visits. A veneer case typically spans three to four weeks. Cases that include aligners first run several months longer.

Purely elective work generally is not, though treatment with a functional component — such as restoring worn or fractured teeth — often receives partial coverage. We check before you commit.
