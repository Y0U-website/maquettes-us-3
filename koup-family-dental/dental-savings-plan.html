In-House Dental Savings Plan Preview

Affordable Dental Coverage for You and Your Family Coming Soon! Call Us with Inquiries.

Don’t have dental insurance? Not a problem!

More affordable dental care is now within your reach.

BENEFITS OF OUR IN-HOUSE SAVINGS PLAN:

  • No yearly maximum
  • No deductibles
  • No claims or pre-authorizations required
  • No limitations for pre-existing conditions
  • No waiting period for treatment
  • 1 free consultation

SAVINGS PLAN FEATURES

  • 1 comprehensive exam (new patients)
  • 2 dental cleanings
  • 2 dental exams with oral cancer screening
  • 1 set Diagnostic Bite-Wing Xrays (taken as needed) *
  • 1 flouride treatment (children only)
  • 1 emergency exam
  • 1 emergency X-ray
  • 1 free consultation

PLAN DISCOUNTED SERVICES

* Our practice follows the ADA recommendation for bitewing X-rays, which is every 12-24 months. Full mouth X-rays and/or panoramic X-rays are recommended every 3-5 years on a case-by-case basis in adults, more often in children.

Have more questions? Ask our staff for our X-ray information sheet. Please note, if X-rays are refused, our office will require you to sign a statement indicating refusal and, in some cases, may result in terminating patient treatment.

  • 25 % off additional full-mouth X-rays
  • 15 % off additional emergency X-rays
  • 15 % off additional cleanings
  • 15 % off restorative procedures (fillings, crowns, sealants, etc.)
  • $500 off Invisalign Treatment (full treatment only)
  • $100 off office teeth whitening

ANNUAL PLAN COST

Plan Guidelines

  • Membership fees are due the day of plan enrollment
  • No refunds of premiums will be issued even if plan benefits are not used during the participation period
  • Membership expires one year from joining the program
  • Re-enrollment is required at the conclusion of the 12-month period and may be automatically billed

Plan Exclusions and Limitations

  • Is standalone coverage cannot be combined with insurance benefits
  • Cannot be combined with Care Credit
  • Cannot be used in a workman’s compensation treatment plan
  • Is for use in treatments that lie within Koup Family Dental’s realm of expertise
  • Does not apply for dental procedures that are referred to specialists
  • Does not apply to hospitalization or hospital charges of any kind
  • Does not apply for costs of dental care that are covered under automobile or medical insurance
  • Does not apply to consumable items such as Sonicare, Waterpik, ClinPro toothpaste, etc.
