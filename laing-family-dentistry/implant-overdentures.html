How Do Implant Overdentures Work?

  • Placement of Implants Typically, 2-4 implants are placed into the jawbone to provide support for the overdentures.
  • Attachment Mechanism The overdentures snap securely onto the implants using attachments, offering a snug fit without the need for adhesives.
  • Removability While implant overdentures remain securely in place during daily use, they can be removed for easy cleaning.

The Process of Getting Implant Overdentures

  • Initial Consultation Dr. Robert Laing will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and goals to determine if implant overdentures are right for you. If bone loss is a concern, bone grafting may be recommended.

FAQs About Implant Overdentures

How long do implant overdentures last?

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime, while the overdentures typically last 5-10 years before needing replacement.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes, implant overdentures are designed to snap securely onto implants but can be removed for easy cleaning.

How many implants are needed for overdentures?

Typically, 2-4 implants are used to support an overdenture, depending on your jawbone health and personal preferences.

Does insurance cover implant overdentures?

Insurance may cover part of the cost. We’ll work with you to maximize your benefits and offer financing options for any remaining balance.
