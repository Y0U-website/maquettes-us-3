Procedures Offered

Adult orthodontic treatment may include any of the following:

Metal Braces

Metal braces are the traditional stainless steel brackets and wires that are silver in color.

Clear Brackets

Clear brackets work like metal braces but they blend in more discreetly with your teeth.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable plastic trays that shift your teeth into alignment gradually. They are a popular choice for adults because they are virtually invisible on your teeth.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces attach to the back side of your teeth and are less visible.

Frequently Asked Questions About Adult Orthodontics

How long does adult orthodontic treatment take?

Just like with children and teens, the length of orthodontic treatment varies from patient to patient. It depends mostly on the extent of the corrections you need. Adult treatment tends to take slightly longer because the teeth are more firmly set in place and the jaw is no longer growing.

Is it worth getting orthodontic treatment as an adult?

You only get one set of permanent teeth in your lifetime, and orthodontic treatment can help keep your natural teeth healthy so that they last for the rest of your life. Treatment may also be worth it if you consistently experience jaw pain and headaches due to a misaligned bite.

How does orthodontic treatment help with snoring and sleep apnea?

Snoring and sleep apnea are primarily caused by the tongue sliding back toward the throat to block your airway while you sleep. If your teeth are in proper alignment and the jaw is in the correct position, it prevents the tongue from sliding back and your airway stays open.

How do I know if I need orthodontic treatment as an adult?

We provide complimentary consultations for adults as well as children. If you come in for an evaluation we can assess the current position of your teeth and make a recommendation for treatment if needed.

Does insurance cover adult orthodontics?

Dental insurance does not always cover adult orthodontics, but it may in some cases. Most plans cover orthodontic treatment up to age 19. If you know you want to undergo treatment as an adult, you may be able to find a plan that provides coverage for adult orthodontics.

Are there any disadvantages to getting orthodontic treatment as an adult?

here are a few disadvantages to getting orthodontic treatment as an adult, such as:

  • Treatment may take longer.
  • TMJ symptoms may increase at first, but improve as treatment goes on.
  • In rare cases root resorption can occur due to the shifting of the teeth, but will be carefully monitored.
  • Teeth may develop sensitivity.

Am I a good candidate for adult orthodontics?

You may be a good candidate for adult orthodontics if your teeth and gums are healthy, but the teeth and bite are not properly aligned. After a brief evaluation we can make a recommendation for treatment.
