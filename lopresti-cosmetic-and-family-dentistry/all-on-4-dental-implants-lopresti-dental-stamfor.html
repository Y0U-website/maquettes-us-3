Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • How can I clean my All-on-4 dental implants? Gently brush with a soft-bristle toothbrush twice daily, use a water flosser to clean under the bridge, and don’t forget the regular dental checkups. A little daily care ensures your smile stays beautiful and your implants last a lifetime.
  • Is the All-on-4 procedure painful? Most patients experience only mild discomfort, thanks to modern techniques. Recovery is typically smooth, and any soreness fades quickly. Our team is here every step of the way to ensure your comfort—and your new smile—are worth every moment.
  • How long will All-on-4 implants last? All-on-4 dental implants are designed to last many years—often 20 years or more with proper care. They’re a long-term investment in your smile, offering strength, comfort, and confidence. With regular checkups and good hygiene, your new smile can stay strong and beautiful for decades.
  • Do All-on-4 dental implants look like natural teeth? All-on-4 dental implants are designed to look and feel just like natural teeth. With custom shading, shape, and placement, they beautifully restore your smile. Patients often can’t tell the difference—and neither can others. It’s a life-changing, confident step toward a natural-looking, fully functional smile you’ll love to share.
