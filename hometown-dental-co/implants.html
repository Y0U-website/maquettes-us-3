Simple extractions

Simple extractions involve teeth that have erupted fully from the gums.

In the case of simple extractions, we can see the crown of the tooth before we begin.

Are You a Candidate?

Most adults in good overall health are potential candidates for dental implants. We evaluate:

  • Sufficient bone density
  • Healthy gum tissue
  • Overall medical history
  • Commitment to oral hygiene

Special Considerations

Don’t worry if you’ve been told you lack sufficient bone density in the past. Modern techniques like bone grafting have expanded possibilities for many patients who were previously considered unsuitable for implants.
