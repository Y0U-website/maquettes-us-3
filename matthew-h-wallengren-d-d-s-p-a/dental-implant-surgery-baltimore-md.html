Why do dental implants cost more than other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants involve multiple components and advanced clinical planning. The process includes placing a biocompatible implant post into the jawbone, allowing time for healing, and restoring the tooth with a custom crown. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants replace both the tooth and its root, which helps preserve jawbone health. Their durability, stability, and natural feel contribute to their long-term value, even though the initial investment may be higher.

What factors affect how much dental implant surgery costs?

Several factors influence the overall cost of dental implant surgery. These may include the number of implants needed, the condition of the jawbone, and whether preparatory procedures such as bone grafting are required. The type of restoration placed on top of the implant and the complexity of your individual case also play a role. As every patient’s needs are different, a consultation is necessary to determine an accurate treatment plan.

Does dental insurance help cover dental implant surgery?

Dental insurance coverage for implants differs widely. Some plans may contribute toward portions of the procedure, such as extractions, imaging, or the final crown, while others may offer limited or no coverage for the implant itself. Dr. Wallengren’s team can help review your benefits and explain what may apply to your restorative care, while the surgeon’s team can help you understand the costs related to the surgical care, allowing you to better understand your financial responsibility before treatment begins.

Are dental implants worth the cost over time?

Many patients find dental implants to be a worthwhile investment because of their longevity and function. Unlike removable options, implants are designed to function like natural teeth and can last for many years with proper care. They do not rely on neighboring teeth for support and help maintain bone structure, which may reduce the need for future dental work. For many patients, this long-term reliability adds significant value.

Can dental implant treatment be planned in stages to manage cost?

Yes. Dental implant treatment is often completed in phases, which can help make the process more manageable. As healing time is required between steps, treatment naturally occurs over several months. This phased approach may allow patients to plan financially while still moving forward with care. During your consultation, Dr. Wallengren will review your options and outline a timeline that fits your needs.
