Fixed retainers are convenient because of their permanent bond to your teeth. They are not like removable retainers; you cannot get them off before eating or brushing your teeth. Moreover, loosening up your retainers is not a good idea because they are tightly attached to your teeth. At our Leander, TX, practice, we provide fixed retainers that are packed with benefits for misaligned teeth!

However, one issue that is a given with fixed-type retainers is their maintenance of oral hygiene. Since they are locked in at the back, the food you eat can easily entrap in that area, increasing the risk of plaque buildup. This, in turn, may lead to gum inflammation and consequent bleeding. Contact our experts for dental cleaning and oral health maintenance in such a situation.
