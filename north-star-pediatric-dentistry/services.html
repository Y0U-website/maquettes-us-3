Pediatric Dental Services - Preventive, Restorative & Emergency Care

Our Services

Comprehensive dental care for your child at every stage

Infant Dentistry

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a child&#x27;s first dental visit by age one. Early visits help us monitor development, catch potential issues early, and establish good oral health habits from the start.

Preventive Care

Prevention is the foundation of good oral health. Our preventive services include regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, dental sealants, and comprehensive exams to keep your child&#x27;s smile healthy and cavity-free.

Restorative Care

When cavities or damage occur, we provide gentle restorative treatments including tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and pulp therapy. We use the latest techniques to ensure comfort and optimal results.

Emergency Care

Dental emergencies don&#x27;t wait for convenient times. Whether it&#x27;s a knocked-out tooth, severe toothache, or dental injury, we offer same-day emergency appointments to address urgent issues promptly.

Sedation Dentistry

For anxious patients or complex procedures, we offer safe sedation options including nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation to help your child relax and have a comfortable, stress-free experience.

Dental Exams & X-Rays

Regular exams and digital X-rays help us monitor your child&#x27;s oral development and detect any issues early. We use low-radiation digital X-rays for the safest imaging possible.

Orthodontic Evaluation

We monitor your child&#x27;s bite development and can identify orthodontic issues early. When appropriate, we&#x27;ll refer to trusted orthodontic specialists and coordinate care for the best results.

Parent Education

We believe in empowering parents with knowledge about proper oral care, nutrition, and habits. Our team takes time to answer questions and provide guidance for maintaining your child&#x27;s oral health at home.

Special Needs Dentistry

We welcome patients with special healthcare needs and provide individualized care plans. Dr. E has specialized training in treating patients who require extra attention and accommodation.
