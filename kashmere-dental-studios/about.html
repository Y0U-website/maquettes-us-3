Behind the scenes at Kashmere Dental Studios

At Kashmere Dental Studios, dentistry isn’t just about teeth—it’s about people, stories, and creating experiences that leave you smiling long after you leave our chair.

Led by Dr. Kash, a dentist with a background in film and a passion for comfort-first care, our studio blends high-quality clinical expertise with a bold, cinematic atmosphere that feels anything but ordinary. From sedation options for anxious patients to compassionate care for kids and families, we’ve built a place where every detail is designed to make you feel safe, seen, and at ease.

Our team of 3 believes great care starts with genuine connection. Together, we go above and beyond to make sure every patient feels comfortable, supported, and truly treated like family—from the moment you walk in to the moment you leave. It’s our shared promise and daily approach: All For One… One For All.

The first visit experience

Welcome and check-in

Step into a bright, spa-like space where calming scents, soft music, and friendly faces set the stage for a relaxed experience. Our team will guide you through check-in and answer any quick questions.

Getting to know you

Dr. Kash takes time to connect—whether that’s asking your child about their favorite superhero or chatting with you about past dental experiences. We want every patient to feel heard and comfortable before we begin.

Gentle exam & X-rays

Depending on age and needs, we may take digital x-rays and complete a thorough but gentle exam. Kids who feel anxious can go at their own pace, there’s never any rush or pressure.

Clear next steps

Dr. Kash explains everything in simple, relatable terms. You’ll leave with a personalized plan focused on prevention, comfort, and confidence for your family’s smiles.

Smart technology for safer, smoother care

Digital X-rays Our digital imaging allows us to detect issues faster and with less radiation exposure, helping us keep your visit efficient and informative — especially for squirmy little ones or patients with dental anxiety.

Our advanced digital scanner creates fast, highly accurate 3D images of your teeth, eliminating the need for messy, uncomfortable impression material in most cases. This means a more comfortable experience, greater precision for crowns, aligners, and other restorations, and often fewer appointments with a better-fitting final result.

Intraoral photos allow patients to clearly see what the dentist sees inside their mouth, making diagnoses and treatment recommendations easier to understand. These images improve communication, build trust, and help patients make informed decisions about their dental health.
