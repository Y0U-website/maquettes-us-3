The practice

We offer a full range of general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and restorative dental procedures. Our team is led by Jacob Wallach, DDS who is highly trained and has considerable experience treating complex dental problems such as worn teeth and broken teeth.

Located along the beautiful Hudson River, Nyack Smiles combines the friendliness and warmth of an old-fashioned family practice with the latest advancements and techniques of modern dental practice. Dr. Wallach and his staff are committed to advanced training and regularly attend continuing education courses to remain at the forefront of modern dentistry techniques.

Dr. Wallach considers cosmetics in every aspect of his dentistry with the goal of helping each patient achieve a beautiful, natural-looking smile.

Restorative dentistry can include treatment for gum disease, root canals and tooth extractions.

As partners in your health, our hygienists provide comprehensive preventative care and in-depth education to identify potential issues early and maintain optimal oral health.

We use sophisticated dental technology in our Nyack dental office to aid in our diagnosis and treatment planning that allows us to deliver safer, more precise and more comfortable treatment to our patients.

Jacob Wallach, DDS is a family dentist in Rockland County, New York. He treats children as well as adults at his dentist office located in historic downtown Nyack along the Hudson River.

Dr. Wallach completed his dental education at Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine. He furthered his education with a General Practice Residency at Stony Brook University Hospital where he received extensive training in implant and cosmetic dentistry, as well as complex restorative dental care.
