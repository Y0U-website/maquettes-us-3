Meet Dr. Zachton Lowe

Dr. Zachton Lowe is proud to call this community home. Born and raised in Edmonds, he attended King’s High School—just down the road from our office. His journey into orthodontics began with a passion for helping people achieve confident, beautiful and healthy smiles through expert care and genuine connection.

In 2009, Dr. Lowe returned to Shoreline to join and take over his childhood orthodontic practice and serve the community that raised him. He brings advanced training and extensive experience in cutting-edge orthodontic technologies, including 3D radiography, TADs (temporary anchorage devices), clear aligners (invisalign), laser-assisted treatments, and surgically facilitated orthodontics.

Outside the office, Dr. Lowe enjoys a full and vibrant life with his wife, Dr. Nikki Chin, a general dentist. Together, they share a passion for continuing education, travel, food (ask him for restaurant recommendations!), and movies. They have twin daughters, Jemma and Zoe, and a son, Davis. Dr. Lowe cherishes coaching his kids’ basketball teams and spending time outdoors golfing or running. He’s also a lifelong fan of the Seahawks, Mariners, Sounders, and is still holding out hope for the return of his beloved Seattle SuperSonics.
