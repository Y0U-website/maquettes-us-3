Kristofer Popelas, D.M.D.

In 2006, Oak Hill Dental opened their doors to serve the same Township where he grew up. This comfortable and very friendly environment has made him the go-to Neighborhood Dentist . In order to keep their dental practice on the cutting edge though, a great deal of time is devoted to continuing education and research. This allows their practice to remain current with the latest dental technologies and advanced procedures, while still retaining that small-town feel.

While away from the office, Dr. Kris enjoys spending time with his lovely wife, Mary, and their two sons Benjamin and Parker. He is an avid golfer and fisherman and has a passion for all Pittsburgh sports, especially the Steelers.

Kristin

Kristin has been the Executive Director/Dental Hygienist since 2000. She loves educating her patients in a relaxed and friendly manner, which in turn strengthens their rapport and comfort level. In addition, her energetic and lively personality is a constant reminder that oral health care can be fun! In her free time, she loves spending time her wonderful friends and family, especially watching her husband Don, and daughter Aly, play ice hockey.

As an interesting side note, Dr. Kris and Kristin are actually brother and sister and opened Oak Hill Dental together in 2006. This has given a brand new perspective to what “Family Dentistry” really means. Not only are they family, but also make their patients feel like it too!

Kim

Kim has been a Dental Hygienist since 1994. She had a desire to make Oak Hill Dental her professional home after her very first visit to the office. Their up-to-date work environment and great team atmosphere made for a perfect fit. She always has an upbeat attitude and an exceptional way of easing a patient’s anxieties. She always enjoys the opportunity to help her patients understand why routine oral care is so beneficial to one’s overall health. In her free time, Kim enjoys spending time with her husband, Kevin, and their three children.
