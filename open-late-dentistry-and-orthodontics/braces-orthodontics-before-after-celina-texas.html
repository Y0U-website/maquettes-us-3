F.A.Q.’s

We post answers to our patients’ best questions regarding their dental care to be as transparent about your visit to our office as possible

How much does orthodontic care typically cost?

Cost can vary depending on your insurance coverage, the type of orthodontic treatment, and whether you need limited or comprehensive care. To help you plan ahead, we’ll check your insurance benefits before your visit. If you’re self-paying, we’ll review your treatment options and provide a clear estimate during your consultation—so there are no surprises when it’s time to start.

How late can I get an appointment?

We are open as late as 7pm currently with existing patients able to book prime-time cleanings from 6pm-7pm. New patients are required to come in for their 90 minute new patient visit as late as 5:30 if all paperwork is completed and insurance is verified if using insurance benefits.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

We are in network with most every PPO plan. We recommend letting our staff do a Complimentary Benefit Check for you to confirm. Please just ask during your new patient phone call. That is what we are here for 🙂

What is a Virtual Ortho Consultation?

A Virtual Ortho Consultation is an online appointment that allows you to meet with our orthodontic team from the comfort of your home. Using photos, videos, or live video chat, we can evaluate your smile, discuss your concerns, review possible treatment options, and answer your questions—no in-office visit required. It’s a convenient first step to see if orthodontic care is right for you before scheduling an in-person exam.
