How the Process Works

Start to finish usually takes 3–6 months — healing between placement and final crown does most of the waiting:

  • Consultation and Evaluation: Digital imaging maps your bone and plans exact placement — with an exact quote.
  • Implant Placement: Precise, fully-numbed insertion of the titanium root into your jawbone.
  • Healing and Integration: The implant fuses with your bone over a few months for rock-solid stability.
  • Restoration: A custom crown is attached — matched to your natural teeth so nobody can tell.
  • Some patients need dental bone grafting first to rebuild support — we’ll tell you up front if you do.
