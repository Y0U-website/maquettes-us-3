Services Offered for Preventative Dental Care in Missoula Dental

Missoula Dental offers a wide range of services to help prevent dental problems from occurring. These services include:

Dental check-ups Regular dental check-ups every six months can help identify potential dental issues early on.

Dental cleanings Professional dental cleanings remove built-up plaque and tartar from teeth, which can lead to gum disease and tooth decay.

X-rays Dental x-rays can identify issues that are not visible to the naked eye, such as cavities or issues with the root of the tooth.

Fluoride treatments Fluoride treatments can help strengthen teeth and prevent tooth decay.

Dental sealants Dental sealants can be applied to teeth to prevent cavities from forming.

Oral cancer screenings During dental check-ups, dentists can also screen for signs of oral cancer, which is most treatable when caught early on.

In addition to these services, our dentist in Missoula Dental can also provide education on good oral hygiene practices, as well as advice on healthy eating habits that promote strong teeth and gums.
