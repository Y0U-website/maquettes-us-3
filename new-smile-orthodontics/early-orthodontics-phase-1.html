Early Orthodontics for Growing Smiles in Manteca & Tracy, CA

Early orthodontic treatment—also known as Phase 1 orthodontics—focuses on guiding jaw development and addressing orthodontic concerns while your child is still growing.

What Is Early Orthodontics?

Phase 1 orthodontics is designed to guide the growth and development of your child’s teeth and jaws. Rather than waiting until all permanent teeth come in, early treatment allows us to intervene at the right time to prevent more serious issues.

This phase may include appliances such as expanders, limited braces, or other devices that help create space, correct bite alignment, and support proper jaw development.

The goal is not always to complete treatment in one phase, but to set the stage for a smoother, more efficient Phase 2 (full treatment) if needed later on.

Signs Your Child May Benefit from Early Treatment

Every child develops differently, but there are several signs that may indicate a need for early intervention or general Phase 1 orthodontics :

  • Mouth breathing
  • Snoring or noisy sleep
  • Restless or poor‑quality sleep
  • Narrow or “V‑shaped” upper jaw
  • Crossbite or jaw shifting
  • Early crowding of baby teeth
  • Persistent thumb or pacifier use
  • Mouth‑open resting posture
  • Speech issues from tongue posture
  • Cheek or lip biting
  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Jaw shifting or popping sounds
  • Teeth that don’t meet properly when biting

Benefits of Phase 1 Orthodontics

Starting treatment at the right time allows us to work with your child’s natural growth rather than against it.

  • Helps guide proper jaw growth and alignment
  • Creates space for incoming permanent teeth
  • Reduces the risk of impacted or severely crowded teeth
  • Can shorten or simplify future orthodontic treatment
  • Improves bite function and facial balance
  • Supports healthier airway and sleep quality
  • Encourages better tongue posture and oral habits
  • Helps reduce mouth breathing and snoring
  • Makes chewing and speaking more comfortable
  • Builds confidence at an early age

Our Process

Our Process at New Smile Orthodontics

We make early orthodontic care simple, comfortable, and personalized for every child.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation: We begin with a detailed exam, digital imaging, and a discussion of your child’s growth and development.
  • Customized Treatment Plan: If Phase 1 treatment is recommended, we design a plan tailored to your child’s specific needs and timing.
  • Gentle, Effective Treatment: We use modern orthodontic techniques and appliances designed for comfort and efficiency.
  • Growth Monitoring: After Phase 1, we continue to monitor your child’s development to determine if and when Phase 2 treatment is needed.
