Tina

Meet Tina, our dedicated office manager and the wife of Dr. Mark. With a background in nursing and a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Hope College, Tina combines her medical expertise with her passion for making the business side of the practice run smoothly.

Tina s journey in dentistry began in 1992 when her husband, Dr. Mark, started the practice. Over the years, she has worn different hats, managing accounts, working as a dental assistant, and taking on marketing responsibilities.

One of the aspects Tina loves most about her job is the opportunity to make both our team and patients feel special and appreciated. She is dedicated to creating a welcoming atmosphere where patients feel like part of our dental family, helping to ease any anxieties they may have about dental visits.

Outside the dental office, Tina s passion for community service shines through her volunteer work for various organizations, including Taste of Heaven and Kids Food Basket. She currently serves as the President of a local P.E.O. chapter, supporting women s education. Tina s diverse interests include gardening, reading, and traveling, with a unique flair for researching and planning trips. She is also a flautist and enjoys the art of calligraphy.

Holly

Holly has been working in the dental field since 1995 and alongside Dr. Mark since then as well. She lived out-of-state for a little while, then rejoined our team in 2004. Holly plays a crucial role in various procedures, from dental emergencies to crowns and fillings.

Holly s journey into dentistry began with on-the-job training under Dr. Mark s guidance, supplemented by x-ray courses at Kalamazoo Valley Community College. What she loves most about her role is creating a comfortable environment for patients, helping them achieve confident smiles. Holly s team spirit shines through as she collaborates with coworkers to ensure a smooth workflow and an enjoyable work environment.

Outside the dental office, Holly finds joy in tending to her flower beds, bird feeding, and playing with her grandsons.

Nancy

With nearly 30 years of experience as a licensed dental hygienist, Nancy brings a wealth of knowledge and compassion to her role at Dr. Richardson s office. She earned her associate s degree in Applied Science from Kalamazoo Valley Community College in 1996 and has been dedicated to helping patients achieve healthier smiles ever since. Nancy is passionate about creating a welcoming environment to ease dental anxiety and values the teamwork that drives exceptional patient care.

Outside of work, she enjoys embroidery, the intricate Ukrainian art of Pysanky egg writing, and tending to the shared backyard garden she maintains with her two sisters.

Valerie

With over 37 years of experience in dentistry, Valerie brings a deep commitment to patient care and oral health to Mark E. Richardson DDS. As a registered dental hygienist, she helps patients achieve and maintain their healthiest smiles through routine hygiene visits, always practicing with compassion, respect, and positivity.

Valerie earned her training from Kellogg Community College and recently joined Dr. Mark’s team, where she s excited to build meaningful relationships with both patients and colleagues. Her passion for dentistry started at a young age she loved visiting the dentist growing up and knew early on she wanted to be part of the field.

Outside the office, Valerie enjoys staying active and exploring the outdoors. You’ll often find her camping, running, kayaking, biking, hiking, or planning her next travel adventure. She’s also an avid reader and has a creative side she loves spray painting treasures she finds at garage sales. And if you re looking for a thrill-seeker, look no further: Valerie is a big fan of ziplining, even after a wild (and questionably safe!) experience in Jamaica.

Valerie looks forward to getting to know you and helping you feel comfortable and cared for during every visit!

Alex

Alex is a Care Coordinator who is passionate about creating a warm, welcoming experience for every patient who walks through the door. From the moment patients arrive to the moment they leave, Alex strives to make everyone feel comfortable, cared for, and valued. Her friendly personality and attentive approach help create a positive environment for both patients and team members alike.

Originally from Battle Creek, Michigan, Alex graduated from Lakeview High School and began her dental career in October 2025 before joining the practice in April 2026. Although she is still new to dentistry, she quickly discovered a love for being part of a team that helps improve the health and confidence of the community in a positive and supportive way.

One of Alex’s favorite parts of working in the dental field is building meaningful connections with patients while contributing to a caring team environment. She enjoys helping people feel at ease and ensuring they have the best experience possible during their visits.

Outside of the office, Alex enjoys playing video games on her PlayStation 5 and Xbox, with interests ranging from heartwarming story-driven games to thrilling horror adventures. She also has a unique passion for cosplay, where she creates costumes inspired by her favorite movies, shows, cartoons, anime, and video games. Alex even enters cosplay contests to showcase her creativity and craftsmanship, with hopes of winning one someday!

A fun fact about Alex is that she’s a huge fan of the Detroit Tigers. She loves watching games with her boyfriend and can name most of the players on the roster. Some of her favorite players include Dillon Dingler and Javier B ez.
