Get to know your pediatric dentist

Dr. Carrie Loewen

Dr. Carrie Loewen has dedicated her life to helping children develop healthy, happy relationships with dental care. Born in Northern California to a military family, Dr. Loewen learned early on how to adapt to change quickly, a skill that now helps her connect deeply with young patients and their evolving worlds and schedules. Her journey to dentistry began in high school when she helped brush her sister’s teeth during her orthodontic treatment. That small act sparked a lifelong passion for healthcare, one she brings into every patient visit.

With over 14 years of experience, Dr. Loewen brings a uniquely personal and comprehensive approach to pediatric care. Whether she’s polishing baby teeth while singing the ABCs or using advanced laser tools for tongue-tie releases , her gentle, hands-on style helps children feel comfortable and safe in the dental chair. She’s especially passionate about preventive care and empowering families with the tools and knowledge they need for lifelong oral health.

From digital x-rays with AI-read capabilities to intraoral scanning and ozone therapy, Dr. Loewen integrates the latest dental tech with the same care she’d want for her own children. Her scope of care expands to include your child’s overall health, airway issues, orthodontic development, and calming anxiety through sedation when needed. She’s also known for her personal accessibility and Saturday hours, which make scheduling easier for busy families.

Dr. Loewen’s vision for Pacific Palisades Pediatric Dentistry was to build a trusted, boutique-style dental home in Santa Monica, especially for families who moved here after the local wildfires. Honoring her mentors, Dr. Kamins and Dr. Trask, she has carried forward their legacy of excellence, caring, and community connection. Outside the office, she finds joy in hiking, swimming, teaching, and staying active as Parent Association President at her child’s school. Not to mention, she has a solid spider dribble - just in case basketball breaks out in the breakroom.

Continued care every change A Dental Home That Endures When circumstances required our practice to move to Santa Monica following the Palisades fires, Dr. Loewen’s vision remained clear: to preserve the sense of community and continuity families had come to rely on for years. This practice was always meant to be more than a dental office, it was created to be a true dental home. For families navigating displacement and change, maintaining familiar faces, trusted care, and a sense of normalcy became more important than ever, and that commitment continues today.

The friendly faces who make every visit special

Meet the team that treats your kids like family

From the moment you walk in, our team ensures your child feels seen, heard, and cared for. We’re here not just to provide excellent dental care- but to create a joyful, stress-free experience that makes kids feel right at home.

New to our practice? Here’s what you should know What to expect at your child’s first visit Your child’s first visit is the start of a lifelong foundation of trust and wellness. We take time to get to know each child and family, explain every step in kid-friendly ways, and ensure that comfort comes first.

Patient Experience From playful introductions to gentle hands-on care, every visit is designed to put children at ease. We walk kids through each part of their appointment, demonstrate tools on their hand if needed, and always let them know they’re free to ask questions or take a break. It’s a space where smiles and laughter are welcome - whether for a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure.

What To Bring Please bring the following to help make your first visit as smooth as possible:

Completed digital or paperless new patient forms

Your child’s insurance card (if applicable)

A list of medications and medical history

Comfort item for your child if they’d like (stuffed animal, blanket, etc.)

Exceptional amenities and technology for exceptional care

Comfort and innovation go hand-in-hand in our office. With amenities kids love and technology parents appreciate, we make every visit engaging and stress-free, so your child feels right at home while receiving top-quality care.

Comfort-First, Kid-Friendly Care

We’ve created a space where both children and parents feel comfortable the moment they step inside. From cozy seating areas to upbeat, welcoming decor, every detail is designed with families in mind.

TVs, Netflix & Headphones Every treatment room is equipped with a TV and Netflix to keep kids entertained and relaxed. We also offer headphones for an immersive - and quiet - experience during treatment.

Beverage Station Parents can help themselves to a fresh cup of coffee, tea, or water while they wait, making your time here as enjoyable as your child’s.

Advanced technology for easy visits

We invest in the latest pediatric dental tools to make procedures faster, easier, and more comfortable. Our technology allows for highly accurate diagnosis and treatment - while helping kids feel at ease along the way.

AI Digital Radiographs Our digital x-rays are read with AI-enhanced accuracy, providing enhanced diagnostics with far less radiation exposure than traditional methods.

Intraosseous Injection This advanced form of anesthesia allows for fast and localized numbing with minimal discomfort- making even more complex procedures easier on little ones.

Intraoral Scanner No more goopy impressions- our digital scanner takes 3D images quickly and comfortably, perfect for little mouths and tight schedules.

Silver Diamine Fluoride & Ozone Therapy We offer advanced non-invasive decay treatments, including silver diamine fluoride and ozone, to halt cavities in their tracks with kid-friendly ease.

CBCT & Intraoral Camera With detailed 3D scans and high-definition imagery, we gain a comprehensive look at your child’s oral health, ensuring precision in every diagnosis and treatment plan.

Smiles for our community, not just our practice

Our commitment to giving back is at the heart of who we are. During the challenges brought on by the fires, we were deeply grateful for the support of our community and colleagues who helped ensure families could continue receiving the care they deserve.

That same spirit of service shapes how Dr. Loewen stays involved beyond the dental chair. She serves as a volunteer on the cleft palate team at Providence Saint John’s and teaches future dentists as faculty at UCLA Pediatric Dentistry. She also visits local schools to educate children about oral health and is the proud president of the Parent Association at her child’s school. Through these efforts, we remain committed to giving back and helping our community stay as healthy and resilient as the smiles we treat.
