The practice

Welcome to Lofton Creek Dental, office of Yulee dentists, Dr. Alesia Apana and Dr. Jordan Carey. We are glad you have chosen us for your oral health needs and hope that every encounter with our office and staff exceeds your expectations. We know our patients have a choice in dentists, so we go out of our way to deliver the level of dental care and compassion we would want for our own families.

Medical technology has been advancing at a breakneck pace for the past several decades, and dentistry hasn’t been left behind. We are constantly looking for the newest forms of treatments and technology that will aid in providing both our younger and older patients with the best, personalized dental care in Yulee.

We value honesty, transparency, compassion, and precision, making Lofton Creek Dental one of the most highly rated dental offices in the Yulee area. We put our hearts into our work every day and consider it an honor and privilege to take care of you.

Dr. Apana is passionate about dentistry and how she can connect with her patients by giving them a beautiful and healthy smile.

She attended the University of Florida College of Dentistry and graduated in the top 10 % of her class. She then went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center in Bronx, NY. Because she demands the best for her patients, she is continually furthering her clinical education by learning new technologies and techniques aimed at providing the highest quality of care.

She also maintains an active membership in the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, and Northeast District Dental Association. In her free time, Dr. Apana enjoys spending time with her husband and son and traveling.

Dr. Carey earned her Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from the University of North Florida before completing her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine in Bradenton, Florida.
