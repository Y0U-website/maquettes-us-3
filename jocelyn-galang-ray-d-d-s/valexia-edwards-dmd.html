Valexia Edwards, DMD

Dr. Valexia Edwards grew up in Macon, Mississippi. She received her Bachelor of Arts degree in biology from Fisk University where she graduated Magna Cum Laude.

Dr. Edwards obtained her dental degree from the University of Mississippi Medical Center and furthered her education by completing a General Practice Residency at St. Peter’s hospital in Albany, New York.

In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family and friends as well as traveling. Dr. Edwards is passionate about helping others and strives to make every patient’s experience as comfortable as possible.
