Flexible Financing Options to Fit Your Monthly Budget

When insurance leaves a remaining balance or when a procedure is not fully covered, third-party financing offers a convenient way to divide costs into smaller, stress-free monthly payments. Oral surgeon Dr. Chaudhry partners with trusted healthcare lending platforms to provide flexible solutions for every financial situation.

CareCredit : CareCredit works like a dedicated healthcare credit card designed specifically for medical and surgical expenses. Qualifying patients can choose short-term financing plans with no interest if the total balance is paid within 6, 12, 18, or 24 months. For larger treatment plans, CareCredit offers extended terms with competitive interest rates.

Lending Club : Lending Club provides customized personal loans for healthcare expenses with predictable fixed monthly payments. Their transparent terms allow you to review sample payment plans, giving you peace of mind before making any commitment.

Cherry Financing : Cherry offers rapid approval processes directly from your smartphone, making it simple to split treatment costs into manageable installment options. With high approval rates and flexible payback terms, Cherry allows you to proceed with urgent or elective surgery without delay.

What Determines the Cost of Oral Surgery?

Every smile presents unique anatomical requirements. During your consultation, oral surgeon Dr. Chaudhry performs a comprehensive evaluation utilizing advanced 3D CBCT imaging to assess your bone density, nerve pathways, and structural health. Based on these findings, we build an itemized financial outline before any treatment takes place.

Key factors that influence total treatment costs include:

  • Complexity of the Procedure: Simple extractions require fewer resources than impacted wisdom teeth or complex full-arch reconstructions.
  • Supplemental Procedures: Treatments such as bone grafting or sinus lifts add structural support for implants but increase overall treatment scope.
  • Type of Anesthesia: Local anesthesia, nitrous oxide, and deep IV sedation carry different fee schedules based on duration and monitoring needs.
  • Insurance Coverage Limits: Annual maximums, deductibles, and co-insurance percentages vary depending on your specific policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does insurance cover oral surgery procedures like dental implants or wisdom teeth removal?

Coverage depends on your specific insurance provider and policy details. Many dental insurance plans offer partial coverage for wisdom teeth removal and tooth extractions, especially when deemed medically necessary.

Can I combine medical and dental insurance for my procedure with Dr. Chaudhry?

Yes. Certain oral surgery treatments such as impacted wisdom teeth extractions, facial trauma repair, bone grafting, or jaw surgery may qualify for coverage under medical insurance in addition to dental plans. Our financial coordinators review both policies to submit claims through the channel that yields the highest savings for you.

How do third-party financing plans like CareCredit work for oral surgery?

Third-party healthcare financing platforms allow you to pay for your procedure upfront while breaking the cost into affordable monthly payments over time. Programs like CareCredit and Lending Club offer promotional options with zero interest if the balance is settled within a specified timeline. You can apply online or in our office with quick approval results.

What should I bring to my financial consultation?

To help us provide an accurate estimate during your visit, please bring your current dental and medical insurance cards, a valid government-issued photo ID, any recent X-rays or referral slips from your general practitioner, and a list of any questions you have regarding payment options or treatment plans.
