Braces

Braces are a great appliance to help enhance the appearance and feel of your smile and bite. There are various types of braces that we offer, including traditional metal braces and clear braces. To learn more about braces, simply call our practice!

The most common version of braces is traditional metal braces, which are more comfortable than ever before with their sleek, low-profile design. Metal braces use metal brackets and archwires to straighten your teeth and are made of high-grade stainless steel. Colored elastics (rubber bands) can be added to metal braces to create a more distinctive and colorful look and are used to hold the wire in place.

Braces don’t always have to be made of metal. In fact, many modern braces are constructed of transparent or tooth-colored materials to give patients a more appealing, less conspicuous option than classic braces. Note that clear aligners aren’t the same as clear braces. Clear braces work just like traditional braces.

Invisalign Ⓡ

Our clinic is delighted to offer Invisalign clear aligners, which are a clear, convenient, and pleasant approach to get the smile you’ve always wanted. Invisalign’s unique SmartTrack® material will be used to create your custom aligners for a snug, comfortable fit and for optimal tooth movement. Invisalign is very popular among adult patients, but can also be a great option for teens who are disciplined and committed to their aligner schedule.. Our doctors plan your treatment in advance, creating an efficient plan so you can enjoy your new smile even sooner!

In-House Clear Aligners

We provide state-of-the-art, in-house clear aligners using iTero® digital scanning equipment and our own 3D printers in our Eau Claire & Chippewa Falls offices. Our in-house clear aligners are custom-made to fit your mouth and are nearly undetectable. So, most people won’t even know you’re wearing them while straightening your teeth. For optimal results, patients must wear their clear aligners for 20 to 22 hours daily to keep their teeth moving and on track.

Mini Implants/Temporary Anchorage Devices (TADS)

Temporary anchorage devices (TADs) are mini implants that can be placed in-office to provide stable anchorage. Placement is minimally invasive and typically not uncomfortable. TADs are removed once they are no longer needed during treatment. TADs can be used when closing space where permanent teeth are missing and correcting bites that would otherwise require surgery.

Complex Surgical Cases

Although the vast majority of cases do not require any surgical movement, we excel in providing superior surgical planning to address severe cases of coordination with an oral and maxillofacial surgeon, who focuses on treating malocclusion, poor bites, and jaw bone abnormalities. These cases require difficult craniofacial problems. Although these cases can be more involved, they provide an amazing difference that can truly be life-changing!

Advanced Technology

The iTero Element Scanner is cutting-edge digital scanning technology that removes the need for uncomfortable and conventional impressions—no more goop and gag! In 5 minutes, we can digitally scan your teeth to create a 3D model for appliances, retainers, and even create an on-the-spot before and after treatment simulation.

Keeping up with the latest tech is one of the many ways we continue to provide superior care. CBCT (cone beam) scanners are cutting-edge 3D x-ray units that provide us with a complete 3D view, rather than the flat two-dimensional image that a normal x-ray delivers. That extra dimension allows us to create more accurate and efficient treatment plans, especially in cases with impacted teeth, extra teeth, or joint (TMJ) issues.

3D printing is the next big evolution in digital dentistry. We are able to spend more time providing outstanding treatment and a pleasant office experience by eliminating the longer processing times of outside labs. With our in-house 3D printing service, there is no need to send a model to the lab for aligners or retainers. We can do it all right here.
