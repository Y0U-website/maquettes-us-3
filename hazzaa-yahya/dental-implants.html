What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are advanced, permanent solutions designed to replace missing or damaged teeth. These artificial tooth roots are placed into your jawbone and integrate naturally over time. Once healed, a custom crown is attached to complete the restoration. Dental implants offer a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for those who have lost teeth due to injury, decay, or other dental issues. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants provide more stability and improve overall oral health by stimulating the jawbone.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

The process of getting dental implants involves placing a titanium post into your jawbone, where it naturally fuses with the bone in a process known as osseointegration . Once the implant has integrated with your jaw, a custom-made crown is attached to the post, restoring both the appearance and functionality of your tooth. This secure connection ensures that your new tooth feels as natural as possible, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

What Is the Dental Implant Procedure Like?

The dental implant procedure is straightforward, though it is completed in stages to ensure the best possible results. The process typically involves:

At Spring Valley Dental Care, we make sure you are comfortable throughout each stage of the procedure, and we offer sedation options to minimize discomfort.

  • Consultation: We assess your oral health to determine your candidacy for dental implants.
  • Placement: The titanium post is carefully placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing: The implant naturally fuses with your jawbone over several months.
  • Crown Attachment: After healing, a custom crown is placed on the implant, completing your restoration.

What Are the Recovery and Aftercare Requirements for Dental Implants?

After your dental implant surgery, it’s important to follow aftercare instructions to ensure proper healing. You may experience some mild discomfort or swelling in the first few days, but most patients recover quickly. During the healing period, which can last a few months, it’s important to avoid hard foods and practice good oral hygiene. Regular follow-up visits will be scheduled to monitor your progress. With proper care, your dental implant will integrate seamlessly with your jawbone for a lasting, strong foundation.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution for missing teeth. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. The crowns attached to the implants generally last 10-15 years, and they can be replaced if necessary. Regular check-ups and good oral hygiene will help extend the lifespan of your implants.

How Should I Care for My Dental Implants?

Proper care of your dental implants is essential to ensure their longevity and functionality. Here are a few tips for maintaining your implants:

  • Brush your implants just like your natural teeth, using a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss daily to remove food particles and plaque from around the implant.
  • Avoid smoking, as it can affect the healing process and longevity of the implant.
  • Visit Spring Valley Dental Care regularly for check-ups and professional cleanings to maintain the health of your implants.
