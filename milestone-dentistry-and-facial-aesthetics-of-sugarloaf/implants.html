What are Implants?

Implants are an excellent solution for missing teeth or those that need to be replaced due to damage or decay. An implant is a small metal post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone, acting as an anchor for a dental crown or bridge.

The Benefits of Implants

Implants are a popular choice for many reasons, including their natural look and feel, their durability, and their long-lasting results. Unlike other tooth replacement options, such as dentures or bridges, dental implants are anchored in the jawbone, which prevents them from shifting or moving around in your mouth. This makes them a comfortable and convenient choice, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

What Happens After My Implant Surgery?

Once your implant procedure is complete, we will give you detailed instructions on how to care for your new implant to guarantee a successful outcome. This may involve advice on brushing and flossing, as well as guidance on which foods to avoid while the implant heals.

Furthermore, we will schedule regular follow-up appointments to keep track of the progress of your implant and ensure that it is working correctly. Rest assured that our team will always be available to address any questions or concerns you may have throughout the entire process.
