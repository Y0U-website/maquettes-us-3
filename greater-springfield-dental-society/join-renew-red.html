Renewal Terms & Information

With your membership renewal, you agree to :

Contact Stacey Hemmel (573-634-3436, Ext 106) for questions.

  • Pledge adherence to the ADA Principle of Ethics & Code of Professional Conduct
  • Opt in to MDA communications
  • Give permission for image use in MDA media ( see complete terms )

Automatic Renewal Ease

Sign up with automatic renewal for your membership dues and we’ll do the rest. Be sure to check the auto-renew option when you join or renew!

  • Hassle-Free: Your membership continues without the need to manually renew.
  • Convenient: Less mail. No stamps or checks needed. No need to go online.
  • No-Risk: We’ll send you a reminder. You can cancel anytime. See complete terms .

Update My ADA Information

After you’ve logged in to renew in the My ADA dashboard ... don’t forget to ensure your contact information is up to date, which is part of your Find-a-Dentist and Credentialing Service profiles. Access to your ADA Membership cards are only available online at ADA.org/MyADA and in the ADA App . You will receive a membership window decal in the mail.

Stronger Together

Stronger together isn’t a slogan. It’s how progress happens.

Membership isn’t just a benefit list — it’s a voice. The MDA listens to dentists, responds to real needs, and advocates where it matters most. Member support makes this work possible and strengthens dentistry across Missouri. If you want a voice in how dentistry is shaped in this state, we invite you to be part of the MDA.

  • Insurance fairness & professional autonomy.
  • Advocacy that moves the needle.
  • Workforce solutions that strengthen teams.
