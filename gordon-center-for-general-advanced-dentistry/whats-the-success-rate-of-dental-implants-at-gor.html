All-On-4 Dental Implants Dental Implants Success Missing Teeth Dental Implants May 23 Written By Guest User The most advanced restoration techniques available allow us to have a success rate of more than 98 %. This rate can only be achieved by giving the utmost attention to each implant that a crown is put on every time.

We don’t just “put a crown on an implant”. We pay attention to the shape and quality of the gum around it, to the proper bite and contour of surrounding teeth and appropriate color and size so that it feels natural in your mouth.

We carefully select the skilled surgeon to extract the teeth and place the implants, while we only concentrate on the crown on top. This way you get the very best skilled dentist to do each part that they do best.

“One stop shopping” may work in retail, but dentists can’t be the best in all aspects. That’s why our success rate is greater than 98 %.
