The practice

Just Kids Pediatric Dentistry is the pediatric dental office of Steve K. Rayes, DDS, MS and Rachel Davis, DDS. Our Norwich, Vermont office sits just across the river from Hanover and Lebanon, New Hampshire — minutes from Dartmouth — and families come to us from both sides of the Connecticut River, across the Upper Valley. We provide specialized dentistry for children and adolescents in a…

Pediatric dentists complete two to three additional years of specialty training after dental school, focused entirely on the dental needs of children — from a baby’s first tooth through the teenage years, including children with special health care needs.

Some of our best comfort care doesn’t wear scrubs. Copper is our office therapy dog, specifically trained for this work — and he takes it seriously. He can often be found lying with a child while they have a procedure done, a steady, warm presence that makes hard moments easier.

Copper has been with the practice for over ten years, and he is — by a wide margin — a patient favorite.

We hold ourselves to the highest standard of care, and we insist on evidence-based dentistry. Just as important, we look at every child through an individual lens. There is no one-size-fits-all technique in this office — every child receives a unique, specific treatment plan that may draw on the full range of modern pediatric dentistry: minimally invasive dentistry when it’s the right choice…

Both Dr. Rayes and Dr. Davis are Board Certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry — and since 2023, Dr. Rayes has also served as a Director of the ABPD, helping oversee the certification process for pediatric dentists nationwide.

One office, one team, and doctors your child gets to know — caring for Upper Valley families in this community for over a decade. And yes, Copper counts as a familiar face.

How we care for your child Preventive care Exams, gentle cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants — the foundation of a lifetime of healthy teeth.

Les soins

Prevention, restorative care, in-office sedation with anesthesia staff in dedicated suites, and hospital dentistry at Dartmouth Hitchcock — whatever your child needs, we can provide it, without referring you somewhere else.
