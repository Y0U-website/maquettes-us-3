Frequently Asked Questions

Question text goes here

What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses clear aligners to gradually move teeth into alignment.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment length varies, but many cases are completed within 12 to 18 months.

Can adults use Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign is commonly used by both adults and teens seeking a discreet orthodontic option.
