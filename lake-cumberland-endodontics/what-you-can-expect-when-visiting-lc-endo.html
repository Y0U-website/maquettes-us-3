Our process

Experience personalized treatment plans tailored to your unique dental health requirements, utilizing the latest advancements to ensure precise and effective endodontic procedures.

Lake Cumberland Endodontics combines expertise and the latest technology to provide exceptional care for your endodontic needs.

Prepare for a Seamless Experience

Before you arrive, gather all necessary information: your complete list of medications, detailed medical history, and the contact details of your referring dentist. This preparation will streamline your visit, allowing us to focus on what matters most – your dental health.

Expert Evaluation and Personalized Consultation

Your journey with us begins with a thorough review of your paperwork by our skilled assistant followed by precise X-rays of your teeth. Our experienced endodontist will then discuss your symptoms in detail before conducting a meticulous examination. We believe in transparency; hence, you’ll engage in an open discussion about treatment options tailored specifically for you, weighing both benefits and risks.

Your Treatment Plan: Tailored & Responsive

If treatment is necessary, rest assured that it will either begin immediately or be scheduled at your earliest convenience. Complex issues may require multiple visits – but each step is taken with precision and care to ensure optimal outcomes.

Open Communication at Every Stage

our comfort is paramount. Feel empowered to discuss any questions or concerns before, during, and after your procedure with our compassionate team. At Lake Cumberland Endodontics, you’re not just another patient – you’re part of our family committed to achieving the best dental health together.

Payment Information

Lake Cumberland Endodontics offers the finest endodontic care at the most reasonable cost. To maintain this practice, all fees are due at the time of treatment. Patients with insurance are asked to pay 50 % of the service fee at the time of treatment and are responsible for any amount not covered by insurance. Any overpayment will be refunded promptly.

Insurance we accept

We accept insurance from the following companies: Guardian, Ameritas, Paramount, Health Resources.

At the moment, we can only accept Aetna PPO plans. We do not accept any Medicare or Medicaid plans.
