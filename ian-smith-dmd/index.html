Cost should never be an obstacle to receiving much-needed dental care, and that’s why our office is determined to have financing options available. Dr. Smith and our team want to see patients for their regular dental checkups and teeth cleanings at least twice a year. During these visits, our hygienists will generally perform any necessary scaling and root planing for gum disease, plus we will polish your teeth.

Additionally, Dr. Smith will examine your teeth, gums, and jaw to ensure there are no complications or signs of oral cancer. If we do come across a dental problem, we may require you to come more frequently to our office.
