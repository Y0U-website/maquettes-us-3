Welcome to our Practice

We strive to provide a lifetime of dental excellence in a relaxed and caring environment in Ithaca, NY.

The practice

It is our goal to guide our patients along a path of optimal health and wellness by delivering the highest possible level of care with empathy and understanding.

We recognize that every patient has unique wants and needs, and it is our highest priority to provide individualized care for every patient, while making the experience pleasant and enjoyable.

By providing a team of professional, honest and compassionate employees, we aim to improve patient health, appearance, self-confidence and overall quality of life.

First, don’t take our word for it please spend some time looking at our reviews from past and current patients. We work very hard to deliver high quality and professional care, while providing a comfortable atmosphere. Our reviews are never edited or deleted and are listed in the order they are given from our patients.

As a single provider practice , patient care is our top priority. While many practices are going for bigger and bigger footprints, Dr. Zobel feels it very important to stay a size where the office can continue to know their patients and understand their needs personally.

As a patient of our practice, we will know you and you will know us. Establishing and maintaining this relationship is extremely important to us to establish trust and reassure you that you will be well cared for. When you see the dentist there will never be any question as to which doctor you will be seeing at your visit, it will always be Dr. Zobel.

We pride ourselves on practicing standard of care dentistry and using the most advanced dental technologies while avoiding costly and sometimes gimmicky equipment that unnecessarily raise the cost of care.

All of our root canals are preformed under a Zeiss Surgical Microscope; microscope dentistry is the Standard of Care for endodontic therapy and we suspect we are the only General Dental office in Ithaca currently utilizing such technology.
