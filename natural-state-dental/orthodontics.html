What is Orthodontics

Orthodontics is more than just straightening teeth; it’s a comprehensive approach to improving dental health and appearance. This specialty addresses diagnosing, preventing, managing, and correcting mal-positioned teeth and jaws. By aligning the teeth properly, orthodontics boosts your self-esteem and contributes to better oral hygiene, reducing the risk of decay and gum disease.

Our Orthodontic Options

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners represent a revolutionary advancement in orthodontic treatment. These virtually invisible trays are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth, gently shifting them into the desired position over time. Ideal for those who prefer a discreet solution, clear aligners offer the flexibility to eat, brush, and floss normally throughout your treatment.

Invisalign in Springdale, AR

As a leading brand of clear aligners, Invisalign has transformed millions of smiles worldwide. This system uses a series of clear, removable aligners that are precisely engineered to move your teeth gradually. Invisalign aligners are comfortable, nearly invisible, and designed to fit seamlessly into your daily life without interrupting your routine.

Appliances

Orthodontic appliances play a crucial role in guiding jaw growth, correcting bite problems, and aligning teeth. From palatal expanders to space maintainers, these devices address a variety of orthodontic issues beyond what braces and aligners can achieve alone. At Natural State Dental, Dr. Brumley will assess your needs to determine if an orthodontic appliance is right for your treatment plan.

Retainers

After the active phase of orthodontic treatment, retainers are essential for maintaining your new smile. Retainers prevent teeth from gradually shifting back to their original position, ensuring the long-term success of your orthodontic treatment. Whether fixed or removable, your retainer is custom-designed for comfort and effectiveness.
