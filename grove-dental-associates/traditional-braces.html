Traditional Braces

Achieving a healthy and picture-perfect smile is possible when you select Grove Dental Associates for your orthodontic needs.

For many, having perfectly straight and aligned teeth, reaches far beyond the obvious cosmetic benefit. While aligned teeth can improve self-confidence and a brighter smile, the proper occlusion – or bite – can improve chewing ability, prevent tooth wear, and maintain gum health with better cleanability. Our entire clinical staff is trained to treat pediatric orthodontic and pre-orthodontic cases, as well as our high number of adult braces and Invisalign patients. We love seeing how healthy smiles change lives!

We offer a wide range of orthodontic products to meet the needs of our patients that include metal or clear ceramic brackets, custom–made removable appliances that correct crowding or spacing, Invisalign with V-Pro treatment accelerators, and several types of retainers to maintain your new smile.
