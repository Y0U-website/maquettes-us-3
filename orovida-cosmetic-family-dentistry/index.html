Dental crowns are a durable, long-lasting, and natural-looking way to restore damaged teeth. A dental crown is basically a “false tooth” that’s built to fit right over your existing tooth after it’s been trimmed and prepared. Once your crown is attached, it completely restores the natural appearance and function of your tooth!

Dental bonding is an excellent choice for patients who may be looking to reshape their teeth and enhance their smiles. In this treatment, Dr. Juste will apply a soft, tooth-colored material directly to your teeth. Then, she’ll shape, harden, and trim it to enhance your appearance. Dental bonding is affordable, safe, and typically can be reversed in the future if you change your mind.
