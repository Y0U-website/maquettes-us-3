The practice

Patient education is extremely important to us. We strive to provide our patients with ample information to make good decisions for long-term dental health.

At Hutton Family Dental, we blend experience with the newest principles in education, as well as with advances in the latest technology, to offer our patients the best quality dental care in New Lenox!

The sooner dental problems are detected, the easier they are to correct. It costs less to prevent disease through proper care, periodic dental visits and quality dental care, than to correct dental problems once they occur.

Once you’ve been professionally diagnosed, we offer the best course of treatment to address the issues, while making you comfortable in our friendly and caring environment.
