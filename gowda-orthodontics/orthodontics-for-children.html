What a first visit looks like

Your first visit is relaxed and informative. We’ll chat about goals and concerns, take kid-friendly diagnostic records (photos, digital scans, and X-rays as appropriate), and discuss personalized options with clear next steps. You’ll leave knowing whether we recommend observation, an interceptive appliance, or simply a future check-in.

Comfortable, child-friendly technology

Say goodbye to goopy impressions. We use an iTero digital scanner for quick, comfortable 3D scans, along with modern imaging. 3D CBCT is not taken routinely, we recommend it only when truly necessary for diagnosis or treatment planning. Our A-dec chairs and welcoming reception area (with easy check-in and refreshments) help kids feel right at home.

Treatment options we may consider in Phase 1

Our goal is minimal treatment for maximum long-term benefit, and to set your child up for a shorter, simpler Phase 2 (or none at all).

  • Growth-guiding appliances (e.g., expanders)
  • Limited braces for specific tooth movement
  • Space maintainers to protect room for adult teeth
  • Habit appliances (thumb- or tongue-related)
  • Clear aligner approaches when appropriate

FAQs: Orthodontics for Children & Early Treatment

When should my child have their first orthodontic check?

An early visit in the mixed-dentition years helps us spot crowding, crossbites, bite imbalances, and eruption patterns. From there, we’ll recommend either observation, a short interceptive phase, or waiting until the teen years, whatever best fits your child.

What happens at the first appointment? Is it painful?

It’s easy and kid-friendly: a warm welcome, a conversation about your goals, quick photos, digital scans, and X-rays if needed. No shots. We review findings together and outline next steps with clear timelines and costs.

Do you always treat early?

No. Many kids simply need monitoring as they grow. We recommend early Phase 1 treatment only when it meaningfully improves growth, space, or bite, and reduces the complexity of later care.

What kinds of early treatments do you provide?

Depending on your child’s needs, options may include expanders, space maintainers, limited braces, habit appliances, or selective clear aligner therapy. We’ll tailor the plan to your child’s growth and comfort.

Will you take a 3D scan or CBCT of my child?

We take comfortable iTero digital scans for precision. CBCT is reserved for cases where it’s truly necessary for diagnosis or treatment planning—we don’t do CBCT routinely.

How long does Phase 1 usually last?

Most interceptive treatments are short and focused. We’ll give you a customized timeline at the consultation and explain how it supports future growth and (if needed) Phase 2.

How much does early treatment cost? Do you take insurance?

We accept major insurances and offer flexible payment plans, including options like CareCredit. You’ll receive a clear estimate before any treatment begins.
