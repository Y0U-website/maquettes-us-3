Meet Dr. Tuka Sada: A Leading Expert in Pediatric Dentistry

Years of Expertise: Delivering Exceptional Pediatric Care

Dr. Sada, with over five years as a clinical instructor at Case Western Reserve University, has mentored dental students and led a pediatric dental outreach program, providing care to thousands of children in Northeast Ohio. She recently completed a postgraduate residency in pediatric dentistry, enhancing her skills in complex cases. Her dedication to underserved communities highlights her commitment to children&#x27;s oral health.

Building a Foundation of Excellence: Dr. Sada’s Education

Dr. Sada began her dental career at the University of Kalamoon in Syria. After moving to the U.S., she completed a research fellowship in orthodontics at Case Western Reserve University, which led to her faculty role in pediatric dentistry there. She further honed her skills with a pediatric dentistry residency at CWRU and University Hospitals Rainbow Babies and Children’s Hospital. As a member of AAPD and IAPD, she stays updated on the latest in pediatric dentistry.
