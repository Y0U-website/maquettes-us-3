New Patient Registration Asset 22 Make An Appointment Asset 23 Referring Doctor Dr. Adam Stanley DMD After attending the University of Utah, Dr. Stanley graduated cum laude from the Creighton University School of Dentistry, receiving multiple recognitions and special awards in oral surgery, prosthodontics and endodontics. Dr. Stanley completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, TN.

His wife, Lisa, and their four children are the most important things in his life. He has multiple interests but maintains that his only real hobby is his family. Dr. Stanley’s family is extremely important to him, and he strives to treat every patient as if they were a member of his own family.

Dr. Stanley values being someone his patients can feel comfortable with and in whom they can have confidence – that they are receiving the best care possible.

It is essential for Dr. Stanley to make every effort to accommodate patient concerns and keep their experience as comfortable and anxiety-free as possible. Coordinating patient treatment with their general dentist and physician is a vital part of our care, and we take extra care to communicate effectively with our referring doctors.

Dr. Stanley is board-certified by the American Board of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and is constantly involved in continuing education to be aware of the latest advances in the field. This ensures his patients are receiving the best standard of care possible.

Dr. Andrew M. Sohn

Upon completion of his maxillofacial surgery training, Dr. Sohn completed his training with a one-year fellowship in cosmetic surgery with Facial Plastic and Cosmetic Surgery Center in Abilene, TX with Dr. Howard Tobin and one-year fellowship with Tulsa Surgical Arts in Tulsa, Ok with Dr. Angelo Cuzalina.

Dr. Sohn provides care in all aspects of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery with advanced experience in wisdom tooth removal, comprehensive reconstruction and bone grafting, dental implants, corrective jaw and TMJ surgery as well as full scope of cosmetic surgery. He is a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, American Academy of Cosmetic Surgery, American Dental Association, American Medical Association, Texas Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, and Texas Dental Association. He is certified in BLS, ACLS, PALS, and ATLS.

We know you have many options when it comes to oral surgery. Thank you for trusting our team to walk with you on this journey. If there is anything we can ever do to improve, please let us know!
