Reidsville Orthodontics - Get to Know the Reidsville Orthodontics Team

History

After outgrowing the former office of Dr. Scott Duncan, we renovated the Carolina Farm Credit Building on Front Street, transforming it into a beautiful, modern, and spacious facility. With triple the space available, we’re proud to provide you with modern treatment options in a comfortable setting.

At Reidsville Orthodontics, we are passionate about providing you a friendly environment, high quality work, and on-time appointments. Reidsville Orthodontics has received national attention for it’s innovative design and is visited internationally by more than twenty orthodontists each year for tours and seminars.

Get in touch

At Reidsville Orthdontics, we know that finding the right Orthdontist for your smile is not a choice to be taken lightly. That’s why we offer free consultations to discuss your expectations, provide you with individualized treatment options, and explain insurance and payment plan options.

Meet The Doctor

Orthodontist

Christopher G. Gibson, DDS, MS

Dr. Gibson grew up in Charlotte, NC where he was an active participant at Providence Presbyterian Church and the Boy Scouts of America. He attended Duke University where he received a degree in Biomedical Engineering. He then went on to the University of Maryland Dental School where he graduated valedictorian of his dental class. Returning home to North Carolina, he earned his Masters degree and certificate in Orthodontics at UNC.

Dr. G and his wife, Marisa, have two children, Stela and Alexander, and a scrappy but lovable dog, Harvey. His favorite hobbies include auto mechanics, wood working, wake boarding, kayaking, and other outdoor activities with his family. Away from the office, he can typically be found tinkering with gadgets and technology, wrenching on his Jeep, or getting lost in various other projects.
