Meet the dentist

Dr. Jordan Campbell

Dr. Jordan Campbell earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2009. She graduated from Miami University with a Bachelor of Arts in Zoology, minoring in Human Resource Management. She is a member of the CDA, ODA, and ADA professional organizations.

Dr. Campbell moved to Milford and joined the practice to work with her father, Dr. Mark Ford, in 2012. They shared 6 working years together and she takes great pride in having his mentorship both in technical skillset and in establishing strong patient relationships. She took ownership in 2016 and is now the sole practitioner since Dr. Ford’s retirement in 2017. Dr. Campbell is devoted to providing quality dental care with a gentle touch.

Dr. Campbell and her staff participate in charities such as Team Smile and Leave no Vet Behind to provide dental benefits to two very important undeserved populations, children and U.S. Veterans. She also sponsors local youth groups and enjoys giving back to the community.

Dr. Campbell is a proud wife to her husband Tom, and together they have two children, Jonathan and Elle. When not at the office, you can find the Campbells at the Milford ball fields, swimming pool, St. Elizabeth Ann Seton, or cheering on the Buckeyes…OH!

Dr. Jacob Amato

Dr. Amato grew up in Avon Ohio, a small suburb on the west side of Cleveland as the son of an engineer and a math teacher. Dr. Amato graduated from the University of Dayton with a Bachelor of Science in chemistry and a minor in biology and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery at OSU.

As a dentist, Dr. Amato finds joy in working with patients to help them meet their dental goals. Dr. Amato prides himself on a caring touch, excellent care, and his ability to build relationships with patients. Dr. Amato is also an avid reader, not only in his love of classic literature but also enjoys reading dental journals in his free time to help him keep up to date on current trends to deliver the best care to his patients.

In his free time, Dr. Amato is somewhat eclectic. In winter you may find him snowboarding and in the summer you may find him pushing himself on the bike trail. On rainy days, he loves to cook, read, and listen to music–always looking for a new sound or flavor to obsess over for the next month.
