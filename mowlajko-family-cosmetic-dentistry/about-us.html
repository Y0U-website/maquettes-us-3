Meet the Team at Mowlajko Family and Cosmetic Dentistry

For over 45 years, Mowlajko Family and Cosmetic Dentistry has served generations of families in our community with exceptional care. We are proud of the friendly and caring environment our team creates with positive attitudes and a dedication to your family s comfort.

Excellence in Dentistry

Each team member contributes uniquely to our environment, where patient care blends genuine personal engagement and professional excellence. We prioritize understanding and transparency to ensure every patient, from the youngest smiles to those with decades of stories, feels at home.

A Passion for Helping Others

Our team has a passion for dentistry because it allows us to do what we love: helping others. We will support you in your successes and provide judgment-free care should you have a setback. We aim to help restore your confidence in your smile and celebrate the transformation that quality dental care can bring to your life.

Join the Mowlajko Dental Family!

We invite you to join the Mowlajko Family Dentistry community, where generations of families have trusted us for their dental care. Please take a few minutes to learn more about the exceptional people who will serve you, and then give us a call to schedule your next dental appointment.

Andy Mowlajko, DMD

He takes great pride in carrying on the tradition of compassionate care that generations of families in Anderson have come to respect and appreciate.
