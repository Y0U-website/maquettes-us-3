TMJ and jaw pain is a common disorder that can gradually become a significant health issue when not treated - not to mention trying to function with the debilitating migraines that often accompany it. When you treat your TMJ and alleviate jaw pain , you restore control over your life and preventing more serious medical issues from occurring.

Sleep apnea is a common problem that can cause serious medical issues if left untreated and can often be a symptom of another disease. By resolving sleep apnea , you’re improving your ability to rest at night and function during the day. You’re also averting potentially life-threatening conditions that can permanently damage your health.
