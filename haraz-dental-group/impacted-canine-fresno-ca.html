How much does impacted canine surgery cost?

The cost of treating impacted teeth varies based on several aspects, including the degree of the impaction, the tooth&#x27;s location, the type of treatment required (extraction, surgical removal ), and the dental provider&#x27;s costs. The chosen method for exposing the impacted canine (surgery, braces, or a combination) will influence the cost. Surgical procedures typically cost more than orthodontic treatment with braces alone.
