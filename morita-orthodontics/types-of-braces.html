Types of Braces

Metal

Metal braces are the most traditional and most common form of braces. They use a series of brackets and wires that are made from high-grade stainless steel that are bonded to your teeth. Today’s metal braces are smaller, more comfortable and more attractive. Metal braces are still a popular choice for many patients due being economical, effective, and reliable.

Clear

If you want a treatment similar to metal braces, but prefer a slightly less visible look, then Ceramic braces are right for you. They offer a variation of the traditional orthodontic treatment yet are made of translucent ceramic material that blends in with the tooth’s natural color, rather than stainless steel. They are most popular with adult patients, due to their cosmetic appeal. The only drawback to ceramic brackets is that they are more fragile, and the elastic ties can discolor between orthodontic visits.

Clarity™

Clarity™ braces feature a comfortable design with a translucent appearance. Unlike traditional ceramic braces, Clarity™ braces are made from a hard ceramic material, so they won’t stain or discolor. They are ultra small and come in a dome-shaped design for a more comfortable fit. With the same performance as metal braces, many patients are now choosing the clear option – Clarity™!

Harmony™

Harmony™ are placed behind your teeth so no one will know that you are wearing braces unless you tell them. These unique braces are created using state-of-the-art technology and are 100 % customized to the shape of your teeth and to your dental situation. Harmony have the unique prescription that your orthodontist creates for you built-in to ensure you get the results that you want. With Harmony, you get efficient, effective tooth movement and great aesthetics.
