Meet our Team

Dr. Ras graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 1986 and has been providing high quality dental care in the same location for over 28 years. Since he began practicing dentistry, Dr. Ras has remained dedicated to continuing his education, ensuring that his patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced dental care. At Hope Dental, Dr. Ras and his staff make every effort to make sure that your visit is as stress-free and comfortable as possible. We look forward to meeting you.
