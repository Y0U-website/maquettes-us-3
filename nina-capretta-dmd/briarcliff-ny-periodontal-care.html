Don’t Ignore The Warning Signs Of Gum Disease

Unfortunately,the fact of the matter is that all human mouths are filled with bacteria. If, over the years, this bacteria has not been consistently removed through brushing, flossing and regular check-ups, symptoms of gum disease may begin to present themselves.

If you have noticed redness or swelling around your teeth and gums, even a slight tenderness to the touch or bleeding when you floss or brush, you should not ignore these warning signs.

  • Tender | Bleeding Gums
  • Receding Gums
  • Sensitive Teeth
  • Persistent Bad Breath
  • Red | Swollen Gums
  • Painful Chewing
  • Loose Teeth
