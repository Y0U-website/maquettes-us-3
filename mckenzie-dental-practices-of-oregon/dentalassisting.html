Dental Assisting Program

  • Our Program Program fee: $12,500.
  • This program includes all required materials: a set of scrubs, a textbook and the fee for your first attempt at the Dental Assistant National Board exams.
  • Looking for flexible payment options? View Our Payment Plan Options.
  • Please note that this is a non-credit program, and at this time, FAFSA or federal financial aid is not available. However, we do offer two flexible payment plan options to help make tuition more manageable.
  • Eligibility: No prerequisite courses are required. Students must be at least 16 years old and have a high school diploma or GED to enroll. Access to a computer and a stable internet connection is necessary for completing the online coursework.
  • The coursework is delivered online , with students attending one in-person, 8-hour lab session each Thursday for hands-on clinical training.
  • During the program, students participate in a 120-hour internship that builds clinical skills and supports preparation for the Dental Assistant National Board exams.
  • In our experience, many students are offered employment by their observation or externship sites.
  • Once students pass their exams, they will become certified in Radiation Health and Safety (RHS) and Expanded Functions Dental Assistant (EFDA) along with the following Oregon credentials: Pit and Fissure Sealant Certification
  • Denture Soft Reline Certification
  • Gingival Retraction Cord Certification
