Dr. Apana is passionate about dentistry and how she can connect with her patients by giving them a beautiful and healthy smile.

She attended the University of Florida College of Dentistry and graduated in the top 10 % of her class. She then went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center in Bronx, NY. Because she demands the best for her patients, she is continually furthering her clinical education by learning new technologies and techniques aimed at providing the highest quality of care.

She also maintains an active membership in the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, and Northeast District Dental Association. In her free time, Dr. Apana enjoys spending time with her husband and son and traveling.
