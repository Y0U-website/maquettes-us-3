Common Questions about Dental Implants What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support a crown, bridge, or denture, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth.

  • Dental Crowns - High Plains Dental provides custom dental crowns to restore damaged or weakened teeth. These durable crowns improve function and appearance, blending seamlessly with your natural smile.
  • Bridges - Our dental office offers dental bridges to replace missing teeth, restoring your smile and maintaining proper bite function. These custom-made solutions provide a natural look and improve oral health.

  • How long to dental implants last? With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crowns or restorations on top of the implants may need replacement over time.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Care for dental implants by maintaining regular oral hygiene practices such as brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups to ensure their longevity.
