The practice

Start your baby’s dental health journey on the right foot! Our practice is committed to guiding new parents through the first critical stages of dental care, from caring for gums to that first tooth.

We offer a full spectrum of routine and preventative treatments designed to set the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Setting a Good Example : Children learn by example, and good oral hygiene is no exception. We’ll guide you on how to be the best role model for your child.

Regular dental check-ups should start by age 1 or when the first tooth appears to help catch any potential issues early on.

From your first visit onward, our practice aims to provide a comfortable, educational, and enriching experience for your child. We work at the pace of the child; if they are not ready to ride in the chair, that’s okay! We understand that each child is unique and we never want to make them uncomfortable.

We abide by the guidelines set forth by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry to ensure optimal oral health for your child.

The toddler years are critical in establishing good oral health habits. Our friendly team ensures a comfortable experience, focusing on preventative and routine care tailored for toddlers.

As your child grows, so do their dental needs. We offer specialized treatments that factor in the rapid developmental changes occurring during these years.

Les soins

Say goodbye to painful pricks and drills. Our laser technology delivers precise, efficient fillings with just a sensation of cold air.

Whether it’s fillings, crowns, or more, our restorative treatments are done with utmost care to ensure your child’s comfort and dental health.

Should your child require orthodontic care, we provide trusted referrals to local orthodontists, ensuring a seamless transition and continuity in excellent care.
