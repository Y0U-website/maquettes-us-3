Board-certified oral & maxillofacial surgeon

Dr. Elizabeth Consky

Dr. Elizabeth Consky didn&#x27;t stumble into oral surgery, she grew up alongside it. Raised in Ontario, her father is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon who has held leadership positions across Canada for decades. As a child, Dr. Consky folded patient paperwork and stuffed envelopes in his office. By high school, she was working as a sterilization technician and surgical assistant. Long before she ever set foot in dental school, she understood what it meant to care for patients with skill, intention, and heart.

That dual DDS and MD credential is genuinely rare. It means Dr. Consky approaches every surgical case with both a dentist&#x27;s precision and a physician&#x27;s understanding of the whole patient — their medical history, their systemic health, and the full picture of what safe, effective care looks like for them specifically.

Dr. Consky took ownership of Georgia Oral Surgery in 2024, continuing a practice legacy that began in 1968. She was drawn to practice ownership for a reason she&#x27;ll tell you plainly: being the owner means the decision to do the right thing is always hers to make. Her personal philosophy is straightforward — do things once, and do them right. Treat everyone as if they were family. Listen before offering a solution. And if something goes wrong, make it right, every single time.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Consky is a mother of three, married to an ER physician, and very much a part of the Decatur community she serves. You&#x27;ll find her on the soccer sideline, in the garden, on a bike trail, or on a ski slope with her family. She loves that her patients wave to her around town — it&#x27;s what makes a city like Atlanta feel like a small, close-knit place. For Dr. Consky, that&#x27;s not a coincidence. It&#x27;s the whole point.

Designed for your comfort

Our new Decatur office was built from the ground up with patients in mind — every detail, from the warm waiting room to the bright, modern operatories, reflects our belief that a great surgical experience starts long before anyone picks up an instrument. We&#x27;ve invested in both the comforts that ease anxiety and the technology that ensures precision, so you can feel confident from the moment you walk in.

A space that puts you at ease

We designed our office to feel less like a clinical environment and more like a place you&#x27;d actually want to spend time — warm, welcoming, and thoughtfully equipped for patients of every age, from young children to grandparents. Whether you&#x27;re here for a quick consultation or a longer procedure, our amenities are here to make the experience as comfortable as possible.

Warm, library-inspired waiting room Our waiting area features bookcases, comfortable seating, and a calm, curated atmosphere that feels more like a well-loved living room than a typical surgery waiting room. There&#x27;s also workspace available for parents accompanying younger patients, so you can stay productive and close by at the same time.

Warm blankets, music, & comfortable chairs We do what we can to make you at ease, especially for patients who experience procedural anxiety. We want your time in our chair to feel as calm and as supported as possible.

Our waiting room is designed to help everyone feel more at ease, with books, coloring, and toys for kids, plus chargers, magazines, and a TV for patients, parents, and caregivers.

Technology that supports you

Dr. Consky believes that excellent surgical care requires excellent tools. Our office is equipped with advanced imaging and top-line sedation equipment to support the highest standard of in-office oral surgery — so you get hospital-quality care in a practice that actually knows your name.

CBCT imaging Our cone beam CT machine provides detailed, three-dimensional imaging of your teeth, bone, and surrounding anatomy — allowing Dr. Consky to plan every procedure with exceptional accuracy before she makes a single incision. Better imaging means better planning, and better planning means better outcomes.

Top-line sedation equipment From nitrous oxide to IV and general anesthesia, our office is fully equipped to administer and monitor sedation at the highest standard of care. Dr. Consky&#x27;s surgical and medical training means she manages your sedation with the same expertise and attention she brings to every other aspect of your procedure.

Premium implant systems Georgia Oral Surgery uses Nobel Biocare, BioHorizons, and 3I implant systems — trusted names in implant dentistry known for long-term reliability and excellent clinical outcomes. You&#x27;re not getting a generic solution; you&#x27;re getting a system that&#x27;s been proven to last.

Opioid-free pain control — Exparel We offer Exparel and other opioid-free pain management options as a safer, more modern alternative to narcotic prescriptions following surgery. It&#x27;s one of the most meaningful ways we can support your recovery — keeping you comfortable without the risks associated with opioid-based care.
