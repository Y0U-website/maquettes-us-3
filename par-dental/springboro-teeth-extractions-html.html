🧠 Types of Extractions

  • Simple Extraction: For visible teeth, using gentle tools and local anesthesia.
  • Surgical Extraction: For impacted or broken teeth, often requiring incisions and stitches.
  • Wisdom Tooth Extraction: Specialized removal of third molars, often impacted or misaligned.
  • Impacted Tooth Extraction: For teeth trapped beneath gum or bone tissue.
  • Orthodontic Extraction: Teeth removed to create space for braces or aligners.
  • Periodontal Extraction: Teeth removed due to advanced gum disease and bone loss.

🩺 What to Expect

  • Evaluation & X-rays – We assess the tooth and surrounding bone.
  • Anesthesia - Local numbing or sedation for comfort.
  • Extraction - Gentle removal using specialized instruments.
  • Aftercare - Gauze placement, pain management, and healing instructions.

⏱️ Recovery Tips

  • Rest and avoid strenuous activity for 24–48 hours
  • Use cold compresses to reduce swelling
  • Eat soft foods and avoid using straws
  • Follow all post-op instructions carefully
