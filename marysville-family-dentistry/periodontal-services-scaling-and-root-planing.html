Scaling and Root Planing

The treatment involves two key actions. Scaling removes bacterial buildup and tartar from the teeth and beneath the gums, where harmful bacteria can thrive. Root planing then smooths the root surfaces so gum tissue can reattach more effectively and bacteria have a harder time clinging to the tooth again. Together, these steps help reduce infection, shrink pocket depth, and protect the bone that supports your teeth.

Scaling and root planing is usually completed in sections, and local anesthetic is commonly used to keep you comfortable. After treatment, it’s normal to have mild soreness, slight bleeding, or temporary sensitivity—especially to cold. Your dental team will provide instructions for home care, including gentle cleaning techniques and ways to support healing. In some cases, antimicrobial rinses or localized medications may be recommended to further reduce bacteria.

Follow-up care is important. Many patients transition to periodontal maintenance visits scheduled more often than routine cleanings to keep bacteria levels controlled and monitor gum healing. Daily brushing, flossing, and cleaning along the gumline are essential for lasting results.

If you’ve been told you have periodontal pockets or you notice bleeding gums or persistent bad breath, scaling and root planing may be an effective step toward restoring healthier gums. Marysville Family Dentistry will guide you through treatment and help you protect your smile long term.
