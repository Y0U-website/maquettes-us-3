The Dental Implant Process

The dental implant procedure is straightforward and has an excellent success rate. During your initial consultation, Dr. Perez will perform a comprehensive examination, including X-rays or 3D imaging, to evaluate your bone density and overall oral health. The implant is then carefully placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Over the next several months, the implant fuses with your bone. Finally, a custom crown is attached to complete your restoration. Throughout the process, Dr. Perez ensures your comfort and provides clear instructions for care.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most people with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have adequate bone density, healthy gums, and good overall health. If you’ve experienced bone loss, bone grafting procedures can often restore the necessary bone structure. During your consultation, Dr. Perez will evaluate your specific situation and explain all your options in detail.

Time Is Critical

The longer you wait to replace missing teeth, the more bone loss you’ll experience. This can make implant placement more difficult and costly down the road. If you’re missing teeth or facing extraction, now is the time to explore dental implants. Early treatment preserves your bone structure and often leads to better outcomes.
