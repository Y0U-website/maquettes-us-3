Meet Dr. Randall

Joseph Randall, DDS

Dr. Joseph Randall comes to us from Nashville, Tennessee where he practices as a General Dentist with over 7 years experience. Originally Dr. Randall is from Huntsville, AL. He met his wife, Abby, at the University of Alabama where he completed his Bachelor’s Degree in biology. He then earned his DMD at Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, FL in 2017 and received a full scholarship through the military with the Health Professions Scholarship Program.

While serving in the Military from 2017 to 2021, he surrounded himself with highly trained mentors who helped develop his skills in cosmetic, prosthodontic, restorative dentistry, endodontics, and oral surgery. Since 2021, he has been in private practice expanding his services including orthodontics and implant dentistry.

Dr. Randall has a passion for creating a stress-free and comfortable environment from the moment the patient walks in the door. He holds himself to the highest standard of care and partners with patients to create the optimal plan for their goals and desires.

In his free time he likes spending time with his wife and two daughters, running, traveling to his family’s farm in Southern Tennessee, cooking, and barbecuing on his smoker and grill.
