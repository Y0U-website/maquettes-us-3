At Lotus Dental in Mishawaka, Indiana, Priya Anne, DDS, and her skilled team are committed to delivering exceptional dental care in a welcoming, patient-centered environment.

The practice

We believe that informed patients make the best decisions about their oral health. This is why our website provides helpful information about common dental concerns, treatment choices, and preventive care.

We also provide periodontal disease treatment, porcelain veneers, root canals , sealants , and professional teeth whitening . We care for patients with temporomandibular joint disorder (TMD) and promptly handle tooth pain and dental emergencies.

To enhance our patients’ comfort and accuracy, Lotus Dental uses advanced technologies like laser dentistry, laser decay diagnostics, the air abrasion drill-less technique, single-tooth anesthesia wand, cone beam CT imaging, intraoral cameras, digital X-rays, and digital dental impressions. We also provide thorough oral cancer screenings to protect your health.

We understand that life can be busy, so our office is dedicated to making your visits as convenient and stress-free as possible. From clear explanations of your treatment choices to a friendly, supportive atmosphere, our goal is to help you feel confident in your dental care.

Whether you’re seeking preventive services, advanced restorative treatments, or a smile makeover, we’re here to keep you smiling.
