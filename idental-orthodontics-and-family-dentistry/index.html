The practice

iDental Orthodontics and Family Dentistry is your trusted dentist in Racine, WI, offering complete care for patients of all ages. Our modern office uses advanced technology, including intraoral scanners, to provide a comfortable and precise experience for both children and adults.

We offer a full range of services in one place, including family and pediatric dentistry , orthodontics , oral surgery , dental implants , and advanced denture solutions like next-day and All-on-4 dentures. From cleanings to braces and emergency care, everything you need is here under one roof .

Enjoy convenient walk-in hours from 8am to 11am and fast, paperless scheduling. Book your appointment online or call today to get started.
