What Are Dental Implants?

Dentist Durham

Dental implants are a reliable and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth, designed to restore both the function and aesthetics of your smile. An implant consists of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth, offering a durable, comfortable alternative to traditional dentures or bridges.

The implant process involves careful planning, and the titanium post integrates with the jawbone over time in a process called osseointegration. Once fully healed, the post provides a strong foundation for attaching a natural-looking dental restoration. Dental implants offer a permanent solution that enhances your smile, improves chewing and speaking ability, and supports the overall health of your mouth. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing a reliable, low-maintenance option for replacing missing teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dentist Durham

Improved Functionality

Dental implants restore the function of missing teeth by providing a stable base for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike traditional dentures, implants allow you to chew, speak, and smile with ease, offering the comfort and function of natural teeth. This helps you return to your normal daily activities without worrying about slipping or discomfort, improving overall quality of life.

Long-Lasting and Durable

Dental implants are known for their longevity. Made from durable materials such as titanium, implants can last a lifetime with proper care. They provide a permanent solution for missing teeth and eliminate the need for frequent replacements or adjustments like other dental options. Their strength and resilience make them a dependable choice for patients seeking a long-term solution to tooth loss.

Preservation of Jawbone Health

When teeth are missing, the jawbone can deteriorate over time due to a lack of stimulation. Dental implants help preserve the structure of the jawbone by stimulating it just like natural tooth roots. This prevents bone loss, maintains facial structure, and ensures the long-term health of your mouth. Implants also prevent the shifting of surrounding teeth, which can occur with traditional dentures.

The Dental Implant Process

Dentist Durham

Consultation and Planning

Dr. Mary Gaddis begins with a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, using advanced imaging to determine if dental implants are the right option for you. She creates a custom treatment plan tailored to your unique needs.

Implant Placement and Healing

During the surgical procedure, a titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, the post fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a solid foundation for your restoration.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant. This final step restores your smile’s appearance, strength, and function, giving you lasting confidence and comfort.
