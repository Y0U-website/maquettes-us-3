Three Generations of Dental Excellence

Dr. J Richard Landgraf graduated from Saint Louis University’s School of Dentistry and served three years in the Navy. In 1980, he opened the location on New Florissant Rd. Dr John Landgraf graduated from Washington University and joined his father’s practice in Florissant. In 2014, Dr. Sean Landgraf joined his father after graduating from UMKC’s dental school.

Our Family Will Care for Yours

Our office works hard to treat our patients like family. We undergo continuous training to improve so that we can deliver the best outcome possible. We hope you will consider Lake St Louis Dentisty to be your dental home. We can’t wait to help restore your smile or help you keep it for a lifetime. We serve the St. Louis Missouri area and beyond.
