Dr. Shera Newcomb

Dr. Shera Newcomb has been a part of Laurel Dental Care since 2011 and has been practicing dentistry since 2005. She earned both her undergraduate degree in Biology and dental degree at the University of Kentucky.

Dr. Newcomb is originally from Manchester, Kentucky and currently lives in Corbin. She is married to Dr. John Newcomb and has two daughters, Laura and Harper. She is a member of the Southeast Dental Society and serves on the Board of Directors for the Whitley County Health Department. In her spare time, Dr. Newcomb enjoys traveling, reading, and spending time with her family and friends.
