Invisalign FAQs

What is Invisalign, and how does it work?

The Invisalign clear aligner treatment is a form of orthodontic solution that utilizes removable, clear aligners to straighten teeth. These aligners are custom-crafted to gradually shift teeth into the desired position. They work by applying gentle pressure to specific teeth, guiding them into proper alignment over time.

What is the treatment duration of Invisalign?

Treatment duration for Invisalign clear aligner treatment will vary depending on the patient’s needs, but treatment typically takes about between a year to a year and a half for adults. For teens, it may take a similar duration or slightly longer, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient’s compliance with wearing the aligners.

Can I remove Invisalign aligners to eat and drink?

Yes, it is recommended that you remove your Invisalign aligners before eating or drinking anything besides water. You are able to enjoy the foods and drinks you love without any imposed restrictions. However, wearing the aligners for at least 20 to 22 hours per day is essential for optimal results.

How does Invisalign impact my daily life and activities?

Invisalign generally has minimal impact on daily life and activities. Since the aligners are removable, you can eat, drink, brush, and floss normally without any restrictions. However, you must maintain discipline in wearing them for the recommended duration each day to ensure treatment progresses effectively.

Are Invisalign aligners comfortable to wear?

Invisalign aligners are designed to be comfortable to wear. They are made of smooth, clear plastic that fits snugly over your teeth without causing irritation to your gums or cheeks. Most people find them much more comfortable than traditional braces, with fewer instances of discomfort or mouth sores.

Do I need to wear Invisalign aligners while sleeping?

Yes, it’s recommended to wear Invisalign aligners while sleeping. Consistently wearing them for at least 20 to 22 hours per day, including during sleep, helps ensure optimal progress in your orthodontic treatment. Removing them for extended periods can prolong treatment time and compromise results.

How often should I switch to a new set of aligners?

You should switch to a new set of Invisalign aligners roughly every week to two weeks, as prescribed by our team at New Leaf Dental. Each set of aligners will gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. Adhering to the recommended schedule is crucial for achieving the best possible outcome in your treatment.
