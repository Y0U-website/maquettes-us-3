About us

Meet Dr. Rodriguez-Reyes Oral Surgeon Cape Coral, FL

Board Certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Dr. Manuel M. Rodriguez-Reyes is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon born in Holguin, Cuba.

Dr. Rodriguez-Reyes earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree after graduating from the Santiago de Cuba Institute in 1995. He graduated as a Maxillofacial Surgeon in Cuba in 1999, where he completed a head and neck fellowship program at the National Oncologist Institute, Cuba.

After practicing for six years as a general dentist in the Miami and Fort Myers area, Dr. Rodriguez-Reyes was accepted into the University of Puerto Rico Medical Science Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery program. Upon completing this residency program, he entered private practice in Virginia and, after two successful years, decided it was time to relocate back to Florida with his family.

Dr. Manuel M. Rodriguez-Reyes provides the best medical and surgical care to all his patients while contributing to society, such as participating in surgical mission trips to the Dominican Republic. He has experience in Dental Alveolar Surgery, multiple dental implant restoration cases, OMFS trauma, Oncology Surgery, and Orthognathic Surgery. He participated in several Academic activities throughout his career, with numerous awards and publications on Head and Neck Reconstruction.

Outside of surgery, Dr. Rodriguez-Reyes enjoys an active lifestyle, including working out, walking on the beach, and fishing. He also enjoys spending time with his family and friends and visiting counties with his family.
