FAQs About Selling Your Dental Practice

What is a DSO/DPO?

A dental support organization (DSO), sometimes called a dental service organization (DSO) or a dental partner organization (DPO), offers non-clinical administrative support services to help dental practices operate and build their businesses. This can include tasks such as assistance with patient marketing, billing and accounts receivable, purchasing, patient scheduling support, and patient communications. Not all DSOs work the same way, so it’s important to find what level of support is best for your practice.

What makes Heartland Dental different from other DSOs/DPOs?

What criteria are Heartland Dental and a supported practice looking for in a dental practice?

Heartland Dental and a supported practice look for practices that are a strong fit in four areas: clinical quality, culture, financial performance, and operational alignment. During the early evaluation stages, these areas are carefully reviewed to help ensure a fit and long-term success. Heartland Dental and a supported practice are especially focused on supporting general dentistry offices that meet the following criteria: Five or more operatories; more than $800K in annual revenue; and locations in attractive, well-populated communities.

How long does it take to sell my dental practice?

There is no “typical” time frame for selling a dental practice, but most agreements are finalized in less than six months. Transition planning considers your goals (retirement, phased exit, partnership, etc.), sets the right pace for you, and ensures the process is smooth for your team and patients. For those who wish to move quickly, onboarding can be completed in just a few months. For practice owners with a longer timetable, we are always here to answer any questions.

What information is considered when making a dental practice valuation?

Valuation looks at both financial performance and market potential. Key factors include location, number of operatories, annual revenue and profitability, operational efficiency, accurate financial records, and the doctor’s commitment to practice growth. In short, it’s about understanding your practice’s financial health, growth potential, and overall strength.

Will dental support organization (DSO) affiliation impact my earnings, and how can it support dental practice growth?

Most affiliated doctors maintain or increase their personal earnings as a result of the systems and support that Heartland Dental and supported practices offer along with the multipronged wealth-building model. After affiliation with Heartland Dental and a supported practice, who makes clinical decisions at a Heartland Dental supported practice?

Supported doctors maintain complete clinical autonomy over patient care and are responsible for all treatment planning. Heartland Dental supported practices will never tell providers what treatments to perform. There are no production requirements, product endorsements, or sales quotas.

During a dental practice transition, will the name and identity of my practice change?

The vision of our founder, Dr. Rick Workman, was always to help supported doctors build their practices for themselves, not for Heartland Dental supported practices. Supported offices have unique brand names that resonate with their communities. Many are legacy brands, maintaining their original brand after a private practice and/or small group has affiliated with Heartland Dental and supported practices.
