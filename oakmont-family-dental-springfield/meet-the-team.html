Dr. Jeb Andrus, DMD

Dr. Andrus grew up in Eugene and attended Willamette High School and the University of Oregon. He met his wife Heather while at the UofO and despite her graduating from Oregon State they have remained married and have three children. Dr. Andrus graduated with Honors from Oregon Health and Science University School of Dentistry and completed his general dentistry residency training while in the Army at Ft. Lewis Washington.

In his free time, Dr. Andrus coaches his kids’ football and basketball teams and enjoys watching the Ducks (GO DUCKS!).

Dr. Andrus is an active member of several prestigious peer study groups, including the Spear and CEREC® study clubs. Dr. Andrus is also a certified member of the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. Dr. Andrus has a focus on Invisalign® treatment and Sleep dentistry and is passionate about helping his patients improve their lives and health with some of the services he provides at Oakmont.

Dr. Jillian Rose, DMD

Dr. Jillian Rose grew up in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. After earning her Bachelor of Science from the University of Florida, Dr. Rose returned to her hometown to complete dental school at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine, where she graduated at the top of her class.

Dr. Rose and her husband, a local ER physician, are happy to now call Eugene home! In her free time, Dr. Rose enjoys hiking with her dog Sampson, biking with her husband, and exploring all that the Pacific Northwest has to offer.

Dr. Rose has attended several postgraduate educational courses, and she takes seriously her responsibility to her patients to stay ahead with the latest techniques and technologies dentistry has to offer. She loves the personal relationships dentistry allows and she strives to provide her patients with a gentle and comprehensive approach to oral health.

Dr. John Hardy, DMD

Dr. Hardy is a graduate of the University of Arizona where he played baseball until he was selected in the 7th round of the 2004 Major League Baseball Draft by the Philadelphia Phillies. After baseball, he attended Western University of Health Sciences College of Dental Medicine, where he graduated at the top of his class.

Dr. Hardy and his wife Dr. Laura Hardy, a local veterinarian, enjoy exploring the Pacific Northwest with their rescue dog. They love to hike, golf, kayak, and spend time with family here in Eugene. Dr. Hardy is a member of the prestigious Frank Spear Study Club for continuing education. Dr. Hardy offers Invisalign® and CEREC® Same-Day-Crowns. He enjoys taking care of patients of all ages and is always available to see new patients.

Dr. Ian Salisbury, DMD

Dr. Ian Salisbury grew up in Eugene, graduating from Churchill High School. After earning his undergraduate degree from the University of Oregon, he completed dental school in Portland at Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU), where he graduated with Honors.

Dr. Ian married his high school sweetheart McKenzy, and they have 3 young children together. In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his family/friends, playing soccer, hiking/backpacking, coaching youth sports, golfing, and watching pretty much any UO sporting event!

Dr. Ian is passionate about General Dentistry because it offers him the opportunity to have a positive impact on the community through helping patients achieve a healthy and confident smile. He enjoys working with patients of all ages and looks forward to establishing meaningful, long-term relationships.

Dr. Claire Skach, DDS

Dr. Skach grew up in Portland and graduated from Oregon State University. She earned her dental degree at the University of California, San Francisco, and then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) Residency at the University of California, Los Angeles. During her time in California, she enjoyed retiring her rain jacket and learning to surf, however, she is an Oregonian at heart as she enjoys nature, camping, hiking, and greenery.

Dr. Skach went into dentistry because of a love for healthcare and working with her hands. She is passionate about building patient relationships, providing oral health education, and promoting preventative dentistry while delivering detail-oriented dental restorative care.

In her free time, Dr. Skach can be found baking, hiking, crafting, enjoying water recreation, or spending time with her dog.

Dr. Jasen DeBrine, DDS

Dr. DeBrine grew up in Broomfield, CO, and is the oldest of five siblings. He graduated from Utah University with a bachelor’s degree in Emergency Services and served as a volunteer paramedic and firefighter. He later earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Utah School of Dentistry, followed by a Mastership with the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics.

Dr. DeBrine is a dedicated father to his four children, Aspen, Fox, Hazel, and Primrose. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his family and being outdoors. His hobbies include fly fishing, hunting, and golfing.

Clinically, Dr. DeBrine focuses on complete smile restoration, specializing in All-on-4 implant systems to help patients regain full confidence in their smiles.
