The Benefits Of Dental Implants

Natural Appearance

Dental implants are designed to look and feel like your own teeth. They blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, giving you the confidence to smile, laugh, and engage in social activities without worrying about your appearance.

Improved Oral Health

Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants don’t require altering neighboring teeth. This means more of your natural teeth are left intact, improving long-term oral health. Implants also make it easier to access between teeth, promoting better oral hygiene.

Enhanced Comfort

Because dental implants become a part of you, they eliminate the discomfort of removable dentures. You won’t have to deal with the hassle of removing them or the fear of them slipping out, ensuring a comfortable and worry-free experience.

The Dental Implants Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Planning

The journey to a new smile begins with a thorough consultation at Mount Airy Family Dentistry. During this session, Dr. Laura Mercer will assess your oral health, take detailed images, and discuss your specific needs and goals. This step is crucial for developing a personalized treatment plan that outlines the entire process, from the initial surgery to the final restoration. You’ll have the opportunity to ask questions and understand what to expect, ensuring you’re comfortable and informed every step of the way.

Implant Placement

Once the treatment plan is finalized, the next step is the surgical placement of the dental implant. Under local anesthesia, Dr. Mercer will carefully insert the titanium post into the jawbone. This post acts as a sturdy root for your new tooth. Over the next few months, a process called osseointegration occurs, where the implant fuses with the jawbone, providing a solid foundation. During this healing period, temporary teeth can be placed, allowing you to maintain normal function and appearance.

Restoration and Follow-Up

After the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, the final step involves placing the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture onto the implant. This restoration is crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless blend. Dr. Mercer will make any necessary adjustments to guarantee a perfect fit and function. Regular follow-up appointments will be scheduled to monitor the health of your implant and surrounding teeth, ensuring long-term success and satisfaction with your new smile.
