What Are Dental Implants and Why Are They Important?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to act as sturdy roots for replacement teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants fuse with your bone, providing stability that looks and feels like your natural teeth. This fusion helps preserve jawbone density, preventing the bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.

Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, dental implants in Rye, NY or dental implants in Scarsdale, NY can significantly improve your oral function and aesthetics. They enable you to eat your favorite foods without discomfort and speak clearly without worrying about shifting dentures.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

The dental implant process typically unfolds over several stages, ensuring the best results for your smile and health. Here’s what you can expect when choosing dental implants in Rye, NY or dental implants in Scarsdale, NY:

  • Initial Consultation: Our oral surgeons evaluate your oral health, bone density, and overall suitability for implants.
  • Treatment Planning: Using detailed imaging, we design a personalized implant plan tailored to your anatomy and needs.
  • Surgical Placement: The implant post is gently inserted into the jawbone under local or general anesthesia for comfort.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Over several months, the implant fuses with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation.
  • Attachment of Abutment and Crown: Once healed, a connector (abutment) and custom-made crown are placed, completing your natural-looking smile.
