Dr. Rayyan Jaber

Dr. Rayyan is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Michigan Dental Association. When she is not having fun with our kiddos and fighting "sugar bugs”, she is hanging out with her friends and family, among them many nieces and nephews. Dr. Rayyan loves to travel, go out to eat, shop, read, and do pilates.

Dr. Rayyan cannot wait to meet you and your little ones and is grateful to be a part of their dental journey.
