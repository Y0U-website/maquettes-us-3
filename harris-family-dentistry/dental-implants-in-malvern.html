Dental Implants in Malvern, PA

Our team prioritizes your comfort at every stage, offering sedation options and clear guidance to make the process as stress-free and comfortable as possible.

  • Implant Placement and Healing : Dr. Harris uses 3D printed surgical guides to precisely place the titanium implant into your jawbone during an in-office surgical procedure. Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your bone in an osseointegration process, creating a strong, permanent foundation.
  • Restoration : Once the implant has fully integrated into the jawbone, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached. This final restoration is designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, completing your smile.

The Benefits of Dental Implants:

Natural Appearance and Function : Dental implants are designed to match your existing natural teeth’s color, shape, and size. They feel just like real teeth, allowing you to smile, eat, and speak with complete confidence.

Durability and Longevity : Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants do not require frequent replacements, making them a cost-effective solution over time.

Improved Oral Health : Dental implants stimulate your jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth. This helps preserve your facial structure from a hollow look and keeps your smile youthful.

Protect Adjacent Teeth from Moving : Dental implants prevent adjacent teeth from shifting into gaps left by missing teeth, maintaining proper alignment and preventing future complications such as bite issues or uneven wear.

Comfortable Fit : Unlike removable dentures, with dental implants, you’ll never have to worry about slippage, using adhesives, or being uncomfortable.
