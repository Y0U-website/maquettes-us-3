Admission Criteria

Dental Radiology Technology performs many tasks under the direction of a licensed dentist and other dental staff, including:

  • Positioning patients for dental radiographs for diagnostic purposes.
  • Taking all conventional intraoral and extraoral dental radiographs. ensure patients are comfortable in the dental chair, Aligning X-Ray tubes to image receptors, Setting tube distance and exposure factors.
  • Maintain proper radiation protection protocols.
  • Job opportunities in the field of Dental Radiology Technology are expected to grow much faster than the average for all occupations through 2026 according to the U.S. Department of Labor.
  • From a student perspective, the school recognizes: current employment commitments, family obligations, convenience, cost, affordability, and financing options, the importance of hands-on, practical training in a functioning dental practice, the value of externships and partnering with other dental practices in the area so students can get real practical experience
  • Need for a full or part-time, career-based program, with high graduation rates, that can be completed in the shortest possible time and without compromising its quality.

Teaching Methods

A variety of teaching methods are employed to achieve the goals and objectives of this course including lectures, laboratory practice, demonstrations, in-office clinical training, reading, homework, and written exams.

Students are required to prepare in advance for lectures where the lecturer uses slide presentations and videos. This is followed by questions, answers, and reviews and all students are encouraged to participate. The laboratory sessions focus on practicing skills in radiology manikins.

OCCUPATIONAL PREPARATION The vocational objective of this program is to sufficiently train students to seek employment in a dental practice as entry-level dental Radiology Technologists.
