About

Here at Mississippi Center for Orofacial Pain & Dental Sleep Medicine, we provide targeted care for TMJ disorders, migraines, unexplained pain in the head, mouth, and neck, as well as treatment for sleep apnea in patients who are intolerant to CPAP.

Our goal is to offer exceptional, focused care in a welcoming and compassionate environment. Our dedicated team is here to address your specific needs with the utmost attention and expertise.

If you need care or have questions about our services, please don’t hesitate to contact us today at (601) 351-5651 ! Wesley Shields, D.M.D. General Dentistry Wesley Shields, D.M.D of general dentistry that practices in Flowood, MS. Dr. Shields graduated in 2020 from the University of Mississippi Medical Center’s School of Dentistry. Dr. Shields is a member of the American Dental Association, Mississippi Dental Association, and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. Dr. Shields is recognized by the AADSM, as a Qualified Dentist for the treatment of Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA).

Dr. Shields has limited his general dentistry practice to the treatment of OSA, TMJ dysfunction, and orofacial pain because of his personal experiences with sleep apnea, chronic pain, anxiety, and depression. Dr. Shields has a passion for educating his patients on the overlap of sleep disorders, chronic pain, TMJ, and mood disorders. Dr. Shields feels success isn’t found only in the practitioner’s ability to find and treat problems, but rather is found when patients also understand what is going on and learn ways to help themselves prevent painful problems from reoccurring.

Dr. Shields was born and raised in Richland, MS where he graduated from high school in 2000. Dr. Shields is a former firefighter/emt-b that returned to pursue his education shortly after marrying his wife, Tiffany, in 2010. In 2012, Dr Shields and Tiffany became parents to their beautiful and lovely daughter Piper. Tiffany works with Dr. Shields, and when you call the office, it is her kind and compassionate voice you will likely hear.
