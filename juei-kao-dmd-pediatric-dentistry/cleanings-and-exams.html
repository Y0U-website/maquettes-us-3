How often should my child come in?

Every six months is the standard recommendation. However, Dr. Kao may suggest more frequent visits if your child has a higher cavity risk or specific dental concerns. We’ll always explain our reasoning and work with you on a schedule that makes sense.

Making it a positive experience

We know that "going to the dentist" can sound scary to a child. That’s why our team takes extra care to keep things calm, friendly, and even fun. Dr. Kao has been doing this for over 25 years. He’s seen every level of nervousness, and he knows how to help your child feel safe.
