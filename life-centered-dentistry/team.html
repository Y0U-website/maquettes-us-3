Meet our Patient Services executives

Ashley

I love to encourage and educate patients on good oral health, as well as help them along on their dental journeys! =]

I’ve been in the dental field since 2011 and have been with Life-Centered Dentistry ever since! I started my career here as a Certified Dental Assistant, working alongside Dr. Ludwig for 10 years before transitioning to the front office. Over the past several years, I’ve enjoyed expanding my role into management—overseeing daily operations, supporting our amazing team, and helping provide the high-quality care our patients deserve.

Outside of the office, I’m a proud mom of three! Our oldest is a teenager who is very active in sports, so we’re always on the go. When I’m not at the office or at a game, I love spending time and traveling with my family, making memories wherever we go. We’re also actively involved in our church community, where we’ve built wonderful friendships that feel like family.

Laurie

I began teaching at Grand Rapids Community College as a Dental Hygiene Instructor. I teach Local Anesthesia, Nitrous Oxide Sedation and 1st and 2nd year Dental hygiene clinics. I also travel with the CDCA-WREB-CITA serving as a Dental Board Examiner. I stay current with dentistry by continuing my education and I stay connected with the community by volunteering.

I am married to a wonderful Man. We have two amazing children, one super cute grandson and three loving dogs. In my spare time I enjoy spending time outdoors, biking working out and relaxing by the pool.

I was born and raised in Walker. This is my home town where “Life-Centered Dentistry” is located. I love walker and I am very proud to say I work with one amazing team. I am truly blessed.

Trish

​Registered Dental Hygienist Hi my name is Trish. I have been a Registered Dental Hygienist for over 28 years and recently joined this amazing team here at Life- Centered Dentistry. I absolutely love providing exceptional care for my patients and building long lasting relationships.
