We offer fixed or removable appliances so you can choose what works best for your lifestyle. Our treatment options include:

Traditional Braces

One of the most common orthodontic treatment methods, traditional braces are fixed appliances that move teeth using a combination of metal brackets, wires, and bands. The brackets are bonded to each tooth with a wire running through each bracket that’s tightened and adjusted to correct tooth alignment and bite. You’ll have consistent appointments over the course of your treatment to make any necessary adjustments and track your progress. Braces may also be paired with rubber bands to facilitate tooth movement and bite correction.

Retainers

Once your orthodontic treatment is complete, you want to maintain your new smile. You’ve just spent anywhere from a few months to a few years receiving corrective care, so you don’t want it to go to waste. We offer fixed retainers and removable retainers to keep your teeth straight and prevent the natural shifting that occurs over time. Your orthodontist will determine which option is the best fit for your needs.

Caring for Your Teeth During Orthodontic Treatment

It’s important to remember that tooth decay can still strike while you’re receiving orthodontic treatment. Brushing and flossing are the best methods of prevention and we encourage you to brush and floss at least twice per day. Don’t chew anything hard, like ice cubes, and avoid sticky, sugary foods and beverages, like chewy candy or sodas.

If you’re using Invisalign, be sure to take out your aligners while eating and keep them clean by rinsing them with warm water and scrubbing them with a soft-bristled toothbrush. For deep cleaning, we recommend using Invisalign Cleaning Crystals.
