The practice

We believe that everyone can have a healthy smile for life, and with our help it s easier than you think. We are a family dentistry office serving the four-county area — Frederick, Carroll, Montgomery, and Howard counties — for over 40 years.

From routine cleanings to complete smile restorations, we offer the full range of dental services for adults and children.

Preventive & Family Care Exams, cleanings, deep cleanings, pediatric dentistry, and oral health & head/neck cancer screenings to keep the whole family healthy.

Cosmetic Dentistry Teeth whitening, Invisalign, Botox, and dermal fillers to help you feel confident in your smile.

Restorative Dentistry Dental implants, crowns, bridges, and dentures — including implant-supported overdentures, the best long-term option for replacing missing teeth.

Les soins

Routine checkups and professional cleanings (prophylaxis) to remove plaque and tartar and keep your teeth and gums in top condition.

Scaling and root planing for patients with gum disease — a thorough cleaning below the gumline that helps stop periodontal problems from progressing.

The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth — and implant-supported overdentures offer the best long-term, healthy option for replacing multiple missing teeth.

Straighten your teeth discreetly with clear, removable aligners — no brackets or wires, and fewer office visits.

Personalized smile makeovers combining whitening, bonding, and restorative techniques to achieve the look you want.
