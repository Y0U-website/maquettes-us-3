Dental laser technology emits a beam of concentrated light that can be used as an alternative to more traditional dental procedures. Laser dentistry allows for many procedures to be performed with minimal pain, less bleeding, and faster recovery times.

Our Concord, NH dental practice offers comprehensive services for the whole family using the latest dental techniques and technology. Our services include:

Restorative Dentistry – When a tooth is damaged or decayed, we use restorative dentistry to restore their function, appearance, and strength. These procedures include fillings, dental crowns, bridges, dentures, and dental implants.

Cosmetic Dentistry – Cosmetic dentistry focuses on the aesthetics of your teeth and gums. We offer teeth whitening, dental veneers, bonding, and microabrasion to help you feel more confident about your smile.

Dental Implants – Dental implants are the only solution for missing teeth that addresses the consequences of tooth loss both above and below the gum line. There are a wide range of dental implant options, ranging from single-tooth implants to full dentures.

Laser Dentistry – Laser dentistry makes getting the dental care you need a more comfortable, stress-free experience. There is no noise, minimal pain, and an easier recovery.
