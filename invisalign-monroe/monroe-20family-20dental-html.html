Excellence isn’t just in our care.

Monroe Family & Cosmetic Dentistry was built on a belief — that world-class dentistry should feel as welcoming as it is precise. Dr. Pete and Dr. Alex lead a practice that doesn’t just treat teeth. We build lasting confidence, one smile at a time.

Digital radiography, intraoral scanners, 3D CT scanners, and a full digital workflow — faster, clearer, and more comfortable diagnostics and treatment.

No judgement, no pressure, no gimmicks. Welcome to a place where you’ll feel at home right away, where your concerns are actually heard, and where the advanced dental care you deserve is accessible, affordable, and easy.

No surprises. No overtreatment. No overcharging. No hidden fees. Nothing to hide. It’s the way dentistry should be.

Dr. Pete and Dr. Alex trained at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies — one of the most respected post-graduate programs in the world.

Comprehensive care for every smile.

Whether it’s aligning, whitening, bonding, or implant work — we start with you. Your goals, your comfort, your story. This isn’t a one-size approach; it’s your custom dental journey.
