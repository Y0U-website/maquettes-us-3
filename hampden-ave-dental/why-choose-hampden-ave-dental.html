Emergency Dental Care Available

Dental emergencies are unforeseen situations. But when they do happen, you need prompt and reliable care.

Our team understands the distress and urgency of dental emergencies. That’s why we offer same-day emergency dental care for our patients, providing a trusted place to turn to in the case of sudden tooth pain , knocked-out teeth, or other urgent dental issues.

Our goal is to relieve your discomfort quickly, address the root of the problem, and help you return to daily activities with confidence and peace of mind.

Affordable Options with Medicaid

We are committed to making our services as accessible as possible. We accept all major insurance plans, including Medicaid (Health First Colorado and DentaQuest).
