Missing a Tooth? Dental Implants May Be the Fix

  • Implant Placement The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Healing takes several weeks to a few months as the implant integrates with the bone.
  • Abutment Placement Once the implant is fully fused, a small connector (abutment) is attached, which will hold the final restoration in place.
  • Final Restoration A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is fabricated to match your natural teeth and securely attached to the abutment.

BENEFITS OF DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants offer numerous advantages over traditional tooth replacement options:

  • Natural Appearance and Feel: Implants look and function like real teeth.
  • Durability: With proper care, implants can last decades or even a lifetime.
  • Improved Oral Health: They help preserve jawbone density and prevent neighboring teeth from shifting.
  • Enhanced Comfort: No slipping or discomfort like with removable dentures.
  • Restored Function: Speak, chew, and smile with confidence.
  • Easy Maintenance: Care for them just like natural teeth with brushing, flossing, and regular checkups.
