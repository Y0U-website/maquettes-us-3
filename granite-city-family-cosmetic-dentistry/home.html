Join Our Family of Friends!

Granite City Family and Cosmetic Dentistry would love the opportunity to show you what truly exceptional dental care is all about. Our goal is to provide our patients with care that lasts a lifetime, and we are always very happy to have new patients become part of our family.

We emphasize family dentistry-that means we’re here to help everyone from the first toddler appointment through a conversation about dentures. Dr. Peterson and her entire team have received special training in providing comfortable, quality dental care, and we love what we do!

Our dental services include: cosmetic, implant and family dentistry, Invisalign, Zoom, general dentistry and preventive dental procedures. We welcome the opportunity to provide your routine care, and we strive to treat emergencies the same day.
