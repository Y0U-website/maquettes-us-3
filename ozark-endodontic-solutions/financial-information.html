Financial Information

Transparency

We believe in fee transparency. We always want you to know what to expect. Please call our office to discuss fees, insurance, and financing options.

Insurance

We file insurance claims for all insurances and are preferred providers for Delta Dental. If you have dental insurance, please let our office know. Our staff will gladly file claims for you and will make every effort to maximize your insurance reimbursement.

Financing

We know that paying for dental treatment can be stressful. We offer several financing options, including Cherry and CareCredit. Please call us – our staff are always happy to discuss your financing options and will do their best to find an affordable way for you to receive specialty care at Ozark Endodontic Solutions.

You can also apply online using the links below:

Cherry: https://pay.withcherry.com/ozark-endodontic-solutions Payment options through Cherry Technologies Inc. are issued by these financing partners .

CareCredit: https://www.carecredit.com/apply
