Early Orthodontic Treatment in…

  • Prevents harmful habits from causing damage: Habit appliances gently deter behaviors like thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting, both of which can lead to bite problems.
  • Reduces future orthodontic needs: By addressing issues early, orthodontic care during childhood may eliminate or reduce the need for braces later.
  • Encourages long-term oral health: Straight, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, which minimizes the risks of cavities and gum disease as your child grows.

Procedures Offered

  • Space Maintenance: If your child loses a primary tooth too early, we use space maintainers to hold the space open until the permanent tooth erupts. This prevents neighboring teeth from crowding into the gap and causing alignment issues.
  • Habit Appliances: Habit appliances gently correct behaviors like thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting that could disrupt natural jaw development, protecting the alignment of your child’s teeth and bite.

Is Your Child A Good Candidate for Orthodontics?

If your child has lost a primary tooth early, has persistent habits like thumb-sucking, or shows signs of bite or alignment issues, they may benefit from orthodontic care. Our team can help evaluate your child’s needs and recommend the best treatment options.
