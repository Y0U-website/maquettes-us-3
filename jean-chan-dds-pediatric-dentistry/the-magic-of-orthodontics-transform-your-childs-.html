The Benefits of Orthodontics for Children

While some people might associate orthodontics with teenagers, early intervention can be incredibly beneficial for children. Orthodontic treatment can address a variety of issues in children’s developing smiles, including:

  • Crowded teeth: When there’s not enough space in the jaw for all the permanent teeth to erupt properly, crowding can occur. Orthodontics can gently guide teeth into their correct positions, preventing future problems like impaction or difficulty cleaning.
  • Spacing issues: Gaps between teeth can be unsightly and sometimes affect speech development. Orthodontics can close these gaps for a more aesthetically pleasing and functional smile.
  • Malocclusions (bad bites): Misaligned bites, such as overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites, can cause difficulty chewing, speaking, and even jaw pain. Early orthodontic intervention can help guide jaw growth and correct these issues before they become more complex.
  • Improved oral health: Straight teeth are easier to clean and maintain, reducing the risk of tooth decay and gum disease.

Early Orthodontic Evaluation: Why It Matters

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that all children have an orthodontic evaluation by age seven. This early assessment allows Dr. Chan to identify potential problems early on when they can be addressed more easily and effectively.

During the initial consultation, Dr. Chan, who specializes in orthodontics in El Cajon , will conduct a thorough examination of your child’s teeth, jaws, and facial structure. X-rays and other diagnostic tools might be used to assess the underlying cause of any misalignments and develop a personalized treatment plan.

Treatment Options for Growing Smiles

There are various orthodontic treatments available for children, each with its own advantages. Dr. Chan will discuss the most suitable option for your child’s unique needs and preferences. Some common options include:

  • Interceptive appliances: These are used in young children to guide jaw growth and create space for permanent teeth to erupt properly. They can be removable or fixed and are often worn for a short period.
  • Space maintainers: These appliances hold open space in the jaw after a baby tooth is lost prematurely to ensure the permanent tooth can erupt into its proper position.
  • Traditional braces: Braces are the most common type of orthodontic treatment and use a system of brackets, wires, and elastics to gradually move teeth into their correct positions. While traditional metal braces are still widely used, Dr. Chan also offers clear or tooth-colored ceramic braces for a more discreet look.
  • Clear aligners: Clear aligners, like Invisalign for teens, are removable trays that gradually shift teeth into their desired positions. They are popular for older children and teenagers who prefer a less noticeable treatment.
