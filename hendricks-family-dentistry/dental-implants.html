What to Expect

  • Consultation & Imaging We evaluate your bone health using 3D CT scanning and cone beam imaging for precise planning.
  • Implant Placement The implant post is placed into the jaw and allowed to heal, fusing naturally with bone.
  • Restoration with Crown or Bridge We use custom restorations—often with CEREC same-day crowns —to complete your smile.
  • Ongoing Care Implants are maintained like natural teeth with regular brushing and dental cleanings .

FAQs on Dental Implants

How long do implants last?

With routine care, dental implants can last several decades or longer.

Will it hurt?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after surgery.

Can anyone get dental implants?

You need healthy gums and enough bone—our team will assess this during your consultation.

Are implants better than dentures?

Yes, implants offer better function, comfort, and bone preservation over time.
