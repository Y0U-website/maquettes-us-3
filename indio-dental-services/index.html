The practice

Super Smiles Family Dental offers a wide variety of dental treatments to help you smile your best.

Our dedicated and compassionate team cares for families from Indio, CA, and the surrounding communities of the Greater Coachella Valley, La Quinta, and Palm Desert.

We know your time is important, so we offer evening appointments and see patients on time.

Patients will immediately feel the familial atmosphere as they enter our building and see why many of our patients have been with us for over 40 years. Every patient is cared for like family and it’s a feeling that is clear throughout every appointment.
