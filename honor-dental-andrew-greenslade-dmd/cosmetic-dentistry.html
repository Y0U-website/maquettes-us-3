Veneers

Veneers are often used to eliminate any tooth imperfections. This ranges from eliminating small gaps to completely covering up stained, misshaped, or uneven teeth. They are expertly crafted out of wafer-thin porcelain shells, made to look tooth-colored and natural. This procedure begins with the removal of 0.5mm of enamel so the veneer can merge to the tooth. This makes it an irreversible procedure because enamel doesn’t grow back. Even so, veneers are still a highly popular procedure as they can last for up to 20 years, giving you an impeccable smile that will boost your confidence.

Cosmetic Dental Makeovers

Cosmetic makeovers are specialized dental plans that address your every need to give you a smile that you are satisfied with. They utilize multiple treatments to cover everything. This is favorable because everyone has different desires and unique needs; cosmetic dental makeovers can be vastly different depending on the individual. Our cosmetic dentist in Rochester, Dr. Greenslade, will outline the cosmetic and restorative dental procedures that will be used to best suit your smile wants and needs.

Invisalign

Invisalign straightens your teeth without the hassle of uncomfortable metal, ceramic brackets, or wires. Invisalign is created with durable plastic called SmartTrack for the clear aligners. One of the major benefits of Invisalign is they are easily removable, so you don’t have to worry about having dietary restrictions. The technology for Invisalign is becoming increasingly more advanced, meaning that they are capable of treating complex orthodontic solutions. Feel free to consult with the team at Honor Dental to determine if clear aligners are the best option for you.

Do You have questions about cosmetic dentistry?

What dental issues can cosmetic dentistry solve?

Cosmetic dentistry offers a wide range of options that can treat nearly any aesthetic dental issue. Yellow or stained teeth can be fixed with teeth whitening; minor cracks, chips, or evening out your smile can be solved with dental bonding; Veneers or Lumineers can completely refurbish any aesthetic concerns, and dental crowns are used on teeth that have severe damage or decay.

Am I a good candidate for cosmetic dental treatments?

Having good oral and overall health is the main requirement for cosmetic treatments. You are a good candidate if you meet those and are looking to improve the look of your smile. Oral health issues like gum disease, cavities, or occlusal (bite) issues usually require restorative or orthodontic treatments before cosmetic treatment. It’s important to get an initial oral health exam with Honor Dental before proceeding with any procedures.

Can I get more than one cosmetic dental procedure done at the same time?

Yes you can! In fact, it’s a common practice. Receiving multiple cosmetic treatments in one appointment makes it way more efficient to get the results you want. Some procedures, such as dental bonding and enameloplasty, go hand-in-hand. Our practice prioritizes keeping procedures as straightforward and effective as possible.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by dental insurance?

Every insurance plan is different but generally, cosmetic procedures are not covered by insurance. Insurances typically don’t cover procedures that aren’t medical necessities. Some plans may cover veneers or crowns if they provide restorative benefits, but this is an exception rather than a rule. The best way to get exact details on your coverage plan is to call your insurance provider directly. However, we can provide financing options and plans; we will help you work out a solution that will give you the beautiful smile you deserve. Just give us a call so that we can help assist you.
