Meet The Doctors

I can also assure you that my entire staff at All About Smiles is also trained to provide a warm, nurturing, friendly environment for your child. We all work as a team to meet the fears, anxieties and emotional needs of your child.

Dr. Kourtsounis or Dr. K is a pediatric dentist originally from Long Island. He received his Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology from the State University of New York at Stony Brook in 1998. After graduation, he returned to Maryland where his family had moved and was accepted into the top ranked University of Maryland, Baltimore College of Dental Surgery where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2003.

Dr. Shawn is a New York native. He was born in Queens, NY and later moved to Long Island. He received his undergraduate degree from New York University where he graduated with honors. Dr. Shawn then made his way to Columbia University where he earned his dental degree. At his graduation he was recognized for outstanding promise in the field of pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Jenna Permaul pursued a career in pediatric dentistry because of her passion to provide a positive dental environment for children. She believes in fostering healthy oral practices with every family that she encounters and strives to provide patient-centered care.

Dr. Kimia Pouyafar is a passionate pediatric dentist who grew up in Nassau County, Long Island. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Adelphi University.

Dr. Pouyafar attended New York University College of Dentistry, where she obtained her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. Her love for children led her to specialize in pediatric dentistry, and she completed her residency at New York University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Williamson grew up just outside of Boston, MA. She attended Brandeis University where she earned her Bachelor of Arts in Health: Science, Society, and Policy. In the summer after her freshman year of college, Dr. Williamson participated in the Summer Medical and Dental Education Program at Columbia University, which ignited her passions for education, public health, mentorship, and community outreach.

Dr. Shelley grew up in Norfolk, Virginia, and earned her undergraduate degree from Virginia Tech before moving to New York. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from Touro College of Dental Medicine and completed her specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at Stony Brook Hospital, where she served as Chief Resident in her second year.

Dr. Danielle Silver first discovered her passion for pediatric dentistry after experiencing dental trauma as a child. The care and kindness she received from her pediatric dentist helped her heal her smile. Inspired by that impact, Dr. Silver set out to create the same sense of comfort and trust for her own patients.

She values the importance of early, positive dental experiences and is committed to creating a supportive environment where children and their families feel nurtured and cared for.
