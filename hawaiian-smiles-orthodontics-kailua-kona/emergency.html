What is considered an orthodontic emergency?

Keep in mind that even if a situation is alarming, it may not necessarily be an emergency. However, if you notice swelling around a hurting tooth or uncontrollable bleeding, you should immediately contact emergency services and let your orthodontist know. It’s always best to be on the safer side and get professional help if you suspect you have an orthodontic emergency in Kaneohe, Hawaii.

Is a broken bracket an emergency?

Not exactly. A broken bracket is an inconvenience but not an orthodontic emergency in Kaneohe. If a bracket breaks, you can try and cover it with orthodontic wax. However, contact your Orthodontist to have it replaced as soon as possible.

What do you do when your braces bracket falls off?

If one of your brackets falls off during an Orthodontic emergency in Kaneohe, Hawaii, contact your orthodontist as soon as you can to schedule a visit and have it replaced or reattached. In the meantime, make sure you remove any broken pieces to avoid swallowing them and cover the bracket with orthodontic wax.

When should you go to the ER for orthodontic emergencies?

You should go to the ER if you undergo an orthodontic emergency in Kaneohe, Hawaii. Some scenarios include swallowing a bracket, heavy bleeding, or swelling that could indicate an abscess.

What is the most common orthodontic emergency?

Most common orthodontic emergencies in Kaneohe include: Loose brackets, protruding wires, food stuck in gums, unbearable pain, swallowed orthodontic device parts, mouth irritations and infections. All these events vary in severity and it’s always best if you get professional help.
