Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

To qualify for dental implants, patients must be in good general health. X-rays are typically used to assess jawbone density, which is essential for supporting the implant. If the bone is insufficient, a bone graft may be recommended to build up the area. There’s no age limit for implants anyone with adequate bone and overall good health may be a candidate.

Dental Implant Procedure: From X-Ray to Aftercare

We want our customers to be as informed as possible.

Here is our step-by-step guide for our dental implant procedure:

  • X-rays and digital scans to plan implant placement
  • Incision in gums.
  • Titanium implant anchored into the jawbone
  • Temporary crown placed during healing
  • Healing Period: 3 6 months for osseointegration (bone fusing with implant)
  • Abutment attached to the implant
  • Custom crown bonded to abutment
  • Wait for anesthesia to wear off before eating hot foods
  • Use gauze to manage bleeding
  • Take prescribed painkillers and antibiotics
  • Brush and floss gently with a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Avoid touching the surgical site
  • Quit smoking to promote healing
  • Avoid biting hard substances to protect the implant

Maintaining Your Dental Implants

Proper care is key to ensuring your implants last a lifetime. Dental implants function like natural teeth but still require daily hygiene and routine professional checkups. Brushing twice a day, flossing carefully around the implant, and using an antibacterial mouth rinse are essential. Regular dental visits allow us to monitor the health of your implants and surrounding tissues, preventing issues before they develop.
