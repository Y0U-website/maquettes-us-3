Meet the Team at Martik Family Dental LLC

At Martik Family Dental, our goal is to provide every one of our patients with exceptional dental care in a friendly, stress-free setting. This wouldn t be possible without the hard work of our caring, professional staff.

They know how to work together as a team to make each visit an enjoyable experience with patient-focused care that will exceed your expectations and leave you smiling. Everyone is unique when it comes to dental care, and our goal is to get to know you personally to provide you with treatments that are tailored to your specific needs.

Please take a moment to learn more about our amazing team!

Dr. Adam Martik

Dr. Martik had several dental issues growing up and received such great dental care that he was inspired to pursue a career in dentistry. He is passionate about helping people achieve healthy smiles and providing relief when they are in pain. He stays up to date with the latest in dental treatment advancements through rigorous continuing education and uses only the best available materials and methods when providing care to his patients.

He grew up in Finleyville, Pennsylvania, but now lives in Mt. Lebanon with his wife, their 3 year old daughter Kendal, and their dog, Moose. They love living in the South Hills of Pittsburgh with all it has to offer. In his spare time, Dr. Martik enjoys riding dirt bikes, exercising, and spending time with his growing family.

Jill - Receptionist

Jill is a graduate of the Median School of Allied Health Careers, and hers is the first friendly face you’ll see when you arrive at our office. Jill enjoys interacting with our patients to make them feel welcome and put them at ease.

She is a native of Pittsburgh and has been a member of the team at Martik Family Dental for 26 years. Outside of work, Jill is happy to spend quality time with her children.

Aleta - Dental Assistant

Aleta is a native of Pittsburgh and has been with the practice for an incredible 38 years. Her obvious commitment to helping patients improve their oral health is reflected in the strong relationships she has built over the years.

Aleta received her training at the Median School of Allied Health Careers, and in her free time she enjoys Zumba, working out, and traveling.

Stephanie - Dental Hygienist

Stephanie has been a member of our team for 15 years, and is a graduate of the University of Pittsburgh. She enjoys making a difference in our patients’ lives by helping them achieve happy, healthy smiles.

Stephanie grew up in Pittsburgh and loves bike riding and spending time with her puppy.
