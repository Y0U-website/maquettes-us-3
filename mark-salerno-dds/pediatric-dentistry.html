Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. Typically, x-rays are recommended every few years, though children with a higher risk of oral disease may need more frequent scans.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.

Pediatric Dental Emergencies

When your child has a dental emergency, getting the right care quickly can make all the difference. Whether they have a toothache, a chipped or broken tooth, or another urgent dental concern, our team is here to help relieve their discomfort and guide you through the next steps.

Special Needs Dentistry

Your child&#x27;s unique needs are at the heart of everything we do. Our practice focuses on providing thoughtful, individualized care for all children, including those with sensory sensitivities, autism spectrum disorder, and other special needs. Our experienced team creates a warm, supportive environment where every child can feel comfortable and every parent can have peace of mind. We&#x27;d love to discuss how we can tailor our care to your child&#x27;s specific needs – reach out to learn more about our accommodations and approach.

Infant Dentistry

According to the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), a baby should see a dentist within six months of the eruption of their first tooth, or by age one. These early visits are essential for ensuring your child&#x27;s teeth are developing properly. During these appointments, we&#x27;ll also inform you of proper hygiene techniques to keep that little smile strong and healthy, along with nutritional guidance to keep cavities at bay.
