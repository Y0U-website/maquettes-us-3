Why Preventive Care Saves You Money

The American Dental Association estimates that every dollar spent on preventive care saves $8–$50 in restorative treatment. A cavity caught early is a small filling — left alone, it becomes a crown or a root canal.

Preventive care also protects whole-body health. Research links oral health to heart disease, diabetes, respiratory illness, and pregnancy outcomes. Keeping your mouth healthy is one of the simplest things you can do for your overall health.
