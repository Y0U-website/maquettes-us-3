The practice

Root canal treatment commonly is associated with anxiety and fear and each person has unique past experiences. We strive to make each experience as smooth as possible from utilizing online scheduling and forms, patient-centered and individualized, one-on-one care, and seamless communication with your primary dental provider.

Our Vision Dental anxiety and fear are barriers that keeps patients from seeking the treatment that they need. This can have significant downstream impacts for not only their oral health, but also overall quality of life. We would like to be well prepared before you even walk through our doors. Prior to your first visit we will send you an in-depth new patient form to know how we can best serve your needs and address any concerns you may have.

We do understand how financially daunting medical and dental treatment can be, and we will do our best to provide affordable yet high quality services.

We will assist you with your dental plan benefits by checking your eligibility/verification to have an estimate prior to your visit, submitting charges to your insurance company on your behalf, and accepting payment directly from them.

We ask patients to pay an estimated percentage on the day of services rendered based on the most accurate information we receive from your insurance company, however, upon receipt of your company’s dental plan benefit, if there is a difference, we will refund you or may need to bill any remaining balance to you.

Les soins

Cavity, history of having a large cavity and filling, defective filling, trauma, or tooth crack that has led to symptoms including temperature sensitivity, throbbing pain, difficulty eating or chewing, pressure on the gum, a bump on the gum, or swelling

A tooth that has been previously treated with a root canal, but has been reinfected due to exposure of the root canal, a failing filling or crown, or persistent infection.
