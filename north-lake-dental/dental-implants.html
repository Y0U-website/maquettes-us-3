Dental Implants

Do you hesitate to smile because of a missing tooth? Are you self-conscious about a gap a missing tooth has left behind? With dental implants, your smile can be fully restored!

Dental implants have also been effective in implant-supported dentures, a popular denture option for people who want more stability in their dentures. The implants act as anchors for the dentures.

What Is the Dental Implant Process?

A dental implant is designed to be more permanent, so the process has several important steps:

During your procedure, we’ll keep you comfortable using dental sedation. Our team also keeps up good communication so we can check to see if you are feeling okay and that the procedure goes well.

  • You will need an exam from our Dentist to ensure that your jawbone is healthy enough for an implant placement.
  • The titanium alloy post is placed during surgery.
  • You may need to give the implant some time to fuse with your jawbone.
  • You’ll come back in to receive your tooth replacement.

What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Our patients love implant-supported dentures for the following reasons:

Restore your full smile with our implant-supported dentures. You can also find out more about our other dentures options here .

  • Easy to Clean : Instead of removing traditional dentures for cleaning, you can easily continue to brush and floss your teeth.
  • Tooth Stability : The implant posts anchor your dentures so you don’t need to worry about your dentures slipping or sliding while you eat.
  • Natural Smile : You can enjoy a natural-looking smile without worrying about your dentures dropping out of your mouth.
  • Restore Your Jaw : Dental implants encourage jawbone regrowth. The posts also help keep remaining teeth in place.

Restore Your Smile and Your Confidence

Having a full, beautiful smile can increase your confidence. We all use our smiles everyday to communicate with the people around us. A dental implant can make you feel more confident in your smile.

You shouldn’t feel like you have to hide your smile because of a missing tooth. Our friendly dental team can show you how a dental implant can change your life and add to your self-esteem.
