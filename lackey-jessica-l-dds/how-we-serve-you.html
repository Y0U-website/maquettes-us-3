About the Practice

Shelby Dental Care Center is devoted to providing exceptional patient care. Our dentists maintain that communication and education are essential for delivering dental services effectively. Knowing what your treatment options are and being armed with plenty of information is imperative to make the best decision to meet your dental health needs .

From the moment you arrive at our office , we hope you will feel a sense of warmth, welcome, and friendship . Patient satisfaction is our highest priority, and our staff members take the time to make sure you are comfortable and relaxed. If possible, we even try to make sure you are laughing by the time you leave .

Dr. Taylor Beard is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered dental care that prioritizes comfort, trust, and understanding. Originally from Oak Harbor, Ohio, Dr. Beard pursued her passion for healthcare by earning her bachelor’s degree from Ohio University in Athens before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at The Ohio State University.

Her journey into dentistry began during her senior year of high school after receiving veneers, which sparked her interest in the transformative impact of dentistry on confidence and overall well-being. During her education, Dr. Beard was an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, reflecting her commitment to continued growth and excellence in patient care.

Dr. Beard is passionate about making patients feel valued and involved in their care, creating a positive and comfortable dental experience. While she is not sedation certified, her gentle and attentive approach helps patients feel at ease.

Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her fiancé—whom she met in undergrad—as they plan their wedding, explore the outdoors, and try new restaurants. She is also an avid reader who loves discovering new books.

Pauline E. Cahill, DDS

Focused on creating truly life-changing, transformational care and catering to the needs of anxious adults, Dr. Cahill practices patient-centered dentistry defined by poise, precision, compassion, and excellence. She combines exceptional clinical training with the advanced technologies available at Shelby Dental Care Center to deliver a calm, comfortable, and elevated care.

Dr. Cahill earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Dalhousie University School of Dentistry in Canada. She subsequently served seven years with the Canadian Department of National Defence, where she practiced comprehensive general dentistry with an emphasis on complete oral rehabilitation.

She has completed advanced training in dental prosthodontics, including beautiful full mouth implant-supported bridges, as well as oral surgery and orthodontics. She remains committed to ongoing, technology-focused continuing education to provide the highest standard of care.

She and her husband, Dr. Calvin Caunt, have one son, Ben. She enjoys the company of her cats, gardening, travel, and mentoring the award-winning SigmaCorns, Thermal Equilibrium, Pi Rho Eagles, and Autonomous Angels robotics teams.
