new patients

Although most of our patients are adults, we’re happy to treat patients of all ages — from ages 3 to 103! Our team welcomes the entire family and provides care tailored to each patient’s needs.

During your first visit, you’ll receive a comprehensive exam with one of our doctors. This includes collecting any necessary digital X-rays, a thorough evaluation of your oral health, and a detailed discussion of your findings and next steps in care. We take the time to understand your goals and help you choose the best treatment options for your unique situation.

We believe that achieving and maintaining a healthy smile plays an important role in your confidence, happiness, and overall well-being. Together, we can help you and your family achieve optimal oral health. Contact us today to schedule your visit!
