Meet Dr. Nitin Joshi, DMD

With nearly 16 years of clinical experience, Dr. Nitin Joshi is a distinguished expert in digital dentistry and implant rehabilitation and complex implant cases . His career spans both military and civilian dentistry , showcasing a unique combination of precision, innovation, and dedication to advanced dental care.

Dr. Nitin Joshi, DMD Precision, Experience and Compassion

Dr. Nitin Joshi earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Boston University and holds a certificate in Oral Surgery and Trauma from the Armed Forces Medical College in Pune, India. He proudly served as a Major in the Army Dental Corps, where he encountered lifechanging clinical and humanitarian experiences that continue to shape his compassionate and precise approach to patient care.

During his military tenure, Dr. Joshi managed complex surgical cases under highpressure conditions, gaining invaluable experience that informs his surgical precision and calm confidence in practice today. Dedicated to lifelong learning and clinical excellence, Dr. Joshi completes over 300 hours of continuing education annually, with advanced training in dental implant surgery, prosthetic reconstruction, TMJ disorders, and full–mouth rehabilitation. His comprehensive skill set allows him to treat patients with even the most challenging dental needs.

He has also served on the Ethics Board of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, where he contributed to shaping professional standards in implantology.

Dr. Joshi is passionate about delivering life-changing dental care, especially for patients who have been told they have no options. His practice philosophy centers on accessibility, trust, and long-term outcomes through personalized, high–quality care.

Outside the office, Dr. Joshi enjoys spending time with his family, is an avid cook, and leads an active lifestyle as a competitive polo player and enthusiastic golfer.
