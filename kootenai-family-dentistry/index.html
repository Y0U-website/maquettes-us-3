A Healthier Alternative Traditional materials like metal amalgam can feel bulky and unnatural, sometimes weakening the tooth over time. Biomimetic Dentistry provides a modern alternative with biocompatible materials that are healthier, longer-lasting, and far more comfortable.

At Kootenai Family Dentistry , we’re always looking for ways to preserve healthy tooth structure and treat problems as early as possible. That’s why we now offer Curodont® , an advanced biomimetic treatment designed to help remineralize enamel and treat early tooth decay without drilling.

Curodont supports the natural rebuilding of enamel in areas affected by early, non-cavitated decay. The treatment is quick, non-invasive, and designed to help prevent cavities from progressing.

For patients looking for a more conservative approach to dental care, Curodont is another way our team focuses on protecting your natural teeth whenever possible.
