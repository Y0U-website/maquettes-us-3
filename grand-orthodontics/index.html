Orthodontic treatments are not just for your smile, even though they can really improve your appearance and self-esteem.

At Grand Orthodontics, our philosophy is to provide treatment to the whole person, with a special focus on how your teeth and gums relate to your total body health. We want you to be confident that your smile achieves your goals for comfort, function, and appearance. We’re here to make that happen!

Our orthodontic specialist, Dr. Kendall Woolridge, understands that choosing an orthodontist is an important decision. After all, your smile is your greatest asset and says a lot about your overall health. From the moment you walk in the door, you’ll be welcomed by a caring staff in a relaxed, stress-free environment where the whole family can feel at ease.

Dr. Woolridge loves helping others improve their self-confidence by transitioning their existing smiles into healthy and beautiful ones! She enjoys seeing the transformation of her children, as well as adult, patients. It makes her ecstatic to know that she contributes to making so many people happy!
