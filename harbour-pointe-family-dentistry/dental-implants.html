Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Edmonds, WA

Most adults with good general health are candidates for dental implants, including many patients who have been missing teeth for years. Key factors include sufficient jawbone density, healthy gum tissue, and controlled systemic conditions. Patients with bone loss may still qualify after a bone grafting procedure. At Harbour Pointe Family Dentistry, Dr. Kris Johnson evaluates bone quality and quantity through digital imaging at your consultation to determine the best path forward for your situation.

The complete dental implant process typically takes three to nine months from consultation to final crown, depending on whether bone grafting is needed and how quickly osseointegration occurs. Surgical placement is one appointment. Healing and osseointegration — the process by which bone cells grow onto the titanium post — takes three to six months. Final crown placement is a separate appointment once integration is confirmed. Patients who require bone grafting should expect additional time before the surgical phase.

The surgical placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so patients feel pressure but not pain during the procedure. Post-surgical discomfort is similar to a tooth extraction and is typically managed with over-the-counter pain medication for a few days. Most patients report the procedure was more comfortable than expected. At our Edmonds-area practice, Dr. Johnson takes time to discuss anesthesia options and post-operative care before your procedure begins.

Dental implants are a significant investment compared to bridges or dentures, reflecting the permanence and bone-preserving benefits they provide. Some dental insurance plans provide partial coverage — we verify your benefits before treatment and provide itemized written cost estimates. We offer financing through Cherry Payment Plans and CareCredit to make implant treatment accessible for Edmonds patients.
