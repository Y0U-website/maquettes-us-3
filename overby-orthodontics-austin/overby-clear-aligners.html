FAQ about Clear Aligners

  • Are Clear Aligners Cheaper than Braces? Invisalign® and Spark clear aligners is similar in cost to braces. Despite the similar cost, clear aligners provide unbeatable benefits like the freedom to live your life without worrying about metal brackets and wires that get in the way of special events or foods you love.
  • How Much Does It Cost to Get Clear Aligners? The cost of Invisalign® or Spark clear aligners varies based on individual treatment, but some dental insurance providers may cover costs. Please contact Overby Orthodontics directly to discuss your smile goals and treatment plan, which will determine the total cost of treatment.
  • Do Clear Aligners Make Teeth Yellow? No. To avoid staining or a yellow appearance of your clear aligners trays, it’s important to remove your aligners while eating or drinking and brush immediately after consuming food and drinks. By following proper hygiene, you’ll avoid a yellow appearance of teeth.
