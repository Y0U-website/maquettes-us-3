Advanced Orthodontic Solutions for Complex Cases in Tracy & Manteca, CA

At New Smile Orthodontics, we offer surgical orthodontics (also known as orthognathic surgery) for patients with severe jaw misalignment or bite irregularities that cannot be corrected with braces or aligners alone.

What Is Surgical Orthodontics?

Surgical orthodontics is used to correct significant issues with jaw alignment, facial symmetry, and severe bite problems. It involves a collaborative approach between your orthodontist and an oral and maxillofacial surgeon. Braces are typically worn before and after surgery to help guide your teeth into their proper positions as the jaw is realigned.

When Is Surgical Orthodontics Needed?

Surgical orthodontics may be recommended for patients who:

  • Have difficulty biting, chewing, or speaking due to jaw misalignment
  • Experience facial imbalance or asymmetry
  • Suffer from chronic jaw pain or TMJ issues
  • Have an open bite, crossbite, or severe underbite/overbite
  • Are over the age of 16 (girls) or 18 (boys), once jaw growth is complete

Benefits of Surgical Orthodontics

  • Corrects severe bite and jaw issues
  • Improves chewing, breathing, and speech
  • Enhances facial balance and symmetry
  • Relieves TMJ discomfort and jaw strain
  • Provides long-term functional and cosmetic improvements
