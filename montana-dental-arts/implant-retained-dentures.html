Implant Retained Dentures

  • Bar-Retained Dentures : This type of implant-supported denture uses a thin metal bar attached to several implants for secure, stable support. It can be removable or fixed. They offer 100 % function, comparable to natural teeth.
  • Fixed-Hybrid Dentures : This permanent, implant-supported prosthetic combines the stability of implants with the coverage of a complete denture for optimal function and aesthetics. It can be removable or fixed. They offer 100 % function, comparable to natural teeth.
