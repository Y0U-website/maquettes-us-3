Are you wondering what to expect for your first visit to Knoxville Orthodontics? Our orthodontists and team will conduct a full examination of your mouth and smile to understand what your smile needs are and plan your orthodontic treatment. If you would like to schedule this initial visit with Dr. Kristin Knierim or Dr. Eric Hatten, call us today at (865) 522-7572.

When you visit our office for the first time, we will perform an initial oral examination to evaluate your smile. During this exam, we will discuss your goals with you and determine your eligibility for our various treatments to help you find the right solution for your individual needs. Our team is pleased to offer several services that are accommodating to a variety of needs and desires; we want to help you reach your goals with the treatment that is optimal for your smile.

We invite you to call our friendly team today to learn more about our orthodontic services and schedule your initial oral examination. We are eager to give you a reason to smile!
