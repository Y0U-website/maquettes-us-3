our mission and Approach

PREVENTIVE, CONSERVATIVE APPROACH

Here at Limestone Pediatric Dentistry, we approach our care with a preventive, conservative lens to help little ones maintain their beautiful and strong natural smiles. That’s why it’s our mission to create happy dental experiences that encourage kids to seek preventive dental care as they grow and maintain optimal oral health for the rest of their lives. We do this by taking the time to educate our patients and their parents on how to best protect and preserve their teeth, only recommending the most minimally invasive treatment options whenever we can.

GETTING THE WHOLE FAMILY INVOLVED

We love what we do because family is what we value most. We truly believe we’re better together, so we keep you and your loved ones in mind in every step of the way. From moms and dads to our young patients and their siblings, Dr. Lindsey and the team invite the whole family to be a part of the conversation, ask questions, and engage in fun-filled learning at our dental home in Athens.

CUSTOMIZED CARE

Thoughtful details are our specialty because no two children are exactly alike! We customize our care by truly getting to know each child we treat, learning what makes them light up and feel at ease, and tailoring appointments to their unique health history, developmental stage, and individual habits. Each family will also get their own treatment room to provide a safe, private, and comfortable visit from start to finish!
