Patient Info

The following visit, before you begin treatment, you will have a thorough and personalized discussion with the doctor in her office. She will explain all of your treatment options, timelines, allow you to ask questions, and engage in thoughtful discussion with you and your child about what the orthodontic journey will look and feel like. The office manager will then discuss all financial details with you, so that before you begin, you have a very clear understanding of costs and payment options.

At Sipkin Orthodontics, we prioritize our patients’ satisfaction and ensure they are our partners during their treatment by taking this extra time to discuss all of their orthodontic needs before they get started. This leads to a smooth partnership between the doctor and patient where everyone is relaxed and smiling!

Investing in Your Smile with Insurance Coverage

At Sipkin Orthodontics, we are dedicated to maximizing your orthodontic insurance benefits to help reduce your out-of-pocket costs. Our team will work with you to understand your policy and determine the best course of action. Many policies offer a lifetime orthodontic benefit separate from regular dental insurance. To ensure a seamless process, we will file your insurance claims on your behalf. We believe that orthodontic insurance is a benefit for you or your child, and we are here to help you take advantage of your coverage.
