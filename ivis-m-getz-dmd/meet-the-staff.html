Meet the Staff

Debra Bochiccio , RDH, a registered dental hygienist, has more than 20 years of experience in pediatric dental hygiene. At every visit Debra gives both parent and child detailed oral hygiene instructions, teaching the best brushing technique to maintain optimum dental health. Debra has two sons and enjoys spending time with her family, reading, and taking long walks on her days off.

Tamisha Abrahams , a dental assistant, has nearly 10 years experience in dentistry. She joined our practice in 2010. Tamisha loves working with children and has a knack for helping them to be at ease. She makes sure that each child’s visit is as pleasant as possible. In her free time Tamisha enjoys spending time with family and friends, especially her young daughter.

Matt Getz works full time as a Lieutenant in the New York City Fire Department, In addition, he manages many of the business aspects of our office. He strives to keep our patients and their parents well-informed. In his spare time Matt enjoys working on home improvement projects and spending time with his family.

Maria DeMartis , RDH, is a graduate of Fairleigh Dickinson University. She received her Bachelor’s Degree in Applied Science in 1986. She has been practicing preventive and therapeutic hygiene care for over 20 years. Maria is a member of the ADHA and is certified in CPR. She continues her education through online classes. She enjoys working closely with her patients to ensure their optimum health.

Rosie Scavello , RDH, obtained a Bachelor of Science Degree in Dental Hygiene at New York University. She is also a member of the American Dental Hygiene Association. Rosie has been married to her husband Rob, for eight years and has three beautiful children Joey, Gianna, & Vincent. She loves spending time with her family.

Trayce Torres , RDH, graduated from Hostos Community College in 2000 with a degree in dental hygiene. She is currently licensed in the state of New York and Connecticut. She has been in the dental field for many years, starting as a dental assistant with experience in assisting periodontists, orthodontists, endodontists, oral surgeons, and pedodontists. She also has experience with working with special needs children and general sedation. Trayce is also active in continuing education classes.
