The practice

As a conservative orthodontist, Dr. Harper recommends early children’s orthodontics when a child’s bite problems interfere with their tooth and jaw development. He never pushes patients into treatment and always takes their lifestyle, concerns, and budget into consideration when recommending orthodontics.

Comfort is a priority at Harper Orthodontics. The practice is home to Spectrum Diode lasers, which minimize the need for needles and scalpels. Dr. Harper uses these lasers to perform a wide range of treatments with only topical anesthetics. He can extract baby and adult teeth, perform soft tissue procedures, and other treatments with precise laser technology.

At Harper Orthodontics in Walnut Creek and Martinez, California, board-certified orthodontist Stephen Harper, DDS, offers complimentary orthodontic consultations. Dr. Harper makes sure to give you all the information you need to move forward with orthodontic treatment at your consultation. To learn…

An orthodontic consultation is an initial meeting between you and Dr. Harper to discuss a treatment plan. During the visit, Dr. Harper performs a comprehensive orthodontic exam that includes a visual assessment, bite evaluation, and digital X-rays to view your mouth’s internal structures.

Dr. Harper is proud to offer complimentary orthodontic consultations at no charge. Once he determines the appropriate type of orthodontics you need, Dr. Harper can answer any questions about the cost or length of treatment.

When you arrive for your orthodontic consultation, Dr. Harper reviews your dental records. He may also ask questions regarding your oral health, diet, and other lifestyle factors that affect your tooth health.

If the consultation is for your child, Dr. Harper asks about their tooth development, speech patterns, or chewing abilities.
