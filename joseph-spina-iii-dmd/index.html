The practice

Routine dental cleanings are the least expensive, least painful, and fastest way to maintain great oral health. It also allows Dr. Spina to find potential issues early.

Cosmetic dentistry offers advanced solutions for a range of dental problems. Tooth bleaching , porcelain veneers , white fillings and porcelain crowns can all be used to give you the smile of your dreams.

A dental crown or “cap” is a covering that fits over a damaged, decayed or unattractive tooth. Crowns are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth and make for a wonderful long-term restoration.
