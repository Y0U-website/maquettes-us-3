A straighter, healthier smile starts with CandidPro.

Want to share your most confident smile with the world? CandidPro is here to help.

Unlike many clear aligner options, CandidPro offers a connected treatment experience that doesn’t require in-office appointments to check your progress.

Instead, our CandidPro patients use their smartphones to conveniently share progress photos with us remotely.

Best of all, straightening your teeth with CandidPro means you’re protecting your long-term oral health, so you can smile with confidence for years to come.
