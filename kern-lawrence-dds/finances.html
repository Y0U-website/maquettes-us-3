Insurance and Finances

Our office accepts a variety of insurance plans. Because policies vary, we will contact your insurance company to ensure that you can use your benefits here. The complexities of insurance contracts make it impossible for us to guarantee coverage, but we will strive to give our best estimate of what your out of pocket expenses will be.

We participate in a number of dental networks, but if you are covered by a PPO (preferred provider organization) plan, it is likely that your coverage will extend to this office even if we are not in the plan’s network. We will be happy to verify the extent of your coverage prior to your first appointment.

If you have no insurance coverage, you may wish to consider one of our in-house dental plans . Our Premier Plan includes routine preventive care and discounts our other fees by up to 25 %. Our Basic Plan discounts all of our fees by up to 20 %.

Any fees owed directly by you can be paid in cash or by check, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and/or CareCredit.
