Meet Our Pediatric Dentists and Orthodontists in McKinney, TX

The heart of care is the people who lead it. Mindful’s pediatric dentists and orthodontists lead with clinical skill, steady communication, and a shared approach to helping patients grow into healthy smiles.

Care Led With Skill and Heart

Mindful brings pediatric dentistry and orthodontics together under one roof, with care led by doctors who understand both the clinical details and the family experience. Children, teens, and parents receive thoughtful guidance from specialists who know how to explain care clearly, respect each patient’s needs, and make every step feel easier to understand.

Dr. Noha Abdel-Salam

Dr. Noha leads pediatric dentistry at Mindful with a focus on infants, children, adolescents, and patients with special healthcare needs. Her work centers on helping families understand their child’s oral health early, with care that respects each child’s age, comfort level, and stage of development.

Dr. Noha earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (2008) and Master’s degree in Pediatric Dentistry (2011) from Loma Linda University in California. Her training in pediatric dentistry is supported by advanced experience across both U.S. and international dental systems, which gives her a broad foundation in children’s oral health care.

Dr. Noha earned her Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry , the highest level of certification in her field, in 2012. Her clinical background includes working with children who have special healthcare needs. She has practiced in private practice, community clinics, hospital operating rooms, and academic settings. She has served as a Clinical Assistant Professor at NYU Langone Health and Texas A&M University.

Dr. Noha combines evidence-based pediatric dentistry with a strong focus on prevention, early intervention, and parent education. As a certified yoga teacher and social-emotional learning facilitator, she brings an added layer of emotional awareness into pediatric visits, which helps children feel supported while families receive care they can understand and trust.

Dr. Noha is deeply committed to community service and mentoring the next generation both inside and outside of dentistry. She has participated in numerous volunteer outreach initiatives throughout her career, including Give Kids A Smile (GKAS) events, children’s health fairs, and international mission work in Mexico providing dental care to underserved populations.

In addition to her dental volunteer work, Dr. Noha is actively involved in youth leadership and community engagement as a leader of a local Girl Scout troop, where she enjoys mentoring young girls, encouraging confidence, leadership, service, and healthy habits.

Her passion for service and education reflects her dedication to supporting the overall well-being of children and families both within the dental office and throughout the community.

She also has a special place in her heart for the Type 1 diabetes community and volunteers with several T1D organizations like Breakthrough T1D and Type one together.

Dr. Mohamed Karkuri

Dr. Mohamed Karkuri leads orthodontic care at Mindful for children, teens, and adults. As a board-certified orthodontist, he helps families understand how the teeth, bite, and jaws work together, with treatment guidance that feels clear from the beginning.

Dr. Karkuri completed his orthodontic specialty training at New York University. His training gave him advanced expertise in dental and facial growth, bite alignment, and smile aesthetics, which supports his work with patients at different stages of orthodontic care.

Dr. Karkuri is a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics , a distinction he earned in 2015. He has more than 10 years of experience serving families across the DFW Metroplex and has treated patients with traditional braces, self-ligating braces, and Invisalign.

Dr. Karkuri believes orthodontic care should feel clear, personal, and well guided. His treatment plans are shaped around each patient’s needs, while his communication helps families understand what is being recommended and why. As a father, he also understands the reassurance families often need before and during treatment.
