Protect Your Foundation

Gum disease is one of the most common dental conditions, and it is also one of the most manageable when caught early. We treat gum disease and provide ongoing maintenance for patients who need more than a standard cleaning.

  • Periodontal (gum disease) evaluation
  • Scaling and root planing
  • Periodontal maintenance cleanings (every 3 months)

Extractions & More

Some situations require a tooth to come out. We perform routine and surgical extractions in our office.

  • Simple (non-surgical) extractions
  • Surgical extractions
  • Alveoloplasty (jaw bone reshaping after extraction)

Care from Infancy Up

  • Infant and toddler dental visits (ages 0–3)
  • Children’s cleanings and exams
  • Fluoride and preventive treatments for kids
  • Children with special healthcare needs
