The practice

We welcome you to a dental assisting school where passion meets profession. Located in League City, TX, GLG Dental Assisting School offers a program for students ready to take their next step. From the moment you enroll to the moment you land your first job, we’re here to help you rise.

Our dental career training school isn’t just a classroom—it’s a launchpad. Join a community that cares about your goals and delivers the support and experience you need to succeed.

Choosing the right dental assisting school can change everything. At GLG Dental Assisting School, we give students the power to pursue a meaningful, rewarding future in the dental field. We aim to turn your ambition into action—with tools, support, and training that truly make a difference.

Our dental assistant training school offers a streamlined, focused path into the field. Classes are held every Saturday from 8 AM to 5 PM for 12 weeks (excluding holidays)—a schedule designed for students with weekday commitments.

Through our dental certificate program, you’ll learn chairside skills, office procedures, and exam readiness in an environment that encourages learning by doing.
