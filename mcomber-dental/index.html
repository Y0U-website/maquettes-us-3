The practice

Dr. Nathalie Zenian McOmber graduated with honors from the University of Maryland College of Dental Surgery, Baltimore.

There she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and was selected for academic and clinical excellence to be a member of Gamma Pi Delta Prosthodontic Honor Society and the University of Maryland Odontological Honorary Society. As an undergraduate, she received a Bachelor of Science degree in Neurobiology and Physiology from the University of Maryland, College Park.

Dr. McOmber completed her post-graduate studies at Monmouth Medical Center, Long Branch, New Jersey. At the conclusion of this program, she was the recipient of the Betty Weinstein Memorial Award for Excellence in Research.

She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, American Association of Implant Dentistry, New Jersey Dental Association, and Monmouth-Ocean County Dental Society. Dr. McOmber enjoys all aspects of dentistry, with a particular interest in cosmetic dentistry and pediatric dentistry. She resides in Red Bank with her husband Armen McOmber, an attorney practicing in Red Bank and her two children.

Dr. Gluck, a Board Certified Periodontist, attended New Jersey Dental School where he spent seven years of study, earning his degree in Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD), obtaining his certificate as a specialist in the field of Periodontics, and receiving a Masters in Dental Science.

Dr. Gluck currently works in private practice but is also a part-time clinical assistant professor at UMDNJ – New Jersey Dental School in the department of periodontics. He has authored articles that discuss implant dentistry and the effects of diabetes on periodontal disease.

During Dr. Gluck’s training in periodontics, he learned traditional periodontal therapy as well as implant surgery and its associated procedures. He had the opportunity to train on five top implant systems and discover the strengths and weaknesses of a variety of implant manufacturers.
