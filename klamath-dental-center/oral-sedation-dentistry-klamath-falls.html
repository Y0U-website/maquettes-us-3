Many patients choose oral sedation combined with nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for an even more comfortable, calming experience. This combination ensures you feel at ease while maintaining safety and cooperation throughout your procedure.

  • Allows patients with dental phobia to receive essential care
  • Helps individuals with a sensitive gag reflex tolerate treatment
  • Easy administration with no need for IVs

Who Can Benefit from Sedation Dentistry?

Our team at Klamath Dental carefully evaluates your medical history and treatment needs to determine the safest and most effective sedation option for you.

  • Patients with severe dental anxiety or phobia
  • Individuals requiring extensive dental work
  • Patients with sensitive teeth or a strong gag reflex
  • Children or adults who struggle with routine dental care
