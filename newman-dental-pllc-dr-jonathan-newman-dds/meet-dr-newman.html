Meet Dr. Newman

Owner and Clinical Director, Newman Dental PLLC

Jonathan Newman, DDS

Dr. Jonathan Newman is known for his meticulous attention to detail, advanced restorative expertise, and warm, patient-centered approach. A graduate of the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, Dr. Newman has dedicated his career to combining artistry and precision in every aspect of care. Since acquiring Newman Dental in 2021, he has transformed it into a modern, technology-driven practice that still feels deeply personal.

With a special focus on esthetic and complex restorative dentistry, including crowns, onlays, veneers, and full-mouth rehabilitation, Dr. Newman integrates digital workflows, occlusal analysis, and advanced materials to deliver outcomes that are both functional and beautiful.

Dr. Newman has pursued advanced training from some of dentistry’s most respected clinicians, including Drs. Gordon Christensen, Bud Mopper, John Nosti, and Michael Apa. This mentorship and hands-on education have shaped his clinical philosophy, blending evidence-based techniques with esthetic artistry to provide truly world-class dentistry in a welcoming environment.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Newman’s greatest joy comes from time spent with his wife and children, who inspire his balance and perspective every day. Deeply connected to his synagogue and community, he values faith, family, and giving back as cornerstones of a meaningful life. He also enjoys staying active through tennis and pickleball and is committed to continual personal growth, always learning, improving, and striving to bring his best to every area of life.
