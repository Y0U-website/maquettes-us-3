Dentist Compensation FAQs

What is the compensation model for Heartland Dental supported doctors?

What makes the compensation model at a Heartland Dental supported practice different?

Heartland Dental’s compensation model is proven, fully transparent, and backed by a 29-year legacy of stability. According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, as well as Heartland Dental supported practice payroll data, supported doctors take home more than the top 10 % of dentists in the U.S. The average supported doctor earns 32 % of collections, while the top quartile earns 36 % or more. And with additional profit sharing, hygiene bonuses, and equity growth, many doctors learn that their take-home pay significantly exceeds what they could achieve in private practice.

How quickly will my dental compensation grow within Heartland Dental’s wealth-creation model?

Growth is tied to each supported doctor’s individual goals for themselves and their practice. As a result of the proven systems and support available, supported doctors on average see a 23 % growth in their compensation after the first year and a 60 % growth after five years.

How do I earn beyond my guaranteed salary?

When collections exceed four times the base salary, supported doctors can be paid 25 % of personal collections. On average, supported doctors make 32 % of collections, and the top quarter earn 36 % or more. Supported doctors can participate in quarterly practice profit sharing, in which the earning potential is always uncapped based on personal collections. Additionally, supported doctors are able to buy stock and become eligible for a hygiene bonus to further grow long-term wealth.

How does practice performance affect my dental compensation and progress in the wealth creation model?

Heartland Dental’s supported practice compensation model helps empower you to grow your practice and your revenue. Supported doctors benefit directly from 20 %-40 % average savings on supplies and labs, achieve higher reimbursement rates through Heartland Dental’s best-in-class payor negotiations team, and participate in quarterly profit sharing tied to practice growth.

What makes Heartland Dental’s stock ownership lucrative for doctors?

Supported doctors can participate in the Heartland Dental stock ownership program, creating another revenue stream. From 2018 to 2025, Heartland Dental’s equity value grew 2.9x, turning a $250,000 investment into $725,000, nearly twice the stock market’s annual rate of return. We’ve also paid out six liquidity events over our company’s history. Stock participation also unlocks an additional monthly hygiene bonus, creating another stream of income.

What benefits come from a Heartland Dental supported practice’s compensation package for me and my team?

Heartland Dental supported practices provide excellent compensation and benefits packages designed to support both doctors and their teams. Comprehensive health benefits are available for team members, which help attract and retain top talent while giving your staff the support that they need outside of the office. In addition, supported doctors and team members gain access to the latest technology, materials, and leadership development opportunities, creating an environment where everyone can grow professionally while contributing to the success of the practice.
