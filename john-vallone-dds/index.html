Patients who are missing one or more teeth can look forward to great results with dental implants. This excellent tooth-replacement service can do what traditional crowns, bridges, partial dentures, and full dentures can t. Instead of just replacing what you re missing with a restorative piece, dental implants also replace the missing root underneath, mimicking nature s own design.

Why is this significant? Because of the vital role tooth roots play in oral health. Not only do tooth roots anchor teeth to the jaw, they also preserve the jaw by encouraging the growth of new bone tissue over time.
