Notes on Specific Plans at the Fayetteville Location

United Concordia Tricare — Active Duty Families and Military Retirees

The Tricare Dental Program is administered through United Concordia, and O2 Dental Group of Fayetteville accepts United Concordia Tricare. For active duty family members enrolled in the TDP, coverage typically includes preventive care, basic restorative work, and in many cases orthodontic benefits for enrolled dependents. For retired service members and their families, Tricare Retiree Dental Program benefits are structured differently and vary based on enrollment tier.

Military families near Fort Liberty often encounter the same frustration: a dental office says it accepts Tricare, but the actual coverage verification and claim filing process is handled inconsistently. Our Fayetteville team is accustomed to working with Tricare documentation, understands the difference between active duty and retiree dental program benefits, and will verify your specific plan details before your appointment rather than leaving you to sort it out after the fact. Call : (910) 484-5141 with your Tricare information and we will confirm coverage before you schedule.

GEHA / Connection Dental — Federal Civilians and DoD Contractors

Fayetteville has a substantial population of Department of Defense civilians, federal contractors, and employees of the various agencies operating in and around the Fort Liberty installation. GEHA/Connection Dental is one of the most commonly carried plans in this group, and it is accepted at our Fayetteville office. If your coverage comes through federal civilian employment or a DoD contractor position and you have been uncertain whether we take your plan, a quick verification call will settle it.

Aetna — Standard and Medicare

The Fayetteville location accepts both standard Aetna and Aetna Medicare dental plans. Standard Aetna is commonly carried through private employers and some military-adjacent organizations in the Cumberland County area. Aetna Medicare serves patients on Medicare Advantage plans with dental benefits. Our team will verify which Aetna product you carry and what it covers at this location.

BCBS — Multiple Plan Variations

Blue Cross Blue Shield dental coverage is accepted at our Fayetteville office. BCBS offers numerous plan types, and the benefits and contracted rates vary between them. State Health Plan, Blue Options, employer-sponsored BCBS plans, and BCBS Federal Employee Program plans all operate differently. We will confirm your specific BCBS variation during the benefits verification call so there are no assumptions going into your first visit.

Medicare Advantage Dental Plans

Original Medicare does not cover most dental procedures. If you are a military retiree or civilian retiree in the Fayetteville area with a Medicare Advantage plan that includes dental benefits, O2 Dental Group of Fayetteville accepts Aetna Medicare, Humana Medicare, and United Healthcare Medicare plans. Coverage levels and annual benefit amounts vary significantly between Medicare Advantage carriers, so our team will work through what your specific plan actually covers before any treatment is discussed.

How Our Fayetteville Team Handles Your Insurance

The insurance verification process at the Fayetteville office is built around the reality that many patients here carry plans with specific military, federal, or government employment origins that require more than a standard eligibility check. Our front office team does not just confirm that a plan is active — they work through the specific benefit categories, frequency limitations, and coverage percentages that apply to your plan so you have a realistic picture before you sit down in the chair.

Specifically, before your first appointment our team:

Insurance estimates reflect what we expect based on your verified benefits — final payment is always determined by your carrier after the claim is processed. Our goal is to make that outcome as predictable as possible before treatment begins.

  • Verifies your dental insurance benefits with your carrier — including Tricare, GEHA, and federal employee plans that require additional documentation or verification steps
  • Explains your annual maximum, deductible status, and any waiting periods in plain language
  • Coordinates primary and secondary insurance when your family has dual coverage, filing both claims so you do not have to manage the process yourself
  • Provides a written cost estimate before treatment is scheduled, showing what insurance is expected to cover and what your estimated responsibility is
  • Files out-of-network courtesy claims for patients whose specific plan variation falls outside our direct participation, helping you recover whatever reimbursement your plan allows

No Dental Insurance in Fayetteville? You Have Options.

Not everyone in the Fayetteville area has dental coverage — including some veterans and retired service members whose dental benefits did not carry over from active duty in the form they expected. For patients without insurance, two practical alternatives are available at our Fayetteville office.

The O2 Advantage Plan is an in-house membership program that gives uninsured patients discounted rates on preventive and restorative care with no deductibles, no waiting periods, no annual limits, and no pre-authorization requirements. Cosmetic dentistry is included. Enrollment is immediate and the discounted rates apply from your very first visit. Learn more about the O2 Advantage Plan.
