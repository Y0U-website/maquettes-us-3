Common Questions About Our Emergency Dental Care

  • What should I do if my child has a dental emergency? If your child is experiencing a dental emergency, it’s important to stay calm and contact a pediatric dentist immediately. At Next Level Pediatric Dentistry, we offer same-day emergency pediatric dental services to provide prompt care. Whether it’s a toothache or a dental injury, our skilled team is ready to help.
  • How do I care for a knocked-out baby tooth? If your child has a knocked-out baby tooth, it’s important not to panic. Baby teeth usually don’t need to be reinserted, but you should save the tooth in case the pediatric dentist needs it for examination. Gently rinse the tooth with warm water and keep it in a clean container until you can reach the dentist.
  • My child knocked out a permanent tooth. What should I do? In the case of a knocked-out permanent tooth, saving the tooth is crucial. Find the tooth and rinse it with cold water (do not scrub it) and try to place it back in the socket. If this isn’t possible, store the tooth in milk or a saline solution and call our office immediately for assistance. If it’s after hours, you may need to head to the emergency room or contact a dental professional who handles after-hours care.
  • Should I go to the emergency room for a pediatric dental emergency? In most cases, a visit to the pediatric dentist is recommended for dental emergencies. However, if your child is experiencing severe pain or has a serious injury (such as a broken jaw), calling 911 or heading to the emergency room may be necessary. After initial care, our pediatric dentist can guide you on the next steps for ongoing treatment.
  • Is it normal for baby teeth to cause dental emergencies? Yes, baby teeth can cause dental emergencies. Whether due to accidents, cavities, or other issues, it’s important to address any problems with baby teeth promptly to ensure your child’s overall oral health. Our pediatric dentist can help with emergency care and preventive measures to avoid future issues.
