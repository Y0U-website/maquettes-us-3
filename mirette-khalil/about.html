About Our Practice

Our mission here at OC Pearl Family Dental is to provide you and your family with the highest quality dental care in a comfortable setting. You will be treated professionally and courteously at all times with our state-of-the-art technologies. We are here to provide you with a caring touch and the healthy smile you deserve!

Why Choose Us?

Due to our convenient location in Berlin, MD, we are able to care for patients from the surrounding towns of the Eastern Shore. Our patients come to see us from Ocean City, Ocean Pines, Salisbury, Fenwick, Selbyville, Bethany Beach, Rehoboth Beach, Lewes, Millsboro, and others.

Under the skilled guidance of Dr. Mirette Khalil, our compassionate team provides high quality comprehensive dentistry in a state-of-the-art facility. We pride ourselves on continually learning the latest techniques and use only the highest-quality materials to ensure our patients have a dental experience unlike any other.

We believe that whatever your dental needs may be, you will be treated with the highest quality dentistry available and with the compassion, the respect, and the care you deserve.

Why Choose Us?

At our practice, we strive to treat our patients as members of our own family. We provide high quality, comprehensive dental care for adults and kids in an honest, compassionate and relaxing atmosphere. We utilize the latest advances in techniques, equipment and technology to ensure that you have access to state-of-the-art treatment.

Meet The Doctor

Dr. Mirette Khalil

Dr. Mirette Khalil was born and raised in Alexandra, Egypt, the beautiful port city located on the Mediterranean Sea. She studied at a French Catholic School, then obtained her high school diploma from Cambridge University, England. She finished her high school classes a year early and graduated top of her class. She then moved to the United States to join her extended family and pursue her undergraduate and graduate education.

When not caring for her patients, Dr. Mirette enjoys spending time with her family, and going to the beach. One of her most enjoyed hobbies is craftwork specifically knitting and crochet.

Dr. Mirette Khalil is looking forward to meeting you and providing the highest quality dental care and the healthy smile you deserve.
