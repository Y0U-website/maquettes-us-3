Tooth Extraction

Although permanent teeth were meant to last a lifetime, there is a multitude of reasons why a tooth may need to be extracted. This could be due to a tooth that is badly damaged (from trauma or decay), to be repaired. Or other reasons may include but are not limited to:

Sometimes dentists pull teeth due to orthodontia. The goal of orthodontia is to properly align the teeth, which may not be possible if your teeth are too big for your mouth. If a tooth cannot break through the gum (typically referred to as an erupt tooth) because there is no room in the mouth for it, your dentist or oral surgeon could recommend pulling it

If tooth decay or damage extends to the root -- the center of the tooth containing nerves and blood vessels -- bacteria can enter the root, resulting in the tooth becoming infected. Often this can be corrected through root canal therapy (RCT), but if the infection is so severe that antibiotics or a root canal are unable to cure it, extraction may be needed to prevent the spread of infection to other parts of the mouth, teeth or gums.

Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMJ)

Mid Penn Oral Suergery treats a full range of TMJ disorders. TMJ is a common disorder that can be alleviated through the proper diagnosis and treatment. Some of the symptoms of TMJ Include:

If you hear a clicking or a popping sound, that isn’t a sign of TMJ (unless the popping is accompanied by other symptoms, such as pain, along with the popping sound. If you suspect that you are experiencing symptoms of TMJ, we encourage you to schedule an appointment for further review, diagnosis and treatment. Contact us today!

  • Pain in one or both sides of your jaw
  • Earaches
  • Pain or discomfort when chewing
