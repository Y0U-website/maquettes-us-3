Dr. Mensah’s Journey in Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Mensah has worked in private practice in the Hudson Valley for over a decade, and education has become the cornerstone of her professional life. Currently serving as adjunct faculty at the St. Barnabas Hospital Pediatric Dentistry Residency Program, she appreciates the opportunity to inspire and educate future pediatric dentists.

Dr. Mensah is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Her commitment to staying at the forefront of her field ensures that her young patients receive the highest standard of care.

Dr. Mensah’s Journey Beyond Dentistry

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Mensah embraces many interests. Whether she’s exploring new destinations, playing tennis with her husband, or learning the Spanish language, she approaches each endeavor with the same enthusiasm she brings to pediatric dentistry. Yet, among her various pursuits, the role she cherishes most is being a full-time mother to her two young, energetic children.
