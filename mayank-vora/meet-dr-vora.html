Meet Dr Vora

DR. MAYANK D. VORA has practiced endodontics as a specialist since 2003 with over ten years of practice right here in Livingston County. He received his Master of Science degree in chemical engineering at Wayne State University in 1991. The dental treatment that Dr. Vora underwent during his seven years as an engineer kindled his interest in dentistry as a career. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan in 2001 and his specialty training in endodontics at the University of Illinois in 2003.

In his spare time Dr. Vora enjoys hiking, model-making, sketching and photography. This summer he climbed Cirque Peak (9,820 ft) in the Canadian Rockies and he has also hiked the Annapurna Circuit Trek in Nepal, a grueling 3-week hike that tops out at almost 18,000 ft. He is an avid gardener and enjoys creating bonsai. Two of his miniature trees took first and second place in the Intermediate level at the Four Seasons Bonsai Club’s Annual Show in Rochester Hills this August.

Dr. Vora with his wife Jyoti, his two children, Nupur and Abhay, and their Siberian Husky, Diesel.
