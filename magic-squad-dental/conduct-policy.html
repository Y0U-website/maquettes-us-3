Patient Code of Conduct Policy

  • Communicate in a calm and respectful manner during interactions with staff.
  • Follow all practice policies, procedures, and guidelines, including scheduling and appointment-related policies.
  • Provide accurate and timely information as needed for your care and financial arrangements.

Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated under any circumstances:

  • Verbal Abuse : Use of offensive, abusive, or insulting language toward staff, providers, or other patients.
  • Threats : Making any verbal or physical threats toward staff, providers, or other patients.
  • Profanity : Use of vulgar, obscene, or inappropriate language directed at staff or others.
  • Harassment : Behavior that is intimidating, demeaning, or inappropriate, including excessive aggression or hostility.
  • Disruptive Behavior : Actions that disrupt the normal operations of the practice or compromise the safety of staff or other patients.

Diagnostic Imaging (X-Rays)

We use digital imaging, child-sized settings, thyroid collars when appropriate, and follow ALARA/ALADA. Imaging is individualized to your child’s age, risk, and clinical needs; we obtain only what’s necessary for safe, accurate care. Clinical imaging decisions are made by the pediatric dentist.

  • The dentist may recommend posterior molar bitewings and either a panoramic or selected periapicals to evaluate developing teeth/jaws (eruption, missing/extra teeth, asymmetry, pathology), trauma, or orthodontic planning. If not indicated, we won’t take them.
  • Bitewings every 6–12 months based on caries risk, findings, and whether contacts can be visualized.
  • We accept recent diagnostic images from other providers to avoid unnecessary exposure; if they don’t answer today’s concerns, additional imaging may be recommended.
  • We explain reason, benefits/risks, and alternatives. With consent, we obtain the minimum imaging needed.
  • If indicated imaging is declined, the dentist may limit or defer care to uphold the minimum standard of care for all patients. Persistent refusal may mean our practice isn’t the right fit ; we’ll assist with records transfer upon written request.
  • We record the clinical rationale, caries-risk status, type/date of images, any outside films, and consent/refusal.

Grounds for Dismissal

Violation of the above expectations may result in immediate dismissal from the practice . Patients who engage in repeated or severe violations, such as verbal abuse, threats, or profanity toward staff, will no longer be able to receive care at Magic Squad Dental.

If dismissed, it is the patient’s responsibility to arrange for alternative dental care. Patients with insurance are encouraged to contact their insurance provider for assistance in locating other in-network providers.

Reporting Issues

If you experience a problem or have concerns regarding your care, we encourage you to communicate with our office staff or management in a respectful manner. We are committed to resolving any issues while maintaining mutual respect and professionalism.

Acknowledgment

By continuing as a patient of Magic Squad Dental, you acknowledge and agree to abide by this Patient Code of Conduct Policy . Your cooperation ensures a safe and respectful environment for all.
