What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is actually a small titanium post, and it’s placed directly into the jawbone where a tooth used to reside. This replicates the root structure, and it provides support for a new crown, bridge, or denture on top. Thanks to this two-part structure, dental implants offer a host of benefits that you just can’t get with other treatments.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Your healing period will be essential due to the need for osseointegration to occur the process in which your implants fuse to your jawbone. This is what will allow your new teeth to feel incredibly stable and last for decades to come with proper care. This should take anywhere from three to six months to complete. After your implants have fused properly, you’ll return to our team to receive your abutments, or small metal connector pieces that link your permanent restorations to your implants. You’ll then go back home so that your gums can heal.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Security: Dental implants are held within the jawbone just like natural teeth, so whenever you speak or take a bite of food, you never have to worry about them moving out of place.
  • Lifelike Appearance: Unlike regular bridges and dentures, which often have metal or acrylic attachments, implants are entirely self-supporting and topped with all-porcelain restorations, giving them a realistic look that blends into any smile.
  • Bone Loss Protection: Implant roots stimulate the jawbone and restore blood flow to the area after tooth loss, preventing bone loss that could lead to more missing teeth.
  • Extremely Durable: While traditional bridges and dentures typically last for about 5-10 years before needing to be replaced, implants sport an impressive 30+ year lifespan.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term investment in your smile and like natural teeth, they need consistent care to stay healthy. At Lacquaniti Dental , Dr. Lacquaniti and Dr. Krueger guide patients through every stage of dental implant care. With the right habits and support, your new restorations can last a lifetime. Read more below to learn how to keep your dental implants strong, clean, and looking great now and forever.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Keeping your mouth clean is the most important part of dental implant care. Brushing twice a day and flossing each night before bed helps remove plaque and prevent gum disease, which can put your implants at risk.

Use a soft-bristle toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to avoid scratching the surface of your implants. If you’re unsure about your brushing or flossing technique, we’ll be happy to show you the way and recommend tools that make oral hygiene easier and more effective.

Eat a Healthy Diet

The food you eat matters, not just for your body, but also for maintaining your implants. Nutrient-rich foods like leafy greens, lean proteins, and dairy products support bone health and help your gums stay strong.

At the same time, try to limit sugary snacks and acidic drinks, which can irritate your gums or increase the risk of infection. If you’re unsure about the best foods to support your dental implants in Mt. Dora, Dr. Lacquaniti and Dr. Krueger are happy to offer guidance.

Break Bad Habits

Certain habits can damage your dental implants over time. Avoid chewing on ice, pens, or fingernails, which can crack your restorations.

Smoking and vaping are also especially harmful. The chemicals contained within them slow down the healing process and can increase the risk of implant failure.

Reducing and eventually breaking these habits will have a net positive effect on your smile and keep your implants safe. If you need help, talk to our Mt. Dora dental team. We can offer advice and guide you to resources that can really make a difference.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Just like natural teeth, implants need protection from accidental damage. If you play sports or participate in other physical activities where falling or contact is possible, a mouthguard can prevent chips, cracks, or wear.

If you grind your teeth at night, we also offer custom-fitted nightguards to protect your implants while you sleep. Taking these precautions gives your new restorations the best protection possible, so they can stay secure and functional for decades.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Routine checkups are essential to long-term dental implant care in Mt. Dora. Our team recommends visiting every six months so we can monitor your implants, clean hard-to-reach areas, and catch any issues before they get worse.

These visits also allow us to review your hygiene routine and make adjustments as needed. Keeping up with appointments helps ensure your smile stays clean, healthy, and cared for.

Because dental implants are so similar to your natural teeth, you can care for them in practically the exact same way.
