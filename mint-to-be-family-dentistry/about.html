Mint To Be Family Dentistry Our Philosophy

With our emphasis on infection control, we know we will never be perfectly green, but we feel we can do our part to promote a sustainable and healthy future for all.

  • Re-usable and sterilizable dental products where appropriate
  • Digital x-rays and intra-oral scanners eliminating the need for goopy impressions, when possible
  • Recycle all non-medical paper and plastic in the office
  • Partnered with a national recycling brand to encourage patient’s to recycle any brand of oral care waste such as toothpaste tubes & caps, toothbrushes, and associated packaging

Infection Control and COVID-19 Protocols

At Mint To Be we work to ensure our office is as safe as possible for all patients and team members. As a result, we continue to observe safety standards and recommended guidelines set by The American Dental Association, CDC, and local and state health departments. As this is a healthcare facility, we ask all patient’s to wear masks when not in the dental chair.

  • If you are experiencing any illness, or have been in contact with someone who has, we will be happy to assist in rescheduling your appointment.
  • We do not accept walk-in patients. Please call ahead so we may plan for your visit.
  • All patient’s are asked to rinse their mouths with an anti-microbial mouth rinse prior to dental procedures.
  • Private exam rooms to limit patient exposures and aerosols.
  • We use disposable PPE including high level rated face masks, gowns, faceshields, and gloves. Eye protection is used for both patients and providers.
  • All instruments and operatories are disinfected and thoroughly cleaned in between patients.
  • Social distancing measures to keep the waiting area clear to minimize contact among patients and staff.
