When to Contact a Professional Dentist

Not sure if it’s time to see a dentist? Here are some signs you shouldn’t ignore:

Don’t wait until it’s too late. Contact Hewlett Family Dental for your dental care needs.

  • Persistent tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Swollen or bleeding gums
  • Difficulty chewing or swallowing
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Bad breath that won’t go away
  • Jaw pain or stiffness
