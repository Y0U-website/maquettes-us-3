Dr. Jeffrey Brenner, DMD

Biography Over Three Decades of Beautiful, Healthy Smiles For over 30 years, Dr. Jeffrey Brenner has dedicated himself to the art and science of dentistry. After graduating from Temple University School of Dentistry in 1992, Dr. Brenner opened his clinical doors with a clear focus: to provide premium, long-term, and comprehensive dental health services in a setting that feels warm and supportive.

In 1995, he established Brenner Dental Group in Southampton, PA. Since then, he has served multiple generations of local families, performing thousands of treatments spanning preventive care, teeth restorations, dental implant placements, and customized cosmetic smile improvements.

Dr. Brenner’s approach to dental care is built on a foundation of conservative dentistry. He believes in preserving as much of your natural tooth structure as possible, only recommending treatments that are genuinely necessary for your long-term health and comfort. This philosophy has earned him the trust and loyalty of patients throughout Southampton and the surrounding Bucks County communities.

To remain at the forefront of his field, Dr. Brenner regularly participates in advanced continuing education. He maintains active memberships in the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Pennsylvania Dental Association (PDA), collaborating with peers to integrate the safest, most effective dental techniques into his daily practice.
