Meet the team

Meet Hope Mooney

Hope’s people skills make people smile as soon as they enter the office. She’s in charge of scheduling appointments, verifying insurance, and ensuring the office runs smoothly.

A huge animal lover, Hope also enjoys reading, the beach, and spending time with her friends and family. She resides in Essex County with her husband, son, and daughter.

Meet Jennifer Psota

Jennifer has been in the dental field for 35 years! She started with her dad, Dr. Anthony M Pallotta, and her mom who was the practice office manager. She learned the administrative aspect from her mom and assisted her dad for a few years before starting her own family.

Jennifer has 5 accomplished children, all of whom she’s very proud. She enjoys cooking, hiking, biking, skiing, snowboarding, gardening, and living healthy. She enjoys singing in a band and loves people.

“Taking care of patients makes my day complete. I’m so excited to start with Dr. Givner’s practice because we are going to be an awesome team! We are here to answer questions and take care of you!” said Jennifer.

Meet Tonya Salley

A dental assistant for over 25 years, Tonya Salley loves what she does! Tonya loves to treat patients with the best possible care. Tonya emphasizes the importance of having a good work ethic, which she espouses; and takes great pride in being responsible, efficient, organized and kind. In doing so, she receives the gift of seeing people feeling happy with the care they receive from Givner Family Dental care. For Tonya, satisfaction comes when she knows she made someone’s dental visit a more comfortable and healthy experience.

Tonya has been working with Dr. Givner since 2019, and the two of them are amazing together! Multiple friends, colleagues, neighbors, who have received care from Dr. Givner tell Tonya “you two are a great team!”

“It is important to me that every patient is treated like a family member and anyone who really knows me knows how much I love my family. I have a large close family. I am the youngest of 5 children,” said Tonya.

Tonya lives with her husband Roy and daughter, a dog and a fish. She loves spending time with all of them.
