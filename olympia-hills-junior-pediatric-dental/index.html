The practice

Call us today to schedule a checkup, and feel free to visit us for a tour of our friendly facility before your child’s first appointment.

Olympia Hills Junior Pediatric Dental provides laser dentistry on most restorative services. Why laser dentistry? Because it allows our team of dental professionals to treat your child without needles, drilling, or numbing! Call our office today or click here to learn more on why laser dentistry is the Gold Standard of pediatric care.

We believe in nurturing your child’s smile right from the start. Regular oral exams help ensure their whole mouth teeth, gums, and everything in between is healthy and developing properly. Our exams are interactive, helping your child learn about their teeth in a fun way while giving you peace of mind that their smiles are staying healthy.

We specialize in gently treating lip and tongue-ties, a condition where the lip or tongue is tightly attached to the mouth floor or gums. This affects basic activities like speaking or eating. Our use of a dental laser to release the tethered soft tissue for tongue and lip ties can dramatically improve children’s lives. At Olympia Hills Junior Pediatric Dental, your child receives personalized care during every visit. We believe in treating every child as if they were our own, and we always involve the parents and children in the decision-making process.

Our philosophy is that preventative and simple solutions should be considered first and your children should always leave our office with a smile. We will spend time reviewing your child’s health history and talk you through the process before any treatment to ensure your child is comfortable at all times.
