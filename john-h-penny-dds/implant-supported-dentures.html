Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

  • Good general health – You should be in good enough overall health that you’re able to undergo a minor surgical procedure.
  • Great oral health – Gum disease, tooth decay, and other oral health issues could compromise the success of your new teeth, so they must be addressed beforehand.
  • A strong jawbone – Without enough jawbone density, the implant posts won’t have a sturdy foundation to merge with. If you’ve suffered from jawbone loss, we can recommend a bone graft, which adds bone material to the area to strengthen it.

Types of Dental Implants

  • Single Tooth – A dental crown will be created and placed on top of a single dental implant that will be surgically placed into your jawbone to replicate the original tooth structure.
  • Multiple Teeth – We will place what is known as a fixed bridge on top of two dental implants, which will be inserted into the outer sockets to create a stable foundation for your restoration.
  • All Teeth – The most expensive dental implant is an implant denture. This custom creation looks just like a traditional denture, but instead of sitting on your gums and using your natural suction to remain in place, it will be secured onto an average of four to six implants.

Implant-Supported Dentures are a Long-Term Solution

Conventional dentures usually require constant adjustment depending on the changes brought about in your jaw. On the other hand, implant-supported dentures rarely need any adjustment because they prevent changes in the jaw brought about by tooth loss. Further, many people who wear conventional dentures are usually embarrassed to do a lot of things in public because their teeth might either slip or they might get lost. This is something that you don’t have to worry about with implant-supported dentures. It is the perfect solution to your tooth loss problem as it gives you little to no hassle.
