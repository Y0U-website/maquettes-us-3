How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

Treatment time with braces varies by case, but most patients are in treatment for roughly 12 to 24 months. Factors like bite complexity, crowding, and how well appointments and oral hygiene routines are kept all play a role in your exact timeline.

Gwin Monitoring doesn’t replace the biology of tooth movement, but it does help keep your treatment on track between visits. Comfort, Care & Daily Life with Braces or Aligners

Most patients feel mild pressure or soreness for a few days after getting braces placed or starting a new Invisalign® aligner. This is simply a sign that your teeth are moving. It’s typically manageable with over-the-counter pain relief and fades quickly. Many patients find Invisalign noticeably more comfortable day-to-day than traditional braces.

With traditional braces, it’s best to steer clear of hard, sticky, or chewy foods, such as ice, popcorn, caramel, and hard candy, since they can damage brackets and wires. Invisalign® patients don’t face this restriction at all, since aligners are removed before eating.

With braces, brushing after every meal and flossing daily (a floss threader or water flosser helps) keeps food and plaque from building up around brackets. With Invisalign®, oral hygiene is simpler: just remove your aligners and brush and floss normally, then rinse your aligners before putting them back in.

Gwin Monitoring is our digital remote progress-tracking tool. From home, you’ll send quick scans of your smile so our team can monitor your treatment between visits. It means fewer unnecessary appointments, more flexibility for your schedule, and care that fits into your life instead of disrupting it.

Once active treatment ends, you’ll transition to a retainer to protect your results. Teeth can naturally shift over time, so consistent retainer wear, especially in the months right after treatment, is essential to keeping the smile you worked for.
