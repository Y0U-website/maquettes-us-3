Dr. Chen

Dr. Ziwei Chen completed her undergraduate studies at the Johns Hopkins University, and earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Harvard School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Chen completed her pediatric residency at Children’s National Hospital in Washington DC.

With her extensive hospital dentistry experience and medical knowledge, Dr. Chen aims to provide the highest standards of pediatric dental care for all children, especially those with special healthcare needs. Dr. Chen is also passionate about research and innovation. She is a published author in dental journals, and received the “Resident Recognition Award” by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Chen enjoys baking cakes for special occasions, learning random facts, and spending time with her family.
