The practice

A tooth may need to be extracted due to fractures, infections, gum disease, or large cavities.

Once a cavity destroys natural tooth structure, a filling or crown may be needed to restore function and esthetics.

With enough bone, implants are the best option to replace missing teeth.

Full or partial dentures and bridges are other options to replace missing teeth.

There are many reasons teeth hurt. Testing and diagnosis is the first step to stopping pain.
