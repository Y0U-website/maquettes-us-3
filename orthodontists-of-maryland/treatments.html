All Ages

Starting orthodontic care early can set the stage for a lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that all children should have their first orthodontic exam by age 7. Our early treatment options are designed to catch and correct developing issues and guide your child’s dental growth, potentially reducing the need for more extensive treatments later on. Let’s work together to give your child the best start for their dental journey!

Teen years are a great time to perfect that smile, and we’re here to make the process smooth and enjoyable. We understand the importance of a great smile during these formative years, so we’re dedicated to delivering results that will make your teen shine.

Braces

Braces are a classic and effective solution for achieving a beautifully aligned smile! Modern braces use sleek, low-profile brackets and advanced wires to gently and efficiently straighten your teeth. They offer reliable results for even the most complex orthodontic cases. With fun options like colored bands, traditional braces can also be a way to express a bit of personal style while on your journey to a perfect smile.

Clear braces are a subtle alternative for those who want the benefits of braces without the noticeable metal. These braces use tooth-colored ceramic that blends in with your natural teeth, making them less visible. They’re just as effective as traditional braces, offering the same precise control over tooth movement. Clear braces are a fantastic choice for anyone who wants a more discreet way to achieve a straight, confident smile.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners, such as Invisalign® and Angel Aligners™, offer a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth without the need for traditional braces. These custom-made, removable trays gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions, all while being virtually invisible. Ideal for those who prefer a less noticeable treatment option, clear aligners fit seamlessly into your lifestyle, allowing you to eat and brush with ease. Our team will guide you through every step, ensuring a smooth process and a stunning, confident smile.

Technology

Our iTero scanner creates precise, 3D digital impressions of your teeth and eliminates the need for messy, uncomfortable traditional molds. This innovative tool allows us to plan and visualize your treatment with remarkable accuracy and efficiency. With iTero, you can see a preview of your new smile before starting treatment, making the process both exciting and transparent.

At our digital practice, we use cutting-edge technology to enhance every aspect of your orthodontic care. From digital impressions to advanced treatment planning software, our digital tools streamline the process and ensure precise, efficient results. This modern approach not only makes your visits more comfortable but also allows us to create personalized treatment plans tailored to your unique needs.
