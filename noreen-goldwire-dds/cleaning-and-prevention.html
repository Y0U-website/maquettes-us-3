Dental Cleaning and Prevention

Protecting Your Smile

- Can help detect early signs of oral cancer

We want to assist you in achieving optimal oral health. While brushing your teeth twice a day and flossing once a day are foundational dental hygiene practices, there are additional tools and products that can enhance your at-home dental care. Depending on your unique needs, special types of toothpaste, rinses, and even flossing aids can contribute to a healthier smile. Noreen Goldwire DDS can help you find the best tools to improve your daily hygiene routine, making your professional cleaning appointments shorter and less stressful.

Diet also plays a significant role in your oral health. We can discuss how to optimize your diet for better oral health.

Noreen Goldwire DDS is committed to ensuring you have the best oral health possible. By keeping up with your regular professional exams and cleanings, dedicating yourself to effective at-home hygiene practices, and maintaining a healthy, balanced diet, you can prevent minor issues from escalating into major procedures.

Oral Cancer Screening

At each visit, our team does a medical history update and reviews any changes. Even the smallest change in the soft tissues of your mouth will be examined closely. Early detection of oral cancer saves many lives each year, and our team is highly trained to detect even the smallest abnormality.

Teeth Cleaning

Who doesn’t love the feeling of freshly polished teeth? Our compassionate registered dental hygienists do more than just polish your teeth. They remove plaque, tartar buildup, check your gums for signs of periodontal disease, and much more!

Home Care

Your oral health is dependent on your dedication to brushing and flossing at home. This keeps your teeth and gum strong and healthy between your cleaning appointments. Together we can create an individual plan with the latest tools and knowledge to help protect your smile at home.

Periodontal Therapy

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults. However, it doesn’t have to ruin your smile. A targeted, strategic cleaning schedule and a dedicated home care regimen can help keep this chronic condition under control.
