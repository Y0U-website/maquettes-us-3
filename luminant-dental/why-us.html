Our Difference

Dentistry You Can Trust

We are a dentist-owned private practice in Highland, Indiana, focused on providing personalized care and genuine hospitality. As a local dental practice, we aim to offer high-quality dental services with a focus on personal attention. Our doctor and team spend time with each patient to ensure all questions are answered and that treatment options and costs are clear. We believe our friendly environment and thorough approach to dental care will make you feel like part of our dental family from your very first visit.

Personalized Care You can rely on Dr. Hasan and his dedicated staff to treat you and your family as unique individuals and provide you with highly personalized care. We approach our work with the principles of honesty, integrity, respect, and compassion.

Comfortable Office Your comfort and the well-being of your family are our top priorities. We aim to provide high-quality dental care in a friendly and calming environment. We are here to address any additional needs you may have, ensuring that you feel completely at ease.

Same-day Treatments We offer same-day dentistry services to ensure you receive prompt and high-quality dental care exactly when you need it. Whether you have an urgent dental issue or a tight schedule that makes planning ahead difficult, our same-day dentistry is designed to provide you with timely and effective treatment.

Family Appointments We simplify family dental care by offering family block appointments. Instead of scheduling individual visits for everyone, you can bring your entire family in for checkups and cleanings during one convenient time slot. This approach saves you time and ensures comprehensive care for all ages.
