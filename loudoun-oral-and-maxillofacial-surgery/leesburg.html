Dental Implants in Leesburg, VA

Missing teeth affect your bite, your confidence, and the health of your jawbone. Dental implants are the most reliable, long-term solution. Our oral surgeons in Leesburg use the i-CAT 3D cone beam scanner to plan every implant case with precision. We place single-tooth implants, multi-tooth solutions, and implant-supported bridges that look and function like natural teeth.

For patients who need to replace an entire arch, All-on-4 dental implants (Teeth-in-a-Day) use four to six strategically placed implants to support a complete set of fixed teeth. This eliminates the need for removable dentures and restores full function in as few as one surgical visit. For patients interested in same day dental implants in Leesburg, VA, a consultation at our South King Street office will show you whether this approach is right for your situation.

Wisdom Teeth Removal in Leesburg, VA

Impacted or partially erupted wisdom teeth can lead to pain, infection, and damage to neighboring teeth. Our surgeons evaluate each case using 3D imaging to understand the exact position of your wisdom teeth before planning the safest extraction. Wisdom teeth removal is performed under IV sedation or general anesthesia so you can sleep through the procedure comfortably. Whether you need one wisdom tooth removed or all four, our team handles every level of complexity.

Bone Grafting in Leesburg, VA

After a tooth is lost or extracted, the jawbone beneath it gradually shrinks. Bone grafting rebuilds the volume and density needed to support a dental implant. Our surgeons perform socket preservation at the time of extraction, ridge augmentation, and sinus lifts using advanced grafting materials and Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) to promote faster healing. If you have been told you do not have enough bone for implants, bone grafting in Leesburg may make placement possible.
