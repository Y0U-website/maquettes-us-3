From fillings to caps to full mouth reconstruction, Dr. Crystal takes pride in helping patients achieve happy, healthy smiles. With a focus on comprehensive treatment, he is committed to building a practice that ensures clinical excellence and proper dental care for all patients and all diagnoses.

Main street dental provides attentive care for all ages.

Dr. Matt Crystal

Dr. Crystal studied Biochemistry at Lafayette College and continued right into dental school at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. Following dental school, Dr. Crystal was fortunate to be mentored under Dr. Roger Kriete for nearly 5 years before taking over the reins of his practice. He is excited to be heading up his own practice where he looks forward to seeing you soon.

Dr. Crystal and his family reside in Morristown, NJ. His wife, Emily, is a civil engineer for Turner Construction and a fantastic mother to their son August (Augie) and their daughter Pauline (Poppy.) He is an avid woodworker and jewelry maker, and enjoys both when he has spare time!

Dr. Leelah Jaberi

Dr. Jaberi is known for her clear, open communication style. She takes the time to talk through treatment options, answer questions, and make sure every patient fully understands their oral health and care plan. Her goal is to create a comfortable, informed experience where patients are confident making decisions about their dental care. She is passionate about patient education and believes informed patients are empowered patients.

Dr. Jaberi is a big fan of the Baltimore Ravens and enjoys spending time with her two, beautiful daughters, and her loving husband. Outside of the office, she enjoys playing the piano, cooking, hiking, and travelling. We look forward to having Dr. Jaberi as part of the Main Street Dental team. She will only add to the quality and compassionate care you are accustomed to. Please join us in welcoming her to our team!
