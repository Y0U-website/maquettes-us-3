Our Doctors

Dr. Asma Abdulrazzak, DDS Dr. Razzak graduated from the The University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2005. She practices all aspects of general dentistry including oral surgery, implants, and orthodontics. She is an active member of the International Association of Orthodontics. Dr. Razzak was born and raised in Genesee County and she still resides there with her family.

Dr. Razzak chose dentistry because a smile is the first thing people see when they see you. When you fix a patient’s smile, you make their life better. It helps people with their relationships, work, and everyday life. When you help people take care of their smile, you allow them to feel better about themselves and be more productive with their life. It’s a gateway for a healthier life. When we help patients have a healthy smile, it reflects on the rest of their body.

Dr. Noor Hammoud, DDS Helping nervous patients feel more comfortable is Dr. Hammoud’s favorite aspect of dentistry. Having a bad experience at the dentist as a child, motivated her to become a dentist herself so she could offer patients a better encounter than what she had. Dr. Hammoud is a University of Michigan School of Dentistry graduate as well. Her calming demeanor will help put any patients fears at ease.

Dr. Lamees Shamieh, DDS Dr. Shamieh grew up in Flint and has been practicing in Genesee County since 2021. She received her BA in Psychology and BS in Biology from the University of Michigan-Flint, and went on to obtain her DDS degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She is an active member of the Michigan Dental Association and Genesee District Dental Society.

Dr. Shamieh was drawn to dentistry because it connects service to others with creativity, and she hopes to help patients achieve confidence and healthy smiles. In her free time, she enjoys working out, making desserts, and developing her pottery skills.
