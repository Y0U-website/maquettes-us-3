Who Is a Good Candidate for Tooth Implants ?

  • Healthy adults with adequate bone volume
  • Non-smokers or those willing to quit
  • Patients free from uncontrolled diabetes or gum disease
  • Individuals willing to maintain excellent oral hygiene. If the bone is thin, bone grafting can create a solid foundation before placement.

How Long Do Implants For Teeth Last?

With diligent brushing, flossing, and routine checkups, dental implants can last 20 years or longer, often a lifetime. Their titanium structure resists decay, and regular maintenance protects the surrounding gum tissue.

Benefits of Dental Implants Over Dentures

  • Stable & Secure – No slipping, clicking, or pastes
  • Bone Preservation – Prevents the facial collapse common with long-term denture wear
  • Natural Feel – Eat crisp apples or juicy steaks without worry
  • Clear Speech – Articulate confidently without a bulky plate covering the palate

Implants vs. Bridges What’s the Difference?

Implants: Stand alone, stimulate bone, and don’t compromise neighboring teeth. Bridges: Depend on healthy teeth for support and require enamel removal, leaving those teeth vulnerable to decay or fracture. Over time, the bone under a bridge can resorb, causing the gums to sink.

How Do I Care for Dental Implants ?

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle brush
  • Floss or use interdental brushes around the implant crown
  • Schedule professional cleanings every six months
  • Wear a night guard if you grind or clench
  • Avoid smoking to preserve bone health

What Is the Recovery Time for Dental Implant Surgery ?

Most patients resume normal activity within 24 to 48 hours. Mild swelling or discomfort peaks at 48 hours and subsides within a week. During the 3–6-month integration phase, a soft diet may be advised while the bone heals around the implant.
