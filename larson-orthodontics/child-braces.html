Signs that your child might require pediatric orthodontic treatments

If your child is showing any variety of these symptoms, please get in touch with our practice to set up a pediatric orthodontic evaluation and consultation.

Don’t forget, early treatment now will certainly provide your child a healthier, gorgeous smile for the future; early orthodontic braces might be an option. Early orthodontic treatment, such as dental braces, may prevent or intercept additional major orthodontic problems from developing and might make orthodontic treatment at a later age shorter and less complicated.

Therapy at a young age (7-9 years) usually takes 6-12 months and a variety of oral appliances may be utilized to correct specific problems. Dr. Larson and his team at Larson Orthodontics will go over pediatric orthodontic options with you to provide the best care and treatment program for you and your little one.
