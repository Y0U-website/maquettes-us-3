Meet Dr. Gerald Johnson

Gerald Johnson, D.D.S.

Dr. Johnson is a member of multiple professional organizations, including the American Association of Orthodontists , Ohio Dental Society, Cincinnati Dental Society, and other local dental study clubs. He also attends many continuing education courses throughout the year to stay abreast of current changes in the field of dentistry and orthodontics.

When not straightening teeth, Dr. Johnson enjoys spending time with his children. His other interests include traveling, running, cycling, music, boating, fishing, and classic automobile restoration.
