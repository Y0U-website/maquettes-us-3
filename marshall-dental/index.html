The practice

With our convenient location in Columbus, OH, we are your local family friendly dental office, extending our services to the surrounding communities of Reynoldsburg, Pickerington, Bexley, Blacklick, Gahanna, Grove City, New Albany, Upper Arlington, Pataskala, Lockbourne, Ashville, German Village, and Grandview.

We treat everything from your entire family’s checkups to fillings, crowns, dentures, and Invisalign. We even offer a special in-office whitening treatment called Smile Perfected.

We use state-of-the-art technology like intraoral cameras before and during a procedure to show patients exactly what is going on in great detail. It makes it easier to explain the need for treatment when a patient can see what we see. We want our patients to be as informed as possible, because we believe excellent care can only be achieved when our patients can make an educated decision.

Our practice has been established for over 60 years, and Dr. Marshall is the fourth generation of dentist to treat our family of patients. We are a very close knit practice. The majority of our patients and their families have been treated at our office since it was founded and we are proud to carry on this legacy.

We don’t double book patients and try our hardest to stay on time, so each patient gets our undivided attention. Our patients see Dr. Marshall at every visit, even if it’s only for hygiene. Our caring, outgoing demeanor goes a long way to alleviate the anxiety most people have when going to the dentist.
