Dental Implant Technology Options That Fit Your Needs

Dental implants in Denver, CO can be used in several situations, but all methods are designed to give you a stable smile, which dramatically improves function, aesthetics, and oral health.

Single Dental Implants

Even one missing tooth can make you feel insecure and cause you to hide your smile. It can also contribute to migrating teeth teeth slipping toward openings which puts you at risk for misalignment, tooth wear, decay, and future fractures. You are also more likely to experience jawbone loss in that location, which can change your lower face profile and contribute to gum health issues later on.

If you have one or two missing teeth, single dental implants may be the recommended course, especially if you’re looking for a permanent and hassle-free solution. Single dental implants prevent tooth migration and may preserve your jawbone. A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that considers your ongoing jawbone health.

Implant-Supported Dentures and Bridges

A denture is an arch, plate or restoration teeth that take over for missing teeth. Traditionally, full and partial dentures rely on suction, gravity, and the contours of your jaws for stability. Over time, dentures require relines in our dental office and store-bought adhesives as your jawbone shrinks.

A dental bridge is one or two replacement teeth with a crown on each side. Your Denver dentist uses the crowns to cap the healthy teeth on either side of your smile gap. This allows the restoring bridge teeth to complete your smile.

For added stability, patients can opt for implant-supported dentures and bridges. This implant option usually calls for four to six implants to secure a denture and one or two to support a dental bridge.

With this technology, you will not need a dental implant for every lost tooth!

5 Ways Dental Implants Enhance Oral Health

Dental implants indeed make your smile look amazing. But they also benefit your oral health!

BONUS: Implants lower your risks for oral health complications associated with lost or missing teeth. Implants also benefit your teeth and gums, give you a winning smile, and are created to last with proper care.

  • Natural Look and Function Implant- supported teeth look and work like natural teeth. As time goes by, you may even forget which of your teeth is an implant. Certainly, no one else will notice.
  • Better Jawbone Health Dental implants are made of strong titanium, which integrates with your jawbone, making them a permanent and accepted part of your anatomy. Because of this, they support jawbone health.
  • Durability and Longevity Dental implants are sturdy and built to last. Even if you have to replace your crown, bridge, or restoring denture in the future, your dental implant is there to support that replacement.
  • Tooth Alignment Support Dental implants support surrounding teeth and keep them in the proper position. With dental implants, you’ll never have to worry about migrating teeth because the implant is fixed and not removable, which means constant support for your teeth.
  • Healthy Teeth Remain Intact With dental implant technology, your dentist does not have to remove enamel from healthy teeth to place a prosthetic as with a dental bridge with supporting crowns, for example.

The Dental Implant Procedure in Denver

The dental implant process is surprisingly simple. One of our local specialists uses anesthesia to keep you comfortable while surgically implanting your replacement root relying on image-guided technology for precise placement.

After a few months when your new root has integrated with your jawbone we top it with a dental crown or quality prosthetic.

This restores your smile, your bite force, and allows you to indulge in a diet without restrictions. It really is like a do-over for tooth loss.

Caring for Implants and Preventing Future Tooth Loss

Fortunately, caring for dental implants means you brush, floss, and visit your dentist. There are no added steps because implants are treated just like natural adult teeth.

Preventive dentistry means seeing our dentists in Denver every six months for exams and teeth cleanings , and having dental implants does not change this. The integrity of your gums is crucial for implant longevity and to prevent further tooth loss. Additionally, your dentist can check on your implants, screen you for oral cancer, and make sure there are no emerging issues like cavities or gingivitis.

Of course, if you notice anything between checkups gum pain, swelling, or tooth sensitivity we encourage you to reach out to our team to schedule an appointment.

Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process involves implanting a small, titanium post in the jaw that will eventually fuse with the bone, creating a solid foundation to support a tooth crown or prosthetic.

It takes several months for the posts to fully heal and integrate into the bone, and you will need to use caution when eating. However, the healing and integration are not painful. You ll likely only feel mild discomfort for a few days following implant placement.

Why are dental implants so popular?

It’s no wonder that dental implants have quickly become the tooth replacement of choice by both dentists and patients. Implants resolve the aesthetic problem of a missing tooth, but more than that, they are completely functional and act and look just like a natural tooth.

Replace Missing Teeth in Denver with Dental Implants

The secret to the success of dental implants is the small titanium post that is surgically placed in your jaw. The titanium used for implants is the same medical grade material that has been used successfully for years as replacement joints.

Titanium is biocompatible the body accepts this man-made material, allowing the titanium post to fully integrate with the jawbone and tissue. Once healing is complete, the implant actually becomes part of the structure of your jaw.

This provides a measure of stability that is simply not found with other tooth replacement options. When the implant has fully integrated, our dentists in Cherry Creek complete the process by creating a dental crown that is affixed to the implant. The result is strong and durable, and functions and looks like a natural tooth.
