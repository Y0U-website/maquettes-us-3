Financial and Insurance Options

We strive to deliver the finest care at the most reasonable cost to our patients, therefore payment of your estimated patient portion (after insurance) is due at the time service is rendered unless other arrangements have been made in advance.

Please remember you are fully responsible for all fees charged by this office regardless of your insurance coverage. If you have an insurance benefit, we will help you understand your benefit in our office and, with your authorization, file claims when necessary. Any remaining balance after your insurance has been paid is your responsibility.

We offer a 5 % bookkeeping courtesy on balances paid in full the day of your appointment for patients without an insurance benefit.

Membership Club

We’ve created a membership club as an alternative to dental insurance. Our plans include annual preventative care (cleanings, exams, x-rays and fluoride) and discounts on restorative and cosmetic treatment for a low monthly or annual fee.
