Benefits of Orthodontist

The benefits of orthodontic treatment are numerous. The cosmetic results are so clear that many do not even realize the many ways ortho can help prevent other health maladies. Ortho can help your path to a healthier, happier you!

More About Orthodontists : Choosing the Right Orthodontist

  • Jaw issues: Ortho treatment not only straightens your teeth but it aligns your bite, ensuring ideal jaw placement. A misaligned bite, or malocclusion, puts stress on the jaw and over time, can cause jaw disorders and bruxism. A proper bite keeps your teeth from wearing down and helps alleviate bruxism. Bruxism is the unconscious clenching and grinding of the teeth which causes sensitivity, chipping and fracturing in the teeth. Using ortho to correct your bite will greatly reduce your risk of pain and injury that can be caused when left untreated.
  • Injury: Correcting a bite can help younger children and adults avoid injury in physical activities. If a bite is overjet (over protrusion or “buck teeth”) can be a risk, especially for active children playing contact sports.
  • Other health risks: A malocclusion can cause improper chewing which in turn will lead to digestive issues and other gastrointestinal problems and poor nutrient absorption. Swallowing larger bites of food can cause reflux which in turn can damage the stomach lining and tooth enamel.
