About Old Towne Dental

Proudly Serving Tehachapi, CA & Beyond

Old Towne Dental was established in 1994, and provides the highest quality general dental care for patients in Tehachapi, CA and across the Tehachapi Valley. Our practice is owned and operated by Dr. Michael Jones, a general dentist with over 20 years of dental experience. Together with our experienced, fully trained staff, Dr. Jones provides a comprehensive range of dental services to patients of all ages. Together with our patients , we design personalized, gentle care plans to help then achieve and maintain great oral health.

Our staff and dentist care about you and your overall health. We go the extra mile to make sure every patient feels comfortable in our care. Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and equipment. In addition, Dr. Jones and our staff participate in on-going continuing education courses and training. This ensures we are up-to-date on current dental methodologies and the newest products and procedures.

Dr. Michael Jones

Dr. Michael Jones is a 1988 graduate from UCLA. He has been practicing general dentistry for over 20 years. He is well known in our community and has a reputation for excellent patient care. He has owned and operated Old Towne Dental since 1994.

Dr. Kevin Bibera

Dr. Kevin Bibera (“Dr. Kevin”) earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from UCLA School of Dentistry, one of the most prestigious dental programs in the nation. He received several awards and scholarships including the American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry Award, Dean’s Scholarship, and Regents Scholarship which is presented on the basis of academic excellence, leadership and exceptional promise.

During his 4 years of training, Dr. Kevin was privileged to work alongside many outstanding mentors who have helped him cultivate his attention to detail, high quality work ethic and drive for excellence. He constantly strives to improve his clinical skills and knowledge by investing in continuing education courses throughout the year. It is his goal to provide you with the most current, evidence-based dentistry.

Dr. Kevin is an active member of the community and has participated in several volunteer services throughout his time at UCLA including Give Kids a Smile, where he provided oral hygiene instructions to kids in underserved areas, and annual dental mission trips to Ensenada, Mexico where he provided free dental work to people in need.

In his free time, Dr. Kevin enjoys eating with good company, exploring new areas both urban and outdoors, playing basketball, traveling abroad, and doing photography/videography.
