About

We realize that each child and family is unique in their medical and dental needs, as well as their preferences for treatment procedures and materials. We offer a full range of dental services including mercury free, BPA free, and monomer free fillings. Family members are welcome to be present for all treatment.

Our office is equipped with high-efficiency air purifiers in each operatory. We use distilled water in every unit, ensuring a chemical-free experience.

We have parking in both the front and rear of the building. Our handicap ramp is located on the right side of the building.
