Our Dentist

Our mission Accessible, quality dental care for every family. Huntington Park Dental Clinic was built with one goal: to make excellent dental care comfortable and accessible for every family in our community. Great dentistry starts with listening, understanding your concerns, your goals, and your history before recommending anything.

We serve patients of all ages, teens, adults, and seniors, and we’re proud to be a trusted part of the Huntington Park community.

  • 10+ years of clinical experience in Huntington Park
  • Specializes in family, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry
  • Fluent in English and Spanish
  • Gentle, patient-centered approach, no pressure, ever
  • Same-day urgent care available, call to confirm

What sets us apart

  • No pressure, we present options and let you decide
  • Written cost estimates before any treatment begins
  • Full bilingual support in English and Spanish
  • Specialist available one Sunday a month
  • Denti-Cal and most PPO plans accepted

Our promise to every patient.

These are the standards we hold ourselves to, every visit, every patient.

  • We listen to your concerns and goals before making any recommendation.
  • We explain your diagnosis and options in plain language. English or Spanish, your choice.
  • We provide written estimates of your insurance coverage and out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins.
  • We respect your time with organized, efficient appointments and minimal waiting.
  • We only recommend care we would choose for our own families.
