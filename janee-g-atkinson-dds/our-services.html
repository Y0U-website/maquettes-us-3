General Dentistry

At the Texarkana dental office of Janée Atkinson, we prioritize oral health for all of our patients. That foundation begins with our general comprehensive services: routine cleanings, checkups, fillings, crowns, bridges, gum disease therapy, or implants. We cover all of your General Dentistry needs.

Cosmetic Dentistry

From Whitening and Bonding to Crowns or Veneers, our professional, experienced staff is equipped to give you a beautiful smile. We also offer Invisalign treatments for teeth alignment for those looking for comfort and convenience. Our goal is to send you out into the world with confidence!

Restorative Dentistry

Our extensive and advanced training allows diagnosis and successful management of teeth and gum disease. From the simple to the extremely complex, we provide options and solutions to restore your teeth and gum health.

Dentures & Implants

Tooth replacement offers a range of options, including full and partial dentures and implants. If any of your teeth are beyond repair, we can help you decide the best option for your budget and needs. Nothing can undermine your confidence and mouth health like missing teeth.
