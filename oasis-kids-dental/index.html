Located in Glen Burnie, MD, Oasis Kids Dental cares for your child’s oral health with compassion in a warm, supportive atmosphere.

Dr. Oluwayemisi Akinrefon believes in early intervention, treating issues while they are small, and emphasizing a strong home care routine and regular dental exams and cleanings.

Teeth cleaning and exams are necessary for your child’s teeth to stay healthy during childhood. Oasis Kids Dental in Glen Burnie, MD recommends two cleanings a year for optimal oral health.

1st Tooth = 1st Visit Children who receive dental exams at an early age are less likely to need dental treatment down the road. Dr. Oluwayemisi Akinrefon recommends oral exams for children when their first tooth erupts.
