What to Expect at Your First Visit

When you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by the smiling faces of our friendly team. We’ll answer any financing or insurance-related questions you may have and help make your visit run as smoothly as possible. Then, one of our hygienists will conduct a thorough cleaning, followed by a comprehensive exam and x-rays from Dr. Greenslade. Lastly, you’ll receive a customized treatment plan and will be able to schedule any future appointments to maintain a healthy and bright smile.

Giving Back to Our Community

At Honor Dental, we aim to create and strengthen community bonds within our great state of Minnesota that extends throughout the globe. We are dedicated to lending our financial support to two organizations that are dear to us: Hope Station, which serves children in China who are orphaned or disabled, and Trout Unlimited, which conserves, protects, and restores North America’s rivers, streams, and other cold water fisheries.
