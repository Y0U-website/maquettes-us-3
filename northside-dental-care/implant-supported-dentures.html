Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer several advantages compared to traditional dentures.

Implant-supported dentures stay securely in place.

You won’t have to worry about your denture slipping while eating or talking.

Because the denture is supported by implants, there is less pressure on the gums .

Implant-supported dentures often look more natural than traditional dentures.

Because the implants provide support, less bulky plastic is needed , which helps create a more natural-looking smile.

When teeth are missing, the jawbone can begin to shrink over time.

Dental implants help stimulate the bone and may help slow bone loss , which supports long-term facial structure.

Implant-supported dentures stay secure, helping patients speak more clearly and naturally .

Traditional dentures often require messy adhesives to stay in place.

With implant-supported dentures, the denture snaps securely onto the implants , so adhesives are usually not needed.
