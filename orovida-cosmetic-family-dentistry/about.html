Get To Know Dr. Juste Ruzelyte

Dr. Juste was born and raised in Lithuania, where she was interested in dentistry from a young age. She attended the prestigious Lithuanian University of Health Science and obtained her dental degree.

Shortly after arriving in the US, Dr. Juste realized that she simply couldn’t pursue any career other than dentistry. It was her calling! She decided to attend the UCLA School of Dentistry and completed a program that would allow her to practice dentistry in the United States.

Now, Dr. Juste is thrilled to have the opportunity to open her own practice. As the owner and operator of Orovida Cosmetic & Family Dentistry, she can bring her vision of patient-focused, judgment-free, and education-first dentistry to life. Come in for your first visit and see the difference for yourself!

When she’s not busy working at our office, Dr. Juste loves exploring the great outdoors by hiking in state parks, as well as heading down to one of Orange County’s many beaches to play volleyball. She also loves spending quality time with family and friends or curling up with a good book. Have any reading recommendations? Let her know!

What to Expect

The New Patient Experience

Wondering what to expect from your first visit with Dr. Juste? First, you’ll stop by our front desk to fill out a few forms. You can also complete these online to save some time!

After checking in, you can enjoy our comfortable waiting area. Watch some TV, enjoy a free drink from our beverage bar, and relax! One of our team members will come and get you when it’s time for your appointment.

You’ll be brought back to one of our operating rooms, where you’ll meet Dr. Juste. She will learn a little bit about you, then perform an oral exam and take X-rays of your teeth.

Dr. Juste will review these findings and let you know if she sees any issues that need further attention. Then, she will use our AIRFLOW Prophylaxis Master dental suite to remove plaque and tartar and clean your teeth. No rubber cups or scraping required!

Finally, she will floss your teeth and you’ll be all done. Stop by our front desk on the way out to schedule your next checkup or any other necessary follow-ups and you’ll be able to head home to enjoy your bright, healthy smile.
