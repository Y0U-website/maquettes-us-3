0 Meet Dr. Richardson A native of Roanoke, Dr. Richardson earned her bachelor’s degree in biology from BYU and her Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S) from Virginia Commonwealth University. She has 20 years of experience as a general dentist and is very excited to join the team at North Roanoke Dental Associates.

In her spare time, Dr. Richardson loves to spend time with her husband and three children. They love being outside, exploring new places, and reading together. An animal lover, Dr. Richardson also makes her home with two silly dogs, one of which is her competition agility dog. She also has two cats, a normal orange one named Ronald Weasley and a crazy calico named Sparkle. Dr. Richardson also loves to play the piano and read.

Experience care that feels like family.
