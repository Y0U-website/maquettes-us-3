Your path to a fixed smile.

Every stage is carefully planned and, when you choose, performed under gentle sedation — so the entire journey is calm and comfortable from start to finish.

Comfort First Sedation Keeps It Easy Implant placement and every step of your treatment can be done under oral conscious sedation . You’ll be fully relaxed, comfortable, and often won’t remember the procedure at all. Learn about sedation →

We review your goals and take a 3D scan and digital X-rays to assess bone volume and plan exact implant positions. You’ll receive a clear treatment plan with transparent pricing.

A quick, comfortable scan captures a precise 3D model of your mouth — no messy impressions — to design teeth that fit your bite and face perfectly.

Using guided planning, we place the implants precisely — performed under sedation for a relaxed, comfortable experience. A temporary set of teeth is often provided the same day.

Over the following months, the implants fuse with your jawbone, creating a permanent, rock-solid foundation for your final teeth.

Your custom milled zirconia arch is secured onto the implants — delivering a beautiful, stable, natural-feeling smile built to last.

Denture questions, answered.

They’re a full arch of teeth anchored onto a small number of dental implants — typically four to six — instead of resting on the gums. They stay securely in place, eliminating the slipping and adhesives of traditional dentures.

All-on-6 uses six implants to support a full, fixed arch of zirconia teeth. The six-implant design spreads bite force evenly for outstanding stability and a strong, permanent, non-removable restoration.

Yes. Every step — including implant placement — can be performed under gentle sedation. You’ll be completely relaxed and comfortable, and most patients are surprised by how easy the experience is.

We use an intraoral scanner to capture a precise 3D model of your mouth in minutes — no messy molds. The scan is used to plan implants and mill your teeth for an exact, comfortable fit.

A fixed All-on-6 zirconia arch stays in permanently and feels closest to natural teeth. A snap-in overdenture is removable for cleaning and more budget-friendly. We’ll help you choose what fits your goals.

The implants can last a lifetime with proper care. A zirconia fixed arch is extremely durable and resists staining and wear for many years, making it an outstanding long-term investment.
