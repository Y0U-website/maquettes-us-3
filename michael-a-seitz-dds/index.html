The practice

Visit us for the best in family and cosmetic dentistry . Good cosmetic work starts with healthy teeth and gums. With regular visits to Dr. Seitz you can look and feel your best. Dr. Seitz is known for his conservative approach to dental reconstruction and restoration, only correcting what needs be corrected. He also caters to patients with "dentalphobia."

Dr. Seitz’s Dental Philosophy: I’m not here to sell you stuff. I’m here to help you keep your teeth. Often, what people need most is a dental education: Here’s what is happening in your mouth. Here’s how to floss. Here’s how to brush.

I have a simple life and no debts. That means the meter is not always running. I will listen to you, examine your mouth, and give you your options. Then I will give you my advice. But I won’t push, unless you want to do something unwise, like extract a good tooth. I want this to be fun for both of us. I’ll joke with you. Maybe you’ll joke with me.
