How Does Invisalign Work?

The Invisalign process begins with a consultation with Dr. Patel, where she will examine your teeth and evaluate your bite. Once you receive your trays, you’ll begin wearing them for 20-22 hours per day, only taking them out to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush your teeth.

Every month or so, depending on your treatment schedule, you’ll return to our Dracut office for a brief check-in and receive your next set of aligners. This process will continue for the duration of your treatment until your teeth have shifted into their desired positions.
