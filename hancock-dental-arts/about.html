About Hancock Dental Arts

About Us

Hancock Dental Arts is a boutique dental lab located in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, with a well-established reputation for excellence. Founded by Master Ceramist Lance Hancock in 2020, he was looking for the freedom to fulfill his vision to create consistently gorgeous high-end anterior porcelain veneer smile designs and stunning full mouth restoration cases, along with a commitment to personalized service.

The entire team at Hancock Dental Arts is committed to providing you with sterling craftsmanship, distinct artistry, and premier service.

Our Team

Lance Hancock

Lance grew up in Minneapolis, MN. He is a third-generation dental lab technician and began his career at just 15 years of age. He has been honing his craft ever since.

Lance has seen thousands of veneers being prepared and cemented through specialty hands-on programs. He has been receiving advanced training by the best clinicians around the country since the age of 17: attending LVI with Dr. Bill Dickerson, Hornbrook Group courses with Dr. David Hornbrook, Pac Live and Heartland Dental Aesthetic Continuums with Dr. Ed Lowe and Dr. Mike Koczarski. His extensive experience and training make him an invaluable resource for our Dentists when it comes to case consultation and material selection.

When he isn’t creating beautiful smiles in the lab, Lance enjoys fishing for walleye with his family on Lake Vermilion, golfing with his buddies, and playing every sport imaginable with his son Oliver.
