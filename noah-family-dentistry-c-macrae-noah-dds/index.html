Welcome to Noah Family Dentistry

We are a locally owned dental practice caring for patients of all ages from Clayton, NC, and the surrounding Johnston County communities.

The practice

We offer a full range of dental services in a small office atmosphere. From preventive cleanings and exams to complete smile makeovers, restorative treatments, and family dentistry services we have all the tools to keep your smile healthy and dazzling.

Our practice is devoted to creating a positive dental experience. You will never be just another chart number at Noah Family Dentistry. We are a patient-centered practice, focused on providing high-quality, customized care to each patient. Dr. Noah will listen to your needs and work with you to create the best treatment plan for you.

Schedule an appointment today and start your journey to optimal oral health with Noah Family Dentistry!
