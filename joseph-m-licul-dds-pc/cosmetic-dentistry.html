Partial and Full Dentures

Dentures provide their user with a removable replacement for their missing teeth. The team at Licul Family Dentisty is fully equipped at our Southampton Office to perform two types of denture fittings. The first fitting is a partial denture where the patient has some of their natural teeth. The second is a full or complete denture set where all the natural teeth are missing or need to be removed.

Dental Crowns

A dental crown is a special cap that is designed to fit over your tooth to maintain its size, shape, and appearance. There are many reasons why an Oral Care Professional may recommend a dental crown. A few common reasons Joseph M. Licul, DDS, may recommend a Dental Crown is to protect a weakened tooth, as a cap to be fitted around a dental implant, and as a cosmetic addition to improve your smile.

If you don’t have an appointment for your dental crown or think you need one, click here .

Dental Implants

A dental implant is an alternative procedure to dental bridges and dentures. It provides an artificial anchor that acts as the root of your tooth. This procedure can lead to a more naturally displayed tooth replacement as it is designed to look and feel like your natural tooth in a more permanent way than other procedures.

If you’d like to explore improving your oral health and smile with a dental implant, you can request an appointment by clicking here .

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge is used to fill in the gap left by one or more missing teeth. To perform this procedure, your Oral Healthcare Professional will use two crowns on both sides of the missing tooth or teeth to anchor the bridge in place. There are many reasons to have a dental bridge procedure, including to restore your smile fully without any gaps.

Our Ongoing Response to COVID-19
