Dr. Spatz grew up in Mt. Lebanon and graduated from Mt. Lebanon High School in 1990. He attended Tufts University in Boston and the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 1998.

He completed his General Practice Residency at Forum Health System/Northside Hospital in Youngstown, OH, and has practiced in Washington, PA and Pittsburgh, PA since 2002.
