Lakeway Dental Associates is now under new ownership following the retirement of Dr. Dupper, who led the practice for the past five years. The new owner, Dr. Fawzy, is a licensed general dentist with over 15 years of clinical experience in preventive, restorative, and cosmetic care. His approach is grounded in clear communication, steady professionalism, and long-term treatment planning. He was personally selected by Dr. Dupper to carry the practice forward based on shared values around clinical integrity, patient care, and transparency.
