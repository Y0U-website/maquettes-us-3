Dental Implants in Philadelphia…

  • Dental implants don’t sacrifice the quality of your adjacent teeth like a bridge does because neighboring teeth are not altered to support implants. Thus, more of your own teeth are left untouched, a significant long-term benefit to your oral health!
  • Dental implants look and feel like your own teeth. Since Instant Implants integrate into the structure of your bone, they prevent the bone loss and gum recession that often accompany bridgework and dentures. In addition, no one will ever know that you have replacement teeth.
  • Dental Implants are also used to securely attach full dentures directly to the jaw, eliminating the need for adhesives that often fail, causing dentures to loosen. Implant-supported dentures can be worn by anyone who doesn’t have any teeth in their jaw (a current denture wearer), but has enough bone in their jaw to support dental implants. With Instant Implant supported dentures, you’ll never have to think about what to eat, when to smile or laugh, or if your dentures may come loose.

FAQs

Who is eligible for same day dental implants?

While anyone is eligible for same day implants, the most common age is 65 and over, this is because it’s common for men and women to have at least one missing tooth. It’s never too late to get dental implants, and if you’re unsure whether or not you’re eligible, it’s always best to see a certified dentist such as Dr. Gelbart at Independence Dental Group to further assess the matter.

How much do same day dental implants cost?

You can expect to pay between $3,000 to $4,500 for a single tooth dental implant, however, for more accurate pricing, it’s advised to see a dentist to get this information as they’ll be able to help you with any questions you might have.
