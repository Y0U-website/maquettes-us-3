All-on-4 Dental Implants In Independence & Blue Springs, MO

  • How do you clean All-on-4 dental implants? All-on-4 dental implants are cleaned much like natural teeth, using daily brushing and flossing to remove plaque and debris. Special floss, interdental brushes, or water flossers may be recommended to clean around the implants and gums. Regular dental visits and professional cleanings are also important to maintain implant health and long-term success.
  • Who is a good candidate for All-on-4 dental implants? Good candidates for All-on-4 dental implants include patients missing most or all of their teeth who want a stable alternative to traditional dentures. This option is often suitable for individuals with some bone loss, as fewer implants are required. A dental evaluation is needed to assess overall oral health and determine candidacy.
