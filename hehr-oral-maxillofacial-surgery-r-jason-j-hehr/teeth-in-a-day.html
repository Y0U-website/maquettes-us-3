Teeth-in-a-Day in Charleston &…

This procedure is ideal for patients seeking a rapid and reliable alternative to removable dentures or those with failing teeth in need of full-mouth rehabilitation.

  • Surgical Placement: Dr. Hehr places 4–6 implants per arch using minimally invasive techniques.
  • Immediate Restoration: A custom temporary bridge is placed the same day, allowing you to leave with a complete smile.
  • Final Prosthesis: After the implants fully integrate with the bone (typically 3–6 months), your permanent restoration is placed by your restoring dentist.

Benefits of Teeth-in-a-Day

  • One Visit, One Transformation – Immediate results and restored function
  • Eliminates the Need for Dentures – Fixed solution with long-term stability
  • Natural Appearance – Custom prosthetics that mimic real teeth
  • Preserves Bone Structure – Prevents the jawbone loss common with missing teeth
  • Streamlined Process – Fewer appointments and faster recovery

Are You a Candidate for Teeth-in-a-Day?

Dr. Hehr carefully evaluates each patient to determine if Teeth-in-a-Day is the best solution. Most patients who are missing multiple teeth or are facing full extractions can be considered for this procedure, provided they have adequate bone density or can benefit from bone grafting.

  • Patients with failing bridges or dentures
  • Individuals with extensive tooth loss or severe decay
  • Those seeking a fixed, long-term solution

Frequently Asked Questions About Teeth-in-a-Day

How is Teeth-in-a-Day different from traditional dental implants?

Teeth-in-a-Day eliminates the need for months of healing with temporary dentures. You receive implants and a fixed, functional prosthesis in one appointment.

Will I be awake during the procedure?

We offer multiple anesthesia options, including IV sedation and general anesthesia, to ensure maximum comfort and relaxation during your procedure.

How long do the implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades. Regular cleanings, follow-ups, and good oral hygiene are key.

Is this procedure covered by insurance?

Coverage varies depending on your plan. Our team will review your benefits and financing options during your consultation.

Can I eat normally with my new teeth?

Yes. While some dietary restrictions apply during the healing phase, your new teeth are immediately functional for speaking and light chewing.
