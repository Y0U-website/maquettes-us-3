About Us

At Lone Mountain Family Dentistry we are dedicated to helping our patients maintain healthy teeth, gums, and an overall smile! A beautiful, toothy grin is the key to confidence and a great start to a new quality of life. Dr. Chris Kleintjes, D.D.S., started Lone Mountain Family Dentistry 35 years ago to help the members of his community gain that wonderful confidence and to provide outstanding and affordable dental care.

In 2006, Dr. Kevin Peterson joined Lone Mountain Family Dentistry with the same dedication and enthusiasm for dental excellence. Dr. Peterson is now the leading practitioner and remains up to date with the latest technology while offering a variety of dental services to serve the Northern Nevada area.

If you are looking for a trusted, family dentist look no further than Lone Mountain Family Dentistry.
