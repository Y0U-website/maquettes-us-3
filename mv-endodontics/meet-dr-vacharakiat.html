Meet Dr. Vacharakiat

Malissa Vacharakiat D.M.D.

Dr. Vacharakiat is originally from Ft. Worth, Texas but moved to South Florida in 1990. She is married to her husband Jamie, and they have two wonderful children Oliver and Alsatia. She enjoys family, traveling, learning different cultures and foods as well as giving back to her community.

Dr. Vacharakiat began her collegiate journey at University of Florida (GO GATORS!) where she earned her undergraduate degree. Her passion for dentistry led her to Nova Southeastern University where she earned her DMD in 2003. She practiced as a general dentist for 10 years before furthering her education and career as an endodontist. She received her endodontic certificate which requires 3 more years of specialty training from Nova Southeastern University in 2013.

Dr. Vacharakiat is actively involved in the local dental community. She currently attends several dental study groups so she may provide the best care available to her patients. She is an active member of the listed associations:

• American Association of Endodontics • Florida Association of Endodontics • American Dental Association • Florida Dental Association • West Coast District Dental Association • Pasco County Dental Association • Upper Pinellas County Dental Association • Hillsborough County Dental Reseach Clinic • Delegate to Florida Dental Association (2019-2023)
