Intraoral Camera

Our intraoral camera transforms how you understand your dental health. This pen-sized device captures detailed, high-resolution images of your teeth and gums, which are immediately displayed on our chairside monitor.

Dr. May uses this technology to show you exactly what she sees, from early cavity formation to gum conditions, allowing you to make informed decisions about your treatment options. This visual communication tool builds trust and clarity during your visits, as you can see dental issues firsthand rather than just hearing about them.

Digital Records

Dr. May has implemented a state-of-the-art digital record system to enhance patient care. Our paperless practice maintains comprehensive electronic health records to access your complete dental history efficiently.

Digital radiography delivers detailed images with minimal radiation, while secure data storage ensures your information remains protected yet accessible when needed. This technology allows for seamless insurance coordination and streamlined appointment scheduling, providing the most convenient dental experience possible.

Noise-Cancelling Headphones

We offer premium noise-canceling headphones at May Dentistry to enhance your dental experience. These advanced devices use active noise control technology to reduce ambient sounds, creating a more peaceful environment during procedures.

Patients can enjoy soothing music of their choice while dental equipment noise fades into the background. This thoughtful comfort amenity reflects our commitment to minimizing anxiety and maximizing relaxation during your visit.

Digital Impressions

At May Dentistry, we ve replaced traditional putty impressions with advanced digital scanning technology for a more comfortable and precise experience. Our handheld digital scanner captures thousands of images of your teeth and gums, creating a highly accurate 3-D model in minutes, without any more gagging or discomfort from conventional impression materials.

These digital impressions allow Dr. May to design custom restorations with exceptional precision, resulting in better-fitting crowns, bridges, and other dental appliances. The technology also reduces turnaround times, often eliminating the wait between appointments. Experience the difference of modern dentistry in North Palm Beach with our digital impression system, designed to enhance your comfort while delivering superior results for your smile.
