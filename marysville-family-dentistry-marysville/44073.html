Dr. Michael Halasa

Dr. Halasa recently purchased Marysville Family Dentistry in June 2025. What he loves most about his work is the rewarding feeling that comes from building long-lasting relationships with patients and genuinely improving their lives. He s equally passionate about his team, surrounding himself with caring, dedicated people who brighten each day and share his commitment to excellent patient care.

When he s not in the office, Dr. Halasa enjoys spending time with his wife, Meredith, and their family. His engineering roots still shine through in his hobbies. He loves computer programming, app development, and game development. He s also an avid reader and enjoys diving into a good book.

Fun fact: Before settling in Marysville, Dr. Halasa and his family spent two years in Denver following dental school, then moved back to Ohio to be closer to family. His journey from engineer to dentist to practice owner reflects his commitment to finding meaningful work that allows him to connect with and serve his community!
