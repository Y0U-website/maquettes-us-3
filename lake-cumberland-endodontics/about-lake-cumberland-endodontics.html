About Lake Cumberland Endodontics

Established in October of 2003, we’ve been committed to creating a welcoming and modern environment for patients across Southeastern Kentucky and Northern Tennessee. At Lake Cumberland Endodontics, we focus on relieving your pain and preserving your natural teeth whenever possible.

Meet Dr. Shea Cheuvront

Dr. Shea Cheuvront, a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics, is committed to providing exceptional root canal treatment in his hometown. With extensive training from the University of Louisville and affiliations with leading dental organizations, he ensures top-tier care for Lake Cumberland Endodontics patients. Trust your smile to a dedicated expert who values community and excellence.
