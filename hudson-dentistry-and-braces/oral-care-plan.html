What is Oral Care Dental plan?

Oral Care Dental Plan is a subscription-based discount health care plan that charges a discounted rate for certain services rendered in our office in accordance with the plan fee schedule. To access these exclusive discounts, you must become a plan member by submitting your enrollment information and paying the low annual membership fee. The discounts are applied at the time of service, and you pay the office directly for all services provided.

Oral Care Dental Plan is not insurance, and no limitations or maximums apply. Also, there are no claim forms to submit or waiting periods for services you need right away. You and your enrolled family members can use the plan as often as you would like.

How much will the membership cost?

Oral Care Dental Plan is designed to be affordable and will typically pay for itself in only one visit to a participating office location.
