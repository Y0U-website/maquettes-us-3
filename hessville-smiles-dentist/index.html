The practice

At Hessville Smiles Dentist, we are dedicated to providing outstanding dental care with a focus on comfort, quality, and personalized attention. Led by Dr. Umar Haque and Dr. Maria Rodriguez, our practice serves the community of Hammond, IN with a full range of comprehensive dental services designed to keep your smile healthy and beautiful for life.

With our general dentistry services, we keep your teeth in excellent condition. These services range from preventative care to dentures and emergency care. We also offer crowns, bridges, root canals and full mouth rehabilitation.

For individual tooth replacement or to anchor bridges or partial or complete dentures, dental implants are your most satisfactory choice.

Our orthodontic services include metal braces for strong, reliable alignment and clear aligners for a discreet, removable option. Whether you prefer traditional braces or nearly invisible aligners, our team ensures a customized treatment plan for a straighter, healthier smile.

Dental emergencies can’t wait. At Hessville Smiles Dentist, Dr. Umar Haque offers prompt care for tooth pain, injuries, and infections. Call us for same-day emergency treatment in Hammond, IN

Caring for our patients means that we offer as many services under one roof as possible. This includes most of the oral surgery procedures our patients might need.

Gum health is essential if you are to keep your teeth for your whole life. We support gum health with expert periodontal care to treat problems in the gums.

Our doctors are highly trained in America but can speak multiple languages. Our staff speaks English and Spanish.
