FOREVERSMILE

  • No More Retainer Emergencies – If your retainer gets lost, broken, or worn out, FOREVERSMILE has you covered—quickly and affordably.
  • Hassle-Free Process – Your 3D digital impression is securely stored, so new retainers can be made without the need for extra office visits .
  • Cost-Effective & Convenient – Instead of paying full price for a new retainer, FOREVERSMILE offers a one-time sign-up fee and low co-pays for replacements , saving you money in the long run.
  • Protect Your Orthodontic Investment – Retainers keep your teeth from shifting, so you avoid expensive re-treatment.
  • Additional Benefits – Retainers help protect against teeth grinding and can be used for teeth whitening as well.

How It Works

  • Enroll in FOREVERSMILE – Sign up when you complete your orthodontic treatment.
  • Digital Impression Stored – We take a 3D scan of your teeth and keep it on file for future orders.
  • Request a Replacement – If your retainer is lost, broken, or worn out, simply contact us.
  • Receive Your Retainer – Your new retainer will arrive within 5 business days , right at your doorstep—no extra visits required!

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is FOREVERSMILE for? FOREVERSMILE is designed for both teens and adults who want an easy, cost-effective way to replace their retainers.

When should I enroll? The best time to sign up is when you complete your braces, Invisalign, or clear aligner treatment to keep your teeth perfectly aligned.

How do I sign up? Simply ask our team at Helotes Pediatric Dentistry , and we’ll take care of everything!

How long does it take to receive my replacement retainer? Your new retainer will be delivered within 5 business days after your request.

Can I order a backup retainer just in case? Yes! Many patients choose to keep an extra retainer on hand for peace of mind.
