Dental Implants vs Dentures, Which Is Right for You?

Many patients exploring missing teeth solutions in Stamford, CT find themselves weighing dental implants against traditional dentures. Here’s an honest side-by-side comparison:

Traditional Dentures

  • Removable, can slip while eating or speaking
  • No stimulation to jawbone, bone loss over time
  • Require adhesives and daily removal for cleaning
  • May need replacement or refitting every 5–7 years
  • Covers gums, can affect taste and sensation
  • Lower upfront cost, higher long-term maintenance cost
