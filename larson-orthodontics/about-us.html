In The Practice

As an orthodontist, the best part of my job is helping to build self-esteem in my patients. For patients of all ages, a new smile can mean a boost in self-confidence, which brings a positive change to their lives! There is nothing as fulfilling as seeing a patient’s proud smile at the end of treatment, especially if they came to their first appointment shy about showing their teeth.

I am fortunate to have found orthodontics as a profession, as it has become extremely rewarding for my patients and for myself. I believe a good orthodontist must possess several attributes: communicating well with patients, understanding of teeth movement and spacing, and commitment to perfection. My dedication to improving facial aesthetics and promoting overall long-term oral health has become my foundation for a great practice.

I tailor each treatment plan to achieve my patient’s desired results. I take pride in each treatment, and use only the highest quality appliances and the latest technology to produce perfect smiles and happy patients!

Family and Community

When I’m not in the office, I enjoy spending time with my fiancée, Jessica. We enjoy physical fitness and visits to cultural and science museums. I am also a bit of a gear-head with a strong motivation towards rebuilding and restoring automobiles and motorcycles. I appreciate the challenge, imagine the end result, and plan each precise step. I also travel frequently to my father’s olive ranch to assist in the family business. Much of my time there involves a heavy emphasis on automotive and equipment repairs, including welding and rebuilding engines.

I believe that businesses should have a positive impact on the communities they serve. In our practice, we dedicate ourselves to earning the trust and respect of our patients, schools, service organizations, youth programs and our neighbors. I am committed to helping create a thriving community. Yuba City Orthodontics embraces the responsibility to be good neighbors in this community!
