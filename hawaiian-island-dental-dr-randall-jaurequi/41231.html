What About Bone Loss?

One of the most reassuring aspects of All-on-4 is that many patients with bone loss still qualify. The angled placement of the back implants lets us use existing bone more effectively, which often means treatment without extensive grafting.

Every smile is unique, however. In some cases Dr. J may recommend minor bone grafting for added support, or a sinus lift if bone height is limited in the upper jaw. If either is needed, he ll explain it clearly so you feel comfortable and informed.

Health and Lifestyle Considerations

Active gum disease should be treated before implants are placed. Conditions like uncontrolled diabetes, heavy tobacco use, or certain medications can affect healing, so be open about your full health picture during your consultation.

The good news: many of these factors can be managed, and Dr. J will let you know honestly whether All-on-4 or a different solution gives you the best long-term result

Frequently Asked Questions

What are All-on-4 implants?

All-on-4 is a full-arch tooth replacement that uses four dental implants to support a fixed set of new teeth upper, lower, or both. It s an alternative to traditional dentures and to placing an individual implant for every missing tooth.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

Are All-on-4 implants permanent?

Yes they are fixed in place and only Dr. J can remove them for service. They are designed as a long-term solution for patients who want to be done with removable dentures.

What is recovery like?

Most patients are comfortable returning to light daily activities within a day or two. You ll start with a softer diet while the implants integrate, then gradually return to all the foods you love.

How do I care for my All-on-4 teeth?

Brush twice a day, clean carefully around the prosthesis using the techniques our team demonstrates, and keep up with regular exams and cleanings every six months.

Will my insurance cover All-on-4?

Coverage varies widely. We ll review your benefits with you, and if you re uninsured we can help you finance treatment through Cherry. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.
