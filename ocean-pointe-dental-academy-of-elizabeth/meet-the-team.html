Meet The Team Meet The Team Newton Peña, Owner and Instructor

Newton A. Pena is a seasoned financial professional and strategic advisor with a proven track record of success across diverse sectors, including financial services and dental practice management. With a career spanning nearly three decades, Newton has consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership and analytical skills, contributing to significant achievements in both corporate and entrepreneurial environments.

Newton’s academic journey began at Hunter College in New York, where he earned a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science in 1995. This foundation in technology paved the way for his initial career path. Furthering his expertise, Newton obtained a Master of Science in Telecommunication Management from Stevens Institute of Technology in 2000, equipping him with advanced knowledge and strategic insight into the telecommunications industry.

Newton’s career began with AT&T in Basking Ridge, NJ, where he held various roles from 1995 to 2005, including Senior Operations Manager and Senior Technical Associate. His work involved optimizing operational processes and managing technical systems, setting the stage for his future success.

In December 2005, Newton transitioned to Cingular Wireless as a Senior Vendor Relations Manager. Here, he was instrumental in managing vendor relations, improving key performance metrics, and achieving substantial cost savings for the company. His adeptness at renegotiating contracts and enhancing operational efficiency was noteworthy.

Newton’s career progressed as he joined AT&T Corp. in June 2007, where he continued to excel in vendor management, overseeing major centers and driving improvements in vendor performance. His leadership contributed to reduced attrition rates and improved operational metrics.

In March 2008, Newton ventured into entrepreneurship with Elite Smile, LLC, a dental practice in Elizabeth, NJ. As the principal strategist, he expanded the practice by acquiring a second location to serve a large Hispanic community. His initiatives led to impressive growth, including a 275 % increase in new patient flow and a 300 % rise in both collections and production. Newton also modernized the office’s IT infrastructure and implemented data-driven management systems, showcasing his versatility and strategic vision.

Since November 2016, Newton has been a Financial Services Professional at New York Life Insurance Company in Saddle Brook, NJ. His role involves conducting comprehensive financial needs analyses, developing tailored financial plans, and educating clients on a range of financial products. Fluent in Spanish, Newton excels in building client relationships and navigating complex financial landscapes.

Newton is renowned for his expertise in financial planning, customer relations, and strategic management. His skills encompass financial needs analysis, compliance, sales proficiency, and relationship management. He holds several licenses, including Series 6, Series 63, and Life and Health licenses across multiple states.

Newton A. Pena’s career is a testament to his adaptability, leadership, and commitment to excellence. His diverse experience, from managing large-scale operations to running a successful business and advising clients on financial matters, highlights his comprehensive skill set and ongoing impact across industries.

Dr. Claudia M. Rodríguez-Peña is an accomplished and dedicated dentist with a career spanning over 25 years, distinguished by her exceptional skill in a broad range of dental disciplines and her unwavering commitment to patient care. Based in Elizabeth, NJ, Dr. Rodríguez-Peña has garnered a reputation for excellence in both restorative and cosmetic dentistry, as well as advanced orthodontics.

Before establishing Elite Smile LLC, Dr. Rodríguez-Peña was the owner and primary dentist at

Claudia M. Rodríguez-Peña, DDS PC from 2001 to 2013. Her practice was recognized for its comprehensive approach to dental care, including urgent and surgical treatments, as well as her proficiency in managing complex orthodontic cases. Her role involved direct patient care, coordination with specialists, and staff training, demonstrating her leadership and collaborative skills.

Dr. Rodríguez-Peña’s career began at Kearny Health Center, where she worked from 1998 to 2003, providing a wide range of dental services and collaborating with clinic staff to enhance patient education. She also gained valuable experience at Torres Dental Group (1998-2002) and Dentista Hispana (1997-2003), where she honed her skills in general dentistry, including extractions, root canals, and prosthodontics.

Dr. Rodríguez-Peña earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from New York

University (1994-1997) and Universidad Nacional de Colombia (1984-1991). She further advanced her expertise with specialized orthodontic training from Roundeau Seminars (1999- 2000) and Lightforce America (2000-2002), enhancing her proficiency in orthodontics and functional appliances.

Her professional skills include a wide range of dental procedures, from cosmetic smile makeovers to advanced implant placement and periodontal care. Dr. Rodríguez-Peña is known for her strong interpersonal skills, attention to detail, and dedication to patient-centered care.

Outside of her professional endeavors, Dr. Rodríguez-Peña enjoys traveling, reading, and spending time with her family. Her career reflects a commitment to both personal and professional growth, ensuring that she remains at the forefront of dental care and continues to provide exemplary service to her patients.
