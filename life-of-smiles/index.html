The practice

Life of Smiles brings caring and fun pediatric dental care to every child so they feel safe, confident, and excited about their smile! We’ve been doing it for 50 years and counting.

Finding the right dentist for your child is a big deal — and we don’t take that lightly. At Life of Smiles, your child’s first visit is about so much more than just checking teeth. It’s about getting to know your family, making your little one feel safe and excited, and showing you exactly why Sonoma County families trust Dr. Bell and Dr. Vo with their kids’ smiles. We can’t wait to meet you!
