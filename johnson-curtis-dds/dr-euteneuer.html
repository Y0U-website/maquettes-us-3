About Dr. Euteneuer

Dr. Euteneuer earned her undergraduate degree in Biology from Minnesota State University Moorhead and graduated from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in May 2026.

Coming Home

Dr. Euteneuer’s interest in dentistry started early, inspired by her mother’s career as a dental assistant. Originally from the St. Cloud area, she’s glad to be back in the community she knows and loves. We’re confident she’ll provide exceptional, personalized care for local families.

I enjoy that dentistry allows me to combine creativity with patient care and am passionate about creating a positive and comfortable experience for my patients.

We’re delighted to support her as she builds her career and forms lasting relationships with her patients in Sauk Rapids.

Beyond the Dental Chair

Outside the office, Dr. Euteneuer enjoys traveling with her husband, playing tennis, crafting, trying new restaurants, and catching a good show. The rest of her time goes to her family, two cats, and dog.
