Accepting New Pediatric Dental Patients

At Olde Orchard Pediatric Dentistry , we do our best to schedule appointments as soon as time permits and at your convenience. Preschool children should be seen in the morning because they tend to be well-rested and, therefore, more cooperative - this allows our staff to work more leisurely with the child for their comfort. School-aged children requiring extensive work should also be scheduled in the morning for the same reason. While we understand that missing school is a concern, it can be kept to a minimum when regular dental care is sustained.

Please do not hesitate to call or text with any dental care or appointment questions.

Scheduling a Dental Appointment

Appointment times are reserved exclusively for each patient; therefore, please notify our office at least two business days before your appointment if you cannot keep your appointment. Other patients needing our care could be scheduled if we have sufficient time to notify them. We understand and realize that emergencies occur, but we ask for your assistance in this regard. You may be charged a broken appointment fee if you do not call to cancel and fail to show up as scheduled.

Your Child’s First Visit

During your child’s first visit, we’ll ask you to fill out a medical history form, vital in developing their treatment plan. Next, we’ll examine your child’s teeth, gums, and oral tissues. We will take x-rays if needed and examine their bite. Later, using our findings from the exam, we will determine a treatment plan and go over it together.

If you have any questions about our practice, please call or text us anytime. We welcome your family to the practice and look forward to meeting soon.
