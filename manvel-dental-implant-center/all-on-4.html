Restore Your Missing Teeth With All-On-4 Dental Implants

Most people must realize that four implants are all it takes to replace all your teeth at the top or bottom. With the advancement of dental technology, replacing the entire top or bottom row of teeth with implants has never been more straightforward, convenient, or cost-effective!

With TEETH-IN-A-DAY, patients receive fully functioning teeth in one single procedure! In addition, this procedure can be performed on single or multiple missing teeth. This way, you will immediately receive a temporary fixed prosthesis, eliminating the need to go without teeth for a while!

The Patient Experience

An All-on-Four® dental implant treatment begins with a preliminary consultation with Dr. Collins, who determines the best approach that is best suited for you. After exceptional guides and temporary teeth are made, patients schedule the surgery with us.

A patient’s teeth are removed from their dental arch, and Dr. Collins places dental implants during an All-on-Four® treatment. We can offer the procedure through an outpatient surgery center for the patient’s comfort. The surgery takes around an hour and a half. It can be performed under local or I.V. sedation.

With All-on-Four® dental implants, patients can receive a same-day treatment solution! Dr. Collins can connect new, transitional teeth several hours after the implants are placed, which the patients will use for four to five months until the implants become stronger. Dr. Collins then trades a permanent hybrid set of teeth at the end of this period.

All-on-Four® dental implant is a treatment concept that looks and feels like your own natural teeth and can last for years. So if you are missing or decaying teeth, this is the solution you need. If you want to learn more and schedule an initial appointment, contact Manvel Dental & Implant Center by phone or email.

Benefits Of All-On-4 Dental Implants

A high success rate and the ability to virtually eliminate the need for bone grafting make All-on-Four® implants the best choice for patients who need to replace almost all of their natural teeth!

You will have a full function after the procedure, unlike traditional dental implants that require multiple appointments over 3-6 months.
