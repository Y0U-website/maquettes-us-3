Treatments

All Ages

We recommend early orthodontic evaluations for children beginning at age 7. Some patients may benefit from early interceptive treatment to help address issues early before they become bigger problems, while others will be ready for treatment down the road. Schedule your child for a consultation today so we can evaluate their unique orthodontic needs.

We offer braces and Invisalign for our teen patients, helping them achieve beautiful, healthy smiles that build confidence as they enter adulthood. We also educate our patients about the importance of retainer wear and how to maintain optimal alignment and aesthetics long-term.

Orthodontic treatment among adults is more popular than ever. Whether this is your first time pursuing orthodontic treatment or you are in need of a touch-up, we have a variety of treatment options to fit your lifestyle.

Braces

Traditional metal braces are one of our most popular treatment options for children and adults alike. The MicroArch brackets we use in our office have a smaller profile than many other metal braces, making them more comfortable and easier to keep clean throughout treatment.

Our clear Innovation-C ceramic braces are convenient and practical just like traditional metal braces, while offering a more discreet aesthetic.

Retainers

Retainers are crucial for maintaining your smile after treatment. We offer various options, including fixed and removable retainers, tailored to your needs and lifestyle. Our team will help you choose a comfortable solution that fits your routine.
