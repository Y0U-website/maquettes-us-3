0 Who we are Welcome to Malone Family Dentistry where we smile, you smile too! At Malone Family Dentistry, we are dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care for patients of all ages. From routine cleanings and preventive care to cosmetic and restorative treatments, our team is committed to helping every member of your family achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.

We believe visiting the dentist should be comfortable and stress-free. Our modern office uses advanced technology and personalized care to ensure every patient receives the best possible experience.

Born and raised in West Virginia, Dr. Malone is proud to serve his local community. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Zoology from Brigham Young University and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University. Dr. Malone is a dedicated family man, married to his wife Sarah, and together they have six wonderful children. In his free time, he enjoys spending quality time with his family and golfing.
