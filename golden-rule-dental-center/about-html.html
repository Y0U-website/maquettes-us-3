Grayson Tucker Hunley, DDS

Grayson “Tucker” Hunley, DDS graduated from Halls High School in Knoxville, TN where he competed on the swim team and achieved the BSA rank of Eagle Scout with Troop 506. Growing up, he saw his dentist-dad, Allen Hunley, DDS, greet patients out in public who would approach him and compliment him on how gentle he was and thank him for how much he had helped their smiles. Seeing how practicing dentistry could help so many people in valuable ways, Dr. Tucker worked hard through school to make that dream a reality.

Dr. Tucker looks forward to building life-long relationships with his patients and treating them like family. “Just like Jesus has restored my soul’s relationship with God, I want to help people restore their smiles to be healthier, feel more confident, and receive encouragement to take good care of themselves and never forget how valuable they are. I hope that by doing so more people may know Jesus’ saving power.”
