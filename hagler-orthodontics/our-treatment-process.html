Adult Treatment

Do you think you are too old for orthodontic treatment? Think again. Technology is available today that is attractive to adults that simply did not exist when you were young. These include clear braces and Invisalign aligners. Common orthodontic problems in adults are crowding, space between teeth, overbite, or drifting of teeth after an extraction.
