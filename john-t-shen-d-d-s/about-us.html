About Dr John shen

Dr. John Shen was born and raised in Taipei, Taiwan. He and his family immigrated to Cupertino, CA where he graduated from Monta Vista High School. Despite the language and cultural barrier, Dr. Shen was accepted to the University of California, Berkeley, where he obtained his Bachelor of Science in Microbial Biology.
