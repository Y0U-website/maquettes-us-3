Single Dental Implants

A single dental implant is an ideal solution for patients who have lost one tooth and are looking for a permanent, stable replacement. In this procedure, we place a single titanium metal post into the jawbone, which functions just like the natural tooth root.

Later on, we attach an abutment and a custom dental crown that perfectly matches the rest of your teeth. This implant not only restores chewing function but also prevents bone loss and maintains the alignment of surrounding teeth.

Multiple Dental Implants

For patients missing more than one tooth, our clinic in Duncanville offers multiple dental implants as a fixed alternative to partial dentures. If you have two or more adjacent missing teeth in a row, we can replace them with a custom-designed implant bridge.

It involves placing an implant post on either end of the gap, which supports and stabilizes the bridge. As a result, you can have a restoration that looks and functions like your natural teeth, without the need for adhesives or clasps.

Benefits of Choosing Single and Multiple Implants

Whether you need replacement for one or more teeth, dental implants can be an ideal solution as they offer:

  • Natural look and feel: You can have a custom-crafted restoration that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Long-term replacement: Unlike removable dentures, implants are more durable and meant to last for decades. Improved function: They restore your ability to chew, bite, and speak comfortably.
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure: Once they integrate with the bone, they prevent further bone loss and facial sagging.
  • Easy maintenance: You can care for your implants just like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups.

Single and Multiple Dental Implants Procedure When considering your candidacy for single or multiple dental implants, our Duncanville specialist will assess your remaining teeth, bone thickness, and overall oral health. We will also take 3D scans to determine the best type of implant replacement for your needs.

During the procedure, we will surgically place one or more implants in your jawbone, depending on the number of missing teeth. Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implants will fuse with the bone and create a stable foundation for your replacement teeth. When the healing period is complete, we will place the final restoration, which is carefully matched to your natural tooth color and shape for a beautiful smile.
