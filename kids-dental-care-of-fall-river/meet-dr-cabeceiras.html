Meet our orthodontist Dr. David Cabeceiras

David Cabeceiras, D.M.D.

Dr. David Cabeceiras is a Board Certified Orthodontist and has practiced the specialty of orthodontics in Massachusetts for over 25 years. Prior to dental school, Dr. Cabeceiras received a Bachelor of Science degree from Providence College. He continued his education and earned a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University and an orthodontic specialty certificate from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he continues to serve as an orthodontic faculty member since 1991 as a Clinical Assistant Professor.

He treats patients at our Fall River and Middleboro offices and is passionate about creating healthy, confident smiles for children and teens.

“No two cases are alike,” he says. “The challenge of diagnosis and customizing each treatment plan is what I love most about orthodontics.”

One of Dr. Cabeceiras’ proudest moments was reconnecting with a former patient who had been crowned Miss Massachusetts and personally thanked him for the smile that helped her shine.
