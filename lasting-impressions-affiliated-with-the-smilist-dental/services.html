Your first visit will include a complete examination of your teeth, gums and mouth. Digital X-rays will be taken as part of the examination to help the doctor fully assess your dental condition and determine the care you need.

Your first visit will also include a review of your medical history, provided to us on the forms you will complete. Learning about your general health, medical history and any medications you may be taking is very important for your dentist prior to undergoing any procedure. The health information you provide will help us choose the safest, most effective methods of dental care for you.

During your visit to our practice, your needs will be met by our friendly, outgoing team as you relax in the comfort of our state-of-the-art reception area and offices.
