What Is Surgical Orthodontics?

Surgical orthodontics (orthognathic surgery) is used to correct severe jaw discrepancies and complex bite problems that cannot be resolved through orthodontics alone. By repositioning the jaws, this advanced procedure improves both function and aesthetics – creating a healthier, more balances and lasting smile.

Surgical orthodontics may be recommended to correct:

  • Severe overbites, underbites, or crossbites
  • Jaw misalignment that affects chewing, speaking, or breathing
  • Open bites unresponsive to braces or aligners alone
  • Facial asymmetry or uneven jaw growth

The Benefits of Surgical Orthodontics

Surgical orthodontics provides long-term, comprehensive improvements to both oral health and appearance:

  • Improved bite and function : Restores efficient chewing, speaking, and breathing.
  • Balanced facial aesthetics : Enhances symmetry and natural facial proportional.
  • Healthier outcomes : Reduces tooth wear, TMJ strain, and jaw discomfort.
  • Long-term stability : Ensures lasting results when combined with orthodontic care.
  • Comprehensive correction : Addresses skeletal imbalances that braces or aligners alone cannot fix.

Your Path to a Healthier Bite and Smile

At Glow Dental, we coordinate every stage of your treatment for a smooth and successful experience:

Step 1: Free Consultation & Comprehensive Evaluation

Your journey begins with a complimentary consultation. We use advanced 3D imaging and diagnostics to evaluate your teeth, bite, and jaw structure. Our orthodontist will review your smile goals, explain your options, and confirm if surgical orthodontics is right for you.

Step 2: Orthodontic Preparation

Braces or clear aligners are placed before surgery to align the teeth and prepare for proper jaw positioning post-surgery.

Step 3: Orthognathic Surgery

In partnership with a skilled oral and maxillofacial surgeon, the jaw is repositioned to correct the skeletal imbalance. The surgery is performed under general anesthesia in a hospital or surgical center, followed by a brief recovery period.

Step 4: Post-Surgical Orthodontic Refinement

After healing, orthodontic treatment resumes to fine-tune tooth alignment and ensure a stable, well-balances bite.

Step 5: Smile Completion & Retention

When treatment is complete, we gently remove your braces or aligners to reveal your new smile. Custom retainers are provided to preserve alignment – most patients wear them at night for maintenance.
