The practice

Our Mission is simple: to provide you with the finest endodontic treatment available anywhere.

When it comes to our teeth, nothing is as good as the real thing! We also understand how difficult it is for some individuals to have dental work done, especially a root canal. We want you to enjoy your visit with us, so we strive to provide you with a relaxing, comfortable experience.

At League City Endo, Dr. Schaefer is combining advanced technology with the best practices and approaches that she has gained over more than two decades and from across the country. In doing so, she has created an endodontic option that offers patients the choice of root canal therapy in a comfortable, modern, and patient-centric setting.

We pledge to work with your dentist to achieve the best possible outcome, and welcome any feedback from you about your time with us so that we can make your experience the best it can be. This is how we maintain our reputation for excellence in endodontics!

Dr. Kate (Katrina) Schaefer began her career in the biotechnology industry. Although it was a challenging and fulfilling career, she missed having direct and positive interactions with people. So after two years working in advanced medical research labs, she applied for and was accepted into the prestigious University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine.

Her broad experience has accumulated through her work and studies in major medical centers from Boston to Washington, DC to Virginia (before moving to Texas in 2012). These moves – from dental school to two advanced residencies to private practice – have underscored one common theme: whether on the East Coast or the Gulf Coast, patients are not excited to get root canal therapy!
