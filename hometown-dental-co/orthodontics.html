Comprehensive Orthodontic Services Our practice offers a variety of orthodontic treatments, designed to cater to different age groups, lifestyle needs, and dental conditions. Here’s an overview of the orthodontic services available at Hometown Dental Co.:

Traditional Braces

As a tried and true method, traditional braces are extremely effective for correcting complex tooth and jaw issues.

Using metal brackets and wires, this treatment method is ideal for patients needing significant adjustments to achieve a perfect alignment.

REMOVABLE APPLiaNCES

Removable orthodontic appliances are designed to be easily inserted and removed. Common examples include clear aligners, expanders, spring retainers and acrylic plates with wires or springs. These appliances are usually used to correct minor dental problems such as slight tooth misalignment, spacing issues, minor bite corrections, and to maintain teeth position after more complex orthodontic treatment.

They are custom-made for a comfortable fit and offer the flexibility of being removable for important social occasions.

CLEAR ALIGNERS

Clear Aligners are a highly sought-after treatment among our patients. These clear aligners are virtually invisible, offering an aesthetic and comfortable alternative to metal braces.

Perfect for adults and teens, clear aligners are removable, making it easier to eat, brush, and floss, and they require fewer visits to the dentist compared to traditional braces.

Functional appliances

CLEAR BRACES Clear braces, also known as ceramic braces, work similarly to traditional metal braces but use tooth-colored or transparent braces making them much less noticeable than metal braces . Both types effectively correct a wide range of dental issues, but clear braces offer a subtler aesthetic, especially appealing to adults and teens looking for discreet orthodontic treatment.

Clear braces blend with natural tooth color, making them significantly less visible than traditional metal braces.

RETAINERS

Orthodontic retainers are custom-made devices, either removable or fixed, that are used to maintain the position of teeth after active orthodontic treatment like braces or aligners. Their primary benefit is preventing teeth from moving. Retainers help preserve the your teeth’s position and can also assist in minor bite corrections or preventing issues like tongue thrusting.

What to Expect During Treatment

During your initial visit, we’ll conduct a thorough examination of your teeth and jaws and discuss your dental history.

This may include taking X-rays or creating a digital scan of your teeth. Once we’ve established a detailed understanding of your orthodontic treatment needs, we’ll discuss the treatment options available to you and outline the steps, duration, and cost associated with each.

Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit Hometown Dental Co. for regular adjustments or follow-ups to monitor your progress and make any necessary changes to your treatment plan.

Our team will also provide you with tips on how to care for your braces or aligners and maintain good oral hygiene.
