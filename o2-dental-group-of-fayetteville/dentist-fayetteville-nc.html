Emergency Dentist in Fayetteville, NC

O2 Dental Group of Fayetteville keeps same-day availability for emergencies whenever possible. We move quickly to find the problem, get you out of pain, and explain what comes next. Infections in particular deserve urgency: a localized abscess treated promptly is straightforward, but left to spread it becomes more complex, more expensive, and occasionally a genuine medical emergency.

  • Severe toothaches and dental infections
  • Broken, cracked, or chipped teeth
  • Knocked-out teeth (time-sensitive — call immediately)
  • Facial swelling or abscess
  • Lost crowns or fillings
  • Broken dentures
  • Wisdom tooth pain
  • Dental trauma
  • Pain after recent dental work

Invisalign in Fayetteville for Adults & Teens

Straighter teeth without the look of metal brackets — Invisalign is a discreet way to fix crowding, spacing, and bite concerns for both adults and teens. O2 Dental Group of Fayetteville is an Invisalign provider and offers orthodontic evaluations.

At your consultation we use digital treatment planning to map what treatment would actually achieve before you commit to anything. If Invisalign is the right clinical fit, we’ll show you the process, a realistic timeline, and your financial options. If another approach serves you better, we’ll tell you that instead.

The clear aligners are removable for eating and brushing — no dietary restrictions and no broken-bracket emergencies. Most adult cases run about 12 to 18 months.
