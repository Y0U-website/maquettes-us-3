Braces

Braces have come a long way, and they remain one of the most reliable ways to create a healthy, confident smile.

We offer a range of braces types, including clear braces , Iconix® champagne-gold braces, WildSmiles® braces, and traditional metal braces .

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

We offer Invisalign clear aligners for kids, teens, and adults in Sammamish or Issaquah, WA.

These virtually invisible, custom-designed aligners fit comfortably over your teeth to gradually move them into their ideal position.

Because they’re removable, all of your favorite foods are still on the table, and you can take them out for special occasions, photos, sports, and brushing your teeth.

Combined with our virtual treatment monitoring , Invisalign makes it even easier to fit orthodontic treatment into your busy lifestyle.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

A child’s first orthodontic visit is all about understanding how their smile is growing and developing.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial evaluation around age 7 , but that doesn’t necessarily mean treatment will begin right away.

Many children simply benefit from periodic monitoring, while others may benefit from early treatment to help guide jaw growth, create space for developing teeth, or address concerns before they become more complex.

Orthodontics for Teens

The teen years are often an ideal time for orthodontic treatment because the smile and jaws are still developing.

Whether your teen chooses braces or Invisalign, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan designed around their needs, goals, and lifestyle.

Our goal is to create beautiful results while making treatment feel as comfortable and convenient as possible along the way.

Orthodontics for Adults

The confidence that comes with a healthy, beautiful smile is always worth pursuing.

Many adults choose orthodontic treatment to improve their smile, address shifting teeth, or finally tackle concerns they’ve been putting off for years.

With discreet options like clear aligners and clear braces, and convenient virtual care with Invisalign, treatment can fit comfortably into your professional and personal life.

Retainers

Once you achieve your new smile, you’ll want to keep it in its best condition for life. A beautiful smile is worth protecting, and wearing your retainers consistently is how you ensure that.

After braces or clear aligner treatment, you’ll receive a set of custom retainers that keep your smile beautifully aligned long after treatment ends.

Dentofacial Orthopedics

Orthodontics isn’t just about straightening teeth. In growing children, it can also help guide the development of the jaws and facial structures.

Dentofacial orthopedics uses specialized appliances and carefully timed treatment to encourage balanced growth and create the space needed for healthy alignment.

When recommended, these treatments work alongside your child’s natural development to help create a strong foundation for a healthy smile.

Orthodontic Appliances

Some smiles need a little extra help along the way.

In certain cases, we may recommend a specialized appliance to help guide jaw growth, create space for developing teeth, improve bite alignment, or support more efficient treatment.

From palatal expanders and TADs to other growth-guiding appliances, each recommendation is carefully selected based on your unique needs and treatment goals.

MARPE (Miniscrew-Assisted Rapid Palatal Expansion)

MARPE is an advanced, non-surgical appliance used for older teens and adults who need to widen the upper jaw after growth has slowed or completed.

It’s a fully customized appliance recommended when there isn’t enough space for proper tooth alignment, the bite is too narrow, or airway concerns are present.

MARPE helps create the space needed for a healthier bite, improved breathing, and a more balanced smile.

Surgical Orthodontics

Some orthodontic concerns involve differences in jaw position that cannot be fully corrected with braces or aligners alone.

When jaw surgery is recommended, you’re not navigating the process on your own.

We’ll help connect you with the right expert, coordinate your care, and guide you through every step of the process so you can move forward with confidence.

Airway/Growth-Focused Orthodontics

As part of every orthodontic evaluation, we look at more than just the position of the teeth.

We assess jaw development, facial balance, bite function, and overall growth patterns to help determine the most appropriate treatment approach.

In some cases, this may include recommending expansion or other orthodontic therapies designed to create space, support healthy development, and improve how the teeth and jaws work together.
