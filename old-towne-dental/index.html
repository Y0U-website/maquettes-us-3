We recommend visiting us every six months for a routine dental cleaning and exam . Regular checkups help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other oral health problems.

Common signs of gum disease (periodontal disease) include bleeding gums , swollen or tender gums , bad breath , receding gums , and loose teeth . Early detection during your dental visits at Old Towne Dental can prevent more serious issues.
